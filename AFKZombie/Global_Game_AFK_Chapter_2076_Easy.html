Chapter 2076

Easy

From original Chinese Chapter 2077 · 轻松

The teammates exchanged looks.

They'd cleared an A-class mission in only half an hour, and it had felt effortless—easier than some elite-monster fights they'd done in the past.

Nothing like it had ever happened to them.

“Hahahaha! I had no idea Brother Fang Heng was this powerful. We really have you to thank! Maybe next time we can try something harder,” Lindro said cheerfully.

He knew perfectly well that Fang Heng was the reason everything had gone so smoothly. He wanted to build a lasting friendship and, ideally, form a regular party with him.

He just feared Fang Heng would reject them for being too weak.

With strength like that, no wonder he'd been willing to enter Eternal Holy City alone!

“Sure.”

The artifact's simulated identity had its own fully simulated friends list. Fang Heng exchanged survival-radio contact information with Lindro.

“Almost forgot—this is your first visit to Eternal Holy City, right? Let me explain how the place is laid out.”

Lindro led him away from the teleportation formation and toward the center of the hall.

“Eternal Holy City looks like a high-level game world, but we trial participants are restricted to a tiny part of it.”

“The lower level is divided into four sections based around a central courtyard. We're in the courtyard now.” He pointed right. “The eastern section is for rest and recovery. After a battle, you can heal and recuperate there quickly.”

“The western section is a library. You can research some Holy Court material, although the library is small and the collection is incomplete. Plenty of players spend their spare time there reading and resting, earning a bit of academic experience.”

“The south contains the Temple of Zeus and training rooms. You can advance your skills and learn basic techniques from NPCs.”

“Finally, to the north is the mission hall we just used. Most players form parties before entering it.”

Fang Heng frowned. “Where's the exit? Can't we leave this area?”

Leave?

Lindro found the question odd. “Why would you want to?”

Crusader Trial Talismans weren't extremely rare, but obtaining one still took some effort. Normal players entering Eternal Holy City wanted to use every minute of their seventy-two hours.

Why did Fang Heng seem in no hurry at all? Was he actually thinking about going sightseeing outside?

“Nothing in particular. I just wanted to look around the world, maybe find some unusual NPCs.”

“I'm afraid that isn't possible,” Sak, the team's healer, interjected. “Eternal Holy City is a training base the Divine Realm built in a high-level game world to cultivate Crusaders. I've never heard of anyone being allowed to leave the designated area and explore outside.”

Fang Heng inwardly groaned.

He'd assumed Eternal Holy City was similar to Punoze, but the two were very different.

Players could roam freely in Punoze. In Eternal Holy City, their permitted area was tiny.

He'd have to find another way out, probably through the trials themselves.

Accepting a solo mission meant entering the sacred domain beneath the statue, where his identity could be exposed. So he still needed to work in a team.

He'd thought completing a mission quickly would leave plenty of time to search its world. Instead, completion triggered an immediate forced return.

This time he'd need a different strategy.

Fang Heng quickly analyzed his options, then turned toward Lindro.

“How much longer can you stay in Eternal Holy City? Want to keep going?”

“Keep going?” Lindro looked puzzled. “Doing what?”

“Another mission.”

The others quickly understood.

Normally, a single trial was exhausting. Players consumed potions and other items, equipment suffered wear, skills went on cooldown, and everything had to be replenished.

Hours of continuous concentration also took a heavy toll.

But this time…

With Fang Heng in the party, they'd barely used anything at all.

“Of course! We can go again right now!” Sak answered eagerly.

What a joke. They'd finally found someone who could carry them. They weren't letting go!

“Then perhaps we should raise the difficulty…” Lindro looked at Fang Heng, considering the option.

“Let's stick with A-class. I want to become more familiar with how the trials work.”

“Fine.”

They nodded.

An S-class mission awarded four to eight times as many points and experience as an A-class one, but its success rate was lower and the resources consumed were greater.

Since Fang Heng cleared A-class missions so quickly, running more of them was a perfectly reasonable approach.

Besides, everyone was curious whether his astonishing performance against the serpent really had been, as he claimed, a fortunate matchup.

…

[Hint: You have entered an unknown game world. Obtain more information to determine its coordinates.]

[Hint: Your Crusader Trial Mark remains active. All behavior will be monitored and recorded.]

[Hint: You have crossed into another plane. The Divine Realm's pursuit team will attempt to locate you again in 9 + 15 hours (the additional delay is caused by the artifact's ability).]

Fang Heng had been paying close attention to the pursuit countdown. Seeing it return to twenty-four hours, he pondered the implications.

It seemed the Holy Court's tracking was tied to different planes.

If he kept jumping between the same few planes, perhaps the pursuers would be able to pinpoint him faster each time.

He needed to hurry!

[Hint: Your party has triggered a trial mission—Demon Cavern Trial.]

[Mission: Demon Cavern Trial.]

[Description: Half a month ago, a large number of demons emerged from a cavern in a maple forest. They have been ravaging the human world. Faithful believers prayed to the Divine Realm for rescue. Eliminate the demons in the cavern.]

[Objective: Kill normal demons (0/50,000) and elite demons (0/100).]

[Time remaining: 6 hours.]

This trial was different. They had a full six hours!

It actually looked promising.

Fang Heng read the description carefully and approved of what he saw.

The objective was simply to kill a fixed number of monsters.

In other words: farming.

He could control the mission's completion percentage.

Once they reached 99%, he could stop attacking, explore the surrounding world, and search for another way out before finishing the mission.

— End of Chapter 2076 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Trial limits. The Demon Cavern Trial requires 50,000 ordinary monsters and 100 elites over six hours.
  • Holy City layout. Trial participants can access only a small designated area, not the entire Eternal Holy City.
  • Progress control. Fang Heng plans to pause close to 99% progress so that he can explore the trial world.
