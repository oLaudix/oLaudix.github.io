Chapter 3004

Transmutation

From Chinese Chapter 3005 · 转化

By that reasoning...

The mark really might have been left on Fang Heng by Xia Xi.

To cultivate him as a Newborn of the Ouroboros Seed.

Whatever the truth, it was something valuable, and it had now fused with his Titan bloodline.

He wondered whether the Titans had any more similar marks he could absorb.

Fang Heng pondered briefly before pulling his wandering thoughts back on track.

Forget all that for now!

The temple hall still contained a tremendous amount of energy.

He'd absorb every last drop first and study it later!

Whoosh!

He activated his mental power once more, drawing the hall's energy into his body!

Huh?

This time, Fang Heng quickly noticed something unusual.

The energy he'd absorbed earlier had flowed directly into his body, remodeling and strengthening his ancient Titan bloodline.

Now, however, it entered his body and rapidly moved to his Sea of Consciousness, where it settled.

A special energy without any attribute.

A thought suddenly occurred to Fang Heng.

Could this attribute-less energy be 'Qi'?

That's right!

According to the Titans' energy theory, attribute-less Qi could be converted into other types of energy, and other energies could likewise be converted into Qi.

Fang Heng tried converting Qi into sacred energy.

No good.

It wouldn't work!

He couldn't complete the conversion at all.

Where was the problem?

Fang Heng frowned.

Whatever. The entire hall was filled with Qi energy.

He could work out how to use it later. It was bound to be valuable, so first he'd swallow every last bit!

Whoosh!

Under the stimulation of Fang Heng's mental power, the jewel at the center of the temple glowed even more brilliantly!

Its energy output increased tenfold in an instant!

Absorb it!

Fang Heng failed to notice more teams of players arriving one after another while he focused on absorbing Qi.

Another Eastern Federation squad followed the guidance of the Key of Apocalypse into the hall.

Dr. Li Zhixuan, one of their researchers, happened to be among them.

Unlike the others, Dr. Li Zhixuan concentrated on the hall's layout and decorations.

Surprise appeared in her eyes, quickly giving way to a grave expression.

...

Meanwhile, the Southern Federation group left the temple and entered a hidden side hall elsewhere on the plain.

When they had first followed the directions to the temple, the main door had been closed.

They searched nearby and discovered the concealed side chamber.

At its center, atop a high stone seat, a giant nearly five meters tall sat upright with his eyes closed. His body had almost merged with the stone, making him look like a statue.

"Ancestor!"

Dorian led the Southern Federation players into the hall. They immediately dropped to their knees before the statue and kowtowed.

The statue slowly awakened. Layers of rock fell away as its eyes gradually opened.

"What is it?"

"Ancestor, a group of people forced their way into the sanctuary you left behind. They showed no respect for the rules, destroyed the guardian Titan statues, barged into the hall to steal its energy, and even drove us out!"

The Southern Federation players kowtowed frantically, embellishing their accusations with plenty of added fuel.

The ancestral Titan Guardian listened, anger gathering on his face.

"Such a thing happened?"

The ancient Guardian had remained petrified for so long that he could no longer remember how much time had passed. Even his memories had grown hazy.

His purpose in remaining here was protection and inheritance.

If someone intended to destroy the legacy of the Titans...

Unforgivable!

"Take me to see."

Dorian was overjoyed. He rose and bowed respectfully. "Yes, Ancestor!"

...

Temple of Apocalypse.

The jewel hovering at the center of the hall released visible rings of scorching energy.

Most of that energy immediately gathered into a vortex above Fang Heng's head and was drawn swiftly into him.

The other players kept their distance, forming a broad ring around the temple's edge and absorbing whatever Fang Heng left behind.

"Clack, clack, clack..."

Hearing the metal door open again and heavy footsteps approaching from behind, the players opened their eyes.

What sort of monster was that?

Was it human too?

At the entrance, the Southern Federation players had returned.

Behind them stood a giant five meters tall. He resembled a human, but was more than twice as large and extraordinarily muscular.

A Titan?

The Eastern Federation members exchanged quick glances before turning toward Dr. Li Zhixuan.

She nodded slightly.

The Eastern Federation had begun investigating the Titans before anyone else and knew more about them. According to the evidence they'd gathered, this giant was very likely an ancestral Titan!

Seeing him finally confirmed their earlier conjectures!

Dorian swept his gaze around the hall and declared coldly, "This is humanity's ancestor, the Titan Guardian! Why aren't you paying your respects?"

Players from the other Federations and smaller teams were completely bewildered.

A Titan? Their ancestor?

What game was the Southern Federation playing now?

The Titan Guardian strode into the hall and immediately fixed his gaze on Fang Heng at its center.

"Hm?"

Surprise flashed in his eyes.

This inheritor...

How strange!

He could actually activate the Origin Light Jewel's power?

Had he already completed his bloodline awakening?

Then why could he still absorb so much Qi?

And at such a terrifying rate? Wasn't he afraid his Sea of Consciousness would burst?

The Titan spoke slowly. "Stop."

Sensing the newcomer, Fang Heng temporarily halted his absorption, opened his eyes, and turned to the Guardian.

"Young one, your abilities are remarkable. You've surprised me."

Fang Heng looked up without the slightest fear. "And who are you?"

"I am the Titan Guardian of this place. I came to this world to protect the branch of Titan bloodline living here, safeguard your people, and preserve the Land of Apocalypse until you possess the strength to protect yourselves."

The Titan stared at Fang Heng and said in a low voice, "You destroyed the guardian statues and oppressed members of your own race to seize their power. You are unfit to remain here. I require you to leave immediately."

Fang Heng curled his lip and glanced coldly at the Southern Federation group.

"Destroying guardian statues? Oppressing our own race? Is that what they told you?"

Dorian flushed and hurriedly interjected, "Guardian, don't waste words on him! He wants to destroy our inheritance and use the Holy Court's faith to enslave us! Please kill him!"
