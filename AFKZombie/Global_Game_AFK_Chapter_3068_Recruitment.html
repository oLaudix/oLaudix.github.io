Chapter 3068

Recruitment

From Chinese Chapter 3069 · 招募

The next day, Fang Heng and the others received their new identity papers. They used them to buy tickets and took a suspended train into the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's central district.

The city center glittered with lights and towering buildings. Compared to the slums, it might as well have been another world.

“The Noel Clan recruits at the beginning of every month. As it happens, that's tomorrow.”

One of Klaus's companions had gone to the reception desk of the Noel Clan's building to inquire. He returned looking excited and reported what he'd learned.

“Great. We'll stay here for one day and meet tomorrow.”

Among the seven overlords, the Noel Clan had a fairly good reputation. They were relatively neutral. Their strength was considered average among the Seven Domains, and they rarely sought attention, giving people the impression of being harmless, easygoing fellows.

Klaus and his friends had come here specifically to seek the Noel Clan's patronage.

“What about you, Fang Heng? Are you coming back with us?”

“No, I'll look around a little more.”

“All right.”

In high spirits, Fang Heng began wandering around the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

He had spent so long embroiled in intense confrontations and schemes that he rarely had the leisure to simply explore a city.

The bustling streets were lined mostly with shops buying and selling secondhand weapons and equipment, private training institutes, and assorted security companies.

One building caught Fang Heng's eye.

New Universe Advanced Vocational Training Center.

“Advanced...”

How advanced was advanced?

Fang Heng muttered to himself and walked inside.

A female employee of the Dikoni Clan, dressed in a low-cut blouse and a tight skirt, immediately rose from the reception desk and asked gently, “Sir, how may I help you?”

“Just looking around.”

“Of course. You may consult the self-service panels over there. If you have questions, please ask me.”

Women of the Dikoni Clan were exceptionally charming, and most species considered them blessed by the Goddess of Beauty.

The employee maintained a professional smile as she spoke.

But Fang Heng clearly sensed no trace of blood or vital energy within her body.

Listening carefully, he could even hear the clicking of mechanical gears inside her.

She was a mechanical life-form.

Fang Heng wasn't interested. He moved aside and opened a control panel to browse.

“Hm?”

Something stirred in his mind. When he activated the panel, his personal identification record appeared immediately.

According to it, he was from the slums, had paid no social security contributions, was twenty-two years old, possessed a basic education, and had no combat rating whatsoever.

Old Li had done a flawless job with the identity card.

Fang Heng silently praised him and looked through the interface again.

In the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, combat ratings had to be assessed under the academic and professional system jointly established by the capital's authorities.

The system was recognized throughout the universe.

Fang Heng examined it carefully.

What a coincidence.

The universe's power-ranking system was strikingly similar to that of the game.

Basic, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Master, Grandmaster, Transcendent, and finally Demi-God.

The difference was that each rank was further divided into lower, middle, and upper grades, rather than being expressed directly through numerical levels as in the game.

Fang Heng nodded thoughtfully.

To obtain a professional rating, one had to take an assessment at an authorized examination center and pay an examination fee.

Many jobs required applicants to possess a corresponding rating.

It was quite a professional setup.

Then there was training.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital offered free introductory courses and academic aptitude tests. Anyone could register at any time.

Once someone crossed the basic threshold, however, further academic study required paid enrollment.

The prices could only be described as astonishing.

Far too expensive.

A single thirty-minute training session for Grandmaster advancement cost roughly as much as an entire starship hold full of ordinary ore.

And starting at Grandmaster level, lessons were one-on-one and had to be booked in advance.

Even if you had the money, you might not find an instructor.

Fang Heng sighed inwardly.

Knowledge truly was priceless.

Speaking of which, was he qualified to teach necromancy himself?

Curious, Fang Heng quickly checked.

Hm?

He soon discovered something odd.

Most academic disciplines had counterparts in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, but necromancy, sacred studies, and Hell studies were nowhere to be found.

“Strange...”

“Sir, would you like to enroll in a course? We're holding opening promotions, and we work with the Clan of Evil. We have a powerful team of instructors who can meet any of your needs...”

“No, thank you.”

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital used Cosmic Coins, and he didn't have a single one.

...

The next day.

At the Noel Clan's headquarters.

The main hall of the Martial Soul Palace on the first floor was packed with warriors who had come to register.

The Noel Clan looked remarkably similar to humans, but they were not a pan-human species.

The main difference was their skin structure. They had evolved a powerful ability to absorb free energy from their surroundings and replenish themselves continuously.

When they became highly emotional, their skin even changed color.

An elder of the Noel Clan stood at the front of the platform. His face was aged, but his muscles were extraordinarily robust.

The old man said calmly, “The assessment is divided into two groups.”

“Those under thirty can join the Noel Clan's reserve talent program and receive years of training within the clan. This group's assessment focuses primarily on individual potential.”

Most species in the universe were long-lived, and with sufficient talent, even beginning to study academic disciplines at fifty wasn't necessarily too late—though success was comparatively rare.

Klaus and his young companions were eager to try.

In their home world, they were considered remarkably talented. To pursue their dreams, they had spent tremendous effort entering the Seven-Realm Martial Capital illegally, even though power within their own species was already within reach.

The future prosperity of their entire species rested on their shoulders.

If they succeeded here, their whole star system would benefit.

“Those who pass the assessment will receive rigorous training within the Noel Clan. It will last three years. Afterward, anyone who passes the final test will enter the Martial Palace as a member of the Noel Clan. I demand absolute loyalty. If you cannot offer it, leave now.”

The elder swept his piercing gaze across the crowd and declared, “Those who still wish to be assessed may enter through this passage.”

“Brother Fang Heng, we're heading in first. Good luck!”

The youths nodded farewell to Fang Heng and hurried off to take the assessment.

Before long, roughly two thirds of the hall had emptied. Those who remained were clearly warriors confident in their own strength.

None had been trained by the Noel Clan's Martial Palace. They were all self-taught outsiders.

“Next are the warriors the Noel Clan is recruiting from outside. I don't require undying loyalty from you. This is a transaction of benefits. But if you take our rewards and then betray us, the Noel Clan will make you pay.”

The elder's tone was grave. “You may have heard rumors about the Noel Clan lately. Several species have recently offered generous terms to recruit warriors, and many fighters formerly serving the Noel Clan have chosen to work with other races.”

— End of Chapter 3068 —
