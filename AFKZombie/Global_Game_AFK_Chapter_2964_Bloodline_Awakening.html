Chapter 2964

Bloodline Awakening

From Chinese Chapter 2965 · 血脉复苏

[Game Hint: Player is awakening their bloodline.]

[Game Hint: Current bloodline awakening progress: 0.06%.]

[Game Hint: Due to bloodline awakening, player has gained 0.1 base Strength.]

Fang Heng abruptly opened his eyes and looked up at the stone pillar.

The pillar was still releasing energy.

So the source of the energy was...

Fang Heng focused his perception on the flow of energy.

Looking closely, he could see small crystalline fragments embedded in the pillar.

The energy was seeping out of those fragments.

There wasn't much of it.

'Pitifully little' would be more accurate.

Even if he absorbed every last bit himself, it would raise his bloodline awakening progress by less than 10%.

Fang Heng closed his eyes and continued absorbing the energy.

A little more than ten minutes later, the pillar stopped releasing energy.

Just as he'd expected.

The energy in the fragments had been exhausted. His bloodline awakening progress reached 3.42%, and his Strength attribute had increased by 1.6 points.

The surrounding players opened their eyes one after another.

“Damn, you should've told us sooner it could raise our stats!” Zaka felt the changes in his body's strength and sighed, clearly wanting more. “Now that's an ancestor I can rely on! I'll acknowledge him!”

Liu Haiyang said, “Mr. Fang Heng, this pillar should be the inheritance artifact we've been looking for. The energy it releases can awaken our bloodlines. It seems to accumulate slowly within the pillar over time. The Eastern Federation will need some time to analyze and examine it.”

“Of course. Go ahead.”

They had found the so-called inheritance artifact, but for Fang Heng at his current level, the improvement was actually very limited.

For humanity as a whole, however, this might open up an entirely new path of bloodline power.

The researchers were all excited.

A major discovery!

If they could truly develop a method to fully awaken the bloodline, humanity's overall strength could improve enormously.

As the researchers discussed their next steps and considered whether they could move the pillar, Wayne noticed something on a nearby mural. He narrowed his eyes, pointed toward the black mist depicted near the edge of the stone pillar, and asked, “What's that? Black mist?”

Dr. Li Zhixuan approached the rock wall and stared up at the painting in silence for a moment.

Fang Heng walked over as well and examined the information depicted in the mural.

The painting was mottled with age, but many of its distinctive features were still recognizable.

For example...

The Titan race.

The migration of the Ancient Humans...

An invasion by extraterrestrial lifeforms.

And... spreading black mist...

Fang Heng turned to Dr. Li Zhixuan. “Is that the Black Fog Game? I remember Su Guantong telling me the truth about the Black Fog Game might be found here.”

“Allow me to explain.”

Liu Haiyang approached and said, “When we first discovered the records concerning the Ancient Humans, we also found references to the Black Fog Game. We'd had those materials for a long time, but it was only a few years ago that we finished translating them and understood what they said.”

“Yes, just as you're thinking. The Black Fog Game already existed in the era of the Ancient Humans, and they tried to make use of it and control it.”

Dr. Li Zhixuan continued, “But certain events later caused the Ancient Humans to regard the Black Fog Game as extremely dangerous. They attempted to break free of it.”

Fang Heng asked, “What kind of danger?”

“Given the limitations of the translation, our rough understanding is this: the Ancient Humans believed players could obtain special abilities after first entering the Black Fog Game. But those who participated in the 'game' would also be influenced without realizing it and become part of the game itself.”

“They believed the black mist was devouring the world in some bizarre way, gradually transforming the entire world into another form of black mist.”

Everyone listening found the explanation utterly baffling.

What did it mean to become part of the game?

And what the hell did transforming the world into black mist even mean?

“The Ancient Humans believed that participating too deeply in the game would cause them to be assimilated into the black mist. They tried to halt its spread, but clearly the black mist wasn't so easily stopped.”

Wayne asked, “Are you saying the Ancient Humans' migration was related to the Black Fog Game?”

“Very possibly.” Liu Haiyang's expression turned grave. “The Ancient Humans considered being 'assimilated' by the Black Fog Game an utterly terrifying fate. We don't even know whether we're already being assimilated ourselves. In any case, our world is facing the threat of assimilation by the black mist...”

“Director Liu!”

Just then, excited shouts rang out from the researchers behind them.

“Please come and take a look! We've found something!”

“Excuse me.”

Liu Haiyang excused himself and hurried into the deepest part of the cavern to join the researchers.

Fang Heng and the others exchanged glances and followed.

“We found it! We found it!”

Several researchers shouted in excitement.

Liu Haiyang quickly stepped forward to inspect the discovery, unable to suppress the smile spreading across his face.

Curious, Zaka asked, “What did you find?”

“The Eastern Federation once established a New Humanity project. In fact, besides records about the Ancient Humans, we also found a small amount of Titan technology.”

Dr. Li Zhixuan approached Fang Heng's group and explained quietly, “It included the Titans' extraterrestrial spacecraft and spatial-jump technology.”

“Huh?” Zaka was astonished. “You mean Titan spaceships?”

“Mm. You could put it that way.”

Not far away, a researcher cried out, “Spacecraft wreckage! Everyone, come look!”

The researchers nearby immediately crowded around him.

“A Titan spacecraft's propulsion unit! With this, there's hope of solving several problems with Titan starship jump propulsion!”

Liu Haiyang instructed the researchers to handle the wreckage carefully. Only after watching them pack the entire fragment into a metal container did he return to Fang Heng's group.

“Fang Heng, we've confirmed that we've found part of a Titan spaceship. Together with what we've learned from studying Kinsfolk starships, developing spatial-jump technology should only be a matter of time.”

“We still don't know exactly what danger the game poses, but the Eastern Federation will prepare an escape route in advance. If an uncontrollable threat arises, we'll use Titan starships to evacuate some of our people from the planet and completely escape the black mist's control.”

“As for those who remain...” Liu Haiyang paused, a fierce will to fight burning in his eyes. “We'll stay behind and find out what this black mist really is. If it tries to devour us, then we'll naturally fight it to the death.”

“I understand.”

Fang Heng got it.

No wonder the Eastern Federation had been so eager to infiltrate the Western Federation. What they truly needed were the Western Federation's Kinsfolk extraterrestrial starship systems.

“Fang Heng, we need your help. The Kinsfolk system allows you to redeem advanced Kinsfolk materials, many of which are indispensable for building Titan starships. The Eastern Federation is willing to cooperate and trade for those supplies.”

“Please believe in our sincerity. We're willing to offer a fair exchange.”

“That might be worth considering.”

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

Using the strength of the entire Eastern Federation to help him farm Kinsfolk contribution points?

Now that sounded great!

His next objective was clear, then.

Use the starship to make short-distance jumps and farm Kinsfolk contribution points like crazy!

Keep grinding until he had enough contribution to upgrade the starship and enough time for the Eastern Federation to develop Titan jump-capable vessels.

Meanwhile, during those missions, he could seek out tasks involving Ouroboros Seeds, devour the seeds, and rapidly strengthen his Licker horde.

So...

Fang Heng glanced around at the players.

Time to grind!
