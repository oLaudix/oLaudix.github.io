Chapter 2169

The Past

From original Chinese Chapter 2170 · 往事

“The Tree Spirits have always been arrogant and domineering. They consider themselves a race favored by destiny and have never regarded humans as equals. They held the Human Federation in particular contempt. Time and again, they destroyed cities on a whim and aggressively expanded their forests. Back then, the Federation could only grit its teeth and endure. Publicly, it accepted the Tree Spirits' unreasonable demands; secretly, it built up its strength and searched for a means of striking back.”

“The arrival of the Dependents gave humanity hope. After we began cooperating, the Federation and the Dependents spent several years secretly constructing the Spirit Domain blockade network, gradually sealing away the Tree Spirits' power.”

“Once that network took effect, and with the spatial-jump technology and mechanically modified organisms supplied by the Dependents, the balance of power slowly reversed. The Tree Spirits retreated into primeval forests, hiding themselves from the outside world.”

Ji Haiting paused, accepted a cup of coffee from his assistant, and took a small sip.

“The arrival of the Dependents was a tremendous boon to humanity.”

“But sometimes, what benefits us in one respect harms us in another.”

“Take my own experience. I entered the company at sixteen under my father's guidance, became a director at twenty-four, and formally took it over at twenty-nine. Throughout that period, I focused on developing cutting-edge technology. Eventually, I realized that scientific progress in our human world had been stagnant for a very long time. I had the distinct feeling that our technological development was being invisibly constrained by the Dependents.”

“They brought our technology to unprecedented heights, but they also restricted the paths by which we could explore beyond those heights.”

“Every road we travel is one they've already walked, whether it ended in success or failure. In their immensely long lifespans, they've explored far more than we possibly could. We cannot surpass them by following the same path. If we continue like this, humanity will live forever in their shadow.”

Ji Haiting drew a deep breath. “That makes me deeply uneasy. To some extent, I believe we're being controlled by the Dependents. If they wished it, they could destroy us with a single thought.”

“That's why I've been pursuing different sources of power, hoping to find a path distinct from their psychic and mechanical technologies. The Tree Spirits, who once ruled this world, are one example.” Ji Haiting looked at Fang Heng again. “And you're another. I've always been curious about where your powers come from. It seems they're not limited to Ouroboros after all.”

Fang Heng shrugged.

The sources of his power?

There were far too many to count!

He knew a little about practically everything.

“I understand. I think we could cooperate very well.”

“I hope so too.” Ji Haiting returned to the matter at hand. “The Dependents are tracking you through an advanced psychic positioning network. Once they've locked onto you, they can obtain your precise location at regular intervals.”

“However, I've noticed you possess a special ability that interferes with their tracking.”

“Mm. It only makes the process less efficient. They can still find me.” Fang Heng asked, “Is there a way to escape their positioning system?”

Ji Haiting had thought about this before coming to meet him.

“Yes. We could infiltrate the main control terminal of the Spirit Domain network and temporarily disable part of its operating system. That would interrupt the connection between the Dependents and our world, cutting off the external links through which they use the warp network to enter. Rebuilding those connections would take the Federation some time, buying us a brief opportunity.”

Fang Heng frowned. “Only a brief opportunity?”

“Achieving even that much won't be easy, Fang Heng. I can hardly influence the Federation at present.” Ji Haiting shook his head helplessly. “But don't worry. While the network is disabled, we can completely erase the Dependents' tracking lock on you.”

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

That sounded promising.

“There are risks, though. If you expose yourself to the Dependents or the Federation for long enough afterward, they'll mark you again. And next time, the Federation will double its precautions. Using the same method to remove their tracking will be much harder.”

Ji Haiting set down his coffee. “You know the Federation controls Wanling Technology behind the scenes. I can't personally take action to paralyze the Spirit Domain blockade. I can only support you covertly.”

“Mm. I understand. Your help behind the scenes will be enough.”

This wasn't going to be easy.

“Where is the Spirit Domain blockade network's main control terminal? How well defended is it?”

“In the lower levels of the Federation Central Research Institute. Security there is at the highest level.”

Ji Haiting looked grave. “The Spirit Domain blockade network was constructed by the Federation under the Dependents' guidance. The entire Central Research Institute is directly administered by the Federation, and I've heard that the core area is operated by the Dependents themselves. To be frank, even I can only enter the institute's outer sections.”

“I see…”

“That's the general situation. If we've decided to act, I'll need some preparation time. The intensity of the Dependents' pursuit will only increase, so I suggest we move quickly.”

Ji Haiting checked his watch. “Barring complications, I'll need about ten hours. I'll notify you immediately once everything is ready.”

“Mm. All right.”

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

Ji Haiting's plan treated the symptoms, not the underlying problem.

Even if all the information about Fang Heng was erased this time, another encounter with the Federation or Dependents would get him tracked again.

Wait…

A thought suddenly occurred to Fang Heng. He looked up. “Chairman Ji, you just said we could temporarily disable parts of the Spirit Domain blockade network. Doesn't that mean the Tree Spirits would be able to open a portal to the Inner World while it's offline?”

Ji Haiting frowned, considered it, then nodded. “In theory, yes. The Spirit Domain network has multiple layers. We can't voluntarily shut down the centralmost layer, which the Dependents control, but we can temporarily disable several outer layers.”

(End of chapter)
