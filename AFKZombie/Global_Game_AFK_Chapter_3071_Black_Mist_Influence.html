Chapter 3071

Black Mist Influence

From Chinese Chapter 3072 · 黑雾影响

“Thank you for your guidance.”

Fang Heng nodded with satisfaction.

He could worry about Transcendent advancement later. For now, he needed to find a way to max out his Grandmaster spatial studies skill.

The Abe Akaya summoning array in his villa's basement was almost finished. All he needed was for Abe Akaya to release natural energy, which he could then absorb and convert into spatial energy to grind his skill levels!

Fang Heng was invigorated and could hardly wait.

As usual, Anxi took the opportunity to question Gaspar about Fang Heng's training while Fang Heng was away.

This time, however, Gaspar merely shook his head, his expression difficult to read.

If Fang Heng hadn't been acting, then he was a genius.

“A genius?”

Anxi rarely heard such praise from the instructors. She quickly recorded it in her notes, intending to report it faithfully to the Noel Clan.

Among the warriors recruited by the Noel Clan, reaching Grandmaster wasn't particularly impressive. Spatial studies, however, was a rare and difficult discipline, with comparatively few practitioners.

Perhaps the clan should select Fang Heng for specialized training.

...

After completing his advancement, Fang Heng immediately returned to his villa's basement.

The zombie clones had completed most of the Abe Akaya summoning array overnight. In addition, they had finished an alchemical formation covering the basement to isolate its aura and prevent too much natural energy from leaking outside.

Fang Heng spent another two hours completing the summoning array, then stood before the alchemical formation, closed his eyes, and pressed a hand against it.

“Hiss... hiss, hiss!”

The array began rotating slowly.

Hm?

Fang Heng noticed something unusual again.

Just as when he had entered the game last time, constructing and operating Abe Akaya's summoning array met some resistance from an unknown source.

Some invisible force seemed to be obstructing the formation's operation.

But the resistance wasn't as severe as before.

Concentrating his mental power, Fang Heng poured it forcefully into the array.

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!”

The array began spinning rapidly, and wisps of Black Mist seeped out.

Then Abe Akaya's vines slowly emerged from the formation. They climbed outward, spread across the basement, and covered the entire room in a layer of tendrils almost immediately.

“Abe Akaya...”

Fang Heng quickly established a mental link with Abe Akaya and ordered it to remain in its present form, confined to the basement.

Then Abe Akaya began gently releasing natural-element energy.

Within moments, the basement was filled with a faint bluish-green mist.

Anyone else standing there would have been astonished by the extraordinarily dense natural power filling the room.

That bluish-green mist was actually a crystalline manifestation of highly concentrated natural energy.

Fang Heng sat cross-legged and extended his perception.

The natural energy crystals were swiftly absorbed into his body. They gathered so quickly that a thin layer of pale-green energy crystals formed on his skin.

Natural energy entered Fang Heng's body and rapidly transformed into Qi (炁).

He then used his bloodline ability to convert that Qi into spatial energy.

A game notification appeared again.

[Hint: Player has obtained spatial energy. Current Spatial Studies EXP +2,901.]

[Hint: Player has obtained spatial energy. Current Spatial Studies EXP +3,233...]

Excellent.

Time to keep grinding!

While Fang Heng sat quietly grinding experience, Korkalot and Kappa were at their wits' end in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

They had used nearly every resource available to search for Fang Heng over the past several days, but there was still no news.

Boss Fang was strong enough that they weren't too worried about his safety.

It was just...

Without Fang Heng around, Kappa felt deeply uneasy.

They had confirmed that a riot had broken out in the border prison that night, allowing many prisoners to escape, Boss Fang among them.

Unable to locate him, Kappa and Korkalot agreed to split their efforts. One would keep searching while the other began finding buyers for the ore Fang Heng had brought.

Once they received payment, they could use it to grease the necessary palms and arrange official registration for the human world in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

...

Seven days later, in the villa's basement.

Fang Heng slowly let out a long breath.

After seven days and nights of relentless grinding, he had finally filled his Grandmaster spatial studies experience bar, reaching LV: MAX.

To level up further, he'd have to break through again to the Transcendent rank.

Fang Heng rose, left the basement, and found Anxi's contact number.

“Mr. Fang Heng, how may I help you?”

Anxi answered within two seconds of receiving his call.

After spending time around him, she'd developed a basic impression of Fang Heng. Compared to warriors with all manner of eccentric habits, he was remarkably easy to work with.

If Anxi had to describe him, she would call him a warrior devoted entirely to mastering the martial path.

As soon as he joined the Noel Clan, he'd taken two private training sessions, then shut himself inside the villa to focus on cultivation. He hadn't left even once.

“Please arrange a spatial studies instructor capable of teaching Transcendent-level material. As soon as possible.”

Anxi's heart skipped a beat.

Last time he had only needed a Grandmaster spatial studies instructor. Now he wanted a Transcendent one?

Had he broken through again in a single week?

Anxi thought briefly before answering, “Of course, Mr. Fang Heng. I've registered your request and will arrange a private instructor as soon as possible. I'll contact you immediately after the appointment is confirmed. Please be patient in the meantime.”

“Will that take long?”

“It may take some time,” Anxi explained patiently. “There aren't many instructors qualified to teach Transcendent-level disciplines. Spatial studies is also particularly difficult, so qualified Transcendent instructors are very rare.”

Fang Heng considered that, then nodded. “I understand. How about this? I'll send you a list. Arrange private sessions with whichever instructors are free, prioritizing mysticism and alchemy.”

Mysticism was closer to the origins of power. Besides granting knowledge, it improved one's command of most academic disciplines and the body's resonance with different types of energy. It was a versatile discipline.

Alchemy, meanwhile, could further improve the efficiency of alchemical formations. Both fields were highly applicable in many situations and worth studying first.

“Of course...”

Anxi received the rough list Fang Heng sent and skimmed it. Astonishment immediately filled her eyes.

“Ah, this...”

The list contained row after row of academic disciplines. It covered practically every subject imaginable.

“Is there a problem?”

“No, no problem at all. Please give me a moment. I'll make the arrangements immediately.”

Anxi collected herself and quickly filtered the schedule. “If it's convenient, a Transcendent-level mysticism instructor has an opening this afternoon. Shall I book it for you?”

“Yes, please.”

“The appointment is confirmed, Mr. Fang Heng. I'll come to the villa to pick you up in half an hour and take you to the designated location. Is that acceptable?”
