Chapter 3026

Destruction

From Chinese Chapter 3027 · 破坏

“Abe Akaya...”

Fang Heng murmured softly.

“Hiss, hiss...”

Abe Akaya's vines responded at once, slipping through cracks in the rock and quickly spreading along the cliff toward the battlefield.

Jing'er's group was still locked in combat with the other Demon Seeds.

How could Jing'er not see what the others were thinking?

They simply wanted the Demon Cores for themselves.

He had the very same idea.

He'd already spent so much effort dealing with Envy and the Demon Cores. How could he hand them over to someone else at the end and do all that work just to sew another person's wedding clothes?

Whoosh!

Fang Heng lowered his body and accelerated suddenly, darting onto the battlefield.

“Whoosh!!!”

Huh?

Jing'er noticed Fang Heng's sudden appearance and grew alert.

The other Demon Seeds noticed him too, but they did nothing, continuing their methodical assault on Eseklyna.

Jing'er and Zhuang Yufei were focused mainly on the Demon Cores behind them. Seeing Fang Heng rush toward them instead of joining the fight against Eseklyna, they frowned slightly.

As he approached, Fang Heng asked casually, pretending nothing was wrong, “How are the Demon Cores looking?”

“They're...” Jing'er had just opened his mouth when he saw Fang Heng's movement. His expression changed instantly, and he shouted, “Stop!”

But it was already too late.

“Whoosh!!”

The sacred greatsword flashed into Fang Heng's hand, and he smashed it fiercely toward the five rotating Demon Cores!

It happened so suddenly that Jing'er and the others beside the cores had no time to react.

“Boom!!!!”

A tremendous impact rang out!

Sacred power collided with Hell power and exploded. The violent force at the center flung all five Demon Cores away, while Jing'er and the others closest to them were sent flying by the shock wave.

For an instant, the entire battlefield fell silent.

What was going on!?

That bastard!

Looking closely, they saw that the protective layer surrounding the five Demon Cores had been destroyed. Two cores had been driven into bottomless holes in the rock wall, while the remaining three had broken free of Eseklyna's seal and control. They flew toward the depths of Hell and vanished in the blink of an eye.

“The Demon Cores!”

The scene had completely overturned the Demon Seeds' expectations.

“Bang! Bang!!!”

Amid the silence, two more heavy impacts sounded. The cores embedded deep in the rock burst from its crevices and flew toward the depths of Hell as though alive.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

The Demon Seeds were stunned for a moment, then reacted and all rushed after the fleeing cores!

Forget about Eseklyna.

If they could obtain the Demon Cores, perhaps they too could control other Demon Seeds the way Envy had!

Fang Heng sneered at the sight.

Demon Seeds.

Nothing but idiots blinded by the profits right in front of them.

A few Demon Seeds still on the battlefield hadn't immediately chased the cores, but they couldn't restrain Eseklyna with so few people. They backed away too and decided to settle for pursuing the cores.

“Hahahahaha!”

Eseklyna threw back her head and laughed.

As expected, they were nothing but mud that couldn't be plastered onto a wall!

At first, Fang Heng's arrival had made her wary. But now, seeing the Demon Seeds scatter like frightened birds and beasts, she couldn't stop laughing.

What a perfect opportunity!

Even under the Demon-Binding Chains' suppression, in terms of individual strength she was in a league of her own among the Demon Seeds.

As long as they scattered, none of them posed the slightest threat to her!

Eseklyna immediately sped after one of the fleeing Demon Cores.

“Fang Heng!”

Jing'er stabilized himself after the shock wave knocked him away and glared at Fang Heng, unable to conceal his rage.

His original plan had been to use the Demon Seeds' combined strength to finish Eseklyna off, then seize the cores and control the other Demon Seeds.

As for Fang Heng...

If Fang Heng had been willing to obey him, Jing'er would even have given him a chance to become his subordinate.

But the brat had courted death and ruined his plan.

“Jing'er, stop staring! Let's go!”

Seeing Jing'er glare at Fang Heng, Zhuang Yufei urged him quietly.

“Hmph!”

Jing'er snorted, forced down his anger, and followed Zhuang Yufei after one of the Demon Cores.

Fang Heng watched Jing'er leave with a disdainful snort, then tapped the ground and quickly pursued Eseklyna.

...

Somewhere on Hell's twenty-ninth level.

A Demon Core representing Wrath floated in a cavern.

“Found it!”

Liu Mo darted in from outside. The sight of the core suspended in the air made greed flash in his eyes.

What luck.

This core even belonged to his own original Demon Seed power.

As long as he found somewhere safe and slowly absorbed its strength...

Just as he was about to act, Liu Mo's expression changed. He suddenly felt a terrible sense of danger from behind.

“Boom!!!”

A concentrated projectile of Hell rock slammed into his back. The explosion sent him flying, leaving his back a bloody mess.

“Pfft!”

Eseklyna!

Damn it!

Liu Mo spat blood and turned to find the Envy Demon Seed behind him. His elation plunged straight from heaven into hell. Gritting his teeth, he endured his injuries and fled through an exit on the cavern's right side.

“Hmph!”

Eseklyna glanced coldly in the direction of his escape but didn't pursue. She floated in midair, gazed at the Demon Core, and slowly raised her hand toward it.

“Hum...”

Responding to her power, the Demon Core began floating slowly toward Eseklyna.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

Before it could reach her, two arrows of sacred light streaked through the air and broke her control over it.

“You?”

Eseklyna turned toward Fang Heng at the cavern entrance, surprise flashing in her eyes.

“You still dare come here? Are you really not afraid of death?”

As she saw it, she'd already drained most of Fang Heng's sacred power and left him with less than a tenth of his strength.

And he still dared confront her alone?

“Heh, Eseklyna...” Fang Heng stared at her. “We haven't settled our fight yet. Why are you leaving?”

“Oh?”

Eseklyna looked at Fang Heng and was about to dispose of him quickly when surprise suddenly crossed her face.

The aura around Fang Heng had changed.

Even as they spoke, his sacred aura rapidly withdrew, replaced by an immensely powerful aura of necromancy surging out of his body!

Necromancy?

Sacred energy and necromancy.

How could he switch so smoothly between two completely opposite kinds of energy?

Just what was this man!?

— End of Chapter 3026 —
