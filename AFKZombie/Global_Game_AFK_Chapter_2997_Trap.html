Chapter 2997

Trap

From Chinese Chapter 2998 · 陷阱

A corridor in the labyrinth.

A small player squad was searching its way forward. They stopped frequently along the route, leisurely clearing the ice-crystal monsters they encountered.

They had noticed something too.

Ice-crystal creatures had enormous health pools and dealt substantial physical damage.

But as long as the players were careful and relied on teamwork, they could defeat them without taking damage.

Once an ice creature was killed, its killer received an infusion of elemental energy.

For players specializing in Elemental Studies, it was like discovering a treasure.

The energy from one kill was worth several weeks of hard cultivation. Ordinary players without Elemental Studies could also store the energy temporarily in their Sea of Consciousness, where it formed a protective barrier.

If their Sea of Consciousness came under attack later, that stored power would provide resistance.

The squad was farming monsters in an orderly manner when a figure suddenly streaked past along the corridor on their right.

Hm?

Was that...?

Whoosh!

“BANG! BANG!!”

Two explosions rang out as the figure passed.

The players who had been kiting the Ice Crystal Puppets nearly jumped out of their skins.

What the hell?!

The two puppets they had spent so long wearing down shattered instantly as the dark figure swept past them!

Was that a player?

They stared at his rapidly disappearing back, then exchanged astonished glances.

Good grief!

What terrifying explosive strength!

He had destroyed the Ice Crystal Puppets with a single blow.

They hadn't even seen how he attacked, let alone managed to get a clear look at his face.

Who was that person?

Fang Heng was running swiftly through the corridor.

He'd spent more than an hour exploring in fits and starts, absorbing the energy of four ice-crystal trial plazas along the way.

Three were Nature Studies trials, while the fourth concerned Elemental Studies.

He could already sense that the Ouroboros Seed was getting closer.

Suddenly, Fang Heng's heart jolted.

The Ouroboros Seed's aura had erupted on a massive scale.

And it was moving?

Perfect timing!

The spreading aura gave him a clearer fix on its location.

Fang Heng immediately searched in the direction from which he sensed the expanding power.

Even so, he still spent more than ten minutes winding through the icy maze.

He could sense that he was drawing ever closer to the Ouroboros Seed.

After rounding another ice-crystal corner, he heard violent combat fluctuations ahead.

Hm?

The Central Federation?

Rather than jumping into the battle immediately, Fang Heng slowed and cautiously approached the rear of the Federation team, observing as he went.

The Central Federation had assembled nearly two hundred elite players. Each had partially unlocked their Lock in the real world.

From a distance, he saw the Federation personnel maintaining sacred barriers while fighting and retreating.

As for their opponent...

Fang Heng looked up from afar.

A huge number of tiny black beetles had gathered on the ice, tumbling together into an enormous sphere. It looked like a ball covered in a thick layer of scales.

The beetle sphere rolled toward the Central Federation forces.

Spells of every element bombarded the clustered insects.

Hm?

Sacred power?

Fang Heng quickly noticed several Holy Court members among the team.

Judging from their attire, the leader was an archbishop.

Since the incident in the Divine Realm, he'd heard very little about the Holy Court. According to information passed along by the Undead Council, the Holy Court had withdrawn into its bases across many worlds, suspended hostilities with the major powers, and adopted a policy of shutting itself away.

Rumors circulating among players claimed the God King had issued a series of orders soon after his revival, devoting most of his effort to rebuilding the Divine Realm.

The God King had then entered seclusion once again.

The Central Federation had long been known to maintain close ties with the Holy Court, so their presence in this expedition was not unusual.

“Watch out!”

While Fang Heng assessed the situation, the Central Federation team was steadily being driven into a desperate position.

Almost every attack was absorbed by the beetles composing the Ouroboros Seed.

Commander Luo Ao grew increasingly anxious.

The situation was turning badly against them.

They couldn't win.

Their present abilities couldn't penetrate the beetle swarm's outer defenses. Worse still, the Ouroboros Seed's strength was gradually increasing.

It would only become harder to defeat!

Forget the Apocalypse treasure—if this continued, the entire squad might be wiped out!

Could this labyrinth actually be a trap?

What should they do?!

As he struggled for a solution, Luo Ao heard a player whisper beside him, “Sir, the key on your chest...”

Hm? The key?

Luo Ao instinctively looked down at the Key of Apocalypse rune hanging around his neck, and his heart skipped a beat.

At some point, the key had begun glowing faintly under the influence of an unknown force, floating and pointing toward a corridor behind them in the ice-crystal maze.

The key was offering directions?

Luo Ao frowned deeply as a suspicion took hold.

The location marked as the Apocalypse treasure might only be a switch. Perhaps the Key of Apocalypse was meant to guide them to the treasure's true location!

Seeing the key, Archbishop Whiteman also looked thoughtful. He broke the silence. “Everyone, this Calamity's power is astonishing. In my opinion, the ice-crystal labyrinth may be some kind of trial. We must find the power of the Apocalypse treasure to resist the Calamity. Why don't we split up? You search for the treasure with your key while we stay behind and hold the Calamity off.”

Luo Ao felt Whiteman's theory was probably right, but hesitated. “But...”

“Don't hesitate! Go quickly. Sacred power is effective against the Calamity. Obtain the treasure's power, then come back and help us.”

“All right! Archbishop, we're counting on you. We'll leave this place to you.”

Luo Ao stopped hesitating, nodded to Whiteman, and ordered the team to withdraw at once.

“Form the array—Sacred Sealing Imprisonment!”

Whiteman roared. Behind him, more than ten Holy Court archbishops took their positions and formed a magic array together.

Dark-golden sacred light spread swiftly from beneath the priests' feet, flooding the icy corridor ahead.

Tiny motes of light flickered through the space under the influence of the sacred energy.

In an instant, sacred power permeated the entire area. The Ouroboros Calamity rolling forward was affected by the holy domain. Its movements slowed rapidly, as though the surrounding space itself had hardened, until it stopped completely.

“Go!”

Seeing the Holy Court's skill take effect, Luo Ao led his squad away immediately. As he did, he frowned and glanced at a young man standing farther back in the corridor.

The young man was all alone.

He wore a strange mask and had been standing there for several minutes.

Something about his build and aura felt oddly familiar, as though Luo Ao had seen him somewhere before.
