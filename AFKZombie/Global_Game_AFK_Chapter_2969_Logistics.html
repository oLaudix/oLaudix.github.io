Chapter 2969

Logistics

From Chinese Chapter 2970 · 后勤

Once the meeting ended, everyone returned to their respective fleets through the teleportation passages.

Captain Giselle, Vion, and the others who'd attended were still completely bewildered.

What on earth had just happened?

“What is going on with that fleet? Why didn't we receive a single piece of intelligence about it?”

It would have been one thing if the Eastern Federation had secretly built a single starship.

But this was a whole fleet!

You needed at least sixteen ships for it to qualify as a fleet group!

“Captain, calm down. Isn't it possible Fang Heng was already cooperating with the Eastern Federation?”

“Could Fang Heng and the Eastern Federation somehow have taken control of an alien race's...?”

“Impossible. There must be some mistake. The information has to be wrong.”

“Enough!”

Giselle slammed the table, cutting off the Presidential Office experts' wild speculation.

“Our mission comes first. Humanity's assignment is to help construct the sixth prism tower. We'll head over there. Everything will become clear once we arrive.”

“Understood!”

The seven starships immediately began preparations for a short-range jump toward the designated coordinates.

...

Ten minutes later.

Jump complete.

The expert group members raised their heads to look outside the ships.

More than a hundred ships were arranged in three overlapping rows, forming a massive fleet.

Kinsfolk weapon systems aboard the ships continuously fired beams of many colors, shredding the approaching insect swarms.

At the center of the formation was an enormous, heavily equipped starship.

“Look at that one! That...”

“Mm. That's the Eastern Federation's insignia.”

Among the ships, more than thirty were flying the Eastern Federation's flag.

Inside the cabins, the Western Federation personnel fell silent, their faces filled with shock.

It was all real!

Even the so-called Nethersea Fleet under Fang Heng's command was real!

So...

No wonder humanity's contribution within the Kinsfolk Alliance had risen so dramatically lately. They'd all been riding on Fang Heng's coattails!

Without him, they wouldn't even have qualified to join a mission of this level.

The experts' faces gradually took on expressions of defeat.

The Western Federation had spent an enormous amount of time and resources secretly laying groundwork for an advantage in outer space. They'd believed their interstellar strength would overwhelm the other Federations and give them a powerful voice...

Only now did they discover that they themselves were the clowns.

Even the Eastern Federation's space forces were larger than theirs!

Giselle ordered, “Send a message to the Nethersea Fleet. Ask whether there's anything we can do to help.”

“Yes, understood!”

A reply soon arrived. The fleet invited the Western Federation group to join temporarily in fighting the insect swarm, but once they joined, they would not be permitted to operate independently.

Vion felt he'd never suffered such humiliation. Through gritted teeth, he began, “Fang Heng! Don't think that we...”

“Shut up, Vion! I don't care who's backing you. Sit down and behave yourself! If I catch you making even the slightest move behind our backs, I'll have you executed for war crimes on the spot!”

Giselle glared furiously at Vion, eyes blazing.

The sight of these useless allies made Giselle's blood boil.

Could these fools really still not understand the situation?

Who was actually in charge of this fleet?

Hadn't it been made perfectly clear at the meeting just now?!

The Eastern Federation was merely accompanying Fang Heng.

The true master of the Nethersea Fleet was Fang Heng himself!

The longer Giselle looked at these fools, the angrier he became.

If these people hadn't kept holding them back, it would be the Western Federation cooperating with Fang Heng now. Those thirty-odd ships would belong to the Western Federation.

What chance would the Eastern Federation have had?

And now the advantage they'd built up over more than ten years had vanished into thin air.

What the hell was the point of building a star map now?!

Vion was stunned by the torrent of abuse. When he finally recovered and anger surged within him, the leader of the expert group beside him pushed him back into his seat before he could respond.

“Vion, we'll discuss this after we return. Our mission remains to observe the various races, gather intelligence, and assist the alliance in clearing the SSS-grade insect nest. Everything else can wait until we're back in the Western Federation.”

Meanwhile, Fang Heng's side was also carrying out its battle plan.

After receiving their assignment from the alliance council, the fleet successfully jumped to the construction site in Area Six of the Eye of Stars and established a defensive perimeter.

Once they'd driven back the insect swarm, Fang Heng gathered representatives from the various ships for a meeting.

Chen Yu nodded. “Boss Fang, just as we expected, the various alliance interest groups led by the Shadow Clan have seized control of the alliance's decision-making. They constantly threaten other partners to extract more benefits. As a rising faction, we were bound to be squeezed by them.”

The Flying Squirrel clan's ships were better suited for terrain reconnaissance and mineral collection. They quickly scanned the battlefield, and their captain reported, “The insect swarms in Area Six are relatively high-level. We may be hit by a major assault.”

“The Shadow Clan alliance had no good intentions when they invited us. Partly, they want to test our strength; partly, they want to use us against the insects. That's why they've assigned us to guard the area and fully cooperate with the Iron-Eater Clan in constructing the Eye of Stars.”

A member of the Spirit Clan said, “Boss Fang, the meeting also made no mention of distributing resources after the war. Normally, the fleets divide up a world's resources once fighting ends. This time, there's a good chance they don't intend to let us share in the spoils.”

The Western Federation representatives attended the council too, but they had no standing to speak at a meeting like this and merely listened in silence.

So that was it.

They were expected to do the work but wouldn't get any of the final rewards?

How disgusting.

Everyone discussed the matter in low voices.

Listening, Alec curled his lip.

The Shadow Clan?

They actually wanted to exploit Boss Fang? They were courting death!

Who knew who would end up using whom?

Ke Wei, the Eastern Federation's military minister, asked with some concern, “Boss Fang, should we try contacting the Shadow Clan?”

“No need.”

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

The other side plainly didn't take his team seriously and only wanted to exploit them. There was no point wasting his breath.

“No rush. We've only just arrived, so let's see how things develop. For now, we'll guard the Iron-Eater Clan while they build the prism towers.”

Fang Heng had plans of his own.

He had three goals in joining this alliance mission.

First, observe how the Eye of Stars was built and how well it performed in combat. Second, find a way to obtain the Ouroboros Seed from the SSS-grade nest. Only third did he intend to acquire more Kinsfolk honor and contribution points.

He would assess the situation first.

There was no hurry.

As he considered the matter, Fang Heng recalled hearing that the Iron-Eater Clan possessed special construction abilities. Perhaps he could contact them and trick them out of some technology.

Just as everyone was preparing to leave after the discussion, a Federation soldier hurried into the hall and reported, “Boss Fang, the Iron-Eater Clan has requested a meeting.”
