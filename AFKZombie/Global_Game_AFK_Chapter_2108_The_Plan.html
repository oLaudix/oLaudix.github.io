Chapter 2108

The Plan

From original Chinese Chapter 2109 · 方案

If he could only operate in those few places, his rate of growth would undoubtedly suffer.

Perhaps that was exactly what the Holy Court wanted.

To restrict his movements to a limited area?

Fang Heng fell into thought.

How could he keep making trouble for the Holy Court while the Divine Realm's pursuers hunted him?

Or...

Was there some way to deal with those pursuers directly?

“What a nuisance!”

As he considered his options, Fang Heng sensed sacred energy from the ruined cathedral approaching them again and frowned.

“Everyone, the explosion wasn't enough to seriously injure the Divine Realm's pursuers. It's no longer safe here. Split up and withdraw!”

Lord Dani nodded slightly to Fang Heng and quickly led the undead faction forces away.

Fang Heng took a teleportation scroll from his backpack and activated it, preparing to return to the teleportation point in the Lost Forest before fleeing back to the Zombie Apocalypse to hide.

Thinking about it, his situation really was rather miserable.

With the Divine Realm's pursuers so close behind, he didn't even have time to visit the quartermaster at the undead front to turn in his mission.

He would have to run first, then teleport back to Chiboreya after some time had passed to submit it.

· · ·

Two hours later.

At the Myriad Worlds Library,

Fang Heng had washed up and was lying on a bed, resting.

His phone rang. It was Lord Dani.

“Fang Heng, I've just received word that the Divine Realm's pursuers have withdrawn. It seems they're subject to certain restrictions. They're permitted to pursue you, but they won't interfere in other matters.”

Dani pursed her lips and thought for a moment before continuing, “It may also be an unspoken agreement between the leaders of the undead faction and the Holy Court. Conflicts among the higher powers aren't allowed to spill over into the lower worlds.”

Fang Heng nodded. “Mm. That's my assessment too. Entering lower worlds costs the Divine Realm a great deal of energy, and the Holy Court can't stay for long. Otherwise, stopping the Yin Vein fissure in Duncha temporarily would be easy for them.”

Dani continued, “We'll regain full control of Chiboreya and eliminate the remaining Holy Court forces. Even so, they'll keep looking for ways to infiltrate the world and destroy the Mork Temple.”

“All right. I can't free myself to help right now, so please take care of the Mork Temple.”

“Understood. I'll be careful.”

Fang Heng ended the call and threw himself heavily onto the bed.

The more he thought about it, the more troublesome everything seemed!

As long as those Divine Realm pursuers were around, he couldn't do anything freely.

Was there any way to shake them off for a while?

Maybe he should simply hide at the Necromancy Research Association!

Fang Heng weighed the idea.

The next task there would probably involve advancing his necromancy to Grandmaster level.

The task location would likely be somewhere connected to necromancy.

He could ask about it.

But would mastering demigod-level necromancy really transform his combat strength overnight?

And then there was the time required.

For an ordinary player, even advancing a Master-level skill could easily take a year. Completing it within that time would already be considered fast, never mind Grandmaster or demigod-level advancement.

How long would it take him?

A month?

That was too long.

This option didn't seem particularly promising.

Then there was the Black Fortress.

Several days ago, he'd received word from them that they'd made some progress investigating a teleportation passage related to an extraterrestrial dependent race. They were considering an expedition into the world inhabited by that race.

At the time, Fang Heng had been too busy fighting the Holy Court to get involved.

The Black Fortress was no weak organization.

That woman Shalin, for example, was terrifyingly strong.

Even now, Fang Heng couldn't judge her true strength.

He couldn't defeat the Holy Court alone, but what if the Black Fortress helped?

If he could set a trap and lure the Holy Court into it...

There was a real chance!

But it would also be extraordinarily dangerous.

The dependent race's world was already difficult enough, and adding the hostile Holy Court would only make the situation more complicated.

Unless he had no other choice, he'd rather not do that.

Besides, there was no guarantee the Black Fortress would agree to fight the Holy Court with him.

That plan had plenty of problems too.

Wait!

Something suddenly occurred to Fang Heng. He shot upright in bed.

There was another place!

Apart from the Zombie Apocalypse and the necromancy faction's strongholds, there was another location where he could avoid being discovered by the Holy Court!

In fact, he'd been carrying a triggerable quest connected to it all this time.

Origin of the Vampires—The World of Myriad Forests.

It was a hidden world he'd uncovered through the vampire main storyline.

The hidden world was classified as high-tier.

After all, his vampire bloodline, the Divine Tree Abe Akaya, and even the Holy Court were all intricately connected to that place!

When he'd first triggered the quest, he was still wandering around lower-tier worlds. Believing himself too weak, he hadn't rashly entered the World of Myriad Forests to explore it.

But now seemed like an excellent opportunity!

Long ago, the vampire progenitor had been cursed by the Divine Realm and thought his death inevitable. Instead, he accidentally entered the World of Myriad Forests, survived, and went on to found the entire vampire race.

That suggested Fang Heng might find a way to deal with the Holy Court there!

There was another, even more important consideration.

Special rewards!

He had perfectly completed the prerequisite main quest and chosen two special rewards.

The first would allow him to receive the main quest directly, along with relevant mission guidance, as soon as he entered the World of Myriad Forests.

The second granted him an additional fifteen days of exclusive exploration after opening its entrance!

That meant no other players or factions could enter the World of Myriad Forests during those fifteen days!

And if he added the fifteen days he'd earned during an earlier prerequisite mission...

He had a full thirty days!

Thirty days free from the Holy Court's pursuit.

An entire month!

That should be enough to complete the questline.

Yes!

He would go there!

A sharp light shone in Fang Heng's eyes.

· · ·

After two days of rest and preparation, Fang Heng returned to the prison laboratory.

His contribution toward completely destroying the Holy Court's forces in Chiboreya had earned him more than 800,000 Main God Points in total.

He immediately invested all those points into Licker modifications.

In addition to the Primal Devourer form, some zombie clones now had permanently stabilized Crystallized-form modifications.

The next round of modifications, however, would require even more Main God Points.

Fortunately, with the Holy Court defeated in Chiboreya, the pressure on the Mork Temple had eased considerably.

The Yin Vein continued causing destruction.

New faction honor points came in every hour.

“Hmm? You mean those Fusion Tyrants...?”

Hearing Fang Heng mention the Fusion Tyrants, Qiu Yaokang raised his head and gestured to an assistant, who quickly pulled a document from a thick folder and handed it over.

“Come with me.”

Fang Heng followed Qiu Yaokang into an elevator heading underground beneath the prison.

— End of Chapter 2108 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Hidden world. 万森之界 is rendered World of Myriad Forests, the hidden high-tier world connected to the vampire progenitor and Abe Akaya.
  • Protection period. Two rewards of fifteen days each give a total of thirty days of exclusive access after the passage opens.

Chinese Expressions

  • 束手束脚 (shù shǒu shù jiǎo). Literally “hands and feet bound”; Fang Heng feels restricted by the pursuing Divine Realm team.
