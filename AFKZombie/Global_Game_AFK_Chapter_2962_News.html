Chapter 2962

News

From Chinese Chapter 2963 · 消息

“The Ancient Humans and modern humanity are both branches descended from the tribal bloodlines the Titans left behind when they sought refuge.”

“The coordinates we obtained point to a world where the Ancient Humans once lived. Later, they became entangled in certain events involving the Kinsfolk and feared their entire race might be wiped out. They were forced to leave that world, but before departing they left behind certain so-called 'inheritance artifacts'.”

“We believe those artifacts are stored in the Ancient Humans' ruins.”

Fang Heng said, “Mm. We've carried out a general survey of the world. Its environment is very similar to ours and is well suited to human habitation, but we found no traces of other races living there, nor any clues pointing to inheritance artifacts or ruins.”

Hearing this, the accompanying players discussed the matter quietly.

“That makes things difficult.”

Liu Haiyang continued, “Perhaps too much time has passed, or perhaps some accident occurred. Either way, all traces of the Ancient Humans' settlements might have disappeared.”

“We'd like to visit the world at those coordinates and investigate. If necessary, we may even need to build a special detection tower there, but that would take a long time.”

Liu Haiyang asked, “Fang Heng, the Eastern Federation doesn't actually possess interstellar travel capabilities, and we're not entirely sure about your ship's condition. If possible, could it return to pick up some of our people and take them aboard?”

Fang Heng shook his head helplessly.

“No. The starship has only just been built. It can't travel freely yet.”

Liu Haiyang scratched his head, at a loss for ideas.

A researcher nearby suggested, “Perhaps we could first draw up detailed topographical maps and then analyze them here.”

“Sending everything back and forth like that would waste enormous amounts of time...”

“What about remote projection? We could set up a remote projection system...”

“I think we should try geological analysis and verification...”

The researchers began debating all at once.

Fang Heng remained silent.

Even after a lengthy discussion, nobody came up with a suitable solution.

With no other choice, Liu Haiyang decided to take the team out of the game for the time being. They would continue studying the problem, see if they could find a better approach, and return with a concrete plan in a couple of days.

“Your Majesty, I have a question...”

Prince Bulle had listened quietly from the sidelines. Now that the meeting was over and only the two of them remained in the hall, he couldn't help asking in a low voice.

Fang Heng's head was aching from the researchers' incessant chatter. He rubbed his temples and asked, “What is it?”

Bulle said softly, “Why don't you open the Vampire Teleportation Gate and send our people there? The vampires' strength has grown explosively of late. We could be of assistance to you.”

Huh?

Fang Heng opened his mouth, suddenly struck by a realization.

That's right!

The Vampire Teleportation Gate!

He had almost forgotten it!

After fully unlocking his powers in the real world, he could use all his abilities from the game there as well.

In theory, that should include the Vampire Teleportation Gate!

He could try opening the gate and sending a group of researchers over first to investigate!

Fang Heng immediately sent vampires to catch up with Liu Haiyang's group before they left the Vampire Temple and bring them back.

Liu Haiyang and the others had barely left the temple when the vampires hurriedly called them back. They looked perplexed. “Fang Heng, did you forget to tell us something important?”

“Mm. Something just occurred to me. The starship can't return at the moment, but I can send your people into that world through a teleportation passage.”

“Huh?”

Everyone was startled and turned to look at Fang Heng.

That was possible too?

“That's wonderful! May I ask how?”

“A secret vampire technique.” As he spoke, Fang Heng glanced at Prince Bulle beside him. “However, there's a condition for using it. Anyone being transported must be a vampire.”

Vampires!

The players' expressions shifted slightly.

Wasn't that a wonderful thing?

Becoming a vampire meant, in a sense, instantly acquiring a powerful physique and a longer lifespan.

For ordinary players, it was practically a dream come true!

Fang Heng paused before continuing, “To preserve the purity of the vampire bloodline, I can offer you up to thirty places. If the Federation agrees to this arrangement, draw up a list of candidates as soon as possible.”

“Excellent! Mr. Fang Heng, thank you for your generosity. I understand. I'll contact the researchers immediately. I also need to join the team as its leader.”

“Mm, of course. No problem.”

Fang Heng nodded toward Prince Bulle at his side.

“Make your arrangements with Prince Bulle from now on. And Bulle, prepare a group of vampires. I need you to enter that world as soon as possible and help us explore it.”

Bulle's spirits rose. He nodded at once. “Yes! As you wish, Your Majesty.”

Fang Heng hadn't expected those thirty places to cause such an uproar within the Federation.

The vampire bloodline was no longer what it once had been.

Dimensional power had greatly strengthened it, making its effects exceptionally potent. On top of that, the vampires had deliberately stopped spreading their bloodline, driving the price even higher.

In fact, the various Federation governments had all been stockpiling vampire bloodline skill books.

Each one could fetch an astronomical sum on the market.

And now thirty places had suddenly become available. Wouldn't everyone fight tooth and nail for them?

Especially when only researchers taking part in the special mission were eligible?

Who could possibly stand that?

The arguments kept escalating until the matter reached the Eastern Federation's upper leadership.

In the end, Liu Haiyang and Chen Yu had to swallow their pride and offer all sorts of favorable terms, hoping to secure just ten more places.

Fang Heng had originally capped the number at thirty because too many vampires would be difficult to manage.

Unfortunately, the Eastern Federation offered far too much.

In the end, the Eastern Federation agreed to provide sixty additional senior starship engineers and thirty researchers for two years of service. In exchange, thirty-five members of its research expedition and another fifteen elite Federation guards underwent vampire bloodline advancement under Bulle's arrangements.

...

The VF-203 star system.

A blood-red teleportation passage blossomed high in the sky, and the vampires swiftly passed through it into the world.

“Your Highness.”

On seeing Prince Bulle, Alec hurriedly led his subordinates to kneel in greeting.

As the team's leader, Liu Haiyang had also undergone vampire bloodline advancement.

Only now did he realize how rigid the vampire hierarchy truly was.

Absolute obedience from those of lower rank to their superiors.

It wasn't quite as severe for human players as for NPCs. Yet even Liu Haiyang failed to notice that the vampire bloodline was subtly influencing his behavior.

At Fang Heng's command, the vampires transformed into bats and spread out to explore the world.
