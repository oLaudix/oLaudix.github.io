Chapter 2176

The Fortress

From original Chinese Chapter 2177 · 堡垒

Several hours later.

High in the sky.

Fang Heng flew toward the Federation's secret base in bat form.

A small clump of vines remained firmly attached near the base of his wing.

“Hey, Levin.”

“What is it?”

“Have you contacted my friends?”

“My clone has found them. Do you need to speak to them now?”

“Right now? Whenever I want?”

“Yes.”

Fang Heng was rather surprised.

He'd originally planned to contact Mo Jiawei offline to confirm their arrangement before completing the exchange.

Then he'd use the game notifications as a second layer of verification.

He hadn't expected Levin to have long-distance communication abilities too.

That was convenient. It would save him a great deal of trouble.

“Not yet.” Fang Heng nodded inwardly. “And if we run into trouble during the operation, you'll help me, won't you?”

“My abilities are more suited to support, and I'm not particularly strong. I suggest you don't expect too much from me.”

“Tch. No fun.”

Fang Heng pursed his lips and glanced down.

They were now approaching the Federation Central Research Institute, constructed in the middle of a desert.

The central Spirit Domain network control center was located inside.

Even from the outside, the institute looked like a defensive fortress.

Vast stretches of uninhabited land surrounded it.

All manner of supplies were delivered daily by armored vehicles.

Its security clearance was the highest in the Federation.

There were undoubtedly countless hidden energy-beam turrets around the fortress.

Fang Heng circled twice in the sky, then turned back. He landed on the outskirts of a small Federation military town bordering the restricted zone.

After waiting at a street corner for a little while, a Federation squad approached.

“Good day, Dr. Fang.”

A clean-cut young man in a Federation officer's uniform recognized him, nodded, and gave a military salute.

“I'm Leot, captain of the Sixth Command Squad of the Central Federation Guard. Under orders from my superiors, I'll escort you into the Central Research Institute. Both its security and confidentiality levels are the highest possible. You won't be authorized to leave for the next five years. If you agree, please sign this agreement, and observe all precautions.”

Leot handed Fang Heng a document and a miniature earpiece.

Fang Heng put on the earpiece.

Ji Haiting's voice sounded in his ear.

“Fang Heng, security inside the Central Research Institute is extremely tight. The surveillance system uses targeted facial recognition. We've already hacked the network and created a new identity for you: Dr. Fang, a researcher employed by the Federation.”

“The Spirit Domain network automatically screens for infiltrators. Eventually, it will notice this fabricated identity. I can guarantee it'll hold up for at least three hours; after that, we'll be relying on luck. So we must move quickly.”

“There's also a pinhole camera in the earpiece. We'll be monitoring your situation continuously. And Captain Leot is one of my people. His team will escort you inside.”

Fang Heng glanced at Leot and murmured, “All right.”

Ji Haiting continued, “Fang Heng, I'm already inside the research institute. I'll guide you through the sabotage operation. There's no time to lose. Sign the document, and let's begin.”

Fang Heng scribbled his name on the agreement and handed it back.

Captain Leot inspected the document and nodded. “Dr. Fang, if you're ready, we can depart now.”

“Let's go.”

Leot gestured toward the vehicles.

His squad escorted Fang Heng into an off-road vehicle and drove slowly toward the research fortress at the center of the desert.

Along the way, Ji Haiting explained the institute's internal security arrangements and the precautions Fang Heng needed to take through the earpiece.

He kept talking practically the whole journey.

The vehicle bumped across the wasteland for an entire hour before finally stopping.

Only after an exhaustive inspection at the institute's outer checkpoint did the fortress gates open.

“We've arrived.”

The vehicle stopped in a parking area inside the fortress complex.

Leot got out and opened Fang Heng's door. “Dr. Fang, I'll take you through the research area so you can familiarize yourself with your future working environment. Afterward, I'll show you to your quarters. Does that suit you?”

“Fine. I'll follow your arrangements,” Fang Heng replied hoarsely.

“This way, please.”

As Leot helped him out of the vehicle, he discreetly slipped a small black magnetic device into Fang Heng's palm.

The virus-breaking software!

Without revealing anything, Fang Heng accepted the device and stored it in his backpack.

[Hint: The player has obtained Sabotage Virus Software (Special, Quest Item).]

Excellent.

Throughout the journey, Fang Heng had been silently impressed.

Ji Haiting was far more competent than the Tree Spirits.

If Fang Heng had attempted this sabotage operation by himself, it would have taken considerably more effort.

“Let's go.”

Leot waved his hand, leading Fang Heng toward the research area with an escort of more than a dozen soldiers.

The internal surveillance network continuously captured and scanned the faces of everyone passing through.

Because Fang Heng's records had been modified through authorized channels, the system raised no alarms.

Eventually, the group reached the inner research sector.

Leot stopped before a glass door. “Dr. Fang, this will be your research area. The main control computers inside can connect to the Dependents' upper-level psychic network.”

“We're not authorized to enter. We'll wait outside while you look around, then escort you to your quarters.”

“Mm. All right.”

Fang Heng nodded and stepped through the glass doors into the laboratory.

Ji Haiting's voice came through the earpiece again.

“Fang Heng, you're entering the outer research sector. This is where the Federation connects to the Dependents' psychic network. The Spirit Domain blockade's main control hub lies deeper inside. Your cover identity is a Federation-employed Spirit Domain network expert.”

“I've never entered this sector myself, so I don't know the layout. Try asking to inspect the Spirit Domain network. Get them to take you into the central control area. As long as you can reach the hub and attach the magnetic block, leave everything else to me.”

Fang Heng listened as he walked down a corridor toward the main control hall.

Several researchers were working inside.

They all stopped what they were doing when he entered and looked toward him.

Research supervisor Heli frowned as she stared at Fang Heng.

“You are… Dr. Fang?”

Heli found the situation very strange.

About two hours earlier, they'd received an internal email stating that a Dr. Fang would arrive as deputy supervisor to participate in Spirit Domain network work.

Dr. Fang?

Where had he come from?

Every detail of his background was classified.
