Chapter 2427

A New Trial

From original Chinese Chapter 2428 · 新的考核

Inside the Death Knight's space.

Fang Heng and his companions' wills returned to their bodies once again.

“Welcome back.”

Blue flames danced in the Death Knight's eyes as he studied the trio. “It seems you've become familiar with fighting through your wills.”

“Death Knight, who were the intruders we just saw?”

“Merely thieves coveting Queen Persephone's power.” Displeasure flickered in his eyes. “They've been appearing more and more frequently. I've long wanted to teach them a lesson they won't forget.”

The Death Knight slowly shifted his gaze toward Fang Heng.

“You did well this time. As promised, I will reward you.”

As he spoke, the outlines of twelve doors reappeared around the space.

Li Shaoqiang raised an eyebrow and immediately smiled with delight.

Here it came!

Perhaps this would be his chance to grind straight to Master-Tier Undead Studies!

“Hiss!”

The doors slowly opened, releasing dense undead energy.

Li Shaoqiang was eager to begin, but noticed Fang Heng had stopped and wasn't moving.

What was going on?

Was something wrong?

Li Shaoqiang immediately wiped the smile from his face.

A chill entered the blue flames of the Death Knight's eyes as he watched the three men make no move to absorb the power.

“What is it? Did you not say you needed strength? The power lies right before you. Why do you not take it?”

Fang Heng stared at him. “I'm curious. What are the inscriptions on the mausoleum's coffins?”

“The coffins?”

The Death Knight seemed surprised. He lowered his head and thought for a moment. “Prayers, perhaps. Queen Persephone wrote prayers for her servants. To those intruders, anything she left would be enough to benefit them for the rest of their lives.”

“Do you have any other questions?”

Fang Heng shook his head slightly.

That explanation was plausible enough.

“Begin. The intruders are pushing deeper into the mausoleum. You have little time left to improve your strength,” the Death Knight said, lightly stroking his sword, “if you wish to continue.”

“Mm.”

Indeed.

He still had plenty of questions.

But now clearly wasn't the time to investigate them.

First, claim the rewards!

Whoosh!

With a thought, the Bone Reliquary appeared before him.

Fang Heng quickly formed a hand seal.

“Whoosh!”

The undead energy in the hall formed an enormous vortex and surged into the Bone Reliquary!

…

The Death Realm.

Empress Persephone's Mausoleum.

Within a small burial chamber, Lord Dani stood guard beside her mentor.

Small numbers of walking corpses continued approaching through the different entrances, but they no longer posed a threat.

Lord Dani spoke quietly with several fellow disciples who were also protecting their mentor.

“Ancient prayers?”

It was the first time Lord Dani had heard of them. “Were they left by Empress Persephone?”

“Mm. Persephone once ruled the entire Death Realm and is regarded as the greatest practitioner of undead studies ever to exist. The prayers of death she established gradually spread and have been passed down ever since. Her original prayers are enormously valuable to our exploration of undead studies.”

Lord Dani glanced at her mentor, who was carefully recording the prayers, then asked, “Senior Brother, there's something I've always found strange. If Persephone was the strongest person in the Death Realm, how did she die?”

“Who said she died?”

“Hmm?”

“She simply disappeared.” Her senior brother shook his head. “Persephone understood undead studies and death better than anyone. Some scholars believe she's already immortal and indestructible.”

“Then why…?”

“Careful!”

Suddenly, their mentor shouted a warning, sprang away from the coffin, and retreated several steps, looking wary.

“Whoosh!”

A huge quantity of pure undead energy poured out of the coffin.

Lord Dani's heart stirred.

Such pure undead energy!

“Dani, be careful. Don't absorb it.”

Lord Dani was startled.

Only then did she realize her mentor and fellow disciples were all watching the mist cautiously. Nobody tried to approach or absorb it, and some even seemed afraid.

“Absorbing undead energy can indeed greatly improve your Undead Studies. But this mist contains Empress Persephone's icy imprint, and that imprint will affect your will.”

“In extreme cases, you could even come under Persephone's control and become her eternal servant.”

A servant?

Surprise flickered in Lord Dani's eyes.

Her senior brother continued, “Don't worry. The mist itself isn't dangerous. We only need to wait until it disperses.”

Lord Dani took a deep breath. “Senior Brother, could you tell me exactly what's going on?”

“It's a long story. From the information the Undead Council has disclosed so far, Persephone once ruled the whole Death Realm, commanding seventy-two Death Knights and tens of thousands of immensely powerful Death Gods…”

Lord Dani's pupils contracted. “Wait! Senior Brother, what did you just say? Death Gods?”

“Yes. Death Gods.”

Their mentor slowly rose and nodded. “Dani, Death Gods do exist. Even now.”

…

The Death Knight's space.

[Hint: Undead energy absorbed. Grandmaster-Tier Undead Studies EXP +651,100.]

[Hint: Grandmaster-Tier Undead Studies has advanced to LV: 28.]

Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

He had to admit that the Death Knight's experience rewards were extraordinarily generous.

Grinding that much experience outside would have taken at least a year of hard work.

Li Shaoqiang and Tan Shuo also opened their eyes, barely containing their excitement.

Whatever was strange about the mission, their advancement in Undead Studies was unquestionably real!

“Your strength is still far too weak. In the past, you would not even have qualified to attempt the trial. But you are fortunate; Her Majesty urgently needs people at present.”

The Death Knight looked down from his throne. “Repel those intruders and teach them a lesson. Then you may receive Queen Persephone's blessing—namely, you may become Death Gods.”

A dark passage portal gradually materialized behind the trio.
