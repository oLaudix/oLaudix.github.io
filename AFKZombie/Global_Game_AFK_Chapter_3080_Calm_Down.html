Chapter 3080

Calm Down

From Chinese Chapter 3081 · 冷静一下

Mu Shunan shook his head. He really wanted to find somewhere to calm himself down.

Seeing that Mu Shunan hadn't answered, Fang Heng was a little disappointed. “Is there something else planned?”

“Not really.”

After being thoroughly shaken by Fang Heng's string of feats, Mu Shunan steadied himself and drew a deep breath. Even then he could barely keep his composure. “I've just been sitting too long. Stay here for a while. I'll go outside, get some air, and rest.”

“Okay.”

Fang Heng was preoccupied with the skill he'd just advanced. Without thinking much of it, he watched Mu Shunan leave and began inspecting the changes to his body.

Overall...

After mental power had strengthened his bones, muscles, skin, and internal organs, his body's affinity with mental power had risen dramatically, as had his efficiency in using spells.

The improvement wasn't reflected in his game statistics. It was something he could feel directly.

From now on, he could cast magical skills faster, at lower cost, and with greater control.

Mysticism really was excellent.

Having broken through this stage, he'd effectively crossed the threshold into Master-level mysticism.

He'd finally made up most of what he'd missed before.

Next came the Grandmaster threshold.

His body's tempering had reached its current limit, though he wasn't sure which direction the next stage of training would take.

Fang Heng closed his eyes once more and slowly examined his body, familiarizing himself with the changes.

Outside, on the other side of the wall, Mu Shunan released a long breath.

“Phew.”

Damn it! He'd seen a bloody ghost!

A fellow warrior happened to pass by. Seeing Mu Shunan's pale face and shaken expression, he couldn't help asking, “Mu Shunan? What's wrong? Did something go wrong with your cultivation?”

“Brother.”

Mu Shunan was popular among the other warriors. He grabbed the man's arm. “Would you believe me if I said someone tempered his bones, muscles, organs, and skin through mysticism in just two hours?”

“Huh?”

The man studied him and replied with perfect seriousness, “I think there's genuinely something wrong with you. Want me to take you to get checked out?”

“No. I just want a little peace and quiet.”

Mu Shunan waved him away.

Had he not seen it himself, he wouldn't have believed it either.

But he'd watched it happen right before his eyes.

Who could possibly take that in stride?

Fortunately, anyone who had cultivated mysticism to Grandmaster level had a resilient will, and Mu Shunan was no exception.

After nearly half an hour gathering himself and sorting out his thoughts, he finally recovered his composure and returned to the training room.

“Brother Mu, I've rested enough.” Fang Heng opened his eyes and looked at him expectantly. “Can we begin whenever you're ready?”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng had nearly shattered Mu Shunan's Dao heart. Having barely recovered, he nodded. “I won't hide it from you, Brother Fang Heng. Your talent is extraordinary—I've never seen its equal. Since you feel you can keep going, let's begin the next stage at once.”

“Good.”

“Once the body can resonate with mental power, the next step is connecting the body to external spiritual perception. That's the defining breakthrough into Grandmaster-level mysticism. The secret is opening the body's switches so it can receive power from outside.”

“To achieve this, you must use hand seals to open the body's various acupoints.”

Seeing Fang Heng deep in thought, Mu Shunan continued, “With hand seals, you can gradually establish a connection between your body and the outside world. Don't worry about understanding it immediately. I'll demonstrate first. Given your talent, you'll probably grasp it faster if you cultivate and comprehend it at the same time.”

“Okay.”

Fang Heng agreed and carefully practiced each hand seal under Mu Shunan's instruction.

Unlike the original sequence of 128 seals, this advanced Grandmaster sequence had to be coordinated with the circulation of mental power.

The difficulty increased considerably.

However, his vastly strengthened mental power had also improved his concentration, so Fang Heng learned quickly.

With Mu Shunan's assistance, in just an hour and a half Fang Heng memorized all 256 seals and their corresponding mental-power circulation. He even completed the entire sequence once.

Five repetitions were enough to master it.

Mu Shunan couldn't find a single remaining error to correct.

He remembered taking more than half a year to learn this stage himself.

A bitter smile tugged at his lips.

By now, he supposed he was used to it.

Overwhelming talent simply wasn't reasonable.

Quest Name: Mysticism Advancement (Grandmaster).

Quest Requirement: Complete External Perception (Current Progress: 0.03%).

Fang Heng opened his eyes and took a look.

So slow.

Even after Mu Shunan helped him adjust both his hand-seal transitions and the way his mental power was activated and circulated, perfectly completing the full 256-seal sequence only yielded about 0.01% progress.

If he had to grind it out alone, it would take ages.

He needed his zombie clones to help.

It was getting close to midnight, and Mu Shunan was growing sleepy. He waved a hand. “Fang Heng, you've memorized all the circulation methods. Let's stop here for today. If anything's unclear, come find me anytime. We can discuss it together.”

“Of course. Thank you for your guidance.”

Fang Heng thanked him with a cupped-fist salute.

Time to go back and grind!

Fang Heng returned to the ground floor, where Anxi was still waiting in the lobby.

“Mr. Fang Heng, you've worked hard. Your training is over, and it's quite late. Would you prefer a room at the inn next door, or would you like to return to the villa?”

“The villa for now.”

“Very well.”

Anxi glanced at Fang Heng and Mu Shunan as they emerged from the elevator together, feeling thoroughly puzzled.

Fang Heng was the one who'd trained all day, while Mu Shunan had merely guided him. Why did Mu Shunan look exhausted in body and spirit while Fang Heng seemed bursting with energy?

...

Back in the villa's lounge, Fang Heng closed his eyes and resumed practicing the seals.

There were 256 of them, and each had to be coordinated with mental-power circulation. Unfamiliar with the sequence, he took a long time to finish a single cycle.

[Hint: Player completed one training cycle (Normal). Current Quest Progress +0.0062%.]

Damn it!

Fang Heng completed another round of seals, opened his eyes, and frowned.

He'd made a small mistake while circulating his mental power through the seals, so his progress had suffered.

It seemed his earlier time estimate had been optimistic. At this rate, he'd need at least a month.

Fang Heng swept his gaze around the room, his attention returning to his zombie clones.

Come on, zombie clones. Let's try it!

The clones couldn't circulate mental power, so they couldn't coordinate it with their seals. Even if they finished, they probably wouldn't generate much progress.

Whatever.

No harm in trying!

One zombie clone received Fang Heng's command, sat down unsteadily, and began forming seals in its palms with painfully stiff movements.
