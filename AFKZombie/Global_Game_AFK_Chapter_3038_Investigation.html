Chapter 3038

Investigation

From Chinese Chapter 3039 · 探查

Kappa felt a little calmer now that he had managed to fool Doris for the time being. He began wandering around the enormous cavern.

On closer inspection, vast sections of writing were engraved on its stone walls.

Much of the writing had been corroded and was extremely difficult to make out.

He didn't know a damn thing about ancient Elven script.

All he could do was put on an act and wait for Fang Heng's response.

In truth, the cavern hall was very spacious. Besides Kappa, more than a dozen small translation teams from different races were present.

No one noticed that Fang Heng, in his soul form, was floating before a wall and studying it carefully.

“Hmm...”

Fang Heng looked over the inscriptions on the rock, thoughtful.

Very interesting.

He couldn't understand most of the characters, but an enormous magic array had been drawn across the entire wall.

“Whoosh!”

The Omniscient Eye spun rapidly in Fang Heng's right pupil.

The array had been there for so long that its traces had become badly worn. Even its overall outline was hard to distinguish, and huge sections were missing.

Even with his Alchemy knowledge and the Omniscient Eye, he could only make out the array's general structure.

An alchemical magic array connected to the ancient Tree of Life.

And it bore similarities to quite a few ancient magic arrays used by the Tree Spirit Clan!

One thing was certain: the Elven Clan and the Tree Spirits, and the Tree of Life and Abe Akaya, were connected in some way.

Fang Heng silently memorized the alchemical arrays from several murals, then floated back to Kappa in his soul form and whispered so only Kappa could hear. “Kappa, we're already on the Elven Clan's planet. I'm going to leave for a while and investigate some clues. Stay here and wait. You'll be fine.”

Hearing him, Kappa nervously shrank his neck again.

Seriously? He was leaving already?

Could they please stop taking such enormous risks?

“Don't worry. Believe in yourself. You can bluff your way through this.”

Before Kappa could respond, Fang Heng's soul form slowly rose and flew toward the opening high in the cavern ceiling.

Fang Heng had already surveyed the place with his senses.

Perhaps the elves had made limited progress studying soul forms, or perhaps they'd simply lived peacefully for tens of millions of years. In any case, he found no seals or protective measures against spirit bodies anywhere in the cavern.

The outer defenses were little more than a simple layer.

In his soul form, he could pass through them easily.

After leaving the cavern, Fang Heng floated over a towering mountain peak and extended his senses to survey the surrounding area.

The entire Elven Clan world was covered in lush greenery.

It resembled the World of Ten Thousand Forests somewhat.

Fang Heng turned his gaze east and narrowed his eyes.

That...

An ancient tree towering into the clouds.

It bore a considerable resemblance to Abe Akaya.

No wonder Klevi had once mistaken Abe Akaya for the ancient Tree of Life.

But Fang Heng could sense that the Tree of Life's power was gradually fading.

Even with the naked eye, he could see piles of yellowed leaves beneath it and broad patches of dark, dried-out roots along the ground.

Perhaps ten years earlier, the area covered by the Tree of Life had been at least a third larger than it was now.

And its power was still declining, slowly withering and shrinking the ground it occupied.

“Hmm...”

Had some external influence caused this?

Was the Tree of Life decaying from within, driving the elves to seek new energy and a way to save their failing ancient tree?

Was that why they'd targeted Abe Akaya, hoping to sustain the Tree of Life by absorbing its power?

Fang Heng had formed a rough theory.

But could it really work?

Absorbing so much power still hadn't changed the fact that the Tree of Life was slowly dying.

In any case, he needed to find Klevi first.

She was probably somewhere near the Tree of Life.

Fang Heng extended his senses to search and darted toward the ancient tree.

...

The outer region of the Tree of Life.

An elven patrol was standing guard.

A gentle wind passed, carrying yellowed leaves to the ground.

The patrol captain, Tewen, shook his head sadly at the sight.

Year after year, the area covered by the Tree of Life had shrunk. Countless vines and branches withered, and its roots were steadily losing vitality.

He had thought he'd grown used to it.

But over the last two years, the tree had been withering faster and faster.

Even he could feel that the Tree of Life was approaching its end more rapidly.

They didn't have much time left.

The High Priest had begun searching everywhere for a way to revive the Tree of Life a hundred years ago.

Unfortunately, none had been found.

At this rate, how much longer could the Tree of Life survive?

Ten years?

Or twenty?

“Hmm?”

The elven patrol suddenly sensed something and turned sharply toward a thicket to the right.

“Who's there? Show yourself!”

Whoosh!!

A dark figure suddenly burst from the shadows.

An enemy!?

Tewen's pupils contracted. He immediately released his psychic perception to lock onto the intruder.

The elven world hadn't encountered an invader in tens of thousands of years.

Had someone from another elven tribe wandered in by mistake?

While Tewen wondered, two of his patrolmen had already launched attacks at the dark figure.

Arrows of Nature!

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!”

Dozens of arrows condensed from highly concentrated psychic power and natural energy shot toward Fang Heng.

“Bang! Bang, bang!!”

Before they could hit, the arrows abruptly exploded in midair near the dark figure!

What?

Tewen's heart jumped. Before he could see anything clearly, he heard two dull impacts and both his subordinates were thrown backward. Then the dark figure was suddenly in front of him, with one hand clamped around his throat.

What the hell!

What was this thing?!

Only then did Tewen clearly see the intruder's face. His pupils widened enormously.

A human!?

How could a human be here?

Had the elven world been exposed?

Forcing himself to stay calm, Tewen asked hoarsely, “W-who are you?”

“I'm looking for Klevi. Where is she?”

“I don't know any Klevi. Human, you've come to the wrong place! You have no business here! How did you enter our world?”

Fang Heng looked coldly at Tewen.

He seemed to be a low-ranking patrolman with little strength. It wasn't surprising he knew nothing about Klevi.

But Fang Heng remembered that while searching Klevi's house, the two elves had said she'd been imprisoned.

“You're in no position to ask questions.” Fang Heng shook his head. “Where is the Elven Clan's prison?”

Tewen's face changed. He fell silent, his thoughts racing.

The mysterious young man had asked for the prison's location at once. He didn't look like anyone with normal intentions.

“Hmph!”

Fang Heng snorted and raised a hand beside Tewen's ear.

The elf on the right, who had just been knocked away, had secretly crept up behind Fang Heng to attack him. Suddenly, he found himself floating helplessly in midair.
