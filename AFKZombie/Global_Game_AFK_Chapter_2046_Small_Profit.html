Chapter 2046

Small Profit

From original Chinese Chapter 2047 · 小赚

Fang Heng waved a hand without much enthusiasm. His undead skeletons shuffled forward and smashed the framework of the defensive barrier the players had just spent so long building.

[Notice: You have obstructed the Holy Court's construction of a defensive barrier against the Yin Vein aura and killed some Holy Court personnel. Additional reward: 520,000 Undead Faction Reputation and 210,000 Undead Faction Honor.]

Oh?

Fang Heng brightened at the notification.

Even that earns Main God Points? And so many? Two hundred and ten thousand Honor—that's about two thousand Main God Points!

Such an easy opportunity!

After a moment's thought, he understood. The game's calculations must have judged that by destroying the construction site, he'd interfered with the Holy Court's efforts to contain the Yin Vein. That wider effect was why the reward was so large.

In that case…

Fang Heng licked his lips and looked into the distance. It was still some time before dawn. Perhaps he could keep farming a little longer.

His Vampire bloodline let him sense numerous concentrations of Holy Court energy nearby, offering him plenty of targets. Several particularly powerful sacred auras were also rapidly approaching his position.

Reinforcements, apparently.

Time to go!

There was no point fighting elite enemy teams head-on. He could simply move somewhere else and keep attacking.

Leaving the wreckage behind, Fang Heng directed his skeletons back into the Yin Vein's frosty territory.

Meanwhile, the Holy Court–aligned players had retreated a safe distance and were crouching there, anxiously waiting for reinforcements.

Then they witnessed something utterly baffling.

What?

Just as the reinforcements were about to arrive, the undead skeletons started withdrawing into the frost again.

Were they messing with us?!

Watching their entire night's work lying in ruins, the players were furious. They didn't dare chase the undead, so they could only curse.

A short while later, Commander Muhiting arrived with his elite troops.

One look at the ruins was enough for him to guess what had happened.

“Where are they?” he asked.

The player squad leader answered bitterly, “They've retreated into the frost. Should we pursue them?”

Muhiting stared at the disappearing skeletons and clenched his fists. The vast cloud of Yin Vein aura was blocking their senses.

Chase them? Fight undead inside the Yin Vein's own territory? Was that anything but suicide?

After a moment, he shook his head.

“Withdraw another kilometer. Start building the barrier again.”

[Notice: You have triggered the quest: Construct a Defensive Barrier.]

[Notice: This is a compulsory mobilization quest. Refusal will result in significant additional penalties.]

Not again!

The players exchanged miserable looks, sighed, and replied without enthusiasm, “Understood.”

Not far away, Fang Heng had been waiting within the frost. He'd deliberately left a few slow-moving skeletons visible in retreat, hoping to lure the reinforcements closer and gain another easy reward.

“Shame…”

The Holy Court hadn't fallen for it. He led his skeletons on toward the next target.

The Yin Vein's influence stretched across an enormous area. To construct an effective containment network along its outer boundary, the Holy Court had to defend an equally vast perimeter.

With so little time to prepare, its forces were spread extremely thin.

And that gave Fang Heng exactly the opening he needed.

Time to farm points!

Using blood sensing, he estimated the number and positions of Holy Court defenders in each region, then picked out the weak spots.

His primary objective wasn't to exhaust the enemy troops. It was to destroy the defensive barriers while they were only half built.

Usually, players accepted the construction quests while a small Holy Court squad helped guard the site.

Now those players were being harassed relentlessly. Frost skeletons emerged again and again from the mist, forcing desperate calls for reinforcements. Each time the workers made progress, their half-finished barrier was destroyed yet again.

The Holy Court was helpless.

Unlike yesterday's zombie-tide strategy, the undead skeletons had switched to hit-and-run attacks, using the Yin Vein aura for cover.

The Holy Court didn't have enough personnel to respond properly. Its forces were constantly being dragged from one crisis to the next.

But there was no better solution. They could only grit their teeth and endure.

…

The situation in Qiboreya wasn't much better than in Duncha.

The number of zombies that had flooded through the passage was so enormous that the Holy Court couldn't possibly eliminate them quickly. Vast groups of zombie clones spread toward the rear of the Holy Court's front-line forces, disrupting the war against the undead.

The Holy Court was forced to fight on two fronts.

Lord Dani and the undead-aligned player guilds were engaged in fierce battle with the Holy Court when reports from outside the game began reaching the various guild leaders.

Duncha!

The Holy Court's headquarters in the mid-tier worlds had been invaded by undead! Worse, a terrifying Yin Vein fissure had appeared there.

Reliable intelligence also confirmed that the Yin Vein fissure in Punoze had vanished!

Everyone began connecting the dots. Their eyes turned toward Lord Dani.

Clearly, she had known about this beforehand.

At the start of the day's offensive, she had told them that Fang Heng would be carrying out a secret operation behind enemy lines. She'd urged them to fight with two hundred percent effort—to stake everything on the chance of a better future. When the time came, Fang Heng would deliver an astonishing surprise.

They'd followed her instructions and fought desperately, eagerly awaiting that surprise.

But nobody had imagined Fang Heng's surprise would be quite this outrageous!

He'd somehow attacked Duncha itself, the Holy Court's headquarters, and even transferred the Yin Vein fissure there, dragging the entire world into a state of war!

Duncha was the Holy Court's home base and had many teleportation routes to other worlds. Naturally, a large proportion of its reinforcements would now be diverted to save Duncha first.

That would dramatically reduce the pressure on the undead front line.

And already, they could see the first portions of a vast zombie horde emerging behind the Holy Court's battle formations.

A pincer attack!

Perhaps tonight they could finally achieve a decisive breakthrough on the main battlefield.

— End of Chapter 2046 —
