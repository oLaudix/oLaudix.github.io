Chapter 3110

Emergency Assignment

From Chinese Chapter 3111 · 紧急事件

"It's not that nobody has reached that level. It's just that mentors with Demigod-level strength have never offered courses at that level. I'm not clear on the exact reason."

"Is that so..." Fang Heng thought for a moment. "Forget it, then. Help me book lessons in other disciplines."

He couldn't help feeling disappointed.

From his recent training, it was clear that both Mysticism and Alchemy could substantially increase his cultivation speed.

He had planned to focus on those two disciplines and raise them as high as possible first.

Once he'd maxed them out all the way to God level and improved his efficiency, he could fill in the other disciplines one by one.

He hadn't expected to hit a bottleneck at the Demigod level already.

It was strange, too.

Why did every academic instructor stop offering lessons at the Demigod level?

Fang Heng decided he'd find a chance to ask Nasir about it.

"Of course, Mr. Fang Heng. I'll make the arrangements."

Anxi relaxed slightly when Fang Heng didn't insist, then continued, "There's something else, Mr. Fang Heng. I've just received notice of an urgent protection assignment. The Noel Clan needs you to proceed immediately to the Lanyue Clan's territory on a guard mission."

"You've been selected to accompany an escort team as one of its warriors. Your duty will be to protect the group and follow the orders of the elder in charge. I'll pick you up tomorrow morning and take you to the assembly point. Will that be all right?"

Fang Heng's interest was piqued.

Work had arrived.

Becoming a warrior of the Noel Clan came with all kinds of benefits, but there was one important obligation: he had to answer the clan's call for special temporary assignments.

The Kinsfolk worked similarly. Yesterday's assassination mission had probably been assigned to warriors they'd recruited.

Fang Heng nodded. "All right. Understood."

...

The next day, Anxi took Fang Heng to the meeting point.

It was a starship docked in the world's port district.

Fang Heng spotted a familiar figure in the distance and waved. "Nasir!"

By coincidence, Nasir had also accepted the assignment and was chatting with several other warriors on the team.

"Brother Fang Heng! You're here! I was the one who recommended you to the elder for this mission."

Nasir came over enthusiastically to greet him and gave him a reassuring look.

"First time on one of these assignments, right? Don't worry. Just stick with me. I've read the mission briefing. It should be almost effortless. We're basically there to put on a show and act as a deterrent. Chances are we won't have to fight at all."

"I expect the mission to last quite a while, though. Maybe more than half a month."

Fang Heng found that rather odd.

Why had Nasir specifically recommended him for this assignment?

Before he could ask, Mu Shunan noticed them and came over.

"Fang Heng, you're here too. How has your Mysticism training been going? You couldn't have broken through again already, could you?"

"Not yet."

Mu Shunan immediately felt a wave of relief.

If he'd broken through again, wouldn't he already be approaching the threshold of the Demigod level?

And the brat was still so young. Terrifying!

They chatted briefly before returning to the subject of the mission.

Mu Shunan asked, "What exactly are we being sent to do? This assignment seems awfully urgent."

"It's related to the Lanyue Clan. I took part in some preliminary work. They've discovered a valuable metal in their star system, and the Noel Clan needs it. We've obtained mining rights, but the clan is concerned that the Lanyue people may resist the collection of resources. So we're accompanying the mining operation as protection."

"Just treat it like a vacation. The assignment isn't difficult." Nasir shrugged and looked at Fang Heng. "The main reason I recommended you was your cultivation."

"Hm?" Fang Heng frowned in puzzlement. "It's related to training?"

"Yes. The Lanyue Clan has been known as specialists in Alchemy since ancient times, though their inheritance has become somewhat fragmented in the last few generations. I've wanted to visit them for a long time. When I went there previously, I discovered something interesting."

As he spoke, Nasir gave Fang Heng an almost imperceptible wink.

Fang Heng understood immediately.

There was more to the story, but Nasir didn't want to say it in front of so many people.

"I see. Then we really should take a good look together."

"Don't get your hopes up. It might not be useful. Consider it a lucky draw. I'll explain the details later."

"All right."

"Warriors, everyone's here. I'm Captain Hoffman."

As they were talking, the Noel Clan's starship captain Hoffman entered the main cabin, accompanied by several guards.

Hoffman surveyed the gathering. His gaze lingered briefly on Fang Heng before he nodded. "Thank you all for coming. The mission objective has been arranged in advance, and everyone should have read the briefing. This is a relatively easy mission. The target system is some distance from the Seven-Realm Martial Capital; hyperspace travel will take approximately one hour and twenty minutes. Cabins have been prepared for you aboard the ship. If you're all ready, we'll begin the jump in ten minutes."

Fang Heng checked the roster. There were only seven warriors participating in the mission.

Fortunately, both Nasir and Mu Shunan were familiar faces.

He recognized most of the others too. They were mid- or low-ranking warriors.

The assignment really didn't appear difficult.

Nasir waved a hand. "Come on, let's get some rest. Spatial jumps feel awful. See you when we land."

Hyperspace travel was uncomfortable, so the warriors all returned to their cabins to rest.

...

A little over two hours later, the starship completed its jump and landed on the planet's surface.

The captain accompanied the warriors to meet the Lanyue Clan's leadership.

The Lanyue representatives appeared reasonably respectful.

Next, the Noel Clan would carry out its mining operation in the region.

The warriors left their rooms and came onto the deck to observe the world.

In the Lanyue Clan's world, blue clouds drifted across the sky. The air was fresh and cool, and enormous stretches of tubular green vegetation covered the ground.

"Ah, not a bad place." Nasir took a few breaths of fresh air, glanced at Fang Heng, and asked, "Don't you think so, Brother Fang Heng?"

Fang Heng examined the surroundings and thought for a moment.

The Kinsfolk had installed Black Mist detection prisms throughout the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, but not in this world. Even if Black Mist spread here, no one would detect it.

Back in the capital, he had felt obvious resistance whenever he used Abe Akaya's summoning array or his special abilities to enter and leave the game.

Fang Heng suspected the Kinsfolk had installed some devices interfering with his game abilities.

Now that he was outside their territory, he could test whether those abilities were still affected.
