Chapter 2159

Reconnaissance

From original Chinese Chapter 2160 · 侦查

At the center of the battlefield.

The Federation semi-mechanized squad watched Fang Heng, wrapped in a blood curtain, rushing toward them!

Every injection fired from their guns was intercepted by the crimson barrier!

No effect!

The squad captain gritted his teeth and shouted, “Use beam weapons! Break through that shield first!”

Boom! Boom, boom!

Dense beams struck the blood curtain.

Still nothing!

Though it looked no thicker than a sheet, the blood curtain absorbed the energy beams with ease!

And Fang Heng was getting closer!

Perhaps only much larger focused beam weapons could break through that defense.

“Withdraw!”

The captain barked the order.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh...

Spatial-transition lights immediately appeared around the semi-mechanized squad. Before Fang Heng could reach them, they vanished from the spot.

Fang Heng frowned.

With the Spirit Domain blockade fully restored, the Federation's transition efficiency had improved drastically.

When they couldn't win, they simply fled.

And he had no good way to counter it.

Forget it.

His mission was only to buy time for the Tree Spirits.

Changing targets, Fang Heng turned and chased another Federation semi-mechanized squad.

In truth, the Federation had no particularly effective way to deal with Fang Heng either.

In the Federation's central command room, the atmosphere grew tense as everyone watched the remote surveillance feeds.

“What exactly is that blood curtain?”

“We haven't been able to collect samples, so we have no analysis data. The fluctuations we've recorded aren't sufficient either. Judging by its properties, it probably isn't a conventional energy beam...”

“Find a way to restrain him!”

“Understood!”

“He has spatial movement capabilities. Set up a spatial blockade!”

“We're already preparing one. It'll take at least half an hour...”

One semi-mechanized Federation squad after another arrived on the battlefield through spatial transition.

Fang Heng darted around disrupting them for some time. The Lickers at the rear were gradually worn down by the Federation forces, until several red game notifications flashed across his retina.

[Hint: The player's current quest—Cover the Tree Spirits' Retreat—is complete.]

[Hint: Quest completion rate: 3,515%. The player gains 3 Tree Spirit favorability and 350,000 Tree Spirit faction points.]

Done!

Quest complete!

Fang Heng gave the battlefield one final glance, then used Secondary Spatial Projection and vanished in front of everyone.

Time to head back!

Within the secondary space projection, Fang Heng sped toward the town.

Next, he needed to find the Tree Spirits quickly and exchange his quest contribution points.

After all, his relationship with them had already fallen to -46. Who knew when they'd forcibly kick him out of the faction?

Wouldn't that be a huge loss?

Under the cover of night, Fang Heng stepped out of Secondary Spatial Projection, materialized in the air, transformed into bat form, and sped toward town.

Outside the research institute.

Seeing Fang Heng disappear into the spatial projection, the Federation's semi-mechanized troops actually felt a wave of relief.

That unkillable monster!

They'd finally driven him off!

Now they still had to deal with the remaining Lickers.

“All right! Stay sharp, everyone!”

The squad commander shouted to his exhausted soldiers.

They pulled themselves together.

Heavy war machines and semi-mechanized Federation squads continued arriving through spatial transition to fight the remaining Licker army.

Even with the specially tailored anti-virus injections, the Lickers still possessed enormous health reserves and were exceedingly troublesome to eliminate.

Amid the countless flickering transition points...

Two pale-blue lights were hardly conspicuous.

The lights gradually dimmed.

Two Dependent scouts stepped out of the spatial-transition glow.

For a moment, an eerie stillness settled over the entire battlefield.

The Federation soldiers couldn't help turning to look at the Dependents.

The Dependents!

It was their first time seeing living members of that race!

The Dependents looked different from humans. They were unusually tall, with elongated but sturdy skeletons and deep blue-gray skin that had a metallic sheen.

Strangely, their faces were covered by a special gelatinous substance, making them resemble faceless figures from a film.

Most striking of all, strips of fine, fur-like material grew from their heads down along their spines to their tailbones. The strands extended behind them like flowing cloaks.

Additionally, each Dependent possessed a symbiotic psychic gemstone at the position of the heart.

With every heartbeat, the gemstone emitted a rhythmic pulse.

Seeing the battle still raging, a grave look entered one scout's eyes. He extended a hand and pointed forward.

The joints of the Dependents' fingers were slender and much longer than those of ordinary humans.

Whoosh...

Not far away, a Licker that had just been blasted backward by beam artillery was seized by an unseen force, lifted slowly into the air, and drawn toward the scout.

A grave look flashed in the scout's eyes beneath the gelatinous facial covering. His fingers tapped the air several times.

Bang!

The Licker suspended in midair suddenly exploded!

The shredded flesh and blood didn't scatter. An invisible force contained it all within a spherical boundary.

Rows of analytical data appeared on the scout's retina.

Suddenly...

A dazzling red warning filled his entire field of vision.

Both scouts' expressions changed sharply. They exchanged glances, then looked toward the battlefield again.

· · ·

At the Federation's remote command headquarters, morale was somewhat low.

After a bitter battle, not only had the Tree Spirits escaped, but Fang Heng had slipped away as well.

“Sir.”

A soldier hurried forward and whispered something in the commander's ear.

The commander's expression shifted. “What? Are you certain?”

“Yes. The Dependents' verification team seems to have discovered some suspicious traces. They entered the central institute's battle zone by spatial transition a short while ago to collect samples personally. They've just confirmed detecting traces of an Ouroboros sample.”

The soldier continued quietly, “The Dependents have already issued a threat alert through the Spirit Domain system. Our world has been placed into risk mode. The Dependents will soon activate a purification procedure and deploy a cleanup team.”

“However, they've only detected fragments of a genetic sequence associated with Ouroboros. There is no detailed evidence establishing a definite connection yet, so the alert is currently at the lowest risk level...”

The commander frowned deeply as he listened.

Ouroboros?

A calamity?

Could it be real?

Had something like that truly descended upon their world by coincidence?

Did that mean Fang Heng was some kind of calamity?

Countless thoughts crossed the commander's mind.

Certainly, Fang Heng was strong—so strong that he'd shocked them all.

But...

Fang Heng, an incarnation of some mysterious calamity?

It seemed a little too far-fetched.

— End of Chapter 2159 —
