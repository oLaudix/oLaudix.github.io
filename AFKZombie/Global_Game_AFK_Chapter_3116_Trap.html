Chapter 3116

Trap

From Chinese Chapter 3117 · 陷阱

"Don't celebrate just yet. I suspect something troublesome has been sealed here. Why else would they need so many layers of alchemical sealing arrays?"

Fang Heng turned away from the wall and examined the passage stretching into darkness behind them. He said calmly, "Or perhaps... all these seals are meant to imprison *us*?"

Nasir grew tense at once and swallowed nervously.

That was true.

He'd been too excited to think about it. Law-based alchemical arrays were extraordinarily powerful, especially when combined with high-level Sealing Studies.

So what terrifying thing had been sealed inside this cavern?

"No point guessing. We'll know when we see it."

Fang Heng headed deeper into the dark passage.

After more than thirty paces, the tunnel gradually widened.

A huge cavern lay ahead.

In the distance, a massive dark shape lay sprawled at its center.

Fang Heng took several more steps and narrowed his eyes, slowly making out the black mass on the ground.

"Gulp..."

Nasir swallowed nervously at the sight of the enormous monster in the cavern. Fear flickered in his eyes, and even his voice trembled.

"A Dimensional Evil Embryo—the manifestation of the Evil God Phoenicia."

Looking carefully, Fang Heng saw a mound of rotting flesh as high as a small hill. Scattered dark eyeballs covered its surface, and reddish-brown blood vessels extended from the flesh, seeming to pierce directly into space.

The vessels twitched from time to time, as though drawing strength from space itself.

Hearing Nasir's murmur, Fang Heng asked softly, "What?"

"It's a legendary creature, a manifestation of an evil god—a Dimensional Evil Embryo. I've read descriptions. Its vascular tendrils can enter different dimensional spaces and absorb their energy to sustain it. Every eyeball embedded in its flesh possesses catastrophic power sufficient to destroy a small planet."

Nasir explained in a low voice as he slowly retreated. "This thing is supposed to exist only in the universe's Endless Void. It can enter the universe only if summoned through a special ritual. They say that if you feed one, it'll reward whoever feeds it. Damn it. The Lanyue Clan has been raising this monstrous thing! Aren't they afraid it'll destroy them too?"

"Feeding it?" Fang Heng asked as he studied the mountain of flesh. "What exactly do you feed something like that?"

"Suffering."

Just describing the creature sent a chill down Nasir's spine.

They were finished.

Trapped in this godforsaken place with a creature on the level of an evil god, how could they possibly survive?

Fang Heng watched the mass of flesh at the cavern's center from a distance.

The universe was vast and full of unknowns. Even the Seven-Realm Martial Capital had explored only a tiny fraction of it.

An Evil Embryo.

What rank of strength did it possess?

Nasir wanted nothing to do with such a terrifying creature. He led Fang Heng back the way they'd come, searching their surroundings for an exit.

Meanwhile, in the upper section of the cavern...

Numerous Lanyue priests knelt around the central ritual array, chanting in low voices.

As their chanting and prayers continued, the central array began to rotate slowly.

Dark light surrounded the magic array, and thin shadowy tendrils gradually extended from within it.

"Hiss, hiss-hiss-hiss..."

The tendrils crept toward the outside of the array until they attached themselves to one of the chanting priests.

Within seconds, the priest's body aged visibly, as if all his life force had been drained away. Soon, only a dried skeleton remained.

Terror spread across the remaining priests' faces. They shut their eyes and chanted faster, silently praying that the Evil Embryo would not choose them next.

Edwin watched from the side with contempt in his eyes.

These lunatics of the Lanyue Clan.

They were feeding an evil god from the Endless Void. No wonder the entire race had become something neither human nor ghost.

Whoosh!!

Only after devouring the lives of more than a dozen priests did the ritual array finally settle down. The black tendrils gradually withdrew, and a pale-yellow glow spread across the formation.

"Lord Evil God!"

The Lanyue Clan's patriarch immediately fell to his knees and shouted, "Please help us eliminate the intruders! The Lanyue Clan is willing to become your faithful followers, offer you the power of suffering forever, and sing praises of your greatness day and night..."

A dull yellow light flashed across the ritual array, then gradually faded.

Edwin frowned at the Lanyue patriarch, who was little more than skin and bone. "It's done?"

"The ritual has succeeded. The Evil God will help us kill the intruders," the patriarch replied calmly. "Fang Heng has been dealt with. You may report back. Remember what the Kinsfolk promised us."

"Tch."

Edwin snorted disdainfully. "I understand. I'll deliver your message."

The Lanyue Clan was nothing but a bunch of short-sighted fools.

Of course he would pass their message to the Kinsfolk.

But they actually expected the Kinsfolk to come and save them?

They would have to see whether they could survive until help arrived.

"Lead the way. Activate the spatial teleportation passage. We need to report this to the Kinsfolk as soon as possible."

"Follow me."

...

Outside the cavern, Mu Shunan finally managed to escape through the entrance and rejoin the team stationed there.

Seeing only Mu Shunan emerge, Captain Hoffman frowned deeply. "What happened? Why are you alone? Where are the others?"

"We've run into trouble. Edwin and Nick were bought off by the Kinsfolk. Fang Heng and Nasir were trapped by a magic array."

Mu Shunan quickly recounted what had happened inside.

When Hoffman finished listening, his brows remained tightly furrowed as anger surged in his chest.

The Lanyue Clan!

And the Kinsfolk!

Before the mission, one of his clan's elders had warned him that the Kinsfolk might target a pan-human warrior. They had therefore urgently assigned that warrior to this mission to avoid a direct confrontation.

Yet the Kinsfolk still refused to relent. They had pursued Fang Heng all the way here to kill him.

The Noel Clan had already yielded so much.

And still the Kinsfolk kept pressing forward without showing the slightest intention of stopping, as though the Noel Clan counted for nothing!

"Report this to the leadership. Edwin and Nick are suspected of colluding with outsiders to harm our clan's interests. Issue a universal wanted notice for both of them, and request reinforcements from the clan."

Captain Hoffman said gravely, "We'll hold this position and wait for the reinforcement team."

Mu Shunan frowned as he looked toward the cavern's dark opening.

The Lanyue Clan itself wasn't powerful, but this cavern was extremely troublesome. With the two traitorous warriors Edwin and Nick involved, they still didn't know what dangers lay inside.

What if the Kinsfolk had planted other forces there in advance?

Trying to force their way in now could easily cost them the entire starship and its crew.

Mu Shunan knew perfectly well that Hoffman's decision was the correct one.

But...

Fang Heng, you had better not die in there!

That brat might be infuriatingly talented, but he was a genius who appeared only once in ten thousand years. Mu Shunan didn't want him to die in a place like this for no good reason.

— End of Chapter 3116 —
