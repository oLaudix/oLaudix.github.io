Chapter 2045

Low Morale

From original Chinese Chapter 2046 · 士气低落

At that moment, more than half of Duncha was in chaos.

Emergency evacuation announcements played constantly over the broadcasts. Cities expected to be affected by the Yin Vein aura were organizing complete evacuations, while Holy Court reinforcements converged on the source of the eruption.

Players in the Duncha game world received quest notices as well, assigning them to escort evacuees, construct defensive barriers, and undertake similar emergency duties.

For Fang Heng, Duncha had practically become paradise.

He only needed to direct his undead skeletons to cause a little destruction, and Undead Faction contribution, reputation, and Honor would flow into his account. There were so many possible targets that he hardly knew where to start.

Duncha was a Holy Court stronghold, after all. Almost everyone born there had been raised in the faith from childhood.

The cities should have the most believers. I'll take a look there first.

He led his hard-earned army of undead skeletons toward the nearest city, hoping to cause trouble.

When he arrived, however, he was disappointed.

Unlike Punoze, Duncha had no defensive barriers built up over centuries to hold back the Frost Prison's aura. The cold had spread much faster than Fang Heng anticipated.

By the time he reached the city, the whole place was frozen solid. The civilians had already evacuated.

A wasted trip!

Since he'd come all this way, he kept going anyway, hoping to find some Holy Court forces farther ahead.

“Hm?”

After another half-hour of plodding forward, Fang Heng noticed something.

There they are! Holy Court troops!

What were they doing?

A scarlet sheen spread across his pupils as he focused on the distance.

Beyond the region already overrun by the Yin Vein's aura, Holy Court teams were urgently constructing a defensive network.

Fang Heng frowned. It appeared they intended to use the same method employed in Punoze: build a perimeter of barriers around the spreading frost to intercept and partially purify it.

“Tsk. They're really sparing no expense.”

The Frost Prison's aura was extraordinarily powerful. Even a completed defensive network could probably only stop seventy to eighty percent of it, as in Punoze.

The rest would still cover Duncha in ice and snow. The Holy Court would pay a terrible price either way.

Hold on…

Another thought occurred to Fang Heng.

I can't let them do that! If their barrier works, won't it reduce the contribution points I can earn?

He'd been counting on farming Main God Points during this disaster to fund permanent upgrades for his zombie clones. He wasn't going to let the Holy Court interfere so easily.

Take them down!

“Attack!”

Fang Heng commanded his elite frost skeletons to assemble and charge en masse toward the Holy Court personnel building the barrier.

…

On the outskirts of the nearby city, the players tasked with construction were miserable.

Just last night they'd been celebrating, expecting to march alongside the Holy Court into Qiboreya and pick up easy rewards in its wake.

Before dawn, that dream had been shattered.

They'd woken up to discover that Duncha itself was under undead invasion!

Every Holy Court–aligned player had received a compulsory mobilization quest. Refusal wasn't an option unless they wanted to abandon it and lose huge amounts of reputation and contribution points.

Those points were difficult for ordinary players to earn, so the prospect of a massive penalty extinguished any thought of refusal.

They had no choice but to accept and report for duty.

Their task was to build a defensive barrier within a designated area before the time limit. Failure would still be punished, but not as severely as abandoning the quest outright.

The most aggravating part was that the difficulty was variable. No one knew what unexpected trouble might arise halfway through.

And, as if summoned by their fears, trouble appeared.

While the players worked diligently alongside the Holy Court, a mass of pale undead skeletons emerged from the distant blue mist.

The players shuddered.

You have got to be kidding. Are we really that unlucky?

The relatively small group of Holy Court NPCs immediately advanced to confront the approaching skeletons.

“Damn it!” the player squad leader cursed under his breath. The quest description hadn't mentioned having to fight undead!

Still, there was a silver lining.

At least they were only skeletons. The players were no longer most afraid of ordinary bones, high-tier undead, or even wraiths.

It was zombies that terrified them.

And at least they weren't facing Fang Heng himself.

The leader steadied himself and gave orders.

“Hold them off! Be careful and keep your distance. Hit them with ranged spells!”

“Understood!”

No one wanted to die, so the players began bombarding the approaching frost skeletons with ranged sacred magic.

But the Frost Prison environment greatly enhanced the skeletons' strength.

When a small group of Holy Court NPCs charged forward, they quickly realized they couldn't stop the enemy at all.

More and more undead skeletons emerged from the slowly spreading blue mist. The paladins lost their nerve and began withdrawing.

The players were certainly not about to fight to the death after seeing that.

The squad leader gave a bitter smile.

Wonderful. This game is impossible.

It was bad enough that they couldn't defeat the zombies. Now they couldn't even beat skeletons.

Maybe we should just surrender.

“Fall back! We're leaving!”

These players had been forced into the quest in the first place. The instant the battle turned against them, their morale collapsed. After a moment's consideration, they decided their lives mattered more.

They withdrew, accepted a failed quest and the resulting deduction of reputation and contribution, and left the problem to the Holy Court's elite reinforcements when they arrived.

Bad luck. Better than dying.

Hidden among his vast army of frost skeletons, Fang Heng watched the players retreat decisively.

“What's wrong with them? They ran without even trying to fight. Wouldn't they at least give it a shot?”

He found it strange, and a little disappointing.

What was wrong with young people these days? Where was their courage?

Unfortunately, although frost skeletons were faster than zombies, they couldn't catch players who were determined to flee.

A pity. Without kills, his rewards would be limited.

Before long, the skeletons eliminated the small group of paladins left behind.

— End of Chapter 2045 —
