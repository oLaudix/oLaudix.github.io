Chapter 2077

Again

From original Chinese Chapter 2078 · 再来一次

As Fang Heng considered his plan, his vision gradually cleared.

He was standing in a maple forest. A light autumn breeze stirred the leaves, which drifted down like a brilliant red curtain, spreading a patchwork of color across the ground.

The dark entrance to a cavern was faintly visible in the distance.

Lindro and the other teammates materialized nearby.

Lindro looked toward the cavern. “Let's begin. The targets are inside. There may be huge numbers of demons this time, so everyone be careful.”

“Right.”

They nodded and prepared themselves.

At least they wouldn't need to defeat a boss this time. The mission looked simpler than the previous one.

Lindro and the other paladin entered first, leading the party down the passage.

They advanced cautiously until they encountered a shallow downward slope.

Soon, rustling sounds emerged from the darkness.

The party immediately moved into its combat formation.

Whoosh!

More than a dozen dark figures darted out of the shadows.

“Mole demons!”

Having identified them through his reconnaissance skill, Sak immediately called out the information from the rear.

“Book of Sacred Radiance!”

Sacred light flared from his starting book, instantly dispelling the surrounding darkness.

Fang Heng could now see the creatures clearly. They were large, dark-brown rodents with long, sharp front teeth and bloodthirsty eyes.

“Attack!”

Lindro gave the order and moved with the other paladin to block the approaching swarm.

They were only ordinary monsters. A competent five-person party could deal with them safely, provided it stayed alert. There was plenty of room for mistakes.

Fang Heng sensed the blood-vitality fluctuations hidden around them and flipped his wrist, summoning his sacred scepter.

Farming this way was too slow!

Time to speed things up!

Go!

While Lindro and the paladin were occupied fighting the mole demons, a figure suddenly shot past them.

Fang Heng!

He charged deeper into the cavern alone!

That reckless?

Lindro frowned. More demons immediately burst from the shadows and rushed toward Fang Heng.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng swung the Scepter of Sacred Blessing again, its strike whistling through the air before smashing into an approaching creature.

BANG!

The demon was sent flying.

Fang Heng stopped a few hundred meters ahead of Lindro's party.

Whoosh!

The Book of Oaths floated before him.

Skill—Thunder of Judgment.

He applied a buff to himself at remarkable speed, then channeled mental strength through his sacred scepter.

Hum!

Sacred energy surged through the weapon and erupted outward in a spreading wave.

The attack wasn't especially powerful.

But the burst of sacred energy enraged the masses of mole demons lurking in the darkness, drawing them straight toward Fang Heng!

In the blink of an eye, he was almost swallowed by the horde.

Wasn't that going too far?!

Faced with the unexpected situation, Lindro hesitated, afraid his team would be surrounded if it rushed forward.

Then his eyes widened.

Despite being completely surrounded, Fang Heng stood calmly in the center, letting the mole demons attack him.

Tiny damage numbers rose continuously above his head as he occasionally swung his scepter to knock back enemies that came too close!

Crackle! Crackle, crackle!

Electric arcs flashed throughout the swarm.

Passive buff—Thunder of Judgment!

Every time a mole demon struck Fang Heng, the passive lightning effect had a chance to activate, shocking nearby enemies and dealing damage across a wide area.

From a distance, Lindro's teammates watched in silence, exchanging disbelieving glances.

Sak even stopped preparing the healing spell he'd started casting.

It looked like Fang Heng didn't need healing at all.

Even under the constant assault, his wounds closed almost immediately!

Sak stepped closer to the defensive paladin beside him and whispered, “Brother, that skill of his looks pretty amazing…”

“Amazing? That's a talent skill!” the paladin replied with absolute conviction, watching Fang Heng withstand the densely packed horde without budging. His eyes were full of envy. “It's definitely S-rank!”

Thunder of Judgment continued triggering. The mole demons lacked natural recovery and quickly grew weaker.

[Hint: Your party has killed a Mole Demon.]

[Hint: Your party has killed an Elite Mole Demon…]

Half a minute later, kill notifications began rolling in faster and faster.

“We're getting carried…” the paladin muttered as he watched his game log.

Fang Heng could handle the entire fight alone. There was no need for the others to interfere. In fact, helping might only make things worse.

After a quick discussion, the party decided to guard the cavern entrance and let him handle the monsters.

They watched the quest progress rise all the way to 98%.

Then, with victory almost within reach, Fang Heng suddenly stopped. He swept the creatures aside with his scepter, retreated quickly toward his teammates, and shouted, “Fall back!”

Lindro frowned. Assuming Fang Heng had discovered some unexpected danger, he immediately signaled the party to protect him and withdraw.

The group formed around Fang Heng and retreated out of the cavern.

Once outside, everyone looked at him.

“Brother Fang Heng, did you discover something?” Lindro asked.

Sak's eyes lit up with anticipation. “Could it be a clue related to a hidden quest?”

Hidden quests sometimes existed even within trials. If players uncovered and completed one, the ultimate rewards could rival those of an SSS-class mission.

Of course, finding such quests was extremely rare.

But after witnessing Fang Heng's strength over the past few hours, Lindro's team was convinced they were traveling with an exceptional expert.

Surely someone like him might notice a hidden quest during the cavern fight!

Otherwise, why interrupt the mission and mysteriously lead them outside?

Fang Heng had been struggling to invent a reason to leave. Their question made him pause.

A hidden quest?

Well, that worked nicely!

— End of Chapter 2077 —

  • Thunder of Judgment. 审判之雷霆 is a buff whose passive retaliation repeatedly shocks attackers.
  • Quest interruption. The party stops at approximately 98% and assumes Fang Heng has found a hidden-quest clue.
