Chapter 3081

Inefficient Grinding

From Chinese Chapter 3082 · 低效的刷

The zombie clone's movements were still stiff. With tremendous difficulty, it formed the seals in its palms one by one.

It took nearly half an hour.

Only after it completed the entire sequence did a game notification appear on Fang Heng's retina.

[Hint: Player's zombie clone completed seal training (Extremely Inefficient). Current Quest Progress +0.0001%.]

Success!

The zombie had no mental power to circulate alongside the seals, and the game had rated its training extremely inefficient. Nevertheless, it counted as completing a session and contributed to the quest progress.

That meant he could cultivate with an entire horde of zombies!

Fang Heng raised his head and looked around.

Over the past two days, while he cultivated, his zombies had expanded part of the basement on their own and dug down another four levels.

Let's begin!

The zombie swayed, and its body gradually began splitting.

One became two, two became four...

Soon the basement was packed with a dense mass of zombies. Their bodies contorted into strange postures as they repeatedly formed seals before their chests.

All set!

Having assigned the zombies to seal practice, Fang Heng slowly closed his own eyes and concentrated on cultivating.

...

“Morning! What's wrong? Didn't sleep well?”

“Ah, yeah.”

At the Noel Clan company building, Mu Shunan forced a smile and greeted the approaching warrior.

He hadn't slept well for days. Every time he closed his eyes, he remembered Fang Heng's terrifying aptitude for mysticism, leaving him mentally dazed.

He'd only felt better after taking some sleeping pills the previous night.

At least he hadn't seen Fang Heng over the past two days.

His cultivation was probably going fine, right?

Stop thinking about it. Stop thinking!

Mu Shunan shook his head, trying to drive away those distracting thoughts.

He still needed to visit the combat preparation room later and discuss the next arena match with several companions.

Higher-level competitions often determined how lucrative interests were distributed. The various star-sector powers took them extremely seriously and almost always committed their full strength.

“Hm? Fang Heng?”

In the corridor, Mu Shunan suddenly spotted Fang Heng and Anxi approaching, much to his surprise.

“Brother Mu! Hello. Didn't sleep well?” Fang Heng waved in greeting. Seeing the dark circles under his eyes, he added, “I know a few methods for treating insomnia. Want to try them?”

How strange.

Mu Shunan was a warrior who had already entered the Transcendent level. Why was he so troubled that he had dark circles from exhaustion?

Fang Heng thought Mu Shunan was a decent fellow and was quite willing to help.

Psychological suggestion from the study of the mind worked wonders against insomnia.

Provided Mu Shunan was willing to cooperate.

Natural studies had a Tranquility technique that could lower one's guard and allow peaceful rest.

Add an astrologically arranged room based on the positions of the stars...

With all three methods combined, mere insomnia would hardly be a problem.

“No, no, I'm fine. I did have some things on my mind, but I've put them aside now.”

Mu Shunan thought, *Who do you think gave me these dark circles in the first place?* He smiled awkwardly. “I heard you've been staying home cultivating. What brings you here today?”

“Mm. I've arranged a private alchemy lesson with another warrior recruited by the Noel Clan. He has some time, so I came specifically to ask for instruction.”

Mu Shunan froze, his heart jolting. His barely repaired Dao heart seemed about to crack again. He hurriedly asked, “You've already advanced to Grandmaster-level mysticism?”

“Not yet.”

After grinding for three straight days with help from his zombie clones, he'd reached 45% progress.

He was getting close.

At most, another week of AFK training should finish it.

Of course, after advancing to Grandmaster-level mysticism, he'd still have to absorb natural-studies energy and convert it into mysticism energy. Reaching the Transcendent breakthrough bottleneck would take some more time.

Since joining the Noel Clan, Fang Heng could clearly feel himself growing stronger every day.

And unlike before, he no longer had to wander around outside all day, scheme against others, or fight for his life.

It felt fantastic.

His motivation was sky-high.

Mu Shunan relaxed slightly.

Thank goodness he hadn't advanced yet.

Otherwise, Mu Shunan would lose another night's sleep.

Fang Heng explained with a smile, “Advanced alchemy instructors are difficult to book, so I came in today.”

“Oh, so it's alchemy.” Mu Shunan considered this. “Is your instructor Nasir?”

“Yes. You know him?”

Mu Shunan's gaze grew even stranger.

Last time he'd sensed elemental mental power surging in Fang Heng's body, he'd assumed the youngster studied elemental magic. It turned out he had achievements in alchemy too?

Alchemy wasn't especially popular among the academic disciplines. It was far more obscure than mysticism.

And didn't Nasir only teach Transcendent-level alchemy?

Could this kid possibly...

Already be a Grandmaster alchemist?

There were outsiders present in the building, and Mu Shunan felt it would be rude to directly ask about Fang Heng's academic rank. Suppressing his curiosity, he smiled. “Yes, Nasir and I are old friends. Mention my name to him. It'll help.”

“Haha, thanks. I've arranged a time with Instructor Nasir. We'll talk next time.”

Fang Heng saluted Mu Shunan with cupped fists and departed for the training room.

Watching Fang Heng leave, Mu Shunan felt as though ten thousand ants were crawling inside him.

The itch was unbearable.

Damn it!

He desperately wanted to go and watch.

He had a feeling Fang Heng's talents weren't confined to mysticism.

It was entirely possible that his aptitude in the other academic disciplines was just as terrifying.

Meanwhile, Fang Heng entered the appointed training room and looked at the middle-aged man seated inside.

Nasir looked like a man of considerable life experience. His long hair flowed freely, and his somewhat artistic clothes gave him an easygoing air.

“Instructor Nasir.”

Nasir waved casually. “We're both warriors working for the Noel Clan. No need to stand on ceremony. Just call each other by name. You're Fang Heng, right?”

“That's right.”

“Mm.” Nasir sized him up and nodded repeatedly. “I was away for a while. When I came back, I heard an interesting fellow had joined us. That must be you.”

“Right, enough chatter. Let's get started.”

Fang Heng sat opposite Nasir with a serious expression. “Please, I would appreciate your guidance.”

Of all the academic disciplines, apart from necromancy and sealing studies, alchemy was the one Fang Heng felt most confident about.

After all, he'd read plenty of related books and researched a great many ancient texts, not to mention the support of the Omniscient Eye attached to his right eye.

His theoretical knowledge and accumulated experience were fairly comprehensive.

He had a decent foundation.

He probably wouldn't need many remedial lessons.
