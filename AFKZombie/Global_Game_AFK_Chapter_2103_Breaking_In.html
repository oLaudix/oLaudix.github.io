Chapter 2103

Breaking In

From original Chinese Chapter 2104 · 攻入

At the front, Commander Georgi was equally astonished by what he saw. He froze for half a second, then immediately recovered and ordered his men, “Have the high priest report to the Divine Realm at once! It's Fang Heng. Fang Heng has appeared! Tell the Divine Realm to get here immediately!”

Fang Heng!

He actually dared to come here!

Was he really going to disregard the Divine Realm's pursuit?

Let's see how long he could keep acting so arrogantly!

“Legion Commander.”

A Holy Paladin accompanying him spoke gravely. “The sacred defensive shield is consuming energy at an extreme rate. At this pace, it will be completely drained in less than ten minutes.”

Georgi frowned and raised a hand. “Suspend the sacred shield for now. Wait for my orders.”

“Understood!”

Georgi had already made his decision.

Since the shield couldn't withstand the Fusion Tyrants' beams, there was no point wasting its energy. Better to deactivate it.

The swamp had protected the Brighton Empire for thousands of years. Naturally, it had its own extraordinary defensive advantages.

Anyone who tried crossing it would pay a heavy price!

All the Holy Court had to do was conserve its strength and wait for the undead to reach the middle of the swamp—then strike them a fatal blow!

“Stay behind the swamp. Hold your positions and be ready to attack at any moment.”

“Understood!”

Meanwhile, on the opposite bank, a game notification flashed across Fang Heng's retina.

[Game Hint: Because of the influence you have exerted in Chiboreya, the Holy Court has located you. The Divine Realm pursuit team is expected to arrive in 2 hours and 12 minutes. Please exercise caution.]

“Tch. That was quick.”

Fang Heng lowered his head and surveyed the swamp ahead.

The bombardment had cleared away a large number of its creatures.

The sacred shield was gradually fading as well.

It seemed the Holy Court had given up on defending it.

How thrifty. Once they realized they couldn't hold it, they simply stopped wasting energy.

Then it was time for the next step.

Fang Heng split his attention to direct the zombie clones onward.

At the swamp's edge, the players were thrilled to see the great sacred shield over its center disappear.

Clearly, the Holy Court couldn't hold out much longer!

“Huh? Is that...?”

Several players soon noticed something else.

Catapults?

What did that mean? Were those things actually going to be useful?

At the rear, Fusion Tyrants pushed catapults into position along the right side of the battlefield.

The players turned to watch.

After a brief preparation, Lickers pouring out of the forest scrambled into the catapults' loading cradles.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

The next moment, the catapults fired.

Black silhouettes streaked through the night at incredible speed, clearing a huge stretch of the swamp before vanishing into the mist.

“Holy shit!”

They could fire that far?

The players couldn't contain their startled cries.

What kind of catapults were those?

In the Chiboreya game world, certain advanced technologies incurred additional game penalties.

After weighing his options, Fang Heng had decided to use the most basic catapult design.

Doctor Ding Min, however, had made a few special modifications.

The catapults used the latest electromagnetic rail acceleration system.

They had been specially designed to 'transport' zombies!

Their name: corpse-launching cannons.

On the other side of the swamp,

the Holy Court players were waiting in formation for the undead to attempt a crossing, ready to deal them a devastating blow halfway through.

Instead, they saw black dots flying toward them from the sky.

“What the hell is that?!”

The dots sailed straight over their heads, falling toward the rear of the Holy Court's ambush positions.

Thud! Thud, thud, thud!

Lickers slammed into the muddy ground, leaving craters everywhere and sprawling in broken heaps.

After tumbling several times, however, they immediately regained their fighting stances and charged the Holy Paladins as lively as ever.

“Lickers!”

The Holy Court forces immediately fell into chaos, rushing forward to engage the monsters in a furious melee.

The players stood stunned for a moment.

“Damn it! The undead!”

“Bring them down!”

“Don't just stand there! Attack!”

Weren't the undead supposed to cross the swamp? Why were they flying over it instead?

Only now did the players realize what the airborne dots were. They hastily gathered spells and fired upward, trying to shoot the Lickers down.

Targeted spells struck the flying Lickers, sending them plummeting into the ground. Some were smashed into lumps of mangled flesh!

Then the blackened pulp began visibly pulling itself back together!

These were second-stage modified Lickers.

Having been fused with the Primal Devourer virus, they possessed the terrifying power of revival from ashes.

Watching Lickers regenerate from sticky pools of black matter, the players stared in disbelief.

Compared with before, the Lickers...

They seemed different.

Their bodies were covered with a thin layer of black material. It was difficult to tell what it was, but it made them look far more dangerous.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

The Lickers that had been knocked out of the air immediately lunged at the players!

“Hold them back!”

The player commander shouted and charged forward, leading his team to meet the Lickers.

They had come this far.

Then they would fight!

The Holy Paladin players met the attack head-on!

Clang!

A sacred longsword struck a Licker's body and produced a faint metallic ring.

The Paladin player frowned deeply.

What was going on?

They had fought Fang Heng's Lickers before. In previous battles, their attacks had always inflicted substantial damage.

The monsters' terrifying regeneration had been the biggest problem.

So why was this different?!

What was this reinforced black defensive substance coating their bodies?

Thanks to his strength and the sharpness of his sword, the Paladin could still cut through some of the Licker's defenses. But the damage he dealt was incomparably lower than before!

“Whoosh! Whoosh!”

More Lickers leapt at the Paladins!

Watch out!

Bang!

A Licker's claws scraped across a sacred shield, throwing off a shower of holy sparks.

What was happening?

The player was horrified. He hurriedly retreated several steps, watching the Lickers crouched before him with growing caution.

Different!

These were nothing like the Lickers they had encountered before!

Not just their skin—even their razor-sharp claws were coated in that mysterious black substance.

What on earth was it?

“Whoosh! Whoosh!”

Before the player could make out anything more, the modified Lickers charged again!

The same scene was unfolding across the battlefield!

The Holy Court quickly realized that the Lickers had undergone another almost qualitative leap in power!

Their greatly enhanced defenses, combined with their already formidable self-healing, had turned them into true living shields!

One Licker could even withstand attacks from several Holy Court priests and charge more than ten meters before being blasted away.

— End of Chapter 2103 —

  • Zombie evolution. The second-stage Lickers are fused with the Primal Devourer virus, granting regeneration from black remains after lethal impacts.

Chinese Expressions

  • 卧槽 (wò cáo). A strong colloquial exclamation of shock; rendered “Holy shit!” when the players see Lickers launched across the marsh.
