Chapter 2999

Dealing with It

From Chinese Chapter 3000 · 处理

Under the continuous burning of sacred fire, the corrupted matter composing the Ouroboros Seed was completely reduced to ashes.

Fang Heng waved a hand, and a gray cocoon flew out from the embers.

“Hiss...”

A fissure opened in his palm and quickly swallowed the cocoon.

[Game Hint: You have killed the Ouroboros Seed—Haye.]

[Game Hint: You have obtained an Ouroboros Seed (High-Tier) (Damaged) (Fusion in progress).]

[Game Hint: You may directly fuse this Ouroboros Seed to obtain skill EXP, or consume it to obtain the special optional modification—Attribute Absorption.]

[Skill: Attribute Absorption.]

Skill Description: Attacks can absorb enemy attributes for your own use. Each attack that damages an enemy absorbs a small amount of that enemy's attribute points. (Absorbed attributes are retained for 8 hours.) (Absorbed attributes can stack, up to a total of 30% of the original body's highest attribute.)

Special Note: This skill consumes 2 vacant modification slots.

Fang Heng quickly read the new cocoon's description and fell into thought.

Another cocoon undergoing fusion. He could choose between an optional skill modification and converting it directly into skill experience.

Attribute Absorption looked very promising. It was clearly the special power of the Ouroboros Seed he'd just encountered.

At first glance, each attack absorbed only a tiny amount, and there was a cap on how much a single body could absorb.

But on second thought, he had an enormous number of zombie clones!

Ideally, in a large-scale battle with many combatants on both sides, his zombie army could gradually gain an extra thirty percent in overall attributes through Attribute Absorption.

And it could work even better when an entire group attacked a single boss or leader-class creature!

All he'd need was for a swarm of Lickers to rush in and bite away. The boss's attributes would decline, and under ideal conditions, they might even drain every last attribute point from it!

Especially when combined with the zombie swarm's Division effect.

Still, the price of two modification slots really was steep.

That left him short on available slots again.

Fang Heng considered the trade-off and chose to retain the skill.

“Greetings, Grand Sage.”

Archbishop Whiteman and the Holy Court believers approached and bowed together.

If any doubts had remained, seeing the Grand Sage's signature Divine Punishment technique had erased them completely.

Whiteman marveled at Fang Heng's youth.

He'd heard recently that Fang Bai, a Grand Sage from the Divine Realm, had arrived in their world. He'd never imagined meeting him under such circumstances.

And the Grand Sage's power far exceeded their expectations.

He'd single-handedly eliminated the terrifying Calamity creature.

The Holy Court's reputation and influence in the real world had declined sharply of late, and its relationship with the Western Federation was strained. The arrival of the newly advanced Grand Sage Fang Bai greatly bolstered the real-world Holy Court's confidence.

“Rise, everyone.”

Fang Heng looked across the believers and finally settled his gaze on Whiteman. He nodded. “Tell me what's going on. How did you end up here?”

“Yes, Grand Sage.”

Whiteman stood and explained from the beginning. “The Holy Court has maintained close cooperation with the Central Federation. Not long ago, news of the Keys of Apocalypse began spreading, and the Central Federation invited us to assist in the exploration of the Land of Apocalypse...”

Fang Heng found something strange in the explanation. “And where did the Federation learn the location of the Apocalypse treasure?”

“We don't know. They never mentioned that.”

“Where are the Central Federation people? They left you behind and ran?”

“After the Calamity's seal was broken, the Key of Apocalypse produced unusual fluctuations and offered guidance. We chose to remain here and temporarily suppress the Calamity's power to buy everyone time. The Central Federation team went ahead toward the indicated area.”

Hearing this, Fang Heng pulled his Key of Apocalypse from his backpack.

Hummm...

Just as Whiteman said, the key glowed faintly and floated into the air. Its tip pointed toward the labyrinth corridor behind the group.

“Let's go take a look.”

“Yes, Grand Sage!”

...

The ice-crystal labyrinth.

It wasn't only Luo Ao's Central Federation team. Almost every player team's Key of Apocalypse had begun offering directions, guiding them toward a particular location inside the maze.

Most teams were already advancing along the indicated route.

As Archbishop Whiteman and his companions followed Fang Heng, they became even more astonished by his strength.

Fang Heng practically jogged at the front. Along the way, Ice Crystal Puppets were blown apart before they had even finished forming.

Everyone else had to speed up just to keep pace.

Eventually, the key guided them into another ice-crystal trial hall.

The sound of intense fighting came from ahead.

Fang Heng gestured for the Holy Court group to slow down. They entered the hall and observed from a distance.

What a coincidence.

Were those Eastern Federation players?

Chen Yu and more than fifty Eastern Federation players were trapped near the center of the trial hall.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

White orb-like creatures moving as fast as lightning repeatedly emerged from flickering points in space and lunged at the players.

Chen Yu's team was far from weak, but against these elusive spheres, it could gain little advantage. Most of the time, the players could only defend passively, with very limited ways to attack.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and studied the glowing orb creatures.

So these were spatial abilities.

The creatures could rapidly leap in and out of Secondary Space Projection, moving at tremendous speed as they attacked the Eastern Federation players from unexpected directions.

This apparently was another trial area like those he'd encountered before.

This time, however, it tested spatial power.

Even a rare discipline like Spatial Studies appeared here.

Fang Heng considered this and focused on the ice-crystal pillar at the center of the hall.

“Grand Sage, look over there.”

Whiteman pointed toward a nearby passage.

A player squad entering the trial area from the right noticed that all the monsters were occupied with Chen Yu's Eastern Federation team. They cautiously followed the wall, taking a wide detour around the trapped players, avoiding the spheres' attacks, and leaving the hall.

Fang Heng looked down at the key hanging at his chest.

The Key of Apocalypse pointed forward.

So those players had simply gone around the fight.

“Grand Sage, should we...?”

While the Holy Court observed the Eastern Federation, the Eastern Federation team noticed the Holy Court group watching from a distance as well.

A bad feeling rose in the heart of Yi Qingzhi, the Eastern Federation team leader.

The Holy Court and the Eastern Federation had never gotten along.

The main dispute concerned religion.

The Eastern Federation was generally unreceptive to organized religions and had repeatedly rejected Holy Court demands to spread its faith.

Yi Qingzhi didn't expect the Holy Court to help. He only hoped they wouldn't kick his people while they were down and make matters worse.

But the Holy Court members simply stood there and wouldn't leave.

Fighting the spatial creatures was already stressful enough. With the Holy Court watching from the sidelines, the Eastern Federation players felt even greater pressure.

— End of Chapter 2999 —
