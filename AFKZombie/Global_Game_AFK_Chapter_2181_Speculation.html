Chapter 2181

Speculation

From original Chinese Chapter 2182 · 推测

“Very soon.”

Ji Haiting’s voice came through the miniature earpiece. “Fang Heng, it’s extremely dangerous down there. If the Tree Spirits enter, they’re very likely to clash directly with the Dependents. My advice is to withdraw immediately.”

“Boss Ji, what do you make of what the Tree Spirits just said?”

“What?”

Showing no sign of urgency, Fang Heng asked calmly, “The Spirit Domain blockade network’s energy source. If the Tree Spirits really destroy it, does that mean the entire network will fail?”

There was a noticeable pause at the other end, followed by heavier breathing. “Down there is the network’s primary energy supply. Destroy it and the whole Spirit Domain network will be completely paralyzed.”

“Understood.”

With that, Fang Heng jumped straight down the shaft!

Either way, he might as well go down and see for himself!

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Azure light surged all around him.

Fang Heng realized he was plummeting at tremendous speed.

Whoosh!

In midair, he immediately transformed into his vampire bat form, slowing his descent and gliding toward the bottom.

After descending more than a hundred meters, he began hearing the sounds of battle far below.

Looking down, he finally caught sight of the bottom.

A vast hall.

Fang Heng dropped through the circular opening in its ceiling and swiftly surveyed his surroundings.

Levin and the Tree Spirits were fighting dozens of Dependent guards!

Without Fang Heng’s support, the Tree Spirits were at a slight disadvantage.

He landed.

Fang Heng quickly returned to human form.

Not far away, Levin was wielding nature spells with both hands to support his clansmen, his expression exceptionally grave.

“Fang Heng…”

Seeing him arrive, Levin spoke quietly. “Just as I suspected. It’s that thing.”

Fang Heng followed Levin’s gaze, and his pupils contracted abruptly.

What was that?

At the far end of the hall floated a mass of blue energy, twisted and compressed into a tangled knot.

The substance was sealed inside a glass vessel and continually released blue electrical tendrils.

Outside the glowing blue sphere, overlapping pieces of scale-shaped alloy formed a metal shell.

Electrical tendrils occasionally leaked out through the gaps between the scales.

Fang Heng frowned.

A spherical life-form unlike anything he had seen before.

It appeared to consist entirely of blue energy.

Numerous thick cables connected the outer alloy-and-glass barrier to a massive machine beside it.

Fang Heng turned his head.

Levin looked extraordinarily furious.

“Levin, what is that?”

“I don’t know.” Levin shook his head, then added, “But I can sense incredibly powerful fluctuations of natural energy coming from it. It has used the Spirit Domain network to absorb nature’s power and suppress the flow of natural energy throughout this world.”

Fang Heng examined the enormous machinery. “Mr. Ji, can you see what I’m looking at?”

“Yes. I’ve got a rough view through your pinhole camera. If I’m right, that’s very likely one of the Dependents’ Electronic Light Spirits.”

Elsewhere in the research institute, Ji Haiting stared at the images transmitted by the tiny camera, his eyes filled with shock.

The Dependents had always guarded the energy section below with extreme caution, forbidding everyone else from entering.

Previously, Ji Haiting could only glean hints from the occasional report.

Such as the high-frequency energy reactions detected below.

No one had ever dared to charge down there and investigate the way Fang Heng had.

Ji Haiting took a deep breath. “Fang Heng, this reminds me of something. A few years ago I managed to obtain some Federation records. Apparently, when the Dependents first arrived on our planet, they were very interested in the power of the Tree Spirits.”

“They spent some time trying to contact the Tree Spirits, but made no meaningful progress. Since the Dependents couldn’t remain in our world for long, they eventually chose to cooperate with us humans instead, inviting us into the Star Alliance.”

Ji Haiting stopped there.

At the time, the Tree Spirits had ruled the world, and humans could only resort to underhanded schemes in the shadows.

Levin was standing right beside Fang Heng, and there were things Ji Haiting could not say too openly.

“Hmph.”

Levin had listened to the entire account. He snorted and took up the story. “We did make contact with the Dependents back then. They had some ability, I’ll grant them that. They proposed cooperation and said a few things about this Star Alliance.”

“We were busy finding a way to reopen the passage to the Inner World and had no time to entertain them. Those ill-mannered outsiders… When their pestering became intolerable, we fought them and soon drove them from our lands.”

Levin looked toward Fang Heng and addressed Ji Haiting. “So afterward, the Dependents approached you humans with the same proposal?”

“Yes. They subsequently contacted the Human Federation.”

With matters having come this far, Ji Haiting decided to speak plainly.

“The Tree Spirits were much more active in those days. After several attacks on human cities, the Federation became extremely wary of them.”

“The Dependents’ arrival was practically a lifeline. After some discussion, the Federation quickly agreed to join the cosmic alliance founded by the Dependents—the Star Alliance. In exchange, the Star Alliance would teach humanity some advanced technologies and build a Spirit Domain network on the planet to suppress the Tree Spirits’ power.”

Fang Heng pursed his lips.

Well, then.

Comparing their two accounts, he could practically reconstruct the whole story.

The Dependents were bound by some restriction that prevented them from staying in this world for long.

After discovering the world, they hadn’t tried to conquer or destroy it by force. Instead, they chose a path of cooperation and development.

They had therefore approached its inhabitants.

Given the Tree Spirits’ temperament…

Working with them probably hadn’t gone very smoothly.

Fang Heng suspected that after dealing with the planet’s intelligent species, the Dependents had decided humans were much easier to negotiate with.

So they struck an agreement with the Human Federation.

Then, to counter the Tree Spirits, the Dependents and humanity secretly built the Spirit Domain blockade network.

The Tree Spirits had always been arrogant and dismissive of the Dependents’ power. They likely hadn’t taken the supposed blockade network seriously at all.

They could never have imagined how severely it would affect them once completed.

And then came the slow boiling of the frog.

As time passed, the Spirit Domain network gradually took effect, steadily weakening the Tree Spirits.

Meanwhile, humanity’s power surged thanks to technology supplied by the Dependents.

By the time the Tree Spirits realized something was wrong, it was far too late.
