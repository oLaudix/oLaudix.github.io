Chapter 2195

Spirit-Prayer Temple

From original Chinese Chapter 2196 · 祈灵圣殿

A short while later, a group of Tree Spirits entered the Spirit-Prayer Temple.

They were from the Wood Clan.

Fang Heng watched them from hiding.

Several Wood Clan members quickly searched the temple, then returned to the courtyard to report to their elder.

“Elder, we found no one. There are no signs of fighting either.”

“Then something must have happened.”

Wood Clan Elder Halier frowned.

Since passing through the portal into the Inner World, they hadn't seen another Tree Spirit anywhere. On their Grand Elder's orders, they'd immediately come to investigate the nearest Spirit-Prayer Temple.

But the temple was deserted too.

The Spirit-Prayer Temples were of enormous importance to the race.

They would never have abandoned one unless faced with a crisis threatening their very survival.

Could it be…

Were they already too late…?

Halier thought for a moment, then looked up. “Search the place. See if there's any information left behind.”

“Yes, sir!”

Before long, another clansman hurried over. “Elder, we've checked. Everything here is gone.”

“What?” Halier frowned. “Everything?”

“Yes, Grand Elder. We found signs that someone's been searching the place. The traces are fresh, probably left not long ago. Everything that was stored here has disappeared.”

Two Wood Clan members exchanged looks.

The place was terrifyingly clean!

Every drawer was empty. Even the cabinets where cooking utensils had been kept contained absolutely nothing.

Whoever had searched it had practically stopped just short of peeling the plaster from the walls.

“Hmm. It seems someone arrived before us.”

Halier found the situation baffling.

Someone from one of the other clans?

Why take absolutely everything?

And why search so thoroughly?

Had they been hunting for something in particular?

After some consideration, Halier announced, “The Grand Elder has already left for the Council, and all the major clans are gathering there. We'll remain here on guard. No one is to enter until the Grand Elder returns.”

“Understood!”

Seeing that the Tree Spirits had no intention of leaving, Fang Heng was puzzled too.

These people…

What were they staying here for?

Did the Spirit-Prayer Temple have some other special purpose?

For a moment, he wondered whether he should just grab a Tree Spirit and question them.

After thinking it through, he abandoned the idea.

At least for now, it wasn't wise to fall out completely with the Tree Spirits.

It did seem that something serious had happened within the race.

From their conversation, the Tree Spirits were all gathering at the Council of Elders?

Why?

Fang Heng thought about it.

Since entering the Inner World, he hadn't seen another soul apart from this group of Tree Spirits. He hadn't even encountered monsters, apart from a peculiar kind of beetle.

Those beetles were tough but barely aggressive.

They posed virtually no threat.

It was hard to imagine what external danger could have threatened the Tree Spirits here.

No point overthinking it. Staying here wouldn't help. Better to visit the Council of Elders!

Let's go!

Without wasting more time, Fang Heng left the temple and sped toward the Council's position on the map.

…

The Inner World was vast.

By his rough estimate, its scale was about 1.1 times that of the real world.

The region where he had arrived was a considerable distance from the Council of Elders.

Even with flight, it took Fang Heng over a full day of nearly nonstop travel to reach it.

During that time, he maintained his four-hourly check-ins with Mo Jiawei.

Things at the teleportation passage were going well enough.

Ji Haiting had secretly dispatched a group of Wanling Technology personnel to begin constructing a camp in the Inner World.

Gu Qingzhu had also led Mo Jiawei and the others around the surrounding area, eliminating some beetle-like creatures without discovering any trace of Tree Spirits.

Meanwhile, in the outside world, the Tree Spirits had fallen silent just as they'd expected, as though the collapse of the blockade had never happened.

A tremendous crisis seemed to be lurking beneath the calm.

Hm?

Just as he approached the Council's territory, Fang Heng, speeding through the air in bat form, noticed something strange. He frowned at the sight ahead.

A pale-green shield enveloped an enormous region.

According to the map, the Council of Elders lay within it.

From the Tree Spirit records he'd examined, the Council's great hall stood near the very top of the Divine Tree, a sacred place believed to connect heaven and earth.

“The withered Divine Tree…”

Fang Heng murmured.

Through the outer barrier, he could see a colossal tree standing within.

The Tree Spirits' Divine Tree!

The giant tree reaching toward the sky was utterly withered. Its once-luxuriant leaves and branches had fallen away, and its massive trunk was riddled with cracks and decay.

If even the Divine Tree had become like this, then the Tree Spirits truly had suffered some terrible disaster.

Fang Heng circled high above for a moment, then suddenly sensed something.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

More than a dozen green arrows erupted from a mass of vines and foliage below!

Fang Heng twisted sharply to evade them.

Several arrows grazed his body.

Vines instantly wrapped around the places they'd brushed!

“BANG!”

Fang Heng forced himself free and plunged rapidly toward the right.

Landing on the ground, he returned to human form and scanned his surroundings.

Tree Spirits!

They were using a special concealment technique, blending perfectly into the forest. His vampire blood-sensing ability hadn't even detected them.

A dozen or so hidden Tree Spirits rushed toward the place where he'd fallen.

“Where is he?”

They arrived and searched their surroundings in confusion, unable to find him.

Nothing?

“Where did he go?”

“Looking for me?”

“Who's there?!”

The sudden voice from behind startled several Tree Spirits. They spun around together.

A human?!

Surprise flashed in their eyes.

It really was a human.

How had he gotten here?!

Fang Heng calmly watched them, prepared for the worst.

He had no desire to fight the Tree Spirits openly right now.

Especially since he'd received a large amount of Tree Spirit contribution points for destroying the Electronic Light Spirit.

He couldn't let those points go to waste.

Before the Tree Spirits turned hostile, he ought to redeem his rewards as quickly as possible.

And perhaps visit his old acquaintance Senzo along the way.

“Tree Spirits. I need to see your Grand Elder Senzo.”

The Tree Spirits exchanged glances. Hearing this human casually mention Grand Elder Senzo by name made them unsure what to think of him.

Had the Grand Elder brought him into the Inner World?

“Who are you?”

“Fang Heng.”

Surprise appeared in the patrol captain's eyes.

So this was Fang Heng?

It wasn't safe to remain here, so the captain decided to take him back first.
