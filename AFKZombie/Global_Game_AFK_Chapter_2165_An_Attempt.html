Chapter 2165

An Attempt

From original Chinese Chapter 2166 · 尝试

The two Dependent scouts exchanged glances.

Inside the Federation command center.

The screen dissolved into white static once more, plunging everyone into silence.

Even…

Even the Dependents themselves couldn't capture Fang Heng?

At some point, Ji Haiting had quietly appeared at the rear of the command center.

Watching the combat footage, he nodded inwardly.

With Fang Heng's strength, escaping the first wave of Dependent pursuit shouldn't be difficult.

But then again…

Their investigation of Ouroboros was still at its earliest risk-identification stage. The scouts sent over were merely the Dependents' initial reconnaissance team, not their real combat forces.

Ji Haiting was looking forward to what came next.

He very much wanted to see how far Fang Heng could go while being hunted by the Dependents!

…

[Hint: The player has successfully escaped pursuit by the Dependents. The player's current threat rating (internal Dependent classification) has risen to Grade E.]

[Hint: The Dependents are expected to reacquire the player and begin another pursuit within six hours.]

[Hint: The player's equipped item — Joke of the Illusory God — has taken effect. Tracking time extended to nine hours.]

Fang Heng stepped out of Secondary Spatial Projection and read the notifications appearing on his retina, his expression turning peculiar.

Seriously? Was this never going to end?

He reckoned that the threat rating in the game notifications corresponded to the intensity of the Dependents' pursuit.

Every time he escaped, their pursuit level would increase.

What a pain!

Fortunately, he had the divine artifact Joke of the Illusory God, which could at least buy him some extra time.

Never mind that for now.

He had lured the Federation pursuers away, so Gu Qingzhu should already have moved the Niam Alloy barrier.

Better return and check.

With that thought, Fang Heng assumed his bat form and made several quick loops through the coastal sky. Only after confirming that nobody was following him did he turn toward the private island where he'd arranged to meet Mo Jiawei and the others.

…

On the private island.

Mo Jiawei spotted a bat approaching from the distance and waved both hands enthusiastically.

Fang Heng flew up to him and transformed back into a human.

“I've got bad news. The Dependents are after me.”

“Huh? You're kidding!” Mo Jiawei's mouth fell open. “Come on, let's get inside first.”

More than half an hour earlier, Gu Qingzhu and her companions had transported the Niam Alloy barrier—now successfully calibrated to mimic its surroundings—back to the underground base beneath the island.

The island had once been uninhabited. A wealthy businessman had purchased it to develop a tourist resort.

Unfortunately, his funding collapsed halfway through the project, forcing construction to stop before completion.

After Mo Jiawei bought the island, the zombie clones had excavated an underground level in just ten days.

That space had been prepared specifically for Divine Tree Abe Akaya.

Sandy seemed even more excited than Fang Heng. As soon as he saw Fang Heng return, he hurried forward. “Boss Fang! You're finally back! Victor has installed the Niam Alloy barrier underground, and we've confirmed it's simulating the surrounding environment. We can try awakening the Divine Tree!”

“Good. Let's go.”

Beneath the island.

Thanks to the zombies' efforts over the past few days, an enormous area had been hollowed out underground.

Victor had covered the outer perimeter with a massive barrier made of Niam Alloy.

He was still carrying out the final on-site adjustments with several technicians brought over from the Zombie Apocalypse.

“Calibration complete. We can begin whenever you're ready.”

Victor wiped sweat from his forehead and nodded. “The shielding device has run autonomously for twenty consecutive minutes. Every reading is within normal limits. It has now perfectly simulated the energy flow outside. As long as we keep fluctuations within a certain range, the Niam Alloy barrier can provide one hundred percent energy shielding.”

“Excellent work, Victor.”

Fang Heng jumped down from the second-level railing and nodded gently toward Klevi.

“Let's begin.”

“Mm.”

Klevi had prepared in advance. She gathered her concentration and looked toward Fang Heng.

Fang Heng walked over to an open patch of earth.

He extended a hand.

In his palm, the seed formed by Abe Akaya gradually appeared, then fell into the soil before him.

A pale-green glow spread around it.

Fang Heng and Klevi worked together, slowly pouring natural energy into the seed and guiding Abe Akaya's power to grow anew in the Forest of Myriad Trees.

Wrapped in pale-green light, the seed gradually sank into the ground.

A moment later, vigorous vines burst from the soil!

The young Abe Akaya rapidly absorbed energy from its surroundings.

In almost no time, the tiny seed became a tree more than three meters tall.

Realizing that the natural power he could supply was no longer enough to help, Fang Heng lowered his hands and attempted to establish a mental connection with Abe Akaya.

“Abe Akaya!”

After a period of dormancy, Abe Akaya's consciousness stirred awake, initially somewhat confused.

Then came a feeling of weakness.

Following its biological instincts, Abe Akaya began absorbing energy from the earth, growing branches and leaves, and reaching for sunlight to generate its own natural power.

Vines and branches spread rapidly in every direction, climbing upward through the surrounding soil and rock until they slowly broke through the surface.

Small thickets of vines suddenly appeared across portions of the island.

Victor watched the numbers on his monitoring platform and warned him, “Fang Heng, the energy inside the barrier is rising rapidly. We're already at sixty percent of its maximum capacity. Keep it under control. Above one hundred percent, there's a high chance we'll be discovered.”

Fang Heng frowned and called out with his mental strength once more.

“Abe Akaya!”

After more than a dozen attempts, Abe Akaya finally responded with a mental signal.

The two completed a mental connection with some difficulty.

Following a period of communication, Abe Akaya stopped growing so rapidly and restricted its power to a small area of the island.

“Phew…”

Victor breathed a little easier as he checked the screen. “Energy readings have reached ninety percent of the shield's maximum tolerance. Just maintain this state.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng nodded and communicated with Abe Akaya again, asking it to try activating the Inner World teleportation magic array they'd previously obtained.

He had already tried making Abe Akaya activate that array back in the Zombie Apocalypse.

Unfortunately, it hadn't worked.

The array had seemed to react, but nothing actually happened.

He hoped things would be different now that they were in the Forest of Myriad Trees.

Fang Heng had arranged the magic array on the ground beforehand.

Upon receiving his command, Abe Akaya tried again.

A mass of vines extending from Abe Akaya's roots quickly converged on the array.

“Hiss, hiss…”

The vines gradually encircled the formation.

Energy supplied by Abe Akaya began flowing rapidly into the magic array.

— End of Chapter 2165 —
