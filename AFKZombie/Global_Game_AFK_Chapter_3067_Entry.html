Outside.

Kappa silently cursed that idiot Korkalot.

That brat had displayed the Elven Clan's haughty temperament to perfection, forcing them to spend more than ten times the usual amount to shake off the charge of assisting an illegal entrant.

After locating Fang Heng, the two hurried to the border prison, prepared to spend some money to buy his release.

If a problem could be solved with money, it wasn't much of a problem.

“Hahaha! Don't worry, gentlemen. I'll take you there right away. Nothing will happen to your friend...”

At the border prison's guard post, the officer in charge had accepted their money and was cheerfully preparing to escort Kappa and the other man inside. Suddenly, a piercing alarm sounded from the prison.

Hm? Prisoners were escaping!

Why were they making such a fuss?

Escape attempts weren't unheard of, and they usually didn't even trigger an alarm. Unless...

“BOOM!”

Just as the junior prison officer was wondering about this, a deafening crash came from the massive prison wall behind him!

He turned to see a huge cloud of smoke billowing from the prison's outer stone wall. Someone had smashed an enormous hole through it from the inside.

A crowd of illegal entrants rushed out through the opening and scattered in all directions.

The officer's face changed. “Gentlemen, there's been an incident at the prison. We'll have to postpone today's business. Perhaps we can work together another time.”

“Wait! You haven't refunded the money...”

Korkalot and Kappa watched the officer hurry away, then exchanged a silent glance.

We're screwed.

Judging by the scale of the chaos inside the prison, Boss Fang had probably lost patience and made his move.

They weren't far wrong. After causing a disturbance, Fang Heng had taken advantage of the confusion to leave the border with Klaus and the others, helped by their accomplices. They were now racing through the sky in a battered flying vehicle.

“Hahaha! That was satisfying, Brother Fang Heng!”

Klaus laughed heartily. “We're inside the territory now. We're heading to Old Li's place next. He'll make an identity card for each of us. Then we'll be people with legitimate identities. Don't worry. Old Li is the best at this. His cards are registered in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's database, so they'll pass as genuine without a problem!”

“Good.”

Fang Heng nodded calmly.

It was Korkalot's first trip to the Seven-Realm Martial Capital too, but the elves had contacts here, so he and Kappa should be fine.

On the other hand, tracking them down would take time.

He might as well spend a while with these youths. Once he had identity documents, he could find a way to contact Kappa and the others.

“Everyone, get ready! There's an ordinary patrol checkpoint ahead. We're going to accelerate straight through it...”

The pilot pushed the vehicle to maximum speed and flew the escaped prisoners toward the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's outer city.

The capital was divided into many districts.

Klaus's companions were clearly no strangers to smuggling people in. They expertly guided Fang Heng's group into the outer city's slums.

Half an hour later, they stopped at the edge of an abandoned garbage dump.

Fang Heng hadn't expected a random old man living in a place like this to be capable of hacking the registration network of the universe's foremost administrative center.

The old man known as Old Li collected everyone's fingerprints and blood samples and told them he'd prepare their identity documents overnight. They could pick them up the next morning.

The group rested in a nearby shack.

“Brother Fang Heng, where are you planning to go next?”

After thinking for a moment, Fang Heng answered, “I'm looking for ways to advance high-level academic disciplines. I heard there are lots of strong people in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, so I came to try my luck.”

The others' expressions changed as they listened.

So he was a martial arts fanatic.

No wonder he was so frighteningly strong.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital was the central hub of the entire star region. In a universe where fists determined everything, it naturally attracted the region's strongest fighters.

Every year, tens of thousands of peak-level experts arrived at the capital through various channels.

“Then you've come to the right place, Fang Heng,” Klaus said, nodding. “In that case, I'd suggest becoming a warrior for one of the Seven Domains.”

“Hm? A warrior?”

“Right! Think of it as being a mercenary.”

Klaus patiently explained, “Conflicts happen everywhere. The Seven-Realm Martial Capital is no exception, and all across the universe, disputes break out every moment.”

“To prevent minor disputes from growing into major conflicts and triggering wars on a cosmic scale, the seven great overlords established the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's arena duels under their joint authority.”

“Many disputes and divisions of interests are settled through arena battles, witnessed and certified by all seven cosmic overlords.”

“Major powers, including the seven overlords themselves, recruit capable warriors. The benefits are excellent, and they definitely have methods for advancing academic disciplines.”

“I see...” Fang Heng considered this, then asked, “Which faction possesses the greatest number of academic inheritances?”

“Uh... well...”

Klaus opened his mouth.

“I really don't know. But if I had to guess, I'd say the Kol Clan.”

“The Kol Clan is one of the seven cosmic overlords. They're known for their ancient history, and their people are inherently strong and extremely long-lived. I imagine their academic inheritances must be very complete.”

“There's also the Clan of Evil.”

Another youth spoke up excitedly. “I hear the Clan of Evil has some special secret technique that lets them turn an enemy's power into their own and master many academic disciplines at once.”

“The Kol Clan... the Clan of Evil...”

Fang Heng committed the names to memory and nodded.

“Brother Fang Heng, we're planning to try our luck with the Kol Clan too. If you don't mind, we could go into the inner city together tomorrow.”

“Sure.”

The youths remained enthusiastic well into the night. Listening to their conversation gave Fang Heng a general understanding of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

Only late at night did everyone return to the thatched huts to rest.

Fang Heng wasn't sleepy, so he sat cross-legged on his bed and contemplated the changes in his body and sea of consciousness.

After awakening the Titan Bloodline's Academic Unification ability, his different academic powers no longer conflicted with one another. Instead, he felt them merging together.

But apart from necromancy, which he had studied for a while at the Necromancer Council, he had advanced most of his other academic skills simply by absorbing energy.

It was rather like the martial arts novels in which a great master forcibly pours hundreds of years of cultivation into a novice, only for the novice to use less than ten percent of it in a real fight.

In actual combat, forget subtle techniques—he could barely claim to have mastered the complete use of many of his skills.

Fortunately, Fang Heng had been undergoing intensive daily training with the help of necromancy instructors recently. He felt he could make use of about fifty percent of his necromantic power.

But now that he had entered the super-god level of necromancy, Fang Heng was walking a path no predecessor had ever explored.

The old masters of the Necromancer Council all declared they no longer dared teach him, lest they accidentally lead his development astray.

Fang Heng didn't think it was much of a problem.

Most academic disciplines were support abilities, after all.

Super-god-level necromancy combined with his terrifying physical attributes was more than enough.

On this trip to the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, Fang Heng had also set himself a modest goal.

Consolidate his foundations.

Learn to properly use his skills and find a way to advance all of them to the Demi-God level.

That shouldn't be too difficult.
