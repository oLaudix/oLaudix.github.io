Chapter 3102

Comprehension

From Chinese Chapter 3103 · 感悟

"Bang!!!"

The defensive barrier shattered in an instant!

Grand spat a mouthful of blood and flew backward, staring at Fang Heng in disbelief.

He could feel it: the outer barrier hadn't broken under an external attack. It had shattered under the impact of unbalanced laws!

"Hiss, hiss-hiss-hiss..."

At some point, fine vines extending from the swamp had wrapped around both his legs.

Grand stared in horror at Fang Heng, who was suspended in midair.

Nature Studies!

That brat was also a Nature Studies scholar, and he'd even broken Grand's domain!

Fang Heng landed on the ground, contemplating the power of manipulating laws.

Game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Hint: Player is attempting to control the law power of Nature Studies.]

[Hint: Player has successfully gained control of the law power of Nature Studies.]

[Hint: Player's Nature Studies has advanced to Transcendent-level Nature Studies.]

He had broken through?

Fang Heng checked the game notifications.

That was pretty easy.

Fewer people studied Nature Studies than Elemental Studies. He hadn't even found a mentor yet, but he had unexpectedly advanced in Nature Studies first.

That saved a great deal of time.

Who would have thought a trip to the arena could bring such a reward?

Fang Heng nodded in satisfaction.

On reflection, though, his previous alchemical training and repeated use of the Tree Realm Descent domain had given him a profound grasp of natural laws. Otherwise, the advancement wouldn't have been so effortless.

Reproducing this method of advancement in Elemental Studies or other disciplines would be considerably harder.

Probably impossible.

Still thinking, Fang Heng looked away from Grand and toward the referee.

The referee had already recognized the gap in strength. Secretly astonished, he glanced at Grand's stunned face on the ground and asked, "Do you wish to continue?"

Only then did Grand slowly recover his senses. A bitter smile tugged at his lips.

What a monster!

"No. I concede."

The entire arena erupted into cheers again.

Most warriors hadn't reached the level where they could understand laws, so they could only enjoy the spectacle. But seeing a low-ranking warrior defeat a high-ranking one—something that might not happen for centuries—was enough to bring them to their feet.

Fang Heng stepped back and glanced down at the Kinsfolk waiting area.

They still had two mid-ranking warriors left.

Nothing to worry about.

The fight had barely drained him. He could easily handle several more rounds.

The Kinsfolk's waiting area was deathly quiet.

They were finished.

Even Grand had lost! What the hell was the point of continuing?

Fang Heng still looked fresh after defeating Grand, and the Noel Clan still had an unused high-ranking warrior. The disparity was overwhelming, and the morale of the warriors recruited by the Kinsfolk sank to rock bottom.

The elder leading the Kinsfolk hadn't come in person, but he had watched the entire fight on a screen. His eyes darkened.

Fang Heng!

A pan-human.

Could this man be connected to the Black Mist discovered recently?

Whatever the case, he was an enormous threat!

...

The final two rounds passed without incident. The two Kinsfolk warriors went onto the platform in turn and were each dispatched by a single punch from Fang Heng.

The arena competition ended in an overwhelming victory for the Noel Clan.

Hainiya of the Sea Clan watched Fang Heng's departing figure with interest.

The entire match had ended without her throwing a single punch. She never imagined there would be a day when someone else carried her to victory.

After the match, Anxi took Fang Heng back to the villa.

He considered it nothing more than an ordinary arena competition.

A single match would hardly be enough to persuade the Kinsfolk to abandon their hostility toward humanity.

One step at a time.

At least for now, the Kinsfolk's plan to exterminate humanity was viewed as immoral by the universe's other races. Implementing it would be difficult, so he still had some time.

Perhaps...

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

He needed somewhere to release the accumulated Black Mist anyway. Since he had nothing else to do, why not cause the Kinsfolk a little trouble in the process?

In the driver's seat, Anxi concentrated on driving. Catching sight of Fang Heng deep in thought out of the corner of her eye, she inexplicably felt uneasy.

...

Back at the villa, Fang Heng immediately returned to the seventh basement level and began setting up an alchemical magic array.

Participating in the competition had been worthwhile.

The mid- and high-ranking warriors had broadened his horizons, and he had even taken the opportunity to advance Nature Studies beyond the Grandmaster level.

He had also thought of a way to alleviate the Black Mist accumulating in the basement.

A two-way spatial passage magic array!

Keeping such an array operating continuously would consume a considerable amount of energy.

The greater the distance between its two ends, the more energy it would consume and the stronger the spatial fluctuations. Beyond a certain distance, the passage would fail entirely.

That wasn't much of a problem.

He still had some energy crystals in his backpack. Enough for the time being.

Fang Heng spent two hours setting up one end of the teleportation array in the basement and inscribing the other end onto a stone slab.

Next, he only needed to activate the passage from the stone-slab side, connect the two ends, and let the basement's Black Mist flow out through the spatial channel.

After some thought, Fang Heng put the slab into his game backpack. His body gradually faded in midair as he entered soul form and left the villa.

The Kinsfolk had installed detection prisms throughout the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, covering the entire city. The slightest disturbance would immediately trigger an alarm.

Fang Heng approached another corner of the Kinsfolk-controlled district until he entered the underground parking garage of an office building.

In soul form, he glanced at the surveillance cameras in the basement.

"Bang! Bang, bang!!!"

Several cameras burst apart under the influence of Mentalism.

Fang Heng gradually returned to physical form, took the stone slab from his backpack, and held it aloft in one hand.

"Whoosh!!"

The alchemical magic array spun rapidly within his right pupil.

Omniscient Eye!

Remote control of the magic array.

The spatial passage on the slab slowly opened.

"Hiss, hiss-hiss-hiss!!"

The two-way spatial passage gradually connected.

The instant the link was established, thick Black Mist spilled from the spatial channel!

It worked!

The difference in Black Mist concentration between the two ends was enormous. He didn't need to do anything else; the mist naturally flowed out through the passage.

Black Mist surged into the parking garage, filled it rapidly, and spread through every entrance and exit.

The prism detection towers detected the mist and sounded their alarms immediately.

The Kinsfolk control center received yet another warning.

A little over ten minutes later, fully armed Central Guards and Kinsfolk security teams surrounded the entire building.

To the assembled warriors outside, it looked as though dense Black Mist was continuously billowing out of the whole building.

What the hell was going on!

Guard Captain Balin led the cordon around the building, his brows tightly drawn.

This Black Mist was far denser than during the previous attack on the Kinsfolk building!

— End of Chapter 3102 —
