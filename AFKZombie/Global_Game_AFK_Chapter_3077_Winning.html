Chapter 3077

Winning

From Chinese Chapter 3078 · 取胜

He won!

And so easily!

Good grief! None of them had even seen how Fang Heng attacked!

Liu Hu swallowed nervously.

Forget the other newcomers; even he hadn't been able to follow Fang Heng's movements!

What was going on?

They'd both joined the Noel Clan as newly recruited warriors. Was the gap between them really this enormous?

If he were honest with himself, Liu Hu knew he couldn't have withstood that punch either.

“Brother Fan Chen.”

Liu Hu turned to Fan Chen beside him and whispered, “Did you see what he did?”

Fan Chen looked equally astonished and shook his head.

“That guy is at least a mid-tier warrior.”

Silence descended over the Kata Clan's waiting area.

That punch had placed them under tremendous pressure.

The two remaining warriors were the most anxious of all.

They'd expected an easy victory after seeing the opposing side's weak roster. Who would have thought there was a monster hiding among them?

At best, the two had caught a glimpse of Fang Heng's dark silhouette. Neither could follow his attack.

He wouldn't be easy to handle!

“I'll go.”

As the deadline approached, a Grandmaster spatial studies warrior leaped onto the platform and nodded at Fang Heng. “Rein.”

“Fang Heng.”

Below the platform, Liu Hu and Fan Chen looked worried when they saw Rein enter.

Rein was a reasonably famous mid-tier warrior.

He wasn't among the very strongest of that tier, but he was exceptionally difficult to face.

Could Fang Heng win this one?

Seeing that both fighters were ready, the referee nodded. “Begin!”

Just as before, Fang Heng dashed toward his opponent the instant the order was given, planning to finish the fight with a quick punch.

As he closed in, however, his expression shifted slightly.

Hm?

Spatial abilities?

Fang Heng suddenly realized that the flow of space around him had been visibly suppressed by spatial power.

This was only a Level One arena match. He hadn't expected anyone here to have a countermeasure.

Fang Heng immediately understood. This was probably what his Grandmaster instructor had described: after reaching Grandmaster, a cultivator could use the energy cycle within their body as a foundation to influence the external cycle.

Interesting!

It was an ability that couldn't be mastered merely by leveling up directly through the game without understanding it personally.

But his opponent didn't seem very practiced at it—or perhaps his power was simply limited. The effect on Fang Heng was minimal.

After all, Fang Heng had an overwhelming advantage in raw attributes!

“Whoosh!”

Fang Heng accelerated again!

What?

Rein's heart jolted as Fang Heng slowed for only an instant, then turned into a dark blur and rushed straight toward him again.

Not good!

Rein instinctively tried to evade through a subspace projection, but just as tiny spatial lights began flickering around him, an enormous impact surged toward him.

“BANG!”

The force blasted Rein backward and sent him flying off the platform.

“BOOM!”

Cheers erupted across the hall again.

Two victories in a row!

Both clean and decisive, settled with a single punch!

Eight to six!

The Noel Clan's waiting area erupted into cheers once more.

One victory might be luck, but ending two matches with one blow each meant Fang Heng's strength far surpassed theirs.

Liu Hu and Fan Chen exchanged glances and both thought the same thing.

What a terrifying kid.

Where on earth had he come from?

In the Kata Clan's waiting area, the elder leading the team looked at his final two warriors. “Who's next?”

The two exchanged glances.

“Lor, do you think you can win?”

When the elder called his name, Lor's face fell.

Confidence?

He had none whatsoever!

He couldn't beat this guy!

His abilities were entirely spell-based. Against a rare close-combat specialist like Fang Heng, he had no chance unless he seized the initiative.

With that monstrous speed, he'd probably be defeated in one move.

“Give it a try.”

Unfortunately, both remaining warriors specialized in elemental abilities and were countered by Fang Heng.

It made no difference who went first.

Lor drew a deep breath, entered the platform, and regarded Fang Heng with a grave expression.

“Begin!”

At the command, Lor didn't even bother tracking Fang Heng's location. He immediately formed a seal before his chest and poured mental power into it.

Immortal Ice Curtain!

“BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!”

The platform's temperature plunged as several thick barriers of ice rose before the elemental mage.

Whoosh!

At the same instant, Fang Heng turned into a dark streak and closed the distance at terrifying speed.

Then Lor heard a series of explosive crashes.

The high-strength ice barriers he'd formed were no tougher than paper before Fang Heng. One after another, they were smashed apart!

A sonic boom rang in Lor's ears, and the force of Fang Heng's punch launched him off the platform.

Another win!

When Fang Heng won again, the Noel Clan's waiting area erupted into cheers.

Who would have imagined that a warrior hastily recruited as a substitute would turn out to be their team's savior?

Liu Hu stared at Fang Heng, mouth slightly open, unable to make sense of what he was seeing.

This guy...

Who was he, really?

On the other side, Lor was helped to his feet by two companions. He cupped his fists toward Fang Heng and returned to the waiting area.

“How was it?”

“He held back. He only used a blast of compressed air to knock me away. If that punch had struck my body, I'd be dead.” After experiencing it firsthand, Lor finally understood how terrifying Fang Heng was. He sighed. “I couldn't even see him attack before all seven ice barriers shattered. His frontal attack power is extremely high.”

Everyone exchanged grim looks before turning toward their last remaining elemental warrior, Chris.

Under their gazes, Chris smiled bitterly. “I can't win. I specialize in high-damage attacks against heavily armored opponents. He's too fast. My mental power can't even lock onto him.”

There was nothing else for it. Things had reached this point, so he'd have to fight.

In a ten-versus-ten match, once someone won two bouts in a row, the warriors waiting below could usually identify that person's weaknesses and choose an opponent suited to countering them.

But that was only in theory.

Fang Heng's personal strength exceeded theirs by such a huge margin that they had no effective counter, especially with only one fighter remaining.

“Go on, then.”

They'd come this far. They couldn't simply surrender.

“All right.”

Chris patted his cheeks to steel himself, leaped onto the platform, and cupped his fists toward Fang Heng, hoping the man wouldn't hit him too hard.

“A pleasure to meet you. I'm Chris.”

“Fang Heng.”

Fang Heng nodded in greeting.

The referee glanced at both competitors.

“Begin!”

Chris's pupils contracted, and he instantly launched several fire-lance spells at Fang Heng.

— End of Chapter 3077 —
