So...

Fang Heng patiently tested the energy of every discipline he had learned.

Apart from Necromancy, Sacred Studies, and Hell Studies, every other energy type could be converted successfully.

It was just painfully slow and required intense concentration, making it difficult to use in battle.

Which meant... this technique...

It was an extremely powerful auxiliary skill!

Most academic skill upgrades consumed energy. He could simply convert Qi into whatever energy he needed to replenish his reserves!

Unfortunately, all his current skills were already at MAX, so he couldn't test the theory.

But he was almost certain he was right.

Now things were getting interesting.

Suppose, for example, a Spatial Studies skill required spatial energy to level up.

Spatial energy was extraordinarily scarce, making upgrades a nightmare.

In theory, he could absorb the much easier-to-find natural energy, convert it into Qi, and then transform that Qi into the spatial energy he needed.

Wouldn't that allow him to level up rapidly through secluded cultivation?

He had to try!

Fang Heng converted the natural energy he'd just formed back into Qi, then attempted to turn that Qi into spatial energy.

Success!

The process took a while, and every conversion lost 50% of its energy, but it definitely worked!

What a treasure of a skill.

Academic cultivation bottlenecks would be much easier to overcome from now on.

As far as Fang Heng was concerned, the Titan bloodline's other attribute bonuses were mere extras. This bloodline had been made to bring academic disciplines together!

No wonder cosmic legends described the Titans as the most learned race in the universe, capable of integrating numerous disciplines.

With such a bug-like learning ability, the hardest part of mastering multiple disciplines was simply completing their advancement requirements.

While Fang Heng happily experimented in his Sea of Consciousness, the Federations outside had nearly finished exploring the ice-crystal labyrinth.

Once the jewel at the central hall lost all its power, the monsters inhabiting the labyrinth disappeared completely and stopped spawning.

The Federation teams searched back and forth without finding anything else, but they did discover a side chamber hidden outside the trial hall.

This was where the Titan Guardian had originally resided.

Searching it again, they found murals and written records carved into the rock walls.

Representatives of the Federations gathered before the inscriptions and copied the writing in separate groups.

The Eastern Federation had studied the Titans earlier than anyone else. Several members of Dr. Li Zhixuan's research team were present too, so they immediately began translating.

The Central, Northern, and Western Federations knew next to nothing about the Titans. Most were hearing of them for the first time.

Seeing the Eastern Federation begin translating the inscriptions, they shamelessly stayed to see what they might learn.

Relying on their previous translation experience and plenty of educated guesswork, Dr. Li Zhixuan's team soon deciphered most of the records.

Yi Qingzhi received the newly compiled report and looked grave.

Inheriting the Titan bloodline was not nearly as easy as it sounded.

Luo Ao approached with a group of Central Federation subordinates. "Friends from the Eastern Federation, have you discovered something? Could you share some of the information? The Central Federation would owe you a favor."

Representatives of the other two Federations gathered around and made similar promises.

"Well, the matter does concern all humanity. There's no reason to keep it from you."

Yi Qingzhi knew the other Federations would decipher the text eventually if given enough time. Better to earn their goodwill early. He handed out copies of the translated report.

The commanders thanked him and gathered to read it.

Their expressions shifted after they finished.

The inscriptions were lengthy records written from the Titan Guardian's first-person perspective. Three main conclusions emerged from them.

First, humans really were descendants of the Titan race and carried part of its bloodline, though their bloodline was extremely diluted.

Long ago, the Titans faced extinction. To preserve the race, their ancestors deliberately concealed the strength of humanity's bloodline and dispatched a Guardian to escort part of the bloodline into this world.

Later, an unknown intruder attacked the ice-crystal labyrinth the Guardian had created. The intruder grievously injured him, forcing him into a long hibernation. When he finally awoke, his memories of the intervening time were blurred. He only remembered that the treasure he'd brought from his clan—the Wandering Scholar Mark—had vanished.

Second, the jewel discovered in the cavern could absorb energy from its surroundings and slowly convert it into Qi. Humans could use Qi to refine and strengthen their bloodlines, restoring their Titan heritage.

Finally came the most critical point.

To escape their enemies and prevent the last descendants from being exterminated, the Guardian created a special seal that concealed the entire world's aura and coordinates.

With the Guardian dead and the jewel's energy depleted, the whole world would now be exposed to the outside universe.

Their world was essentially defenseless.

Silence descended over the secret chamber after everyone finished reading.

Dr. Li Zhixuan looked up. "The translated records mention that previously untranslatable word again. From certain contextual clues, we think it's the name of a race—the enemy whose existence made the Titans fear extermination."

"Gods."

The silence deepened.

What did that mean?

Had the Titans been exterminated by gods?

After thinking through the inscriptions, the Federations quickly realized something else.

The sphere in the hall absorbed ambient energy and converted it into Qi, which they needed to complete their bloodline refinement and awakening.

But that energy sphere was now in the Holy Court's possession...

Clearly, nobody present had successfully completed their bloodline refinement.

Did that mean anyone who wanted to absorb Qi and refine their bloodline in the future would have to depend on the Holy Court?

With the exception of the Eastern Federation, the other groups began reconsidering their future relations with the Holy Court, wondering how they might obtain the jewel.

At the very least, they wanted access to its power for cultivation.

They were screwed...

Augustine of the Western Federation sighed inwardly.

They'd made relations with Fang Heng so ugly earlier. How could they possibly have the cheek to ask him to lend them the jewel?

They were willing to swallow their pride, but Fang Heng might not be willing to accept that!

"Everyone, perhaps there's a more urgent matter to address. Our world has lost its protection. We could face a threat from outside at any moment."

— End of Chapter 3008 —
