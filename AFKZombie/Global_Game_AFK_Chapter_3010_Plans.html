Chapter 3010

Plans

From Chinese Chapter 3011 · 打算

Ten days later.

Fang Heng stopped cultivating and abruptly opened his eyes.

Ever since he'd set the Federations competing to fulfill his requirements, they'd been bringing him academic skill books one after another.

So he simply remained in the ice-crystal labyrinth to cultivate.

Qi was genuinely useful.

He could turn natural energy into Qi, then convert Qi into any of the other supported energies.

Whenever a Federation supplied the required advancement book, he could advance the skill directly.

His levels shot up like crazy.

"Your Grace." Whiteman, who had guarded Fang Heng around the clock with his men, approached respectfully. "Grand Sage, Mr. Chen Yu from the Eastern Federation requests an audience. He's waiting outside the hall."

Over the past several days, Whiteman had come to regard Fang Heng as an absolute grinding fanatic.

Neither Whiteman nor any of the Holy Court guards had ever seen someone cultivate as relentlessly as Fang Heng.

Practically twenty-four hours a day, without sleep or rest.

He did nothing but cultivate.

Despite already holding the rank of Grand Sage, he never stopped honing his strength.

Whiteman regarded him with deep reverence.

"Really? Please invite them in."

After cultivating continuously for more than ten days, Fang Heng felt rather stiff himself. He stood and stretched his limbs.

Soon, Chen Yu and Augustine of the Western Federation entered the hall under the escort of Holy Court guards.

Chen Yu greeted him with a simple, cheerful smile. "Boss Fang."

"Sorry to keep you waiting. I've been cultivating lately and only just finished. What's going on?"

Fang Heng was curious. Since when had Chen Yu gotten mixed up with the Western Federation?

"It's fine, really."

Chen Yu waved away the apology, feeling sincere admiration for Fang Heng.

He'd been among the first people to meet him.

He'd watched Fang Heng rise from a complete newcomer to where he stood today.

Chen Yu also knew Fang Heng disliked beating around the bush, so he got straight to the point. "Boss Fang, we've run into trouble. It concerns the Kinsfolk."

The Kinsfolk?

Fang Heng frowned. "What happened?"

Chen Yu nudged Augustine with his elbow. "There. You've met him. You caused this mess, so explain it yourself."

Augustine gave a bitter smile.

There was no helping it. The Western Federation had created the problem; now it had to find a way to fix it.

"It's like this, Grand Sage. As you may know, the Western Federation's plan to build the Star Map Main Control Center was temporarily suspended because of certain concerns."

"The Kinsfolk have been increasingly displeased by our delays, and the Western Federation has been under considerable pressure."

"A few days ago, the Kinsfolk issued us a final ultimatum."

"They say they've invested substantial resources in humanity. They warned that we must complete the first stage of the Star Map Control System within the prescribed thirty days. Otherwise, they'll send personnel into our world to 'help' us finish construction."

Fang Heng thought, You Western Federation people created this Star Map problem yourselves. And now that you can't handle it, you want me to clean up your mess?

"The Kinsfolk used to treat humanity quite mildly. Why have they suddenly become so aggressive? Did something happen?"

Augustine explained, "They've probably been unhappy with us for a long time. It's just that they didn't know the precise coordinates of our world before, so they couldn't enter it."

"Perhaps because the Titan Guardian's protection is gone, the Kinsfolk now have our exact spacetime coordinates. That's why they can threaten us and give us thirty days."

"I see."

Fang Heng nodded and rubbed his chin thoughtfully.

Still, he recalled the Kinsfolk were afraid of contamination. Unless faced with a truly extreme crisis, they would never willingly enter black fog or areas linked to Ouroboros Seeds.

In all likelihood, the Kinsfolk wouldn't come themselves. They would dispatch some of their allied races instead.

Augustine sounded deeply worried. "Boss Fang, all they need to do is post a mission on the Kinsfolk network platform. Huge numbers of allied races might enter our world, creating untold chaos."

That was true.

Take the Shadow Clan, which had always been hostile toward humanity.

If those people entered the real world, they'd cause no end of trouble.

"Before coming here, we coordinated with the other Federations. We'll do everything possible to prevent this, but if the Kinsfolk or their allies attack, we'll join forces to resist the invaders."

After the Titan incident, regardless of how much each Federation knew about Fang Heng's true identity, no one dared underestimate him anymore.

At the same time, the Federation authorities were terrified of the approaching Kinsfolk-alliance offensive. They hoped to ease tensions, but were also preparing for the worst and could mobilize for battle at any moment.

At least according to the intelligence gathered when the Eastern and Western Federations had previously joined a Kinsfolk alliance campaign, humanity's prospects were grim.

"Understood. I'll keep an eye on it. If necessary, I'll help fight off the invaders."

"Thank you, Boss Fang."

After Augustine left, Fang Heng turned to Chen Yu, who had stayed behind. "All right, tell me. What other trouble have you encountered? How can I help?"

"Boss Fang, the fleet has a problem. The Kinsfolk revoked our alliance status several days ago. We can still log into their system, but only basic functions remain. The jump passage to Kinsfolk headquarters has been shut, along with the jump-exchange system."

Fang Heng frowned.

That was troublesome.

While he was grinding academic skills here, his fleet had also been farming Kinsfolk Contribution to expand its strength.

The Western Federation's attitude had apparently caused all humanity to be targeted.

"We made contingency plans in advance and currently control several worlds that we can explore and mine for resources. But we can't submit those resources to the Kinsfolk for now. We also have very few world coordinates, so this can't continue indefinitely. We should resolve the Kinsfolk problem as soon as possible. Boss Fang, tell us whenever you need our help."

"Mm... I understand." Fang Heng nodded gravely. "Keep gathering resources for now. Once you've cleared those few worlds, pause operations. I'll finish my secluded cultivation and prepare for a battle with the Kinsfolk alliance."

"All right."

Chen Yu nodded solemnly and took his leave.

Fang Heng watched him depart, quietly calculating his next moves.

First he'd consolidate his strength and grind for a while, using the books the Federations collected to advance his disciplines. Once he'd raised them as far as he reasonably could, he'd strike first and go looking for trouble with the Demon Seed Envy and the Kinsfolk's allied armies.

— End of Chapter 3010 —
