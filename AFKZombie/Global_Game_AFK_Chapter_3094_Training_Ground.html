Chapter 3094

Training Ground

From Chinese Chapter 3095 · 修炼场

“What's more, the entire training ground has explosion-proof facilities. Even if you accidentally blow something up, the overall risk is controlled.”

“Still, few alchemy scholars in the association reach this stage. These training rooms haven't been used in quite a while.”

Fang Heng nodded inwardly.

As expected, specialized work was best left to specialists.

“Director.”

People passing through the Alchemy Association building greeted Nasir with nods.

“I'll have someone arrange your membership card. Come, I'll show you the training ground first.”

Nasir obtained a special-approval application from reception, had Fang Heng sign it, and handed it to Anxi to fill in the details. He then led Fang Heng toward the training facility.

“Okay.”

Fang Heng followed him up to the seventh floor in an elevator. They pushed open the door of a large training room.

The automatic lights came on.

The entire circular training ground was covered in magic-array runes.

Nasir sighed nostalgically. “It's been a long time since I came back here. I remember cultivating in this very room. Why don't you try?”

“Mm. All right.”

Fang Heng agreed, looked around, closed his eyes, and expanded his perception.

“A large portion of the chaotic energy fluctuations have been screened out here. It's much easier to arrange arrays that affect laws.”

Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

Indeed, the various energies in the room fluctuated very steadily and were much less active than outside. Some power fluctuations had disappeared entirely.

That meant he could exclude a great deal of redundant interference and use alchemical arrays to target the surrounding laws.

“Excellent. Nasir, may I begin?”

“Of course. Whenever you're ready.”

Nasir stepped back and watched Fang Heng with interest.

It had taken Nasir more than ten years to break through this stage.

He'd scarcely left the Alchemy Association in all that time, spending day after day perceiving laws and attempting to arrange magic arrays.

Only in the end had he succeeded and advanced to the Transcendent level.

Fang Heng's aptitude was astonishing. How long would he need to advance?

“Hiss, hiss...”

Fang Heng focused and raised his pen. Without appearing to think at all, he began drawing magic-array patterns directly on the floor.

Already?

Nasir was surprised.

An array capable of influencing regional laws had to be at least a large-scale array.

Was he really that confident?

Half an hour passed, and the large array was complete.

Fang Heng pressed both hands onto it.

“Hum...”

With mental power flowing in, the array glimmered. Then the light faded and everything returned to normal.

Apparently it had failed.

Nasir shook his head slightly.

Failure was only natural.

Success on the first attempt would have been unbelievable.

At least Fang Heng's construction process showed that he understood how to perceive laws and use them in designing an array.

What happened next would depend on his own understanding.

“Good comprehension. You seem to understand how to use alchemy arrays to influence laws. It's normal to fail repeatedly when first searching for the right method. There's little more I can teach you. Feel free to discuss any insights with me. We can improve together.”

“Mm. Thank you, Nasir.”

Fang Heng nodded, though most of his attention remained fixed on the changes within the array.

Only after actually attempting to arrange one did he appreciate the difficulty.

Environmental energy was just one factor that had to be balanced. There were many other considerations, and every minute change could trigger a chain reaction.

The smallest error would cause the alchemical array to fail.

The basic prerequisites were extremely high perception and enormous mental power.

He also needed microscopic control of mental power and absolute concentration throughout the process. He couldn't afford the slightest distraction.

Seeing Fang Heng lost in thought, Nasir nodded approvingly.

He'd been much the same while studying: so absorbed that he forgot food and sleep, barely hearing what others said, his mind filled with laws, energy flows, and alchemical arrays.

In Nasir's view, the difficulty Fang Heng faced now was only the first step.

Even if an array were completed perfectly according to the intended design, it would most likely still fail.

That was because an individual's assessment of environmental energy could never be completely accurate.

There would always be errors.

It was like trying to determine every precise measurement of a delicate object hundreds of meters away using only the naked eye.

Through repeated trial and error, however, an alchemist's perception of active energies in the environment would improve rapidly, as would their fine control over arrays.

That step alone had cost Nasir ten years.

“The training facility is open to association members. You're one of us now, and nobody else is using it. You can cultivate here whenever you like.”

“Thank you.”

Fang Heng thanked him again, but his attention remained so focused on the array that he barely noticed Nasir's words, much less when the man left.

“Whoosh!”

A rapidly revolving magic array appeared reflected in Fang Heng's right eye.

The failed array on the ground began spinning again.

This time was different. Under the Omniscient Eye's observation, the array operated extremely slowly, every step proceeding dozens of times slower than normal.

“So that's it.”

Half a minute later, having found the point where the array stalled and broke down, Fang Heng let the image in his right eye fade.

Law-level alchemical arrays demanded exceptional precision and perception. Even the tiniest discrepancy was unacceptable.

An ordinary scholar would have to spend ages searching for an error, probing over and over again.

But Fang Heng was different.

With the aid of his Omniscient Eye, the amplification of his formidable mental power over array control, and his high perception's support for sensing laws, finding errors became remarkably easy.

Once he identified the exact mistake, he could correct it directly.

“Hiss!”

Fang Heng waved his hand, instantly wiping away the array on the floor.

Again!

Nasir exited the room and saw Anxi waiting outside. He smiled. “You must have heard. Fang Heng is very talented in alchemy. He's attempting his Transcendent breakthrough now, and I have no idea when he'll emerge.”

“That's fine. I'll wait outside. As his assistant, it's my responsibility to look after and coordinate his daily needs.” Anxi nodded respectfully. “And thank you for helping the Noel Clan nurture such an outstanding warrior.”

“Very well. I'll leave him in your care.”

— End of Chapter 3094 —
