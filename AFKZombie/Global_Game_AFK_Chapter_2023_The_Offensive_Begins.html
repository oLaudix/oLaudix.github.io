The Offensive Begins

From original Chinese Chapter 2024 · 展开

On the war front of the Engma Empire, tonight's operations against the Holy Court were about to begin.

Lord Dani had a clear understanding of the situation. She knew that, like previous attacks, tonight's offensive probably wouldn't achieve much. Even so, she would gather the necromancer players and launch broad attacks against important Holy Court positions as usual.

It was a war of attrition, intended to slow construction of the Holy Court's teleportation passages. How effective it would be was anyone's guess.

At least they would have tried.

Besides, tonight would be different in one important way. While attacking the enemy, they would also officially begin their withdrawal!

Originally, they'd expected the Holy Court to take another five to eight days to build its teleportation passage. But events had moved faster than anticipated. No one had imagined the Holy Court would invest so many resources and spare no expense.

The rate of construction had far exceeded their estimates.

Lord Dani had just received reliable intelligence: with help from player teams, the passage at Canmu City had already been fully built and was expected to open tonight!

Those players were practically insane. Their all-out effort had cut the construction time almost in half.

With their current strength, the undead faction couldn't possibly withstand the endless reinforcements the Holy Court could bring in from other game worlds.

Retreat was their only option.

And the three days she'd agreed on with Fang Heng had already passed. He still hadn't given her a better answer. Apparently, he'd given up as well.

Lord Dani sighed deeply.

She had poured years of work into this land. Now she was being forced to witness its destruction.

A moment of reluctance passed through her eyes before it was replaced by fierce resolve.

This was both an ending and a new beginning.

Trading the territory for an enhancement to her personal talent wasn't necessarily a loss. And today's retreat was meant to create more room for a future counterattack.

One day, she would make the Holy Court repay everything!

Then Dani sensed something. Her expression shifted as she turned toward the night sky behind her.

A dark silhouette flashed across it.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng descended in bat form, landed quickly, and returned to his human shape in front of her.

“Fang Heng.”

Dani looked at him in surprise. Something about him had changed again.

Was it his blood vitality?

She studied him carefully. He must have made another major breakthrough!

How many days had it been? Every time she saw him, his strength seemed to increase at a frightening pace.

“Another breakthrough since we last met. Should I congratulate you?” she asked coolly. “But that amount of strength still won't stop the entire Holy Court. What is it? Have you come to say goodbye?”

“No. Don't be so pessimistic. Remember our agreement?”

Fang Heng smiled and stepped closer. “Lord Dani, I've come up with a better plan. I think you'll be interested.”

“A better plan?” She frowned. “What is it?”

Fang Heng leaned toward her ear and whispered a few words.

“What?!”

Dani stared at him, disbelief written across her face.

“Is what you're saying true?”

“Of course. I've never lied to my partners.” Fang Heng casually dusted off his shoulder. “Naturally, I'll need your cooperation to pull it off.”

Seeing her hesitation, he laughed lightly. “Relax. I've already arranged everything. All you need to do is coordinate the attack.”

Dani regarded him in silence.

Defend by attacking? Counterattack the Holy Court?

Move the battlefield to the Holy Court's own headquarters?!

Wasn't that a pipe dream?

Yet Fang Heng didn't look as though he were joking at all.

She had never imagined he could be this bold. And somehow he'd managed to make the preliminary arrangements in just a few days?

That man!

He never stopped giving her surprises—and shocks.

After thinking it over, Dani felt strangely reassured. She'd already seen it when they faced Saintess Viona: Fang Heng had always been audacious.

Perhaps this was simply normal for him.

As for whether the plan would succeed…

At least the risk to her was low.

“When do you intend to start?” she asked.

Fang Heng checked the time. “Just about time. Right now.”

“All right.”

…

Inside the Forest of Bewilderment, thick necromantic mist covered the entrance to a valley. The Dark Night Pearl on top of the Ghost Tower flickered with a dim glow.

Light and shadow shifted as Fang Heng stepped out of the teleportation passage.

Li Qingran had remained in the valley for the past two days, watching the Holy Court's movements closely. At Fang Heng's request, she'd also recruited more than twenty trustworthy necromancer players from outside the game to help.

She had selected them carefully. Trustworthiness came first: there could be no Holy Court spies. Next, they had to know how to keep their mouths shut.

This operation was extremely secret. Not a single detail could be allowed to leak.

Only after meeting those two requirements did she look for players with sufficient strength. On top of that, they needed skills involving mechanical repairs and operation.

Few players met every condition. Gathering them had cost Li Qingran considerable effort.

She was relieved to see Fang Heng return. The other players turned to look at him too.

“Fang Heng, you're here. I was just about to look for you.”

“What's wrong? Trouble?”

“Yes. Chu Yan just learned that the Holy Court plans to launch a surprise attack on the Forest of Bewilderment tonight and completely destroy the mage tower.”

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

A surprise attack? What a coincidence!

They'd been planning to attack the Holy Court tonight as well.

Ever since Fang Heng had infiltrated the Holy Court's heartland and caused enormous destruction, the Holy Court had learned from its mistake and quickly devised countermeasures.

It had set its sights on the Forest of Bewilderment, sending numerous elite squads to scout the area and stationing a large army around the forest's perimeter.

With Fang Heng away, Li Qingran could only hold a small part of the valley. It hadn't taken long for the Holy Court to discover the Ghost Tower's location and capabilities.

The Holy Court had launched several probing attacks, but Li Qingran had learned of them in advance and barely held them off with the help of undead players and zombies.

Now Chu Yan had brought reliable news: tonight the Holy Court would launch its full assault to destroy the Ghost Tower within the Forest of Bewilderment.

“Heh. Fine. Let them come,” Fang Heng said. “Contact Chu Yan and get ready. We'll proceed with our original plan tonight.”
