Chapter 2059

Shackles

From original Chinese Chapter 2060 · 枷锁

The Book of Oaths opened before Fang Heng, its pages flipping rapidly.

Special ability activated—Energy Absorption!

“Hiss, hiss, hiss…”

Under the glow of the open book, specks of sacred light began rising from the shackles around his hands. The light streamed toward the Book of Oaths and disappeared into its pages.

Hum…!

Game notifications flashed across Fang Heng’s vision.

[Hint: The Book of Oaths has absorbed a portion of power. The energy reserves of the Book of Oaths (Fragment) are recovering at an accelerated rate…]

The sacred energy within the shackles was rapidly drained away, and their glow gradually dimmed. Once most of it had been absorbed, Fang Heng gave a sudden, powerful pull.

Bang!

The shackles snapped apart!

Broken!

The prisoners stared at Fang Heng and the broken restraints on the floor, dumbfounded. The shackles that had tormented them for so long had been destroyed just like that!

Was the Demon Seed really that extraordinary?

Then a realization dawned, and excitement spread through the cells. If those shackles could be removed, didn't that mean…?

Their breathing quickened. Every pair of eyes followed Fang Heng's movements.

Fang Heng stepped up to the bars again and gripped one in each hand, just as he had before.

Crackle!

Sparks of sacred energy danced around his fingers.

Creak, creak…

He steadily applied pressure. With a series of cracking sounds, the bars bent apart easily, leaving a gap wide enough for him to pass through.

It worked! He was free!

The monsters imprisoned in the other cells watched him step through the twisted bars in disbelief. Had he really escaped? And that easily?

“Brother!”

The dwarf was the first to shout, practically beside himself. “Over here, brother! Come give me a hand! I contributed two crystals to help you, remember? You can't just forget a favor like that!”

A low-ranking bull-headed troll looked at Fang Heng and rumbled, “Young man, get me out of here and name your price.”

“I misjudged you, young friend. Congratulations on getting free,” said a lizardfolk prisoner with deliberate calm. “But this is hardly the time to celebrate. The Holy Court's guards are outside. Why not take us with you? We could fight our way out together and watch each other's backs.”

Fang Heng ignored the growing clamor. He walked slowly toward the flowerbed in the middle of the hall.

Escaping could wait. The important thing was dealing with the Demon Seed inside him—even if he could only suppress it temporarily!

The closer he got, the stronger the fluctuations of sacred energy became.

He had been curious about this flowerbed for some time. Dark-gold crystal clusters grew from it like flowers in bloom, releasing an energy signature remarkably similar to the crystals in the Temple of Zeus.

Up close, he discovered an intricate magic array hidden beneath the flowerbed, matching much of what he had observed earlier. A large portion of the enormous suppression array's power originated from these crystals.

The array released sacred energy, which merged with the crystals' power. That combined energy then fed back into the crystals to nourish them before being released once more.

“What exactly is this stuff?”

Fang Heng crouched and reached toward one of the crystalline flower clusters.

Strange. It looked like stone, but on closer examination, it behaved more like a plant—rooted in the flowerbed and slowly growing under the illumination of sacred energy through some unknown process.

A game notification appeared.

[Hint: You have discovered a special item—Sacred Luminous Lattice Stone (Unknown).]

[Item: Sacred Luminous Lattice Stone (Unknown)]

[Description: A special sacred-type Luminous Lattice Stone cultivated through an infusion of sacred power. Its structure manifests power in an unusual form. This item cannot be activated directly.]

[Description: This object cannot be transplanted.]

[Description: You lack the necessary skills and research data. Further information about this item's properties and use cannot be obtained.]

Fang Heng withdrew his hand.

“Luminous Lattice Stone…”

He had never heard of it.

Never mind. For now, it looked like the stone was the focal point of the magic array.

According to the evil spirit, sacred power could temporarily solve the trouble caused by his Demon Seed. In simple terms, if he released the Demon Seed's aura, the lattice stones and the array would react!

The prisoners held their breath as they watched him. An inexplicable tension settled over them. Somehow they sensed something enormous was about to happen.

No way…

BOOM!

An immense surge of black energy erupted from Fang Heng's body. The Demon Seed's power roiled inside him like a flood unleashed from a dam!

Instead of suppressing it, Fang Heng did the opposite: he drove that power outward without restraint!

Hum…

The great influx of corrupting energy immediately activated the magic array in the Demon-Sealing Cavern. Bathed in holy light, the crystal clusters in the flowerbed released an intense wave of sacred power.

Brilliant sacred radiance filled the cavern!

“Shit!”

The old man cried out and scrambled backward, curling up in the corner of his cell and wrapping himself tightly in his clothes.

Hiss…!

His exposed skin sizzled under the concentrated sacred energy and turned bright red in moments. The other prisoners also sensed the danger and used whatever means they possessed to resist it.

Blinding sacred light poured down from the gigantic array overhead.

Fang Heng stood directly beneath it, immersed in the heart of the energy. Black mist billowed from his body only to disintegrate as soon as the sacred light touched it.

He breathed deeply, feeling the energy wash through him.

It was working!

The core power of the Demon Seed in his sea of consciousness was being drawn out. Its reserves had begun to diminish, if only slightly.

But not enough! He had to keep going!

Fang Heng inhaled deeply and pushed the Demon Seed's power outward with everything he had.

Dense black mist streamed from his body and rose toward the ceiling, where the descending sacred light crushed it into clouds of thick white vapor.

Under the enormous pressure, more and more of the Demon Seed's power was forced out of him. The cavern's giant magic array gradually ramped up to full output to suppress Fang Heng.

Even the floor of the entire hall began to tremble faintly.

By now, the evil spirit had been reduced to one last wisp of spectral essence, huddled in the farthest corner of its cell.

— End of Chapter 2059 —

  • Sacred Luminous Lattice Stone. 神圣流光栅石 is described as stone-like but plant-like crystalline clusters nourished by sacred energy. The system explicitly says it cannot be transplanted.
  • Demon-Sealing Cavern. 镇魔窟 is the Holy Court’s prison and suppression installation; the same English term is used as in the preceding chapter.
  • Jealousy. The Demon Seed is being restrained by an external power associated with 嫉妒 (Jealousy).
