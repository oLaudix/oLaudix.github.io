Chapter 2149

Worms

From original Chinese Chapter 2150 · 蠕虫

Fang Heng raised his head toward the battlefield again.

Time to begin. All he needed was a little chaos to give the worms a chance to enter the fight!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Almost simultaneously, the regrouped Lickers charged forward once more!

Under cover of the Licker assault, huge numbers of worms crawled along the ground toward the battlefield!

Seeing the Lickers move, the Tree Spirits immediately joined the attack and advanced toward the institute head-on.

“It's the Lickers! They're back! Use the injections! Don't let them come close!”

The Federation combat squads spotted the returning Lickers and promptly switched to long-range injection guns, aiming and firing at them one after another!

Under the effects of the special agents, the Lickers' regenerative abilities were dramatically weakened.

Combined with the heavy area bombardment from war machines farther behind, this kept the Lickers outside the defensive line.

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Licker) has been affected by an unknown agent. Its 'Undying Body' effect has been greatly weakened...]

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Licker) has been struck by an explosive beam attack. Its health has decreased significantly...]

Fang Heng rapidly surveyed the battlefield.

This wasn't going well.

He'd have to step in personally!

In the next instant, he sped toward the front.

The Tree Spirits who had been following him hesitated, exchanged glances, and hurried after him into the fighting.

They watched him charge all the way into the very heart of the front line!

Stray beams flashed past him in every direction.

The Tree Spirits following him didn't dare go any farther. They stopped and stared at Fang Heng as he plunged toward the center of the battlefield.

“Kill him!”

Several Federation squads spotted Fang Heng advancing alone and concentrated their fire on him!

Bang!

The white cloth wrapped around his back burst apart!

A huge blood-red greatsword was revealed beneath it!

Dense blood energy erupted and formed a visible crimson curtain before Fang Heng!

Puff! Puff, puff!

The incoming beams struck the blood curtain like raindrops hitting water, creating ripples and soft popping sounds.

To the semi-mechanized Federation soldiers' horror, their beams were completely absorbed by that strange crimson barrier!

Even their concentrated fire couldn't harm Fang Heng at all!

Not far away, the Tree Spirits were astonished. This was the first time they'd seen a human wield such tremendous power.

“Great Elder, the power he's using...”

Senzo watched Fang Heng and nodded slowly. “Mm. It's rather peculiar.”

Ignoring the surrounding fire, Fang Heng raced toward the semi-mechanized soldiers in front.

His goal wasn't to kill them, but to cause still more confusion.

He needed time for the worms to reach the battlefield!

Besides, Fang Heng had noticed that Wanling Technology didn't actually have many injection agents in reserve.

As a result, its soldiers were reluctant to use them too freely.

In the command room, everyone fell into a long silence as they watched Fang Heng on the monitors.

No one had imagined that a seemingly ordinary human could possess such terrifying power!

The entire front-line semi-mechanized force couldn't stop him alone!

That dreadful blood-energy fluctuation...

What exactly was it?

Ji Haiting clenched his fists, looking at Fang Heng on the screen with burning eyes.

He had to admit that this was precisely the kind of power he wanted!

Power fundamentally different from that of the Dependents.

Power sufficient to oppose the Tree Spirits—and even the Dependents themselves!

And Ji Haiting could sense that Fang Heng was still nowhere near his limit.

Sweat formed on the institute director's forehead as Fang Heng rapidly approached the building. Clenching his fists, the director shouted into the communicator. “Use focused beams! Set the energy output to maximum! Kill him! Now! Don't let him get any closer!”

“Understood!”

The director stared fixedly at Fang Heng on the screen.

Ordinary beam weapons were no longer effective against him.

Then they'd try concentrated high-energy focused beams!

Clack, clack, clack...

Nearly every defensive cannon mounted around the institute exposed its barrel and locked onto Fang Heng.

After five full seconds of charging...

Whoosh!

A dense, multicolored barrage of high-frequency focused energy beams struck toward Fang Heng almost simultaneously.

Facing even heavier ranged fire, Fang Heng frowned. Sensing the unusual energy fluctuations, he glanced toward the institute on his right and vanished from the air!

Secondary Spatial Projection!

The next instant, every focused beam passed harmlessly through the afterimage he'd left behind!

That move again!

The director gritted his teeth.

That strange spatial ability!

They had no means of countering this power, which resembled an emergency spatial transition!

They could only watch him break through!

Several seconds later, Fang Heng reappeared directly in front of the institute's outer energy-defense field.

He stared at the invisible barrier ahead.

One last obstacle.

Unlike ordinary zombie clones, the worms couldn't use spatial abilities to bypass the force field.

He had to break through it to give them an opening.

Then he'd do it by force!

Stepping out of Secondary Spatial Projection, Fang Heng threw a punch.

Boom!

His fist slammed into the force field with a dull impact.

Still not enough attack power!

Fang Heng gritted his teeth.

Great quantities of blood energy hovering around him rapidly condensed into crimson spikes!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The spikes shot toward the barrier!

Bang! Bang!

Each spike exploded against the force field in a vast cloud of blood mist!

Once dispersed, the mist rapidly coalesced into more spikes, which struck the barrier again, dealing uninterrupted damage.

In the main surveillance room, everyone was horrified by Fang Heng's bizarre blood-energy attacks.

Watching the readings on his display, an operator grew pale. “Sir! The force field's energy level is dropping rapidly! These blood-energy attacks are corrosive and extremely effective against it!”

Veins bulged along the institute director's neck as he gritted his teeth and shouted, “Drive him back! Use whatever means necessary!”

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Several Federation semi-mechanically modified soldiers arriving from the right side of the force field concentrated their long-range fire on Fang Heng.

— End of Chapter 2149 —
