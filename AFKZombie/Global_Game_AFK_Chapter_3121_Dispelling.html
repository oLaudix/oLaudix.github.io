Chapter 3121

Dispelling

From Chinese Chapter 3122 · 驱散

“It's fine. I have a way.”

As Fang Heng spoke, he raised his hand and cast a Holy Purification spell at the cloud of ancient knowledge.

Holy light descended.

“Hiss, hisss…”

A layer of golden light instantly appeared over the black mist, and white vapor billowed upward.

Moments later, the dark mist had turned into a pale-blue cloud of energy.

Watching Fang Heng so easily purify the evil embryo's aura, Nasir couldn't help feeling a little envious.

If he'd tried to remove that aura himself, it would have taken considerable effort.

Fang Heng stepped forward and reached out to touch the energy.

The next instant, a cool current flowed into his body.

Mysterious images accompanied by vast quantities of esoteric knowledge poured rapidly into his sea of consciousness.

[Hint: You have obtained a portion of knowledge.]

[Hint: You have absorbed occult energy. Occultism EXP +57,000.]

[Hint: You have absorbed occult knowledge. Occultism EXP +12,380,000…]

[Hint: Your current Occultism level has reached MAX. Please advance your Occultism rank.]

So much ancient knowledge flooded his sea of consciousness that Fang Heng's Occultism reached MAX before he even had time to examine and digest it properly.

What a treasure!

Fang Heng's eyes lit up.

This was far more efficient than grinding experience in his basement!

He had absorbed only a small fraction of the knowledge energy, leaving a huge amount untouched, yet Occultism was already maxed out.

Absorbing any more now would be a waste.

After a moment's thought, Fang Heng contained the remaining knowledge energy in a safe region of his sea of consciousness. He would use it once he broke through to the Demigod level in Occultism.

After taking a while to organize his sea of consciousness, Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

“How does it feel?” Nasir asked.

“Very good. It's excellent stuff.”

Fang Heng nodded. “The evil embryo is dealt with. Let's look around and see if we can find a way out.”

“Right.”

Nasir had the same idea. He and Fang Heng split up to inspect the magic arrays engraved into the cavern walls.

Fang Heng wasn't in a hurry. He patiently studied the arrays arranged throughout the cave, occasionally exchanging observations with Nasir as they inspired each other.

While the pair were absorbed in academic discussion of alchemy, several Lanyue Clan elders in the upper caverns stood rooted to the spot.

It was over.

Completely over.

Although the evil god's magic array was still operating, the evil god's power within the ritual array had completely dissipated.

The evil god had actually perished!

And that was after devouring the life force of so many sacrifices.

Just what sort of terrifying existence had they encountered?

The Kinsfolk had demanded Fang Heng's elimination in exchange for protecting the Lanyue Clan.

They didn't know whether Fang Heng was dead, but the evil embryo had certainly lost all its life force.

With the evil embryo dead, they had no means left to protect themselves and no bargaining chips whatsoever.

Hoping for reinforcements from the Kinsfolk was now nothing but a fantasy.

Everyone turned toward the Lanyue Clan chief.

“We have lost…”

The chief drew a deep breath and slowly accepted reality. As though the last of his strength had drained away, he sighed. “Come out with me, all of you. Perhaps only our deaths will appease the Noel Clan and allow the last spark of the Lanyue Clan's bloodline to survive.”

“Yes, Clan Chief…”

…

In the lower caverns, Mu Shunan and the Noel Clan's warriors still didn't know that Fang Heng had escaped danger. They continued searching carefully, trying to locate where the spatial array had transported the two men and where the Lanyue Clan was hiding.

The Noel Clan had dispatched extremely powerful high-ranking warriors. Even with their senses obstructed, finding the Lanyue Clan was only a matter of time.

“Nasir, please hold on…”

Mu Shunan was deeply uneasy and silently prayed.

Then he saw a purple glow suddenly appear ahead.

The Lanyue Clan!

The high-ranking warriors instantly detected the newcomers, grew alert, and assumed combat stances.

Yet the Lanyue Clan chief was the first to kneel before them.

All the other Lanyue Clan members knelt as well.

“Members of the Noel Clan, I am the current chief of the Lanyue Clan. We have lost. We are willing to surrender.

All we ask is that our bloodline be spared.”

Huh? They were surrendering?

Seriously?

Wariness appeared on Mu Shunan's face.

The Noel Clan elder didn't respond to the surrender. Instead, he stared coldly at the man and demanded, “Where are Fang Heng and Nasir?”

Several Lanyue Clan elders exchanged glances, bitterness welling up inside them.

“They were sent through a teleportation passage into the sealed cavern below. When our ancestor created that cavern, he never built an exit. There is only a way in, no way out.”

Mu Shunan couldn't help pressing him. “What's inside that sealed cavern?”

“The dimensional evil embryo raised by our Lanyue Clan.

It is a manifestation of the evil god Phoenicia.”

What?! The Lanyue Clan had dared to imprison an evil god from the Endless Void beneath their caves?

The warriors' expressions immediately turned grave.

So that explained it.

Had the Lanyue Clan killed Fang Heng, accomplished their objective, and only then come out to surrender?

The Kinsfolk was far too audacious! Did they really have so little regard for the Noel Clan?

“Where are Fang Heng and the others?!”

“We don't know.” The Lanyue Clan chief shook his head with a bitter smile. “We can no longer sense the evil god's aura. I believe it has been destroyed. Whether Fang Heng and Nasir were responsible, however, we cannot say.”

At those words, silence settled over the cavern for a moment.

Huh?

What? Were the Lanyue Clan saying that Fang Heng and his companion had fallen into their trap, only to slaughter the evil god inside it?

Just the two of them?

Was an evil god really that easy to deal with?

Several Noel Clan elders exchanged looks.

Something about this didn't add up. The whole affair felt distinctly strange.

The elder in command ordered the warriors to detain the Lanyue Clan's senior members first, then separate and question them thoroughly.

“Elder, Fang Heng and the others…”

The leading elder gestured for Mu Shunan to remain calm. “Mm. Don't rush. We need to proceed more cautiously in a situation like this. Let's establish the facts first.”

…

They spent another half hour or more questioning every surrendered Lanyue Clan member. Only then did the Noel Clan elders summon everyone again to discuss their findings.

“We've questioned them all. The Lanyue Clan's account checks out. They colluded with the Kinsfolk and made an agreement to teleport Fang Heng and Nasir into the deepest part of the labyrinth, where they raised the evil embryo.”

Another Noel Clan elder proficient in magic arrays nodded. “I also inspected the array used to sacrifice to the evil god. Traces of its aura are indeed present. Just as the Lanyue Clan said, the evil embryo's aura vanished not long ago.”

The warriors looked at one another.

So Fang Heng and Nasir really had killed an evil god?

Was the evil god too weak, or were those two simply too strong?

“The magic array leading to the evil embryo's breeding ground deep in the labyrinth works in only one direction. Given the evil god's terrifying strength and the consequences of releasing it, the array was designed as one-way from the beginning. At present, we cannot confirm Fang Heng and Nasir's condition.”

“We must go down and check as soon as possible.”

“Even if the Lanyue Clan is telling the truth and Fang Heng and Nasir exhausted themselves killing the evil god, they can't possibly be in good condition. They need immediate treatment.”

The elders nodded in agreement.

“Let's move. The first team has already been selected. We'll use the teleportation passage to enter the lower labyrinth and support Fang Heng and Nasir.”

“Yes!”

— End of Chapter 3121 —
