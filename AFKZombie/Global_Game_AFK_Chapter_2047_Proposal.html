Chapter 2047

Proposal

From original Chinese Chapter 2048 · 提议

“The Holy Court is in chaos. This is our chance!”

Lord Dani looked solemn. “We may only get one opportunity to win this battle. Whether we succeed depends on all of us working together—”

As she spoke, she formed a hand seal.

Behind the battlefield, tens of thousands of coffins that had been prepared in advance suddenly opened together!

An immense wave of soul power erupted from within them.

Innate Skill: Dead Soul Reincarnation.

Dense masses of wraiths streamed out of the opened coffins.

Everyone stared in surprise.

Lord Dani…

Once, the entire undead faction had placed great hopes in her. She'd been regarded as one of the leading figures of her generation, blessed with the extraordinarily powerful talent Dead Soul Reincarnation.

But in recent years, she had become stuck at a bottleneck while trying to advance that talent and had largely disappeared from public attention.

Until now.

The players suddenly realized that Dani's terrifying innate ability had undergone another qualitative transformation!

Evil spirits wailed in the night sky as they gathered overhead.

Dani hadn't used this talent since advancing it. Its power came with demanding activation conditions, including the need to prepare vast numbers of soul vessels in advance.

She'd been waiting for the right moment.

Either she would keep holding back, or she would strike once with lethal force!

Tens of thousands of souls converged above the battlefield, then swooped toward weaknesses in the Holy Court's formations.

For the first time, its main forces were pushed into a major disadvantage by the wraith assault.

…

Canmu City, elsewhere in Qiboreya.

Li Qingran watched her quest completion percentage climb, bit by bit, until it reached 380%.

That much?!

She stared at the notification, wondering whether she was dreaming. Her heart thudded in her chest.

Three hundred percent had already exceeded the quest's requirements. She could go to Instructor Gu Luo, claim her reward, and receive the academic advancement she wanted.

Yet the progress hadn't stopped increasing!

If it passed five hundred percent, could the rewards improve even further?

Then she remembered something important: the team quest still had two empty slots.

If she invited two people to join now, those two would immediately qualify for an academic advancement reward.

What an incredible opportunity!

The quest was guaranteed to succeed. Selling the remaining team slots would fetch an unimaginable price.

She could trade them for useful items—ideally the Main God Points Fang Heng needed. There was little more she could do to help him directly in battle.

It would be better to log out early, find buyers, and sell the positions while there was still time.

Li Qingran made up her mind and logged out immediately.

…

Fang Heng spent most of the night directing his undead skeletons in battle against the Holy Court, playing cat and mouse with its troops from the small hours of the morning until the sky began to brighten.

The Holy Court had tried entering the regions engulfed by the Yin Vein several times. Each attempt failed, and some teams were nearly wiped out by Fang Heng's skeletons.

After those experiences, the defenders learned their lesson and stopped pursuing the enemy deep into the frost.

As the sun rose, large numbers of additional Holy Court reinforcements arrived throughout Duncha, substantially strengthening the defenses.

Meanwhile, the skeletons had been steadily consumed in combat all night. Their overall strength was much lower than before.

Eventually, Fang Heng could find no more openings. He reluctantly abandoned the harassment campaign and left the Yin Vein's affected region.

…

On the outskirts of the city, Chu Yan and his team had been waiting for most of the night.

Watching the footage posted online, the players of the Devout Guild were secretly astonished.

World Lord Fang Heng was terrifyingly powerful!

The city was frozen solid, a place of absolute silence. Frost continued spreading out and consuming every trace of life.

And it wasn't just Duncha in turmoil. The entire Holy Court had mobilized, with large numbers of teams from other game worlds rushing to help.

Fang Heng jumped into Chu Yan's jeep, and they drove away.

On the road, he asked Chu Yan for an overview of the Holy Court's situation.

For the moment, its forces could only defend against the Yin Vein's aura. They were still a long way from eliminating the source of the disaster. Internal affairs were extremely chaotic.

But Fang Heng had a small problem of his own. The passage he'd used to get here had been destroyed, and he currently had no way back through it.

“How are things on your end?” he asked.

“Not good. A bit worse than I expected.”

Chu Yan and his Devout Guild had lost both Canmu City and the teleportation passage they were responsible for defending. Their Holy Court reputation had plummeted, and they'd lost eligibility for major faction quests.

The Holy Court had also begun suspecting Chu Yan's involvement in the disasters. It was only too busy with the Yin Vein to investigate him at present.

He figured it was time to get out before someone came looking for him.

Fang Heng thought for a moment.

“What do you suggest we do next?”

“I've gathered some information,” Chu Yan said. “The Holy Court is concentrating nearly all its attention on the Yin Vein eruption. Large numbers of its combat units are heading there. I suggest we attack the Holy Court's headquarters cathedral.”

“The cathedral?”

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

Attack their headquarters directly? Wasn't that taking things a bit far?

Although, he had to admit, this young man thought much the same way he did.

There was no convenient way back for now anyway.

Strictly speaking, Fang Heng could return by deliberately dying and resurrecting. The Vampire King's coffin and the Divine Tree were already bound to him and would remain effective, allowing him to resurrect. He also had a bound mechanical rebirth device.

However, doing so would mean losing access to any items in his backpack that dropped upon death.

“There's a stone inscription left by the Divine Domain inside the cathedral,” Chu Yan continued. “It's said to have the power to cultivate reserve Crusaders. If we can destroy it completely, it would deal an enormous blow to the Holy Court.”

Fang Heng liked that idea even more.

A huge blow to the Holy Court meant a great deal of faction Honor—which meant plenty of Main God Points!

Besides, the cathedral might contain other valuable things. If he had a chance to take them with him…

His eyes lit up.

“Good suggestion. Since we're here, we might as well take a look.”

“Great!” Chu Yan was excited too.

“I've learned that Holy Court reinforcement teams from every direction are traveling toward the Yin Vein, but I haven't heard anything about the Archbishop. I suspect she's still at headquarters, so we need to be careful.”

Playing a double agent inside the Holy Court had earned Chu Yan plenty of benefits. But after everything that had happened, he doubted he could ever regain its favor.

He had to strike while the Holy Court was still confused.

“Boss Fang, I've already arranged to use a privately controlled long-distance teleportation passage belonging to another player. We're heading there now…”

— End of Chapter 2047 —
