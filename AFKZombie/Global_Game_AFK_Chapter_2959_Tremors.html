Chapter 2959

Tremors

From Chinese Chapter 2960 · 晃动

Vion was among those watching, his bloodshot eyes fixed firmly on Fang Heng.

Fang Heng!

Let's see what you're really capable of!

So far, Fang Heng's side hadn't made any unusual arrangements. The surface was covered with summoned creatures in artillery positions, apparently preparing to defend the base to the death.

More than half an hour later, an operator shouted, "Report! Captain! We're detecting powerful spatial-energy fluctuations!"

Spatial fluctuations!

The Cangniao mechanical battle group.

Everyone looked toward the sky.

The sand-filled space above distorted unnaturally. Far more black mechanical spheres than before gradually emerged from the warped space.

Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh...

The moment the Licker swarm locked on, they began firing biological cannons into the air.

The black mechanical spheres dodged and attacked in return.

Black, short-frequency beams and biological fireballs filled the sky.

Giselle frowned deeply.

More and more mechanical spheres were arriving, forming several layers around the base's outer defensive shield.

Countless dark beams continued striking and exploding against the barrier.

The barrier's stored energy was being consumed rapidly.

At this rate, it certainly couldn't last much longer!

In addition to attacking the shield, many of the black spheres began targeting the ore-conveyor lines outside it.

The conveyors had already stopped and lacked protective shielding, so the mechanical beams shattered them one after another.

What were they waiting for?

What could delaying the inevitable possibly achieve?

Vion looked coldly at Zaka and the other players trying to resist below, sneering inwardly.

Once they realized they weren't strong enough, they'd come begging for rescue, wouldn't they?

"Huh? What's that now?"

Suddenly, Vion felt a violent tremor beneath his feet.

Looking toward the source, he saw the layer of antigravity blocks covering the ground begin to shake and slide apart to either side.

Only now did Wayne notice that the ground beneath the blocks had been hollowed out, revealing an enormous pit.

What was that?!

As an immense starship slowly rose from deep underground, Vion's eyes widened with shock.

That was...

A ship?!

Vion wondered whether his sleepless nights had finally made him hallucinate.

What the hell?

A starship?! Where had it come from?!

He was so shocked that he almost shouted on instinct, "Where did he get a starship?!"

Starships weren't things you could find lying around. The Western Federation had worked secretly with the Kinsfolk for years and invested enormous manpower and resources, yet it had acquired only seven ships.

Apart from the large starship currently commanded by Giselle, all the others were small or medium-sized.

And the vessel rising before them was clearly at least a large starship.

What the hell was going on?!

Where had Fang Heng gotten it?!

Unless...

Cold sweat broke out on Vion's forehead as a terrifying thought occurred to him.

Had Fang Heng been constructing a starship underground all this time, rather than an ore-storage base?!

Impossible!

Absolutely impossible!

They couldn't build a ship that large in six months, never mind half a month!

The players aboard the Federation ship were equally stunned.

The newly revealed vessel was no low-tier craft.

At the very least, it was a large starship.

Its exterior lacked additional Kinsfolk modules and weapon systems, making it look like an empty shell, but the overall frame was of the highest grade.

The President's Office experts all turned toward Vion with questioning looks.

What was going on?

He had been the one left behind to watch Fang Heng. And now he had the nerve to ask what was happening?

The team chief rounded on him reproachfully. "Vion, explain this starship."

Vion was speechless. "I... I don't... I don't know either..."

"Building a starship requires a compatible key. The Kinsfolk provide very few of them, and in such a short time, they can't possibly have built it themselves..."

"Could he have found a starship key here?"

"The mission briefing mentioned several Kinsfolk-allied mercenary teams that were wiped out on this planet. Could Fang Heng have been lucky enough to discover a surviving starship key?"

Everyone fell silent.

The odds were far too low.

Could Fang Heng really have been that lucky?

Was his luck truly that outrageous?

None of them imagined that the starship key had originally belonged to their own Federation.

Even if he really did have a key, how could he have built the ship so quickly?

He had arrived with nothing and was now leaving aboard a starship he'd built himself?

Had they heard such a story before, they would have laughed it off as a joke.

Yet now it was happening right before their eyes.

The starship rose from below and hovered above the ground.

Li Zhixuan appeared on its starboard deck and contacted Zaka's team outside over the radio.

"The compartment docking procedures aren't fully operational yet. We'll have to dock the compartments manually. Move them into the warp zone and we'll transfer them into storage. Help me position them."

"Understood!"

Zaka called to his men and directed operations outside as the three compartments were moved one after another into the starship's warp-transfer zone.

Vion could only watch as all three compartments vanished into Fang Heng's ship in beams of warp light.

"Ship recovery complete! Prepare to withdraw!"

"Yes, sir!"

The last group of players entered the starship, and its enormous doors closed.

The Lickers remained in their artillery positions on the ground, continuously firing biological cannons at the machines beyond the shield, while faint points of light began gathering around the starship.

Captain Giselle raised an eyebrow.

This was bad!

If Fang Heng left now, the outer defensive barrier would disappear, and the Federation starship might become the Cangniao swarm's next target!

"Initiate warp immediately! Prepare to return!"

"Yes, sir!!"

Two white spatial flashes enveloped the starships one after another, and both ships swiftly vanished.

A deathly silence settled over the presidential experts aboard the Federation vessel.

They had spent so much time and effort and schemed so hard, yet Fang Heng had escaped effortlessly aboard his own starship.

He had taken at least several thousand tons of ore mined recently, three large compartments, and even an entire group of valuable researchers.

"Ladies and gentlemen, I want an explanation for everything that's happened on this mission. The military will lodge a formal protest with the President's Office and the council."

Giselle was struggling to contain his anger.

After exhausting so many resources, manpower, and trips, and damaging their relationship with Fang Heng so badly, what had they actually gained?

Wasn't all of this the fault of these clueless idiots?

If they had simply cooperated with Fang Heng honestly, how could things have ended up like this?

"The expert team will submit a report. There must be something wrong with Fang Heng's starship. He..."

"I don't give a damn whether there's something wrong with his ship! He's already left aboard it!"

Giselle finally lost his temper, cutting the expert off and unleashing a furious stream of curses.

"What use is a report to me?! Tell me, how exactly do you plan to retaliate? Are you going to ram him with one of our starships? Well? Answer me!"

The most infuriating part was being saddled with these useless teammates.

First Charlotte, and now Fang Heng.

Was the Western Federation determined to make an enemy out of everyone it met?

Useless teammates! Impossible to carry!
