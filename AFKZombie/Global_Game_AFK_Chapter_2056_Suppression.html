Chapter 2056

Suppression

From original Chinese Chapter 2057 · 压制

While Fang Heng had been struggling to suppress the turmoil in his sea of consciousness, the sacred light now felt unbelievably good.

It was like chugging an ice-cold cola after running five hundred meters on a sweltering summer day!

Bathing in the light, every part of his body felt an unprecedented sense of relief.

But it still wasn't enough. His sea of consciousness remained on the brink of losing control.

Xi Lufei watched his condition closely.

“Now! He's been suppressed! Quickly!”

Whoosh! Whoosh!

The three cardinals flew toward Fang Heng.

His eyes narrowed, and he instinctively prepared to dodge. But just as he began moving, he noticed the special shackles they were carrying.

The sacred energy emanating from them felt extremely similar to the energy of the huge magic array overhead.

Could those things help suppress my Demon Seed temporarily?

Fang Heng hesitated, missing his best chance to counterattack.

Hiss!

The three cardinals seized him simultaneously. Sacred shackles snapped shut around his neck and wrists, immobilizing him completely.

A tight sensation pressed against Fang Heng's chest. He couldn't help throwing back his head.

“ROAR!”

Thick black mist poured from his mouth and was immediately purified by the sacred light filling the hall.

The shackles reacted with the magic array's power, amplifying the suppression effect by more than a hundred percent!

[Notice: You have been affected by an unknown item.]

[Notice: All of your attributes are suppressed to a maximum of 65 points.]

[Notice: You can no longer cast spells.]

[Notice: Logging out and using your inventory are disabled.]

[Notice: While inside the current magic array, you will be affected by additional unknown effects.]

For Fang Heng, it was like someone had added ice to that cola.

Absolutely wonderful!

After one final surge, the Demon Seed's energy within his sea of consciousness rapidly retreated to a corner.

The turbulence finally stopped.

At last, he regained full control over his sea of consciousness.

“Success!”

The three cardinals didn't linger. They pushed Fang Heng into one of the Demon-Sealing Cavern's prison cells, then hurriedly retreated to a safe distance.

Watching the black mist still occasionally leaking from him, they all looked toward Xi Lufei.

She nodded.

“That should do it.”

Everyone breathed a long sigh of relief.

Maintaining the seal while escorting Fang Heng had consumed an enormous amount of their energy. Fortunately, they'd succeeded in imprisoning him.

“Let's leave.”

They withdrew from the cavern and closed its entrance completely.

Outside, Xi Lufei addressed several elite Holy Court oracles who had come to reinforce the guards.

“Stay here and help guard this door. Do not leave your posts until I return.”

“Yes, Archbishop!”

“Archbishop, may we ask… who was that person?”

Everyone looked at her expectantly. They were still deeply confused about what had happened.

Xi Lufei shook her head.

She didn't know the complete truth herself. She could only infer a plausible explanation from what she'd witnessed.

“If I had to guess, Fang Heng took advantage of the chaos outside Duncha and entered our cathedral to cause trouble. He may have accidentally activated the sacred Luminous Grating Stone in the Temple of Zeus. The released energy then stimulated the Demon Seed within him.”

“The Demon Seed's power in his body is very strange. He cannot control such a powerful force. I suspect his own consciousness has fallen into a deep sleep.”

She paused before adding, “It's even possible that the Demon Seed has completely erased his mind, turning his body into a puppet.”

The others nodded. It sounded like a very reasonable explanation.

“For now we've brought the Demon Seed under control, but we must not become careless. Aohui, take some people and eliminate the remaining undead inside the cathedral. Then return here to stand guard.”

“Understood, Archbishop!”

Aohui immediately selected his personnel and departed.

Xi Lufei then turned to the three cardinals.

“The Demon Seed is critically important, and Fang Heng is someone the Divine Domain is actively searching for. Come with me. We need to pray and report this incident immediately.”

“Very well.”

…

Behind the tightly sealed Holy Court door, the magic array on the cavern ceiling continued rotating slowly.

Sacred light shone through the bars of Fang Heng's cell.

He felt indescribably comfortable.

The sacred suppression had finally calmed the Demon Seed's energy inside him and relieved the dreadful dizziness he'd suffered earlier.

At last, he could gather his thoughts and consider what to do next.

In retrospect, although this unexpected backlash had been disastrous and had nearly killed him, Fang Heng felt oddly fortunate.

He had accidentally removed part of Envy's outer seal early.

If he'd waited another month before that layer broke, the surviving restrictions protecting his sea of consciousness would have been weakened even further. The subsequent eruption of Demon Seed power would have been much stronger.

In the worst case, he could have lost his mind completely.

But he was still in a terrible situation. He needed to find a way out.

After resting for a while, Fang Heng took a deep breath, stood up, and examined the surroundings.

He was trapped in a small cell overlooking a huge circular hall. Holy Court magic arrays had been carved into the floor and ceiling.

Large prison cells lined the circular outer wall. More than half were occupied. His was just one of many.

The prisoners appeared to hate the circulating sacred energy. They were curled up in the corners of their cells, trying to avoid its light.

And in the middle of the hall stood something like an enormous flower bed.

But the things growing in it weren't ordinary plants. They were clusters of dark-yellow crystals.

Crystals?

Fang Heng focused on them.

They opened outward like bunches of flowers. The sacred energy they released was identical to that of the gigantic stone he'd seen in the Temple of Zeus, although their shapes differed.

These looked natural and unprocessed; the crystal upstairs had clearly been carefully cut and polished.

“Idiot! Stop it! Stop releasing your aura!”

Hearing someone shout, Fang Heng looked toward a cell on his right.

A short old man was curled up in its corner, using his ragged clothes to shield himself from the sacred light coming through the bars.
