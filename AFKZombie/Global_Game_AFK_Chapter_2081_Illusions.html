Chapter 2081

Illusions

From original Chinese Chapter 2082 · 幻象

The sight of those grotesque creatures covering the cliff made Lindro and the others' scalps tingle. Before they could settle on a battle plan, Fang Heng was already walking toward the wall.

"Brother Fang Heng!"

[Hint: You have been targeted by an illusion attack from a Hellish Evil Eye. Due to the special effect of your talent, Omniscient Eye, you are immune to this illusion.]

The notification flashed before his eyes.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

As soon as he entered the Evil Eyes' attack range, several dark-purple beams shot straight toward him.

This time, Fang Heng didn't dodge.

Pop!

A beam struck him and exploded, tearing a bloody hole in his body.

[Hint: You have taken 1,000 damage from a Hellish Evil Eye's Hell Beam. This damage ignores resistance.]

[Hint: You have been subjected to a Hellish Evil Eye's Petrifying Gaze. Your Constitution exceeds the required threshold; the effect has failed.]

Hiss, hiss...

The wound closed almost instantly.

An attack that ignored defensive resistance?

Fang Heng frowned and looked up. Every eyeball on the densely packed cliff had turned toward him.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!

A continuous barrage of dark-purple beams followed.

Damn it!

Fang Heng didn't dare underestimate them. He jumped backward, retreating to the middle of Lindro's group.

"Brother Fang Heng, are you all right?"

The others breathed a sigh of relief. For a moment they'd feared he might actually die there.

"Nothing serious."

Fang Heng flexed his body and studied the masses of eyes covering the cliff again.

Sak suggested, "These things look difficult to handle in melee, but they're attached to the cliff and can't move. Why don't we bombard them from a distance?"

"Hmm. Ranged attacks could work."

Hellish Evil Eyes weren't easy opponents. With so many packed together, their concentrated fire was too much even for Fang Heng to endure, never mind the additional illusion effects.

Still, there might be a way to pick them off one by one.

As Sak had pointed out, the Evil Eyes grew attached to the rock. Their limited mobility made it hard for them to dodge ranged attacks.

While they spoke, another player moved outside the creatures' attack range and cast a casual Holy Punishment spell at the cliff.

Whoosh!

A flash of holy light struck an Evil Eye, bursting against its body.

"Well?"

"It works." The player checked his notification and nodded. "They take considerably less damage while their eyes are closed, but they still take some damage."

That settled it.

The team relaxed. The simplest approach was to attack them from a distance and trade fire.

Once the other Holy Court teams arrived, everyone could combine forces and gradually clear the area.

That was probably the Holy Court's original intention in assigning the trial: use the trainees to eliminate the Hellish Evil Eyes.

And judging by their numbers, it wouldn't be a quick task.

Fang Heng thought for a moment, then looked higher up the cliff. Openings of various sizes dotted the towering rock face.

There might be passages behind those openings.

With the Crusader instructors blocking the route back, the only way to search for another exit from this world was to keep moving forward.

Perhaps he should investigate the caves above. First, though, he needed a way past the Evil Eyes.

"Looks like that's our best option. Let's begin immediately, starting with the elite creatures," Lindro said.

Now that they understood the Evil Eyes' weakness, Lindro quickly devised a strategy. They had reached the cliff ahead of the other trial teams, giving them a head start. They could concentrate fire on the elites, fulfill that portion of the quest first, then farm the ordinary enemies at their leisure.

"All right."

They started erecting holy defensive barriers and preparing their attacks. The safest option was to farm the creatures slowly.

A more aggressive tactic would be to probe the Evil Eyes' attack range, bait them into opening their eyes, and strike while they were exposed for additional damage.

At that moment, Fang Heng interrupted. "I'm sorry, everyone, but with those Evil Eyes attached to the cliff, I won't be much help in a ranged battle."

Lindro waved the apology away. "Don't say that, Brother Fang Heng. We only made it here ahead of everyone else thanks to you. Leave the farming to us."

"Thank you." Fang Heng looked toward the upper cliff. "There are quite a few small caves up there. I'd like to take a look. Maybe I'll find something that helps with the quest."

Go up there?

The others were startled. Following his gaze, they noticed the scattered cave openings among the sea of black eyeballs.

"Well..." Sak hesitated. "Isn't that a little too dangerous?"

Reaching those caves meant enduring concentrated attacks from all the Evil Eyes covering the cliff. How could anyone survive that?

"It's fine. I can try. There might be dangers inside as well. If I don't come back, don't worry about me. Just continue the mission."

The others exchanged glances.

So the expert was planning to challenge an even higher difficulty level.

What could they say? Try to dissuade him?

Forget it. He wouldn't listen anyway.

"Very well, Brother Fang Heng. Please be careful."

"Mm."

Fang Heng nodded, lightly pushed off the ground, and darted toward the cliff at astonishing speed.

The moment he entered range, masses of Evil Eyes swiveled toward him.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

A dense volley of purple beams shot straight at him.

Then—

"Huh?!"

Lindro and the others, watching his every movement, cried out in surprise.

Fang Heng's figure was fading in midair, as if merging with space itself!

Spatial abilities!

An instant later, he vanished completely.

The purple beams pierced the afterimage he'd left behind and struck empty air.

The others stared at one another in astonishment.

Fang Heng had even mastered a special spatial ability!

Meanwhile, Fang Heng entered his secondary-space projection and swiftly climbed the cliff.

Trusting his instincts, he selected a relatively large cave opening and slipped inside.

— End of Chapter 2081 —
