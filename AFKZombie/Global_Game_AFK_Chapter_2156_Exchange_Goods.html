Chapter 2156

Exchange Goods

From original Chinese Chapter 2157 · 兑换品

In addition, the exchange list contained a jumble of miscellaneous items.

Fang Heng even found Water of Life among them.

Its price was only 100 contribution points.

As expected, the Water of Life had been one giant trick!

By now, Fang Heng had reached a high enough level that most of the Tree Spirits' exchange items held little appeal for him.

Hm?

As he scanned the list, his eyelid twitched, and his gaze settled on one particular item.

Leaf of Life.

A relatively rare special material with many uses. It could be consumed to greatly enhance the power of Nature Studies spells or healing, or worn as equipment to increase the success rate of Nature Studies skills.

Alternatively, it could be absorbed in one use to gain a huge amount of Nature Studies experience.

Very versatile.

“Not bad...”

Fang Heng patiently examined every item the Tree Spirit faction offered.

He had no idea whether some items were hidden from the exchange privileges the Tree Spirits had granted him.

But judging by what he could currently see, the Leaf of Life was by far the most valuable.

A single leaf cost a staggering 100,000 contribution points.

After all that work helping the Tree Spirits, he'd only accumulated 157,000 points.

Not even enough for two leaves.

Considering that his relationship with the Tree Spirits could collapse at any moment, Fang Heng decided to spend his contribution points as quickly as possible.

Once it was safely in his pocket, he could rest easy.

“I'd like to exchange for this.”

When the Tree Spirit realized Fang Heng wanted a Leaf of Life, hesitation flashed through his eyes.

“What? Is that not allowed?”

The Tree Spirit nodded. “Please wait a moment. I need to consult the Great Elder.”

Fang Heng waved impatiently. “Then hurry. The Federation isn't going to wait patiently for you.”

Soon the Tree Spirit returned after obtaining the Great Elder's permission, reluctantly handing Fang Heng a Leaf of Life.

[Hint: The player has spent 100,000 Tree Spirit contribution points and obtained an item—Leaf of Life.]

It was a leaf shaped like a five-leaf clover.

Holding it in his palm, Fang Heng could already sense the intense vitality radiating from within.

[Item: Leaf of Life (Five-Leaf Clover).]

Type: Special Item.

Description: When worn, increases the success rate of Nature Studies spells by 5%. The Leaf of Life may also be consumed to increase the effect of the next Nature Studies spell by 100%–2000%, depending on skill level (without exceeding the skill's maximum effect). Alternatively, it may be used on a target to accelerate the target's recovery of vitality.

Description (triggered by the player's Advanced Nature Studies): The player may absorb the energy contained in the Leaf of Life through Advanced Nature Studies to rapidly restore vitality and mental strength, or convert it into a large amount of Nature Studies skill experience. (Estimated experience gained upon absorption: 820,000 Nature Studies skill EXP.)

Excellent!

The actual item description was much better than expected!

One Leaf of Life held enough experience to raise his Nature Studies by an entire level!

Of course, there was no need to absorb it yet.

Fang Heng placed the Leaf of Life in his backpack, turned, and looked up at the spatial-transition points flickering high above the research institute.

Come on, then!

He still had time.

Time to farm some more Leaves of Life!

· · ·

Inside the institute.

The Tree Spirits had expended considerable effort smashing through the Federation's defensive layers. At last, they found Vannis and his companions imprisoned in a hall on the first floor.

“I'm sorry, Great Elder. I...”

The failure of the covert infiltration left Vannis deeply frustrated.

“This isn't the time for that, Vannis. We must find the Niam Alloy. Do you know where it is? Take us there immediately.”

“Understood.”

Vannis nodded and pulled himself together. “We investigated the alloy's precise location in advance. It's in an underground secret laboratory. I'll take everyone there now.”

The Tree Spirits immediately followed him down into the underground research area.

Along the way, defeating scattered guard teams cost them yet more precious time.

Eventually, Vannis led them into the underground secret laboratory section.

When he saw that the metal gate at the entrance was standing open, his heart tightened with foreboding.

What was going on?

The door was open?

He'd studied the central institute's defensive arrangements.

Once it entered emergency status, the gate ahead should theoretically have been sealed.

Then why was it open now?

Several accompanying Tree Spirit elders noticed that Vannis had stopped. “Vannis? What's wrong?”

“N-nothing.”

Suppressing his unease, Vannis led everyone through the alloy gate and headed straight for Laboratory No. 3.

Once inside, the elders fell silent at the sight before them. They turned toward Vannis.

The laboratory was spotless.

There was nobody inside.

The two research tables stood empty.

Where was the Niam Alloy?

“Vannis.”

A Wood Clan elder's expression turned icy. “Where is the Niam Alloy you promised us?”

Vannis stared at the empty research tables, his face rigid with shock.

The Niam Alloy!

Where had it gone?

Everything else inside the laboratory matched the intelligence he'd obtained beforehand.

The only thing missing was the alloy.

The entire Tree Spirit race had devoted so much effort to this operation, sacrificing so many of their clansmen.

And in the end, the objective wasn't there?

Because his information had been wrong?

The assembled elders' expressions grew exceedingly ugly.

The Leaf Clan!

This wasn't the first time they'd made a mistake like this!

Last time it had happened too.

And now again!

For a moment, some even suspected the Leaf Clan might be doing it deliberately!

Were they really willing to harm their own race when facing a crisis like this?

Vannis's expression changed repeatedly before he shouted, “No! Impossible! The Niam Alloy must be here! My intelligence couldn't have been wrong! Search! It has to be somewhere here! Find it!”

The elders nodded and ordered their accompanying Tree Spirits to turn the laboratory upside down.

Before long, someone discovered something.

A Tree Spirit found an experimental report log and respectfully handed it to Great Elder Senzo.

Senzo's eyes grew grave as he read it.

“Great Elder, did you find anything?”

“Read it yourself.”

Vannis took the report and glanced at it. His brows instantly knitted together.

21:12 — On instructions from the institute director, the institute has entered a crisis situation. Niam Alloy transferred ahead of schedule.

Transferred?

Damn it!

There was no emergency-transfer provision in the crisis-handling handbook!

An unexpected decision?

Could their luck really be that bad?

Had the director suddenly decided to move the alloy today?

Everyone's faces turned stiff.

The interference device would fail in about ten minutes. They had very little time left.

“Search!”

Senzo's voice hardened. “Niam Alloy can't pass through a spatial-transition passage. The Federation couldn't have taken it out of the institute that way. Search! We must find it!”

“Understood!”

— End of Chapter 2156 —
