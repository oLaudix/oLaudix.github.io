Chapter 2067

Pursuit

From original Chinese Chapter 2068 · 追查

Duncha game world.

Divine Adjudicator Okney descended into the lower world, accompanied by five Divine Realm adjudicators, to hunt down Fang Heng.

They had arrived two days ahead of schedule.

The Divine Realm had paid an enormous price to send them. For some time afterward, it would be unable to use that passage to enter the lower planes again.

Seeing the devastation suffered by Duncha, Okney sighed inwardly.

The arrival of the Demon Seed would bring disaster to the world. Divine prophecy had never been wrong.

It was unfortunate that Fang Heng had escaped them once again.

“Lord Divine Adjudicator, can you sense Fang Heng's whereabouts?” Central Bishop Xilufei asked.

Okney gently shook his head.

Ever since Fang Heng had completed the mission Recreate Merk's Great Vision, the tracking mark the Holy Court had placed on Merk's notebook had disappeared.

They'd originally hoped to use that mark to locate him. No one had expected Fang Heng to complete the mission so quickly.

“We've just used Greater Prophecy to track him. We're certain he's still in Duncha, but he's currently in another protected plane—what they call the 'players' world,'” Okney explained. “As soon as he logs back in, we'll be able to sense him and find him.”

“Please tell us if there's anything we can do.”

…

Fang Heng slept straight through to the following afternoon.

After waking, he logged onto the website to check for responses.

His commission had attracted more than twenty expressions of interest.

That many?

Hopeful, he opened them one after another. By the time he'd finished the last message, however, he could only sigh.

Many of the respondents wanted to meet him face-to-face inside Duncha. Others did have items that could take him out of the game world, but those items came with significant negative side effects.

None of the options seemed suitable.

He would have to wait a little longer.

Fang Heng stretched, washed up, and entered his gaming cabin to check on things.

The moment he logged in, his brow furrowed.

He could immediately feel some force locking onto him.

A torrent of notifications appeared.

[Hint: By relocating the teleportation passage linked to the Yin Vein Rift, you have negatively affected the Holy Court, damaged Duncha, and killed some Holy Court members. During your offline period, you earned a combined 46,220,000 Undead Faction Reputation and 32,410,000 Undead Faction Honor Points. Exact totals cannot be calculated until the event ends; rewards are settled hourly.]

[Hint: Progress for the main mission, Rescue Qiboreya, has increased by 329%.]

[Warning: You are being tracked by a power originating from the Divine Realm. Your approximate location can now be determined and will be refreshed every 120 seconds.]

[Hint: Leaving this plane will interfere with the tracking effect and force the Divine Realm to spend additional time locating you again.]

[Hint: You have triggered a new mission—Escape Duncha.]

[Mission description: The entire Duncha game world has been placed under complete lockdown. Attempt to escape.]

[Objective: Leave the Duncha game world.]

[Reward: None.]

[Failure penalty: Deduction of 10,000 Main God Points.]

Trouble!

Fang Heng developed a headache just looking at the notifications.

His previous mission rewards hadn't even finished being calculated, and already the Divine Realm was hunting him down. What a miserable situation!

He scrolled through his game log and examined the scattered messages on his survival radio. Most were from Chu Yan.

Chu Yan had found a safe place to regroup in Duncha and was still searching for a way out. Meanwhile, the Merk Temple guarded by Ji Xiaobo had suffered increasingly frequent raids and reconnaissance missions by Holy Court player squads.

The temple probably wouldn't hold out much longer.

Fang Heng reviewed the messages, then remotely adjusted the marching route of his zombies in Qiboreya, clearing a path for Lord Danni's forces to attack the Holy Court's heartland.

Done!

Sensing the pursuers closing in on him, he shook his head.

There was no way to escape their tracking while staying in the game. He might as well log out again and hide.

Fang Heng closed his eyes once more.

Just wait a little longer. Sooner or later he'd find a way out of Duncha!

Back in the real world, he discovered a new pile of messages from Northern Federation's Levomiton University.

Folk had been trying to contact him at regular intervals. The poor man was suffering—Fang Heng was so difficult to get hold of!

The university's teaching and research team had prepared a mountain of problems that required Fang Heng's answers, only for him to disappear again.

Folk had even prepared a stack of Beast Taming textbooks specifically for him.

Reading the messages, Fang Heng thought Folk really knew how to make himself useful. He'd even obtained advanced Beast Taming books.

Since there was nothing else to do in the real world, he might as well go help. It would be a pleasant change of pace.

Whoosh!

The Shadow Demon Rat leapt onto his shoulder.

Fang Heng affectionately scratched its head. “Come on. Let's go learn something new.”

…

Two days later, Fang Heng was awakened from a deep sleep by his ringing phone.

He reached to his bedside and checked the caller ID.

Li Qingran.

“Fang Heng, are you okay? I heard you're trapped in Duncha.”

“Mm.”

He glanced at the darkness outside his window, still half-asleep. “What about you? How are things over there?”

“I've sent friends from the undead faction to help Ji Xiaobo defend the Merk Temple. The Holy Court hasn't managed to break in yet.”

But Li Qingran wasn't optimistic.

“I don't think it will last much longer. The Holy Court has realized that the Yin Vein Rift's relocation is linked to the Merk Temple. They've sent people to investigate, and their main army will probably arrive soon.”

“Mm. Do what you can.”

Fang Heng nodded.

If the Holy Court destroyed the Merk Temple, the Yin Vein Rift might meet one of two fates.

It could remain in Duncha permanently. Or it could be reverse-summoned back to Punoze.

Both were possible, and even Fang Heng, who had arranged the magic array, couldn't predict which would occur.

Naturally, the first outcome would benefit him and the undead faction more.

“There's also Lord Danni's situation. We missed our best opportunity to attack, and the Holy Court has brought in high-level reinforcements. As things stand, we probably can't drive them completely out of Qiboreya.”

Li Qingran paused before adding, “I hear this new group of Holy Court soldiers was also sent to deal with the Merk Temple.”

“Lord Danni has made the necessary preparations. She won't let them succeed easily.”

— End of Chapter 2067 —
