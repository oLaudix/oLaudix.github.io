Chapter 2111

Breakthrough

From original Chinese Chapter 2112 · 突破口

“The World of Myriad Forests...”

Fang Heng murmured the world's name once more.

It didn't seem quite like he'd imagined.

After downing a large mouthful of beer, Sandy belched and turned to him. “By the way, Fang Heng, where are we going next?”

NPCs couldn't receive game notifications, so Sandy had no idea what the quest objective was. He could only tag along with the players.

“First, we need to find the Tree Whisperer Alliance.”

“Of course, of course! Tree Whisperer Alliance, understood! Leave it to me! I'll show you what I'm capable of. You certainly won't regret bringing me here!”

Sandy rose with his drink raised and shouted, “Everyone! I have wonderful news! Today is our Young Master Mo's eighteenth birthday! All your expenses are on Young Master Mo! Oh, and we're new here. Does anyone know where we can find someone from the Tree Whisperer Alliance? Young Master Mo will richly reward anyone who helps!”

“Woohoo!”

“Happy birthday, Young Master Mo! Need someone to warm your bed?”

Cheers erupted throughout the bar as patrons raised their glasses in thanks.

Mo Jiawei rolled his eyes.

Young Master Mo had always kept a low profile.

When had he ever made such an outrageous display?

Klevi had rarely experienced this sort of attention. Terrified by all the eyes turning their way, she buried her face deep in her folded arms.

Before long, an attractive woman in a short-sleeved top approached the table.

She seemed very friendly. She brushed a hand across Sandy's cheek. “Hey there, friends. I'm Eyegobeni. I hear you're looking for the Tree Whisperer Alliance?”

Fang Heng raised his head. “You know them?”

“Of course. I could even take you to them. But...” Eyegobeni winked at Fang Heng. “First I need to make sure you're people I can trust.”

Fang Heng felt a stir of interest.

Sandy asked, “And how will you do that?”

Eyegobeni sat down at their table. “I know you're not from this town. Like so many outsiders, you've come looking for the Tree Whisperer Alliance and its so-called Water of Life that grants immortality. But the alliance doesn't appreciate being disturbed...”

Listening to her, Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

Finding the Tree Whisperer Alliance would probably involve some sort of test.

He hoped it wouldn't be too troublesome.

Thirty days was neither particularly long nor terribly short. He couldn't afford to waste them.

Clack!

While Eyegobeni was still talking, Sandy set his glass on the table. “I understand. Name your price! How much?”

“Haha! Not much. This many!”

Eyegobeni held up five fingers.

“No problem!”

“Wow! You're so handsome!”

Eyegobeni rewarded Sandy with a passionate kiss.

“Sweet words won't be enough, though. Remember, if I don't get to meet them, I promise you'll regret it.”

Fang Heng opened his mouth, then glanced at Mo Jiawei.

Mo Jiawei looked just as dumbfounded.

What the hell!

That was it?

You could clear a quest stage like this?

But how much did those five fingers represent?

Fang Heng suspected it wouldn't be that simple. He quietly asked, “Did you bring enough money?”

Mo Jiawei nodded. “I've got gold bars in my backpack. More than enough.”

Problems that could be solved with money usually weren't problems at all.

Mo Jiawei always kept gold bars and gemstones in his backpack for emergencies.

Under the bartender's suspicious, wary gaze, Mo Jiawei paid the bar's entire bill with gold bars and handed another five bars to Eyegobeni.

She immediately clung to him again, looking almost ready to swallow him whole.

Outside the bar, the group followed Eyegobeni into two separate taxis.

“Where are we going?”

“My house.”

“Your house?”

“Shh. I'll tell you a secret. My younger brother is a member of the Tree Whisperer Alliance, and he's at home right now. I'll take you to see him. Whether you get what you want out of him is up to you.”

Soon the taxis stopped in front of a villa.

Klevi stepped out and stood by the entrance, sensing her surroundings.

Gu Qingzhu stood beside her and asked quietly, “Klevi, what do you think?”

Klevi shook her head. “I can't sense any natural energy.”

“Let's go in and see.”

Fang Heng glanced at Mo Jiawei and followed Eyegobeni inside.

It was time to test Mo Jiawei's luck!

· · ·

Inside the villa,

Glenan stared suspiciously at the unexpected visitors, immediately adopting a hostile posture.

“How did you find me? Eyegobeni must have told you! That stupid sister of mine!”

Anger colored Glenan's voice as he glared at Fang Heng's party. “Leave! I don't know anything about a Tree Whisperer Alliance! You've been tricked. Go demand your money back from her!”

“There's no need to get upset, young man. We're seekers of nature, and we have important business with the Tree Whisperer Alliance.”

Seeing Glenan's agitation, Sandy stepped forward and waved a calming hand, launching into his usual persuasive nonsense. “The God of Nature guided us here.”

“Hah! God of Nature? Do you seriously expect me to believe that? Don't think I don't know what you're after. It's the Water of Life, isn't it? Forget it! I've never seen the stuff, and I suggest you stop dreaming. The Water of Life is nothing but a legend! It doesn't exist. I've said everything I have to say. Now get out!”

Seeing his attempt fail, Sandy spread his hands helplessly. “Believer in nature, you're far too suspicious. Very well. Then witness a divine miracle with your own eyes.”

He nodded toward Fang Heng.

Fang Heng understood immediately. He extended his hand toward Glenan and slowly opened his palm.

Mental energy gathered at its center.

“Rustle... rustle...”

What?

To Glenan's astonishment, a faint green glow began gathering in Fang Heng's palm.

The green light slowly stirred.

A tiny sapling pushed up from the center of his hand!

A sapling!

The power of life!

How was that possible?!

Shocked, Glenan stared at Fang Heng and shouted, “How did you do that?! The Tree Spirit race? Are you a Tree Spirit?! I actually got to see a real Tree Spirit!”

“Which branch are you from? The Root Clan? The Leaf Clan? The Soul Clan?”

“None of them.”

Fang Heng withdrew his hand and shook his head. “We are followers of the God of Nature. We came here to fulfill a task entrusted to us by the God of Nature. Now, may we have a proper conversation?”

As he spoke, Fang Heng exchanged a glance with Klevi.

Something was wrong!

There was something wrong with this world!

He had noticed it while using his natural magic.

In an ordinary world, with his high-tier natural magic and a talent rating in the tens of thousands, growing a sapling in his palm should have been effortless.

But just now, even that tiny act had been extraordinarily difficult.

He hadn't even managed to make the sapling grow properly.

Why was that?

Meanwhile, Fang Heng's display had thoroughly overwhelmed Glenan. He nodded repeatedly. “Y-yes, of course! Please follow me upstairs!”

— End of Chapter 2111 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Water of Life. 生命之水 is rendered “Water of Life”; its alleged immortality is a rumor the characters discuss, not an established ability.
  • Names. 埃耶戈贝尼 and 格伦南 are provisionally rendered Eyegobeni and Glenan.
  • Payment. Eyegobeni indicates five with her fingers; Mo Jiawei ends up handing her five gold bars.

Chinese Expressions

  • 暖床 (nuǎn chuáng). Literally “warm the bed”; someone jokingly asks Young Master Mo if he needs a bed companion after the lavish birthday announcement.
