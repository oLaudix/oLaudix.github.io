Chapter 2128

The Hole

From original Chinese Chapter 2129 · 坑洞

By now, a large group of zombie clones had assembled on the institute's fourth underground floor.

For security, the floor was partly constructed of metal, with heavily reinforced concrete underneath.

After more than an hour of continuous digging, Fang Heng finally heard a dull thump.

He looked back. His zombies had at last dug an actual hole through the floor.

Done!

Fang Heng walked over to inspect it.

The opening led straight into the sewer below.

That was only the first step. Once they entered the sewers, they'd still need to break through two underground passages to reach the outside network.

The good news was that the Federation troops and Wanling Technology's guards still hadn't managed to penetrate the institute's lower floors. They were only on the first floor, slowly clearing the Lickers blocking their route.

Fang Heng made a rough calculation.

At this rate, the Federation would need another three hours to reach the third underground floor.

And that was assuming he left everything running on autopilot.

“No rush. Take your time...”

Muttering softly, Fang Heng issued the next set of orders to his zombie clones.

The zombies carried the dead Divine Tree offshoots, wrapped in white cloth, slowly around the corner of the corridor. One by one, they lowered the offshoots through the hole and into the sewer below.

Several fused Tyrant forms were waiting underneath to receive them. They hoisted the dead offshoots onto their shoulders.

Then they carried them deeper into the sewer system!

Once all twelve dead Divine Tree offshoots had been moved, Fang Heng leaped down into the sewer.

According to the map, he needed to proceed another five hundred meters.

Then he'd have to break through the wall on the right and dig a little more than ten meters to connect with the external sewer network.

After that, they'd be able to escape completely!

“The sewers are as useful as ever.”

Fang Heng sighed appreciatively to himself and directed the zombie clones to begin preparing the next excavation.

He was just about to leave them to work when his eyebrows suddenly rose.

Huh?

What was going on?

Why had several Licker death notifications suddenly appeared over the past few minutes?

They were dying several times faster than before.

That was clearly abnormal!

Fang Heng immediately opened his game log and scanned the entries one by one.

He quickly found the anomaly.

[Game Hint: Your zombie clone (Licker form) has been affected by an unknown injection.]

[Warning: The effects of your zombie clone's 'Undying Body' have been reduced by 90%. Remaining recovery time: 30 minutes.]

Huh?

What was this?

The Undying Body ability inherited by his Lickers had actually been disabled?!

Because of some special compound drug?

Fang Heng's heart sank.

Undying Body was his greatest trump card.

It was precisely because of that skill that his zombie horde could display such terrifying strength.

And now its effect had been neutralized?

Wanling Technology?

Or the Federation?

They had the capability to develop targeted technology in such a short time?

That was far too strange.

It wasn't as though Fang Heng had never visited high-tier game worlds. Even Vaz hadn't pulled anything like this!

He frowned deeply.

He had a vague feeling that there was more to this drug than met the eye.

Whatever the case, Wanling Technology's drug had now suppressed most of his Undying Body effect, and the zombies blocking the entrances outside were dying faster and faster...

He needed to open the escape route first!

Fang Heng looked away from the notifications, stepped up to the rock wall, and drove his fist into it!

Boom!

A thunderous impact.

The entire wall shook violently for several seconds!

Crack! Crack, crack...

Dense fractures spread across its surface!

The fused Tyrant forms following him strode forward and began hammering the wall with their fists as well!

This wasn't good!

Fang Heng compared their excavation speed with the Lickers' mounting losses.

At this rate, time was going to be tight.

He might not be able to escort all the Divine Tree offshoots out intact.

Looks like he'd have to go back and keep the enemy occupied a little longer!

And while he was at it, he could investigate that strange drug.

Having made up his mind, Fang Heng left his zombie clones digging and headed back toward the passage alone.

· · ·

First floor of Wanling Technology's Northern District research institute.

The mechanized soldiers had already been equipped with the specialized drug provided by Wanling Technology.

After successfully injecting the Lickers with dart guns, the creatures' regeneration and ability to restore severed limbs both dropped dramatically!

Even so, the Lickers' basic vitality remained terrifying.

Standing behind his troops, Boris watched coldly as the mechanized soldiers gradually took control, clearing away the Lickers and pushing deeper into the institute.

Wanling Technology had done well.

To develop a countermeasure so quickly.

Hm?

Suddenly, Boris raised an eyebrow.

He looked up to see a figure appear at the entrance to the passage.

“It's him!” A soldier who had undergone psychic modification recognized Fang Heng and shouted, “That's the man who invaded the institute!”

Almost everyone immediately turned toward Fang Heng at the far corridor entrance.

“Invader.” Boris fixed his gaze on Fang Heng. “Who sent you?”

Fang Heng simply stood at the entrance, staring coldly at the troops.

“Hmph!”

Boris snorted heavily and waved a hand. “Go! Take him down!”

The semi-mechanized soldiers raised their beam weapons and advanced toward Fang Heng in formation.

What?

Fang Heng slowly raised one hand.

A book emitting dark-golden light floated in front of him.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Dense streams of dark-golden radiance spilled from its pages and shot toward the semi-mechanized soldiers!

Boom! Boom, boom, boom!

In an instant, golden streaks struck the soldiers, setting off a succession of violent explosions!

The Book of Oaths.

The second-stage explosions produced by the Sacred Remnant sent the semi-mechanized troops tumbling in all directions.

The Lickers on the other side seized the confusion to dash down another corridor branching off the lobby.

Boris glared at Fang Heng, veins bulging at his temples.

“Stop him!”

Boris shouted and formed a hand seal in front of his chest.

Psychic Pierce!

Fang Heng immediately felt a stabbing sensation invade his sea of consciousness.

[Game Hint: You have suffered a Psychic Pierce attack from an unknown target. Your Mental Strength has passed the check, granting additional damage reduction. You have suffered 3,000 Psychic Pierce damage.]

The needle-like pain lasted only an instant before disappearing.

Undying Body restored his lost health immediately.

It had no effect whatsoever!

— End of Chapter 2128 —

  • Escape route. Fang Heng uses the institute’s sewer system to transport the offshoots out of the building.
