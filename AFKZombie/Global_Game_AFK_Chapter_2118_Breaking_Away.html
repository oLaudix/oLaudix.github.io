Chapter 2118

Breaking Away

From original Chinese Chapter 2119 · 脱离

Fang Heng immediately flipped his wrist, and an alloy metal staff appeared in his hand.

“Whoosh!”

Enduring the stabbing pain in his head, he swung the long metal staff forward with one hand!

Bang!

The weapon struck empty space with a heavy, muffled impact.

A web of cracks appeared across the invisible barrier where it had been hit!

Fang Heng breathed a little easier.

Fortunately, these enemies weren't especially strong.

He could break through with brute force!

“Again!”

Fighting spirit flashed in Fang Heng's eyes as he swung the staff forward with all his strength once more.

Bang!

Another heavy impact rang out.

The invisible mental-force barrier shattered before him!

Three Wanling Technology guards spat blood simultaneously, and the diamond-shaped gemstone patterns in the centers of their suits shattered!

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

When he'd first seen their uniforms, he'd thought those gemstones were ordinary decorative patterns. Unexpectedly, they were actually thin crystal layers.

The suits seemed connected to the guards' unusual psychic power.

Ji Haiting was already preparing to leave, but the commotion made him turn around. Seeing Fang Heng smash the mental barrier, he stared in amazement.

What was going on?

What sort of man was this?

How much monstrous physical strength did it take to smash a wall of mental force directly?

The three guards who had formed the barrier suffered a backlash and fainted.

Fang Heng weighed the metal staff in his hand, gathered the unconscious Klevi into one arm, and strode toward Ji Haiting.

“Stop him!” the guards shouted.

Meanwhile, Gu Qingzhu leapt down from the cliff and joined the fight.

“Careful! Enemy attack!”

The armed guards immediately opened fire!

Gunshots erupted throughout the cavern. The researchers ducked and scrambled for cover, shielding their heads from the chaos.

The remaining Wanling Technology guards repeatedly attacked Fang Heng with ranged mental-piercing strikes!

But it was useless!

They watched him approach step by step as though he were a monster!

It was almost as if he were completely immune to mental attacks!

Fang Heng stopped before Ji Haiting, tilted his head, and said, “Director Ji, it seems you can't keep me here.”

Admiration flashed in Ji Haiting's eyes. He chuckled. “Fang Heng, to be frank, I'm even more impressed by you now. What a pity that our paths differ. Unfortunately, you've chosen to become our enemy. I look forward to our next meeting. Farewell.”

As he spoke, Ji Haiting's figure slowly began fading before everyone's eyes.

Huh?!

Spatial transition!

Seeing blue points of light flicker around Ji Haiting, Fang Heng's pupils contracted as a realization struck him.

The power of the extraterrestrial dependent race!

The next moment, transition lights appeared around the guards accompanying Ji Haiting.

[Game Hint: Your quest—Invitation to Cooperate—has failed. Current quest—Glenan's Request—completion has increased to 120%.]

[Game Hint: Your favorability with the Tree Whisperer Alliance has increased. Your favorability with Wanling Technology Corporation has decreased significantly.]

[Game Hint: You have automatically joined the Tree Whisperer Alliance faction and entered a hostile relationship with Wanling Technology Corporation. Please exercise caution.]

“Stop them!”

Fang Heng roared and swung his metal staff toward Ji Haiting!

Bang!

The staff struck the spatial transition lights surrounding Ji Haiting!

The entire transition barrier shuddered violently, and the teleportation stalled for half a second.

Damn it! He couldn't break the teleportation!

Fang Heng's heart sank.

By the time anyone could try to stop the escape, it was already too late.

Every Wanling Technology employee in the cavern, including the researchers, vanished through spatial transition one after another. Even the scientists Gu Qingzhu had knocked unconscious with a punch were teleported away.

Within moments, Fang Heng's group was alone in the ruins.

[Game Hint: You have driven back Wanling Technology Corporation and disrupted its research. You have gained 2,000 Tree Whisperer Alliance favorability and 1,000 faction contribution points.]

The others quickly gathered around Fang Heng.

Mo Jiawei approached. “Fang Heng, we can't stay here long. Wanling Technology will be back soon. Psychic powers are usually encountered only in high-tier game worlds. They're relatively advanced abilities and aren't easy to handle.”

“Mm.”

“How is Klevi?”

As they spoke, Klevi began recovering consciousness.

Still groggy, she looked around in confusion. “Brother Fang Heng, I... I fainted just now...”

Fang Heng nodded gently and asked, “Did you discover anything when you examined the Divine Tree's offshoot?”

“Mm.”

Klevi soon remembered what had happened before she passed out. Her expression turned grave. “I used my natural abilities to examine it and found remnants of a special natural-magic array in the center of the offshoot. That array could help the branch perform teleportation and energy transmission.”

Teleportation?!

Fang Heng frowned deeply at the revelation.

Did that mean the Tree Spirits used Divine Tree offshoots to travel across this world?

Klevi shook her head and sighed. “Unfortunately, it hasn't received energy for a very long time. Once its power was exhausted, the offshoot lost its vitality completely and died.”

“Can we restore it somehow?”

“It's been dead too long. I can't revive it with my abilities. But some traces of the teleportation array remain inside the wood. I could try to reconstruct part of it.”

As she spoke, game notifications appeared on Fang Heng's retina.

[Game Hint: You have triggered a main-story branch quest—Remnants of an Unknown Magic Array.]

Quest Name: Remnants of an Unknown Magic Array.

Difficulty: S.

Quest Description: While exploring the Tree Spirit ruins, you found a dead Divine Tree offshoot. Klevi has discovered an unknown magic array within it. If you can reconstruct the array, you may uncover clues relating to the Tree Spirit race.

Quest Requirement: Repair the unknown magic array.

Quest Reward: Major increase in main-story quest progress.

A major increase?

Excellent!

Fang Heng studied the new quest, his eyes lighting up. He nodded to Klevi. “Klevi, try repairing the array.”

“Mm. I'll try.”

Klevi returned to the dead tree, placed a hand on its trunk, and closed her eyes to concentrate.

A moment later, she opened them again.

“How did it go?”

“I can't.” Klevi shook her head and sighed in disappointment. “The Divine Tree offshoot has been dead for too long. Much of the array inside it has worn away over time and can no longer be recognized. It's impossible to restore it in this condition.”

— End of Chapter 2118 —
