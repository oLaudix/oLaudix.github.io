Chapter 3033

Departure

From Chinese Chapter 3034 · 脱离

But putting himself in Fang Heng's shoes, Dean had to admit that even if their abilities were exchanged, he certainly couldn't do what Fang Heng had done. He wouldn't even have the courage to come to Hell and pick a fight with Envy.

The envy in Dean's eyes turned into admiration. He nodded and praised him. “Grand Sage Fang Bai has done another great service for the Holy Court and its believers. By the way, you saw those plants in the lower levels of Hell, didn't you? Do you know what they are?”

“I'm not sure. They absorbed some of the Demon Seeds' power during the fighting. Perhaps they underwent a mutation.”

Unwilling to explain further, Fang Heng looked around and said, “Grand Sage Dean, now that Eseklyna is dead, our mission in Hell is complete. Staying here consumes a great deal of power. Let's return to the Eternal City first.”

“Mm, very well.”

That was exactly what Dean wanted. Hearing Fang Heng suggest it, he immediately waved for the Holy Court troops to withdraw.

Their expedition into Hell was over, and the army began the return journey.

Fang Heng and Dean followed near the rear of the column, remaining alert for pursuers from the depths of Hell.

“Fang Bai, initially condensing the Will of the Twelve Sages is only the beginning of advancing to the rank of Twelve Sages. For the next step, we'll need to return to the sacred temple in the Divine Realm.”

Assuming Fang Heng had entered Hell to advance along the Twelve Sages path, Dean naturally offered suggestions and quietly reminded him, “I once read records saying that advancing to the Twelve Sages requires the assistance of the other Grand Sages and the God King. The Holy Court is much weaker now than it used to be, so I fear it won't be so simple.”

“Besides, the God King has entered seclusion again, and no one knows when he'll emerge.”

Fang Heng wasn't eager to rush off and advance his sacred power, but the mention of the God King caught his attention. “Has something happened to the God King? Why has he entered seclusion again? The Ouroboros Seed has already been cleared away. He should have recovered.”

“Mm, you haven't returned to the Divine Realm, so it's understandable you don't know the details,” Dean explained quietly. “The God King's current seclusion actually has nothing to do with the Ouroboros Seed.”

“Then what is it?”

“The God-Bestowed Jewel has developed an abnormality.”

Dean sighed again as he said it.

Their Divine Realm had truly been struck with terrible luck lately.

One disaster after another, with no sign of stopping.

He could only hope that having Fang Bai, this new Grand Sage, strengthen the Holy Court would bring some good fortune and turn their luck around.

“We don't know exactly what caused the God-Bestowed Jewel to change. We suspect its outer protective mesh was damaged while it was being used against the Ouroboros Seed.”

“The loss of that mesh wasn't a major problem in the short term. But as time passed, the sacred energy fluctuations released by the jewel became increasingly powerful and gradually began destabilizing the Divine Realm.”

“As more time went by without the outer protective mesh, even moving the God-Bestowed Jewel became extremely difficult.”

Dean shook his head and sighed again. “The God King has entered seclusion to consume the surplus energy released by the jewel. At the same time, he hopes to use its power to strengthen himself and break through the final barrier.”

Fang Heng nodded.

After all that explanation, the God King was cultivating with the help of the jewel, right?

Fang Heng understood how dangerous that was.

Take the Death Realm Jewel, for example.

After advancing Necromancy to the Super-Divine level, he'd also tried remaining in front of the Death Realm Jewel and using its power to improve himself.

But it had been extremely dangerous.

And the cultivation speed hadn't been particularly fast, even with the protective layer surrounding the jewel.

Without its protective layer, the Divine Realm Jewel would be harder to cultivate beside.

However...

An odd thought suddenly occurred to Fang Heng.

The God King of the Divine Realm.

What level was his strength?

Divine level?

Or Super-Divine level?

Come to think of it, given the God King's age, if he'd cultivated at the Divine level for tens of thousands of years, he might not be much weaker than Fang Heng, who had only just reached the Super-Divine level.

If the two of them actually fought, who would win?

“Fang Bai, what are you thinking about?”

“Hmm...”

Fang Heng looked up. “Nothing. I was just curious where the God-Bestowed Jewel originally came from. Perhaps if we traced its origins, we'd find a way to control it.”

“I really don't know. The jewel has always been in the Divine Realm. Only the God King can use it, and it's always been kept on the attic's highest floor. I suppose it might be part of the Divine Realm itself.”

Dean paused before continuing, “With the God King in seclusion, advancing to the Twelve Sages could be rather troublesome. But don't worry. Once we return to the Divine Realm, all the Grand Sages will discuss it together and find a way to help you break through. Besides, although you've advanced to Grand Sage, the proper ceremony hasn't yet been held. We can use this opportunity to hold the ceremony...”

The Holy Court's reputation among humans was declining. Their great victory in Hell had lifted morale, and if they could publicly introduce a powerful new Grand Sage as well, it would be a perfect chance to restore their standing.

“No, Grand Sage Dean. I still have things to handle, so I won't return to the Holy Court just yet.”

“Hmm?”

Dean looked at Fang Heng in bewilderment.

Strange. Hadn't the brat rushed here precisely to advance to the Twelve Sages?

Why was he suddenly in no hurry?

Fang Heng smiled faintly.

After all, they wouldn't find a way to advance to the Twelve Sages any time soon even if he returned to the Holy Court. Meanwhile, Klevi might be in danger and needed his help immediately.

He wouldn't waste time.

“Just a few odds and ends. I'll be quick. Once I've finished dealing with them, I'll return to the Divine Realm at once.”

“Very well.”

Dean thought the new Grand Sage certainly had a lot on his plate. There was no talking him out of it, so he simply nodded. “Then I'll return to the Divine Realm first and discuss things with the Grand Sages. We'll try to find a path for your advancement.”

“Thank you, Grand Sage Dean.”

Fang Heng cupped his hands in thanks.

From his experience in Necromancy, Fang Heng knew that a Super-Divine level existed above the Divine level.

If he successfully advanced to the Twelve Sages, it should correspond to the Lesser-Divine or Divine level of Sacred Studies.

But advancing still further...

Could it be...

Did he have to become the God King?

One thing was certain: there was only one slot at the Super-Divine level.

In other words, if the God King was already Super-Divine, then as long as the God King remained, Fang Heng would never be able to advance his Sacred Studies to the final level.
