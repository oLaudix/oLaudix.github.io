Chapter 2230

Burial

From original Chinese Chapter 2231 · 埋葬

Huh? Where did he go?

The Wood Clan guards couldn't find Fang Heng after the explosion. They looked at one another in confusion.

They'd clearly seen the intruder engulfed by the blast. How had he vanished so suddenly?

Had he used some special method to escape?

The guards carefully scanned their surroundings. Finding no trace of him, they reluctantly spread out and concealed themselves in the surrounding forest again.

By then, Fang Heng had already entered a secondary-space projection and withdrawn to the edge of the forest.

The Tree Spirits' concealment techniques had improved tremendously.

Even a vampire's blood-sensing ability couldn't locate them!

What a nuisance!

Fang Heng silently cursed the difficulty.

After careful observation, he estimated that more than a dozen Wood Clan members were hiding in the forest ahead.

And those were only the ones he could detect.

“Dr. Fang, please be careful.”

Federation command had witnessed the ambush through the miniature camera he was carrying. Alarmed that something might have happened to him, they immediately urged caution.

“Our combat teams are on their way. Estimated arrival in fifteen minutes.”

“Okay.”

Fang Heng nodded, withdrew beyond the Tree Spirits' detection range, and found a safe place to conceal his aura. Then he pulled a demon mask out of his backpack.

Sit around waiting for the Federation?

He wasn't patient enough for that!

Why not try taking the initiative?

With that thought, Fang Heng put on the demon mask.

The face he had modeled with his divine artifact seemed to melt away like wax.

His passive talent activated!

Magic arrays swirled and formed around him.

Swish! Swish, swish!

Lickers burst from the arrays and charged straight into the forest!

“What are those things?!”

The concealed Wood Clan guards had never encountered creatures like Lickers before!

And these weren't ordinary Lickers. Their bodies were coated with a dark metallic sheen.

Primordial Devourer-fused, modified Lickers!

“Stop them!”

As the black Lickers lunged toward their positions, the Wood Clan guards called out, concentrated their power, and unleashed wooden-spike spells in response!

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

The incoming spikes caused a dense chain of explosions!

A large number of the leading Lickers were hurled backward by the blasts.

Fine cracks appeared in the black keratin armor coating their bodies. Dark fluid immediately seeped into the fractures and rapidly repaired the outer shell.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Even before the blasted Lickers hit the ground, they used double jumps in midair, twisted into an unnatural burst of acceleration, and pounced toward the Tree Spirits again.

So troublesome!

The Wood Clan guards frowned, their expressions turning grim.

What kind of monsters were these?

The Lickers' astonishing defenses were beginning to make the Tree Spirits feel threatened.

Deeper in the primeval forest, other Wood Clan members noticed the disturbance at the outer perimeter and hurried over to reinforce the guards, casting nature spells from behind to block the attackers.

Huge wooden spikes repeatedly formed and rained down on the mass of Lickers, attempting to keep them outside the defensive line.

The explosions kept knocking the Lickers back.

But every time the nature spells paused, the Lickers seized the opening and charged again!

Wave after wave of attacks left the Wood Clan guards struggling to keep up.

“Bury them!”

The Wood Clan squad leader realized ordinary measures weren't working and abruptly changed tactics. “Wooden-Spike Burial!”

“Understood!”

The guards formed special seals in front of their bodies.

CRACK! CRACK, CRACK!

Enormous wooden spikes suddenly thrust upward from the soil!

The Lickers were crawling low to the ground when the spikes erupted beneath them, lifting them bodily into the air!

The sharp wooden points pierced straight through them, pinning them to the massive spikes.

Trapped and suspended, the Lickers thrashed frantically in an effort to free themselves.

Hiss! Hiss!

More slender spikes and vines grew rapidly from the wooden stakes, wrapping around the impaled Lickers in layer after layer.

The ground slowly subsided.

Dragging their captives along, the wooden spikes began to sink beneath the surface.

The Lickers seemed to sense the danger and struggled madly.

But it was useless.

The spikes and vines held them fast until both the creatures and their wooden prisons disappeared beneath the earth.

[Hint: Your Lickers have been affected by the special skill Wooden-Spike Burial and have died instantly.]

Elsewhere, Fang Heng slowly emerged among the bushes with a frown.

Another high-tier skill with a special kill judgment.

If successfully executed, it could kill a target outright regardless of remaining HP.

The Tree Spirits really were difficult opponents.

Fang Heng gave a quiet snort.

A high-tier game world.

It certainly wasn't easy!

Still, he'd grown stronger too.

Over the past five days, continuous Psychic Studies training had raised his skill to the maximum level of the intermediate tier. His Spirit and Will attributes had increased substantially again.

Advancing further to Advanced Psychic Studies would require a special advancement quest.

He still didn't know how to trigger it.

In any case, regardless of the various techniques Psychic Studies offered, his increased Spirit attribute had greatly improved his ability to control zombie clones.

“Then let's have some fun!”

Fang Heng focused his mind and began remotely directing the Lickers!

Swish! Swish, swish!

The tightly packed Lickers immediately scattered through the forest, spreading out to attack the Wood Clan from every angle!

The guards' Wooden-Spike Burial could instantly kill any Licker it struck. But to use the skill, a guard had to focus, lock onto a single target, and maintain the spell. It wasn't so simple in practice.

That might work against a handful of Lickers. Against a large swarm, the Wood Clan would struggle.

A war of numbers!

Fang Heng recognized their weakness and sent the Lickers around the defenders from every direction.

“Wooden Swamp!”

The guards had another countermeasure. They formed different seals in front of them and shouted together.

Hiss!

The soil began to writhe, transforming a large area ahead into a treacherous swamp intended to slow the approaching Lickers.

Wooden spikes also kept erupting from within the mire!

“Heh…”

Fang Heng chuckled and focused again.

Secondary-space projection!

Swish! Swish, swish!

Points of pale spatial light materialized around the Lickers already leaping through the air!

The swarm swiftly entered the secondary-space projection.

What?!

Where were the Lickers?

They'd vanished?

The Wood Clan guards froze as the creatures disappeared amid glittering spatial ripples.
