Chapter 2058

Control

From original Chinese Chapter 2059 · 控制

The instant their eyes met, Fang Heng felt a profound loneliness emanating from the spirit's soul.

“It can't be done,” the evil spirit said.

Its thoughts seemed sluggish. After a long pause, it added, “He cannot control his power.”

As it spoke, the spirit's figure began fading away again.

BANG!

The dwarf struck the bars of his cell with a loud clang.

“Hey! Evil spirit! Don't leave! At least try! Damn it! Come back here! I'll give you two Soul Stones if you come back!”

The spirit, which had almost disappeared, slowly materialized again. Its hollow eyes turned toward the dwarf.

“Soul Stones…”

“Damn it,” the dwarf muttered.

He glanced at Fang Heng.

“You'll owe me a favor for this, kid. If we get out, you'd better repay it!”

Reluctantly, he fished two rune-like stones from his pocket and threw them toward the spirit's cell.

Pop!

The moment the Soul Stones approached the evil spirit, they sank into its misty form like pebbles dropping into water and disappeared.

“Soul Stones…”

The spirit murmured again, a faint expression of contentment appearing on its pale face.

“Come on! You've taken the payment! Get to work!” the dwarf urged.

“The Demon Seed's power belongs to you,” the spirit said slowly. Its figure had become slightly more substantial after absorbing the stones. It stared at Fang Heng, apparently perplexed.

“Why are you unable to control it? You are very strange…”

Fang Heng frowned.

So the evil spirit thought I should be able to control the Demon Seed?

The dwarf was growing impatient.

“Forget about why! We don't have time! Think of a way to get us out of this damned place!”

The spirit looked at Fang Heng and paused for several seconds.

“I can't think of one.”

Can't think of one?!

The dwarf nearly exploded with rage. He banged on the bars repeatedly.

“Damn it! You said you knew everything! Now you tell us you can't do it? What use are you? Give me my Soul Stones back!”

The evil spirit slowly turned toward him, considered the demand, and shook its head.

“No refunds.”

“DAMN IT!”

Furious, the dwarf rattled the bars and shouted a string of insults in what sounded like the dwarf language. They were clearly not polite.

Fang Heng ignored him and asked the spirit,

“Do you know any way to stop the Demon Seed inside me from running out of control? Right now. As quickly as possible.”

The spirit opened its mouth, but Fang Heng interrupted before it could answer.

“A temporary solution is fine.”

The spirit turned its head toward the opposite side of the room.

“You could try consuming its energy with a conflicting power. Temporarily release and expend the Demon Seed's strength. Sacred energy, for example.”

“Consume it?”

Fang Heng followed the spirit's gaze to the flower bed in the center of the hall.

Use sacred power to expend the Demon Seed's energy? Through those crystals?

That actually made some sense.

The original problem was that the Demon Seed's power was far too great for him to control, so it kept leaking from his body.

If he used opposing sacred energy to consume some of that power, he could weaken the Demon Seed's core strength enough to stabilize it temporarily.

It would probably only address the symptoms, not the cause, but that was better than waiting to die inside a cell.

Fang Heng understood his position all too well.

He was concealing secrets about both the Demon Seed and his Vampire bloodline. Even the death of Saintess Viona was connected to him.

The Holy Court would never simply let him go. It had probably confined him here while waiting for instructions from its higher authorities.

His time was running out. He needed to act quickly.

“Wait! What the hell are you talking about?” the dwarf protested. “Consume his energy? If he uses it all up, how are we supposed to escape? Are you making fun of me?”

He'd gained nothing and lost two Soul Stones into the bargain.

“Hey, you! Don't pretend you can't hear me! Pay back my two Soul Stones!”

Fang Heng gave the noisy dwarf a cold glance, then surveyed his cramped cell.

It was only about twenty square meters.

To escape…

He approached the barred door and gripped the metal bars with both hands.

Bzz! Bzz!

Brilliant sacred energy instantly surged through them, scattering dark-gold sparks against his palms.

[Notice: You are taking countermeasure damage: 1,000 points. This damage ignores defense.]

The notice reappeared every half second.

The damage wasn't particularly high. His Undying Body could restore it quickly.

Frowning against the searing pain in his palms, Fang Heng tried to bend the bars apart by brute force.

[Notice: Due to your special shackles, Strength is currently suppressed to 65.]

The sizzling noise attracted the other prisoners' attention.

The old man shook his head.

“Kid, give it up! That won't work. Those shackles on your wrists are linked to the entire hall's defenses. If you could just pull the bars apart, do you think the rest of us would've been stuck here for so many years?”

Fang Heng paused, then released his grip.

So it was connected to the shackles.

With his Strength capped at 65, he couldn't force the cell door open.

What if I could get rid of the shackles first?

He looked down at his wrists.

The restraints worked together with the external magic array, suppressing all his attributes and preventing him from using inventory items or skills.

As long as the suppression remained, brute force wouldn't break them either.

Right! That thing!

An idea came to him. He raised his shackled hands before his chest.

Switch forms! Sacred Studies form!

Whoosh!

A dark-golden book materialized in front of Fang Heng.

The Book of Oaths!

Sacred energy flowed from its pages.

The prisoners in the surrounding cells stared in astonishment.

An artifact! That was actually a divine artifact!

The Demon-Sealing Cavern and its shackles could seal the use of ordinary items and equipment carried by prisoners.

But divine artifacts were an exception!

What shocked the prisoners even more was that the Book of Oaths floating in front of Fang Heng continually released sacred energy.

What?! The Book of Oaths? Wasn't that a Holy Court ability?

In fact, the cavern possessed a third layer of defenses. But because Fang Heng's Book of Oaths carried sacred-aligned power, that extra defense—and its alarm—hadn't activated at all.

— End of Chapter 2058 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Soul Stones. 魂石 is translated Soul Stones. The dwarf pays two to persuade the spirit to keep speaking.
  • Divine artifact exception. The prison blocks ordinary items and skills but not divine artifacts. Fang Heng can summon his sacred Book of Oaths without triggering a further alarm because its energy is holy-aligned.
