Chapter 2151

Defensive Field

From original Chinese Chapter 2152 · 防御场

Inside the research institute, everyone watched helplessly as the external fortifications were destroyed one after another and the outer force field's energy level plummeted.

Yet they could do nothing!

In less than ten minutes, the outer defense field was shattered by the combined assault of the Lickers and Tree Spirits!

Boom!

The instant the field collapsed, great numbers of Lickers poured into the institute grounds and charged directly toward the main building.

Alarms blared throughout the complex!

“There is no need to panic, everyone.”

As the external defenses fell completely, the institute director actually regained his composure. “We've activated the internal defense mode in advance. The institute is still safe. They won't be able to get inside.”

“The Tree Spirits want Niam Alloy. They won't casually use powerful explosive weapons. We can hold them off simply by sealing every entrance and exit and blocking all routes the Lickers might use. The interference with the Spirit Domain transition network is already being eliminated. In less than forty-five minutes, the transition passages will be fully restored.”

“Understood!”

Everyone gradually calmed down and began redeploying personnel to defend the institute.

As the director had said, the institute had been designed with contingency plans for external invasions.

As long as they held the entrances, they could resist enemies a hundred times their number for at least three hours.

In the institute's emergency plans, those three hours were intended to give the staff enough time to destroy their research data.

Through the monitors, they could see the first wave of Lickers attempting to enter through various passages being stopped by beam turrets and Federation semi-mechanized troops. The offensive was temporarily contained.

Everyone breathed a little easier and proceeded with the defensive plan.

Suddenly, a researcher monitoring an instrument noticed something. His expression changed slightly, and he immediately switched the display.

On taking a closer look, his eyes widened in disbelief. Cold sweat drenched his back.

“Director.”

“What is it?”

“P-please, come and look at this.”

The technician swallowed nervously and enlarged the image on the monitor.

What the hell?!

The director frowned at the image.

Looking closely, he saw the floor covered in a dense carpet of wriggling worms.

The creatures were no bigger than fingernails, but there were vast swathes of them!

Damn it!

They'd somehow already entered the research institute!

“Worms?!”

The sight of so many squirming creatures made everyone in the command room feel their scalps go numb.

The director's face changed drastically as a thought struck him. “Quick! Check the ventilation ducts!”

The display switched.

Inside ventilation ducts throughout the institute, immense numbers of worms were slowly climbing upward, entering through the outside vents and crawling along the passageways!

The technician's forehead was covered in sweat as he cycled through the surveillance feeds.

Closer inspection revealed that the entire ventilation system was already infested with worms!

Dead silence fell over the command room.

The institute's defenses had been designed to repel humans and Tree Spirits. No one had anticipated an invasion by worm creatures!

Which meant the worms were about to reach every corner of the institute through its ventilation system!

“Quickly!” the director shouted. “Everyone get moving! Seal every ventilation-duct entrance and exit! Guard any that can't be sealed! Use flamethrowers! Don't let those worms into the institute's secure areas!”

“Understood!”

The central command room descended into chaos once again.

Nearby, an assistant approached Ji Haiting and asked quietly, “Chairman, it's dangerous here. Should we evacuate?”

“Mm.”

Ji Haiting watched the screen with admiration glimmering in his eyes. He murmured, “Fang Heng, you've opened my eyes again today...”

Then he turned toward his assistant and nodded. “Let's go. This place is no longer safe. We'll withdraw first.”

“Understood. I'll arrange the emergency spatial-transition device immediately.”

· · ·

Meanwhile, outside, the Federation's semi-mechanized soldiers had retreated into the institute before the force field collapsed. As the first Lickers rushed toward the building, the second wave of worms also slipped through gaps all around the exterior.

The heavy war machines and spiderlike constructs outside hadn't yet been completely eliminated. Amid the confusion, Senzo and the other Tree Spirit elders breached the remaining blockade and entered the institute grounds first.

Their people immediately seized control of the main entrances.

A Tree Spirit reported, “Great Elder, we've secured the entrances and exits.”

“Mm.”

Senzo nodded and surveyed the interior.

Although the outer defenses were nearly destroyed, Wanling Technology and the Federation still controlled the building itself.

Semi-mechanized soldiers and psychic-enhanced guards held the entrances to the internal passageways.

At the left-hand passage ahead, more than twenty armored mechanical soldiers could be seen blocking the route and continuously suppressing the Lickers within it using beam weapons.

The institute hadn't surrendered. The battle had merely shifted from an external siege to an internal assault.

Senzo closed his eyes and concentrated.

Using their life perception, the Tree Spirits could sense the life signatures of the clansmen trapped inside.

After several seconds, Senzo opened his eyes and looked right. “Vannis and the others are over there.”

“Rescue Vannis and his companions first. They know where the Niam Alloy is stored.”

“Understood!”

The Tree Spirits nodded and went back to direct the forces following them to push through the right-hand passage.

An elder looked around and noticed Fang Heng wasn't present. He asked quietly, “Great Elder, that human from earlier...”

“Mm.” Senzo thought for a moment and nodded. “I'll deal with him.”

Fang Heng had remained a priority target for Federation and Wanling Technology fire throughout the fight. He'd directed the Lickers to destroy more than a dozen troublesome heavy war machines before finally finding an opening to use Secondary Spatial Projection to avoid the enemy and enter the institute.

At the building's main interior passage, a dozen Tree Spirits were guarding the way. On seeing Fang Heng, two immediately approached him.

“Fang Heng, we've successfully entered the institute, but the danger isn't over. Our interference with the external spatial-transition system is weakening, and Federation war machines are arriving by transition at an increasing rate. We need you to help eliminate the war machines gathering outside. They'll pose an enormous threat to us.”

— End of Chapter 2151 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Air ducts. Worms infiltrate the ventilation system, bypassing defenses designed to stop larger enemies.
  • Emergency protocol. The institute’s three-hour defensive endurance was originally intended to give researchers time to destroy data.

Chinese Expressions

  • 头皮发麻. Literally “the scalp goes numb”; the command room is horrified to see the ventilation ducts full of worms.
