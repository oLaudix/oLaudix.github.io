Chapter 2357

Corruption

From original Chinese Chapter 2358 · 污染

In the primeval forest of the Inner World.

Root Clan Great Elder Geoffrey was moving rapidly through the dense woods.

He still didn't know what had happened to Senzo and Tamsanga.

Their chances of survival were probably slim.

After escaping the Demon Seed of Pride's pursuit, Geoffrey had hurried straight back to the Root Clan settlement.

Remembering what had just happened sent cold sweat down his back.

“Great Elder!”

Root Clan guards hurried over.

They rarely saw Geoffrey in such a sorry state, and couldn't help wondering what had happened.

What terrible trouble had he encountered to leave even the Great Elder looking like that?

Geoffrey said gravely, “The black mist has erupted. Gather everyone immediately. We're evacuating the Inner World!”

“Yes!”

The guards were alarmed. They immediately began organizing supplies for evacuation.

Geoffrey understood the situation clearly: the Demon Seed's strength was terrifying, and he had no hope of resisting it alone.

If he couldn't stop it, then there was no point trying.

They needed to run, and get out of the Inner World immediately!

If they couldn't afford to provoke it, surely they could at least hide from it?

When the Tree Spirits had faced an existential crisis long ago, their decision to send the four major clans out with Divine Tree saplings had partly been intended to preserve their bloodlines beyond the Inner World.

While Geoffrey was thinking, a guard rushed over.

“Report, Great Elder!”

“What is it?”

“We've just received word that the Federation and the Blood Clan jointly attacked the Leaf Clan settlement. At present, the settlement has already…” The guard hesitated, a trace of sympathy appearing in his eyes. “The Leaf Clan settlement has been completely destroyed. Reports say the Federation and vampires are now pursuing their attack toward the Wood Clan settlement.”

“What?!” Geoffrey's expression changed drastically. “When did this happen?”

“An hour ago.”

“An hour ago?!”

Geoffrey's pupils contracted sharply.

An hour ago was exactly when they had first met Fang Heng at the sealed cavern!

Fang Heng!

The brat had never intended to cooperate with them in the first place!

Geoffrey felt furious, but a chill also rose in his heart.

This was bad.

Today, he had conspired with the Wood and Leaf Clans to deal with Fang Heng.

That brat would certainly hold a grudge against the Root Clan as well!

After the Federation and Blood Clan finished dealing with the Wood Clan, the Root Clan might very well be their next target!

Cold sweat broke out on Geoffrey's forehead again.

He was beginning to regret everything!

If they remained in the Inner World, they would have to face an exceptionally dangerous Demon Seed. If they fled to the Outer World, they might have to face the Blood Clan and the Federation instead.

If he'd known this would happen, he would never have chosen this particular day to attack Fang Heng.

“I understand. We're withdrawing from here. Immediately.”

Geoffrey drew a deep breath and decided to evacuate at once.

However he looked at it, the Demon Seed was a greater threat than Fang Heng.

“Yes!”

The Root Clan guard had barely acknowledged the order and turned to leave when he suddenly saw something. His expression changed dramatically.

“Great Elder…”

Geoffrey looked up in the direction the guard was staring.

A dense bank of black mist was rushing toward their camp from the distance.

Geoffrey's face drained of color.

The Demon Seed! Sharuba!

It had actually followed him here!

Under the cover of the mist, a dark figure burst into the Root Clan camp!

The black mist surrounding the figure followed closely behind and swallowed the entire camp almost instantly!

Before Geoffrey could even tell his clan members not to resist the Demon Seed, a dark figure shot out of the fog.

“Bang!”

A single punch!

Geoffrey's outer shield shattered instantly. He coughed up blood and was hurled backward by the blow, crashing heavily into the rock wall behind him and collapsing.

The Demon Seed Sharuba briefly teleported through the air and appeared before the fallen Geoffrey.

“Sharuba…”

Geoffrey braced himself against the ground with one hand and tried to stand, staring at the figure wrapped in black mist with fear in his eyes.

“You're very lucky. I'm in need of subordinates at the moment, so I've decided to forgive your disrespect toward me.”

Sharuba looked down at Geoffrey, who had completely lost the ability to fight, and slowly raised his right hand.

“Submit, my follower.”

“Hiss… hiss, hiss…”

The dense black mist all around them churned violently and surged toward Geoffrey!

The next instant, the mist poured into his body with terrifying force!

“Hiss, hiss…”

A few breaths later, Geoffrey's pupils had been covered in pitch black.

Geoffrey stood up again.

Facing Sharuba, he clenched his teeth and knelt on one knee. “Lord Demon Seed… What are your orders?”

“Very good, Geoffrey. I have many things for you to do.”

…

Meanwhile, in a secluded forest of the Inner World, two Fusion Tyrant Forms finished their long journey and stopped before the ruins of the Council of Elders.

“We're here.”

Fang Heng jumped down from the Fusion Tyrant's back and surveyed the ruins in the distance.

Tipper had used the journey to recover. Her condition was much better than before.

“Since the four clans last explored the council grounds, we've returned to the perimeter more than once. The Stone Spirit Puppets are lying in wait inside. As soon as we approach the council grounds, they attack.”

Tipper studied the ruins from a distance and explained quietly, “Fang Heng, there are enormous numbers of Stone Spirit Puppets, and they're extremely troublesome. I've already informed the Great Elder through a mirror of what's happened. Soul Clan reinforcements are on their way, but they still need time to arrive…”

“All right. Let's try it first.”

“What?”

Tipper was taken aback.

Try what?

Break in?

Just the two of them?

Fang Heng studied the grounds ahead and rapidly formed a seal with his hands.

“Whoosh!”

A drop of crimson blood gathered between Fang Heng's brows.

The blood slowly rose into the air and burst apart with a pop!

The dispersed blood energy rapidly coalesced into a scarlet teleportation gate in midair.

A spatial teleportation gate!

Tipper's heart lurched when she saw it, and she hurriedly backed away a couple of steps.

The next instant, Vampire Prince Jia'er emerged from the gate carrying a stone slab on his back.

“My King!”

Jia'er immediately knelt on one knee after stepping through the passage. He glanced briefly at Tipper beside Fang Heng and reported, “The Leaf Clan's main settlement has been cleared out according to your plan. A small number of Leaf Clan members escaped amid the chaos, and tracking them is proving difficult. We're currently assisting the Federation with an attack on the Wood Clan settlement.”

“Mm.” Fang Heng nodded. “Understood. Well done. Now go and find Sandy for me. Tell him we're searching the Tree Spirit Council of Elders and bring him here to have a look.”

“Yes!”

Jia'er accepted the order and hurried away.

For safety's sake, Fang Heng thought it best to bring Sandy along.

After all, he couldn't make sense of the Tree Spirits' ancient records himself.

What if the Soul Clan tried to fool him?
