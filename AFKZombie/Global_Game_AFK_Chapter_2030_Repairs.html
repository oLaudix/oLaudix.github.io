Chapter 2030

Repairs

From original Chinese Chapter 2031 · 修理

Chu Yan wiped the sweat from his forehead. “We've confirmed that the main teleportation passage is intact. Once we identify the cause, repairs should be quick. But we can't force it to activate yet. Just a little longer—please give us some more time…”

City Lord Saino was frantic.

He knew pressing them wouldn't help.

But this was precisely when the battle was at its fiercest!

His brows knotted. He opened his mouth to speak, only to hear a commotion from the city's north gate.

Everyone turned toward the northern side of the city.

“Report!”

Two Canmu City guards hurried over, their faces anxious. “City Lord! A large number of zombies have attacked the north gate! It's already fallen, and some of the creatures have broken into the city! We urgently need reinforcements!”

What?!

Everyone was stunned by the report.

The north gate had been attacked—and had already fallen?!

Where had these undead come from?!

Before Saino could respond, Chu Yan spoke up urgently. “City Lord, those undead are probably targeting the teleportation passage. Protecting it must be our top priority! We absolutely cannot let them get close. Otherwise, there'll be no way to turn this around…”

Saino gritted his teeth. “You few, stay here! Defend the teleportation passage at all costs! Everyone else, come with me to reinforce the north gate!”

“Yes, sir!”

The Holy Court troops answered in unison and rushed after Saino toward the north gate.

Before long, only the Devout Guild players led by Chu Yan and some twenty Holy Court guards remained in the teleportation area.

Chu Yan watched the others leave, nodded faintly, and silently mouthed a command to his subordinates.

“Begin!”

Saino had no idea there was a traitor in his own ranks.

He was hurrying toward the north gate with his elite troops, desperate to provide support.

But they hadn't gone far from the teleportation area before the scene ahead left them stunned.

The city was in ruins!

Just as the guards had reported, the entire north gate had been overrun in less than three minutes!

The surviving imperial guards were fighting a losing battle against the Lickers and zombies, retreating as they fought.

Foul-smelling blood and severed limbs lay everywhere, while newly formed zombies kept crawling out of fleshy cocoons and joining the battle.

Saino clenched his teeth and shouted, “Stop them!”

His elite Holy Knights immediately rushed into battle to hold back the undead.

Not far away, Fang Heng watched Saino from behind a corner, his eyes narrowing.

There he was—the man they wanted!

“Everyone, see that man?” Fang Heng turned toward the undead-faction players. “That's City Lord Saino. He's no pushover. We'll work together to take him down, and the faster we do it, the better.”

“Right!”

“Move!”

At his words, the twenty-odd undead players recruited by Li Qingran emerged from the surrounding shadows and headed straight for Saino!

The city lord had just driven off a charging Licker with his sword when he felt more than a dozen dangerous presences closing in. His expression changed drastically.

Over a dozen necromancy spells flew toward him first.

“Get away from me!” Saino roared.

Boom!

Sacred flames suddenly blazed to life, engulfing his entire body!

Every spell directed at him was burned away by the holy fire!

The undead players spread out around Saino, sealing off every escape route.

“So that's your plan…” Saino looked around with a sneer. “All of you against one man? I'm flattered!”

“Enough talking! Kill him!”

Li Qingran sprang forward with a shout and rapidly formed a hand seal.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

A gigantic black hand shot out of the Book of the Dead before her, sweeping toward Saino!

Almost simultaneously, the other players activated their Books of the Dead, sending a dense barrage of soul chains crashing toward him!

Saino knitted his brows and dodged to the side, forcing a holy shield into existence as he sought an opening to counterattack. Then, all of a sudden, he felt something strange.

What?!

It was as if an invisible hand had seized his heart. Blood surged uncontrollably through his body, interrupting the holy spell he had been casting to raise his defensive barrier.

This was bad!

That moment's interruption caused the barrier spell to fail!

Boom! Boom, boom!

The next instant, countless soul chains slammed into Saino.

Bang! Bang, bang!

The city lord was blasted backward!

“Keep going!”

Li Qingran stared at the airborne Saino and shouted.

The players immediately directed their soul chains after him to finish the job!

Saino's face turned deathly pale. The repeated necromancy attacks only intensified the chaotic surge of blood within him!

Before he could even catch his breath, another dense wave of soul chains was upon him!

He had no choice but to grit his teeth and endure it!

Yet the disturbance in his blood kept growing stronger, leaving him no chance to retaliate!

Saino's heart sank.

Seeing this, the players pressed their attack even harder!

Strike while the enemy is weak!

Even they found Saino's condition somewhat strange.

Their combined strength was greater than his to begin with, so defeating him had always been a matter of time.

But who could have expected it to be this easy?

Keeping him completely suppressed, they watched his health steadily fall until the soul chains finally pierced his body!

Was it over?

Just like that?

Was City Lord Saino really that useless?

“Focus!” Li Qingran shouted. “Don't let his soul escape!”

At the same instant, Li Qingran sent a soul chain from her Book of the Dead stabbing toward the spirit that was trying to flee!

Hiss!

The chain struck the barrier protecting Saino's soul, erupting in a flash of holy light.

The necromancy players around her joined in, concentrating on controlling their Books of the Dead.

More than a dozen soul chains hammered Saino's spirit without pause.

Within moments, the barrier could take no more and burst with a thunderous crash.

Saino's soul was utterly destroyed!

The nearby city guards lost their fighting spirit at once. Scattering in panic, they became easy prey for the Lickers.

“Intercept the souls of the Holy Court elites when they die. Don't let them carry word of what's happened! Not a single one can escape!”

Fang Heng glanced at the game notification, emerged from the rear, and nodded to Li Qingran.

“Understood.”

Li Qingran immediately called on the twenty-odd players to go and help.

Few high-level Holy Court NPCs possessed soul-protection abilities; they were generally at least elite squad leaders. There couldn't be more than five in the entire city.

Fang Heng continued directing his zombie clones toward the teleportation area to provide support.
