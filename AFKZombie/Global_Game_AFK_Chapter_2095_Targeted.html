Chapter 2095

Targeted

From original Chinese Chapter 2096 · 锁定

Whoosh!

Fang Heng poured his spiritual power into the runestone!

Then he raised the altar stone and smashed it against the ground.

CRACK!

The stone struck the floor and shattered!

At that same instant, somewhere across the countless worlds...

A pair of dark-purple eyes opened.

A provocation?!

The Seed of Envy instantly locked onto Fang Heng's location.

· · ·

The Eternal Holy City.

As the runestone broke apart, Fang Heng immediately felt a powerful presence fix itself on him!

Something was observing him from afar.

[Hint: An unknown power is observing you.]

[Hint: Protection from your divine artifact has partially reduced the effect of this observation.]

It had come!

Fang Heng knew the Demon Seed of Envy had noticed him.

The only question was whether his actions would actually draw Envy here.

Well, he'd done everything he could!

Time to take a gamble!

Next, he needed to try escaping from this place!

[Hint: You have created chaos in the Eternal Holy City, negatively affecting the Holy Court. You have gained 120,000 Undead Faction Reputation and 100,000 Undead Faction Honor. These gains cannot be calculated precisely until the incident ends; rewards will be settled once every hour.]

Huh?

Fang Heng was surprised by the sudden notification.

This counted toward the war quest between the undead and the Holy Court too?

Excellent news!

From within the chaotic crowd, Fang Heng scanned the notification and looked toward the teleportation passage again.

Despite the turmoil sweeping the Eternal Holy City, the Holy Court guards around the passage were still standing firm at their posts.

Fang Heng's heart sank.

He couldn't find a way through.

He glanced back toward the courtyard.

The Crusader instructors were powerful, and within the Eternal Holy City's environment their strength wasn't suppressed. They'd already brought the dozen or so second-stage Glutton's Touch creatures under control.

The paladin guards had begun regrouping the players and restoring order.

The crisis seemed almost over.

BOOM!

Suddenly, a thunderous roar struck everyone's ears.

Their hearts seemed to seize, and an inexplicable fear rose from deep within them.

A game notification appeared before Fang Heng.

[Hint: You destroyed the Seed of Envy's Sacrificial Rune. The Demon Seed of Envy has detected you. You are about to be subjected to its highest-level attack. Exercise caution.]

[Hint: You are being affected by an unknown effect in the current environment. Exercise caution.]

As the thunder faded, a gray mist began spreading throughout the Eternal Holy City.

The Crusader instructors fighting Glutton's Touch in the courtyard and teleportation hall frowned, quickly realizing something was wrong.

This wasn't right!

They exchanged glances.

Demon Seed corruption!

With their abilities, suppressing a dozen Glutton's Touch creatures wasn't difficult.

But the corruption now filling the halls clearly wasn't coming from those worms.

It originated from something far more powerful.

Who?!

Where had it come from?!

BOOM!

Another thunderclap shook the entire Eternal Holy City.

Dark clouds descended over the city!

A pair of dark-purple eyes appeared intermittently within the clouds, and the lights throughout the Eternal Holy City began flickering.

Bzzzt! Bzzzt!

Electrical energy crackled continuously inside the hall.

BANG! BANG, BANG!

Almost simultaneously, every active teleportation array in the Eternal Holy City exploded!

"Not good!"

A Demon Seed!

The Crusaders' faces changed dramatically.

This was a Demon Seed's Domain!

Why had a Demon Seed's Domain suddenly descended upon the Eternal Holy City?!

Why?!

Under the city's divine protection, attacking it was an enormous waste of effort for a Demon Seed. The domain's effects would be severely weakened.

Yet the Demon Seed had done it anyway!

More than a dozen Crusader officers couldn't begin to understand the reason.

But this was no time to wonder about it.

Ordinary people exposed to such highly concentrated demonic energy would become the seed's puppets almost immediately. Even Holy Court players would suffer corruption after prolonged exposure.

"Quickly! Evacuate the people!"

"Evacuate everyone immediately!"

"All paladins below the God-Blessed rank, escort the trainees out of the Demon Seed's Domain!"

They could no longer afford to keep fighting Glutton's Touch. Protecting and evacuating the people had become their highest priority.

Worse still, exposure to such concentrated energy from Envy would greatly enhance Glutton's Touch, making the creatures harder to defeat!

They couldn't stay here!

The divine statue!

They had to use the power of the Statue of Zeus to fight back!

Everyone else had to withdraw immediately. If the people became the seed's puppets, the situation would only deteriorate further!

The Crusader officers decided at once. Two instructors would lead paladins in escorting the players out of the domain, while ten remained behind to defend the Eternal Holy City and try to lure the surviving Glutton's Touch creatures toward the Statue of Zeus.

Meanwhile, the sudden escalation sent the players gathered near the teleportation area into a frenzy.

What was going on?!

Nobody understood what had happened. It felt as if the entire Eternal Holy City had descended into chaos in an instant, as though the end of the world were upon them.

Should they stay or run?

Running now might mean missing out.

Perhaps they should stay?

Something was clearly happening in the Eternal Holy City. Maybe a special quest would be triggered soon, and they could earn some rewards by remaining.

But...

Was this really a quest they were strong enough to profit from?

With too little information to judge the situation, most players were still confused. The more timid among them had already decided to flee.

They tried using their Crusader Trainee Marks to leave.

Huh?

Why wasn't it working?

Their faces changed. Something was seriously wrong.

Looking down, they saw that a faint layer of gray mist had appeared on the dark-golden marks on their wrists.

What the hell was that?!

Some players tried rubbing it off, but the mist clung to the marks and wouldn't budge!

Panic spread through the crowd.

They were finished!

The marks that allowed them to leave the Eternal Holy City had been sealed!

Even if they wanted to run now, it was too late!

The next instant, a notification appeared before every player in the city.

[Hint: You have triggered a Special Quest — Evacuate the Eternal Holy City.]

Quest Name: Evacuate the Eternal Holy City.

Difficulty: ???

Quest Description: The Eternal Holy City is facing a grave crisis. An unknown domain ability has enveloped the skies above the city. You may be unable to contribute meaningfully during this crisis and could instead become a puppet of demonic forces. Immediately follow the defenders and withdraw beyond the unknown domain. Of course, if you are absolutely confident in your strength...

Quest Requirements: Escape the range of the unknown domain, or assist the Crusader officers in defending the Eternal Holy City.

Quest Hint: In the current special environment, all spatial abilities of Master level or below have become ineffective, and teleportation passages are disabled. Players whose Holy School proficiency is below Master level are advised to evacuate.

— End of Chapter 2095 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • 120,000 and 100,000. The source writes 12W Undead Reputation and 10W Undead Honor, where W (万) means ten thousand.
  • Spatial suppression. Master-level and lower spatial abilities and teleportation passages cease functioning inside Envy’s domain.
