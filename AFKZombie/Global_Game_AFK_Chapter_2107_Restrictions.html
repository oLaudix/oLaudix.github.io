Chapter 2107

Restrictions

From original Chinese Chapter 2108 · 限制

Under the holy radiance, the Lickers continuously suffered sacred damage. Their health rapidly emptied, and they dissolved beneath the intense light.

As the black fog and exploding sacred energy dissipated, brightness returned to the hall.

Okney raised his head and looked around, surprise flickering in his eyes.

The cathedral had been transformed. The once spacious, orderly hall was littered with broken tables, chairs, and decorations. Dense necromantic energy and the faint smell of blood hung in the air.

Hmm?

Okney gave a soft exclamation.

Most of the Lickers had been shattered by his earlier strike, but some had left patches of black slime behind.

The slime was actually writhing and gathering together on the floor.

In the blink of an eye, numerous Lickers rose from it once more!

“Evil spirits...”

Okney watched the resurrected Lickers and murmured to himself, raising his sacred longsword again.

“Purify them.”

At his command, the Holy Court inquisitors behind him extended their hands and formed seals before their chests.

Hiss, hiss...

Faint sacred energy flowed from the inquisitors.

The Lickers that had just revived and were preparing to leap at Okney abruptly froze. Dark-golden chains materialized across their bodies.

Black fog poured wildly out of them!

[Game Hint: Your Licker (Primal Devourer Fusion Modification) has been affected by Sacred Imprisonment...]

[Game Hint: Your Licker (Primal Devourer Fusion Modification) has lost 5,000 HP...]

[Game Hint: Your Licker...]

Not far away, Fang Heng was watching from concealment.

Game notifications continued flashing across his retina.

A film of cold sweat rose involuntarily on his forehead.

This was troublesome!

They had carefully prepared the ruined cathedral beforehand, filling it with necromantic energy. They planned to use the Lickers, that energy, and the wraiths summoned by Dani's group to teach the Divine Realm's pursuers a lesson.

Who could have expected this?!

The powerhouses of the Divine Realm were overwhelmingly strong!

With just one attack!

They had scattered the summoned wraiths, the huge concentrations of necromantic energy, and Fang Heng's Licker horde!

Only a few hundred Lickers that had undergone Primal Devourer modifications managed to revive and survive.

And even those Lickers seemed completely ineffective against these enemies!

How could they possibly win?

The difference in strength was absurdly large!

Fang Heng was secretly shaken.

The enemy's strength far exceeded his expectations.

He strongly suspected the Divine Realm was a world of an even higher level than ordinary high-tier game worlds.

He had originally planned to take advantage of the chaos to test his own attacks, but that idea was gone now.

Time to run!

Just as Fang Heng prepared to leave, he heard Okney's voice.

“Fang Heng!”

Fang Heng turned to look behind him.

Okney stared at him, shook his head, and issued a warning. “The Demon Seed was never meant to exist in this world. Return to the hell where you belong.”

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

Whoosh!

Okney swung his sacred sword with both hands!

A blade of sacred light erupted from its edge and flew directly at Fang Heng!

Bang!

The crimson greatsword on Fang Heng's back instantly burst apart, forming a huge curtain of blood!

The bloody curtain swiftly moved in front of him.

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!”

The sacred sword light struck the curtain in an instant. Two mighty forces collided in midair, tearing and grinding against each other!

The blood curtain continuously evaporated under the sacred purification effect!

More surprise appeared in Okney's eyes.

He could actually withstand that?

Boom!

The blood energy and sacred power exploded simultaneously!

The next moment, Fang Heng was gone from behind the blood curtain.

Okney gave a disdainful snort.

Fang Heng's strength might well be at the peak of what someone in a lower-tier world could achieve.

But that was all.

Okney sensed Fang Heng's location and was about to pursue when his heart suddenly lurched.

Not good!

Boom! Boom, boom, boom!

Violent explosions erupted all around him!

The entire ruined cathedral blew apart!

Outside the cathedral,

Fang Heng's figure gradually emerged through a teleportation scroll.

Dani and the undead faction players glanced at him, then all turned to look at the distant cathedral.

BOOM!

An enormous mushroom cloud rose from the ruined cathedral.

The massive explosion destroyed more than half the city along with it.

Dust and smoke engulfed the streets.

More than half an hour earlier, the undead faction had concealed every piece of high-energy explosive material it could gather beneath the cathedral.

They had timed the explosives to detonate together, intending to give the Divine Realm's pursuers a nasty surprise.

But even so...

Dani and the others exchanged grave looks.

The smoke gradually cleared in the distance.

Sacred energy leaked from the cracks between the collapsed structures.

Even that hadn't killed them!

[Game Hint: You have inflicted damage exceeding 0.5% of the Divine Realm pursuit team's total health.]

[Game Hint: Your current mission—Escape the Divine Realm's Pursuit—has changed. The original mission is canceled.]

[Game Hint: You have triggered a new mission—Desperate Counterattack.]

Mission Name: Desperate Counterattack.

Difficulty: SSS.

Mission Description: After being pursued relentlessly by the Divine Realm, you can no longer tolerate the harassment. Perhaps only bloodshed can end this once and for all. It is time to teach the Divine Realm a lesson it will never forget!

Mission Requirement: Kill the Divine Realm Sacred Arbiter, Okney.

Mission Reward: Unknown.

Fang Heng drew a sharp breath when he saw the notifications.

Then a bitter expression crossed his face.

The game's mission system certainly dared to dream bigger than he did.

All their careful planning and ambush had reduced the pursuit team's total health by a mere 0.5%.

Even if he unleashed the Everlasting Moon, he would be lucky to take off another 10%.

Defeat them completely?

In his dreams!

Still, the ambush hadn't been entirely wasted.

At least they had learned how strong the Divine Realm's pursuers really were.

Several undead guild commanders exchanged glances when Fang Heng said nothing.

What they'd done had already been crazy enough!

Regardless of whether it succeeded, merely having the courage to challenge the Divine Realm's hunters was something they could boast about later.

And Fang Heng still wasn't satisfied?

Did he seriously intend to wipe out the entire pursuit team?

Guo Lianhai couldn't help advising him. “World Lord Fang Heng, are you all right? The Divine Realm's enemies are on a completely different level from us. Being overwhelmed was only to be expected. Perhaps you should...”

“I'm fine.” Fang Heng waved a hand. “Thank you. I understand.”

If he couldn't stand against them head-on, he would have to keep running.

There were very few safe places where he could avoid the Divine Realm's pursuit.

For now, the only ones he could think of were the Zombie Apocalypse world and the Vampire Apocalypse world...

And the undead faction's main city.

His freedom of movement had become severely restricted.

— End of Chapter 2107 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Damage assessment. Despite destroying much of the city with explosives, the ambush inflicts only more than 0.5% of the entire pursuit team’s total health.
  • New mission. The game cancels the escape mission and offers an SSS-rank mission to kill Sacred Arbiter Okney.
  • Estimated ultimate damage. Fang Heng speculates that even the Everlasting Moon might only remove another 10% of the team’s health; this is his estimate, not a measured result.

Chinese Expressions

  • 给…一点颜色瞧瞧 (gěi… yīdiǎn yánsè qiáoqiao). Literally “show them a little color”; an idiom meaning to teach someone a lesson or give them a nasty surprise.
