Chapter 2048

Investigation

From original Chinese Chapter 2049 · 巡查

The Divine Domain.

Alaierding had received the latest news from the lower worlds.

The Yin Vein had appeared in Duncha. Both Duncha and the reserve Crusader corps had suffered devastating losses.

“Merk…” he murmured.

Descending from the higher realm to the lower worlds took a considerable amount of time. He had assumed that after placing a tracking mark, finding whoever activated Merk's surviving notes would be easy.

He never imagined that Merk's magic array would be triggered in a matter of days!

Worse, Fang Heng had caught them entirely off guard, using the array to transfer the Yin Vein fissure and launch a surprise attack on Duncha itself.

The undead! They intend to begin a war on every front!

Thinking back carefully, he realized the undead had already been making moves from the very beginning of the Vampire incident. The Holy Court simply hadn't noticed it at the time.

This had all been part of the undead's plan!

For years, they had appeared to be lying low. In reality, they had been secretly preparing their counterattack.

Alaierding had no doubt their next move was already underway.

“Part of the fog has lifted. Everything the undead are planning is closely linked to Fang Heng. He's also connected to the Vampires and the Demon Seed. He's the key to everything. Bring him to me—without fail!”

“Yes!”

A Divine Domain adjudicator knelt on one knee.

“I'll take a team to the lower worlds at once and try to locate Fang Heng.”

“Aokni, abandon your illusions. The war between us and the undead has already begun. I need your complete concentration this time. Remember: never underestimate Fang Heng.”

Aokni was startled, then committed the warning to memory.

“Yes! Understood!”

…

Holy Court Headquarters Cathedral, Duncha.

Early that morning, several buses entered the cathedral grounds and stopped beside the parking lot. Fang Heng and Chu Yan's group disembarked.

The grounds outside were packed with Holy Court believers. Holding their Books of Beginnings, they knelt in devout prayer.

A resonant bell rang from the tower above the cathedral, stirring an involuntary sense of reverence in those who heard it.

Large numbers of believers came here to pray every day. Now that news of the distant disaster had spread through Duncha's news networks, the number of worshippers was especially high.

The Holy Court had begun to suspect Chu Yan and had revoked his and the Devout Guild's permission to participate in war quests. But its formal investigation hadn't yet begun, so Chu Yan still retained his Holy Court faction identity and could enter the cathedral grounds.

“Boss Fang, Duncha is under emergency measures,” Chu Yan explained as he led Fang Heng through the crowded square. “The frost is expected to reach the cathedral district tonight. Most of the guards have been deployed to establish barriers along the edge of the Yin Vein's influence.”

“Still, as far as I know, some of the Holy Court's core fighting strength remains here. Archbishop Xi Lufei hasn't left the cathedral either.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng looked up at the cathedral's tallest bell tower.

He could sense that the ringing contained a power capable of influencing people's minds.

“Tsk.”

Despicable Holy Court. Always using these flashy little tricks.

He had to admit, though, the bell was quite useful. He considered finding an opportunity to steal the great bell from the tower.

With that thought, he switched into his Sacred Studies form and followed Chu Yan and the flow of worshippers inside.

Perhaps because Duncha had enjoyed stability for so long, the cathedral itself was surprisingly open. Anyone could freely enter and leave.

After entering with the crowd, Fang Heng checked the time in his game interface. His previously destroyed zombie clones had roughly an hour left before they resurrected.

“Boss Fang, today is a prayer day.”

Chu Yan quietly guided him around the cathedral's worship area and spoke close to his ear.

“That's the Prayer Hall. Believers pray there, receive God's blessings, and can even gain some skill experience. We still don't know where the stone inscription used to train reserve Crusaders is kept. I suspect it's connected to the underground areas.”

“Underground?”

“Yes. There are rumors of secret chambers beneath the cathedral. They've never been opened to the public, and practically no information has leaked out.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng suddenly thought of something else.

“By the way, is there anywhere here to learn skills? A Temple of Zeus, perhaps?”

Although he'd raised Sacred Studies to the advanced tier, his Book of Oaths didn't contain any advanced skills.

He'd also built a copy of the Temple of Zeus in his Zombie Apocalypse prison, but its level was too low to teach advanced Sacred Studies.

Now that he was at headquarters, he didn't have to rush into destroying things. He might as well learn something first.

“There is.”

Chu Yan nodded. “You've come to the right place. This cathedral has the highest-level Temple of Zeus among the mid-tier game worlds. You can learn skills there and also undergo a baptism.”

Baptism!

Fang Heng's interest was piqued again.

After advancing Sacred Studies to the intermediate tier, a player could receive baptism at a Holy Court cathedral and gain increases to their base attributes.

The maximum increase varied from player to player. According to online discussions, each attribute typically rose about three points; talented individuals could gain five, and rare cases of seven points had been reported.

“The Temple of Zeus opens to believers every day, but both learning and baptism require reputation and contribution points. If you need them, I still have a few left.”

Fang Heng shrugged.

“How long will they last us?”

“Enough for a few hours,” Chu Yan answered with a bitter smile. “They're probably going to be wiped from my account soon anyway. We might as well spend them before that happens.”

“Mm.”

Chu Yan had already prepared himself for what lay ahead. His time with the Holy Court was over. From now on, his best option was to follow Boss Fang.

Six months ago, he could never have imagined making such a seemingly insane choice—siding with Fang Heng instead of the mighty Holy Court.

But after watching Fang Heng's monstrous rate of growth, his opinion had changed.

The Holy Court was vastly stronger than Fang Heng on paper. Yet when had it ever gotten the better of him?

Meanwhile, Fang Heng's strength was increasing at a frightening speed!

At this rate, the Holy Court was doomed sooner or later.

Better to switch sides now and cut his losses.

If circumstances allowed it, Chu Yan would even have considered abandoning Sacred Studies and taking up necromancy instead.

— End of Chapter 2048 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • New names. 阿莱尔丁 and 奥克尼 are rendered Alaierding and Aokni, based on Chinese phonetics. Exact original Western spellings are not confirmed.
  • Temple of Zeus. 宙斯神殿 is literally Temple of Zeus in the Chinese. The sound of the cathedral bell has a mind-affecting property, not merely atmospheric significance.
