Chapter 2258

Digging a Way Out

From original Chinese Chapter 2259 · 现挖

Ovi watched Fang Heng push open the storeroom doors.

When he stepped inside and saw what was happening, surprise flooded his face.

What the hell?!

The room was packed with zombies holding alloy pickaxes and hammering furiously at a hole in the floor!

In a short time, they'd already dug a large pit leading downward.

More zombies constantly wheeled out wood fragments, stones, and soil excavated from the hole. They carried the spoil out of the storehouse and piled it along the third basement level's corridor.

The Prayer-Curse Temple had been damaged and repaired several times, leaving many parts of it weakened.

Fang Heng had investigated earlier and concluded that excavating a tunnel would be only moderately difficult.

Besides, equipping the zombies with high-grade alloy entrenching tools had greatly increased their digging efficiency!

"Heh…"

Noticing Ovi's astonishment, Fang Heng patted his shoulder again.

"Going out through the front is too dangerous. We'll dig our own way out."

In fact, Fang Heng had already used his Lickers to excavate part of a tunnel from outside the Prayer-Curse Temple.

He'd originally prepared it as insurance against the Leaf Clan.

If something unexpected happened, he'd be able to counteract their moves before they caught him off guard.

At worst, he could break off their cooperation, secretly rob the Leaf Clan, and leave with the loot through an underground passage.

However, his prepared tunnel had been dug from the opposite side, outside the temple.

He hadn't started the internal segment until just now.

Meanwhile, another group of zombie clones was working as fast as possible on the outer section.

The two sections were expected to connect somewhere in the middle.

"F-Fang Heng…"

Staring at the scene, Ovi finally understood what Fang Heng planned to do. The sheer audacity left him stammering. "Will… will this really work?"

Digging an escape tunnel right now?

Could they possibly finish in time?

"It's fine. Trust me."

Fang Heng looked at Ovi. "But there's still one important problem."

Ovi steadied himself. "What is it? Ask away."

"You said the other three clans can sense where the Divine Tree sapling is."

"That's right." Ovi frowned. "When the sapling isn't activated, its energy fluctuations are weak, but someone concentrating on tracking it can still determine its approximate location. Elder Falk used to have a special technique that temporarily sealed those fluctuations. Now that he's dying, if the other clans deliberately search for it, they'll find us even if we escape through a tunnel."

"And the sapling?" Fang Heng asked.

Ovi immediately took out the box he was carrying.

"Mind letting me take a closer look?"

Ovi was startled for an instant, then decisively handed it over.

"Be quick. If the sapling is separated from me for too long, it'll wither faster."

Fang Heng took another look at him.

Was this fellow really a Leaf Clan member?

He'd expected a long argument, but Ovi had handed it over without hesitation.

Ovi had thought it through. Trusting Fang Heng was their only chance of escaping. If Fang Heng wanted the sapling, it would fall into his hands eventually anyway. And Ovi genuinely wanted to trust him.

"All right."

Fang Heng accepted the box and opened the lid with a snap.

A tiny tree sapling sat at its center.

"Hummm…"

As the box opened, fluctuations of Divine Tree energy spread outward.

And as soon as the sapling was separated from Ovi, it began to change.

It was withering with alarming speed!

Apparently Ovi hadn't been lying.

At a thought, Fang Heng's blood-energy short blades burst apart and streamed toward the box, forming a protective barrier of blood around the sapling.

Hm?

Ovi had been watching closely and was astonished.

The sapling's energy signature had vanished!

That strange blood-energy power again!

He'd seen Fang Heng use it more than once!

Under the blood-energy shroud, the sapling's natural aura was completely contained and concealed. Only the surrounding blood-energy fluctuations remained detectable.

Game notifications appeared before Fang Heng.

[Hint: You have discovered a special item—Divine Tree Sapling.]

[Hint: You lack an effective method to control this item. Taking possession of it will cause its aura to leak rapidly and accelerate its withering.]

[Hint: If you take possession of this item, the Tree Spirits will become hostile toward you.]

[Hint: This item cannot be placed in your inventory and will drop immediately upon death.]

Ovi had indeed told the truth. The sapling required special handling and preservation.

"Click."

Fang Heng shut the box and handed it back. "The blood energy will lose its effect when it gets too far from me, but it should conceal the sapling long enough for you to leave the temple."

"All right. Thank you."

Recovering from his surprise, Ovi took the box and carefully concealed it on his person.

With the aura issue resolved, they only needed to buy the zombies enough time to complete the tunnel!

Fang Heng looked toward the storeroom exit.

Hopefully the Leaf Clan outside could hold out a little longer.

"I'll go check the situation. You stay here."

"All right!"

…

Outside, at the rear of the Prayer-Curse Temple, the Lickers were still battling the Root Clan.

The two Root Clan elders exchanged wary glances.

These creatures were probably the "Lickers" that Wood Clan Elder Chrono had mentioned.

They certainly were troublesome!

Once they became familiar with the Lickers' fighting patterns, the Root Clan quickly changed tactics.

Since killing the Lickers seemed nearly impossible, the guards simply used root-binding skills to immobilize them against the ground one after another.

They'd trap them first and worry about the rest later!

Without Fang Heng concentrating on their movements, the Lickers were quickly pinned down, unable to break free.

Hm?!

What was that now?!

The Root Clan guards frowned.

Just as the Lickers seemed completely immobilized, their bodies suddenly transformed into throbbing cocoons of flesh.

The cocoons swelled rapidly!

A moment later, enormous figures emerged from them!

Fusion Tyrant Forms!

The towering Tyrants made a terrifying impression. The guards' expressions changed, and they braced for a powerful enemy.

Explosions from long-range nature spells struck the Fusion Tyrants repeatedly, but did nothing to stop their advance!

"Bind them!"

Serpent-like roots surged forward and wound tightly around the Tyrants' feet.

The Fusion Tyrants struggled against the vines for a moment, but ultimately couldn't escape.
