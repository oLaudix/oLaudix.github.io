Chapter 2174

A Deal

From original Chinese Chapter 2175 · 谈妥

Was this some kind of joke?

What if the Tree Spirits went back on their word? Or something unexpected happened and they expelled him from their faction?

Even if everything went smoothly, what happened if Senzo and the Tree Spirits fled into the Inner World? Where was Fang Heng supposed to find him to claim his reward?

Most importantly, there was the timing.

Fang Heng already had a plan.

At the same time that he temporarily disabled the Spirit Domain blockade, Klevi would summon Divine Tree Abe Akaya to power the magic array at full strength, using that opportunity to open a passage into the Inner World.

That was why Fang Heng had made a decision before coming here.

He needed the quest reward in advance.

Or, failing that, at least at the precise moment he completed his part. Klevi could then feed the Leaves of Life to Abe Akaya, supplying the necessary energy to operate the magic array and open the Inner World passage.

Two birds with one stone!

Escape the Dependents' tracking and enter the Inner World at the same time!

Without those Leaves of Life, it would be a pity to waste such an opportunity.

Fang Heng shook his head and tried to negotiate further. “No. I need the Leaves of Life urgently. I can't wait that long. I must receive them first, and I can only guarantee that the Spirit Domain blockade will be interrupted temporarily…”

“Heh…”

Senzo interrupted with a cold laugh. “Fang Heng, the Tree Spirits have already made concessions. Don't push your luck. There's no possibility of paying the Leaves of Life in advance. How do we know you'll actually succeed in disabling the blockade?”

Fang Heng frowned deeply.

There was a last-resort option.

He could trade the Niam Alloy directly for Leaves of Life.

Senzo would probably agree.

But he couldn't quite bring himself to part with it.

Niam Alloy was exceedingly rare. Black Castle also needed it to oppose the Dependents, and it might prove useful in other unexpected ways.

Neither side was willing to give ground, but neither wanted to abandon the deal, so they fell silent at almost the same moment.

Just then, a deep voice rang out from above.

“Senzo, why not leave this to me? I'll keep watch over him. Once I've confirmed that he truly has the means to temporarily disable the Spirit Domain blockade, I'll give him the Leaves of Life.”

Fang Heng was startled by the unexpected voice and looked up.

Not far above them, inside a cage near the corner, an elderly Tree Spirit had spoken.

Senzo looked at him, his brow furrowing again.

Levin.

A criminal imprisoned inside the vine cages.

Once, Levin had also been one of the Leaf Clan's Great Elders.

During a mission in the past, Levin's reckless actions had caused the entire Tree Spirit race to suffer terrible losses.

He had blamed himself deeply ever since.

The other three clans had refused to let the matter pass and demanded an explanation from the Leaf Clan. To appease them, Levin had remained imprisoned ever since, never taking a single step outside.

In this urgent situation, Levin really was the most suitable candidate.

Senzo hesitated only briefly before deciding. He turned to Fang Heng. “You heard him. Great Elder Levin will accompany you throughout the infiltration of the Federation's central research facility. He'll remain with you until he confirms that you can disable the Spirit Domain network, and only then will he hand over the Leaves of Life. This is our final concession. Do you agree?”

[Hint: If Levin dies during the quest, or if the distance between the player and Levin exceeds 1,000 meters, the quest will automatically fail.]

Fang Heng looked up at Levin.

“The destination is far from here. I'll be using a special means of travel. Are you sure you can keep up?”

“Don't worry. I have my own means of accompanying you.”

Levin stepped out of the cage as he spoke and exchanged a quick look with Senzo.

“Before we leave, there are some things I need to discuss with the Great Elder.”

Levin and Senzo walked off to one side, turned their backs, and spoke quietly together.

Watching them whisper in secret, Fang Heng had a strong suspicion they were plotting something again.

Before long, Levin returned.

“All finished?”

“I will accompany you to the Federation Central Research Institute.”

As he spoke, Levin rapidly formed a seal with both hands.

A pale-green flash appeared.

An identical tree-shadow clone materialized beside him.

A cloning ability?

Fang Heng examined it with interest.

The clone was noticeably different from the original, especially its face, which had little more than a vague outline.

He had no idea how well it could fight.

“All right.” Fang Heng nodded. “And just give the Leaves of Life to my companions outside.”

“Agreed.”

Levin accepted immediately. His original body gradually transformed into a stream of light, condensing into a small mass of vine-like material.

“Click…”

The vine mass suddenly sprang forward and stuck itself beneath the armpit of Fang Heng's clothing.

Levin's voice sounded beside Fang Heng's ear.

“Human, I believe this should suffice.”

Looking down, Fang Heng saw the vine-like material clinging beneath his arm, pulsing rhythmically like a heart.

The voice was coming from the vines.

Some kind of special mimicry ability?

“Looks like that settles it.”

Fang Heng shrugged.

Well then.

One more burden to carry.

The quest had become harder.

With Levin in tow, he probably couldn't use Secondary Spatial Projection anymore.

At least the Tree Spirits had finally agreed to cooperate, meeting the minimum conditions he'd set for the deal.

Senzo urged him, “Then act as quickly as possible.”

Fang Heng rolled his shoulders. “No need to rush. Since I'm already here, I might as well help you dispose of the Federation and Dependent pursuers outside…”

After all, he'd come all this way.

And he'd happened to find the Tree Spirits' headquarters under attack.

As an ‘ally,’ shouldn't he help defend it?

And maybe farm another round of contribution points to exchange for Leaves of Life?

…

On the front line.

After the Tree Spirits activated their powerful interference field, the rate at which spatial-jump lights appeared outside dropped sharply.

Before long, the Root and Wood Clans arrived through hidden teleportation passages near the Leaf Clan settlement and joined the battle against the Federation.

By the time Fang Heng and Senzo returned to the front, the Tree Spirits had regained a degree of control over the situation.

More than a dozen Dependents remained behind the Federation's massive war machines, repeatedly firing high-frequency psychic beams at treants surging toward them from every direction.

They looked to be on their last legs.

Who'd have thought the Tree Spirits were this capable?

“My turn!”

Fang Heng's eyes lit up. Spotting the perfect opportunity to kick an enemy while he was down, he charged back into the battle before Senzo could say a word.

After being hunted by the Dependents for so long, he had quite a bit of resentment to work off.

Besides, this was the perfect moment to rack up kills and claim rewards!

(End of chapter)
