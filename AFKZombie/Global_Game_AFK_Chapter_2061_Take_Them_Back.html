Chapter 2061

Take Them Back

From original Chinese Chapter 2062 · 带回去

Above the Demon-Sealing Cavern, the Grand Adjudicator monitoring the magic array had barely had a moment to rest when another anomaly caught his attention.

Spatial fluctuations were coming from below.

Fortunately, they weren't particularly strong.

With the archbishop absent, the Holy Court adjudicators managing the formation weren't sure what to do. They discussed whether someone should go into the cavern to investigate.

“No!”

After a moment's thought, the Grand Adjudicator spoke firmly. “That man is cunning. He could be creating spatial fluctuations specifically to distract us and lure us into opening the cavern. With the archbishop still communicating with the Divine Realm, he may be trying to exploit our absence.”

“If the cavern erupts again while we're away from the array, we may be unable to suppress it.”

“Agreed. We should remain outside and keep guard until the archbishop returns.”

The others nodded. They had only just managed to stabilize the magic array after an enormous struggle. None of them could guarantee that the cavern was safe. Opening it now could invite disaster.

Staying outside was safer. The archbishop would be back soon.

“Besides, the cavern has specialized defensive barriers that interfere with spatial abilities. These fluctuations aren't especially strong. He's probably just putting on a show.”

That made sense.

With the three cardinals and Central Bishop Xilufei absent, they were already straining their abilities simply to maintain the array. They needed to be doubly cautious.

They would watch and wait.

The operators continued maintaining the main formation. As time passed, the spatial fluctuations below remained at roughly the same intensity. No one dared enter. They would wait until the archbishop finished communicating with the Divine Realm.

Minute after minute passed.

A full hour later, Central Bishop Xilufei and the three cardinal archbishops returned to the Demon-Sealing Temple.

They had successfully contacted the Divine Realm. The news of Fang Heng had attracted considerable interest there. Upon learning of his capture, the Divine Realm had praised Xilufei highly and immediately dispatched personnel to take Fang Heng into a high-level game world for judgment.

In twelve hours, divine adjudicators would arrive in Duncha to escort him back to the Divine Realm.

But almost as soon as Xilufei left the prayer hall, she heard of trouble with the cavern's magic array and hurried over.

“What happened?”

“Not long after Fang Heng was imprisoned, an extremely violent disturbance erupted below. We worked together to suppress it. Fortunately, Fang Heng didn't break through,” explained the Grand Adjudicator, wiping his forehead. “About an hour ago, we also detected sustained spatial fluctuations.”

Spatial fluctuations?

The archbishop felt uneasy. “Did you go down and investigate?”

“Not yet. The fluctuations were weak, and we feared it was a trap set by Fang Heng, so we stayed here to maintain the suppression array.”

“You made the right decision. Remain here and continue operating it. I'll—”

Xilufei was still speaking when an immensely powerful spatial fluctuation exploded from the cavern below!

Every face changed.

This was bad! Such powerful spatial energy…

“Something's gone wrong!”

“Stay here and operate the suppression array!”

“Understood!”

…

Meanwhile, deep inside the cavern, the miniature spatial teleportation hub gradually solidified before the prisoners' eyes. A spatial gate began to coalesce.

The prisoners stared at it with disbelief.

It had worked. It had actually worked!

Even the evil spirit's hollow eyes registered surprise.

Hiss…

With a violent surge of spatial energy, the spatial rift device tore open a blue portal in the center of the hub. Blue light flickered along its edges, crackling repeatedly.

Time to begin!

Fang Heng fixed his eyes on the portal and directed his zombie clones to move.

Several Lickers darted out of the portal and immediately formed fleshy cocoons on the floor.

Hiss, hiss!

The cocoons expanded to more than ten times their original size.

Pop!

They burst open, and fused Tyrant forms emerged.

After everything Fang Heng had done, the prisoners were once again struck speechless.

What was this now? Some kind of specialized undead-control ability? It didn't quite look like one.

None of that mattered. They were far more curious about what he was doing. The teleportation portal was already open. Why wasn't he escaping?

The fused Tyrant forms walked to the flowerbed and gripped the clusters of Luminous Lattice Stone with both hands.

Crack, crack…

The crystalline clusters creaked under their grip. Unconsciously, the prisoners began holding their breath.

BANG!

With a tremendous heave, a fused Tyrant tore a cluster of Luminous Lattice Stone free!

The prisoners watched it break the sacred cluster off, hoist it onto its shoulder, and carry it back through the teleportation portal.

What…?

They were stunned. He had opened a spatial passage just to carry these things away?

Fang Heng looked toward the ceiling. The flowerbed held many clusters, and the cavern's magic array hadn't weakened from losing one. In fact, its response had grown more intense.

Blinding light shone from the broken ends of the lattice stones.

But the fused Tyrants had already switched to their Sacred Studies form, so the sacred energy didn't affect them. They made repeated trips through the teleportation hub, carrying the stones back to Zombie Apocalypse.

“Hey! Kid!” the demon shouted. “The Holy Court will have sensed a spatial hub that powerful! They'll be on their way here already! Let us out! We can help you hold them off!”

Fang Heng suddenly turned toward the demon.

The demon's heart lurched. An inexplicable fear seized him, and he didn't dare speak further.

After witnessing everything Fang Heng had done, the prisoners had developed a measure of awe toward him—an awe born of his demonstrated strength.

“Heh.”

Fang Heng gave a cold laugh and looked away.

He knew the demon wasn't wrong. The Holy Court was probably already on its way.

It was time to act.

But he couldn't just let this group of prisoners stroll through his portal into Zombie Apocalypse.

With that thought, he looked up at the array overhead. A magic formation spun rapidly within the pupil of his right eye.

— End of Chapter 2061 —
