Chapter 2950

Construction

From original Chinese Chapter 2951 · 建造

Five days later.

Just as Fang Heng and the others had expected, there was still no word from the Federation rescue ship.

Whenever they asked, the answer was the same: the starship was still being inspected and couldn't undertake any missions.

With help nowhere in sight, the soldiers left behind grew increasingly troubled.

Fortunately, they still had plenty of energy and food, enough to last a long time. And there appeared to be no swarm threat on this planet, so the temporary base remained relatively safe.

But...

When could they go home?

"Colette."

Early one morning, Vion arrived with two soldiers. "Where are Fang Heng and his people? I don't think I've seen them once all day yesterday."

"I don't know."

Colette replied coldly.

Caught between the two sides, he wasn't having an easy time of it either.

He knew Fang Heng had been in contact with Dr. Li Zhixuan recently, and that they appeared to have grown quite close.

Under her leadership, the entire research department had been working day and night. They had even completed a second-stage expansion of the Kinsfolk warp passage two days earlier.

And from the look of things, Fang Heng and his players weren't worried at all. They had occupied a base compartment, barred outsiders from entering, and settled in as though they intended to stay for months.

Colette was curious about what they were doing, but he genuinely didn't dare ask.

What if he found out something he shouldn't know?

Would he then have to report it to his superiors?

Better to stick to his own duties and leave everything else alone.

Vion said darkly, "I've heard that Fang Heng took the entire research team out of the ship just now. You don't know about that either?"

Hmm?

Colette was startled.

He truly hadn't heard a word about it.

Vion was furious.

Headquarters had explicitly ordered the research team to suspend its work, yet the researchers had ignored the command and continued working around the clock under Li Zhixuan.

And Colette was turning a blind eye to all of it. He almost seemed to be taking Fang Heng's side.

Whose side was he on, anyway?

And now this!

Fang Heng had apparently taken the researchers right out of the ship.

Who knew what sort of tricks he was planning!

"Report!" A soldier hurried into the reception room and spoke quietly to Colette. "Sir, Fang Heng's group has been seen transporting some reserve supplies out of the temporary base."

What the hell?

Vion was even more astonished.

They were moving supplies outside the base?

Had they lost their minds?

The moment reserve supplies left the energy shield, the storm would begin corroding them. Even a brief exposure would cause damage, and eventually the items would be ruined beyond use.

What was Fang Heng trying to accomplish?

"Come on. Let's see."

Colette and Vion returned to the main control room and observed the area outside through the external cameras.

They saw Wayne's team moving crate after metal crate into the entrance of an underground passage a short distance from the temporary base.

Everyone exchanged looks.

A tunnel entrance?

Could it be...?

The two men looked at one another, a strange expression in their eyes.

Surely not?

Had Fang Heng already started building an underground base?

Hadn't they established that the planet's sand was too unstable for tunneling?

Meanwhile, underground.

After several sleepless nights, Li Zhixuan's research team had finally developed a corrosion-resistant alloy framework ahead of schedule.

Without an alloy-smelting system, Fang Heng used alchemy magic arrays to perform the initial refining.

It was slower, but he had zombie clones to help.

After two days of work, they had finally completed the first foundation with the zombies' assistance. They reinforced an underground area with corrosion-resistant alloy framing, finishing the first stage of their subterranean base.

After all, Fang Heng had built a base from scratch during his early days in the Zombie Apocalypse. Doing it again was much easier with countless zombie clones and advanced precision components supplied by the Kinsfolk system.

The researchers now followed an alloy-framed corridor into the underground base.

Astonishment!

Every last one of them was genuinely amazed.

They had provided the blueprints themselves, after all.

When Li Zhixuan had first suggested this building plan, the researchers had thought she was insane.

They had tested the local geology.

The sand was so fragile that supporting such a structure would require enormous amounts of concrete and an external corrosion-resistant alloy framework.

Forget the materials. Even with everything they needed, building a project of this scale in such conditions should have taken six months!

Yet how much time had passed since they'd handed over the blueprints?

The underground facility was already at least 80% complete. The basic structure of each room was finished. The place was crude and empty, but it was real.

Disbelief shone even in Li Zhixuan's eyes.

"Everyone, the underground base's air-purification system has been installed, and all materials for the antigravity system are ready. At this stage, I need you to focus on assembling the antigravity equipment. Afterward, we'll connect to the Star Map network and complete the third stage of the Star Map warp-system upgrade as quickly as possible."

The antigravity equipment needed adjustments to local conditions, and the zombie clones weren't intelligent enough to do that work. Engineers had to install it themselves.

The researchers nodded and began assembling the antigravity equipment under Wayne's direction, completing the underground fortress so everyone could move around the safe zone without protective suits.

When no one else was nearby, Li Zhixuan approached Fang Heng and said quietly, "Fang Heng, once the Kinsfolk network and warp system reach Stage Three, we'll be able to transport most materials. We can even submit ore from this world directly to the Kinsfolk in exchange for points. But we still can't send living beings through the passage."

"I understand. So?"

"That means that without a Federation starship to collect us, we may never be able to leave. And privately exchanging ore for Kinsfolk Contribution under these circumstances could make the Federation even more hostile."

"Hostile?" Fang Heng gave a dismissive laugh. "Don't worry. If the Federation won't come to collect us, they can keep waiting at headquarters. I have my own way to get you out."

Li Zhixuan froze for a moment, then asked, "I see. You're planning to post an alliance assistance request through the Kinsfolk network? But we lack a starship and don't have the permission required to post missions there."

She thought Fang Heng was oversimplifying things.

If the Federation Council intended to force his surrender, it would naturally have blocked every alternative route in advance, leaving him no way out.

"No." Fang Heng shrugged and walked toward a passage to the right. "Come with me. I have something good to show you."

Something good?

Puzzled, Li Zhixuan followed him into a sealed hall on the right.

"Th-this is..."

The moment she saw the blue three-dimensional hologram hovering at the center of the hall, her expression changed. She wheeled toward Fang Heng, eyes full of disbelief.

A starship!

An enormous capital-class starship.
