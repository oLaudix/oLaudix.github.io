Chapter 3132

Law Orbs

From Chinese Chapter 3133 · 法则宝珠

Almost none of the dead had obvious wounds. Their hands were clamped tightly around their own throats, and their blood-smeared faces were twisted into horrifying expressions.

Who was the intruder?

They'd never seen anyone die in such a bizarre way.

Moreover, the black-mist aura they'd just sensed didn't belong to any known academic discipline.

Qi Yuntao looked at Edmond, who was being helped over by two warriors, and felt a flicker of surprise.

Edmond ranked among the strongest of all their warriors.

“How did you end up in such a state?”

“Don't ask. That intruder was incredibly strong, and his abilities were bizarre. Good thing you arrived when you did. Otherwise you'd be collecting my corpse too…”

Edmond gave a self-deprecating laugh and pressed a hand to his chest, still shaken.

He remembered being trapped in that oppressive, pitch-black environment, his heart racing uncontrollably and his blood pumping faster and faster. It had felt no different from approaching death.

Fortunately, as the Kinsfolk reinforcements arrived one after another, his frantic heartbeat and surging blood had finally begun to settle.

“What about the Kinsfolk elders?”

Edmond turned toward the metal door and nodded. “They've been inside the room the entire time. The intruder never entered it. I don't think there's a problem.”

“You've worked hard.”

Qi Yuntao nodded to Edmond, went to the door of the Kinsfolk chamber, and bowed slightly. “My lords, the crisis has been resolved. The black mist has dispersed.”

Hm?

Why was there no response?

They waited in silence for a full minute. When the door still showed no sign of opening, unease gradually crept over everyone.

Qi Yuntao's expression tightened. He addressed the door again. “My lords, the black mist has completely dissipated. Are you all safe? If you have no objections, please permit us to enter and check on you.”

There was still no sound from within.

Something was wrong!

The sense of dread among the group grew stronger.

Qi Yuntao looked at Edmond, giving him a meaningful glance.

Hadn't he said nobody had entered?

Edmond frowned deeply, equally perplexed.

He hadn't lied. He'd done everything he could to prevent the intruder from entering the Kinsfolk's meeting room. There were still no signs of forced entry on the door.

No one could possibly have gotten inside!

Qi Yuntao reached past Edmond and pressed a hand against the door.

It was still locked.

“My lords, forgive us. We need to enter and make sure you're safe. Pardon the intrusion.”

As Qi Yuntao spoke solemnly, a fierce burst of energy erupted from his hand.

“BOOM!!!”

A breach was blasted through the specially made alloy door.

Qi Yuntao pulled the opening wider until he could force the door open completely.

When everyone finally entered and saw what lay inside, their limbs went cold, as though they'd been plunged into an icy abyss.

It was over!

The entire room was littered with the exploded remains of mechanical armor.

Every high-ranking Kinsfolk official had died in the hall, without exception.

This was a catastrophe, and they were certain to be held responsible!

Qi Yuntao's face turned deathly pale. He stood frozen for a long moment before ordering grimly, “Report to Kinsfolk headquarters immediately! Lock down this entire building! Preserve every piece of data from tonight. Everything must be thoroughly investigated!”

“Yes!”

…

“Ring, ring!”

Late that night, Anxi was awakened by the shrill ringing of her phone.

Ten minutes later, she hung up. Cold sweat soaked her back, and she no longer felt the slightest trace of sleepiness.

The Noel Clan had received word that the Kinsfolk corporate headquarters had been attacked that night, resulting in the deaths of several warriors.

Rumors were also circulating that a number of high-ranking Kinsfolk officials had been killed.

What a coincidence.

Could it have been Fang Heng?

Anxi felt deeply unsettled.

Thinking back to what had happened while she was driving Fang Heng to the villa the previous night, she became increasingly convinced that he might be involved.

Especially after hearing the report describe an unfamiliar special ability used by the intruder, one that closely resembled black mist.

She herself had seen a very similar black mist from the car the night before.

But still…

Could Fang Heng's personal strength really have reached such a terrifying level?

Had he single-handedly infiltrated Kinsfolk headquarters, slipped past so many high-tier warrior guards, and secretly assassinated an entire group of Kinsfolk elders?

Anxi took a deep breath to calm herself, then called Fang Heng.

After a while, Fang Heng answered.

“What is it?”

“Sorry to disturb you, Mr. Fang Heng. We've just received urgent news. Two hours ago, the Kinsfolk headquarters was attacked by black mist. Some Kinsfolk elders are believed to have died, and the Kinsfolk are furious.”

Anxi spoke cautiously, probing for any reaction in Fang Heng's voice.

“Mm… and?”

“And… Mr. Fang Heng, to avoid getting caught up in unnecessary trouble, please try not to go out for the time being. We still recommend that you stay at Noel Clan headquarters for a while…”

“Understood. I'm not going to let something like this interrupt my cultivation. Also, please investigate my two friends as quickly as possible. That's all. I'm hanging up.”

Anxi lowered her phone and gave a bitter smile.

She'd expected exactly that response.

As for whether Fang Heng had anything to do with the attack on the Kinsfolk, she genuinely couldn't say for certain.

On the other end of the line, Fang Heng also put down his phone.

As it happened, Anxi had called just after he returned to his villa.

This time, he'd used almost exclusively undead abilities to conceal his involvement.

Whether the Kinsfolk would ultimately follow the trail back to him was another matter. At least he'd taught them a lesson and vented his anger properly.

But this wasn't going to be the end of it.

Fang Heng returned to the seventh basement level, took out the pale-purple light orb again, and infused it with psychic power to examine Administrator Lila's report.

Soon, he followed the list of the so-called fourth batch of test subjects and continued investigating.

Soon he began looking more closely into the section concerning the list of fourth-round test participants.

Oh? It was actually connected to the Law Orbs he'd found earlier? Different warriors varied in their sensitivity to Law Orbs, and Lila's fourth-round candidates were required to have relatively high sensitivity.

In other words, the test participants needed to be capable of making the greatest possible use of the Law Orbs' effects.

Fang Heng continued reading and couldn't help feeling surprised.

The Kinsfolk had secretly joined forces with two other ruling races of the Seven Realms Martial Capital to carry out a research project called 'Heliom'. It required large numbers of warriors as experimental subjects, including powerful high-tier fighters.

Three phases of testing had already been completed.

They were now preparing for the fourth.

Could this be the real reason why the Kinsfolk and the other two ruling races had suddenly begun recruiting so many warriors recently?

Fang Heng considered the possibility and kept reading.

After participating in the 'Heliom' experiments, thirty percent of the test warriors experienced significant improvements in strength, with many advancing directly by an entire tier.

Of course, there had also been numerous failed cases in which warriors died outright.

Notably, several successful subjects had experienced such tremendous breakthroughs that they were believed to have surpassed the limits of high-tier warriors, perhaps even exceeding the level of honored-guest warriors.

The Kinsfolk believed that perfecting this research would completely disrupt the balance of power in the Seven Realms Martial Capital, enabling them to establish a new order through it.
