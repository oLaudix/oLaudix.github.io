Chapter 3061

Danger

From Chinese Chapter 3062 · 危险

“Hiss, hiss... hiss, hiss...”

Under the effects of the Eyes of Hell, the energy shields outside the ships began to sizzle at once. The vines rapidly absorbed and consumed their energy!

Only now did the allied alien species finally realize something was wrong.

Wait a minute!

Those vines...

They seemed to be coming after them!

“Attack the vines!”

The ships gradually reacted, directing their energy weapons at the vines clinging to their shields.

But the vines had formidable defenses.

Even when an energy beam shattered them completely, fresh tendrils quickly spread across the shields to replace them.

Damn it!

Their ships simply lacked the firepower to deal with these vines!

Worse, the centers from which the vines were spreading were the Shadow Clan vessels. With the situation unclear, the allied species dared not attack the Shadow Clan ships themselves.

And there was still a huge swarm of troublesome alien insects outside to deal with.

“Quick! Prepare an emergency warp! Disengage first!”

Seeing his ship's energy reserves plummet in such a short time, an alien captain broke out in a cold sweat.

The worst part was that he still didn't understand what was going on.

Hadn't the vines come from the Elven Clan's ship and the Shadow Clan's ships?

Were they enemies or allies?

Who cared?

If the situation made no sense, they could always flee first. Staying alive came before anything else!

The control room operators were sweating too. “Report! The fleet flagship has locked the warp zone... We can't execute the warp command.”

“Break away from the fleet! Force a warp transfer!”

“Space around the ship is unstable. Forcing a warp may fail.”

The captain roared, “Are you deaf? I said force the warp!”

To hell with whatever was happening.

There was no point worrying about their allied forces or the comrades still carrying out the mission.

Run! If they fled quickly enough, perhaps they'd survive.

“Understood!”

Chaos spread across the allied fleet. As matters grew steadily worse instead of better, some ships soon attempted to escape by force-warping.

Once someone took the lead, other vessels followed, all attempting to flee while entangled in vines.

Warp failed!

Points of light began to gather around the ships, only for violent spatial fluctuations to disrupt them, causing the warps to fail.

Only the two vessels that had detected trouble first and fled immediately managed to escape the crisis.

The rest had reacted a moment too slowly. Once vines covered most of a ship's shielding, warp controls could no longer function properly.

Forced attempts ended in failure and inflicted massive damage on the warp systems.

Fang Heng stood aboard one of the Shadow Clan ships, watching vessel after vessel become ensnared in Abe Akaya's vines, a faint smile touching his lips.

Having advanced through the power of Hell, Abe Akaya's vines now possessed multiple additional effects.

For instance, pupils resembling the Eye of Envy could cast illusions and corrode energy barriers. Once the vines entangled something, they also inflicted spatial tearing, generating fluctuations that prevented the captive object from escaping through spatial abilities. Tiny thorns could absorb energy, among many other effects.

And Abe Akaya had far more hidden abilities than those.

“Report! Spatial turbulence is increasing sharply. We... we can't teleport...”

Ship after ship discovered that even emergency warps would not work. They were trapped helplessly beneath the coiling vines!

Despair gradually seized the entire fleet.

The alien crews could only watch as layer after layer of dark vines spread across their ships' shields. Eventually, all they could see through their screens were dense tendrils and tiny pupils blinking between them.

The outer shields' energy plunged. Finally, with a loud bang, a shield shattered, and the vines immediately pressed against the hull, engulfing the vessel completely.

They wormed in through every entrance and even the smallest cracks.

“Report! Vine intrusion detected!”

Before the alien soldier could finish, a deafening crash rang out. A massive vine outside the control room slammed into its window, leaving a vast spiderweb of cracks.

The room fell silent.

The windows of ships capable of traveling through outer space were made from materials of the highest strength.

Yet a single impact had done this?

“Bang! Bang, bang!”

The tip of the vine outside twisted into a spiraling point and rammed the glass again.

“Thud! Thud! Thud, thud!”

Every blow left another deep spiderweb of cracks.

Until the cracks joined together.

“BOOM!”

The entire outer window shattered with a roar.

A dense tangle of vines poured through the opening.

“Fire!”

The guards in the control room raised their weapons and opened fire wildly at the vines.

It was useless!

The vines withstood most of the beam weapon fire. Smaller tendrils shot in from all directions, wrapped tightly around the crew's ankles, and hoisted them violently into the air.

The captain felt something snag his feet. The next instant, his entire field of vision flipped upside down, and a single thought flashed through his mind.

We're finished!

...

Meanwhile, the Shadow Clan's main force was still trapped in an immense sea of Lickers.

Wave after wave of alien insects fell.

Yet their numbers seemed endless.

The Shadow Clan commander had already realized the problem.

The insects' special abilities were extremely difficult to deal with. Killing even one for good was troublesome, and the battle would likely drag on for quite some time.

Suddenly, several squads received emergency signals from their ships.

Despite the powerful electromagnetic interference and spatial fluctuations in this area, certain species possessed special means of transmitting information, so simple signals could still get through.

“Commander, several allied species have received warnings from their ships. The vessels we left behind may be under attack by the insect swarm.”

After receiving the reports from several allies, the Shadow Clan grew wary as well.

They believed their ships' defenses should have no trouble handling mere insects.

But the battle against the alien insects had already ground to a stalemate.

Continuing to fight was unwise.

They might as well withdraw and protect their vessels first.

The Shadow Clan made the correct decision and ordered the entire force to retreat in an orderly fashion.

Korkalot also learned of the attack on the fleet over the communications network, and his heart immediately sank.

So this was a diversion, huh?

He had long suspected that Fang Heng would use the absence of the allied fleet's main forces to raid the ships, but he wasn't confident it would succeed.

Korkalot considered delaying the others to give Fang Heng more time. Unfortunately, several allies had received emergency signals and the Shadow Clan had already ordered a retreat. Unable to stop them, he could only try to warn Fang Heng so he could prepare.

“Fang Heng, I've done all I can. Just don't die too horribly.”

There was nothing else Korkalot could do to help. He could only mutter those words silently to himself.

What he didn't know was that...

Fang Heng was already wrapping things up.
