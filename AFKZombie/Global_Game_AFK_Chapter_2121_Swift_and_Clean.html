Chapter 2121

Swift and Clean

From original Chinese Chapter 2122 · 利落

Ji Haiting accepted the tablet his subordinate handed him. As he watched the security footage, his brow furrowed.

So it really was an invasion in the literal sense.

At first, he'd assumed some fool had broken into the institute's external network.

But the surveillance footage made it clear that the entire research institute had descended into chaos.

Those creatures...

Ji Haiting stared intently at the Lickers on the screen, lost in thought.

He had never seen crawling creatures like the ones rampaging through the institute!

They were incredibly fast and agile, but their most terrifying quality was their absurd capacity for self-healing.

Even after beam weapons pierced their bodies, they could fully recover in a single second and charge back into battle!

Ji Haiting asked in a low voice, “Has the special-ability squad deployed?”

“Not yet. They're still guarding the entrance. We've also reported an S-class emergency at the institute to our superiors. A Federation emergency response squad is already on its way.”

A trace of interest flickered across Ji Haiting's face. He nodded. “An ordinary Federation emergency team won't be able to stop them. Have Boris bring his semi-mechanized modification squad here. And awaken the reserve members of Project Y. All research specimens are to enter combat mode. Activate them immediately.”

His subordinate was taken aback.

“Director, shouldn't we submit a report first?”

“This is an emergency. I have emergency authorization. Activate them at once!”

“Understood!”

“And prepare to collect the experimental subjects' data.”

“Yes, sir!”

· · ·

Meanwhile, Fang Heng was steadily advancing toward the institute's lower levels, destroying every surveillance camera along his route.

He had to admit that converting all his zombie clones into Lickers had dramatically improved the horde's overall capabilities!

Above all, they were fast!

The difference between a Licker's movement speed and that of an ordinary zombie was like night and day.

They were clearing out small fry and exploring more than ten times faster than before.

At the same time, Fang Heng began directing the newly spawned mutated infected zombie clones to search the research institute for relevant documents.

“Looks like that fellow can't sit still anymore.”

Through his senses, Fang Heng noticed Ji Haiting's marked position had begun moving. He muttered to himself and picked up his pace.

Ahead of him, the zombie horde had already broken through the first underground level and was pushing toward the second.

That was...

Standing at a corner, Fang Heng watched the fighting farther down the corridor, his expression growing serious.

There it was again.

A psychic barrier!

More than a dozen psychics in blue-striped uniforms blocked the far end of the corridor, using all their strength to maintain a psychic barrier that sealed the passage completely.

Meanwhile, their specialized psychic piercing attacks could penetrate the barrier and continuously injure the Lickers on the other side.

One Licker after another was lifted slowly into the air by psychic force. Gaping tears appeared across their bodies.

The wounds refused to heal, and blood continued to flow.

[Game Hint: Your zombie clone (modified Licker) has been controlled by psychic power and entered an 'Out of Control' state.]

[Game Hint: Your zombie clone (modified Licker) has entered a Bleeding state and suffered 102 damage...]

[Game Hint: Your zombie clone (modified Licker) has suffered 335 damage from the Psychic Laceration skill...]

Troublesome!

Fang Heng glanced at the game notifications and cursed inwardly.

The enemies' offensive power wasn't high enough to kill the Lickers quickly with psychic abilities alone, but they were still holding up the horde.

The corridor was simply too narrow. It was all too easy for the Lickers to get stuck.

And while Fang Heng found this troublesome, the twenty-odd psychic modification specialists of Wanling Technology were having just as miserable a time.

None of them had ever encountered such hideous monsters.

They moved with incredible speed, struck with frightening force, and even possessed a horrifying infection ability.

But what troubled them most was the Lickers' extraordinary vitality!

They had to suspend the Lickers in midair with their psychic abilities, batter them with psychic force, and simultaneously suppress their wounds' natural regeneration.

Actually killing a single Licker took two or three of them working together for four or five minutes!

It was agonizingly difficult!

And there was an endless-looking mass of Lickers gathered outside the barrier.

What were they supposed to do?

For a moment, the psychic squad members felt almost hopeless.

They were barely hanging on!

Suddenly, the captain of the psychic modification squad froze.

He'd spotted a figure at the entrance to the passage.

That person...

His build was remarkably similar to the man in a photograph the captain had just seen.

Looking closer, the figure wore a mask.

There was no mistake!

It was him!

Fang Heng!

An exact match!

Only an hour earlier, the team had received Fang Heng's photograph, and he had been designated an S-class dangerous individual in the company's internal database.

The captain stared at Fang Heng in utter disbelief.

How was this possible?!

Chairman Ji had only met him a little over two hours ago!

Ji Haiting had needed spatial transition to get back here. But this man?

Never mind how he'd managed to find the place—the two locations were so far apart that driving would take more than ten hours.

How had he pursued them here so quickly?

“Y-you... Fang Heng?!”

Hearing someone call his name, Fang Heng nodded almost unconsciously, then raised a hand and pointed forward.

What?!

The captain's heart lurched when Fang Heng pointed at him. An intense sense of foreboding washed over him.

In the next instant, tiny white points of light appeared around the Lickers gathered at the passage entrance.

What were those things?!

Bathed in the white lights, the Lickers gradually blended into the surrounding space.

Until they vanished completely!

They'd disappeared!

What was happening?!

Before the psychic modification squad behind the barrier could make sense of it, a chill ran down their spines. It felt as though something had appeared behind them.

They turned to see tiny white points of light gathering at their backs!

Not good!!!

The captain suddenly realized what was happening and shouted, “Quick! Fall back! Now!”

But it was already too late!

The ripples of spatial power spread rapidly!

One after another, Lickers burst out of secondary spatial projections and lunged at the twenty-odd psychics!

Psychic modification specialists were best suited to keeping their distance and fighting from afar. Once an enemy closed in, their frail bodies and slow movement became crippling weaknesses!

Boom!

In desperation, the captain unleashed a Psychic Blast, forcibly knocking away several Lickers attacking him head-on!

But before he could draw breath, several more Lickers pounced from either side!

Trapped in the narrow passage, they had nowhere to run!

Slash!

A Licker's razor-sharp claws carved a long wound across the captain's body.

Blood sprayed across the floor.

Within moments, the Lickers had devoured every psychic modification specialist in the passage and resumed their pursuit deeper into the institute.
