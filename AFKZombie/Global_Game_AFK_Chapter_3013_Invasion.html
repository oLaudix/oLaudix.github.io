Chapter 3013

Invasion

From Chinese Chapter 3014 · 入侵

"Pfft! Pfft!"

The next moment, both elves spat blood and collapsed unconscious.

Fang Heng frowned deeply.

A conflict of mental powers.

Someone had implanted a mental suggestion in them beforehand, with extremely rigid restrictions. The clash between the two mental forces would probably leave both elves insane even if they regained consciousness.

"The Elven Clan..."

Fang Heng murmured to himself, then walked to the alchemical magic array and examined it.

It was a small teleportation array.

Its function was to transport certain objects to a designated location.

He looked at the jars and bottles arranged around it.

They contained various plants.

He'd heard Klevi liked growing strange plants at home.

Did the elves want those plants?

"Mr. Fang Heng? Mr. Fang Heng! Are you all right?"

Urgent shouts sounded through his earpiece.

"Mm, I'm fine." Fang Heng touched the device. "The problem's solved. You can come in."

Outside, Lu Yunhao's emergency team had heard a few muffled bangs over the connection, followed by silence. They'd been considering whether to move in. On receiving Fang Heng's answer, Lu Yunhao immediately signaled his squad to enter.

The response team stepped into the villa and saw two unconscious elves and a mass of exploded flesh in the room.

"Boss Fang, these two..."

"Unconscious. The other one is dead."

Lu Yunhao cautiously approached Fang Heng, who was crouched near the array at the center of the hall. "May we take these two for questioning?"

"Mm. Take them. They're elves, not players, and they're quite strong. Be careful."

"Absolutely."

Lu Yunhao ordered his men to take the prisoners away, then regarded the array before Fang Heng with a bitter expression. "Boss Fang, what's going on here?"

"I came to investigate the disappearance of a half-elf friend. This villa is her home."

Fang Heng explained, "This is a special elven teleportation array. By the time we arrived, the elves had already transported some of the plants from these bottles and jars elsewhere."

"Plants?"

Lu Yunhao grew curious and crouched to inspect them. "Mr. Fang Heng, may we take these plants back for examination?"

Fang Heng was about to reply when he noticed one jar contained a cutting from Abe Akaya.

Klevi had indeed mentioned trying to combine Abe Akaya with unusual species of plants to promote its further evolution.

"Mr. Fang Heng? Mr. Fang Heng?" Lu Yunhao asked quietly.

"Mm. Take everything back and examine it. Tell me immediately if you find anything."

"Of course! Thank you for your support!"

...

Of the three elves, two had lost their minds and one was dead. The trail had gone cold for now.

The Federation probably wouldn't uncover much more useful information.

Judging from the current evidence, Klevi had most likely gone to the elven world.

Still, wandering around like a headless fly wasn't a plan. Fang Heng could only ask around for a way to reach that world.

Returning to the World of Ten Thousand Forests, he settled back into cultivation.

For a change of pace, he switched to Elemental Studies.

Specks of natural energy gathered and condensed. With Abe Akaya's help, they were absorbed and flowed into Fang Heng's body.

As the natural power circulated, his bloodline ability converted it into Qi.

Qi was then converted again into pure elemental energy.

Fang Heng regulated his breathing and maintained a calm mind.

As he cultivated, game notifications gradually appeared.

[Hint: Player gained 12,192 Master Elemental Studies EXP...]

[Hint: Player's Master Elemental Studies has risen to LV: 21.]

[Hint: Player gained 12,192 Master Elemental Studies EXP...]

After an unknown interval, a dark-red warning suddenly appeared.

[Hint: Player gained 12,192 Master Elemental Studies EXP...]

[Hint: Abe Akaya's energy is being absorbed.]

[Warning: Due to an unknown force, Abe Akaya's EXP is decreasing.]

Huh?

What was going on?

The notifications abruptly jolted Fang Heng out of cultivation.

Energy being absorbed?

How?

Looking more carefully, he saw Abe Akaya's energy declining at an extremely slow rate.

"Abe Akaya! What's happening?"

Abe Akaya immediately established a mental communication link.

Just now, an invisible power had begun affecting it, slowly drawing energy from its body.

The force was highly unusual and entirely one-sided, a predatory extraction. The best Abe Akaya could do was resist with all its might and sharply slow the rate of theft.

Missing Klevi... the Elven Clan... Abe Akaya...

Fang Heng frowned slightly.

It was all connected.

The elves' real target was very likely Abe Akaya.

He asked, "Can you determine where the power is coming from?"

"You can't...?"

Receiving Abe Akaya's negative reply, Fang Heng frowned even more.

Abe Akaya possessed enormous reserves of energy. At this slow rate of depletion, the immediate impact was insignificant.

But if it continued, the enemy might increase the rate of extraction.

He needed to solve this quickly.

While he was thinking, Abe Akaya's teleportation array began operating.

Alec stepped out of the array with Lu Yunhao.

"Your Majesty, this man said he needed to see you."

After traveling through one teleportation passage after another, Lu Yunhao was somewhat dizzy. Seeing Fang Heng, he quickly nodded. "Boss Fang, we've learned something about the Elven Clan and came to tell you immediately."

"Do you have any clues to the coordinates of the elven world?"

"Unfortunately, no." Lu Yunhao shook his head. "But we've confirmed something through the Iron-Eating Beast Clan. Elves have appeared among the Kinsfolk's allied forces. They rarely deploy with entire fleets. Usually one to three of them join individual fleets as mercenaries."

"Individual elves are among the strongest fighters of all races, so fleets welcome them. But the Elven Clan is extremely secretive and has never revealed its world's coordinates."

"We've learned something else. While carrying out missions, the elves have also been asking around for information about the Tree of Life or Tree of Creation."

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

"The Tree of Life..."

He remembered.

Klevi had called Abe Akaya that before.

"Mr. Fang Heng, the two elves we brought back have completely lost their minds. We can't learn anything more from them. This is all we have for now, but we'll continue gathering information and contact you immediately if we discover anything."

"All right."

Fang Heng nodded and signaled Alec to escort Lu Yunhao out.
