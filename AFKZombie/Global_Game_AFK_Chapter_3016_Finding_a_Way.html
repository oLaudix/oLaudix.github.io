Chapter 3016

Finding a Way

From Chinese Chapter 3017 · 想办法

"We've run into a rather difficult problem." Jing'er spread his hands helplessly. "Come with me. You'll understand when you see it."

The fat man cautiously stepped forward and helped the badly injured Liu Mo up. Anger and resentment covered Liu Mo's face.

Jing'er led Fang Heng down to Hell's twenty-ninth layer and headed deeper inside.

Soon they reached a cavern.

Fang Heng glanced around and saw more than twenty Demon Seeds gathered there.

Some lay on the floor, others leaned against rock walls, and a few hung upside down from the ceiling.

Noticing Jing'er personally escorting someone, many looked at Fang Heng curiously.

Most Demon Seeds were proud and unruly. Their appraising gazes carried open provocation.

"Fang Heng, Echidna occupies the thirtieth layer and guards the Demon Cores. To avoid trouble, we need to conceal our auras."

After giving that warning, Jing'er nodded to Zhuang Yufei.

She snapped her fingers, enveloping the three of them, Jing'er included, in a dark protective veil.

"Let's go. I'll show you the Demon Cores."

After winding through a maze of underground tunnels, Jing'er brought Fang Heng to the entrance of Hell's thirtieth layer and stepped through first.

The moment they entered, a cold aura swept over them.

Fang Heng frowned slightly.

Hell usually felt oppressively hot. That heat constantly stirred up negative emotions.

But the thirtieth layer was different. It felt chillingly cold.

Looking closely, he saw pale-blue, mucus-like material coating the rock walls.

Not just the walls—even the surfaces of the ubiquitous lava pools were covered by a slowly moving layer of pale-blue matter.

"That's Echidna."

"This is probably your first time hearing the name. In short, Echidna is one of the Guardians of the Hell Abyss and rules layers twenty-one through thirty."

As they proceeded, Jing'er explained, "Envy has already taken control of her, making Echidna her servant. She's been assigned to guard the Demon Cores."

Seeing how cautiously Jing'er behaved, even concealing his aura, Fang Heng asked, "You seem awfully careful. Is she that strong?"

"She's strong enough." Jing'er nodded. "But the real problem is Eseklyna, the Tongue of Envy."

"Eseklyna has placed a Tongue of Envy projection here, allowing her to sense what's happening at any moment. If a crisis occurs, her true body can enter this place directly through that projection."

"We can handle Echidna, but we can't kill her without alerting Envy."

"I understand."

Fang Heng nodded.

Soon Jing'er led him to a broken cliff wall.

Before it lay what should have been a broad pool of flowing magma. Now it glowed an eerie blue and radiated a bone-chilling cold.

Zhuang Yufei pointed above the lava pool. "Fang Heng, there."

He looked upward.

Five Demon Cores, each about the size of a human head, hung above the frozen magma, rotating slowly in a circle around a towering central pillar.

Blue liquid condensed from the magma below and crept up the pillar like a spiderweb, spreading until it covered all five cores and linked them to the pillar as a single whole.

"Those are the Demon Cores corresponding to our five sources of power."

Jing'er explained coldly, "The blue magma you see, and all the blue material we passed on the way here, are parts of Echidna's actual body."

"That blue mucus is spread throughout layers twenty-one to thirty. It's all her eyes, allowing her to detect approaching danger in advance."

"Fortunately, her senses aren't particularly sharp. By hiding our auras, we can observe this place before she notices us."

Zhuang Yufei shrugged helplessly. "But however weak her perception is, the moment we try taking a Demon Core, Echidna will notice. And then the Tongue of Envy will sense something wrong too."

"See that? That's the Tongue of Envy."

Fang Heng raised his head toward the high ceiling Jing'er indicated.

Above the slowly circling cores, something soft and tongue-shaped hung from the cavern roof, swaying gently.

Looking closely, tiny eyes could be seen covering its tip.

"Ordinarily, Envy controls Echidna through her Tongue. In our last battle against her, she remotely manipulated the Demon Cores, interfering with our origins and draining a huge amount of our power."

Fang Heng rubbed his chin and considered the five floating Demon Cores.

Zhuang Yufei said, "Our plan is to split into two groups. One stays here to watch. The other travels between worlds, recruiting scattered Demon Seeds. Once we've gathered enough strength, we'll find a way to divert Envy's attention and destroy Echidna as quickly as possible while she's distracted."

"Hmph." Jing'er snorted. "Without the Demon Cores, Envy would be within our power to destroy completely."

Zhuang Yufei continued, "As you can see, Fang Heng, we're the group waiting here for an opportunity. Bai Mu is leading the others between worlds. They don't seem to have made much progress yet, but you've arrived."

After hearing the full explanation, Fang Heng thought briefly, then asked, "How much time would you need?"

"What?"

"With your present strength, assuming Envy doesn't interfere, how long would it take to kill Echidna and retrieve the Demon Cores?"

Zhuang Yufei and Jing'er exchanged a look.

"Echidna is powerful, and with her special abilities, she can draw on the Demon Cores to interfere with us if threatened..."

Jing'er interrupted, "Three minutes if we're fast. Ten at the most."

"Is that so...?"

Fang Heng calculated silently.

Even ten minutes, then.

That wasn't particularly long.

"Don't wait for Bai Mu. Prepare to move. I'll find a way to draw Eseklyna's attention and hold her off for ten minutes."

Fang Heng shrugged and continued, "Once you've secured the cores, we'll regroup and deal with Eseklyna together."

Jing'er's face shifted. He stared at Fang Heng.

"You're that confident?"

Both Jing'er and Zhuang Yufei could sense Fang Heng had become much stronger since their last meeting. Even so, neither had imagined he'd be so arrogant as to believe he could restrain Eseklyna alone.

They exchanged another glance.

Confidence itself was no bad thing.

But failure could ruin the Demon Core operation they'd planned for so long. If Envy discovered their attempt, a second chance would be much harder to find.

"Relax." Seeing their doubts, Fang Heng said, "Don't forget, there's no Demon Core here belonging to me. Envy can't use one to interfere with my power. I'm the best person for this task."

Jing'er asked, "How do you intend to draw Envy's attention?"

Fang Heng curled his lip. "By borrowing the power of sacred light, of course."
