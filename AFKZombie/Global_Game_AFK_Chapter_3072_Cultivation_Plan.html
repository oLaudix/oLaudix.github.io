Chapter 3072

Cultivation Plan

From Chinese Chapter 3073 · 修炼计划

Three hours later, Fang Heng arrived at Noel Clan headquarters once again, accompanied by Anxi.

“Mr. Fang Heng, please follow me.”

Anxi led him into the building and toward the elevators.

The first-floor lobby was crowded with young reserve warriors trained by the Noel Clan. When they saw Fang Heng, their hushed discussions stopped almost simultaneously. They watched him and Anxi with respectful gazes as the pair headed upstairs.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital had been unsettled for several months. Three overlord powers, including the Kinsfolk, had been frantically recruiting powerful high-ranking warriors. Many fighters working for the Noel Clan had been approached and poached by rival factions.

The Noel Clan valued peace and had always treated its warriors generously.

But generosity sometimes invited exploitation. The clan's contracts were excessively lenient, making it the hardest hit by the current recruitment turmoil and weakening its forces more than most.

The Noel Clan's leaders hadn't reacted much, but the young warriors raised by the clan were far from pleased.

Between the rumors and the endless poaching of their own people, they felt sick with frustration.

As Fang Heng passed, he caught fragments of conversation but paid them no attention. He followed Anxi into the elevator and up to the eighth floor.

“Mr. Mu Shunan, your mysticism instructor, is also an outside warrior recruited by the Noel Clan. Like you, he isn't originally a member of the clan. He's attained Demi-God-level mysticism.”

Anxi explained this as she knocked and gently opened the door. “Mr. Mu, we're here.”

Mu Shunan had been waiting in the quiet training room for some time.

He was a little surprised by Fang Heng's youth. He rose and greeted him with a smile. “Welcome, welcome.”

Anxi stepped outside and closed the door behind her.

“Since you're also a recruited warrior, we might as well treat each other as friends.”

Mu Shunan looked friendly and studied Fang Heng with interest. “I don't think I've seen you before. Were you recruited recently?”

“Mm. I passed the assessment this month.”

Fang Heng nodded.

“Is that so? You came at a fortunate time.” Mu Shunan chuckled. “It wasn't so easy when we were competing for warrior positions. Still, don't let your guard down. This month's assessment will be another hurdle for new recruits.”

Fang Heng paused.

Since joining the Noel Clan, he'd been cultivating in his villa's basement and had almost forgotten about the monthly performance requirements.

But he remembered Anxi mentioning that the initial requirements for new recruits were fairly lenient. They only needed to win three duels.

Fang Heng asked, “Are those duels difficult?”

“Mm. They aren't that simple.”

Mu Shunan nodded and explained, “Warriors recruited by the various powers in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital are generally divided into high, middle, and low tiers. Anyone who has never participated in a duel is rated low-tier by default. As your rank improves, you'll receive more resources and support within the Noel Clan.”

“You arrived at an opportune moment. The great powers have been recruiting heavily lately, so warriors' market value has risen significantly.”

Mu Shunan frowned unconsciously. “And I don't know who started it, but the recent arena matches have become increasingly brutal. People are getting seriously injured or killed left and right.”

“Many Noel Clan warriors have been poached, and others died in arena matches. We have a lot of vacancies.”

“That's why you're fortunate. Two years ago, with all positions filled, the final recruitment assessment required you to challenge an existing warrior. Now that stage has been removed, and you can earn promotion directly. Isn't your batch lucky?”

Fang Heng nodded to show he understood.

He'd thought the Noel Clan's entrance assessment was suspiciously easy at the time.

And considering the teaching resources available to him here, the arrangement was an incredible bargain.

“For newcomers, winning three duels each month is the real test. Your actual strength only becomes clear in the arena. And with the current rules, it's not just you newcomers under pressure—it's hard on us too.”

Mu Shunan shook his head. “My mental power suffered some backlash during a battle last month. I've been teaching to ease the pressure while I recover. Anyway, enough of that. Let's begin.”

“Good.”

Mu Shunan gestured toward the cushion opposite him. “Concentrate. Don't resist. Let me see how well you've mastered mysticism.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng sat up straight and extended his wrist.

“Whoosh!”

Mu Shunan flicked his wrist and lightly pressed his right index finger against Fang Heng's pulse.

Something was strange.

A thread of Mu Shunan's mental power flowed swiftly through Fang Heng's body. His brows furrowed again.

What was wrong with this kid?

Why did he seem like a complete outsider to the discipline?

“You...”

After sensing for a moment, Mu Shunan opened his eyes and looked at Fang Heng, uncertain how to put it without sounding offensive.

“Never mind. Let's start from the beginning.”

Mu Shunan was a patient man. “Mysticism began as the study of mysterious phenomena. At first, we merely learned about creatures connected to mysticism. Later, we discovered that certain hand seals, runes, and chanting rhythms could resonate with mystic powers and improve the effects of other academic disciplines.”

“Once mysticism reaches the intermediate and advanced stages, we gradually strengthen the body so it can bear greater amounts of mystical power.”

“Memorize this set of secret hand seals and practice it diligently every day. First circulate your mental power through all the meridians in your body, gradually allowing your body to adapt to the intense flow.”

As he spoke, Mu Shunan formed seals before him and broke down an entire sequence of 128 hand gestures, teaching Fang Heng each one.

In fact, using seals to regulate one's meridians was part of the breakthrough into advanced mysticism.

To Mu Shunan, Fang Heng didn't even seem to have studied advanced mysticism.

How to put it?

Well, since they were already here, he might as well teach him everything.

Mu Shunan assumed Fang Heng was a scholar of some other elemental discipline whose mysticism level was low, seeking further improvement by hiring a Grandmaster instructor.

“All right.”

Fang Heng nodded and carefully observed every movement Mu Shunan made, engraving each variation into his sea of consciousness.

It wasn't difficult.

Following dimensional enhancement, Fang Heng's mental attributes were exceptionally high. Just watching attentively once was enough for him to reproduce almost every movement and rhythm.

Once Mu Shunan finished demonstrating the complete sequence, Fang Heng closed his eyes, recalled it, and began reproducing all the movements.
