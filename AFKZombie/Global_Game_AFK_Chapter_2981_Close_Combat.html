Chapter 2981

Close Combat

From Chinese Chapter 2982 · 近战

“BOOM!!!!”

Sura caught Fang Heng in a momentary pause and concentrated all her strength into one punch.

“BANG!!!”

Fang Heng's entire right arm was smashed to pieces by the blow!

Yet a moment later, a strange power caused his arm to grow back and recover completely!

All contempt vanished from Sura's eyes, replaced by admiration. “Fang Heng, I'm surprised you lasted so long against my attacks using physical strength alone. But this is where it ends. If that's all you can do, you won't be able to keep me here. Why don't we call it quits...”

“Whoosh!!”

Sura suddenly froze in the middle of speaking.

What?

“Whoosh!!!”

The projection of a strange magic array appeared beneath Fang Heng's feet.

Dense black mist poured furiously from the array.

At almost the same instant, the surrounding space warped unnaturally. Thick vines and clusters of trees burst out of the array and the distorted space all around them!

What was that?

Surprise flickered in Sura's eyes.

Descent of the Tree Realm!!

This was the first time Fang Heng had used this move in the real world!

It worked almost exactly as it did in the game world.

Under the domain skill's influence, huge masses of trees instantly surrounded him.

The trees seemed alive and sent countless thorny vines stabbing toward Sura!

“Hmph!”

Sura snorted, clenched her right fist, and thrust it forward.

“BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!!!”

The force of that single punch shattered every vine of Abe Akaya that had lunged at her!

By now, the various races that had scattered across the area sensed something unusual and looked toward the Crocodile Clan ships.

Crocodile Clan members aboard several nearby vessels were particularly stunned.

What the hell?

Green vines and roots were suddenly growing from every window and crack in one of their own ships.

The Crocodile Clan guards all looked toward the supreme commander of their fleet.

“What's going on?”

The fleet captain was dumbfounded too.

That was clearly a ship under his command.

What was happening aboard it?

Before the commander could order anyone over to investigate, two consecutive explosions rang out.

“BOOM! BOOM!!!”

With two muffled blasts, two figures shot up through the top of the vine-covered starship.

A moment later, dense vines heaved the entire roof of the ship's cabin up and tore it open!

Everyone stared at the two dark figures launched from the ship.

Who were they?

Vines and roots surged toward the pair, enclosing both combatants in their midst.

Their explosive speed, coupled with the mass of roots and vines, made it impossible for the Crocodile Clan to see what was happening. They could barely make out two figures fighting inside the vegetation.

“BOOM!!!”

Sura's eyes widened with astonishment.

Fang Heng had suddenly vanished before her eyes.

What?!

He could increase his speed even further?!

To the point where even she couldn't follow him for an instant?

To the right!

Alarm flashed through Sura's mind.

He could still change direction so nimbly at such speed?

BOOM!!!

A fist smashed straight into Sura's cheek!

Her entire head exploded, and her body was sent flying!

While she was still airborne, fine worms twisted and stretched out from her severed neck, rapidly rebuilding her head. She turned toward Fang Heng, her expression grim.

He was moving so fast that she could barely track him; she had to rely on her senses to locate him.

It wasn't just his speed. His strength had increased explosively too.

But the surges seemed to come in bursts rather than remain constant.

How was he doing that?

Some special skill?

Sura studied Fang Heng.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and examined Sura as well.

Her regeneration was every bit as terrifying as his. Like him, she was almost impossible to kill.

Then let's go again!

Annual Rings—Acceleration!

Whoosh!!!

Fang Heng became a dark blur again, almost disappearing in midair!

Sura's pupils contracted. She immediately expanded her senses to search for him.

“BANG!!!”

Behind her this time!

“BOOM!!!!”

Even though Sura sensed Fang Heng's position in advance, she reacted a moment too late. His blow split her body into two pieces!

The tide turned in an instant. Fang Heng attacked Sura again and again, blowing her apart in midair, only for her body to reassemble and regenerate each time.

Whoosh!

After regenerating yet again, anger flashed through Sura's eyes. “Fang Heng, I'll show you my trump card!”

Whoosh!!

Sura's speed also surged once more!

Hm?

She actually blocked him?!

Surprise flickered in Fang Heng's eyes as he threw another punch.

“BOOM!!!”

The tremendous collision blasted apart Fang Heng's right arm and Sura's left arm!

Heh!

So that was all.

Again!

They clashed over and over, their bodies exploding into masses of flesh in midair, then recovering through their monstrous regenerative abilities before slamming into each other once more!

“BREAK!”

Fang Heng roared and drove his right fist forward, smashing Sura's head apart again!

At the same time, Sura punched a huge hole through Fang Heng's chest where his heart was!

Both were hurled backward!

“Abe Akaya!”

BANG!!!

As Sura flew through the air and her shattered head began to heal, dense vines of Abe Akaya surged toward her from the ground. They bound her body tightly, wrapped around her layer after layer, then yanked her violently down into the earth!

“Haah!”

Fang Heng landed at the same time, gasping for breath. The hole in his chest closed rapidly. He looked up, staring at Sura, now pinned down by Abe Akaya and the World of Ten Thousand Forests.

“Bang! Bang, bang, bang!!!”

A deep impact rang out.

A circular crater nearly a hundred meters across suddenly exploded into the ground.

Sura stood at its center, drenched in blood, but her wounds had already begun healing at astonishing speed.

She still wasn't dead!

“Then try this!”

Seeing the World of Ten Thousand Forests approaching its limit, Fang Heng abruptly raised a hand toward Sura.

Having expended so much regenerative power, Sura was already at the end of her strength. She was about to attack again when her expression suddenly changed.

What was that?

The power of blood?

Sura's face changed. She discovered that some of her regeneration had been suppressed. Instead of coagulating and healing, the blood in her wounds grew hot and continued pouring out.

At the same time, a gray, gloomy aura rose from the spilled blood.

Aging, decay...

That aura was rapidly destroying her body.

Whatever it was, this power was particularly effective against her.

A long-range magical ability?

Sura raised her head toward Fang Heng. Her sharp gaze gradually softened, and a flicker of resignation appeared in her eyes.

— End of Chapter 2981 —
