Chapter 2097

Misjudged

From original Chinese Chapter 2098 · 走眼

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

The repeated bombardment blasted an opening through the mass of corrupted demons blocking their path!

The other players stared at Fang Heng in surprise.

They'd misjudged him. He'd been loafing around at the back of the team the entire time. Who'd have thought he possessed this kind of strength?

"Now's our chance! Move!"

The players recovered and rushed toward the opening carved by the Book of Oaths!

Fang Heng checked the book's remaining energy, continued controlling it, and fled forward with the team.

For the most part, he returned to letting the others do the work. Only when absolutely necessary did he use the Book of Oaths to blast another opening.

They staggered and fought their way ahead. At last, just before the book ran out of energy, they managed to join the Holy Court reinforcements waiting beyond the horde.

"Come! Follow us! We'll escort you out!"

Under the protection of the rescue team, they quickly withdrew from the area covered by the Demon Seed of Envy.

The moment they passed beyond the shadow of the clouds, Fang Heng felt the pressure on his body ease.

He looked up again.

Hidden within the clouds, the purple eyes that had been staring at him slowly faded from view.

Before long, the heavy black clouds overhead began gathering toward their center, gradually dispersing.

The players had just survived a desperate fight and almost lost their lives. Now they collapsed wherever they stood, sitting or sprawling on the ground.

Watching the dark domain retreat, they felt an overwhelming urge to curse.

What was that supposed to mean?!

They'd finally managed to escape, and now the black mist was withdrawing?

Had the damn thing been chasing them on purpose?!

[Hint: You have successfully escaped the Seed of Envy's Domain.]

Finally...

Only after seeing the notification did Fang Heng relax. Like the other players, he sat down on the ground and gasped for breath.

The drastic loss of attributes had taken a heavy toll on his stamina.

But this wasn't the time to rest.

He needed to use this opportunity to find a way out of here.

Once Envy's domain fully dispersed and the turmoil inside the Eternal Holy City ended, the Holy Court would undoubtedly investigate the incident in detail.

If they weren't careful, that investigation might lead straight to him.

He needed to get away quickly!

"Brother, thanks for what you did back there. Without you, we'd all have been finished."

Fang Heng turned to see a player team leader approaching. The man nodded his thanks.

"You're welcome. We helped each other. I couldn't have escaped on my own either."

Fang Heng nodded, forced his exhausted body upright, and quickly studied their surroundings.

Although he'd left the Eternal Holy City fortress, the Crusader Trainee Mark on his hand was still disabled. He couldn't use it to exit the Eternal Holy City directly.

Outside, the Holy Court had already begun taking its next steps.

The legion commander left some personnel to tend the wounded and watch for possible subsequent corruption. Another elite team was formed to enter the region where the demonic domain hadn't fully dissipated, reconnoiter the situation, and try to establish contact with the forces still inside.

Under the gazes of the other players, Fang Heng approached a paladin captain who was maintaining order nearby.

"Captain Jess."

Fang Heng looked at him. "My Crusader Trainee Mark has stopped working, but I'd like to leave the Eternal Holy City right now. Is there some way you could help me return?"

The paladin captain looked surprised and studied Fang Heng a little more closely.

"Now? Is it that urgent?"

Fang Heng frowned, then nodded with a grave expression.

"Forgive me, sir. I have a very uneasy feeling. I'd like to withdraw and leave this place immediately. I'm sorry to trouble you."

"Uneasy?" the captain repeated, frowning.

"I have an innate ability that lets me sense certain dangers. This place may still be unsafe, so I'd like to leave as quickly as possible."

The surrounding players reacted immediately. Their faces changed, and quite a few stood up at once.

An uneasy feeling?

An innate ability?

He'd practically spelled it out for them!

A talent!

A natural danger-sensing talent!

That meant the danger wasn't over!

Who knew when that terrifying corruption might sweep over them again?

Besides, Fang Heng had used his Book of Oaths to blast a path through the demons and help everyone escape. The players had developed a certain trust in him.

Someone quickly raised a hand.

"Sir, forgive me, but I also have an urgent matter and need to leave the Eternal Holy City as soon as possible."

As more players demanded to leave, unease spread through the group.

Those who didn't understand the situation saw everyone else preparing to flee and began to feel that remaining was a trap. One after another, they raised their hands as well.

At first, the paladin captain hesitated. But seeing so many otherworlder players insist on leaving, he finally nodded.

"Very well. I'll need to report this to the legion commander."

He returned a short while later.

"If you wish to leave immediately, you must first undergo Holy Purification to confirm that you haven't been corrupted by the Demon Seed. Afterward, we'll escort you to the teleportation area."

"Also, the Eternal Holy City's teleportation passages can reach a great many worlds, but some places beyond the Holy Court's sphere of influence cannot currently be reached."

Fang Heng was delighted.

The purification was merely a superficial test for demonic corruption. It posed no problem for him.

At last, he could leave this damned place!

· · ·

Within the Eternal Holy City, still enveloped by the Seed of Envy's domain, more than a dozen Crusader instructors were relying on the holy domain projected by the central divine statue to withstand the invading demonic power!

Light and darkness constantly intertwined inside the temple.

The second-stage Glutton's Touch worms, now berserk, twisted and mutated, their bodies wrapped in black mist. Again and again they hurled themselves against the holy domain, only to be blasted backward each time.

Finally...

As the outer demonic domain began fading, the strength of the worms' attacks dropped sharply.

The Crusader instructors exchanged glances, then struck together, instantly annihilating the remaining Glutton's Touch creatures.

A deep exhaustion settled over everyone.

The Seed of Envy's attack had devastated much of the Eternal Holy City, and huge numbers of Holy Court believers had been corrupted and transformed into demonic creatures.

The only blessing was that the God-Blessed Statue of Zeus had escaped damage during the battle.

But still...

A Demon Seed!

Its appearance hung over the Holy Court like a dark cloud.
