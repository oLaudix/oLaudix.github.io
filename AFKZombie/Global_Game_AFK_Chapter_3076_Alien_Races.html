Chapter 3076

Alien Races

From Chinese Chapter 3077 · 异族

Liu Hu didn't think much of it and explained readily, “Because everyone's physical constitution is different. From birth to adulthood, the level generally rises gradually. Also, different races use secret techniques to control bloodline purity and maintain political stability. Take the Noel Clan. Only those old fellows on the Council of Elders can reach Level Forty-Two bloodline strength. Ordinary clan members are usually granted around Level Thirty. Arena warriors may receive a partial unlock, bringing them to Level Thirty-Five or Forty.”

I see.

Fang Heng roughly understood.

It was just like how he controlled the Vampire Clan through dimensional power.

His own dimensional level was currently LV: 39.

Ordinary vampires didn't have their full dimensional potential unlocked. Fang Heng generally restricted them to LV: 20.

That was only Level Twenty, but it still represented an extraordinary enhancement compared with ordinary players.

Elite vampires who passed repeated assessments and demonstrated sufficient loyalty could receive higher dimensional levels.

Fang Heng had authorized several vampire princes, including Micah, to distribute dimensional power ranging from Level Twenty to Thirty-Five.

The Noel Clan and other species apparently followed similar principles.

Fang Heng cupped his fists toward Liu Hu. “Thank you for explaining.”

“Don't mention it, brother. None of this is secret. Anyone who asks around can learn it.”

Fang Heng pondered this.

So the Noel Clan's dimensional level cap was forty-five—or perhaps even higher.

No wonder they could become cosmic overlords.

“By the way, I'm curious. What's the Kinsfolk's dimensional strength level?”

“No idea. If I had to guess, maybe zero.”

“Zero?”

“Mm. These dimensional strength ratings are estimates derived from data collected at past arena matches. The Kinsfolk have never appeared in arena duels, so we can't calculate their rating.”

“I see.”

Fang Heng nodded and looked back toward the battlefield.

The two young warriors on the platform were fairly evenly matched.

Both possessed Master-level elemental abilities. Combined with their dimensional enhancements and racial talents, the resulting battle was quite entertaining.

At first, the young Noel Clan fighter had the advantage. But the opponent gradually counterattacked, and one capable Kata Clan newcomer won three successive rounds, building tremendous momentum.

The elder had expected something like this. He nodded. “Fan Chen, you're up.”

“Yes, Elder!”

Fan Chen had already prepared himself at the sidelines. His expression turned serious, excitement in his eyes, as he rose and walked onto the platform.

Cheers erupted throughout the arena again.

Fan Chen was relatively strong. Against inexperienced newcomers, he used his power and experience to win three consecutive bouts.

“Excellent!”

Liu Hu's blood boiled with excitement, and he repeatedly shouted encouragement.

The opposing side promptly changed tactics and sent out a muscular alien warrior.

“Trouble. He's from the Rock Clan. That guy is a mid-tier warrior. He won't be easy to handle.”

Apart from resting, Liu Hu had spent most of his time studying the arena. Seeing the next opponent enter, he grew solemn.

“Oh?”

Liu Hu explained, “The Rock Clan's dimensional levels range from twenty-five to thirty-five, and their racial talent is exceptional. They also have a secret technique that hardens their bodies until they're as tough as solid rock. They're born with an extraordinary affinity for natural studies, too...”

Fan Chen had already consumed a great deal of energy in three consecutive battles. His fourth opponent was stronger, and after exchanging blows for a while, he realized he couldn't penetrate the Rock Clan warrior's defense. He sighed inwardly.

His run was probably over.

Even so, Fan Chen gritted his teeth and fought on, determined to exhaust as much of the Rock Clan warrior's stamina as possible before finally conceding.

At this point, both sides had six victories apiece. The score was tied.

Winning just one more bout would be enough to make the final score ten to seven, a respectable result that would let them save face.

The elder looked over Fang Heng and Liu Hu, the two remaining fighters on the sidelines. “Liu Hu, can you handle him?”

Liu Hu was suddenly uncomfortable. He very much wanted to fight, but not right now.

The Rock Clan warrior's defenses were formidable, and Liu Hu's abilities were countered by them. He couldn't break through.

“Elder, his defenses are a bad match for me. If possible, I'd like to wait for another opponent.”

“That's fine. There's no need to force a battle against someone who counters you.” The elder nodded, then turned to Fang Heng. “What about you, Fang Heng? Can you fight?”

“Yes.”

Fang Heng answered, stood up, and walked toward the platform.

Liu Hu cheered him on. “Brother, good luck!”

His opponent was an alien with impressive melee capabilities and a strong constitution, complemented by unusual natural-study attacks.

Fang Heng noticed that the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's arena atmosphere was excellent. Whoever entered the platform—even an unknown newcomer fighting for the first time—received cheers and applause from the spectators.

The referee was a thin, gray-haired elder standing in the middle of the platform with his hands clasped behind his back. After studying the two contestants, he recognized that Fang Heng was new and repeated the rules. “Unrestricted combat. Surrender, incapacitation, or leaving the platform counts as defeat. Are you ready?”

The Rock Clan warrior stared at Fang Heng, adopted a combat stance, and nodded.

“Begin!”

“Whoosh!”

The instant the referee spoke, the Rock Clan warrior saw a blur.

What?!

The next moment, Fang Heng was suddenly standing right in front of him!

Before the Rock Clan warrior could activate his body-hardening technique, he heard an explosive bang. An enormous force struck him from the front, sending his body flying backward uncontrollably.

“Bang!”

The Rock Clan warrior stood up in bewilderment and looked around. He was already outside the platform.

He lost?

How had he lost?

Thinking back, it was as if an overwhelming force had suddenly pushed him straight out of the arena.

After several stunned breaths, the arena erupted in even louder cheers than before.

The giant screen replayed the encounter frame by frame using footage from an ultra-high-speed camera.

Almost at the same instant the referee had announced the start, Fang Heng had flashed forward at incredible speed and punched the space in front of the Rock Clan warrior, producing a visible shockwave that hurled the still-unprepared opponent off the platform.

“Victory to the red side.”

The referee raised his hand and looked at Fang Heng curiously.

An interesting newcomer.

On the Noel Clan's side, the first-time arena fighters erupted into excited cheers.
