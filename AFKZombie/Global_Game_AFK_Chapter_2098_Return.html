Return

"The circumstances of this incident are extremely strange. We must report to the Divine Realm immediately."

"Agreed."

The others nodded.

One Crusader instructor recalled something and added, "By the way, I remember an otherworlder from earlier. There were traces of Demon Seed energy on his body. This incident may have something to do with him."

"Very well. Have him brought here for questioning immediately..."

· · ·

By this time, Fang Heng had already entered the intermediate game world known as the City of Dreams.

It was a modern metropolis where many different faiths converged.

Among intermediate game worlds, the City of Dreams was considered relatively low level.

"What? You need to use our teleportation passage? To Duncha? That's hardly a pleasant destination, you know. The Holy Court and the undead are at war there. Of course, the customer is God, so it isn't impossible. But you'll have to pay a little extra..."

"Ah! Certainly. We provide our most valued customers with only the finest service."

As soon as Fang Heng placed ten Universe Crystals on the counter, the clerk's expression softened into a smile that was almost fawning.

"Please come this way. As a top-tier Black Pearl VIP cardholder of our Starfish Company, you're entitled to our very best service. Your teleportation will be ready within thirty minutes. Would you care for a cola?"

One hour later.

After several transfers, Fang Heng finally returned to Punoze.

Now that he was temporarily safe, he decided to visit Instructor Gu Luo and collect his quest rewards. Afterward, he could visit the undead faction and exchange his accumulated rewards there too.

He'd worked hard for a long time. It was time to relax a little and settle his accounts.

All his coming and going had earned him a considerable number of Main God Points. He could repay his previous debts and still have plenty left over.

At last, he could put the next stage of his zombie-clone enhancement plan into motion.

The Tower of Bones.

Guided by two undead attendants, Fang Heng climbed to its upper floors.

"Fang Heng, you've arrived."

Instructor Gu Luo greeted him with a smile.

He'd been waiting for Fang Heng.

Only moments ago, he'd received another piece of excellent news.

The Holy Court's Eternal Holy City had suffered a devastating attack from an unknown enemy.

As the issuer of the quest, he knew very well that Fang Heng was responsible.

To strike another crushing blow against the Holy Court while being hunted by the Divine Realm itself!

What could be more satisfying than that?

Gu Luo had been in a wonderful mood all morning.

The study of necromancy finally had a worthy successor!

In truth, he'd been in high spirits for some time because of Fang Heng.

His student had performed extraordinarily well—far beyond anything Gu Luo had imagined.

"Fang Heng, you've done splendidly. I'm extremely pleased with you. Sometimes I wonder whether I'm dreaming." Gu Luo patted his shoulder. "Now it's my turn to deliver the rewards I promised."

He gently placed a hand on Fang Heng's forehead.

Game notifications appeared.

[Hint: You have received Instructor Gu Luo's reward.]

[Hint: Your Advanced Necromancy has advanced to Grandmaster Necromancy (Level 1).]

Skill: Grandmaster Necromancy.

Skill Description: Allows you to use the vast majority of necromancy-related items and gain additional benefits from them. Necromancy skills receive an additional 30% effectiveness bonus.

Skill Description: Every increase in skill level grants +7 base Willpower, further extends your lifespan, and increases your soul's strength.

Skill Description: Maximum Level 50.

Description: Follower of death, you have ventured ever farther along the path of souls and mortality. Without realizing it, you've become qualified to teach disciples. But you know that this is far from the end. (You may expend skill experience to teach Basic Necromancy to any character.)

Fang Heng closed his eyes and felt the changes brought about by Grandmaster Necromancy.

Willpower.

Necromancy was different from his other skills: advancing it only increased one base attribute, Willpower.

According to the information players currently possessed, Willpower was connected to a player's resistance and their ability to recover mentally after suffering spiritual attacks.

Gu Luo nodded. "Gu Qingzhu mentioned that you've been rather short on Main God Points lately. So I've decided to award your additional compensation in Main God Points, to the extent I'm able."

[Hint: Your total completion rate for the quest Support Chiboreya is 3,577%.]

[Hint: For exceeding the quest objectives, you have received an additional 2,577,000 Main God Points.]

So many?!

Fang Heng was stunned.

He hadn't expected to receive another 2.57 million Main God Points from Gu Luo for completing the mission!

That solved his immediate financial difficulties!

Instructor Gu Luo really was a good person.

The more work Fang Heng did for him, the more he was rewarded. No unnecessary fuss or delays.

"Thank you, Instructor," Fang Heng said at once.

"There's no need to thank me. You've earned it."

The more Gu Luo looked at Fang Heng, the more satisfied he became.

"But I must remind you that the Divine Realm will not stop hunting you. For some time to come, you should be prepared for repeated clashes with the Holy Court."

"I understand, Instructor."

"You must be careful, of course. But remember that we of the undead faction aren't people who can be bullied at will. If the opportunity presents itself, strike back against the Holy Court whenever you can!"

Gu Luo gave a dismissive snort.

"And don't be afraid of causing trouble. If you run into a problem you truly can't solve, the undead faction will clean up the mess for you. Just flee back to our territory. We'll have your back."

Fang Heng's spirits rose. "Understood, Instructor."

"Good. So what are your plans now?"

Fang Heng paused, then shook his head. "I don't have any definite plans yet."

That was the truth.

If he had to name a priority, it would be finding a way to shake off the Holy Court's pursuit and deal with the Demon Seed inside him.

Gu Luo had expected him to say he wanted to advance his necromancy further. Hearing that he had no plans, he sighed inwardly.

"There's no need to hurry. We have more time than ordinary people to find our path, and you've already surpassed ordinary people by a considerable margin."

He continued, "Advancing necromancy to the Demigod level is extremely difficult. It's unlikely an opportunity will appear in the near future. Breaking through with outside assistance takes time as well. Whenever you've made your arrangements, you're welcome to come discuss it with me."

"Yes, Instructor. I understand."

Fang Heng nodded.

He was busy dealing with the Holy Court and had no urgent need to improve his necromancy. Besides, he genuinely didn't have time to undertake another advancement quest.

After collecting his rewards, he bade Gu Luo farewell and hurried back to the Zombie Apocalypse to begin the second stage of upgrades for his zombie clones.

Now that he had money to spare, it was time to go back and spend freely for a change.

First things first: he needed to strengthen the zombie army's combat capabilities!

— End of Chapter 2098 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Reward value inconsistency. The game message says 257.7W (= 2,577,000) Main God Points, while the following narration abbreviates the amount as 257W (= 2,570,000). The precise message value is retained.
  • Ranks. 宗师级亡灵学 is rendered Grandmaster Necromancy; 次神级 is rendered Demigod level.

Chinese Expressions

  • 顾客是上帝 (gù kè shì shàng dì). Literally “the customer is God.” Unlike “the customer is king,” the original expression specifically uses “God”; retained here in the clerk’s dialogue.
  • 擦屁股 (cā pì gu). Literally “wipe someone’s backside,” meaning clean up the consequences of their mess. Gu Luo promises the undead faction will handle the aftermath if Fang Heng gets into trouble.
  • 任人揉捏 (rèn rén róu niē). Literally “allow others to knead and squeeze us.” Gu Luo insists the undead faction is not something the Holy Court can bully at will.
