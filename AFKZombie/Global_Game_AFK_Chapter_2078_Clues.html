Chapter 2078

Clues

From original Chinese Chapter 2079 · 线索

Fang Heng immediately went along with their assumption.

“Mm. I found some traces, but I can't be certain yet. If you aren't in a hurry, I'd like to pause the mission and look for clues outside the cavern.”

Lindro's teammates accepted the explanation without suspicion.

“Sure. Need our help?”

“No. I have a special ability, so moving alone is more convenient.”

Fang Heng glanced at the cavern entrance. “There are five hours and thirty-five minutes left. If I haven't returned near the deadline, finish the remaining objectives yourselves.”

“No problem. Leave it to us,” Lindro replied.

Fang Heng had already completed almost all the mission. They could easily handle the last little bit.

“Good.”

Fang Heng set off toward the maple forest and quickly disappeared from view.

One particularly troublesome issue was that Eternal Holy City's trial was still monitoring him. He didn't dare take the risk of transforming into a vampire bat.

He had to travel on foot, which dramatically reduced his speed.

It took more than an hour for him to get out of the forest and reach a nearby village.

As soon as he entered, his heart sank.

This wasn't good.

The village had a medieval appearance. Technology in this world clearly wasn't advanced, and transportation would be inconvenient. The chances of finding a teleportation array had just plummeted.

…

[Hint: Your party has completed the trial mission.]

“What a shame.”

More than five hours later, Fang Heng sighed as the completion notification appeared.

A rotating sacred magic array formed beneath his feet, and his figure slowly disappeared into its center.

When he opened his eyes again, he was back in Eternal Holy City.

[Hint: You have crossed into another plane. The Divine Realm's pursuit team will attempt to locate you again in 7 + 14 hours (additional delay from the artifact's ability).]

[Hint: Your party has completed Crusader Trial Evaluation (A). You have received 5,000 Crusader Experience and 500 Crusader Points.]

He'd spent half a day on it, with nothing to show for the effort.

Worse, the time needed for the Divine Realm's pursuers to find him had fallen another hour and a half.

“Don't worry.”

Lindro materialized beside him and, seeing his expression, assumed Fang Heng was disappointed about the hidden quest. He gave him an encouraging pat on the shoulder.

“Hidden quests are difficult to find. There's nothing strange about missing one.”

“Exactly!” Sak joined in. “If hidden quests were easy to uncover, they wouldn't be hidden. We can try again next time.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng agreed and recovered his spirits.

They had a point. He could always try again!

And thinking back, he really had done everything possible.

Over the five hours, he'd asked villagers about the nearest major city, then traveled all the way there.

Because he was posing as a Holy Court member, he couldn't simply break the law to get what he wanted.

He'd eventually reached the city, but unfortunately there had been no teleportation passage there as he'd hoped.

All that time had been wasted.

Fang Heng quickly put the disappointment behind him.

It was only one failed attempt. Bad luck, nothing more. It wasn't a reflection of his strength or his judgment.

He raised his head toward his teammates.

“Everyone, if you still have time…”

Lindro and the others already knew what he was going to say. They nodded immediately.

“Sure! Let's go again!”

They'd rested for more than five hours during the last trial and had barely fought again until just before the time limit expired. They weren't tired at all.

“Brother Fang Heng, what difficulty should we choose this time?”

“A-class again,” Fang Heng said. “S-class is too uncertain. I also want more time to look around and improve the team's coordination.”

The others exchanged glances.

Improve the team's coordination? Did they really need to?

“Ahem.”

Lindro figured Fang Heng was simply a cautious person. He cleared his throat and drew everyone's attention back.

“Brother Fang Heng has a point. Let's follow his suggestion and try another A-class trial.”

…

Duncha game world.

Divine Adjudicator Okney and the Holy Court officials stood around the Greater Prophecy magic array.

Its rotation gradually slowed to a stop.

Okney opened his eyes again.

The tracking of Fang Heng had been interrupted yet again. That meant he had entered another plane.

In the span of just over twenty hours, Fang Heng had already jumped between spatial planes nine times!

So that was his plan—keep moving between worlds to evade their search.

Okney had to admit that Fang Heng had given them a difficult problem.

Let's see how long you can keep running!

He glanced over the other officials and nodded.

“Again.”

They nodded and restarted the magic array.

Meanwhile, Fang Heng and Lindro's team completed another mission and returned from the trial world.

Everyone looked toward Fang Heng.

As usual, he wore a rather bitter expression.

Lindro and the others exchanged weary glances, finding the whole situation increasingly baffling.

They'd completed four trials in barely more than twenty hours—an efficiency level that ranked among the very best players.

Yet Fang Heng's behavior made no sense to them.

He always came up with some strange reason to choose A-class commissions, and during every trial he somehow found clues to a possible hidden quest and went off to investigate.

Unfortunately, he failed every time.

Still, the others couldn't really complain. Fang Heng was simply too effective in combat to criticize.

On one occasion, he'd even beaten a trial boss down to its last sliver of HP, restrained it, and then waited until ten minutes before the mission deadline to finish it off!

After watching tactics like that, Lindro's party had stopped questioning him. They just let themselves be carried.

Fang Heng, on the other hand, was miserable.

He'd made two more attempts, and neither trial world had offered a way to escape.

Was it just bad luck? Or had the game deliberately designed trial worlds so that players couldn't leave them?

Six or seven hours was an awfully short time to search, after all.

Fang Heng patted his cheeks and forced himself to stay calm and think.

At least he still had plenty of time to consider a solution.

By continuing to jump between planes, he could keep the Divine Realm's pursuers from catching up—for now.

— End of Chapter 2078 —
