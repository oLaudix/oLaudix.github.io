Chapter 2389

Seventh Layer

From original Chinese Chapter 2390 · 七层

Fang Heng quickly examined the materials required to construct the array and frowned.

It was extremely difficult to build and required a special intermediate material called Abyssal Demonstone.

He'd never heard of the material, much less knew where to obtain it.

But…

Fang Heng looked toward the vast field of black pillars.

Perhaps he could tear them down and extract the Abyssal Demonstone directly from the wreckage. Or he could raid the Root Clan again.

The timing wasn't right for that, however.

He mentally added the stones to his list of things to loot and patiently observed the surroundings a while longer.

He still hadn't seen Pride. Was he in the deepest part of the pillar forest?

Fang Heng was wondering whether to investigate farther when he sensed a group approaching. He immediately slipped behind a pillar to watch.

Root Clan Great Elder Geoffrey was coming slowly toward him with a group of guards.

Geoffrey appeared rather anxious.

Urged on by another elder, the guards pushed several carts tightly bound in vines and roots toward the depths of the black stone forest.

Fang Heng examined the carts with interest.

Several layers of white cloth covered their contents, which were further secured with thick roots, making it impossible to see what they carried.

The carts looked extremely heavy, leaving deep tracks in the earth.

What was on them?

Materials for the special magic array, perhaps…

Fang Heng rubbed his chin and secretly followed.

Geoffrey and his party moved slowly into the forest until they stopped where the black mist was thickest.

In the passage ahead, dense dark energy blended into the surrounding space, creating bizarre fluctuations.

It looked as though the boundary between worlds was gradually being erased and merged away.

Black mist poured from the passage.

The Root Clan members stopped at its entrance, hesitant.

Geoffrey looked around at them. “Let's go.”

The group advanced again, pushing the carts into the passage until they disappeared into the fog.

Fang Heng emerged from behind his pillar and examined the twisting space ahead.

Good grief. Pride had already succeeded in opening a passage to Hell!

The area ahead was a point of fusion between the Inner World and Hell.

If he passed through that short stretch of distorted space, he'd probably enter Hell itself!

So Pride had already returned to Hell?

The Hell world seemed to exert a strange attraction, drawing Fang Heng toward it step by step.

Since he'd come this far, he might as well take a look!

Fang Heng pushed off lightly and dashed into the passage.

[Hint: You have entered a special area—Seventh Layer of Hell.]

[Hint: Due to your special effects, you are immune to the environmental attribute penalties here.]

Two game notifications appeared across his retina.

After passing through about five or six meters of fused and fluctuating space, the mist thinned considerably. The terrain opened up and his visibility mostly returned.

The air was oppressively hot.

Fang Heng concealed his form and aura and studied his surroundings.

Lava terrain. The familiar landscape of Hell!

Large numbers of Fire Demons lounged in the surrounding pools of molten rock.

In the distance, Geoffrey's party continued pushing the carts deeper into the area.

Fang Heng followed at a safe distance.

Geoffrey crossed the lava fields and finally entered a cave farther back.

Fang Heng could faintly sense powerful energy fluctuations from within.

A Demon Seed!

Pride was probably inside that cave!

He didn't dare approach too closely. Suppressing his aura to the absolute minimum, he crept to the cave mouth and peered cautiously inside.

Rows of dark-black altars stood throughout the cavern.

Black energy seeped continuously from them.

Pride sat at the center of the cave, apparently meditating.

He seemed to be absorbing the power of the black aura.

Altars!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes as he surveyed the many altars.

He had seen similar structures when he first entered the edge of the Hell world.

Chaos Power!

Pride was absorbing Chaos Power!

Fang Heng focused on Pride again.

Compared with their last encounter, his strength had increased enormously.

So he'd spent the past few days recovering his power here.

With his current strength and the favorable environment of Hell, could Pride defeat the pursuers from the Divine Realm?

Fang Heng grew increasingly excited at the possibility.

He looked up again. Geoffrey had brought his guards and carts before Pride.

He knelt respectfully. “Demon Seed Lord, we've prepared the materials you requested. We've also encountered some difficulties. There are many watchers around the camp. If we leave the primeval forest, we'll probably clash with the vampires and the humans…”

“A bunch of useless fools who only know how to make excuses.”

Pride opened his eyes, snorted, and cut Geoffrey off with a wave. “Forget it. Once I've completely recovered my strength, I'll destroy that world myself.”

Geoffrey lowered his head and dared not respond.

“Why are you still standing there? Leave the materials and get lost!”

“Yes.”

Geoffrey stood, waved his subordinates along, and hurried out with his clansmen.

Pride raised one hand.

“Bang! Bang, bang!”

The carts on the ground exploded, sending black mineral crystals concealed inside floating into the air.

“Whoosh! Whoosh!”

Black energy surged from Pride's body toward the crystals.

They slowly revolved within the mist, and dark light spread over their surfaces.

Watching from outside the cave, Fang Heng's heart stirred.

Those crystals looked remarkably like Abyssal Demonstone!

So Pride was personally manufacturing the material through conversion?

While Fang Heng wondered, game notifications appeared.

[Hint: The World of Myriad Forests' special reward-protection period has ended. Other players and factions may now enter the world through various methods.]

[Hint: The Divine Realm's pursuing team will attempt to lock onto your location again in 9 + 15 hours (additional time from an artifact skill) + 6 hours (due to the failure of the Descent passage in the high-tier game world Tapetel).]

Here it came!

The moment he saw the notifications, Fang Heng frowned and stopped watching.

He'd found Pride too.

Time to carry out his original plan!

Fang Heng turned and surveyed the cavern.

The Hell cave system was enormous, with many scattered grottoes beyond the region Pride occupied.

That one would do!

He selected a secluded cave and quickly slipped inside.
