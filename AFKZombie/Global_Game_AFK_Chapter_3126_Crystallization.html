Chapter 3126

Crystallization

From Chinese Chapter 3127 · 结晶

“Mm. I'd like you to investigate two people for me.”

“Of course. Do you have any information about them?”

“I have their names. Kappa of the Flying Squirrel Clan and Korkalot of the elves.”

Anxi quickly wrote down the names and nodded. “Mr. Fang Heng, I've already assigned people to investigate. I believe we'll have results soon.”

“Thank you.”

Fang Heng thanked her and closed his eyes to rest again.

Before long, Anxi's phone began vibrating repeatedly.

“Any news?”

Anxi examined the messages on her phone and frowned slightly. “Mr. Fang Heng, we've discovered that both of your friends have been arrested on charges of possessing and transporting prohibited items. They were detained by the Seven Realms Martial Capital's joint law enforcement department about a month ago.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng responded quietly, betraying no emotion.

More than a month ago.

Exactly when they'd parted ways.

As for the charges…

What the hell kind of contraband could Kappa and Korkalot possibly have been carrying?!

It didn't take much thought to figure out that the Kinsfolk were behind this.

Still, Fang Heng felt a little relieved.

“People don't get executed just for carrying and transporting ordinary prohibited goods, do they? Where are they being held? Is there a way to get them out?”

“This concerns a security agreement jointly signed by the Seven Realms Martial Capital's powers. Getting them released will be difficult. They're currently detained in the Northern District Prison, which is in Kinsfolk-controlled territory. The presiding judge is also one of the Kinsfolk. I suspect they've been implicated in some other matter, which is why they're being detained and put on trial.”

Anxi continued reading the messages and said softly, “We'll try, but given the recent tensions between us and the Kinsfolk, it's highly unlikely we'll be able to overturn their charges and secure an acquittal. We'll explore other ways to rescue them.”

“All right. Thank you.”

Fang Heng nodded.

Apparently, relying on the Noel Clan to rescue Kappa and Korkalot would be difficult.

If that failed, he'd find a way himself.

First he'd locate where they were imprisoned, then use force to take them out directly.

But that could cause quite a disturbance.

The Seven Realms Martial Capital was an excellent place to cultivate, and he hadn't finished his training yet. He didn't want to interrupt it so soon.

He needed a quieter solution.

As Fang Heng considered his options, he suddenly sensed several auras carrying killing intent. He raised his head and looked toward the road ahead.

Five figures stood shoulder to shoulder across the middle of the road.

Anxi, who was driving, felt her heart skip a beat.

Not good!

The car was out of control!

“Mr. Fang Heng, watch out!”

Anxi slammed on the brakes and wrenched the steering wheel to the right. Unable to control the vehicle, she swerved toward the roadside and crashed into the guardrail before finally coming to a stop.

“Mr. Fang Heng!”

Anxi turned toward the rear seat.

“I'm fine.”

Fang Heng remained seated in the back, watching the five figures approach through the window. “Looks like they're here for me.”

Anxi bit her lip tightly.

Fang Heng had only returned today, and the sole place he'd appeared publicly was inside the Noel Clan headquarters. Clearly, someone had leaked his movements.

There had to be someone inside the Noel Clan informing outsiders!

The Noel Clan knew there were traitors in its ranks, but no one had expected the Kinsfolk to move so quickly or so brazenly. They actually dared to attack him right in the street!

“Stay in the car.”

Fang Heng spoke calmly, looking at the five figures who had begun to surround the vehicle. He stepped out and walked toward the apparent leader.

“Looking for me?”

Fang Heng studied the five people across from him.

Each wore a mask. They had broad, bulky builds and didn't look human.

Judging by their auras, all five had reached the level of high-tier warriors.

Sending five high-tier warriors at once—the Kinsfolk were certainly willing to spend heavily.

The Kinsfolk had dispatched five high-tier warriors this time. One stared at Fang Heng and said coldly, “Fang Heng, you're quite lucky to have escaped the Lanyue Clan. But you're also a fool. If I were you, I wouldn't have come back to the Seven Realms Martial Capital. I'd have found some hole to hide in.”

Fang Heng glanced over them. “The Kinsfolk sent you?”

“Hmph. That's none of your concern.” The five appeared to regard Carlton as their leader. He took another step forward and continued, “The point is, someone wants you dead. How about making things easy for us and ending your own life?”

“Heh.”

Fang Heng laughed disdainfully. “Interesting. How about you kill yourselves instead?”

“Heh, heh.”

Carlton chuckled. “So you want to play with us.”

What Fang Heng didn't know was that almost every pan-human race suspected by the Kinsfolk had already been exterminated during this period.

The others had either fled the city entirely or accepted the Kinsfolk's psychic cleansing and joined them outright. Of all those still under suspicion, Fang Heng was the only one left in the Seven Realms Martial Capital.

The Kinsfolk didn't need to identify precisely what had caused the black mist. They could eliminate the threat simply by slaughtering every suspect.

“I'd really like to know what the Kinsfolk offered you to make you work so hard for them.”

“Benefits beyond your imagination,” Carlton replied with a cold smile. Then he barked, “Attack!”

At the same time, multiple waves of mental-control pressure surged toward Fang Heng.

Psychic Suppression!

With five high-tier warriors attacking simultaneously, Carlton had never considered the possibility that Fang Heng could turn the tables.

“Whoosh!”

Yet in the next instant, Fang Heng broke through the psychic and spatial-gravity suppression almost immediately. His figure became a black blur as he charged straight toward Carlton!

So fast!

Carlton's heart lurched.

He'd never imagined Fang Heng could move this quickly, especially under both psychic and spatial suppression. Alarm seized him.

Before accepting the mission, Carlton and the others had repeatedly studied recordings of Fang Heng's earlier arena matches. Believing they understood him thoroughly, they'd prepared specific combat tactics and hadn't underestimated him in the slightest. All five had attacked together from the start.

They'd never expected Fang Heng's speed to be on a completely different level from what he'd shown in the arena!

He hadn't been using his full strength back then!

Carlton felt a terrifying aura erupt from Fang Heng, as though it were about to devour him whole!

“Whoosh!!!”

“Whoosh!!!”

Fang Heng abruptly appeared right in front of Carlton!

Not daring to be careless, Carlton raised both arms in defense while his body rapidly entered a crystalline state.

Racial Ability—Crystallization! “Whoosh!!”

“Whoosh!!”

Fang Heng bent low and threw a punch!

“Smack!”

His fist slammed into the crystallized flesh of Carlton's lower abdomen.

“BOOM!!!”

A shock wave exploded outward!

Carlton was hurled backward by the blast, while the violent energy ripped the mask from his face.
