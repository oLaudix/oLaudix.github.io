Chapter 2987

Purification

From Chinese Chapter 2988 · 净化

Fang Heng deliberately controlled the rate at which he consumed mental strength. Each lightning strike was relatively weak and the intervals short, but they kept Proust continuously restrained, unable to break free, while slowly wearing him down.

Clearly, Proust's regeneration had been severely weakened.

It seemed the earlier Eye of Stars attack had worked extremely well.

Watching from afar, Mu Qingxin frowned to herself.

The energy beams Fang Heng had summoned were far more powerful than she'd expected, inflicting another grievous injury on Proust.

And now he had switched back to attacking with Divine Punishment...

Mu Qingxin studied Fang Heng.

Surely he knew that attacks of this level could not pose a fatal threat to Proust.

Was he stalling for time?

And the reason would be...

Mu Qingxin's heart suddenly lurched. She looked up again into the cloud-covered sky.

Most likely, the Eye of Stars could fire a second time!

“Whoosh!”

She could not wait any longer.

If this continued, Proust would lose!

Mu Qingxin chose to intervene.

She had spent tens of thousands of years cultivating this Ouroboros Seed.

She couldn't bear to watch Fang Heng destroy Proust before her eyes.

“Whoosh!!”

Mu Qingxin sped toward Fang Heng, ready to interrupt the battle, when a dark figure suddenly appeared in her path.

Hm? What?

“BOOM!!!”

The dark figure's punch forced Mu Qingxin heavily backward. When she looked up at her attacker, astonishment filled her eyes.

“Sura?! What are you doing here?”

Sura stood between her and Fang Heng. She shook her head calmly. “That isn't important, Mu Qingxin. I understand how you feel. But I promised Fang Heng that I wouldn't let you interfere in their battle.”

Mu Qingxin's heart sank.

Damn it!

Fang Heng had come thoroughly prepared. He'd even arranged for this in advance?

Mu Qingxin asked coldly, “What did he offer you?”

Sura said nothing, merely watching her.

“Whoosh!”

Mu Qingxin became a shadow, attempting to rush past on the right.

Sura moved like a ghost and appeared before her again, blocking her path.

“Move!”

Mu Qingxin snapped, driving her right fist toward Sura.

“THUD!!”

Sura's expression hardened. Without another word, she struck back.

The two became dark blurs in midair, continuously colliding with one another.

Their strength was close enough that Mu Qingxin knew she couldn't break through Sura's blockade in a short time.

Worse still, Sura had always believed in an evolutionary path based purely on physical strength, without excessive fusion with other powers.

“BANG!!”

After several minutes of fighting, they exchanged a full-force blow and both retreated.

Mu Qingxin grew more anxious by the moment.

Yet the more desperately she struggled, the harder it became to break free.

This was bad!

Looking up, she saw violent spatial fluctuations erupt once again. The cloud layer was pushed aside by warped space, exposing a metallic cannon muzzle.

The alloy barrel locked firmly onto Proust in the center below.

With Sura keeping her occupied and no means of escaping, Mu Qingxin could only shout, “Proust! Get away from him!”

“I know!”

Proust was still pinned down by Divine Punishment, unable to move. His bloodshot eyes remained fixed on Fang Heng.

“Fang Heng! Are you insane?!”

Having already endured one Eye of Stars barrage and suffered grievous damage, Proust had no desire to survive another. He glared at Fang Heng and shouted, “Let me go!”

Fang Heng stared back with the look of someone willing to perish together with his enemy. A mocking smile crossed his lips. “What? Are you afraid of death, Proust?”

Fear finally appeared in Proust's eyes. “You lunatic!”

“BOOM!!!”

The next instant, a purple annihilation beam poured down from above, enveloping both of them!

“BOOM!!”

Game notifications flashed repeatedly across Fang Heng's retina.

Under the Eye of Stars beam, the fundamental power of his Ouroboros Seed was slowly being weakened.

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

He knew the Kinsfolk had developed these annihilation beams specifically to counter Ouroboros Seeds, but he hadn't expected them to be this powerful. They could even damage the Seed's origin!

Nevertheless...

It was still within the limits he could bear!

Fang Heng stared intently at Proust, already reduced to an unrecognizable mess by the annihilation beam.

Keep wearing him down!

Fang Heng held the advantage over Proust in every respect!

Watching from the distance, everyone was stunned by Fang Heng's apparent willingness to trade his life for Proust's. Like a madman, he had chosen to endure the beam alongside his enemy, and they could only stare at the purple light falling from the sky.

For a full five minutes!

When the light dissipated, the two figures came back into view.

After two concentrated Eye of Stars attacks, Proust's origin power had suffered enormous damage and his regeneration was severely suppressed. His health had fallen to just one-tenth.

Beneath his charred outer skin, the writhing red worms composing his body were continually dying and falling away.

“Proust!”

Hm?

Proust was barely conscious. Hearing the shout, he raised his head.

“Divine Punishment!”

“BOOM!!!”

Scarlet lightning poured down from above again, battering Proust's ruined body repeatedly.

“Goodbye!”

Fang Heng pushed Divine Punishment to its limit, draining the last sliver of Proust's HP!

BOOM!!

Sacred fire ignited instantly across Proust's body.

The tiny insects composing him emitted piercing cries as they burned and crumbled into nothing!

Fang Heng watched coldly as Proust turned to ashes in the sacred flames.

Unlike the fragmented Ouroboros Seeds he'd killed before, Proust was a genuine complete form.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng raised a hand. A dark-golden cocoon rose from the corpse amid the sacred fire.

“Hiss!!”

A fissure opened in Fang Heng's palm, swallowing the dark-golden cocoon instantly.
