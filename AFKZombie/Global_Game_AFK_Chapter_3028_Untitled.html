From Chinese Chapter 3029 · No subtitle in source

Overwhelmed with terror, Eseklyna's will gave way. She sank into a daze yet again, only to be jolted awake by the sensation of her power draining away. It was like living through the same nightmare again and again.

Barely three minutes later, a game notification appeared.

[Hint: Abe Akaya has completed the absorption of Envy's power.]

Oh?

It was already finished?

Fang Heng looked at Eseklyna, who was barely alive and had fallen into another dazed state.

Demon Seeds could be reborn and resurrected without limit.

Even if he killed her now, she would soon reincarnate and be reborn.

But still...

The next time the Envy Demon Seed was reborn, it wouldn't be Eseklyna. A new inheritor would take her place.

As the Death Realm around them gradually faded, Eseklyna slowly recovered her awareness and looked at the man before her.

“You... who are you really...?”

“Heh, that doesn't matter anymore.” Fang Heng raised his hand toward Eseklyna, a smile appearing at his lips. “Goodbye, Eseklyna.”

“Boom!!!”

Divine Punishment!

Deep-purple lightning crashed down.

“Boom!!!”

Under the lightning strike, Eseklyna's nearly shattered will finally broke. She let out an agonized scream as violent sacred flames ignited across her body, gradually burning her completely to ash.

With Eseklyna's death, the power of the Envy Demon Seed occupying Fang Heng's Sea of Consciousness also dissipated completely.

[Hint: The player has successfully killed Eseklyna (Legendary). Additional reward: 1,000,000 Main God Points.]

[Hint: Current quest—Distant Holy War—completion has reached 100%.]

[Hint: The player may continue the quest to increase completion and obtain additional rewards.]

[Hint: Current quest—Distant Holy War—completion has reached 590.7%.]

[Hint: Current Will of the Twelve Sages (Initial) condensation: 78%.]

Fang Heng glanced over the game notifications.

Quest completed.

After being targeted by Envy for so long, he'd finally dealt with that nuisance once and for all.

And there were unexpected gains as well.

It seemed the quest to condense the Will of the Twelve Sages could continue.

As the Death Realm domain faded, the cavern slowly returned to its former appearance.

Fang Heng looked toward the only Demon Core left in the center of the cavern.

Demon Cores were originally united with the Demon Seeds. Eseklyna had somehow separated them, however, so her death hadn't caused a Demon Core to drop.

As for this remaining one...

With this thing, perhaps he could threaten and control other Demon Seeds?

Fang Heng thought for a moment and tried approaching the core.

Because his own attributes weren't compatible with it, the core immediately resisted him.

“Crack!”

Fang Heng forcefully enveloped the core with his psychic power and tried to make it float slowly in front of him.

It was difficult.

Once controlled, the core produced tremendous resistance.

Even with his terrifying current psychic strength, Fang Heng couldn't maintain control of it for long.

No wonder Envy hadn't carried the Demon Cores on her person. They weren't easy to control at all.

Fang Heng checked the game notification. It was listed as an unknown item.

Troublesome.

He couldn't even put this thing into his backpack.

That made the Demon Core far less practical.

He would have to find some way to take it back and hide it somewhere safe.

Worse, the core continually released fluctuations of Hell power that would affect the surrounding environment.

“Hmm?”

As he pondered, Abe Akaya's vines coiled toward him, conveying their interest in the Demon Core.

Surprised, Fang Heng looked at the nearby vines. “You mean you can absorb this thing?”

That was fine too.

If Abe Akaya could absorb it, they might as well consume it and save themselves the trouble later.

“Hiss, hiss...”

With Fang Heng's approval, Abe Akaya eagerly wrapped its vines around the Demon Core, layering them until the core was completely enclosed in a ball of vines.

Dense, tiny spikes extended from the vines and pierced the core's surface, greedily absorbing the energy within.

[Hint: Abe Akaya is absorbing unknown energy...]

[Hint: Abe Akaya's current experience has increased. As Abe Akaya has reached the maximum level of its current stage, surplus experience is being placed in storage.]

It really could absorb it.

Fang Heng examined the quest description again.

This Demon Core belonged to the Wrath Demon Seed.

In Abe Akaya's quest to advance to its Complete Form, the power of Wrath had not yet been fully absorbed. But the energy taken from the core hadn't increased the percentage of Wrath power absorbed.

It seemed Abe Akaya could only absorb energy from a Demon Core and turn it into the natural energy needed for its own advancement.

What a pity.

But it was still acceptable.

Abe Akaya hadn't reached its Complete Form yet, and its level was already at the maximum for its current stage. At least it could store the surplus and advance rapidly once its evolution was complete.

Fang Heng patiently let Abe Akaya keep absorbing the Demon Core's energy.

After all, this was a Demon Core, an undying, indestructible existence. It should contain a great deal of energy if drained completely.

Fang Heng communicated with Abe Akaya, instructing it to absorb at full strength.

But after a period of absorption, Abe Akaya soon sent another message through their psychic link.

“Is that so? You're saying the Demon Core continuously generates energy, and you can't drain it dry at all?”

Fang Heng sensed the information conveyed through Abe Akaya and murmured to himself.

There was such a good thing?

There were many theories about the source of Demon Seeds' power. Some said it came from Hell; others believed it came from the countless sins of the world.

As long as sin existed, Demon Seeds would remain undying and could resurrect countless times.

Abe Akaya could absorb a small portion of a Demon Core's power.

And its conversion efficiency wasn't particularly high.

But that power was inexhaustible and could continually provide Abe Akaya with energy for growth.

Huh?

Wait a minute!

Fang Heng suddenly realized that since Abe Akaya could absorb demonic energy, convert it to natural energy, and either use it or store it, it should also be able to release that converted power!

With that thought, Fang Heng immediately contacted Abe Akaya through their psychic link.

Once he received a definite affirmative answer, he became excited.

“Abe Akaya, give it a try.”

The vines trembled gently, releasing natural energy. In the blink of an eye, the entire cavern was filled with dense natural energy.

Great!

Fang Heng immediately closed his eyes and tried absorbing it.

It worked!

After entering his body and completing one cycle, the energy quickly transformed into the Qi granted by his Titan bloodline and flowed into his Sea of Consciousness.

He'd struck it rich!

Fang Heng snapped his eyes open, delighted.

With a Demon Core, he could not only control Demon Seeds but also establish a source of energy that would never run dry.

Previously, Abe Akaya had only relied on naturally absorbing and releasing energy on its own, which was rather inefficient. With five Demon Cores, everything would be much easier.

Seen that way, the various negative effects of controlling the Demon Cores were well within tolerable limits.
