Chapter 3108

Fight to the Death

From Chinese Chapter 3109 · 搏杀

Something was wrong.

Cold sweat immediately formed on Dario's forehead.

He suddenly realized something.

The arena matches had been competitions. This was a genuine fight to the death.

And this brat was clearly much better at killing than competing.

Alray, who had been knocked backward by a single punch, propped himself up with one hand and sat on the ground, breathing heavily.

Looking down, he saw a layer of strange black energy clinging to the place where the punch had struck him.

Wariness flickered in his eyes.

Hm?

Alray tried to push himself to his feet, but suddenly felt his chest tighten and his arm go weak. An overwhelming exhaustion flooded his body.

What!?

Alray looked down at his chest.

The rapidly spreading black energy was devouring his life force at terrifying speed!

Hm!?

Suddenly he noticed that the blood running down his face was creeping toward his neck, gradually forming a ring and tightening around his throat.

This was bad!

Alray reached up and tried to tear away the blood wrapped around his neck.

He couldn't pull it loose!

For some inexplicable reason, the blood was extraordinarily tough, while his body had become so weak that he could barely muster any strength.

"Whoosh!!!"

Elsewhere, Fang Heng flickered through the crowd like a phantom, moving so quickly that his figure was almost impossible to follow.

"Stop him!"

Dario and several mid-ranking warriors hadn't noticed anything unusual about Alray. Their attention was entirely on Fang Heng, and they attacked him together.

"Mental Shock!"

"Spatial Lock!"

The Transcendent-level Mental Shock affected Fang Heng for only an instant, while its illusion was shattered immediately by the Omniscient Eye. Spatial Lock, however, placed a considerable burden on him.

He felt an extraordinarily powerful gravitational force pressing down on his body, restricting his movement speed.

Gravity manipulation?

Was this a spatial ability involving laws?

Interesting!

Too bad he wasn't in the arena. Otherwise, Fang Heng would definitely experiment to see exactly how the technique worked.

"Whoosh!!!"

Fang Heng ducked beneath a combined attack from two mid-ranking warriors and dashed toward another opponent.

Horror flashed across Dario's face. Even with law power suppressing Fang Heng, his monstrous physique still allowed him to move like this while forcibly resisting spatial laws.

Under the spatial pressure, the targeted mid-ranking warrior could barely distinguish Fang Heng's movement trajectory. Seeing him approach, he desperately formed a hand seal.

"Blizzard!"

"Roar!!!"

A roaring fissure hurled thick shards of ice straight toward Fang Heng.

"Boom!"

A fist shot forward!

"Bang!!!"

The ice crystals exploded under the shock wave, and the mid-ranking warrior was reduced to a cloud of blood in midair without making a sound.

What?

The grisly sight made everyone's scalp crawl.

Compared with the mid-ranking warrior, they finally understood just how powerful Alray was.

Both men had taken a direct punch from Fang Heng. Alray had merely been sent flying with blood streaming from his orifices, while a mid-ranking warrior who didn't specialize in close combat had been blown apart into a bloody mist.

Such terrifying strength was packed into that fist.

"Whoosh!"

After dispatching one opponent, Fang Heng tapped the ground again and nimbly dashed toward another mid-ranking warrior on his right.

The warrior's face changed drastically. Panicking, he began forming a seal before his chest.

"Ice Curtain! Thousand-Layer Ice Curtain!!"

Sheets of ice as thin as cicada wings formed before him.

"Bang! Bang, bang, bang!!!!"

Fang Heng threw a punch. Every ice curtain blocking his path shattered under the pressure wave before his fist even touched it. Then his punch hit the warrior himself, turning him into a spray of blood!

Fang Heng quickly surveyed the battlefield out of the corner of his eye.

Aside from the gravely wounded Alray, who was still struggling on the ground, four opponents remained.

The remaining four warriors, including Dario, turned pale when Fang Heng fixed his gaze upon them.

In less than a minute of fighting, one high-ranking warrior in their seven-man team had been incapacitated and two mid-ranking warriors had been blown to pieces.

In fact, there was a further rank of warrior above the high-ranking level: the Revered-level warrior.

Warriors of that rank generally didn't participate in competitions. They served as deterrents and held honored positions among the various overlord races.

This brat might very well be a Revered-level warrior!

Before coming here, the warrior team had never imagined that Fang Heng could possess such terrifying strength.

In their eyes, Fang Heng now resembled a demon emerging from the abyss.

He was going to devour them!

"Scatter! Run!"

Dario shouted, turned decisively, and fled to the right, praying that Fang Heng wouldn't follow him.

As a Spatial Studies scholar, he had an advantage when it came to escaping.

And the other warriors could buy him a little time.

He might still survive!

But three muffled bursts sounded behind him in succession, each one striking his heart like a hammer.

This was bad!

Sensing a presence closing in behind him, Dario immediately entered a secondary spatial projection and changed direction as he fled.

"Heh..."

Spatial Studies!

Such a nuisance!

Fang Heng had already locked onto Dario and the others using his perception and vampire marks.

These people had seen his face and knew his identity. He absolutely couldn't let them escape!

Having resolved to kill them, Fang Heng likewise entered the secondary spatial projection and chased after Dario at high speed.

Dario fled desperately for more than ten minutes. Only after he could no longer sense Fang Heng did he believe he'd shaken off the pursuit. He relaxed, emerged from the projection, and prepared to find a way to contact the Kinsfolk.

But in the next instant, warning bells rang through his mind again.

Space suddenly rippled and ruptured to his right!

Fang Heng stepped directly out of the distortion and launched a punch at him.

"Boom!!!!"

What!

The blow came straight at his face.

Only now did Dario fully sense the terrifying power contained in Fang Heng's fist.

Spatial Curtain!

In desperation, Dario played his final trump card!

"Thud!!!"

Fang Heng's punch struck what felt like a solid wall of space.

Laws?

Dario's face drained of color as a violent tremor rocked his body through the spatial wall.

He had never imagined that one of Fang Heng's punches could be so powerful. The defense created by compressing spatial energy in front of him had only absorbed part of the force.

The remaining impact traveled through space and struck him head-on. Blood gushed from all seven orifices again as he was hurled backward.

Leave no evil alive!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and raised a hand toward the airborne Dario.

Hm?

Still flying backward and dazed from the punch, Dario suddenly tasted something sweet and metallic at the corner of his mouth.

— End of Chapter 3108 —
