Chapter 3003

Awakening

From Chinese Chapter 3004 · 觉醒

The players hesitated and exchanged looks.

Dorian, commander of the Southern Federation team, drew a deep breath and clenched his fists.

The Southern Federation had always been among the weaker Federations. This time they'd finally located the Apocalypse Treasure ahead of everyone else, and were on the verge of gaining the strength—and influence—they'd long wanted.

Who could have expected some Holy Court Grand Sage to turn up?

They had to fight for it!

This might be the Southern Federation's best chance to rise!

Dorian secretly signaled one of his trusted subordinates.

The man understood at once. He stepped forward and shouted angrily, "Hmph! The power of the Land of Apocalypse belongs to all humanity! What's it got to do with your Holy Court? If the Holy Court thinks it can monopolize this power, it's dreaming!"

Before his words had even faded, an explosive shout rang out.

"You're too damn noisy!"

The subordinate who'd been shouting so arrogantly felt the sound detonate beside his ears. The blood throughout his body surged beyond his control. He spat a mouthful of blood, flew backward, slammed into the wall, and fell completely unconscious.

Everyone's expression changed.

What terrifying strength!

Nobody had even seen how Fang Heng attacked.

Fang Heng opened his eyes and swept them with an icy stare. "The Apocalypse Treasure is right here. How much anyone gets depends on their own ability. Don't imagine your little tricks will affect me. That was just a warning. Take one more step toward me and you die."

The Southern Federation players' faces turned extremely ugly.

A threat, plain and simple.

But he was too powerful for them to have even the slightest hope of winning.

Fang Heng closed his eyes again.

Visible waves of energy formed another vortex above his head and streamed rapidly into his body.

Seeing this, the Northern Federation players wisely said nothing and withdrew.

Fine.

The situation was stronger than they were.

What else could they do?

If they backed off now, they might still get a sip of the soup. Keep pushing and they'd probably get nothing at all.

The Southern Federation players remained in place, glaring furiously at Fang Heng.

They had arrived first and even earned the Titan God's trust.

Yet Fang Heng had snatched everything away from them!

"We're leaving!"

Dorian knew they couldn't win and refused to force a confrontation. He helped his badly injured subordinate to his feet and withdrew quickly.

Huh?

Archbishop Whiteman was puzzled to see the Southern Federation group leave the temple hall entirely instead of staying to cultivate. They headed back toward the entrance they'd used.

They were leaving?

Just like that?

Even if they didn't dare fight, couldn't they stay and absorb some of the remaining energy?

Whiteman turned to glance at Fang Heng, who was still cultivating.

Nothing seemed wrong with the Grand Sage. He continued his cultivation undisturbed.

With the Grand Sage's seemingly boundless wisdom, he must have foreseen everything. Whiteman only needed to follow his instructions.

Shaking his head, Whiteman looked away from the Southern Federation and resumed standing guard beside Fang Heng.

In reality, Fang Heng had simply decided they wouldn't dare attack. Once he closed his eyes again, he focused entirely on absorbing energy, suppressing his awareness of the outside world almost to nothing. He hadn't noticed the Southern Federation leave at all.

The surrounding energy gathered into a vortex above his head and poured through his body, tempering his bloodline.

...

Fang Heng became completely immersed in absorbing the energy.

After an unknown amount of time, lines of game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Hint: Player has completely absorbed the mark's power.]

[Hint: Current player bloodline concentration has increased to 100%.]

[Hint: Player's hidden ability—Unknown Mark—has awakened.]

[Hint: Unknown Mark has fused with skill—Titan Bloodline.]

Skill: Ancient Titan Bloodline (LV: MAX) (Foundational Bloodline; cannot evolve or level up).

Description: The inheritor of the ancient Titan bloodline. The Titans were wanderers and explorers among the stars. Besides possessing tremendous strength, they mastered and combined many forms of power, including Natural Studies, Psionics, and Elemental Studies.

Bloodline bonus skill: Erudite Wanderer.

Skill description: Environmental adaptability increases by 1,000% upon entering any world.

Bloodline bonus skill: Academic Unification (Pseudo).

Skill description: During their travels, the ancient Titans studied every discipline and tried to unify the powers of all academic systems. They believed that every form of power ultimately followed the same path and could be transformed into another. They therefore refined a form of energy called 'Qi' and developed methods of using it. However, this academic system was never fully completed.

Skill effect: Reduces additional penalties for learning academic disciplines by 50%. Players may convert energy from different academic systems into Qi and convert Qi back into those energies. (Conversion entails additional loss depending on the energy's grade and form.) Because the system remains incomplete, certain energies cannot be converted.

Special bloodline bonus skill: Titan Mark (Wandering Scholar Branch Mark).

Skill description: Upon entering any world, the player suffers no additional special penalties originating from that world. Absorbing the world's energy becomes more efficient. A portion may be absorbed into the mark, granting an additional 50 points to all base attributes.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng abruptly opened his eyes.

Titan bloodline!

So the Eastern Federation's findings about the Ancient Humans had been correct. Humanity descended from a branch of the ancient Titan race.

And the Titan bloodline's bonus skills looked extremely useful.

From the beginning of bloodline refinement until its completion, every base attribute had increased by 50 points.

That might not mean much to him, but for an ordinary player it would be an absolutely transformative improvement.

Fang Heng reopened the skill description and examined it carefully.

The bloodline granted three skills.

First was increased adaptability to different worlds, which wasn't particularly useful to him.

Next came Academic Unification.

Now that was good!

Fang Heng reread the description several times to make sure he understood.

It reduced the penalty for studying multiple disciplines!

And he had already learned a great many of them.

The skill provided a 50% reduction to the additional academic learning penalty. Combined with his Academic Fusion skill, the total reduction reached 87%.

But what did it mean to convert academic energy into 'Qi' and back?

Qi seemed to be an attribute-less form of energy developed by the Titan race.

What exactly did that mean?

Could he, for instance, turn Necromancy energy into sacred energy?

Fang Heng quickly tested it, only to discover there wasn't any Qi in his body.

He didn't get it!

And the final skill, the Titan Mark?

Fang Heng had never known where the special mark on the back of his hand came from. He'd assumed Xia Xi had left some special item on him, or that it was an innate ability of his own body.

Now it seemed the mark was also a creation of the ancient Titans.

— End of Chapter 3003 —
