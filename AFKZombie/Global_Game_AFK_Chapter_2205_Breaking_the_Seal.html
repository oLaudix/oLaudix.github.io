Chapter 2205

Breaking the Seal

From original Chinese Chapter 2206 · 解封

A single boulder could deal tens of thousands of points of physical damage!

They were that powerful?!

Fang Heng hadn't expected the rocks to hit anywhere near this hard.

And every hit inflicted knockback and stun as well.

Stone Spirit Puppets.

They definitely qualified as monsters from a high-tier game world!

Battered backward by the rocks, the scenery retreating rapidly around him, Fang Heng fought through the pain and forced himself to think clearly.

Right. Those three seeds he'd just obtained.

They corresponded to three special abilities of the old Divine Tree.

Space, barrier, and sealing.

One of them was a sealing ability!

Most likely, the Tree Spirits' Divine Tree had originally sealed the Stone Spirit Puppets inside itself.

Even after its death, the remaining energy had maintained that final seal.

But when Fang Heng used the magic array to drain the very last trace of the tree's power, the seal had completely dissolved, allowing the Stone Spirit Puppets to awaken from their imprisonment!

Fang Heng quickly pieced it all together.

But what now?

How was he supposed to deal with this?

As soon as Undying Body cleared his stun, Fang Heng entered his secondary space projection.

Behind him, the dense barrage of boulders came to an end, and the rocks reassembled themselves into stone giants.

The puppets bore a special hatred for anyone carrying the Divine Tree seeds. They remained locked onto Fang Heng, chasing him even after he entered the secondary space projection where they could no longer strike him.

He couldn't maintain his high-speed movement within that space for very long. After darting forward, he emerged—only for the giants to close in again, clinging to his trail.

Heh…

So eager to chase him?

A sharp light flashed through Fang Heng's eyes. He suddenly turned to face the advancing Stone Spirit Puppets.

The Book of Oaths appeared before him.

Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!

A dense swarm of Sacred Fragments shot out of the Book of Oaths toward the pursuing puppets!

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

The fragments struck the puppet at the front, triggering a series of explosions and forcing it back step by step.

A string of game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Hint: The player's Sacred Fragment has dealt 2,033 damage to a Stone Spirit Puppet (magic damage reduction calculated after the passive skill Granite Skin).]

[Hint: The player's Sacred Fragment has dealt 1,033 area explosion damage to a Stone Spirit Puppet (magic damage reduction calculated after Granite Skin), inflicting knockback.]

[Hint: The player…]

Fang Heng was startled.

Tough as nails!

The puppets obviously resisted physical attacks, so he'd deliberately attacked with the magical Sacred Fragments from a distance.

He hadn't expected them to resist spells just as effectively!

It wasn't that the attacks were completely useless.

He could probably defeat a single puppet if he focused all his firepower on it. But in the short time he'd spent attacking, the dense ranks of puppets behind it had already caught up.

A moment later, the stone giants broke apart into flying boulders again, sending another barrage hurtling toward him!

Fang Heng felt a headache coming on.

With his current strength, he was still a long way from dominating a high-tier game world. He was the one being chased all over the place!

Fine.

The Stone Spirit Puppets' projectiles dealt pure physical damage.

With Undying Body, he could counter that type of attack. At least he didn't have to worry about being beaten to death by flying rocks.

But…

There were too many of them!

The repeated impacts left him stunned almost continuously, making it difficult to find an opening to retaliate.

Combined with the puppets' enormous damage resistance, his attacks simply couldn't kill them quickly.

If he got pinned down, there was no telling how long he'd be stuck here.

As another wave of boulders came flying toward him, Fang Heng lost all desire to continue fighting.

Time to run!

Whoosh!

He immediately slipped into his secondary space projection once more and sped away.

The Stone Spirit Puppets pursued him relentlessly.

They appeared to have some special tracking ability that allowed them to keep him locked on.

Even hiding in the secondary space projection wasn't enough to shake them.

If he wanted to lose them completely…

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

Got it!

Head outside!

If you wanted to fish in troubled waters, you first had to muddy them!

A moment later he emerged from the secondary space projection and hurried toward the outer reaches of the Valley of Annual Rings.

There were Leaf Clan members waiting outside. Let them deal with the Stone Spirit Puppets!

Then he could make off with the seeds!

Perfect. What a plan!

It sounded good, at least.

There was just one rather serious problem.

The condensed Divine Tree Seed Essences couldn't be placed in his backpack.

Even in his hands, the three seeds continued to release waves of natural energy.

That meant not only would the Stone Spirit Puppets follow him, but once he escaped, the Tree Spirits would certainly sense that power radiating from his body too.

They definitely wouldn't let him leave.

What to do?

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

An idea!

His eyebrows rose. He suddenly thought of a solution and opened his palm.

Hiss, hiss…

The blood-red longsword hovering behind him dissolved into liquid blood, which rapidly gathered around his hand, wrapping all three seeds in layer after layer.

“Not bad…”

Fang Heng nodded in satisfaction.

That reduced the natural aura spreading outward by dozens of times.

It was no longer nearly so conspicuous.

Even so, the Stone Spirit Puppets behind him weren't fooled.

And some of the natural aura was still leaking through.

“This probably won't be enough to fool them…”

What if…

Fang Heng thought for a moment, then removed the damaged seed from its blood covering.

Natural energy immediately surged outward once again.

All right.

He hated to part with it, but time was short and he couldn't think of a better solution.

He'd have to use the damaged seed as bait!

…

Meanwhile, the Tree Spirits still fighting in the Elder Council's main hall had all seen the pillar of light rise into the sky.

It lasted only three seconds before fading away completely.

The clans exchanged uneasy glances.

The disturbance had come from the Valley of Annual Rings.

Had something happened inside?

This was bad!

The clans immediately remembered the Divine Wood Altar inside the valley.

Someone had made a move on the altar!

Cursing the Leaf Clan for its treachery, they hastily dispatched groups toward the valley.

At the entrance to the Valley of Annual Rings…

Vannis and his group were still facing off against the Wood Clan.

As the minutes passed, more and more Tree Spirits gathered there.

Before long, members of all four clans had assembled.

No one made a move.

Under so many watchful eyes, none of them wanted to be the first to act.

The fluctuations coming from the altar had grown clearer and clearer.

What in the world was going on inside?

Vannis was wondering the same thing when he suddenly frowned.

What?!

— End of Chapter 2205 —
