Chapter 2218

Diminishing Attributes

From original Chinese Chapter 2219 · 削减

The human trial participants exchanged excited looks.

They could feel it!

Their psychic abilities had grown considerably!

It had been a long time since they'd experienced improvement like this!

And they were still nowhere near their limit!

After a period of recovery, they'd be able to train again and improve even further!

Cheng Fan exchanged glances with the participating Wanling Technology members. Suppressing his excitement, he calmly asked, “Did any of you experience unusual reactions during the experiment?”

Everyone shook their heads.

“Nothing so far.”

“Mm.” Cheng Fan turned to Fang Heng. “Doctor Fang, they'll need some rest after Psychic Tempering. I'll take them to the laboratory for more detailed physical examinations while they recover.”

“Go ahead. Have them drink recovery potions before they leave.” Fang Heng pointed toward the last crate on the right side of the warehouse. “Bring them back after the tests.”

“Understood.”

The subjects were much more cooperative this time. They quickly drank the blue HP recovery potions and followed Cheng Fan back to the laboratory for physical testing.

Fang Heng remained in the warehouse with Shi Tingwu and his people.

Shi Tingwu looked at him with much greater interest than before. “Doctor Fang, forgive my bluntness, but do your potions have side effects? Are there any special requirements for the people taking them?”

“No side effects, and no special requirements either. The supply is limited, though. We probably can't equip everyone immediately.”

No side effects at all?

Did that mean the entire Federation psychic corps could undergo a qualitative leap in strength?

Shi Tingwu was thrilled. He could barely restrain himself from trying Wanling Technology's new potions and attempting to break through his own Psychic Studies limit!

Just a little longer.

He took a deep breath and forced himself to calm down.

For safety's sake, he'd wait until the test report arrived!

It wouldn't take long!

While they waited for the data, Fang Heng walked forward and sat cross-legged.

More than a dozen zombie clones gathered around the Darkglow Stone, accelerating his Psychic Studies training alongside him.

…

A little over half an hour later, a series of game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Hint: The player's Psychic Studies has reached Level 10.]

[Hint: The player may advance Psychic Studies. To do so, the player must sacrifice 10 points of a basic attribute (excluding mental and hidden attributes). The chosen attribute will be permanently reduced by 10 points.]

Fang Heng's interest stirred.

Advancing from Basic Psychic Studies was surprisingly simple.

All it required was the sacrifice of an ordinary basic attribute.

Perhaps that was only simple for him.

For an ordinary player, losing ten attribute points would be a serious blow.

After a moment's consideration, Fang Heng chose to sacrifice Strength.

Once Strength reached a sufficiently high threshold, its practical effects stabilized. Improving its actual performance much further would probably require a great deal of gradual accumulation.

Ten Strength points might only amount to a few hundred points of damage per punch.

[Hint: The player has sacrificed 10 Strength points. Psychic Studies has advanced to Low-Tier Level 1.]

Skill: Low-Tier Psychic Studies (Level 1).

Type: Academic.

Skill description: You have begun to sense the power of the mind. Through Psychic Tempering, you are gradually strengthening your psychic abilities.

Skill description: Grants 10 Spirit and 10 Willpower. Each additional skill level grants 2.5 Spirit and 2.5 Willpower. Maximum skill level: 15.

Additional skill: Psychic Protection.

Advancing to Low-Tier Psychic Studies hadn't unlocked any new secondary abilities. It had merely retained the Psychic Protection skill from Basic Psychic Studies.

Each new level would also grant a combined total of five attribute points.

Of course, he suspected leveling would still randomly deduct some of his basic physical attributes.

Even so, the total gains were clearly worth it.

Then he'd keep going!

Grind those levels!

…

Federation Research Institute.

The six psychic practitioners who'd taken part in the experiment had each had more than twenty vials of blood drawn and were now lying inside large precision instruments for full-body scans.

Behind the glass observation wall, more than a dozen researchers gathered before the monitors and stared at the readings in astonishment.

All their measurements were normal!

The only difference was that the subjects' physical fitness had increased dramatically in a very short period.

Tests also showed their ability to withstand Psychic Tempering had risen significantly.

Various other measurements indicated they were still far from reaching the maximum amount of tempering they could endure!

Was it the potions?

Had the drugs directly improved their physical attributes?

They'd never heard of anything like it.

The researchers were discussing the results in hushed tones when the door behind them clicked open.

“Sir!”

Everyone straightened and fell silent, respectfully moving aside for the newcomers.

The first was the laboratory team's director.

Following him was Kleiman, deputy minister of the Federation's military department.

Kleiman was responsible for developing the Federation's entire special psychic corps.

“Doctor Li, brief Deputy Minister Kleiman on the results.”

“Yes, sir!”

The laboratory team leader stepped forward. “We've conducted comprehensive blood tests and analyzed residual substances in the first group of subjects. We found no residual drugs or abnormal reactions, and confirmed that the potions fall into the lowest risk category. In addition, after receiving the potions, the subjects showed marked improvements in physical fitness, with corresponding increases in their ability to withstand Psychic Tempering…”

Kleiman frowned as he listened and interrupted. “One moment. Are you saying their bodies became stronger immediately after taking these drugs, which allowed the soldiers to withstand higher levels of Psychic Tempering?”

“Yes.”

“Permanently?” Kleiman pressed. “Do the potions only work on psychic practitioners? What about ordinary people? Could they use them to improve their physical fitness too?”

The researchers exchanged glances.

They were curious about that themselves.

“We weren't given samples of the potions, so we can't confirm it yet.”

Kleiman frowned.

Wanling Technology had arranged these special human trials in a great hurry. He hadn't taken the matter seriously at first. Only when the laboratory director sent him the results did he realize Psychic Studies had made such a significant breakthrough.

A potion with no side effects!

Kleiman asked, “What's your estimate for the final percentage increase in psychic abilities after taking the drugs?”

The team leader looked at his colleagues and took a deep breath. “The exact outcome depends on the individual, and we can't guarantee absolute accuracy. But based on the data available, we estimate a final increase of 90 to 150 percent in the subjects' basic psychic capabilities.”
