Chapter 2049

Appraisal

From original Chinese Chapter 2050 · 鉴定

Fang Heng patted Chu Yan on the shoulder.

“Sorry. You've taken a loss because of me this time. I'll make it up to you later.”

“Don't say that, Boss Fang. I wouldn't call it a loss. I gained some things and lost others. Either way, I trust you.”

They arrived together at the entrance to the Temple of Zeus. Two Holy Court guards stepped forward to stop them.

Chu Yan handed over a dark-gold ornament shaped like a three-leaf clover.

“This is my friend, a believer from far away. I've brought him to receive baptism. Here's his admission token. You can deduct the required points from my account.”

The guards examined Fang Heng and Chu Yan, briefly checked their identification, and moved aside.

Chu Yan stayed at the entrance and watched Fang Heng walk into the hall.

…

The interior of the Temple of Zeus glowed with sacred light. Fang Heng looked around in surprise.

It was furnished with extraordinary luxury. Glittering jewels embedded in the walls shone brilliantly, while polished marble floors reflected a soft, dark-golden radiance.

Exquisite murals covered the vaulted ceiling, depicting the heroic deeds of the gods.

In the center stood a six-meter statue of a heavenly deity in ornate armor, wielding a spear of lightning.

The moment Fang Heng entered, sacred chanting filled his ears. His emotions stirred, and he felt a sudden impulse to kneel before the central statue in worship.

He immediately steadied his mind.

The Holy Court's ability to influence people's thoughts was as frightening as ever.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng looked toward the statue. A rapidly rotating magic array appeared in his right pupil.

His senses told him that the objects decorating this temple were anything but ordinary.

[Notice: Using the identification function of the Omniscient Eye, you discovered Statue of Zeus (Rare Treasure)…]

[Notice: Using the identification function of the Omniscient Eye, you discovered Saint's Gem…]

Just as he suspected.

Tsk, tsk…

Almost every decoration in the temple could increase its overall quality. Most also gave off sacred energy and could slightly accelerate the study of Sacred Studies.

The Holy Court really was rich!

If he could move all these things back to the Temple of Zeus in his own Zombie Apocalypse world, he would probably be able to upgrade it—at least by an entire tier.

Fang Heng wasn't alone inside. More than a dozen people stood around the central statue, receiving baptism under the sacred light. Many were players.

A few glanced toward him when he entered, then closed their eyes again and returned to their rituals.

Every minute here cost contribution points. No one wanted to waste time minding another person's business.

Fang Heng walked forward, stepped into the sacred light, and closed his eyes.

[Notice: You are attempting to record an advanced Sacred Studies spell in the Book of Oaths. You may spend 30 Main God Points to randomly record one advanced sacred spell.]

More Main God Points?!

Even the price of learning advanced skills had gone up!

Fang Heng stared speechlessly at the notification. Luckily, he'd earned a fortune earlier; otherwise, he couldn't even afford to learn a few skills.

He still had a huge reserve of Undead Faction Honor, which he could exchange for points as soon as he got back. For now, he could borrow from the system.

He chose a loan of 1,000 points—enough to experiment a little.

[Notice: You have borrowed 1,000 Main God Points. Please repay them promptly.]

[Notice: Random advanced sacred skill obtained — Sacred Retribution.]

Skill: Sacred Retribution

Description: Fires a beam of sacred retribution at a target, dealing sacred damage. On impact, the beam causes a sacred explosion that damages the target and creatures within a range of 100. Damage scales with the player's attributes.

Casting time: 0.5 seconds. Cooldown: 20 seconds.

“An attack skill…”

It included an initial single-target strike followed by an area explosion. For an ordinary player, it would have been respectable.

But Fang Heng found it underwhelming.

He still lacked some offensive techniques, but he needed more powerful abilities, not something at this level. Since advancing his Vampire King's bloodline and obtaining Domain of Blood, his attack capabilities had improved enormously.

Compared with Domain of Blood, Sacred Retribution was simply too weak.

Gritting his teeth, he spent more points on another draw.

[Notice: You spent 30 Main God Points. The skill has been forgotten and a new skill will be drawn…]

[Notice: You obtained Light Wave…]

“Another useless skill…”

[Notice: You spent 30 Main God Points…]

[Notice: You obtained Healing Touch.]

“What are these garbage skills…?”

He clicked his tongue.

Perhaps the Holy Court's advanced skill pool was simply mediocre. After more than twenty draws, he still hadn't seen a single ability he wanted.

He didn't lack escape skills. Most healing techniques were unnecessary for him. And none of the attack skills he'd found were more reliable than Domain of Blood.

His borrowed 1,000 points were almost gone when a new skill finally caught his eye.

[Notice: Skill obtained — Thunder of Judgment.]

Skill: Thunder of Judgment

Type: Applied status effect. Ineffective against certain types of creatures.

Description: After this effect is applied to a target, every physical attack that target receives has a 10% chance of triggering a bolt of Thunder of Judgment against the attacker. The lightning can interrupt actions. Damage scales with the player's attributes.

Duration: 30 minutes.

Now that's good!

Fang Heng's eyes brightened.

It wasn't particularly useful on himself because the lightning's damage wasn't very high. But he could cast it on his allies!

A buff for the Lickers could help make up for their relatively weak attacks.

More importantly, his zombies lacked reliable ranged attacks. With this skill, they would have at least some means of retaliating against enemies attacking from afar.

Get attacked and deal damage right back! Combined with their Undying Body effect, it was a perfect match.

A genuinely practical skill. It had taken nearly a thousand points, but he'd finally drawn something worthwhile.

He recorded Thunder of Judgment in his Book of Oaths.

The zombie clones still had some time before they respawned. He could test the buff on the Lickers later.

Fang Heng raised his head and looked at the statue in the middle of the hall once more.

Since I'm here, I might as well try receiving a baptism next.

— End of Chapter 2049 —
