Chapter 3115

Going With the Flow

From Chinese Chapter 3116 · 顺势而为

"Nasir, this cavern can devour perception. That's highly abnormal. Could it be connected to a law-based alchemical magic array?"

"Huh?"

Nasir was startled by Fang Heng's question and abruptly raised his head.

"That's right! It's entirely possible! And an array capable of producing this effect could very well be Demigod-level!"

So much time had passed, and no one had maintained the array. In theory, it should have stopped functioning long ago. Yet the cavern continued to suppress perception.

A Demigod-level magic array that permanently altered laws!

That was entirely possible!

If they really found such an array here, it would be immensely helpful for their advancement to the Demigod stage.

"Be careful. Let's explore farther inside."

The cavern was enormous. Purple bioluminescent microorganisms clinging to the walls provided enough light for the group to find their way.

Another altar-like area appeared ahead.

It looked as though someone maintained it regularly.

At its center, a gigantic magic array several times larger than the previous one lay before them.

Nasir's eyes lit up. He immediately approached the array and bent down to inspect it.

"Tch. Another magic array."

Of the five members of the team, only Nasir and Fang Heng were proficient in Alchemy. The other three had little interest in alchemical arrays, but they followed the pair forward and stood on the formation while looking around.

Edwin asked, "Well? Found anything?"

Without realizing it, Nasir gradually stepped toward the array's center. As he examined it, he nodded. "Mm, impressive. An enormous fusion-type alchemical magic array. There are signs of repairs, but it isn't a law-type array."

Fang Heng also examined the array's condition. Thinking it over, he took a few steps toward the center and added, "There are Spatial Studies markings. The array possesses spatial effects."

As he spoke, everything changed.

Several figures suddenly lunged out of the darkness.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

The hidden figures possessed techniques for blending into darkness. Combined with the special environment's suppression of perception, they had even managed to evade his blood-sensing ability!

But the moment they unleashed their killing intent, Fang Heng's perception caught them.

Hm?

Suddenly, Fang Heng noticed a deep-blue glow rising from the array beneath his feet.

The magic array!

It could still activate!?

"Watch out! Fang Heng! Something's wrong! Get back!"

Nasir was the first to sense the danger. He shouted and retreated, trying to escape the array's range. But in the next instant, a dark streak flashed past the right side of his body, missing him by a hair.

The attack had come from a teammate!

Nasir's expression changed as he turned to Edwin.

"Are you insane?!"

Edwin rapidly withdrew, his expression gloomy. Without a word, he flicked his hands and sent a barrage of elemental spheres flying at Nasir!

"Fuck!"

Nasir suddenly understood.

That bastard was a traitor!

Fang Heng frowned, and his aura erupted instantly!

The Lanyue attackers lunging toward the group from every direction suddenly felt the blood in their bodies freeze. They couldn't move at all.

"Bang! Bang, bang, bang!!!"

In the next instant, a powerful burst of blood energy erupted outward from Fang Heng, hurling all the Lanyue attackers backward at even greater speed.

Hm?

Fang Heng gave a surprised exclamation.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!"

The array underfoot began spinning rapidly as spatial energy surged through it.

A flash of white light followed. Fang Heng and Nasir, who were still near the center, vanished from the cavern simultaneously.

Mu Shunan, who remained beyond the array's range, saw the two disappear and his expression changed abruptly.

They were in trouble.

Of the five warriors, two were traitors, and his two companions had been teleported somewhere unknown. They were bound to be in danger.

Mu Shunan's mind raced as he searched for a way out. He made his decision almost instantly.

Run!

Edwin and another warrior immediately pursued him.

"Edwin, why? Aren't you afraid the Noel Clan will hold you accountable?"

"Hahaha!" Edwin laughed wildly, launching a dense barrage of elemental spheres at Mu Shunan. "So what? With the Kinsfolk protecting us, what can you possibly do to me?"

"Hmph! So it really is the Kinsfolk!"

A cold light flashed in Mu Shunan's eyes as he rapidly formed a hand seal.

White frost spread outward from him at high speed.

"Boom! Boom, boom, boom!!!"

The frost erupted around his body!

Edwin and his accomplice were forced to stop as a cloud of exploding icy powder filled their path.

Watching Mu Shunan escape, they cursed inwardly.

The cavern's suppression of perception would make tracking him extremely difficult.

Were the Lanyue people all idiots!?

The original plan had been to teleport Fang Heng and both his companions into the underground cavern. But Mu Shunan hadn't been interested in the alchemical array and had escaped.

That might not have mattered. They'd expected Lanyue assistance to be enough to stop him.

But those Lanyue idiots had no fighting ability whatsoever! Fang Heng had sent all of them flying with a single move, and they hadn't helped in the slightest!

...

Deep beneath the cavern.

Fang Heng and Nasir had been transported by the alchemical magic array to the bottom of an underground passage.

"Where is this...?"

Nasir surveyed the surroundings, then felt his heart leap.

Good grief. Layer after layer of alchemical magic arrays had been inscribed all along the rock walls.

"These are protective and sealing arrays."

Nasir quickly scanned the arrays on the walls. Then he noticed something. His eyes brightened, and he lowered his voice in delighted excitement. "Fang Heng! We've struck gold! They're law-level magic arrays! Demigod-level!"

"Law-type... Demigod-level..."

Fang Heng noticed it too. He murmured quietly as he studied the patterns carved into the walls.

"They're not limited to Alchemy. They also incorporate Sealing Studies."

Fang Heng was astonished.

He had already reached the Demigod level in Sealing Studies.

That was also a law-level discipline.

But while studying at the Sealing Studies Association, none of the elders had mentioned the concept of laws.

Instead, they had used the term 'momentum.'

Starting at the Transcendent level, practitioners were taught to borrow 'momentum.' By the Demigod level, they could use it effectively. Now it seemed the two concepts were actually the same thing.

Fang Heng, however, had advanced directly using game skill books. He hadn't understood the underlying concept clearly, so he could only bring out less than twenty percent of his Sealing Studies power.

Perhaps learning Alchemy in depth would greatly improve his actual mastery of Sealing Studies as well.

"Brother Fang Heng, we've hit the jackpot!"

Nasir examined the alchemical runes on the walls like priceless treasures, desperately wishing he could engrave every symbol into his memory on the spot.

— End of Chapter 3115 —
