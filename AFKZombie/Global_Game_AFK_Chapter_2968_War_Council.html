Chapter 2968

War Council

From Chinese Chapter 2969 · 作战会议

Giselle took a deep breath.

Humanity had a weak foundation. Only through the influence of the Black Fog Game had some humans 'evolved' and acquired strength beyond ordinary people.

There was no point blaming heaven or others for their circumstances.

They just had to work hard and build their strength little by little.

One day, they would surpass those races!

As he thought, a starship crew member suddenly noticed another incoming message. Startled, he looked up and reported, “Captain, we've received a message from the Shadow Clan of the alliance. They've invited us to the Red starship to discuss the battle plan.”

Hmm?

Everyone's eyes lit up, and they exchanged glances.

What did this mean? The allied forces had just told them to get lost, and now they were suddenly inviting them to join a strategic planning meeting?

Something wasn't right!

They weren't planning to use the humans as cannon fodder, were they?

“Mm. Let's go and see.”

Giselle made the decision immediately.

They might be weak now, but they could work to become stronger. If they didn't even dare attend a battle-planning meeting, they couldn't blame others for looking down on them.

A teleportation passage was quickly established inside the ship, and Giselle led several Federation members through it to the Red starship.

“Welcome aboard the Red.”

The Crocodile Clan was a branch of the Beastmen and ranked among the top ten Kinsfolk allies in overall strength.

To human eyes, Crocodile Clan members were ugly, with naturally thuggish faces and rather stocky builds.

Several crocodile people greeted Giselle's group in a language they spoke rather awkwardly, their gazes carrying a trace of curiosity. “Is it only you? Are the rest of your companions still on the way?”

Vion nodded. “Mm. It's just us.”

“Hmm? That doesn't seem right.” The crocodile looked puzzled. “We received more than one teleportation request...”

“Never mind. Perhaps there was a small error somewhere. In any case, please follow me. I'll show you to your seats.”

The crocodile guard led Captain Giselle and the other players to a large round conference table.

The meeting hadn't begun, but many aliens were already gathered around the table, conversing in different languages.

Unfortunately, Giselle's group couldn't understand a word of any of them.

After a while, the crocodile's awkward speech sounded behind them again. “Your companions have arrived.”

Hmm?

Companions?

Giselle and the others instinctively turned around.

Huh?

What the hell?

Why was it Fang Heng?!

Led by crocodile guards, Fang Heng and several companions entered the conference hall and walked toward them.

So Fang Heng was the companion the crocodile had meant?

Had his group accepted the same mission?

Fang Heng spotted Captain Giselle's group as he entered and looked a little surprised. After taking his seat, he raised a hand in greeting. “Captain Giselle, long time no see. I didn't expect to run into you here. What a coincidence.”

Giselle and Vion also noticed several players following Fang Heng, and their expressions changed.

People from the Eastern Federation!

And they were all high-ranking Eastern Federation officials!

Military officers, at that.

Although Giselle hadn't met these Eastern Federation military officers personally, the Western Federation had gathered basic intelligence on their identities.

How had they gotten here?

Could it be...?

Captain Giselle immediately drew a conclusion.

It seemed that after departing aboard his interstellar starship, Fang Heng had turned around and established contact with the Eastern Federation.

Unexpectedly, the Eastern Federation's intelligence control was so effective that the Western Federation hadn't heard a whisper of it recently.

Captain Giselle's expression darkened. He shot a sidelong glance at the two Presidential Office experts beside him.

Those idiots!

If they hadn't constantly been stirring up trouble, the Western Federation military would be earning points alongside Fang Heng right now. How could the Eastern Federation have beaten them to it otherwise?

Captain Giselle nodded to Fang Heng and asked a question he already knew the answer to. “Are these gentlemen friends of yours?”

Fang Heng introduced them openly. “Mm. They're friends from the Eastern Federation. We've entered into some cooperation.”

Two captains among the Eastern Federation's senior military officers nodded to Giselle. “Captain Giselle, we've heard much about you. We never expected to meet under these circumstances. A pleasure to make your acquaintance.”

Although the Eastern and Western Federations competed behind the scenes, they could still maintain an outward show of cordiality and exchange pleasantries.

Giselle sighed deeply inwardly and asked, “I didn't realize the Eastern Federation had finished building starships too. When did that happen?”

“It's been a month or two now. This is confidential Eastern Federation business, so we'd appreciate your discretion, Director Giselle.”

“Of course.”

Giselle had mixed feelings.

Vion's expression was equally unsettled.

A month or two ago?

Wasn't that precisely when the Western Federation had fallen out with Fang Heng?

Vion was about to probe further with a few questions when a crocodile approached the head of the conference table, cleared his throat, and gestured for the attendees to put on the signal wristbands lying on the table.

Fang Heng picked one up and put it on. Almost immediately, the crocodile's words were translated directly into human language by the device and sounded in his mind.

The objective of this Kinsfolk mission was to destroy the ultimate SSS-grade insect nest.

It was no easy task.

The Crocodile Clan began explaining the mission in detail.

Fang Heng frowned slightly, puzzled.

Wasn't the Shadow Clan supposed to have organized the mission? The invitation they'd received was from the Shadow Clan too. Why, then, had the Crocodile Clan been chairing the meeting from beginning to end?

“Mr. Fang Heng.”

After discussing the battle plan for a while, the crocodile captain turned the conversation toward Fang Heng. He rose, fixed Fang Heng with a rather officious look, and said, “This is humanity's first time joining an allied military operation. After discussion, the alliance has assigned the human Nethersea Fleet to construct and defend Area Six of the Eye of Stars. Does humanity consider this arrangement acceptable?”

Captain Giselle and his companions, who had been silent throughout, were utterly shocked. They all turned toward Fang Heng.

What? A fleet?!

What was going on?

Since when did Fang Heng have an entire fleet?

Fang Heng remained calm. “That's fine. What about the distribution of benefits afterward?”

“The Kinsfolk mission system will provide the appropriate mission points. As for the final offensive, humanity probably won't be allowed to participate. This is our first cooperation, and humans have yet to prove their strength. The alliance won't take the risk of including you.”

“If humanity still has strength to spare after completing its assignment, you may help the logistics teams transport supplies.”

The conference hall fell quiet for a moment. Delegates from every race looked toward Fang Heng.

A rear-line logistics assignment was really just a job collecting scraps around the edges.

Not only was it exhausting work, but it also offered no chance of claiming the lion's share of the mission rewards.

Fang Heng shrugged. “All right. We accept. We're newcomers, and this is our first time working together. I hope you'll all look after us.”

The aliens looked away one after another.

The Crocodile Clan representative was quite pleased. He thought the humans were surprisingly sensible.

If so, once the mission was over, he could throw them a few leftover scraps of bone.

“Excellent. Then the Nethersea Fleet will assist with security until the Eye of Stars has been constructed. Finally, I must warn all our allies to attend to your own duties. Do not allow your actions to delay the alliance's overall operation.”

— End of Chapter 2968 —
