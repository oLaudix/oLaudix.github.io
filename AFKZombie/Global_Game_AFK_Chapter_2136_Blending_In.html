Chapter 2136

Blending In

From original Chinese Chapter 2137 · 混入

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Pale-purple beams pierced the black mist filling the corridor ahead.

Immediately, wet thuds and shrill squeals sounded from within the shrouded area.

After several volleys, the guards' energy beam guns overheated.

They switched magazines, staring intently at the corridor entrance.

Every breakable window had been shattered, and the ventilation system was running at full power.

The black mist was finally showing signs of dispersing.

At least it wasn't as thick as before.

Through the haze, the guards could vaguely make out a few shadows in the corridor.

What...?

Those were...!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!

In the next instant, a huge number of Lickers leaped from the black mist, bursting out of the passage and pouncing on the guards!

With their guns still awaiting fresh magazines, the guards couldn't mount an effective defense. More than twenty of them were swallowed by the horde in an instant!

The Lickers' claws easily tore through their protective clothing. After devouring the guards, the creatures charged onward toward the research institute's various entrances!

Meanwhile, in Wanling Technology's central control room.

Everyone's expression changed as they watched the surveillance footage from Area A.

In just a few seconds, an entire elite security squad had been wiped out!

Not a single survivor!

Ke Wei, the research institute's supervisor, turned deathly pale. Even his lips were trembling.

“Does anyone know what those monsters are?”

The others exchanged looks.

After studying the monitor for a moment, the company's security director steadied himself and said, “Sir, they're called Lickers.”

What?

Everyone turned toward the security director.

Ke Wei asked, “What exactly are they?”

“Chairman Ji Haiting issued an emergency notice two days ago concerning a newly discovered, artificially cultivated high-tier life-form called a Licker. These creatures possess extraordinary regeneration and infectious capabilities, along with considerable offensive power. The attack on the branch research institute two days ago was connected to them. The person suspected of controlling the Lickers is named Fang Heng.”

“The company is still examining their genetic samples and hasn't made its findings public, but an internal notice requires all information concerning Lickers or Fang Heng to be reported immediately to the chairman.”

Ke Wei frowned. “Has the chairman been informed?”

“We reported it immediately, as required by the emergency procedures. We've also raised the unidentified intrusion to SSS, the highest possible threat level. All noncombatants are evacuating, and the outer research building has been sealed.”

“Mm.” Ke Wei nodded. “Activate the transition device in Area Two and prepare for defensive spatial transition.”

“Understood!”

After issuing several more orders, everyone hurried off to carry out their duties.

Meanwhile, Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei were feeling their way toward the upper floors of the research institute.

Besides obscuring vision and spreading corruption, the black mist had another advantage.

It disrupted most communication equipment.

Alarms were now blaring throughout the research section.

Taking advantage of the chaos, the pair secretly changed into research institute security uniforms inside the mist. They forced their way against the stream of fleeing people and ran up the stairs.

Soon they encountered a group of panicked researchers trying to escape.

In the confusion, most researchers and staff assumed from their uniforms that Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei were company security officers. All they knew was that the institute was under attack by something unknown. They barely had time to save themselves, much less pay attention to others.

Everyone simply hurried down the emergency exit routes.

Fang Heng gave Mo Jiawei a meaningful look.

Mo Jiawei understood and sighed helplessly. He grabbed a passing researcher at random and barked, “Stop! Where is the Water of Life project team?”

“What? I don't know!”

Mo Jiawei frowned and clamped his hand around the researcher's neck, glaring fiercely. “The Water of Life! Listen to me! The boss ordered us to protect that stuff! If anything happens to it, we're dead! Tell me where it is right now, or I'll drag you down with me!”

“Twenty-sixth floor! The Water of Life laboratory is on the twenty-sixth floor! But there's no point going there now. They've activated the highest-level security measures. The entire S-class research area is sealed, so you can't get inside!”

The researcher shouted as he tore himself free and hurried away. “Lunatic! Forget your idiot boss's orders and run for your life!”

Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei exchanged a look.

The twenty-sixth floor!

Keep going!

They continued upward, grabbing a few more unfortunate researchers along the way to verify the information.

It checked out.

The laboratory studying the Water of Life was indeed on the twenty-sixth floor.

They hurried up the stairs until they reached the twenty-fifth floor.

A thick metal door blocked the passage ahead.

Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei exchanged another glance.

“Give it a try.”

“Mm. Stand back.”

Fang Heng stepped forward, clenched his fist, and punched the door with all his strength.

Thud!!!

A muffled impact!

A faint fist-shaped dent appeared in the alloy door.

Fang Heng looked up and saw a long health bar appear in his vision.

The metal alloy door had more than twenty million health points.

His punch had removed fewer than two thousand.

“Can you get through it?”

“I can, but it'll take some time.”

Fang Heng spoke softly as he looked around for another way inside.

Worst case, he could summon another horde of Lickers and have them claw through the door by force.

It would only cost him time.

“Hey, Fang Heng, look over there.”

Mo Jiawei had noticed a fingerprint scanner mounted beside the door. “Looks like the entrance lock. Think you can crack it?”

Fang Heng approached and examined the electronic lock.

[Game Hint: Detecting your current skill—Lockpicking Proficiency...]

[Game Hint: Your current skill is incompatible with this advanced intelligent electronic lock. Attempting to use it will greatly reduce your chance of success.]

[Game Hint: Current cracking success rate: 12%. Failure may cause the lock to enter a permanent lockdown mode.]

“It's pretty difficult. I'll give it a try.”

Fang Heng figured he might as well attempt it once. If it failed, he'd just claw the door open.

Besides, with Mo Jiawei standing beside him, who knew? He might get lucky.

As they discussed it, a voice suddenly sounded from the intercom mounted on the wall beside the fingerprint scanner.

“Is someone out there? Thank goodness! Hurry up and open this damned thing! Of all times, they choose now to conduct a drill? My research is being delayed! Who's going to take responsibility if the experiment fails?!”

Mo Jiawei's heart leaped with delight. He hurried to the intercom and answered in a serious tone, “I'm sorry, but I can't open the door until the drill is over. Please be patient.”

— End of Chapter 2136 —
