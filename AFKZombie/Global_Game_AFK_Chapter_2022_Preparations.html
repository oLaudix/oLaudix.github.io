Chapter 2022

Preparations

From original Chinese Chapter 2023 · 准备

The timing should be about right.

If everything had gone smoothly, the teleportation passage in the unknown game world should already be complete. They wouldn't need the demi-humans to keep supplying it with energy after that.

Besides, his zombie clones had spent a full two days clearing out almost a quarter of that world. He had recruited more than enough zombies—tens of millions, by his estimate. Gathered together, they formed an endless sea of undead.

Keeping the mutated infected zombies alive any longer would consume enormous amounts of his energy points every day.

Things with Miyamoto Teru were settled for the time being. It was time to move on to the next part of the plan.

Fang Heng opened his game cabin and entered the game. His first stop, via teleportation, was the Chiboreya game world.

All three mage towers in the world had now been completed, allowing troops and supplies to be transferred between them. Fang Heng headed to the Temple of Mork first.

Ever since they'd agreed on their grand plan, Ji Xiaobo and the brothers Yuan Jize and Yuan Jixian had sprung into action. Once they finished their assigned tasks, they'd stayed at the temple awaiting Fang Heng's instructions.

When the teleportation passage flashed with light, everyone immediately stood up from the pile of stones they'd been resting on.

“Brother Fang!”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng quickly surveyed the surroundings. In addition to Ji Xiaobo and the others, Sandy was nearby studying an ancient magic array, muttering to himself as he worked.

Fang Heng ignored him completely.

“You've all worked hard. How are the preparations?”

“All done! The magic array is fully repaired!”

Ji Xiaobo nodded excitedly. “Brother Fang, I tried a few more methods and managed to collect another three Mork Runes. I'm still looking for the remaining ones, but I've got a lead on one of them. Lord Dani has agreed to try helping us…”

“Good. That should be enough.”

Fang Heng took the three runes from him. Their main purpose was to control spatial fluctuations and maintain the stability of the magic array while it operated.

In the past, the great necromancer Mork had relied on the runes to control the entire exit to the Death Realm. Fang Heng only needed to control a fractured rift in that realm. There was no need to collect the complete set.

Six runes would suffice.

He walked forward, crouched, and gently touched the magical symbols on the floor.

Game notifications flashed across his vision.

[Hint: You have completed the restoration of Mork's magic array.]

[Hint: Mork's magic array requires partial charging before use. Other factions may detect the charging process (triggering hostile missions). Exercise caution.]

The Yuan brothers approached.

“Fang Heng, the defensive barrier outside has been constructed,” Yuan Jixian explained. “We got hold of the external construction blueprints for the Fortress of Ice and built an imitation outside.”

“But we couldn't reproduce many of the required techniques, and we were short on time. As things stand, it'll provide full protection for only the first five minutes. After that its defensive strength will decline sharply, and after ten minutes it will start collapsing altogether.”

The brothers had been puzzled from the moment they began building it. The deadline was so tight that the resulting defenses could only be weak. In practice, the barrier could protect them from the icy breath of the Yin Vein for five minutes.

What good were five minutes?

Was it merely supposed to buy them time to escape?

“Excellent work! I couldn't have done it without you.”

Getting the plans and arranging the protective magic barrier had required a tremendous amount of time and effort. Fang Heng hadn't had the time, which was why he'd entrusted the job to the brothers. And they'd done very well.

“By the way, Fang Heng,” Yuan Jixian said, “quite a few Holy Court players have come to investigate over the last couple of days. Li Qingran passed us information in advance, so we managed to deal with several groups before they arrived.”

“But their scouting has been getting more intense since this morning. We can still hold them off for now, but it won't stay that way.”

“Understood.” Fang Heng patted Yuan Jixian on the shoulder. “Don't worry. After tonight, we won't need to guard the temple anymore.”

Yuan Jixian froze.

“We won't?” Yuan Jize asked bluntly. “Why not? Has the plan changed? Are we abandoning it?”

“No. We're making our move tonight.”

Both brothers' expressions changed.

Ji Xiaobo, on the other hand, was thrilled. He immediately crowded closer.

“Brother Fang! We're launching the great counteroffensive tonight, aren't we? Aren't we?!”

A great counteroffensive?

What was he talking about now?

The brothers exchanged looks, then turned back to Fang Heng. They couldn't shake the feeling that he was putting a huge plan into motion.

“Mm.”

Fang Heng nodded, looking serious. “Starting Mork's magic array consumes an enormous amount of energy. We can't supply all of it at once, so we'll have to charge it first. I estimate it'll take three hours.”

“It's seven thirty now. Start charging at eight thirty; you should be finished by eleven thirty.”

He calculated for a moment. “I'll coordinate from the magic array on the other side. Activate this one at midnight, on the dot.”

“No problem! Leave it to me!”

Ji Xiaobo thumped his chest enthusiastically, his heart racing. The great undead counteroffensive he'd dreamed about might actually become reality tonight!

The Yuan brothers grew nervous too.

What counteroffensive? Why hadn't anyone told them about it?

Was Fang Heng planning to do something with the Death Realm rift?

Yuan Jize felt uneasy. “Fang Heng, our barrier can only last five minutes. When the time comes, we might…”

“It's fine. As soon as you activate the array, leave the Temple of Mork. Even if something goes wrong, it won't prevent you from completing the main mission.”

Fang Heng rested a hand on Yuan Jize's shoulder. “That's the worst-case scenario. If everything goes according to plan, we'll teach the Holy Court a lesson.”

Yuan Jixian nodded. “All right, I understand.”

Strictly speaking, their agreement with Fang Heng only covered the main mission: restarting Mork's magic array. They hadn't known about his counterattack against the Holy Court beforehand.

But now Yuan Jixian could vaguely sense it. Fang Heng was planning something big!

Whatever he intended to do, they'd already come this far. There was no point arguing about it now.

They'd just have to do it!

“Right. I'll go on ahead!”

With that, Fang Heng turned and left the Temple of Mork.
