Chapter 2204

Condensation

From original Chinese Chapter 2205 · 凝聚

At that very moment, in front of the Divine Wood Altar.

The pillar of light slowly faded away.

Fang Heng was somewhat puzzled.

It seemed that a large portion of the withered Divine Tree's power had already been extracted.

Less than a third of it remained.

What was going on?

Had the dead tree been losing energy in some abnormal way?

Or had the former Elder Council already extracted two-thirds of its power, leaving only the final third?

Something about this didn't feel right.

As Fang Heng pondered the matter, a violent tremor suddenly shook the ground beneath them.

With the tree's true body completely disintegrating, the surrounding dead trees rapidly turned to ash as well.

The mist shrouding the area dispersed at astonishing speed.

The outer sealing barrier gradually vanished, and the teleportation passage behind them swiftly ceased functioning.

Everyone looked up, feeling the ground continue to shudder beneath them.

What was that?

The vibrations were coming from far away.

In the distance, enormous figures the size of small hills slowly came into view.

Stone giants?

Living creatures formed entirely from piled-up stone, each seven or eight meters tall.

They seemed somewhat clumsy on their feet.

They were absolutely massive!

Fang Heng sensed waves of power radiating from the stone giants.

The giants gradually awakened and climbed out of the ground, then started striding toward them.

Anna felt danger prickling at his skin. “Fang Heng, what are those monsters?”

Fang Heng frowned.

Clearly, he had no idea either.

Everything had seemed normal. All he'd done was drain the remaining power from the dead Divine Tree.

So where had these stone giants come from?

Never mind that for now!

Fang Heng looked back toward the Divine Wood Altar.

On top of the altar.

There they were!

Three seed-like objects were floating in the air above it.

Seeds?

Whoosh!

Ignoring the stone giants rapidly approaching them, Fang Heng darted toward the altar and snatched all three seeds into his palm.

[Hint: The player has obtained Divine Tree Seed Essence ×3.]

Item: Divine Tree Seed Essence.

Description: Seeds left behind when a Divine Tree dies and its remaining energy is extracted and condensed. They contain a portion of the former Divine Tree's power. A newly born Divine Tree can absorb them to temporarily gain certain abilities. These abilities may be made permanent through special methods.

Additional description: The energy seeds may also be absorbed as natural energy.

Additional description: Special item. Cannot be placed in the backpack. Guaranteed to drop upon death.

Additional description: Possessing this item generates additional aggression from the Stone Spirit Puppet race.

Seeds of the Divine Tree!

Fang Heng was overjoyed.

They were the lingering essence of the previous Divine Tree, preserved in seed form after its death.

The three seeds differed in size, and each came with a different ability.

Abe Akaya was descended from a Divine Tree too.

That meant Abe Akaya could probably absorb these seeds as well!

Fang Heng's heart started pounding.

What a haul!

He immediately checked the attributes of the three seeds.

[Item: Divine Tree Seed Essence—Sealing.]

Special power: Seed of Sealing.

Description: Uses the Divine Tree's power to seal a target. Once sealed, the Divine Tree slowly absorbs the target's power. Maximum number of seals, maximum seal strength, and absorption efficiency depend on the strength of the Divine Tree itself.

Ability duration: 48 months.

Excellent!

Sealing was a special ability.

Creatures with extraordinarily powerful vitality, or those that couldn't be killed by ordinary means, could be dealt with by sealing them away.

Better still, the Divine Tree could gradually absorb the power of those it sealed and use it to grow stronger.

Nothing wrong with that!

And there were two more!

Fang Heng took a deep breath and checked the second seed.

[Item: Energy Seed—Super-Large Protective Barrier (Damaged).]

Special power: The Divine Tree may create a super-large defensive barrier with environmental mimicry and energy absorption effects. Inside the barrier, all Nature Studies spell effects are increased by 20%, and targets receive an additional 1,000 HP of healing per second.

Ability duration: 72 months.

Additional description: This seed is damaged and must be repaired before use. If used without repair, there is an 80% chance the Divine Tree will fail to inherit the ability, and a 20% chance it will inherit a weakened version.

Damaged?

Fang Heng frowned.

Frankly, this second seed was considerably less impressive than the Sealing Seed.

A barrier and healing, huh.

Defensive abilities, in other words.

He didn't relish the thought of sitting there and taking a beating.

Besides, Abe Akaya already had a similar barrier ability, and this seed was damaged to boot.

The game hadn't even provided a hint about how to repair it.

Fixing it would probably be troublesome.

Still…

If he didn't want to go through all that trouble, he could simply let Abe Akaya absorb it as energy.

After all, the seed was packed with power.

It certainly wasn't as valuable as the first one.

But it wouldn't be a loss either.

Fang Heng moved on to the third seed.

[Item: Energy Seed—Spatial Extension.]

Special power: Spatial Extension.

Description: Upon awakening this power, the Divine Tree may transcend spatial restrictions and extend its branches into other worlds. All Divine Trees share the same HP pool.

Ability duration after absorption: 24 months.

What?!

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

This thing…

He stared at the short item description in disbelief.

This was incredible!

Even better than the Sealing Seed!

If Abe Akaya could absorb it and inherit the skill…

The main thing preventing Abe Akaya from growing any further was its inability to spread across different spaces. If it could extend into other worlds…

Fang Heng's heart hammered wildly.

Take them!

He had to take all three!

According to the descriptions, the inherited abilities would only be temporary. But even the shortest duration was two whole years.

That was more than enough time for him to find a way to make them permanent!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Just as Fang Heng finished reading the seed attributes, a barrage of whistling sounds came from behind him.

He spun around.

An enormous volley of boulders was hurtling straight toward him!

What the hell?!

The dense hail of rock blocked every route of escape!

Fang Heng immediately raised his arms to shield himself.

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

Huge rocks slammed directly into his body!

Fang Heng gritted his teeth.

Every boulder seemed to carry the force of a thousand pounds!

Pain flared wherever they struck, and the impacts sent his entire body flying backward!

The relentless barrage kept pounding into him, hurling him farther and farther away!

[Hint: The player has been attacked by Stone Spirit Puppet (Giant). HP −12,423. Stunned for 0.42 seconds (reduced by Constitution). The player has entered a knockback state…]

— End of Chapter 2204 —

Chinese Expressions
