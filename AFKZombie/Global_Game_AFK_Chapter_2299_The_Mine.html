Chapter 2299

The Mine

From original Chinese Chapter 2300 · 矿区

"Everyone, rise."

Fang Heng raised his head and motioned for the vampires to stand. His gaze swept across the magic array in the hall as he nodded. "The vampire race is still far from reaching the limit of its strength. Prepare yourselves. We've got another tough battle coming up."

"Yes, Your Majesty!"

Meanwhile, in the real world.

Although it was already the middle of the night, players who had felt their attributes undergo a dramatic transformation were in an uproar!

After the enormous increase in vampire bloodline power last time, all of their basic attributes had suddenly surged again tonight!

With both bonuses combined, the power provided by the vampire bloodline had more than doubled—nearly tripled in total!

Their overall combat capability had instantly risen to another tier!

What an unexpected surprise!

On examining the game ratings more carefully, the players were even more excited to discover that the vampire bloodline had now been classified as Legendary grade.

What exactly was this dimensional power?

How could it provide such an enormous improvement?

Players flooded the forums, trying to work out what had triggered the change.

But even after extensive discussion, no one could find an explanation.

Spurred on by the breakthrough, more and more players began searching for ways to inherit the vampire bloodline.

Its value for money was simply incredible!

Unscrupulous traders who happened to have stockpiled a couple of vampire bloodline skill books could barely contain their excitement.

Rich overnight!

They'd struck gold!

...

Inside the Federation.

Another round of comprehensive tests on the Psychic Studies-enhanced soldiers was being conducted at full speed.

"Report! The latest test results are in."

"How are they?"

"Excellent! The subjects' physical attributes have increased dramatically again. At this rate, they're fully capable of entering the next stage of Psychic Tempering."

The researchers were almost speechless when they received the detailed test report.

The numbers were staggering.

The subjects' attributes had improved to an extraordinary degree yet again.

The enhanced psychic soldiers now possessed physical attributes suitable for combined-arms combat.

Studying the two modified soldiers on the monitor, Minister Lei asked, "If we disregard their Psychic Tempering training, how powerful are their physical capabilities alone?"

Understanding the question, the laboratory director nodded. "By our estimates, each could match a semi-mechanically augmented soldier. In fact, their bodies may be even more adaptable!"

"Report!" A soldier hurried over. "Sir, the Leaf Clan became active five minutes ago. They've attacked one of our mineral reserves."

Minister Lei frowned deeply.

The Leaf Clan again!

It wasn't the first time.

The Federation had previously tried cooperating with them, only to find that the Leaf Clan accepted supplies while repeatedly failing to deliver the promised Leaves of Life. Later, several temporary depots intended for the exchanges were attacked by Leaf Clan members, and large quantities of ore went missing.

After suffering several losses, the Federation decisively ended the arrangement.

But now the Leaf Clan had become even more brazen, directly seizing a mining site.

"How strong is their force this time?"

"We've identified twelve ordinary Leaf Clan guards. No elder-level members have been detected so far."

An accompanying military adviser nodded. "Minister, this Leaf Clan team isn't particularly strong. I recommend sending a force to defeat them. Otherwise, they'll only become more reckless."

"Hmm."

Minister Lei nodded and turned to Shi Yanwu. "You'll command this operation. A semi-mechanized squad will accompany you. Are you confident?"

Shi Yanwu straightened and saluted. "Yes, sir! I won't let you down!"

Minister Lei nodded approvingly, but just then the door opened again.

Another soldier hurried in to report.

"Sir! Chairman Ji has sent word that Mr. Fang Heng has returned safely and is preparing an operation against the Leaf Clan. He wants to know whether we have a suitable target." The soldier glanced toward the researchers. "Fang Heng has joined our communications network. He's on Channel Seventeen."

"Quickly, establish the connection."

Moments later, the connection opened and Fang Heng's image appeared on the large screen.

"Fang Heng, it's been a while. This is Lei Changhong. There's an urgent situation, so I'll skip the pleasantries. The Leaf Clan occupied one of our mining sites a short time ago. Its coordinates are (12831, 03223). We were planning an operation to drive them out in thirty minutes."

Hearing this, Fang Heng's interest was immediately piqued.

Having completed his upgrade in the Inner World, he had planned to cause some trouble in the Outer World and find a way to obtain more World Coordinate Branches from the Leaf Clan.

Conveniently, the Leaf Clan had presented itself as a target.

"Understood. I'll bring my people over immediately. We'll meet there."

After ending the call, Fang Heng led the vampires behind him swiftly toward the Leaf Clan's location.

There were only a dozen ordinary Leaf Clan members at the target site.

Truthfully, Fang Heng no longer considered such a small group worth worrying about.

He figured that eliminating them would probably draw more Leaf Clan reinforcements.

He could then destroy the newcomers in small groups, one batch at a time.

Wouldn't that be perfect?

...

At the mining site occupied by the Leaf Clan.

The mine had previously been on the verge of abandonment, but the Federation had bought it up in an emergency and begun operating it around the clock.

The Tree Spirits were generally poor at mining.

Most of the time, they preferred seizing existing supplies or having the Tree Whisperer Alliance assist with extraction.

This time, with information supplied by the Tree Whisperer Alliance, they had located the abandoned mine and simply occupied it by force.

The Alliance would handle the subsequent mining operations.

After losing several elders and Ritual Vessels in succession, as well as suffering the destruction of their Divine Tree sapling's offshoot, the Leaf Clan was desperate to reverse its losses. That urgency had driven it to disregard the Federation entirely.

Compared with the other clans, the Leaf Clan was much more reckless.

It had never taken humans seriously.

The moment the Leaf Clan squad leader learned the mine's location, he led his subordinates there.

Just as expected, the mine's human guards were exceptionally weak.

They seized it with almost no effort.

Huh?

Suddenly, the Leaf Clan guards noticed something and looked toward the sky.

What was that?

Against the darkness of the night, tiny black specks seemed to be rapidly approaching from above.

"Enemies!"

Before they could identify the shapes in the air, a dense volley of blood-energy bombs rained down!

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM, BOOM!

The concentrated barrage immediately threw the mine, occupied by the Leaf Clan and Tree Whisperer Alliance, into chaos!

The blood-energy bombs struck the ground and erupted into enormous globes of blood!

Their explosive power was three times greater than before!

The improvement was visible to the naked eye!

The Vampire Princes and Dukes accompanying Fang Heng into battle wore excited expressions.

They could clearly feel just how much their strength had increased!
