Chapter 3009

Dividing the Spoils

From Chinese Chapter 3010 · 瓜分

The others exchanged glances.

In fact, the Federations had long wondered why no extraterrestrial life had ever visited their world after so many years.

Even contact with the Kinsfolk had happened only because humanity followed various clues, located them, and reached out first.

And during earlier exchanges between the Western Federation and the Kinsfolk, it had become clear that the Kinsfolk didn't possess their world's exact coordinates.

As far as the Kinsfolk were concerned, their world was effectively invisible.

Now that the Titan's protection had failed, any race hostile toward humanity might come knocking.

Perhaps even the gods who had once exterminated the Titans.

The Northern Federation leader snorted. "Hmph! What's there to fear? Have humans faced too few disasters? Haven't we survived every single one? Instead of trembling and worrying about what might happen, we might as well fight with everything we've got and carve out a future!"

The others smiled bitterly but nodded along.

What else could they do?

At least they couldn't afford to lose their morale.

First the Calamity, then black fog, and now some kind of gods.

What a disaster-plagued world...

...

Half an hour passed.

At the temple's center, Fang Heng ended his secluded cultivation and opened his eyes.

Qi really was useful.

It could convert among different forms of academic energy.

The only problem was how long it took. Since the energy was stored in his Sea of Consciousness, his zombie clones couldn't do it for him.

"Grand Sage."

Archbishop Whiteman bowed respectfully and handed over a note. "The Federations found a secret chamber next door. This is the translation of the inscriptions recorded on its stone walls."

Fang Heng read the note and silently considered it.

The records were probably genuine.

By his reasoning, the Titan Guardian they'd encountered really had existed to protect humanity.

The problem was that while guarding the ice-crystal labyrinth, he'd encountered an intruder: Xia Xi.

Xia Xi defeated him, took the Wandering Scholar Mark, and left an Ouroboros Seed in the labyrinth.

So the person secretly controlling the Land of Apocalypse was probably Xia Xi.

She'd guided him here to obtain Titan power.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes slightly.

Xia Xi had usually paid him little attention. Yet recently she'd been giving him hints, both openly and indirectly, helping him grow stronger quickly.

Most likely a far more dangerous mission was waiting for him afterward.

He raised the note and read it again.

What concerned him most was the mention of gods.

If the Titan ancestors had been exterminated by gods, then what exactly were those gods?

The Federation teams left in the hall noticed he'd awakened and came forward.

The Central Federation didn't know Fang Heng's true identity and thought him a Grand Sage from the Divine Realm. Relying on its longstanding friendship with the Holy Court, Luo Ao approached first and bowed respectfully.

"Grand Sage, the Central Federation hopes to borrow the jewel in your possession to help us awaken our bloodlines."

Borrow the jewel?

Fang Heng glanced at the Titan Jewel's information in his inventory.

Its energy had already been drained. Although it could slowly convert ambient energy, the efficiency was poor. Its main function was storage, so it wasn't terribly useful to him.

The Qi in his Sea of Consciousness would already last him a long time.

Collecting more Qi through the jewel would be painfully slow.

Lending it to the Federations might actually be a good idea.

Fang Heng considered this and asked, "Oh? What are you willing to offer in exchange?"

Luo Ao looked surprised.

The Holy Court had always acted arrogantly in its dealings with the Federations, but it at least cared about appearances. It had never asked for benefits so bluntly.

This Grand Sage certainly had an unconventional way of doing things.

"The Central Federation has always cooperated closely with the Holy Court. If you need anything, you need only ask, and we'll do our best to provide it."

"Ahem."

Afraid the Central Federation would beat them to it, Yi Qingzhi of the Eastern Federation cleared his throat. "Grand Sage, the Eastern Federation would also like to offer something in exchange for temporary use of the jewel."

"We'd like to borrow it for a while too."

Augustine of the Western Federation and the Northern Federation leader quickly nodded in agreement.

"Since everyone is so interested in the jewel..."

Fang Heng thought for a moment. "Very well. I always believe in fairness. Right now, I need advancement books for various academic disciplines. Whoever provides higher-level advancement books can use the jewel longer."

He took pen and paper from Archbishop Whiteman and began listing the academic skills he currently possessed.

There was no need to list Sacred Studies or Necromancy. There were no books that could directly advance them anyway.

As for the others...

Comparing the list with his existing academic levels, Fang Heng wrote down his requirements.

Astrology: Advanced-level advancement book or higher.

Alchemy: Master-level advancement book or higher.

Mysticism (Curse Studies): Master-level advancement book or higher.

Summoning Studies (Contract Studies)...

Spatial Studies...

Natural Studies...

Elemental Studies...

Psionics...

Sealing Studies...

Arcane Studies...

Astrology...

"Oh, right." Halfway through, Fang Heng remembered something and added, "Special bloodline skill books will also do."

The Federation representatives stared at one another in confusion.

Then Fang Heng handed them the completed list.

They grew even more puzzled. Their expressions turned strange as they looked at him.

Wasn't he supposed to be a Holy Court Grand Sage?

Why did he need so many academic advancement books?

And books of such extraordinarily high levels, at that.

Looking closely, apart from Necromancy, Sacred Studies, and Hell Studies, the list included practically every academic discipline currently known!

"We'll do our best to look," Luo Ao said with a headache. "Grand Sage, high-tier advancement books are exceedingly rare, and most are bound to individuals. But if we can find a related quest or information..."

"That works too, though it'll buy less time with the jewel. Also, I only need one copy of each. Don't submit duplicates."

"Understood."

Luo Ao committed the requirements to memory and hurried away.

The Federations all had some academic advancement books in reserve.

They were kept for elites they were training themselves.

If they searched their stores carefully, Master- and Grandmaster-level books might still be found, though they were exceptionally rare.

Related advancement quests were particularly promising!

The sooner they found one and delivered it to Fang Heng, the sooner they'd gain access to the jewel and strengthen their own Federation's players!
