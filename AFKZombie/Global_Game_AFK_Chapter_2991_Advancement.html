Chapter 2991

Advancement

From Chinese Chapter 2992 · 进阶

For more than a month, Fang Heng had remained in the Death Realm, meditating and using his mental strength to condense the pearls.

Today, at last, he'd advanced!

He closed his eyes again, sensing the explosive growth in Mental Strength and Will within him.

Under the amplification of his Will attribute and the skill itself, he felt that his soul had become terrifyingly powerful.

His Mental Strength had also soared.

Fang Heng extended an open hand.

The surrounding death aura quickly converged in his palm, forming a black sphere.

The sphere was only the size of an egg, yet contained a horrifying amount of power.

Fang Heng tossed it lightly into the sky.

Whoosh!

“BOOM!!”

It exploded violently overhead, releasing a wave of death aura that blanketed several hundred meters with a dense Death Realm atmosphere.

After the massive improvement in Mental Strength, Fang Heng could sense another explosive leap in his overall power.

He drew a deep breath and studied the skill description again.

Super-Divine-Level.

He hadn't imagined there was another stage beyond Divine-Level Necromancy. Even the Undead Council probably didn't know it existed.

And it seemed Super-Divine-Level was the final stage. There was no limit to its advancement: every level could further raise his Will, Mental Strength, and soul power.

But...

Fang Heng frowned slightly.

The conditions for improving the skill were rather strange.

Rather than absorbing different forms of energy as before, he had to expand the black-fog worlds?

How was he supposed to expand a black-fog world?

Puzzled, Fang Heng continued examining the details.

Advancing to Super-Divine-Level Necromancy had also granted several additional skills.

Additional Skill 1: Netherworld Imprisonment.

Skill Description: Select a target and attack its soul, sending it into the Death Realm to enter reincarnation. (Usable only inside black-fog zones.)

Skill Cooldown: 60 minutes.

Additional Skill 2: Netherworld Pearls (24/24).

Skill Description: Netherworld Pearls condensed from the Death Realm's power have formed a mental link with the player. During battle, they can absorb Death Realm energy and fight autonomously to assist the player. Each pearl additionally increases the effect of Necromancy-attribute skills by 3.3% and grants +33 Strength, +33 Constitution, and +33 Agility. Destroyed pearls can be condensed again within the Death Realm.

Additional Skill 3: Descent of the Death Realm (Domain Skill).

Skill Description: Summon the Death Realm's descent, greatly strengthening the Death Realm environment. The domain applies an additional 5,000% soul pressure. Enemies within its area continuously suffer the effects of reincarnation power. Domain duration and area depend on the player's Mental Strength.

Skill Cooldown: 6 hours.

Fang Heng reviewed everything carefully.

There were only three new skills, but each was incredibly powerful. Descent of the Death Realm had a cooldown of just six hours, relatively short for an ultimate move.

Then there were the Netherworld Pearls that had taken him more than half a month to condense.

They were somewhat like the pearls he'd used in his Underworld God state, but the amount of death energy contained within them was on a completely different scale.

They didn't even need to join a battle. Simply keeping them active provided tremendous attribute bonuses.

As for their precise effects, he'd have to test them in actual combat.

Fang Heng stood up and surveyed his surroundings.

But weren't all these pearls circling around his body a little too conspicuous?

With a thought, the twenty-four pearls spun rapidly and sank into his body, leaving black marks upon his skin.

Much less conspicuous.

His period of seclusion was over.

Fang Heng left the hall and took a breath of fresh air outside.

He'd solved the Death Realm's troubles, broken through the Lock in the real world, and gained access to all his abilities. After advancing his Necromancy to Super-Divine-Level, his attributes had risen explosively again. He could even reincarnate after death inside black-fog game environments, greatly improving his margin for error.

But it was not time to relax. One more great threat remained.

The Demon Seed—Envy.

With his current strength, could he take the initiative and challenge Envy directly?

Fang Heng examined his Sea of Consciousness.

As his strength grew, the shadow left there by the Demon Seed Envy had almost ceased to restrict him.

No. Wait a little longer.

He wanted to strike with everything he had and leave Envy no chance whatsoever!

...

The real world.

After the Western and Eastern Federations confronted each other in the game, both sides tacitly withdrew to reality.

The Kinsfolk Star Map Project was abruptly declared suspended. The Western Federation announced that construction would remain at its current stage and urged the other Federations to stay calm.

The whole world faced threats from the black fog and outside forces. Fighting among themselves at a time like this would be incredibly foolish.

The public had long since become immune to the Western Federation's shameless statements. Hadn't the Western Federation been the one to start the Star Map Project in the first place? And now it was calling for calm?

Players were much more interested in why the Western Federation's position had suddenly reversed completely.

Only those in the know understood: the Western Federation had been scared out of its wits.

What a joke.

The Eastern Federation already had an entire fleet of warships!

All because of Fang Heng.

It was obvious that Fang Heng and the Eastern Federation stood far above them in the Kinsfolk hierarchy.

If the Star Map were completed, who would call the shots in this world?

The Eastern Federation, of course.

Wouldn't that make the Western Federation a complete laughingstock?

They'd originally hoped to use the Kinsfolk's power to become the world's overlords. Now they would only be carrying the Eastern Federation's sedan chair—doing all the work so someone else could reap the reward!

Naturally, the Western Federation was absolutely unwilling.

They didn't dare offend the Kinsfolk, but neither would they work to benefit their rivals. So they started dragging their feet, pushing back the Star Map's construction schedule.

The Kinsfolk apparently noticed that humanity was delaying deliberately, expressed displeasure, and repeatedly pressed for progress.

The Western Federation simply smiled, apologized, said it understood—and still refused to budge.

Rather than easing tensions among the major powers, the suspension of the Star Map intensified their competition to cultivate stronger players.

The Central Federation and the regional Federations relaxed the restrictions surrounding the Lock even further.

Every major faction began seeking new breakthroughs in strength within the game.

As a result, the Vampire Apocalypse became a treasure trove in the eyes of players everywhere.

There was no faster way to achieve a massive increase in strength than acquiring a Vampire bloodline.

Players called it the latest generation of ‘fully automatic wheelchair’—an effortless shortcut to power.

Obtaining a Vampire bloodline was practically a one-step ascent to the heavens!

Coincidentally, the Vampires had also released a mysterious special quest chain.

The Main Battlefield Plan.

Players could board Vampire starships, enter various special worlds, complete commissions including ore collection, and accumulate points to exchange for a range of in-game items.

Among the rewards was even the extraordinarily precious Vampire bloodline!

— End of Chapter 2991 —
