Chapter 2091

The Leader

From original Chinese Chapter 2092 · 首领

"It's a leader-class creature!"

The players descended into panic. Even those who'd intended to resist abandoned the idea and fled toward the rear.

A three-headed Hell Horned Demon! A boss-class creature! How were they supposed to fight that?!

"Fall back! Retreat to the teleportation area!"

The two Crusader instructors frowned and rushed forward, trying to stop the leader and cover the other players' withdrawal.

As Lindro's team watched the Holy Court formation descend into chaos behind them, they silently thanked their luck.

Without Fang Heng's early warning, they might have been in serious trouble too.

But how had Fang Heng known something bad was about to happen?

Everyone stole a glance at him.

Surely it couldn't be...

"Come on! Get back to the teleportation passage first!"

Fang Heng returned with the fleeing crowd to the cavern housing the magic arrays that linked to the outside world.

The Holy Court force stationed there had already learned about the crisis and immediately sent a team to assist.

At the same time, notifications appeared in the players' vision.

[Hint: Emergency Trial Quest triggered — A New Test.]

Quest Name: A New Test.

Quest Description: During the mission, the Hell Horned Demon tribe from the abyss has become unusually agitated and attacked the Holy Court's forces. Assist the Holy Court in eliminating the rampaging horned demons.

Quest Requirement: Kill Hell Horned Demons.

Quest Rewards: Points and Crusader EXP.

Failure Penalty: None.

[Hint: If you are unable to continue fighting, you may return directly to the Eternal Holy City through the teleportation passage. Doing so will deduct some Crusader points and experience.]

Fang Heng's interest was piqued by the notification.

A new quest had been triggered. And they'd unlocked the option to leave the trial early.

With that thought, he hurried toward the teleportation passage in the direction of the retreat.

In front of the magic array, the last Crusader instructor and the Holy Court members around him watched the three-headed demon approaching with grave expressions.

How strange!

What had driven the horned demons mad?

Couldn't even two Crusader instructors stop this creature?

At Hell's entrance, the surrounding environment suppressed a large portion of the instructors' strength.

Whatever happened, they couldn't allow the creature to destroy the teleportation passage the Holy Court had worked so hard to build!

"Together! Stop it!"

The instructor gathered a dark-golden sphere of holy light before him. Behind him, dozens of Holy Court priests acted in unison, pouring their spiritual power into it.

The glowing sphere expanded rapidly.

"Now!"

They launched it straight at the charging three-headed demon!

BOOM!

Scorching holy radiance erupted across its body!

The impact hurled the demon five or six meters backward, where it crashed to the ground. Most of the black mist pouring off it was dispersed with a hissing sound. The place where the sphere had struck was a bloody, burned mess, with sacred flames still clinging to the wound.

At least they'd stopped it for the moment!

The instructor breathed a sigh of relief and exchanged a glance with the other two instructors, who had caught up from behind.

"Continue! Keep it restrained!"

Seeing the three instructors suppress the boss, the players finally recovered from their panic. They began reforming their teams to fight the hordes of ordinary horned demons pursuing them.

Lindro's party reunited and started discussing how to deal with the boss.

Sak suddenly found something strange.

Where was Fang Heng?

How had he vanished again?

He'd clearly been running away with them just a moment ago!

But Fang Heng was now hiding not far from the teleportation passage.

"Excellent," he murmured.

Seeing the three Crusader instructors occupied with the boss, he turned around and dashed back toward the high-grade altar he'd visited earlier.

He'd already come this far!

Now was the perfect chance to claim that altar!

· · ·

In the lava region guarded by the Hell Horned Demon tribe.

A large portion of the tribe had not been lured away and still occupied the area around the altar.

After circling back toward the entrance, Meng Si's group had returned as well.

They were secretly studying the massive altar in the distance.

What a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity!

Fang Heng had lured the three-headed leader away. It wouldn't be back for a while.

If they could somehow seize the altar...

They were preparing to act when they suddenly sensed a disturbance.

What?!

The horned demons in the lava field were becoming agitated again, staring toward the entrance with angry, uneasy expressions.

Meng Si and the others immediately followed their gaze.

A figure had appeared in the passage and was approaching rapidly.

Fang Heng!

Damn it!

He'd come back again!

Fang Heng was surprised to see Meng Si's group still there. With a quick push off the ground, he changed direction and appeared directly in front of them.

"Haven't left yet?"

Meng Si's heart lurched under his gaze. He lowered his head in a hurry.

"No! Nothing like that! We're leaving immediately! Right now!"

Meng Si turned to flee, only to feel a hand clamp down on his shoulder.

"Wait!"

He turned back with a bitter expression. "World Lord Fang Heng, do you have any other instructions?"

"Help me with something. Wait here a little longer. Afterward, I want you to lead me out of this place. Take me back to the Holy Court—quickly. Understand?"

Meng Si stared at him, clearly baffled. "What did you say?"

When he looked up again, Fang Heng tossed him a magic scroll.

"Hmm?"

A spatial magic scroll?

By the time Meng Si raised his head, Fang Heng was already sprinting toward the horned demon horde!

Going back in again?

Startled, the players hurried forward a few steps to see what he intended.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The instant Fang Heng stepped onto the lava field, hundreds of scorching concentrated hellfire spheres came flying toward him!

Whoosh!

Just as before, he vanished into thin air, avoiding all the follow-up attacks.

A spatial ability!

Spatial powers were incredibly effective in battle!

Meng Si couldn't help feeling envious.

Unfortunately, they weren't something anyone could learn. You needed the right talent.

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

The fireballs crashed into empty ground, shattering rocks and making the earth tremble beneath their feet.

Where was Fang Heng?

The players searched the cavern anxiously.

About a minute later, Fang Heng passed through the mass of horned demons and reappeared directly beside the altar.

— End of Chapter 2091 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Optional evacuation. The emergency quest permits a premature return to the Eternal Holy City with a deduction of Crusader points and experience.
  • Three instructors. Two delay the boss outside, while the third and the priests protect the teleportation array before the instructors regroup.

Chinese Expressions

  • 来都来了 (lái dōu lái le). Literally “since I’ve already come here.” A familiar Chinese justification for making the most of a visit; Fang Heng uses it to rationalize going back for the altar.
