Eighty star-seas away from humanity's planet in the real world.

The Shadow Clan and various allied races that had accepted the relevant missions arrived one after another.

The Kinsfolk hadn't yet issued a mission to attack humanity, but the Shadow Clan had already impatiently assembled an allied army. As soon as the mission appeared, they would invade the human world.

The Shadow Clan thought it rather unfortunate.

The human world was affected by the Black Fog, and the Kinsfolk wanted to preserve it for research and as a front line against the fog. Thus, according to the Kinsfolk, they didn't want to exterminate humanity entirely, but they did want to teach the humans a lesson.

The Shadow Clan had suffered a terrible loss at humanity's hands during the previous SSS-rank insect-nest mission.

Now countless eyes were watching them.

If the Shadow Clan failed to teach those arrogant humans a proper lesson, how would they ever command respect again? How could they lead the Kinsfolk alliance into battle?

They were simply waiting for the Kinsfolk to publish the exact coordinates of the human world. The Shadow Clan would attack at once.

“Welcome, Prince Korkalot of the Elven Clan.”

Korkalot was an esteemed prince of the Elven Clan, heir to the next Elven King, and possessed tremendous influence among his people.

The Shadow Clan captain found his visit strange.

The elves rarely undertook outside missions in large groups. Yet this time, when the Kinsfolk rallied forces to attack humanity, they had shown exceptional enthusiasm, even sending more than twenty high-grade ships.

Elven vessels weren't particularly powerful, but their individual warriors were generally strong.

Out of consideration for the Elven Clan, the Shadow Clan captain rose to greet him. “Your Highness, what brings you here in person?”

Korkalot, cold-faced and arrogant, glanced at the captain. “Captain, the Elven Clan has already discovered the precise coordinates of the human world. We needn't wait any longer. I'll have my people share the coordinates with everyone. Order the allied army to attack humanity immediately.”

The Shadow Clan captain resented Korkalot's commanding tone.

They'd shown him a little respect for his people's sake, and now he thought he could act like a prince over them?

Ordering the Shadow Clan around?

The Shadow Clan alliance wasn't run by the Elven Clan.

“Is that so... The elves really are impressive, finding even the hidden coordinates of the human world so easily.” The Shadow Clan captain's expression cooled. After that faint compliment, he asked, “But can the Elven Clan confirm that the coordinates are correct?”

“Of course. We've already made a reconnaissance trip into the human world and confirmed that humans live there.” Korkalot said coldly, “In exchange, once the human world is conquered, our Elven Clan needs to search for something there...”

“I see. Understood.”

After saying that, the Shadow Clan captain fell silent. He didn't agree or refuse. He merely sat back down and treated the elven visitors as though they weren't there.

Korkalot frowned, his expression turning colder. “Captain, what is the meaning of this?”

“The Kinsfolk have already said they'll give humanity some more time. Officially, humans are still their allies. Since the Kinsfolk haven't issued an order to attack, the Shadow Clan will not strike at an ally on its own initiative.”

The captain's initial warmth had vanished. He said icily, “If your Elven Clan is interested, you're welcome to attack humanity yourselves. But don't say I didn't warn you: humans aren't weak.”

“Hmph!”

Having been rebuffed, Korkalot gave a heavy snort and returned to his ship.

The two Tree Spirit elders accompanying him shook their heads inwardly.

Korkalot was too young and had too little experience outside his homeland. In a few short sentences, he'd revealed the Elven Clan's cards to the Shadow Clan.

The elves had already investigated humanity ahead of time.

Black Fog level twelve.

That meant all humans on the planet had been affected by the Black Fog. The world had been partly invaded by it, and some humans had already gained partial control over its power.

It would be difficult for the Elven Clan to conquer the human world completely with its forces alone.

For now, they could only wait for the Kinsfolk to give the final order to attack.

...

Five days later.

Aboard the Mouse King, a ship sailing through the sea of stars.

Cargo hold seven.

Dense vines had completely engulfed the warehouse.

A large Abe Akaya summoning array occupied its center. Thin strands of black aura seeped continuously from its edge, while thick tangles of vines and roots emerged from the middle and extended outward.

If anyone else had been present, the density of natural energy in the hold would have shocked them.

Since Abe Akaya had evolved to Complete Form and gained control of most of Hell's twentieth through thirty-fourth levels, it had been absorbing Hell's power every moment to strengthen itself.

And it was absorbing energy from the Demon Cores at the same time.

Even with the Elven Clan continuously draining power from Abe Akaya, it still had tremendous energy to spare.

That surplus could fuel its own advancement, or Abe Akaya could convert it into natural energy for Fang Heng's cultivation.

At the moment, Fang Heng sat beneath Abe Akaya's vines, absorbing natural energy and then converting it to elemental energy through Qi.

Done!

Another discipline finished.

Fang Heng opened his eyes.

With Abe Akaya's teleportation array, he could cultivate in seclusion almost anywhere, even aboard a ship making continuous spatial jumps.

There was one thing that concerned him.

As they sailed farther into the sea of stars and away from the human world, Abe Akaya's magic array had been slightly affected, operating with a little resistance.

Overall, though, the effect was minor.

There was also the matter of the wisps of black fog that appeared around Abe Akaya's summoning array whenever it operated.

It was the same aura as the game's Black Fog.

Very strange. He didn't remember seeing anything like it when operating the array before.

Fang Heng had examined the aura but found nothing abnormal, so he'd eventually left the fog alone and focused on cultivation.

That would do for today.

Fang Heng rose and left the cabin.

By his calculations, they should be able to locate the Elven Clan within the next couple of days.

Outside, two Flying Squirrel guards were waiting at the door. When they saw Fang Heng emerge, they nodded respectfully. “Boss Fang, Captain Kappa invites you to the main control hall.”

“All right. Lead the way.”

Through directional tracing, Abe Akaya had confirmed that it had locked onto the source siphoning off its energy.

But most functions of the human ship's Kinsfolk system were currently restricted. They couldn't link the elves' physical coordinates to the virtual positioning system on the Kinsfolk network.

Put simply, they couldn't use the Kinsfolk jump system to travel directly to that world.
