Chapter 2064

Spreading

From original Chinese Chapter 2065 · 散布

“Report!”

Just then, another Holy Court member hurried over.

“Sir! Undead creatures have been spotted in Cookville City! Fang Heng may be there!”

Xilufei's pupils abruptly contracted.

Damn those undead! It had to be Fang Heng!

They had barely finished cleaning up the mess he had left behind, and the brat had already moved on to the nearest city to cause more destruction!

Xilufei was so furious she wanted to suppress Fang Heng on the spot. Yet until reinforcements from the Divine Realm arrived, she honestly couldn't think of an effective way to contain him.

After all, even the Demon-Sealing Cavern hadn't held him!

And the entire Duncha game world was still facing the grave threat of the Yin Vein.

Xilufei took a deep breath and forced herself to calm down.

“Send reinforcements to Cookville immediately. Don't confront Fang Heng directly. Just drive him away. The priority is protecting the city and its inhabitants.”

“Understood!”

…

At that moment, inside Cookville City, Fang Heng was standing on the roof of a tall building.

Freshly revived zombie clones and Lickers kept leaping down from the roof, launching attacks toward the bustling city center!

Panic erupted in the heart of the city.

People brought down by the zombies became breeding grounds for new infected creatures. One zombie after another rose from the bodies of the fallen.

Terror spread rapidly outward from the city center!

The Holy Court's defenders arrived within five minutes of the outbreak and began organizing an effective resistance. Their response was remarkably quick.

But in a densely populated metropolis, five minutes was more than enough time for a zombie virus to do catastrophic damage!

It gave the Lickers time to generate a huge wave of mutated infected!

Game notifications raced across Fang Heng's vision.

[Hint: You have caused destruction in Cookville City, killed numerous Holy Court believers and members of its reinforcement teams, and negatively affected the Duncha game world. Rewards: 1,770,000 Undead Faction Reputation, 1,330,000 Undead Faction Contribution Points, and 600,000 Undead Faction Honor Points.]

[Hint: Based on the above, progress for Rescue Qiboreya has increased by 22.2%.]

[Hint: Progress for your current mission, Rescue Qiboreya, has reached 500%.]

Excellent!

His overall mission completion had reached 500%. Under his agreement with Mentor Dickey, that meant he could advance his Necromancy to the Grandmaster level!

But…

Now wasn't the time to stop.

Ever since he'd fallen out with the Holy Court, Fang Heng had felt like its members were constantly targeting him. Now that he finally had a chance to strike back, how could he pass it up?

Might as well squeeze out some more rewards while he was at it!

Several players from the Devout Guild stood nervously behind him. In just a few minutes, they had watched a prosperous city descend into a living hell.

“Let's go.”

“Go?”

The guild players were momentarily stunned. Weren't they winning?

“The Holy Court is coming after us,” Fang Heng explained calmly. “There's no need to fight them directly. Let's move on to the next city.”

“Okay…”

They nodded.

Onward!

Fang Heng's reasoning was simple. As long as the Holy Court couldn't do anything about him, he could keep farming.

The mission might already be complete, but he could continue raising the completion percentage, and he would earn faction Honor Points on top of it.

Honor Points could be exchanged for Main God Points. There was no such thing as having too many!

Strike once, then move!

…

“Report! The undead in Cookville have been eliminated. Casualties are being assessed…”

“Report! Holy City Wayne is under undead attack. Reinforcements have been dispatched immediately.”

“Report! Luminala's team has completed its protective barrier and is moving west to help construct the next section.”

“Report! Anomalous fluctuations have appeared in the Yin Vein. Its energy is moving south more rapidly and may destroy the unfinished defensive barrier ahead of schedule. Legion Commander Muhiting is moving his forces to rebuild the defenses fifty kilometers farther back.”

“Report! Large numbers of undead have appeared in Luochuan. Local defenders have organized resistance…”

At the Holy Court's cathedral headquarters in Duncha, Central Bishop Xilufei and her judges listened to the never-ending reports with pounding headaches.

The Yin Vein was extremely unstable. Besides building barriers against its energy, they had to deal with Fang Heng starting fires all over Duncha.

He was stretching the Holy Court's forces to the breaking point!

Fang Heng attacked one place and promptly moved to another. Worse, he was effectively acting alone and was astonishingly mobile. The Holy Court could do little but trail behind him and clean up the wreckage.

Duncha had originally been a modern game world in which the Holy Court held dominant authority. Every major region had its own cathedral.

But as the world developed and its population grew, the guards assigned to each cathedral were no longer numerous enough to defend an entire modern metropolis.

Then the Yin Vein leak forced the Holy Court to redeploy many of those guards elsewhere.

Whenever Fang Heng arrived in a new city, the local cathedral could only finish gathering its defenders after mutated infected creatures had already spread. By then, it was too late.

Through the Mutated Infection talent, the zombie clones multiplied by the hundreds as the infection spread.

The Holy Court could only evacuate civilians, slowly wear down the infected hordes with its available forces, and wait for reinforcements before completing the cleanup.

Fang Heng, meanwhile, was having the time of his life.

He spent the entire afternoon farming to his heart's content!

Outside the game, Li Qingran had initially been negotiating prices with players. After all, a Grandmaster-level academic advancement mission could fetch an extremely high price—especially one with additional rewards.

That morning, she'd spent a considerable amount of time reaching an agreement with two players. Once they'd settled on a price acceptable to both sides, she'd hurried into the game to finalize the deal.

But as soon as she logged in, Li Qingran froze.

She rubbed her eyes, wondering whether she'd read the display correctly.

In just one morning, her mission completion had rocketed upward from 300%…

All the way to 800%!

And it was still slowly climbing!

The two other players were equally dumbfounded.

Well, that deal was definitely off!

A smile slowly spread across Li Qingran's face as she looked at the two buyers.

She would have to renegotiate the price.

At 800% completion, the mission had been vastly overfulfilled, and the value of its rewards had risen accordingly!

Yet she couldn't help wondering…

“What in the world has Fang Heng been doing over in Duncha?”

At that very moment, Fang Heng had just finished farming another city. He stretched in the back seat of a jeep, preparing to continue on to the next one.

Suddenly, a new game notification appeared before his eyes.

— End of Chapter 2064 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Cookville. 库克维尔 is a phonetic place name rendered Cookville; its original spelling cannot be recovered from Chinese characters alone.
  • Mutated Infection. 异化传染 is the zombie talent that allows infected bodies to multiply the army.
  • Mission percentage. The quest surpasses 500% and later 800% in this chapter; these are not typographical mistakes in the English.
