Chapter 3104

A Different Approach

From Chinese Chapter 3105 · 思路

In truth, while Fang Heng was in soul form, ordinary guard patrols might as well not have existed.

Especially in a large commercial office complex like this.

His vampire blood-sensing ability had already marked the positions of every guard and every employee who hadn't yet left the complex.

Fang Heng easily avoided the patrols and slipped into a random room in one of the buildings.

Still in soul form, he slowly returned to human form.

With a raise of his hand, the stone slab inscribed with an alchemical magic array appeared in his palm.

Whoosh!

His right pupil spun rapidly!

Thick black fog immediately poured from the spatial channel opened by the array, filling the room at astonishing speed.

The Black Mist spread rapidly!

Security throughout the complex had been reinforced, but ordinary guards had no hope of detecting an intruder of Fang Heng's level. By the time they realized something was wrong, the mist was already spilling out of the building's windows.

A guard pointed at the building in the distance, his legs shaking. "C-Captain... look at that..."

"It's Black Mist!"

"Quick! Get everyone out, evacuate the area, and sound the alarm!"

The guard captain's face turned pale with fright.

Even the Kinsfolk were terrified of that stuff. It might look harmless, but who knew what hidden negative effects it could have? Better run as far away as possible!

As the Black Mist continued to pour out, the guards, security teams, joint Seven-Realm Martial Capital defense force, and Kinsfolk warrior teams arrived in succession.

But just as before, a thorough search of the building uncovered absolutely nothing.

Fang Heng had long since slipped away in soul form.

Captain Balin didn't enter the building. He remained outside with some of his men to support the search.

Listening to the reports over his radio, Balin could only shake his head with a bitter smile.

Just as he'd expected, the search teams had found nothing!

He could see what was going on now. Someone was definitely making mischief behind the scenes, deliberately tormenting the Kinsfolk.

If his guess was right, another Black Mist alarm would probably sound somewhere else soon.

Sure enough, less than five minutes later, another alarm rang out.

Good grief.

Were they really going to pull this stunt every few days?

Who could possibly endure that?

Balin sighed inwardly and waved his hand. "Move out! Let's check the new alarm location!"

"Yes, sir!"

For yet another entire night, Black Mist leaks occurred repeatedly throughout the Kinsfolk-protected districts. The response teams were led around the city in circles like dogs on leashes, leaving them exhausted and miserable.

It wasn't only the regular teams. The warriors recruited by the Kinsfolk were dragged into it as well, and they had plenty of complaints.

But what could they do?

They couldn't find a single clue related to the Black Mist.

As the sun rose again, Balin stretched and rubbed his aching eyes.

He had finally survived another night.

That was enough. Time for everyone to go home and rest!

Meanwhile, in the council hall of the Kinsfolk building...

A pale-yellow teleportation beam flashed, and Administrator Lila appeared before the central seat.

The assembled Kinsfolk bowed respectfully.

"Greetings, Administrator."

"The council has learned of the Black Mist incidents. The Governing Council is extremely dissatisfied with your performance."

The Kinsfolk all lowered their heads.

"We are ashamed."

"The Supreme Council has appointed me to take charge of this matter. From this moment on, I am the supreme commander of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, with authority over all affairs here, including the Black Mist incident."

"Yes, Administrator!"

Administrator Lila calmly surveyed the assembly and said sternly, "I've already heard the reports on my way here. From what we know, the sources associated with Black Mist contamination have all been pan-humans—remnants of the Titan bloodline."

"We don't need to chase the mist itself. Change our approach. Investigate every pan-human in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital. I want a complete list of their names and individual surveillance of each one. At the first sign of anything suspicious, initiate the elimination protocol."

The Kinsfolk stiffened and nodded in unison. "Yes, Administrator!"

...

"Hahaha! The Kinsfolk have really landed themselves in trouble this time."

"Tell me about it. The Black Mist keeps popping up all over their territory. It's hilarious."

Early that morning, Fang Heng arrived at the Noel Clan building. Along the way, he heard groups of new clan recruits chatting among themselves.

"I wonder what the Kinsfolk are so scared of. They're acting like they've got a guilty conscience."

A warrior spotted Fang Heng and waved. "Brother Fang Heng! What a coincidence—you're here today too?"

"Mm."

Fang Heng nodded casually and entered a training room on the seventh floor.

Mu Shunan was sitting cross-legged. When he saw Fang Heng enter, he swallowed involuntarily and asked nervously, "You broke through?"

"Mm."

Fuck!

Upon receiving confirmation, Mu Shunan silently cursed.

This guy wasn't even human!

Yet despite that, he felt as though a heavy stone had finally been lifted from his chest.

He'd already guessed something when Anxi contacted him yesterday.

He just hadn't expected to be right.

The talent of this brat was terrifying!

But Fang Heng's next words almost made his jaw hit the floor.

"Over the past couple of days, I've been approaching the bottleneck in Mysticism. I'd like to break through to the next stage."

"Huh? Y-you mean you've already reached the perfected Grandmaster level in Mysticism too?"

Looking as if he'd seen a ghost, Mu Shunan stared at Fang Heng in disbelief and couldn't resist lightly touching his wrist.

A surge of spiritual power immediately flowed into Fang Heng's body.

Holy hell!

Mu Shunan stared fixedly at him.

It was absolutely true!

His Grandmaster-level Mysticism really had reached perfection.

What the hell was this brat made of?

Mu Shunan felt his confidence in his own cultivation crumble. Scratching his head, he studied Fang Heng from top to bottom before asking, "Do you need to consolidate your foundation? Haven't you been training a bit too intensely? Your body might not be able to take it."

"Mm. It shouldn't be a problem. I think I can handle it."

"All right. Maybe your constitution is simply far beyond that of ordinary people."

Mu Shunan had gradually grown accustomed to Fang Heng's monstrous talent. He took a deep breath and nodded. "Everything below the Transcendent level involves adapting and modifying your body. Once you enter the Transcendent stage, that transformation is complete. Further advancement is about controlling your body."

"Good."

Fang Heng was eager to acquire new knowledge. He stared at Mu Shunan enthusiastically and pressed, "How exactly do I control it?"

"No rush. First, we need to establish a basic concept. The world and region we inhabit are actually surrounded by a vast amount of free-floating energy. Its activity, magnitude, and other properties are all..."

Fang Heng couldn't help interrupting. "You mean laws?"

"Exactly! Laws!"

— End of Chapter 3104 —
