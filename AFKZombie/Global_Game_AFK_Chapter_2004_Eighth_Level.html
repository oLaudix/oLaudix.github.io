Chapter 2004

Eighth Level

Translated from Chinese Chapter 2005

“Dudley, what's on basement level eight?” Fang Heng asked.

Dudley blinked. “The eighth basement?”

“You don't know?”

Dudley glanced at the building's structural diagram on the large screen and shook his head. “Maybe… I think it's some kind of storage area. I'm not sure. I've never been down there.”

Fang Heng closed his eyes and extended his perception.

There was no mistake. That faint fluctuation of blood vitality was coming from below.

And it was growing stronger, spreading outward!

Something was definitely wrong down there. Fang Heng opened his eyes. “There's something strange about the eighth level. Is there a hidden area down there?”

“I really don't know.”

Dudley looked miserable.

He was just an ordinary research-team leader Miyamoto Teru had hired at a generous salary. Since arriving on Frostmist Island, he'd barely left the laboratory. How could he know about any secret areas? Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

Now this was interesting.

What could possibly be hidden on basement level eight that was producing such powerful fluctuations of blood vitality?

More strangely, the alarms on that level had only appeared after the power went out and was restarted. Fang Heng himself hadn't noticed the faint blood-vitality fluctuations until the power had been cut.

Could it have something to do with the Dragon Soul Crystal?

Rather than immediately heading downstairs, Fang Heng stayed where he was.

He waited for nearly another half hour.

Then, with a series of electrical crackles, arcs of current began dancing across the main teleportation gate.

Dudley finally breathed a little easier. He turned to Fang Heng and spoke quietly. “The portal is restarting. It should finish within five minutes. If the restart fails, that means a component inside the teleportation device is faulty. I won't be able to fix it quickly. I don't understand that side of the system at all, so killing me won't help…”

“Stop talking about killing people all the time. You make me sound like some kind of demon lord… I have high hopes for you, Dudley.”

Fang Heng said this with a cheerful smile, his eyes fixed on the portal.

“Oh, and you're five minutes past the deadline we agreed on.”

Dudley's brief sense of relief vanished. His heart leaped into his throat.

“Relax. I'm a reasonable man. If the portal starts successfully, I'll let it slide this once.” Fang Heng patted his shoulder. “Keep up the good work.”

“Y-yes…”

Feeling as though he'd narrowly escaped the gates of hell, Dudley hurried back to monitoring the portal's operating data.

Before long, Gu Qingzhu returned to the main hall with the demi-humans they had rescued from the third underground level.

Several members of the group had suffered serious injuries during the fighting.

Just then, the portal in the center of the hall completed its restart.

Hummm!

With a rumble from the huge machine, a pale-blue curtain of teleportation light appeared in the center of the gate.

The restart was complete! It worked!

Dudley pumped his fist, elated that his life appeared safe for the moment. Gu Qingzhu saw the portal and nodded to Jimmy beside her. “Take them into the game world first. We can work out how to evacuate everyone later.”

“Got it!”

Jimmy immediately organized the group, leading the wounded and the rescued demi-humans into the portal.

Gu Qingzhu approached Fang Heng. “Well? Did you find Miyamoto Teru's exact location?”

“Sort of. But he isn't in the institute. And I'm afraid we can't get out of here right now, either.”

Fang Heng spread his hands and quickly explained how he'd deliberately locked down the entire underground research complex and what that meant.

Gu Qingzhu frowned.

According to Fang Heng's plan, they'd successfully infiltrated Miyamoto's main research facility and rescued the captive demi-humans.

Their most important objective had been achieved.

The only disappointment was that Miyamoto himself wasn't there.

“It's a shame we can't just copy the institute's research data and take it with us, and that Miyamoto isn't here either.”

Gu Qingzhu sighed inwardly as she listened to Fang Heng's explanation.

“In that case, this operation ends here. The Federation has the outside sealed off. Killing Miyamoto under these circumstances would be nearly impossible. We should withdraw.”

Contrary to Fang Heng's expectations, Gu Qingzhu hadn't let anger overwhelm her. She quickly regained her composure. “Fang Heng, we found our people imprisoned on level three. They've suffered terrible injuries, probably connected to Miyamoto's experiments. I need to get them back to the Zombie Apocalypse as soon as possible.”

“That's what I was thinking. Let's get your people back to the Zombie Apocalypse first. Klevi can use the Divine Tree to help treat them.” Fang Heng nodded and looked back at the map on the big screen. “But we still have a little time. Would you mind helping me with something?”

“What is it?”

“Look at these locations.”

Gu Qingzhu followed his finger to the map, a thoughtful expression appearing on her face.

“Basement level eight,” Fang Heng explained. “Several alarms appeared there after the power was restored. Something about them doesn't look right.”

“True.”

“And none of the researchers seem to know much about that level.”

As he spoke, Fang Heng switched the surveillance display to a passageway leading farther underground.

“There's surveillance everywhere else in this institute, but nothing at all on level eight. Doesn't that seem odd?”

“You think there's something down there?” Gu Qingzhu asked.

“Yeah. I sensed a fluctuation of energy coming from the eighth level. I suspect it might be connected to the Dragon Soul Crystal.”

“All right. I'll come with you.”

Gu Qingzhu left Jimmy in charge of guarding the portal. Then she and Fang Heng headed back into the facility to investigate level eight.

A staircase connected the seventh and eighth underground levels.

They went down and entered the eighth level.

There wasn't a single surveillance camera in sight.

Judging by appearances alone, Dudley might have been right. The whole place looked like an ordinary storage area full of miscellaneous supplies.

Cold! The moment Fang Heng stepped into the corridor, a wave of chill swept over him.

The temperature here was dramatically lower than on the levels above. Why was it so cold?

Fang Heng and Gu Qingzhu exchanged a glance.

Gu Qingzhu shook her head.

She didn't have a vampire bloodline, so her perception of blood vitality was nowhere near as sensitive as Fang Heng's. She couldn't detect the fluctuations he'd described.

“Over there.”

Fang Heng focused on the blood-vitality signature and approached a large door at the far end of the corridor.

The door wasn't completely closed. A narrow gap remained.

Visible wisps of cold air seeped continuously through the opening.

How strange. It was an electronically locked door, yet for some reason it had been left slightly open rather than sealing shut.

— End of Chapter 2004 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Jimmy vs. Jite. 吉米 is “Jimmy,” distinct from 吉特 (“Jite”) in the previous chapter. They should not be silently merged.
  • Klevi: provisional name. 克莱薇 is a phonetic name, provisionally rendered “Klevi.” The original Chinese does not prove a canonical Western spelling. Let me know if the earlier English chapters consistently call her something different.
  • Temperature phrasing. The source literally says the temperature is “more than twice lower,” an imprecise expression; this is rendered “dramatically lower” rather than inventing a temperature.
