Chapter 3020

Pressure

From Chinese Chapter 3021 · 逼迫

Echidna's true body didn't dare join the battle, so she was guiding Hell creatures by attaching herself to them?

Wishful thinking.

Unfortunately for them, the Hell creatures faced a Holy Court army enhanced by all sorts of powerful buffs from him!

They still weren't nearly good enough!

The paladins swung their knightly swords with all their strength, cutting the horned demons to pieces. Behind them, the priests kept up their ranged attacks to help clear the enemies while casting healing spells to restore everyone's condition.

“Hiss!!”

Suddenly, a gray fissure opened in the rocky ceiling high above the cavern, revealing a gigantic vertical pupil.

The eye peered down, fixing its gaze on the Holy Court party.

“That eye!”

Sensing that they were being watched, Dean immediately looked up at the vertical pupil and pointed his staff toward it.

“Whoosh!”

A dark-golden streak shot from Dean's staff toward the eye.

“Boom!!!”

The pupil seemed to have very little defense. It burst apart instantly beneath the golden attack.

“Fang Bai, I can confirm that was a Demon Seed.” Dean's face turned grave as he studied the place where the eye had vanished. “It looked like the Envy Demon Seed. She's watching us from the shadows.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng gave a quiet acknowledgment, slowly raised his sacred staff, and released a Sacred Ripple.

“Hiss, hiss, hiss...”

Bathed in the ripple, the blue Echidna fluid clinging to the Hell monsters hissed and was instantly purified away.

“She'll come out sooner or later. Don't bother with her. Keep going.”

Not far from the cavern, Zhuang Yufei and several other Demon Seeds hid in the shadows, not daring to approach. They observed the Holy Court team from afar.

“This guy...”

When the Demon Seeds had first seen Fang Heng lead a Holy Court team into Hell, they had been stunned for quite some time.

To be fair, they had already prepared themselves for the speed at which Fang Heng's strength was increasing.

Even if he could now face a Grand Sage of the Divine Realm on his own, they wouldn't have been terribly surprised.

But...

There was one thing they simply couldn't understand.

How in the world had Fang Heng fooled so many people from the Holy Court into working for him?

“Using the Holy Court to invade Hell and wipe out Echidna is a brilliant move. If Eseklyna, the Envy Demon Seed, doesn't dare appear, the Holy Court will completely eliminate her demonic pet Echidna. Then the Demon Cores that have been troubling us will slip free of her control.”

The fat man beside them looked impressed and continued, “If Eseklyna can't resist coming out to stop them... No, she'll definitely come out to stop the Holy Court! That'll be our best chance to deal with Echidna!”

If even they hadn't expected Fang Heng to command the Holy Court, the Envy Demon Seed certainly wouldn't have seen it coming!

Liu Mo said nothing, keeping his eyes on Fang Heng in the distance.

Remembering the humiliation he had just suffered at Fang Heng's hands, hatred surfaced in Liu Mo's eyes.

Jing'er said, “We've seen enough. It's time for us to prepare.”

“Jing'er.”

“What?”

Liu Mo said, “Fang Heng is very strong. With him here, I'm afraid he'll ruin your plans.”

Jing'er turned and stared at Liu Mo.

Unflinching, Liu Mo continued, “If he can deal with Eseklyna, he can use the same methods on you. Be careful we don't all end up being used by him.”

“I don't need you teaching me how to do things. Once this is over, I'll deal with him myself.” Jing'er spoke coldly, looking away from Liu Mo. “Envy won't be able to endure much longer. Get ready to ambush her.”

...

The Shadow Realm.

Eseklyna, the Envy Demon Seed, was observing the Hell caverns through her Eyes of Envy.

With her present abilities, and relying solely on the remote Eyes of Envy, she had no way of seeing through Fang Heng's disguise.

She found the whole situation rather strange.

What possessed the Holy Court to suddenly come stirring up trouble in Hell?

And why had they destroyed one cavern occupied by Echidna's offshoots after another?

Surely the Holy Court's operation wasn't aimed at her.

Perhaps they had simply stumbled across some other Holy Court operation?

At first, Eseklyna really hadn't intended to interfere. But the Holy Court seemed determined to make trouble for her, relentlessly clearing out the regions where Echidna had gathered in Hell, weakening Echidna's overall strength.

A cold gleam appeared in Eseklyna's eyes.

The Holy Court!

They had gone too far!

Did they really think she was afraid of them?

“Whoosh!!”

Along the cavern walls in the Shadow Realm, hideous Eyes of Hell slowly opened, revealing dark-yellow pupils.

Eseklyna raised both hands high.

“Servants of Hell, the time has come! Go forth into Hell and unleash your fury as you please!”

...

A cavern on the twenty-fifth level of Hell.

A force of mutated horned demons and Hell Desecrators was fighting the Holy Court's paladins.

Suddenly, the surrounding rock walls began to ripple and turn insubstantial. Eyes embedded in the stone opened one after another, displaying yellow eyeballs and red pupils.

Sensing the psychic pressure emanating from the eyes, Dean's expression darkened. He surveyed the cavern and warned quietly, “Fang Bai, be careful. It looks like they're coming.”

“Mm.”

In the blink of an eye, the eyes on the cave walls became solid. Hideous Hell creatures emerged from the walls and surged toward the Holy Court forces.

“Hell fiends!”

Every creature emerging from the illusory Eyes of Envy was a high-level Hell fiend!

As the high-level Hell creatures entered the battlefield, those specializing in ranged magic unleashed the first barrage of spells straight at the paladins, while their melee counterparts charged forward.

“Hum...!”

Several archbishops led the priests in erecting a joint sacred defensive shield.

“Fall back! Maintain formation!”

The high-level elite creatures had appeared without warning and were exceptionally powerful. The Holy Court forces began an orderly withdrawal.

They fought as they retreated.

Eyes of Envy continued to appear on the rock walls. Each blood-red eye marked the arrival of another high-level Hell fiend teleporting onto the battlefield.

“Grand Sage Dean.” Standing in the middle of the formation and watching the fiends appear, Fang Heng said calmly, “It seems the real enemy has arrived. I'll go meet her. Please stay here and command the Holy Court team. Above all, make sure we don't suffer too many losses.”

“All right. Leave it to me.”

Dean had just agreed and was about to say something else when Fang Heng added, “Oh, one more thing. Until I ask you for help, please wait and don't intervene without warning.”

“Huh?”

“Trust me. I have a plan.”

“Whoosh!”

Without waiting for Dean's reply, Fang Heng tapped the ground with his toes, darted past him, and charged straight toward the Hell fiends.

The God-Bestowed Staff in Fang Heng's hand swept through the air with a sharp whistle.

“Bang!!”

Two high-level, three-headed dark hounds charging directly at him were smashed apart by a single blow of his staff, exploding in midair.
