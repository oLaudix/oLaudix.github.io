Chapter 2037

Fallen

From original Chinese Chapter 2038 · 失守

After another lengthy delay, Muhiting's team had barely emerged from the underground passage when two Holy Court guards waiting outside hurried over to report.

“Commander, the situation has changed! The central teleportation square is under attack by a large number of undead creatures. We fear…”

Muhiting froze at the news, then suddenly understood.

Oh no!

It was a diversion!

They were trying to destroy the spatial teleportation passage!

“Quick! Back to the central square!”

Muhiting immediately ordered his elite troops to return to the teleportation point and reinforce it.

But it was already too late!

By the time his team forced its way back through repeated Licker attacks to the teleportation area, it was far too late!

They looked up into the distance.

Ahead lay a massive black sea of zombies!

Packed shoulder to shoulder, the creatures shuffled toward every exit from the central square!

There had to be at least several hundred thousand of them!

Damn it!

At this rate, the teleportation passage must already have fallen!

The players were equally astonished.

How had they merely gone outside to destroy the undead's teleportation gates, only to return and find so many more undead right at home?!

After being dragged from place to place, the players felt as though the undead had been leading them around by the nose.

Fortunately, by this point, units from throughout the city and its surroundings were converging on the center. Large formations were beginning to contain the spreading undead.

Muhiting's face darkened. “Don't panic! Fall back in good order! These zombies aren't much of a threat as long as we hold formation. Wipe them out!”

“Yes, sir!”

Meanwhile, inside the central square,

Fang Heng watched Muhiting gradually withdraw with his army and couldn't help looking disappointed.

If Muhiting had dared enter the square, Fang Heng was certain he could have killed the commander with the help of his massive zombie army and the undead players beside him—and made sure he never left!

What a pity.

Muhiting hadn't lost his head and charged in.

Fang Heng had more important matters to attend to, so he had no choice but to let him leave.

Fang Heng nodded to Li Qingran. “I'm leaving this to you. Keep the passage stable, and if things turn bad, withdraw immediately. Don't worry about me!”

“Right. Be careful!”

Fang Heng nodded, transformed into a bat, and flew into the air, heading out of the city.

Beside a road on the city's outskirts,

two Devout Guild players kept looking around. When they spotted Fang Heng approaching, they waved eagerly. “Boss Fang! Over here! Everything's ready. Please come with us!”

“Okay.”

Fang Heng approached and climbed into the red jeep they'd parked by the roadside.

“Boss Fang, we've set up the magic array in the basement of an amusement park. It's well hidden, and we've arranged everything according to your instructions.”

“Good work.” Fang Heng checked the time and nodded calmly. “We have fifty-seven minutes left. We'd better hurry.”

“We'll get there!”

The player floored the accelerator, and the jeep sped away with Fang Heng inside.

…

Fifteen minutes later, the jeep stopped at an amusement park in the city.

After an accident a few days earlier, the park had been temporarily closed for a complete safety inspection and repairs.

It was late at night now, so no one would be entering anyway.

“Boss Fang, we've secured the entire amusement park. I guarantee no one will come here tonight.”

The player spoke confidently as he led Fang Heng inside.

They made their way to a basement at the rear of the park.

By this time, the basement had been completely transformed.

Dense magic-array runes covered the floor.

Everything had been arranged according to Fang Heng's earlier instructions.

Fang Heng quickly inspected the array.

Because of the rush, the Devout Guild had been unable to find more skilled players. A small portion of the array remained incomplete and would need Fang Heng to finish it himself.

There was still time.

He'd deliberately set aside time to repair the array personally.

“Understood. Leave this place for now. Seal off the surrounding area, and don't let anyone disturb me.”

“Understood!”

The two players nodded and immediately departed.

Fang Heng checked the time once more and calculated inwardly.

He had more than enough time!

News of the undead outbreak should already have spread outside. Based on his estimates, the Holy Court's peripheral forces would begin arriving in the city within half an hour.

By then, all their attention would be drawn to the undead over there.

That was why he'd arranged for his entire plan to be completed within one hour.

Only the final step remained!

Fang Heng took a deep breath, forced himself to calm down, and concentrated on completing the magic array.

The array Merk had left behind for transferring a fissure connected to the Death Realm.

He would relocate the fissure using this basement array!

Fang Heng stepped forward and inserted the two prepared Merk runes into slots at the center of the magic array.

Whoosh!

A rapidly rotating magic array appeared in the pupil of Fang Heng's right eye, and a game progress bar materialized above his head.

[Hint: You are attempting to repair a magic array… Current completion probability: 88% (base) + 25% (Alchemy Saint's Inheritance) + 25% (Eye of Omniscience bonus). Current repair success rate: 100%. You also gain +270% magic-array repair speed.]

With the combined bonuses of the Eye of Omniscience and the Alchemy Saint's Inheritance, the progress bar raced forward until it was complete.

[Hint: You have completed the current magic-array repair.]

Done!

Fang Heng checked the system time again.

More than half an hour remained before the time agreed upon with Ji Xiaobo.

To play it safe, all he needed to do now was wait where he was.

When the appointed time arrived, he and Ji Xiaobo's group in the distant Chiboreya game world would activate Merk's magic arrays simultaneously. They would complete the mission [Recreating Merk's Grand Conception] while drawing the Yin Vein fissure from the Punoze game world into Duncha!

Bring the war to Duncha!

And show the Holy Court what it was like to be on the receiving end!

However…

Simply sitting around waiting would be rather boring.

He'd come here to earn faction contribution points and increase his mission completion progress.

With such a rare opportunity before him, shouldn't he be a little greedier and collect even more?

Do it!

Fang Heng made his decision and left the basement.

He returned to the amusement park, near the basement entrance.

Taking one last look around the park, Fang Heng swiftly brought his hands together.

Hiss! Hiss, hiss!

Blood continued spilling from his forehead!

The blood collected overhead and transformed into a series of chaotic vortices!

Bang! Bang, bang!

The vortices burst open in midair, turning into blood-red teleportation passages!

Game notifications appeared rapidly before Fang Heng's eyes.

[Hint: You have consumed 10% of your maximum blood-pool capacity to summon a vampire teleportation passage…]

[Hint: Your skill has caused strong spatial fluctuations within a certain range. These fluctuations may be detected…]

[Hint: You have consumed 10% of your maximum blood-pool capacity to summon a vampire teleportation passage…]

[Warning: Large spatial fluctuations have been detected around you. Their effects are overlapping and spreading continuously. Both the intensity and range at which they can be perceived are increasing rapidly…]

— End of Chapter 2037 —
