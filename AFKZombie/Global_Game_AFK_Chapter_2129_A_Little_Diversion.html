Chapter 2129

A Little Diversion

From original Chinese Chapter 2130 · 混一混

He had the backlash from the corruption in his sea of consciousness to thank for that. Fang Heng had long since grown accustomed to the pain of attacks against his mind.

Compared with the agony he'd suffered when his sea of consciousness nearly collapsed, this little prickling sensation was nothing!

Boris knew none of that. He stared at Fang Heng in astonishment.

His Psychic Pierce had been completely ineffective?!

“Heh, heh...”

Fang Heng cast Boris an amused look from a distance, chuckled, and immediately retreated down the passage on the right.

“Trying to run? After him!”

Boris felt as though Fang Heng was looking down on him. Scowling, he gave chase.

The Federation soldiers and several of Wanling Technology's psychic modification guards hurried after him!

Fang Heng ran much faster than any of them had expected!

With Boris in pursuit, they raced into the right-hand section of the institute's first floor.

Boris frowned as he watched the direction Fang Heng was taking.

That passage was a dead end.

What was he trying to do?

Had he panicked and chosen the wrong way?

Before long, the Federation's semi-mechanized soldiers had nearly driven Fang Heng into the end of the corridor.

But Fang Heng showed no sign of slowing down.

Boris's heart sank. He shouted, “It's a dead end! Be careful! Surround him!”

Whoosh!

The instant he spoke, a greatsword appeared in Fang Heng's hands.

“What?”

Boris's pupils contracted.

Fang Heng maintained his speed, charging straight at the wall at the end of the passage.

He gripped the divine greatsword with both hands.

Boom!!!

The sword smashed into the wall, punching a huge hole straight through it!

Fang Heng charged through the research institute's outer wall.

Outside, the Federation emergency response officers had already surrounded the institute with several layers of security.

But nobody had expected someone to smash a massive hole through the wall from inside!

Almost every gun immediately swung toward Fang Heng.

A squad leader shouted, “Don't move! Hands up!”

Fang Heng acted as though he hadn't heard. He accelerated toward the right.

“Open fire!”

Bang! Bang, bang!

Hundreds of officers along the perimeter opened fire almost simultaneously!

Fang Heng moved with incredible speed, avoiding most of the bullets.

Even the bullets that struck him merely broke his skin.

They caused almost no meaningful damage.

Useless fools!

Boris was the first to emerge through the hole Fang Heng had made. Seeing the scene, he cursed the Federation emergency response officers inwardly. They were so useless they couldn't even slow the intruder down! He hurried after Fang Heng.

Under Fang Heng's deliberate control, the distance between him and Boris gradually narrowed.

Boris had no idea Fang Heng was luring the tiger away from the mountain to buy the zombie clones in the sewers more time. He simply felt he was on the verge of catching the intruder, so naturally he refused to give up and stuck close behind him.

Fang Heng sprinted through the outskirts of the city.

After running for more than half an hour, he was about to round a corner when something caught his attention.

Hm?

More than twenty psychically modified guards wearing Wanling Technology uniforms had been stationed ahead of him.

He'd been surrounded.

Fang Heng stopped and slowly turned to face his pursuers, glancing at the game notifications out of the corner of his eye.

The timing was about right!

His zombie clones had already dug through the final stretch of the underground sewer route.

There was now enough time for them to carry the Divine Tree offshoots out through the sewers.

Boris and the semi-mechanized soldiers pursuing him surrounded Fang Heng, blocking every direction of retreat.

Hum...

Invisible psychic barriers formed in a matter of moments, enclosing Fang Heng completely.

Hundreds of gun barrels pointed at him.

Not one Federation soldier dared relax.

“You're pretty impressive. You even managed to get ahead of me and block my path.” Fang Heng shrugged and looked at Boris. “Well, you've found me. Before we start fighting, could you satisfy my curiosity and answer a few questions?”

Boris stared at him. “I'm afraid you're in no position to negotiate.”

“Don't be so sure of yourself. We could trade answers. One question for one question?”

Boris merely regarded Fang Heng coldly.

“Is that so? I believe I have my answer. What a pity...”

A red glow suddenly spread across Fang Heng's pupils.

Boris's heart jolted.

What was that?

Bang!

The white cloth Fang Heng had been carrying on his back suddenly burst apart!

A thick scent of blood spread from within.

It was blood!

Everyone could see it now!

What Fang Heng had carried wrapped on his back was a greatsword made entirely of condensed blood!

Boom!!!

The greatsword exploded, dispersing through the air and condensing into razor-sharp spikes that shot forward at incredible speed.

Bang! Bang, bang!

As the countless spikes struck the psychic barrier, violent explosions erupted one after another!

Everyone's expression changed!

Several of the psychic modification specialists maintaining the barrier struggled to hold on. Blood poured from their eyes, noses, and mouths as they suffered a terrible backlash.

They lasted less than half a minute before the psychic barrier collapsed with a tremendous crash!

Boris stared at Fang Heng, his expression utterly grave.

What kind of bizarre power was that?!

Before coming here, he'd never imagined he'd fight a battle this strange.

“Who exactly are you? Where do you come from? And what is your purpose in our world?!”

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

So they already knew he wasn't from this world?

Boris stared at him fiercely.

“Heh. Don't think keeping silent will save you. I'll make you spit out every last word!”

As he spoke, hundreds of semi-mechanized soldiers behind Boris opened fire.

A dense barrage of beam weapons shot toward Fang Heng!

Whoosh!

A curtain of blood unfolded in front of him!

Bang! Bang, bang!

The energy beams struck the crimson curtain, sending ripples across its surface.

Even their beam weapons couldn't do anything to him?!

A sense of helplessness began to spread through the Federation ranks.

When the barrage finally paused, the blood curtain dispersed and Fang Heng appeared before them once more.

“Seems you know quite a bit about my identity. How did you find out? We've got time now, so why don't we have a proper talk?”

“Talk?” Despite his shock at Fang Heng's abilities, Boris refused to show weakness. “Come back with me, and we'll talk for as long as you like.”

“Forget it, then. How boring.”

Fang Heng shook his head and glanced up at the sky.

— End of Chapter 2129 —
