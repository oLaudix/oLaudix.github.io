Chapter 3069

Easy Advancement

From Chinese Chapter 3070 · 轻松进阶

“That doesn't count as betrayal. Parting on good terms is something the Noel Clan accepts. And it's good news for you: when people leave, vacancies open up. We currently have twelve warrior positions available. This assessment will last ten minutes. After ten minutes, the twelve people still standing here will be the winners.”

The elder picked up his teacup and took a leisurely sip.

“Begin.”

Everyone immediately understood the Noel Clan elder's rules. They spread out and went on guard.

Several warriors who were clearly of the same species quickly grouped together.

“Get out of the way, brat!”

Seeing Fang Heng alone, an alien covered in mutated scales approached stealthily from behind on his right and threw a punch.

“Hm? What?!”

The alien's pupils contracted.

Bang!

No one could see how Fang Heng moved. The young attacker spat out blood, was flung violently backward, hit the wall, and fell unconscious.

“Hm?”

The Noel Clan elder noticed something and raised his head. His gaze settled on Fang Heng with renewed interest.

Fang Heng stood motionless.

Several nearby warriors saw the alien sent flying and immediately realized that Fang Heng was dangerous. They hurried to keep their distance.

In mere moments, several small groups had formed across the hall.

Everyone eyed everyone else warily, afraid that making the first move would leave them surrounded and attacked.

The elder set down his teacup and said calmly, “Five minutes remain. If more than twelve people are still standing when time runs out, everyone fails the assessment.”

The moment he finished speaking, a chaotic melee erupted.

Fang Heng continued standing in a corner of the hall.

Anyone who approached him spat blood and went flying.

After several such incidents, no one dared provoke him again. The others left a clear safety zone around Fang Heng while fighting among themselves elsewhere.

Fang Heng glanced around and realized he didn't need to do anything more. He simply stood there, taking it easy.

When the ten minutes ended, only ten people remained standing, including Fang Heng.

The Noel Clan elder rose and looked around the room.

Aside from Fang Heng, the others' strength was mediocre at best.

It would do for now.

“Welcome, everyone, as warriors of our Noel Clan.”

The Noel Clan employed over five hundred external warriors in total.

They needed these fighters for ordinary arena duels, and sometimes the clan assigned them special missions as well.

The warriors didn't need to worry about anything else. Their responsibility was simply to fight.

Once recruited, they received a substantial monthly income, and as their warrior ranks improved, that income could rise dramatically.

“Mr. Fang Heng, I'm Anxi, your personal assistant. I'll help arrange your schedule and look after your everyday needs. Please come to me whenever you require anything.”

Anxi was a sweet-looking woman who greeted Fang Heng with a smile.

The Noel Clan provided a personal assistant for every warrior.

Fang Heng asked, “I heard the Noel Clan offers free cultivation training?”

“Yes, Mr. Fang Heng.” Anxi kept her professional smile and nodded respectfully. “The Noel Clan provides private one-on-one lessons. We'll arrange the best instructors for you. You may also hire outside teachers, though that carries an additional fee.”

“Not bad...” Fang Heng said casually. “Do you have any instructors specializing in spatial studies? I'd like to train in that field.”

“Of course. I'll register your request and arrange an appointment. What level of instructor do you require?”

“Grandmaster.”

Fang Heng's spatial studies had been stuck at Master level for quite a while.

Spatial studies were extremely rare. The Federation had only one quest lead connected to Grandmaster spatial studies advancement, and they weren't even certain the reward would be relevant. The task itself was troublesome; Fang Heng estimated it would take at least one or two months to complete.

He wanted to see whether the Seven-Realm Martial Capital could help him break through instead.

“I've registered your request in the Noel Clan's internal system. We'll make arrangements as soon as possible.”

Not bad.

Fang Heng nodded lightly. “Please arrange it quickly.”

“Yes, sir.”

Anxi withdrew her gaze from Fang Heng.

Spatial studies...

It seemed this warrior was obsessed with training.

Anxi pondered the matter.

Personal assistants were the people closest to the warriors. The Noel Clan had instructed them to keep track of every movement their assigned warriors made.

...

Less than half an hour later, Anxi was still showing Fang Heng around the facilities when his spatial studies instructor was confirmed.

“Mr. Fang Heng, the instructor for your private lesson has been arranged. He happens to be free right now. Shall I take you there?”

“Good. Let's go now.”

Fang Heng silently praised the Noel Clan's efficiency.

During their walk, Anxi had noticed that Fang Heng wasn't fond of conversation, so she avoided disturbing him unnecessarily. She led him to a large training room on the sixth floor.

“Your instructor is waiting inside, Mr. Fang Heng. I'll wait for you at the door.”

Anxi bowed respectfully and stepped aside.

Fang Heng entered and saw a middle-aged man sitting on a cushion, studying him with a bored expression.

A member of the Noel Clan.

Fang Heng recognized the man's species from his appearance.

“Fang Heng, is it? You're a newly recruited Noel Clan warrior? I'm Gaspar, a certified Grandmaster in spatial studies. Let's not waste time. Shall we begin?”

Lessons were billed by the minute.

Gaspar had a fixed teaching quota to fulfill every month.

“All right.”

Fang Heng agreed and walked toward him.

“Whoosh!”

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

Gaspar's figure swiftly slipped into a subspace projection.

When he materialized again, Gaspar had appeared to Fang Heng's right.

“What level are you at now?”

“Peak Master-level spatial studies. I haven't found a way to break through.”

“Fine. Show me.”

Fang Heng nodded and swiftly merged into a subspace projection.

Gaspar frowned as he watched.

Good grief! Why did merging into a spatial projection cause so much disturbance?

This young man...

He was far too strange.

His actual proficiency seemed barely above early Master level. In terms of his understanding of spatial studies, however, he had almost no advantage over a beginner.

How had he managed to reach this level?

Fang Heng emerged from the spatial projection.

“Please instruct me, Master.”

Gaspar shook his head. “You've missed far too many fundamentals. If you want to reach Grandmaster, you'll have to start again from the beginning.”

Fang Heng had no objection. “I understand. In that case, please begin with the basics.”

“Fine. But you'd better leave me a good review afterward.”

Gaspar didn't particularly mind.

He was paid by the minute anyway.

Teaching the basics was easier, if anything.

“Perceiving spatial energy is the first step in studying space. After that, we must draw upon power from the surrounding space... At the advanced stage, we continuously increase our adaptability to spatial abilities, gradually allowing our bodies to accommodate spatial energy...”

“As for the bottleneck you've reached, the real problem is your comprehension of the rules governing external spatial energy. Once you understand those rules, breaking through is actually not difficult.”

— End of Chapter 3069 —
