Chapter 2021

Transfer Ability

From original Chinese Chapter 2022 · 转移能力

What's more, even his zombie clones could pass through the portal and reach the battlefield after switching into vampire bloodline form!

The skill could last for up to twenty minutes.

In other words, the teleportation passage now allowed him to move troops on a massive scale. Even if the passage in the real world wasn't built in time, the zombie clones he'd “recruited” in this world could swiftly join the fighting on the front lines!

Naturally, the skill had a hidden drawback. Activating it produced tremendous spatial and blood-vitality fluctuations, enough to attract enemies over a very wide area.

But that small disadvantage was nothing compared to the powerful teleportation ability!

Fang Heng clenched his fist in delight.

Advancing to the highest-grade vampire bloodline had dramatically increased his personal combat strength and greatly improved the fighting capabilities of his summons as well. With this ability, their mobility had undergone a qualitative leap!

“Fang Heng, are you all right?”

Gu Qingzhu came up beside him.

“I'm fine. What about you?”

She shook her head. “Just a few minor injuries. Nothing serious. Eight Federation players escaped during the chaos. We can't let them get away.”

“That's not a problem,” Fang Heng said with a nod. “I'll deal with them. You're injured, so stay here and recover. Don't damage your foundation.”

“All right.”

As he spoke, Fang Heng brought his palms together before his chest.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Blood from the surroundings gathered swiftly, forming an exceptionally dense mass of liquid blood that hovered in front of him.

The Domain of Blood really was powerful. In pure combat terms, it was perhaps no weaker than an artifact.

But…

To draw out its full potential, he'd need to carry large quantities of highly concentrated blood wherever he went.

That was troublesome.

Walking around with a puddle of blood following him would look pretty strange, wouldn't it?

Wait!

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow. Under his control, the mass of blood slowly changed shape, gathering into the form of a greatsword.

Whoosh!

The sword made a wide loop through the air and came to rest behind his back. To anyone looking, it would seem as though he simply had a crimson greatsword strapped there.

The only problem was its pungent, bloody odor and the intense blood-vitality fluctuations it gave off.

“I'll have to make a proper scabbard when I get back, something that can seal off its aura…” he murmured.

Then he looked into the distance. All eight fleeing Federation players had already been firmly marked by his Mark of Blood skill.

First, he'd finish them off!

…

In the real world, the glowing orbs in the hall rapidly dimmed. Miyamoto Teru's expression turned uglier by the moment.

Standing beside him, Lei Yu frowned. “What is it?”

“The Pearls of Destiny,” Miyamoto Teru replied gravely. “They were bound to the Fox Hunting Squad members who entered the game world. Every single one of them is dead.”

The news sent shockwaves through the hall.

All dead?!

And in a single instant?!

The Fox Hunting Squad had been an elite team in intermediate-tier games, already extraordinarily powerful. With the effects of the Dragon Soul Crystal extract, their overall strength was comparable to that of teams in high-tier games.

Someone capable of killing them all instantly…

Gu Qingzhu…?

Lei Yu frowned as his mood darkened. His own elite team was about as strong as the Fox Hunting Squad. If the mercenaries had been wiped out in an instant, then his people might also…

Why?

According to Ji Shaojie, Gu Qingzhu had forcibly activated her berserk state again yesterday. She should have been at no more than ten percent of her peak strength. There was no way she could defeat the Federation squad!

That was precisely why Lei Yu had agreed to send his people into the battle.

Had their intelligence been wrong?

Had the demi-humans set another trap?

“Mr. Miyamoto.”

A subordinate hurried over and whispered a few words in Miyamoto Teru's ear.

Creak… creak…

Veins bulged on Miyamoto's aged hand as his grip made his cane creak.

“Mr. Miyamoto, is there news?”

Miyamoto Teru drew a deep breath. “Ji Shaojie's in-game character has disappeared from the friends list.”

Silence fell over the hall again.

A game friend disappearing meant the player had died permanently!

The veins on Miyamoto Teru's neck stood out as he spat bitterly, “Demi-humans! This feud will end only when one of us is dead!”

…

One day later, at the Myriad Realms Library.

“Mr. Fang Heng, I really can't thank you enough.”

Folke accompanied Fang Heng to the elevator with a broad smile.

He hadn't been able to contact Mr. Amanda for two days and had been pacing about anxiously. Fortunately, Mr. Amanda had contacted him at the last moment and worked overtime that night to finish the magic array.

The two had agreed to continue three days later.

In return, Folke respectfully handed Fang Heng the beast-taming advancement skill book he'd gathered over the past two days.

Back in his room, Fang Heng took out the advancement book and examined it.

[Hint: Your Basic Beast Taming skill has reached MAX level. Consume the corresponding skill book to advance it?]

“Yes. Advance immediately.”

Bang!

The book exploded into a cloud of dark-golden dust that slowly dispersed.

[Hint: Basic Beast Taming has been removed. You have acquired Elementary Beast Taming.]

[Elementary Beast Taming (LV 1)]

Skill: Basic Beast Taming.

Type: Academic.

Description: Learning this skill grants Constitution +2 and Spirit +2. The player can create basic contracts (Mutual Assistance Contract and Control Contract), and attempt to form basic contracts with contracted beasts (requires Favorability 80).

Description: Maximum skill level: LV 20.

Fang Heng quickly checked the description.

The advancement hadn't granted much in the way of attributes: only four points in total. Still, something was better than nothing.

More importantly, after advancing to Elementary Beast Taming, his perception of contracts had grown stronger. He could now form a more binding Control Contract with summoned creatures.

A Control Contract placed greater restrictions on a summon, but it also lowered the creature's favorability.

For now, there was no need to use such a contract on his Shadow Demon Rat.

He might as well leave a small number of zombie clones to grind experience in the game world, raise Beast Taming to the intermediate or advanced levels, and see whether a more suitable contract appeared.

With that thought, Fang Heng put away the intermediate-tier skill advancement book and checked the time.

A day had passed, and Miyamoto Teru's side had gone quiet again.

Gu Qingzhu and her demi-human companions were staying at the prison temporarily to rest and recover. They wouldn't be leaving anytime soon.

That was fine. The demi-humans were powerful, and their presence would provide additional protection for the prison.

Editing and Source Notes
  • Beast Taming system-panel inconsistency. The game announces Elementary Beast Taming (初阶驭兽学), but the next field labels the skill “Basic Beast Taming” (基础驭兽学). This mismatch is in the Chinese original, so both are reproduced.
  • Names. 福尔克 is tentatively rendered Folke; “Mr. Amanda” is reproduced as in the Chinese source, even though Amanda may sound unusual as a man’s name in English.
  • Skills and stat values. The advancement gives +2 Constitution and +2 Spirit; the Control Contract requires sufficient favorability and may lower it. The source specifies an initial favorability requirement of 80 for basic contracts.
  • Mark count. The preceding chapter marks Ji Shaojie and the eight fleeing Federation players, explaining the nine simultaneous marks mentioned there.
