Chapter 2026

Assembly

From original Chinese Chapter 2027 · 集结

Several Holy Court paladins hurried into Gulanchi's tent to report.

“Commander, our patrols stationed outside the Forest of Bewilderment have discovered something unusual. Large groups of zombies have appeared inside the forest. They seem to be assembling for a breakout toward the perimeter.”

Hm?

Gulanchi raised an eyebrow.

They'd really come?

“Mobilize troops to reinforce the defenses! Take me there to see for myself!”

“Yes, sir!”

Gulanchi followed his men to the edge of the forest where the disturbance had been reported. When he looked ahead, his brow furrowed.

Shadowy figures filled the woods. It was too dark to make out the details, but he was certain there were at least tens of thousands of zombies hiding among the trees.

Holy Court paladins had already gathered outside the forest, forming a defensive line and holding their positions.

Here they came!

Before Gulanchi could issue his next orders, the horde completed its assembly and moved out.

Zombies spread across the hills and fields, staggering out of the Forest of Bewilderment in vast numbers.

So many?

And it was an army composed entirely of zombies?

Surprise flickered in Gulanchi's eyes.

“Attack!”

At his command, the Holy Court priests at the rear of the defensive line began chanting sacred spells.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Dense beams of holy light streaked into the ranks of zombie clones shuffling out of the forest.

BOOM! Boom, boom!

Sacred power exploded throughout the horde. Zombies caught within the blasts were sent tumbling in every direction, and the first wave of the attack was checked.

But Gulanchi frowned.

On the surface, his side appeared to have the overwhelming advantage.

Yet in reality, the sacred attacks were far less effective against these zombies than he'd expected.

There was something wrong with them!

Their resistance to damage seemed to have more than doubled since yesterday!

The Holy Court priests felt the difference even more keenly. They'd been fighting these strange zombies for days. Previously, a direct hit from a Holy Prayer beam would have been enough to kill one outright.

Not today.

Even after taking a direct hit and being engulfed in burning holy flames, the zombies continued struggling until they barely managed to rise from the ground and rejoin the battle.

It took a second direct Holy Prayer to finish one off completely!

This wasn't good!

Gulanchi suddenly realized his team's damage output wasn't keeping up.

And worse still was the sheer number of zombies.

Unlike the small-scale attacks they'd dealt with before, there were simply too many this time.

Holy Prayer spells could inflict area damage, but now they merely scattered the zombies at the front and pushed them back a short distance. They couldn't destroy the horde outright.

Behind the first ranks, countless more zombies poured out of the Forest of Bewilderment, seemingly without end.

What frightened Gulanchi even more was the length of their battle line. Zombies were emerging all along a stretch of the forest edge almost three or four hundred meters wide!

Their usual tactic of wearing the enemy down with ranged units was useless today.

“Follow me!”

Gulanchi raised his sacred longsword and roared, “Charge! Destroy these undead!”

“Charge!”

The melee paladins shouted and followed him straight into the horde.

In moments, great masses of zombies swallowed the paladin force.

There were too many of them!

And they were far too tough!

Even Gulanchi needed more than ten seconds to kill a single zombie!

This was bad!

The longer they fought, the heavier his heart grew.

The zombies' defenses weren't actually that high. One sweep of his area-effect holy attack could hit a whole swath of them.

But their health was terrifying!

He'd struck them more than a dozen times without seeing a single one completely die!

His squad's charge had inflicted very little lasting damage. Instead, the zombies were quietly closing in around them.

At this rate, they'd soon be surrounded with no easy way out.

“Fall back!”

Gulanchi immediately ordered the team to withdraw slowly and keep its distance.

Looking up, he saw an endless sea of zombies ahead. Compared with a moment earlier, their numbers hadn't diminished at all—they'd increased more than tenfold!

And they were still growing!

Dense crowds of undead emerged from the forest without the slightest sign of stopping.

This was a disaster!

There were just too many!

Even if every zombie stood completely still, purifying them all would take an enormous amount of time!

“Quickly! Gather reinforcements! We need help! Avoid fighting them head-on if possible, and find a way to lure them away!”

“Understood!”

Meanwhile, within the Forest of Bewilderment, the others watched the distant battle.

Fang Heng nodded inwardly.

Since his vampire bloodline had advanced to its highest stage, his zombies' strength had improved significantly too!

The most obvious change was their terrifying ability to heal themselves. That made the plan even more likely to succeed.

Definitely worth it!

Li Qingran and the necromancer players didn't know exactly what had happened. All they could see was that the zombies were harder to kill and regenerated health much faster.

Their Agility and Strength had improved slightly as well, though these gains were less noticeable next to the improvement in recovery.

Li Qingran felt that even a straightforward charge with this horde would give the Holy Court a serious headache.

The other necromancer players were stunned too.

Had World Lord Fang Heng really become this powerful?

What frightened them most wasn't the individual zombies' strength, but their total numbers!

More zombies were still being transferred in through the Ghost Tower, without pause.

“Senior Qingran, I'll leave things here to you for now,” Fang Heng said. “If anything unexpected happens, contact me through the Survival Radio.”

“All right.”

Li Qingran nodded and watched Fang Heng leap into the air, transform into a bat, and fly away.

On the battlefield ahead, Gulanchi and his troops, together with paladins rapidly assembling from surrounding areas, continued to fight while maneuvering away from the zombie clones.

They exploited the horde's slow movement, maintaining a safe distance and retreating while attacking.

But…

Gulanchi grew increasingly troubled.

He'd tried several times to divert the horde's attention, and every attempt had failed.

It was obvious that someone was directing the zombies' advance!

If they kept moving in that direction…

Could it be…?

Cold sweat broke out on Gulanchi's forehead as a dreadful thought took shape.

Canmu City!

The undead's target was very likely Canmu City!

— End of Chapter 2018 —
