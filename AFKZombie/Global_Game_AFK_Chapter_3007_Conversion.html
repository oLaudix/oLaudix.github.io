Chapter 3007

Conversion

From Chinese Chapter 3008 · 转换

At the same time, Eternal Tree Domain stopped circulating.

The vines gathered around Fang Heng dispersed and disappeared.

The hall returned to silence, leaving only players wearing a variety of expressions.

What the hell had just happened?

The Southern Federation squad looked at Fang Heng as though he were a terrifying demon.

Was this guy insane?

He'd actually killed humanity's Titan ancestor!

"Retreat!"

Dorian met Fang Heng's gaze and felt his heart jump. This was bad. With a wave of his hand, he led his people away at once.

They were finished!

They'd thought they could summon reinforcements to deal with Fang Heng. Now even their reinforcement had been destroyed.

Why weren't they running already?

Terrified Fang Heng might pursue them, Dorian and the Southern Federation fled the hall in a panic.

Archbishop Whiteman and the Holy Court members gathered around Fang Heng and watched them flee. "Grand Sage, shall we give chase?"

"Leave them alone."

Fang Heng shook his head and looked away.

There was no reason to waste time on them.

The Titan was dealt with, but the energy jewel was still here.

Keep absorbing!

Ignoring the astonished looks of everyone else, Fang Heng sat cross-legged again, stimulated the jewel with his mental power, and resumed absorbing its energy at full strength.

Absorb!

Keep going!

Immediately, a huge amount of energy burst from the jewel and formed a vortex above Fang Heng!

The remaining players exchanged uneasy looks.

What a terrifying man.

People outside had recently been saying the Holy Court had declined. Whoever spread that nonsense had almost fooled them!

This Grand Sage had shattered even the Titan ancestor!

Fortunately, the Holy Court didn't seem inclined to drive them away.

What were they waiting for? They should hurry and absorb more energy!

They sat down again and resumed cultivating without hesitation.

...

Six full hours passed.

The glowing sphere at the center of the hall gradually lost its radiance.

Fang Heng opened his eyes.

He'd absorbed almost enough.

As the sphere's output weakened until only thin traces of power escaped, the players cultivating around the ice crystal also opened their eyes and looked toward Fang Heng.

He slowly approached the central jewel.

It was still releasing Qi energy.

But only a very small amount.

Fang Heng reached out and pressed his hand against it.

The jewel felt cool to the touch.

It appeared to be an object capable of storing Qi. It also drew elemental power from the surrounding ice-crystal labyrinth and slowly converted it into Qi.

The conversion was slow, but it was definitely happening.

Did that mean the Qi released earlier had accumulated inside the jewel over tens of thousands of years?

As Fang Heng studied it, a thought struck him.

He'd noticed a resemblance before. Looking more closely, this jewel really did resemble the Death Realm Jewel and Divine Realm Jewel.

Was that just a coincidence?

They were all round jewels, so some resemblance was natural.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng tried placing the jewel into his inventory.

Huh? It actually worked?

[Hint: Player has obtained item—Titan's Refined Jewel.]

Item: Titan's Refined Jewel.

Description: A jewel refined using the secret techniques of the ancient Titan race. It can store Qi. When placed in an energy-rich environment, it absorbs energy of other attributes and slowly converts it into Qi.

Just as expected!

It could convert energy into Qi.

A thought occurred to Fang Heng, and he looked at the pedestal beneath the jewel.

The slab that had supported the floating jewel was engraved with dense, intricate patterns.

They resembled an alchemical magic array.

The array served two functions: suspending the jewel in midair and accelerating its absorption of energy from the environment.

Fang Heng activated his All-Knowing Eye at full strength. The pattern spun rapidly in his pupils as he forcibly recorded the entire array in his Sea of Consciousness.

"Grand Sage." Whiteman saw Fang Heng standing there, apparently deep in realization, and approached respectfully. "Are you all right?"

"There's something I don't understand. You can go ahead. I need to stay here and think."

Fang Heng closed his eyes again and sat down on the spot.

"Yes, Grand Sage. We're willing to remain and guard you."

Whiteman spoke with formal precision, then signaled his men to form a protective ring around Fang Heng and watch the others.

The remaining players and Federation groups didn't dare speak. They merely exchanged glances.

They'd come here to obtain the so-called Apocalypse Treasure.

Now it seemed that treasure had been the jewel they'd just seen.

And the Holy Court had taken possession of it.

Steal it from the Holy Court?

Everyone had witnessed Fang Heng's strength. Ordinary teams lacked the courage to try.

The Western Federation had already been frightened out of its wits by him, so it certainly wouldn't dare.

The Central Federation, mindful of its relationship with the Holy Court and wary of Fang Heng's power, also refused to act rashly.

The Eastern Federation knew Fang Heng's true identity, but with so many outsiders watching, it couldn't conveniently approach and speak with him.

After waiting a while and seeing no sign of Fang Heng emerging from cultivation, the Federation teams conferred. Each left a small detachment behind to await his awakening while the others went to explore the remaining ice-crystal labyrinth.

Fang Heng had already withdrawn his attention completely from the outside world. Eyes closed, he focused on studying the Qi he'd absorbed.

According to the skill description, Qi could transform into other forms of energy.

But during his earlier test, he couldn't convert it into sacred power.

Now that he had time, he carefully performed further experiments.

If converting Qi outward didn't work, why not try the reverse? He would transform energy from another discipline into Qi.

Fang Heng attempted to convert the Natural Studies energy in his Sea of Consciousness.

Success!

The natural energy swiftly circulated through his body, following an instinctive pathway, and returned to his Sea of Consciousness in a different form: Qi.

So it really worked!

But the process wasn't easy. It required absolute concentration.

And there was also the question of efficiency.

The conversion cost 50% of the energy.

If he could convert it in reverse, why couldn't he convert it forward?

Fang Heng considered the problem and tried once more.

Huh?

After Qi circulated through his body, it successfully transformed back into natural energy.

But after two conversions, fully 75% of the original natural energy was gone.

Of course!

Understanding suddenly dawned on Fang Heng.

The Titan system of Academic Unification was only partially completed. Sacred Studies energy might simply fall outside the range of energies it could convert!
