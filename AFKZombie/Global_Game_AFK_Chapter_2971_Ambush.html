Chapter 2971

Ambush

From Chinese Chapter 2972 · 偷袭

“You fucking—”

Zaka was furious and wanted to march up and argue, but Fang Heng caught him by the arm.

“Let's go.”

“Hahaha! Crawl back where you came from! This is a battlefield, not somewhere for little children like you!”

The Crocodile Clan had already heard that the human fleet was stationed near their mining area and had prepared to deal with them. Seeing Fang Heng's group retreat, the guards laughed and jeered even louder.

Zaka followed Fang Heng back, seething as he listened to the crocodiles' mockery. “Boss Fang, the Crocodile Clan was ready for us. What do we do?”

“No rush, no rush.”

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

He had just observed the Crocodile Clan's defenses, and they were actually quite weak.

He could seize the mine through force without much difficulty.

The only problem was the Shadow Clan...

He wasn't prepared to tear his relationship with the Shadow Clan and the various allied groups apart and start an all-out war.

After all, he still needed the Shadow Clan to deal with the insect nest.

In that case...

Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh...

Far away aboard the Nethersea, large numbers of Lickers obeyed Fang Heng's control and crawled out through openings all over the starship.

They dropped from the ship's underside onto the barren ground, then rapidly burrowed beneath the surface using their Stealth ability.

...

The Crocodile Clan actually had very few guards stationed at each mining area.

After all, the insect nest hadn't been destroyed yet. Most combat units were concentrated on the front line, and with insect swarms still threatening the planet, this wasn't the time to mine ore.

The guards were stationed there mainly to prevent other allies from robbing them behind their backs.

Such incidents had happened before, after all.

Deep inside the mine, the crocodile guards were keeping watch when they suddenly felt the ground tremble slightly beneath their feet.

What was that?

“Hiss, hiss, hiss...”

Looking down, they saw cracks abruptly open in the earth nearby.

Then large numbers of brown, spiny insectoid creatures burst out of the ground!

“Insects! Watch out! Don't let them get close!”

The crocodile people immediately opened fire with their energy-beam weapons!

Once again, the Lickers displayed their complete disregard for death.

Even when energy beams pierced their bodies and sent them flying, they needed only to tumble across the ground before recovering and charging straight back at the crocodiles.

The Crocodile Clan had never seen such outrageous regeneration. As the swarm closed in almost instantly, the front-line crocodiles drew energy blades with both hands and rushed forward into melee combat!

Every member of the Crocodile Clan was a born warrior!

But their expressions soon changed, revealing outright terror.

These insects were horrifying!

Their attributes were exceptionally high, their claws inflicted devastating tearing wounds, and they were incredibly agile—all on top of that monstrous regenerative ability!

Even when the insects' limbs were severed, they regenerated within seconds and hurled themselves at the crocodiles again without fear of death.

After fighting for a while, the crocodiles were stunned to discover they hadn't managed to kill even half an insect.

In less than thirty seconds, the entire crocodile guard unit of more than twenty warriors was slaughtered by the Licker horde.

Hearing the commotion from within, the crocodiles posted outside hurried over and were horrified to find the mine filled with Lickers.

What was going on?

Before they could make sense of it, they saw the oncoming Lickers and instinctively pulled their triggers.

That response cost them their best chance to escape.

Once the Lickers reached them, the remaining crocodile guards had no hope of survival.

In moments, the entire mine was left in ruins.

“Hiss, hiss...”

After eliminating the crocodile guards, the Lickers rapidly formed fleshy cocoons on the spot.

Seconds later, zombie clones crawled out of the cocoons and headed deeper into the mine to start digging ore.

Meanwhile, some Lickers excavated a tunnel on the spot, packing the ore and transporting it back to the starship through the newly opened underground passage.

Not far outside the mine, Zaka and the others watched several crocodile guards fleeing from the Lickers in such terror that they practically pissed themselves. They exchanged glances, then broke into smiles.

You had to admit, the Lickers' disguise was perfect.

The crocodiles probably thought they were ordinary insectoid creatures, didn't they?

Everyone continued waiting quietly in hiding.

Some time passed. By the time a third group of Lickers had carried military shovels through the underground passage, no crocodile reinforcements had arrived.

What did that mean?

Even Zaka was puzzled.

Had the Crocodile Clan simply abandoned the mine?

In fact, after the surviving guards fled, the Crocodile Clan's flagship soon received news that insects had attacked the mining area.

What a nuisance.

The crocodile captain was currently commanding forces against the insect swarm on the main battlefront.

The battle was at its fiercest point.

Forget it.

The captain weighed the costs and benefits.

It was just a low-grade mine.

The loss wouldn't matter much. It hadn't been seized by another race, and once the battle was over, the mine would still belong to the Crocodile Clan.

There was no reason to waste troops and attention on it now.

And so the matter was temporarily forgotten.

Aboard the Nethersea.

The Flying Squirrel clan members quickly identified nearby mineral deposits and marked several rich mines on the map.

Mousse had just finished drawing the map and was preparing to hand it to Fang Heng when he received some news and hurried to the ship's storage area to meet him.

There, he saw box after box of ore being delivered into storage by advanced transport robots along the passage.

Mousse shrank back involuntarily.

That much ore...

So Boss Fang had gone ahead and attacked several allied factions after all?

Terrifying...

Mousse rubbed his poor little heart.

The Shadow Clan and its allies were powerful, but they probably couldn't imagine that Boss Fang's greatest strength was actually his ability to steal ore.

Putting Boss Fang in charge of logistics was like letting the entire Flying Squirrel clan loose inside a rice warehouse!

As the multifunctional military shovels and underground ore transport passages continued expanding, the zombie clones' mining speed rose faster and faster.

One mine after another was rapidly stripped clean, and increasing quantities of ore flowed through the passages and poured into the starship without pause.

The front line was fighting itself into a frenzy, while Boss Fang was stealing ore in the rear with even greater enthusiasm?

Before long, the Flying Squirrel clan had completely forgotten their fear of the Shadow Clan and immersed themselves in the joys of sorting and refining ore.

A quick calculation left Mousse delighted.

Even after setting aside the materials required for the Iron-Eater Clan to build the prism towers, they could still make a handsome profit by handing the refined ore over to the Kinsfolk!

Several hours later.

After reports of insect attacks from several mines in succession, the Crocodile Clan finally began paying attention.

The crocodiles found it strange. Why did the insects keep attacking their mines, of all places?

They contacted a few other fleets and discovered that it wasn't only the Crocodile Clan. Mines belonging to other races had been attacked by insects too.

Good grief.

What were these insects playing at?

Their forces had been comprehensively suppressed on the main battlefront, yet they still had the ability to employ tactics and dispatch units to raid rear-area mines?

What was the point of that?

Pure harassment?

Were they trying to distract the allied forces?

Sensing something suspicious, the Crocodile Clan reported the matter to the Shadow Clan alliance and dispatched several warships to investigate.

After all, their own mines were in trouble. They couldn't expect their allies to solve it for them. They would have to go take a look themselves.

— End of Chapter 2971 —
