Chapter 3131

Energy Capture

From Chinese Chapter 3132 · 能量捕捉

Lila, a Kinsfolk Administrator, said coldly, “So it's a spirit. Activate energy capture!”

Fang Heng's exact location was immediately projected onto the mech's cockpit display.

Mental Shock!

More than a dozen mental shocks, amplified by the mech's prisms, slammed toward Fang Heng!

His sea of consciousness was struck by a violent psychic disturbance. He briefly lost his concentration, and his vision blurred for an instant.

By the time his Eye of Omniscience broke through the illusion, great waves of energy beams from the mechs were already bearing down on him!

Hmph!

Hmph! Fang Heng snorted coldly and rapidly materialized in midair. Netherworld Orbs seeped from beneath his skin and revolved around him at tremendous speed, crashing into and detonating the incoming beam attacks!

“BOOM! Boom, boom, boom!!!”

Amid the exploding waves of energy, Fang Heng advanced instead of retreating. Lightly touching the ground with his toes, he sprinted toward the Kinsfolk to kill them!

“Tap…”

The distance between the two sides was already short, and he closed it almost instantly!

The Kinsfolk possessed terrifying mental strength and excelled at mechanical technology, but their actual physical bodies were comparatively weak.

“BANG!!!”

Fang Heng threw a punch!!! Hm?!

Huh?!

A gentle glow appeared on the surface of the high-grade alloy mech, absorbing roughly seventy percent of the force behind his punch!

“BOOM!!!”

The remaining thirty percent struck the mech and sent it flying backward.

Such powerful defenses!

Fang Heng snorted inwardly and pounced at the mech once more!

Administrator Lila, who was controlling it, was horrified.

Even with both a defensive barrier and a reinforced alloy shell, his single punch had broken through their defenses. How could his physical attributes be so terrifying?!

Panicking, Lila barked, “Activate the electromagnetic network! Restrain him!”

“Detection complete. Activating electromagnetic network!”

“Hum…!”

The mechs simultaneously released an invisible, high-frequency electromagnetic network.

Caught at its center, Fang Heng felt his heart lurch as its effects took hold.

Electromagnetic Control!

His body seemed to be severely restricted, and the surrounding space felt like a deep swamp.

“Hmph!!”

Damned troublesome Kinsfolk!

Spatial abilities!

Fang Heng vanished instantly into a subspace projection.

Whoosh!

Whoosh! When he emerged from the projection again, he was already standing before another Kinsfolk mech.

The Kinsfolk piloting it grew extremely tense upon seeing Fang Heng and raised his defensive barrier to maximum power!

“BOOM!!!!”

Another punch flew forward!

A layer of dark light covered his fist.

Empowered by Super-God-level Necromancy, the dark light began fluctuating violently the instant it touched the defensive barrier!

“Hiss, hissss…!”

The Kinsfolk pilot was horrified.

The energy readings on his display were plunging crazily!

They dropped all the way to the bottom.

“BOOM!!!”

The defensive barrier lasted less than a second before shattering completely!

Fang Heng's punch landed squarely against the mech behind it.

“Hiss!!”

The dark light coating his fist corroded a huge opening in the alloy. Death energy rushed inside and erupted, causing the entire mech to explode!

Most Kinsfolk had frail bodies. With so little time to escape, the pilot was obliterated in the explosion.

“Whoosh!!”

Inside the great hall, the remaining Kinsfolk watched their companion's mech explode in shock. Before Administrator Lila could issue another order, Fang Heng passed through space again and appeared before another pilot.

“BOOM!!!!”

Another punch, and another mech exploded!

In the space of a few moments, Fang Heng smashed all dozen or so mechs in the room apart with his fists.

Done.

Fang Heng looked around. He could already sense a large number of high-tier warriors reaching the upper floor and fighting his Lickers near the corridor entrance.

The spatial passage he'd opened was small, too narrow for Lickers to pass through freely. Consequently, he hadn't brought many of them this time. They couldn't hold the warriors off for long.

It was time to withdraw.

At least eliminating those senior Kinsfolk had allowed him to vent some anger.

Thinking this, Fang Heng swept the room once more in search of anything valuable.

Unfortunately, this wasn't inside the game.

Otherwise, killing so many high-ranking Kinsfolk would surely have caused something worthwhile to drop.

Hm?

Fang Heng suddenly raised his brows, noticing a cluster of pale-purple light hovering over the conference table.

The Kinsfolk's language system was unlike that of most races. It was based on a special kind of psychic construction.

The glowing cluster resembled a letter in human communication and carried a strong personal imprint.

Fang Heng approached the table and used psychic power to inspect the information contained in the purple light.

Interesting!

The information had been recorded by Lila, the highest-ranking Kinsfolk official stationed in the Seven Realms Martial Capital. She was responsible both for investigating the black-mist incidents and for a separate special assignment.

Because no new black-mist incidents had occurred for more than a month, the Kinsfolk considered their operation to eliminate the black mist's suspected pan-human sources preliminarily complete.

They had originally planned to end the operation entirely in another two weeks.

The purple orb contained a report Lila was preparing to send to Kinsfolk headquarters.

It gave an exhaustive account of the Kinsfolk's activities during the past month.

Fang Heng skimmed through it rapidly using his psychic abilities.

Hm?

Hm? Lila's special assignment? A proposed list of participants for a fourth round of testing?

Puzzled, Fang Heng browsed through it.

Every person had an accompanying dossier, and none were weak. Each was at least a low-tier warrior.

The selection list contained a full 203 people.

What were they selecting these people for?

Fang Heng wondered.

Not good. The people outside were closing in.

Sensing more than a dozen powerful mental presences rapidly approaching the door, Fang Heng wasted no more time. He put away the purple light orb, returned to his spirit form, passed through the wall into the adjacent room, and made a quick escape.

…

Meanwhile, Qi Yuntao arrived in the corridor with a group of elite high-tier guards.

Seeing the dense black mist ahead, Qi Yuntao's eyes turned exceptionally grave.

An unfamiliar, unusual aura.

Very different from the game's black mist.

“Disperse it!”

Every warrior who'd come as reinforcement was high-tier. They immediately cast purification spells to begin cleansing the area.

Suddenly, Qi Yuntao narrowed his eyes and looked upward.

“Who's there?! Who's up there?!”

Qi Yuntao shouted and flicked his hand, sending several sharp dark needles toward the ceiling.

“Puff! Puff, puff!!”

The densely packed needles dissolved a series of dark holes through the ceiling.

Qi Yuntao frowned deeply.

“My lord.”

Several guards hurried over and looked up as well, but saw nothing.

“There was someone there just now. He got away.”

Qi Yuntao offered a brief explanation, withdrew his gaze, and turned his attention back to the black mist.

Two minutes passed, and the mist in the corridor outside the room was dispersed.

Qi Yuntao and the high-tier warriors who'd come to help looked at the bodies of their comrades sprawled in disorder across the floor, unease and wariness in their eyes.
