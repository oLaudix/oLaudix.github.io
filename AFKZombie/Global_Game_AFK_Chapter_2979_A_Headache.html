Chapter 2979

A Headache

From Chinese Chapter 2980 · 糟心

The ships under Captain Giselle's command had just completed their jump calculations and positioning.

“Captain, we can return to the main base at any time.”

“Mm.”

Captain Giselle acknowledged the report and looked toward Vion. “Have you found anything?”

Vion shook his head slightly.

During the final preparations for departure, Vion had remained in the control room, looking through the glass toward Fang Heng's flagship outside.

He was intensely curious.

What were Fang Heng and his people still planning to do here?

Hadn't they stopped mining ore already?

Captain Giselle said, “In that case, let's initiate the jump...”

“Wait!”

Suddenly, Vion noticed something and stared at Fang Heng's enormous Nethersea starship in the distance.

“Look! What's that?!”

Everyone aboard turned their eyes toward the distant ship.

They saw Kinsfolk machines moving large pieces of equipment from underground tunnels in the ground into the Nethersea.

What were those enormous mechanical structures?

They looked like parts of some larger device.

Vion frowned deeply.

“Prisms?”

Then they saw part of a dark-green prism exposed outside one of the components, and everyone's expression changed.

Could it be...?

That's right!

Those were Eye of Stars prism positioning devices!

Good grief!

At last, Vion understood why Fang Heng had stayed behind.

He intended to haul away the entire Eye of Stars positioning system!

Wouldn't that offend the entire alliance, including the Shadow Clan?

Was he really not afraid?

Vion and Captain Giselle exchanged glances, each seeing the surprise in the other's eyes.

“Outrageous!”

Perhaps from fear, Vion's voice shook as he exclaimed angrily, “And what if all of humanity gets dragged into this because of him?”

Still stunned by Fang Heng's audacity, Captain Giselle drew a deep breath and was about to speak when he noticed Iron-Eater Clan members coming up from underground and entering the Nethersea as well.

He was even taking the Iron-Eater Clan away with him?

Giselle paused, then gave a bitter smile.

Now he genuinely didn't know what to say.

That man had guts.

“Perhaps...”

Looking toward Fang Heng's fleet in the distance, Captain Giselle slowly said, “Perhaps Fang Heng is confident he can deal with the Shadow Clan.”

“Hmm?”

Vion turned to look at Captain Giselle in astonishment.

The Iron-Eater Clan had its own doubts about Fang Heng's actions.

Were humans really all this insane?

Were they truly not afraid of retaliation?

In any case, the Iron-Eaters had no choice now.

Similar things had happened before. The races left behind had suffered the Shadow Clan's wrath and been wiped out completely.

Better to follow Fang Heng than remain behind and wait to die.

Even a miserable life was better than a good death.

One more day alive was one more day.

“Boss Fang, all supplies are ready. The fleet can begin the jump.”

“Mm. Initiate the jump procedure.”

“Yes! Preparing to jump.”

Fang Heng looked at Chen Yu beside him, who seemed to have something to say, and asked, “What? Is there something bothering you?”

“Boss Fang.”

Chen Yu chose his words carefully. “I'm a little worried. Things have ended badly between us and the Shadow Clan and its alliance. We may run into trouble if we meet them again.”

“Don't worry about it. We'll deal with that when we meet them.”

Fang Heng curled his lip.

Again? Who knew when that would be?

He was an Ouroboros Seed himself now. Sooner or later, the Kinsfolk would place him on their enemies list.

If he wasn't even afraid of the entire Kinsfolk faction, why should he fear these little allied groups?

If the Kinsfolk and their allies weren't still useful to him, he'd have flipped the table and walked away long ago.

“Oh, right. Return to base first and relocate the Eye of Stars prisms. They're very important to me. I'll temporarily hand command of the fleet to the Eastern Federation. I have another important matter to take care of.”

“Huh? Right now?” Chen Yu was startled. “But the jump procedure has already...”

“It's fine. I have a way to get back.”

Fang Heng nodded lightly and leapt off the flagship before the jump began, swiftly disappearing from everyone's sight.

...

With the SSS-grade insect nest destroyed, the war entered its final battlefield cleanup phase.

The Shadow Clan and the other races had just finished clearing the main battlefield and were regrouping to divide up the world when news arrived from the Eye of Stars sites in the rear: the humans had dismantled and packed up the various Eye of Stars prisms and taken them away.

And not just the Eye of Stars. The humans had packed up the Iron-Eater Clan that built the prism installations and taken them too.

The assembled delegates were stunned. They fell silent for a moment as they tried to digest the news.

“How dare they!”

“What outrageous audacity!”

Were humans actually this crazy?

Who had ever dared do such a thing before?

Their excuse was even more ridiculous.

What did they mean, they had built the equipment, so they were entitled to take it away?

They were nothing but robbers!

“I'll go stop them!” a crocodile representative growled. “The humans have no respect for us whatsoever!”

The Shadow Clan's allies erupted into furious indignation.

Looking back now, they finally recognized how cunning the humans had been.

No wonder they'd volunteered so eagerly to help construct the Eye of Stars prism towers, acting like such generous souls.

They'd had their eyes on those prism towers from the very beginning!

It had all been premeditated.

Several Shadow Clan captains exchanged glances.

They had tested humanity's strength in the recent battle. The humans were no weaklings.

And to quietly load the prism towers and the entire Iron-Eater Clan onto their ships in such a short time, while everyone else was clearing the battlefield...

They were not to be underestimated.

“Calm yourselves. The humans' present whereabouts are unknown. Even if we intend to retaliate, now isn't the time.”

Once the Shadow Clan spoke, the other races fell silent.

Only the Crocodile Clan and the Red-Ghost Clan kept cursing inwardly.

Of course the Shadow Clan wasn't in a hurry!

But the bases and prism crystals had been supplied by the Crocodile Clan and Red-Ghost Clan respectively!

If Fang Heng divided them up and carried them off, those would be enormous losses for them.

“Issue an alliance-wide order to track humanity's movements. The next time we encounter humans, the Shadow Clan will take action.”

...

An hour later, once the surviving Ouroboros Seeds on the battlefield had all been eliminated and divided among the victors, the various races finished negotiating ownership of the planet's mines and jumped away from the main battlefield in their ships.

The smaller races recruited to do the grunt work had outlived their usefulness and were gradually told to leave the world.

The final cleanup had begun.

The Crocodile Clan was in a foul mood after the humans stole its prism defense towers.

The Iron-Eater Clan wasn't just good at construction; it also had decent mining abilities. Originally, the crocodiles could have enslaved them to extract ore, but now they'd have to leave that work to Kinsfolk machines.

Mining efficiency would predictably fall sharply.

And that wasn't the end of their troubles.

The mining teams they'd just dispatched discovered that the ore reserves in their mines had shrunk dramatically. Less than 20% of the expected deposits remained.

What was left was low-grade, inferior ore of very little mining value.

Humans!

Those damned bastards!
