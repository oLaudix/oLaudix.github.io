Chapter 3100

No Problem

From Chinese Chapter 3101 · 问题不大

Everyone understood what Hainiya meant.

She could certainly go into the arena.

But winning this round would take a great deal out of her. The opposing side still had three fighters, including a high-ranking warrior of her own level. Even if she won, she would have almost no chance against another opponent of equal rank.

Winning the entire match would be extremely difficult.

"Fang Heng."

Elder Leander's gaze swept over the two substitutes before finally settling on Fang Heng. "How about you go in?"

"Sure. I've studied a little Mentalism, so I have some resistance to mental attacks. It shouldn't be a problem."

Fang Heng was already on his feet, eager to enter the arena.

"Good. You're up."

Elder Leander gave a slight nod.

If Fang Heng could win this round, that would be a tremendous gain.

Even if he couldn't, wearing down the opponent a little would still be worthwhile.

"Yes."

Fang Heng answered calmly, leaped lightly onto the arena platform, and looked up at his opponent.

A wind-spirit demon.

The referee glanced at the two combatants and confirmed that both were ready.

"Begin!"

"Whoosh!!!"

Fang Heng became a streak of darkness and shot toward the demon.

A cold glint flashed through the demon Xili's eyes. Her spear formed a wind blade that came howling straight at Fang Heng!

"Bang!!"

The wind blade struck Fang Heng's fast-moving afterimage and exploded!

Such powerful defenses!

Xili quickly retreated, staring at Fang Heng as a soul-piercing dark light flickered within her demonic pupils.

Mental Illusion!

Fang Heng suddenly noticed ripples appearing in the air before him.

Mental Shock!

Bang!!!

The Omniscient Eye spun furiously within Fang Heng's pupil. The ripples shattered before him like panes of glass!

It didn't work?

Seeing that both her illusion and mental attack were ineffective, Xili frowned. Several green whirlwinds appeared beneath her feet as she rapidly withdrew.

Her wind affinity gave her exceptional agility. Normally, she could use high-speed movement and wind attacks to suppress opposing warriors.

But Fang Heng was different!

Watching him close the distance at a terrifying speed, a trace of panic flashed across Xili's eyes.

He's so fast!

Fang Heng's speed had actually surpassed the maximum boost from wind affinity!

I can't dodge!

In desperation, Xili frowned and gathered a series of dark-green arrows before her.

Force him back!

But by the time those arrows flew, Fang Heng was already right in front of her!

"Whoosh!!!"

Fang Heng threw another punch.

"Bang!!!"

His fist collided with the arrows, detonating them all at once and sending successive waves of green air bursting outward!

A high-level spell had been smashed apart with a single punch!

The resulting blast hurled Xili off the arena platform. She landed heavily on the ground.

Silence descended over the spectators, followed by thunderous applause and cheers.

"He won! I knew my brother was no ordinary guy!"

Nasir excitedly slapped his thigh.

Excellent!

With Fang Heng's incredible talent for alchemy, there was no way he could be an ordinary fellow.

In the Kinsfolk's waiting area, the assembled warriors looked at Fang Heng in an entirely new light.

He had broken through through sheer strength.

That meant his attributes had reached a level that completely overwhelmed Xili's.

Was this the result of taking physical enhancement to its extreme?

Demons generally possessed very high dimensional levels. Xili had a pure demon bloodline, placing her dimensional level above thirty.

Her innate physical enhancement was hardly weak.

Yet the force of the blast had sent her flying.

This one would be difficult to deal with.

The Kinsfolk's waiting area began discussing who should fight next.

Three warriors remained who had not been eliminated.

Yet not a single one of them wanted to face Fang Heng.

From their perspective, Fang Heng was simply too fast.

His resistance to spells was also terrifying.

Other than Grand, none of them could possibly withstand that monstrous speed and explosive power.

Going up would be suicide.

And they probably wouldn't even manage to exhaust Fang Heng.

"I'll go."

Grand, the only high-ranking warrior left, saw the expressions on his companions' faces. Knowing that sending either of them would be pointless, he volunteered and stepped onto the platform.

Nasir and the other fighters waiting on his side grew tense at Grand's appearance.

"It's hardly surprising. Xili lost too decisively. They must have realized ordinary mid-ranking warriors can't defeat Fang Heng—or even wear him down."

Elder Leander explained calmly, "Everything until now has merely been an appetizer. To measure Fang Heng's true strength, he needs to face a high-ranking warrior. In my judgment, Fang Heng has the better chance of winning."

The warriors were astonished to hear the elder's assessment.

Good grief.

Did the elder really favor Fang Heng over Grand, who had been famous in the arena for years?

"You're impressive for a newcomer, Fang Heng."

Grand stood on the platform and regarded Fang Heng approvingly. "Among close-combat warriors, you're already near the top. But do you know why so few close-combat fighters make it into the very highest ranks?"

"Why?" Fang Heng asked.

"The difficulty of training is one reason. The other is that they simply aren't strong enough. At the same peak level, physical enhancement falls far short of mental enhancement."

Grand shook his head as he looked at Fang Heng. "I'm sorry, but you've chosen the wrong path."

"Tch."

Fang Heng curled his lip, unwilling to waste any more words.

Seeing his unconvinced expression, Grand shook his head. "I know it's difficult to accept, but I'll prove it to you."

"Enough talking." Fang Heng looked across at him indifferently. "Get on with it. I'd really like to see where all that confidence comes from."

The referee checked that both fighters were ready and waved a hand. "Begin!"

"Whoosh!!"

As usual, Fang Heng immediately darted toward Grand.

This first move was only a test.

A perfectly ordinary straight punch.

With his monstrous physical attributes, ordinary low-ranking warriors would lose before they could even react.

Mid-ranking warriors could sometimes counter it, but the results were rarely impressive.

Hm?

Surprise appeared in Fang Heng's eyes as he examined his opponent.

A warrior specializing in Nature Studies?

That was rather uncommon.

"Hiss, hiss-hiss!!"

The ground underfoot rapidly decayed into a swamp. Fine vines and branches emerged from the mire, winding toward Fang Heng's feet.

Sharp spikes shot up from the rotting marsh and launched a furious assault on him.

At the same time, Grand's figure split in two before his eyes.

Two completely identical Grands.

And the duplicates kept multiplying.

One became two, two became four!

Sixteen identical Grands appeared around the edge of the arena. Each formed a different hand seal and cast Nature Studies spells, attempting to delay Fang Heng's advance.

— End of Chapter 3100 —
