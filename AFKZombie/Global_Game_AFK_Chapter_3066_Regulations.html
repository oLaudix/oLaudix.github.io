Chapter 3066

Regulations

From Chinese Chapter 3067 · 律条

A guard looked at Fang Heng and said coldly, “Illegal entrant, you've violated the laws of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital. We're taking you to the border prison to await trial. Don't resist. That will only make your sentence worse.”

Fang Heng frowned slightly.

He hadn't expected this.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital was more capable than he had anticipated. Even using the Black Mist to conceal himself, he'd been detected.

Did the authorities here understand the Black Mist better than humanity did?

Come to think of it, where had the others aboard the ship gone?

Fang Heng turned to look around. Before he could ask, a guard behind him struck him hard on the back.

“Come along. And remember, from now on your number is G1923.”

Fang Heng followed the guards while inspecting the restraints on his wrists.

They possessed a certain power-suppressing effect.

But it was extremely limited.

Breaking free by force wouldn't be a problem.

Even without breaking them, he could probably escape their restraints directly by entering his spirit form.

But this was the elves' ship. Making too much trouble might bring reinforcements, and it would be a shame if the vessel were damaged during the confrontation.

He could even cause problems for the Elven Clan.

After a moment's thought, Fang Heng decided to do nothing for now. He followed the guards along the ship's corridors, passed through several warp portals, and was escorted into a temporary group cell at the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's immigration prison.

“In you go! Think hard about what you've done! And don't try anything, or you'll regret it!”

The guard glared at Fang Heng, threw out the threat, and hurried away.

Hundreds of illegal entrants were thrown into these cells every day. He had no interest in paying this one any special attention.

The cell was large. More than a dozen alien prisoners were already inside when Fang Heng entered. Most sat apart from one another, either in the corners or leaning against the metal bars.

Only after the guards left did everyone turn to look at Fang Heng.

“A human? Haven't seen one in ages.”

A young man with scattered blue speckles on his skin stood up and studied Fang Heng.

Several of the prisoners seemed to regard him as their leader, moving closer and gathering at his sides.

“What's your name, brother?”

“Fang Heng,” he answered calmly. “What is this place?”

“The Seven-Realm Martial Capital's border prison.” Klaus pursed his lips toward Fang Heng. “The humans I met before were pretty tough. What about you? Got any special skills?”

Skills?

Fang Heng was rarely asked that question. After thinking a moment, he asked, “Does being able to take a beating count?”

“Haha! You're a funny one. Taking a beating isn't a skill. Being able to beat someone else is what counts.”

Klaus laughed, then glanced toward a guard standing at the far end of the passage outside and lowered his voice. “Brother, I'm Klaus. I don't want trouble. We're planning a jailbreak soon. Either come with us or stand aside and don't get in the way. Can you do that?”

“Of course.”

Fang Heng shrugged, stepped aside, and lowered his head to study the energy patterns on his shackles.

Interesting!

From an alchemical perspective, the patterns converted and redirected energy to produce the cuffs' effect, significantly suppressing the wearer's energy circulation.

But their suppressive power was quite limited.

They could probably seal around ninety percent of the abilities of someone below the Master level in academic disciplines.

Once the wearer's academic level reached Grandmaster, however, the effect would decrease sharply.

They were little more than toys, after all. That they worked this well was already impressive.

Nearby, the young prisoners were quietly conspiring about their escape.

Fang Heng caught a few of their words.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital sounded impressive, but corruption had long since eaten away its border prison system. Breakouts happened frequently, and the prison administration simply covered them up.

After all, no one cared if a few low-level prisoners like them went missing.

In most cases, anyone who didn't escape would be sent to mine ore in worlds with brutal living conditions. Depending on the severity of their crimes, they would be freed only after working a specified number of years.

Many prisoners were also secretly sold as slaves.

The officers in charge knew perfectly well what was happening and simply turned a blind eye.

With so many prisoners, getting rid of a few was convenient—and they could make a little extra money.

Why wouldn't they?

The dozen or so youths in this cell and young members of their species in two other cells belonged to the same group. They had already decided to escape while being transported.

They had even arranged for accomplices to meet them halfway.

Several of the youths whispered together.

Their greatest obstacle was the handcuffs.

Besides sealing their power, the cuffs were extremely heavy. Running while wearing them was exhausting, and forcibly breaking them would cause them to explode!

The prison hadn't designed the explosions to kill people.

Anyone strong enough to shatter the cuffs through sheer force would be extremely powerful. The explosions were meant to alert the guards.

The cuffs also functioned as tracking devices.

“Boss, everything's ready. If all goes well, once we're outside the protected zone, we'll head straight to the mining district and find Mr. Li. He can remove our cuffs and get us identity papers.”

“Relax. Even the Seven-Realm Martial Capital couldn't tell that Old Li's identity papers were fake...”

“It'll take a while to get there, though. The prison might send people after us...”

While they were discussing the plan, a sharp click suddenly came from Fang Heng's direction.

Hm?

Everyone turned to look.

Fang Heng had casually removed the handcuffs from his wrists.

What the hell?

The youths stared at him as if they'd seen a ghost.

“Mm.” Seeing their expressions, Fang Heng shook the cuffs in his hand. “I've studied a bit of alchemy. These cuffs have an interesting design...”

Gulp...

The prisoners swallowed nervously and exchanged glances, feeling as though something utterly absurd had happened.

These cuffs had been custom-designed for the prison by the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's Alchemy Association. They were mass-produced, made of ordinary materials, and nowhere near their theoretical limits, but someone had just dismantled one with ease?

This kid's alchemy had to be at least Master level, right?

“Uh, well...” Klaus collected himself, scratched his head, and asked awkwardly, “Brother, could you do me a favor and take mine off too?”

“Of course.”

Fang Heng lightly tapped the restraints on Klaus's wrists with his right hand.

Klaus froze, then lowered his head. The alchemical runes on the cuffs began moving rapidly, and the entire restraint dropped to the ground.

What the hell?!

How had he done that?

Klaus stared at Fang Heng in astonishment. “Brother, you...”

“I heard you can find someone to make genuine-looking identity documents for you?” Fang Heng asked Klaus. “How about doing me a favor in return? I'm new here and don't know the place. Take me into the Seven-Realm Martial Capital and help me get a set of identity papers. What do you say?”

— End of Chapter 3066 —
