Chapter 2210

Calculations

From original Chinese Chapter 2211 · 盘算

Fang Heng looked at Ji Haiting beside him.

Ji Haiting nodded. “Don't worry about the Federation. They have systems for detecting energy fluctuations, but most are barely operational now. With the Tree Spirits regaining their strength, the Federation has changed its military policy. At most they'll investigate. They won't attack first.”

“Good!”

Fang Heng nodded and called out, “Victor, it's fine! Release the energy controls.”

“Understood!”

Fang Heng had already worked it out.

With the Spirit Domain blockade network completely disabled, the Tree Spirits were much stronger, and the Federation couldn't beat them. Even if it detected the natural energy, it would likely assume the Tree Spirits were responsible and avoid picking a fight it couldn't win.

His greater concern was the Tree Spirits themselves.

As Abe Akaya absorbed the seeds, the natural energy spreading outward didn't weaken. It grew stronger and stronger!

The intensity made even Fang Heng frown.

The fluctuations were far too powerful.

The Tree Spirits in the outer world would undoubtedly sense them!

If he wanted to avoid a direct confrontation, he needed to find a way to leave quickly.

The absorption process lasted more than half an hour.

Only then did Abe Akaya's energy fluctuations gradually subside.

[Hint: The player's Soul Symbiote—Abe Akaya has absorbed Divine Tree Seed Essence.]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has awakened Tree World Sealing (not permanently fixed; duration: 48 months).]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has awakened Spatial Extension (not permanently fixed; duration: 24 months).]

Skill: Tree World Sealing.

Skill description: Abe Akaya constructs a special 'Inner World' within itself. It can seal external targets inside this world and continuously absorb their power. If the target's strength exceeds the world's suppressive capacity, it has a chance of breaking free.

Fang Heng's interest stirred.

Compared with the description he'd seen when collecting the seed, the skill now had much more detailed information.

“Tree World Sealing…?”

Reading the new description gave Fang Heng an odd feeling.

The Inner World inhabited by the Tree Spirits seemed almost like a seal created by a Divine Tree.

Setting that aside, couldn't the sealing ability also serve as an escape or survival skill, allowing him to enter the sealed Tree World himself?

Fang Heng tried communicating with Abe Akaya.

Indeed.

His guess was correct.

But Abe Akaya had only just entered its growth phase and was still relatively weak. The sealed world it could create was very small, and the strength of creatures it could confine was limited.

There was also a restriction on casting range: targets had to be within Abe Akaya's reach.

Still, not bad.

It was an ability that could grow stronger over time.

Fang Heng considered it briefly, then examined the second skill.

Skill: Spatial Extension.

Skill description: Abe Akaya can transcend spatial restrictions and enter other worlds, absorbing energy, growing, and fighting across multiple worlds at once. Its branches can be extended through ritual magic arrays. All Divine Trees share the same HP pool. Branches existing in different worlds remain subject to those worlds' suppressive effects.

Skill description: If a teleportation passage is destroyed, Abe Akaya's branches in the corresponding world will gradually lose energy until they die completely.

Skill description: If Abe Akaya's main body in the primary world is destroyed, its branches in secondary worlds will gradually lose energy until they die completely.

[Hint: The player has comprehended the ritual magic array—Descent of the Tree World.]

[Hint: The player can use the ritual magic array to extend Abe Akaya's branches into any world with a compatibility rating greater than 0.]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has different compatibility ratings in different worlds. Its combat strength may be enhanced or reduced. A small number of worlds are entirely unsuitable for it, preventing any growth.]

“Spatial Extension…”

Fang Heng murmured, reading through the skill description carefully.

As he understood it, once Abe Akaya had established a primary world, he could summon branches of a mature Abe Akaya directly into other worlds.

Setting world compatibility aside, those extended branches would theoretically share all the main body's HP, attributes, and abilities!

He would no longer have to rely on Klevi to turn Abe Akaya into a seed, transport it into another world, and slowly grow it from scratch each time.

For Abe Akaya in its current state, this was an absolutely essential divine-tier ability!

Fang Heng couldn't help clenching his fist.

What a worthwhile haul!

With this skill…

He raised his head, considering the problem immediately ahead.

Given the immense fluctuations the seed essences had just released, there was a good chance the Tree Spirits would come investigating.

Their strength had increased dramatically, and the main quest still required their cooperation. It wasn't wise to confront them directly at this stage…

To be safe, he should move Abe Akaya back first!

He could transfer Abe Akaya's main body into the Zombie Apocalypse, then use the ritual array to summon its branches back here.

That would avoid directly antagonizing the Tree Spirits.

And since the Zombie Apocalypse was still a protected beginner world that couldn't be invaded…

Abe Akaya would be practically immortal!

Time to act!

He'd just acquired more than seventy Leaves of Life from the Leaf Clan. With some more resource gathering, he should be able to help Abe Akaya regain strength fairly quickly.

Besides, once Abe Akaya returned to the Zombie Apocalypse, it could spread without restriction and gradually grow stronger.

Fang Heng quickly worked through the plan.

Sandy noticed him standing there lost in thought and walked over. “Fang Heng, how'd it go? Everything okay?”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng came back to himself and looked at Klevi. “Klevi, can you teleport Abe Akaya back again?”

“Huh?”

Klevi was surprised.

They'd only just brought it here, and now they had to send it back?

They'd spent so many Leaves of Life getting it to its present stage.

Were they going to start over yet again?

Fang Heng explained, “The Divine Tree has gained spatial abilities. It can grow across worlds. The Tree Spirits will arrive soon, and it's dangerous for Abe Akaya to stay here. We'll move it back this time, and we'll never need to transplant it again.”

Klevi understood the general idea and nodded. “All right. But if we do that, Abe Akaya's remaining energy will only keep the Inner World teleportation passage open for one hour at most.”

“That's enough. We need to hurry. Start now.”

Klevi immediately began dispersing Abe Akaya's power, returning it to seed form.

Fang Heng rubbed his temples, feeling a headache coming on.
