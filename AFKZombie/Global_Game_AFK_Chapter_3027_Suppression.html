Chapter 3027

Suppression

From Chinese Chapter 3028 · 压制

Eseklyna watched Fang Heng, her expression gradually growing more solemn.

That wasn't all.

She could sense it.

The necromantic aura coming from Fang Heng was far stronger than his earlier sacred aura!

As Fang Heng entered his Necromancy form, twenty-four Nether Orbs emerged from the tattoos on his body and began circling him.

“Whoosh!!”

Fang Heng became a streak of shadow and closed in to attack first!

Eseklyna's heart jolted.

That bastard!

Fang Heng was moving even faster than before!

What was going on?

Why did the draining effect of the Tongues of Envy seem to have had absolutely no effect on him?!

Faster even than Fang Heng were the Nether Orbs floating behind him.

They shot toward Eseklyna before he reached her.

Not daring to be careless, Eseklyna retreated rapidly, extending Tongues of Envy from her body to block them.

“Bang!!!”

A Nether Orb struck a Tongue of Envy and exploded!

Huh?

Eseklyna's heart skipped a beat.

Although she'd knocked the orb aside, the dense necromantic aura released by its explosion instantly corroded more than half of her Tongues of Envy.

What was happening?

This brat's Necromancy level was actually higher than his Sacred Studies level!

And not just by a little!

“Whoosh!!!”

Eseklyna retreated at high speed to evade the black orbs, staring at Fang Heng in bewilderment.

How was that possible?

His strength had suddenly undergone another explosive increase.

Had he been concealing his true power all along?

The Nether Orbs alone were giving her an awful headache, and Fang Heng hadn't even launched his own attack yet.

“Whoosh!!”

Domain of the Tongues of Envy!

Not daring to underestimate him, Eseklyna immediately activated her innate ability.

Domain activated!

The surrounding space descended into chaos. Countless Tongues of Envy covered in brown eyes drilled out from the cavern walls and the distorted space itself, converging rapidly on Fang Heng!

“Heh...”

Fang Heng gave a cold laugh.

Using the same move twice in a row was stupid enough. This time it was even weaker than before.

Perfect. Time to test the skill he'd recently learned!

“Descent of the Death Realm!”

“Whoosh!”

The instant he used the skill, a gray-white shadow covered Fang Heng's pupils, and Eseklyna's spectral soul image appeared in his vision.

Gray-white space rapidly expanded outward from Fang Heng!

The Death Realm domain instantly engulfed the entire cavern. Even the Tongues of Envy rushing frantically toward him acquired a layer of gray-white light.

The two different domains didn't clash. Instead, they merged into one another in a bizarre way.

As soon as the gray-white layer coated the Tongues of Envy, their speed dropped drastically. Decay, aging, weakness, and other negative effects began appearing across their surfaces.

Eseklyna was engulfed by the Death Realm domain as well. Suddenly, the surrounding space became utterly silent—so silent that she could hear her own heartbeat clearly.

It was as though everything in that environment would slowly come to a standstill.

Even her thoughts were gradually slowing.

Not far away, a phantom altar slowly materialized behind Fang Heng.

A swirling passage appeared on each side of the altar.

Eseklyna didn't notice that her body was corroding and aging little by little. She only felt an invisible force tearing at her soul and will, slowly drawing them toward one of the passages.

Not good! An illusion!

“Hiss!!”

Eseklyna abruptly realized what was happening. She bit the tip of her tongue, trying to shock herself awake.

But it didn't work.

Her tongue felt no pain. Within the deathly stillness of that gray-white world, all five of her senses had been stripped away.

This wasn't an ordinary illusion. It was the influence of some higher-level force directed at her soul and will.

Eseklyna realized that her enemy had also cast a domain ability—and his domain was stronger than hers.

Under the gray-white domain, large numbers of Tongues of Envy had already aged and withered away.

Once the two domains merged, her Tongues of Envy were clearly unable to withstand the Death Realm's power.

“Crack!!!”

Unaffected by the environment of Descent of the Death Realm, Fang Heng abruptly appeared before Eseklyna. He extended his palm, and the surrounding Death Realm energy quickly formed a Scythe of the Nether God in his hand.

Hiss!!

The scythe swept forward.

Eseklyna's pupils contracted.

“Bang!!!”

Her body, shattered and dismembered on the spot, quickly re-formed to the right.

Fang Heng glanced coldly at her.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!!”

The Nether Orbs around Fang Heng slammed into Eseklyna together, blasting her body apart yet again!

Resurrection?

Fang Heng looked coldly at the revived Eseklyna.

So what if she could resurrect countless times?

Within the Death Realm, each time Eseklyna's body was destroyed, her soul and will suffered another impact.

Eseklyna's face had turned ashen, and her soul's origin was already badly damaged.

Although her original Demon Core was unharmed, she could feel her body growing steadily weaker.

Dying and reviving over and over, her thoughts slowed to a standstill. Most of the time she remained in a confused haze, fighting only on instinct, shattered by Fang Heng again and again before resurrecting through her original power.

Endure it!

Eseklyna briefly regained consciousness once more. She clenched her teeth and held fast to her fundamental will.

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!!!”

Every second was agonizing. Time crawled by, until Eseklyna intermittently regained awareness again and suddenly froze.

Huh?

What?

Fang Heng had actually stopped attacking her.

At some point, the gray-white cavern had become filled with creeping vines.

“Hiss, hiss...”

The plants had somehow climbed onto her body, binding her tightly and pinning her against the wall.

What!?

The next instant, Eseklyna's pupils contracted violently!

The power of her soul was being absorbed uncontrollably!

[Hint: Abe Akaya has currently absorbed 68.5% of Envy energy.]

Fang Heng watched Eseklyna from afar, assessing the actual strength of Descent of the Death Realm.

Very strong.

Descent of the Death Realm and Descent of the Tree Domain emphasized different things. The Tree Domain mainly used its environment to strengthen the caster, while the Death Realm imposed terrifying debuffs on the enemy.

Most people couldn't remain for long within the extreme Death Realm environment created by the Death Realm Jewel.

Even Fang Heng had needed months of painful training in the Death Realm before he could move normally in it.

Moreover, within the domain of Descent of the Death Realm, enemies suffered continual interference with their souls and wills, along with physical decay, aging, and other negative effects.

An excellent skill!

If he had to compare the two, Descent of the Death Realm was clearly far stronger!
