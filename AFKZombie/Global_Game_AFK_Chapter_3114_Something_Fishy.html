Chapter 3114

Something Fishy

From Chinese Chapter 3115 · 蹊跷

Seeing Fang Heng emerge from his training, Mu Shunan and Nasir asked, "Well? How was it?"

"Mm, it's genuinely useful. It sharpens perception of external laws considerably, though the effect doesn't last very long."

Fang Heng nodded, puzzled. "Strange. I don't remember anything like it in the Mysticism texts. Where did the Kinsfolk get them?"

"It's not just the Kinsfolk. Recently, three of the major races—including them—have been trying to lure warriors away from other factions. Their recruited warriors are generally improving very quickly too. I suspect these beads have something to do with it."

"Let's not dwell on it. The Noel Clan has far greater strength and resources than unaffiliated warriors like us. If even we've noticed something unusual, they probably found out much earlier. They don't appear to be doing anything, but I imagine they already have a response planned."

Everyone nodded.

Fang Heng considered the matter silently.

His current training speed was still too slow.

If only he had an endless supply of those beads.

After returning, he could try investigating where they came from.

For now, he'd continue training and explore the Lanyue Clan's secrets with Nasir tonight. Hopefully they'd find a way to break through to Demigod-level Alchemy soon.

As he was thinking, a crew member hurried up from the rear of the starship's deck.

"Warriors."

The crew member bowed respectfully to Fang Heng and the others and reported, "The captain has something important to discuss. He requests your presence."

Nasir grew serious, stretched, and said, "Let's go. Looks like there's work to do."

"Come on, let's find out what's happening."

...

In the captain's quarters, Captain Hoffman was pacing anxiously. When Fang Heng and the others entered, he nodded. "Everyone, we've encountered some trouble."

"The Lanyue Clan suddenly turned hostile and attacked our ore-transport personnel. Some of our people are trapped in a mountain cavern. I've ordered a squad to prepare for an investigation. The situation may have changed, so I'd appreciate your assistance in accompanying them."

The warriors exchanged varied looks.

Had the Lanyue Clan gone mad?

They actually dared attack the Noel Clan's people directly, knowing this entire group of warriors was present?

Something was fishy!

"Let's go. We'll accompany you."

"Thank you. I appreciate it."

Captain Hoffman gathered his personnel and initiated a starship jump to the edge of a cliff near the mountain-cavern mining area.

Looking down from the cliff edge, they could see a bottomless pit below.

"This mine produces an exceptionally rare ore. It's also sacred ground to the Lanyue Clan, and outsiders aren't permitted inside. Previously, the Lanyue people handled all the mining and then handed the extracted ore over to us."

"For some reason, they went berserk today and attacked our ore-transport squad, then retreated into the cavern to hide."

As he spoke, several guards disembarked and assembled outside the starship, preparing for battle.

Hoffman briefly outlined the situation and warned, "The cavern entrance is at the base of the cliff. The Lanyue people have lived on this planet since childhood and know the terrain intimately. I'm concerned that entering recklessly might lead us into an ambush."

"Mm. Let's go down and take a look first."

One warrior spoke and jumped down the cliff ahead of the others.

Fang Heng and the rest followed him down.

Hoffman gestured, leaving some personnel to guard the starship, then led his combat squad after them using flight equipment and slowly descended to the bottom of the cliff.

Fang Heng landed at the base and surveyed the area, focusing on a cavern entrance hidden along the edge of the cliff wall.

Interesting.

The cavern possessed a strange power that seemed to swallow perception.

Fang Heng glanced at Nasir beside him.

Nasir looked equally perplexed.

The laws here weren't normal.

"Something really is off," a warrior accompanying them said after peering into the entrance. "Captain Hoffman, why don't we send a few people inside to investigate while the main force waits outside? Once we've confirmed it's safe, the rest can follow."

Hoffman nodded. "That would be best. I'll leave it to you."

The seven warriors quickly discussed their options. They decided to leave two outside to support and protect the main group, while the remaining five would enter together.

A warrior named Edwin stared at the dark opening and snorted disdainfully. "Tch. I'd like to see what tricks the Lanyue Clan are playing."

With that, Edwin entered the cavern first.

Every warrior present could be considered a top-tier combatant on an ordinary planet. Any one of them could destroy a normal world without much difficulty.

Meanwhile, the most powerful member of the entire Lanyue Clan was only as strong as a low-ranking warrior.

This arrangement was already extremely cautious.

Fang Heng and his two companions exchanged looks and entered the cavern as well.

A soft violet glow shimmered inside, illuminating the cave.

"Apparently, the Lanyue Clan's world has a special kind of biological ore. It helps with Elemental Studies training, and the Lanyue people possess a secret technique for stimulating its growth."

"That's why the Noel Clan left the mining operation in their hands. The Lanyue Clan contributes a batch of resources to the Noel Clan every month."

Nasir surveyed the cavern as he walked slowly deeper inside. Suddenly, something caught his eye, and he pointed toward a nearby section of the cave.

"Brother Fang Heng, look over there!"

Fang Heng followed his finger and narrowed his eyes.

An alchemical magic array!

Unfortunately...

It was only an ordinary law-type array.

Around the Transcendent level.

And it was damaged, so there wasn't much to learn from it.

"These are all ancient magic arrays. The Lanyue Clan was extremely accomplished in magic-array studies in antiquity. Unfortunately, their knowledge was interrupted across generations. Nowadays, their understanding of Alchemy is merely average. Even their strongest elder has only reached Grandmaster-level Alchemy."

Nasir spoke quietly as he followed near the rear of the group.

As they ventured deeper, everyone gradually sensed something abnormal.

The entire cave seemed imbued with a strange magic that could devour perception.

And throughout their journey, they hadn't seen a single person.

What the hell were the Lanyue people doing?

"Hey, look over there. Another magic array."

Edwin noticed torches ahead and went over to examine them.

Several burning torches were wedged into cracks between the rocks.

An ancient, medium-sized magic array was inscribed on the ground.

"Mm, a law-type array." As soon as Nasir spotted it, he hurried over and bent down to inspect it. Surprise glimmered in his eyes as he admired it inwardly. "It's a fusion array... There might actually be something worth learning here."
