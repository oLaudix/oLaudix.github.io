Chapter 3130

Intrusion

From Chinese Chapter 3131 · 闯入

The corridor wasn't particularly wide. Fragments of ice soul scattered across almost its entire breadth in an instant, engulfing the insect creatures!

“Thud! Thud, thud!!!”

The ice-soul fragments didn't explode when they struck the creatures. Instead, they dissolved into crystals of extremely low-temperature ice, which spread rapidly until they covered great portions of the Lickers' bodies and froze them solid!

Phew.

With three high-tier warriors and a number of lower-ranking ones present, dealing with a handful of insects shouldn't be too difficult. The security team relaxed a little.

But although the creatures had been halted, the black mist pouring from behind them showed no sign of stopping. It advanced rapidly around the corner, growing denser as though it meant to swallow everyone whole.

“Purify!”

A high-tier warrior formed a sigil before himself, summoning multiple purification arrows through a nature-element spell and sending them flying into the mist.

“Puff! Puff, puff!!!”

More than a dozen natural purification arrows disappeared into the mist and exploded, producing hissing noises within it.

It worked.

But the effect was hardly significant.

The guard's expression changed.

The purification energy in his arrows was being completely suppressed.

The academic power contained within the black mist was of a far higher tier than his nature-element spells. As a result, his purification effect was almost useless.

Edmond noticed it too.

Most high-tier warriors had reached at least Transcendent-level proficiency in their disciplines, and a small minority had even broken through to the Demigod level.

The mist before them was unquestionably of an even higher rank!

Very possibly God level!

Edmond frowned. “It might be an evolved form of the game's black mist. Everyone, don't let it reach the rooms! Attack! Find a way to stop it!”

No one held back any longer. Three warriors formed seals simultaneously and combined their abilities to create an invisible spatial barrier in front of the group, blocking the black mist's advance.

A high-tier warrior drove his nature-element purification abilities to their limits, while the other guards unleashed great sheets of elemental Leaping Thunder ahead.

Leaping Thunder could deal with the Lickers and inflicted a troublesome paralysis effect. It also had a thunder-based purification effect against the black mist.

The mist reacted with a more intense series of popping explosions and dimmed slightly.

For the moment, they appeared to have stopped it.

But before anyone could truly relax, Edmond's heart suddenly jolted.

What?!

“Whoosh!!!”

Several black spheres suddenly shot from deep within the mist.

Edmond sensed an unsettling aura in them. His pupils contracted as he shouted, “Watch out!!!”

“BOOM!”

A Netherworld Orb slammed heavily into the spatial barrier.

Countless tiny cracks instantly spread across it.

At the same time, dense black mist erupted from the orb, sharply increasing the concentration of the surrounding fog.

The three warriors controlling the spatial barrier turned pale. They forcibly gathered their mental strength and slowly repaired the cracks.

Edmond relaxed a little.

Fortunately, they'd blocked it.

But in the very next instant, his expression changed again as he looked deeper into the passage.

What the hell?!

Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!

Netherworld Orbs emerged from the black mist one after another and hurtled directly toward the spatial barrier!

“BOOM! BOOM! Boom, boom!!!”

After four successive impacts, the three barrier controllers could no longer hold on. They simultaneously spat blood and were flung backward, crashing heavily into the metal wall behind them.

The spatial barrier shattered completely. Dense undead energy erupted along with the sinister undead mist, rapidly engulfing Edmond and the other guards in black fog.

Damn it!

Where was everyone?

He couldn't see!

Cold sweat formed on Edmond's forehead.

Not only did the mist obscure his vision, it also severely impeded his perception. Even so, he felt an intense sense of danger.

As though the god of death himself were about to descend.

Fang Heng slowly emerged from the sinister undead mist behind Edmond.

The black fog gathered into the shape of a scythe in Fang Heng's hand.

“Whoosh!”

Death Scythe!

Just as the scythe descended, Edmond felt the hairs on the back of his neck stand on end. He spun around, only to glimpse a cold, dark gleam sweep across his vision.

“BOOM!!!”

Undead power exploded, almost instantly smashing apart the elemental armor gathered around his body. The force hurled him backward until he slammed into the metal wall, leaving a deep dent.

“Pfft!”

Edmond spat a mouthful of dark blood and stared at the shadowy figure concealed by the black mist ahead.

Who was this enemy?

Not even in the Seven Realms Martial Capital's highest-level battlefields had he ever been reduced to such a miserable state by a single attack.

Hm?

Hm? Edmond suddenly realized that his opponent's assault wasn't finished.

The black mist around him was being manipulated by an invisible force, flowing into his body through the openings in his shattered elemental armor.

Now Edmond noticed something was wrong with his body, too.

His heart was beating far too quickly.

The blood circulating within him also felt unnatural—much too fast.

“Pfft!”

Edmond felt his chest tighten violently and couldn't help coughing up another mouthful of blood.

Without his noticing, blood began seeping from his facial orifices, then seemed to move of its own accord, rapidly winding toward his neck.

What was happening?

What was happening? Edmond snapped awake and struggled to his feet, hurriedly wiping the blood from his neck.

An illusion?

He'd lost track of the enemy in the black mist and could hear only a faint rustling.

Here it comes!

“Whoosh!!!”

Ice Soul!

Edmond didn't dare take risks. He thrust his hand forward, sending a dense barrage of ice crystals toward the direction of the sound!

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

It was an insect!

The creature was struck by Edmond's desperate blow, covered in ice crystals, and sent flying into the depths of the black fog.

Edmond remained on high alert, pressing his back tightly against the wall.

Where was the one who'd ambushed him earlier?

Edmond expanded his perception as far as it would reach.

Meanwhile, Fang Heng had used the black mist as cover to eliminate several troublesome guards in rapid succession.

Sensing that reinforcements were already coming up from below, he decided to waste no more time. He fixed his gaze on the room's entrance.

Some high-ranking Kinsfolk should be inside.

Fang Heng stepped forward, entered his spirit form, passed directly through the door, and walked into the room.

The Kinsfolk!

Their true bodies resembled a state somewhere between a physical being and a spirit, though they were visible to the naked eye.

At this moment, however, every Kinsfolk member was fully equipped and seated inside a towering humanoid mech.

Humanoid mechs.

Enhanced with the Kinsfolk's cutting-edge technology.

Although they couldn't see him, two Kinsfolk had already pinpointed Fang Heng with their keen senses. Both turned to look directly at his position.

— End of Chapter 3130 —
