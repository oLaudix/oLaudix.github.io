Chapter 2998

Assistance

From Chinese Chapter 2999 · 协助

Luo Ao didn't think about it further, nor did he have time. His gaze swept past Fang Heng, and he immediately led the squad after the directions indicated by the Key of Apocalypse.

Fang Heng likewise glanced quickly over Luo Ao and the others.

Heh.

So when they couldn't win, they ran away?

As usual, they left the mess for someone else to clean up.

It was a little strange, though. Since when did Holy Court members volunteer to stay behind and cover a retreat?

Fang Heng rolled his shoulders lightly.

Very well.

The Ouroboros Seed was already trapped. What was he waiting for?

“Whoosh!”

Fang Heng pushed lightly off the ground, switched into his sacred form, and became a dark streak, rushing toward the restrained beetle-like Ouroboros Seed.

Archbishop Whiteman and the Holy Court group had noticed Fang Heng appearing behind them some time ago.

They'd assumed he was just an ordinary player passing through. When his aura suddenly erupted and he charged toward them, they were stunned.

What the hell?!

What was wrong with this guy?

Whiteman had already concluded that with their current abilities, they could not defeat the Calamity Seed.

Fortunately, the Seed had shown exceptionally strong defenses but relatively limited attacking power.

Their plan was to restrain it temporarily with Sacred Sealing Imprisonment, wear it down, slow its advance, and find a way to weaken it.

But Fang Heng's arrival completely upset that balance.

As he charged in, the sacred sealing skill began fluctuating violently and its restraining power dropped sharply.

“BOOM!!!”

What?!

The next instant, the Holy Court players' expressions changed drastically as they sensed the sacred power suddenly erupting from Fang Heng.

It was actually sacred power!

The enormous sacred energy he released instantly disrupted Sacred Sealing Imprisonment, then concentrated at a single point and burst toward the beetle cluster.

“Shattering Strike!”

“Whoosh!”

With a piercing shriek, a golden streak of light flashed before everyone's eyes!

“BOOM!!!”

The sacred golden radiance struck the beetles' outer shell, punched through it in an instant, and exploded the entire carapace. A dark figure shot out from the shattered shell and was hurled violently backward.

Under the powerful sacred shock wave, Sacred Sealing Imprisonment lost stability and dissipated.

Every Holy Court player turned toward Fang Heng with a look of disbelief.

That immensely powerful fluctuation of sacred energy...

He belonged to the Holy Court!

And he'd shattered the entire beetle aggregate in a single attack!

With just that one strike, everyone understood that his strength exceeded that of any one of them!

Archbishop Whiteman stared fixedly at Fang Heng.

“Whoosh!”

Having landed his first blow, Fang Heng became a dark blur and quickly pursued the Ouroboros Seed figure thrown from the beetle shell.

Shattering Strike's damage depended on the remaining energy of his Sacred Shield. Therefore its first use, when the shield was full, produced maximum damage and destroyed the beetle cluster in one blow.

“THUD!!!”

The figure revealed inside the beetle swarm was still more than half decayed. Haye's body flew backward and struck the icy wall with a heavy crash.

Dense masses of beetles immediately converged from every direction, trying to gather around Haye again. At the same time, he slowly raised his head and lifted a hand.

Whiteman's heart lurched. “Careful!!”

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!”

Sticky black corruption clinging to Haye's body shot violently outward!

Every lump carried terrifying corrosive and poisonous effects.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng abruptly vanished in midair.

Secondary Space Projection!

By the time he reappeared, he was at the right side of Haye's rotting body.

BANG!!!

He threw a punch!

The beetles that had just gathered around the rotten flesh shattered again!

Fang Heng reached forward and grabbed, driving his right hand straight through the corruption covering Haye. Fierce sizzling sounds immediately came from his hand as the substance corroded it.

Hm?

Fang Heng frowned. It was faint, but he could sense that the decaying substance was absorbing some of his power.

“Be careful! Don't touch him directly! Attacking at close range will only let him absorb your attributes and abilities!”

Attribute absorption?

Was that this creature's special ability?

Fang Heng understood at once.

No wonder the fellow seemed so weak apart from his exceptionally hard shell.

Apparently this was an Ouroboros Seed evolved in the direction of a special ability.

In that case...

“BANG!!”

Fang Heng flung Haye away with tremendous force, slamming him into the icy wall again. He then raised a hand and pointed at him.

Haye was immediately caught by Divine Punishment's power, his body slowly rising into the air against his will.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

“Divine Punishment!”

BOOM!!

A deep-purple lightning bolt crashed down from above!

Part of the filthy sludge coating the Ouroboros Seed was purified and dispersed by the sacred strike. More rotten slime peeled from its blackened body, gradually revealing the dark-brown skeleton within.

Although Fang Heng's Sacred Studies had not advanced correspondingly, his overall Mental Strength had soared after his Super-Divine-Level Necromancy advancement, greatly enhancing Divine Punishment as well!

Heh...

Fang Heng could sense that a single Divine Punishment strike had already dealt severe damage to the Ouroboros Seed.

Was it really this weak...?

It wasn't even in the same league as Proust!

Then let's do it again!

“Continue!”

BOOM!

BOOM, BOOM, BOOM!!!

More than thirty successive lightning strikes slammed down!

Haye's HP was nearly emptied. His body had been tortured beyond recognition, and raging sacred flames began burning all over him.

Rotten sludge composing his body slid away continuously in the fire, releasing a foul burnt stench.

Fang Heng watched the rapidly dying Ouroboros Seed coldly.

Meanwhile, in the shadows not far away,

several pairs of eyes were fixed on Fang Heng.

Surprise finally broke through Xia Xi's normally unruffled expression.

Fang Heng!

When had his strength risen to this level?

The subordinates beside Xia Xi were even more visibly frightened.

Haye, the Ouroboros Seed sealed inside the ice-crystal maze, possessed terrifying strength, yet Fang Heng was crushing him with Divine Punishment!

The fight hadn't actually been very fast. It had simply been one-sided suppression from beginning to end.

Whether in attributes or abilities.

The subordinates swallowed nervously. “My lady, should we...?”

Xia Xi fell silent too.

According to her original expectations, Fang Heng wouldn't have been strong enough to defeat Haye directly. This was supposed to be a test for him.

If things had gone according to plan, Fang Heng would obtain the Titans' power hidden in the ice-crystal maze, awaken his Titan bloodline, and only then challenge the Ouroboros Seed after becoming stronger.

Xia Xi had intended to use the opportunity to assess his strength and stimulate his potential.

Never had she imagined that Fang Heng would defeat the Ouroboros Seed with his own strength, without even awakening the Titans' power.

And from where she stood, he'd done it with remarkable ease.

After considering this for a moment, Xia Xi smiled faintly and murmured, “You've surprised me, Fang Heng...”

All the better!

“Let's go. There's no reason for us to stay any longer.”

— End of Chapter 2998 —
