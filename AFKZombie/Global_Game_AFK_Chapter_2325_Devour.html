Chapter 2325

Devour

From original Chinese Chapter 2326 · 吞

"Abe Akaya! Faster! As fast as you can! Devour it! We don't have much time! Take as much as possible!"

Sensing Fang Heng's urgency, Abe Akaya realized the opportunity was limited and immediately accelerated its absorption to the maximum!

Under the influence of the black fog, the magic array on the ground formed a rapidly spinning vortex!

Abe Akaya's roots and vines surged out of it, converging on the Divine Tree sapling and engulfing it completely as they absorbed its energy!

Fang Heng clenched his teeth and focused his mental strength on drawing the corruption out of the sapling.

Every extra second he held on meant another second of energy for Abe Akaya!

At the same time, a few hundred meters away at the other end of the passage leading to the cultivation hall, Chrono and his people had withdrawn into a separate chamber.

Once they stabilized their position, the guards used natural energy to purify the corruption remaining in the passage.

Then they saw Shawu and another elder stumble out from the depths, badly wounded.

"Elder Shawu!"

Seeing his condition, everyone was shocked.

What had happened inside the Divine Tree cultivation area?

Shawu stopped two clansmen who approached to support him, then looked at Chrono and quickly summarized the situation. "We encountered an intruder. He used corruption to affect the Divine Tree. It's now extremely unstable. Where's the Great Elder? When will he be back?"

Chrono's heart sank. "He's on his way. Five minutes at most."

Five minutes.

Shawu clenched his fists and stared down the passage behind him.

He wanted desperately to rush back in, but he couldn't!

Chrono asked, "Was it the Leaf Clan?"

"Most likely." Shawu was furious enough to grind his teeth to pieces. "That eruption of corruption couldn't have anything to do with ordinary humans. It must be the Leaf Clan. Cough, cough..."

His fury aggravated his injuries. He clutched his chest and coughed.

Behind him, the Wood Clan guards were doing everything they could to purify the fog emerging from the passage and eliminate the Lickers.

Huh?

Suddenly, Shawu gave a small exclamation.

Was the fog's power weakening rapidly?

What was happening?

Shawu grew puzzled and increasingly uneasy. He looked up toward Chrono.

Their eyes briefly met.

"Mm. Let's go back inside and look."

The corruption's concentration had dropped greatly, and visibility had improved from less than half a meter to six or seven meters. Chrono immediately led an elite squad into the passage.

"The enemy is powerful. Be careful!"

A few Lickers still emerged from deeper inside, slowing their progress.

After nearly two minutes in the passage, Shawu and Chrono simultaneously sensed something, and their pupils contracted.

Their senses were being swallowed again!

The far end of the passage seemed like a void that devoured every trace of perception they extended toward it!

Not good!

Chrono's expression changed dramatically. "Quickly! RETREAT!"

The group fled back the way they'd come.

The next moment, a massive cloud of black fog swept toward them!

It was several times more concentrated than before!

The guards barely escaped the passage.

"BOOOOM!"

Highly concentrated corruption burst out of the exit!

Everyone looked grim.

The endless black fog showed no sign of stopping. It was so dense that it rapidly filled the entire rear hall!

"Fall back! Outside, now!"

"Yes!"

Only after Chrono and the others retreated outside did the fog find room to disperse, allowing its concentration to decrease gradually.

Before they could relax, a hissing sound came from the passage ahead.

Looking toward it, they saw a vast swarm of Lickers burst out!

Again?!

Chrono said grimly, "Move! Hold them back!"

The Wood Clan guards rushed forward to intercept them.

Shawu stared toward the passage's depths.

How had such concentrated black fog been produced...?

"Hiss! Hiss, hiss..."

What was that now?

Hearing the sound emerge from the fog, Shawu looked up into the air.

Bats?!

Bats were mixed into the dark mist drifting out of the passage, soaring into the sky and disappearing into the night.

Shawu frowned.

He never imagined Fang Heng himself was among those bats!

High overhead, Fang Heng flew away from the Wood Clan settlement as soon as he escaped.

Recalling what had just happened, he couldn't help feeling regret.

He'd forcibly used mental strength to pull the Seed of Jealousy's corruption back into himself for a full five minutes, holding on until Abe Akaya had drained most of the sapling's energy.

Then he simply couldn't maintain it any longer. The moment his concentration slipped, the corruption surged back like a flood through a broken dam.

The sapling, already reduced to a fraction of its strength, was instantly filled with enormous amounts of corruption.

And then...

It exploded.

Right before his eyes.

"Such a waste."

It was a pity Abe Akaya hadn't managed to drain the Wood Clan's sapling completely.

Come to think of it, how much EXP had Abe Akaya actually gained?

Only after leaving Wood Clan territory did Fang Heng have time to check his game log.

There it was!

The notifications.

[Hint: You have completely destroyed the Divine Tree sapling. Current quest The Leaf Clan's Scheme: 100% complete.]

[Hint: Your current main quest Transaction with the Leaf Clan has reached 100% completion. You may visit the Leaf Clan to claim your reward.]

[Hint: Abe Akaya's level has increased to Lv. 148.]

Not bad at all.

After that feast, Abe Akaya's level had soared to 148.

It had also absorbed more than ten million additional points of dimensional energy.

A bonus reward.

Together, the gains were worth more than half a month of relentless grinding.

And that wasn't even counting the extra Ritual Vessels and Nature's Heart Crystals he'd looted.

The one disappointment was that he hadn't found any Divine Tree branches.

Fang Heng glanced down toward the ground.

Next, it was time to have a word with that fellow Sergey.

The Leaf Clan had supposedly been joining the battle amid the confusion. Yet in the end, Fang Heng hadn't seen a single one of them.

There was no need to guess. It had been deliberate.

He suspected the Leaf Clan had never intended to come. They'd merely used him to divert the Wood Clan while they collected loot elsewhere.

They'd made a fortune from this arrangement.

Unless they gave him a satisfactory explanation—and handed over enough valuable goods—he wasn't going to let it pass.

And if the Leaf Clan refused...

...

Outside, the Federation and the Lickers continued their saturation assault on the Wood Clan settlement.

Sergey was growing uneasy.

Strange.

The corruption contained in those seeds was nowhere near sufficient to last this long.

In theory, it should have dissipated ages ago.

Why was the black mist still spreading?

What was happening to Fang Heng in there?
