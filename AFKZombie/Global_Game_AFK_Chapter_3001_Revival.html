Chapter 3001

Revival

From Chinese Chapter 3002 · 复苏

The metal door wasn't completely shut. An extremely narrow gap remained.

A stream of exceptionally pure energy seeped through the crack.

Hundreds of players had already gathered on the ice outside. They sat cross-legged, absorbing the energy escaping from within.

This power...

It felt so familiar!

Titan!

Fang Heng suddenly remembered. This familiar power belonged to the Titan bloodline!

Why was it here?

Was the treasure of the Land of Apocalypse a Titan bloodline?

He immediately closed his eyes and tried absorbing the energy flowing out of the door.

[Hint: Player is awakening bloodline.]

[Hint: Current bloodline awakening progress: 7.16%.]

[Hint: Due to bloodline awakening, player has gained 0.1 base Constitution.]

Just as he'd thought!

No wonder the spatial, elemental, and natural energies he'd absorbed earlier had seemed familiar. Like the Titan bloodline's energy, they could be stored temporarily in his Sea of Consciousness.

Having confirmed its source, Fang Heng opened his eyes and glanced at the people around him.

Their expressions were solemn too.

Luo Ao and his Central Federation team noticed the Holy Court archbishop and the Eastern Federation group arriving and immediately approached. "Archbishop Whiteman! It's wonderful that you've returned safely. What happened with the Calamity...?"

"Thank you for your concern. Everything has been settled."

Whiteman gestured in introduction. "This is Mr. Fang Bai, a Grand Sage of our Holy Court's Divine Realm. Without his intervention to drive back the fiends, we would hardly have made it here alive."

Fang Bai!

Luo Ao's pupils contracted as he looked at the young man standing behind Whiteman.

According to rumor, every Grand Sage of the Divine Realm possessed terrifying power.

It made sense that he could drive back the Calamity.

Luo Ao carefully studied Fang Heng, stole a glance at the nearby Eastern Federation group, and pondered their relationship. Then he bowed. "Grand Sage, a pleasure to meet you. I'm Luo Ao, head of the Central Federation's team for this mission. Thank you for your help."

Fang Heng looked toward the metal door. "What's happening here?"

"It's the Southern Federation."

Luo Ao drew a breath and looked at the players guarding the entrance. "We suspect the Apocalypse Treasure lies beyond the metal door. When we arrived, the Southern Federation had already occupied the entire entrance and was preventing us from reaching the hall behind it."

"They claim they discovered the treasure first and are cultivating in the hall. Supposedly, anyone else entering would interrupt their cultivation, so they refuse to let us through."

Hearing this, Chen Yu grew even angrier.

"Those bastards are rotten to the core."

If the Southern Federation hadn't tricked them, his team would have been the first to reach the treasure.

And now those people were monopolizing the treasure's energy pool and refusing to let anyone else inside?

Fang Heng curled his lip contemptuously. "What? There are so many of you, and you're afraid of a few people from the Southern Federation?"

"It's not that simple."

Luo Ao lowered his voice. "These players are all on the danger list. The Central Federation knows what they're capable of, but when we encountered them just now, we realized their strength had undergone a qualitative change."

"It's very likely that exposure to the Apocalypse Treasure altered them, causing their strength to soar in a short time."

"We also avoided fighting because escalating the conflict could destabilize the various Federations..."

Is that so...?

Now this was getting more interesting.

Fang Heng eyed the people at the entrance and interrupted with obvious interest. "If your Federations can't conveniently take action, I'll do it."

He didn't care about the Southern Federation or avoiding a direct confrontation.

They'd come all this way. Were they supposed to stand outside and eat someone else's leftovers?

What a joke!

Since when had he ever put up with that sort of insult?

With that, Fang Heng led the Holy Court group forward.

Yi Qingzhi of the Eastern Federation and Luo Ao of the Central Federation exchanged a quick glance and signaled their subordinates to follow.

The other players gathered outside the door stopped absorbing energy when they saw the movement. They watched to see whether they could join the trouble and at least get a sip of the soup.

Among the Western Federation's party, Augustine and his companions felt their hearts lurch when they spotted Fang Heng with the Eastern Federation.

Fang Heng was here too!

Everyone in the Western Federation knew how terrifying he was. He'd recently even assembled a fleet in the game. Forget the Southern Federation—even the Kinsfolk's great allied armies weren't worth his concern.

Augustine sensed that a good show was coming. He told his people to stay alert and prepare to follow Fang Heng.

"Stop!"

The Southern Federation players guarding the entrance had been on their guard from the moment they saw the group approaching. They held out their arms to block the way.

Fang Heng stared coldly at them. "What's this supposed to mean?"

"Everyone, we've already explained. Our people are inside undergoing the trial. If you wish to enter later, please wait your turn. Once our people finish, it will naturally be your turn."

"Really? And what if I think your rules are unreasonable and refuse to follow them?"

The guards saw Fang Heng and the others approaching aggressively. They knew mere words would never turn them away.

If they failed to deal with the one leading the charge, there would be even more trouble.

"You're courting death!"

One Southern Federation guard cursed and lunged at Fang Heng first!

Kill the chicken to frighten the monkeys!

They would seize the ringleader with overwhelming force and intimidate the other players.

"Whoosh!"

Lawrence stepped forward half a pace and swung his battle saber, sending out a silver arc of blade light.

Bang!

The blade light exploded in the air.

Then everyone clearly saw Lawrence himself fly backward even faster, crashing hard into the metal door behind him.

Dark-golden cracks spread over his skin like a shattered spiderweb.

Fang Heng gave him a cold glance.

A sudden surge in strength?

It seemed to be connected to that dark-golden protective layer on his skin.

But that was all.

Their underlying strength was simply too poor.

The guards accompanying Lawrence froze. After seeing Fang Heng send even Lawrence flying with one punch, none dared advance.

Lawrence's overall power had risen considerably after absorbing energy from the Key of Apocalypse.

But compared with the terrifying man before them, he was still completely outclassed.

"Go! Stop him!"

The remaining guards shouted.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Before their words had faded, several enormous dark figures burst through the gap in the metal door behind them and charged Fang Heng, heavy footsteps pounding against the floor.
