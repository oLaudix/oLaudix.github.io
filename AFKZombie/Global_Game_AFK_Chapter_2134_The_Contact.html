Chapter 2134

The Contact

From original Chinese Chapter 2135 · 接应

They took the elevator up to the fifth floor.

Next, they were supposed to wait in the fifth-floor lobby for a while.

In roughly two hours, the insider planted at Wanling Technology by the Tree Spirits would come to meet them and help carry out the theft.

The two sat down in a rest area beside the fifth-floor elevators and placed their tool bags aside.

Time to wait!

There shouldn't be any problems.

Fang Heng surveyed the surroundings from his chair. He was about to extend his senses to check the building's defenses when a clear chime sounded ahead.

Ding-dong!

At the noise, Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei both raised their heads and looked toward the front right.

The doors of an elevator leading into the laboratory section suddenly opened.

Five internal elevators faced the lobby rest area.

Only the one on the far right led up to the high-level research area.

A Wanling Technology researcher in a laboratory uniform stepped out.

Seeing Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei waiting near the elevators, he walked straight over and nodded. “Are you the pipe maintenance workers? I've been waiting for you. Come with me.”

Huh?

Mo Jiawei was startled, but immediately stood up.

Already?

They'd agreed to begin at seven in the evening, when the staff would be taking their dinner break and changing shifts. There would be fewer people around, making it easier to avoid detection.

Why had the operation suddenly moved forward by two hours?

Good thing they'd come early. Otherwise, they might have missed their chance!

Fang Heng was equally surprised, but stood up as well.

“Keep up.”

The researcher hurried them into the elevator on the far right, verified his identity with a facial scan, and continued, “Remember, we're entering the laboratory section. Security and confidentiality are very strict. Just do your work. Whatever you see or hear inside, forget it completely once you leave. Understood?”

Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei exchanged another look and nodded.

Something felt a little off.

Ding-dong.

The elevator doors opened again.

Two armed Wanling Technology guards stood outside.

“These two are here to repair the leaking pipes in the storeroom. Go with them.”

After speaking, the researcher led the group down the corridor.

They passed through several winding passages before the researcher stopped outside a storeroom.

“This is it. See? Water's even seeping through the floor slab up there. Fortunately, we're not storing anything particularly valuable here. Otherwise, who'd pay for the damage?”

He pointed toward a corner of the storeroom, frowning with obvious dissatisfaction. He gestured at the ceiling and continued, “I've called you people to fix this several times already. Why hasn't it been repaired? What's the problem? Are you actually capable of doing your jobs?”

Mo Jiawei gave an apologetic laugh. “Please don't be angry, sir. The people who came last time weren't professionals. Leave it to us. We'll get it fixed properly this time.”

“How long will it take? Give me a time.”

“That's hard to say. As you mentioned, it's been a problem for a while. We'll need to inspect everything thoroughly, identify the exact cause, and make sure we fix it for good.”

The researcher was clearly growing impatient. He waved a hand. “Fine. Get to work, then. This is a restricted research area, so don't wander around. If there's a problem, call the guards outside. If you cause trouble here, your lives won't be enough to pay for it.”

“Of course, of course. We'll start right away.”

“Honestly, it still isn't fixed... I don't know what they're doing...”

Muttering under his breath, the researcher shut the storeroom door and left.

Fang Heng could still hear him giving orders to the guards outside.

“Stay here and watch them. Don't let them wander off.”

Fang Heng turned toward Mo Jiawei again.

What was going on?

Why weren't things following the plan?

Wasn't this man supposed to take them to find the drug?

Not only had he arrived two hours early, he'd abandoned them here!

And then walked off by himself?

Had he gone to fetch the Water of Life?

Mo Jiawei was equally baffled. He looked at Fang Heng and whispered, “Should we just wait a while?”

“Mm. No rush. Let's wait. I'll check up there first.”

Fang Heng nodded and looked at the opening in the floor slab overhead.

With a light leap, he climbed into the space above the broken ceiling.

Mo Jiawei looked up through the hole and whispered, “Boss? What's it like up there?”

Fang Heng had studied Mechanical Proficiency and Maintenance Proficiency. A few glances were enough to identify the issue.

“Looks like a ventilation system. Some parts are damaged. Farther inside, I can see what looks like the ventilation system's main electrical circuit.”

Mo Jiawei frowned.

“Old Fang, do you think that guy deliberately left us here so we could follow the ventilation ducts and find the Water of Life ourselves?”

Fang Heng considered it.

The man really had behaved strangely.

“No. It won't work.”

He examined the space more carefully.

“The passage has been sealed off. The only openings are tiny air vents—no person could fit through. I suspect the ducts are linked to the security network. Breaking through by force would make a lot of noise and might trigger an alarm.”

Fang Heng jumped back down.

“Then what do we do?” Mo Jiawei thought for a moment. “Something still doesn't feel right. Wasn't that guy's acting a little too convincing? And he dumped us here without telling us anything. What if he mistook us for someone else and genuinely thinks we're maintenance workers?”

“Mm. Now that you mention it, it does seem likely.” Fang Heng made a small sound of agreement and looked toward the door. “In that case, let's take the initiative and have a look outside.”

Mo Jiawei blinked. “You're going that far?”

“If the Tree Spirits' actual contact arrives and can't find us, that could ruin the operation. Forget waiting. We'll make our move.”

“Okay, but we don't even know where the Water of Life is being kept, do we?”

“That's fine. We'll just find someone and ask.”

Fang Heng formed a hand seal in front of his chest.

A gray magic array rapidly materialized before him.

Whoosh!

A small shadow sprang out of the array and scrambled up Fang Heng's shoulder, nuzzling affectionately against his neck.

The Shadow Demon Rat!

Seeing it, Mo Jiawei hurriedly popped a small pill into his mouth.

It was a special pill produced by the prison research institute that granted resistance to the Shadow Demon Rat's corruption attacks for about an hour. Together with the purifying effect of holy power, it ensured he wouldn't be affected by the corruption.

Fang Heng stroked the rat's head. “I'm counting on you.”

The Shadow Demon Rat nodded, drew a deep breath, and puffed out its cheeks. Then it exhaled a mouthful of dense fog.

Whooooosh!

Thick black mist immediately filled the room!

A great mass of the mist spread outward through the gap beneath the door.
