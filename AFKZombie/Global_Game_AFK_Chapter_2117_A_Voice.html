Chapter 2117

A Voice

From original Chinese Chapter 2118 · 话语权

Fang Heng didn't refuse outright. He looked up and asked, “Is that so? Then I'll need to think carefully about it. Tell me, what sort of cooperation do you have in mind?”

“Working together to find this so-called Ancient Divine Tree, of course. There are plenty of methods. For example, we could locate the Tree Spirits and obtain information from them. Or perhaps...”

Ji Haiting paused and gestured behind him. “Perhaps we could seek clues elsewhere.”

Fang Heng looked at him. “Is it really that simple?”

“Very well. I'll speak frankly. We've recovered fragments of information from ancient texts. The Tree Spirits were once the rulers of this world and possessed immense power. Yet despite their strength, they vanished completely during some mysterious crisis.”

“To be candid, we do have selfish motives. We covet that power. Wanling Technology wants to master it and put it to our own use.”

Ji Haiting continued, “But our greater concern is preparing for danger. Remember, the Tree Spirits were extraordinarily powerful, yet some outside force still caused their sudden disappearance.”

“That troubles me deeply. I worry that history could repeat itself—and that next time, humanity might be the race facing extinction. Seeking the Tree Spirits' power is also a way to prepare for such a crisis in advance. We're doing it for the survival and future of our species.”

Fang Heng pursed his lips. “That sounds rather noble. But ultimately, you're still after power, aren't you?”

“Hahaha! Quite right. Aren't you? Only those with power have a voice. That principle has never changed. We can't place all our hopes in someone else's mercy.”

Ji Haiting laughed heartily. “If you're willing to cooperate, we'll share the Tree Spirits' power with you once we obtain it. What do you say?”

“I think I'll pass. I prefer working independently, especially since I know so little about you.”

Ji Haiting frowned and shook his head. “I'm sorry to hear that. Still, I'm willing to give you some time. If you ever change your mind...”

From the cliff above, Glenan couldn't bear listening any longer and shouted down, “Get lost, you bastards! Stop spouting nonsense! All you want is to steal the Tree Spirits' treasures! You're after the Water of Life! I won't let you succeed!”

Ji Haiting cast Glenan a cold look.

“By the way, could I examine that? It's my first visit, and I haven't had the chance to see it up close.”

As though unconcerned by Glenan's outburst, Fang Heng turned to Ji Haiting and pointed toward the nearby withered tree.

“Certainly. Be my guest.”

Ji Haiting stepped aside and explained, “We've fitted the ruins with activation devices, partly for experimentation and partly to monitor the tree's condition. We've been trying to revive it, but progress has been limited. Frankly, of all the ruins we've discovered, this is the best-preserved dead tree.”

Fang Heng and Klevi walked past Wanling Technology's researchers and approached the tree.

Ji Haiting followed them.

Under everyone's gaze, Fang Heng slowly placed his hand against the ancient tree.

He couldn't sense the slightest fluctuation of natural energy.

The tree had completely lost its vitality.

To put it another way, it was dead.

Fang Heng turned toward Klevi.

She slowly reached out, rested her hand against the trunk, and closed her eyes.

She remained in that position for a full minute.

Just as the guards were starting to grow impatient, an excited voice suddenly rang out behind them.

“Director Ji! The dead tree's life readings are showing abnormal fluctuations!”

What?!

Ji Haiting's heart lurched. He turned and stared at Klevi.

Under her touch, a faint green glow had actually begun appearing across the once completely lifeless trunk.

What sort of power was that?!

Astonishment flashed in Ji Haiting's eyes.

Everyone remained silent, waiting for Klevi.

Another two full minutes passed.

At last, Klevi withdrew her hand and exhaled. She looked somewhat exhausted.

Ji Haiting couldn't restrain himself. “May I ask what you discovered?”

Klevi shook her head and retreated half a step behind Fang Heng.

Fang Heng said, “It seems we haven't found anything particularly special. Thank you, but I think it's time for us to leave.”

Ji Haiting stood directly in their path, watching them without moving.

“What? You're not going to let us go?”

“I don't know what the Tree Spirits may have told you, but Wanling Technology has always obeyed the law. Everything we do is for humanity's continued survival. I hope you'll reconsider my proposal.”

“There's nothing to consider. I don't like being forced into things.”

“Then I'm sorry. Under company regulations, I cannot simply allow you to leave. I genuinely wanted us to work together. Unfortunately...”

Ji Haiting sighed in disappointment, slowly turned, and waved for his subordinates to take Fang Heng and Klevi away.

The dozen or so guards who had accompanied him stared at Fang Heng's group, their expressions grave.

“Brother Fang Heng, I...”

Klevi was about to say something when her vision suddenly blurred. Her body went limp, and she collapsed toward Fang Heng.

At the same time, stabbing pain shot through Fang Heng's mind, and game notifications appeared across his retina.

[Game Hint: You have been struck by an unknown mental-piercing attack.]

[Game Hint: Your Mental Strength has passed the check. Stun resisted. You have sustained 234 points of mental damage.]

[Game Hint: You have been struck by an unknown mental-piercing attack...]

[Game Hint: Your Mental Strength has passed the check...]

Huh?

Fang Heng's heart stirred. He immediately caught the unconscious Klevi and looked toward the guards with a deep frown.

A special mental attack?

He'd seen something similar from Shalin before!

These people were all psychic ability users!

“Crack... crack, crack...”

Cracking sounds spread through the surrounding space.

Fang Heng immediately sensed an invisible barrier field rapidly forming around him.

A mental barrier!

He'd miscalculated!

His vampire bloodline could indeed sense blood energy.

But psychic abilities belonged to an entirely different energy system.

To his vampiric senses, these psychics had no more blood energy than ordinary humans!

— End of Chapter 2117 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Power system. The guards’ diamond-patterned suits and attacks are connected to psychic abilities, which Fang Heng’s blood-energy senses cannot identify.
  • Game damage. Fang Heng resists the stun but suffers 234 mental damage from one piercing attack.

Chinese Expressions

  • 话语权 (huàyǔquán). Literally “the right to speak” or “say in decisions”; Ji Haiting argues that only power grants you a voice.
