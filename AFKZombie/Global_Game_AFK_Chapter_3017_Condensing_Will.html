Chapter 3017

Condensing Will

From Chinese Chapter 3018 · 凝聚意志

The Divine Realm.

After the Calamity crisis ended, the revived God King hastily issued several orders before entering seclusion once again.

Rumors claimed that years of fighting the Calamity had brought him new insight and that he needed secluded cultivation to achieve a breakthrough.

The truth was quite different.

During the battle against the Calamity, the Holy Court's God-Bestowed Jewel had shifted position because of Fang Heng.

And with its outer protective mesh removed, the jewel's power was rapidly leaking away.

The God King had no choice but to cultivate using that power, consuming it to prevent it from spreading too widely.

Meanwhile, reconstruction work throughout the Divine Realm began in earnest.

After the destruction wrought by the Demon Seeds and the Calamity, almost everything needed rebuilding.

Following discussions with the Divine Realm's Grand Sages, the God King decided to focus on reconstruction for the time being. The Holy Court would stop making enemies everywhere as it had before.

"Report!"

A Divine Realm guard hurried into the Pantheon and bowed respectfully. "Grand Sage!"

Grand Sage Alairdin slowly opened his eyes. "What's happened? Why are you so agitated?"

"Grand Sage, Captain Hain of the Divine Punishment Knights left a message. He received word from Grand Sage Fang Bai and immediately led his knights to the lower worlds to meet him."

"What? Fang Bai?"

Alairdin was surprised.

Fang Bai's background was certainly peculiar. He'd left the Divine Realm with an enormous mess to clean up, but he'd also helped greatly in the battle against the Calamity. The Divine Realm hadn't seriously questioned his identity.

Since Fang Heng disappeared, they'd merely sent people to look for news of him.

As a Grand Sage, Fang Heng couldn't be ordered around by anyone except the God King.

Even the other three Grand Sages were his equals.

If they found him, they could at most pass along a few messages. There was no possibility of forcing Fang Bai to return.

Besides, the Grand Sages assumed he had good reasons for visiting the lower worlds, so unnecessary interference would be unwise.

And with no word of Fang Bai for so long, most had nearly forgotten the matter.

Grand Sage Dean, sitting nearby, asked, "So the Divine Punishment Knights have news of him? Where is he?"

"The Eternal Holy City."

The guard nodded respectfully. "We only just learned that Grand Sage Fang Bai arrived in the Eternal Holy City last night. He immediately began recruiting Holy Court crusaders and elites from multiple worlds, including but not limited to paladin teams, priest teams, archbishops, adjudicators, and guard units."

"The summoned teams are assembling now and rapidly gathering in the Eternal Holy City."

What was going on?

Such a huge mobilization? Why did he need so many people?

Dean and the other Grand Sages were stunned.

They'd only recently agreed with the God King that the Holy Court should lie low for a while. How much time had passed before Fang Bai began mobilizing everyone?

"What does he intend to do?"

"Reportedly, he's preparing to enter Hell through the Eternal Holy City's trial passage."

The three Grand Sages exchanged looks.

This newly appointed Fang Bai was usually impossible to find. Now he'd suddenly appeared and picked a fight with Hell?

True, the Holy Court had always opposed Hell and considered it a mortal threat. But why would he launch an unprovoked assault at a time like this?

The main issue was the cost.

Hell had been spreading slowly for ages. Pouring the Holy Court's precious fighting strength into it would hardly be worthwhile.

Thus, unless Hell expanded drastically, the Holy Court generally avoided pushing deep into it. At most, it sent small teams for training.

Alairdin frowned. "Did Grand Sage Fang Bai mention a specific objective in Hell?"

"Uh... about that..."

The guard lowered his head, cautiously glanced at the three Grand Sages, and murmured, "He said he intends to avenge the Holy Court members who died fighting Hell. Apparently, he plans to wash Hell in blood."

"Huh?"

All three Grand Sages were stunned, then broke into bitter smiles.

This newly appointed Grand Sage certainly had a wild imagination and an unconventional disposition.

Wash Hell in blood? That didn't sound like something a Holy Court Grand Sage should be saying at all.

"Very well. You may withdraw."

Alairdin dismissed the guard, turned to the other two, and asked, "What do you think?"

For a moment, nobody could understand what Fang Heng was trying to accomplish.

"Fang Bai has always been eccentric, but he is one of our Grand Sages. I doubt he'd deliberately harm the Holy Court. Still, invading Hell is extremely dangerous. Perhaps we should send someone to support him and learn the circumstances before deciding anything."

The three exchanged another look.

"Grand Sage Fang Bai's strength is sufficient for most problems. Sending ordinary troops probably won't persuade him to change course. One of us may have to go personally."

"I'll go."

Grand Sage Dean rose from his magic array. "I've been cooped up here too long. I wouldn't mind stretching my limbs. And I rather like that kid's personality. I'll pay him a visit myself."

"Very well. Take plenty of people with you, Grand Sage Dean, and please be careful. Oh, and one more thing..."

Lucius paused, considered something, and looked around. "Do you think Fang Bai might be trying to condense the Will of the Twelve Sages?"

At those words, the three Grand Sages fell silent.

Dean, already about to leave, stopped and turned back.

The Will of the Twelve Sages!

Well, now that was a bold idea!

Condensing the Will of the Twelve Sages was extraordinarily difficult. All of them had attempted it, and every attempt had failed.

And how long had Fang Bai been a Grand Sage?

He was daring enough to attempt it already?

But then again...

Thinking it over, why else would the kid suddenly go mad and charge into Hell?

And Hell really was an excellent place to temper and condense one's will!

...

Hell, sixth layer.

As the Holy Court's spatial passage opened, crusaders, reserve crusaders, and priest and paladin teams from the lower worlds assembled and entered Hell one after another.

Burning sacred light drove back the oppressive heat hanging over Hell.

Fang Heng had already wiped out the first wave of hellish creatures. Under the intense waves of sacred power that followed, no monster in the vicinity was foolish enough to approach.

Hain soon arrived with the Divine Punishment Knights.
