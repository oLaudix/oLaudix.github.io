Chapter 2202

Extraction

“That's right.”

Fang Heng nodded. Grand Elder Senzo had mentioned it to him only recently.

That explained why the Tree Spirits were now so deeply divided.

“Exactly. Which is why this thing is so important to them.”

“Huh? Why?” Fang Heng was even more confused. “What does the crystal have to do with choosing their next leader?”

“What? You don't know?”

Sandy scratched his head at the question.

“All right, let's start from the beginning. According to what I found in these books…”

Sandy patiently spent some time explaining the Tree Spirits' rules for selecting their next leadership.

By the time he finished, Fang Heng felt as though a veil had been lifted from his eyes.

The Tree Spirits.

What an interesting race!

So this was what had been going on. Elder Senzo had been keeping crucial information from him all along.

The next Tree Spirit succession wasn't determined by an outright battle, as Fang Heng had imagined, but through another method—one that appeared more peaceful, at least on the surface.

Put simply, each clan would cultivate its own Divine Tree under the Elder Council's supervision. After a hundred years, the clan that had raised the strongest successor Divine Tree would win.

The winning clan's tree would absorb nutrients from the trees cultivated by the other clans, as well as the dying Divine Tree of the previous generation, giving it the best possible foundation for growth.

That tree would then become the new Divine Tree of the entire Tree Spirit race.

So the clans now rushing into the Elder Council, even fighting one another, were really competing for resources with which to raise their own trees.

But after the tremendous upheaval among the Tree Spirits, the Elder Council had vanished entirely. No one remained to distribute its stockpiled resources.

Whatever a clan could seize would belong to it.

And the Nature's Heart Crystals in the boxes weren't merely sources of energy. They were special conversion devices.

With the assistance of a ritual magic array, they could transform other kinds of energy into natural energy that a Divine Tree could absorb.

Fang Heng finally understood. He weighed the crystal in his hand again.

Good stuff!

No wonder the Tree Spirits were willing to offer Master-level Nature Studies in exchange for it.

But wouldn't turning over these crystals as quest items to the Leaf Clan be selling himself short?

With gems like these, he could demand a much higher price!

Why not try selling them to the other Tree Spirit clans?

Maybe hold an auction?

Sell to the highest bidder?

Surely he ought to squeeze every possible benefit out of this!

“Nice work, Sandy.” Fang Heng nodded approvingly as he considered his options. “All right, I understand. I've still got things to do here. Once I've brought the crystals back, we can discuss the rest…”

“Wait!”

Hearing Fang Heng about to end the call, Sandy hurriedly stopped him, shouting excitedly into the projection. “Hang on, you idiot! Mo could've passed on all that information for me. Why do you think I insisted on talking to you myself? Ever heard of throwing away a pearl to keep the box? Losing the big prize over a tiny profit? Getting your priorities backward? We're talking about something much more important!”

“Huh?” Fang Heng stared at him. “What are you talking about?”

“Just wait. I need to confirm one more thing.” Sandy took a deep breath and tried to calm himself. “Turn the projection around. I need to examine the Divine Tree's true body.”

The Divine Tree's true body?

Fang Heng glanced toward the completely withered tree behind the Divine Wood Altar, then turned the token in his hand to show it through the remote projection.

Sandy leaned closer, studying the tree with an unprecedentedly solemn expression.

“Fang Heng, I read something in the records about Divine Trees that have died.”

As he examined the tree, Sandy explained, “According to the books, before a Divine Tree reaches the end of its life, it gathers its remaining power within itself and passes that power on to the next generation's Divine Tree. In the past, this procedure was performed under the Elder Council's direction.”

“I've just checked, and I'm fairly sure that's the original Divine Tree. What I can't tell is whether its final remaining life essence has already been extracted.”

Fang Heng's pupils contracted. He immediately understood what Sandy meant.

“You're saying the power of this withered Divine Tree might still be inside it?”

Sandy nodded emphatically.

“There's a very good chance.”

Of course!

The realization struck Fang Heng too.

The Inner World passage had disappeared abruptly, the old Divine Tree had died suddenly, and all the remaining seeds had developed abnormalities, making it impossible to cultivate a successor.

There was every possibility that the old tree's energy extraction had never been completed!

The Nature's Heart Crystals in the boxes weren't energy extracted from the Divine Tree, either.

Sandy was absolutely right.

If Fang Heng simply took the crystals and left now, he'd be walking out of a treasure vault empty-handed.

There was still one question, though.

If something had happened to the Divine Tree's seeds, why were all four clans fighting so desperately over the energy crystals needed to cultivate them?

Without viable seeds, what could they possibly grow?

Never mind that for now. Fang Heng suppressed his doubts and asked, “Sandy, how do I extract the Divine Tree's remaining seed energy?”

“Wait there. I'll be right back.”

Sandy disappeared from the projection. Five minutes later he hurried back, waving a battered notebook and holding one of its pages up for Fang Heng to see.

A fairly large magic array was drawn on the page.

“This is the one! It's recorded as a way to draw out and condense the remaining life-energy essence of a dead Divine Tree.”

Fang Heng looked up at the diagram. A rapidly spinning phantom magic array appeared in his right pupil.

[Hint: The player is attempting to replicate the magic array using the Omniscient Eye…]

[Hint: Target magic array rank: Intermediate.]

[Hint: Success rate: 100%…]

After a full minute of progress, the array in his pupil gradually faded.

[Hint: Magic array replication complete.]

“That'll do.”

Fang Heng turned his gaze back to the withered Divine Tree.

He didn't know for certain whether any energy still remained inside it.

That was fine.

He'd simply give it a try.

If there was nothing left, so be it.

But if there was…

Fang Heng lightly licked his lips.

That might be the most valuable treasure in the Tree Spirits' sacred Valley of Annual Rings!

The connection had been running for too long. The remote projection was becoming unstable, and Klevi's face was pale, beads of sweat gathering on her forehead.

Setting up the array would take some time anyway, so Fang Heng ended the communication for the moment.

“Hey, you three.”

Fang Heng turned to Anna and the others.
