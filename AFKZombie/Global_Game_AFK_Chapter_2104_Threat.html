Chapter 2104

Threat

From original Chinese Chapter 2105 · 威胁

In the past, such a thing would have been unimaginable!

The sudden increase in the Lickers' strength left the entire Paladin force struggling to adapt. They could only defend themselves, and even that was becoming difficult. The Holy Court's lines slowly began falling back!

This was bad!

At the rear, Legion Commander Georgi watched with a deep frown and muttered under his breath.

“Fang Heng!”

He had to admit that he now feared Fang Heng more than ever!

When he'd first heard of Fang Heng, he hadn't thought much of him.

But every time they met, Fang Heng's abilities had improved by leaps and bounds.

That frightening rate of growth filled Georgi with dread.

Now he didn't even know where to begin dealing with these Lickers.

Since when had the undead become this terrifying?

A dreadful thought even occurred to him.

If Fang Heng was given just a little more time...

He might even become a threat to the Divine Realm!

Most of the Holy Court's forces were concentrated at the front. Practically all its remaining combat strength was gathered here.

Behind them lay the last main city held by the Holy Court and the Brighton Empire.

Its defenses were extremely thin.

They absolutely could not allow the enemy to break through this line!

Georgi raised his knight's longsword high. “Follow me! Advance and reinforce the line! We must stop them!”

“Understood!”

The Holy Court's elite knights and priests immediately rearranged their formations.

At the rear, the sacred priests cast powerful blessings. Up front, the elite Paladins accelerated into a charge toward the Lickers!

“Charge!”

Warhorses neighed!

“Forward!”

As the Paladins charged, sacred radiance enveloped the entire formation, turning it into a sharp arrow aimed straight at the Lickers!

Boom!

A lance plunged through a Licker, piercing its entire body!

The massive critical damage from the charge, combined with the sacred blessings of the elite priests, produced more than ten times the normal damage!

Lickers shattered one after another under the penetrating holy light!

After charging through the horde, Georgi and his troops paused briefly to catch their breath.

What happened next, however, made the Paladins' expressions change.

Where the Lickers had fallen, each corpse had left a pool of thick black substance.

The strange material was slowly flowing and drawing itself together.

Within moments, Lickers re-formed from the unknown black mass!

They had revived!

The resurrected Lickers immediately charged the Paladins again, as energetic as ever!

What was going on?!

Why wouldn't they die?!

Even the elite Paladins, whose willpower was exceptionally high, struggled to accept what they were seeing.

These Lickers simply couldn't be killed!

The Holy Court's players noticed the terrifying phenomenon too.

“Mutations! They're mutated Lickers!”

Soon a player was shouting a warning.

Some of the Lickers had undergone special modifications. Coated with a layer of black matter, they had stronger attack and defense than ordinary Lickers!

Even after being killed, they could resurrect on the spot!

Encountering Primal Devourer-modified Lickers for the first time, the Holy Court had no idea how to respond.

Its morale immediately collapsed.

The players lost all desire to fight.

Normally you exchanged blows, one strike for another. Fair and square. But now the enemy had become something that couldn't even be killed...?

What was the point of fighting anymore?!

Better run!

The player teams were the first to retreat!

Across the swamp, the undead players had no idea what was happening at the front. They could only see the Holy Court's forces apparently falling into chaos.

Was it because of those catapults?

At the rear camp, Danni and the others watched the battle through a vision spell and fell silent.

Who could have imagined it?

In just two short days, the Lickers' combat strength had increased enormously again.

World Lord Fang Heng had practically suppressed the Holy Court through his own power alone.

Fortunately, Fang Heng was on the undead faction's side.

Everyone couldn't help wondering whether they could stop such Lickers if they were the enemy.

Their own situation probably wouldn't be much better than the Holy Court's.

“It's time.”

Lord Danni rose and turned to the player commanders of the undead legions. “The Holy Court is already in chaos. I imagine they'll soon discover how to counter the modified Lickers. We should launch the general offensive while we can.”

“Yes.”

They nodded together and issued orders for every unit to attack.

On hearing the order, the undead players immediately began laying specially made wooden planks across the marsh and advancing toward the far side.

Seeing that the time had come, Fang Heng also sprang into action. He transformed into a bat, flew across the swamp, and landed in a large tree to observe the battle below.

The Primal Devourer-modified Lickers were exceptionally effective!

Unfortunately, each modification consumed a great many Main God Points, and he had only managed to modify 120 of them.

Yet those 120 alone had played an enormous role. They tied down the Holy Court's elite troops, shattered the players' morale, and bought the ordinary Lickers plenty of time!

Before long, however, the Holy Court recognized the Primal Devourer form's weakness.

“Purification! There's something wrong with those things! Purify them at once!”

a high priest shouted.

Only then did everyone understand.

After a modified Licker was killed, it left a patch of viscous black material on the ground. If the priests purified that residue with sacred magic, the Licker could no longer revive so easily.

But doing so was not simple.

An ordinary purification spell only worked temporarily, delaying its revival. To purify the black mass completely, they needed to cast high-tier purification repeatedly.

“What a pity...”

Fang Heng sighed inwardly.

There were still too few Primal Devourer-modified Lickers, making them easy to counter.

Once he gathered enough Main God Points and their numbers rose into the thousands, the Holy Court wouldn't even have time to cast purification spells!

It was time to move.

He had to stop them from purifying the Lickers!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes, leapt down from the dead tree, and entered a secondary-space projection, racing toward the priests on the Holy Court's right flank!

“Who's there?!”

The sacred priests on that flank were concentrating on purifying the black residue left behind by the Primal Devourer-modified Lickers. Suddenly sensing a spatial fluctuation nearby, they jumped in alarm.

The next instant, a figure stepped out of the faintly shimmering space.

“Whoosh!”

Fang Heng!!

The elite priests recognized him, and their faces changed at once.

Boom!

— End of Chapter 2104 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Modification count. Fang Heng has only 120 Primal Devourer-modified Lickers, because each conversion consumes considerable Main God Points.
  • Countermeasure. Repeated high-tier purification can destroy the black residue, preventing resurrection; ordinary purification merely delays it.

Chinese Expressions

  • 这还打个屁 (zhè hái dǎ ge pì). Literally “what the hell are we even fighting for?” The players lose their will to fight creatures that keep resurrecting.
