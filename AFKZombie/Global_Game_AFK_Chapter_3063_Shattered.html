Chapter 3063

Shattered

From Chinese Chapter 3064 · 破碎

“Pfft!”

The Shadow Clan commander was knocked out of shadow form at once. Blood sprayed from his mouth, and the entire right half of his body was smashed apart, leaving a bloody ruin.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

The next instant, the twenty-four Nether Orbs swirling around Fang Heng flew outward, hurling away the remaining Shadow Clan warriors rushing to the commander's rescue!

Hmph!

Having landed his blow, Fang Heng swept an icy gaze over the commander as though looking at a corpse, then vanished again.

“My lord!”

The Shadow Clan guards immediately crowded around to support their commander.

Most of the right side of his body had been destroyed, and a black shadow was spreading across the mangled, bloody wound.

That shadow swiftly crept toward the left half of his body!

Corrosion, aging, plague, and other negative effects compounded one another. The commander's face turned deathly pale as his life force drained rapidly away.

Even more frightening, the blood flowing from his body had already turned black. Influenced by some mysterious force, it gathered around his neck and tightened like a noose.

Before the Shadow Clan members could call over any allied healers, their commander had stopped breathing.

The surrounding allied species fell utterly silent; the only sounds were sharp intakes of breath.

Who had attacked them just now?

Was it the Ouroboros Seed?

It was horrifying. From beginning to end, they had barely glimpsed a human-shaped silhouette.

Even a Shadow Clan commander of such power had been killed by a single attack from that person.

Korkalot was even more shaken.

Only he had seen clearly.

That person was Fang Heng!

That brat had effortlessly killed the heavily protected Shadow Clan commander in the middle of so many guards!

When Fang Heng sparred with them earlier, he hadn't shown even a tenth of his strength!

A belated fear crept over Korkalot.

Perhaps Fang Heng had been right all along.

This was humanity giving the elves a chance. If the elves had followed the Shadow Clan and joined the assault on humanity, their fate probably wouldn't have been any better.

With the commander dead, the entire allied force lost its direction.

Nels, the vice-captain of the Shadow Clan flagship, gritted his teeth and stepped forward. “The captain has fallen. I will now take command of the whole force. All allies, heed my orders...”

Whoosh!

Before Nels could finish, the mysterious figure appeared before him again, descending like the God of Death.

Not good!

Nels was terrified and instinctively turned his body into shadow.

“BOOM!”

Under Fang Heng's punch, the shadowy figure boiled and steamed violently once more.

“BANG!”

After the fist passed through him, Nels shattered completely without even returning to his normal form!

The mysterious attacker had already vanished again.

An atmosphere of unspeakable terror descended on everyone present.

No one dared say another word.

They all understood: whoever stepped forward as the next commander would become that mysterious person's next target.

But without anyone directing such an enormous coalition, the force would lose its backbone and be gradually eaten away by the insects and vines.

No—it wouldn't even take a gradual assault. Their army would collapse very soon!

They were helpless!

Everyone felt trapped in a dead end.

The only thing they could do was...

Korkalot edged backward and said, “Everyone, forgive me. The Elven Clan has other business to attend to, so I'll be leaving first.”

Before anyone could answer, Korkalot hurriedly retreated.

The others silently cursed the shameless elves but nodded along. “Shadow Clan, our apologies. As it happens, we have business to attend to as well...”

And just like that, the species making up the alliance scattered in all directions, fleeing for their lives.

Their army was enormous, and its battle line stretched over a vast distance.

The allied forces at the front were still fighting desperately, completely unaware that their leaders at the rear were already breaking ranks and fleeing.

By the time the frontline troops realized what was happening, the chaos had spread throughout the entire army.

Abe Akaya's vines and the insect swarm seized the chance to surge forward and butcher the retreating troops.

Fang Heng remained in his spirit form, hiding among the crowd. From time to time, he materialized to kill isolated Shadow Clan experts.

The Shadow Clan was troublesome.

So he needed to deal with them specifically.

He could leave the rest to Abe Akaya and his zombie clones.

As chaos swept through the whole force, the alliance's collapse came in an instant.

Each squad fled in panic, terrified of being the last to escape. Those left behind were utterly unable to resist as Lickers and Abe Akaya's vines swept over them mercilessly.

Half an hour later, the battlefield finally fell silent again.

Korkalot approached Fang Heng, his body trembling slightly against his will.

The man before him...

He was terrifying.

Fang Heng said calmly, “Your Highness, nicely done. Congratulations to us on crushing the Shadow Clan coalition in one stroke.”

“Ah, yes... It's all thanks to you.”

Korkalot was normally proud, but Fang Heng's smile now seemed frightening beyond words. He returned a smile uglier than a grimace.

Fang Heng was in an excellent mood.

Some of the Kinsfolk Alliance forces had escaped, but that wasn't much of a problem.

Every ship was under his control. They couldn't leave the planet. A pack of Lickers could chase them down and clean up the rest.

Besides, this operation had netted him a huge number of ships.

Abe Akaya had tried to preserve the ships' interiors during the assault. After some repairs, he could either sell the ships or refit them for his own use.

Kappa quickly completed an inventory of the ships' supplies and approached Fang Heng. “Boss Fang, we've checked everything. The Crocodile Clan still has a huge shipment of ore it didn't manage to transport out. This world also has excellent mineral reserves. Should we...?”

“Of course!”

Now that the trouble with the Envy Demon Seed and the Shadow Clan had finally died down, he could happily get back to grinding.

Fang Heng nodded lightly.

He'd start by farming the minerals in this world!

With the threat hanging over his head temporarily lifted, he finally had time to use Abe Akaya's power to raise his academic skill levels and consolidate his gains for a while.

Kappa immediately brightened when he heard Fang Heng agree.

Exactly!

What was so interesting about fighting and killing? Mining and making money—that was the real business!

“Got it! I'll notify the fleet right away...”

...

News of the Shadow Clan force's defeat soon reached the Human Federation.

Humanity was still completely dumbfounded.

At first, they suspected the reports might be mistaken.

Both the Eastern and Western Federation had witnessed the strength of the Shadow Clan alliance firsthand. Those monsters could even eliminate SSS-tier insect nests.

Fang Heng?

Could he really handle them on his own?

Only after verifying the news repeatedly did the Federation dare to consider it genuine. Even then, they maintained comprehensive battle preparations until three days had passed.

Everything remained calm, as though nothing had happened at all.

Had it really been resolved?

It wasn't that they refused to believe it. It was simply too hard to believe.

Once they confirmed that the human world remained peaceful and had suffered no crisis, they were forced to acknowledge the result.

There was no further news from the Kinsfolk either. They appeared to have fallen silent and abandoned their plans to attack humanity.

Nevertheless, the Human Federation did not let down its guard.

The Flying Squirrel Clan noticed that the hostile missions the Kinsfolk had posted against humanity still remained on their mission system. No one had accepted them for the time being, that was all.
