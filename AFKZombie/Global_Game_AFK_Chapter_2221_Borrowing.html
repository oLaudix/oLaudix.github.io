Chapter 2221

Borrowing

From original Chinese Chapter 2222 · 借用

Fang Heng turned to the laboratory supervisor. “If possible, Wanling Technology would like to borrow one of the largest Dark Radiance Stones for further research. It'll help keep the experiments moving.”

“Well…”

The supervisor clearly lacked the authority to decide something like this. He looked toward Kleiman.

“Of course. You can take it today. We can complete the paperwork afterward.”

The way Kleiman looked at Fang Heng had already changed completely.

Under ordinary circumstances, lending Wanling Technology a Dark Radiance Stone for research wouldn't have been out of the question.

But the required approvals would have taken at least half a month.

Now, however…

Extraordinary circumstances called for extraordinary measures.

Unlocking the potential of Psychic Studies and strengthening the entire army…

The sooner, the better!

Kleiman was more than willing to give Wanling Technology preferential treatment. He waved an aide over to arrange the necessary coordination and paperwork.

“Thank you.” Fang Heng looked at Cheng Fan. “Let me know the moment it arrives at my laboratory.”

“Of course, Dr. Fang. I'll escort you back first.”

Everyone watched Fang Heng and his party leave.

Only Federation personnel remained in the underground storage hall.

The laboratory supervisor glanced at the two subjects still undergoing psychic tempering, then cautiously asked Kleiman, “Sir, should we wake them?”

“No. Let them continue.”

Kleiman turned to one of his researchers. “Why can they maintain psychic tempering for so long?”

“According to Dr. Fang's theory, the subjects' physical strength somehow increased dramatically in a very short period. That allowed them to push past their previous limits and withstand the temporary negative effects of psychic tempering.”

“Are you saying they're completely immune to those negative effects?”

“Yes. Our calculations suggest a person needs at least three times the constitution of an ordinary human to become entirely immune to the short-term effects at this stage.”

Kleiman stared. “You mean their bodies became three times stronger? Just now?”

“Yes.” The research team leader nodded, watching the two subjects meditating before the stone. “It's difficult to believe, but the data doesn't lie. I'll arrange physical examinations as soon as they're finished, and we'll submit the detailed results afterward.”

Kleiman was shaken. Then he asked the question that mattered most.

“How did he do it?”

How had Fang Heng improved someone's physique so enormously in mere moments?

With the Federation's current technology, the only method was mechanical augmentation.

But countless previous Federation trials had already proven that mechanical augmentation had no effect on psychic tempering!

The researchers exchanged glances.

Not one of them could answer Kleiman's question.

They thought back to the man who had been following Fang Heng.

All he'd done was point a finger toward the two soldiers from a distance.

And their bodies had suddenly undergone a transformation?

What kind of nonsense was that?

What had Dr. Fang done that they hadn't been able to see?

“Find out who that man called Jia'er is.”

“Understood!”

…

By the time the meeting at Federation Central Headquarters ended, it was already noon.

Ji Haiting had spent almost the entire meeting distracted, keeping in contact with the outside world.

Cheng Fan had informed him of Fang Heng's astonishing breakthrough as soon as the news emerged.

Preliminary examinations led the laboratory to conclude that, in theory, subjects who underwent Fang Heng's second-stage treatment and pushed psychic tempering to its limit could improve their Psychic Studies abilities by at least tenfold!

The laboratory's projected increase was a staggering 1,220%!

When Ji Haiting first saw the figures in the report, he wondered whether his eyes were playing tricks on him.

At that rate, humanity's progress in Psychic Studies would surge forward so quickly that it might finally begin to catch sight of the Dependents ahead of them!

Reading the full report only deepened his shock.

Just how had Fang Heng increased the soldiers' physical strength so quickly?

Ji Haiting hurried to wrap up the meeting and head straight back to the laboratory.

He wanted answers as soon as possible.

But before he could even leave the meeting room, two senior Federation officials approached.

“Mr. Ji, Minister Lei would like to see you.”

The Federation leadership was anxious too.

How far had Wanling Technology's Psychic Studies experiments progressed?

Where had this astonishing breakthrough come from?

Could the new potions be used to modify Federation troops on a large scale? Could production keep pace?

And might this advance in Psychic Studies become the lifeline humanity needed against the Tree Spirits?

Ji Haiting soon met Minister Lei in a small conference room next door.

“Chairman Ji, I've heard there has been a major breakthrough in Psychic Studies. What an unexpected surprise you've given us! Excellent work.”

Lei Changhong was a deputy minister in the military department.

The Federation's Psychic Studies Corps fell under his jurisdiction.

The fact that Minister Lei had come personally showed just how seriously the Federation was taking this development.

Lei Changhong smiled. “Though I have to say, your secrecy is remarkable. I've only just heard about it. How long have you been working on this? There's no need to keep us in the dark!”

“Minister Lei, you're too kind. As it happens, I was just about to report this matter to you.”

Ji Haiting gave a rueful smile inwardly.

It had all happened so suddenly.

Only recently had he himself discovered that Fang Heng's breakthrough in Psychic Studies could be so tremendous.

It had shocked him every bit as much as the Federation.

“To tell you the truth, Minister Lei, Fang Heng is involved.”

Seeing that nobody else was nearby, Ji Haiting leaned closer and whispered a few words into Lei's ear.

“Fang Heng…”

The minister's expression turned solemn. He repeated the name quietly.

He remembered the Tree Spirits' recent attacks on the Federation's central research institute.

Fang Heng's name had appeared in those incidents.

Then there was the joint investigation into Ouroboros initiated by the Dependents.

Lei Changhong said, “That man is dangerous. You must be extremely careful in any dealings with him.”

“I understand.”

Ji Haiting knew how important this Psychic Studies breakthrough was to the Federation. Rather than continue hiding it, he gave Lei a broad account of his cooperation with Fang Heng.

Lei Changhong lowered his head in thought.

For now, the human Federation's connection with the Dependents had been completely severed.

At the very least, it could not be restored in the short term.

But the Dependents were bound to return to this world in a few years…

And Fang Heng was connected to Ouroboros.

Once the Dependents returned, the Federation would have to consider very carefully what relationship it wanted to have with him.
