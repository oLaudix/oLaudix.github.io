Chapter 2943

Suspicious Inheritance

From original Chinese Chapter 2944 · 可疑的传承物

"We located the coordinates of the Ancient Humans' world, but a virtual spatial coordinate alone isn't enough for teleportation. We tried. It isn't an ordinary coordinate; it's encoded in a special way."

"Only recently did one of our agents embedded in the Western Federation's Star Map Project discover that the coordinates actually refer to a Kinsfolk interstellar route."

Fang Heng thought for a moment. "Our own world hasn't been entered into the Kinsfolk's interstellar route network, but they've already located that one. That means the place has probably come to their attention and might even have been surveyed."

"Yes. We don't yet know its current condition."

Su Guantong nodded. "But that inheritance is important to us, so we have to try. To reach the world, the Eastern Federation needs internal network data from the Kinsfolk Star Map to decode the coordinates. Our embedded agent managed to copy the relevant information. But her assignment is unusual, and she's completely isolated. My mission is to meet her, receive the Star Map intelligence, and bring it back."

Fang Heng asked, "And what do you need me to do?"

"Because of the Ouroboros Seed outbreak, the entire Star Map Control Center is on the highest level of alert. I've lost contact with my superiors. They told me beforehand that if I ran into trouble, I could ask you for help."

"We need to find Dr. Li Zhixuan, our undercover agent, and complete the handover. As for payment, you needn't worry. I'm authorized to negotiate. Name your terms, and we'll do everything we can to meet them."

Fang Heng quickly weighed their previous dealings, the potential benefits and losses, and the difficulty of the mission.

He wasn't particularly interested in uncovering the game's supposed truth.

But this assignment was far too easy.

He could do it along the way.

"I understand. We can talk payment later. You want me to handle the handover in your place. Where is she?"

"From what I've learned, she's in a Black Fog Core world—one of the 'Semi-Game Worlds' controlled by the Western Federation. Dr. Li Zhixuan currently heads a research department project team there."

"Hmm?"

Fang Heng was surprised at first, but on reflection, a Black Fog Core world was an excellent place for confidential, isolated research.

The military guarded both the entrance and the exit, making it difficult for anyone to come or go.

And an undercover agent had somehow become a project-team leader? Not bad at all.

"Do you have the world number?"

"I do."

"All right. I'll do it. I'll make the trip."

"Thank you." Relieved that he had accepted, Su Guantong took a pendant from around her neck and handed it to him. "Li Zhixuan may not know who you are. Show her this insignia and she'll understand."

Fang Heng caught the pendant, weighed it in his hand, then tossed it back. "No need. You can speak to her in person. You'll see her soon enough."

"Huh?"

Su Guantong stared at his departing back in surprise.

Li Zhixuan was under strict Western Federation protection. Meeting her would be difficult even for Fang Heng, never mind Su Guantong.

Had his influence inside the Western Federation already grown to that extent?

After leaving the room, Fang Heng headed directly for the underground sections of the Star Map Control Center.

His previous investigation into the Ouroboros incident had earned him permission to enter the restricted Black Fog Core area below.

After passing the routine outer inspection, he returned smoothly to the Black Fog Core zone.

Two Federation squads were still stationed in the hall and looked at him in confusion.

They found his appearance rather odd.

The theft case involving the Ouroboros Seeds had essentially been concluded. The world prism into which Emery had escaped had shattered, making entry impossible, and the Federation had turned its attention back to the front lines.

What was Fang Heng doing here now?

Still, he possessed special clearance, so the Northern Federation personnel did not stop him. They let him enter one of the Black Fog Prism worlds through its crystal.

Before long, he entered the Black Fog Prism game world.

The teleportation exit opened directly into the research institute's teleportation hall.

Several Federation personnel noticed the unexpected arrival and immediately approached, studying Fang Heng.

The Federation military's security chief recognized him and approached with two soldiers. "Mr. Fang Heng, this is a classified Federation laboratory. I'm Colonel Jared, head of security. How may we help you?"

Fang Heng glanced at the laboratory entrance. "Where's the person in charge?"

"Director Lin is conducting a closed experiment. I'm afraid you'll have to wait a while to see him..."

"Never mind." Fang Heng raised a hand. "I need a list of every member of your laboratory staff."

"I'll need to verify the purpose of your request."

"I'm investigating the Ouroboros Seed incident. The Western Federation gave me that authority. I only need to look at the list."

"Understood."

The personnel list was soon placed in Fang Heng's hands.

He quickly found Li Zhixuan's name and then imprinted the names of the other researchers into his memory.

Since removing all his Locks in reality, his ability to memorize information by force over short periods had reached a terrifying level.

"That's all. Nothing else."

He finished memorizing the list, handed it back, and retraced his steps through the teleportation passage, leaving the Federation soldiers staring after him.

"What..."

They exchanged bewildered looks.

Colonel Jared said coldly, "Record his entry in the log and report the incident to our superiors."

"Yes, sir!"

...

The next day.

A high-level Western Federation video conference was underway.

Late the previous night, the Ouroboros Seed nest aggregate had been completely destroyed, temporarily lifting the crisis threatening humanity.

Yet Vice President Jasper of the Western Federation looked anything but happy.

They had won.

But somehow, it didn't feel like a victory.

Emery had vanished, taking with him the documents, cocoons, mother insect, and crucial starship key he had stolen from the Western Federation.

Even after destroying the Ouroboros nest and thoroughly searching the battlefield, they hadn't found any trace of a cocoon.

The Western Federation had suffered enormous losses.

"Charlotte has disappeared too..."

Jasper murmured when he heard the latest report.

That much was expected.

They had originally approached the Black Fortress, offering to help Charlotte unlock her mental power in reality in exchange for her assistance to the Western Federation.

Fearing her frightening strength, the Federation had dragged its feet and tried to keep her working for their benefit for as long as possible.

Neither side had openly broken relations, but the partnership had hardly been pleasant.

Now that their obligations had been settled, it was only natural for Charlotte to leave.

"Next is the report concerning Fang Heng."

Augustine displayed a document on the screen, his expression grave.
