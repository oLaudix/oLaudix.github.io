Chapter 2410

Breaking In

From original Chinese Chapter 2411 · 闯入

Meanwhile, Fang Heng followed Norbert's group into the Tower of Bones district. They hid behind a wall some distance away and observed the situation.

The undead attendants at the perimeter were still standing guard.

During this emergency, the entire Tower of Bones district had been locked down under martial law.

Everyone looked at Norbert.

Norbert seemed more confident now. He nodded. “Just as I expected. Most of the Tower of Bones guards have been reassigned.”

Li Shaoqiang glanced toward the distant entrance and whispered, “How are you planning to get us inside? I don't want to fight the undead and end up branded a rebel by our own faction.”

“We've come this far. What are you still afraid of?”

Norbert looked impatient and shook his head. “Forget it. I won't make you fight. Just stand back and watch. Leave it to me.”

With a gesture to his companions, Norbert stepped out from behind the wall with two of his guildmates and walked openly toward the entrance.

Two undead attendants stationed at the gate stopped them.

“Katantul is currently under martial law. The Tower of Bones district is closed to outsiders.”

“I know,” Norbert replied with a nod. “We've received a special mission and need to enter the Tower of Bones to collect war supplies.”

The undead attendants did not move aside.

“We've received no such mission notice.”

“Really? There must have been a misunderstanding. Here's an entry permit from the tower master. Please examine it.”

One attendant looked puzzled and accepted the permit from Norbert.

“Hum…”

In the next instant, a dark ripple spread from the unfolded permit.

As soon as it touched them, the nearby undead attendants trembled violently. Their bodies twisted and dissolved until they were reduced to piles of ash, vanishing before everyone's eyes.

“Tap…”

The permit fell to the ground.

Norbert crouched to pick it up and waved toward Fang Heng and the others hiding around the corner.

“Let's go.”

A group of more than thirty players swept out from behind the wall and followed Norbert into the Tower of Bones district.

Li Shaoqiang couldn't help curling his lip.

Norbert had assembled so many players for this operation…

Even an idiot could have forced his way in with that many people.

So much for carefully screening participants to prevent leaks!

…

With the whole city under martial law, only the entrance was guarded by several undead attendants. Inside, not a single person could be seen.

Norbert led the group smoothly into the central square of the Tower of Bones district.

Fang Heng stood in the square.

Looking up, he saw five enormous undead towers reaching into the sky.

He had visited the tallest of the five, the Tower of Bones, many times before.

Everyone turned to Norbert.

“The entrance to the Death Knight's special space is hidden inside the Tower of Bones.” Norbert gazed up at the tower for a moment and immediately said, “No time to lose. Let's go.”

Under his lead, everyone pushed open the Tower of Bones' doors and hurried inside.

Fang Heng followed them into the tower.

From the first-floor hall, he could see a spiral staircase winding upward. Above it lay Mentor Guro's study and reception room.

During his previous visits, undead attendants had always guided him upstairs to meet Guro.

This time was different. Norbert showed no intention of going up. Instead, he and his group lingered in the first-floor hall.

Norbert and several subordinates seemed to be looking for something. They slowed down, cautiously tapping and prodding along the hall's walls.

“Knock, knock, knock…”

One player discovered something unusual when he struck a section of the wall.

It produced a strangely hollow sound.

“Found it!”

Norbert's eyes lit up, and he hurried over to inspect it.

There was a passage behind the wall!

Norbert took out a small knife, carefully worked it along the seams between the bricks, and pried the blocks loose until a pitch-black passage appeared behind them.

Fang Heng was intrigued.

There really was one!

He'd been here many times and thought he knew the tower well, but he'd never imagined there was a secret passage here.

“Come on. Let's go in and see.”

Norbert led the way into the darkness.

The passage was deep, completely unlit, and seemed not to have been used for a very long time. A thick layer of dust covered the floor.

Norbert took out a prepared Netherworld Candle and used it to illuminate the way.

Before long, he spotted stairs ahead and led everyone downward.

Following the steps, Fang Heng felt as though they had walked a very long way.

“It's this far down? How much farther?”

Li Shaoqiang was growing impatient.

“Mm. I estimate we've descended about fifteen floors.”

Tan Shuo had been silently counting as they walked.

As they spoke, the staircase finally reached its end.

After passing through another corridor, an enormous underground palace hall appeared before them.

“Looks like this is it.”

Norbert glanced around, then floated the lit candle into the air to illuminate the hall.

The circular chamber was pitch-black, with battered clay jars scattered around its perimeter. Thick cobwebs covered everything, and even the paint on the walls had faded and peeled.

Nobody had come here in a long time.

The place resembled an abandoned temple.

Fang Heng studied his surroundings, thinking silently.

Punoze had originally contained a Yin Vein fissure connecting to the Death Realm, and the undead had occupied it largely for research and cultivation.

Ever since he transported that entire fissure to Duncha, he'd assumed the greatest secret of Punoze had already been revealed.

So what was this place for?

“Everyone, if my information is correct, the passage to the Death Knight's sealed prison is here.”

Norbert stepped forward into the center of the circular hall, knelt down, and swept away a thick layer of dust.

Huh?

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

Once the dust was cleared, an ancient bronze-colored door appeared in the floor.

The door looked as though it had been carved into and embedded in the stone itself. It was still sealed, wrapped in dense layers of undead chains.

“The undead mentors have placed restrictions on the seal. If the door is damaged, they'll sense it immediately.”

Norbert stood before the locked door, looking rather nervous as he inspected it. “But the mentors aren't in Punoze now. They won't be able to return in time.”

“What are we waiting for, then? Let's begin.”

Several players could wait no longer and stepped forward to start undoing the seal together.

The seal wasn't especially difficult. It was the most common kind used in undead studies.

A few of the first players attempted it, but their mental power wasn't sufficient to open it completely, so they called the others over.

“Everyone, come help.”

The group crowded around and poured their mental power into the sealed door.
