Chapter 2090

Demonic Patterns

From original Chinese Chapter 2091 · 纹路

Fang Heng reached the massive altar, emerged from his secondary-space projection, and pressed a hand against it!

At the same time, he swept his eyes over its markings.

That pattern was...

The instant he recognized it, his mind supplied the answer instinctively.

The Flame of Wrath.

This altar had originally belonged to the Demon Seed of Wrath.

But just like the others, its original markings had been overlaid with a second layer: the demonic pattern of Envy.

Whoosh!

As his fingers touched the altar, Fang Heng felt a cool sensation at his fingertips.

Chaos Power!

His body reacted automatically, drawing an enormous flow of Chaos Power into him!

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

The energy inside this one altar was several times greater than all the altars he'd touched previously combined!

[Hint: You have absorbed and converted Chaos Power. Intermediate Demon Bloodline skill EXP +162...]

Before he could absorb much more, Fang Heng felt an overwhelming presence lock onto him.

It was the three-headed Hell Horned Demon leader nearby!

The boss whipped around, all three grotesque heads glaring at Fang Heng with savage fury in their eyes!

ROAR!

The three-headed demon unleashed a deafening roar. The sound reverberated throughout the cavern, painfully battering everyone's eardrums!

How dare this intruder interfere with the altar?!

Whoosh!

The three-headed demon charged at full speed, surging toward Fang Heng like a raging hurricane!

[Hint: You have triggered an unknown altar in Hell.]

[Hint: The altar's guardian, a leader-class creature, has locked onto you. Exercise caution.]

Fang Heng's heart sank. He immediately stopped absorbing the energy and darted away from the altar toward the right.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The demon opened its mouths and launched dozens more concentrated hellfire spheres straight at him!

Fang Heng entered his secondary-space projection again to avoid them and sprinted forward.

Almost simultaneously, points of white light containing spatial energy appeared throughout the cavern.

Meng Si and his companions stared as the immense zombie army vanished from the cavern in a matter of seconds!

This was bad!

Meng Si shivered, breaking into a cold sweat. "Run! Everyone, run!"

Fang Heng reappeared elsewhere and raced toward the tunnel exit ahead of the pursuing boss. While running, he quickly switched back to his divine-artifact disguise and headed toward the Holy Court's gathering point outside.

The three-headed demon gave chase immediately!

Behind it, the horned demon horde poured together like a torrent and chased Fang Heng along the same route.

From a rocky ledge high above, where they'd taken shelter, Meng Si wiped cold sweat from his forehead as he watched the terrifying procession.

That was close.

He'd almost lost his life.

Even now, they couldn't fully understand what had just happened.

But they were certain of one thing.

The Holy Court was about to have a very bad day.

· · ·

At the entrance to Hell.

Less than three hours had passed since the trial began.

The assembled trainee teams were already exchanging volleys of attacks with the densely packed Evil Eyes on the cliff.

Whenever an Evil Eye's HP was depleted, it fell from the wall, and the players collected the corpses for harvesting.

But huge numbers still clung to the rock face.

Because of the suppressive effects of Hell's environment, the players' damage output was far lower than usual.

At this rate, they might not finish clearing the area even after a full day.

Just then, one player noticed something and looked toward a spot high on the cliff.

What was that?

Whoosh!

A shadow flashed across the rock face.

Hmm?

A person?

Before he could see clearly, the figure vanished into thin air.

Had he imagined it?

The player frowned, rubbed his eyes, and tried to look more carefully.

A moment later, Fang Heng stepped out of his secondary-space projection behind the Holy Court teams and glanced at the back of his hand.

Now that he'd left the region covered by red mist, his Crusader Trainee Mark was functioning again.

"Fang Heng, you're back already? Are you all right?" Lindro asked, noticing how disheveled he looked. "Did you run into serious trouble?"

The others found it strange too.

Even when Fang Heng fought boss creatures in previous trials, he'd never returned looking quite this battered.

And there was something even stranger.

Usually, once Fang Heng disappeared during a trial, he simply never returned to the party.

But this time he'd come back?

Fang Heng didn't answer. His attention shifted to the entrance leading into the cliff region.

Huh?

Crusader instructors!

He'd been planning to lure the horned demons all the way to the teleportation point, but two Crusader instructors were already stationed near the cliff!

Even better!

Fang Heng nodded quickly. "I'm fine. But I found something dangerous inside the caves. Things might get bad very soon. I suggest everyone withdraw farther back."

Danger? Retreat?

The party couldn't quite make sense of those words coming from Fang Heng.

Sak suspected only a boss could have made him describe something as dangerous.

"Don't tell me you ran into a boss inside the cliff?"

"More or less. It's complicated, and there's no time to explain. Let's go!"

Lindro's heart jolted. He didn't hesitate any longer and waved to the others.

"Move! Withdraw first!"

The team immediately began retreating along the passage they'd used to reach the cliff.

They hadn't gone far when two Holy Court instructors shouted from behind them.

"The situation has changed! Quickly! Everyone, retreat immediately!"

Lindro's group turned instinctively and went pale.

Huge numbers of Hell Horned Demons were suddenly pouring out of the caves all across the cliff!

The player teams had carefully arranged their formations. None of them had expected such a massive horde of hellish creatures to appear out of nowhere!

The sudden chaos shattered their formation. Some teams were wiped out almost instantly.

ROAR!

A deafening roar spread through the area, making the players' ears ring.

Looking toward the source, they saw an enormous leader-class three-headed Hell Horned Demon smash straight through a section of the cliff wall!

Its gigantic body leaped out into the open!

Waves of sinister black energy rolled off the three-headed creature.

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

The instant it landed, the demon unleashed more than a dozen concentrated hellfire spheres.

Sensing Fang Heng's location, it immediately charged after him!
