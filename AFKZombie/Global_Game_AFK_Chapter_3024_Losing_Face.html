Chapter 3024

Losing Face

From Chinese Chapter 3025 · 丢脸

Dean had been waiting for Fang Heng to call for help all along. When he finally heard him shout after being badly injured and knocked down, Dean instantly darted out from the right and pursued Eseklyna.

“You brat! Why go to such desperate lengths!?”

Dean had been cursing Fang Heng furiously in his mind.

Demon Seeds were immortal creatures. Kill one, and sooner or later another would take its place.

Was it really worth damaging his own fundamental power just to get a little peace after killing one Demon Seed?

But matters had already reached this point. Dean had been put on the spot and had no choice but to step forward, forcibly blocking Eseklyna's escape route.

“Holy Court!”

Eseklyna had already expended strength fighting Fang Heng, and the Demon-Binding Chains had weakened her further. No longer at her peak, she sent her Tongues of Envy surging toward Dean.

“Shield of Protection!”

A protective shield gathered in front of Dean, completely isolating the Tongues of Envy's power.

“Bang!!!”

The tongues shattered one after another.

Huh?

To Eseklyna's surprise, the Tongues of Envy couldn't simply bypass Dean's sacred shield.

“Heh...”

Dean chuckled softly.

He represented Protection, one of the Twelve Sages.

His specialty was close combat, with more emphasis on support and absorbing damage.

The Sacred Shield was reputed to withstand every form of damage.

Players outside the Holy Court had even bestowed the title “King of Turtle Shells” upon the Holy Court's Glory Guard under his command.

Seeing that Eseklyna wanted to flee and judging her to be weakened and restrained, Dean said coldly, “Fiend, you cannot leave this place. Surrender.”

“In your dreams!”

Eseklyna flashed around repeatedly, trying to slip past Dean and escape.

But Dean blocked her attacks every time, intercepting her path again and again.

What a coincidence, wasn't it?

Protection just happened to be Dean's specialty.

He might not excel at dealing explosive damage in a short fight, but when it came to restraining someone, he had ten thousand ways to torment his opponent!

A perfect match for his profession!

The more anxious Eseklyna became, the calmer Dean was. He methodically blocked her escape route.

They remained entangled for a full three minutes.

Then Eseklyna's pupils contracted.

Not good!

Her connection to her demonic pet was growing faint.

“Roar!!”

Eseklyna's eyes turned bloodred, and dense hellfire erupted violently from within her!

The Tongues of Envy extending from her heart burst outward, ripping her flesh apart. Accepting injury to her very essence, she forcibly broke through part of the Demon-Binding Chains' suppression!

The raging tongues expanded dozens of times over and swept wildly in all directions.

Not good!

Dean's heart jolted.

She's going all out!

Even more unexpectedly, the Tongues of Envy weren't aimed at him. Instead, they swept past him toward the Holy Court forces fighting the high-level Hell fiends behind him.

“Aksu! Fall back!”

Dean shouted, gathering his psychic power and forcibly raising a sacred barrier to intercept them.

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!!”

The wildly thrashing tongues slammed into the barrier, spreading deep cracks across it!

Aside from physical attacks, the vertical pupils on the tongues carried illusion effects.

Dean could endure them, but large numbers of paladins fell into confused states under the illusions.

Meanwhile, tiny Tongues of Envy emerged from beneath the skin at the backs of the Hell fiends' necks. Under their influence, the high-level fiends fighting the Holy Court entered a berserk state one after another.

The berserk Hell fiends' strength rose sharply!

Caught by the two attacks at once, the sacred barrier protecting the front of the Holy Court's main force collapsed. Large numbers of fiends poured in, and the entire formation threatened to rout.

Damn it! How despicable!

Dean inwardly cursed. Unable to keep intercepting Eseklyna, he hurried back to rescue his troops.

Those were the Holy Court's elites, its treasured fighters.

He couldn't allow them all to die here!

Sacred light poured down, and Dean sheltered the Holy Court forces behind him, stabilizing the battlefield.

At the same time, he could only watch helplessly as Eseklyna, having created the chaos, turned into a ghostly streak and fled the cavern.

There was nothing he could do.

Dean felt deeply helpless.

In front of everyone... He'd clearly promised Fang Heng he'd stop the Demon Seed, yet he'd been forced to give up.

That was rather humiliating...

As Dean thought this, he cast Fang Heng an apologetic look.

Fang Bai had sacrificed so much of his Grand Sage power to severely injure Eseklyna, yet Dean had failed to hold her.

Then again, the Demon Seed was burning her own essence and sacrificing so many subordinates to cover her escape. There was little he could do.

This was Hell, not their home battlefield. Otherwise, they might have had a chance...

Huh?

What?

As Dean glanced toward where Fang Heng had fallen, he saw something completely different from the half-dead, exhausted figure he'd expected. Fang Heng had already jumped to his feet and was racing after the escaping Eseklyna!

What?

Wasn't he seriously injured? How could he still move so nimbly?

Dean's heart skipped a beat.

Something wasn't right!

He was still chasing her?

Hadn't Eseklyna's Tongues of Envy just drained him dry? He had almost no sacred power left. Even a hundred years of bitter cultivation might not make up for the loss, so where did he get the confidence to chase her again?

...

Meanwhile, in Echidna's cavern on the twenty-ninth level of Hell.

A sword was buried deep in the center head of Echidna's true body, the three-headed giant serpent. Her body shook violently before finally collapsing into the lava under the combined assault of over twenty Demon Seeds.

The blue fluid coating the magma gradually dissipated.

The cavern's temperature quickly rose, returning to the lava's usual scorching heat.

Meanwhile, the blue secret patterns on the five Demon Cores spinning around the stone pillar faded.

The cores were no longer under control and began revolving around the pillar even faster.

“Whoosh!!!!”

Jing'er and Zhuang Yufei had prepared themselves and leapt toward the Demon Cores together.

But they were already a step too late.

Plenty of Demon Seeds had their eyes on the cores.

With Echidna dead, the backlash from the Demon Cores vanished. This was the perfect chance to seize them!

“Whoosh!!”

The instant Echidna fell, two Demon Seeds, as though they'd arranged it beforehand, flew toward the spinning cores from opposite sides.

“Stop!”

Ignoring Jing'er's shout behind them, both reached greedily toward the Demon Cores!

“Boom!!!!”

Just as their hands were about to touch them, a mighty force erupted from the five spinning cores, blasting the two right back the way they'd come!

“I said stop, all of you!”

Using that brief opening, Jing'er, Zhuang Yufei, and an elderly Demon Seed accompanying them caught up. The three stood before the spinning cores, shielding them and glaring coldly at the others.
