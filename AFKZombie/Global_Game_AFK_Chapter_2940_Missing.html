Chapter 2940

Missing

From original Chinese Chapter 2941 · 失踪

The next moment, a dense swarm of insects poured out of the darkness, rushing straight toward Fang Heng and Charlotte at the front!

Bang! Bang, bang!!!

Any insects that came within twenty meters of the pair either exploded into clouds of bloody mist or were struck by an invisible force and hurled backward even faster than they had approached.

For a moment, the air was filled with the crackling sounds of insects bursting apart.

The other players stared in shock.

Good grief.

Where had these two monsters come from?

Were they really that terrifyingly strong?

The player teams had personally experienced how dreadful the swarm was during their earlier probing attacks.

By comparison, Fang Heng and Charlotte looked as though they were playing a game.

Neither took so much as a step as the swarm surged toward them, as though the insects were throwing themselves forward to commit suicide.

They remained where they were for three minutes. Only when the swarm had been reduced by more than half did the pair resume walking, approaching the Black Fog Crystal nest step by step.

Bang! Bang, bang, bang!!!

The insects kept coming, and one after another burst apart.

Even elite Zerg were hurled backward twice before exploding into bloody mist on their third attempt.

At last, they reached the spatial-crystal entrance deep within the black fog.

Fang Heng stopped and slowly raised a hand toward the mass of Ouroboros Seed nests entrenched at the entrance to the Black Fog Crystal.

Thump! Thump!!!

Affected by the fluctuations of Fang Heng's blood and vitality, the Ouroboros Seed nest began contracting and pulsing wildly, shedding clumps of thick gray slime.

Charlotte fixed her gaze on the nest.

Mental Pierce!

Countless tiny points of tearing light flashed through the air.

In an instant, wounds covered the nest, and black pus streamed from the cuts.

"Attack!"

Augustine shouted.

Seeing that Fang Heng and Charlotte had completely disabled the nest, the Federation soldiers immediately concentrated their fire on it.

In less than half a minute, the small nest attached to the Black Fog Crystal was blown apart.

Fang Heng glanced around, stepped forward again, and passed swiftly through the spatial passage.

Behind him, Augustine's Federation troops and the mercenary players watched him enter the Black Fog world. They lowered their weapons, exchanged looks, and fell silent for a moment.

Terrifying.

A mission they had considered nearly impossible had been steamrolled by these two.

Where had the Federation found such reinforcements?

"Follow them!"

Augustine drew a deep breath and waved his hand. "With me! Let's press our advantage and crush the swarm in one push!"

"Yes, sir!"

The Federation soldiers raised their beam weapons again and filed rapidly into the spatial passage.

Then they entered the "Semi-Game World."

The sight before them left everyone astonished all over again.

Vast, densely packed masses of Zerg!

Brown, spiny insects continually emerged from cracks in the ground and fought pale-purple insects across the battlefield. The air reeked of blood.

Charlotte extended a hand and formed a visible barrier of mental power before the soldiers.

Any insect approaching the barrier was immediately suspended in midair by its influence, becoming a helpless target for the soldiers.

Fang Heng stood calmly beside her, directing his summoned creatures to slaughter the swarm.

When he saw Augustine arrive with the soldiers, he turned and called, "Augustine."

Augustine hurried over and asked quietly, "Mr. Fang Heng, do you have a battle plan?"

"I spoke with Instructor Benny before coming here. I know there are secrets surrounding Emery and the nest. Charlotte and I don't want to get tangled up in any of that. We'll help you hold the swarm under control. Everything after that is up to you."

Augustine's heart stirred.

He had expected Fang Heng to make some outrageous demand. Was he really being this accommodating?

"Thank you!"

Augustine cupped his fists toward Fang Heng and ordered his men to attack the swarm with everything they had.

"Quickly!"

The mercenary players following behind were equally eager to join the fighting.

Some impatient player squads even pushed past Charlotte's barrier and hurried toward the distant nest.

The Federation's rewards were generous. Anyone who seized a cocoon and handed it over could earn a fortune!

Under the combined assault of the Federation and the players, the swarm rapidly collapsed!

The creatures summoned by the Ouroboros nest lacked ranged attacks, and the players had already prepared for the purple, hallucination-inducing mist it emitted.

They had taken every illusion-resistance potion they could before launching the assault.

And on top of that, they had Fang Heng and Charlotte—two absolute monsters—controlling the swarm from behind.

This was practically a dream come true!

The elite mercenary players and Federation soldiers advanced rapidly toward the front lines, fighting alongside the insect swarm attacking the nest!

Then the nest came within striking range.

Charlotte narrowed her eyes. Her mental power surged hundreds of times over and gathered into a single Mental Pierce that slammed into the nest!

Bang!!!

Charlotte's mental attack collided with the psychic control frequency emitted by the Ouroboros Seed aggregate, producing an invisible explosion in midair.

The Ouroboros Seed's special control ability shattered instantly, and the hallucination-inducing eyeballs growing across the nest burst one after another.

Invisible mental ripples spread outward from the collision.

The affected players felt their seas of consciousness tremble slightly.

The Ouroboros Seed aggregate nest's HP plummeted!

Augustine's heart pounded wildly.

Everything was going far too smoothly.

The military had been telling the truth.

Fang Heng and Charlotte were terrifyingly powerful and could be an enormous help to the Federation.

But something was strange.

Where was Emery?

Why had no one found any trace of that bastard?

Boom!!!

As Augustine pondered this, the enormous fleshy cocoon formed by the Ouroboros Seed aggregate suddenly exploded before everyone's eyes, scattering flesh in every direction.

Almost immediately, the hired player teams surged toward the scattered remains, frantically searching for cocoons.

Fang Heng looked at Charlotte. "Are you all right?"

"That degree of mental manipulation is nothing."

Charlotte gave a small nod.

Fang Heng turned to Augustine. "We've fulfilled our promise to the Federation. Well? Satisfied?"

"Very satisfied. Thank you both for your help."

Augustine was more concerned with the missing cocoons and Emery's disappearance. "There's one other thing. Have either of you seen Emery?"

"No."

"Hmm. I haven't seen him once over the past few days, not since our last meeting. I keep feeling he's plotting something. It's unsettling."

"Is it possible..." Fang Heng looked around, then continued, "That he slipped away amid the chaos when the nest erupted a couple of days ago?"
