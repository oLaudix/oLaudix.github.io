Chapter 3133

Unknown Research

From Chinese Chapter 3134 · 未知研究

“The 'Heliom' experiment… I wonder whether the Noel Clan knows about it.”

Fang Heng frowned and muttered to himself as he continued searching through the psychic information.

The fourth group of warriors had already been selected by Administrator Lila. They were scheduled to be transferred to Prism Starship V on the third day of next month, from which they would be sent straight to the experimental site.

After sorting through all the information, Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and unconsciously clenched his fists.

“Laws… power surpassing high-tier warriors, perhaps even honored-guest-level warriors…”

That was enticing!

Most high-tier warriors had at least one discipline at the peak of the Transcendent level. Once they reached that stage, improving further meant either mastering another discipline or crossing the threshold into the Demigod level.

At that level, improvements in strength were generally measured in years.

Yet these people could make enormous breakthroughs within just a few months?

What exactly was the Kinsfolk's so-called 'Heliom' experiment?

And there was more.

According to Mu Shunan and Nasir, advancing academic proficiency all the way to the God level meant gaining power equal to that of a god…

Fang Heng grew more and more interested.

Unfortunately, the list of high-tier warriors taking part in this round had already been finalized. At first glance, infiltrating the experiment wouldn't be easy.

After some thought, Fang Heng continued inspecting the remaining contents of the purple light cluster.

Huh?

Hm? Part of the report also concerned the spread of black mist among pan-human races.

Just as he'd suspected.

The Kinsfolk hadn't established that he was responsible. They'd simply investigated all the pan-human races living in the Seven Realms Martial Capital. Apart from those they could definitively clear, they'd massacred everyone else who remained under suspicion.

Fang Heng spotted Kappa and Korkalot's names.

The Kinsfolk had originally planned to dispose of both of them as well.

But they'd decided to use the pair as bait, so they'd imprisoned them temporarily and set a trap in their cell. Anyone attempting to contact Kappa and Korkalot would be caught immediately.

“Heh…”

Fang Heng sneered and kept reading.

It turned out Kappa and Korkalot were still publicly listed as prisoners in a border-region prison. In reality, however, they'd already been transferred as convicts to the Frozen Soul Land.

Troublesome.

Fang Heng inwardly cursed. He'd believed the two were still somewhere in the Seven Realms Martial Capital, where the Noel Clan might have been able to negotiate and get them released.

He hadn't expected the Kinsfolk to have moved first.

After thinking it over, Fang Heng abandoned his plan to continue cultivating.

Now that he knew where Kappa and Korkalot were being held, he needed to rescue them as soon as possible.

…

Following the previous night's intrusion, the entire Kinsfolk building remained under lockdown until a fleet carrying high-ranking Kinsfolk officials arrived in the Seven Realms Martial Capital the next afternoon.

The city's joint investigative department was extremely concerned about the incident.

But the Kinsfolk declared that they would direct the investigation themselves and wanted no outside interference.

The Kinsfolk soon discovered that, in addition to the attack on their headquarters, the warriors they'd sent to kill Fang Heng had disappeared completely.

Fang Heng became one of their primary suspects.

By then, however, Fang Heng had contacted the elves of the Seven Realms Martial Capital during the night. With the help of their covert connections, he'd found a way to sneak aboard a ship bound for the Frozen Soul Land.

Only one starship departed for the Frozen Soul Land each week, and it happened to be leaving that very afternoon.

The elves had very little influence in the Seven Realms Martial Capital, so they could arrange only a simple disguise. Fang Heng passed himself off as an elf convicted of a crime and joined a group of prisoners being transported to the frozen region.

It was all rather rushed. Only after boarding did Fang Heng learn from another inmate that the Frozen Soul Land was a special prison planet directly controlled by the Kinsfolk.

The planet possessed unusual mineral resources, but they were difficult to exploit. As a result, new groups of prisoners were transported there each week to work in the mines.

There were now more than three hundred people crammed into Fang Heng's ship compartment.

The prisoners were packed into the cramped space, giving off an unpleasant odor.

Several ship guards apparently couldn't stand the smell and remained on watch beside the compartment entrance.

Fang Heng lay on the floor against a wall among the prisoners.

He didn't intend to cause trouble. He just wanted to reach the Frozen Soul Land, find Kappa and Korkalot, and take them away.

Unexpectedly, trouble came looking for him first.

A burly, middle-aged alien approached Fang Heng and deliberately lashed out with a heavy kick, clearly trying to pick a fight.

“Move it, you stinking elf!”

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes at the newcomer, a trace of killing intent appearing in his gaze.

The alien man's heart jumped when Fang Heng fixed his eyes on him.

“He's new and doesn't know the rules. Please don't hold it against him.” An elderly man rose and stepped between them, repeatedly cupping his hands toward the aggressor in apology. “I apologize to you on his behalf.”

Realizing he'd shown fear a moment earlier, the alien grew inexplicably furious and took it out on the old man. “Damn it! Old bastard! Aren't you too old to play the hero? Who the hell do you think you are? Move aside, or I'll break your legs!”

Hearing the abuse, several young people behind the old man rose and stood at his back.

“Clack!!!”

The compartment door opened.

Two ship guards entered, looked over the opposing groups, and barked, “What are you doing?! Got nothing better to do? Back off, or we'll add ten years to every one of your sentences!”

The burly alien glared viciously at the old man and snorted before finally backing away.

“Young brother, thank you. It's your first time here, isn't it? Don't bother arguing with people like that. Once we reach the Frozen Soul Land, someone will teach them a proper lesson.”

Fang Heng gave a quiet acknowledgment and asked, “Why did you help me?”

“We're all pan-human races. When you're away from home, you help your own if you can.” Uncle Hong waved a hand. “I'm older than you. Just call me Uncle Hong.”

Fang Heng was somewhat surprised.

His elf disguise wasn't perfect, but he hadn't expected anyone to see through it so easily.

“Uncle Hong, what were you sentenced for?”

“Ordinarily it wouldn't have been a big deal. A little money would have settled everything. But our luck was bad, and we ran afoul of the Kinsfolk at the wrong time. The Kinsfolk punish pan-human races severely. A group of youngsters from our clan got sentences of more than ten years each. I was worried about them, so I came along.”

Fang Heng nodded.

“The Frozen Soul Land is full of all kinds of people. You should believe at most half of what other prisoners tell you, or you'll suffer for it.” Uncle Hong offered his advice. “If you don't mind, stick with us when we arrive.”

“Thank you.”

Fang Heng nodded and closed his eyes to rest again.
