Chapter 2086

All Five

From original Chinese Chapter 2087 · 齐了

Boom! Boom, boom!

They were completely ineffective!

More than a dozen compressed fireballs struck the figure and exploded together, yet he endured them with the sheer toughness of his body!

What kind of monster was this?!

Meng Si's fear deepened. He tried to flee again, but his pupils suddenly contracted. It felt as though the blood in his veins had frozen, stopping his body in its tracks.

What?!

Cold sweat broke out across his forehead.

Whoosh!

An instant later, the figure behind him burst through the successive explosions.

A hand shot out of the flames and clamped tightly around his throat.

"I... cough... I..." Meng Si stared at Fang Heng, terror in his eyes. "Who... who are you?"

"Fang Heng."

Meng Si's pupils shook violently.

Fang Heng?

That Fang Heng?

Whoosh!

The next moment, the world spun.

Fang Heng lifted him high into the air and hurled him into the opposite wall!

Thud!

Meng Si slammed into the stone. It felt as if all his internal organs had been displaced. Gravely injured, he couldn't move another inch.

He lifted his head just in time to watch Fang Heng drag the other fleeing players out of the shadows one after another, swing them around, and smash each against the walls until they, too, were incapacitated.

"Five. That's all of them..."

Fang Heng counted once more, muttered to himself, and walked toward Meng Si.

"Now it's my turn to ask questions. What is this place?"

Under Fang Heng's gaze, Meng Si felt as though a demon were staring him down. He shivered.

"Hell. This is an entrance to the outer reaches of Hell."

"So it's Hell..."

Fang Heng nodded slightly.

Hell!

An advanced game world!

As far as he knew, Hell was different from most other worlds.

Though classified as an advanced game world, it was actually a collection of multiple regions. More precisely, numerous advanced game worlds contained entrances to Hell.

Unlike other worlds, Hell possessed one unique characteristic.

Expansion.

It was constantly spreading outward or contracting inward.

As fear spread, Hell expanded. When the Holy Court and other factions suppressed Hell and eased that fear, it gradually contracted again.

Hell had been hostile to almost every faction for as long as anyone could remember.

It seemed to possess an innate urge to destroy—to spread fear throughout the world and ultimately bring about its ruin.

Fang Heng continued questioning him. "What about the altar? What's going on with that structure?"

"We're followers of the Demon Seeds. We accepted a mission from a Demon Seed to protect several nearby altars."

Fang Heng looked at them with interest. "Go on. Tell me everything else you know."

Meng Si and the others exchanged glances.

This man was too strange!

He clearly belonged to the Holy Court, yet he could absorb energy from a demonic altar.

What was his story?

"A long time ago, large numbers of altars were built throughout this area. But as the Holy Court has gradually pushed into Hell, the altars near the outer regions have been destroyed. We've had no choice but to withdraw farther into Hell."

"Recently, we discovered that the Holy Court had reached an entrance to the outer region and constructed a spatial teleportation array there."

"Then we received a mission. We were supposed to protect the altars while intercepting the Holy Court's forces—delay them or defeat them outright."

"After testing their strength several times, we realized the Holy Court was too powerful for us to handle on our own."

Meng Si glanced toward his teammates.

"So we discussed it and agreed to abandon the altar itself. Instead, we'd use its power to set a trap for the Holy Court."

"Oh?" Fang Heng's eyes brightened. "What kind of trap? Explain."

There was no point concealing anything now. Meng Si told him the whole plan.

"There are many similar altars around Hell's outer reaches, and quite a few are protected by powerful guardians. We're going to lure as many guardians out as we can and trigger the altars' defensive power. If we're lucky, we might deal a devastating blow to the Holy Court's forces."

Fang Heng's interest was piqued.

Interesting!

If their plan worked, it really could make the Holy Court suffer. After all, the Holy Court's abilities would be significantly weakened in Hell's environment.

But there were three Crusader instructors outside. Destroying the Holy Court's teleportation passage completely would still be difficult.

Perhaps they could add a little something to the plan?

Lure even more creatures over?

The Glutton's Touch he'd just encountered was actually quite promising. It was only unfortunate that his vampire bloodline happened to counter it so effectively.

The Crusader instructors wouldn't have such an easy time dealing with those worms.

Fang Heng considered this, then asked, "You said there were many other altars besides this one? Do they all belong to the Seed of Envy?"

Meng Si looked at him suspiciously.

Most players would be lucky even to have heard of the Demon Seeds. Yet this man knew enough to distinguish the seven great seeds?

"I'm asking you a question. Don't dawdle."

Meng Si lowered his head and hurried to answer.

"Altars are scattered throughout the Abyss of Hell. We only operate in the outer regions, but as far as we know, most altars belong to one of the seven great Demon Seeds. Some belong to tribes of the abyss. They say Envy's followers carried out a purge here more than ten years ago, so most of the altars now belong to the Demon Seed of Envy."

Fang Heng rubbed his chin thoughtfully.

If he could find more altars to drain, he could level up his Demon Bloodline and perhaps gain greater control over the seed inside him.

Of course, absorbing their power would also accelerate that seed's awakening.

But that was fine.

He had just discovered a rather good solution.

Glutton's Touch!

Those creatures could drain the power from his body—even parts of his original power that were difficult to restore.

Such as the Demon Seed's source!

He'd felt it earlier. Glutton's Touch hadn't merely drained his attributes; it had also been drawing away power from the seed's source within his sea of consciousness.

So when the time came, all he needed to do was refrain from interfering and let the worms absorb more of that power.

Naturally, there would be a downside.

Glutton's Touch drained everything indiscriminately.

It didn't care whether it was drawing demonic source power, natural source power, or holy source power. It simply absorbed it all!

Once drained, his various attributes would probably remain significantly reduced for a long time.

— End of Chapter 2086 —
