Chapter 2995

Treasure

From Chinese Chapter 2996 · 宝藏

Strange.

Even after draining all the energy, he hadn't discovered any sign of another passage.

Had the stone pillar existed solely for the trial?

Fang Heng set the question aside, left the area, and continued exploring ahead.

As soon as he left the open forest area, he found himself in another familiar labyrinth corridor. Two Ice Crystal Puppets gradually formed on the frozen floor.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng became a black blur.

Bang! Bang, bang!!

The two puppets were smashed to pieces before they'd even finished taking shape!

The escaping elemental energy was swiftly absorbed into Fang Heng.

No time to waste!

He picked up the pace, navigating the maze toward the aura of the Ouroboros Seed.

Hm?

After wandering another ten minutes or so, Fang Heng slowed and stopped at a fork in the labyrinth.

The left passage led toward a familiar-looking icy trial hall, while the other branch continued into a long, narrow corridor.

Looking into the ice-crystal hall, he could already see a patch of nature-element creatures.

Another identical Nature Studies trial?

Fang Heng suddenly couldn't bring himself to walk away. He clenched his fist and swallowed.

Damn it! He couldn't resist the temptation.

He'd come this far already.

All that nature energy was right in front of him!

He might as well grind another batch before leaving!

Without further hesitation, Fang Heng strode into the ice-crystal plaza's trial area.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

The moment he entered, dense clusters of icy leaves flew toward him.

“Lightning of Judgment!”

“BOOM! BOOM, BOOM, BOOM!!!!”

...

Meanwhile, the various Federation teams that had entered the Land of Apocalypse gradually split up and began their own operations.

To avoid unnecessary trouble, the Central Federation and the regional Federations explored different parts of the labyrinth independently.

Before long, their players encountered all sorts of creatures and ran into difficulties.

Somewhere in the maze.

The Central Federation had dispatched more than seven hundred people, but they were scattered across different areas upon entering.

Luo Ao was the overall captain commanding the Central Federation's team on this mission.

Even before entering the Land of Apocalypse labyrinth,

he'd had doubts about the assignment.

The Central Federation had clearly acquired advance intelligence on the general layout of the ice maze. It even possessed the marked location of the Apocalypse treasure itself.

But there was something very strange.

If his superiors had ordered them to seize the treasure at any cost, why invite the other Federations into the hunt? Why open the mission to independent players as well?

The Central Federation could have acted secretly.

Wouldn't claiming the treasure for themselves be better?

After assembling his squad, Luo Ao feared someone else might reach the prize first. He immediately led several dozen people toward the marked treasure location.

Along the way, ice-element creatures attacked his team.

Luo Ao chose not to waste time fighting them. They used spells and firearms to restrain the creatures, then hurried past.

They also ignored every trial-pillar area they encountered, heading directly for the marked site.

They absolutely had to reach the Apocalypse treasure before any other group!

“It's just ahead.”

After barely half an hour, the Central Federation elite team had absorbed other scattered squads, reaching more than a hundred members. They stopped before a wall of ice.

“Yes, this should be the place.”

Luo Ao compared their position with the map, approached the wall, and pressed his hand against it.

No mistake. This particular layer of ice was very thin.

It had been added later!

Luo Ao ordered sternly, “Break through it!”

Several elite players stepped forward, summoning Books of Elements.

Skill—Flame Jet.

Deep-red flames poured from the Books of Elements held by more than ten players, converging on the icy barrier.

The wall slowly melted beneath the fire magic.

After a short while, an entrance opened in the ice.

The instant the opening appeared, a visibly white blast of freezing air rushed out.

“The Apocalypse treasure is inside that cavern. Everyone, be careful. Prepare for combat. We'll send a reconnaissance group in first, and the rest can follow in stages.”

Luo Ao raised his hand to signal the team. Then he and an elite Federation squad cautiously entered the cavern.

Hm?

What was that?

As soon as the players entered and saw the chamber's interior, their faces grew solemn.

A stone pillar stood in the middle of the cavern hall.

A twisted, writhing mass of sticky matter clung to the pillar.

It looked like a monster, its body a lump of rotting flesh wrapped and twisted around the column. Pieces of putrid meat kept falling from it, rapidly turning into decayed sludge on the ground.

The players exchanged uneasy looks.

Was this thing the supposed Apocalypse treasure?

What the hell was it?!

As they looked more closely, they gradually made out a decayed face beneath the layers of rotten flesh.

Human?

Luo Ao narrowed his eyes and gestured for the team to keep its distance.

The thing was bizarre. Better to observe it before taking action.

“Archbishop, do you recognize this creature?”

Since the Western Federation's Star Map Project had stalled, the Central Federation had grown closer to the Holy Court again.

The Holy Court had accepted an invitation to participate, dispatching an elite group led personally by Archbishop Whiteman.

“Everyone, please be careful. I can sense an evil aura emanating from it.”

Archbishop Whiteman stepped forward, his face solemn.

Yet even he had never encountered anything so grotesque and terrifying. All he could do was urge caution.

Luo Ao returned to the center of the group and briefly conferred with several invited experts and headquarters representatives.

They couldn't wait any longer.

If they delayed, other player teams would eventually find this chamber.

Luo Ao nodded to one of his trusted subordinates.

“Try something first.”

The subordinate immediately drew a small-caliber pistol from his belt and aimed at the humanoid mass on the pillar.

“Bang! Bang! Bang!”

Three bullets struck the rotting flesh on the pillar, spraying three splashes of black blood.

Only a superficial layer of the strange substance fell away.

It appeared to have suffered no meaningful damage.

Should they try a more powerful stimulus?

While the players were at a loss, one of them, a specialist in alchemical magic arrays, suddenly spoke up. “Everyone, look at the five stone platforms in the hall's corners. Don't they look like components of a combined sealing array?”

Everyone turned to examine the surrounding array structures.

That really did seem to be the case.

In each of the hall's five corners stood a headless statue with an altar in front of it.

When they'd first entered, no one had noticed anything unusual, taking them for ordinary decorations.

Now, on closer inspection, they genuinely resembled a sealing magic array.

“Is it sealing the Apocalypse treasure?”

“We aren't sure.”

Several players proficient in alchemical arrays went over to inspect the structures. After checking, they looked up. “Confirmed. It's a type of sealing magic array, and the seal is still operating.”
