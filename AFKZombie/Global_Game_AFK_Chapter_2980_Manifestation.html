Chapter 2980

Manifestation

From Chinese Chapter 2981 · 显现

Aboard a certain Crocodile Clan starship.

The rest area.

After a hard-fought battle, the Crocodile Clan's elite main-force soldiers were soaking in cultivation fluid.

Bathing in this special solution strengthened their muscles and enhanced their combat power, particularly after training or actual combat.

“Splash...”

A Crocodile Clan warrior climbed out of a cultivation pod, glanced around at the rows of pods, and left the rest area.

“Click.”

The metal door closed behind him.

The warrior's eyes were threaded with blood-red veins. A mocking sneer curled his lips as he strode along the ship's passageway.

Suddenly he slowed to a stop and raised his head. His expression grew solemn as he stared into the corridor ahead.

A faint ripple appeared in space.

“You...”

The Crocodile Clan warrior stared at Fang Heng.

“Not a bad disguise. Good enough to fool everyone else.” Fang Heng watched the warrior, gradually releasing his own aura. “What should I call you?”

Tiny red worms crawled out from the warrior's bloodshot eye sockets. Within seconds his face twisted into something hideous.

“Sura.”

Sensing the aura of the Ouroboros Seed within Fang Heng, Sura regarded him. “So it's you—the newborn who started another round of war. What's your name?”

“Fang Heng.”

Fang Heng studied Sura calmly. “Taking the Eye of Stars head-on with your body can't have felt good. You're in bad shape, I'm guessing. Otherwise you wouldn't be escaping like this.”

“So that thing was called the Eye of Stars. Indeed, a lot has happened while I've been dormant. The Kinsfolk have made considerable progress since I last saw them...”

Sura turned toward him with a faintly mocking smile. “But I wouldn't call this running away. The Kinsfolk are stronger than these allies of theirs. Yet they'd rather hand their allies all kinds of weapons than enter the battlefield themselves. Do you know why?”

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow. “Why?”

“Fear.”

Sura explained calmly, “The Kinsfolk fear us. We always find ways to spread that they could never anticipate. We can infiltrate anywhere, parasitize them, and take control. They can never wipe us out completely.”

She gazed at Fang Heng with a touch of admiration. “There is one thing you're right about, Fang Heng. I really am weak right now. This is your best opportunity to defeat me.”

Fang Heng stared at her, weighing how much was truth and how much was deception.

Who cared whether she was laying a trap?

Fight first, worry later!

“Whoosh!!”

Fang Heng lightly pushed off the floor and darted toward Sura. Blood energy formed a long, slender blade in his hand and slashed forward.

“Bang!!!”

Blood energy exploded!

Sura retreated rapidly.

The outer skin of the Crocodile Clan host she had parasitized shattered under the shock wave, exposing a writhing mass of insects beneath.

“Hiss, hiss...”

As though shedding an entire skin, Sura emerged from the crocodilian shell, revealing her own body, more than half of it already rotten.

Whoosh!

Sura suddenly halted in a bizarre motion. Instead of retreating further, she lunged at Fang Heng with astonishing speed.

She moved so fast that she became a black blur in midair.

“Bang!!”

Fang Heng raised his forearm to block.

Sura's arm twisted unnaturally and lashed at him from the opposite side like a whip!

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

Sura struck repeatedly, each impact producing a muffled thud within the passageway.

Fang Heng's expression gradually turned grave.

Just as he'd suspected.

On the earlier battlefield, Sura had been deceiving the Shadow Clan alliance, holding much of her strength in reserve.

So was Sura the representative of the Ouroboros Seeds specializing in physical enhancement?

While he considered this, Sura accelerated again.

“Whoosh!!!”

Surprise flickered in Fang Heng's eyes.

She could still get faster?

After three successive explosive surges in her physical attributes, the strength Sura displayed had almost caught up with Fang Heng's.

“Bang!!”

Fang Heng blocked another punch. Admiration shone in Sura's eyes. “Fang Heng! You've done well to hold on this long. How about we try another level?”

“Whoosh!!!!”

Before her words had faded, Sura's speed erupted again, climbing another tier.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and dodged sideways for the first time.

“Bang!!”

Sura's fist struck the starship's metal wall and punched a hole straight through it.

“Woooo...!”

Alarms immediately blared throughout the ship.

“Hmph.”

Seeing Fang Heng begin to dodge, Sura snorted and closed in again.

Another punch shot out!

Fang Heng anticipated it and crossed both arms in front of himself to defend.

“DOOONG!!!!”

The blow sounded like someone striking an enormous bell. Fang Heng was hurled backward until his back slammed into the alloy wall, leaving a massive dent!

He scrambled to his feet, checked his injuries, and resumed his fighting stance.

“You're quite strong yourself, Fang Heng.”

Now that she had seen his enhanced regeneration, a trace of concern appeared in Sura's eyes.

This was going to be troublesome.

She had entered dormancy on this world and had yet to recover fully. Then the Kinsfolk alliance attacked, and the Eye of Stars struck her again, leaving her grievously injured.

What she was displaying now was already the limit of her strength.

Otherwise, she would never have resorted to disguising herself and fleeing while the Kinsfolk alliance pursued her.

...

“Woooo!!!”

The alarm shrieked through the Crocodile Clan starship.

Crew members detected the hull-damage warning and immediately dispatched Crocodile Clan guards to investigate.

They had barely approached when a sonic boom and several heavy impacts rang out ahead.

The guards tensed, slowed down, and advanced cautiously along the corridor.

What was that?

Two dark figures were darting wildly through the passage, occasionally colliding with dull, thunderous thuds. The corridor was already riddled with holes.

Were two people fighting in there?

The Crocodile Clan guard captain swallowed nervously as he watched the figures, moving too quickly for him to distinguish their faces or even their outlines.

Terrifying!

The starship's alloy was exceptionally hard, yet in front of those two it might as well have been paper. They punched holes through it effortlessly.

For a time, warning indicators went off one after another across the ship's main control area.

The Crocodile Clan captain personally brought people to investigate and was dumbstruck by the sight.

“Who are they?!”

“I-I don't know...”

“Find a way to stop them!”

The guards wanted to cry.

Stop them?

Forget stopping them—they couldn't even aim at the two fighters!

The captain's heart ached as he surveyed the destruction.

You can fight all you want, but could you please stop doing it aboard my ship?!

— End of Chapter 2980 —
