Chapter 3079

Fierce Training

From Chinese Chapter 3080 · 很猛

Mu Shunan was very satisfied with Fang Heng's training progress. Seeing how fiercely the young man cultivated, he couldn't resist offering a kind warning.

In his view, the pan-human races had fairly ordinary natural talent and limited self-healing ability. No matter how gifted Fang Heng was, breaking through the Master-level stage ought to take several years.

But...

Come to think of it, this youngster was very young—about the same age as the recruits who had just joined the Noel Clan's training program.

“All right.”

Fang Heng agreed, slowly closed his eyes, and rapidly directed elemental energy through his body, trying to temper his flesh with its impact.

Elemental power raced through his meridians and gradually spilled into his body. He felt like an overinflated balloon as energy continuously leaked from the channels into his flesh.

Under the expanding power, his muscles, bones, and skin gradually strengthened. Then the pressure became too great to contain, and the tissues began to tear.

[Hint: Current Adaptability Enhancement Progress: 0.08%.]

A game notification flashed rapidly across Fang Heng's retina.

Under ordinary circumstances, another scholar would have had to stop training here. Continuing would inflict worse bodily injuries, potentially damaging the internal organs and requiring much longer to recover.

But Fang Heng was different. He possessed an undying body!

The moment his flesh was damaged, the Undying Body effect activated, rapidly repairing the injuries.

Lines of game notifications immediately appeared before his eyes.

[Hint: Current Adaptability Enhancement Progress: 0.12%.]

[Hint: Current Adaptability Enhancement Progress: 0.18%.]

Sensing the changes in his body and glancing at the game messages, Fang Heng immediately understood the principle behind this method of mysticism training.

Use specially controlled mental power to scour the body and improve its adaptability.

Repeated destruction, self-repair, destruction, self-repair...

The key was the body's ability to repair itself.

An ordinary person might need an extremely long time to cultivate this way.

But he was different!

With the Undying Body's terrifying regeneration, minor injuries to flesh and skin could heal in an extremely short time!

This meant he could sustain a high-intensity flow of mental energy, tempering his body continuously without interruption.

At least for now, the damage caused by that mental-energy flow was entirely offset by his Undying Body.

In that case...

Time to grind!

Fang Heng's heart stirred, and he immediately accelerated the circulation and expansion of mental power within his body!

Mu Shunan had been calmly supervising Fang Heng's practice, nodding to himself, when the sight suddenly gave him a tremendous fright.

Good grief!

Had this kid gone mad?

A dark-red hue could be seen beneath Fang Heng's skin, clear evidence that tremendous power was battering his body.

And he still wasn't stopping? He was continuing? Was he trying to get himself killed?

Mu Shunan's heart jumped. He was about to intervene when he noticed that Fang Heng's internal injuries weren't spreading. Instead, they were gradually stabilizing.

Hm? What was happening now?

Mu Shunan hesitated, lowered his hand, and carefully observed Fang Heng from the side.

What on earth was this kid doing?

Was his physical constitution simply far stronger than an ordinary person's?

No!

Mu Shunan quickly deduced that Fang Heng must possess some sort of self-healing ability!

Certain species were born with powerful regeneration or exceptionally robust bodies, giving them an advantage when cultivating mysticism.

But Mu Shunan had never heard of a pan-human possessing self-recovery this strong.

And it was terrifyingly powerful!

Even more startling was the extraordinarily intense elemental aura seeping from within Fang Heng!

It was strong enough to make Mu Shunan apprehensive, draining some of the color from his face.

A horrifying storm of elemental energy surged through Fang Heng's body!

He was like a compressed balloon.

He was forcibly pouring mental energy into his own flesh.

Good grief! His body was actually strong enough to withstand such violent elemental currents?

The information he'd been given was wrong. Fang Heng was definitely not merely a mid-tier warrior. Based on that mental power alone, Mu Shunan could confidently place him at high-tier!

And not only was the boy's mental power terrifying—his physical strength was monstrous too!

This was bad...

If he accidentally exploded, the entire building would be blown apart!

Mu Shunan grew more alarmed the longer he thought about it.

Judging by the violent fluctuations, elemental power should still be tearing Fang Heng's body apart.

How was he maintaining that state?

It seemed his body's regenerative abilities and the destruction caused by the energy had reached an uncanny equilibrium.

Utter madness!

Mu Shunan didn't dare interrupt him. If Fang Heng lost control because of a disturbance, he might really explode.

What had he gotten himself into?

Mu Shunan smiled bitterly. His paid lesson time had long since expired, but the session still wasn't over. Now he had to work overtime for free.

Fine.

He'd treat it as a chance to build goodwill.

Consoling himself, Mu Shunan used his mental power to form a protective barrier around Fang Heng. If something did go wrong, perhaps it would absorb part of the blast.

After a full two hours, Fang Heng gradually drew his violent aura back inside his body.

[Hint: Current Adaptability Enhancement Progress: 0.18%.]

[Hint: Player has completed the current stage of the quest.]

Fang Heng opened his eyes again, excitement gleaming within them.

Success!

Done!

Time to stop making up for skipped lessons!

“Phew!”

Mu Shunan also let out a tremendous sigh of relief.

He gave Fang Heng a reproachful look, dropped the protective barrier, and said, “Brother Fang Heng, I know you're talented, but next time you decide to risk your life, could you at least tell me first?”

Mu Shunan was thinking, If you're going to push yourself that hard, you're going to scare me to death!

“Thank you for protecting me, Brother Mu.”

Fang Heng smiled faintly and looked up at Mu Shunan with an eager glimmer in his eyes. “I've finished the body-tempering stage now. If you don't have anything else to do, Brother Mu Shunan, shall we move straight on to the next stage of training?”

“Huh?”

Mu Shunan stared at him in shock.

“What did you say?”

He'd finished the Master-level tempering stage?

And without any medicinal assistance?

Something that took an ordinary person at least three to five years had taken him barely over an hour?

Without waiting for an answer, Mu Shunan reached over and pressed his finger against Fang Heng's wrist.

As his mental power tried to penetrate Fang Heng's skin, it immediately encountered a smooth, unobstructed flow.

Good grief!

His entire body really had been opened up!

Mu Shunan withdrew his hand and stared at Fang Heng as though looking at a monster.

Unbelievable!

If someone had told him before today that this stage could be completed in just over two hours, he'd never have believed them.

Yet it had happened right before his eyes!

Was this what true talent looked like?
