Chapter 3014

Taking the Initiative

From Chinese Chapter 3015 · 主动出击

The Elven Clan's inheritance was ancient too. It wouldn't be surprising if they possessed some secret technique to control Abe Akaya remotely.

Unfortunately, the elves were also extremely secretive about the world they inhabited. Outsiders didn't even know its entrance.

In that case...

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

Only one path remained.

Strike first!

"Abe Akaya, can you sense how strong the other party is? How do they compare to you?"

A feeling came back through Abe Akaya's link.

Judging solely from the strength of the energy it could sense, the opponent was somewhat stronger than Abe Akaya, and seemed to have an advantage against it.

"I see..." Fang Heng pondered. "What if you advanced smoothly to the next stage and reached your Complete Form?"

Abe Akaya immediately conveyed a feeling of eager anticipation.

"Really...? Heh..." Fang Heng chuckled at its response. "You'd crush them? Abe Akaya, don't go making empty boasts..."

...

Somewhere in Hell.

Withering Flowers growing in cracks in the rock continually released pollen that bewildered the mind.

Several hellish fiends soaked comfortably in a pool of lava.

Sulfur bubbles suddenly rolled through the molten current. A chain-wrapped lava demon raised its head from the red magma and looked toward a three-headed hellhound feeding on the corpse of a lesser demon.

Lava gargoyles hanging from the cavern ceiling remained motionless as statues, with only their dark-red eyes visible.

A picture of peace and tranquility.

Then a faint spatial ripple appeared somewhere in the air.

The boundary between Hell and the outside world gradually blurred.

"Tap."

Fang Heng stepped out of a spatial passage into Hell.

"Flap! Flap!"

The lava gargoyles, extremely sensitive to power, were startled awake from their stone forms and fled deeper into the cavern, beating their wings furiously.

The lava fiends sensed the intruder and slowly rose from the molten pool.

The instant they recognized Fang Heng, terror shook their very souls. They dived back into the lava even faster and vanished.

The three-headed hellhounds lifted their heads from the lesser demon's corpse and growled in warning. Their bodies trembled with fear.

Fang Heng swept the surroundings with a cold gaze, settled it on the hellhounds, raised his hand, and casually snapped his fingers.

"Bang! Bang, bang, bang!"

Before they could escape, the hellhounds exploded into clouds of blood mist.

That was a little too conspicuous.

It would be bad if Demon Seed Envy noticed him.

Muttering to himself, Fang Heng slowly concealed his aura.

Entering Hell was simple for him. He only needed to build a Hell altar and have some Hell worshippers perform the Hell Gate ritual, easily opening a passage connecting Hell to another world.

After cooperating with Jing'er, Bai Mu, and the others in the Divine Realm, Jing'er's group had entered Hell.

Several Demon Seeds from Bai Mu's team believed Jing'er and his companions had found clues in Hell concerning Envy or the Demon Cores. They'd gone there to follow the trail.

Later, Bai Mu had sent Fang Heng a letter through a special channel.

The letter explained that Bai Mu's group had encountered Jing'er while exploring Hell.

Jing'er had indeed discovered how Envy controlled the Demon Cores, but the problem was far from simple. Even Jing'er and Bai Mu's combined teams couldn't solve it.

They planned to build up their strength. One group would remain to watch the Demon Cores in Hell, while another searched other worlds for Demon Seed allies.

Bai Mu said they'd soon travel through different worlds recruiting Demon Seeds to strengthen the resistance, while causing trouble for Envy wherever they could and diverting her attention.

Jing'er's team would stay in Hell, watching the Demon Cores and waiting for a chance to act against Hell's core.

At the end of the letter, Bai Mu had also provided Fang Heng with coordinates in Hell showing where Jing'er's group could be found.

When he received the letter, Fang Heng had still been constructing his starship fleet and was lost in outer space, unable to return home. Naturally, the matter had been put aside.

Nearly two months had passed. Who knew how Jing'er's close-quarters struggle was going now?

At least Envy's aura remained within his Sea of Consciousness, which meant she was still alive and well.

For Abe Akaya to advance to its next Complete Form, it needed to absorb seven different types of Hell origin power.

It had absorbed quite a lot at the Demon-Suppressing Temple in the Divine Realm, but still needed roughly half.

The greatest shortages were the powers of Fang Heng's own Greed and of Envy. Completing Abe Akaya's advancement would be especially difficult because of Envy's power.

After so many delays, it was time to settle the grudge he'd formed with that Demon Seed.

"Anyway, first let's see what trouble those Demon Seeds have run into."

Fang Heng extended his senses, checked his location, and headed toward the lower layers of Hell.

Hell was an enormous labyrinth.

A succession of divided spatial layers.

Each layer varied in size. Some had thousands of entrances to the layer below, while others had none at all. Sometimes reaching a deeper level required returning upward to find the true entrance.

Not even the Demon Seeds knew how many layers Hell contained.

Generally speaking, the deeper a layer, the stronger its fiends.

Jing'er's group was currently on Hell's twenty-ninth layer.

Bai Mu's letter had specifically warned him to conceal his aura, so Fang Heng didn't swagger openly through Hell. He switched academic forms, disguised himself as a Spatial Studies scholar, and used Secondary Space Projection to infiltrate the region.

He avoided hellish monsters whenever possible.

After nearly half a day, he finally reached the twenty-eighth layer.

In theory, Jing'er and his people were just one layer beneath him.

Fang Heng cautiously approached one of the passages leading downward.

As he neared the entrance to the next layer, the surroundings became unusually dark.

His footsteps made faint sounds against the hard ground.

In the darkness, two pairs of eyes tracked his every movement.

A fat man and a thin man exchanged a glance.

Their target possessed spatial abilities and was very good at hiding his aura.

He would be troublesome to deal with.

Attack!

"Whoosh!"

Two cold gleams burst from the shadows and exploded directly in front of Fang Heng!

His gaze instantly shifted toward the attackers.

Oh?

Demon Seeds?

"Bang!"

Fang Heng stepped back half a pace, raised his fist, and punched forward fiercely!

"Thud!"

He struck second but hit first! His punch generated a thunderous sonic boom.

A tremendously powerful shockwave erupted and hurled both Demon Seeds backward.

— End of Chapter 3014 —
