Chapter 2976

Awakening

From Chinese Chapter 2977 · 唤醒

The other alien races were gradually catching on too.

Good grief.

They'd invited the human into battle to test humanity's strength.

And that brat had immediately started farming cocoons?

None of the assembled allied races said anything aloud, but they knew perfectly well that every Ouroboros Seed cocoon could be exchanged for a considerable amount of Kinsfolk contribution.

Fang Heng had already collected a large number of cocoons in the brief time since joining the fight.

Those cocoons had originally been destined for division among the races participating in the main battle.

Now Fang Heng had taken them all for himself.

Worse still, the Shadow Clan had authorized this test of humanity's strength. Under the circumstances, the others could hardly object.

“Hum...”

A moment later, the Eye of Stars gathered energy high above for its second attack. Only then did Fang Heng reluctantly leave its blast radius, retreating to safety while clearly wishing he could farm a little longer.

“Boom!!!”

Purple light descended again, completely enveloping the area ahead, including the SSS-grade insect nest!

Purification!

Under the powerful energy beams' purifying effect, huge swathes of fungal carpet and insect units disintegrated directly into ash.

Even many Ouroboros Seed aggregates melted into pools of red slime beneath the beams.

Whoosh!!!

The moment the beams faded, before the other races had even seen what remained of the battlefield, Fang Heng turned into a dark blur and rushed in first from one side!

“Divine Punishment!”

“Boom!”

A bolt of lightning struck a mass of dark-red slime gathering on the ground, charring it completely. Fang Heng raised a hand, drew the Ouroboros cocoon into his palm, and rushed toward another mass of Seed slime.

What the hell?!

Only then did the other alien squads react. They rushed onto the battlefield and used their various techniques against the Ouroboros Seeds attempting to revive.

The broken Ouroboros Seeds had been gravely wounded and their health drastically reduced by the Eye of Stars' continuing purification effect. Only their special abilities kept them from dying outright.

In moments, the various allied forces flooding into the battlefield divided the spoils among themselves.

The Crocodile Clan was left rather embarrassed.

After all, their captain was the one who had invited Fang Heng into battle.

Who could have imagined he would be so fierce?

“Lord Shadow Guard, this...”

The Shadow Clan captain glanced over the battlefield and said coldly, “Continue advancing.”

“Yes!”

Thanks to Fang Heng's ferocious performance, the threat from the Ouroboros Seed aggregates had been resolved, and the warships once again slowly approached the massive central SSS-grade cocoon.

“Squish, squish...”

The giant SSS-grade cocoon had just suffered a severe blow from the Eye of Stars. It was now swelling and contracting continuously, while foul-smelling black liquid seeped out beneath its dark-brown hardened shell.

The Shadow Clan alliance warships gradually approached until they were within firing range.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!!”

Numerous focused energy beams shot from the ships, concentrating their fire on the central SSS-grade cocoon.

Fang Heng looked toward it. Under the sustained barrage, the cocoon's total health was falling only by the tiniest increments.

“Regroup!!!”

The flagship issued an order to assemble and withdraw.

The combat squads scattered across the battlefield quickly retreated toward the ships.

Although Fang Heng wanted to remain and farm a little longer, every alien unit on the front line was withdrawing. He wasn't prepared to cause trouble over this, so he too fell back and returned to the flagship.

“Thank you all for your hard work.”

When Fang Heng returned to the ship, several allied races felt an inexplicable sense of relief.

If they let him keep fighting like that, the brat would collect every last Ouroboros cocoon himself.

They would have to swallow this loss in silence. Next time, under no circumstances could they allow Fang Heng onto the battlefield again.

The crocodile captain said, “Allies, the cocoon's outer defenses are extremely strong. It seems even the Eye of Stars cannot break through in a short time.”

Everyone nodded inwardly.

Even after being struck twice by the Eye of Stars, the aggregated Ouroboros Seed nest still had an enormous amount of health.

“It seems we'll need more time to destroy its outer defenses completely.”

“We'll have to wear it down for longer...”

“There's no need to rush. We can slowly grind down the nest's strength...”

As the races discussed the situation, Fang Heng watched the Ouroboros Seed under concentrated fire from the warships and felt something stir in his mind.

He could sense power gathering rapidly deep within the nest.

The enemies they'd encountered before had all come from shattered cocoons.

Their strength had been relatively low.

But this time was different.

Judging by the Ouroboros Seed's aura, a complete 'Cultivator' was probably hidden inside the SSS-grade nest.

The Cultivator might have been badly wounded and fallen into dormancy.

'It' was now being rapidly awakened.

Fang Heng said, “I don't think that's necessarily the case.”

Everyone turned to look at him.

Fang Heng's performance just now had earned their respect.

The crocodile captain asked, “Fang Heng, what do you think?”

“The main body of the Ouroboros Seed in the SSS-grade nest was originally dormant. The earlier attacks have gradually awakened it, and it's recovering its strength rapidly.”

Watching the nest, Fang Heng continued in a low voice, “The nest has been drawing in its power and remaining on the defensive to buy time. Once its strength recovers to a certain limit, it will launch its own attack.”

The others listened in silence for a moment.

A captain of the Red-Pupil Clan snorted and retorted, “Hmph! Ridiculous! As far as I know, humanity only encountered Ouroboros Seeds recently. Since when have you known so much about them?”

“Indeed, nothing like that has ever happened with an Ouroboros before...”

A Shadow Clan representative raised a hand, stopping the objections, then looked up at Fang Heng. “Human, what evidence do you have for that claim?”

“None.”

Fang Heng shook his head. “I'm simply saying the Ouroboros Seed is very strong. The Eye of Stars can only assist us. Don't be careless.”

“Gurgle, gurgle, gurgle...”

As they argued, the SSS-grade nest suddenly began pulsing violently again, and dense waves of insects flooded out of the fungal carpet extending from it.

Another counterattack!

Huh?

Zaka suddenly noticed something and shouted, “Look!”

The nest and the ground beneath it trembled violently.

An opening split across the nest's crown, and the surrounding slabs of brown fleshy tissue slid apart like a flower bud unfurling.

At the top of the nest, a brown, balloon-like fleshy tumor slowly rose into the air.

The mass floated upward like a seed being hatched by the nest. Layer after layer of decaying matter peeled off its surface like the petals of a wilting flower, falling continuously from above.

Hmm?

The creature inside that growth...

An Ouroboros Seed.

A humanoid form?
