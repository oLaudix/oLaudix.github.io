Chapter 3035

Waterstar

From Chinese Chapter 3036 · 水之星

To reach the Elven Clan, they had no choice but to take a more direct approach: follow the direction indicated by Abe Akaya's senses and jump through space bit by bit, gradually approaching the region occupied by the elves until they found their actual position in the universe.

But the approach carried considerable risks.

At present, the Shadow Clan and various other races were deeply hostile to humanity, and the Kinsfolk were also extremely dissatisfied. A large human fleet making repeated jumps through outer space would have a high chance of being discovered and attacked by hostile races.

An encounter between major fleets in deep space wasn't part of Fang Heng's plans.

If they were unlucky enough to be located and locked onto in advance, the enemy could interfere with their spatial jumps, leading to even more disastrous losses.

Besides, Fang Heng had never intended to start a war as soon as he entered elven territory.

After some planning, the Flying Squirrel Clan eventually stepped forward and lent Fang Heng their small ship, the Mouse King, to carry him through the sea of stars toward the elves.

The ship used space-folding technology, jumping repeatedly between locations and gradually closing in on the Elven Clan's world.

After all, the Flying Squirrel Clan hadn't offended the Shadow Clan or the Kinsfolk.

Of course, they were taking enormous risks as well.

But considering the generous reward Fang Heng had offered, the Flying Squirrels felt quite confident.

Captain Kappa scratched his head.

There was just one thing he couldn't understand.

Humans clearly had the ability to mine ore happily and profitably. Why would they go looking for trouble with the Elven Clan?

And knowing Boss Fang's personality—he never got out of bed unless there was something to gain.

Could it be that the elves had even richer mineral deposits and resources?

“Click!”

The door to the main control room opened.

“Boss Fang.”

A smile immediately spread across Kappa's face when he saw Fang Heng.

Facing such a wealthy, important customer, Kappa beamed. “We're approaching the coordinates of the Elven Clan's world that you sensed. If all goes smoothly, after our next spatial jump in about ten minutes, we'll enter the elves' alert zone. We may encounter elven patrols then, so we should prepare carefully.”

“Mm, all right. Thanks for your hard work.”

Fang Heng gathered his attention and gazed out through the glass at the sea of stars.

It had been almost a week of travel. They'd finally found where the Elven Clan lived.

“Also, Boss Fang, we just received a message for you from our main base.”

A Flying Squirrel assistant approached respectfully and handed Fang Heng a letter.

Fang Heng opened it and scanned it quickly. His brows drew together involuntarily.

It was an emergency communication from the Human Federation.

Federation fleets had detected large numbers of ships gathering in an outer star system. Their most likely target was the human world.

Meanwhile, the Kinsfolk had issued a final ultimatum, demanding that the Western Federation complete the first phase of its Star Map main control base within seven days, or they wouldn't guarantee what happened next.

Kappa said, “Boss Fang, our Flying Squirrel mission system hasn't been blocked by the Kinsfolk's central intelligence. We just checked and found that the Kinsfolk have issued a mission targeting humanity. The Shadow Clan has already accepted it alongside the allied races and is gathering forces to attack the human world.”

The Shadow Clan again!

What a pain in the ass!

Fang Heng felt deeply annoyed. “How long would it take us to get back?”

“The ship has coordinates for the human world. A direct jump back would take twelve hours.”

“Let's continue. There's still time. First, we'll go and see what's happening with the elves.”

After considering it, Fang Heng had them send a reply to tell the fleet not to worry. He would find a way to return within seven days.

“Understood.”

A few minutes later, another folded-space jump began, and golden light blossomed ahead of the ship.

“Hum...!”

Moments later, the jump ended. The light ahead dispersed completely, revealing a star field.

The Elven Clan's world?

Fang Heng looked out through the main control room's alloy glass, studying his surroundings.

The spatial jump had brought them into a new region of space.

“Abe Akaya, which planet belongs to the elves?”

Hmm?

It wasn't sure?

Fang Heng frowned slightly.

According to Abe Akaya's feedback, the elves' world lay somewhere in this star field, but the source of the connection was affected by a masking force. Its positioning had shifted.

The target indicated a location where nothing existed.

“Is that so?”

Fang Heng murmured to himself.

Now that it was closer to the life-form draining its power, Abe Akaya could sense the enemy more clearly.

By Abe Akaya's estimate, it still wasn't as strong as its opponent.

It was a little weaker.

But Abe Akaya could feel the other entity's abilities rapidly declining.

Meanwhile, its own strength was still rising as it continually absorbed Hell's power.

As one declined and the other grew stronger, even if they did nothing for a few more years, their relative strengths would completely reverse.

“Huh?”

Flying Squirrel captain Kappa noticed something and let out a quiet sound of surprise. He immediately directed a ship operator to search the Kinsfolk system for information.

“Kappa, did you find something?”

Kappa pointed to a planet outside the window. “Yes, Boss Fang. We've been in this star field before. That planet is a gathering place for the Waterstar Clan.”

“Waterstar...”

“The Waterstar Clan is fairly average in strength among the Kinsfolk's allied races. They're peaceful and rarely participate in combat missions issued by the Kinsfolk.”

The ship operator quickly typed on the console. Lines of information about the Waterstar Clan appeared on the screen at the right.

“We've discovered that the Waterstar Clan has recently posted a special mission. They're offering high prices for a special item called an Energy Seed, and they're recruiting scholars proficient in both Natural Studies and ancient scripts.”

Kappa studied the notice and continued, “Hmm. They've had this mission posted for quite a long time.”

Energy Seeds?

Fang Heng immediately sensed something unusual.

He remembered that they were highly concentrated masses of energy capable of storing enormous quantities of natural energy. They looked like tiny grains of rice.

Abe Akaya could condense this kind of seed too.

“Interesting. It seems the Waterstar Clan may have some connection to the elves,” Fang Heng said. “Kappa, accept the collection mission and contact the Waterstar Clan. Don't alarm them. We'll approach through normal channels.”

“All right.”

Kappa's greatest fear was that Fang Heng would start killing and drag the Flying Squirrel Clan into the conflict. Hearing this, he sighed in relief. He accepted the mission and contacted the Waterstar Clan ahead of time to request permission for the ship to approach their planet.

If they could settle the dispute through communication, so much the better.

Soon, the Waterstar Clan replied with coordinates for the ship's next jump.
