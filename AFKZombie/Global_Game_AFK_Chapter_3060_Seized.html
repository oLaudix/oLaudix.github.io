Chapter 3060

Seized

From Chinese Chapter 3061 · 占据

“Pfft!”

The three Shadow Clan members controlling the Shadow Spheres spat out blood almost simultaneously.

Fang Heng broke free of his restraints and appeared behind Evans, the Shadow Clan ship captain, almost as though he had teleported. He thrust out a hand, trying to seize Evans by the neck.

Not good!

Evans caught the trajectory of Fang Heng’s movement. Fear flashed in his eyes as he hurriedly transformed into shadow to dodge.

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss, hiss!”

But this was entirely different from when he had used Shadow Transformation to evade Fang Heng’s earlier attack. As Fang Heng’s right hand passed through the shadow, the entire black mass shook violently as if boiling, accompanied by a continuous sizzling sound.

The shadow evaporated rapidly!

“Pfft!”

Evans coughed up blood. Fang Heng had forcibly knocked him out of his shadow form, sending him flying backward into the wall with a heavy crash.

When Evans raised his head again, he looked as though he had aged more than ten years. He stared at Fang Heng, terror flickering in his eyes.

Just who was this guy?

Not only could he completely break through Shadow Transformation, but that last punch had carried a power so terrifying Evans could barely comprehend it.

An utterly icy aura of death had dissolved more than half his life force in an instant.

“You... who exactly are you...?”

“Who I am doesn't matter, my Shadow Clan friends. What matters a great deal to me is getting rid of you.”

As soon as he finished speaking, the dense cluster of Nether Orbs floating around Fang Heng shot toward Evans.

“Whoosh! Whoosh!”

Evans’s pupils contracted sharply. Instinctively, he tried to enter shadow form again to avoid the attack. But the next instant, the dispersed black shadow was utterly shattered and purified by the Nether Orbs’ successive bombardment.

Fang Heng turned to look at the remaining Shadow Clan members in the hall, all of whom were tense to the extreme.

More than a dozen elite Shadow Clan warriors stared at Fang Heng. Their bodies trembled involuntarily as they slowly edged backward.

Never had they seen someone counter the Shadow Clan’s Shadow Transformation so completely. Not even the Kinsfolk could do that!

“Hmph.”

Fang Heng gave a faint snort.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

The Nether Orbs circling him activated once more, automatically searching for and attacking enemies.

In just a few moments, every Shadow Clan member in the main control room had been eliminated by the Nether Orbs.

“Mr. Fang, your strength is truly astonishing.”

Deborah, the Elven Clan’s Grand Elder, sighed in admiration. To her surprise, she even felt a measure of relief.

Thank goodness the elves had turned back in time instead of following the Shadow Clan all the way down that dark road and joining their attack on the human world.

Otherwise, they might have died without even knowing how.

“You flatter me, Grand Elder.”

Fang Heng glanced around before looking outside the ship.

“Let's move to the next ship.”

“Of course. As you wish.”

At this moment, the various species allied with the Shadow Clan still had no idea that the supreme commander aboard the Shadow Clan flagship had been killed. With no orders coming from him, they continued fighting according to their own circumstances, fending off the alien insects swarming in from every direction.

Suddenly, on one of the vessels, an alien fighting on the deck heard something like a faint rustling. He instinctively turned toward the sound behind him.

The moment he looked, he could no longer tear his eyes away. He stared at another vessel in the distance, his expression turning to utter horror.

“Look! What is that?!”

Everyone followed his shout and looked over.

What the hell?

From the cracks and crevices all over a ship belonging to their own alliance, dark-red vine branches were suddenly sprouting.

In almost an instant, the vines wrapped around the entire hull.

“That looks like the elves' ship.”

Someone recognized it immediately.

“The Elven Clan? I heard they're proficient in natural studies and good at using plants.”

The elves specialized in nature and could control plants, so there was nothing inexplicable about their using vines.

But that wasn't the end of it.

The vines spilling from that ship swiftly gathered toward the vessel on its right!

They climbed onto the other ship at astonishing speed!

And that vessel was the Shadow Clan flagship!

At some point, the Shadow Clan flagship had stopped firing on outside targets.

The dark-red vines visibly swallowed the flagship bit by bit. In the blink of an eye, its exterior was covered, and the tendrils were even pushing into the ship through its various entrances.

What on earth was this thing?

Several captains reacted at once and tried to contact the Shadow Clan flagship.

But there was no response.

Every communication vanished into silence.

Within barely two minutes, the entire flagship had been swallowed and surrounded by vines. From outside, it resembled a gigantic, dark-red cocoon, entirely wrapped in tendrils and constantly pulsing.

One captain of a Shadow Clan auxiliary vessel still had no idea what had happened. He was repeatedly attempting to contact the flagship when a report came in: a request for a warp transfer had arrived from the flagship.

What was going on?

Everything about it was suspicious!

The captain sensed that something was wrong. But he could never have imagined that someone had boarded the flagship and killed its captain. He assumed communications had been unexpectedly disrupted by some interference, leaving them no choice but to send a messenger in person.

The captain made the worst decision of his life.

“Authorize the transfer!”

“Yes!”

The warp passage opened.

To the Shadow Clan members' confusion, a masked young man stepped out of the transfer passage.

“Who are you...?”

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!”

Before the Shadow Clan members could finish questioning him, the twenty-four Nether Orbs circling Fang Heng launched themselves at everyone in the main control room.

“Watch out!”

Confronted with such an attack, the Shadow Clan members almost instinctively entered shadow form to dodge.

Only in the next instant did they realize how badly they had underestimated those strange black spheres.

“Boom! Boom, boom, boom!”

Despite being in shadow form, the Shadow Clan members exploded one after another beneath the Nether Orbs' assault!

The entire main control room fell silent within moments.

...

Outside, the alien species aboard the various ships were still utterly bewildered, with no idea what was happening.

What was going on with those vines?

Could the Elven Clan and Shadow Clan be testing some new tactic?

After a brief lull, everyone saw the vines begin growing and spreading again, racing toward the next Shadow Clan ship!

One ship after another.

In less than five minutes, almost every ship belonging to the Shadow Clan had been wrapped in layer upon layer of vines.

Then the vines suddenly became restless. They shot outward again, spreading toward all the surrounding vessels almost simultaneously!

“Report! Captain, an unidentified object has entangled the ship!”

“Captain! Our external shield energy is dropping sharply!”

The vines were blocked by the energy shields around the ships, but they did not stop. Instead, they spread rapidly over the shield surfaces, and countless tiny eyeballs emerged along the tendrils.

Eyes of Hell!

— End of Chapter 3060 —
