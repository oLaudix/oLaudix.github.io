Chapter 2145

Invasion

From original Chinese Chapter 2146 · 入侵

What?!

The institute director's heart skipped a beat.

They disappeared?!

What was that?

Spatial transition?

“No. It isn't spatial transition. It's another application of spatial power.”

Ji Haiting watched the Lickers rapidly vanish into space, anticipation shining in his eyes.

He'd seen Fang Heng use this ability before.

He'd never imagined the Lickers could possess it too!

Ji Haiting said quietly, “The Dependents aren't the only beings in this world who possess powers beyond our era.”

The director glanced at Ji Haiting and obediently shut his mouth.

A moment later, hordes of Lickers appeared on the other side of the defensive field, having bypassed it entirely!

What the hell?!

Everyone in the institute was shocked.

They'd gone straight through?

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Having crossed the outer defenses, the Lickers immediately charged deeper into the institute!

“Stop them! Activate every defense network! Where are the Federation troops? Have they deployed yet?!”

The director panicked. He'd believed the institute's defenses were impregnable. Yet barely three minutes after the Lickers appeared, they were already on the verge of breaking in?!

“Stop them at all costs!”

The beam cannons arranged around the institute activated together and turned toward the charging Lickers.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Dense beams locked onto the creatures and blasted them!

Explosions erupted everywhere.

The Lickers were blown into bloody ruin.

Yet after a moment's lull, the scattered creatures lying half-dead on the ground began recovering at an astonishing rate!

“Damn it!”

Witnessing their terrifying regenerative ability firsthand sent chills down the onlookers' spines.

“Where are the anti-virus-body agents?!”

“They've been distributed! The teams are on their way! They'll be there any moment!”

Damn it!

The institute director looked outside, his right hand trembling involuntarily.

Three Federation semi-mechanized squads were immediately equipped with injection guns and sent to assist in containing the Lickers.

At that moment, the Lickers exploited a brief pause while the beam cannons overheated and launched a second assault on the institute!

They rushed forward into close combat with the semi-mechanically modified soldiers blocking the entrance, while others leaped onto the external beam cannons to damage and destroy them!

A small number even slipped through narrow gaps and entrances all around the building.

“Report! Several of the institute's defenses are under attack!”

“Report! Lickers have infiltrated sectors A and C! Foreign objects detected at a ventilation-duct entrance in sector D! Unknown-creature intrusion alerts in sectors E and G!”

“Report!” another researcher shouted. “We've detected a persistent source of unknown spatial energy fluctuations. We suspect it's the source of the enemy Lickers!”

The source?

Ji Haiting looked interested and turned around. “Have you located it? Where are the fluctuations coming from?”

“Tracking now.”

On the display, an exterior camera steadily zoomed out and pointed farther forward, until the image became somewhat blurry.

Everyone in the operations room stared at the screen.

A dark magic array appeared at its center.

Suspended above the ground was a crimson teleportation gate.

Lickers were constantly pouring out of the array!

The sight sent a chill down everyone's back.

They were eliminating Lickers far more slowly than new ones were being teleported onto the battlefield.

Studying the crimson gate, the director frowned in confusion. “A teleportation passage?”

“Mm. It seems that if we want to eliminate the Lickers completely, we'll need to find a way to attack that passage.”

Meanwhile, Fang Heng stood at the rear of the battlefield, remotely directing the Lickers.

Rows of game notifications scrolled across his retina.

[Hint: The player's zombie clone (Licker form) has infiltrated the institute and damaged defensive installation A. The player gains 200 Tree Whisperer Alliance favorability and 300 Tree Whisperer Alliance contribution points...]

[Hint: The player's zombie clone (Licker form) has defeated a Federation semi-mechanically modified guard... The player gains 1 Tree Spirit contribution point.]

Heh. Not bad at all.

Lickers really were much more useful than ordinary zombies!

As they infiltrated the research institute and caused destruction, his Tree Whisperer Alliance contribution points climbed rapidly.

Every so often, he even received a Tree Spirit contribution point.

That was unexpected.

But how was he supposed to exchange those Tree Spirit points for anything?

As Fang Heng considered where to look, another notification appeared on his retina.

[Hint: For unknown reasons, the player's favorability with the Tree Spirits has decreased by 10.]

What did that mean?

Fang Heng froze again.

After everything he'd done to help the Tree Spirits, his favorability hadn't increased at all—and now it had dropped another ten points?

What kind of nonsense was this?

Was the main quest deliberately making things harder for him?

“Heh. The Tree Spirits...”

Fang Heng cursed the trouble inwardly and turned toward the Federation semi-mechanized soldiers emerging from the institute's right-hand passage.

The Federation and Wanling Technology had initially been caught completely off guard by the Lickers.

But they soon responded. Equipped with new injection guns, the semi-mechanized soldiers advanced toward the Lickers in formation.

“Fire!”

“Clear those Lickers out!” shouted the Federation officer leading them, Musha.

Under concentrated fire, bloody holes erupted across the Lickers' bodies and they were hurled backward!

The second line switched to long-range injection guns and sent a dense volley of needles into the fallen creatures.

Large numbers of Lickers promptly lost their Undying Body enhancements.

At the same time, heavy war machines entered from both flanks.

With beam weapons sweeping across the battlefield, severed Licker limbs and mangled bodies soon covered the ground!

More and more Lickers rushed forward without the slightest fear of death.

They formed a living wall of flesh and blood!

The Tree Spirits had noticed the Lickers appearing from the rear long ago. Their initial disdain had turned to surprise.

By now, their expressions showed genuine astonishment.

What kind of creatures were these?

The Lickers weren't emitting any discernible energy fluctuations at all.

They appeared to rely mainly on physical strength!

And the Tree Spirits could sense a ferocious, violent aura coming from them.

It made them deeply uncomfortable.

At the back of the battlefield, more than a dozen Tree Spirit elders assembled and exchanged glances.

Apparently these creatures were allies.

Taking advantage of the chaos created by the Lickers, the Tree Spirits seized the opportunity to clear out the defensive machines throughout the research complex.

— End of Chapter 2145 —
