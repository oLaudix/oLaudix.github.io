Chapter 2266

Division

From original Chinese Chapter 2267 · 分裂

The Tree Spirits possessed a special method of dividing a Divine Tree sapling into a main body and subsidiary offshoots.

The advantage was that spreading out the Divine Tree allowed it to absorb energy more rapidly and grow faster.

There were drawbacks, however.

Once separated, the sapling's overall strength was drastically reduced. If an offshoot died, its main body would suffer severe damage. Moreover, an offshoot couldn't return the energy it absorbed to the main body until the two had been reunited.

They discussed the problem for ages but still found no better solution.

Fang Heng rubbed his temples.

All right. In that case, they would have to use another method.

Borrow someone else's hen to lay your own eggs.

There was no doubt that this approach carried greater risks.

"Fang Heng, the White Froststone ore is ready. We can deliver it to Leaf Clan territory whenever you want."

While everyone was discussing the issue, Ji Haiting arrived at the research laboratory, accompanied by two guards. "By the way, a few more things. Our investigation shows that the Wood Clan has reoccupied the Prayer-Curse Temple. The Federation has secured a partial victory in this operation and is preparing to strike several openly rebellious Tree Whisperer Alliance groups to curb the Tree Spirits' arrogance."

"Also, the Federation's Psychic Studies training program is continuing. They've decided to expand their elite psychic corps, and they'll need your help again. They hope you can intensify the conflicts among the various Tree Spirit clans and give us an advantage."

"I will. Next, I'll go back to the Leaf Clan and see how things are going. For now, everything proceeds according to our original plan."

"Be careful, and stay in touch."

Once preparations were complete, Fang Heng collected another truckload of White Froststone from the Federation and transported it to the Leaf Clan's Prayer-Curse Temple in the primeval forest.

...

That afternoon, at a Prayer-Curse Temple near the edge of the primeval forest.

Unlike the Wood Clan, the Leaf Clan had discovered and taken control of five Prayer-Curse Temples that were still operational.

Vannis had other duties to attend to. After settling the Divine Tree sapling, he had hurried away with his people, leaving two newly appointed elders to guard it.

Ovi was busily directing the clan members in an extensive renovation of the temple.

Two Leaf Clan members came forward to report.

"Team Leader Ovi, three Federation trucks are approaching. Should we stop them?"

"No, that's Fang Heng. He must have brought the White Froststone." Ovi rose from his place beside the array. "Come, let's go meet him."

Outside, the heavy trucks slowed and finally stopped in a clearing beside the temple.

The clansmen began unloading crates of ore.

Fang Heng jumped from the cab and waved to Ovi. "Mission accomplished. I got the White Froststone."

"Wonderful!" Ovi came forward and led him into the temple. "Fang Heng, we've made extensive adjustments to the arrays as you instructed, but there are quite a few details we don't understand. We're not sure whether we missed anything."

"No problem. I'll inspect everything myself."

Fang Heng nodded and carefully surveyed the entire Prayer-Curse Temple.

"Is this place secure? We won't be attacked by the other clans again, will we?"

"Don't worry. It's very safe. The clan sent two elders to guard it personally, Elder Vannis will visit regularly, and we're close to the settlement. If anything happens, reinforcements can arrive within an hour."

"Good."

"Fang Heng, there's one more thing." Ovi looked troubled and sighed. "We've run into a problem."

Fang Heng pursed his lips, unimpressed.

Tree Spirits, after all.

For them, running into trouble was perfectly normal.

It would be a miracle if everything went smoothly.

For example, ever since Fang Heng had arrived, several Leaf Clan members had followed him everywhere.

Their surveillance was barely disguised.

"What problem? Don't tell me it's Vannis."

"I've checked the materials list for reconstructing the arrays and the temple, and reported it to Elder Vannis. But the clan can't supply most of the ores and Leaves of Life. They'll only give us a small portion."

Ovi sighed helplessly.

He trusted Fang Heng's ability to modify the magic arrays.

But the clan's hostility toward humans ran deep.

There was no way he could change that alone.

"I can get some of the materials, but even the humans can't obtain certain rare ores. As for the Leaves of Life, I have no alternative source."

Fang Heng was helpless, too.

Leaves of Life were an indispensable consumable in one part of the array modifications.

They were required in small quantities during initial construction and would also be needed for ongoing maintenance.

No wonder the Leaf Clan treasured them so much.

Ovi felt guilty toward Fang Heng.

This human was already spending his own money to help them, yet Ovi couldn't even persuade his own clan to provide supplies.

Gritting his teeth, Ovi made up his mind. "All right, Fang Heng. Let's compare what we can get. Make a list of the ores you can obtain, and I'll figure out how to secure the rest. I promise I'll get them!"

"Mm."

As they spoke, they entered the temple's main hall.

A large magic array had been inscribed across the floor.

In Fang Heng's view, Vannis knew little more than the basics of magic arrays.

He lacked knowledge of many elementary principles of alchemy.

It was as though Fang Heng had studied the subject systematically, whereas Vannis had started halfway through and was trying to reverse-engineer the answers without understanding the questions.

Much of Vannis's reasoning rested on assumptions rather than underlying principles.

In fact, hardly any of the Tree Spirits seemed to understand alchemy very well.

So Fang Heng would have to handle the actual array construction himself!

That was fine.

He still had his zombie clones to help.

They could complete a large proportion of the basic array work.

That would save him considerable effort.

Fang Heng flexed his wrists and walked toward the array.

"Ovi, building the formation will take a while. Let's get started."

"Right!" Ovi nodded eagerly. "What do you need me to do?"

"Just ask everyone who isn't involved to leave."

"Huh?"

Ovi froze.

That was all?

The ridiculous part was that he couldn't even accomplish something so simple.

Vannis was deeply suspicious of Fang Heng and had specifically assigned several people to watch him constantly.

"All right, then. At least ask them to keep quiet."

Soon, everyone except Ovi and the two observers had been cleared from the hall.

Standing before the array, Fang Heng took a demon-faced mask from his backpack and put it on.

"Whoosh, whoosh..."

A magic array spun rapidly around Fang Heng.

More than twenty Lickers emerged and landed on the floor, where they swiftly formed fleshy cocoons.

Ordinary zombies crawled out of the cocoons and began dismantling and rebuilding the formation inscribed on the floor.
