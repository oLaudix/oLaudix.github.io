Chapter 3015

Exploring Hell

From Chinese Chapter 3016 · 探查地狱

What the hell?

Both Demon Seeds were surprised to be repelled with a single strike.

This guy was strong!

Again!

They didn't realize Fang Heng had held back. They merely thought he was formidable, not beyond their combined ability. The blow only aroused their ferocity.

"Whoosh! Whoosh!"

The thinner Demon Seed conjured a seal before his chest. He and his heavyset partner flanked Fang Heng, one on each side and one slightly ahead of the other.

At the same time, the solid ground around Fang Heng rapidly transformed into searing lava. Several molten tendrils rose from the swamp-like magma to bind him in place.

Fang Heng didn't attempt to evade. He simply watched them approach and said calmly, "Don't be so quick to fight. I'm here to see Jing'er."

"I don't give a damn who you are! Get on your fucking knees!"

Perhaps eager to recover his pride after Fang Heng's first blow, the thin Demon Seed, Liu Mo, laughed angrily and attacked even more fiercely.

"Boom! Boom, boom!"

Stone pillars erupted from the molten ground.

Their interiors were honeycombed with crystals. Violent energy vibrations sent tens of thousands of sharp crystal shards spraying outward like a fan-shaped shotgun blast!

Huh?

To their amazement, Fang Heng still made no attempt to dodge, allowing the superheated shards to slam into him.

He was simply standing there and letting them attack?

Fuck! He was looking down on them?!

Liu Mo was even more enraged by Fang Heng's apparent contempt.

Then he'd make him explode!

"Boom! Boom, boom, boom!"

The dense lava crystals detonated one after another on Fang Heng's body.

"Whoosh!"

Using the scattering fragments as cover, Liu Mo flew straight toward Fang Heng.

"Die, idiot!"

"Whoosh!"

He threw a punch into Fang Heng's chest, but to his horror, it felt like striking a wall of solid bronze. His fist couldn't advance another inch.

It was as though the force of his punch had been completely absorbed!

What?

Shock flashed across Liu Mo's face.

He hadn't even broken the defense?

Even Jing'er would never have dared absorb his entire chain of attacks head-on.

Yet this opponent had stood still and taken everything without dodging.

"Enough. I've already told you I'm here to see Jing'er."

Fang Heng spoke calmly, seized Liu Mo's extended fist, and tossed him bodily backward.

Huh?

Looking down, Fang Heng saw a scarlet magic array rapidly forming around the mark Liu Mo's fist had left on his skin.

Liu Mo couldn't control his flight, but a bloodthirsty smile appeared on his face even as he hurtled away.

"Fatty! Kill him!"

"Hm?"

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

Marcus, the Demon Seed who had transformed into a blood shadow and disappeared from his senses during the attack, suddenly reappeared. A face squirmed out of the magic array on Fang Heng's skin. Marcus emerged, opened his mouth wide, and bit viciously into the right half of Fang Heng's body!

"Bang!"

With a single bite, Marcus tore apart and swallowed most of Fang Heng's right side, including his shoulder!

"Ha! Got him!"

The fat man shouted in triumph after swallowing the flesh. But the next moment, Fang Heng clamped a hand tightly around his neck.

"What?!"

Liu Mo hadn't even had time to celebrate. He watched Fang Heng, half his body missing, seize Marcus's neck with his remaining hand and forcibly drag the fat man, inch by inch, out of the magic array fused to his chest!

"Bang!"

Marcus's world spun as he was flung away, crashing hard into the rock wall.

What?!

Marcus and Liu Mo struggled up one after the other and stared at Fang Heng in fear.

The shredded right half of his body wriggled grotesquely and regenerated at a speed visible to the naked eye. The magic array's traces on his chest quickly vanished too.

Fang Heng's expression darkened, and his voice turned colder. "I told you. I'm here to find Jing'er."

After being attacked again and again, anger finally rose within him.

If his strength hadn't increased so dramatically recently, their coordinated combination might genuinely have caught him off guard.

"An interesting technique. Now it's my turn."

Without allowing either man to speak, Fang Heng raised a hand and pointed at Liu Mo.

Liu Mo's heart sank. With Fang Heng's hand directed at him, his body slowly lifted into the air against his will.

What?!

Liu Mo panicked as he realized he'd lost all control of his body.

How was this happening?

Marcus was equally stunned. He stared at Fang Heng, sensed sacred energy leaking from him, and began trembling involuntarily.

They were finished.

Why did he feel such an instinctive, inborn terror?

Who was this kid, and why was he radiating sacred power?

Was he from the Holy Court?

But why would anyone from the Holy Court come to a hellhole like this?

"Boom!"

A thunderclap suddenly reverberated through the dark cavern.

Divine Punishment!

A violet bolt of lightning struck Liu Mo instantly.

His expression changed abruptly.

Unlike the superficial wounds from the earlier attacks, Divine Punishment lightning inflicted devastating damage to his very origin!

"Boom! Boom, boom!"

Lightning descended repeatedly.

Each bolt struck Liu Mo, but Marcus trembled in terror beside him, his heart lurching with every impact.

Liu Mo clenched his teeth so hard he couldn't speak. He glared at Fang Heng, eyes blazing with wrath.

Every lightning strike hit his origin and could permanently diminish his strength for a time. He felt his fundamental power being weakened, step by step.

Soon, perhaps, the lightning would shatter him completely.

"Fang Heng."

A voice emerged from the darkness.

"That's enough, don't you think? He's learned his lesson. Do me a favor and spare him. He'll still be useful later."

Fang Heng turned his attention away from Liu Mo and toward the nearby shadows.

Jing'er emerged with two Demon Seeds from the Divine Realm.

"Hmph."

Fang Heng snorted and flicked his hand.

Bang!

Liu Mo was flung sideways, slamming into the rock wall and leaving a huge depression.

"Congratulations. Your strength has improved greatly since we last met."

Jing'er had been on the twenty-ninth layer. Only after sensing the sacred-energy fluctuations above had he come to investigate. Recognizing Fang Heng, he was privately astonished.

Fang Heng's power had grown considerably since their last encounter. Even Jing'er could no longer see the limits of his strength.

This kid...

How the hell did he cultivate?

How could his strength increase so frighteningly fast?

Fang Heng stepped forward with a smile. "Jing'er, you're not easy to find. It's been a while. What have you been hiding down here doing?"

— End of Chapter 3015 —
