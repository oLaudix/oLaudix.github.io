Chapter 2199

A Speedrun

From original Chinese Chapter 2200 · 快速通关

There was no telling how far the narrow ravine stretched ahead.

How many venomous snakes were hiding along the route?

Who could possibly withstand that?

With nothing but their beam weapons, clearing all those snakes would take forever!

At a loss, the group turned almost as one toward Fang Heng, though no one could say who'd looked first.

Fang Heng was the last person to join their team, personally brought by Elder Osman.

Perhaps he had a solution?

Anna swallowed and was about to ask when his pupils suddenly contracted.

What was that?

Fang Heng stood still, took a mask from his backpack, and put it on.

Hiss… hiss, hiss…

In the next instant, rapidly spinning magic circles materialized around him!

Dark-red creatures sprang out of the circles and raced toward the passage ahead!

Lickers?!

Anna was startled.

The venomous snakes hiding in the gorge were disturbed, surging from the rock crevices and lunging at the intruders!

Their poisonous fangs sank viciously into the Lickers' flesh.

[Hint: The player's zombie clone (Licker Form) has been bitten, taking 1,220 damage and suffering the Severe Poison effect…]

[Hint: The player's Undying Body effect has activated. The player's Severe Poison effect has been removed.]

Severe Poison!

Under the effect of the venom, the Lickers' bodies briefly took on a dark-purple hue. But Undying Body automatically removed negative conditions, cleansing the poison every 0.5 seconds.

The Lickers returned to normal immediately.

From the outside, their skin simply appeared to keep changing color.

Fang Heng quickly scanned the notifications.

The Severe Poison effect could stack. At first, even a low-level player could endure it, but once it accumulated hundreds of stacks, even a boss would be unable to withstand it.

And the snakes had a major terrain advantage, hiding in cracks where it was almost impossible to guard against their attacks.

Ordinary players would have a real headache dealing with them.

Unfortunately for the snakes, his Undying Body was a perfect counter to Severe Poison.

The swarm of Lickers surged down the gorge, even scaling the sheer rock walls to clear out snakes from every crevice they passed.

Anna and his companions watched in astonishment. They finally realized who the real powerhouse in their team was, and wisely stopped making any rash moves, following Fang Heng in silence.

“Let's go.”

Fang Heng took the lead and headed deeper into the gorge.

Anna and the others exchanged glances, put away their beam weapons, and followed.

They moved farther into the gorge along the path the Lickers had cleared. Before long, the terrain began to open up.

After jogging forward for another ten minutes or so, they finally left the gorge behind.

Ahead stood part of the massive trunk and branches of a Divine Tree.

The Lickers had found something and gathered beneath the tree.

Fang Heng looked ahead, beneath the Divine Tree's canopy that blotted out the sky.

A region was shrouded in faint mist.

Enormous roots emerged from the ground, intertwining to form a fortress-like structure beneath the tree.

The entrance was made from several exceptionally thick roots that bent and twisted together into an irregular archway.

Fang Heng frowned as a word surfaced in his mind.

“A maze…”

He murmured to himself.

Anna and the others caught up and stared at the arch leading into the maze.

“Trouble.”

Anna studied the passage with a grave expression. “Looks like a root maze. I've heard there are similar mazes around the outside of the Council of Elders. No two are alike. Entering recklessly can leave you trapped, and finding your way out quickly is extremely difficult.”

“And this one looks even bigger.” Another member peered cautiously inside, clearly still unnerved by the snakes. “Who knows what dangers might be lurking in there?”

Fang Heng extended his perception.

The mist covering the maze seemed to devour it, swallowing most of the perception he projected in an instant.

His senses could extend only a very short distance.

There was no way to map the maze with perception alone.

Anna and the others looked troubled.

They didn't know whether hostile creatures lurked inside. Even if none did, navigating the maze would depend largely on luck, and finding the exit would take considerable time.

“Come on, let's not waste time. We'll go in and take a look… Get some rope ready so we don't get lost in…”

Anna was still speaking when his eyelid twitched.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The Lickers that had been gathered beneath the Divine Tree all moved together, rushing straight into the root maze!

Anna hastily stepped back several paces to clear a path for them.

He and his companions turned to Fang Heng with puzzled expressions.

Was Fang Heng planning to use the Lickers to explore the maze?

Fang Heng said nothing, his expression perfectly calm.

A maze like this would work very well against ordinary players.

But against him…

It was nowhere near enough.

As his Mental attribute had increased, he had become able to sense the positions of nearby Lickers if he focused.

Using that psychic awareness, Fang Heng tracked the Lickers and tried to construct a floor plan of the maze in his mind.

Hm?

He let out a soft exclamation.

So it was a three-dimensional maze.

Built inside the Divine Tree's roots, it had winding passages and an enormous number of branching paths.

That made forcing a way through much harder.

“Interesting…”

Fang Heng frowned, then simply closed his eyes and concentrated on reconstructing the maze as a three-dimensional map in his mind.

Anna and the others exchanged looks.

Seeing Fang Heng standing with eyes closed, evidently calculating something, they didn't dare disturb him and waited quietly to one side.

Found it!

Twenty minutes later, Fang Heng's eyes snapped open.

With more than five hundred Lickers, their incredible speed, and their ability to climb, he had swiftly surveyed most of the maze and located its exit.

“Let's go. Follow me.”

With that, Fang Heng strode straight into the maze.

That fast?

He'd really found the way out?

Anna glanced at Fang Heng cautiously and hurried after him.

Fang Heng had already constructed most of the maze's three-dimensional layout in his mind. At every fork, he took the shortest route toward the exit without a moment's hesitation.

Anna and the others were baffled.

That quickly?

He didn't even need to think about it?

After weaving through the maze for another ten minutes or so, they saw light coming from its exit.

Beyond the root maze, they were still inside the Divine Tree's root system.

An enormous stone wall blocked the way ahead.

Cyan flames burned on either side, and the wall itself was covered in chaotic, scrawled patterns resembling incomprehensible talismans.

“Hmm… Is that a magic array…?”

Fang Heng murmured and stepped forward. He looked up at the runes on the wall, a thoughtful expression appearing in his eyes once again.
