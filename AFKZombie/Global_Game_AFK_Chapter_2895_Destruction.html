Chapter 2895

Destruction

From original Chinese Chapter 2896 · 覆灭

Both soldiers shook their heads.

B-rank nests produced extremely powerful signal jamming. Communications couldn't get through, and transport aircraft didn't dare fly overhead. Nobody had any idea what was happening there.

Colette began to panic. He nodded to the accompanying squad captains. “Prepare yourselves. We're going to reinforce them immediately.”

The four sergeants nodded grimly. “Understood!”

Once they'd unloaded the transport aircraft and finished preparations, the four Federation squads immediately headed toward Zone A-799 to assist.

Colette had already prepared for the worst.

If they'd all been wiped out...

He'd have to gather people to break open the earthen barrier and recover the remaining Prism Tower components, transporting them back to the ship before nightfall!

...

From afar, the soldiers heard gunfire and explosions.

The sounds of battle actually eased Colette's worries a little.

At least things hadn't reached the worst possible outcome.

If guns were still firing, the squads hadn't all been annihilated.

When they climbed a barren hill and saw the battlefield in the distance, Colette and the four sergeants went pale.

Ahead of them...

Vast Zerg swarms were locked in combat!

Countless insects covered the fungal carpet and surged endlessly toward the Federation squads. Many were Elite Mutated Zerg or even leader-class creatures!

Yet rows of brown Zerg-like creatures stood before the soldiers, using their own flesh to hold back the swarm's assaults!

The Federation squads behind them had split into seven groups, killing insects and clearing scattered Plagueblister Earthmothers and low-rank nests as they advanced step by step.

Colette was truly stunned.

This was nothing like he'd imagined!

Boyd's squad and the others were actually...

Pushing deeper into the nest!

The soldiers steadily cleared the occupied fungal carpet with flamethrowers.

The B-rank nest had once controlled most of this area.

Now the enormous B-rank nest mother, over six meters tall, was clearly visible in the very center of the battlefield.

More than a dozen C- and D-rank subsidiary nests stood around it.

The Federation's damage output was clearly insufficient, making their advance difficult.

Colette and the reinforcements found themselves breathing heavily.

Before coming here, none of them had imagined Fang Heng and these few squads could get so far!

“Attack! Go help them!”

Colette immediately mobilized his four reinforcement squads, which began joining the battle one by one.

Boyd and the other teams adjusted their formations to accommodate the reinforcements.

Even then, the Federation's frontal firepower remained inadequate.

Seeing the fierce battle, Colette personally led his men forward, raising his weapon to spray energy beams into the enemy swarm.

He soon noticed something.

This battlefield was utterly different from every Zerg battle he'd experienced before.

The swarm's attacks were completely contained!

The soldiers could devote all their attention to offense.

Was all this because of Fang Heng?

Colette scanned the battlefield, looking for him.

What they really lacked was effective damage output.

Damn it.

If only they had the Kinsfolk's ranged combat machines here.

For a moment, Colette even felt that the war machines the Kinsfolk had just delivered should have been sent to Fang Heng instead of the main front. They would be much more effective here.

“Quick, fall back!”

“Colette! Watch your right!”

Hearing the shout, Colette's heart skipped. He immediately looked right.

Boom! Boom, boom!

A dense forest of spikes erupted from the ground.

The Lickers fighting the Carpet Bugs were suddenly impaled and thrust high into the air!

Damn it!

What kind of Zerg monster was that!?

Colette quickly fell back with his squad.

Fang Heng hadn't entered the thick of the battle. He was staying behind the formation, controlling the overall situation.

He saw Colette's predicament clearly.

B-rank nests really were difficult to fight.

Besides the swarm's enormous numbers, high-level leader-class Zerg appeared frequently. Each possessed bizarre special abilities that could dramatically alter the battlefield.

Such as this one.

Most of the Zerg leader's body was hidden beneath the fungal carpet, leaving only a small section of armored back exposed. It attacked primarily by thrusting giant spikes up through the ground.

The spikes inflicted massive piercing damage and carried a potent toxin.

They even had around a five-percent instant-death chance!

Two unlucky Lickers were killed outright by the piercing attack.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Hundreds of Lickers that had been lying in ambush surged toward the Zerg leader!

Primal Devourer modifications!

This modified life-form could revive itself after death and also fuse with Burrowing. Fang Heng simply hadn't completed the conversion because its Main God point cost was too high.

The Primal Devourer-modified Lickers immediately moved to block the leader's line of attack!

Bang! Bang, bang!

The leader, still mostly hidden below the carpet, repeatedly speared the Lickers and sent them flying. They landed and lunged straight back into the fight.

Even those killed outright quickly revived from their liquid state!

In an instant, the leader was pinned in place by the Lickers.

Colette and his soldiers were badly shaken. They retreated to a safer distance and tried to aim at the leader, but most of its body was concealed beneath the fungal carpet.

The broad scales exposed along its back were difficult to target and absorbed enormous amounts of damage.

What a pain!

Colette gritted his teeth in frustration.

He'd known B-rank nests were hard to fight. Seeing one firsthand proved it.

A single Zerg leader was enough to bring their advance to a standstill.

Without Fang Heng's summons holding it back, the consequences would have been disastrous.

Farther behind the battlefield, Fang Heng fixed his gaze on the leader and gradually released his mental power. He shifted into his Necromancy form and slowly raised his right hand.

Hiss, hiss...

Gray energy mixed with blood aura curled around his palm.

Blood Boil!

Against living beings with circulating blood, Blood Boil was exceptionally useful!

The Zerg leader's movements visibly faltered.

Hiss, hiss...

Blood-red energy and gray necromantic aura began gathering around the leader as well.

Alongside the blood fluctuations caused by Blood Boil came the corrosive and aging effects of Necromancy.

In extreme discomfort, the Zerg leader began thrashing violently beneath the earth!
