Chapter 2033

Counterattack

From original Chinese Chapter 2034 · 反攻

Reverse the teleportation direction?!

The players were startled and all looked toward Fang Heng.

What new arrangement did World Lord Fang Heng have in mind?

“Understood!”

Without further questions, several players operating the teleportation equipment began transmitting the request.

A passage opened by a spatial rift device could only transmit people in one direction at a time.

Allowing both ends to send people through simultaneously would cause chaos, so whenever they needed to change directions, they first sent a request and obtained confirmation from the other end.

Soon after the signal was sent, transmission from the opposite side stopped.

Fang Heng nodded to Gu Qingzhu beside him, then quickly shifted into his sacred-studies form.

“Be careful,” Gu Qingzhu whispered.

“Mm.”

All eyes turned toward Fang Heng. The players were curious to see what he intended to do next.

He took a step forward and walked directly into the teleportation passage, vanishing from sight.

What the hell?!

Everyone's expression changed.

Fang Heng had…

He'd gone alone to one of the Holy Court's strongholds in an intermediate-tier game world?!

…

Several dark-golden flashes passed before Fang Heng's eyes.

Game notifications promptly appeared.

[Hint: You have entered the intermediate-tier game world Duncha through a special teleportation passage.]

[Hint: You have not obtained this world's coordinates.]

[Hint: You entered this world through a teleportation passage. Some functions are unavailable.]

[Hint: You are currently affiliated with a hostile faction. Your status has been classified as “Disguised.”]

He was here!

As soon as he entered the Duncha game world, Fang Heng opened his eyes and quickly surveyed his surroundings.

Unlike the ancient world of Chiboreya, Duncha had reached a modern level of technology.

Fang Heng found himself in a modern city.

More precisely, in a large open-air central square.

The spatial rift device stood in the middle of the square, surrounded by densely stationed Holy Court guards.

Large numbers of elite Holy Court teams had assembled to one side, ready to travel through the passage to reinforce the Chiboreya game world.

Everyone nearby gave Fang Heng curious looks when they saw him emerge from the passage.

Chu Yan was prepared. He hurried forward to explain, “He's one of my subordinates, responsible for checking and testing the passage. Everything's working properly, so transmissions can continue.”

“Stop wasting time! Hurry up!”

The Holy Court personnel noticed nothing wrong. A believer operating the passage called out and urged his subordinates to resume transporting reinforcements to Chiboreya.

Meanwhile, Chu Yan led Fang Heng aside.

“Boss Fang, the Holy Court has deployed a great many elite teams around the city. They were originally assigned to reinforce Chiboreya. If anything unusual happens here, they can arrive within half an hour.”

As he spoke quietly, Chu Yan glanced warily behind him. “If we attack head-on, we'll face enormous resistance. And the commander responsible for these reinforcements is Holy Court Arbiter Muhiting. He's very strong, so be especially careful.”

“Right. We'll proceed with the original plan. All you need to do is keep the teleportation passage safe. Leave everything else to me.”

“Okay.”

Chu Yan nodded and parted ways with Fang Heng, telling the Devout Guild players to remain near the teleportation passage and guard it.

Two other Devout Guild players guided Fang Heng in the opposite direction. “Boss Fang, please follow us!”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng followed them around the edge of the central square and hurried several hundred meters into the city until they came to a flower bed.

After quickly checking their surroundings, the two entered the garden and approached a manhole cover set among the flower beds.

One player crouched down, worked the cover loose, and opened it.

Was this…?

A sewer…?

Looking at the manhole, Fang Heng suddenly felt a wave of nostalgia.

Back when he'd first entered the Zombie Apocalypse, he'd spent plenty of time dealing with urban sewers.

“Here we are, Boss Fang. The underground sewer network connects to every corner of the city. Please follow us.”

“Right!”

With that, Fang Heng jumped down into the sewers alongside them.

Time was short. The Holy Court might discover something unusual about the teleportation passage at any moment.

They needed to make their preparations quickly!

The Devout Guild had examined numerous locations, but in the end, the sewers made the best gathering point.

Convenient and well concealed.

And close by!

Fang Heng followed them through the sewers at a run for more than five minutes before stopping.

“Stand back!”

The two Devout Guild players immediately retreated to a safe distance.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng swiftly raised both hands and formed a seal in front of him.

Hiss!

Blood seeped from the center of his forehead!

The two players hiding nearby suddenly felt their hearts convulse sharply.

Looking up again, they saw drops of blood emerging from Fang Heng's brow.

The blood rose into the air.

A chaotic spatial vortex gradually formed around it, its edges flickering with red light and radiating danger.

A piercing hiss tore through the air, and the vortex suddenly burst open!

It was a blood-red teleportation gate!

Hiss! Hiss, hiss!

The next instant, vast numbers of Lickers darted out through the blood-red portal!

Those were…

The two Devout Guild players stared in astonishment at the gateway before them!

Lickers poured out without pause, then immediately scattered along the various passages of the city's sewer network!

The two gulped. Before they could speak, blood from Fang Heng's forehead began gathering into a second chaotic spatial vortex.

Bang!

A second teleportation passage opened!

Dense swarms of Lickers surged out of both portals, forming a red torrent that rapidly spread through the city!

[Hint: You have consumed 10% of your maximum blood-pool capacity to summon a vampire teleportation passage…]

[Hint: Your skill has triggered intense spatial fluctuations within a certain range. These fluctuations may be detected…]

Fang Heng stopped and glanced back at the gateways behind him.

Opening the two vampire teleportation passages had reduced his maximum blood-pool capacity by a full 20%.

No major problem.

His overall health reserves were still comfortably in his favor.

The Lickers coming through the passages held an overwhelming advantage in numbers. They could buy him all the time he needed!

This time was different from before.

Every zombie clone sent as reinforcement had been transformed into Licker form, without exception!

There were seven thousand of them!

Over the past two days, the modification laboratories in the Zombie Apocalypse had been running at full capacity. Qiu Yaokang had personally led the researchers in converting all seven thousand of Fang Heng's clones into Lickers!

— End of Chapter 2033 —
