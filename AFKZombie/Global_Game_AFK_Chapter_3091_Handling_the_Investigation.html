Chapter 3091

Handling the Investigation

From Chinese Chapter 3092 · 应付调查

Fang Heng was busily helping his zombies excavate tunnels and dispose of construction debris when his phone chimed repeatedly with a message.

It was from Anxi.

She warned him that faint Black Mist fluctuations had been detected near his villa.

Coincidentally, strong Black Mist fluctuations had also appeared in the Kinsfolk district. He might therefore be inspected. She asked whether he wanted to move elsewhere.

“Unusual fluctuations...”

Fang Heng frowned and glanced up at the basement.

So the Black Mist that had escaped really had been detected.

He needed to reinforce the sealing arrays again, making absolutely sure no aura escaped.

Fang Heng replied that it wasn't necessary—he was very comfortable here—then continued expanding the basement.

If they were merely investigating, they probably wouldn't trace anything to him.

Whatever. Keep going!

Fang Heng left the basement and began reinforcing the outer aura-sealing magic arrays to ensure not a wisp of Black Mist could escape.

...

He spent another entire day grinding.

Only after sealing the entire underground area repeatedly with alchemical arrays and sealing techniques, confirming not the slightest aura leaked out, did Fang Heng stop to rest.

“That should definitely do it.”

After a final inspection, Fang Heng nodded in satisfaction and casually tossed his alchemical pen aside.

Abe Akaya's vines stretched out, caught the discarded pen, and placed it back on the table.

“Thanks.”

After spending all day arranging alchemical arrays, Fang Heng rubbed his aching wrist and headed toward the basement.

The zombie clones had also made enormous progress excavating.

With tens of thousands of zombies working AFK, the underground seventh floor had expanded dozens of times over. Ignoring the poor conditions, its area alone was now comparable to a medium-sized underground parking garage.

But it still wasn't enough.

He had to keep expanding to give them sufficient room for experience grinding.

Huh?

As soon as Fang Heng opened the basement door, he let out a quiet sound of surprise.

What was going on?

The entire underground area was filled with a thin layer of Black Mist.

The sealing arrays outside the room trapped it behind a curtain of light across the doorway, preventing its escape.

“Something isn't right.”

Sensing the problem, Fang Heng muttered to himself and descended to the seventh basement level where the zombies were concentrated.

The seventh underground floor.

The same thin Black Mist hung in the air.

Indeed, it was slightly denser than on the upper levels.

In the haze, zombies wielded shovels and spades, tirelessly extending the tunnels. Abe Akaya's vines and massive roots running along the walls served as support beams, stabilizing the space and greatly shortening the construction time.

Fang Heng studied the zombies, expanded his perception completely, and searched the underground area. He grew thoughtful.

The Black Mist covering the basement actually originated from his zombie clones.

With his perception expanded, he could clearly detect tiny wisps constantly seeping from their bodies.

Each wisp was extremely faint, so faint that even Fang Heng would struggle to notice it without actively using his perception.

Previously there had only been a few thousand clones, and the basement hadn't been completely sealed. The mist could circulate out, so nothing seemed unusual.

Now the zombie population had increased dozens of times and the whole space was sealed, allowing a great quantity of Black Mist to accumulate.

After an afternoon of gathering, it had finally formed the visible haze before him.

“This can't go on.”

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

He trusted his alchemical sealing arrays and was sure they could contain the Black Mist completely.

There would certainly be no short-term problem.

But as time passed, the mist would grow denser, gradually increasing the pressure on the outer seal. Once it reached a critical point, Black Mist would leak out.

A leak at that concentration would inevitably attract an investigation.

He needed a way to solve this problem.

...

Outside.

The Kinsfolk prism detection system had indeed registered faint Black Mist fluctuations in Fang Heng's residential district.

But their concentration was extremely low.

Moreover, the Kinsfolk were preoccupied with the attack on their headquarters two nights earlier. They carried out only a cursory investigation near Fang Heng's villa and ended it when they found nothing abnormal.

Their attention remained focused on headquarters.

The sudden Black Mist and Ouroboros Seed outbreak had terrified the Kinsfolk and caused real losses: over a dozen Kinsfolk administrators had been killed.

Nothing like it had happened since the capital's founding. The seven overlord factions were furious and immediately established a joint investigation team led by the Kinsfolk and the Clan of Evil, determined to uncover the truth.

But the investigation hit a wall almost immediately.

They couldn't find a thing!

The night of the incident, the insects seemed to appear out of thin air in the sewers and then launch a frenzied attack on the Kinsfolk building for no discernible reason.

The investigators poured effort into reviewing surveillance recordings and detained more than a dozen suspects, but found no leads.

Several days later, the team began a far more thorough investigation.

Fang Heng even received a visit from investigators at his villa.

Before arriving, they knew the occupant was a warrior employed by the Noel Clan.

So they'd intended only a routine inquiry.

They could never have imagined that hundreds of thousands of Black Mist-emitting zombies were hidden beneath that very neighborhood!

“Have you been home the entire time?”

“Mm. I've been cultivating at home.” Fang Heng nodded calmly. “I'm a warrior.”

“Very well. Have you noticed anything unusual recently?”

“No.”

The investigators weren't suspicious.

Warriors, after all.

Most of them acted as though they were above everyone else.

It would be stranger if he were friendly and enthusiastic.

“Thank you for cooperating. If you discover anything unusual, please call the emergency number immediately and contact our special investigation team.”

“I will.”

Fang Heng dealt with them casually, closed the door without expression, and headed back to the basement.

Over the past few days, he'd reinforced the underground area with more than a dozen additional layers of both sealing and alchemical techniques, ensuring no Black Mist aura escaped.

For at least a week, the mist would definitely be contained!

Fang Heng descended to the seventh level.

More than two hundred thousand zombie clones were now practicing mysticism hand seals.

He'd finished maxing out alchemy half an hour earlier and switched to mysticism training.

He'd started making that switch the previous evening.

Fang Heng looked around the basement again.

Black Mist drifted everywhere.
