Chapter 3106

Conversion

From Chinese Chapter 3107 · 转化

Give it a try!

Fang Heng immediately circulated the law energy that had entered his body and tried to convert it into Qi, sending it into his Sea of Consciousness.

It worked.

And it was easy.

After all, he already had experience absorbing external natural energy and converting it into Qi.

The external energy was extremely mixed, however. Even with his powerful innate abilities forcibly converting it into Qi, the conversion efficiency was very low.

It couldn't compare to absorbing pure natural energy.

At that moment, the game notifications refreshed again.

[Hint: Player has successfully merged with external laws and absorbed law energy.]

[Hint: Quest completed. Grandmaster-level Mysticism has been forgotten. Player has obtained Transcendent-level Mysticism.]

Done!

With the game's confirmation, Fang Heng opened his eyes again and looked at Mu Shunan, who sat before him as if he'd seen a ghost.

"Thank you, Mr. Mu. Your guidance has been a great help."

Fang Heng rose and thanked him with a respectful cupped-fist salute.

He had successfully obtained Transcendent-level Mysticism.

Achieving it so quickly was inseparable from his previous breakthroughs in Alchemy and Nature Studies.

Next, he needed to become familiar with the process and spend natural energy converting it into Qi, gaining experience and grinding levels quickly.

Back to the villa for more grinding!

Fang Heng estimated that this part would take quite a while.

No rush.

You couldn't get fat on a single mouthful. Once he'd maxed out his experience, he could return to Mu Shunan to ask about advancing to the Demigod level.

"Ah, this..."

He really broke through?

As easily as eating or drinking?

Mu Shunan's head was buzzing. He had no idea what had happened afterward, and by the time he truly came to his senses, Fang Heng had already said goodbye and left the training room.

What was that supposed to mean?

He taught him the first step, and the brat taught himself the second and immediately broke through?

Had Fang Heng's comprehension of laws already reached an even higher realm?

If he hadn't confirmed that Fang Heng was merely at the Grandmaster level of Mysticism when he arrived, with no sign that his internal energy had merged with the external laws, Mu Shunan would have suspected he had reached Transcendent Mysticism long ago and had come here just to make a fool of him.

Meanwhile, Fang Heng hurried back to the seventh basement level of the villa.

Abe Akaya radiated a blue-green light, continuously releasing natural energy throughout the seventh underground level.

Fang Heng sat cross-legged at the center of the magic array.

As he opened the pores of his body, external natural-law energy merged with him much faster and was converted through his body into Qi.

[Hint: Player has absorbed Mysticism energy. Current Mysticism EXP +54,000.]

There it was!

Fang Heng was delighted to discover that acquiring Transcendent-level Mysticism had greatly increased his absorption efficiency again!

It had increased by approximately three to four times!

That meant his training efficiency could improve yet again!

Grind!

Keep grinding!

...

"Administrator, we've checked the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's personnel records and screened every pan-human. We've identified 24,272 pan-humans in total, including one high-ranking warrior, three mid-ranking warriors, and forty-two low-ranking warriors."

"The investigation is ongoing. Several people of particular interest have been placed under individual surveillance, but nothing suspicious has been discovered yet."

"Of special note, an Elven prince and a Flying Squirrel Clan member recently applied for racial registration on humanity's behalf. We've tracked them and suspect they may be connected to the humans."

"A good observation."

Administrator Lila considered the matter before looking up. "Those two are indeed suspicious. Whether or not they have anything to do with the Black Mist world, you have my permission to eliminate them directly. And try not to leave any traces."

"Yes, Administrator. The Flying Squirrel Clan's perception is quite sharp. They detected our surveillance during the preliminary investigation. They've since curtailed their activities and spend most of their time hiding in a hotel belonging to the Mechanical Clan. It's difficult to find a chance to strike."

"No hurry. Post people outside."

Lila replied coolly, "Aren't they trying to complete humanity's registration? I'd like to see how they can continue applying while hiding inside that hotel."

"Yes, Administrator."

Another young Kinsfolk stepped forward to report. "Administrator, an elder previously mentioned encountering a very strong pan-human warrior in the arena. I've conducted a preliminary investigation. Coincidentally, he only appeared here recently, around the same time as the Black Mist."

"Very good. Continue investigating. I'm granting our investigators the highest level of authority. You don't need evidence. If you have sufficient grounds for suspicion, you may conduct elimination operations directly. We must cut off the source of the Black Mist."

The young Kinsfolk bowed respectfully. "Yes, Administrator."

...

Another day and night passed.

In the basement, Fang Heng finished another training cycle and opened his eyes.

The experience required to level up Transcendent Mysticism was immense.

Abe Akaya could currently absorb some of Hell's power and the power of the Demon Cores, so there was plenty of energy available. But given the limitations of Fang Heng's absorption and conversion rate, he estimated that finishing would still take more than ten days.

To ordinary people, maxing out Transcendent Mysticism in half a month would be unimaginably fast.

But Fang Heng still wasn't entirely satisfied.

Too slow.

Grinding Alchemy skills was much more convenient.

Fang Heng raised his head and glanced at the game notifications.

Judging by the time, another day of AFK grinding should bring his next breakthrough.

Maybe he should take another break? Go tease the Kinsfolk to unwind?

Fang Heng looked around the basement.

Hundreds of thousands of zombie clones were working nonstop underground, setting up magic arrays to grind experience. After two days, some Black Mist had accumulated again.

It wasn't especially dense, but there was enough for another little outing.

Just then, his phone rang.

Fang Heng checked the caller, saw Kappa's name, and answered immediately.

"Boss Fang, I think we're being watched."

On the other end of the line, Kappa lowered his voice and quickly described the troubles they'd encountered recently.

Over the past two days, Kappa and Korkalot had run all over the place. With help from the local elves, they'd finally struggled through most of the process of registering the human world.

But as they approached the final stage, Kappa sensed murderous intent.

The Flying Squirrel Clan had an exceptionally sharp sixth sense. As soon as they realized something was wrong, they retreated to a hotel run by the Mechanical Clan in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

The Mechanical Clan provided hotel guests with various services and security guarantees, but every night's stay cost a fortune.

"Mm, I understand. I'm coming over now."

The Kinsfolk!

A dark look crossed Fang Heng's face. He shifted into soul form and flew toward the district where Kappa and his companion were staying.

Hm?

Just as he left the villa in soul form, Fang Heng suddenly sensed something and looked to the right.

Who was there?
