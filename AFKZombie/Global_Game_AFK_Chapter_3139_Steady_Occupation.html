Chapter 3139

Steady Occupation

From Chinese Chapter 3140 · 稳步占据

[Hint: Current world's black-mist spread: 0.0221%.]

As the Vampires and large numbers of zombie clones gradually entered the Frozen Soul Land, the world's black-mist spread was slowly increasing.

He was eager to learn what would happen if the world's black-mist infection reached one hundred percent.

The pressure in the Seven Realms Martial Capital had been too great for him to experiment. Here in the Frozen Soul Land, however, he had no such concerns.

Fang Heng didn't hesitate any longer. He looked at Garr and Bule. “You two will be responsible for controlling the situation. I want mining operations throughout the underground caverns restored to normal as quickly as possible. At the same time, prepare to repel a Kinsfolk attack. Can you do that?”

“Yes, Your Majesty!”

Fang Heng turned toward Kappa and Korkalot. “You two are responsible for repairing the starships for now. Gather the personnel you need, restore the main control systems first, and secure control of the vessels.”

Korkalot had commanded a fleet before. Hearing this, he stepped forward in front of Kappa and nodded. “Understood. Leave it to me.”

…

At the Kinsfolk headquarters' administrative council chamber.

After receiving news of the rebellion on the Frozen Soul Land, the Kinsfolk immediately convened another emergency meeting of their senior leadership.

Just the previous night, their corporate headquarters in the Seven Realms Martial Capital had been attacked by insects. Administrator Lila and numerous senior Kinsfolk officials in the building had all been killed.

The incident was considered a targeted assassination against the Kinsfolk. The entire race was furious and determined to eliminate the killer and the organization behind him.

They'd barely drawn up an investigation team and dispatched personnel to investigate when, before they could even catch their breath, another report arrived. One of their important mining planets had been invaded by an alien race.

As reports of the attack were shared among everyone attending through the psychic council, the senior Kinsfolk administrators all wore grave expressions.

They found the incident deeply strange.

During the entire attack, the Kinsfolk had received only three reports. The request for reinforcements had come within a few minutes, and communications from the planet then failed completely. From the initial reports, all they could determine was that the invaders were a pan-human race.

The attackers called themselves Vampires.

Strangest of all, the Vampires seemed to have appeared out of nowhere on the Frozen Soul Land. There were roughly five hundred to a thousand of them, with uniformly high strength. They were clearly elites of their race.

A force of five hundred to a thousand people was already a large formation, usually requiring three or four starships to transport.

Before the attack, the Frozen Soul Land hadn't detected any arriving ships or received any unusual signals from outside.

So how had this group of Vampires appeared?

Furthermore, the planet had a powerful security contingent and no fewer than three warships. Its guards even included a warrior who'd already mastered a Domain.

It was difficult to imagine that such a mid-tier security force could be routed in just five minutes, to the point that all communications were cut off.

And in the few reports they'd received, there had even been descriptions of insect creatures.

Ouroboros insects working with an alien race?

The whole thing was far too strange.

“The Frozen Soul Land is our primary source of Earth's Core Flame Veins. It is of vital importance to us. We must do everything possible to recapture it without delay.”

The Kinsfolk Grand Administrator looked around at the assembled officials.

“Grand Administrator, something about this invasion is unusual. It may not be an ordinary attack. We've suffered several incidents in quick succession recently. It's quite possible we're being deliberately targeted.”

“Mm, indeed. Mobilize the external assault fleet. Prepare the mechanical legions for deployment. Issue a chain of missions through the alliance system, directing allied races to the Frozen Soul Land to investigate. Deploy prism attack arrays and eliminate the threat as quickly as possible!”

“Yes!”

…

The Frozen Soul Land.

Inside a cavern.

Fang Heng casually tossed a large dimensional crystal core into the air and caught it again, repeating the motion as he pondered.

Inside the game, dimensional crystal cores were extremely precious items.

Using alchemical magic arrays and Abe Akaya to activate a dimensional core's power allowed Abe Akaya to absorb the strength of an entire world and improve the bloodline's dimensional level.

Unfortunately, very few of these items appeared in the game.

Fang Heng had also asked around outside the game, but hadn't found any dimensional crystals there.

He'd spent large quantities of them in the game recently to raise his dimensional level at great speed. Now he had only two large dimensional crystal cores left.

His dimensional level had already reached a certain point, making further grinding increasingly difficult.

He was saving those two large crystals for emergencies.

In fact, he'd previously considered using a dimensional core to destroy a world outright when dealing with the Kinsfolk.

Unfortunately, it hadn't worked.

Outside the game world, the dimensional crystal cores had been labeled unusable.

But just now, he'd unexpectedly noticed that the unusable label on the game notification had disappeared, replaced by an additional special explanation.

[Item: Dimensional Crystal Core.]

Special Description: The current world's black-mist concentration is low. Using a Dimensional Crystal Core under these conditions will greatly reduce its effects.

Now that was interesting.

So the black mist was the reason he'd been unable to use dimensional crystals!

In other words, once he'd spread enough black mist, he might be able to use a dimensional crystal to absorb this entire world?

Fang Heng stroked his chin, an intrigued smile appearing on his face.

“Boss Fang.”

Kappa entered the cavern with Garr and Bule.

Kappa reported first. “Boss Fang, we've restored the main control room's defensive systems. We can now detect any starships or spatial fluctuations entering the planet's orbital region.”

“We haven't detected any Kinsfolk activity yet, but we expect it soon. They won't abandon this mining planet easily.”

“Mm.” Fang Heng turned toward Garr. “How are things on your side?”

“All security personnel have been imprisoned. The Vampires have taken control of the entire underground mining complex, and the smelting area has already resumed operations.”

Garr nodded and continued, “This world is enormous. According to the miners, they've developed less than ten percent of the Earth's Core Flame Veins beneath the caverns so far.”

Fang Heng focused his gaze on Garr.

Looking carefully, he could see thin wisps of black mist escaping from his body.

They were extremely faint.

Probably only someone with a Perception attribute exceeding 150 would notice anything unusual.

Fang Heng then looked toward Kappa.

Not the slightest trace of black mist was leaking from Kappa.

Fang Heng himself had never emitted black-mist energy either.

Combining these observations with everything he'd seen previously, perhaps he could draw a conclusion.

Was it only things originating from worlds infected by the black mist that produced the game's black mist after being brought outside the game?

If that was true…

Fang Heng raised his head and ordered Garr, “You have half an hour. Return to the Vampire world and make arrangements. I need more Vampires brought into this world. As many as possible.”

Garr's expression grew solemn, and he immediately nodded. “Yes, Your Majesty!”

“Bule, the prisoners in the Frozen Soul Land come from many different worlds and races. Find some prisoners who have high standing among their respective races and bring them to see me.”

“Yes, Your Majesty!”

“Kappa, take me to see the Earth's Core Flame Vein mining area.”

“You got it.”
