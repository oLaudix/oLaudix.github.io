Chapter 3101

Reversal

From Chinese Chapter 3102 · 翻转

"Tch! All flashy tricks!"

Fang Heng snorted disdainfully as the runes of an alchemical magic array spun rapidly in his pupil.

Huh!?

They weren't illusions.

They were physical clones!

Arcane puppets!

So this fellow combined Nature Studies with Arcane Studies.

What a rich bastard. Arcane puppets cost an absolute fortune.

His own zombie clones were much more practical.

"Whoosh!!"

With a light tap of his foot, Fang Heng darted right, slipping through gaps between the dense roots and vines pressing in on him. He swung a fist straight at Grand!

"Bang!!!!"

A tremendous explosion rang out!

Surprise flashed through Grand's eyes.

What terrifying strength.

"Boom!!!!"

Several solid walls that had risen before Grand exploded under Fang Heng's punch!

Splinters flew everywhere, landing on Fang Heng and triggering a succession of explosions!

Hm!?

Grand's brow tightened further.

Why did the explosions seem completely ineffective against this guy?

Damn it.

Nature-based attacks weren't especially powerful, but surely they weren't *this* weak?

While Grand was still puzzled, Fang Heng ducked and became a dark blur racing toward him again. Grand's heart jolted.

What was going on?

How had he picked out the real Grand among so many puppets!?

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes slightly, his gaze locked firmly onto Grand's true body.

With the Omniscient Eye and his blood-sensing ability, Grand's real body stood out far too clearly!

Fang Heng charged straight toward him.

Heh!

Interesting!

The power of laws!

As he approached, Fang Heng keenly sensed something abnormal in the fluctuations of natural energy around Grand.

Grand was influencing the power of laws.

His Nature Studies had already reached the Transcendent level, perhaps even the Demigod level!

Whoosh!!

Fang Heng suddenly appeared to the right of Grand's true body and drove his right fist forward again.

"Bang!! Bang, bang!!!"

Even before the blow connected, the natural laws gathered in the surroundings reacted to counter it. A layer of natural energy actually appeared around Fang Heng's fist.

Fine vines grew out of thin air around his knuckles and coiled over them, attempting to weaken the punch.

At the same time, a green natural barrier appeared around Grand.

Interesting!

Laws!

Very interesting!

Fang Heng unleashed his aura. The vines wrapped around his fist instantly shattered, and his punch crashed heavily into the barrier formed by those laws.

"Boom!!!!"

A powerful rebound force struck him. Frowning, Fang Heng used it to retreat quickly, then flickered back and forth around Grand, probing how his opponent used laws to serve his own purposes.

Grand stared in astonishment.

What the hell!

That single blow had already cracked the barrier. It only looked intact because it had not completely disintegrated.

Never had he seen a close-combat warrior with such terrifying strength—someone capable of forcibly resisting his law-bound domain through physical power alone.

Where on earth had this brat come from?

Even the power of his domain couldn't completely trap him.

What an infuriating opponent!

A fine layer of cold sweat appeared on Grand's forehead.

Could a physical-enhancement warrior really possess such strength?

Fang Heng wasn't using his full power. He was simply curious about the natural barrier, so he kept attacking it from different angles to test its strength.

Otherwise, with his Strength attribute, he could smash through its outer layer with a few punches through brute force alone.

And if he added an academic power, such as necromancy, a single punch would easily shatter it.

After circling and testing the nature shield for a while, Fang Heng began to understand.

So that's how it works.

Grand was using his own power to create a special space governed by altered laws.

Essentially, he activated his Nature Studies power and circulated it to affect the laws outside his body.

Nasir had been right after all.

Higher academic disciplines, like the Alchemy and Mysticism Fang Heng had been studying, were about understanding and using laws.

He thought back to Tree Realm Descent, which he had used many times.

It was actually a similar domain ability, though its focus was different.

Understand one principle, and others fall into place.

Fang Heng's perception was extremely sharp. By repeatedly touching the defensive barrier, he had already sensed the flow of law power around Grand.

Fortunately, he had practiced working with laws before.

His alchemical training had honed his perception of environmental laws to an exceptionally fine degree.

His perception was up to the task.

Next came control.

That required a foundation of powerful mental strength and Nature Studies ability.

He could do it!

In an instant, Fang Heng had an overwhelming intuition that his current abilities were also sufficient to try manipulating the surrounding laws!

It wasn't difficult! He could absolutely give it a try!

At that moment, several lines of game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Hint: Player has come into contact with certain laws. All attributes have reached the minimum requirements. Quest triggered: Nature Studies Advancement (Transcendent).]

Quest Name: Nature Studies Advancement (Transcendent).

Quest Description: While attempting to perceive the laws of Nature Studies, you have discovered possible paths for advancement.

Quest Requirement: Attempt to use Nature Studies abilities to affect the formation of laws.

Quest Reward: Nature Studies advancement.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and darted forward again.

"Whoosh!!!"

Bang!!!

His fist struck the defensive barrier surrounding Grand.

"Crack, crack-crack, crack-crack..."

Fine vines spread along the barrier and attached themselves to Fang Heng's wrists, quickly wrapping around his body.

Hm?

An opportunity?

Grand's heart leaped. He didn't understand why Fang Heng had suddenly let the vines restrain him, but this was undoubtedly his chance!

Heart-Piercing Thorns!

Sharp wooden spikes erupted out of thin air and thrust viciously toward Fang Heng in midair.

Hm!?

In the next instant, Grand's pupils contracted sharply.

The thorns had barely appeared before they shattered in midair.

What!

His Heart-Piercing Thorns spell had failed to form?

Grand's expression changed drastically.

How was this possible?

Still in shock, he noticed that the vines which had been racing along Fang Heng's wrists had stopped spreading. Then they reversed course, growing even faster toward Grand's outer natural barrier.

"Hiss! Hiss-hiss-hiss!!!"

This was bad!

Grand could sense the laws of Nature Studies around him changing.

They were even being reversed.

Stunned and uncertain, he stared at Fang Heng outside his defensive barrier.

Could that brat be doing this!?

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!

In an instant, fine vines enveloped the entire invisible defensive barrier.

— End of Chapter 3101 —
