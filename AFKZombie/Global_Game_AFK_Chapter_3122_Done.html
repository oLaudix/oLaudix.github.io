At the bottom of the labyrinthine cavern.

Fang Heng and Nasir were still studying the law-based magic arrays on the rock walls when a faint spatial fluctuation suddenly appeared in the corner of a passage at the edge of the cavern.

Fang Heng looked toward it. The one-way teleportation passage they'd used earlier had opened again. Two Noel Clan elders and a group of warriors emerged cautiously, looking as though they were facing a formidable enemy.

“Fang Heng! Nasir! Are you all right?”

Everyone turned their attention toward the two men.

Neither of them seemed injured.

Forget injuries—there were hardly even any signs they'd been fighting.

Where was the evil embryo?

The warriors looked around the cavern.

The rock walls were pitted and scarred, and an acrid stench of rot still hung in the air.

But there was no sign of the evil embryo.

Could Fang Heng have killed it?

Meanwhile, the two Transcendent-level alchemy scholars accompanying the group quickly noticed the alchemical arrays drawn on the cavern walls. Their hearts jumped.

These were advanced law-based magic arrays!

What a find!

They exchanged a quick glance and silently began recording the alchemical arrays separately.

“Good. As long as you two are safe.”

The Noel Clan elder nodded, then continued, “The Kinsfolk cannot escape responsibility for this incident. The Noel Clan will lodge a formal and stern protest. This matter will not end so easily. We will demand an explanation from the Kinsfolk.”

Fang Heng and Nasir exchanged looks before nodding and cupping their fists. “Thank you, Elder, for upholding justice.”

“First Elder.”

A Noel Clan warrior who had noticed something unusual about the alchemical arrays leaned close to the First Elder and whispered a few words in his ear.

The First Elder's expression shifted slightly. He glanced at the arrays on the walls, then at Fang Heng and Nasir. “You two, come with me. I have some questions I'd like to ask you in private.”

“Yes.”

Watching Fang Heng and Nasir being taken aside, Mu Shunan scratched his head.

What was this supposed to mean?

He'd been worried sick about them outside, yet not only were they perfectly safe—they'd apparently been studying alchemy down here the entire time! …

…

A month passed in the blink of an eye.

For the Noel Clan, the ore-gathering expedition to the Lanyue Clan had been rather harrowing, but they hadn't actually suffered any losses. Instead, they'd discovered several ancient law-based magic arrays.

Once the clan elders had committed all the alchemical arrays to memory, they were completely destroyed.

The knowledge was a valuable intangible asset.

The Noel Clan had also immediately issued warrants for the two traitorous warriors, Edwin and Nick.

According to the Lanyue Clan, however, the two had vanished without a trace after fleeing. The Kinsfolk refused to accept any responsibility, insisting that Edwin and Nick had acted on their own, and an argument ensued.

With Edwin and Nick nowhere to be found, dead or alive, the Noel Clan had no concrete evidence.

Fortunately, there had been no casualties. After a period of pressure and negotiations, the affair gradually died down.

Given the peace that the Seven Realms Martial Capital's seven great powers had maintained for tens of millions of years, the Noel Clan had already gone to considerable lengths.

Back in the Lanyue Clan's cavern, all the law-based magic arrays had been thoroughly erased half a month earlier.

But the traces left by the laws they'd altered would remain indefinitely.

Through contemplating his memorized arrays and the traces of altered laws at the site, Fang Heng had finally understood the logic behind permanently changing laws with magic arrays.

Destroy! Reconstruct!

Fang Heng suddenly opened his eyes and rapidly began drawing the patterns of a magic array on the ground.

After two hours of continuous work, a medium-sized magic array had been engraved into the floor.

This should work, right?

Fang Heng drew a deep breath and slowly channeled mental power through his hands into the alchemical array.

“Hum…”

The array slowly rotated, giving off a faint purple glow.

The glow seemed to merge with the surrounding environment. Then the boundaries of that fusion began trembling violently.

Fang Heng stared intently at the magic array. At the same time, an alchemical array spun rapidly within the pupil of his right eye!

Here it comes!

As he'd anticipated, the laws governing the surrounding environment began to clash violently.

Cause a conflict, then an explosion! Shatter!

Reconstruct! “BANG!!!”

A deafening boom rang out!

A holy barrier automatically appeared before Fang Heng.

The various energies permeating the space were completely detonated, and distorted forces of every type slammed into the barrier.

After the laws' power erupted into chaos, it quickly calmed and began reconstructing itself.

At the same time, the magic array began glowing dark green, influencing how those laws re-formed.

Eventually, the spatial fluctuations settled completely.

Fang Heng sensed the changes in the surrounding spatial laws and felt a surge of delight.

It worked!

Although his subsequent alteration had interfered with the laws only slightly, he had, in essence, successfully controlled an alchemical array to reconstruct them.

A game notification appeared on his retina.

[Hint: You have advanced to Demigod-level Alchemy.]

Done!

The advancement was a success!

Fang Heng closed his eyes again and remotely directed the zombie clones in the basement of his villa in the Seven Realms Martial Capital to practice alchemy.

[Hint: Your zombie clone has completed an alchemical magic array. Your Alchemy (Demigod) EXP +1…]

Confirming that his zombie clones could also grind experience for Demigod-level Alchemy while idle, Fang Heng nodded to himself.

He'd finally broken through. It was time to leave this damned place.

Fang Heng rose, went through the teleportation array to leave the passage, and headed outside the cavern.

Warm sunlight fell across his body.

After a month without seeing the sun, Fang Heng stretched and took several deep breaths of fresh air.

As it happened, Nasir descended from higher up the rocky cavern.

Like Fang Heng, Nasir had been using the residual traces of laws in the cavern to cultivate during this period.

But Nasir wasn't as relentless as Fang Heng, who'd stayed in the cave day and night. After two or three straight days of cultivation, Nasir needed to return to the starship for a day to recover his strength.

Seeing Fang Heng, Nasir asked in surprise, “Coming out of seclusion?”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng nodded and asked, “Where's Mu Shunan? Have they all left?”

“Long gone. They weren't interested in alchemy, so they left over half a month ago. A ship makes the round trip to the Seven Realms Martial Capital every three days to pick us up. The next one arrives this afternoon. Hm? Why the sudden question? Are you planning to leave too?”

Halfway through, Nasir seemed to realize something was wrong. He abruptly raised his head and stared at Fang Heng. “Hey, brother, don't tell me you've already made your breakthrough?”

“Mm. Got lucky.”

“This…”

Nasir stared hard at Fang Heng, opened his mouth, and for a moment had no idea what to say.

“You really did break through…”

This kid was terrifying.

In just over a month, his accomplishments in alchemy had actually surpassed Nasir's!

It was infuriating when he thought about it.

But then again, if Nasir had to grind as hard as Fang Heng, staying inside a cavern cultivating for more than thirty consecutive days, he couldn't have done it either.

“Congratulations, Brother Fang. I'm going back to study some more.”

A competitive fire burned inside Nasir. He gritted his teeth and prepared to head back into the cavern for another round of grinding.

“Nasir.”

Fang Heng turned and called him back.
