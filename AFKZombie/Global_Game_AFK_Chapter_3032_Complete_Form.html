Chapter 3032

Complete Form

From Chinese Chapter 3033 · 完全体

A tremendous surge of power flowed into his Sea of Consciousness.

Fang Heng didn't dare be careless. He immediately stopped what he was doing and closed his eyes to concentrate on controlling the energy.

Under the surge of power, his Sea of Consciousness expanded once more.

Only after another two hours had Fang Heng finished organizing the expansion and fully absorbed the soul power originating from Abe Akaya.

He opened his eyes again and examined the game notifications.

Upon reaching Complete Form, Abe Akaya's attributes had changed entirely. It had acquired Hell power and combined it with its original natural abilities, becoming a dual-attribute entity.

The most significant improvement was its adaptability to Hell.

Previously, Abe Akaya had leveled up mainly by spreading branches and leaves, absorbing sunlight, and merging it into natural energy to grow slowly on its own.

Now, however, Abe Akaya could also absorb Hell-attribute power.

Ever since advancing to Complete Form, Abe Akaya had spread rapidly through Hell while Fang Heng's eyes were closed. It had completely occupied Hell's twenty-first through thirty-fifth levels and could even slowly absorb Hell energy and convert it into experience points.

Only upon reaching the thirty-fifth level had it encountered resistance from large numbers of high-level Hell creatures.

Abe Akaya had only just evolved and hadn't fully mastered its power. It also needed to devote much of its strength to absorbing energy from the Demon Cores and Hell itself. Consequently, it didn't force its way through the thirty-fifth level, instead expanding sideways and occupying the region from the twenty-first to the thirty-fifth level.

That was currently the limit of what Abe Akaya could achieve.

Moreover, judging by Abe Akaya's senses, the deeper the level of Hell, the more energy it could absorb.

As for its other skills...

Fang Heng checked Abe Akaya's description.

[Divine Tree—Abe Akaya.]

[Stage: Complete Form.]

[Additional skills: Dual Attribute, Absorption, Energy Barrier, Nourishment.]

Had it actually ended up with fewer skills than before?

“How do you feel, Abe Akaya?”

Extremely good.

Abe Akaya conveyed a feeling of delight.

Complete Form was its true combat state.

According to its feelings, Abe Akaya considered itself terrifyingly powerful and was eager to launch into a proper fight.

After communicating carefully with Abe Akaya, Fang Heng realized that most of its abilities didn't appear as listed skills. Instead, they manifested directly in combat.

Previously, an enemy caught in Abe Akaya's vines might have managed to break free. Now that it had evolved, Abe Akaya could attach the Demon Seeds' negative Hell energy to its vines.

The effect was drastically stronger than before.

“Is that so...” Fang Heng narrowed his eyes. “Then how does it compare with that power?”

He naturally meant the mysterious force that was constantly drawing energy from Abe Akaya.

Abe Akaya immediately responded.

After evolving, it could now trace the source of the force absorbing its energy and determine the enemy's precise location. It even wanted to try breaking free of the connection.

But Abe Akaya wasn't certain it would succeed.

“Mm, no need to rush. Maintain the connection for now, but slow down their absorption. Don't let them notice anything unusual.”

The source draining Abe Akaya was most likely the Elven Clan.

Fang Heng decided not to act rashly. He needed to use the connection to locate the elves and find the missing Klevi.

The Elven Clan had actually dared target something belonging to him.

They would have to pay the price!

Fang Heng paid no attention to the other Demon Seeds who had fled. If any tried reclaiming the cores, Abe Akaya would probably kill and absorb them outright.

He left Abe Akaya behind for the moment and headed toward the upper levels.

The nineteenth level.

The Holy Court forces had just retreated there.

It had been a hard battle.

Those terrifying plants at the end had left an especially deep impression on everyone.

Only after reaching the nineteenth level, beyond the Hell vines' reach, did Dean finally relax a little.

The troops stopped for a brief rest.

Dean was worried about Fang Heng. He planned to wait a little longer, and if Fang Heng still hadn't returned, he would personally venture into the depths of Hell to search for him.

But he absolutely couldn't take the whole Holy Court army any farther.

If the plants began moving again, Dean couldn't protect so many troops on his own.

“Grand Sage Fang Bai!”

Dean was lost in thought when he heard the Holy Court troops calling out. His heart leapt.

Following their gazes, he saw Fang Heng returning through the passage from the lower levels and breathed a sigh of relief.

Finally!

A great weight lifted from his heart.

As long as Fang Bai was safe, everything was fine.

Now he could lead the army out without worrying.

Dean had no idea what had happened in Hell, but he had the distinct feeling those bizarre plants were somehow connected to Fang Heng.

He needed to ask Fang Bai what had actually happened down there.

Yet as Fang Heng drew nearer, Dean suddenly noticed something. The words he'd been about to say stuck in his throat, and he stared at Fang Heng in disbelief.

What!

Right between Fang Heng's brows!

The mark representing the Will of the Twelve Sages!

“G-Grand Sage Fang Bai, you just...”

“Mm. It was thanks to your help, Grand Sage Dean.”

Fang Heng raised a hand, signaling the Holy Court members bowing to him to stand, and nodded. “I'm fine. Eseklyna was very strong. It took some time to deal with her. I'm glad everyone here is safe.”

“Ah, well...”

Dean opened his mouth.

That wasn't what he'd intended to ask.

But when he heard that Fang Heng had killed the Envy Demon Seed, he froze again.

Dean pressed, “What did you just say? You completely eliminated Envy?”

“Mm. It took quite a bit of effort, plus a little luck.”

Fang Heng added, “But Demon Seeds can resurrect infinitely. I've only dealt with this problem for the time being. It will eventually return.”

Dean fell silent, staring at Fang Heng in astonishment.

Luck? Was it really just luck?

The brat had absolutely come prepared!

He'd been aiming for the Envy Demon Seed from the very beginning!

The Holy Court knew something about what had happened in Hell.

The Demon Seeds had been fighting among themselves, and Envy had used some method to seize the power of other Demon Seeds, dramatically increasing her strength.

Among all the Demon Seeds, Eseklyna had been one of the strongest!

And Fang Heng had killed her?

No wonder he could condense the Will of the Twelve Sages.

Dean suddenly understood Fang Heng's motives for coming here. When he looked at Fang Heng again, there was envy in his eyes.

Admittedly, the Divine Punishment path was well suited to this kind of extermination mission.

Thinking this, Dean sighed inwardly.

He and the other old fellows had spent so many years in the Divine Realm, losing their fighting spirit without ever managing to condense even the initial will. Yet Fang Bai, a newly promoted Grand Sage, was fearless and still had that drive to take risks.

And hadn't his gamble paid off?

— End of Chapter 3032 —
