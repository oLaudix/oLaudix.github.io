Chapter 3099

Battle Preparations

From Chinese Chapter 3100 · 备战

Everyone in the preparation area was rather surprised.

Elder Leander might seem approachable on ordinary days, but his standards were high and he was extremely demanding of his subordinates. Recruited warriors normally underwent a period of observation before being accepted.

Was it because they were so short of recruits lately?

Or was there something extraordinary about this youngster?

While everyone wondered, Leander nodded and said, “Fang Heng, you'll participate in tonight's trial match.”

Fang Heng's heart stirred.

That quickly?

The other warriors in the room wore varying expressions.

This was an important match, and they'd been planning their lineup and tactics for days.

Why change the roster now?

Nordin frowned. “Elder, our roster is already full. Isn't it a little early to have Fang Heng compete tonight?”

“It's fine.”

Leander waved lightly. “I came to inform you that two of our team's warriors resigned from the Noel Clan during the day and officially joined the Kinsfolk as their warriors.”

The room fell silent.

Everyone's expression shifted as they absorbed the news, suspicion stirring in their hearts.

Lately, an unusually large number of people had defected to the Kinsfolk and the Kate Clan. The number was strange enough to be alarming.

“No need to be sad. You'll meet them again in the arena someday, only then as opponents.” Leander walked past Fang Heng and patted his shoulder. “This competition is important. Do your best.”

Fang Heng nodded. “Understood.”

After Leander left, the warriors began discussing the news in hushed voices.

“What happened? They're both gone? At the same time? What a coincidence.”

“Weren't you close to them? What's going on?”

“I don't know either. They never mentioned anything to me. They must have arranged it secretly.”

“You really didn't know?”

“Absolutely not. I only heard just now, same as you.”

Everyone had an uneasy sense of how serious the problem was.

Recently, personnel turnover had been very high throughout the capital, not just within the Noel Clan.

Something wasn't normal.

More importantly, they knew both poached colleagues well. Whatever their fighting ability, both had served the Noel Clan for over a decade.

How had the Kinsfolk persuaded them to switch sides?

“Maybe it's because tonight's match is important? Perhaps they reached a secret agreement with the Kinsfolk long ago and announced their departure at this particular moment to put us at a disadvantage and help the Kinsfolk win?”

Fang Heng's interest stirred.

What a coincidence.

Were they facing the Kinsfolk tonight?

Nordin frowned and turned to a nearby assistant. “Bring up the match information.”

“Yes.”

The assistant retrieved the documents, displayed them on the large screen, and explained, “This match concerns a resolution on protecting a star-ranked race. The Kinsfolk and Noel Clan strongly disagree. The Noel Clan believes every race has a right to survive so long as it doesn't threaten universal peace. That right is affirmed in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's peace declaration.”

“The Kinsfolk contend that the race has already been corrupted by Black Mist and poses a major threat. They argue its application should be rejected and the race preemptively exterminated.”

Reviewing the mission description, the assistant added, “The Kinsfolk initiated the dispute, demanding that the Noel Clan support their position in the inter-racial vote. In return, the Noel Clan presented equivalent demands: the Kinsfolk must relinquish certain planets held under pretexts, abandon plans for long-range strikes against the star system, and open shipping and trade rights in some star sectors.”

Everyone looked toward Fang Heng.

They were beginning to understand why Elder Leander had put him on the roster.

After all, Fang Heng belonged to a pan-human race.

And his homeworld was also threatened by Black Mist.

For all they knew, the Kinsfolk might decide to wipe it out someday.

But...

Were the Kinsfolk really so afraid of Black Mist that they'd resort to such underhanded methods?

There had to be more behind it.

“This is an LV:12 arena match. At most one high-ranked warrior may participate. There are no restrictions on the others.”

Fang Heng understood as well, narrowing his eyes slightly.

Could this be aimed at him?

Probably not.

The Kinsfolk didn't know he'd participate in tonight's arena duel. It was most likely a coincidence.

Nordin nodded. “Everyone, don't let outside matters distract you. Our priority is to win this match cleanly.”

...

That night, at the central district arena.

As usual, the arena was buzzing with excitement.

Fang Heng sat in the preparation area, meditating with his eyes closed.

Elder Leander, leading the team, surveyed the competitors and nodded. “Everyone, the match is about to begin. Arena battles have grown increasingly fierce lately. There have already been more than ten deaths this month. If a dangerous situation arises, don't fight recklessly. Above all, don't lose your lives.”

Everyone nodded.

Nasir sat beside Fang Heng, studying the Kinsfolk fighters in the opposing preparation area. He whispered, “Brother Fang, the people the Kinsfolk have sent this time are all very strong. They won't be easy to deal with.”

“Tch. I can't believe the Kinsfolk recruited members of the Demon Clan too.”

“This will be difficult...”

The warriors quietly discussed their opponents.

Fang Heng gave a soft acknowledgment, stared ahead, and felt battle intent rising in his eyes.

The Kinsfolk.

An old enemy.

As the match approached, Elder Leander nodded to a warrior behind him. “You go first!”

“Yes!”

Tan Zhuang nodded, rose from his chair, and stepped onto the arena platform.

In the opening exchange, both sides tested one another, trying to establish what their opponents could do.

More than ninety percent of intermediate warriors had at least one academic discipline at Transcendent level. The evenly matched fighters put on an excellent spectacle, much to the delight of the audience.

After several bouts, both sides had victories, but the Noel Clan gradually fell behind.

Nasir's turn came.

His alchemy had reached the peak of Transcendent level, but his other abilities were fairly ordinary, making him somewhat weak among intermediate warriors.

Nasir soon lost as well.

Committed to staying low-profile and avoiding trouble, Fang Heng obediently followed the elder's arrangements. He sat quietly watching, studying how the combatants applied their academic abilities and nodding occasionally.

He was learning!

Compared with genuine warriors, game players were vastly less proficient in applying their academic skills.

The score gradually widened to eight to six. Things were looking rather grim for the Noel Clan.

The Noel Clan had only two warriors left.

The high-ranked warrior Hainiya of the Sea Clan, and the low-ranked warrior Fang Heng.

Studying the opponent currently on the platform, Hainiya shook her head. “She combines psychological studies with elemental studies. Her mental abilities are a strong counter to mine. I could win, but I'd expend so much energy that I'd be at a major disadvantage in the next round.”

— End of Chapter 3099 —
