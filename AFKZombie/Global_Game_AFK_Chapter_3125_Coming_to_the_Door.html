Chapter 3125

Coming to the Door

From Chinese Chapter 3126 · 找上门

That's right! The unification of all disciplines!

Once everything had been converted into 'Qi', Fang Heng used his tremendous mental strength to establish and stabilize the internal vortex with almost no effort at all.

Now the state of the energies in Fang Heng's body had undergone another unusual change.

Qi was attribute-neutral energy. Powers of different types condensed into a single vortex at its center, maintaining the overall balance.

Meanwhile, three powers—'Holy', 'Hell', and 'Undead'—rotated rapidly around the vortex, forming a stable structure in which each counterbalanced the others.

For now, though, that stability wasn't quite perfect.

His undead power alone exceeded the combined total of all his other energies.

Fortunately, undead energy was relatively still and quiet, unlike the fierce, turbulent power of Hell.

For the time being, he could maintain the balance.

Looking more closely, he noticed that this balance somewhat resembled the distribution of different energies in his sea of consciousness.

In both cases, a mixture of mental powers of various attributes occupied the center, while the 'Holy', 'Hell', and 'Undead' powers each occupied a point around it.

Fang Heng tried sensing and organizing the completed internal vortex, then slowly opened his eyes.

He'd been so deeply immersed in concentration that it felt as though only ten minutes or so had passed. In reality, more than five hours had gone by.

“Gulp…”

Mu Shunan swallowed and asked cautiously, “You finished?”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng tested it again.

Even when he released his control completely, the vortex inside his body continued rotating with remarkable stability.

In fact, it was far more stable than the disordered state his energies had been in before.

Fang Heng looked at Mu Shunan and said earnestly, “Thank you for your guidance, Brother Shunan.”

“Ah…”

Mu Shunan let out a helpless noise and gave a bitter smile. “Brother Fang Heng, stop making fun of me. How could that count as guidance? You're the one with extraordinary comprehension. You've already gone a step beyond me. I should be asking you to share some pointers instead.”

“Of course.”

Fang Heng cupped his fists toward Mu Shunan.

“I'll be going, then.”

Mu Shunan was speechless.

In less than half a day, Fang Heng had traversed a path Mu Shunan still hadn't completed in more than ten years.

“I'm leaving on an assignment tomorrow. It might take several weeks. When I return, I'll ask you for some proper guidance…”

As Mu Shunan spoke, he looked Fang Heng up and down again. This fellow cultivated at a terrifying speed. The assignment might keep him away for over a month. Surely Fang Heng wouldn't have reached the God level by the time he returned?

For anyone else, that would be impossible.

But Fang Heng?

Damn it!

Mu Shunan almost thought it sounded perfectly normal.

He even had a strange feeling.

If he returned to find that Fang Heng hadn't made another breakthrough, that would be the real surprise.

“Of course.”

Fang Heng nodded.

But thinking about it carefully, there probably wasn't much he could do to help.

After all, he had taken something of a shortcut.

He'd started with immense basic mental attributes, combined them with his insights into the laws, and finally used the Titan bloodline's ability to unify disciplines by converting their energies into Qi. Only then had the breakthrough appeared so effortless.

Other people…

Probably couldn't replicate it at all…

…

After Fang Heng finished cultivating, Anxi drove him back to the villa.

“Mr. Fang Heng, did your cultivation go well?”

“Mm. Pretty well.”

Sitting in the back seat, Fang Heng turned to look out the window, silently making plans.

Demigod-level Occultism and Alchemy could both greatly increase his cultivation speed.

Going a step further, the God level should improve that efficiency even more.

Choosing Alchemy and Occultism as his primary academic disciplines had been the right decision.

After returning, he could use Demigod-level Alchemy to remodel the villa and raise the concentration of natural energy in the surrounding environment, accelerating cultivation even further!

There was also a large quantity of occult knowledge from the evil embryo still waiting in his sea of consciousness.

Absorbing all of it would probably raise his Occultism level substantially.

With that thought, Fang Heng glanced at the game notifications again.

His numerous zombie clones were already grinding Alchemy experience in the basement. They had gained eight levels in a single afternoon.

Not a bad pace.

Oh, right. When he got back, he also needed to clear the black-mist energy out of the basement.

After about half a month of grinding, both Occultism and Alchemy should reach their next advancement bottleneck.

Keep grinding!

Work on Occultism and Alchemy simultaneously, on both fronts!

Fang Heng was full of motivation, almost impatient to get started.

“Mr. Fang Heng.”

“Hm? Something wrong?”

“While you were away, two elves came looking for you several times. They claimed to be your friends. Since you weren't here, we simply asked them to leave their contact details.”

Elves?

Fang Heng's interest was immediately piqued.

His first thought was the elven prince, Korkalot.

Hadn't Korkalot returned home after completing the mission?

Fang Heng asked, “Did they say what it was about?”

“No, they didn't.”

Fang Heng had a vague feeling something might be wrong. “Do you have their contact information?”

“I'll send it to you now.”

Fang Heng took out his phone and immediately dialed the number.

“This is Fang Heng.”

“Mr. Fang Heng!”

The elf on the other end sounded instantly excited. “Sorry to disturb you, Boss Fang. Have you heard anything about His Highness Korkalot?”

“Korkalot hasn't gone back?”

“Boss Fang, we haven't been able to contact His Highness for more than a month. The Elven King is worried about his safety and ordered us to investigate his whereabouts, but we've had no success.”

“When did he disappear?”

“The last time we saw His Highness, he was with Mr. Kappa. Mr. Kappa instructed the starship to depart for home three days later. After that, we never saw His Highness or Mr. Kappa again.”

The elves had very little influence in the Seven Realms Martial Capital. They had no means of locating Korkalot on their own, so they'd come to Fang Heng for help.

“And the ship? It never returned?”

“Captain Kappa hasn't returned, so the starship is still in the port district of the Seven Realms Martial Capital.”

“Understood. I'll look into it.”

Fang Heng hung up.

Strange.

After they'd completed the human race's registration application in the Seven Realms Martial Capital, Kappa and Korkalot should have left the city.

Why hadn't the elves seen either of them?

Could something have happened on the way?

Fang Heng's heart sank.

The Kinsfolk!

Kappa and Korkalot had gone to considerable trouble over humanity's registration.

There was a very real chance the Kinsfolk had targeted them.

He'd been careless! Anger rose in Fang Heng's heart.

The Kinsfolk had orchestrated the earlier incident with the Lanyue Clan. But that disaster had unexpectedly led him to the law-based alchemical arrays hidden there, and he'd become so absorbed in studying them that he hadn't found time to retaliate.

He hadn't expected the Kinsfolk to go even further and strike at people close to him.

Noticing a change in Fang Heng's expression through the rearview mirror, Anxi asked softly, “Mr. Fang Heng, has something happened?”
