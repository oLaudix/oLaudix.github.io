Chapter 2188

Taking the Blame

From original Chinese Chapter 2189 · 背锅

Strange.

There was still no sign of Fang Heng.

Had something happened to him?

“Chairman, the Tree Spirits are about to emerge from the passage into the research institute's outer area. There are Federation security units stationed there.”

Ji Haiting frowned.

It seemed matters had spun out of control.

After thinking rapidly for several seconds, he made his decision. “Sound the alarm immediately. You, alter the surveillance footage. Make this incident look like an invasion by the Tree Spirits.”

The Tree Spirits' appearance hadn't been part of their plan in the first place.

Perfect. They needed someone to carry the black pot of blame, so they might as well dump every last black pot onto the Tree Spirits!

Muddying the waters would give Ji Haiting's people more room to maneuver.

“Understood!”

In an instant, intrusion alerts sounded throughout the institute, alarms blaring everywhere.

Federation guards responded immediately, deploying from security posts across the complex to intercept the escaping Tree Spirits.

Vannis and his group, who had only just fled the research hall, had no idea they'd been made to carry the black pot of blame.

At first they were somewhat disoriented.

After all, they'd entered without using the outer corridors and didn't know which way to run.

But soon they spotted emergency evacuation signs.

That made things much simpler. They followed the signs and hurried along the designated escape route.

Before long, they encountered squads of Federation semi-mechanized soldiers converging from every direction.

The Human Federation!

Vannis's eyes were bloodshot with rage.

If these despicable humans hadn't conspired with the Dependents to build the Spirit Domain blockade network, how could the Tree Spirits ever have fallen so low?!

“Attack! Kill them!”

Desperate to break out alive, Vannis led his elite Tree Spirits into another head-on battle with the Federation's semi-mechanized squads.

The longer they fought, the more anxious Vannis became.

He could see additional Federation reinforcements pouring in from corridors on every side.

With their current combat strength, they would be trapped and killed if this continued.

Vannis could hardly believe that without Fang Heng, they couldn't even manage to escape.

What were they supposed to do?!

He was still entrusted with Elder Levin's final request.

Vannis had the sinking feeling that he'd reached the end of the road.

Then, in an instant, the corridor lights began flickering wildly. They flared brilliantly once before plunging everything into darkness.

Huh?

What?

What was happening?

For a moment, the Federation's semi-mechanized squads fighting the Tree Spirits were equally confused.

They'd just received word of a Tree Spirit invasion from within the facility.

The strangest part was that the reported breach had occurred in the central secret research institute.

How had the intruders gotten in there?

And now…

Why had the facility's power systems suddenly failed for no apparent reason?

The entire power supply depended on the Spirit Domain blockade network created by the Dependents.

There had never been a single problem with it since its construction!

“Don't stop! Keep suppressing them with fire!”

Deputy Security Captain Wang Yiwen shouted.

The thermal imaging systems built into their mechanized armor meant that darkness was no obstacle. They could continue pinning the Tree Spirits down with beam weapons.

While the troops were still puzzling over the situation, emergency power came back online within half a minute.

The lights returned.

“Watch out!”

Suddenly, a pale-green arrow streaked out from the opposing side, cutting straight through the dense volley of energy beams!

Hiss!

It struck the outer armor of a soldier standing beside Wang Yiwen.

Creak! Creak, creak…

What was that sound?

Wang Yiwen turned and saw that vines had sprouted from the point where the arrow had struck his companion's armor!

They spread at astonishing speed!

In moments, the semi-mechanized suit was completely covered in a dense web of vines!

The soldier was bound up like a twisted rope, unable to move a muscle!

The vines even kept trying to worm their way into gaps in the armor.

What was going on?!

A moment later, a barrage of emerald arrows came flying back at them!

At the same time, voices burst out of the wireless intercom.

“The Tree Spirits are counterattacking! This is bad! They've still got something up their sleeves!”

“Reporting casualties! Our squad has wounded personnel…”

“The enemy's power has increased drastically!”

Seeing the situation turn against them, Wang Yiwen shouted, “Quick! Fall back for now!”

His squad immediately dragged the comrades ensnared by vines back down the corridor.

Wang Yiwen was shaken.

What had just happened?!

How had the Tree Spirits suddenly become so powerful?!

Meanwhile, Vannis and the Tree Spirits counterattacking the Federation squads looked equally bewildered.

Vannis lowered his head and stared at his hands.

His power…

The seal was gone.

It had happened during those brief moments when the lights went out.

“HAHAHAHAHAHA!”

After the initial shock, Vannis threw back his head and roared with laughter!

Nearly two hundred years of suppression!

The crushing shackles that had weighed him down all that time had finally been broken.

He had never felt so liberated!

His strength!

It had returned!

At that moment, Vannis was absolutely certain that the Spirit Domain blockade network had vanished completely!

His strength had recovered to its former peak!

“Elder Levin…”

Vannis remembered something and murmured the name, his eyes growing moist.

It must have been Elder Levin. He must have wrung out the last of his life force to destroy the network and free their people.

Raising his head, Vannis looked at the Federation soldiers ahead. His grief turned back into boundless fury.

“Federation!”

Now that his power had returned, Vannis's confidence soared. He ordered the Tree Spirit elites to advance down the corridor in pursuit.

A counterattack!

In the monitoring room, Ji Haiting watched the Tree Spirits' sudden surge in strength, his brows tightly furrowed.

Could it be…?

He had a growing suspicion.

“Report! Chairman! The Spirit Domain blockade network failed thirty seconds ago. Every spatial-jump system relying on it has stopped working!”

“Our communications with the Dependents have been completely severed!”

Ji Haiting's pupils contracted, and his heart began racing.

Fang Heng!

Had he really succeeded?!

He'd destroyed the entire Spirit Domain network?!

Truthfully, when Fang Heng first mentioned the idea, Ji Haiting had never imagined he could actually pull it off.

Now the entire world would be transformed by the consequences.

The Tree Spirits had regained enormous power. Having already suffered under one blockade, they'd surely be extremely cautious and never casually allow the Federation to rebuild it…

“Still no word from Fang Heng?”

Ji Haiting turned to his subordinates. “What's happening below? Have you restored communications with him?!”

The staff hurriedly worked their keyboards.

“We're checking now. Electromagnetic interference is rapidly weakening. We expect to restore the link within a minute. Attempting to reconnect…”

Editing and Source Notes
  • Scapegoats. Ji Haiting deliberately edits security footage to blame the Tree Spirits for the institute incident.

Chinese Expressions

  • 背锅. “Carry the cooking pot”: take the blame for someone else’s mess.
  • 把水搅浑. “Stir the water muddy”: create confusion to make further maneuvering possible.
