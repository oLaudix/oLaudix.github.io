Chapter 3025

Infighting

From Chinese Chapter 3026 · 内讧

“Remember our agreement? Envy isn't even dead yet, and you already want to start fighting among yourselves?”

The other Demon Seeds stopped and looked toward Jing'er.

One Demon Seed fixed him with a stare and asked grimly, “Jing'er, what's the meaning of this? Are you planning to use the Demon Cores to keep us under your control?”

“Everyone, I'm sure you understand how important the Demon Cores are. We all want to obtain their power to strengthen ourselves as much as possible.”

Jing'er surveyed the crowd, paused, then said loudly, “But Envy hasn't been eliminated yet. I don't want us fighting one another first and letting Envy reap the benefits.”

Demon Seeds were proud and unruly by nature. They weren't about to accept his explanation.

“Hmph, easy for you to say! Did you learn that smooth-talking bullshit from humans?”

“The Demon Cores are in your hands, so you can say whatever you like. Once we've dealt with Eseklyna, the Tongue of Envy, you'll sit back and enjoy the spoils, then use the cores to control us.”

Jing'er said, “The Demon Cores can stay here for now. Once we've dealt with the Envy Demon Seed, we'll decide how to divide them.”

“How do we know you're telling the truth? Who could trust you? Who would even want to?”

“Exactly, Jing'er! What gives you the right to guard the Demon Cores? I trust no one but myself.”

A thin, sharp-featured old man sneered. “As I see it, since we've already obtained the Demon Cores, we might as well fight it out now. Let the strongest take them. Whoever absorbs a core keeps it. We'd have a better chance of dealing with Eseklyna afterward, too.”

Jing'er said coldly, “It isn't that I disagree, but you've seen the protective layer surrounding the Demon Cores. It'll take time to wear it down before anyone can actually get them...”

“Heh, only a weakling like you would need time. Let me do it, I can—”

As the argument continued, Zhuang Yufei suddenly said sharply, “Everyone, stop arguing! She's here!”

No sooner had she spoken than the other Demon Seeds sensed the mighty aura rushing closer. They fell silent and turned toward the cavern entrance.

“Boom!!!”

A vast wave of Hell energy surged into the cavern, followed immediately by Eseklyna bursting inside.

“You lot!”

Eseklyna had anticipated something like this, but seeing the assembled Demon Seeds still filled her with uncontrollable fury.

“Good! Very good! If you've all come looking for me together, so much the better! It saves me hunting you down one by one! You're all going to die here today!”

Whoosh!!

Laughing coldly, Eseklyna charged straight at one of the Demon Seeds.

“Kill her first!”

The Demon Seeds temporarily put their disputes aside and joined forces against Eseklyna again.

Old grudges and fresh hatred drove both sides to fight furiously.

Jing'er's group had the numerical advantage and no longer had to worry about the cores. Eseklyna was still drained from fighting Fang Heng and suppressed by the Demon-Binding Chains, so she quickly fell behind.

But the Demon Seeds weren't united at heart.

As soon as they gained the upper hand, they deliberately slowed their attacks and started calculating their own gains and losses.

Everyone knew that even after defeating the Envy Demon Seed, they'd still have to fight one another for the Demon Cores.

Only a fool would put his back into the fight now!

Before long, fewer and fewer were making a real effort. Most were slacking off to conserve their strength, handing Eseklyna an opportunity instead.

Meanwhile, on the twenty-eighth level of Hell.

After leaving the cavern, Fang Heng hadn't pursued Eseklyna directly. Instead, he'd doubled back and darted into a hidden cavern on the twenty-eighth level.

Using himself as bait had really cost him. His Sacred Studies level had been drained all the way down to LV:1.

Fortunately, he'd managed to preserve his Grand Sage-tier bloodline level.

And because all his attributes had been strengthened by his various academic skills together, weakening just Sacred Studies had only a limited effect on his overall fighting strength.

That was why Fang Heng had dared to offer himself as bait in the first place.

Had another Grand Sage of the Divine Realm been drained by Eseklyna like that, their combat ability might have fallen below one percent of its peak.

“Tap!”

Fang Heng lightly touched down and darted into the cavern ahead.

At its center lay a large magic array that had only recently been completed.

Fang Heng positioned himself in the array's center. The magic circle in his right pupil spun rapidly as psychic power flowed through his legs into the formation.

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss...”

Activated by his psychic power, the array shone with pale-green light.

Moments later, Abe Akaya's vines emerged from the center of the active array, rapidly spreading to envelop the cavern and the surrounding area.

Done!

“Abe Akaya, follow me.”

Fang Heng calmly stopped channeling his psychic power, left the cavern, and rushed toward Eseklyna's position on Hell's twenty-ninth level.

...

Soon, Fang Heng reached the twenty-ninth level and followed his senses toward the lava where Echidna had been.

As he approached the area and sensed the fluctuations of battle nearby, he gradually slowed down and hid in the distance to observe.

Eseklyna had already returned ahead of him.

Unfortunately for her, she'd been a step too late. Her demonic pet Echidna was dead.

Everything had proceeded as Fang Heng predicted. Eseklyna was fighting the other Demon Seeds.

The most difficult part of his plan was actually complete.

All that remained was to let the snipe and the clam fight while he, the fisherman, profited in secret.

But judging from the scene, Eseklyna seemed to be gaining a slight advantage.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes, studied everyone's movements, and immediately understood.

Good grief.

These Demon Seeds had ten thousand schemes in their heads. On the surface, they all appeared to be fighting Eseklyna hard, entangling her and wearing her down. In truth, their positions and attention were subtly focused on the Demon Cores behind them.

Eseklyna also wanted to seize the cores to control the other Demon Seeds. But they understood the cores' importance and would go all out to stop her whenever she tried approaching them.

Both sides maintained a delicate balance.

Fang Heng concealed his aura, surveyed the battlefield from afar, and considered his options.

The Demon-Binding Chains could theoretically last for more than half a month, but their strength would weaken noticeably over time. If he delayed further, things might change.

It looked like he couldn't play the fisherman after all.

He'd have to find a way to deal with the problem quickly.

Fang Heng fixed his gaze on the Demon Cores spinning rapidly around the stone pillar.

Looking closely, he could see that a dark protective layer surrounded the cores.

The shock waves from the battle outside weren't affecting the cores within at all.

That protective layer was probably why the Demon Seeds hadn't yet tried to seize the cores.

— End of Chapter 3025 —
