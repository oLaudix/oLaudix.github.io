Chapter 2083

The Lure

From original Chinese Chapter 2084 · 引诱

"And that terrifying regeneration of his," someone added.

The players exchanged another look.

No wonder this was an SSS-rank quest. It was troublesome indeed!

Just one unidentified foot soldier from the Holy Court had already given them a serious headache.

"What do we do?"

"We won't win a direct fight. We'll have to outsmart him. Find a way to lure him over there."

Meng Si drew a deep breath and glanced down the passage to the right. "That's the ambush area we prepared for the Holy Court. Lure him toward the other altar and use the Glutton's Touch against him!"

The others nodded.

"Good! He has powerful attributes, doesn't he? Then let the Glutton's Touch drain him dry!"

Not far away, Fang Heng was still moving forward.

Huh?

Again?

He sensed the hidden figures approaching rather than retreating. Anticipating the attack, he whipped his scepter backward and swung!

Meng Si leaped from the shadows with his saber raised, only to find Fang Heng's scepter hurtling straight toward him.

BOOM!

Struck by Fang Heng's scepter for the second time, Meng Si felt as though all his internal organs had been shaken loose. Once again, he was sent flying helplessly backward.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Several dark needles shot from the shadows at Fang Heng.

He tilted his head to evade them and looked toward the attacker.

Five players generally formed a standard player squad. All the signs suggested this group were indeed players.

But did they really need to fight so desperately?

Fang Heng sensed something was wrong.

They knew they couldn't defeat him, yet they kept attacking. It almost seemed as if they were deliberately leading him somewhere.

Suspicious!

He was considering whether to continue when a game notification suddenly appeared.

[Hint: Your Crusader Trainee Mark is being affected by an unknown influence. Exercise caution.]

[Hint: You are approaching a dangerous region. If you continue deeper, the mark may cease functioning, and you may no longer be able to use it to reverse-summon yourself back to the Eternal Holy City.]

Hmm?

Fang Heng glanced instinctively at the Crusader Trainee Mark on his wrist. It had visibly dimmed.

There was such a good thing?!

His interest was immediately piqued.

Until now, every trip into and out of these trial worlds had relied on the mark on the back of his hand. Besides teleportation, the mark also monitored him.

If it stopped functioning...

He could remain in this world for as long as possible, and use his other abilities without the Holy Court finding out.

That would give him far more freedom to act!

Delighted, Fang Heng pretended not to notice the players' attempt to lure him in and followed them deeper down the passage.

As he advanced, the environment gradually changed. At some point, a thin red mist had begun forming around him.

Mist?

Fang Heng examined its energy closely. It felt extremely familiar.

Strange. He was certain he'd encountered it before, but he couldn't recall where.

Hiss, hiss, hiss!

The red mist seemed to be drawn toward him, gathering into a thin veil around his body.

More notifications appeared.

[Hint: Your Crusader Trainee Mark is being affected.]

[Hint: Under the current environmental conditions, your Crusader Trainee Mark has become completely ineffective. You can no longer use it for teleportation.]

[Warning: The current environment is extremely dangerous. Withdraw immediately.]

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

Completely disabled!

The holy shield surrounding him hissed as the red mist corroded it.

Evidently, the power in the mist countered holy energy to some degree. It could even restrict the Crusader Mark!

Excellent!

This was wonderful!

He'd been waiting for a moment like this!

Overjoyed, Fang Heng sensed the five players hiding in the shadows again. They were still retreating, occasionally harassing him with ranged attacks.

Let's see what tricks you're playing.

He immediately gave chase.

Whoosh!

When a dark figure lunged from concealment, Fang Heng swung his scepter hard toward it.

Thud!

The blow sent the player flying into a cavern farther down the passage.

Fang Heng frowned.

His senses told him all five players had entered that cave. More importantly, it was one of the sources of the pale-red mist.

If he wasn't mistaken, they'd deliberately lured him here.

An ambush?

Wait...

As the mist thickened, Fang Heng finally recognized the source of its energy.

A Demon Seed!

That's right. It was definitely the power of a Demon Seed!

It was extremely similar to the energy of the seed inside his body—the power of the Demon Seed of Envy!

Yet there was something different about it, too. It wasn't quite the same as the Seed of Envy occupying his sea of consciousness. That difference was why he hadn't recognized it immediately.

What was going on?

His heart quickened as he looked into the cavern.

What exactly was inside?

"Interesting..." he murmured.

With the Crusader trial rune now inactive, he was free of the Holy Court's surveillance. He could switch forms and use every trump card he possessed.

How dangerous could a trap laid by a few players really be?

Confident he could handle it, Fang Heng stepped into the cavern.

Huh?

An altar?

The dim stone walls looked cold. In the middle of the enclosed chamber stood a stone altar about half a person's height.

It appeared to have been assembled rather crudely from stacked rocks, with no particular order to its construction.

Thin red mist was visibly seeping out of it.

Fang Heng approached cautiously, studying the structure. Suddenly, he raised his eyebrows.

A faint light emerged from within the stone, illuminating markings concealed inside the altar.

Huh?!

No. Something was wrong!

Those markings...

Information he'd obtained earlier from Demon Seed followers flashed through his mind.

Every Demon Seed possessed its own corresponding demonic pattern. Through an altar bearing that pattern, the seed could absorb power from vast distances—even across countless planes.

It was somewhat similar to the Holy Court's power of faith.

But the demonic pattern on this stone altar wasn't Envy's.

It belonged to Gluttony!

And yet...

Fang Heng inspected the designs carefully.

Someone had added another layer of demonic markings over the original pattern of Gluttony.

Those markings belonged to the Seed of Envy!

As he studied the altar, Fang Heng realized he might have stumbled upon information connected to his mission. His brow furrowed as he carefully considered what it meant.
