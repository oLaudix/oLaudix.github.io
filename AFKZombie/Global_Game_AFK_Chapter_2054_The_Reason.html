Chapter 2054

The Reason

From original Chinese Chapter 2055 · 缘由

Perhaps Xia Xi had foreseen this situation. That would explain why she'd placed an extraordinarily powerful restriction around the Demon Seed in Fang Heng's sea of consciousness.

The restriction temporarily sealed its power, preventing it from spreading. The protection was so complete that Fang Heng had never even been aware of the Demon Seed's existence before.

The power Envy had left behind, however, was constantly wearing away at that outer seal while absorbing the Demon Seed's energy.

Whenever part of the protective restriction was removed, Envy's power replaced it, gradually devouring the Demon Seed's strength while maintaining a surprisingly stable balance inside Fang Heng's body.

Now the Luminous Grating Stone had destroyed part of that outer layer of Envy's power.

Freed from Envy's influence and missing some of its original restraints, the Demon Seed's core power had erupted violently.

The surge had come too suddenly and with too much force for Fang Heng to control!

Black mist poured endlessly from his body, spreading in every direction.

And the first person harmed by this loss of control wasn't his enemy. It was Fang Heng himself.

Fortunately, most of the original restrictions still protected the foundations of his sea of consciousness. The remaining traces of Envy's corruption also weakened the Demon Seed's core power.

For now, his consciousness wasn't being destroyed.

His physical body was another matter. Only the constant regeneration of his Undying Body kept repairing the damage fast enough for him to survive.

Even so, his sea of consciousness felt as if it were being tossed by a raging ocean. The agony was unbearable, and his awareness was beginning to blur.

…

Outside the Temple of Zeus, the Holy Court party stared warily at the young man shrouded in thick black contamination.

“Attack!”

Cardinals stationed in mid-tier worlds generally served as overseers, but each possessed elite-level strength by high-tier standards.

A Book of Origins, glowing red, appeared before each of the three cardinals.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Sharp golden Oaths of Judgment streaked through the air like lightning, forming brilliant golden crosses that plunged into the black fog.

Hiss! Hiss!

It sounded like red-hot iron being plunged into the sea. The mist hissed violently as the crosses blasted visible whirlpools through it.

Fang Heng was struggling to stabilize his sea of consciousness. Half-delirious, he sensed danger approaching and instinctively raised a hand to block the attack.

BOOM!

Before his fist could connect, the Demon Seed's power surged outward like a flood!

Thicker black mist condensed instantly and rushed toward the three golden crosses.

BANG! BANG!

The sacred crosses dissolved rapidly within the darkness, exploding with muffled reports.

This is bad. I need to escape somewhere!

Realizing Holy Court reinforcements had arrived, Fang Heng darted forward through the mist. He intended to attack the three cardinals and find an opening to escape.

Damn it!

He'd barely begun moving when another violent wave surged through his sea of consciousness. The world seemed to spin around him. His attack collapsed, and he almost stumbled to the ground.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

At the same time, more than a dozen streams of sacred light shot toward him from outside the fog.

BANG! BANG! BANG!

They blasted holes in the dark aura surrounding Fang Heng, pinning him into a corner where he could barely move.

Silufi observed him closely, realizing his condition was abnormal.

“Send word to the Divine Domain immediately. We've located the Demon Seed!”

“Understood!”

The three cardinals gathered before the Archbishop. Even this brief exchange had shown them how dangerous the situation was.

The Demon Seed's power exceeded their expectations. Ordinary attacks couldn't defeat it completely. Or rather, the Demon Seed itself was essentially immortal.

Yet the man before them couldn't control its strength and was actually suffering from its backlash.

That was deeply strange.

Silufi stared at Fang Heng.

“Use that technique. Try to seal him.”

The three cardinals nodded gravely. There was no other choice.

All three activated their dark-red Books of Origins.

Rustle, rustle…

The books slowly opened. Yellowed pages turned, giving off a faint radiance, until each stopped on a particular page.

Whoosh!

A page transformed into a brilliant dark-gold beam, shot through the layers of mist, and coiled precisely around Fang Heng's right wrist!

Hiss! Hiss!

Still struggling to calm his mind, Fang Heng heard the noise and looked down.

A golden band had locked tightly around his wrist. Its sacred power clung to him, eating away at the Demon Seed's energy and releasing billows of vapor.

[Notice: You are affected by an unknown Binding effect. All attributes reduced by 10%. Other effects of the binding are unknown.]

A sacred binding ability!

Fang Heng frowned.

Strangely, being restrained felt… rather good?

The sacred energy on his wrist had weakened the Demon Seed slightly. The raging ocean within his sea of consciousness had actually become a little calmer.

Before he could think further, another violent surge swept through his mind.

“It's working!”

The cardinals saw that the black fog leaking from Fang Heng had been greatly reduced after his wrist was bound. Their eyes brightened.

The Chapter of Imprisonment was taking effect!

Whoosh!

A second page transformed into a streak of light, pierced the fog, and wrapped around Fang Heng's left wrist.

Hiss! Hiss!

More black vapor rose from his skin.

While Fang Heng was restrained, several inquisitors behind the cardinals launched targeted retribution spells, hoping to weaken him further.

BANG! BANG!

For the first time, their attacks dispersed the protective black fog and actually struck his body.

The onlookers stared in disbelief. Burns left by sacred fire were healing almost as soon as they appeared!

What terrifying regeneration!

Fang Heng was concentrating on restoring order inside his consciousness. His awareness of the outside world had grown hazy.

He couldn't use his full strength, and escaping was impossible. Rather than resist, he decided to focus entirely on stabilizing his mind.

But unexpectedly, the attacks from the Holy Court's sacred energy stimulated the Demon Seed within him—and its power erupted once more.

— End of Chapter 2054 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Protective seal. Xia Xi apparently sealed Fang Heng’s original Demon Seed; Envy’s influence partially replaced the seal while consuming the Seed’s power. Destroying that layer disturbs the balance.
  • Binding vs imprisonment. 禁锢之章 is translated Chapter of Imprisonment, a sacred restraint. It is unrelated to the necromantic Book of Soul Imprisonment acquired earlier.
