Chapter 2050

The Draw

From original Chinese Chapter 2051 · 抽

Recording skills didn't take long. He could simply keep drawing new ones.

Baptism, on the other hand, was clearly not instantaneous. It required more time, which explained why the players and NPCs remained gathered around the statue.

Fang Heng stepped forward again, closed his eyes, and prepared to receive the sacred light.

[Notice: You have mastered Advanced Sacred Studies. You may undergo baptism by spending 10,000 Survival Points per minute and 1 Main God Point per minute.]

That's not too bad.

The Main God Point cost was small at this stage. Survival Points weren't a concern at all; he could easily afford ten or even a hundred times the amount.

A gentle beam of sacred light descended from the crystal chandelier overhead, bathing his body.

[Notice: You have entered the Baptism state.]

An irresistible sense of peace and security rose within Fang Heng. Warmth spread from the light across his skin and gradually circulated throughout his body.

A game notification appeared.

[Notice: You have received sacred baptism. Constitution permanently increased by 0.2.]

Nice, nice.

Ordinary players didn't have an easy time raising their attributes. The Holy Court's baptism system really wasn't bad.

Every few minutes, another notice appeared, increasing one attribute. The gains were small, generally between 0.1 and 0.2 points.

At first, the increases went to Constitution and Strength.

As time passed, Fang Heng felt the warm current within him gradually change direction and flow toward his sea of consciousness.

Was it about to improve Spirit and Will now?

Hm?!

He sensed something strange.

The gentle warmth reached his sea of consciousness. But the previously peaceful inner space immediately began to change.

The gray mist occupying one corner reacted.

Provoked by the incoming energy, the mist stirred and lunged toward it.

Destroy it!

As though confronting a mortal enemy, the gray fog began furiously attacking the warm sacred current.

Trouble! The corruption!

The gray mist was the corruption left by the Demon Seed of Envy.

Hiss! Hiss!

The violently surging fog almost instantly obliterated every trace of the dark-gold warmth that entered his sea of consciousness—as though declaring ownership of the territory.

But the sacred light shining on Fang Heng wasn't willing to give up either. More energy began flowing into his body, moving through his internal circulation and entering the sea of consciousness to assault the corruption again.

The two forces began tearing at each other.

Damn it!

Fang Heng realized what was happening. The outside energy had come to “baptize” and enhance his sea of consciousness, only to encounter the Demon Seed's corruption already entrenched there.

The two powers were opposites, so they'd begun fighting.

The corruption was higher in level and naturally held the advantage.

What the hell? Now my own head has turned into a battlefield?

What if the clash grew worse and damaged his sea of consciousness? He might end up losing his mind!

He was about to stop the process when another game notice suddenly appeared.

[Notice: Due to an unknown influence, the power of the Demon Seed within you has been weakened. Estimated time remaining before the Demon Seed takes control has increased by 3 hours.]

Huh?! What?!

Fang Heng's heart stirred.

The Demon Seed had been weakened—by the sacred energy that had just entered his sea of consciousness?

After thinking for a moment, he understood. While annihilating the sacred power, the gray mist had also lost a tiny fraction of its own strength.

The loss was so small that even he hadn't detected it, but the game system had registered it and issued a notification.

What a surprise!

The corruption in his mind had always been like a ticking time bomb. Any way of delaying its explosion was welcome.

Still, he needed to be careful. The sea of consciousness was far too important to experiment recklessly.

He continued the baptism and cautiously guided the incoming sacred energy into contact with the mist, hoping to wear down the corruption a little further while keeping the internal situation under control.

It was working!

[Notice: Due to an unknown influence, the Demon Seed within you has been weakened. Remaining time before Demon Seed control increased by 12 hours.]

While Fang Heng carefully maintained the balance inside his mind, the other players in the hall began opening their eyes one by one and staring at him.

Several NPCs receiving baptism noticed the abnormality as well.

What was going on with this guy?

The sacred light descending from the crystal chandelier seemed to be drawn toward Fang Heng by an invisible force. More and more of it gathered around him.

He was pulling in well over half the light meant for everyone!

They'd all paid to be here. Normally there was more than enough sacred energy for everyone, with plenty left over. But with Fang Heng present, there suddenly wasn't enough to go around.

Why should we pay the same price while you get the feast and everyone else gets leftovers?

The onlookers were annoyed. They exchanged glances, but nobody dared confront him first.

Fang Heng was unfamiliar to them, and anyone who could draw sacred energy toward himself like that was likely extremely powerful.

With no NPC volunteering to intervene, the players certainly weren't eager to offend him.

They had paid for their time in hourly increments. Reluctantly, they swallowed their complaints, closed their eyes, and tried to continue.

Less energy was better than nothing. What could they do against someone so strong?

But only a few minutes later, they were forced to open their eyes again.

The sacred light had gathered so densely around Fang Heng that it formed a dark-golden film around his body.

It looked as though he were swallowing all the sacred energy in the hall by himself!

Seriously?!

The other players were seething. The NPCs were growing impatient too.

Fine, eat the meat yourself. But at least leave everyone else a little broth!

A paladin in armor finally strode forward and reached out to place a hand on Fang Heng's shoulder.

Whoosh!

Before he could speak, and just as his fingers were about to touch Fang Heng, his face changed drastically.

The sacred energy inside the paladin suddenly surged out of his control like a flood, rushing toward Fang Heng's body!

He recoiled in fright and hastily pulled his hand back.

— End of Chapter 2050 —
