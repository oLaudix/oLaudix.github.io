Chapter 2062

Chaos

From original Chinese Chapter 2063 · 混乱

[Hint: The player has activated the Omniscient Eye, forcing the magic array to accelerate…]

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The array engraved across the ceiling suddenly came under the influence of the Omniscient Eye. Its rotation accelerated out of control, and brilliant light burst from its patterns!

The intensity was even greater than when it had suppressed the Demon Seed's earlier eruption. Sacred radiance poured down, raising the temperature in the hall by more than ten degrees!

The prisoners in the Demon-Sealing Temple cried out in agony beneath the dazzling light.

Fang Heng, however, had already switched to his Sacred Studies form and was completely unaffected.

Above them, the oracles operating the suppression array paled.

“Grand Adjudicator!”

“I know!”

Cold sweat covered the Grand Adjudicator's forehead. He had noticed the abnormality immediately and was desperately trying to force the array back into a stable state.

But he couldn't.

Some unknown force had seized control of the formation and was driving it faster and faster. He was powerless to stop it!

Its speed continued to climb without restraint. A chill crept down his spine.

If this went on, the entire array would…

BOOM!

The magic array exploded before their eyes!

At the same moment, the formation on the cavern's ceiling reached its limit and shattered completely.

The prisoners gradually recovered from the suppression and turned toward Fang Heng with reverence.

Not a single one dared underestimate him now. The array that had kept them imprisoned for decades, even centuries, had been destroyed just like that!

The Demon Seed's abilities were frightening beyond belief!

Fang Heng turned and surveyed the cells, then strode toward the imprisoned demon.

As Fang Heng approached, the demon felt a sudden, inexplicable fear and retreated half a step.

“You…”

Then he seemed to realize something, and delight appeared on his face.

Crack!

Fang Heng placed both hands against the metal bars.

Creeeak!

The bars bent inward, opening a broad gap.

“Remember what you promised,” he said.

As he spoke, game notifications appeared across his vision.

[Hint: You successfully caused chaos in the Holy Court Cathedral in Duncha, killed a number of believers and guards, and inflicted major losses on the Holy Court. Rewards: 1,330,000 Undead Faction Reputation, 2,010,000 Undead Faction Contribution Points, and 420,000 Undead Faction Honor Points.]

[Hint: You destroyed the Holy Court's Demon-Sealing Cavern magic array. Rewards: 1,000,000 Undead Faction Reputation, 1,000,000 Undead Faction Contribution Points, and 2,000,000 Undead Faction Honor Points.]

[Hint: You released the demon imprisoned in the Demon-Sealing Cavern, causing chaos for the Holy Court. Rewards: 20,000 Undead Faction Reputation, 100,000 Undead Faction Contribution Points, and 20,000 Undead Faction Honor Points.]

Huh?

He even earned extra contribution points for releasing prisoners?

What a pleasant coincidence!

Fang Heng kept his face expressionless, but inwardly he was delighted.

When the other prisoners saw the demon freed, the cells erupted with shouts again.

“Open my cell too! I can help you!”

“Demon Seed! Me too! Pick me! The rest are useless—I'm the best fighter here!”

Fang Heng did need people to create trouble and obstruct the Holy Court's pursuers. He went from cell to cell, bending the iron bars apart and releasing the prisoners.

Besides, he could earn a few more Honor Points in the process. Not many, perhaps, but free rewards were free rewards!

The prisoners poured out. Not one of them dared attack Fang Heng, or even approach the miniature spatial hub that was still running in the center.

The corrupting energy he had unleashed earlier had terrified even them. And the methods he had used afterward were completely unheard of!

After being imprisoned here for so long, who wouldn't want to escape? The Holy Court would be back soon. They would only get one opportunity!

The fused Tyrants had finished transporting every cluster of Sacred Luminous Lattice Stone. Less than five minutes later, the teleportation portal closed.

There was only one way out now: the main entrance!

The freed prisoners crowded around the exit, ready to give the approaching Holy Court a nasty surprise.

On the other side of the alloy door, the Holy Court's guards were desperately nervous, unsure what to do.

For thousands of years, the Demon-Sealing Cavern had operated without incident—until today!

Was this Fang Heng's doing? What had happened inside? Could he really have destroyed the array? Or had the Demon Seed's full power erupted?

“Archbishop!”

At the critical moment, the guards saw Xilufei approaching from the opposite end of the corridor to take command.

“What's happened? Has anyone left?”

“No one has come out. There was another violent fluctuation just now.”

Xilufei frowned. The spatial fluctuations had vanished half a minute earlier. That wasn't necessarily good news. Had Fang Heng escaped using spatial power?

“The spatial fluctuations are gone. Beware of deception. Set up seals—we cannot let him escape!”

“Understood!”

The guards began to fall back. The three cardinal archbishops joined forces and laid three layers of defensive magic arrays around the cavern.

Within thirty seconds, the entire exit was sealed behind three protective formations.

The commanders exchanged looks.

“Go,” Bishop Xilufei ordered.

BANG!

The Holy Court guards opened the Demon-Sealing Temple's main door from outside.

Almost immediately, the prisoners who had been suppressed there for decades or centuries rushed out and attacked the Holy Court!

“Hold them back!”

The defenders had suspected something like this, yet their faces still darkened. Their worst fear had come true!

With the suppression array destroyed, the seals had been compromised. The prisoners' power could no longer be restrained.

A prison riot!

The three cardinals immediately moved to intercept the escaping prisoners.

Archbishop Xilufei stayed behind the front line, scanning the battlefield for Fang Heng.

Where was he? Where was Fang Heng?

Suddenly her pupils contracted. Her gaze had settled on the inside of the cavern, and profound shock filled her eyes.

What?!

The Sacred Luminous Lattice Stones in the central flowerbed—what had happened to them?!

The bed was a wreck, littered with thick, broken stumps of crystal clusters.

Damn it! What had they done?!

Xilufei felt her heart bleed. They had spent decades protecting and painstakingly cultivating those sacred lattice stones!

Now they had been devastated!

It was an unspeakable humiliation!

— End of Chapter 2062 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Reward numerals. Chinese 万/W is ten thousand: 133W = 1,330,000, 201W = 2,010,000, and 200W = 2,000,000.
  • Omniscient Eye. 全知之瞳 is the special ability Fang Heng uses to drive the suppression array into overload.
  • Central Bishop. The source uses different ecclesiastical ranks around Xilufei, including 中枢主教 and 大主教. These have not been silently unified.
