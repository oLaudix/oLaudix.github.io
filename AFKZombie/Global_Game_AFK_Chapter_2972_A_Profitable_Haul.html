Chapter 2972

A Profitable Haul

From Chinese Chapter 2973 · 赚到

With a ripple in space, five Crocodile Clan warships jumped into the mining area under attack.

The Lickers stationed outside the mine immediately threw themselves toward the warships.

“Insects! Open fire!”

The ships' beam cannons, focused-beam emitters, and cutting weapons unleashed a dense volley straight ahead.

After that concentrated barrage, the ground was littered with shattered flesh.

Huh?

The crocodile officer commanding the operation quickly noticed something unusual.

Why were the insects at this mine different from the swarms they'd encountered before?

Looking closely, he saw the fragments of flesh covering the ground wriggle and knit themselves together at a speed visible to the naked eye. They re-formed into insect bodies and charged straight toward the ships again.

Keep firing!

The warships unleashed another concentrated barrage.

These insects had such terrifying regenerative abilities that even after being blown to pieces several times, they still returned to normal?

Not far away, Fang Heng, Zaka, and the other players watched from a distance as energy weapons blasted the severed limbs and shredded flesh across the ground.

“Boss Fang.” The Flying Squirrel captain accompanying them was nervous and whispered, “Those ships are very powerful. If they discover we're stealing ore...”

“Shh.”

Before Fang Heng could answer, Zaka raised a hand to hush Janni and whispered, “Don't worry. Just watch carefully and learn.”

Several barrages failed to eliminate the Lickers completely. Finally, the Crocodile Clan had no choice but to deploy physical injection weapons designed for Ouroboros Seeds. Only then did they temporarily suppress the Lickers' self-healing and wipe out the horde.

Once they confirmed the area was secure, the warships hovered in midair.

A crocodile advance reconnaissance team disembarked and entered the mine to investigate.

Hmm?

Once inside, the crocodiles wore expressions of profound confusion.

This was too strange!

There wasn't a single insect anywhere inside, yet obvious signs of excavation marked the entire mine.

What did that mean?

In such a short time, a full third of the mine had been excavated!

The crocodile captain was dumbfounded and ordered his subordinates to search the mine carefully.

Even after checking every corner several times, they couldn't find a trace of the insects.

What was going on?

Where was the ore?

Had the insects eaten it?

The crocodile warriors looked at one another in complete bewilderment.

After thinking it over, their captain contacted the fleet through the communications system and reported the situation.

Fleet headquarters found it equally baffling.

A previously unknown species of insect had been discovered at the mine?

And the insects could devour ore?

What the hell was happening?

With no other clues at the scene, there was no point tying up the combat power of five warships indefinitely. They were ordered to return for the time being.

Fang Heng remained hidden nearby.

Only after watching the Crocodile Clan's warships jump away, leaving just one guard squad to protect the mine, did he emerge from the shadows.

Zaka, Alec, and the others followed him out from behind a boulder.

“Boss Fang, are they looking down on you? They actually dared leave just one squad behind?”

Zaka was at a loss. Those crocodiles really were bold.

One little guard squad?

Weren't they practically offering it up as a gift?

Fang Heng shrugged, silently agreeing.

“Hiss, hiss...”

Fine cracks appeared in the ground at the mine once again.

The remaining Crocodile Clan guards were watching their surroundings when, to their horror, unfamiliar brown, spiny insectoid creatures crawled out of the cracks and rapidly lunged at them!

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

The crocodiles immediately raised their beam weapons and returned fire.

It was completely useless!

Within moments, dense swarms of Lickers surged up from the cracks and devoured the entire crocodile squad left to guard the mine.

The Flying Squirrel captain behind Fang Heng opened his mouth in astonishment and cautiously swallowed.

You could do that too?

But on reflection, Fang Heng's summoned creatures could burrow freely underground and possessed astonishing mobility.

And right now, the Crocodile Clan and the Shadow Clan alike had to concentrate enormous combat strength on the main front to deal with the powerful insect swarms. They had no time to spare for Fang Heng's scattered insects.

Given a little time, those insects could strip a mine completely bare!

Fang Heng waited for a while and continued directing his Lickers to gather ore.

Zaka nudged Janni, the Flying Squirrel captain, and asked, “Well? Pretty good, huh?”

Janni nodded numbly. “Very impressive, but... won't they discover our underground tunnels sooner or later?”

“Tch, don't worry. We'll clear away the tunnels once we've moved the ore. The main battlefront is so fierce that nobody has time to search these mining areas carefully.”

“Ah, well...”

Janni thought about it and felt it did make sense.

At the zombies' terrifying excavation speed, a medium-sized mine would probably be emptied in half a day.

In that case...

What was there to fear?

Time to grind!

Every bit of grinding was pure profit!

Just as the five crocodile warships finished jumping back to the main battlefield and rejoined the fleet, the Crocodile Clan received another report.

They had lost contact with the squad they'd just left behind.

Huh? What did that mean?

Another insect attack?

The crocodile captain's head ached. He dispatched reconnaissance fleets several more times, only to get the same result each time.

They wiped out the insect swarm, but the moment their ships left, the insects returned.

Was this never going to end?!

The main battlefield was relatively stable, but insects were constantly harassing the mines under the Crocodile Clan's control. They couldn't station a warship at every single mine, could they?

After repeatedly sending reinforcements only to fail against the elusive swarms, the Crocodile Clan itself began to buckle under the burden.

Helpless, they were forced to abandon the mines.

They weren't happy about it.

At least the humans were behaving themselves and obediently providing the materials needed to build the defense towers.

Seeing that even the powerful Crocodile Clan couldn't handle the insects in the mines, the other allied races began abandoning their own mines to focus on the main battlefield.

If everything went smoothly, they expected to settle the main battle in another two or three days.

Then they could return and reclaim their mines!

Back at the mining area.

Fang Heng's group watched the Crocodile Clan finally stop appearing after having its guards killed two or three times. The crocodiles could no longer bear the constant costs and losses of sending reinforcements.

“Boss Fang, your wisdom is truly astonishing.”

The Flying Squirrel clan was now thoroughly convinced of Fang Heng's abilities.

Everything had gone exactly as he'd predicted.

The crocodiles had counted on their fingers and realized that repeatedly rescuing the mines was a losing proposition. They abandoned most of them outright, leaving warships stationed only at a few of the most valuable deposits.

They'd decided to hold on to the big ones and let the small ones go.

Fang Heng nodded. “Keep expanding. Empty all the nearby mining areas. We'll need the Flying Squirrel clan's help. As for how we split the final profits, we'll use our usual arrangement.”

The Flying Squirrel captain's eyes immediately lit up. “Of course! No problem! If I may say so, yours is the greatest race in the universe!”

— End of Chapter 2972 —
