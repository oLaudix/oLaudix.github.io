Chapter 2171

Entering

From original Chinese Chapter 2172 · 进入

“I only wish to speak with Great Elder Senzo. Forgive my bluntness, but this matter is so important that I'm afraid you aren't qualified to know the details.”

“What did you say?!”

Fang Heng's words instantly enraged Vannis. He glared at him for a moment. “Human, whatever your excuse, entering Tree Spirit territory without permission is courting death!”

Fang Heng returned his gaze coldly. “Is that so, Vannis? Can you truly make decisions on behalf of the Tree Spirits?”

Vannis clenched his fists.

In truth, he was somewhat afraid of fighting Fang Heng directly.

He'd witnessed Fang Heng single-handedly confront an entire Federation mechanized combat force.

What frightened Vannis most was the possibility that a major disturbance here would cause the Spirit Domain blockade network to detect their secret settlement. That would bring disaster upon them!

He warned himself not to act rashly, took a deep breath, and said, “Elder Senzo has gone out on business. Take him away! Everything can be decided after His Excellency returns! If he resists, use force!”

“Yes, sir!”

Several Tree Spirits immediately surrounded Fang Heng.

Fang Heng considered his options and made no attempt to resist. He merely looked at Vannis. “Vannis, I suggest you notify Great Elder Senzo as quickly as possible. You don't have much time left.”

Vannis gave a contemptuous snort. “Hmph! You needn't concern yourself with that!”

As they spoke, two Tree Spirits seized Fang Heng from either side. Fine vines extended rapidly and wrapped tightly around his wrists behind his back.

“Move!”

Under heavy guard, Fang Heng was escorted into Tree Spirit territory.

Their architectural style was entirely different from that of humanity.

It resembled treehouses.

The Tree Spirits took him around the outer edge of their living quarters and into an open area toward the rear.

Looking upward, he saw cages woven from special vines, suspended upside down beneath an enormous tree.

These vine cages were evidently used to hold prisoners.

Several captives were already inside.

Every one of them was a Tree Spirit.

They looked at Fang Heng in astonishment.

How strange.

A human?!

Could humans really end up imprisoned in a Tree Spirit jail?

“Great Elder Senzo is away on important business. You'll wait here until he returns.”

Vannis gestured toward two guards.

A vine cage descended from above.

Its door opened.

The guards shoved Fang Heng inside.

The door closed, and the cage rose into the air again.

Fang Heng looked down at Vannis through the woven vines.

“You're running out of time. Five hours remain.”

“Hmph.” Vannis snorted disdainfully. “Don't worry. I'll report your arrival to Great Elder Senzo as soon as he returns. Until then, wait patiently!”

Fang Heng shook his head and said no more.

He merely felt more convinced than ever that he'd made the right choice.

Cooperating with the Tree Spirits really was asking for trouble.

Seeing Fang Heng safely locked away, Vannis nodded and instructed the nearby guards in a low voice. “This human is unusual. Watch him carefully.”

“Yes, Elder Vannis. Should we notify Great Elder Senzo and ask him to return immediately?”

“There's no need. Great Elder Senzo has more important matters to attend to. Once he's finished, I'll report this to him.”

“Understood!”

“Hmph!”

Vannis cast Fang Heng one last cold glance before leaving the prison area.

Let the human wait a little longer!

Fang Heng watched them depart, then examined the interior structure of the vine cage with interest.

Interesting.

The cage continuously emitted a strange natural force resembling parasitic spores.

Those spores slowly absorbed and depleted the prisoner's mental strength.

But the absorption rate was much too slow.

It couldn't even match Fang Heng's natural mental-energy recovery.

The vines also bore countless tiny needles.

They were so small they were nearly invisible, and they punctured the outer skin without causing any pain.

Once they entered the skin, however, they continuously released a paralytic toxin that caused exhaustion and drowsiness.

“Not a bad prison.”

Fang Heng was rather impressed.

The Tree Spirits' jail gave him plenty of ideas. Perhaps he could construct an improved, reinforced version back at his Zombie Apocalypse prison.

For now, though, this thing might hold ordinary prisoners.

But hold him?

They were seriously underestimating him.

Once he'd inspected his surroundings and confirmed he was safe, Fang Heng settled into a more comfortable sitting position.

He'd already considered all sorts of things that might happen after entering Tree Spirit territory.

He was prepared.

In fact, being thrown into prison was one of the better outcomes he'd anticipated.

He checked the time again.

Five hours left until the Dependents tracked him down, right on schedule.

He could wait.

His previous dealings with the Tree Spirits had taught him one thing.

Unless they tasted a little suffering, they would never lower their heads and cooperate willingly.

…

Elder Senzo really had gone out that day to discuss matters with the elders of the Root Clan.

The main topic was their previous cooperation.

Judging by the outcome, the Leaf Clan's leadership had undoubtedly landed the other three major clans in trouble.

This visit was mostly an attempt to apologize.

Vannis, meanwhile, deliberately wanted Fang Heng to stew in prison. Even after Senzo returned, he made no effort to contact him right away.

Soon enough, Vannis forgot about the matter entirely.

Until five hours later.

Two Tree Spirits rushed into the Council of Elders to report.

“Elders! We've detected a large number of Federation spatial-jump fluctuations outside the settlement! Federation forces are here!”

What?!

The elders instantly sprang to their feet, faces grave.

Spatial-jump fluctuations?!

What was happening?

Had the Federation found their headquarters?!

So suddenly?

One elder said sternly, “Don't panic. It may just be a routine patrol. Quickly, activate the concealment spells and inspect the simulated-environment barrier. And prepare to counterattack! Put the entire clan on high alert!”

“Understood!”

The clansmen hurried away to carry out their orders.

Meanwhile, outside the Leaf Clan's simulated-environment barrier.

“Whoosh, whoosh!”

Points of spatial-jump light appeared continuously in midair.

Federation war machines emerged throughout the surrounding area.

Apparently finding no sign of Fang Heng, the machines hovered motionless for a few seconds before activating their thermal-imaging systems and patrolling the forest in search of him.

(End of chapter)
