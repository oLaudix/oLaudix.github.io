From original Chinese Chapter 2106 · 溃散

The sheath of the greatsword strapped to Fang Heng's back exploded!

Dense blood energy poured out, rising into the air and rapidly forming a swarm of sharp, solidified blood spikes!

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

Carrying almost tangible killing intent, the blood spikes shot down toward the sacred priests!

Such terrifying fluctuations of blood energy?

The elite priest's expression changed drastically!

“Quick! Raise a shield!”

The elite priest rapidly formed hand seals, while the priests behind her chanted sacred prayers in unison.

In an instant, pale holy light emerged around them and formed a sturdy, dark-golden barrier in front of the group.

Blood spikes slammed against the barrier and burst apart, producing a rapid succession of muffled impacts.

A cold gleam flashed through Fang Heng's eyes.

A sacred shield? Was that all it could do?

Again!

Fang Heng reached out and closed his hand around empty air.

The dispersed blood energy gathered in his palm once more, quickly becoming a solid crimson greatsword.

“Whoosh!”

Gripping the weapon in both hands, he launched himself at the sacred shield!

The elite priest stared at him, her expression grave, and poured all her sacred energy into reinforcing the barrier.

A sword strike overflowing with blood energy crashed into it.

Crack!

A fine fracture appeared in the sacred barrier, then rapidly spread!

Crash!

The entire barrier shattered like glass, breaking into countless points of light that dissolved into the air.

The elite priest grunted and stumbled backward, staring at Fang Heng in disbelief. A thin line of blood appeared across her chest.

Then a sword wound deep enough to expose the bone became visible!

Blood gushed out and stained the muddy ground red.

Behind her, the priests who had helped maintain the barrier were thrown backward like kites with their strings cut, crashing onto the ground.

“Whoosh... whoosh...”

The dispersed blood energy returned again, gathering in Fang Heng's hand into a greatsword.

Compared with before, however, the blade was visibly dimmer.

What?!

The elite priest's pupils contracted sharply.

On the bloodstained mud, droplets were separating from the ground and rising into the air on their own!

Even the blood flowing from her chest began floating upward, beyond her control.

And from the fallen priests, blood gathered into tiny spheres!

Every droplet sped toward the greatsword in Fang Heng's hand!

Looking at him, the elite priest felt genuine fear.

As the crimson sword absorbed the blood drawn from its surroundings, its power slowly began recovering!

With one last trace of fear and unwillingness, the light faded from the priest's eyes.

“Fang Heng!”

An enraged shout suddenly rang out beside him.

Fang Heng immediately sensed a tremendous sacred pressure bearing down on him. Frowning, he spun around and swept the crimson greatsword behind him!

Boom!

Thick blood energy and sacred power collided and exploded once more!

Fang Heng staggered back several steps and looked at his attacker.

Holy Court Commander Georgi!

Georgi stared at him with bloodshot eyes, consumed by rage!

He had noticed the disturbance among the priests immediately and rushed to help!

Yet he'd still been a step too late!

More than half the entire unit was dead or wounded!

“Fang Heng!”

The young man standing before him was responsible for the disastrous state of the war in Chiboreya.

Georgi fixed his gaze on Fang Heng. His fighting spirit surged, and a layer of faint holy light appeared around his body.

Fang Heng slowly raised his head to meet Georgi's eyes. There was mockery in his voice. “Oh? What's the matter? Do you have something to teach me? You look rather angry. How about a fair and honorable duel? Do you dare?”

“Why wouldn't I dare?! In the Holy Court's name, you will pay for what you've done!”

Georgi roared and stepped forward. His sacred longsword erupted with violent energy as he swung it down!

Whoosh!

A long, narrow blade of light shot out from his sword!

“Huh?”

Georgi froze.

Fang Heng's figure faded rapidly in midair and vanished completely into space before the sword light reached him.

After half a second of stunned silence, Georgi realized what had happened and bellowed, “Fang Heng! Get your ass back here!”

Within the secondary-space projection, Fang Heng was already retreating toward the area where the Lickers were gathered.

A one-on-one duel?

What a joke!

There was absolutely no need!

He had only taken the enormous risk of infiltrating the enemy formation to eliminate the sacred priests.

After all, those priests posed too great a threat to his modified Lickers.

Now that the priest unit had been practically wiped out, why would he stay to duel the big boss?

Completely unnecessary.

The repeated fighting had drastically depleted the blood energy stored in his crimson greatsword.

True, he'd managed to absorb another round of blood from the priests.

But that was nowhere near enough to make up for the enormous losses!

The waste made Fang Heng's heart ache.

Seeing the situation turn unfavorable, he fled immediately.

As for Georgi, he could always have his zombie clones slowly grind the commander down later!

Fang Heng swiftly withdrew to the rear of the battlefield.

By now, the undead players had finally crossed the swamp. Seeing the Lickers battling the Holy Court—and even gaining the upper hand—they charged forward shouting, eager to help and earn contribution points.

It was time for the activity players excelled at most.

Beating a drowning dog!

The Holy Court had lost its most effective means of countering the Primal Devourer Lickers. While Georgi and his troops remained entangled with them, the undead players teamed up with ordinary Lickers and zombie clones to attack the Holy Paladins, systematically destroying the enemy's remaining strength.

In a matter of moments, the momentum swung decisively toward the undead faction.

Fang Heng hid in the shadows, occasionally emerging to finish off an elite Paladin with a skill while secretly drawing blood from the fallen knights behind the lines.

His greatsword had consumed far too much blood energy in this battle. He needed to replenish it properly.

Commander Georgi knew the tide had turned, but he still wanted to persevere. He planned to withdraw with the survivors into the city and make one last stand behind its walls.

When he turned around, however, he saw thick black smoke rising from Chinoya City. He had no idea when it had begun.

Editing and Source Notes
  • Rewards. 12W, 6W, and 20W in the source denote 120,000, 60,000, and 200,000 respectively.
  • Battle objective. Fang Heng deliberately avoids dueling Georgi; his objective is to neutralize the sacred priests who can purify the modified Lickers.

Chinese Expressions

  • 痛打落水狗 (tòng dǎ luò shuǐ gǒu). Literally “beat a dog that has fallen into the water.” Means to pile on against an already defeated opponent. Kept as “Beating a drowning dog!” for its distinctive imagery.
