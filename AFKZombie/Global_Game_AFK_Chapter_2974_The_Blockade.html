Chapter 2974

The Blockade

From Chinese Chapter 2975 · 封锁

The alliance's blockade of the Ouroboros Seed continued intermittently for five days.

During that time, the insect nest erupted into several frenzied assaults.

Using the Shadow Clan's powerful racial abilities and advanced Kinsfolk weapons, the allied forces forcibly contained the Ouroboros Seed. Each outbreak from the nest was ultimately driven back into the blockade zone by the Shadow Clan's special powers.

At last, every Eye of Stars prism had been constructed, linked, and positioned.

The Shadow Clan sent messages to its allies, ordering the fleets of every race to prepare for the decisive battle.

The human fleet had been assigned only to defend the Area Six prism tower during the final confrontation, a relatively light task. Fang Heng therefore accepted the Shadow Clan's invitation to board their flagship and participate in the main battlefield mission alongside them.

All the allied races taking part in the operation assembled on the Shadow Clan flagship's deck.

Tens of thousands of warships formed a vast ring around the central battlefield, tightly restricting the insect swarm's movements.

Giselle and the other players were stunned by the sight.

Compared with fighting on this scale, their earlier operations against SS-grade insect nests looked like children playing pretend.

A layer of dark clouds covered the sky.

As violent purple spatial fluctuations erupted, enormous gun barrels appeared through rifts torn open high above.

The barrels formed a ring, aiming down at the dense mass of insects gathered below.

“Energy bombardment!!”

Boom!!!

A dazzling purple radiance erupted!

In an instant, the insects struck directly by the beams were reduced to ashes.

The violent purple beam bombardment continued for a full twenty minutes before gradually dying away.

The area ahead, once covered by fungal carpet, had been completely cleared.

The many warships advanced toward the center!

Across the barren ground, enormous numbers of leaping insects surged forward in a dense tide.

The insectoid creatures of this world were exceptionally high-level. Two types formed the bulk of their forces. One was a swift creature with tremendous jumping power, capable of leaping twenty times its own height. These were known as Leapers.

The other was a special flying unit, the Venomwing.

Venomwings were as small as Leapers and flew extremely fast, but they struggled to move sideways in midair. On the main battlefield, the warships' powerful firepower could destroy them almost instantly.

Yet they were just as dangerous.

Once a Venomwing approached an enemy, it would self-destruct, bursting open and spraying a highly acidic fluid from within its body. This fluid could inflict enormous damage on energy barriers and various alloy metals.

The alliance warships immediately laid down suppressive fire, bursting the approaching insects into clouds of blood in the distance.

Still more Leapers rushed forward over the corpses of their companions.

Further away, massive insectoid creatures gradually emerged into view.

Spiked Elephant Beetles.

Elephant Beetles were rare large-scale insect combat units. Each was as big as a giant elephant and charged wildly across the battlefield. Their main means of attack was physical impact, while their formidable armored bodies could absorb damage and regenerate rapidly.

Faced with the insect swarm's offensive, the fleets began displaying their own special techniques.

Fang Heng quietly observed from the sidelines.

Very interesting.

The Shadow Clan had initiated the mission and was also the strongest race in the entire alliance.

Its warriors could release spherical balls of shadow.

Under the influence of those dark orbs, insectoid creatures were pulled firmly into place and rendered unable to move.

Hmm?

Fang Heng suddenly sensed the aura of an Ouroboros Seed. He raised his head, looked into the distance, and narrowed his eyes.

At this range, the SSS-grade nest aggregate had evidently detected the aura of its own kind coming from Fang Heng too.

As the warships continued advancing, the outline of the SSS-grade insect nest finally became visible in the distance.

The giant nest sprawled across the ground like a rising hill. Its dark-purple, horny outer shell was covered in volcanic-looking folds and fissures. Tens of thousands of reddish-brown tendrils, like blood vessels, sank deep into the fungal carpet, while the entire nest contracted and throbbed continuously.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!

Huh?

Thick layers of hardened fungal matter began peeling away from the nest's outer surface.

As those layers fell to the ground, they rapidly condensed into throbbing fleshy cocoons.

Huh?

A question rose in Fang Heng's mind.

They were actually Ouroboros Seed aggregates!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!

Two cocoons abruptly burst open!

Two humanoid Ouroboros Seed entities flew from them, blended into the swarm, and charged straight toward the warships!

Fang Heng fixed his eyes on the aggregates.

They only barely maintained human forms. Their bodies consisted entirely of twisted, tangled, tiny insects, and without skeletons to support them, their movements looked extraordinarily strange.

Two high-ranking Shadow Clan warriors immediately moved to intercept them, releasing shadow orbs in an attempt to restrain the Ouroboros Seeds.

Under the orbs' illumination, the shadows of the two humanoid Seeds stretched rapidly, and their movement speed fell drastically.

Focused attack!!

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!”

Hundreds of focused energy-beam weapons aboard the warships aimed at the Ouroboros Seeds and fired simultaneously!

“Boom! Boom, boom!!!!”

The two Ouroboros Seed aggregates exploded beneath the focused beams!

Hmm?

The battle wasn't over yet.

The insects scattered across the ground as clumps of flesh wriggled rapidly toward a central point. In the blink of an eye, the Ouroboros Seeds formed humanoid bodies once again and rushed toward the warships!

And they weren't alone. More and more Ouroboros Seed aggregates were emerging from bursting cocoons behind them and joining the frontal battle.

Aboard the flagship, the assembled observers from various races looked grim.

Were they actually beginning to lose ground?

The Ouroboros Seed aggregates could somehow revive even after suffering devastating damage. They were extraordinarily difficult to deal with.

The Shadow Clan captain said solemnly, “Don't let them get close to the ships.”

“Understood.”

Seeing this, the other allied races participating in the battle also dispatched squads to intercept the Ouroboros Seed aggregates.

Each race had its own special capabilities, and none was at a disadvantage against these exceptionally regenerative Ouroboros Seeds.

They might not be able to destroy the Seeds completely, but at least they could keep them outside the security perimeter.

Standing at the back of the crowd, Captain Giselle watched from afar. He regarded the operation as an opportunity to broaden his horizons and quietly reminded himself to stay cautious.

Compared with these races and their special abilities, humanity really was rather weak.

Frankly, it was almost a miracle that humans had survived until now without being attacked by extraterrestrial races.

Perhaps they had avoided the aliens' attention only because the human world possessed no mineral resources worth taking?

From a certain perspective, had the black mist actually given humanity an opportunity to become stronger?

Was the Black Fog Game ultimately a disaster or a savior?

Giselle shook his head and put the question aside.

“The Red-Eyed Clan is quite capable too. They can completely destroy those troublesome Ouroboros Seeds...”

A crocodile captain who had come to watch the battle suddenly looked toward Fang Heng and steered the conversation to humanity. “Captain Fang Heng, this is humanity's first time on an allied battlefield. How about showing us what you can do?”

— End of Chapter 2974 —
