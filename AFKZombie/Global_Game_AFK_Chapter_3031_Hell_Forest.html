Chapter 3031

Hell Forest

From Chinese Chapter 3032 · 地狱树丛

The twenty-fifth level of Hell.

After more than three hours of bitter fighting, Dean finally managed to get the Holy Court forces under control and led them back toward the entrance to the level above.

The Hell creatures sent by Envy had been elite fiends cultivated over many years, all extremely powerful. Only their numbers were slightly inferior to those of the Holy Court.

Combined with their home-field advantage in Hell, they could unleash terrifying strength.

In fact, things had been manageable at the beginning. The Holy Court had been able to retreat in good order.

But as time passed, the buffs Fang Heng had bestowed on the paladins gradually faded, and the force's overall combat ability plummeted.

To prevent the Holy Court army from being wiped out, Dean had been forced to join the fight himself and lead the team in a slow withdrawal.

“Nothing's happened to Fang Heng, has it?”

Dean glanced cautiously toward the depths of Hell, uneasy.

It had been more than three hours. They'd cleared out some of Envy's summoned monsters, but there was still no word from Fang Heng.

That brat had always been ridiculously bold, but this time...

Huh?

As he pondered, Dean suddenly felt the ground trembling faintly beneath his feet.

What?

The Holy Court troops reacted as though facing a great enemy. They backed away, gathered together, and erected a thick sacred defensive barrier.

“Be careful!!”

As the lava rock shook, tiny cracks appeared across the ground.

Looking closer, they saw thin vines crawling from the gaps in the fractured rock.

What the hell?!

Dark-red, veinlike patterns covered their surfaces. They spread quickly across the ground, and the sulfurous rock beneath them hissed as though dissolving.

Thicker roots emerged from the broken ground beneath the vines, driving deep into the gaps in the floor and walls and anchoring themselves firmly.

Within mere moments, the trembling subsided. Every visible part of Hell's twenty-fifth level was covered in dense vines and roots!

The vines were still spreading, growing toward the higher levels of Hell.

“What kind of bizarre plant is that?”

Dean slowly retreated with the Holy Court forces. Looking at the strange “Hell forest” spreading before his eyes, he felt a powerful sense of unease.

Studying the vines, he saw tiny thorns puncturing the rock as the plants clung to the walls, almost as if they were absorbing Hell's energy.

Some kind of Hell creature?

Was it connected to their intrusion?

At first glance, the roots and vines didn't seem hostile to the Hell creatures—or to the Holy Court.

Before Dean could understand the situation, the frenzied Hell creatures serving Envy attacked the nearest vines and roots!

Blades wrapped in hellfire chopped the plants apart, leaving flames burning fiercely around the wounds.

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

The vines and roots were just as savage as the denizens of Hell. Once attacked, they retaliated immediately. Sharp spikes shot from the roots, while extending vines coiled frantically around the fiends.

The plants were exceptionally powerful!

All the creatures still capable of fighting were high-level Hell fiends recruited by Envy.

Yet even they couldn't gain the slightest advantage against the vines and roots!

Faced with countless entangling vines and stabbing roots, even if they destroyed the plants completely, more vines would gather the very next second.

And if they made one mistake...

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!!”

The roots immediately seized the chance, tightly binding one fiend's ankle, hoisting it into the air, and slamming it heavily to the ground!

“Bang!!”

Dense vines swarmed over, bound the fiend completely, and dragged it beneath the ground until it disappeared.

“Gulp...”

The Holy Court paladins swallowed nervously as they watched the battlefield.

“This...”

The roots emanated a strong Hell aura. They had to be some kind of Hell creature, so why were the creatures of Hell fighting one another?

“Stay alert.”

Dean didn't dare be careless. He led the Holy Court troops into a corner.

Hell was not merely vast; it seemed bottomless. The Holy Court's records only explored as far as its fifty-fifth level. So much remained unknown that even Dean had no idea what these bizarre vines actually were.

“Grand Sage, things don't look right here. Should we withdraw first?”

“Mm.”

Dean nodded softly.

While the vines showed no hostility toward them, they had better get as far away as possible and let the Hell creatures fight the plants.

Hmm?

Dean was about to lead the group away when he suddenly frowned and looked toward the right.

Amid the strange tangle of vines, Jing'er and several other Demon Seeds hurried through Abe Akaya's growth toward the exit they'd prepared, crossing paths with the Holy Court troops.

Both sides regarded each other warily but tacitly refrained from attacking.

Jing'er's expression was gloomy, and he was in an absolutely terrible mood.

Judging from the auras around them, part of the restrictions Envy had imposed on their bodies had disappeared.

They would no longer be under Eseklyna's control.

But remembering how Fang Heng had seized the Demon Cores at the last moment filled Jing'er with fury.

He would remember this.

If an opportunity arose, he'd definitely take revenge!

Zhuang Yufei and the others traveling with him looked grave.

The disappearance of Envy's restrictions likely meant that she was dead.

Who had done it?

Thinking back, Fang Heng seemed by far the most likely candidate!

Furthermore, on their journey, dense vines and roots had spread throughout Hell's twenty-ninth to twenty-fifth levels—and perhaps still farther.

There was no doubt that Fang Heng controlled those strange plants!

Just how powerful had he become?

With the Demon Cores in his hands, life probably wouldn't be easy for the other Demon Seeds in the future.

...

Meanwhile, on Hell's twenty-ninth level.

Abe Akaya's vines swiftly gathered the five Demon Cores together and wound tightly around them, continually absorbing their power. At the same time, it took root in Hell and spread rapidly across the twentieth through thirty-fourth levels.

Fang Heng slowly opened his eyes.

Because Fang Heng's soul was already bound to Abe Akaya, as it gained levels, his Sea of Consciousness was nourished again. A mighty flood of soul power flowed into it, continuously soothing and strengthening the origin of his soul.
