Chapter 3137

Overconfidence

From Chinese Chapter 3138 · 膨胀

After spending so long in the Vampire world, Garr had grown somewhat overconfident, almost feeling invincible.

Especially after absorbing dimensional power, which had qualitatively transformed the Vampires' various attributes.

Now, sensing an opponent whose strength was no weaker than his own, his fighting spirit surged.

Attack!

“Whoosh!!”

Garr personally entered the battle, charging straight at Chen Hanyu.

Sensing powerful blood-energy fluctuations approaching, Chen Hanyu raised his hand and sent a dense barrage of rock spikes crashing toward Garr.

“BOOM! Boom, boom, boom, boom!!!”

A series of violent explosions erupted.

Garr burst out of the explosions and retreated, his eyes filled with surprise.

Such powerful magical attacks!

Only after leaving the Vampire world did he truly understand that there were always people stronger than himself, and heavens beyond the heavens.

This opponent wouldn't be easy to deal with!

He had an enormous advantage in spells, and his overall strength might even be slightly superior to theirs.

“Whoosh!”

Chen Hanyu quickly formed a sigil before himself. A blue ice crystal appeared on his forehead.

“Thousand-Mile Freeze!”

The violent, surging mental power surrounding Chen Hanyu materialized into an immense wave of blue ice crystals, sweeping forward in an instant!

The freezing crystals rushed toward them in a vast tide!

Garr, more than twenty Vampires within the spell's range, and nearly a hundred Lickers were frozen solid in the blink of an eye.

“BOOM!!!”

Garr forcibly circulated his blood energy, shattered the ice covering him, and darted toward the right.

What terrifying frost power!

From that one area attack, more than a dozen of his Vampire subordinates had been frozen completely and lost all signs of life.

They could not win!

He couldn't win! Garr stared at Chen Hanyu from a distance, deep wariness shining in his eyes.

Hm?

He raised his head and looked toward the horizon, where tiny points of spatial light were gradually appearing.

Starships were arriving via spatial jumps.

Once they'd stabilized, panels on both sides of the ships opened, and their mounted energy-beam cannons locked onto the Vampires and the Licker swarm.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!”

The Vampires came under precise beam fire and scattered to evade it.

The starships!

They were trying to escape!

Garr saw the ships opening their hatches and small warp passages to receive the fleeing security teams, and his heart sank.

He couldn't let them retreat so easily!

He had to keep every starship here!

Garr gritted his teeth and prepared to charge forward again, when a figure suddenly materialized beside him.

Who was it?

Who? Garr's heart jolted. Then he recognized the newcomer, turned, and respectfully bowed his head. “Your Majesty.”

“Leave those people to me. You deal with the starships. Don't let any of them escape.”

“Yes!”

Without further hesitation, Garr led his Vampire subordinates toward the ships.

Seeing the Vampires move again, Chen Hanyu frowned and charged toward the Vampire leader to intercept him. But in the next instant, his perception detected a terrifying killing intent that locked onto him completely!

Every hair on Chen Hanyu's body seemed to stand on end as he turned sharply toward its source.

“Who goes there?”

Fang Heng slowly materialized less than twenty meters in front of him.

Chen Hanyu's heart sank as he watched Fang Heng appear out of nowhere. Sensing that this was no ordinary opponent, he said grimly, “Who are you people? Do you know whose territory this is?”

“Of course I know. This world belongs to the Kinsfolk.” Fang Heng's mouth curled into a cold smile. “And the Kinsfolk are exactly who I'm looking for!”

“Whoosh!”

As soon as he finished speaking, Fang Heng turned into a dark blur and shot through the air toward Chen Hanyu.

Chen Hanyu didn't dare be careless. He thrust his hand forward, summoning multiple dense ranks of earth-element barriers.

“BOOM! Boom, boom, boom!!!”

Fang Heng smashed straight through the exploding earth barriers! Shattered!

Shatter!

He destroyed every earth-element barrier one after another by sheer brute force!

Astonishment flashed in Chen Hanyu's eyes.

The dust covering Fang Heng quickly hardened into tiny clumps of earth, but layers of black mist seeped from them.

The death aura of the black mist rapidly dissolved those clumps.

Chen Hanyu stared in amazement.

His Guardian Domain of Rock had been broken so easily?

In the next instant, Fang Heng abruptly appeared right in front of him.

“BOOM!!!”

A punch!

An enormous fist-shaped depression appeared across Chen Hanyu's chest, and his armor of earth and rock shattered instantly!

He couldn't withstand it!

Chen Hanyu flew backward, his mind briefly blank.

Who was this man?

How could he be so terrifyingly strong? Since perfecting his Law Domain, Chen Hanyu hadn't encountered a worthy opponent. He feared no ordinary high-tier warrior.

Yet this unfamiliar alien who'd appeared from nowhere was different!

Whoosh!

Fang Heng instantly appeared before Chen Hanyu again and threw another punch!

“BANG!!”

Struck once more, Chen Hanyu spat blood. The earth armor that had just formed around him shattered again, and he plummeted toward the ground!

“BOOM!!!”

Before he could even hit the ground, Chen Hanyu lost consciousness. Unable to withstand or control the wildly surging blood inside him, his body exploded into a cloud of blood in midair.

The blood mist gradually condensed into a single drop of blood essence, which slowly entered the short dagger behind Fang Heng.

Fang Heng withdrew his gaze from below.

This wasn't easy.

Chen Hanyu had already mastered the power of a Domain.

Without activating his own Domain, Fang Heng's attributes were somewhat weakened inside his opponent's Domain. That prevented him from completely overwhelming the enemy.

Killing a warrior of Chen Hanyu's caliber still demanded a fair amount of effort.

And that was when Chen Hanyu wasn't particularly skilled at controlling his Domain. If his control had been more refined, the suppression of Fang Heng's attributes would have been even stronger.

Of course, things would be different if Fang Heng activated his own Domain skill as well.

An opponent of Chen Hanyu's level was naturally nothing he needed to take seriously then.

Seeing Chen Hanyu die so horribly at Fang Heng's hands, the remaining security warriors looked frightened and hesitated to approach.

Who was this kid?

Chen Hanyu was a famous high-tier warrior who'd fought thousands of battles. He'd also undergone one of the Kinsfolk's special experiments recently and returned with greatly increased strength. Rumor had it he'd begun comprehending the power of a Law Domain.

Once someone mastered the laws, they possessed strength approaching that of a god within their Domain.

Wasn't he supposed to be invincible?

Yet someone had forcibly defeated Chen Hanyu inside his own Domain?

The sight completely overturned the warriors' basic understanding of the laws.

Fang Heng swept a cold gaze over the remaining high-tier warriors. They immediately felt as though a deadly venomous snake had fixed its eyes on them, and chills ran down their spines.

“You…”

Fang Heng hesitated, then shook his head. “Forget it. It'll save trouble if I deal with all of you at once.”

Whoosh!

Whoosh! In the next instant, Fang Heng became a dark shadow and flashed rapidly toward the group of warriors.
