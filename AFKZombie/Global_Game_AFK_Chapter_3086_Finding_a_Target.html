Chapter 3086

Finding a Target

From Chinese Chapter 3087 · 找对手

Since opening Abe Akaya's teleportation passage would likely cause trouble, why not choose someone he was already on bad terms with?

Fang Heng licked his lips lightly.

The Kinsfolk!

Over the past few days, he'd discreetly asked around about the Kinsfolk while staying with the Noel Clan.

The Kinsfolk ranked roughly third among the powers of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital and were one of its seven overlords.

Rumor had it that Kinsfolk bodies were extraordinarily frail and easily affected by outside forces. Most of them disliked face-to-face contact with outsiders. Consequently, they were rarely seen in the capital, preferring to communicate through remote systems.

The Kinsfolk controlled only a small, special district within the capital, where they enjoyed autonomous authority.

That was the place!

Time to give the Kinsfolk some excitement!

Half an hour later, Fang Heng entered the district under their control.

Even at night, the city's streets were brightly illuminated.

In soul form, Fang Heng slipped into the underground sewers.

There was none of the foul stench he'd expected.

The entire sewer network had been specially modified. Activated magic arrays partially purified the wastewater and flushed it quickly through the pipes into a treatment area.

The sewers also had around-the-clock lighting and monitoring cameras installed at some intersections.

They actually felt strangely clean and orderly.

“Splash!”

Still in soul form, Fang Heng passed through the barrier and dropped into a cleaned section of drainage pipe, then returned to physical form.

“Sacred Barrier.”

“Hum!”

A barrier expanded, pushing the surrounding water aside and creating a pocket of space underwater.

Water rushed along the outside of the barrier.

“This place will do nicely.”

Muttering to himself, Fang Heng stood at the bottom of the sewer and began constructing an Abe Akaya summoning magic array on the ground.

Ordinarily, a specialized engineering department maintained the sewers. Obviously, though, people rarely came to inspect them unless something went wrong.

It took more than five hours to complete Abe Akaya's magic array.

Fang Heng stood at its edge, the Omniscient Eye spinning rapidly in his pupil.

“Hiss, hiss, hiss...”

The same problem.

Even deep in the sewers, the Abe Akaya summoning array met considerable resistance.

After a moment, as the array operated, Abe Akaya's vines broke free of the obstruction and emerged.

Looking at the vines straining to act, Fang Heng said evenly, “Begin, Abe Akaya.”

“Hiss, hiss...”

A dark light appeared around the center of the vines.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

Unlike the times he'd activated the array elsewhere, thin streaks of black light leaked from the turbulent surrounding space as this one opened.

Just as expected, something was wrong.

According to the sensations Abe Akaya transmitted, teleportation appeared to be suffering interference.

Fang Heng instructed Abe Akaya to force the passage open while spreading his own perception outward.

...

Seven-Realm Martial Capital—the control hall inside the Kinsfolk building.

“Report! The central prism tower has issued an orange alert!”

“Warning! Black Mist fluctuations detected in Sector G-33!”

“Warning! Minor Black Mist fluctuations detected in the area. Dispatch a reconnaissance team to investigate immediately.”

“Warning! Spatial fluctuations detected in the same area. A spatial passage may be under construction!”

“The target area may contain highly infectious Black Mist. Investigators are advised to use Class-A protective measures.”

As alarms sounded in the affected district, Kinsfolk security personnel immediately evacuated the crowds.

Among the somewhat chaotic masses, the elven prince Korkalot and Kappa glanced at each other, then retreated with the others, suspicion in their eyes.

What was going on?

Why had such a commotion suddenly erupted in the Kinsfolk district?

Recently, while continuing to spread word and search for Fang Heng, the pair had also been attending to two other matters.

They'd found a buyer for the goods transported from afar. The buyer had offered an excellent price and promised Kappa substantial help with obtaining proof of the human star sector's status.

As for the second matter, the human world's registration application was progressing slowly.

Apparently the Kinsfolk were deliberately slowing the review process.

With no better option, Korkalot and Kappa had decided to visit a Kinsfolk external-affairs official, sound out their attitude, and then decide how to respond.

Before they could meet anyone, however, the entire district was locked down and they were temporarily evacuated.

The pair didn't go far. They stayed outside the cordon, watching.

Soon, security forces employed by the Kinsfolk arrived to investigate.

The security force itself was staffed by alien personnel hired by the Kinsfolk.

Balin, the direct commander of the district security team, arrived in person and contacted central control over his radio. “We've reached the confirmed Black Mist leak site. No visible mist has been observed. We suspect the alarm originated underground.”

“Most likely from the sewers.”

“We've called up surveillance feeds from across the sewer system, but electromagnetic interference has made some footage unreadable.”

The Kinsfolk considered Black Mist extremely dangerous.

Its appearance in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital was an especially ominous sign.

The Kinsfolk panicked, remaining inside their headquarters rather than venturing out. They ordered the district security forces and contracted guards to locate the source of the Black Mist.

Balin quickly determined the affected area from surveillance cameras and positioned personnel at every sewer entrance to keep civilians away.

“Seal every entrance for now. Prepare to send a team down to investigate.”

The security forces and private security company assembled at the main sewer access point.

Seeing how excessively frightened the Kinsfolk were, the hired warriors privately sneered.

The Kinsfolk possessed powerful technology and mental-control abilities, but their bodies were so frail and susceptible to outside influences that they rarely performed missions personally.

As the mercenaries liked to say...

The richer you are, the more afraid you are of dying.

“Those Kinsfolk bigshots are terrified of death, but they sure pay well. Everyone, no need to stand around. It's just a little Black Mist. Let's show them their monthly payments aren't being wasted.”

The hired warriors from the security company didn't take the Black Mist seriously at all.

What was the worst that could happen if it affected them?

Apart from the Kinsfolk, who practically died of fright whenever Black Mist was mentioned, other races had encountered it before. They'd never heard of anyone suffering ill effects.

The company quickly assembled an exploration team, which entered the deeper sewers without any protection whatsoever.

“Captain, they've gone in. Should we...?”

A member of the district security team glanced at Balin cautiously.

Balin shook his head. “No hurry. We'll wait here for equipment support. Letting them scout ahead isn't a bad idea.”

— End of Chapter 3086 —
