Chapter 2127

Analysis

From original Chinese Chapter 2128 · 检测

They'd tried almost everything they could think of!

And yet they simply couldn't deal with those Lickers.

Their regenerative powers were far too terrifying!

The creatures had all crowded together inside the narrow passages, physically blocking every route!

Unable to think of a better solution, the Federation soldiers remained trapped in the institute's lobby.

“Sir, given the current situation, forcing our way through is almost impossible.”

“Mm.”

Boris watched the battle ahead, secretly alarmed.

What exactly were these monsters?

How could they be so powerful?

Did this world really contain a kind of power he'd never encountered, apart from that of the dependent race and the Tree Spirits?

“Well, then. What do you suggest?”

“The passages are too narrow for us to deploy properly. We could try blasting an entrance through the outside of the institute...”

“No.”

Boris shook his head. “The institute contains large amounts of confidential Wanling Technology data, along with some materials the Federation has collected over the past few years. My superiors explicitly ordered me to preserve the research data unless there is absolutely no other choice.”

“I see... That makes things difficult...”

The deputy commander murmured, then considered for a moment. “We just detected that some spatial transition devices are still operational. Could we use them to send a team into the institute for reconnaissance?”

Boris raised an eyebrow before shaking his head. “No need. Soldiers suffer temporary impairment after spatial transition. They wouldn't be much use once they got inside. By the way, have you sealed off the perimeter?”

“The cordon is being established.”

“Mm. Let's wait a little longer.”

Boris studied the corridor ahead and said gravely, “Headquarters has just informed us that samples of the unknown creatures' genetic material have been uploaded. They're analyzing the gene sequence now. We should receive the results soon.”

The deputy commander's expression turned solemn.

“Understood.”

· · ·

Two hours later.

“Chairman! There's an urgent report!”

At Wanling Technology's Northern District headquarters, the laboratory director hurried into Ji Haiting's office, visibly flustered.

He stopped short when he saw that Ji Haiting had company.

Ji Haiting rarely saw the laboratory director lose his composure like this. He gestured for the two subordinates he'd been speaking with to stand aside. “It's fine. Go on. Is there news from the branch research institute?”

“No.” The director shook his head, opened the documents in his hands, and approached Ji Haiting. “A short while ago, we uploaded the unknown creature's genetic information to the Psionic Mainframe Network. The analysis has just come back. The creature has been identified as a Licker. It's a genetically modified life-form, probably not native to our world—a foreign species.”

“Licker...”

Ji Haiting repeated the name under his breath.

A life-form from another world?

Artificially cultivated?

Ji Haiting grew wary.

Perhaps there was a considerable organization behind Fang Heng.

The laboratory director laid the documents on the desk. “This creature is the result of a mutation caused by a special virus. It has formidable offensive power, agility, and infectious capabilities.”

“The Lickers we encountered have undergone extensive modification. Both their vitality and offensive strength are vastly greater than normal. Even their infection ability differs significantly from that of ordinary Lickers. Moreover, baseline Lickers do not possess that abnormal regenerative ability.”

Ji Haiting nodded lightly. “Mm. Continue.”

The director drew a deep breath. His expression was grave. “It's Ouroboros.”

Ji Haiting's pupils contracted sharply. He stared at the director. “What did you say?”

“According to the information returned by the Psionic Network, a tiny portion of the genetic sequence found in these Lickers is associated with Ouroboros.”

Ji Haiting's expression became extraordinarily grim.

Ouroboros.

The eternal calamity...

Was it connected to that young man?

He sank into deep thought.

That race which even the dependent race feared.

And now it had appeared in their world.

“The Psionic Network has transmitted the relevant files and a formula for a countermeasure. Injecting this drug into Lickers can significantly suppress their regeneration. However, we currently lack several key medicinal ingredients, so our production capacity will be limited.”

The laboratory director noticed Ji Haiting remained deep in thought. He didn't dare interrupt and stood quietly beside him.

After a moment, Ji Haiting raised his head. “Mm. Begin production immediately. Once the drug is ready, send all of it to Boris. Also, who else knows about Ouroboros?”

“I came to report to you as soon as we received the results. Only two of my subordinates know the exact findings. And then there's...”

The director trailed off, looking at the other two people in the office.

“Mm. This matter is classified at the highest level. It ends here. No one else is to learn of it. Understood?”

“Yes, sir! Understood!”

Noticing the laboratory director was still hesitating, Ji Haiting asked, “What is it? Is there something else?”

“Chairman, when we uploaded the Licker data for comparison, it triggered an alarm on the Psionic Network. They've already contacted us asking for details.” The director paused. “How should we respond?”

Ji Haiting pressed his lips together in thought. “Find a way to fob them off.”

“All right. I'll tell them it was a data error. But under the established procedures, the dependent race may send someone to inspect us.”

Ji Haiting waved a hand. “Go. Handle it as quickly as possible and make sure the matter stays hidden.”

“Understood!”

The laboratory director nodded and hurried away.

Ji Haiting's brow remained deeply furrowed.

Troublesome!

He hadn't expected Fang Heng's sudden appearance to attract the dependent race's attention!

As he thought it over, his expression darkened further.
