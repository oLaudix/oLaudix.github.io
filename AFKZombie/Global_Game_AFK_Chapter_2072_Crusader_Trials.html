Chapter 2072

Crusader Trials

From original Chinese Chapter 2073 · 圣教军的历练

Forcing his way through didn't look like a good idea.

More players entered the hall and passed Fang Heng. Most had clearly been here before: they walked straight to one of the Crusader instructors beneath the statue, accepted a mission, and headed for the teleportation array on the hall's right side.

Huh? A teleportation passage!

Fang Heng's eyes lit up.

Luck was on his side. The passage lay outside the purification domain!

Forget the stupid Crusader advancement missions. All he wanted was to get out of here!

He promptly headed toward the teleportation area.

“Wait. Stop there.”

A Holy Court guard stepped into his path and indicated the row of instructors. “You must accept a mission before using the passage to enter the trial grounds.”

Fang Heng shook his head. “I don't want to take the trial anymore. I want to leave.”

“Silence!” the guard hissed sternly. “What do you think this place is? Becoming a Crusader requires unwavering conviction. How could you let a little difficulty frighten you away? Now that you've come here, don't even think of running away! Failure itself cannot break a true Crusader's resolve!”

Even his hushed rebuke attracted the attention of nearby players.

Afraid of drawing more scrutiny and exposing his identity, Fang Heng quickly lowered his head.

“I was wrong.”

He backed away.

So simply walking out wasn't an option either.

Don't panic.

At least nobody was forcing him to accept a mission immediately.

Looking around again, he noticed a rest area on the right side of the hall. About a dozen players had gathered there briefly, but they looked serious and weren't even chatting quietly.

Fang Heng pressed his lips together, went to the rest area, and observed the Crusader instructors while considering his options.

Should he take the risk?

The elderly instructor standing in the center seemed to have the greatest seniority and was probably the strongest. Several players were waiting to speak with him.

No. Definitely not that one!

Perhaps someone at either end of the row.

Fang Heng looked toward the instructor on the far right. He seemed young, yet his blood-vitality fluctuations indicated considerable strength.

Add the statue's unknown domain effect, and the probability of his disguise being exposed was far too high.

Damn it.

Fang Heng hesitated for more than ten minutes. The people around him left, and another group took their places.

Maybe he should just gamble on it?

But he knew almost nothing about this world. Even if he ran, he wouldn't know where to go.

As he wavered, he sensed another player approaching him from behind.

“Hello, friend. I've noticed you've been waiting here a while. Are you meeting someone, or is this your first time in Eternal Holy City?”

Fang Heng turned toward the young man and nodded. “That's right. I happened to get a Crusader Trial invitation and came without doing much research.”

“I thought so. Let me introduce myself. I'm Lindro Pez. One of our teammates had an emergency and couldn't come. If you don't mind, would you like to form a temporary party with us?”

A party invitation!

Fang Heng's eyes brightened.

Of course! Teaming up!

His simulated identity could accept quests, but going face-to-face with powerful Holy Court NPCs inside the sacred domain was likely to expose him.

In a party, however, another player could accept the mission on his behalf. Fang Heng wouldn't need to approach the NPC; he'd join the quest automatically as a teammate!

“Don't worry,” Lindro said with a grin. “We're all pretty strong. Think about it.”

He hadn't approached Fang Heng entirely at random.

One member of Lindro's team specialized in reconnaissance and had tried inspecting Fang Heng with a detection skill. Strangely, the skill had failed completely.

Even with NPCs, such a result was rare.

After a quick discussion, the four team members had concluded that Fang Heng was probably quite powerful and decided to try recruiting him.

“By the way, what should we call you?”

“Fang Heng.”

Fang Heng?

The familiar name made Lindro pause. He examined the man again.

Same name as that person!

Lately, everyone in the Holy Court had been talking about World Lord Fang Heng.

But it couldn't be him. The body shape and face were completely different.

Lindro almost laughed at himself.

As if Fang Heng would openly stroll into Eternal Holy City!

The constant news about that man had clearly gotten into his head.

Eternal Holy City was one of the Holy Court's most important high-level strongholds, and Fang Heng was currently its most wanted enemy.

Rumor had it that even Divine Realm pursuers were chasing him. Surely the real Fang Heng was far too busy running for his life to come here!

“Brother, you've got a scary name,” Lindro joked. “Same as that fellow.”

He assumed it was simply another person with the same-sounding name.

“Anyway, since it's your first time, I should explain the trials.”

“The instructors offer missions in difficulty grades from F all the way up to SSS. Complete a trial and you'll earn trial points and experience toward your Crusader trial rank.”

“At first your trial rank is low, so even the easy missions award experience. But at higher ranks you'll need SSS-class missions to earn enough to advance. Failed missions deduct experience too.”

“You also earn points, which you can spend on consumables and special equipment after completing the trials. Most of those items are intended specifically for use during the trials.”

Fang Heng nodded. “What difficulty is your team choosing?”

“A-class.”

Confidence appeared on Lindro's face. A-class was already a high difficulty among the available missions.

“Not worried I'll drag you down?” Fang Heng asked.

“Hahaha! No worries. I trust my judgment. You're definitely strong!”

— End of Chapter 2072 —

  • Name coincidence. Fang Heng gives his real name despite disguising his appearance; the recruiter dismisses it as merely a common-sounding coincidence.
