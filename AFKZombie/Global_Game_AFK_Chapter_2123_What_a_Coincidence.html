Chapter 2123

What a Coincidence

From original Chinese Chapter 2124 · 巧了

Yule looked taken aback. “Hm? You've heard of that place too?”

Ha! What a coincidence!

Mo Jiawei immediately nodded. “Fang Heng has already gone to Wanling Technology's Northern District research institute. If the dead Divine Tree offshoots they took are hidden there, that would be perfect! We can find a way to take them all back and restore the magic arrays inside them...”

Yule was startled by Mo Jiawei's audacity.

They'd actually gone straight to the branch research institute?

And they were planning to steal back the withered Divine Tree offshoots?

Did they have a death wish?!

“What? He's gone to Wanling Technology's Northern District research institute? Stop him, quickly!”

“Don't worry. We're followers of the God of Nature, and we've got a few tricks of our own. Wanling Technology's people can't stop us. Ask Glenan if you don't believe me.”

Having just witnessed Fang Heng's group's abilities, Glenan nodded vigorously.

Yule's expression remained grave. He shook his head. “It's different! Wanling Technology works closely with the Federation government. There's a special Federation mechanized military factory right beside the research institute's technology park. Fang Heng is in grave danger!”

On the second underground floor of the research building, Fang Heng was slowly advancing behind the Licker horde when the new game notification appeared in his vision. His heart stirred.

Well, would you look at that! What a coincidence!

He'd originally come to have a little chat with Ji Haiting, gather some intelligence, and look for information on other Divine Tree offshoots while he was at it.

He hadn't expected Wanling Technology to have already collected some of those offshoots for him!

And they were right here inside the institute!

This trip was proving well worth it!

Fang Heng continued forward to catch up with the horde.

Around the corner ahead, another group of guards suddenly emerged from the far end of the passage, all wearing metal armor.

Their armor looked enormously heavy. It enclosed them completely from head to toe, and each step they took while running struck the floor with a heavy thud.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

These guards looked different.

A green energy core sat at the chest of each armored suit.

And the emblem on their chests was...

The Federation?!

Were these Federation soldiers?

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Without hesitation, the Lickers leaped at the soldiers!

But the Federation troops were ready. As the Lickers approached, they raised their arms.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Panels in their metal forearm armor lifted, exposing several dark gun barrels that unleashed a dense barrage of beam fire!

In the narrow passage, the Lickers were immediately driven back by the concentrated energy beams!

Energy beam weapons!

The emitters were integrated perfectly into the armor!

Surprise flashed through Fang Heng's eyes.

Hum...

After several volleys forced the Lickers back, the armored soldiers extended their hands. Two visible blue energy blades appeared, one in each hand.

Gripping their short beam blades, the soldiers charged the Lickers together!

Close-quarters combat!

Brilliant-blue cutting beams swept forward!

Slash!

Severed limbs!

The short energy blades sliced through flesh as easily as a knife through butter!

Almost every time a Licker was struck, one of its limbs was severed.

For a moment, dismembered arms and legs flew everywhere in the hall!

Fang Heng watched with a frown.

Semi-mechanized modification?

He'd seen some introductions to it on short-video platforms before.

These were the Federation's latest semi-mechanized soldiers.

The elite of the elite!

After undergoing partial mechanization, their physical capabilities were greatly enhanced. They could carry extremely heavy loads and equip external superalloy armor.

The thick metal armor seemed capable of almost completely resisting damage from the Lickers' claws!

Combined with the severing effect of their short energy blades...

The semi-mechanized soldiers had become living weapons!

Interesting...

Fang Heng pursed his lips.

Even with their ability to regrow severed limbs and their extraordinary regeneration, the Lickers were being suppressed by the semi-mechanized soldiers!

“World of Myriad Forests.”

When he'd first heard the name, he'd thought this was a world of nature. Later, it seemed more like a world specializing in psychic power. Now it looked as though it might actually be a world of mechanical modification?

Hm?

Fang Heng suddenly noticed something and looked behind the soldiers.

That man!

Found him! Ji Haiting!

Behind the semi-mechanized troops, Ji Haiting stood surrounded by a tightly packed escort, his expression extraordinarily grave.

Those horrifying climbing creatures...

Even high-energy beam weapons couldn't completely destroy them?

For the first time, Ji Haiting was genuinely shaken by a species he'd never seen before.

The semi-mechanized soldiers' bodies had been modified, and their external alloy armor gave them tremendous resistance to injury. Yet even they couldn't handle such a vast horde of mutated creatures.

Who was controlling these monsters from behind the scenes?

And another thing!

That strange power of infection—the horrifying ability to raise zombies from the dead.

It dealt a devastating blow to morale!

With a solemn expression, Ji Haiting had already assigned the creatures the highest possible threat rating.

Extremely dangerous!

If an infectious virus like this spread, it might bring about the destruction of the entire world.

The Tree Spirits' natural power?

No!

Nothing like it!

A nervous institute manager hurried forward and whispered into Ji Haiting's ear. “Chairman Ji, it's extremely dangerous here. Please be careful. The emergency passage has been cleared. We'll take you out through the evacuation route imme—”

Ji Haiting raised a hand, cutting the manager off.

“Have you analyzed the creatures?”
