Chapter 2055

Chapter of Imprisonment

From original Chinese Chapter 2056 · 禁锢之章

BANG!

Black mist erupted from Fang Heng's body once more.

BOOOOM!

The cloud exploded outward, rushing back toward the Holy Court forces!

Xi Lufei's expression hardened. She struck her scepter against the floor and raised a purification shield before the group.

A cardinal's face changed.

“Not good! The Chapter of Imprisonment needs more power!”

“Quickly!”

The surrounding Holy Court inquisitors immediately poured their mental strength into the Books of Origins. More than twenty Holy Court oracles who had just arrived joined in, supplying additional energy.

Golden light flowed from the books once again, continuously reinforcing the restraints on Fang Heng's wrists.

“Don't attack him!” Xi Lufei commanded. “Ordinary attacks can't destroy a Demon Seed. They'll only stimulate it further! Take him to the Demon-Sealing Cavern beneath the cathedral and seal him there for now!”

She watched Fang Heng closely.

“He's lost control!”

“Understood!”

Most of Fang Heng's attention remained inside his sea of consciousness, maintaining its stability. He could vaguely hear the Holy Court members shouting around him.

Lost control? Well, not entirely.

After the sacred restraints had bound his wrists and ankles, and after the last small eruption of Demon Seed energy, he actually felt that some of the power inside his consciousness had dissipated.

His condition was a little better now. He could even spare part of his attention for what was happening outside.

But he'd discovered a deeply awkward problem.

Whenever the Demon Seed's power emerged from his body, the sacred bindings purified most of it immediately.

Of course, Fang Heng could force the power inside him to erupt and try to break free from the Holy Court's restraints.

But if he did that, he was certain the Demon Seed's backlash would strike him again—perhaps violently enough to kill him.

What should I do?

For once, he felt completely stuck between two bad options.

Meanwhile, the Holy Court personnel had formed layer after layer of containment around him, holding him firmly in place with their binding powers. Any Demon Seed aura that escaped was purified as soon as it appeared.

Even then, no one dared lower their guard.

Xi Lufei maintained an outer purification shield.

“Take him downstairs!”

“Understood!”

The three cardinals exchanged looks and nodded.

There was a reason Xi Lufei had remained at the cathedral even after the Yin Vein fissure erupted in Duncha. The same was true of the three cardinals.

Contrary to the rumors, there was no mysterious stone inscription used to train Crusaders in the cathedral.

They were here to protect the Demon-Sealing Cavern.

The cathedral itself had been built over the cavern.

Long ago, Duncha had been a world of chaos. The Holy Court had slaughtered countless demons, established its faith, and eventually brought the world under its control.

Yet many of the monsters it encountered couldn't be killed completely.

By a stroke of luck, the Holy Court discovered a special plant native to this location. Cultivating it with sacred energy caused the plant to release an unusually powerful form of holy energy.

Unfortunately, it couldn't be transplanted. So the Holy Court built the Demon-Sealing Cavern here, using the plant as its foundation.

Demonic creatures were imprisoned inside and slowly weakened until their strength had been completely worn away and they could finally be destroyed.

As Duncha gradually became peaceful, the Holy Court constructed its grand cathedral above the old cavern. The name Demon-Sealing Cavern remained in use.

Successive generations of cathedral leaders carried the responsibility of suppressing powerful monsters.

Even now, when the Holy Court encountered a creature too dangerous to handle in a mid-tier game world, it tried to transport the monster to the cavern and imprison it until its power could be fully purified.

And now, perhaps by fate, the Holy Court had encountered Fang Heng.

Xi Lufei could tell that the Demon Seed inside him was unusually complete.

The Holy Court currently had no way to destroy it entirely, and its condition was extremely unstable.

If they carelessly stimulated its core, the worst-case outcome was the release of the Demon Seed's full potential in a single explosion. The entire Duncha world might perish along with it!

That was a risk the Holy Court could never accept.

They had to be exceedingly careful.

First, they would seal the Demon Seed temporarily. Then they would ask the Divine Domain to intervene and bind Fang Heng completely with higher-realm power.

Xi Lufei and the three cardinals escorted the restrained Fang Heng down into the cathedral's basement.

In the Prayer Hall, the sacred guards had reorganized after the initial confusion. With Fang Heng no longer directly controlling the zombies, their combat effectiveness fell substantially, allowing the Holy Court to regain the initiative.

The disturbance was temporarily suppressed.

Holding Fang Heng tightly with the Chapter of Imprisonment, the escort descended to the fifth underground floor.

At the far end of a corridor stood a metal door that radiated a chill. More than a dozen Holy Court arbiters guarded it.

The name Demon-Sealing Cavern dated back to ancient times. After generations of renovations, the place had been converted into a vast underground prison.

Dark-gold light flowed across the door.

“Open the Demon-Sealing Cavern.”

“Yes, Archbishop!”

Hum—!

The huge metal door slowly opened, and Xi Lufei entered first.

Fang Heng was feeling a little better. While continuing to resist the turmoil inside his sea of consciousness, he observed his surroundings.

The cavern was a massive circular underground prison. Rows of stone cells lined the walls. A wide round space occupied the center.

And right in the middle of that hall was… a flower bed?

Before he could examine it closely, Xi Lufei gave another firm command.

“Activate Demon-Suppression Mode!”

Whoosh! Whoosh!

An immense magic array inscribed on the cavern ceiling began to rotate.

Dazzling sacred light instantly filled the entire underground chamber!

The prisoners confined in the surrounding cells grew restless under the illumination.

Hiss! Hiss!

Black mist flowing from Fang Heng dissolved rapidly beneath the sacred light.

Seeing most of the Demon Seed's aura disappear, Xi Lufei finally relaxed a little.

It seemed they had suppressed him for now.

The cavern's magic array had been left by ancient sages. It was capable of restraining every kind of demonic creature—including a Demon Seed.

— End of Chapter 2055 —
