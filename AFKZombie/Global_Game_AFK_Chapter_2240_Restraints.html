Chapter 2240

Restraints

From original Chinese Chapter 2241 · 约束

In the past, although the four Tree Spirit clans had competed to nurture the Divine Tree, most of their rivalry had been conducted openly. The Council of Elders had overseen everything, and its rules imposed strict limits on what the clans could do.

For example, they were forbidden from deliberately damaging another clan's Prayer-Curse Temples or openly fighting one another.

Now that the Council of Elders was gone, those rules would only become less binding.

Even so, the Wood Clan had never imagined that the Leaf Clan would charge straight at them!

They had even killed members of their own race!

"Hah! What a joke!" Ladiard sneered. "The Council told us not to fight among ourselves? Then what were you and the other two clans doing when you seized the Divine Tree essence from us? For the sake of our shared blood, I'll give you a chance to live. Take your people and leave, or you'll stay here forever!"

"In your dreams!"

Xidi roared. Enormous wooden spikes erupted from the giant tree supporting the Prayer-Curse Temple and stabbed furiously toward the Leaf Clan!

Both sides were genuinely enraged, and the battle flared up again.

Fang Heng remained hidden nearby, watching the two Tree Spirit clans fight.

The Wood Clan was somewhat weaker overall, and it had fewer fighters as well. It soon found itself at a disadvantage.

"BOOM!"

After a brief clash, Ladiard gained the upper hand over Elder Xidi. A twisting tornado of razor-sharp leaves swept Xidi high into the air and smashed him into the massive tree behind him.

Xidi struggled to his feet. Deep cuts from the sharp leaves covered his tough body.

Looking around, he saw that several of his clansmen had already been killed by the Leaf Clan!

The five remaining Wood Clan members gathered around him, only to find themselves completely surrounded.

"Ladiard! How dare you…"

Xidi glared at him, fists clenched, his anger reaching its limit.

In truth, during the previous struggle over the Divine Tree Seed Essence, all four clans had exercised considerable restraint. They had maintained a final measure of decorum and refrained from delivering fatal blows against their own kind.

Unfortunately, the Leaf Clan had been the Stone Spirit Puppets' primary target. Several of its people had died under the puppets' attacks.

Its losses had been severe.

Ladiard had come to hate the other clans for it.

They might not have killed his people with their own hands, but what difference did that make?

If the other three clans hadn't blocked their retreat, would those people have died?

They had simply used someone else's blade to do the killing!

"Well? What's your answer?" Ladiard stared coldly at Xidi, murder in his eyes. "Hand over the Ritual Vessels and I'll spare you. Otherwise, don't say I didn't warn you. Every one of you will die here today!"

Xidi's breathing grew heavier.

He realized Ladiard wasn't bluffing.

Ladiard truly intended to kill them!

After weighing his options, Xidi had no choice but to decide.

"Ladiard! The Wood Clan will remember this! Let's go!"

Xidi left behind two Ritual Vessels, waved his hand, and led the injured Wood Clan members away.

"Tell your Great Elder that this Prayer-Curse Temple belongs to me now! If he objects, he's welcome to come find me!" Ladiard smiled coldly and gestured to his followers. "See them out!"

Watching from hiding, Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

Ladiard had been making murderous threats and had shown no mercy toward his own kind, yet he'd actually let them go at the last moment?

Did the Leaf Clan not want this incident to escalate into an outright break with the Wood Clan?

Fang Heng rapidly assessed the situation.

The relationships among the Tree Spirit clans really were difficult to understand.

Whatever!

Hadn't he followed them all this way precisely to steal a kill?

The Wood Clan elder was badly injured. This was the perfect opportunity!

Time to steal that kill!

With his decision made, Fang Heng cast a vampire tracking skill at Xidi's retreating figure from a distance. Then he slipped into Subspace Projection and quickly followed him.

…

The six Wood Clan members in Xidi's party had mostly suffered serious injuries and couldn't move very fast.

After leaving the temple grounds, they slowed beneath a giant tree to catch their breath.

"Elder, are you… are you really all right?"

"I'm fine."

Xidi waved a hand. "We were ambushed by the Leaf Clan and suffered heavy injuries. Oakley, yours are the lightest, and you're the fastest. Go back and report this to Elder Chrono. Have him bring reinforcements immediately. We only possess three Prayer-Curse Temples. If we lose another, the Divine Tree we're raising will fall far behind the Leaf Clan's. Even with the Divine Tree Seed Essence, we…"

As he spoke, Xidi's expression shifted. He braced himself against a nearby tree and struggled to steady his breathing.

The others hurried forward to support him. "Elder, you…"

Suppressing the pain of his internal injuries, Xidi waved them back. "I'm fine. Remember what I just told you and report it immediately."

"Yes, Elder!"

Oakley committed the instructions to memory and set off at once to report to the clan.

Xidi watched him leave, drew a deep breath, and turned to the remaining clansmen. "Once the clan learns what happened, they'll send help quickly. My Sea of Consciousness is in severe turmoil right now. I need some time. Just stand guard while I recover."

With that, Xidi sat down to regulate his breathing and calm his disordered Sea of Consciousness.

"Understood, Elder."

The other four Wood Clan members were not nearly as badly hurt. They immediately took up positions around him and kept watch.

Hm?

Not even two minutes had passed when one clansman gave a startled sound and turned toward a thicket behind them.

What was that noise?!

"Rustle… rustle…"

All four were drawn toward the sound coming from the bushes.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!"

They looked over and saw a massive pack of Lickers burst from the thicket!

"Watch out! We're under attack!"

The Wood Clan members' expressions changed. They leaped into fighting stances.

Several wooden spikes materialized in the air and stabbed toward the oncoming Lickers!

"BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!"

The exploding spikes and a second chain of detonations blasted the first wave of Lickers back into the bushes!

But before the guards could catch their breath, another wave burst out of the surrounding forest from every direction!

"Careful! There are too many of them!"

"Protect the Elder!"

Xidi had left part of his awareness on guard while restoring his Sea of Consciousness. Hearing the shouts, he forcibly interrupted his meditation technique and snapped his eyes open.

His eyes were bloodshot.

When he saw the creatures emerging from the bushes, rage surged through him.

He had seen monsters like these before.

Lickers!

The Leaf Clan!

It had to be them!

Xidi immediately thought of the human who had been working with the Leaf Clan!

Editing and Source Notes
  • Continuity and mechanics. The original distinguishes the clash between the clans from Xidi’s subsequent pursuit. Xidi believes the Leaf Clan is responsible for the Lickers, though Fang Heng is acting on his own.

Chinese Expressions

  • 借刀杀人. Literally “killing with a borrowed knife”: arranging for a third party to kill an enemy.
