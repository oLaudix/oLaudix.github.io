Chapter 3103

Run Ragged

From Chinese Chapter 3104 · 折腾

"Captain, we're evacuating the civilians. We questioned several employees who left the building but found nothing suspicious. What should we do next? Organize a team to go inside and take a look?"

Go inside and look?

Look, my ass!

Balin glared at his trusted subordinate, silently cursing him for a fool.

The last time, Black Mist that was nowhere near this thick had concealed tens of thousands of insects in the sewers.

With the mist this dense, who knew how many insects might be hiding inside?

Go in and investigate?

You really aren't afraid of dying, are you, kid...

Balin glanced at the Kinsfolk's contracted security team, also waiting outside the entrance, and said gravely, "There's no rush. We're the guard force. Our first duty is to protect civilians. Maintain the cordon and wait for instructions from the Kinsfolk."

"Yes, sir!"

The security team recruited by the Kinsfolk had suffered badly last time. They had learned their lesson and stayed outside the building.

Their job, after all, was to protect Kinsfolk lives and property.

This building didn't belong to the Kinsfolk.

They had no reason to risk their lives going inside.

The incidents involving the Ouroboros Seed and the Black Mist from a while ago had occurred again tonight. Many people had supposedly died last time, so the civilians didn't dare gather around and evacuated obediently.

Soon, teams of warriors dispatched by the Kinsfolk arrived and assembled outside the building, which continued belching Black Mist.

Just like the guards and security contractors, the warrior teams were unwilling to enter and investigate.

As the mist grew denser, it gradually interfered with perception as well.

No one could say how many insect creatures were lurking inside.

No one wanted to take the risk.

Everyone waited.

The warrior-team captain suddenly frowned and whipped his head to the right. "Who's there?"

Everyone looked in the direction of his gaze.

There was only a cloud of Black Mist spilling from the building, without a single figure in sight.

"What's wrong, Captain?"

"I felt someone there."

The captain withdrew his gaze from the distant mist and shook his head. "I'm not certain."

"Captain, there's something strange about this Black Mist, but we haven't detected any insects."

"Yes, something isn't right. Don't rush inside. Stay alert and wait for reinforcements."

"Understood."

They waited more than half an hour. Only after various reinforcements arrived, including personnel from the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's military, did the teams enter the building in orderly groups to investigate.

The results were unexpected.

None of the teams found any trace of insects or any clues related to the Black Mist.

It was as though the mist had never existed.

They couldn't find its source. Perhaps that source had vanished already. Whatever the reason, the concentration of mist in the building soon dropped rapidly.

As the investigators prepared to continue, a new alarm suddenly arrived.

Another building nearby had experienced a high-concentration Black Mist leak.

They left some personnel behind and hurried most of the teams to the other building.

All night, the guards, security contractors, military personnel, and Kinsfolk-recruited warriors were dragged back and forth from place to place. They were thoroughly run ragged.

Yet no matter how thoroughly they investigated, neither the Kinsfolk nor the joint Seven-Realm Martial Capital investigation teams found anything abnormal.

Eventually, they were so numb to the alarms that they simply stopped responding.

Fortunately, the Black Mist didn't affect the body. Over the course of several hours, it dispersed and gradually thinned, disappearing completely by daylight the next day.

But...

Why had this strange mist appeared twice in succession in the same area?

The Kinsfolk themselves began to panic.

From their understanding of the Black Mist, something like this should never have been able to exist in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

Neither the Black Mist nor the Ouroboros Seed should have been here.

Yet every investigation had drawn a blank.

An atmosphere of fear spread among the Kinsfolk.

...

Meanwhile, Fang Heng had returned to the villa's underground base.

After wandering around all night, he had finally reduced the basement's Black Mist concentration to a very low level.

Now he could resume AFK grinding.

First, max out Alchemy at the Transcendent level. After that came the Demigod level.

That required his zombie clones to keep constructing alchemical magic arrays and grinding experience nonstop.

Then there were Mysticism and Nature Studies.

Advancing Grandmaster-level Mysticism and Transcendent-level Nature Studies required absorbing natural energy.

His zombie clones couldn't help with that, so he had to grind it himself.

Fang Heng sat cross-legged in the middle of the basement and absorbed the natural energy released by Abe Akaya.

Under the enhancement of the law magic array, the natural energy remained highly active.

His improvements in Mysticism also allowed his body to absorb natural energy more efficiently, even slightly increasing the rate at which it was converted into Qi.

The two effects complemented each other perfectly, producing more than the sum of their parts.

Prioritizing Mysticism and Alchemy had definitely been the right choice!

With that thought, Fang Heng quieted his mind and focused on training again.

[Hint: Player has absorbed Mysticism energy. Mysticism EXP +12,000. Current Mysticism level increased to LV: 33.]

[Hint: Player's zombie clone has completed the construction of an alchemical magic array. Alchemy EXP +9...]

One training cycle followed another.

After an unknown amount of time, Fang Heng slowly exhaled and glanced at the phone beside him.

It was already ten at night.

He had spent another three days continuously grinding.

At this rate, Mysticism should reach its maximum level in another day, while Alchemy would need two or three more.

Perhaps once those two disciplines advanced, his rate of absorbing natural energy would improve even further?

The feeling of continuously upgrading and accelerating his own growth was fantastic. Fang Heng could hardly wait.

For an ordinary person, training for a full day without interruption was already extraordinary. Beyond that, their body would give out first.

Fang Heng, however, possessed a terrifying constitution. Together with the Undying Body effect, he could train around the clock without a problem.

The only drawback was the mental strain, which actually reduced his training efficiency a little.

Fang Heng stood and stretched.

After three days of continuous work by so many zombie clones, another thick layer of Black Mist had accumulated in the basement.

Very well.

He might as well go out for some fresh air and stretch his legs!

Fang Heng shifted into soul form again and headed for the Kinsfolk-controlled district.

This time he was even bolder, heading straight for a Kinsfolk corporate branch complex.

...

The Kinsfolk branch complex.

Since the Black Mist had appeared three nights ago, patrols throughout the surrounding streets, including the complex itself, had been reinforced. The number of guards on night duty had doubled.

The intense workload left the guards thoroughly dissatisfied.

The same miserable wages, but several times the work.

How was that fair?

One guard complained inwardly as he conducted his routine patrol around the building.
