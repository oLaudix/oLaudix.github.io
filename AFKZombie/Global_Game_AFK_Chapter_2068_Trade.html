Chapter 2068

Trade

From original Chinese Chapter 2069 · 交易

As she spoke, Li Qingran couldn't help marveling at how things had changed.

Before Fang Heng appeared, she would never have imagined that one person could completely overturn the balance of the war between the Holy Court and the undead in Chiboreya.

And now both sides were fighting over the Merk Temple.

Fang Heng considered his situation. Since the Divine Realm was watching him and he couldn't do much to help, he spread his hands and decided to leave the fighting to others.

“I'm stuck here anyway. Let Lord Dani handle it. Trust her—she's a professional.”

“Yes. Lord Dani has plenty of experience fighting the Holy Court. We can rely on her. But there's another important matter that requires your decision.”

Li Qingran paused. “It's about the main mission we accepted from Mentor Gu Luo.”

“Rescue Chiboreya?”

“Yes.”

The main mission's completion percentage had now exceeded 1,500%.

An astonishing figure!

When Li Qingran had first invited Fang Heng to help, she had never dreamed he would achieve something like this.

Her breathing grew a little heavier. She steadied herself before continuing.

“I've been negotiating over the past two days. Two undead-faction players want the remaining two places on our mission team. In exchange, they're offering a divine artifact.”

What?!

A divine artifact!

Fang Heng was startled. He hadn't even known that mission completion had reached 1,500%.

At more than a thousand percent, he could expect a direct reward for advancing to Grandmaster-level Necromancy. And with the mission completed so far beyond its requirements, knowing Mentor Gu Luo's temperament, he would almost certainly award additional treasures too.

Those would be extremely valuable.

But were two mission slots really worth an entire divine artifact?

Was he still half-asleep?

Fang Heng shook his head. The last traces of drowsiness vanished.

“What artifact?”

“I've emailed you the item information and screenshots. Take a look. The two players are beside me right now, so we should give them an answer soon.”

“All right. Hang on. Don't end the call.”

Fang Heng immediately opened the laptop beside his bed. Anything involving a divine artifact deserved his full attention.

He found Li Qingran's email and opened it.

An image appeared on the screen.

[Item: Gem—The Illusion God's Joke]

[Type: Divine Artifact]

[Description: Effective while kept in the backpack. Guaranteed to drop upon death. Can be embedded into jewelry such as a necklace or ring to enhance that accessory's attributes.]

[Description: Permanently provides +10 Charisma. Charisma is a hidden attribute that improves the success rate of interactions and initial favorability with others.]

[Additional Skill 1: The Illusion God's Joke]

[Skill description: The player may simulate and create a special identity. It is treated as a new identity and cannot be penetrated by detection or reconnaissance skills below divine rank. This identity may accept quests, complete them, and receive rewards. It lasts up to 72 hours. A new identity may be created every 72 hours. The player may select inherited skills for the alternate identity, but their levels cannot exceed those of the player's original skills.]

[Additional description: The player may claim attribute rewards, experience, items, and similar rewards obtained through quests completed by this identity using their original body. However, all points, contribution, favorability, and similar rewards obtained from completing those quests cannot be inherited.]

[Additional Skill 2: The Illusion God's Protection]

[Skill description: The player is immune to detection and reconnaissance skills below divine rank. When being tracked, the tracker must search a wider area and the duration of successful tracking is shortened. The degree of protection depends on both parties' base attributes; tracking effectiveness is reduced by at least 50%.]

[Additional Skill 3: One More Joke]

[Skill description: Upon death, the player creates an illusory corpse at the point of death for 120 seconds, while the real body is instantly transported to a random point 5,000–6,000 units away. The player revives with 10% of maximum HP and most negative effects removed. Remaining uses: 1.]

After reading the description, Fang Heng fell silent.

This wasn't a combat-oriented artifact. It provided little direct increase in fighting ability.

The final life-saving skill was excellent, though.

No wonder its owners were willing to trade it.

For an ordinary player, the item was undeniably powerful, but it wasn't transformative. Exchanging it for two slots that would allow them to advance straight to Grandmaster-level Necromancy would be a perfectly reasonable deal.

However…

Fang Heng studied the descriptions again.

This thing would be incredibly useful to him!

The Holy Court was hunting him right now. The Illusion God's Protection would clearly provide valuable additional security.

The third skill essentially gave him an extra life, which was equally attractive.

Only the first skill was a little strange.

Creating a new identity?

It would last just 72 hours before disappearing, and when it did, any active quests and progress belonging to it would disappear as well.

What was the point?

He examined the description again.

The first use he could think of was accepting a quest with an enormous failure penalty through the alternate identity.

If he succeeded, his main body could collect the rewards. If he failed, he could abandon the identity and simply create another after 72 hours.

The punishment wouldn't fall on his original body!

Well, he could think about that later.

At the very least, the second and third skills were definitely useful—and urgently so!

Fang Heng picked up his phone.

“Did you find this artifact specifically for me?”

“Mm. Because of the game's restrictions, players can't easily transfer huge amounts of Main God Points in a single transaction. I didn't think any of the other proposed items would interest you. But this one seemed like something you might need.”

“Yes. I need it badly. Let them join the team. To avoid complications, they'll have to sign a contract first.”

High-value player transactions often used contracts notarized through the Main God Space in exchange for Main God Points.

“That's fine,” Li Qingran replied. “But they have one other condition.”

“What condition?”

“They want you to force an early settlement of the mission, complete it immediately, and claim the rewards.”

Fang Heng frowned.

An early settlement? What was the rush?

The Yin Vein Rift was still in Duncha, and the Holy Court wasn't likely to eliminate it soon. If they let things continue, there should be even more mission rewards to earn.

If they forced settlement now…

Based on past experience, they would probably receive only around 30–50% of the rewards projected from the current completion percentage.

— End of Chapter 2068 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Illusion God’s Joke. 幻神的玩笑 is the divine artifact’s name, with separate skills of the same name, Illusion God’s Protection, and One More Joke.
  • Distance unit. The resurrection skill says 5000–6000 in the Chinese but does not state meters; the translation preserves the numerical range without inventing units.
  • Alternate identity. The identity can claim some rewards for the true body, but contribution, points, and favorability do not transfer.
