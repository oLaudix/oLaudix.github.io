Chapter 2073

Challenge

From original Chinese Chapter 2074 · 挑战

Lindro had thought the matter through.

Under normal conditions, with all five members present, his team had about a 60% chance of clearing an S-class trial.

This time, however, one teammate had been unable to come.

With only four people, their chance of clearing S-class was practically zero. Adding an unfamiliar fifth player wouldn't necessarily help; they didn't know the newcomer's strength and had no experience coordinating with him.

So it made sense to play safe and choose A-class instead.

Even if they'd misjudged Fang Heng and recruited a useless teammate, the original four still had around a 60% chance of success.

“Sounds good. Looking forward to working together.”

Fang Heng just wanted to leave this place quickly.

With the team's help, he could enter a trial and look for a route out. Even if he couldn't leave partway through, completing one trial ought to allow him to explore beyond the temple area.

At least it would give him more freedom of movement.

“Hahaha! I like your attitude. Come on, I'll introduce you to the team.”

Lindro cheerfully brought Fang Heng over to the others.

There were five of them now, including Fang Heng.

One specialized in magical healing and reconnaissance, another in defensive paladin techniques, and the other two were flexible fighters who usually dealt damage but could fill other roles in emergencies.

In fact, the higher the level of the game world, the closer players came to being all-rounders.

Strict roles mattered less. Players might have specialties, but everyone was expected to switch positions when needed.

Even the healer could contribute damage in a fight; they just weren't as proficient at it.

That was why Lindro hadn't even asked what position Fang Heng preferred when they first met.

[Hint: Player Lindro has invited you to join his party.]

[Hint: You have joined a normal party. Current permissions: Party Member.]

“Come on. Let's collect the mission from an instructor.”

“I'll stay here.”

Fang Heng stopped and waved a hand.

When everyone looked at him, he explained, “I have a special piece of bound equipment that reduces my Charisma by five points. Better not let that affect the mission assignment.”

The others immediately understood.

Charisma was a hidden game attribute affecting an NPC's initial opinion of a player and the rate at which favorability increased.

After years of research, players had learned that starting Charisma usually ranged between -3 and +3; around 90% of players began at zero.

Losing five points was a substantial penalty. The other attributes of Fang Heng's equipment were probably very good to justify it.

“All right. We'll collect the mission. Wait here, Brother Fang Heng. We'll be right back.”

“Sure.”

Fang Heng watched Lindro lead the others to a paladin instructor in the middle of the hall.

They spoke for a short while.

Then a game message appeared before Fang Heng.

[Hint: Your party has accepted a random Crusader trial mission—Rank A.]

[Hint: This mission is highly dangerous. Exercise caution.]

“Done.”

Lindro returned with his teammates. “We've got the mission. Had a chat with the instructor; no problems. Let's go.”

Fang Heng followed them to the teleportation area on the hall's right side.

Whoosh, whoosh…

Sacred light enveloped the group. A moment later, they disappeared into the passage together.

…

After an unknown period, Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

In the pale morning light, a snow-covered mountain rose before them. Thick drifts had almost buried the road; only the faint outline of a path climbing the slope remained.

Snowflakes drifting from the summit settled lightly on their shoulders.

Game notifications appeared.

[Hint: You have entered an unknown game world. Obtain more information to determine this world's coordinates.]

[Hint: Your Crusader Trial Mark remains active. All trial activities are recorded. Actions that violate Crusader doctrine will cause additional deductions of Crusader experience and points. Under special circumstances, you may be forcibly teleported back to Eternal Holy City.]

[Hint: Your party has triggered the trial mission—Slay the Three-Eyed Demon Serpent.]

[Mission: Slay the Three-Eyed Demon Serpent.]

[Description: An unexpected disturbance has awakened the Three-Eyed Demon Serpent sleeping in the snowy mountains. It has seized the entire mountain range and repeatedly invaded towns below, destroying Holy Court churches. Faithful believers have prayed for divine guidance and the serpent's destruction.]

[Objective: Kill the Three-Eyed Demon Serpent.]

[Time limit: 3 hours. If time expires, the mission automatically fails and players are teleported back to Eternal Holy City.]

A boss-hunting mission?

Fang Heng examined the description thoughtfully.

It was impossible to judge the boss's strength from that alone, or even determine what kind of game world they had entered.

And the time limit was only three hours. When it expired, they would be forcibly returned to Eternal Holy City.

The Crusader Trial Mark was still active, monitoring every move he made.

He'd better avoid using undead or vampire abilities.

What a nuisance.

Without vampire form, he couldn't travel very far in three hours. He doubted he could locate a passage out of the world before the timer expired.

[Hint: You have crossed into another plane. The Divine Realm's pursuit team will attempt to locate you again in 9 + 15 hours (the additional delay is caused by the artifact's ability).]

Huh?

Fang Heng was startled by the new message.

Was there an upside to this after all?!

Of course! Taking a Holy Court trial through a teleportation passage also counted as entering another plane. That meant the Divine Realm's pursuers had to begin locating him all over again!

Now that was good news.

“This is a boss-kill mission. Everyone, be careful.”

Fang Heng looked toward Lindro, who had issued the warning.

The other players had become serious. Boss battles were among the harder mission types, with high risks and heavy resource consumption.

The three-hour limit made things worse: there was little time to devise an unconventional solution.

Most groups would simply have to defeat the boss through brute force!

A red directional marker appeared in Fang Heng's vision, pointing up the mountain toward the Three-Eyed Demon Serpent.

“Let's go.”

Lindro took the lead and headed up the slope.

— End of Chapter 2073 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Trial mark. The Crusader mark monitors players even inside the trial and may force their return if doctrine is violated.
  • Three-Eyed Demon Serpent. 三眼妖蛇 is the boss named in the objective; the Chinese says a demon serpent, not a dragon.
  • Forced return. A three-hour time limit applies even if the boss is not defeated.
