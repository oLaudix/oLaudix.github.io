Chapter 3073

Advancement

From Chinese Chapter 3074 · 进阶

The hand seals possessed a mysterious power.

As they flowed from one gesture to the next, Fang Heng could clearly feel his mental power circulating a little more smoothly than before.

Mu Shunan was quietly stunned.

Good grief.

Did this kid really learn that fast?

Not only had he made no mistakes in the movements, but he'd reproduced the rhythm of the seals perfectly too?

Ordinary scholars needed ten days to half a month just to learn the complete sequence. Some still hadn't corrected all the details even after reaching Master-level mysticism.

Yet this youngster had achieved such precision after watching it only once.

Was it a racial talent?

At the same time, once Fang Heng finished the sequence, a corresponding game notification appeared before his eyes.

[Hint: Player has triggered a chained quest—Mysticism Advancement (Grandmaster).]

Quest Name: Mysticism Advancement (Grandmaster).

Quest Description: You are cultivating Grandmaster mysticism under a scholar's guidance. For certain reasons, your foundations remain unstable. You must reinforce them by learning from the beginning.

Quest Requirement: Complete Meridian Clearing (Current: 0.03%).

Quest Reward: Next stage of the quest.

Fang Heng sensed the change in his mental power and glanced at the game notification.

Foundations, huh...

Just like spatial studies, he'd raised this ability mainly by consuming advancement books through the game. His foundations were unstable, and he could often bring out only twenty or thirty percent of his actual strength.

So be it.

It was time to make up for all the classes he'd skipped in the game.

“Excellent. You have the best talent I've ever seen.”

Watching Fang Heng finish his seal practice, Mu Shunan nodded with satisfaction and praised him freely. “Mysticism instruction differs from that of other disciplines. Much of it involves modifying the body itself. Certain ointments applied to the skin can accelerate training. After our session, I'll have my assistant prepare some for you. Use them while practicing these hand seals at home.”

Compared with the spatial studies instructor, Mu Shunan was clearly much more enthusiastic.

Fang Heng cupped his fists. “Thank you.”

“No need to be so formal. We're both Noel Clan warriors. We may even end up on the same team someday. We should exchange insights when we get the chance.”

After the lesson ended and Fang Heng left, Mu Shunan's puzzled expression still hadn't entirely faded.

Outstanding talent and potential, but his mysticism proficiency seemed rather low...

“What a strange fellow...”

Mu Shunan was about to leave when the door opened and a large, dark-skinned man entered. “Mu Shunan, we're in trouble!”

“You're the one in trouble.”

Mu Shunan gave him an annoyed look. “What's with all the fuss? What happened?”

“The mining rights to Star System V-028! Have you heard? Spatial fluctuations have been detected there. There could be extremely rare spatial ores. They'll divide up the mining rights next week, and the Noel Clan wants a share.”

Brooks's eyes shone with fighting spirit. He kept striking his huge fists together with heavy thuds. “Finally, a chance for a proper fight.”

V-028 was a newly discovered, enormous uninhabited star system. Several mineral resources had already been found during preliminary surveys. With rare spatial ores now added to the mix, no single faction could likely claim it all.

Several powers would probably divide the spoils.

Mu Shunan wondered whether the recent scramble to recruit warriors and poach fighters among the overlord species had something to do with the struggle over V-028.

“Come on. Let's go to the preparation room and see what's happening.”

...

Elsewhere in the Noel Clan building, Fang Heng returned to the lobby with Anxi.

Fang Heng asked, “I remember warriors are required to compete, right?”

Anxi's heart skipped a beat.

Nearly half a month had passed, and only now was he asking about the matches?

Generally, newly recruited warriors fell into two categories.

The first did everything possible to improve their strength, desperately searched for information about upcoming matches, and fought tooth and nail to survive their first month.

The second simply coasted along.

They enjoyed the benefits for one month before the Noel Clan dismissed them.

It was embarrassing, but at least they'd enjoyed a month's privileges. After a year or so, they could try to rejoin the Noel Clan and coast for another month.

Only after being dismissed three times for failing performance requirements would someone permanently lose eligibility to become a Noel Clan warrior.

Since Fang Heng had never asked about arena duels before, Anxi had suspected he was just here to enjoy the benefits.

“Yes. The Noel Clan's requirements are fairly lenient for new warriors. You need to participate in and win three duels each month.”

“What's the procedure? Do I register with you?”

“Each preparation area displays the upcoming matches and a short description of each. Whenever you're free, I can accompany you and explain the process.”

“How about now?”

“Of course, Mr. Fang Heng. Please follow me.”

Soon the elevator arrived at the seventeenth floor.

There were more than twenty warriors gathered in Preparation Area Three.

None looked particularly happy. They wore dark expressions and remained silent.

Several gazes lingered briefly on Fang Heng as he opened the door, then quickly moved away.

Fang Heng scanned the warriors in the room.

Quite a few had passed the entrance assessment alongside him. He recognized their faces.

“Mr. Fang Heng, look at this panel. It displays the arena matches scheduled for the next half-month, with brief descriptions of each. Combat generally follows a King of Fighters elimination format, with each side fielding a team of ten.”

Fang Heng studied the more than thirty matches displayed on the screen and asked, “What do the numbers after the matches mean?”

“They represent the match levels and how important the Noel Clan considers them. Each match also restricts the number of high-tier warriors allowed. For example, Level One matches do not permit high-tier fighters.”

Anxi continued, “Warrior rankings are determined jointly by the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's Arena Association. Since you haven't been ranked yet, you're classified as a low-tier warrior.”

“After every battle, the capital assigns you a score based on your performance. As your individual points rise, so does your warrior rank. The arena also limits how often warriors can fight. High-tier fighters may participate in at most three matches per month.”

“Since you're still a beginner-tier warrior, you have no such restriction. You may participate as often as you like.”

Fang Heng nodded lightly.

The rules of the arena had been established by the seven strongest cosmic overlord races in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

Naturally, those rules heavily favored the seven overlords.

Ordinary small species simply lacked the money and resources to recruit so many warriors.

— End of Chapter 3073 —
