Chapter 2166

Consumption

From original Chinese Chapter 2167 · 消耗

“Bzz…”

A reaction!

Fang Heng's heart leapt as he stared at the slowly rotating magic array.

“Whoosh!”

A soft sound.

The array had barely begun operating when its light started fading again.

Another failure?

Everyone turned toward Fang Heng.

Fang Heng closed his eyes and communicated with Abe Akaya through their mental connection.

“Well? How'd it go, Boss Fang?”

Sandy couldn't resist asking as Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

Fang Heng shook his head.

Judging by the result alone, it was a mixture of good and bad news.

“According to Abe Akaya's response, our earlier theory was correct. It can activate the magic array.”

“Heh! I knew it!”

Sandy pumped his fist proudly.

“But Abe Akaya hasn't recovered its strength yet. It tells me natural energy in this world is heavily restricted. That may be why absorbing the world's energy is so difficult for it, and its growth is greatly suppressed. At present, it can't supply enough power to keep the magic array running.”

As Fang Heng spoke, he turned his head.

Several vines extending from Abe Akaya were slowly creeping toward him.

“Hm?”

Sensing the emotions Abe Akaya was sending him, Fang Heng's interest stirred.

What was it?

He steadied himself, closed his eyes, and attempted to communicate through their mental link again.

Abe Akaya seemed to have sensed something in his backpack that would greatly help it recover.

“You want this?”

Fang Heng soon tried taking the Leaf of Life out of his backpack.

He'd exchanged for it from the Tree Spirits earlier.

Besides being usable as equipment, it could be exchanged for a huge amount of Nature Studies experience.

He hadn't been willing to use it yet, so he'd kept it in his backpack.

Was Abe Akaya interested in it?

Seeing the Leaf of Life in Fang Heng's hand, Klevi looked surprised.

“Is that a Leaf of Life?”

“You know what it is?”

“Mm. I've seen one among the elves. It's an extremely precious item.” Klevi studied the leaf and recalled what she'd heard. “My clansmen once mentioned that a Leaf of Life can provide power to a Divine Tree. I think that's what it wants.”

That was certainly true.

Abe Akaya was communicating a longing for the Leaf of Life.

Fang Heng looked at it, rather speechless.

You've got quite a nose, haven't you? You can even sense it hidden in my backpack.

“All right.” He brought the Leaf of Life closer. “Try this.”

The vines extended slowly and wrapped around the leaf.

Within their coils, the Leaf of Life gradually transformed into a green glow, which Abe Akaya completely absorbed.

[Hint: The player's soul symbiote — Divine Tree Abe Akaya — has absorbed the energy in a Leaf of Life. Some of its abilities have recovered.]

Then the vines began sticking to Fang Heng again.

“You want more?”

Apparently one leaf wasn't enough.

It hadn't been easy to squeeze those Leaves of Life out of the Tree Spirits. He'd originally intended to use them to improve his Nature Studies level.

Oh well.

Completing the main quest took priority right now.

Fang Heng sighed inwardly and simply took every Leaf of Life he'd previously swindled from the Tree Spirits out of his backpack.

Like an innocent little child, Abe Akaya sent another burst of delighted emotion, reaching out its vines to absorb the leaves one by one.

As each Leaf of Life dissolved into green light, Abe Akaya's trunk visibly grew thicker and sturdier.

Three minutes later, the last leaf had been consumed.

Once again, vines reached toward Fang Heng.

“More?” Fang Heng spread his hands. “There isn't any. I gave you everything.”

Abe Akaya's vines stubbornly lingered beside him.

“Seriously, there's nothing left.”

Fang Heng didn't know whether to laugh or cry. Had he raised a Divine Tree or a bottomless glutton?

“Wait. Do you mean this?”

A thought occurred to him, and he took a rune from his backpack.

Abe Akaya grew excited again, its branches even trembling slightly.

The runestone.

He'd previously tricked Great Elder Senzo into giving it to him.

According to its item description, it contained a tremendous amount of natural power too.

Fine.

You can have that as well.

Having come this far, Fang Heng simply pulled out the runestone and tossed it toward Abe Akaya.

“Whoosh!”

A vine shot out and caught the stone in midair.

Layer after layer of vines quickly wrapped around it.

“Bzz…”

The runestone activated, flooding the entire underground area with green light.

[Hint: The player's soul symbiote — Abe Akaya — has absorbed a runestone. Some of its abilities have recovered.]

After more than five minutes spent draining the stone of every trace of energy, Abe Akaya finally fell quiet again.

Before Fang Heng could give another command, Abe Akaya extended its vines of its own accord and enveloped the Tree Spirits' teleportation array on the ground again.

“Hiss, hiss…”

The magic array slowly began operating.

Under everyone's expectant gaze, a dim point of light gradually formed at its center.

Fang Heng watched it intently.

Had it worked?

Or…?

Whoosh!

The energy supply suddenly cut off, and the half-formed magic array came to a halt again.

Failure?

Fang Heng looked back at Abe Akaya.

Having absorbed so many Leaves of Life and a runestone, Abe Akaya was now far easier to communicate with than before.

It quickly sent back a mental response.

Put simply…

Abe Akaya still didn't have enough energy.

It was roughly one-third short of what was required to activate the array completely.

Fang Heng shook his head helplessly and relayed the bad news to everyone.

They exchanged glances and fell into thought.

Why did this feel like another vicious circle?

To enter the Inner World, they needed Abe Akaya's power. Unfortunately, Abe Akaya didn't have enough.

Strengthening Abe Akaya wasn't impossible, though.

There were two approaches.

One was to let it absorb energy on a massive scale, expanding wildly and growing stronger.

But that would be extremely difficult. For one thing, Abe Akaya's growth was suppressed, making natural development painfully slow. For another, if it expanded aggressively, the fluctuations from its power would spread rapidly. The Spirit Domain blockade would certainly detect them, and the Federation and Dependents would investigate.

An open confrontation would be unavoidable then.

The second approach…

Go back to the Tree Spirits and obtain more Leaves of Life or similar treasures to strengthen Abe Akaya.

Wasn't that a joke?

The whole reason Fang Heng had brought Abe Akaya into this world was to avoid dealing with the Tree Spirits' quest line!

Now he'd have to find some way to get even more Leaves of Life out of them…

With that attitude of theirs?

The thought alone was exhausting.

Mo Jiawei scratched his head, feeling a headache coming on too.

(End of chapter)
