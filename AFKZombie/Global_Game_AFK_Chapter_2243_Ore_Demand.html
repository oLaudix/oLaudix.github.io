Chapter 2243

Ore Demand

From original Chinese Chapter 2244 · 矿石需求

As Fang Heng listened to Ladiard's explanation, things suddenly clicked into place.

So…

Everything Ladiard had just described was the Tree Spirits' understanding of the universe!

Their world was divided into countless smaller worlds.

The Tree Spirits called them dimensional worlds.

The Dependents called them the Turbulent Universe.

Players called them world tiers, or game worlds!

In essence, they were more or less the same thing.

Put another way…

It was obvious that the smaller fragments Ladiard spoke of were other low-tier game worlds!

Worlds of a lower tier than the Myriad Forest World!

But one thing remained strange.

Extracting energy?

Could the Tree Spirits actually extract energy from other game worlds?

Fang Heng couldn't help asking, "What exactly do you mean by extracting energy?"

"With Ritual Vessels, we can draw energy out of a world. That energy supplies enormous amounts of power for nurturing our Divine Tree. Understand now?"

Of course Fang Heng understood!

He understood perfectly!

Everything made sense now.

Good grief. So the Divine Tree strengthened itself by plundering the energy of other world tiers!

At heart, the Divine Tree was a robber too.

And White Energy Crystals were used to fuel the teleportation passages.

The Tree Spirits' teleportation passages had limitations. Most likely, they could only use White Energy Crystals as a power source.

So the Tree Spirits really had been searching for energy to nurture their Divine Tree recently. Their method of nurturing it was simply different from what Fang Heng had imagined.

Several ideas raced through his mind.

Then…

Could Abe Akaya grow rapidly through the same method?

He raised his head. "If I can obtain a large quantity of refined White Froststone… what would the Leaf Clan pay me in return?"

"Hahaha, you think you can get it?" Ladiard waved dismissively. "Whatever you want."

"I want to see a dimensional world for myself. And I'd like to see the Divine Tree your Leaf Clan is nurturing."

"Those are small matters, provided you bring enough White Froststone first." Fang Heng was about to ask more, but Ladiard had lost interest. He waved him away. "Enough questions. Get the ore first, then we'll talk."

Fang Heng glanced at the game notification, nodded, and left the hall for the moment.

He still had unanswered questions about dimensional worlds.

Besides, although Ladiard had verbally agreed to let him enter a dimensional world and see the Leaf Clan's Divine Tree, the game hadn't confirmed that promise as part of the deal.

Fang Heng suspected the Leaf Clan had merely said whatever came to mind.

He couldn't trust the promise completely.

Then again…

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

Why not give it a try? He could trust Ladiard for the time being.

At worst, he'd lose a few carts of ore.

He could take back a hundred times as much later!

Even so, Fang Heng prepared a fallback plan.

He intended to summon zombie clones and have them dig an underground tunnel from outside the primeval forest all the way to the Prayer-Curse Temple.

If the Leaf Clan decided to take advantage of him, he would make sure they paid for it!

They could all play dirty if it came to that.

…

The Wood Clan settlement.

News that the Leaf Clan had seized another temple sent shock waves through the entire clan.

The Leaf Clan!

They had openly attacked and even cooperated with a human to kill two of the Wood Clan's elders!

No wonder.

Thinking back over the day's events, the Wood Clan had already sensed that something was amiss.

Now, putting the pieces together, they finally understood.

The Leaf Clan had joined forces with the humans long ago!

And laid a trap for them!

The Wood Clan's Great Elder looked solemn, anger smoldering in his eyes.

The deaths of two elders were an enormous loss.

"Everyone, we must decide on a response immediately."

"The Prayer-Curse Temple is vital. If it falls into another clan's hands, the rate at which we replenish our Divine Tree's energy will plummet. We must reclaim it."

"We have to make the Leaf Clan pay! We can't let this go! Fight them! Avenge our elders!"

The Wood Clan was seething with anger.

It had possessed very few Prayer-Curse Temples to begin with.

Having lost another, it had just two left.

What use was that?

And two elders had been killed besides.

Their deaths couldn't simply be forgotten.

At the very least, somebody had to answer for them!

"My fellow clansmen, please calm yourselves."

The Great Elder had only recently returned from the Inner World and convened this meeting personally. He raised a hand to quiet the crowd. "Our Divine Tree is still absorbing the Divine Tree Seed Essence, and its energy recovery is going better than expected. We still have a strong chance. The Leaf Clan wants to provoke us into acting recklessly. Don't fall into its trap."

"If we fight the Leaf Clan head-on, we'll suffer, and the other two clans will be the only beneficiaries."

The clansmen exchanged glances.

One elder spoke with obvious resentment and grief. "Great Elder, I understand, but are Elders Xidi and the other going to have died for nothing?"

"Of course not."

The Great Elder shook his head. "Slaughtering our own kind is a serious taboo. Even humans insist on having a just cause before going to war. The Leaf Clan has now given us one. The Soul Clan still presides over the current Council of Elders. We can invite the other clans to witness what happened and ask the Soul Clan to pass judgment."

The gathered clansmen immediately grasped his meaning.

Asking the Soul Clan to make a ruling was merely a way to unite the other clans against the Leaf Clan.

"Elder, will the other clans really agree to join us?"

"They will."

The Great Elder nodded. "The Leaf Clan has gone too far this time."

The stronger and more overbearing the Leaf Clan became, the more the other clans would fear its dominance—and the more willing they would be to unite against it.

After all, in the end, only one Divine Tree could remain. There could be only one victor!

That belief had been deeply rooted in Tree Spirit society since ancient times.

No clan wanted the Leaf Clan to pull too far ahead!

…

The following morning.

Lickers dragged two ore carts loaded with high-grade White Froststone back to the Prayer-Curse Temple.

Ladiard was discussing the Wood Clan with several of his people.

He found the situation strange.

Since the previous day, he'd ordered the temple's defenses strengthened, expecting the Wood Clan to return and try to seize it.

But the entire night had passed peacefully.

There had been no movement whatsoever from the Wood Clan.

Meanwhile, his own people had been on high alert throughout the night and barely managed to rest.

Had the Wood Clan been beaten into submission?

Were they too frightened to even complain?

Ladiard couldn't shake the feeling that something was off.

After a brief discussion revealed nothing unusual, a clansman approached with a report.

"Elder, Fang Heng has returned."

Fang Heng?

The clansman leaned in and whispered a few words in Ladiard's ear.

Ladiard looked surprised.

Had Fang Heng actually managed to obtain White Froststone?
