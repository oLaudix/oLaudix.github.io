Chapter 2029

Zombie Tide

From original Chinese Chapter 2030 · 尸潮

Even though he had used a charging skill to lead his elite troops straight into the zombie horde, throwing it into chaos, he knew better than anyone that the charge had barely reduced their numbers.

A hundred? A thousand?

Even if they'd killed a hundred thousand,

it would still be a drop in the ocean compared to that endless mass of zombies stretching out before them.

And after that single charge, his troops had already expended some of their holy energy. They couldn't possibly keep this up for long.

Watching from the rear, Gulanchi's heart sank, and his face turned slightly pale.

He had thought that if they could just hold out until reinforcements arrived, Canmu City could be saved.

Now it seemed that wasn't the case at all!

There were simply too many zombies!

For every group that fell, another two or three took its place.

They weren't killing the creatures nearly as fast as new ones appeared.

Just how many were there?!

Li Qingran and more than twenty players from the undead faction remained hidden among the dense ranks of zombies at the rear, observing the battle from a distance.

In truth, both the number of zombies and the results they'd achieved had far exceeded their expectations.

They were asking themselves exactly the same question as the Holy Court's defenders.

Just how many zombies did Fang Heng control?!

The Holy Court's reinforcements had arrived, and the tide of battle seemed to have shifted slightly.

But Li Qingran knew the Holy Court could never hold out!

With just these reinforcements?

At their current level of strength, there was simply no way!

From their position behind the horde, they could see the full picture more clearly.

The zombies at the front were only a small fraction of the entire army!

Huge numbers of zombies had spread throughout the Forest of Bewilderment.

Still more staggered out of the Ghost Tower and poured toward the front lines.

Even the undead players felt their scalps prickle at the terrifying size of the horde.

Some of them had been wondering throughout the journey: if they'd known Fang Heng was this powerful, why hadn't they just launched a frontal assault and steamrolled the enemy?

And that wasn't even counting the undead-faction players following behind to cheer them on.

Besides…

The players turned to look at Li Qingran.

She had gone to considerable trouble to recruit them, yet now it seemed there wasn't even any need for them to fight.

Why had World Lord Fang Heng asked them to come along in the first place?

Li Qingran lowered her head, opened her game interface, and checked the system time.

“It's about time. Everyone, follow me,” she instructed. “We'll circle around to Canmu City's north gate. Keep yourselves hidden.”

“Understood!”

…

Not far away, inside Canmu City, whose gates stood wide open,

the guards grew tense as they watched the zombie horde resume its slow advance after the initial confusion.

This was bad.

They'd thought the arriving reinforcements would pull Canmu City back from the brink, but…

At this rate, there was no way the city could hold!

Meanwhile, taking advantage of the confusion, Fang Heng had quietly left the teleportation area under the guidance of two Devout Guild players and made his way to Canmu City's north gate.

He quickly surveyed the city's defensive arrangements.

There weren't many guards on the walls.

Most of the garrison had been concentrated at the southern gate to resist the approaching zombie horde.

Clearly, the guards stationed in the north were distracted.

Almost time…

Let's begin!

Fang Heng swiftly put on his demon mask and leaped onto the city wall.

“Who's there?!”

A guard on the wall noticed something amiss and immediately shouted.

“Stop!”

Obediently, Fang Heng stopped where he was.

The guards all stared at him, aiming their enchanted crossbows his way. “Take off your mask! Who are you?! This is a restricted area!”

Fang Heng didn't answer.

“The city is on high alert! Get back immediately!”

Hiss! Hiss, hiss!

The next instant, alchemical magic arrays began forming rapidly beneath Fang Heng's feet and throughout the surrounding space!

Startled, the guards all raised their weapons toward him.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

A mass of Lickers surged out of the magic arrays suspended in midair.

What?!

Shocked, the guards fired their crossbows at once!

The bolts had been enhanced with additional magical effects.

Bang! Bang!

Explosive arrows!

A volley struck the Lickers and detonated!

But it had no effect!

The explosions sent the Lickers flying back for an instant, only for them to twist nimbly in the air and leap forward again, their bodies still wreathed in flames as they lunged at the imperial guards!

The wounds left by the explosions had already healed almost completely within a second or two!

What kind of terrifying creatures were these?!

The ordinary imperial guards on the walls were no match for the Lickers. Stunned by the sight, they barely finished their first volley before the creatures descended upon them, leaving no time to reload!

As soon as a Licker knocked a guard down, others swarmed in and tore into him.

More and more zombie clones continued crawling out of the magic arrays around Fang Heng.

Within moments, the northern gate was plunged into chaos!

Outside the city, Li Qingran and her companions saw the disturbance on the walls and clenched their fists in excitement.

It was Fang Heng!

Just as planned, he'd slipped into Canmu City and begun attacking its north gate from within!

“Come on! Follow me!”

Li Qingran quickly led the undead-faction players toward the northern gate.

The city was preoccupied with the enormous horde to the west. There had been few guards at the north gate to begin with, and Fang Heng's surprise attack from inside had caught them completely off guard. Soon, most of the defenders on the wall had been wiped out.

Amid the chaos, Fang Heng descended to the gate, opened it, and joined Li Qingran's party arriving from outside.

“Stay with me, all of you.”

“Mm!”

They'd really managed to infiltrate Canmu City!

It had been far easier than they'd imagined!

The players nodded, still somewhat nervous.

“Let's go!”

They immediately followed Fang Heng deeper into the city.

The first wave of Lickers had already secured the entire northern gate and was now advancing rapidly toward the center of Canmu City.

Along the way, city guards began noticing something wrong and hurried forward with their weapons to intercept them.

At the same time, in the newly constructed teleportation area in the northern part of the city,

Chu Yan and his team of players and NPCs were carrying out preliminary repairs around the teleportation passage.

Just two minutes earlier, the passage, which had been operating normally, had suddenly developed some kind of fault and become unstable.

With no other choice, the Holy Court had temporarily halted the transfer of troops.

City Lord Saino was growing impatient. “Chu Yan! Have you found the problem yet?! How much longer will this take?!”

“We're still investigating. The passage had only just started operating when its output was suddenly increased. That's what caused the malfunction. We'll have to inspect the whole system to identify the fault…”

— End of Chapter 2029 —
