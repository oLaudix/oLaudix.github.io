Chapter 2079

Emergency

From original Chinese Chapter 2080 · 紧急

He had already tested it. Every time he returned to the same world, the tracking countdown was shortened by another hour and a half from its original twenty-four hours.

Worst case, after seventy-two hours he'd be forcibly teleported back to Duncha. He could go offline and hide until he came up with a solution, then ask Chu Yan to buy another Crusader training card so he could return to the Eternal Holy City.

The only real complication was the Demon Seed inside his body.

As the days passed, its power was gradually awakening. It probably wouldn't be long before it began making trouble again.

He still needed to find a way back to the Zombie Apocalypse as soon as possible.

They had spent an entire day farming trials. Although they'd done relatively little actual fighting, Lindro and the others were tired, so they discussed taking a break with Fang Heng.

Fang Heng also felt his luck had been poor today. Maybe a good night's sleep would turn things around tomorrow.

They agreed to meet again in eight hours to pick up more trial quests, then headed together toward the Eternal Holy City's rest area.

Just as Fang Heng was about to find somewhere to sleep, a series of game notifications appeared.

[Hint: Crusader instructors are issuing an emergency quest. Players may form teams of five and visit the Crusader instructors in the Quest Hall to accept the relevant mission.]

[Hint: Emergency quests have variable difficulty and may involve extreme danger. Players are advised to accept them with caution.]

They had only just started toward the rest area and hadn't even gone their separate ways.

The moment the notification appeared, Sak was the first to exclaim softly, "It's a special emergency quest!"

"What?" Fang Heng asked, looking at him.

Sak hurried to explain. "An emergency special quest. Basically, the Eternal Holy City occasionally issues emergency missions. Any player team can accept them, without restrictions, and rewards are distributed according to contribution and completion rate."

"The difficulty varies every time. The penalties for failure are usually small, and if you rank high enough on the completion leaderboard, there are additional rewards..."

Fang Heng pursed his lips. He wasn't particularly interested.

Even completing quests under the identity created by his divine artifact hadn't yielded much profit.

Lindro and the others, however, saw things differently.

These special emergency quests rarely appeared, and the rewards were quite good. Despite their exhaustion after farming so many missions, none of them wanted to miss this opportunity.

Lindro turned to Fang Heng. "Brother Fang Heng, emergency quests don't come along often. Are you interested in joining us?"

Fang Heng shrugged. "Do these missions last long?"

"Usually, yes. There have even been cases where a mission lasted more than forty-eight hours."

Fang Heng's eyes lit up.

More than forty-eight hours!

Now that was worth considering.

The longest of the previous trials had been nine hours. The time limit had been far too restrictive.

Forty-eight hours was different. His chances of finding another exit from the trial world would increase dramatically.

"All right. I'll join."

With everyone in agreement, they immediately turned around and returned to the Quest Hall.

· · ·

By the time they got back, the hall was already packed with players.

Clearly, everyone else had received the emergency quest notification as well. There were quite a few competitors.

"That's not bad either," Fang Heng murmured.

The more people there were, the easier it would be for him to slack off unnoticed.

[Hint: You have entered an unknown game world. Obtain additional information to locate this world's coordinates.]

[Hint: You are currently undergoing assessment under the Crusader Mark. All your actions will be monitored and recorded.]

[Hint: You have crossed into another plane. The Divine Realm's pursuit team will attempt to locate you again in 9 + 15 hours (additional time granted by a divine artifact skill).]

[Hint: You have entered an unknown region. All player attributes have been greatly reduced... Relevant skill detected. You are immune to this negative effect.]

Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

Hot!

The first thing he felt was a wave of scorching heat.

The surrounding air seemed to have been baked beneath a blazing sun. The intense heat even distorted his vision, leaving a faint shimmer in the air.

A sweltering cavern.

[Hint: Crusader Assessment Quest triggered — Collect Hellish Evil Eyes.]

Quest Name: The Holy Court has discovered a special passage occupied by large numbers of Hellish Evil Eyes. These creatures possess limited illusion abilities and ranged attacks. They are also a highly valuable material.

Quest Requirements: Kill 3,000 Hellish Evil Eyes; kill 100 elite Hellish Evil Eyes; collect 500 Hellish Evil Eyes of standard quality or above.

Time Limit: 48 hours.

Description: After forty-eight hours, you will automatically be summoned back to the Eternal Holy City. If you have reached the trial talisman's seventy-two-hour limit, you will leave the Eternal Holy City directly after this mission ends.

Fang Heng scanned the notifications, then looked around.

A lava cavern?

His luck really was terrible today.

If he wanted to find a teleportation passage in this environment, he would first have to get out of the lava caves.

He turned and looked behind him.

Large numbers of Holy Court paladins occupied the entire cavern entrance. Several teleportation magic arrays had been built at the exit.

Obviously, the Holy Court had constructed the arrays. Three Crusader instructors were even stationed in front of them as guards.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes, silently calculating.

If he used his ultimate move... what were the odds that he could wipe out the three instructors and all those Holy Court guards?

He might have to fight the nearby players too. And somehow he would also need to keep the teleportation passage functioning normally.

After doing the arithmetic, Fang Heng estimated his odds of success at less than ten percent.

Lindro and his companions appeared nearby. They stood still for a moment, their expressions grave.

Sak muttered, "We've seen special environments before, but why are our attributes being suppressed so badly this time...?"

Suppressed?

Fang Heng's interest was piqued, and he looked at the others.

"Yes. This must be an especially unusual environment belonging to the Hell faction. Our attributes have been severely reduced."

Only then did Fang Heng realize that his own attributes hadn't changed at all.

Some worlds rejected outsiders and their attributes, weakening players who entered them. But the Demon Mark on Fang Heng made him completely immune to the negative effect.

In that case...

His gaze returned to the three Crusader instructors.

Both players and NPCs would suffer from the environmental suppression. Perhaps his chances of defeating those instructors had improved a little...

"Phew. At least it's a gathering quest."

Karina, the only woman on their team, breathed a sigh of relief.

They'd encountered gathering quests before, and such missions were among the easiest of the trials.

— End of Chapter 2079 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Tracking countdown. Repeated returns to the same plane shorten the chase countdown by 1.5 hours each time; the later 9 + 15-hour notification includes the divine artifact bonus.
  • Trial objectives. The quest asks for 3,000 normal kills, 100 elite kills, and 500 standard-quality or better collected Evil Eyes; it has a 48-hour limit.

Chinese Expressions

  • 摸鱼 (mō yú). Literally “touching/catching fish,” colloquially means slacking off or quietly avoiding work. Rendered as “slack off unnoticed.”
