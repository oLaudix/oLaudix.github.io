Chapter 2041

Triggered

From original Chinese Chapter 2042 · 触发

“Heh. Now it's my turn.”

Fang Heng wondered whether that man could withstand a direct assault from the Yin Vein's aura.

A dark red glint entered his eyes as he hurried down the basement passage and into the underground hall.

Time! Perfect!

He leapt into the room, switched into his necromancy form, and pressed both hands against the magic array on the floor. His mental strength surged into it.

Almost immediately, the entire array within the passage activated and began to rotate.

Outside the hall, Jige had just smashed through the collapsed tunnel and was closing in on the energy he was tracking.

Suddenly, he stopped.

What?

An immense concentration of power was building just ahead—exactly where his target had gone.

Not good!

Alarm flashed through Jige's mind. The energy contained overwhelming spatial and necromantic fluctuations, more than a hundred times stronger than the blood aura he'd sensed outside. Even he felt frightened, and it was still gathering strength.

That brat! What on earth is he doing?

For an instant Jige hesitated between rushing forward to stop him and retreating as far away as possible.

But no. The power was already too strong. He wouldn't be able to escape it.

Forward! His only chance was to stop the man!

Jige charged around the next corner.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Several spikes formed from refined blood shot straight toward him. His surrounding Flames of Absolution slammed into them, and both attacks burst apart in midair.

Jige darted through the blast waves and entered the hall where Fang Heng stood. The sight inside made his expression change drastically.

What?!

A huge dark-blue fissure was opening in the floor.

A Yin Vein fissure?!

Dazzling blue light flooded the hall, followed by an aura of unbearable cold.

BOOM!

In the next instant, Jige was completely swallowed by an expanse of icy blue.

…

Chiboreya game world.

The Holy Court's gathered forces still didn't know that the teleportation passage had been destroyed. They continued retreating deeper into the empire, falling back again and again.

Fortunately, the Brighton Empire was vast enough to provide room for a retreat. A smaller nation would have been overrun long ago.

The zombie army had taken every town and city along its route. Against the horde, urban defenses might as well have been made of paper. The only difference was how quickly they broke.

The zombies surrounded each city and began clawing at the walls. Durability values plummeted until the defenses collapsed.

For the first time, the Holy Court felt completely powerless before the boundless numbers of undead. They couldn't even imagine when the nightmare would end.

How long would it take to wear them down?

The Holy Court's troops were exhausted after continuous fighting, yet the zombie numbers hadn't decreased at all. If anything, there seemed to be more of them!

They could no longer afford to worry only about the undead advancing deep into imperial territory. The greater problem was the front line.

As the zombie army spread, the Holy Court's front was about to be caught between the horde and Lord Dani's main army!

When had everything gone so catastrophically wrong?

Commander-in-Chief Georgi listened to report after report. His scouts estimated that the zombies from the rear would reach the front within a few hours.

Something had to be done.

Ironically, the main confrontation at the front was now the least dire part of the situation. Compared with the undead closing in from behind, Dani's forces seemed manageable.

After several clashes, both sides had tacitly withdrawn for a rest. Neither made another move.

“Commander! News from Duncha!”

A Holy Court god-blessed messenger hurried into the command tent and relayed the report.

A deathly silence followed. Everyone stared at him, speechless with shock.

What? Duncha was under attack by huge numbers of undead?

Worse, the Holy Court forces there suspected that the attackers had used a special passage from the Chiboreya game world.

A game-world passage? Since when had one existed?

For a moment, the commanders were at a complete loss.

…

Merk Temple.

Ji Xiaobo and the two brothers from the Yuan family had stayed beside the magic array, waiting quietly as time passed.

“It's about time.”

They stood and turned toward Ji Xiaobo.

“Good! I'll do it!”

Ji Xiaobo had been waiting for this moment. Without hesitating, he concentrated his mental strength and poured it into the array exactly as Fang Heng had instructed.

Hum—!

The array stirred and drew in energy. Blue light spread across Merk Temple, gradually filling the grooves carved into its stone walls.

Click, click…

The main array slowly began to rotate.

“It worked!”

Once activated, it would operate automatically along the route Fang Heng had set. They only needed to stand back and watch.

Game notices appeared before their eyes.

[Notice: Player has activated Merk's Magic Array (Main).]

[Notice: Merk's Magic Array is operating. Partial transfer of spatial passages in progress…]

[Notice: Transfer successful…]

[Notice: You have completed the team quest, Recreating Merk's Grand Vision.]

There it is!

The instant Yuan Jixian saw the completion notice, his eyes lit up.

“RUN!”

Everyone in the hall reacted and fled Merk Temple without looking back.

BOOOOM!

As they escaped, a pillar of blue light shot from within the temple and soared straight into the sky, piercing the clouds and painting the dark heavens a deep blue.

Then a tide of dark-blue frost erupted from the temple and swept toward them.

Hum—!

The protective barrier built around the temple activated.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

The frigid energy battered the barrier, setting off thunderous explosions. Its entire surface shook violently, as though it might collapse at any moment.

Within seconds, the surrounding temperature had plummeted so sharply that Ji Xiaobo began shivering uncontrollably.

The others instinctively backed away. They had an ominous feeling that the barrier wouldn't hold much longer.
