Chapter 2294

Another Explosion

From original Chinese Chapter 2295 · 炸了

Before the smoke had even cleared, an enormous wooden spike shot diagonally from the shattered Prayer-Curse Temple!

Almost everyone's attention was drawn to the massive dark-red spike!

A figure hung from its tip.

It was Vannis!

Vannis braced his arms against the point, desperately resisting its penetration, but its force kept pushing him higher into the air.

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

The enormous crimson spike suddenly exploded into fragments!

"Pfft!"

Battered by the successive explosions, Vannis couldn't help coughing up a mouthful of blood.

When he looked down again, he saw someone rapidly closing in on him.

Fang Heng!

"You still dare come here to die?!"

Seeing the culprit, Vannis's eyes blazed with hatred. He forcibly suppressed his internal injuries, gathered all his strength, and threw another punch.

BANG!

Fang Heng met Vannis's fist head-on before being hurled backward to disperse the force.

Even in that condition, the man still had this much strength?

"Attack!"

From behind, Federation squad commander Kleiman immediately shouted an order.

The enhanced elite Psychic Studies squads simultaneously unleashed psychic spikes at Vannis!

Vannis, preparing to chase after his successful punch, suddenly lost focus for an instant and felt a piercing pain inside his head.

What?!

The agony was so intense that he instinctively clutched his skull with both hands.

Whoosh!

Taking advantage of that momentary distraction, Fang Heng used the momentum of his backward flight to dive into the dense forest below.

He couldn't beat Vannis.

Not yet, at least.

Unless he used one of his ultimate moves.

But doing so would expose his trump card.

What a shame. If only he'd had more time to prepare...

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes, hesitating over whether to use his ultimate skill, Everlasting Moon, when he noticed cyan vines winding around his feet.

He looked back.

Ian?

Ian stood a short distance away among the trees, forming a hand seal and breathing heavily.

He was in even worse shape than Vannis.

For one thing, Ian was weaker to begin with. And during the temple explosion, he'd expended an enormous amount of mental power shielding his clansmen as they retreated.

As it happened, Ian had seen Vannis knock Fang Heng out of the air. Believing the human was badly injured and close to collapse, he'd rushed to help Vannis finish him off.

Under his mental control, rapidly spreading vines wrapped around Fang Heng's feet.

Got him!

Ian was delighted to see his vines take hold. But in the next instant...

Hiss! Hiss, hiss!

Dense dark-red vines erupted from the temple ruins and raced across the ground toward him.

Both men were almost simultaneously ensnared by the other's vines.

Ian frowned and raised a hand.

Creak, creak...

Fang Heng was pulled inexorably downward by the vines, slowly sinking beneath the earth!

"A sealing skill?"

Fang Heng was about to struggle against the vines when an idea occurred to him. A cold smile appeared on his face.

Ian noticed that smile, and a chill ran through his heart.

What?

He suddenly saw more fine dark-red vines emerging around his own feet, extending toward Fang Heng.

At the same time, the vines gripping Ian's legs began dragging him underground as well.

Those vines?!

Ian realized that the tendrils around his feet were pulling him beneath the ground.

"Elder Ian!"

Nearby Leaf Clan members rushed forward to help, but after only a few steps, crimson vines shot toward them and pinned them in place.

This was bad!

Cold sweat broke out across Ian's forehead.

Not far away, Vannis had just shaken off the humans' psychic spikes. Seeing Fang Heng entangled in Ian's vines and slowly dragged underground, he was delighted with his companion's efforts and hurried forward to help.

But in the next instant, a swarm of Lickers surrounded him from every direction and delayed his advance. Simultaneously, the human psychic specialists launched another psychic assault against his sea of consciousness.

"Aaaagh!"

Vannis screamed in agony.

"Damn humans!"

After two successive psychic attacks, the second proved less effective than the first, and Vannis recovered more quickly. He looked toward the concealed position of the human elite squad, his eyes filled with fury.

He had no time to deal with those pests!

Ignoring the human squad, Vannis charged back toward Fang Heng.

By then, Fang Heng and Ian had both been dragged more than halfway into the ground, with only their upper chests and heads remaining visible.

There was still time!

Vannis accelerated toward them.

But another wave of Lickers immediately blocked his path, while dark-red vines emerged from the ground and trapped his feet.

"Annoying vermin!"

Vannis struggled fiercely to break free, forced to watch as Fang Heng and Ian sank farther under the earth until only their heads remained.

What?

Suddenly, Vannis saw Fang Heng turn toward him and silently mouth a few words.

"See you next time, Vannis."

In the next instant, Vannis's pupils contracted.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

The entire ruined temple erupted in another violent explosion!

This blast was far more powerful than the first!

A shock wave several times stronger surged straight toward him!

With vines still gripping his feet, Vannis couldn't escape immediately. He raised both arms to protect himself from the blast.

BOOM!

The shock wave struck him head-on like tens of thousands of punches. The vines around his feet disintegrated, and the enormous force hurled him backward!

The remaining Lickers and Leaf Clan members were flung away as well!

BANG!

After flying more than twenty meters, Vannis slammed into a great tree, and his Plant Armor shattered into fragments.

As the dust settled, Vannis struggled upright despite his severe injuries and looked toward the temple.

The ruined building had vanished entirely. Only a deep crater remained.

Where was Fang Heng?!

Gone!

No!

What about Ian?

"IAN!"

Vannis shouted his companion's name.

There was no answer.

A terrible premonition seized him, and he immediately closed his eyes.

He couldn't sense him!

Not only was Ian's aura missing, but the Divine Tree Seed's aura had also disappeared completely.

Around him, several Leaf Clan members badly injured by the explosion struggled to their feet and regrouped with Vannis.

"Elder..."

Vannis clenched his teeth and turned toward the distant Federation squad.

Those humans...!

He could sense that the humans who had caused him so much trouble were already retreating rapidly.
