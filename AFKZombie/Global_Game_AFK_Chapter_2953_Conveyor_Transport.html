Chapter 2953

Conveyor Transport

From original Chinese Chapter 2954 · 履带传输

At the Federation's temporary base, Vion had spent several nights watching Fang Heng. He was so tired that he couldn't stop yawning.

There had been no new activity from Fang Heng's side.

And the quieter things were, the more uneasy he became.

Just as he was about to take a break, something changed on the surveillance display.

What was that?

Wayne's players were pushing floating cubes with sides 1.5 meters long onto the loose sand outside, placing them together in a solid layer.

Vion's eyelid twitched. He couldn't make sense of it.

What were they planning? Laying a floor?

Wait!

Why weren't those blocks sinking?

Gravity on this planet was high, and the ground was so loose that ordinary stone would quickly sink into it like quicksand.

"Team leader, you haven't rested for three days. Would you like to take a break? We can watch the monitors. If anything happens..."

"No."

Vion's bloodshot eyes remained fixed on the scene outside. For some reason, he felt a chill.

Three whole hours!

He sat in the control room for three hours, watching Zaka and the other players lay enormous concrete blocks across the sandy ground.

An area the size of an entire football field!

Good grief!

Were they actually reshaping the terrain?!

Where had they acquired so many antigravity blocks?

As the platform expanded, the Zerg creatures began making repeated trips, spitting the collected ore out at the edge of the field.

Zaka separated a group of players to collect the ore and move crate after crate into the underground facility.

Damn it!

Who had said the underground base was only a bluff?

They had definitely built something underground!

Vion turned to his subordinate. "Notify the team chief immediately. I have reason to believe Fang Heng has constructed an underground base!"

"Understood!"

The man hurried off and returned five minutes later. "Sir, the chief has discussed it with the President's Office. We're not to confront Fang Heng. They want us to keep observing for now."

Keep observing?

Observe my ass!

Seeing Vion on the verge of exploding, the subordinate hurriedly explained, "Without a proper starship, the warp passage can only be upgraded to Level 3. Even if Fang Heng submits ore to the Kinsfolk, the amount he can transport will remain limited."

"The chief believes that without our help, Fang Heng and his people can never leave this planet. No matter how much ore they collect, it won't help them. Sooner or later, Fang Heng will come begging for negotiations. We just need to keep watching."

Vion had no immediate answer. After considering it, he scratched his head in frustration.

The reasoning made sense.

But...

Why did he feel so unsure?

Wait!

What was that now?

Some new piece of equipment?!

On the screen, Zaka and his men emerged from the cavern again, this time carrying thick stacks of sheet metal.

Huh?

What was this?

Vion narrowed his eyes and leaned closer to the monitors.

He watched Zaka squat down and carefully fit the metal sheets onto the antigravity blocks.

Click, click, click...

Each block had a prepared slot that fitted the metal sheets almost perfectly.

What the hell? Alloy conveyor belts?

Once installed, the conveyor belts began moving forward, carrying the stones placed on them toward the underground passage.

The soldiers in the control room swallowed nervously at the sight.

Things were looking more and more suspicious.

The base's reconstruction had extended from belowground to the surface.

It felt as though Boss Fang was playing a very big game.

"Chief, calm down..."

Seeing the veins on Vion's forehead pulsing, a subordinate tried to reassure him. "Maybe Fang Heng really has developed something useful. His mining efficiency might improve a little, but he can't sustain it, can he? Without a higher-level warp passage, even if he mines enormous amounts of ore, he can't send it away. He'll still depend on us."

True.

Level 3 was the warp system's limit. No matter how efficient they made extraction, transport, grading, sorting, and refining, what difference would it make?

The ore would still pile up underground with nowhere to go!

"Chief, shouldn't we be happy? The more efficiently Fang Heng mines, the more ore we'll eventually receive, right?"

"Let's hope so."

Vion drew a deep breath, trying to reassure himself.

"Report!"

"What is it?"

"We've detected powerful electromagnetic fluctuations, possibly an electromagnetic storm within the sandstorm. Communications with Federation headquarters may be severely disrupted for several days."

"Report! Headquarters has contacted us. We're ordered to keep a close watch on Fang Heng's movements and not let down our guard."

...

Three days later.

Vion was still in the ship's main control room, watching the outside world through the monitors.

Outside, an area had been cleared and covered with a floor of antigravity blocks.

The predicted sandstorm had arrived.

But it barely affected the base.

Fang Heng's group had used the blocks' energy-conduction system to establish an outer barrier around the entire compound, protecting even the Federation's temporary base.

Roads built from antigravity blocks spread outward in every direction, vanishing into the sandstorm. Conveyor belts on the blocks carried ore toward the underground passage.

Vion could only watch as enormous amounts of ore disappeared underground.

Even by eye, the amount entering the facility each hour could be measured in tons.

Vion was itching to inspect the place and find out what Fang Heng had actually constructed.

Colette, by contrast, was much calmer.

At some point, he appeared beside Vion, stretched, and glanced sideways at the monitors. "What, another sleepless night?"

"Colette, do you honestly think any of this is normal?"

First there had been the underground base, then the antigravity flooring across the surface. Now they had raised a massive defensive barrier around the entire area.

Such a large barrier and conveyor network would consume a staggering amount of energy.

Where was Fang Heng getting it all?

"That's not our problem. The Federation has issued its assessment. We're to wait quietly for support. Until I receive new orders, I won't cooperate with any action you propose."

Colette shrugged and left the control room. "I can leave the watching to you. See you later."

Vion gritted his teeth at the man's complete indifference.
