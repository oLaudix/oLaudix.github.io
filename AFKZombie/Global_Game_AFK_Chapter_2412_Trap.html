Chapter 2412

Trap

From original Chinese Chapter 2413 · 陷阱

“Whoosh!”

All the undead energy in the hall surged rapidly toward Fang Heng!

It flowed so fast that a black vortex formed above his head in moments!

The other players trying to absorb the energy sensed the strange fluctuations, opened their eyes, and stared at the vortex in astonishment.

What the hell!

Who was that powerful?!

Someone was absorbing the entire hall's energy in one go?

Looking closer, the players saw a black-robed figure with Li Shaoqiang and Tan Shuo standing behind him. Their brows furrowed, and they promptly fell silent.

Anyone capable of swallowing so much undead energy in one breath couldn't possibly be weak!

And Tan Shuo and Li Shaoqiang…

Weren't they from the Federation?

Better not provoke them.

After weighing the consequences, the players decided to pretend they hadn't noticed anything and concentrate on absorbing whatever scraps of undead energy remained. Taking advantage of the opportunity to earn a little was what mattered.

Nearby, Norbert divided his attention between absorbing the energy and watching Fang Heng. When he saw how quickly Fang Heng was drawing it in, surprise flashed across his face.

He could absorb it that fast?

But the surprise soon turned into a sneer of secret satisfaction.

Good, good! Go ahead and absorb it!

Think of it as your last meal!

Norbert sneered inwardly, reached out, and secretly crushed the first shard hidden in his palm.

…

Meanwhile, outside the underground palace, pendants worn by several Divine Realm Sin Cleansers attacking Katantul began to glow faintly.

They exchanged glances.

“It's a message from Norbert.”

“Looks like Fang Heng has entered the underground palace. Report to the Divine Adjudicator immediately. We'll prepare to act.”

“Yes!”

In the Divine Realm, Okney and his companions opened their eyes after learning where Fang Heng was. A sharp gleam passed through their gazes.

“Fang Heng has taken the bait. Make preparations to descend into Punoze at any moment.”

“Yes!”

…

Inside the underground sealing hall.

Norbert kept a close, furtive watch on Fang Heng.

Fang Heng!

He probably couldn't imagine that the Holy Court had expended such enormous effort to invade Punoze for the real purpose of setting a trap to capture him!

And the enemy the Holy Court treated with such extraordinary caution was merely a player!

Norbert both admired and feared Fang Heng.

In fact, Norbert had secretly aligned himself with the Holy Court a long time ago, becoming one of the pieces it had planted inside the Undead Faction.

This time, the Holy Court had been willing to expose even him to deal with Fang Heng.

As everyone absorbed the undead energy scattered through the hall, the clay jars that temporarily stored it burst one after another. Visible streams of gray undead energy spilled out and were swiftly divided among Fang Heng and the others.

Five minutes later.

Everyone opened their eyes again.

Many of the players glanced in Fang Heng's direction.

Good grief! Had it all been absorbed in less than three minutes?

Of the vast amount of undead energy in the chamber, more than 99 percent had been taken by that mysterious man!

If any of them had tried to absorb that much on their own, it would have taken ten days or half a month at least.

[Hint: Grandmaster-Tier Undead Studies has advanced to LV: 6.]

Fang Heng glanced at the notification, smacked his lips, and thought it was a decent result.

One level was worth almost half a month's hard work!

However…

Fang Heng used his mental power to examine his Sea of Consciousness again.

At first he hadn't noticed anything special while absorbing the undead energy. But as he took in more, he gradually sensed something different about the energy here.

There was a faint trace of icy power mixed into it.

Still, it hadn't affected his body at all, and the game notifications confirmed that his Undead Studies experience was increasing.

A closer examination revealed nothing unusual.

Perhaps he'd been worrying unnecessarily.

Fang Heng shook his head.

Punoze was closely connected to the Yin Vein. Perhaps, for some reason, its undead energy simply carried a measure of frost affinity.

It wasn't a major problem.

Raising his head, Fang Heng scanned the hall again.

He'd already noticed something odd about the sealed pillar, but it had never occurred to him that this might be a Holy Court trap. He only felt that coming here had been worthwhile.

“Norbert, what next?”

The undead energy in the hall had now been absorbed completely.

The magic arrays continued operating, and thin wisps of energy still drifted out of the sealed stone pillar.

Norbert swallowed carefully.

There had been a slight change to the plan.

Originally, they had lured Fang Heng here expecting him to absorb the energy. That step was meant to lower his guard while weakening the power of the outer protective magic array.

But Norbert hadn't anticipated how quickly Fang Heng would act!

He'd absorbed the outer layer's energy in just three minutes!

A faint sense of foreboding rose in Norbert's heart.

Perhaps the operation wouldn't go as smoothly as they'd hoped.

“Norbert? What's wrong? Why aren't you saying anything? Did you notice a problem?”

“N-no. Nothing.”

Under everyone's watchful eyes, Norbert steadied himself and took a few steps toward the sealed pillar in the center, examining its condition.

Only the timing was a little off.

It wasn't a big problem.

The Holy Court's trap had a generous margin for error.

Norbert forced himself to stay calm. Examining the large magic array beneath the pillar, he continued, “The undead mentors placed an outer magic seal around it. Our next task is to wear that seal away.”

“Be careful. As the outer layer weakens, the inner sacred seal will gradually be released. Especially around the middle stages, its power might erupt suddenly. Everyone must be extremely cautious.”

The players crowded closer and urged him to begin.

“All right. We don't know how much longer the Undead Faction can hold out. Let's get started!”

“Come on!”

Working together, they slowly channeled mental power into the magic array and carefully disrupted the outer array's operation.

Fang Heng looked up at the array engraved on the ceiling.

Wasn't this all far too slow…?

Was there really any need for everyone to be so careful?

Fang Heng didn't suspect Norbert of anything. He simply assumed the man didn't understand magic arrays very well.

It was a matter of ignorance, nothing more.

In Fang Heng's view, the array placed around the sealed pillar by the undead mentors served mainly to protect and monitor it.

If something happened to the diamond-shaped pillar at its core, the undead mentors would sense the problem immediately, while the outer array would provide a measure of temporary protection.
