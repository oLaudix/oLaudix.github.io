Chapter 2208

The Divided Tree Spirits

From original Chinese Chapter 2209 · 分裂

“The Nature's Heart Crystals we sought in the Elder Council are used to infuse power into the Divine Trees. As a tree grows, its energy requirements increase, and external power must be supplied to make up the difference.”

“Divine Tree Seed Essence, on the other hand, is inherited from the previous ancient Divine Tree. It can grant the new tree immense power and greatly increase its strength. Traditionally, the seed essence is only bestowed after the new Divine Tree has been selected.”

Senzo repeated gravely, “That is why we absolutely cannot let outsiders take it!”

Cultivating Divine Trees…

So that was it.

Fang Heng nodded inwardly as he listened.

The information Sandy had recovered from the records was accurate.

All the major clans were cultivating Divine Trees of their own.

Just then, another main quest notification appeared on Fang Heng's retina.

[Hint: The player has triggered the main quest—The Divided Tree Spirit Race.]

Quest name: The Divided Tree Spirit Race.

Quest description: Traditionally, when a generation's Divine Tree weakens, the Tree Spirits choose their leader through a contest of Divine Tree cultivation. The player must select a Tree Spirit clan, gain its recognition, join its faction, and help it become the next leader of the race.

Requirement 1: Gain recognition from a clan, select and join that clan's faction, and help it win the Divine Tree cultivation contest.

Requirement 2: Help the chosen clan obtain the orthodox inheritance of the Tree Spirit race.

Requirement 3: Raise Tree Spirit favorability to 2,000 and favorability with the chosen clan to its maximum.

Requirement 4: Accumulate 50 million Tree Spirit contribution points and 50 million contribution points with the chosen clan.

Requirement 5: Obtain the 'Grand Elder' inheritance within the chosen clan.

Quest reward: Completing all quest branches increases main quest completion by 30%.

“So that's how it works…”

Between Senzo's explanation and the game prompt, Fang Heng finally understood the broad outline.

The difficulty of the early and middle stages of the main quest had been finding the Tree Spirits, gaining their trust, opening the passage to the Inner World despite the Dependents' hostility, and helping the Tree Spirits return there.

But in the late game, with the Dependents drastically weakened and the Tree Spirits back in the Inner World, the story became an all-out internal struggle between the clans.

Players would have to choose one of the clans and help it reunify the entire Tree Spirit race.

Ordinarily, the late-game quest would likely have involved numerous player factions joining different clans, fighting one another until the strongest clan emerged with player support.

But because Fang Heng had completed the early and middle quests so rapidly, no other players had even entered this game world. For now, he was the only player faction involved.

Fang Heng frowned.

Troublesome.

Now he understood why the game had repeatedly given clear hints from the beginning that he should follow the Tree Spirit faction.

It had been laying the groundwork for the middle and late-game quests all along.

And the difficulty wasn't merely helping a clan win and seize control of the race. He also needed enormous amounts of favorability and contribution points.

Given the Tree Spirits' temperaments, how was he supposed to accomplish that?!

Not to mention that his favorability with the entire race was currently in the negative.

As for the Leaf Clan…

He'd offended them pretty badly too.

What a trap!

All right.

There was some good news.

At least he finally had a reasonably clear understanding of the game's objectives.

Of course, several mysteries remained.

For instance, why had the Tree Spirits suddenly vanished? Why had the passage to the Inner World been severed? And where had each clan obtained its Divine Tree to cultivate?

For the moment, though, those questions no longer seemed quite as important.

“Fang Heng, the Divine Tree Seed Essence is vital to us. There's bound to be a fierce battle shortly. Come with us.”

[Hint: The player has triggered a side quest—Protect the Divine Tree Seed Essence.]

Quest name: Protect the Divine Tree Seed Essence.

Quest requirements: With your help, Vannis has obtained a Divine Tree Seed Essence. He is now surrounded by the other clans and Stone Spirit Puppets. Assist the Leaf Clan in rescuing Vannis and escorting both him and the essence out of the ruined Tree Spirit Elder Council.

Quest reward: Leaf Clan favorability, Leaf Clan reputation, and Tree Spirit reputation.

Fang Heng sighed inwardly as he read the new quest.

What a headache.

The Leaf Clan, at least, was hardly an ideal partner.

Their favorability kept dropping for no apparent reason. Despite everything he'd done for them, they hadn't even invited him to join their faction…

And a difficult quest like this only rewarded a little favorability and reputation?

So they expected him to work for free?

Fang Heng turned to Senzo. “Grand Elder Senzo, do you remember what you promised me earlier… about advancing my Nature Studies inheritance?”

His meaning was perfectly clear.

They could discuss the next job afterward.

How about settling the previous debt first?

Senzo looked even more displeased. “Fang Heng, this is an emergency. We have no time to help you advance your Nature Studies.”

“Come with us first. If we fail to rescue Vannis and the seed essence falls into another clan's hands, the entire Leaf Clan may become subordinate to them. In that case, the ceremony we promised might not be possible at all. Think carefully!”

Just then, a Leaf Clan member hurried over to report. “Grand Elder, we're ready. We can depart at any time.”

“Mm.”

Senzo nodded and hurried away, surrounded by his clansmen.

[Hint: The player's favorability with the Leaf Clan has decreased by 1 point.]

[Hint: The player's favorability with the Leaf Clan has reached a critical threshold. Please exercise caution.]

Fang Heng watched Senzo go, narrowing his eyes.

Heh.

The Tree Spirits were threatening him?

Withholding his reward?

They were even trying to renege on a reward acknowledged by the game itself?

Was this how they treated him?

His suspicions had been right. Working with the Leaf Clan was a terrible choice.

He'd be better off choosing a more suitable partner from among the other Tree Spirit clans.

So be it.

That was enough for today.

He had no intention of joining the mission to rescue Vannis either.

After all, he was still carrying two Divine Tree Seed Essences.

The blood surrounding the seeds was like a balloon being inflated over and over.

As the natural power they released was compressed, the pressure on that balloon kept building.

It was manageable for now, but who knew what would happen if he delayed much longer?

He needed to get back quickly.

Better than having an accident.

With that in mind, Fang Heng decided to leave ahead of everyone else.

— End of Chapter 2208 —
