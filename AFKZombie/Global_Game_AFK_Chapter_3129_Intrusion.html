Chapter 3129

Intrusion

From Chinese Chapter 3130 · 闯入

This time, Fang Heng intended to infiltrate the Kinsfolk's corporate headquarters and spread black mist.

If he was going to do it, he might as well make a big mess.

It would help him vent his anger, too.

In his spirit form, Fang Heng slipped into the Kinsfolk headquarters once again.

The building's exterior surfaces and interior walls had undergone special reinforced modifications, making them impossible to penetrate directly with spatial abilities. But there appeared to be no measures for detecting spiritual forms.

Fang Heng entered easily as a spirit, took the elevator to the third floor, and found an empty conference room.

After advancing to Demigod-level Alchemy, he'd developed a deeper understanding of alchemical-array construction, significantly improving his efficiency when setting them up.

What had once taken nearly three hours now took only half as long. He could even attach an alchemical array directly to empty space without using any external support.

But Fang Heng had a more straightforward method.

He raised a hand, and several alchemical stone tablets flew out of his backpack, assembling themselves together in midair.

“Whoosh!!”

The reflected runes of an alchemical magic array appeared in the pupil of Fang Heng's right eye.

The array began rotating slowly in midair, allowing thin wisps of black energy to leak out.

Late that night.

Most of the lower floors of the Kinsfolk headquarters had already shut down for the night.

Suddenly, a surge of black mist poured from beneath a conference-room door on the third floor!

The detection prisms installed throughout the building immediately flashed with dangerous red warning signals.

“Ring, ring, ring!!!”

The black-mist alarm had been triggered.

The control room inside Kinsfolk headquarters immediately lit up with warning messages.

The black mist again!

After more than a month, it had reappeared.

And this time, it was right inside the Kinsfolk headquarters!

The building's internal security teams mobilized at once, heading toward the source of the black mist on the third floor.

Edmond, the head of headquarters security, was a formidable high-tier warrior recruited by the Kinsfolk. Upon receiving the alert, he immediately led an elite team to the third floor.

By then, other security squads and warriors still in the building had also arrived. They were beginning to explore deeper into the third-floor corridors, toward the spreading mist.

“Captain, we sealed off the third floor and searched the building as soon as the alarm sounded. So far, we haven't found any signs of Ouroboros insects.”

Edmond felt slightly reassured and asked, “Are the lords upstairs safe?”

Several direct Kinsfolk superiors had recently been staying inside the building. Edmond's main duty was not merely protecting the premises, but, more importantly, ensuring the safety of those high-ranking Kinsfolk.

“The mist is mainly spreading from the third floor. Nothing has been detected upstairs, so there shouldn't be a problem.”

Edmond didn't dare take chances. He nodded. “Security can handle things down here. You keep watch downstairs. Everyone else, follow me upstairs. We need to make sure the lords are safe.”

“Yes!”

Edmond had just made his assessment and taken a few steps forward with his trusted guards when he suddenly sensed something. Frowning, he glanced back down the corridor.

For a brief instant, he'd felt as though something was over there. But when he looked carefully, no one was visible. Only a few warriors hurried past along the corridor and disappeared into the black mist ahead.

“Captain? What's wrong?”

Edmond didn't answer. He merely stared fixedly down the passage.

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

The next instant, thin ceiling ducts above the black-mist-filled corridor on the third floor burst apart, and several brown, spiny-skinned Lickers leapt out from above.

The Lickers immediately sprang forward!

“Insects!!”

Spotting the creatures breaking through the mist, several warriors rushed forward to intercept them.

“Captain?”

“Mm. Nothing. Let's go upstairs.”

Probably just his imagination.

Edmond shook his head. He must have been so nervous that he'd misread the sensation. Turning away, he led the others into the elevator to check on the high-ranking Kinsfolk upstairs.

At that very moment, Fang Heng was following the group from behind. He watched the elevator's floor numbers rise rapidly until they stopped on one of the upper levels.

A high-tier warrior's perception could already reach the level of sensing laws. Even if they couldn't actually see a spirit, they could detect one that came within a certain distance.

Edmond had noticed his presence earlier precisely because he'd gotten too close.

He simply couldn't see Fang Heng with his naked eyes.

Fang Heng stopped tailing him at close range and followed Edmond's group from farther behind.

He'd originally intended to cause some destruction inside Kinsfolk headquarters. But after hearing that high-ranking Kinsfolk officials were also in the building, he'd changed his mind.

Rather than causing a little damage that barely hurt them, he could take things a step further.

For example, he could exploit the chaos to kill a few Kinsfolk.

…

“Ding-dong!”

The elevator stopped on an upper floor.

Edmond hurried toward the rooms occupied by the Kinsfolk officials, his guards following close behind.

Turning a corner, he saw several fellow warriors still guarding the corridor entrance outside the hall where the Kinsfolk executives were staying.

Edmond breathed a sigh of relief.

Nothing seemed amiss.

Before he could ask, two warriors guarding the executives spoke first. “Edmond, what's happening downstairs? Sounds like quite a commotion.”

“It's the game's black mist. We detected it on the third floor, though we don't know why it's appeared. Just like last time, Ouroboros Seeds appeared with it. Security is dealing with things downstairs, but I was worried about the Kinsfolk officials, so I came to check.”

“Tch. Those disgusting bugs again.” Just thinking of the insects made the two warriors feel sick. “Everything's safe here. We're guarding the place. Nothing will happen.”

While they were talking, one member of the security team suddenly noticed something and stared in surprise at the corner behind Edmond.

What was that?

“Captain, that…”

Everyone turned to follow his gaze. A black aura had appeared from around the corner behind them.

The game's black mist!?

But it looked different from the black mist they'd encountered before.

The game's mist ordinarily did little more than block electromagnetic signals at high concentrations and slightly interfere with perception. This strange mist, however, was clearly aggressive, severely disrupting their ability to sense and investigate their surroundings.

Was it some evolved form of the game's black mist?

“Stay alert!”

The team instantly went on guard, taking up defensive positions before the entrance to the room where the Kinsfolk were resting.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!”

More than a dozen brown insect-like creatures abruptly burst from the sinister undead mist.

“Insects!” Edmond's expression shifted. He barked, “Stop them!”

As soon as he spoke, two high-tier warriors beside him gathered mental power. A dense barrage of elemental ice-soul attacks immediately swept toward the charging creatures!
