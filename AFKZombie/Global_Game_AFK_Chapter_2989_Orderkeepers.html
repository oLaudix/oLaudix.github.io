Chapter 2989

Orderkeepers

From Chinese Chapter 2990 · 秩序者

Escape!

She might look only a little weakened, but Mu Qingxin knew she had suffered a grievous injury.

Her soul and will were on the verge of shattering.

Run for her life!

Simply surviving would be the best possible outcome now.

“Everyone, it's our turn! Don't hold back—attack together!”

“Right.”

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!

The Undead Council mentors had been waiting in readiness. They all moved to block Mu Qingxin's escape route, sending dense soul chains stretching toward her!

The council mentors' target had always been Mu Qingxin, just as the plan specified.

The moment she tried to flee or move completely outside the Eye of Stars attack range, they would intervene.

Against an utterly exhausted Mu Qingxin, the council's old masters might not be able to kill her themselves, but they could certainly prevent her escape!

Black soul chains formed a huge net and swept toward Mu Qingxin.

With her soul already severely damaged, Mu Qingxin instinctively felt a chill at the sight of the soul chains. She didn't dare force her way through, and the space available to her quickly shrank.

The warriors of the four Nether Clan empires also knew the war had reached its final moment and sealed off the entire surrounding area.

The whole Death Realm had been pushed to the brink of collapse.

And she was the culprit!

The Nether Clan would never let Mu Qingxin leave.

Seeing layer after layer of soul chains ahead, Mu Qingxin knew she couldn't dodge them. She gritted her teeth, preparing for one desperate last fight.

Whoosh!

Suddenly her body trembled and froze in a staggered state.

A cold voice sounded behind her.

“Divine Punishment!”

“BOOM!!!”

Purple light crashed down from above and struck Mu Qingxin mercilessly!

Not far away, Sura watched coldly, displeasure in her eyes.

First Proust, and now Mu Qingxin.

Both were going to die by Fang Heng's hand.

“Mu Qingxin, you've made a mistake. Your bond with the Seed you cultivated grew too deep. You still had the duty to nurture the successors of our species. You should never have died here...”

Under the relentless Divine Punishment barrage, Mu Qingxin's HP finally ran out.

BOOM!

Roaring sacred fire engulfed her, burning until nothing remained.

Whoosh!

A cocoon rose from the ashes and flew into Fang Heng's palm, where a black fissure opened and swallowed it completely.

[Game Hint: You have killed the Ouroboros Seed Cultivator—Mu Qingxin.]

[Game Hint: You have absorbed an Ouroboros Seed. Your current Ouroboros Seed level has risen to LV:25. You have gained 1 additional optional modification.]

Was it over?

The Nether Clan watched Fang Heng still standing in the middle of the battlefield, disbelief in their eyes.

The legendary, terrifying Calamity.

The Calamity that had nearly swallowed the entire Death Realm.

Had it finally been destroyed?

Fang Heng approached the Undead Council mentors and bowed respectfully. “Thank you, Mentors, for your assistance.”

The Undead Council might have shown weakness in its dealings with the Holy Court, but it had always treated him well.

When he'd suggested dealing with Mu Qingxin, the council had offered its full support without hesitation.

Chairman Georgi had even come personally with the council's most powerful combatants.

“There's no need for such courtesy between us. With Mu Qingxin dead, the Calamity has been removed, and the Death Realm can return to peace.” Georgi waved his hand. “We've done a good deed for the Death Realm too.”

In fact, the senior council mentors had made extensive preparations for their battle with the Cultivator.

They had drawn up all manner of contingency plans.

In the worst case, they had even considered using divine artifacts to temporarily hold off the Calamity while evacuating Fang Heng first.

Not one member of the council had predicted that Fang Heng would dispose of Mu Qingxin so smoothly.

None of the fallback plans had been needed.

Who could have imagined it?

Even the Empress of the Death Realm hadn't been able to finish off the Ouroboros Seed, yet Fang Heng had destroyed it with his own hands.

The rulers of the four empires looked at Fang Heng with similarly complicated emotions.

Caution, fear, respect, and gratitude.

The Gabu Empire's ruler stepped forward first. “Fang Heng, thank you for your help. Now that the source of the Calamity is gone, we'll continue clearing away its remnants. Your support and friendship will be remembered by the Nether Clan for generations.”

[Game Hint: You have killed an Ouroboros Seed and successfully stopped the spread of the Calamity. You gain Death Realm reputation. Your deeds are being passed from person to person among the Nether Clan.]

[Game Hint: You have obtained a special status: Savior of the Death Realm (duration: 3 months). While this status remains active, your favorability, reputation, and support among the Nether Clan and within the Death Realm will continue to rise substantially.]

Fang Heng checked the game notifications.

Not bad at all!

An unexpected gain.

With the Savior of the Death Realm buff, once the news spread over the next few days, he might push his approval across the Death Realm all the way to 100%.

Then he could smoothly complete his Nether Lord advancement quest.

Perhaps it would even open a way to advance his divine-level Necromancy further!

“Fang Heng.”

Fang Heng turned to Sura beside him.

“Congratulations. The matter is settled. According to our agreement, it's time for me to leave.”

“Of course. Good luck.”

Suddenly remembering something, Fang Heng stopped Sura as she was about to go. “One more thing, Sura.”

“What is it?”

“Have you heard of gods?”

Gods!

Sura's eyelid twitched.

“Gods call themselves the Orderkeepers of this world. Their power is immense. They destroyed us last time. But as I told you, the Ouroboros Seed will never truly perish. The next time we return, we'll be stronger. So you've done well, Fang Heng, but I can sense your strength is still insufficient.”

Sura turned away and waved a hand. “Goodbye. I hope you'll surprise me again the next time we meet.”

Fang Heng watched her walk away and lowered his head in thought.

Strange.

According to the Kinsfolk, they were the ones who defeated the Ouroboros Seed last time.

But from Sura's account, it sounded as though the Seeds believed the gods had been the ones to destroy them.

Or had Sura been so badly injured that her memories were incomplete or distorted?

Fang Heng scratched his head.

Gods.

The last time he'd heard about them was from stone tablets in the Ancient Human ruins. The Titans had apparently been threatened by gods and spread their bloodline far and wide. Now Sura was saying the gods had destroyed the Ouroboros Seeds too?

Gods? Orderkeepers?

What the hell were they, exactly?

Come to think of it, the term ‘Main God Game’ had been around for a long time.

Were the game worlds created by gods?

Forget it.

The clues he had were still too scattered to fit into a coherent picture.

Fang Heng shifted his attention back to his Ouroboros Seed skill.

With its advancement to LV:25, he now had six whole optional modification branches!

— End of Chapter 2989 —
