Chapter 2990

Tier

From Chinese Chapter 2991 · 级别

Doing the math, Burrowing, Biological Cannon, and Fungal Carpet Modification each occupied one modification branch slot. Division and the newly acquired Red-Worm Modification each took up two slots.

He was still just a little short.

Burrowing and Biological Cannon complemented each other extremely well and were useful in more situations. For now, he could only set Fungal Carpet Modification aside.

What annoyed him was that he was running short of Main God points again.

Even the one million points he'd just gained from the kills were a drop in the bucket, barely easing the immediate shortage.

He needed to find another opportunity to grind some more.

“Fang Heng, we must take our leave as well.”

After reorganizing their forces, the commanders of the four imperial armies came to say farewell.

“The source of the Calamity has been eliminated. We'll return to our respective empires now and hope to restore peace to the Death Realm as quickly as possible.”

Looking at the confident Nether Clan rulers, Fang Heng sighed inwardly.

He'd already taken away the Death Realm Jewel. This world probably wasn't going to recover.

They would be better off returning to the new world with him.

“Very well. I won't keep you. If any of you wish to enter the new world, you're always welcome.”

“Thank you for your help, Fang Heng. Your name will be recorded in our imperial histories.”

After thanking him again, the imperial delegations departed with their Nether Clan armies.

Chen Yu came over. “Boss Fang, our ships are ready to warp, and the fleet is preparing to leave. Will you return to the Dust Realm with us to continue mining?”

Every minute spent in the Death Realm consumed huge amounts of the ships' energy to resist its atmosphere. Now that the operation was over, the fleet intended to depart immediately and return through the Kinsfolk system to mine resources in the Dust Realm.

Fang Heng thought for a moment and shook his head. “No need. You go ahead. I still have some matters to take care of, and I'll join you after they're settled.”

“Understood.”

...

“Is that so... Mu Qingxin. What a pity.”

Xia Xi frowned slightly when she sensed that Fang Heng had killed both Mu Qingxin and the newborn she'd cultivated.

In her opinion, Fang Heng was nowhere near strong enough to kill those two outright.

He must have relied on some outside power.

And Mu Qingxin and Proust had both been gravely wounded, with less than a tenth of their original strength remaining.

Even so, the fact that Fang Heng had found them in the Death Realm and defeated them both meant his rate of growth had surpassed all her expectations.

“Master, Mu Qingxin is dead. Within the black-fog worlds, I'm afraid no one remains who can threaten Fang Heng.”

“I understand what you mean. Humans are lazy creatures. They need to remain hungry at all times. Only then will they fight desperately to compete and acquire more...”

Xia Xi nodded in agreement. “Since he has already broken through the Lock, it's time to remove the barrier completely and give him some fresh pressure. Lais, I'm leaving this to you.”

Fear flickered in Lais's eyes. “My lady, isn't that a little too aggressive? If the barrier is completely broken... I'm afraid the entire world will be threatened.”

“Wouldn't that be perfect?”

“Yes!”

Lais shuddered inwardly, acknowledged the order, and vanished into the night.

Xia Xi gazed into the distance. “Sura, it's been a long time. I didn't expect you to find this place.”

Sura gradually emerged from the shadows behind her. “And I never expected that interesting little fellow was the one you cultivated. When you and the others planned to use the black fog's power to nurture Seeds, I thought you were dreaming of the impossible.”

Sura chuckled self-mockingly and spread her hands. “Well, it turns out you were right and I was the shortsighted one. You really are a lunatic.”

“Thank you. I'll take that as a compliment.”

“But isn't this step a little hasty?” Sura approached her. “If the Titans' protection is destroyed, the danger facing this world will multiply. And as far as I know, she's already halfway to succeeding.”

Xia Xi's expression shifted. “You mean her?”

“Exactly. She's even more aggressive than you. She chose to cultivate and pass on her newborn through the body of a god.”

“Interesting. But what of it?” Xia Xi pursed her lips and looked into the distance, murmuring, “Fang Heng is the one I chose. Even if the gods stand in his way, I'll slaughter them all.”

...

Forty-seven days later.

The transformed Death Realm.

Before the Death Realm Jewel altar.

The reincarnation passages leading to the Divine Realm and Hell had been completed.

Souls shuffling like walking corpses followed the Jewel's guidance, slowly entering the two passages to be reborn.

Fang Heng sat cross-legged before the altar.

Around him, twelve Netherworld Pearls representing the Death Realm's power gradually condensed and floated in the air.

Until the last pearl was fully formed.

[Game Hint: Verifying... You are currently in control of the Death Realm Jewel.]

[Game Hint: Nether Clan support has reached 100%.]

[Game Hint: Unification of the Death Realm is complete.]

[Game Hint: Netherworld Pearls successfully condensed: 12/12.]

[Game Hint: Quest completed—Advancement of the Nether Lord.]

[Game Hint: Your Divine-Level Necromancy has advanced to Super-Divine-Level Necromancy (Unique).]

Skill: Super-Divine-Level Necromancy (Order Level) (Unique).

Current Level: LV:27.

Skill Description: Following the path once traveled by the Empress, you earned the Nether Clan's recognition, unified the Death Realm, and became its new ruler. This is not the end. You must maintain the Death Realm's order and expand its influence as far as possible to comprehend the true power of death.

Additional Attribute Description 1: Necromancy skills no longer require the Book of the Dead and gain an additional 500% effect. All basic attributes +120. Damage taken from Necromancy-attribute attacks is reduced by 95%.

Additional Attribute Description 2: Gain Will equal to the current skill level ×80 and Mental Strength equal to the current skill level ×40. Your lifespan is extended, and your soul strength is greatly increased.

Additional Attribute Description 3: If you die inside a black-fog zone, your soul automatically enters the Death Realm's reincarnation region and is reborn. If reincarnation succeeds, it does not count as death: equipment and items will not drop, and no death penalties apply.

Additional Note: This skill has no upper limit, and its level is not restricted by the player's own level cap. You can raise it by spreading the black-fog-covered area, thereby expanding the Death Realm's influence.

Whoosh!!

Fang Heng suddenly opened his eyes, and the sharp light within them gradually faded.

Soon after he eliminated the Calamity a month and a half earlier, Nether Clan support had reached 100%.

Likewise, once the Death Realm Jewel was transferred into the Vampire Apocalypse, the game designated that world as the new Death Realm, so the objective to gain control of the Death Realm was completed as well.

That triggered the final task: condense twenty-four Netherworld Pearls.

— End of Chapter 2990 —
