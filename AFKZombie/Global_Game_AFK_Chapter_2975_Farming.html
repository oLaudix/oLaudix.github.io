Chapter 2975

Farming

From Chinese Chapter 2976 · 刷

At those words, representatives of every race on the ship's deck turned toward Fang Heng.

The fleet captains wore lofty, superior expressions, eager to see what their newest ally could do.

Giselle and the other Western Federation players also looked toward Fang Heng, feeling somewhat nervous.

They'd witnessed Boss Fang's strength before.

Extremely powerful.

Terrifying, even.

But...

Their opponents were monsters from an SSS-grade insect nest, and they were frighteningly powerful too!

Giselle had already realized that the Kinsfolk Alliance was a place where the strong devoured the weak.

If Fang Heng performed poorly, humanity would have to bow its head before the other races from now on.

Only when he heard the question did Fang Heng snap out of his thoughts. He turned toward the crocodile who'd asked and immediately brightened.

What?

Such a wonderful opportunity had fallen into his lap?!

He'd been watching the more than thirty Ouroboros Seeds on the battlefield gradually lose ground and get picked off by the different races' squads, and he'd been itching to join in.

Unfortunately, the alliance had previously stated that humanity wasn't to interfere during the final battle. He'd been trying to think of an excuse to enter the fighting.

Who would've guessed the crocodile would offer him an opening on a silver platter?

He couldn't have asked for anything better!

“Well, in that case...”

Fang Heng could barely contain his smile. He stepped out of the crowd, flexed his wrists, looked at the gathered aliens, and nodded. “I have been itching for a fight. Since you've invited me, it would be rude to refuse.”

“Whoosh!”

Without giving the other races a chance to take back their invitation, Fang Heng lightly pushed off the ground and shot toward the battlefield.

The assembled aliens looked startled.

Good grief.

Every other race sent an entire squad into battle, but humanity had sent its fleet's commander in chief alone?

Hmm?

The Shadow Clan captain felt a faint stir of suspicion.

On the battlefield, an Ouroboros Seed close to Fang Heng sensed his aura. It immediately abandoned its original target and pursued him instead.

Was this some kind of taunt skill?

In an instant, the three nearest Ouroboros Seeds converged on Fang Heng and attacked.

Facing the nearest aggregate, Fang Heng simply threw a punch.

“Bang!!!!”

A dull, heavy impact rang out.

The Ouroboros Seed shattered instantly before Fang Heng, exploding into a cloud of minced flesh!

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

Fang Heng darted toward the remaining two Seeds and threw two more punches in rapid succession.

The other two incoming Ouroboros Seeds burst apart in midair one after the other.

So strong!

Aboard the ships, observers from the various races gasped.

What tremendous physical strength!

In the blink of an eye, three Ouroboros Seed aggregates had been smashed to pieces by Fang Heng without any chance to resist!

Some aliens who weren't skilled in close combat hadn't even seen how he'd moved. They'd simply watched the Seeds explode in midair.

That brief instant had already been enough to demonstrate Fang Heng's strength!

The Shadow Clan and other high-tier allied races watched Fang Heng's movements, quietly evaluating him.

Humanity was widely distributed throughout the worlds. This particular human's strength was indeed beyond what they'd expected.

But if that was all he had...

It wasn't enough.

“Hiss, hiss, hiss...”

The flesh of the aggregates Fang Heng had shattered fell to the ground and began wriggling together, attempting to restore their humanoid forms.

Fang Heng landed and turned his gaze toward a mass of flesh on his right that was rapidly gathering and regenerating. He raised a hand.

Blood Control!

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!!!”

The temperature of the Ouroboros Seed's flesh soared instantly. Within moments, the mass was boiling, and a layer of white mist rose from the regenerating tissue!

The scattered pieces couldn't complete their reassembly. They twisted and writhed frantically, hissing as though being burned.

Blood Explosion!

“Bang! Bang, bang, bang!!!”

The flesh exploded again, breaking into even smaller fragments, which fell to the ground and immediately began gathering once more.

Fang Heng stared at the flesh on the ground.

Under the repeated explosions and consumption caused by his Blood Control, the Seed's health had rapidly fallen to 1%. Yet that final 1% stubbornly refused to disappear.

It seemed vampire power alone couldn't eliminate these creatures completely.

“Hmph!”

Then he'd try this move!

Fang Heng gave a cold snort, and his aura suddenly changed. A wave of divine power erupted from his body.

Divine Punishment!

“Boom!!!”

A bolt of purplish-red lightning suddenly descended from the clouded sky and slammed into the writhing flesh on the ground.

The Ouroboros Seed aggregate burst into flames. Fierce sacred fire engulfed it, releasing an intense burnt stench as its final 1% health rapidly fell to zero.

Success!

Seeing this, Fang Heng turned his wrist forward.

From the charred pieces of flesh, a tiny Ouroboros Seed cocoon flew into his palm.

Another shattered cocoon.

Small as it was, it could still provide a fair amount of Ouroboros Seed skill experience.

Fang Heng turned his head and extended his hand toward another aggregate on the ground that was rapidly gathering itself together.

“Boom! Boom!!”

Two consecutive lightning bolts fell.

The wriggling fragments of flesh on the ground once again ignited with sacred fire.

Easy.

Sacred Studies exerted a powerful restraining effect on Ouroboros Seeds.

After dealing with three Seeds in succession and collecting three cocoons, Fang Heng quickly scanned the battlefield and charged toward the next Ouroboros Seed aggregate.

Keep going!

Next!

Aboard the ship, the Kinsfolk Alliance races fell silent at the sight of Fang Heng so effortlessly disposing of three Ouroboros Seeds.

Beyond his formidable physical strength, humanity had no shortage of offensive techniques!

In comparison, even the Shadow Clan didn't seem to have such an easy time handling Ouroboros Seeds.

Beautifully done!

Captain Giselle and the other players kept their faces composed, but inwardly couldn't help cheering.

Hmm?

As they continued watching, the crocodile representatives frowned slightly.

What was going on?

On the battlefield, Fang Heng showed no intention of stopping after eliminating three aggregates. Instead, he continued pursuing the nearest one!

The Ouroboros Seeds seemed affected by some unknown taunting force. They abandoned their current targets and rushed toward Fang Heng to attack!

“Bang! Bang! Bang, bang!”

Not one Ouroboros Seed construct could withstand even a single punch from Fang Heng.

Once shattered into flesh, they were rapidly purified by some unknown method.

Ever since Fang Heng joined the battlefield, the Ouroboros Seed aggregates had been disappearing at an astonishing pace.

Was that brat really fighting this hard?

Surely he wasn't...

Farming insect cocoons?
