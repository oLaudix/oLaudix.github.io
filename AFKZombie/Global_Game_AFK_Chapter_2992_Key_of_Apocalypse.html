Chapter 2992

Key of Apocalypse

From Chinese Chapter 2993 · 天启之钥

Players immediately flocked to the Vampire Apocalypse in droves. Even veterans who'd reached intermediate- and high-tier game worlds gritted their teeth, bought Rebirth Stones, and transferred there.

After all, plenty of players had struggled for decades in high-tier worlds, only to have weaker attributes than a newcomer who'd just acquired a Vampire bloodline.

Who could endure that kind of humiliation?

Even Fang Heng hadn't expected players to be so enthusiastic. He couldn't help marveling at it.

The Western Federation's practice of recruiting outside contractors really was effective. Fang Heng simply copied the model, made a few adjustments, and profited as a middleman without putting in anything himself.

He could even pick outstanding ordinary human players to inherit Vampire bloodlines.

Three birds with one arrow!

Time to grind!

Fang Heng hardly needed to manage anything. As long as he assigned Vampires to operate the ships and travel between worlds, enormous amounts of Kinsfolk Contribution poured in every day.

After gradually raising his personal Kinsfolk rank, Transcendent-Level Psionics also appeared among the system's individual exchange rewards.

Keep going!

Grind!

...

Another half a month passed.

Outside the Vampire Temple, Alec and a group of Vampires waited patiently.

A full seven days had passed since the latest battle.

Seven days earlier, Fang Heng's ships had been carrying out Kinsfolk missions in an alien star sector. Friction arose with an allied species over ore mining, and the Shadow Clan joined the dispute, turning it into armed conflict.

The fighting continued to grow in scale.

Shadow Clan forces and their support teams rapidly arrived afterward.

Both sides suffered losses.

Fang Heng's fleet lost over thirty ships, with several hundred more damaged to varying degrees.

Since Fang Heng was still in seclusion, the Eastern Federation commander chose to withdraw to prevent even greater losses, avoiding a larger battle.

Only afterward did they learn the allied species had deliberately provoked the encounter. The Shadow Clan had already announced across the Kinsfolk's various internal alliances that they intended to cause trouble for humanity.

“Your Majesty.”

Seeing Fang Heng leave the cultivation chamber, Alec and the Vampires bowed together.

“Rise.”

Fang Heng raised a hand.

Advancing to Transcendent-Level Psionics required an enormous amount of experience. His zombie clones couldn't do the work for him, and even continuously absorbing Psionic Crystals had taken almost a week.

Noticing Alec's serious expression, Fang Heng asked, “What happened? Did you run into trouble?”

“Yes. While you were in seclusion, two incidents occurred that require your decision.”

Alec drew a breath and first recounted the encounter with the Shadow Clan.

Fang Heng frowned slightly.

He'd just been considering whether to keep farming Kinsfolk Contribution for an even higher, perhaps Sub-Divine-Level, Psionics exchange—or to confront the Demon Seed Envy first.

Now the Shadow Clan had come looking for trouble before he could decide.

The Shadow Clan was a particularly troublesome species.

For one thing, they had no blood in their bodies.

Blood Control couldn't affect them. Moreover, they could transform into shadows and become immune to physical damage; even very few spell attacks could harm them in shadow form.

While Fang Heng considered this, Vampire Prince Bulle hurried over with several others after learning that Fang Heng had emerged from seclusion.

“Your Majesty.”

“Yes, Bulle? What's wrong?”

“Three days ago, Prince Jia'er was gravely injured and fell unconscious. We haven't been able to determine his condition. He's currently recuperating inside the Vampire King's Coffin.”

What?

Fang Heng's eyelid twitched.

After strengthening them with dimensional power, the entire Vampire Clan had become much stronger, and he'd placed few restrictions on the princes' use of that power.

Even in high-tier game worlds, the princes' strength could be considered top-class.

Someone had injured Prince Jia'er so badly?

Who could it be?

“Come on. Take me to him.”

“Yes!”

...

The Vampire Elders' Council.

The Vampire King's Coffin slowly rose out of the blood pool in which it had been submerged.

The coffin opened.

Prince Jia'er lay inside, pale-faced and unconscious.

On closer inspection, a gray, wriggling organism clung to his chest, gradually fusing with his body.

Bulle explained, “Your Majesty, when we found Prince Jia'er he was already unconscious. We found no signs of an intruder. The organism on his chest is unlike anything we've ever seen. We didn't dare interfere and could only place him in the coffin for the time being.”

Fang Heng stared at the organism.

It was Ouroboros!

He raised a hand toward the gray mass.

“Hiss, hiss...”

The organism slowly tore free from the Vampire prince's chest.

“BANG!!!”

It exploded into a cloud of blood in midair.

“Ding!”

A dark, key-shaped object fell from the blood mist and struck the floor.

With the insects clustered around his heart destroyed, Jia'er began to awaken. He struggled upright in the Vampire King's Coffin and bowed to Fang Heng. “Your Majesty, forgive my incompetence.”

“Who did this?”

“She called herself ‘Xia Xi.’ She asked me to deliver a key to you. I sensed something strange about her identity and tried to stop her, but she attacked before I could act.”

Jia'er recalled the encounter with a shamefaced expression. “Your Majesty, I didn't expect her to attack so suddenly. I fell victim to her technique.”

Fang Heng frowned.

Xia Xi.

She couldn't contact him, so she'd simply attacked a Vampire prince?

Fang Heng picked up the key dropped by the Ouroboros creature and examined it in his palm.

[Item: Ancient Unknown Key.]

Description: An unknown key bearing directions to open a path through an unknown labyrinth.

“Ah, that key...”

Alec, who had accompanied the senior Vampires, suddenly remembered something at the sight of it.

Fang Heng asked, “You recognize it?”

“Your Majesty, it's a Key of Apocalypse. They've become extremely popular among players recently.”

“Hm?”

“Some high-level teams have obtained similar keys through various means. Rumors say a Gate of Apocalypse is about to open in the real world, allowing teams that hold keys to enter the Land of Apocalypse and search for its treasures.”

“Xia Xi... treasure...”

Fang Heng murmured and put the key into his backpack.

A mysterious Gate of Apocalypse...

Was this a clue Xia Xi had sent him?

Bulle frowned. “Your Majesty, speaking of the Key of Apocalypse, several Vampire players have recently received invitations from outside groups to join expeditions seeking the Power of Apocalypse. Should we intervene?”

“No need.” Fang Heng looked up. “When does the Land of Apocalypse open?”

“It's expected to begin in three days.”

“Understood.”

Fang Heng waved a hand and pondered.

Only three days.

Xia Xi's clue was more important, and the deadline was tighter. He didn't have a good solution to the Shadow Clan problem anyway, so he might as well set it aside for now.

— End of Chapter 2992 —
