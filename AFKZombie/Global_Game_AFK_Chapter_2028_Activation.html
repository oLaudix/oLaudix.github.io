Chapter 2028

Activation

From original Chinese Chapter 2029 · 开通

Two Holy Court members hurried forward to report to Saino.

“City Lord, Commander Gulanchi has sent a request for assistance. A huge number of zombies have been spotted outside the Forest of Bewilderment, and they're heading toward Canmu City. There are too many for his army to stop. He's asked us to make preparations for battle as soon as possible.”

Saino was a sharply featured man in his early seventies. His brows knitted tightly.

“How much time do we have?”

“Commander Gulanchi will do everything he can to delay them. He expects to arrive here in about half an hour, when his forces can join ours against the horde.”

“Hmph!”

Saino snorted and turned toward Chu Yan, who had just arrived beside him.

“Chu Yan, what's the status of the teleportation passage?”

“My lord, we're carrying out the final inspection. The first phase of testing will begin shortly. But City Lord, construction has been rushed from the beginning, greatly increasing the likelihood of mistakes.”

“We have to test the device before using it. Otherwise, a serious malfunction could destroy the passage's entire structure, and repairs would be even more troublesome.”

“There's no time!” Saino snapped. “Ten minutes. I'll give you ten minutes to inspect the critical sections. Then activate the teleportation device immediately! The Holy Court's elite reinforcements are already waiting in Duncha. Once the passage opens, they'll enter Chiboreya at once!”

Saino made his decision and turned to his deputy.

“Take your men outside to reinforce the defenders. Hold those zombies back at all costs! Buy us more time!”

“Understood!”

The deputy immediately assembled a detachment and led them out of the city.

Fang Heng stood at the entrance to the teleportation area. When he saw Chu Yan shake his head subtly from a distance, he understood what had happened.

Apparently, their first attempt had failed.

Chu Yan hadn't managed to trick Saino into leaving the city.

There might be a few complications later. But that was fine.

With the first stage of the plan unsuccessful, Fang Heng stayed where he was and glanced at the game notifications flashing past at a rate of more than a dozen pages per second.

The zombie clones were doing extremely well on the front line!

After his vampire bloodline had grown stronger, the horde had improved enormously too, giving his plan a much greater margin for error.

Fang Heng simply disabled the ordinary notifications and waited patiently for the portal to open.

Before long, the central teleportation passage completed its initial charging and inspection.

“Prepare to activate!” the head of Canmu City's mechanical maintenance team shouted.

Energy poured into the spatial tearing apparatus, which released powerful fluctuations and began trembling faintly.

Hummm…

A dark-golden curtain of light rose in the center of the teleportation gate, slowly spreading into a thin spatial membrane.

Saino stared at the gate. “Can we teleport now?”

“Please wait a little longer. We still need to establish coordinates with the other side. The passage has only just been completed, and some testing is required.”

The city lord had run out of patience. He waved the explanation aside.

“We don't have time! Skip the tests and activate it immediately!”

“Understood!”

Chu Yan nodded to several players on his right.

“Send the receiving signal!”

Everyone stayed where they were, watching the passage in silence.

A moment later, faint ripples appeared across the dark-golden light.

A young man in paladin armor stepped out.

He glanced around the area and saluted Saino with a clasped fist.

“Honasan, squad captain of the Holy Court's Seventh Legion! Are you City Lord Saino?”

“That's right. Canmu City is facing a grave crisis. Assemble your team and proceed outside immediately to reinforce our forces and intercept the undead!”

Honasan's sharp eyes lit with excitement. “Yes, sir! Understood!”

Duncha was one of the strongest intermediate-tier game worlds, with an unusually high overall level of power.

Moreover, the Holy Court had brought the entire world completely under its control. Faith in the Holy Court was the only path available there.

But with no enemies left to fight, Duncha had enjoyed peace for far too long. Opportunities to earn military merit were scarce.

Now one had finally arrived!

“Follow me! Reinforce the Holy Court on the front lines!”

Soldiers from Duncha poured out through the teleportation passage. Once Honasan's squad assembled, they headed straight out of Canmu City to support the defenders.

…

Outside Canmu City, the zombie horde was still slowly staggering closer.

It wasn't moving quickly, yet its steady advance exerted an invisible psychological pressure on everyone watching.

Gulanchi had tried every method he could think of and still couldn't stop it. The Holy Court's defensive line was being forced backward step by step.

The good news was that more reinforcements were arriving, slowly increasing the rate at which they could wear down the horde.

Against that seemingly infinite sea of zombies, the improvement was only a drop in the bucket…

But at least it was a good sign, wasn't it?

“Look over there!”

A player helping to resist the undead suddenly noticed something behind them.

Canmu City's gates were slowly opening.

A well-equipped Holy Court force marched out in formation, rapidly moving toward their position.

“Reinforcements! Our reinforcements are here!”

“They're from Duncha! The teleportation passage has opened successfully!”

“Hold your formation! Stay alert!”

Morale among the Holy Court forces soared. The elites arriving from Duncha were considerably stronger than ordinary Holy Court troops!

At that moment, Legion Commander Yiluoda arrived from the right with his knightly force.

He stared at the dense mass of zombies ahead in astonishment.

There were this many?!

In the past, talk of the undead possessing mountains of corpses and oceans of bones had seemed like exaggeration. Looking at the scene in front of him, Yiluoda felt an enormous pressure.

He'd expected the situation in the Forest of Bewilderment to be bad. He hadn't imagined it could be this bad!

“Follow me! Charge!”

Yiluoda led his knights straight into the horde. Every paladin was enveloped in holy light.

The charging cavalry resembled a sharp sword, forcing a corridor directly through the mass of zombies!

At the rear of the main battlefield, the players were elated to see Yiluoda arriving with more reinforcements like a divine savior.

Their hard work hadn't been wasted!

They'd managed to delay the horde long enough for the teleportation passage to open!

With fresh forces joining the battle and more Holy Court detachments arriving from across the empire's heartland, the zombies' advance was slowed considerably for a time.

Yiluoda heard the cheers coming from the main army nearby, but he felt no joy. Instead, he frowned deeply.

The horde had become far stronger since the last time he'd fought it!

These zombies were getting harder and harder to deal with!
