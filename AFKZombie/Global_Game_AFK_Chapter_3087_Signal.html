Chapter 3087

Signal

From Chinese Chapter 3088 · 讯号

Any private security company capable of establishing itself in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital had considerable overall strength.

The security team entered the underground passage, carefully feeling its way forward.

At some point, the lights that were supposed to illuminate the passage around the clock began going out.

Ahead lay absolute darkness.

The guards switched on the searchlights mounted on their guns.

Before long, their handheld Black Mist detectors began buzzing with alarms.

“Strong Black Mist signals ahead. Everyone be careful.”

The low-ranked warrior leading the team seemed to sense something. He gradually slowed, signaled the others to stop, and had them maintain a defensive position.

Faint hissing came from the darkness ahead.

It sounded like something scraping rapidly across the ground.

The sound grew denser.

The security captain stared into the darkness and adopted a fighting stance.

Those were...

Insects!

Brown, spiny alien insect creatures burst from the depths of the sewer passage!

Why were there insects here?

Hadn't the surveillance footage shown nothing unusual?

The captain's heart lurched, though he didn't yet realize how serious the situation was. He warned, “Everyone, watch out! They're insects, and from the sound of it there are lots of them.”

The guards behind him took formation and raised their firearms.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!”

A dense barrage of energy beams poured into the darkness!

The beams exploded against the Lickers, illuminating the area ahead.

Huh?

The guards' faces grew grim.

Something was wrong.

They couldn't kill them?

Even high-frequency beam weapons couldn't penetrate the Lickers' external defenses?!

“Wall of Fire!”

“Scorched Earth!”

The security team immediately supplemented its attacks with elemental spells, erecting layer upon layer of defenses.

Based on experience, fire elemental attacks were usually most effective against insectoid creatures. High-damage, wide-area fire spells rained down on the swarm!

They seriously wouldn't die?

The captain frowned deeply.

The insects scuttled rapidly across the ground, enduring the flames as they passed straight through the wall of fire and even sprinted across the burning earth.

Once through the flames, the charred wounds on their bodies healed rapidly. They rushed toward the security team at terrifying speed.

“Something's wrong with these insects!”

Realizing the danger, the captain shouted, “Restrict their movements! Fall back!”

Although his ordinary guards hadn't reached even low-ranked warrior level, nobody employed as security in the capital was incompetent. They reacted quickly.

Everyone switched techniques at once.

“Quagmire!”

“Ice Storm!”

Elemental abilities were so useful precisely because they could shift forms, combining attack, defense, and restraint.

The front line of creatures became coated in ice crystals, and their feet sank into muddy ground, drastically reducing their speed.

But before the guards could relax, even more insects emerged from the sewer depths.

More?

How many were there?!

The guards' eyes filled with dread.

This was bad!

Given the creatures' confirmed defensive capabilities, one security squad had no hope of handling them.

“Withdraw first!”

The employer paid the wages, but their lives belonged to them.

Thinking clearly, the captain immediately led his squad in retreat.

Meanwhile, two street intersections away from the sewer entrance...

The Flying Squirrel Clan was small in stature. Kappa had therefore climbed onto the elven prince Korkalot's shoulder to peer toward the cordoned district.

“Kappa, can you see anything?”

“Hold on. Too many people. Can't see clearly.”

“If you can't see, then get down.”

Korkalot's expression darkened.

Since when had a Flying Squirrel been allowed to stand on his head?

“Hey, don't get worked up. Wait, don't move! I can see reinforcements arriving. People are coming out of the sewer. Looks like things aren't going well.”

Korkalot grabbed Kappa by the scruff and lifted him down. “Don't test my patience, Kappa.”

“Hahaha, I was only joking.”

Kappa laughed.

As the two spoke, a familiar voice suddenly sounded behind them.

“What a coincidence. So you're here too.”

Both men were startled. They turned and saw Fang Heng standing behind them.

“Boss Fang?”

Kappa was instantly delighted.

“What are you doing here? We've been looking everywhere for you!”

After hearing about Fang Heng from the elves yesterday, the two had immediately begun gathering intelligence on the Noel Clan. That morning they'd even tried visiting the Noel Clan headquarters to contact him.

But the Noel Clan had been particularly jumpy lately, suspecting the elves of trying to poach their recruits. They'd refused to reveal anything and sent the visitors away.

Kappa had planned to visit the Kinsfolk today and try another approach to finding Fang Heng tomorrow.

Who would have thought they'd meet him here?

“Mm, I ran into a little trouble. I'll explain later. What about you? Why are you here?”

“Because of the Kinsfolk.”

Korkalot's mood improved at the sight of Fang Heng, and he quickly explained the problems they'd been having.

The Kinsfolk had made things difficult for them repeatedly.

In short, they'd submitted the human world's registration paperwork, but Kinsfolk interference had left it stuck under review with no approval in sight.

The elves had learned through unofficial channels that a Kinsfolk external-affairs official was applying the pressure.

They'd come specifically to meet that person today.

“So you're here to find a solution?”

“Mm. We wanted to meet the official first and then decide what to do. But the district was suddenly locked down, and they threw us out before we could see anyone.”

Seeing Fang Heng, Kappa felt as though he'd found someone to lean on, and a heavy burden lifted from his shoulders.

It seemed that with Fang Heng around, every problem could be solved.

And they might make a fortune along the way.

Fang Heng raised his head toward the nearby skyscraper. “So the Kinsfolk official you're looking for is inside that building?”

“Exactly, that one. Getting an audience with a Kinsfolk isn't easy.”

Kappa nodded repeatedly and complained, “We registered and made an appointment ages ago. Finally got a meeting, and then this happened! It'll probably be postponed again. I'd love to know who caused this mess...”

Fang Heng gently rubbed his chin without answering.

Korkalot seemed to make a connection. He turned to Fang Heng and asked cautiously, “Boss Fang, I heard something happened in the sewers. Is it connected to you?”
