Chapter 3136

Obstruction

From Chinese Chapter 3137 · 阻碍

“Hiss, hissss…”

As a bead of blood blossomed at the center of Fang Heng's forehead, fine streams of black mist began leaking from it.

Fang Heng could sense it.

Even here in the Frozen Soul Land, his special game abilities encountered some resistance.

Fortunately, it wasn't severe. The main effect was that his skill activation times were extended by more than tenfold.

Supported by Fang Heng's enormous mental power, the blood seeping from his forehead slowly floated into midair.

“Hiss, hisss…”

The blood gathered and gradually formed a scarlet spatial gate in the air.

The blood-colored portal slowly fused with the surrounding space.

Unlike the previous times he'd used the Vampire Teleportation Gate, thin strands of black mist now leaked continuously from the seams where it merged with the torn fabric of space.

The portal also opened far more slowly than usual.

“Click!!!”

With a soft crack, the spatial gate slowly opened high in the air.

The Vampire Prince Garr, accompanied by two Vampire elders, flew out of the passage first. Arriving before Fang Heng, they all knelt on one knee. “Greetings, Your Majesty.”

“Garr, I want you to occupy this planet as quickly as possible. Take control of everything in this world and sever all contact with the outside,” Fang Heng said calmly, gazing into the distance. “Go. The insects will cooperate with you.”

“Yes, Your Majesty!”

Garr's spirits rose.

The Vampires' bloodline power had increased dramatically several times in succession. They felt they'd been accumulating strength for ages, but Fang Heng had kept them largely confined to the Vampire world, and chances to train in the outside world were rare.

In truth, most Vampires longed for an opportunity to fight outsiders.

Now their chance had come!

Korkalot raised his head and stared at the unfamiliar people emerging continuously from the Vampire Teleportation Gate overhead, amazement shining in his eyes.

What race was this?

He'd never seen them before.

And Fang Heng seemed to be their leader?

At the very least, Korkalot could sense power from Garr that was in no way inferior to his own.

“Stop staring!” Kappa gave Korkalot a shove. “They're Vampires. Come on, take them to the starship area. First, cut off the ships' communications with the outside world. Don't let any of them escape!”

“Mm…”

Korkalot withdrew his gaze.

Under the leadership of Garr and Bule, elite Vampires flew continuously out of the spatial gate and joined Kappa in charging toward the starship district.

The security guards inside the caverns were going mad.

Where had all these monsters come from?

They were ordinary guards with limited strength. Their beam weapons couldn't inflict lethal damage on the insects, and before they could even flee, Vampires joined the battle.

Thanks to their racial characteristics and immense attributes, the Vampires completely overwhelmed ordinary security personnel in one-sided battles.

With support from the Licker swarm, Garr led his forces straight through the front lines.

Fang Heng looked at the Vampire elders kneeling before him and nodded slightly. “Elders, let's begin.”

“Yes, Your Majesty.”

Several elders highly proficient in alchemy immediately helped Fang Heng construct Abe Akaya's summoning magic array.

They were among the Vampires most skilled in alchemical arrays, selected specifically to help establish Abe Akaya's array quickly.

Hm?

Fang Heng raised a hand and tapped lightly at several points in empty space. The outline of a magic array slowly appeared on the ground.

The Vampire elders were astonished.

In the time since they'd last met, their king's accomplishments in alchemical magic arrays had advanced terrifyingly far.

They put aside their surprise and concentrated on helping Fang Heng complete the formation.

Just over ten minutes later.

Dense black mist seeped from the array, followed by Abe Akaya's vines bursting forth.

The array had likewise suffered interference.

Fang Heng raised his head and glanced toward the distant disturbance in blood energy. Then he stopped what he was doing, looked at the elders, and said solemnly, “Stay here and continue constructing the array. I'll go take a look.”

“Yes, Your Majesty!”

…

Two Vampire Princes led a group of elite Vampires out of the underground caverns.

Meanwhile, the Kinsfolk's outsourced security company on the Frozen Soul Land began to respond to the Vampires emerging from underground, immediately sounding the alarm.

At first, the company assumed the prisoners had started a riot and organized personnel to suppress it.

But when they entered the underground caverns and saw a vast swarm of insect creatures charging toward them, their scalps tingled with horror.

Where had so many insects come from?

Before long, an elite guard team assembled by the company's leadership arrived on the battlefield.

Chen Hanyu was the chief commander of the interstellar security guards. By the time he'd assembled his men and reached the cavern entrance, an overwhelming number of insect creatures were already pouring out!

The security company's defensive teams fired beam weapons furiously into the swarm while slowly retreating.

The battle was almost entirely one-sided.

They couldn't hold them back at all!

Chen Hanyu frowned deeply.

The insects' defenses were ridiculously strong!

Even the highest-grade high-frequency beam weapons couldn't injure them!

This was difficult.

Security on the Frozen Soul Land had been designed to control prisoners. Ordinary-grade beam weapons were more than adequate for that.

They had hardly any equipment suitable for repelling a planetary invasion.

Hm? What was that? Chen Hanyu looked up and saw numerous unfamiliar flying beings emerging from the cavern.

The airborne Vampires were continuously launching blood-colored spheres at the security teams, disrupting their formations.

Chen Hanyu looked surprised.

An invasion by pan-human races?

Were they controlling the insect swarm from behind the scenes?

Damn it! Why hadn't there been any warning?

Where had they come from?

“Report to headquarters and request reinforcements. Prepare the starships for emergency departure.”

Chen Hanyu immediately made his decision and issued orders. “Evacuate all personnel. All starships are to prepare for warp departure at once.”

“Yes!”

The security teams hurried away to prepare their retreat.

“Everyone else, follow me! Help cover the evacuation!”

“Yes!”

With that, Chen Hanyu immediately led a group of warriors beside him into the battle.

He understood the situation clearly.

The enemy's numbers were certainly far greater than what he could see, and their individual strength was generally high.

His security force had only one high-tier warrior—himself—along with five mid-tier warriors and a little over twenty low-tier warriors. Even if they managed to win a small local engagement, they couldn't hold out against so many insects.

At best, they could delay the enemy long enough for the others to evacuate.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

More than thirty elite warriors entered the fray and immediately selected the Vampires as their targets, carrying out focused attacks to kill them!

Sensing his subordinates dying one after another, Garr grew solemn. He looked toward the right side of the battlefield, fixing his gaze on Chen Hanyu.
