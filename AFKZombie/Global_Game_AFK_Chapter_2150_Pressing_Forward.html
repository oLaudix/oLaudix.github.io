Chapter 2150

Pressing Forward

From original Chinese Chapter 2151 · 突进

Fang Heng glanced right with disdain and casually raised one hand.

A blood curtain immediately rose along his right side!

Puff! Puff, puff!

Every incoming beam was blocked, sending ripples across its surface.

After another short charging interval, the institute's internal defensive beam turrets activated again!

An overwhelming barrage of focused beams surged toward Fang Heng!

Contempt curled at the corner of his mouth.

Time to dodge!

He took one step to the right and slipped into Secondary Spatial Projection again.

After the beams struck empty space, he stepped out once more.

Continue!

Dense crimson spikes hammered continuously into the force field, draining its remaining energy.

Fang Heng's combat approach was perfectly clear.

Whenever focused beam weapons concentrated their attacks on him, he'd slip into Secondary Spatial Projection ahead of time to evade them.

Against the ordinary firepower of Federation semi-mechanized squads, he'd simply rely on his blood curtain and natural durability!

He wasn't afraid in the slightest!

Meanwhile, on the rear battlefield, the Lickers and Tree Spirits surged toward the institute while Fang Heng occupied most of the enemy's firepower!

The Federation's attention was so focused on Fang Heng that they failed to notice the terrifying creatures they'd never encountered before crawling beneath the Lickers!

Huge numbers of worms were swiftly making their way toward the center of the battlefield, concealed by the grass!

And soon...

The worms reached the central fighting zone!

Large war machines!

The creatures immediately climbed onto the unmanned war machines.

Unfortunately, those machines were made entirely of mechanical components, their exteriors covered in thick armor.

The ordinary worms crawled around outside for a long time without finding a way in. After several failed attempts, they simply went around the machines.

Likewise, the war machines' databases contained no records of this type of enemy, and their targeting systems couldn't even lock onto the tiny creatures. They therefore couldn't attack them.

The two sides briefly encountered one another before peacefully passing on, as though nothing had happened at all.

The Lickers, by contrast, kept throwing themselves at the war machines, entangling them and buying time for the forces behind.

Soon, the worms approached a section of the battlefield farther ahead.

The Federation semi-mechanized troops!

They immediately surged toward the soldiers!

The worms climbed rapidly up from their boots!

Here they came!

[Hint: Worm creatures released by your zombie clones (Fusion Tyrant forms) are attempting parasitic infection—human.]

Even while attacking the defensive field, Fang Heng had been watching the game hints. His eyes lit up when he saw the notification reporting the worms' attack.

Excellent!

The worms couldn't do anything against unmanned heavy war machines, but against semi-mechanized armored soldiers, they proved unexpectedly effective!

After all, the armor worn by the semi-mechanized soldiers wasn't completely sealed; there were still small gaps!

The worms climbed over the armor until they caught the scent of flesh, then located openings and slipped inside!

Following that tempting smell, they found the semi-mechanically modified soldiers concealed within the armor.

What happened next was straightforward.

They crawled onto the soldiers, used their mouthparts to tear tiny openings in the skin, and burrowed straight into their bodies.

“What...?”

“Damn it! What are those things?”

“Something's crawled inside my outer armor!”

“Ah! What is it?!”

“Bugs!”

At first, the semi-mechanized troops had no idea what was happening. They only heard their comrades shouting frantically over the communication channel.

Then they saw the soldiers beside them suddenly lose control of their bodies, convulse wildly where they stood, scream in terror, and topple stiffly to the ground.

Bugs? What bugs?

“Damn it! Down there! There are bugs on the ground!”

Only after hearing their teammates' warnings did the others realize what was happening and look down.

Beneath the grass, an enormous carpet of dark-brown worms was hiding!

Many were already crawling up from their ankles and burrowing beneath the outer armor.

“Damn it! What are these things?!”

Their faces changed in horror.

Nobody wanted those bizarre worms crawling inside their armor.

“Do something! Quickly!”

The soldiers began hopping and stamping around like ants on a hot griddle, desperately trying to dislodge the worms that had already climbed onto them!

In the surveillance room, the institute director saw the semi-mechanized force descend into collective chaos. Not understanding what had happened, he demanded, “What's going on now?!”

“Worms! Report, sir! The semi-mechanized squads are being attacked by unknown worm creatures!”

The technician watching the surveillance feed felt a chill down his spine and hurriedly switched the front-line footage onto the main display.

Seeing dense waves of worms trying to climb onto the soldiers, everyone in the room drew a sharp breath.

What terrifying creatures were those?!

Was this another of the enemy's special abilities?

Fang Heng!

Who on earth was he?!

He kept revealing one bizarre, terrifying ability after another!

The director clenched his teeth. “Fire! Burn them with flamethrowers!”

“Yes, sir!”

Receiving the order, the semi-mechanized soldiers recovered their wits and switched to the flamethrower nozzles built into their armor.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

High-temperature flames roared from the nozzles, scorching the ground and the worms crawling toward them.

It worked!

The worms seemed instinctively afraid of fire. Once scorched, they dared not approach and instead crawled around to the rear or flanks.

At last...

The soldiers sighed with relief when the worms stopped approaching.

The grisly deaths of their comrades had filled them with deep fear.

But there was a problem. They had to keep using their flamethrowers to prevent the worms from approaching, which meant they could no longer attack their other enemies.

Elsewhere, noticing the confusion among the Federation's semi-mechanized forces, the Tree Spirits immediately seized the opportunity and pressed forward with everything they had!

Dense showers of turquoise-green arrows fell from afar!

The Tree Spirits concentrated their attacks on the heavy war machines!

In moments, the machines outside were blown apart one after another!

Then the Lickers and Tree Spirits turned their attention to the outer defensive force field.

— End of Chapter 2150 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Target mismatch. The worms cannot penetrate unmanned war machines, but exploit small gaps in the soldiers’ semi-mechanical armor.
  • Fire countermeasure. Flamethrowers repel the worms while reducing the soldiers’ ability to attack other targets.

Chinese Expressions

  • 热锅上的蚂蚁. Literally “ants on a hot griddle”; the armored soldiers hop frantically to dislodge the worms.
