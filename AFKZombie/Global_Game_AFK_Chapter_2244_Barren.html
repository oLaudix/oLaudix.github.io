Chapter 2244

Barren

From original Chinese Chapter 2245 · 贫瘠

"Elder, we've inspected it. It's White Froststone, and its quality is better than anything we've seen before."

In the main hall of the Prayer-Curse Temple, a Leaf Clan guard whispered his report to Ladiard after examining the ore, then stepped respectfully aside.

"There's too little."

Ladiard glanced at the two ore carts temporarily parked in a corner. Without examining them closely, he shook his head. "Fang Heng, is this all you can get?"

"The Federation has limited reserves. I can get more, but most of it will have to be transferred from elsewhere. That will take some time."

Fang Heng looked at him. "The rest of the ore is on its way. In the meantime, I'd like to visit a dimensional world. And I'd like to see the Divine Tree the Leaf Clan is nurturing."

Ladiard instinctively disliked the idea of Fang Heng seeing either the Divine Tree or a dimensional world. He was only considering it for the sake of the White Froststone…

While he hesitated, a Leaf Clan member hurried over and whispered in his ear.

"The Soul Clan?"

Ladiard frowned in puzzlement. "Understood. We'll go meet them."

Seeing his expression change, Fang Heng asked, "Has something happened?"

"Internal Tree Spirit affairs."

Ladiard waved a hand. "Didn't you want to visit a dimensional world? We'll be opening a passage shortly. Go along and help. But don't say I didn't warn you: it's not a pleasant place. Look with your eyes, don't ask unnecessary questions, and don't interfere. Otherwise, nobody will be able to protect you."

Ladiard turned to a younger clansman and nodded. "Ovi, take him with you when you go to the dimensional world."

A young Leaf Clan member bowed respectfully.

"Yes, Elder. Understood."

"Mm."

With a quiet acknowledgment, Ladiard left the hall in a hurry with two other Tree Spirits.

What was the rush?

Watching him go, Fang Heng wondered what new trouble had arisen among the Tree Spirits.

He was rather tempted to follow and see.

Ovi approached with two Leaf Clan members. "Human, please follow us. The teleportation is about to begin."

"All right."

Well, then.

But dimensional worlds interested him more.

One thing at a time. He'd investigate the dimensional world first.

Following Ovi, he returned to the Prayer-Curse Temple's central hall.

"Hm?"

The sight inside made Fang Heng exclaim softly in surprise.

The hall looked completely different from when he'd left the previous day!

Green vines covered entire sections of the walls and ceiling.

It looked as though the room had been rebuilt.

Several Leaf Clan members had already gathered around the magic array at the center.

An elderly Leaf Clan elder sat behind the formation.

Faint waves of natural energy emanated from him.

Ovi stepped forward respectfully and bowed. "Elder Falk, I've been ordered to open the dimensional passage and collect dimensional energy."

Fang Heng studied Elder Falk for a moment.

He hadn't seen this elder when he left yesterday.

So the Leaf Clan had assigned two elders to protect the temple?

Falk opened his eyes and looked over Ovi and Fang Heng standing behind him. He nodded. "I know. Begin. I'll protect the ritual while you're away."

"Thank you, Elder."

Ovi stepped forward and respectfully received a Ritual Vessel from Falk with both hands.

Then he gestured to the dozen or so Leaf Clan members behind him and entered the center of the magic array.

Fang Heng stepped into the formation with them.

As several Leaf Clan members outside began chanting, the teleportation array slowly activated.

Fang Heng noticed that Falk was holding a green branch.

The branch emitted spatial fluctuations mingled with the aura of nature.

A blue glow slowly brightened, and a small teleportation passage appeared at the heart of the formation.

Spatial fluctuations!

"Let's go."

Carrying the Ritual Vessel, Ovi stepped into the passage and disappeared.

Fang Heng followed Ovi inside.

His surroundings quickly dissolved into emptiness.

When his vision returned, he found himself standing on a barren hill.

[Hint: You have entered an unknown world.]

[Hint: Due to the effects of this world, all your attributes are reduced by 6%.]

[Hint: You have crossed into another world tier. The pursuit team dispatched by the Divine Domain will attempt to lock onto your position again after 9 + 15 hours (the additional time is provided by an artifact skill).]

Just as he'd thought!

Fang Heng's heart skipped a beat.

A dimensional world was indeed what players understood as a game world!

And the Myriad Forest World could connect to external worlds!

Likewise, the Holy Court could track him in this external world.

He would need to complete the assignment before the Divine Domain's tracking finished, or he'd be in serious trouble.

Growing more cautious, Fang Heng quickly surveyed the surroundings.

Everywhere he looked, cracks spread across the barren sand like the withered veins of a dead landscape.

His attention soon settled on a raised stone slab in the sand to the right of the teleportation formation.

Its surface was densely covered in magic-array patterns.

That slab was Ovi's destination.

Ovi went over immediately, briefly checked it, and nodded to his companions.

The other Leaf Clan members came forward and formed a circle around it.

Fang Heng followed and observed the slab carefully.

It was extremely old, with cracks all across its surface.

However, it had clearly undergone some recent repairs, and those cracks no longer prevented the magic array from functioning.

"Prepare to begin."

Ovi had brought a total of twenty-two Leaf Clan members into this dimensional world.

Six took positions at the array's six nodes while the others stood guard around the stone slab.

Before starting, Ovi took out a crystal, glanced at Fang Heng, and said, "Human, we need you to help defend the array. Whatever happens, you must not interfere with its operation. Understood?"
