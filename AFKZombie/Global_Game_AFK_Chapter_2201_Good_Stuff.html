Chapter 2201

Good Stuff

From original Chinese Chapter 2202 · 好东西

Anna and the others stared at Fang Heng in confusion.

None of them understood what that glance was supposed to mean, and they didn't dare move. They were still wondering whether they ought to say something when…

Whoosh! Whoosh!

A magic array spun rapidly into existence beneath Fang Heng's feet!

Thick blood immediately surged out of the array, enveloping his entire body.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng vanished along with the blood.

What was going on?!

He was gone?

Anna and his companions stood dumbfounded, exchanging bewildered looks.

Fang Heng had disappeared completely, leaving only the four wooden boxes behind.

Everyone turned toward Anna.

Anna had no idea what to do either. He eyed the four boxes Fang Heng had left and swallowed nervously before taking a cautious step forward.

The moment his foot moved, the teleportation passage behind them reacted.

Hundreds of Lickers poured out one after another and gathered around the wooden boxes.

It looked as though they were guarding them.

“Uh…”

Seeing how alert the Lickers were, Anna promptly pulled his foot back.

Well, this was awkward…

What were they supposed to do now?

They'd suddenly found themselves trapped between a rock and a hard place.

Go and pick up the boxes?

There were layers upon layers of Lickers around them. They didn't exactly look friendly…

Attack first?

Wouldn't the Lickers tear them into shreds?

Run away?

What was the point of leaving without the boxes?

And without Fang Heng, they'd only end up trapped in the forest outside!

What now?

The group looked from one person to another.

“Let's wait a little longer. Maybe Fang Heng just left temporarily…”

…

In the real world.

Fang Heng was in no particular hurry.

The Valley of Annual Rings imposed severe restrictions on the Tree Spirits.

Even if they forced their way in, the root labyrinth would delay them for quite some time.

That gave him more than enough time to gather information.

Before long, Mo Jiawei logged out of the game as well.

The two got in touch, then quickly contacted Sandy to ask whether he'd found anything in the records recovered from the Tree Spirits' Spirit-Prayer Temple.

Unfortunately, Mo Jiawei once again had to serve as a human telephone, logging in and out repeatedly to carry messages between them.

On his fifth trip back online, Mo Jiawei said, “Boss Fang, Sandy thinks you may have found something incredibly important. He's demanding to speak with you himself. He's getting worked up, and we can't calm him down. You still have that leaf-shaped crystal Klevi gave you, right? She'll help set up a communication link in a moment.”

“Mm. All right, got it.”

Fang Heng also found it far too troublesome to rely on Mo Jiawei as a go-between. Sandy kept asking about the area he was in, but what Fang Heng had seen wasn't easy to describe in a few words.

They might as well establish a remote visual connection.

Soon Fang Heng returned to the game world.

The whole round trip had taken nearly half an hour.

Anna and the other humans were still standing there in bewilderment.

Before they'd entered the Valley of Annual Rings, Osman had given them a stamped communication token.

It could be used to contact Osman outside.

But the token was consumable, and the suppression inside the valley caused its energy to drain rapidly. It only had enough power for one remote communication.

Should they use it already?

But…

Anna suspected that, in their present situation, even if Osman came in person rather than speaking remotely, he wouldn't be able to solve their problem.

Should they take a chance?

Charge the Lickers and fight their way through?

They honestly didn't dare.

After all, the sight of the Lickers slaughtering the venomous snakes earlier was still fresh in their minds.

Those Lickers were far more terrifying than the snakes!

Whoosh, whoosh!

Just as they hesitated, a magic array appeared again in the spot where Fang Heng had vanished. Anna and the others stiffened and hurriedly stepped backward.

Blood spilled from the array once more!

It was Fang Heng!

He was back!

As Fang Heng's body gradually took shape in the blood, Anna gritted his teeth, mustered his courage, and edged forward. “Mr. Fang Heng, we…”

Fang Heng emerged from the teleportation array, gave the group a cold glance, and shook his head, signaling them to keep quiet.

“Er…”

Anna's carefully prepared words died in his throat, leaving him awkwardly silent once again.

Fang Heng walked aside and took a leaf-shaped crystal from his pocket.

It gave off a faint glow.

It looked as though Klevi had prepared the communication link.

Fang Heng poured his mental strength into the mark.

The valley greatly suppressed Nature Studies, though the suppression in this particular area wasn't as severe as outside.

After absorbing his mental strength, the crystal worked perfectly.

Humm…

The crystal activated, projecting pale spatial ripples before him.

Those ripples gradually converged into a single image.

Before long, a familiar face emerged from the shifting light.

It was Klevi.

Her eyes were tightly shut.

Powerful interference was affecting the forest communication system, forcing her to devote all her attention to maintaining the link.

Then another head poked in from the side, its enormous face completely blocking hers.

“Boss Fang! My goodness! I just know you've stumbled onto something incredible!”

Sandy was clutching an old, damaged book, still holding it open to a particular page. His expression was wildly excited as he urged, “Quick! Show me that precious crystal you found! And the altar too. That thing is important.”

“Okay.”

Fang Heng nodded and stepped forward.

The holographic ripples moved with the token in his hand.

Under Anna and the others' watchful eyes, Fang Heng walked through the crowd of Lickers to the wooden boxes.

He opened one and lifted out the irregularly shaped Nature's Heart Crystal, holding it up for Sandy to see.

“Nature's Heart!”

Sandy examined the crystal's surface closely, then flipped through his book and compared the two several times. His eyes grew brighter with excitement. “Fang Heng, if my guess is right, that thing is the Tree Spirits' Nature's Heart!”

Sandy exclaimed, “It's worth a fortune… Ahem! I mean, it has immense collectible value! Absolutely immense!”

“What do you mean? Sandy, explain yourself. What exactly does it do?”

Sandy wiped the corner of his mouth and continued, “All right. Remember those books you brought back from the Spirit-Prayer Temple? I've spent the last few days reading through them. The Nature's Heart Crystal is actually used to produce energy for cultivating the Tree Spirits' Divine Tree.”

“Produce energy?” Fang Heng was getting more confused by the minute. “What does that mean?”

“You know the Tree Spirits are divided into four clans, and that whenever an old generation's Divine Tree declines, they choose a new clan leader to take charge, right?”

— End of Chapter 2201 —
