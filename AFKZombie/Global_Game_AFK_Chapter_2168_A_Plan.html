Chapter 2168

A Plan

From original Chinese Chapter 2169 · 方案

Over the past two days, they had gradually gathered more information about Wanling Technology.

It wasn't quite what Fang Heng had expected.

Ji Haiting and the Federation didn't actually get along very well.

He had inherited the family business at a young age, joined the board after reaching adulthood, and then shifted the company's focus toward high-tech research and development, gradually expanding it.

Afterward, the Federation had begun quietly infiltrating Wanling Technology.

Under pressure from his family, Federation departments, and factions within the company itself, Ji Haiting had ultimately accepted Federation investment and oversight, helping develop its advanced projects ever since.

Everyone outside believed Wanling Technology was simply a puppet propped up by the Federation.

But after going through a great deal of material, Mo Jiawei believed Ji Haiting's relationship with the Federation was nowhere near as good as it appeared.

Perhaps Ji Haiting was an opening they could exploit.

In any case, it was worth a try.

As Fang Heng departed at high speed, prisms slowly rose from the ruins of the exploded factory.

The Dependent warriors exchanged glances, and pale-blue spatial-jump light appeared around them.

…

Meanwhile, at Federation Central Command.

Fang Heng!

Watching him escape yet another combined pursuit operation by the Federation and the Dependents, the Federation's senior officers fell silent.

First the Tree Spirits, and now a Fang Heng had appeared out of nowhere.

Once again, they had been forced to rely on the Dependents.

Ji Haiting stood at the back, observing everything.

Fang Heng was certainly strong.

But how long could one man hold out?

As the Dependents' tracking level rose, Fang Heng needed longer and longer to shake off each wave of pursuers.

While Ji Haiting was thinking, an assistant approached and whispered in his ear. “Chairman, your private magnetic card has been activated. We've pinpointed the coordinates.”

Ji Haiting's interest stirred.

He had only ever given that private card to five people.

And the person contacting him now…

Could only be Fang Heng!

Had he finally come around?

“Prepare a spatial jump immediately. I'll go personally.”

“Yes, sir.”

…

In a quiet corner of the city, Fang Heng sat alone outside a retro-style café, holding a hot cup of coffee between his hands.

A moment earlier, he'd activated the magnetic card Ji Haiting had given him using his perception.

The Dependents had been chasing him around, determined to kill him.

What a nuisance.

In truth, he wasn't entirely out of options.

He could simply hand the Niam Alloy over to the Tree Spirits and follow them down one road until it turned dark, come what may.

But that left a bad taste in his mouth.

And it wasn't reliable.

Click… click, click…

At the sound, Fang Heng looked up toward a nearby street corner.

Several spatial-jump fluctuations appeared there.

Ji Haiting stepped out with five psychic bodyguards.

“Fang Heng! Long time no see.”

Like an old friend meeting someone after a long absence, Ji Haiting waved and walked toward him.

His guards discreetly secured the area around the café and began clearing away bystanders.

“Have you made your decision? I must admit, I've been looking forward to it.”

“Mm. I've thought it over. I believe we can cooperate, but only you and me, on a personal basis.”

“Excellent!”

Ji Haiting's eyes glinted. He pulled out a chair and sat opposite Fang Heng.

“Don't agree too quickly. Hear me out first.”

Ji Haiting gestured for him to continue.

“There's one thing I need to make clear from the beginning. I know absolutely nothing about this so-called Ouroboros.”

“You don't know?”

Ji Haiting was visibly surprised.

“It's a long story. Some of my memories are missing, so I can't even be certain whether Ouroboros exists within my body. The creatures I control are part of a special ability. They're effectively my clones, which means they also possess some of my power.”

Ji Haiting listened and fell into thought.

A moment later, he looked up. “I see. It doesn't matter. Even without Ouroboros, the power you possess fascinates me. We can cooperate on research in the future. In exchange, I'll give you unrestricted access to all the information I've personally obtained from the Dependents.”

Fang Heng nodded.

Whether Ji Haiting was trying to trick him or not, he sounded a good deal more pleasant than the Tree Spirits.

Ji Haiting considered for a moment, then continued, “Apart from that, there's also the Tree Spirits…”

“I don't have much to do with them. Some circumstances brought us together, and we cooperated temporarily. It wasn't a pleasant partnership.”

“All the better. I know you're not like those fools in the Tree Whisperer Alliance. With your abilities and my resources, whether we're dealing with Ouroboros, the Tree Spirits, or the Dependents…”

Ji Haiting seemed to glimpse a bright future for their partnership. He clenched a fist lightly. “But that's for later. Right now, there's an urgent problem we need to solve.”

“The Dependents?”

“Precisely. Even if you know nothing about Ouroboros, they won't stop hunting you.”

Curious, Fang Heng asked, “All right, why exactly are they after me? Just because of Ouroboros?”

“That's more than enough. Perhaps you don't understand the Dependents very well yet.”

Ji Haiting signaled for an assistant to order him a coffee, then began explaining.

“Just over a hundred years ago, the Dependents entered our world. They claimed to be a race that had reached a higher stage of evolution. They established an alliance known as the Star Alliance and invited us to join.”

“The Star Alliance was founded for one purpose: to resist a calamity.” Ji Haiting looked at Fang Heng. “That calamity is Ouroboros.”

Fang Heng frowned deeply.

What was that supposed to mean?

Were the Dependents telling him they'd established an alliance spanning entire star systems just to deal with him?

Wasn't that a little excessive?

“The Dependents promised us many benefits if we joined the Star Alliance—access to their advanced technologies, their protection, and more. Humanity's technological capabilities have undergone a qualitative leap in just a century, largely thanks to their assistance.”

“But there was another extremely important reason…”

Ji Haiting paused, then looked at Fang Heng. “To deal with the Tree Spirits.”

Fang Heng's pupils contracted sharply.

So the Tree Spirits and the Dependents didn't get along either?

“You may have heard that the Tree Spirits were once the rulers of this world. I assure you, that's no exaggeration.”

“Even after the calamity that befell them, when the passages between the Inner and Outer Worlds disappeared and more than ninety-nine percent of their people vanished into the Inner World, the fewer than one percent who remained still posed an enormous threat to human survival.”

— End of Chapter 2168 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Star Alliance. Ji Haiting says the Dependents founded the Star Alliance to oppose Ouroboros, called a calamity; the nature of Ouroboros is not yet established.
  • Ji Haiting. Fang Heng proposes personal cooperation with Ji Haiting, not cooperation with the Federation.

Chinese Expressions

  • 一条道走到黑. Literally “follow one road until it is dark”; stubbornly stick with one faction or course of action despite the consequences.
