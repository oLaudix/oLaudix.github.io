Chapter 2131

The Meeting

From original Chinese Chapter 2132 · 会见

With that in mind, Fang Heng quickly inspected the Tree Whisperer Alliance exchange store.

The alliance seemed to be a fairly low-level organization.

There were pitifully few useful items available.

The only things worth looking at were a handful of basic Nature Studies skill books.

Unfortunately, their levels were too low compared with his own Nature Studies proficiency. Even reading all of them wouldn't give him much experience.

There were, however, several books related to the Tree Spirits. He could exchange for those and let Sandy examine them later. Who knew what he might discover?

Fang Heng looked up at Mr. Yule and tentatively asked, “Mr. Yule, about the Tree Spirits...”

“Mm. I contacted the Tree Spirits last night. They're very interested in the discovery of the teleportation magic array. We've arranged to meet them tonight.”

[Game Hint: You have triggered a stage quest—Unveiling the Tree Spirits' Mystery.]

Quest Name: Unveiling the Tree Spirits' Mystery.

Difficulty: D.

Quest Description: You have earned the trust of the Tree Whisperer Alliance. Mr. Yule has arranged for you to meet a member of the Tree Spirit race tonight.

Quest Requirement: Meet the Tree Spirits with Mr. Yule.

Quest Reward: Follow-up quest.

Current Main-Story Quest Progress: 7.8%.

Excellent!

His main-story quest progress had increased again!

As they spoke, the Tree Whisperer Alliance members working under Yule carefully unloaded the dead Divine Tree offshoots from the truck and moved them into a temporary warehouse.

Klevi followed them inside and began checking the remnants of the magic arrays within each offshoot.

Fang Heng asked, “How does it look?”

Klevi crouched and examined one for a while before looking up. “Mm. These Divine Tree offshoots aren't as well preserved as the one we found in the cavern, but I can still find traces of the magic arrays. I'll do my best to reproduce the patterns in drawings.”

“Good. As quickly as you can. We're meeting the Tree Spirits tonight, so let's try to finish before then.”

“Mm.”

While Klevi gathered the information from the offshoots, Fang Heng took Sandy to the alliance store and exchanged contribution points for every book they could obtain.

Sandy promptly directed several Tree Whisperer Alliance members to carry box after box of books back to their cottage.

· · ·

Three hours later.

Fang Heng received thirteen magic array diagrams completed by Klevi.

Almost every Divine Tree offshoot had a slightly different array inscribed within it. They couldn't simply be fitted together without thought.

“Better get to work.”

The Tree Spirits would reach the town in the evening. He needed to finish before they arrived.

Fang Heng arranged the diagrams across the floor.

The Omniscient Eye in his right pupil spun rapidly.

With his high-level Alchemy and the Omniscient Eye working together, a slowly filling progress bar appeared above Fang Heng's head.

[Game Hint: You are attempting to combine fragments of different magic arrays. Based on your attributes, Alchemy talent, and relevant skills, the current success rate is 88.9%.]

The progress bar inched forward.

For more than two hours.

Success!

[Game Hint: You have successfully combined the incomplete magic arrays and restored the magic array. You have obtained the blueprint for a Special-Location Teleportation Magic Array.]

Fang Heng examined the magic array patterns he'd laid out in the temporary warehouse once more. Taking a deep breath, he stood up.

The first step was complete!

Just as he'd suspected, reconstructing the array confirmed that it was a magic array capable of spatial teleportation.

But activating it again wasn't so simple.

Its energy input interface was unlike that of any magic array he'd encountered before.

He'd already tried several times while arranging it, without finding a compatible power supply.

That meant he still needed to figure out how to provide energy.

In the past, power had been supplied to the array through the Divine Tree offshoot's interface. Now that the offshoot was completely dead, they'd need another way to power it.

Fang Heng had no ideas in that regard.

Perhaps the Tree Spirits would know something.

After spending the past few days running around, Fang Heng was finally about to uncover the mystery surrounding the Tree Spirits. He couldn't help feeling a little excited.

For now, he'd wait until he'd met them tonight before deciding what to do next.

· · ·

Fogthunder Town was a very traditional little settlement.

Its residents kept regular hours and rarely indulged in nightlife. After ten in the evening, the town fell completely quiet.

At midnight.

Yule stood with Fang Heng, Mo Jiawei, and Sandy at the edge of a forest outside town.

“The Tree Spirits dislike exposing themselves to outsiders, and they dislike noise. So please remain quiet when they arrive. No photographs or recordings.”

At Yule's strong insistence, the meeting with the Tree Spirits was limited to the four of them.

Originally, Sandy hadn't been included.

Unfortunately, even Mr. Yule had been unable to withstand Sandy's persistent pestering.

A gentle breeze passed through the trees.

The branches at the forest's edge trembled faintly.

Ahead, a figure flashed through the undergrowth and appeared before them.

They'd arrived!

The Tree Spirits!

Fang Heng's gaze instantly settled on the newcomer.

In build and facial features, the Tree Spirit didn't look much different from a human.

The most striking difference was the skin.

It carried a greenish tint, rather like the green of leaves.

The eyes were also slightly longer and narrower.

On closer inspection, even the Tree Spirit's skin seemed to contain delicate, leaf-like patterns.

The Tree Spirit cautiously studied the people before him, then turned toward Yule. “Yule, are these the friends who believe in nature you told me about?”

Yule was extremely respectful in the Tree Spirit's presence. He bowed and nodded before replying, “Yes, esteemed Tree Spirit Elder. This is Fang Heng, and these two are Mo Jiawei and Sandy. They helped us recover Wanling Technology's secret research records and the dead Divine Tree offshoots the company forcibly took away.”

“Mm.”

The Tree Spirit gave a slight nod and surveyed Fang Heng's group with a distinctly arrogant expression.

Mo Jiawei pursed his lips.

Being inspected like that left him rather annoyed.

What's this guy acting so high and mighty for?

“Furthermore, they discovered the information hidden within the Divine Tree offshoots and used it to reproduce the magic array,” Yule added.

He turned to Fang Heng and the others to make the introduction. “This is Vannis, an elder of the Tree Spirits. Their people retain their youth for a long time. He may look only a few years older than you, but he's actually more than a century old.”

“Enough, Yule. Don't waste time explaining such trivial things.” Vannis shook his head. “My time is limited. I didn't come here to listen to nonsense. Take me to see the magic array you've restored.”

“My apologies. Please follow me.”

Yule led Vannis back toward the temporary warehouse in town.
