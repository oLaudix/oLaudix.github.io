Chapter 2611

Ice-Crystal Golems

From original Chinese Chapter 2612 · 冰晶魔像

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

The places struck by the white streaks were instantly covered in frost.

Ice Banshees!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and looked ahead.

Ice Banshees were special spirit-type creatures. Their forms became even more indistinct and difficult to discern within the cold white mist.

They also possessed a peculiar ability to confuse their targets and could fire freezing rays from a distance.

Tigrant had clearly prepared for this. He led his team forward again, raising Nether Clan shields and swiftly disposing of the banshees one by one.

Fang Heng and Kotar followed behind.

The deep gorge really was packed with high-level creatures. If Fang Heng had come alone, advancing would have been extremely difficult.

Even Tigrant's team had to stop repeatedly to deal with the monsters while maintaining their safety, only gradually reaching the deeper region.

“Everyone, over there.”

Fang Heng soon noticed something and pointed ahead, following his sense of the Empress's will.

Kotar and the others immediately looked in the direction he indicated.

In the distance, deep within the thick ice-crystal fog...

They could make out tall, dark silhouettes standing on the ground.

Kotar frowned and asked quietly, “Fang Heng, are you certain what we're looking for is over there?”

“Yes. I'm certain.”

Kotar frowned even more deeply. He exchanged a few words with Tigrant before asking again, “Could we find a way around them?”

“I'm not sure. But we're very close to the source of what I sensed. It should be somewhere among those shadows.” Fang Heng looked at him. “What's wrong?”

“Ah...” Kotar sighed and shook his head. “This won't be easy. Those dark figures are Ice-Crystal Golems, the most troublesome things in the Frozen Gorge.”

“They've absorbed the cold of these lands for tens of thousands of years, then fused it with necromantic power and formed wills of their own, becoming new living beings.”

“Ordinarily, they look like common ice-crystal statues. They move slowly, but their Strength and Constitution are terrifyingly high. They're highly resistant to most spells and immune to nearly all negative effects. They also absorb the surrounding cold to restore their health rapidly.”

Kotar drew a long breath. “In this environment, killing them is extremely difficult.”

Fang Heng understood.

They were among the gorge's most formidable creatures.

Their defenses were extraordinary too.

The Nether Clan would rather not provoke them unless necessary.

Kotar spoke with Tigrant again, and they nodded in agreement.

“Well?”

“The commander thinks it's unusual for so many Ice-Crystal Golems to be here. They may be guarding one of Empress Persephone's secrets. We've already spent some time here, and waiting much longer could be dangerous. He'll act at once to clear some of the outer golems and reduce their numbers. Then we'll find a way inside and investigate before deciding what to do next.”

“All right.”

It was a sensible plan.

Fang Heng agreed with a nod.

Tigrant and the five members of his accompanying team carefully advanced in formation toward the cluster of ice golems.

Sensing intruders near their territory, the golems gradually emerged from their statue-like state.

“Crack, crack, crack...”

As their bodies moved, the layer of snow covering them shook loose, exposing gleaming crystalline forms.

“Whoosh!”

Tigrant flashed forward, sweeping his sword in a sharp arc.

“Bang!”

The blade carved a massive wound more than a meter long across a golem's body.

“Whoosh!”

As it took the hit, the golem swept a fist toward Tigrant.

Fortunately, the punch was slow enough for him to dodge easily.

Whoosh!

The next moment, a bitter wind carrying ice crystals blew past, and most of the enormous wound closed up at once.

Fang Heng watched in surprise.

The Ice-Crystal Golems were extraordinarily tough! Within the gorge's special environment, they also received a huge bonus to health regeneration!

Each of Tigrant's attacks appeared to deal tremendous damage. Previously he'd killed most gorge creatures with a single sword stroke.

Even spellcasting enemies usually took only two strikes.

One to break the shield, the second to finish them off.

“Bang! Bang, bang!”

All around them, the ice golems awakened from their statue-like stillness and slowly converged on the group.

This wasn't good.

“I'll help,” Fang Heng said quietly.

“Huh?”

Kotar had barely opened his mouth when Fang Heng was already closing in on the golems.

Underworld God Form!

Whoosh!

Fang Heng entered his Underworld God state. A shield appeared around him, accompanied by four floating Underworld God's Prayers.

He raised a hand toward the nearest Ice-Crystal Golem.

Death's Grasp!

[Hint: Death's Grasp cannot affect supersized life-forms. Skill failed, triggering a Death Realm spatial eruption.]

Boom!

Dark Death Realm energy exploded against the golem. The force drove its massive body backward.

At the same time, the Underworld God's Prayers around Fang Heng launched netherfire at the golem every 0.5 seconds.

Fang Heng quickly checked the damage numbers.

Not bad. The netherfire worked far better than Death's Grasp!

Unfortunately, the flames clinging to the golem went out in less than two seconds.

Its magic resistance must have activated.

Fang Heng dashed toward it again, curling his right hand as though gripping something.

Whoosh!

Death Realm energy rushed toward his palm, forming a scepter.

“Whoosh!”

As Fang Heng closed in, he smashed the scepter into the Ice-Crystal Golem.

“Boom!”

The golem staggered backward again.

Fang Heng frowned.

Underworld God Form wasn't as useful as he'd hoped. Death's Grasp also had an internal cooldown and couldn't properly affect huge, heavily armored creatures like these.

On top of that, the environment greatly enhanced the golems' natural regeneration.

Then...

He needed a different tactic.

Fang Heng left Underworld God Form and quickly pressed his hands together.

Eternal Tree Domain!

The outline of a gigantic magic array emerged beneath his feet.

As an energy utterly different from that of the Death Realm erupted, dense vines and roots surged from the ground, covering vast stretches of snow and ice!

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!”

More and more branches erupted in every direction!
