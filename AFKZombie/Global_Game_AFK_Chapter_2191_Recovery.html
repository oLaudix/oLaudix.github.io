Chapter 2191

Recovery

From original Chinese Chapter 2192 · 恢复

“The situation on the Tree Spirits' side is less clear.”

Ji Haiting's expression grew more serious, worry showing on his face.

“Judging from our recent clashes, losing the blockade has strengthened the Tree Spirits dramatically. Ordinary Federation semi-mechanized units probably aren't a match for them anymore. On top of that, most Federation spatial-jump systems have gone offline without the Spirit Domain network, so our overall strength has fallen sharply.”

“And there's more. Earlier, the Federation located several Tree Spirit settlements and deployed large forces to attack them. But now that the blockade network is out of control and the Tree Spirits have regained their power, our frontline troops have taken heavy losses. I hear they've already withdrawn, and the Tree Spirits have returned to their homes.”

Fang Heng could more or less imagine what had happened.

Previously, the Federation had discovered their settlements. Unable to fight back, the Tree Spirits had been forced to move their entire clans away.

Now the blockade had been lifted and their strength had surged. Feeling powerful again, they'd simply decided to stay where they were.

So what if the Federation knew their location?

The Tree Spirits believed they could defend it!

They'd lived there for so long, after all.

If they finally had the strength to protect their homes, it was natural they wouldn't want to move.

Worry flickered in Ji Haiting's eyes. He sighed. “I fear the balance between the Federation and the Tree Spirits is about to change dramatically.”

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

He'd known that lifting the blockade would strengthen the Tree Spirits, but from Ji Haiting's account, the increase seemed considerably greater than he'd anticipated.

That could introduce all sorts of new uncertainties.

Then again, the Tree Spirits had always been unpredictable.

Fang Heng said, “The Tree Spirits have been trying to open a passage into the Inner World all along. Now that the blockade is gone, I suspect they'll devote more of their attention to that. They probably won't seek a major confrontation with the Federation right away.”

“Let's hope so. In any case, escaping the Dependents' pursuit is good news. It means we can focus on dealing with the Tree Spirits.”

“Mm.” Fang Heng nodded. “How are things on your end? This isn't going to cause you trouble, is it?”

“Nothing too serious. I've already arranged for the blame to fall on the Tree Spirits. But now that they've become so powerful, are you still confident you can work with them to find the passage into the Inner World?”

“Not really, if I'm being frank.” Fang Heng shrugged. “But that's fine. I've already found a passage to the Inner World.”

“What?!”

Ji Haiting froze and stared at him. “You've found one already?”

“Of course.”

Anticipation shone in Ji Haiting's eyes as he looked at Fang Heng.

They had made an agreement.

They would explore the Tree Spirits' mysterious Inner World together.

“If all goes well, preparations should already be under way. Is your jump-transport system ready? I'll take you there now, and we can have a look together.”

“All right. I just need the exact coordinates. I'll have my people prepare and jump us to the nearest base station.”

The destruction of the Spirit Domain network hadn't made the old jump-transport system completely unusable, but its efficiency had fallen by 99%, and it now needed independent power supplies.

In addition, teleportation required a receiving base station near the destination.

“But are you physically up to it?”

Ji Haiting was concerned about Fang Heng's condition.

For an ordinary person without semi-mechanized modifications, a single spatial jump could nearly kill them.

And Fang Heng had barely rested after what looked like an enormous battle.

“I'm fine.”

Fang Heng rose from the hospital bed and accompanied Ji Haiting to the jump-transport hall.

…

The island.

The newly excavated underground area.

After completing their deal with the Tree Spirits and securing the Leaves of Life, Mo Jiawei and the others had immediately returned to the island base.

They still had no idea what had happened outside.

Klevi was just preparing to feed the Leaves of Life to Abe Akaya when Fang Heng arrived with Ji Haiting and his group.

They were accompanied by several psychically modified guards.

It was Ji Haiting's first visit to the island.

He hadn't imagined it could be transformed into a small secret base in so little time.

Though it still looked somewhat ramshackle.

Small as a sparrow, it had all its vital organs.

And the huge tree growing in the underground section…

Ji Haiting stared at the Divine Tree Abe Akaya, faintly sensing an extraordinary power within it.

The psychically modified guards accompanying him noticed something unusual too. They faced Abe Akaya warily and positioned themselves protectively around Ji Haiting.

“This is Ji Haiting, chairman of Wanling Technology.”

“Good to see you all. We've met before. I'm Ji Haiting.”

Ji Haiting nodded in greeting to Mo Jiawei and the others.

“Good, good! Welcome, Chairman Ji. We'll all devote ourselves to art together from now on! Boss Fang, everything here went smoothly. We've got the Leaves of Life and we've only been waiting for you. Come on, let's get started!”

Sandy had waited days for the chance to explore the Inner World. He had no interest in wasting time on pleasantries with Ji Haiting and kept urging Fang Heng to begin.

Now that the moment had arrived, even Ji Haiting felt a little nervous. He nodded toward Fang Heng.

“He's right. Let's get started as soon as possible.”

“Mm. All right.”

Fang Heng walked up to Abe Akaya and nodded to Klevi standing nearby.

As before, Fang Heng would lead the process while Klevi assisted.

He slowly closed his eyes, trying to establish a psychic connection with Abe Akaya.

Sensing the presence of the Leaves of Life, Abe Akaya conveyed a surge of delight to Fang Heng.

At his signal, Mo Jiawei tossed the leaves directly toward the Divine Tree.

Hiss! Hiss, hiss!

Vines rapidly reached into the air and enveloped the Leaves of Life.

Hummm…

An emerald glow spread outward from Abe Akaya!

Since the blockade network's collapse, Klevi's ability to manipulate the power of nature had improved noticeably!

Leaves of Life processed by Klevi were now easier for Abe Akaya to absorb.

Game notifications began appearing rapidly across Fang Heng's vision.

[Hint: Soul Symbiote — Divine Tree Abe Akaya has absorbed the energy within the Leaves of Life. Some of its abilities have been restored…]

As Abe Akaya absorbed more and more energy, its roots extended deeper into the soil while its luxuriant branches and leaves gradually spread across the entire island.
