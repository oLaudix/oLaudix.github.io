Chapter 3089

Breach

From Chinese Chapter 3090 · 击破

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!”

Immediately, great waves of insects surged in, surrounding the fallen guard and tearing at him savagely.

“Boom!!”

After more than a dozen successive strikes, the guard's energy shield shattered.

The other guards panicked.

Any remaining hope that the creatures wouldn't actively kill them vanished at last.

The insects revealed their ferocity, swarming among the guards in a frenzy of slaughter!

Their sharp claws carried enhanced tearing effects. Once they knocked a guard down, a dozen or so attacks were enough to shred even a high-strength energy shield.

“Retreat! Retreat!!!”

Unable to fight them head-on, the guards began falling back into the building.

But their speed was nowhere near that of Lickers running at full tilt. The creatures caught them almost instantly, knocked them down, and devoured them in moments.

The building's outer defense line lasted less than half a minute before the Lickers breached it.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!”

Reaching Kinsfolk headquarters, Lickers forced their way through the main entrance and windows. Others ran straight up the outer walls at extraordinary speed, climbing through windows on every floor.

“Warning! Black Mist intrusion detected. Black Mist Level LV:1.”

“Warning! Traces of Ouroboros Seed infection detected...”

“Warning! Warning! Building under attack!”

“Warning! Warning! Building entering Level One alert status.”

Inside the building, warriors recruited by the Kinsfolk received the alarm and only then realized something had happened. They emerged from their rooms to investigate.

“Insects?”

Several high-ranked warriors took an elevator downstairs. When its doors opened, they saw insects scuttling along the ceiling into the corridor on the right.

Looking around, they discovered that the first floor had already become a scene of carnage.

The pungent smell of blood filled the air. Corpses and severed limbs lay scattered everywhere.

“Hiss, hiss...”

Hm?

The warriors frowned.

Looking toward the movement, they saw something writhing strangely in the corpses of fallen guards. Flesh cocoons rapidly formed on their bodies.

The cocoons pulsed and swelled until they burst, and zombie creatures crawled out.

“What is this...?”

Infected pan-human creatures?

The aliens had little experience with zombies and exchanged uneasy looks.

“It's an Ouroboros infection form. Destroy them.”

The warriors hesitated no longer and began eliminating the insects with various techniques.

“Flames!”

One fighter raised his hand, launching several flaming spikes toward the zombies.

Only now did they begin to realize how difficult the creatures attacking the building were to handle.

These were exceptionally powerful Ouroboros Seed aggregates.

The Lickers' attributes were absurdly high, and they possessed a terrifying capacity for revival.

When killed, they could turn into thin red threadworms.

Those worms had to be eradicated too.

And even that wasn't the end.

After killing every worm, the black slime left behind had to be destroyed as well.

If the fighters hadn't all been mid- or high-ranked warriors with extraordinary perception, even figuring out how to destroy the creatures completely would have been extremely difficult.

After fighting for a while, they discovered that the vast swarm kept growing. No matter how many insects they killed, their numbers showed no sign of decreasing.

Every time one fell, several more took its place.

“You go back first. Protect the employers.”

“Mm. All right.”

...

At a street corner not far from Kinsfolk headquarters, Fang Heng mingled with the curious spectators, watching secretly.

The insects could easily crush even the capital's security forces.

But they weren't nearly as effective against mid- and high-ranked warriors.

That made sense. Any such fighter could qualify as a world boss in a small or medium-sized world.

Their power was on an entirely different level.

“Boss Fang, look over there! The military's here! Reinforcements have arrived.”

Fang Heng followed Kappa's pointing finger.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital's defenses were jointly organized by the seven overlord factions, with the military and other departments cooperating to maintain security.

Usually, however, the military stationed most of its forces in the outer city.

Once it confirmed enemy intelligence about the Kinsfolk emergency, it rushed to the scene.

Through a spatial-jump system, dense prismatic points of light appeared across the air.

Countless small combat machines emerged from those points.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!”

Red beams shot from the prisms, sweeping across the Licker horde on the ground.

Hm?

Fang Heng frowned.

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Licker) has been affected by an unknown chemical agent. All attributes reduced. Paralyzed. Undying Body effect weakened.]

Struck by the red beams, the Lickers immediately slowed. All their attributes fell sharply, and their self-regeneration weakened dramatically.

Fang Heng looked up at the machines in the sky.

The beams carried a specialized chemical agent effective against Ouroboros organisms.

He glanced down at the time.

Only three minutes had passed between the insects emerging from the sewers and the military's arrival.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital's security response was impressive.

Combined with the Kinsfolk's hired mercenaries and warriors, and the regular ground troops now arriving, the military began gradually reducing the number of Lickers outside.

But his Lickers had already penetrated the building and were wreaking havoc inside.

After clearing the outer perimeter, small beam fighters entered through the building's various entrances to attack the intruders within.

Fang Heng turned to Kappa. “Which floor did you say those Kinsfolk who kept obstructing you were on?”

“The twenty-seventh.”

“Good.”

Fang Heng immediately ordered the Licker horde remotely to flood toward the twenty-seventh floor.

At that moment, in the upper-floor office area on the twenty-seventh level...

Several Kinsfolk administrators were hiding inside an office.

At first everything had been fine. The door was locked, and they hadn't been attacked.

Then the situation changed in the blink of an eye.

Lickers in the corridor charged frantically toward their office, slamming into the metal door again and again.

Above them, scraping claws sounded against the metal ducts in the ceiling.

This was bad!

The reinforced metal door couldn't withstand the repeated attacks.

“Boom!!!”

The door burst open, and several Lickers lunged straight toward the Kinsfolk inside!

The administrators, wearing armed protective suits, immediately released a mental shock wave!
