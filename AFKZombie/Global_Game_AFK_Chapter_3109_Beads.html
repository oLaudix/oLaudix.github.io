Chapter 3109

Beads

From Chinese Chapter 3110 · 圆珠

What?

Dario was violently startled and immediately regained his senses.

The blood flowing from his facial orifices was moving out of control, covering his mouth and nose and completely cutting off his breathing. More blood coiled around his neck, tightening into a deadly noose.

Dario panicked, a chill running down his spine.

What kind of sinister power was this?

Water-element magic?

No, it seemed more like blood manipulation!

It belonged to none of the academic disciplines he had encountered before.

Dario struggled frantically, but it was utterly useless.

The blood gradually sealed off his breathing.

Standing nearby, Fang Heng watched Dario's face grow pale as he struggled and finally fell silent.

Done.

Fang Heng lightly clenched his fist, and Dario's entire body exploded in midair!

The blood rapidly evaporated, shedding its impurities until it became a mass of purified blood. That mass condensed into a tiny droplet and merged into the short blade behind Fang Heng's back.

Hm?

What was that...?

Fang Heng's gaze settled on a few fragments left on the ground where Dario had died.

Normally, after an enemy's death, the refining power of the Vampire King's bloodline could melt their blood into a tiny droplet of pure blood essence, which could then be stored in the blood-red short blade for later use.

Most items carried by the enemy would likewise turn to ashes during blood refinement.

Only a very small number of special ores or divine weapons might resist the process.

But these...

Fang Heng walked over and bent down, picking up several semitransparent beads roughly the size of pearls. He was puzzled.

Even his blood refinement couldn't dissolve these things?

What were they?

A high-ranking warrior wouldn't carry worthless trash around.

He would keep them for now and ask someone about them later.

Suppressing his curiosity, Fang Heng pocketed the beads and turned away. He carefully checked Alray as well and confirmed that the blood wrapped around him had strangled him to death.

And Dario wasn't the only one. Two other warriors also carried several similar translucent beads.

The beads emitted no detectable energy and couldn't be absorbed.

How strange.

What on earth were these things?

...

In the council hall of the Kinsfolk building...

"What? They failed?"

News that every member of the warrior team dispatched to carry out the assassination had died plunged the council hall into silence. Then the Kinsfolk began whispering among themselves.

The matter was connected to the Black Mist, so the Kinsfolk hadn't dared act openly. Instead, they had sent a team of hired warriors.

To ensure success, that team had even included two high-ranking warriors. They had shown the Flying Squirrel Clan member and Elven prince more than enough respect.

But the result was entirely unexpected.

Their seven-man team had been wiped out!

Not one had returned alive, and the battle traces left at the scene indicated an extraordinarily powerful opponent.

The Kinsfolk official responsible for assigning the mission looked grim. "Administrator, I misjudged the enemy's strength..."

"Judging by the time, the elves should have completed the hearing already..."

"Once the application is formally submitted, it enters the review procedure of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's seven great domains. The process is lawful and compliant. I'm afraid we won't be able to openly threaten humanity with total extermination afterward."

"Everyone, I believe you've misunderstood the important part. I don't care whether humanity's application passes." Administrator Lila interrupted their discussion. "What concerns me is that human. He's extremely strong, and the spread of this Black Mist is quite possibly related to him. We need to find him and kill him."

Lila looked around the chamber. "Have we identified any suspects?"

"As instructed, we've assigned personnel to watch every pan-human warrior around the clock. When the incident occurred, we immediately tried to verify each target's whereabouts. After screening them, three people remain under suspicion."

"We'll continue our investigation."

"Could it be Fang Heng?"

A Kinsfolk elder rose and said, "I encountered him during an earlier arena match. His strength is considerable, and I couldn't see through his background. His identity is also suspicious."

Lila appeared calm, but her patience was nearly exhausted. She said coldly, "If they're suspicious, find an opportunity and kill them. Better to kill the wrong person than let a suspect escape."

"Yes!"

"Fang Heng is a warrior of the Noel Clan and rarely goes out. Attacking him rashly might provoke the Noel Clan's displeasure."

"That doesn't matter."

Lila answered icily, "What I want is the Black Mist problem resolved as quickly as possible."

...

Upon returning to the villa, Fang Heng soon received another call from Kappa.

Good news.

Humanity had successfully completed its registration with the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, becoming a member of the universal alliance. The official announcement would be issued within seven days.

If the Kinsfolk attempted to coerce humanity with military force in the future, humanity could file a protest with the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, seek support from other factions, and put pressure on the Kinsfolk.

It was a major step forward.

But given the Kinsfolk's nature, Fang Heng doubted the matter would end so easily.

No rush.

At least they'd bought themselves a good deal of time.

He could use that time to keep increasing his strength.

Once he became powerful enough, he would have the means to resist whatever dangers arose.

During his stay in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, Fang Heng had grown fond of the feeling of steadily improving his strength.

Soon.

He checked his quest prompts. His Alchemy experience was almost maxed out.

Keep grinding!

Fang Heng simply sat down patiently and resumed training, absorbing the natural energy released by Abe Akaya.

Another full five hours passed before lines of game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Hint: Player's current Transcendent-level Alchemy has reached LV: MAX. Player can no longer obtain Alchemy EXP. Please attempt to advance your Alchemy rank.]

Success!

Transcendent Alchemy was maxed out!

Fang Heng nodded in satisfaction.

Tomorrow, he could go one step further and attempt to advance to Demigod-level Alchemy.

Although it was already the early hours of the morning, Fang Heng immediately called Anxi.

The higher the academic level, the harder the instructors were to book. He needed to make arrangements early.

Anxi answered Fang Heng's call almost immediately. When she heard he wanted to book a one-on-one lesson with a Demigod-level instructor as soon as possible, she fell silent for a moment.

After a while, she asked for confirmation. "Mr. Fang Heng, did you say... the Demigod level...?"

"Mm. Is something wrong?"

"I'm sorry, but I'm afraid we can't find a Demigod-level instructor at present." Anxi apologized repeatedly and explained, "Not only is the Noel Clan unable to find one, but no courses on Demigod-level studies are offered publicly anywhere."

"In any discipline? No Demigod-level courses at all?" Fang Heng frowned and pressed, "Has nobody ever reached the Demigod level?"

— End of Chapter 3109 —
