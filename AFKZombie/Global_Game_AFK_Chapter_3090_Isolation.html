Chapter 3090

Isolation

From Chinese Chapter 3091 · 隔绝

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

The solidified mental impacts struck with physical force and knocked the Lickers backward!

But immediately afterward came another thunderous crash.

The office's enormous floor-to-ceiling windows shattered!

Large numbers of Lickers poured through the broken reinforced glass!

At the same time, even more creatures crawled in through the front door from the corridor.

They were finished!

The Kinsfolk desperately projected mental barriers, combining their powers into an inverted-pyramid shield to keep the swarm at bay.

“Boom, boom! Boom, boom, boom!!”

They'd barely held on for a few seconds when successive explosions tore apart the ceiling.

Lickers crawled through holes in the ceiling and rapidly lunged toward the Kinsfolk.

Two fists couldn't fend off so many hands.

The Kinsfolk drove their mental power to the limit, but under the swarm's relentless assaults, visible cracks covered their inverted-pyramid barrier.

“Boom!!!”

The shield shattered!

The Kinsfolk were instantly buried beneath a mass of Lickers.

...

“All right, done. That fellow shouldn't bother us anymore.”

Through his mental connection, Fang Heng had a rough sense of what had happened inside the distant building. He broke the link and slowly withdrew his gaze.

The job was about finished. Knowing when to stop, he nodded toward Kappa and Korkalot.

“Let's go.”

The test was over.

He could now be certain that the Seven-Realm Martial Capital had exceptionally strong defenses. Of course, his zombie clones weren't weak either, and he still had plenty of trump cards unused.

That was fine. There would be more opportunities later. This had only been a test.

“Boss Fang! Wait for me!”

Kappa hurried after him.

They'd temporarily eliminated several Kinsfolk, but their replacements would probably cause trouble too.

At least things might remain quiet for a while.

“Boss Fang, aren't you coming back with us?”

“No. I'll be staying with the Noel Clan for now. It's inconvenient for me to handle the human world's registration openly, so I'll leave that to you.”

Korkalot paused. “You're staying with the Noel Clan to cultivate?”

“Mm. The Seven-Realm Martial Capital has a somewhat different system of power from those I've encountered before. I expect to remain here and cultivate for a while. Contact me anytime if you run into trouble.”

As he spoke, Fang Heng's form gradually faded in space until it disappeared completely.

Watching him vanish, Korkalot clenched his fist slightly, secretly filled with admiration.

He'd witnessed Fang Heng's strength himself. It had already reached an extraordinary level.

And yet he still seized every second to keep cultivating.

Damn it.

He was already that talented, and he still worked this ridiculously hard. Did he intend to leave no chance for anyone else?!

...

After taking a little trip and giving the Kinsfolk some excitement, Fang Heng returned to find the villa's front and rear yards piled high with cartons of alchemical mixtures.

Come on!

Time to grind!

Fang Heng established a mental connection with Abe Akaya.

“Hiss, hiss...”

Abe Akaya's vines slipped through cracks beneath the doors, wrapped around the cartons in the courtyard, and dragged them into the basement.

To give the zombies enough room to grind experience, nearly ten thousand clones had been digging continuously, extending the underground area downward and outward.

They'd excavated another seven whole levels.

The clones had construction skills, so collapse wasn't a major concern.

The excavated stone rubble was pulverized by Abe Akaya, then packed into sacks and piled underground.

Fang Heng used sealing studies to seal the sacks one by one and send them into a secondary sealed space for disposal.

Afterward, still feeling uneasy, he reinforced the aura-blocking magic arrays throughout the villa's underground levels.

By the time he finished, dawn was breaking.

Fang Heng returned to the newly excavated fifth underground floor, sat cross-legged, and quietly waited for the zombie respawn countdown.

Three, two...

One!

The zombie clones' revival time arrived.

Illusory magic arrays appeared around Fang Heng.

Huh?

His brow furrowed again.

Unlike previous zombie revivals, the arrays now operated with extraordinary sluggishness.

It was as though they were resisting a powerful force.

“Hiss, hiss...”

Faint Black Mist seeped from the revolving magic arrays.

As it gathered, the arrays gradually accelerated, causing still more Black Mist to escape.

He waited nearly a full minute before Lickers began leaping from the arrays.

Previously, the automatic revival summons had been practically instantaneous.

This time it took a minute, and the number of summoning arrays that could appear simultaneously was severely restricted.

“Is it because of the Black Mist...?”

Fang Heng studied the continuously forming arrays and formed a suspicion.

Both connecting worlds through Abe Akaya and entering or leaving the game had encountered resistance before.

In his view, bringing innate abilities from the game into reality probably still depended on the Black Mist game's power.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital clearly imposed strong restrictions on that power.

Those restrictions were what made the Black Mist operate so laboriously.

Fortunately, he'd installed many aura-sealing arrays in the basement ahead of time, trapping most of the Black Mist underground.

But the arrays weren't entirely connected, leaving a few gaps.

Mist would seep through them very slowly.

The quantity was small, so the Kinsfolk probably wouldn't detect it.

Fang Heng raised his head and looked around the basement with lingering unease.

His zombie summoning talent activated passively. He couldn't stop it even if he wanted to.

He could only wait and see.

More than an hour passed.

Zombie clones now crowded the basement.

And that was without using their splitting ability.

Fang Heng inspected his surroundings again.

The summoning was slower, but at least it worked.

He could worry about the Black Mist later. First, grind!

He ordered his clones to spread out and formed a seal before his chest.

“Scatter!”

The stone wall in front of him softened under an elemental spell, flowing downward like melting rock and pooling on the ground.

The zombies picked up the shovels lying nearby and began digging, expanding the underground area to both sides.

To avoid attracting attention, Fang Heng had even enchanted the shovels with special sound-suppression arrays, ensuring the entire process remained silent.

Keep going!

Expand the tunnels! Give the zombie clones more room to move and more room to grind experience!

— End of Chapter 3090 —
