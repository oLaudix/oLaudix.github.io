Chapter 2031

Blocking the Exit

From original Chinese Chapter 2032 · 堵口

“They're coming!”

In Canmu City's teleportation area, Chu Yan and his subordinates were still repairing the spatial rift device.

Seeing the zombies approaching from afar, the elite Holy Court squad left behind grew solemn and drew their weapons, preparing to fight.

City Lord Saino was nowhere to be seen.

Had something happened to him?

The elite soldiers' hearts sank.

“Undead! They're here! Don't let them anywhere near the teleportation passage!”

Chu Yan immediately shouted and ordered the nearby Holy Court guards to move forward and intercept the horde.

With the city lord missing, the panicked guards followed Chu Yan's orders and charged out.

Only Chu Yan and his fellow players remained beside the passage.

He immediately raised a hand to signal his companions.

“Quick! The barrier!”

The players erected a defensive barrier around the teleportation passage, protecting it from the fighting.

From a distance, Chu Yan nodded toward Fang Heng, who was standing behind the approaching zombies, before turning to his teammates.

“Restart the teleportation passage! Send a transmission request to the other side!”

“Understood!”

The players immediately pressed a series of controls on the machinery.

Hum…

After flickering several times, the dark-gold curtain of light in the spatial rift device stabilized once more.

Watching the zombie clones rapidly devour the remaining Holy Court guards, Chu Yan waved to his team. “Boss Fang's finished his part. Let's go, everyone—into the passage!”

He led the Devout Guild players through the portal in single file.

Not far away, Fang Heng continued clearing out the Holy Court guards with his horde while keeping an eye on Chu Yan and his party.

Only after the last defenders had been dealt with did he enter the teleportation area and approach the passage.

The outer protective barrier left behind by the Devout Guild gradually faded as its energy ran out.

The teleportation passage beyond it remained stable and operational.

Soon, Li Qingran hurried over with the undead-faction players to join Fang Heng.

Seeing that the spatial teleportation passage was still running, everyone was surprised.

Had the fighting outside really left the spatial rift device untouched?

Li Qingran stepped up to Fang Heng and said quietly, “We've eliminated all the high-level Holy Court elites of captain rank or above. Their souls have been destroyed too.”

“Good.” Fang Heng turned toward the spatial rift device and nodded to the undead players behind Li Qingran. “Everyone, let's get started. Learn the device's controls, send a request to accept incoming teleportation, and prepare for battle.”

The undead-faction players invited to take part in the operation were all skilled in mechanical engineering. They exchanged puzzled looks.

The other side of the passage?

Wasn't that Duncha?

One of the Holy Court's main headquarters in the intermediate-tier game worlds!

What did World Lord Fang Heng want to open a connection there for?

“Come on! We don't have much time!”

Seeing them hesitate, Li Qingran urged them forward.

The players stopped overthinking it and went to familiarize themselves with the spatial rift device's controls.

Before long, zombie clones began flooding into the teleportation area, packing the entrance until there was barely any space left.

One player reported gravely, “World Lord Fang Heng, we've sent the signal. The other side is ready, and transmission has begun!”

Fang Heng nodded. “Here they come! Everyone, get ready!”

Hum…

The dark-gold curtain of light over the passage flickered continuously.

Meanwhile, in the Duncha game world,

a large number of Holy Court reinforcement teams had assembled around the teleportation passage.

They had no idea that the undead had already taken over its far end.

When Chu Yan and the Devout Guild players had come through earlier, they'd merely reported a small problem on the other side, asked everyone to wait a few minutes, and said the passage would reopen when a signal arrived.

So the gathered forces waited quietly.

A few minutes later, a signal came through from the other side, and they promptly resumed teleportation.

“My turn.”

A high-ranking elite Holy Court inquisitor nodded to the others and led her subordinates into the passage.

Whoosh!

The moment the inquisitor came through, before she could even take in the scenery of the Chiboreya game world, an overwhelming sense of danger struck her.

Oh no!

Danger!

An ambush!

By then, it was already too late for her to respond.

A dense barrage of necromancy spells came crashing down upon her!

Inquisitors didn't have much health to begin with. Caught completely off guard, she was instantly blasted to death by spells from the twenty-odd necromancy players waiting at the entrance!

“Quick!” Li Qingran shouted. “Don't let her soul escape!”

The players were ready. They activated their Books of the Dead and together pierced the inquisitor's soul!

[Hint: Your team has killed the Holy Court reinforcement team's captain—an elite inquisitor. You have received 1,000 faction honor points…]

The players guarding the teleportation passage grew excited as the game notification appeared before their eyes.

This was thrilling!

Now they finally understood.

After all that elaborate preparation, World Lord Fang Heng had actually planned to camp the Holy Court's teleportation passage!

In other words, they were blocking a monster spawn point and farming Holy Court soldiers like ordinary mobs!

Li Qingran quietly looked toward Fang Heng and let out a long breath of relief.

The first step of his plan had gone exactly as intended!

And she knew perfectly well that what Fang Heng wanted went far beyond camping a spawn point for kills!

Sensing her gaze, Fang Heng nodded to her, then promptly turned and hurried toward the southern part of the city.

They could only keep “farming monsters” for a short while.

Even if they killed every incoming reinforcement and destroyed their souls to prevent word from spreading, the Holy Court wasn't stupid. It would surely notice something was wrong before long.

He didn't have much time.

He needed to finish this quickly!

The entire city had yet to fall, and the remaining imperial guards were still putting up a last resistance.

None of that mattered anymore.

Leave them be!

Fang Heng immediately gathered his Lickers and mutant infected zombie clones and raced toward the western city gate!

Along the way, imperial guards rushed together in an attempt to stop the horde's advance!

But they couldn't hold it back!

Hundreds of Lickers lunged at the guards in a mass, tearing into them!

Their razor-sharp claws ripped through the guards' armor.

Blood sprayed everywhere!

The deaths of their comrades, and the mutant infected zombies emerging from the corpses, filled the imperial guards with fear. That fear weakened their fighting ability even further. They became lambs awaiting slaughter, eventually serving as breeding grounds for new infected zombies.

Soon, the Lickers fought their way to Canmu City's southern exit and scrambled straight up the city wall!

— End of Chapter 2031 —
