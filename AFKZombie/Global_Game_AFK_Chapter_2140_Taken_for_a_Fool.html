Chapter 2140

Taken for a Fool

From original Chinese Chapter 2141 · 被忽悠

Before long, Fang Heng and Klevi found Mr. Yule in the town garden.

Surprise flashed across Yule's eyes when he saw Fang Heng. “Messengers of nature, how is the task the Tree Spirits entrusted to you going? Have you returned so soon because you encountered trouble? If you need help from our Tree Whisperer Alliance, please don't hesitate to ask.”

“Not at all. We've successfully completed the Tree Spirits' task, but no one came to meet us as promised.” Fang Heng looked at Yule. “What did the Tree Spirits tell you? They aren't coming here today, are they?”

Confusion flickered in Yule's eyes. “The Tree Spirits are coming today? I haven't received any such message.”

Fang Heng frowned deeply.

According to the original plan, after obtaining the Water of Life they would meet the Tree Spirits, withdraw together, and return to town.

And now the Tree Spirits had vanished?

“Mr. Yule, do you know how I can find them?”

Yule shook his head. “I'm not entirely sure. You've returned much sooner than expected. The Tree Spirits did mention that they would be carrying out an operation tonight, though. I assumed you would be acting together and returning to town together...”

Ring! Ring!

His phone rang in the middle of their conversation.

Fang Heng took it out. Seeing that Mo Jiawei was calling, he answered immediately.

“Hello? Fang Heng, I've just found something out. Wanling Technology's central research institute is in serious trouble. Apparently, the Tree Spirits were discovered infiltrating the place. From what I've heard, they're already fighting openly over there.”

What?

Fang Heng's eyelid twitched.

“Damn it, we've been played!” Mo Jiawei was so furious he could hardly keep from cursing. “I'm telling you, the Tree Spirits never intended to come and pick us up. They just wanted us to draw enemy fire away from them!”

The situation immediately clicked into place for Fang Heng.

The Tree Spirits' plan had actually been very simple.

Their objective had never been the so-called Water of Life.

Wanling Technology was already concentrating its forces on hunting Fang Heng down. The Tree Spirits had deliberately sent him into one of the company's facilities and arranged for his presence to be exposed, drawing its reinforcements away.

Then, while the central research institute was comparatively undefended, they would launch their real attack!

A textbook diversion—make a commotion in the east while striking in the west.

Which meant...

The supposed Water of Life had been completely useless to their actual plan!

Thinking back, Mo Jiawei's luck had been almost too good. They had accidentally wandered into the upper-floor experimental area of Wanling Technology's building, decided to act immediately, and begun their operation two hours early. That had thrown the Tree Spirits' entire deployment into disarray.

Fang Heng couldn't help growing angry.

What a joke.

The Tree Spirits had made monkeys out of them!

And the most infuriating part wasn't even that he'd made a pointless trip to the research institute.

It was the two entire days he'd wasted!

Why hadn't the Tree Spirits simply sent him straight to the northern district's central research institute?

Why all this unnecessary scheming?

Because they didn't trust him?

Or because they were prejudiced against other races?

The sharpness faded from Fang Heng's gaze.

He needed to stay calm and think about how to recover his losses and maximize whatever benefits he could still obtain.

“Fine, I understand. You might be in danger over there. Come back as soon as possible.”

Fang Heng ended the call and turned to Yule. “Mr. Yule, we've received information that the Tree Spirits are attacking Wanling Technology's central research institute in the northern district. Are you certain you know nothing about it?”

“The central research institute? Why would they go there?” Puzzlement flashed across Yule's face as he tried to recall. “Although, we did collect some information about that institute in the past...”

As he spoke, a member of the Tree Whisperer Alliance hurried over and whispered something in his ear.

Yule's face instantly turned rigid.

“What is it?”

“The Tree Spirits have issued an emergency warning. They're attacking the central research institute and calling upon all nearby Tree Whisperer Alliance forces to come to their aid.”

Yule's expression grew exceedingly grave. “Nothing like this has ever happened before. It seems the Tree Spirits have encountered enormous trouble.”

“Idiots...”

Fang Heng couldn't help cursing inwardly as he stared at the game hints.

Judging from the attitude the Tree Spirits had displayed so far, the subsequent quests would only become more troublesome.

He was even beginning to wonder whether the supposedly optimal quest-chain route suggested by the system as an earlier reward was actually correct.

Forget it.

No point dwelling on that now.

At least one thing was clear: the Tree Spirits were nothing but trouble.

Blindly following the game's hints and placing all his hopes in the Tree Spirits was a sure way to land himself in a pit.

He needed a backup plan.

But he didn't have to abandon the quest either.

He could go and take a look first.

Fortunately, with his highest-tier vampire bat form, reaching the quest location would take little more than half an hour.

Hopefully he'd make it in time!

Having made up his mind, Fang Heng nodded to Klevi. “Klevi, I'm going to Wanling Technology's central research institute. Stay here, be careful, and keep an eye on the Divine Tree offshoots.”

“Mm. All right.”

Fang Heng nodded, transformed into his vampire bat form on the spot, and flew into the distance.

Half an hour or so.

He hoped it would be enough!

Wanling Technology's central research institute.

A high-end weapons research facility jointly financed by Wanling Technology and the Federation.

Several kilometers surrounding it were designated as restricted grounds. The Federation handled day-to-day security, outsiders were ordinarily forbidden to enter, and the defenses were exceptionally strict.

In bat form, Fang Heng circled high above the research complex, looking down.

A powerful electromagnetic barrier filled the airspace ahead.

Below it, scattered flickers of spatial-transition energy were slowly gathering.

Countless mechanical constructs guarded the institute's perimeter. Among them were a great many enormous war machines.

On the opposing side stood the Tree Spirits.

Like elves, the Tree Spirits possessed considerable talent for archery.

By combining their bows with nature spells, they could fire extraordinarily accurate shots from immense distances, destroying the forming spatial-transition points one after another before the transitions could finish.

  • 声东击西. Literally “make noise in the east and attack in the west”; a classic diversion tactic.
