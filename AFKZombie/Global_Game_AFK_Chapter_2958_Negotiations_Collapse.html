Chapter 2958

Negotiations Collapse

From original Chinese Chapter 2959 · 谈崩

Even without Fang Heng's Lightning Storm, the sheer density of biological cannon fire had increased so much that the black mechanical spheres were beginning to suffer losses.

As Vion watched Fang Heng gradually seize control of the battlefield, fear entered his eyes.

The mechanical spheres themselves had relatively weak physical defenses. A direct hit from an energy beam or biological cannon was enough to destroy one.

And as the rate of biological fire increased, the machines had to devote so much processing power and movement to dodging that their own attack frequency plummeted.

Each time Fang Heng unleashed another thunderstorm, entire swaths of mechanical creatures were blown apart.

After fighting for a while, the black spheres had lost more than half their number. Their offensive weakened drastically, and they began to retreat, vanishing into the yellow haze of the sandstorm.

Good grief! Had they really held them off?

Vion was dumbfounded.

"Hahaha! What bullshit Cangniao? That's all they've got!"

Alec raised his middle finger toward the retreating machines.

Zaka strolled toward Vion, grinning. "Well, Mr. Expert? What do you say now?"

Vion hated Fang Heng and his people with a passion. Outwardly, he forced himself to answer through gritted teeth. "Don't worry. The Federation won't go back on its word. Once everything is over..."

Before he could finish, powerful spatial fluctuations appeared nearby.

A large starship emerged from a white warp passage and slowly moved into position beside the platform.

The starship completed its warp.

Captain Giselle looked out at the platform surrounding the base and froze in disbelief.

Good grief.

He'd been away little more than half a month, and the place had developed this much?

He'd worried about Fang Heng the entire time. Yet from the look of things, the man was doing better than anyone!

Giselle personally led a group out of the ship to meet Fang Heng and his companions.

"I'm sorry we're late."

Captain Giselle looked around, then turned to Fang Heng. "Some things happened. We've done everything we could."

Vion approached with his men too.

Emboldened by the arrival of reinforcements, Vion began thinking about making things difficult for Fang Heng again.

Giselle glanced at him and said, "The Cangniao's main battle formation will be here soon. We must evacuate immediately. We can discuss everything in detail once we're back at headquarters."

"No."

Fang Heng shook his head. "We've made other arrangements. I have an agreement with Mr. Vion, so I won't return with you. The Western Federation's researchers will also remain here to continue their work. They won't be gone long. They'll return safely within three months at most."

The Federation soldiers stared at Fang Heng as though he had gone mad.

Vion was equally stunned, staring at him in disbelief.

He was really staying?

Was he not afraid of dying?

"Hey! What are you doing?!"

While the others were still confused, Zaka stepped forward and blocked some soldiers who were preparing to detach and load the temporary base compartments. "You lot, stop right there! Those are ours!"

Huh?

The soldiers trying to load the compartments back aboard their starship were dumbfounded.

What did he mean?

Those compartments had originally come from their own ship. When had they become someone else's property?

Zaka swaggered up to the soldiers and pointed at Vion. "This brat lost them to us in a wager. They're payment for repelling the Cangniao combat group. Ask him if you don't believe me!"

Faced with everyone's stares, Vion said darkly, "Yes, we did make that wager. But this isn't the time for a transfer. Once we transport the compartments back to our world, then..."

Fang Heng said calmly, "There's no need to wait. Don't take the compartments back. Leave them here. We'll take care of them."

Vion clenched his fists. "Fang Heng! Are you determined to make an enemy of the Western Federation?"

Giselle stood silently to the side, apparently content to watch the show.

He had no idea what had happened between them—or how his ship's compartments had ended up being wagered away to Fang Heng.

But after thinking it over, perhaps this was for the best.

The three compartments were getting old anyway. Since the President's Office had caused the trouble, he could demand that they pay for replacements when he got back.

Besides, he disliked those damn presidential experts too.

Zaka sneered. "Why are you suddenly so angry? Can't stand losing?"

"Fine! I'd like to see how long you survive here! And those researchers were trained by the Western Federation. If a single hair on their heads is harmed, the Federation will not let the matter rest!"

Vion waved angrily and returned to the starship with his subordinates, leaving Fang Heng and the military personnel staring at each other.

Colette approached Fang Heng, worried. "Boss Fang, are you really not evacuating? You may have defeated one Cangniao formation, but their forces will only grow larger. I'm afraid..."

"Thank you. We'll be fine."

"Very well. Take care of yourselves. We have orders to follow, so we'll leave you to it."

...

Vion returned to the starship in a foul mood.

He had originally considered negotiating with Fang Heng again. If Fang Heng had been willing to yield, Vion might have offered him a small share of the profits. But after such defiance, he had no desire to speak with him further.

The rest of the expert team had come aboard as well.

The experts were in fairly good spirits.

They'd heard that Fang Heng had extracted a huge amount of ore from this planet, and they had come to take possession of it.

But when they saw Vion's expression, their mood turned serious. Something had clearly gone wrong.

"Vion, what happened?"

"Negotiations collapsed."

Vion snapped the answer and quickly explained what had happened.

The expert team fell silent again.

What did he mean?

They weren't getting the ore?

And they'd lost the compartments too?

Not just the equipment—they'd lost the accompanying researchers as well?

They looked at one another.

This was absurd.

How had they invested so much energy and so many resources in the journey, only to return with no profit and lose everything they'd brought?

Soon the military personnel returned to the starship too.

For Captain Giselle, there was at least one piece of good news.

With the compartments and researchers lost to Fang Heng, only Colette's soldiers and Vion's men needed evacuation.

Preparations took less than two minutes. The starship was ready to warp at any moment.

"Report!" A soldier in the main control area nodded. "Warp positioning is complete. We can activate the spatial warp and return to the main base whenever you give the order."

"Mm."

Captain Giselle acknowledged him and looked out through the glass, but did not immediately order the ship to leave.

Everyone else fell silent as well, staring through the reinforced windows.
