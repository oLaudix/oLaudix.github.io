Chapter 2242

Dimensions

From original Chinese Chapter 2243 · 次元

[Hint: You have obtained Iridescent Crystal ×8.]

Hm?

"What's that?"

After collecting all the Iridescent Crystals, Fang Heng noticed a darker crystal still lying in the middle of the dense thicket.

Could there be something else valuable?

With a thought, he formed a seal before his chest.

Psychic Control!

Using his Psychic Studies ability, he slowly levitated the dark crystal. It floated toward him until he reached out and caught it.

[Hint: You have obtained an item—Nature's Heart Crystal (Energy Crystal).]

[Hint: You may submit this item to the Leaf Clan in exchange for clan favorability, clan contribution points, and reputation. You may also submit it to another clan.]

"So it's a Nature's Heart Crystal."

Fang Heng murmured to himself.

It was the very thing the Tree Spirits had fought over in the Inner World.

He'd already obtained four of them before, and had brought them back for Klevi to examine.

Strangely, Abe Akaya, his Divine Tree, couldn't absorb them directly, so he still wasn't sure what they were for.

Ji Haiting was also having the crystals researched, but hadn't reported any results yet.

He hadn't expected Xidi to be carrying another one.

Well, he would gladly take it.

There was no chance he would turn it in.

The Tree Spirits could change their attitudes in an instant. Who knew when his favorability with them might disappear altogether?

Something this valuable was better kept for himself!

Since the Nature's Heart Crystal couldn't be placed in his inventory, Fang Heng used his usual method: he wrapped it in a short blade of blood energy behind his back and carried it with him.

Once that was done, he surveyed his surroundings and hurried back toward the temple.

He'd lost a little time.

Hopefully nothing had changed on Ladiard's side.

…

By the time Fang Heng returned, more than ten minutes had passed.

He soon realized he'd worried for nothing.

The Leaf Clan was as ready as ever to discard an ally once he'd served his purpose.

They had occupied the temple completely and thoroughly searched it. Not one of them had apparently cared whether Fang Heng was alive or dead.

In the central hall, the magic array had been more or less repaired.

It didn't appear to be operating yet.

An additional jar had appeared beside the formation.

Apparently, it was one Ladiard had just taken from Xidi.

"Excellent."

Looking at the repaired ritual array, Ladiard smiled and nodded. "You've all done well. I'll report your contributions honestly to the Great Elder. Elder Falk will be here soon to help us activate the array. Until then, stay here to prevent the Wood Clan from returning. We must protect the formation at all costs."

"Yes! Understood!"

After checking the temple, several Leaf Clan guards reported back. "Elder, there is still some White Froststone in the neighboring storehouse, but not much. We fear it's only enough to sustain a small amount of teleportation."

"Mm. Mark it and store it carefully. I've spoken to the Great Elder. Elder Falk will bring another shipment of White Froststone shortly to help us complete the teleportation."

As he spoke, Ladiard glanced at Fang Heng, who had just entered the hall.

Ladiard had no particularly good solution to the shortage of energy.

"And have you found out where the Wood Clan obtains its ore?"

The Tree Spirits exchanged looks and shook their heads.

"Elder, we're still investigating."

Fang Heng heard their conversation as he entered and felt a little curious.

Apparently the Tree Spirits were extremely interested in White Froststone.

In this world, it was a very rare mineral.

The Federation had always considered it an unusable energy ore. Stockpiling it would only take up space. Aside from a small quantity kept for research, it had never mined White Froststone on a large scale.

However, Mo Jiawei had already contacted Sea Star Company. As a huge mineral-resource company, Sea Star could supply not just White Froststone but even highly refined White Energy Crystals produced from it.

In short, if you had enough money, supply wasn't a problem.

Once Ladiard had finished assigning tasks, Fang Heng asked, "Are you that short of White Froststone?"

"Short? Hah, of course we are." Ladiard looked at him. "What, can you obtain some? Then again, I hear you have quite a few connections in the Federation. Perhaps you can use them?"

"I'm not that close to the Federation, but I can try." Fang Heng considered it. "As far as I know, they haven't mined much because they don't know its value, though they may have some reserves. I can't promise I'll get any."

Ladiard waved dismissively. "Fine. Go ahead and try."

"White Froststone is a special energy mineral. Extracting its usable material is difficult for the Federation, and transporting large quantities is even harder. But if I could get hold of a high-grade refined product…"

Fang Heng looked at Ladiard. "It would still be difficult. I'd like to know what you need the White Froststone for."

"Oh? And knowing that will magically help you obtain it?"

Ladiard gave him a contemptuous smile, not taking his proposal seriously.

He thought Fang Heng was boasting.

After being mined, White Froststone had to be purified using a special Tree Spirit method before it became a usable spatial energy crystal.

The process was extremely troublesome. Ordinary humans couldn't carry out the purification at all.

Not just Fang Heng—even the entire human Federation couldn't possibly produce the crystals.

At most, humans could refine the ore slightly.

Then Ladiard thought better of arguing with a human who understood nothing about it.

"Hahaha! Very well. I'll be waiting. Whatever quantity you bring, I'll take it all."

Ladiard was in an excellent mood after seizing the Wood Clan's altar, which was why he bothered talking to Fang Heng at all.

He remained convinced Fang Heng was just boasting.

Besides, White Froststone wasn't a secret among Tree Spirits. He casually explained, "Do you know about dimensional worlds?"

Fang Heng frowned.

"Ignorant human."

Ladiard shook his head with the superiority of someone who believed himself a higher life-form. "The world was originally a crystal. An external impact shattered it into countless fragments. Those fragments are the dimensional worlds."

"Larger fragments are stronger; smaller fragments are weaker. Ours is merely one of the larger worlds."

"Every fragment contains immense energy, which we badly need. But extracting natural energy on a large scale would damage a fragment severely. That's why we must enter other fragmented dimensional worlds to gather energy."

"And White Froststone is the fuel consumed by the magic arrays that allow us to enter those other dimensions. Now do you understand?"

— End of Chapter 2239 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Continuity and mechanics. Nature’s Heart Crystals are distinct from the White Froststone used to fuel teleportation. Ladiard describes dimensional worlds as fragments of an original world-crystal.

Chinese Expressions

  • 过河拆桥. Literally “demolishing the bridge after crossing it”: discarding help once it is no longer needed.
