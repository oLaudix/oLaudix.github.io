Chapter 2110

Entering

From original Chinese Chapter 2111 · 进入

A little over ten minutes later, Sandy returned, practically dragging Mo Jiawei along with him.

Mo Jiawei wore a miserable expression and looked decidedly unwilling.

In fact, he hadn't entered a game world for serious training in quite some time.

Most days, he stayed in the Zombie Apocalypse, gathering and organizing resources from various worlds, overseeing the prison's construction, contacting major factions for intelligence, and maintaining normal communications with the Federation.

He had become something like the prison's chief administrator.

That was exhausting work in its own right.

After all, there were many matters Fang Heng didn't trust anyone else to handle.

Of course, Mo Jiawei hadn't neglected his own levels.

The surplus resources from Fang Heng's leveling had been enough to raise him to level forty.

At present, Mo Jiawei was still stuck at the stage of his talent skill's second advancement.

As he put it, his reconnaissance talent had been useful early on, but it was becoming inadequate later in the game.

The second-stage talent advancement was a rare opportunity for a qualitative improvement.

If the right opportunity didn't come, he could simply wait.

Either do nothing at all or advance into the best possible talent!

After all, he had been playing the game for less than a year.

Compared with ordinary players, Mo Jiawei was already several major updates ahead.

But compared with Fang Heng, he'd fallen far behind.

He considered following Fang Heng from place to place far too dangerous, especially when there was little he could do to help. He'd rather remain at their Zombie Apocalypse headquarters.

This time, Sandy had forcibly dragged him along.

Sandy lifted Mo Jiawei's hand high in triumph. “Let's go! Fang Heng! Time to set off! We'll collect the treasures of an alien civilization! We are the great protectors of history and cultural relics!”

Amid the noisy excitement, Fang Heng stepped into the teleportation passage.

At the same time, in the Divine Realm,

“Hm?”

Ockney let out a surprised murmur.

During their earlier pursuit, Fang Heng's ambush hadn't seriously injured them, but it had left them battered and thoroughly embarrassed.

It had been a disgraceful spectacle.

Afterward, Fang Heng fled into the Zombie Apocalypse.

The game's special protection prevented even the Divine Realm from entering.

Ockney had received new instructions from the Great Sage. He was to remain on standby in the Divine Realm and lead a pursuit team out immediately if Fang Heng made another move.

The Divine Realm's attitude couldn't have been clearer.

Wasn't Fang Heng supposed to be so capable? Didn't he enjoy running around stirring up trouble everywhere?

Then they'd put a team on his heels and make sure he couldn't act freely!

What they never imagined was that Fang Heng would suddenly appear in a place they hadn't considered.

“The World of Myriad Forests.”

Ockney raised his head, concern in his eyes.

That world was deeply connected to the origins of the vampires.

The Holy Court had once investigated the World of Myriad Forests and the vampire race extensively.

They knew Fang Heng was connected to the vampires, though they hadn't possessed conclusive evidence at the time.

Besides, they hadn't thought much of Fang Heng back then.

Yet he had fled to that very world.

The Divine Realm inquisitors all turned toward Ockney.

Through Great Prophecy, they could track Fang Heng and identify the World of Myriad Forests' location. But the world was currently enveloped by a special power that barred their entry.

“Fang Heng... Is this another part of your plan?”

Ockney closed his eyes and shook his head gently.

He had exercised the utmost caution when accepting this assignment.

Yet even now, he had failed to capture Fang Heng.

This time, Fang Heng had run to the World of Myriad Forests.

Was it a desperate, impulsive choice?

Or was there another trap waiting for them?

Either way, the world was closely tied to the vampires and even to the Divine Realm's prophetic arts. Now that they had discovered it, they couldn't ignore the matter.

“You remain here and continue monitoring Fang Heng. I will report this to the Great Sage at once.”

“Understood.”

· · ·

Game notifications flashed rapidly across Fang Heng's retina.

[Game Hint: You have entered the World of Myriad Forests through Abe Akaya. Abe Akaya's remaining energy: 5/10.]

[Game Hint: You are the first player to enter the hidden special world, the World of Myriad Forests. Due to your previous quests, you have received thirty additional days of protection. During this time, no other players or factions can enter the world.]

[Game Hint: Because of the reward you selected during the prerequisite quests, you will receive additional mission reminders and recommended choices. Completing all main quests will grant you control over the hidden high-tier game world, the World of Myriad Forests.]

[Game Hint: You have triggered the optimized first-stage main quest—Tree Whisperer Alliance: Secrets of the Ancient Divine Tree.]

[Game Hint: Under the current environmental conditions, you cannot construct a high-tier spatial teleportation passage.]

[Game Hint: You will appear at a random starting location and receive a random local identity.]

[Game Hint: You have entered as a team. All team members will share the same random starting location.]

Quest Name: Tree Whisperer Alliance—Secrets of the Ancient Divine Tree.

Difficulty: C.

Quest Description: An organization devoted to nature and life exists within the World of Myriad Forests. It is known as the Tree Whisperer Alliance. Find the alliance and ask about the Ancient Divine Tree.

As the notifications dimmed, noisy conversations sounded all around him.

Fang Heng slowly opened his eyes.

A bar built in a treehouse style?

The furnishings looked vaguely medieval.

He quickly surveyed the room and confirmed his surroundings.

Yes, a bar.

All five of them were seated around a table.

Once Fang Heng collected himself, he couldn't help looking at Mo Jiawei.

And this fellow claimed his luck wasn't good enough?

On previous random teleports, being lucky meant landing somewhere in the wilderness. Being unlucky meant getting dropped directly into an abyssal cavern.

Usually, players needed at least half a day just to find a city.

How could any of that compare with spawning directly inside a tavern?

Gathering information in a tavern was far easier than doing it outside.

Sandy glanced around, apparently caught up in the lively atmosphere. Laughing heartily, he raised a beer bottle from the table. “Come, brothers! A toast to the success of our expedition!”

Under the bar lights, Klevi's little face turned red. She lifted a beer mug even larger than her face, clinked glasses with the others, and drained it in one go.

Everyone was startled by her drinking capacity.

Fang Heng took a big gulp of beer too.

Hm?

Wait. Something was strange.

The beer actually tasted perfectly normal.

And there was a DJ booth and lighting equipment?

A dance floor, too?

Fang Heng looked down at his modern clothes, then felt in his pocket and pulled out a mobile phone.

Orange 13.

Good grief. So this was a low-tech world!

Its technological level seemed fairly close to the real world.

Editing and Source Notes
  • Teleportation. All five party members spawn together due to a team-entry rule. The game says Abe Akaya has 5/10 energy remaining.
  • Technology. Although the party first expects a fantasy world, the bar has DJs, dance lighting, and modern telephones; the source describes the setting as low-technology yet comparable to the real world.
  • Phone name. 桔子13 is literally “Orange 13,” a fictional-sounding smartphone name.

Chinese Expressions

  • 灰头土脸 (huī tóu tǔ liǎn). Literally “dusty-headed and dirty-faced”; it describes the humiliation Ockney’s team suffered despite surviving Fang Heng’s ambush.
