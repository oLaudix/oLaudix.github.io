Chapter 2038

Ignored

From original Chinese Chapter 2039 · 忽略

Fang Heng ignored the game warnings and repeatedly used Blood Sacrifice to summon more teleportation passages!

A full seven gateways hovered above the amusement park.

The spatial fluctuations grew more intense as they overlapped. Even the space high above the park began to distort!

By now, Fang Heng's maximum health capacity had fallen to just one-tenth of its original level.

“Spatial fluctuations this strong should attract a few big fish…”

Fang Heng gazed at the gateways in the air and murmured to himself. Then he closed his eyes and directed his mutant infected zombie clones through the passages to join the main battlefield.

In fact, an enormous number of zombie clones had already gathered in the ant-race game world controlled by the Federation. The problem was that its teleportation entrance was too narrow, and the zombies moved too slowly for all of them to make it through in time.

But these zombies could use their vampire-summoning form to pass directly through the new portals!

And reach the battlefield immediately!

The next moment, a faint red glow lit up all seven passages suspended overhead.

Zombie clones staggered out of the other ends, fell to the ground, and slowly picked themselves up. They then streamed toward the amusement park's exits and spread outward into the city!

…

Outside,

news of the undead invasion of Duncha sent shock waves through the Holy Court!

Duncha's response was fierce.

Since when had even the supposedly weak undead dared attack Holy Court territory?!

It was an outright humiliation!

Fang Heng's actions had thoroughly enraged the Holy Court's local forces in Duncha.

Throughout its history, Duncha had been one of the Holy Court's most important lower-realm bases for storing troops and spreading its faith, supporting vast numbers of believers.

Holy Court garrisons throughout Duncha were immediately mobilized and rushed to provide support!

The first reinforcements stationed near the city arrived within ten minutes of the incident.

Only after entering the city did their commanders gradually realize the undead offensive was anything but ordinary.

There were far more undead creatures than they'd imagined!

The zombies looked slow and mindless, but possessed terrifying defenses and self-healing abilities!

Most important of all was their sheer number!

More than half the city was already filled with zombies, while Lickers darted wildly between the buildings.

And the numbers of undead continued to grow!

However, the Holy Court had amassed enormous forces around the city. This time, unlike in Chiboreya, its troops were not completely overwhelmed. They managed to contain the spread of the horde by brute force!

They relied on the quality and quantity of their troops to wear the zombies down gradually!

Even so, before further reinforcements could arrive, their rate of killing was painfully slow.

They could maintain their defenses and inch forward.

That was all they could do.

Before long, the Holy Court's communication network—temporarily disrupted by the spatial fluctuations—was restored.

The reinforcement teams connected to the network and joined an online video conference, demanding that Muhiting explain what was happening in the city.

“Muhiting, what's going on with these undead?! Where did they come from? Why are there so many of them all of a sudden?”

“I don't know!”

Even now, Muhiting remained utterly bewildered.

He had no idea where the zombies in front of him had come from.

He hadn't even seen the person responsible for this entire disaster!

The teleportation passages in the sewers had clearly been destroyed. So why had the number of undead outside continued to increase after they left the tunnels?

At the same time, Muhiting was secretly alarmed.

For so many years, the undead had been suppressed by the Holy Court and had shown no apparent desire to challenge its dominance.

The Holy Court really had grown complacent over the long passage of time.

Who could have expected the undead's strength to have increased so dramatically in secret?!

Wait!

Muhiting suddenly thought of something and looked toward the central square.

The teleportation passage!

A new teleportation passage to the Chiboreya game world had recently been constructed in the city's central square!

Could the zombies have come from there?

The thought made Muhiting's heart sink.

If he was right, that meant huge numbers of zombies had been transported through the passage from the other side.

From the Chiboreya game world!

Had something terrible happened in Chiboreya?!

“Muhiting!”

Hearing his name called during the remote video conference, Muhiting turned toward the screen.

The reinforcement teams gathered around the city's outskirts were arriving one after another.

Holy Court headquarters had joined the call as well.

Central Archbishop Xilufei appeared at the center of the large conference display, watching him with a questioning expression.

“Muhiting, have you thought of something?”

Muhiting stiffened and answered gravely, “Your Eminence, we believe the zombies originated from the central square. A teleportation passage was built there not long ago. I'm afraid…”

At his words, everyone attending the conference fell silent for a moment.

Archbishop Xilufei's face turned solemn. “Muhiting, continue containing the undead inside the city. Further reinforcements are on their way. In addition, find a way into the central square and inspect the teleportation area. We need to confirm the undead's movements.”

“Understood.”

As he spoke, several players hurried over to Muhiting. One leaned close and whispered, “Arbiter, we've just obtained aerial footage using a drone. Please have a look.”

Muhiting's eyes lit up.

Of course—he'd forgotten!

The low-level undead in the city couldn't attack aerial targets. They could use flying units to survey the central square directly from above.

The Holy Court usually resisted relying on advanced technology, but with the situation so dire, there was no room for its usual reservations. They'd use whatever worked.

“Quick! Put the footage on screen!”

The display soon switched to the reconnaissance drone's camera.

Muhiting's heart sank when he saw the images captured from above.

This was a disaster!

The very thing he'd least wanted to see had happened.

In the footage, an endless stream of zombies was pouring from the newly constructed passage in the central square!

And there were enormous numbers of them!

The passage had clearly been modified to run in overclock mode!

How had the undead gained control of the entire teleportation passage?

Something was wrong!

Muhiting immediately realized there had to be a problem within the Holy Court's own ranks.

But the situation was too urgent to worry about who had done it.

If the undead were able to enter Duncha through the passage in such numbers, then the Chiboreya game world on the other side…

was in serious trouble!
