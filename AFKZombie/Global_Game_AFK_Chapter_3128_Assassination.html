Chapter 3128

Assassination

From Chinese Chapter 3129 · 袭杀

Fang Heng!

His strength was far beyond anything he'd shown in the arena!

“Run!”

Carlton's back was soaked in cold sweat. Any desire to avenge his fallen companion was gone. He wanted only to get away from Fang Heng, as far away as possible!

“Hmph! Trying to run?”

A cold smile tugged at Fang Heng's mouth. Moving like a ghost, he appeared directly in Carlton's path of escape.

“BOOM!!!”

A fist flew forward!

Although Carlton had sensed Fang Heng coming and raised both arms to defend himself in advance, it did no good.

Overwhelmed by Fang Heng's superior attributes, he took the punch head-on and was knocked back into the area covered by sinister undead mist once again.

The other three Crystal Clan warriors trying to break out of the black fog fared no better. Every time they tried to escape, Fang Heng burst from the mist and punched them straight back inside.

Not only had they failed to flee, their injuries had only grown worse.

Despair filled the sinister undead mist. All four could feel death drawing steadily closer.

What could they do?

Carlton could almost feel his life counting down toward its end.

When they'd accepted the Kinsfolk's mission, they'd spent most of their time planning how to block Fang Heng's escape routes. They'd never imagined ending up in this situation.

Fang Heng had trapped all of them by himself?

Now they were the cornered beasts fighting for their lives!

“Bang!!!”

Another muffled explosion sounded, followed by a powerful smell of blood.

Carlton's heart jolted.

Not good!

Inside the sinister undead mist, most of his aura-sensing abilities were blocked, making it impossible to determine what was happening around him. But from the sound and the bloody smell in the air, he could tell that another companion had succumbed to his injuries and died.

“Whoosh!!”

Carlton's pupils contracted as he saw another figure spring from the black mist.

Whoosh!!

This time the attack wasn't aimed at him, but at another companion concealed within the undead mist.

“BOOM!!!”

Another dull impact rang out, accompanied by a wave of bloody odor.

“Fang Heng! Come out!”

Carlton clenched his fists tightly.

He could sense his fellow clansmen falling one by one around him, yet he could do nothing to save them!

Consumed by rage, Carlton erupted with crystalline energy and charged into the black mist, determined to take Fang Heng down with him even at the cost of his own life.

But he still couldn't do it!

Fang Heng moved like a ghost through the sinister undead mist. Carlton couldn't catch him merely by trying to lock onto his aura!

Rage boiled within Carlton, and his blood circulated faster and faster. He felt a pressure building in his chest that he couldn't release.

If it continued like this, he'd probably suffer a backlash before anything else.

No other choice!

Carlton forced himself to sense Fang Heng's location and hurled a punch toward it!

“Whoosh!!!”

A blast of ice crystals infused with crystallization power erupted in the direction of his fist!

“BOOM! Boom, boom, boom!!!”

Crystalline and elemental forces combined into a series of violent explosions inside the undead mist.

Damn it!

Damn it!

He'd missed again! Carlton's heart sank. Then he heard a calm voice beside his ear.

“Not bad. Your aim needs work, though.”

Carlton's pupils contracted.

“BOOM!!!”

An immensely heavy punch smashed into Carlton's abdomen again. Cracks instantly covered his body as he was sent flying backward once more, coughing up blood.

Black mist penetrated layer after layer of his crystallized abdomen until it burst apart with a pop, scattering pink crystalline dust through the air.

The broken upper half of Carlton's body rolled across the ground. The light slowly faded from his eyes before he exploded on the spot with a bang, leaving a cloud of bloody mist.

Not far away, inside the car, Anxi anxiously watched the area ahead engulfed by black fog.

She couldn't see a thing. All she could hear were repeated muffled impacts.

The impacts were accompanied by a heavy scent of blood.

Ten minutes later, as the black mist slowly dispersed, Anxi saw Fang Heng emerge from it.

Where were the others?

Were they all dead?

Anxi swallowed nervously as she watched Fang Heng stroll back to the car and open the rear door.

“It's taken care of. We can go back now.”

“O-okay…”

Anxi glanced toward the area still shrouded in black energy, but didn't dare ask further questions. She started the car, turned around, and drove Fang Heng home by another route.

“Mr. Fang Heng, those men just now were sent by the Kinsfolk.”

“Mm. I know.”

Fang Heng responded quietly and opened his palm.

More than a dozen small, white crystalline beads rested in his hand.

Law Orbs.

Swallowing them helped a person comprehend the surrounding laws, making them very useful for cultivation.

The Kinsfolk pursuers he'd killed previously had carried similar items.

He'd just recovered more than a dozen Law Orbs from the warriors he'd killed.

Earlier, he and Nasir had discussed a theory that the Kinsfolk were using these things to control the warriors they recruited.

If that was true, a never-ending stream of warriors might come after him from now on.

Fang Heng slowly clenched his fist, anger stirring within him.

If the Kinsfolk kept hunting him, when would he have any time left to cultivate?

Seeing that Fang Heng didn't seem inclined to speak and that his expression remained gloomy, Anxi obediently kept quiet.

As she sat behind the wheel, unease gradually settled over her. Ever since they'd been attacked by people apparently sent by the Kinsfolk, she could sense that Fang Heng was suppressing his anger.

Half an hour later, the car stopped outside the villa.

“Mr. Fang Heng, we're here.” Anxi looked at him in the rearview mirror, hesitated, then couldn't help advising him, “Please give us a little more time. The Noel Clan will take responsibility for your safety. Please don't enter into a direct confrontation with the Kinsfolk.”

“Mm. I understand. Thank you.”

Fang Heng got out, entered the villa, and shut the door behind him.

Since the Kinsfolk dared openly send assassins after him, he had no objection to teaching them a lesson.

With that thought, Fang Heng flexed his wrists and slowly walked toward the basement.

On the seventh underground level, black mist had accumulated into a thick layer.

Fang Heng swept his gaze across the large hall.

His zombie clones were gathered in the basement, endlessly constructing magic arrays to grind experience.

Thin wisps of black mist leaked from their bodies.

It was strange, now that he thought about it.

Why were the Kinsfolk so afraid of the game's black mist when other races didn't seem to care about it at all?

Never mind that for now.

Fang Heng entered his spirit form, left the villa, and headed for the Kinsfolk headquarters district.

…

The Kinsfolk had no idea what had happened inside the Lanyue Clan's territory after their agents left. At first, they'd assumed that their plan to kill Fang Heng had succeeded.

During the following month of investigations, they'd adopted an attitude of killing the innocent rather than risking letting anyone escape. Pan-human races connected to the matter were directly eliminated.

Kappa and Korkalot had also been detained by the Kinsfolk, who intended to use them as bait to lure their target into the open.

But after more than a month, everything seemed to have returned to normal.

The black mist hadn't appeared again.

Fang Heng wasn't certain exactly where Kappa and Korkalot were being held, and he suspected the Kinsfolk might have deliberately kept them alive as bait. Until the Noel Clan clarified the situation, he decided not to attempt a direct rescue. Instead, he'd make trouble for the Kinsfolk and retaliate as much as possible.

By spreading the black mist, for example.

— End of Chapter 3128 —
