Chapter 3096

Reinforcement

From Chinese Chapter 3097 · 加固

“Is that so? Thank you.” Fang Heng nodded and thought for a moment. “Take me to the library first.”

“Certainly. Your Alchemy Association membership card has already been issued. Please follow me.”

The association had invested enormous resources in constructing its library.

Members could enter and consult the materials, but they weren't allowed to borrow books.

In truth, scholars who had reached Grandmaster level usually possessed impressive mental attributes. Even if they couldn't fully understand a magic array, most could force themselves to memorize it within a short time.

Borrowing books was unnecessary.

Fang Heng entered the library hall and began searching the shelves for books on law-type magic arrays.

He reached a corner.

With a light gesture, he brought down a book on natural laws from a high shelf.

He opened it and turned the pages one by one, nodding frequently.

He rapidly committed the various runes used to manipulate laws to memory.

Two hours passed unnoticed.

Fang Heng closed the book.

A game notification appeared.

[Hint: Player has finished reading a book (first reading). Player gained comprehension. Alchemy (Transcendent) Skill EXP +1.28 million.]

Right.

He remembered that reading books could also increase experience.

Gaining 1.28 million experience from one book meant it was extremely valuable.

Unfortunately, the library contained at least tens of thousands of books. There was no way to take them all home and grind experience.

After considering it, Fang Heng returned the book to a gap on a high shelf.

Better to play it safe. He could have the zombies continue grinding experience in the villa's basement just as well.

He walked roughly through the rest of the library without finding anything on Black Mist-related alchemical arrays. Disappointed, he approached the librarian.

“The Black Mist game?”

The librarian gave Fang Heng a surprised look, considered it, and pointed to the right. “Section B, all the way at the end. I remember two books on the second shelf.”

“Thank you.”

Following the directions, Fang Heng soon discovered two slightly yellowed handwritten volumes in a corner.

Black Mist that spread through certain corners of the universe.

Locals referred to it as a kind of Main God's game.

Those who participated were called players, and they could acquire tremendous power from the game.

It had been verified that at least a dozen previously obscure, weak races had grown rapidly stronger through the Black Mist game.

The seven great overlord races of the Martial Capital didn't share a unified opinion of the Black Mist.

Two factions, led by the Kinsfolk, strongly opposed its spread. They regarded it as an extremely dangerous virus capable of harming the whole universe and believed it should be eliminated by every possible means.

Most remaining powers were far calmer. They believed Black Mist hadn't reached the threat level described by the Kinsfolk. Although low-ranked races had indeed grown stronger through it, nobody had found evidence that it influenced minds or caused other harmful effects.

Thus Black Mist hadn't been formally classified as harmful.

Nevertheless, given its high infectivity and potential unknown risks, the Seven-Realm Martial Capital had signed an agreement at the Kinsfolk's strong insistence to conduct regular inspections for Black Mist and prevent its spread.

Similar screening measures operated inside the capital to prevent Black Mist from infiltrating unnoticed and causing irreparable consequences.

The book had been written more than a century earlier, when most discovered Black Mist regions lay within Kinsfolk-controlled territory. Its author suspected there was much more Black Mist elsewhere in the universe that the capital hadn't detected.

Another noteworthy observation was that pan-human races seemed more susceptible to its influence.

Fang Heng turned the pages, reflecting on what he read.

Both books clearly came from the same author, who had been intensely interested in Black Mist and researched it extensively. Most of their contents described the phenomenon.

After reading them, Fang Heng concluded that the capital knew surprisingly little. Its scholars apparently weren't even aware of the game worlds created by Black Mist, only that different races gained great power from it.

At last, when he reached the final page of the second book, his eyes lit up.

A magic array that could influence Black Mist!

Every known type of power struggled to eliminate Black Mist completely. The author suspected it belonged to a higher-dimensional level of power and therefore couldn't be directly dispersed.

However, the author had obtained a medium-sized magic array during the research that could attract and concentrate Black Mist within a small area.

Interesting.

It wasn't an array for eliminating the mist, but one that gathered it was useful too.

Better than nothing.

It might prove remarkably effective someday.

Fang Heng attempted to memorize the array.

[Hint: Player finished reading a book. Player gained comprehension. Alchemy (Transcendent) Skill EXP +25,000.]

He returned both books to the shelf and left the library, pondering how to use the newly discovered Black Mist-gathering array to handle the mist in his basement.

“Mr. Fang Heng.”

Anxi couldn't enter the library and had been waiting outside. Seeing him emerge, she asked, “Did you find the books you needed?”

“Mm, I found them.” Fang Heng nodded, took out pen and paper, and quickly wrote down a list of materials. “Anxi, I need supplies for arranging magic arrays. Please prepare these and send them directly to my villa.”

“Certainly. I'll arrange it immediately.”

...

Back at the villa, Fang Heng's first task was to inspect the basement.

Dense Black Mist covered the entire seventh underground level.

Fortunately...

After maxing out mysticism training, the zombies had stopped practicing.

While remaining motionless, the clones emitted Black Mist at roughly half their previous rate.

Fang Heng relaxed a little, then used his new knowledge of laws to reinforce the basement's sealing arrays and prevent leaks.

Once that was done, he redesigned the layout and placed dozens of additional natural-law arrays around the villa, increasing the activity of natural-law energy throughout the grounds.

It was an enormous task, and his zombie clones couldn't arrange law-level magic arrays for him.

Consequently, Fang Heng worked without sleep until the afternoon of the third day before finally completing the project.

“Phew!”

Fang Heng stretched and returned to the seventh basement level.

With the reinforced seals complete, the basement could contain a higher concentration of Black Mist than before.

He calculated roughly on his fingers.

While he was in seclusion, his AFK training had raised mysticism to Grandmaster level.

Next, he needed to personally absorb and convert natural energy to raise the skill's level.

Alchemy had also entered the Transcendent level.

The zombies could handle grinding experience in that discipline.

But the Black Mist in the basement remained a ticking time bomb.

At the current rate, he probably had another week at most.
