Chapter 3097

Hitting the Jackpot

From Chinese Chapter 3098 · 发达

Following his memory, Fang Heng quickly constructed a small magic array on the ground.

“Whoosh!!!”

When he poured mental power into it, the surrounding Black Mist immediately gathered toward the array.

The concentration rose sharply, forming a visibly dense region of dark mist around the formation.

It worked, but how was he supposed to use it?

Fang Heng shook his head.

Whatever. Start grinding first and think of a solution as he went!

He stopped worrying and ordered the zombies to sit down and construct simple alchemical arrays on the ground to grind experience.

While the zombies cultivated alchemy, he'd use the time to grind mysticism.

They'd all grind together!

After setting their AFK routines, Fang Heng sat down in the basement and directed Abe Akaya to release natural energy.

Pale-green natural energy flowed from Abe Akaya's vines and gathered underground, soon forming a rich emerald glow.

Natural energy and Black Mist seemed like two entirely different kinds of power, neither affecting the other.

With the huge increase in natural-energy activity throughout the villa, both the rate at which Abe Akaya released natural power and the rate at which Fang Heng absorbed it rose dramatically.

Excellent stuff.

Fang Heng's eyes lit up.

The natural energy was so active that he could easily control it with his mind and absorb it into his body.

By his rough calculations, he could absorb it more than twice as fast as before.

Grind!

Lines of small notifications rapidly flooded his retina.

[Hint: Player absorbed mysticism-attribute energy. Grandmaster Mysticism EXP +8,723.]

[Hint: Player's zombie clone completed an alchemical magic array (Poor). Transcendent Alchemy EXP +0.6...]

[Hint: Player's zombie clone...]

...

“Ring, ring, ring...”

In the basement, Fang Heng was absorbed in grinding levels when his phone rang.

Annoyed at the interruption, he suppressed his irritation and checked it.

Hm? An unknown number?

Puzzled, Fang Heng nevertheless answered.

“Hello?”

“Brother Fang Heng, it's Nasir. I got your number from Anxi.”

“Brother Nasir.”

“I didn't see you today. Have you finished your secluded training? How did it go?”

Nasir was quite concerned about Fang Heng and often stopped by the Alchemy Association to check on him.

When he came by today and learned Fang Heng had gone home, he immediately called to ask how he was doing.

Nasir was worried.

Had the difficulty of reaching Transcendent alchemy caused the youngster to give up?

It would be such a shame if this rare alchemy genius abandoned his efforts.

“Mm, I've made a breakthrough and finished that stage of training, so I left the association. Haha, I should've let you know, Brother Nasir. My apologies.”

“What? You broke through?!”

Nasir was stunned on the other end of the phone.

He'd advanced to Transcendent alchemy in just one week?!

Was this some kind of joke?!

Nasir opened his mouth, then immediately exclaimed, “Brother Fang, you're home now, right? Stay right there. I'll come see you immediately!”

Fang Heng froze, only to discover that Nasir had already hung up.

He'd heard the man's excitement through the phone.

Was it because he'd advanced so quickly?

That made sense.

People outside the Black Mist game probably couldn't comprehend such cultivation speeds.

Nasir was probably Transcendent-level himself. He was highly sensitive to surrounding laws and might detect the Black Mist.

Fortunately, Fang Heng had reinforced the basement seals again. Otherwise there might have been a genuine risk of discovery.

Fang Heng closed his eyes and resumed grinding mysticism experience while he could.

More than an hour later, Nasir hurried to the villa.

Before even entering, he stopped in his tracks.

Something was wrong!

Very wrong!

Why were the surrounding natural-energy fluctuations so incredibly active?

Could it be...?

Surely not?

A frightening suspicion rose in Nasir's heart, and he closed his eyes to sense the area.

Good grief!

The environmental fluctuations of natural energy really were being altered by alchemical magic arrays!

Had Fang Heng arranged them?

Of course!

Transcendent alchemists weren't as common as cabbages. If it wasn't Fang Heng, who else could it be?

A chill ran down Nasir's spine. He felt as if he'd seen a ghost.

Successfully testing a law-type array inside the association's training hall might qualify someone as a Transcendent alchemist, but arranging a functioning law-type array in the outside world was another matter entirely.

The two tasks belonged to completely different difficulty levels!

Nasir himself had spent more than a year after leaving the training hall before successfully constructing his first law-type array outside.

And this youngster had...

Damn it! Could his talent really be that monstrous?!

Shocked, Nasir drew a deep breath and approached the villa.

The sight almost made his eyes pop from their sockets.

What in the world?!

The garden surrounding the villa was covered with one alchemical magic array after another.

Even the villa's outer walls were engraved with compact magic arrays.

Good heavens!

Nasir had asked and knew Fang Heng left the association two evenings earlier. Had he really arranged so many arrays in barely two days?

And every one was a law-type array.

The more law-type arrays installed in a single area, the more they disturbed the environment, making later designs and arrangements much more troublesome.

The sheer computational burden of this dense arrangement made Nasir's scalp tingle just looking at it.

He took a deep breath, wiped the sweat from his forehead, and rang the villa's doorbell.

“Nasir, you're here. Please come in.”

Fang Heng was waiting in the hall and gestured for him to enter.

Although Nasir was already sure, he couldn't resist asking, “Those alchemical arrays outside... did you arrange all of them?”

“Haha, sorry you had to see that.”

Fang Heng laughed a little sheepishly. After arranging the arrays, he'd noticed himself that the densely covered grounds weren't particularly attractive.

“After advancing, I was itching to try things out. I couldn't resist practicing a little.”

“You...” Nasir was at a loss for words. He was genuinely convinced now. Shaking his head, he sighed. “Brother Fang Heng, I mean this sincerely: you're the most talented alchemist I've ever met. Even the association president doesn't have your level of talent.”

“That's too much praise. Without your guidance, Brother Nasir, I couldn't have achieved this.”

Fang Heng saluted him with cupped fists and thanked him again.

“Hahaha!” Nasir laughed. “Excellent. When you've become famous, I'll be able to boast that I once taught you alchemy.”
