Chapter 2945

World

From original Chinese Chapter 2946 · 世界

As the military's representative, Captain Giselle would cooperate with Fang Heng throughout the mission.

The players had accepted an S+-rank assignment.

They were to enter World AU-092, establish a permanent base, expand a Level 5 warp passage, explore the planet, determine whether any traces of the Ouroboros Seed existed, and collect high-tier resources.

Above all, they needed to establish the base.

They already knew that AU-092 contained a special mineral, an exceptionally rare material required to build advanced starships.

The Kinsfolk desperately needed it.

Some time earlier, three starship teams contracted by the Kinsfolk to develop the world had been attacked by an unknown enemy.

All three teams had been wiped out before they could even send a message.

Captain Giselle gathered the accompanying researchers and nodded to Fang Heng. "Fang Heng, all thirty-two researchers you requested are assembled. They're under your command."

"Thank you."

The thirty-two researchers Fang Heng had selected stared at him in confusion.

They had abruptly received emergency notices about a special mission and had been drafted straight into the front lines.

Given a choice, they certainly wouldn't have volunteered to visit a hellhole like this.

Su Guantong stood behind Fang Heng, watching Li Zhixuan among the thirty-two with growing astonishment.

She never would have imagined that after mentioning Li Zhixuan to Fang Heng only the night before, he would have brought her straight to her today.

The entire research team would take part in the construction mission.

That made it incredibly easy for Su Guantong to contact Li Zhixuan and complete the intelligence handover along the way.

"Everyone, you've been summoned here on short notice to identify, research, and refine ore, as well as establish a base. Don't worry too much. The Kinsfolk will provide construction robots to assist us later."

Fang Heng looked across the group and pointed at Li Zhixuan. "You. You'll lead the research team for now. Select a few people to join the exploration party and survey the environment."

Li Zhixuan was surprised to be singled out, but she nodded and began choosing survey personnel.

The crew aboard the starship quickly got to work.

The first exploration team circled the ship after disembarking, checking the surrounding area for danger. Once they confirmed it was safe, a second group of researchers went outside to collect environmental samples and identify a suitable construction site.

Fang Heng glanced off to one side.

With Su Guantong's abilities, she should have no trouble finding a chance to pass information to Li Zhixuan.

"By the way, who are those people?"

Fang Heng approached Captain Giselle and gestured discreetly toward several Federation personnel being helped into combat suits.

"A team of experts sent by the Federation Council. They'll accompany us and supervise the work throughout. Their assignment was personally approved by the President's Office."

Giselle wasn't fond of these amateurs either, but they were presidential appointees. There wasn't much he could do about them.

"Really... All this for a mere A-rank assignment? Even the President's Office got involved..."

For a moment, Captain Giselle had no idea how to respond.

This was no ordinary A-rank mission.

They were in the real world!

If Giselle hadn't trusted Fang Heng's strength, the military would never have undertaken this journey alone.

Under the circumstances, sending presidential representatives wasn't unreasonable.

"Forget it."

Fang Heng spread his hands.

He would simply ignore them.

Time to get to work!

Every report confirmed that the planet contained abundant ore deposits!

Nothing complicated about it!

Just farm resources!

Build his starship as quickly as possible!

...

Ten minutes later, the Federation troops began exploring the area around the starship.

They had visited many worlds before, but only twice had they entered a game world in physical form.

Even inside the game, they had never encountered conditions this hostile.

Sandstorms covered the entire planet.

Their instruments even detected areas of spatial turbulence.

The moment anyone left the starship's protective barrier, their suit's energy began draining at a frightening rate.

"Boss Fang."

Zaka, bundled tightly into his protective suit, spoke quietly. "This place is a nightmare. Our suits are losing energy fast. I doubt we'll be able to operate outside the ship for long."

Among everyone present, Fang Heng was the only one without any protective equipment whatsoever.

The savage sandstorm seemed entirely incapable of harming him.

"Mm."

Fang Heng nodded slightly.

The atmosphere was intensely corrosive.

Even the starship's reserve energy was falling rapidly, never mind ordinary protective suits.

Fang Heng flipped his wrist, produced several rune papers, and tossed them into the distance.

Bang! Bang!

The papers exploded in the air. Several Lickers sprang out, landed, and raced across the desert before disappearing from sight.

Even Lickers were affected by such an extreme environment.

Gravity here was higher than in ordinary worlds, reducing some of their movement attributes. The corrosive toxins in the atmosphere also inflicted continuous damage.

Of course, their Undying Body effect completely negated that damage.

"Fang Heng, the sandstorm reduces visibility to almost nothing. Surviving here is risky enough. If enemies attack, our combat effectiveness will be severely compromised."

Captain Zolan also approached with several soldiers.

Among them was Shilock, leader of the Inspectorate's observation team, who nodded to Fang Heng with an ingratiating smile.

Shilock understood Fang Heng's strength and had no desire to offend him. He had only come along to coast through the mission.

The Emery incident had dealt a severe blow to the Inspectorate, drastically reducing its influence within the Federation.

The entire department was now focused on hunting Emery down and redeeming its reputation. No one had any interest in provoking Fang Heng.

"We've confirmed that the sandstorm isn't restricted to this region. It's a global phenomenon. The environment is extremely hostile, and the storm also produces severe electromagnetic interference, disrupting long-range communications and drastically reducing sensor range."

"Worse still, the energy stored in our defensive barriers is falling rapidly."

"If we encounter monsters and enter combat, our energy reserves will drain even faster." Captain Giselle frowned. "We didn't bring much reserve energy. I hope we can find a local energy source soon, or we won't last long."
