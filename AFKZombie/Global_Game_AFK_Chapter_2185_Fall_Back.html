Chapter 2185

Fall Back

From original Chinese Chapter 2186 · 后退

The metal plates that had encased the Electronic Light Spirit were sent flying by the explosion, embedding themselves deep in the surrounding walls.

Bzzzt, bzzzt…

As the black mist rapidly dissipated, everyone looked up and saw a glowing sphere suspended in the center of the room.

Had it worked?

Fang Heng watched the floating sphere warily.

The Electronic Light Spirit.

Levin's expression turned extremely grave.

He could sense an extraordinarily dangerous presence emanating from it.

Bzzzt… bzzzt, bzzzt…

After a burst of static in his earpiece, Ji Haiting's voice came through again.

“Fang Heng? Can you hear me? Fang Heng!”

“Yes, I can hear you.”

Fang Heng eyed the sphere cautiously. “Is that the Electronic Light Spirit's actual body?”

“Fang Heng, our communications with the Dependents have just stopped completely. The Spirit Domain blockade network has also been affected, and its operational readings have spiked. I'll try to conceal the anomalies, but the Federation will discover them soon. Whatever you do…”

Before he could finish, electrical currents sprang up around the Electronic Light Spirit, and its entire spherical body rapidly expanded!

The link to Ji Haiting was disrupted and cut off again!

Bzzzt… bzzzt… BZZZT!

The Electronic Light Spirit absorbed an immense quantity of power, filling the hall with blue light.

It seemed to be drawing enormous amounts of energy from the entire Spirit Domain blockade network.

Fang Heng sensed the power contained in its body climbing at a terrifying rate!

“Vannis! Attack! Destroy it!”

Sensing danger, Levin took the initiative!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

A dense volley of energy arrows shot from the Tree Spirits' hands toward the glowing sphere!

Sensing the attacks, the Electronic Light Spirit suddenly stopped pulsating.

Hiss… hiss, hiss…

Countless specks of blue light materialized in midair.

They drew the incoming energy arrows toward themselves.

“BANG! Bang, bang!”

One after another, the arrows made of natural energy exploded as they were drawn into the lights.

Every attack failed without exception!

Then a visible curtain of blue energy condensed in front of the Electronic Light Spirit.

Like a solid wall, it advanced rapidly toward them!

What was that?

Sensing the energy fluctuations in the curtain, Vannis immediately shouted, “Smash it!”

The Tree Spirits fired another dense volley of arrows at the blue barrier.

“BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!”

The countless arrows struck the curtain and exploded on contact!

Holding his sacred greatsword, Fang Heng dashed straight toward it.

“CRACK!”

His blade smashed heavily into the blue curtain.

Crack, crack, crack…

Its forward movement slowed briefly, and cracks spread across the point struck by the sacred greatsword.

“Keep going!”

Fang Heng shouted and swung the massive sword again and again with all his strength!

Behind him, the Tree Spirits unleashed another barrage of energy arrows, pounding the curtain without pause.

“BOOOOM!”

After dozens of successive blows, the energy curtain finally gave way and shattered.

Seeing the blue specks left behind by the explosion, Fang Heng felt something stir.

Hm?

What were those?

He sensed an unusual energy fluctuation emanating from them.

Moreover, the lights seemed drawn toward him, drifting slowly in his direction.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng swung his greatsword again, trying to smash the specks approaching him!

“What?”

Surprise flashed in his eyes.

His sword passed straight through them.

And at the very instant it passed through, he felt a trace of his own strength being siphoned away.

Not good!

There was definitely something wrong with these lights!

Recognizing the danger, Fang Heng immediately activated Secondary Space Projection. His body gradually melted into space as he retreated rapidly.

Behind him, Vannis had barely begun to relax after the curtain broke when he noticed something strange.

The tiny blue lights left by its destruction were drifting and gathering rapidly around them!

Something was wrong with those things!

Vannis sensed the danger at once!

“Destroy them!”

A dense rain of energy arrows blanketed the lights!

But it was useless!

The arrows simply passed through them.

Far from being damaged, the pale-blue specks grew even brighter.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

They suddenly accelerated, rushing toward the Tree Spirits!

The Tree Spirits looked shocked.

That power…?!

As the specks drew closer, the Tree Spirits sensed the energy inside their bodies being pulled out of them against their will.

Vannis watched helplessly as countless specks floated toward him, terror appearing in his eyes.

The power inside his body was slowly being extracted.

And there was nothing he could do!

Only when facing the Divine Tree had he ever experienced such complete suppression.

The Electronic Light Spirit's power could actually rival the Divine Tree!

“Fall back!”

At that critical moment, Levin shouted and drove his power to its absolute limit!

Brilliant pale-green light bloomed in his palm!

The Azure Lotus Seed.

A cyan seed in Levin's hand radiated light, unfurling into a protective barrier that sheltered the clansmen behind him.

Beneath its glow, the Tree Spirits’ wounds rapidly healed.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The blue specks throughout the hall were drawn toward the azure lotus in Levin's hand!

Every one of them was stopped by the barrier.

In moments, a layer of blue lights had accumulated around the edge of the lotus's glow.

Levin's expression was exceptionally grim.

The power within him was draining away at an alarming rate!

Or rather, the Azure Lotus Seed was absorbing it!

The seed was a sacred inheritance relic of the Leaf Clan, but using it consumed its bearer's life force.

Levin was horrified.

The blue lights could even absorb the power released by the Lotus of Life.

And that was accelerating the depletion of his life force!

Seeing Levin block the attack, Fang Heng stepped out of Secondary Space Projection and positioned himself behind him, staring at the lights with an unusual gleam in his eyes.

This was bad!

The Tree Spirits had already brought out their trump card, yet far from damaging the Electronic Light Spirit, they hadn't even managed to touch its true body.

“Grand Elder!”

Having caught their breath, several Tree Spirits immediately tried to move forward to help.

“Stay back!”

Struggling against the rapid drain on his life force, Levin shouted, “That's an order! Withdraw from here immediately!”

Vannis knew Levin was burning his own life force to sustain the Azure Lotus Seed. He clenched his fists, unwillingness flashing in his eyes.

“Grand Elder!”

“As we are now, we have no chance of defeating the Electronic Light Spirit. Go back. Tell Senzo to persuade all the clans to use the Divine Tree's remaining branches. That's our last chance.”
