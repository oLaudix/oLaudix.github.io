Chapter 3021

Appearance

From Chinese Chapter 3022 · 现身

Dean watched Fang Heng charge into the crowd of Hell monsters alone, helplessness showing in his eyes.

He couldn't shake the feeling that the brat had talked him into something.

Why did it feel as if he'd come all the way to Hell just to act as Fang Heng's assistant?

Dean shook his head and put the thought aside. He concentrated on coordinating and commanding the Holy Court forces from the rear while keeping a close eye on Fang Heng, ready to come to his aid.

Even without a Demon Seed joining the battle, so many high-level Hell creatures would give the Holy Court plenty of trouble!

Fortunately, Fang Heng was strong enough to hold the front line single-handedly, drawing the vast majority of the Hell creatures' attacks.

Dean reckoned that even if he personally joined the fighting, he probably couldn't do a better job.

That brat!

Watching Fang Heng dart back and forth among the elite monsters, killing one after another, Dean couldn't help praising him inwardly.

They had only met briefly in the Divine Realm last time, but Dean could clearly feel that Fang Heng had grown even stronger since then.

“Whoosh!”

Here it came!

Dean's pupils contracted. His gaze shot toward the distant cavern ceiling.

As a mighty aura erupted overhead, the layer of molten rock began to stir. Dark-red veins of magma appeared, and a long, illusory fissure suddenly opened, revealing the shadow of a pair of eyes.

A hideous demonic face gradually took shape on the lava-coated ceiling.

“Roar!!!”

The demon roared. Its massive right arm, formed from molten rock and wreathed in boiling magma, punched toward Fang Heng!

“Get out of Hell, you Holy Court scum!”

“Hmph!”

Fang Heng snorted and swung his staff directly at the enormous lava fist!

“Boom!!!!”

With a deafening explosion, the hideous demon on the ceiling shattered completely under Fang Heng's strike, sending scalding chunks of lava flying in every direction.

A woman's figure emerged from the shattered stone.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes at the scantily clad young woman.

Her figure could only be described as perfect; merely standing there, she was enough to set hearts racing.

Eseklyna!

Fang Heng lightly licked his lips, excitement appearing in his eyes.

The Envy Demon Seed's true body.

Finally, I've found you!

“Whoosh!!”

Before Eseklyna could speak, Fang Heng suddenly accelerated and charged straight at her.

Eseklyna frowned slightly.

“Whoosh!!”

Dark, curling, ribbon-like tendrils emerged from the gaps in Eseklyna's clothes.

Tongues of Envy!

The Tongues of Envy unfolded and twisted in midair as though alive. Thousands of scarlet eyes suddenly opened across their surfaces, each shining with a strange light that seemed to draw everything around it inward.

Psychic illusion!

[Hint: The player has been struck by an illusion-type effect.]

[Hint: The player's Omniscient Eye has taken effect. Illusion dispelled.]

Fang Heng lost focus for an instant, then his eyes immediately cleared. Sacred power enveloped his God-Bestowed Staff as he swept it forward with a fierce whistle.

“Whoosh!!!”

Huh?

The Tongues of Envy were weightless and offered no resistance, as though they existed within space itself. The staff passed through them without affecting them at all, like two objects occupying different dimensions.

Not good!

Fang Heng swiftly dodged to the right.

A moment too late.

Three Tongues of Envy had already fastened onto his right leg and wrapped around it tightly.

“Hee-hee-hee... Little brother...”

Eseklyna let out a string of eerie giggles and lunged at Fang Heng like a ghost.

“Hmph!”

Fang Heng snorted and dodged right again, glancing down out of the corner of his eye.

The eyes on the dark Tongues of Envy wrapped around his leg clung stubbornly to his body.

The power within him was slowly flowing toward those eyes beyond his control.

Energy absorption?

Interest appeared in Fang Heng's eyes.

Eseklyna probably used this ability to absorb the power of other Demon Seeds for herself.

“Whoosh!!!”

Eseklyna materialized at Fang Heng's right side like an apparition.

“Whoosh!!!”

Sensing her position in advance, Fang Heng spun around and threw a punch without another word!

Layer upon layer of threadlike Tongues of Envy instantly spread in front of Eseklyna.

“Boom!”

One punch!

Fang Heng's fist pierced straight through the layers of demonic tongues and struck Eseklyna, blowing her body apart in midair!

Huh!? He hit her!?

Not far away, Dean was directing the Holy Court troops to retreat out of the cavern to gain more room to maneuver when he suddenly saw Fang Heng smash Eseklyna apart with a single punch.

What tremendous impact.

But something seemed wrong.

“Hee-hee-hee... Little brother, how cruel of you...”

“Whoosh!!”

Eseklyna's shattered body re-formed a short distance from Fang Heng.

Dean stared gravely at the resurrected Eseklyna.

He'd known a Demon Seed wouldn't be that easy to kill.

But it was strange. That punch had definitely connected, and from what Dean saw, it hadn't struck an illusion.

What sort of special ability was this?

Fang Heng withdrew his fist and looked toward the revived Eseklyna on his right.

He had just smashed her apart, but at the same moment his fist connected, Tongues of Envy covered in hideous red eyes had attached themselves to his skin.

[Hint: The player's sacred power is being drained...]

[Warning: Under the influence of an unknown force, the player's current Grand Sage bloodline experience is permanently decreasing...]

[Warning: An unknown effect is draining the player's power. Exercise caution.]

Interesting.

Fang Heng glanced over the game notifications.

The Tongues of Envy could siphon the sacred energy from his body, weakening him. Added to that were Eseklyna's unknown defense and resurrection abilities...

“Hee-hee-hee! What's wrong? Not going to continue?”

Eseklyna's voice changed, and her formerly delicate expression turned vicious. “Then die!”

Hundreds of dark Tongues of Envy spilled from Eseklyna's body and surged at Fang Heng once more.

Fang Heng tapped the ground lightly and dodged at speed while considering his options.

The situation completely reversed. Fang Heng now had to weave through a dense mass of Tongues of Envy.

“Hahahaha! Wasn't this fun? Why aren't you playing anymore?”

Eseklyna had gained complete control of the battlefield. Her eyes gleamed with excitement as she savored the pleasure of tormenting him.

Dean watched anxiously from the side, repeatedly wanting to step in and rescue him.

But Fang Heng had specifically told him not to provide support unless he asked for help.
