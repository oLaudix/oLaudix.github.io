Chapter 2115

Exploration

From original Chinese Chapter 2116 · 探索

Glenan hadn't expected Fang Heng to be so bold. He immediately became nervous. “If the surveillance system goes down, Wanling Technology will definitely notice. They'll...”

“Don't worry, young man. The God of Nature will punish those who desecrate the ruins,” Sandy said, patting Glenan on the shoulder. “Lead the way. Let's go.”

The group crouched low and followed Glenan along a narrow path into the forest.

Before long, flashlight beams appeared ahead.

Glenan stopped at once, his voice tense. “Someone's coming. It must be Wanling Technology's people. What should we do?”

“Stay back. I'll handle this.”

Fang Heng took a demon mask out of his backpack and put it on, then walked forward alone.

Not far away, several Wanling Technology security guards were patrolling.

More than a dozen surveillance cameras had suddenly failed. That was obviously abnormal.

A five-man security team moved cautiously along the path with flashlights, checking the situation.

“It's got to be those forest-protection activists.”

“What a pain, coming out here in the middle of the night. Now we'll have to fill in repair forms tomorrow and deal with all this nonsense...”

“Destroying company property. Once we catch them, a few years in jail ought to make them behave...”

The men chatted quietly as they moved forward carrying firearms.

Suddenly, one guard spotted something flashing through the darkness and jumped.

“Who's there? Identify yourself!”

A figure appeared rapidly from the gloom ahead.

The stranger was wearing a bizarre demon mask.

“Stop!”

The guards raised their weapons together and aimed at Fang Heng.

“Don't move! Hands up! This is private property belonging to Wanling Technology! You're suspected of destroying company property and trespassing. Raise your hands immediately, or we'll take necessary measures!”

Fang Heng ignored them and kept walking toward them.

The captain's brows rose. “I said stop!”

Whoosh!

The next instant, everything blurred before the captain's eyes.

Bang! Bang!

Two sharp impacts sounded.

The two guards beside him flew away in opposite directions.

In the blink of an eye, only the captain was left standing.

“D-don't...”

Panic-stricken, he pointed his gun at Fang Heng and pulled the trigger.

Click...

Nothing happened?

Looking down, he discovered that his weapon had already been crushed to pieces in the stranger's hand.

Fang Heng lifted the captain off the ground with one hand and said coldly, “Tell me what's happening inside the ruins.”

“I-I really don't know! We're only responsible for security around the perimeter. We've never been allowed into the ruins. We have no idea what's happening inside...”

“What a pity.”

Fang Heng flung his arm outward!

The captain felt the world spin as he flew through the air, then struck a nearby tree trunk with a heavy thud and lost consciousness.

Mo Jiawei and the others came forward from behind.

Glenan stared at the scene, mouth open. Clearly, he hadn't expected the God of Nature to solve problems quite so violently. He looked at Fang Heng. “B-Brother Fang... this... um...”

“Don't worry. I know what I'm doing. They'll wake up tomorrow.”

Fang Heng strode past the unconscious guards. “Let's keep moving.”

Following the forest path, they soon spotted a recently established camp.

The tents bore Wanling Technology's logo.

The group hid in the shadows.

Mo Jiawei studied the camp carefully and whispered, “How long have they been here?”

“A little over a month, counting from when they officially took control of the forest.”

“Mm.” Fang Heng nodded to the others. “I'll go take a look inside. Wait here.”

He headed toward the camp alone.

The others remained concealed among the trees, watching him.

As Fang Heng moved forward, his figure became progressively fainter. Before he'd even reached the camp perimeter, he'd merged completely into the surrounding space.

Glenan rubbed his eyes.

Where had he gone? Had he disappeared?

Five minutes later, Fang Heng had used his secondary-space projection to infiltrate the camp and sweep through it completely. He strolled openly out and returned the way he'd come.

Sandy asked, “Boss Fang, what did you find?”

“Not much. It looks like an outer camp. Just basic communications and surveillance equipment. There were only a dozen or so guards, and I've dealt with all of them.” Fang Heng turned to Glenan. “Come on. Take us to the ruins.”

Seeing how effortlessly Fang Heng had dealt with the entire camp, Glenan felt considerably more confident. He nodded. “All right. I'll show you.”

Following Glenan's directions, they passed the camp and soon reached a well-concealed cave entrance in the forest.

“It's in there.”

Glenan pointed at the opening and explained quietly, “This cave used to be secret. Only a few townspeople knew about it. Then the scientific expedition found it, and its location became public.”

There were no guards at the entrance.

Fang Heng stood before it and focused his senses.

He could detect numerous fluctuations of blood energy within the cave.

There were probably quite a few people inside.

But the fluctuations were weak.

These people had very little combat strength.

Fang Heng nodded. “Let's slip inside and take a look. Be careful.”

“Mm.”

Glenan led everyone down the passage into the cave.

They soon reached a hollow that dropped away sharply ahead.

Bright lights shone from below.

“There's a cliff a little over six meters high ahead,” Glenan whispered, crouching down and gesturing for the others to do the same. “At the bottom is the site I was telling you about...”

As he spoke, Glenan carefully crawled to the edge and poked his head over to look down.

What?

At the sight below, Glenan froze.

The cavern barely resembled the place he remembered.

Most of it had been remodeled, with enormous pieces of precision equipment piled everywhere.

Numerous staff members in white laboratory coats hurried back and forth.

Fang Heng looked down over the cliff, then turned toward Glenan.

“This place wasn't like this before. What... what have they been doing in here?” Surprise shone in Glenan's eyes as he raised his head to peer toward the cavern's depths.

“What?!”

Something he saw made anger flash across his face.

“What have they done to the ancient tree?!”
