Chapter 2512

Summoning

From original Chinese Chapter 2513 · 召唤

Seeing Fang Heng calmly gathering and refining blood, Luo Xu and the others instinctively took several steps back, feeling a chill run down their spines.

In moments, vast quantities of blood mixed with severed limbs rushed toward Fang Heng from the battlefield, forming a rapidly condensing pool of blood in midair!

Inside the surveillance hall, the battlefield was displayed in detail on the screens.

Everyone looked grim.

Had the Lickers become that much stronger in just half a year?

They were actually using their exceptional individual capabilities to withstand a terrifying army of Hell creatures!?

"It seems the Lickers possess some sort of symbiotic ability," Guillermo said gravely, watching the screens. "There's no need to worry. Once the attack intensity reaches a certain threshold, their defensive line will collapse completely!"

More and more Hell creatures continued streaming from the Gates of Hell!

They had surrounded the Lickers in layer after layer and were constantly trying to whittle down the swarm trapped at their center.

But they simply couldn't do it!

Ge Tianyu slowly shook his head and focused on another monitor, his expression grave. "Guillermo, forget the Hell creatures for a moment. I'm more concerned about him now."

Everyone followed Ge Tianyu's gaze toward a screen in another corner of the hall.

A huge pool of blood had appeared on the screen at some point.

Its contents surged and condensed, constantly giving off thin wisps of white vapor.

"That's..."

Narliev narrowed his eyes. Looking at Fang Heng standing behind the churning, refining blood, he became visibly serious.

"BOOM!!!!"

Suddenly, a muffled explosion echoed through the area.

Not good!

Guillermo saw one of the Gates of Hell explode on a surveillance monitor. His heart sank. He stepped forward and hit a button. "What's happening?! Quick! What's Team C doing? Get that magic array under control immediately!"

"Reporting to command! The magic array is showing signs of losing control."

"The Gate of Hell is under too much strain! Violent energy fluctuations..."

Damn it!

Guillermo clenched his fists, veins bulging along his neck as his voice rose. "I want you to control that magic array immediately! Put all your strength into it! Do you hear me?!"

"Command! Demon Seed energy fluctuations have exceeded the maximum permitted frequency... We can't control it..."

"Guillermo, I think that's enough for today."

Narliev approached Guillermo and rested a hand lightly on his shoulder.

The failure of the Gate of Hell operators couldn't really be blamed on them.

In previous tests, their summoning arrays had operated for no more than ten minutes. They had never deployed so many Gates of Hell on this scale or drawn such quantities of Demon Seed energy in so short a time.

And this time, their opponents were Fang Heng's Lickers.

The Gates of Hell finally exceeded their operating limits, losing control and destroying themselves one by one.

Watching the gates collapse on the screen, Guillermo drew a deep breath and bowed to Narliev. "I'm sorry, President. I've failed your expectations."

"The Gates of Hell incorporating Demon Seed power have shown impressive strength. Don't worry about it. I'm already very pleased with these results for a first attempt." Narliev stepped forward and quietly addressed the people monitoring the screens. "Fang Heng, though... He's interesting. Perhaps we could persuade him to cooperate."

Everyone's expressions shifted slightly.

Fang Heng was the person Envy had specifically ordered them to capture.

Cooperate with him!?

"But before negotiating, we should give him a taste of our strength," Narliev said as he turned toward the base exit. "Prepare the advanced defensive line."

"Understood!"

Outside the Reform Society headquarters, Fang Heng pursed his lips as he watched the Gates of Hell collapse and explode one after another.

That was it?

They'd only just gotten started!

After refining all that blood from the Hell creatures, he'd obtained less than a fingernail-sized lump of purified blood.

Hardly worth mentioning.

How strange.

Why had the Gates of Hell exploded when he hadn't even attacked them?

Whatever!

Keep going!

Fang Heng commanded his Lickers to continue devouring the remaining Hell creatures while refining their blood.

Huh?

He suddenly noticed the tightly shut doors of the Reform Society headquarters opening slowly ahead.

Looking up, he saw a young man with long white hair emerge.

"That's Narliev!"

Yu Jiayuan's expression changed sharply when she saw him.

"Who's he?"

"The current president of the Reform Society."

Luo Xu whispered, "Demon Seed Lord, be careful. Rumor has it that the Reform Society president is extremely powerful. In the past, all sorts of factions were fighting within the Society. But when Narliev returned, he supposedly suppressed them single-handedly and unified the entire organization in less than half a month."

Narliev seemed to notice them looking at him. He nodded slightly in their direction, a gentle smile on his face.

Sun Zhaoyu pursed his lips. "Hey, guys, I don't know why, but I really want to punch him..."

Fang Heng stared at Narliev and pointed forward.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!

The Licker swarm immediately received the order and lunged together toward Narliev!

Narliev remained completely calm. He rapidly formed a seal with both hands.

Whoosh!!!

In response to his mental power, a scarlet magic array appeared beneath his feet!

"Hummm!"

The array's reflection spread rapidly outward.

In the next instant, the earth centered on Narliev was covered in surging scarlet light.

Demonic heads wearing fang masks of various colors rose from the ground!

The purple demonic heads opened their enormous mouths.

"Hiss! Hiss, hiss, hiss!!!!"

The Tongue of Envy!

Scarlet tongues shot from the mouths of the purple fang masks, coiling directly toward the nearest Lickers!

Huh?

Fang Heng's heart stirred!

The Tongue of Envy didn't simply stab its targets. It twisted rapidly through the air, wrapping itself gently but completely around the charging Lickers.

[Hint: Your Licker has been affected by a Bind effect.]

[Hint: This Bind effect has priority and cannot be removed until the technique is broken.]

The Lickers ensnared by the Tongues of Envy immediately lost the ability to move.

"Whoosh!!!!"

The tongues rapidly dragged the trapped Lickers into the fang masks!

Good grief!

The Lickers had been swallowed whole!

Fang Heng frowned deeply.

And that wasn't all!

Red concentrated spheres of light continued shooting from the yellow fang masks. On impact, they sent Lickers flying and inflicted more than fifty negative statuses, including effects restricting HP regeneration.

Large numbers of Lickers immediately lost part of their ability to recover health.
