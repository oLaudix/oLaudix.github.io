Chapter 3019

Closing In

From Chinese Chapter 3020 · 逼近

More than half an hour later, Grand Sage Dean caught up with the main force, accompanied by a detachment of Holy Court guards.

After leaving the Divine Realm, he'd hurried to the Eternal Holy City, then rushed into Hell without stopping to look for Fang Heng.

He never imagined Fang Heng would be so aggressive. In such a short time he'd led the army all the way to the entrance of Hell's twentieth layer.

If Dean hadn't sent someone to slow him down, he'd probably have pressed through several more layers.

On seeing Fang Heng, Dean laughed. "Grand Sage Fang Bai, you certainly made yourself hard to find."

By now, Fang Heng's Perception attribute exceeded Dean's. Together with the upgraded divine artifact Phantom God's Joke, whose disguise could not be seen through, even Dean couldn't recognize his true identity.

Fang Heng remembered Dean and greeted him with a nod. "My apologies. Everything happened suddenly. I didn't mean to worry the Divine Realm."

"Fang Bai, can you tell me plainly why you've brought such an enormous army into Hell?" Dean paused, then pressed, "Don't tell me you really are trying to advance the Will of the Twelve Sages?"

Fang Heng looked at Dean, glanced at his quest notification, and shook his head. "No."

Dean was puzzled. "Then what for?"

"To deal with a Demon Seed in Hell."

Their conversation reached the nearby Holy Court priests and knights who were resting.

Only now did they realize Fang Heng's actual objective.

So the Grand Sage had invaded Hell to fight a Demon Seed!

Dean froze.

On his way here he'd considered over a dozen explanations, rejecting each one.

Who could have imagined it was to fight a Demon Seed?

He began looking at Fang Heng in a decidedly strange way.

This kid...

What grudge did he have against a Demon Seed in Hell? Was it really necessary to march straight into the enemy's lair and demand battle?

"Grand Sage Dean."

Still thinking wildly, Dean raised his head and found Fang Heng looking at him earnestly.

"Hm? What is it?"

"Since you're here, could you lend me a hand?"

Uh...

Dean suddenly felt he'd walked into a trap.

Had Fang Heng known he'd come? Had he been planning to recruit his help from the start?

And besides...

Thinking about it more carefully, wasn't fighting a Demon Seed practically the same thing as condensing the Will of the Twelve Sages?

"Grand Sage Dean, do you have concerns?"

"Ahem. Of course I should help eliminate evil for the Holy Court. It's just..." Dean leaned closer and lowered his voice. "The Demon Seeds are formidable. Their strength increases even further within Hell, while the environment is extremely unfavorable for us. This is very risky. Are you confident?"

"Mm. I understand the danger. But with your assistance, Grand Sage Dean, I don't think there'll be much of a problem."

Dean smiled bitterly.

He'd only come to find out what was happening. How had a few words been enough to drag him onto this pirate ship?

When Dean didn't answer, Fang Heng said nothing more. He raised the God-Bestowed Staff representing the Divine Realm's Twelve Sages. "Everyone! Follow me into Hell's twentieth layer!"

"Yes, sir!"

The vanguard of elite Holy Court crusaders and archbishops stormed into the twentieth layer. Sacred light spread outward again, reducing the onrushing fiends at the entrance to fragments under its burning radiance.

After securing the entrance, the Holy Court army slowly reformed its ranks and pushed deeper into the layer.

Dean followed in the center.

The army's frightening combat strength left him astonished. He kept glancing toward Fang Heng, sensing something profoundly strange.

How peculiar.

Grand Sage Fang Bai had supposedly inherited the power of Divine Punishment from the Twelve Sages.

Shouldn't his specialty have been powerful close-range attacks?

Then why were his support buffs so terrifyingly effective?

Even a Grand Sage specializing in blessings might not have produced effects this strong!

With Fang Heng's sacred buffs, the monsters of Hell's twentieth layer were being crushed effortlessly.

Dean had prepared to lend a hand in battle, but it seemed unnecessary.

After all, he represented the power of protection.

It was another close-combat branch, focused mostly on restraint, defense, and support, and wasn't particularly well suited to battles on this scale.

There was no need for him to rush to the front.

[Hint: Player has successfully cleansed part of Hell. Holy Court reputation increased. Current Sacred Will condensation: 12.92%.]

Fang Heng glanced at the hint.

He hadn't personally joined the fighting. He remained in the army's center, continually surveying the battlefield for signs of Echidna, Envy's demonic pet.

Parts of Echidna's body were spread across Hell's twenty-first through thirtieth layers, but her senses were only ordinary.

When Fang Heng had infiltrated earlier, he'd hidden his aura and wasn't in Demon Seed form, so Echidna hadn't detected him.

Now the Holy Court's army was invading so conspicuously that Echidna sensed it immediately and passed the information to Envy.

Found it!

Fang Heng's gaze settled on a nearby lava pool.

A faint trace of blue flowed across its molten surface.

Part of Echidna's body!

"Hiss!"

Fang Heng raised a hand, sending a beam of sacred light down onto Echidna.

The blue liquid was immediately dispersed completely by the holy radiance.

Fang Heng withdrew his gaze, looked around, and ordered, "To the right."

"Yes!"

Echidna's main body was concentrated on the twenty-ninth layer. Elsewhere, scattered pieces of her body were linked to one another through some unknown means.

If she wished, she could swiftly condense any one of these fragments into a physical incarnation.

But facing an enormous Holy Court army, Echidna had no intention of rushing to her death.

Besides, she still had to guard the Demon Seeds' power.

Fang Heng was in no hurry. He led the Holy Court troops downward, clearing Hell layer by layer.

If Envy refused to appear, he'd simply destroy Echidna's scattered body piece by piece. He could also earn progress toward the Will of the Twelve Sages quest along the way.

Either way, he couldn't lose!

During his time in Hell, Jing'er had carefully mapped every region occupied by Echidna and later given Fang Heng the detailed maps.

Guided by those maps, the Holy Court army pressed deeper, pursuing and cleansing Echidna's concentrations of power along the way.

Eventually they entered a cavern on Hell's twenty-third layer, occupied by one of Echidna's gathered fragments.

The cavern was bitterly cold. As the Holy Court troops entered, large numbers of horned hell demons swarmed out and charged the paladin formations.

But this time they were different.

Vein-like lines ran across their blood-red bodies.

Echidna!

With Echidna attached to them, all the horned demons' attributes had increased enormously.

Fang Heng remained at the army's center, watching the battlefield coldly.
