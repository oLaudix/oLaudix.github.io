Chapter 3011

Disappearance

From Chinese Chapter 3012 · 失踪

More than half a month later.

Within the World of Ten Thousand Forests.

The vines of Abe Akaya glimmered faintly, radiating abundant natural energy.

Fang Heng sat cross-legged beneath its roots, absorbing the surrounding natural power.

The entire World of Ten Thousand Forests continuously generated natural energy.

The supply here was over ten times greater than in the ice-crystal labyrinth.

So more than ten days earlier, Fang Heng had simply left the Titan Jewel in the labyrinth, handing its management to the Holy Court and the Federations for shared custody and allocated use.

He himself stayed beneath Abe Akaya's vines, using the domain's rich natural energy to cultivate.

[Hint: Player's Grandmaster Natural Studies has reached LV: 44.]

"Phew..."

Fang Heng glanced at the notification and slowly exhaled, bringing this round of cultivation to an end.

He rose and turned to the Tree Spirit elders waiting respectfully behind him. "What is it?"

"Mr. Fang Heng."

Two elders stepped forward and reported respectfully, "Forgive us for interrupting your cultivation. It concerns the Divine Maiden."

Klevi?

Fang Heng's attention sharpened.

Klevi was a half-elf, capable of sensing part of Abe Akaya's consciousness. She was therefore responsible for caring for the Divine Tree in everyday life.

With Fang Heng's encouragement, she'd gradually become involved in managing the World of Ten Thousand Forests's daily affairs as well.

After all, Fang Heng had neither the time nor the inclination. He preferred to be a hands-off boss.

Thus, in the eyes of the Tree Spirits living within the domain, Klevi's ability to communicate with the Divine Tree made her their Divine Maiden, responsible for most of its affairs.

Only Fang Heng stood above her in status.

An elder bowed and reported, "The Divine Maiden has been missing for twenty-four hours. This has never happened before. We searched her room and found no sign of her. We're concerned and wanted to ask whether you know where she might be."

"Missing?"

Fang Heng was surprised. After thinking a moment, he asked, "When did you last see Klevi? Was anything unusual happening?"

"At yesterday's morning meeting. She presided as usual, and nothing seemed out of the ordinary. Afterward, she personally tended the Divine Tree. Nobody has seen her since. She missed today's meeting without sending word, which has never happened before. Everyone is worried."

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

Klevi had been entrusted to his care by Orianna, his Natural Studies mentor.

She shouldn't have gotten into trouble inside the game.

At the very least, everywhere in the World of Ten Thousand Forests touched by trees or vines lay under Abe Akaya's protection and watchful eye.

Could it have happened outside the game?

"Understood. You may go."

Fang Heng waved them away.

"Yes."

The two elders bowed and withdrew.

Fang Heng closed his eyes and reached out to Abe Akaya. Soon he'd established a mental link.

According to the messages transmitted through Abe Akaya's consciousness, Klevi had come looking for him more than once during his secluded cultivation. Unfortunately, he'd remained in seclusion until she disappeared.

After yesterday's morning meeting, she had sought him again and failed to find him. Then she'd logged out and left, never returning to the World of Ten Thousand Forests.

Fang Heng grew suspicious.

So Klevi really had disappeared in the real world.

Abe Akaya's vines slowly gathered around Fang Heng and gently brushed his shoulder.

"Really? You're worried about her? Mm, so am I." Fang Heng nodded. "In that case, we'll go find her ourselves."

...

Fang Heng actually knew very little about Klevi's background beyond her being a half-elf.

No matter. Her Natural Studies mentor ought to know where she'd really gone.

Two hours later, Fang Heng found Orianna in a forest mage's cottage.

"I suspect Klevi may have returned to the Elven Clan."

After listening to his account, Orianna sighed softly and continued, "I expected this day might come. Klevi is a half-elf. The Elven Clan regards her bloodline as impure, and her mother has been imprisoned among the elves because of it. She must miss her mother terribly. Perhaps the clan summoned her back, and she left for a while."

Fang Heng frowned. "You mean Klevi left of her own free will?"

"I can't be sure." Orianna shook her head. "But if she'd resisted, we should have sensed her aura or received some message from her."

"Do you know where the Elven Clan lives?"

"The elves conceal their movements extremely well. I met Klevi in another game world myself."

"Thank you, Mentor." Fang Heng nodded. "Klevi is my friend. Please tell me immediately if you hear anything about her."

"Of course."

After saying farewell and leaving Orianna's cottage, Fang Heng looked down, deep in thought.

Most of the clues pointed to Klevi having left voluntarily, very likely for the Elven Clan.

Unfortunately, nobody had discovered an elven world among the high-tier games, and none of his channels had information about it.

Could it be a hidden world?

For now, at least, he had no way of entering it.

After some thought, Fang Heng decided to search Klevi's real-world home for clues about the elves.

He transformed into a bat and flew straight toward her residence.

Before disappearing, Klevi had spent most of her time inside the game.

Outside the game, she disliked interacting with others, so Mo Jiawei had helped purchase a detached villa in the suburbs for her.

Klevi sometimes stayed at the villa in the real world.

An hour later, as Fang Heng approached the area around it, he frowned.

What was going on?

He looked down from a distance.

A Federation Crisis Response Team had surrounded Klevi's villa with layer upon layer of security.

Something was wrong!

Something had definitely happened.

Fang Heng descended and returned to human form.

"Who's there?!"

Lu Yunhao, leader of the Eastern Federation Crisis Response Team, turned alertly. The sudden appearance behind him gave him a fright, and he and the investigators immediately aimed their weapons at the newcomer.

"It's me. Fang Heng."

A person's reputation casts a long shadow.

As soon as they recognized him, the investigators' expressions changed.

In the Eastern Federation, practically everyone familiar with the game had heard Fang Heng's name.

The Federation players present knew even more about his identity.

He was the World Lord of both Vampire Apocalypse and Zombie Apocalypse.

Rumors had also recently begun circulating that Fang Heng had inherited the power of the Vampire King.

An ordinary high-ranking vampire player was already among the elite. The Vampire King was on another level entirely.

His power was terrifying.

Moreover, Fang Heng was a close partner of the Eastern Federation with the highest possible clearance. To some extent, he even had the authority to temporarily requisition the Crisis Response Team's cooperation.

And they were pointing guns at him? Had they gone mad?

Lu Yunhao hastily signaled his investigators to lower their weapons and apologized. "I'm sorry, Mr. Fang Heng. Forgive us for not recognizing you."
