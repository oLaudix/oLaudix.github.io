Chapter 2194

Traces

From original Chinese Chapter 2195 · 踪迹

Meanwhile, Fang Heng was still flying swiftly above the forest.

The higher he flew, the thinner the air became, making flight much more strenuous.

Even at what felt like an enormous altitude, he could still see layer upon layer of branches and foliage stretching ahead of him.

To maintain his speed, he reluctantly descended a little and wove between the overlapping treetops instead.

That actually proved faster.

After almost two hours, he was nearly at the Spirit-Prayer Temple's coordinates. He circled down onto a thick branch and surveyed his surroundings.

Found it!

A short distance away, nestled among the trees, stood an ancient-looking Tree Spirit temple.

Fang Heng leaped down from the branch, returned to human form, and landed outside it, examining the area.

No guards?

His wariness increased as he approached the temple cautiously, step by step.

He reached the grounds of the temple itself.

Still no response.

Fang Heng looked up at the entrance.

The entire place had an eerie stillness to it.

As though no one had visited in a very long time.

He reached out and tried gently pushing the door open.

Locked?

Frowning, Fang Heng applied a little more force.

Snap!

With a soft crack, the bar on the other side of the door broke.

He opened the door and stepped into the temple's front courtyard.

The main hall stood directly ahead, with a scattering of smaller buildings behind it.

“Strange…”

Fang Heng surveyed the place and murmured to himself.

According to Levin, the Spirit-Prayer Temples were sacred places protected by the Tree Spirits. Ordinary clansmen rarely entered them except during important events or to receive a blessing with an honor seal.

So why wasn't there a single person here?

Where had all the Tree Spirits gone?

Fang Heng recalled Sandy's many theories about the race.

Perhaps some serious trouble within the Tree Spirit race had caused the Inner World to close abruptly.

But it was strange.

Even if Sandy was right and they hadn't managed to cultivate a new generation of Divine Tree seeds, surely that wouldn't have wiped out the entire race?

And there were no signs anyone had forced their way into the temple.

What on earth had happened?

Fang Heng scratched his head, his thoughts in disarray.

All right!

Since nobody was here, he might as well search the place thoroughly!

At a thought, the face covering his own slid away like melting wax.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Magic circles appeared all around him.

He summoned more than five hundred Lickers, then switched back to his disguised human appearance.

The Lickers quickly formed fleshy cocoons on the ground and reverted to ordinary zombie form.

“Go…”

Pointing into the temple, Fang Heng ordered his zombie clones to search every corner. He entered the main hall to investigate personally.

Before long, game messages began appearing.

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Vine Form) searched the designated area and discovered a broken wooden bowl ×1.]

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Vine Form) searched the designated area and discovered a vine-woven Spirit-Prayer Talisman ×1 (Damaged)…]

The zombies piled the miscellaneous objects they'd collected in the courtyard, then went out to search again.

“Not bad…”

Fang Heng muttered, examining the hall.

A ritual magic array!

The first thing he noticed upon entering was the enormous ritual array engraved on the floor to his right.

Closer inspection revealed several special recesses in its surface.

One of them was shaped exactly like the seal Levin had given him.

Fang Heng crouched beside the array to study it.

An exceptionally large, multi-layered ritual magic array.

It would need at least twenty-four people to operate.

From his preliminary analysis, part of the ritual was designed to receive an infusion of natural energy.

Although Levin had dangled a tempting promise in front of him, it hadn't been an outright lie.

The problem was that the Tree Spirits who would conduct the ritual were missing.

Having the honor seal alone wouldn't get him anything…

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Vine Form) searched the designated area and discovered the Spirit-Prayer Temple Chronicle (2891)…]

A chronicle?

One of the notifications caught Fang Heng's eye, and his interest stirred.

That might contain some clues!

He returned to the courtyard, dug out a thick record book from the pile of objects his zombies had collected, and began reading carefully.

Just as Levin had said, the Spirit-Prayer Temple had long been used by the Tree Spirits for sacrifices, nurturing the Divine Tree, and praying for the bestowal of power.

Not every Tree Spirit was entitled to receive that power. Only outstanding clansmen who had made significant contributions to their race could be granted an inheritance.

“Those lying Tree Spirits…”

As he read, Fang Heng couldn't help cursing under his breath.

He'd only just decided Levin was relatively trustworthy. And now—great. He'd been fooled again.

The records explicitly stated that only members of the Tree Spirit race could receive the Spirit-Prayer Temple's inherited powers!

Even with an honor seal, Fang Heng couldn't qualify because he wasn't a Tree Spirit!

He was deeply annoyed.

Admittedly, he hadn't expected all that much to begin with…

But being taken for a fool was still infuriating.

Fang Heng made a mental note of the lesson and continued leafing through the chronicle.

Most entries concerned the temple's everyday affairs.

Who had performed prayers in a particular year or month, when unusual phenomena had occurred, and so on.

Unlike Sandy, Fang Heng soon lost patience. After flipping through a few pages, he skipped straight to the end.

There was no account of why the passage to the Inner World had closed.

But at a certain point, the temple had received an order from the Tree Spirit Council of Elders: close temporarily, and have all Tree Spirits return to the Council to report.

Had something happened at the Council?

Some kind of crisis?

Something serious enough to mobilize the entire race?

Fang Heng pondered the matter.

By the time he looked up again, the temple courtyard was heaped with miscellaneous items.

His zombie clones had searched every part of the temple.

With the Search skill at MAX level, they'd swept it clean, leaving no corner untouched.

Fang Heng rummaged through the pile of junk and found quite a few more books mixed in.

For now, he couldn't tell whether any of them were useful.

Just looking at the books gave him a headache.

Forget it. He'd take them all back and let Sandy have a look.

Sandy loved digging into this kind of thing!

With that decided, Fang Heng ordered the zombies to haul all the collected objects back toward the Tree Spirit area.

Next…

He set the book down and considered his options.

Visiting the other Spirit-Prayer Temples probably wouldn't reveal much more.

Maybe he should return to the Tree Spirits and see what Senzo knew?

As he stood there deciding, Fang Heng suddenly sensed something and looked toward the distance.

Someone was coming!

His vampire bloodline had detected the blood-and-life-force fluctuations of a group approaching from afar.

Tree Spirits?

Judging by those fluctuations, there was no doubt.

After a moment's thought, Fang Heng concealed his aura and slipped into the shadows.

Editing and Source Notes
  • Temple restriction. The chronicle specifies that only Tree Spirit clansmen may receive the temple inheritance, even if another person possesses an honor seal.

Chinese Expressions

  • 画大饼. “Drawing a big pancake”: promising attractive future rewards instead of delivering them now.
  • 不留死角. “Leaving no blind corners”: searching absolutely everywhere.
