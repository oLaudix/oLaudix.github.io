Chapter 3012

Search

From Chinese Chapter 3013 · 搜寻

Fang Heng paid the incident no mind. He nodded toward the villa. "What happened over there?"

"Between 13:30 and 14:15 today, a series of attacks occurred in the city's Lotus District, partially paralyzing local transportation. Seven police officers were severely injured and twelve civilians suffered minor injuries. Three suspects were involved. Based on eyewitness accounts, we suspect they may be players who have achieved second-stage unlocking in the real world."

"Two hours ago, all three suspects entered this villa, apparently intending to establish a temporary base."

"The First Reconnaissance Team was exposed while approaching the villa's interior at 16:23. The targets initiated an attack, leaving eight of our personnel seriously injured and two dead. Based on the strength of their attacks and residual energy analysis, their threat rating has been raised to 2S."

"Thermal imaging shows three moving heat signatures inside. The Federation Crisis Response Office has authorized assembling the Falcon, Red Wolf, and Windrider task forces to enter and investigate. We expect to deploy three SSS-rank and twelve SS-rank players... The personnel are still gathering..."

Fang Heng stared at the villa in the distance and extended his senses.

Only three presences.

Klevi wasn't inside.

"I'll take a look."

Lu Yunhao swallowed nervously.

"All right, Boss Fang. All three have been hiding in the ground-floor hall. They ambush anyone who approaches with ranged arrows, so please be careful. If you can, wear this earpiece. Should anything go wrong, we'll rush in to rescue you immediately."

"Got it."

Fang Heng took the earpiece Lu Yunhao offered and put it on.

Under the watchful eyes of the Federation's elite response team, he walked toward the villa entrance.

The moment he pushed open the front gate and entered the courtyard, hidden automatic traps activated. Several pale-green arrows shot out of the villa.

"Bang! Bang, bang!"

The arrows exploded one after another in midair, shattered by an unknown force.

"Whoosh!"

Fang Heng lightly pushed off the ground and flashed through the courtyard into the villa.

Three elves were crouched in the ground-floor hall, arranging some kind of alchemical magic array on the floor. They were startled when Fang Heng burst in and turned toward him.

Elves!

One look at their slender bodies and sharp, pointed ears was enough for Fang Heng to recognize them.

The three suspects pursued by the Federation weren't players at all—they were elves!

They appeared to be preparing an alchemical array in the hall. Bottles and jars had been arranged all around it.

"Who are you?"

Fang Heng's intrusion disrupted their activation of the array. It failed, and all three elves raised their heads toward him.

The leading elf frowned in annoyance and barked, "Kill him!"

"Whoosh!"

The other two elves moved with extraordinary agility, dodging left and right while green arrows rapidly formed around them and shot toward Fang Heng in succession.

"Bang! Bang, bang, bang!"

Fang Heng didn't move. Every arrow exploded before it came within two meters of him.

Huh? He had blocked them that easily?

The elven reconnaissance captain was about to warn his subordinates when his vision suddenly blurred.

What?!

"Bang! Bang!"

Two dull impacts rang out almost simultaneously. The two elves who'd attacked Fang Heng flew in opposite directions and slammed into the walls.

The captain hadn't seen how Fang Heng struck them at all.

Whoosh!

The next instant, Fang Heng appeared directly before him as if he'd crossed the intervening space in a single step.

"Bang!"

Agony exploded in the captain's abdomen. Like his subordinates, he was knocked backward and crashed into the wall.

"Human!"

The captain looked up, glaring at Fang Heng with seething rage. "Lowly human! Do you know what you're doing? You dare harm the noble Elven Clan? The Elven Council will flatten your entire human world! Kneel and kowtow in apology right now, and I might still give you one chance..."

"Hah. Are you threatening me?"

Fang Heng laughed coldly and raised a hand.

The captain's heart lurched. His blood suddenly surged uncontrollably and his body froze in place. Then he felt warmth trickling from his nose and ears.

What?

Looking down, he discovered blood pouring uncontrollably from his facial openings.

Crimson lines spread across his skin like a spiderweb.

Controlled by the blood within him, the elven captain was slowly lifted into the air against his will.

Fang Heng stared into his eyes. "What are you searching for? Where's Klevi?"

"Let me go!" Fear rose in the elf's heart, but he still glared viciously. "Klevi sheltered outsiders! She's already been taken back to the Elven Clan for punishment. Human, release me, or else..."

Fang Heng frowned.

Blood rapidly crept down the captain's cheeks toward his neck, wrapping around his throat and tightening.

The elf went pale and clawed desperately at his neck, struggling violently.

"Last chance," Fang Heng asked coldly. "What are you looking for?"

"Don't think I'll tell you, human..."

"Bang!"

Fang Heng clenched his fist. The captain exploded in midair into a cloud of bloody mist.

The two other elves, badly injured by Fang Heng's punches, had just begun struggling to rise. Seeing their captain reduced to mist, they stared at Fang Heng in terror.

"What about you two?"

Fang Heng looked at them and slowly raised his right hand again.

Blood began streaming from the two elves' facial openings, just as it had from their captain. They lost control of their bodies and were lifted slowly into the air.

"Answer my question. What are you searching for?"

"Human, you won't get a single word out of us! You'll regret what you've done today! The Elven Council will wash your world in blood! Your world will be destroyed because of you!"

"Really?"

Fang Heng laughed coldly. A rotating magic array appeared in the pupil of his right eye.

All-Knowing Eye.

Illusion effects combined with Psionic suggestion.

When the elves met Fang Heng's gaze, matching rotating arrays appeared in their pupils, and their eyes gradually lost focus.

Fang Heng asked again, "Tell me. Why did you come here?"

"W-we were ordered to search Klevi's residence for..."

"Whoosh!"

Suddenly, as if something had stimulated them, the two elves under mental suggestion turned violently red-eyed. Their faces flushed from pale to crimson, and scarlet veins bulged beneath their skin, making them look horrifying.
