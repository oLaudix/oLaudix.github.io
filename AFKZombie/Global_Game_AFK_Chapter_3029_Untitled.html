From Chinese Chapter 3030 · No subtitle in source

“Heh, not bad...”

Fang Heng lightly licked his lips as he looked at the Demon Core.

“What a treasure...”

Question Envy, understand Envy, become Envy.

Fang Heng slowly withdrew his gaze.

In that case, he would have to find some way of taking away the remaining Demon Cores too.

And Abe Akaya was close to its next evolution, so he should finish that while he was at it.

Abe Akaya's current stage was still too low. Its absorption speed and conversion efficiency were poor. Once it reached its Complete Form, its absorption speed would probably improve dramatically.

For now, the power of Wrath hadn't been completely absorbed.

First, he'd find a couple of lucky Demon Seeds to absorb...

Fang Heng instructed Abe Akaya to release the aura of the Demon Core belonging to Wrath. He concealed his own presence and hid nearby.

Within minutes, two Wrath Demon Seeds followed the core's aura into the cavern, one behind the other.

Seeing layers of vines enveloping the Demon Core and draining it, the two Demon Seeds were startled.

“What?”

How had the Demon Core ended up like this?

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!

While they hesitated, dense vines suddenly shot toward them.

Fang Heng appeared strangely behind the two. Before he could even make a move, he saw them freeze in place, their faces turning deathly pale.

“Oh?”

Fang Heng looked up. As Abe Akaya's vines pierced the Demon Core's surface, pain appeared simultaneously on the faces of the two Demon Seeds of the same origin. Their faces drained of color and their bodies shook uncontrollably.

“Hiss, hiss, hiss...”

Fang Heng didn't even need to intervene. Abe Akaya's vines swiftly approached the pair and enclosed them in a ball of vines in the blink of an eye.

In barely two minutes, the Demon Seeds were drained completely dry, and the ball of vines wrapping them gradually opened.

Wrath was taken care of too.

Apart from his own Greed power, only one remained.

Pride.

Fang Heng asked, “Abe Akaya, where are the other Demon Cores?”

Abe Akaya's vines already covered most of Hell's twenty-ninth level. Hearing his question, it immediately shared the locations of the cores with his Sea of Consciousness.

“Not bad. Come on, Abe Akaya. Let's go make something big happen.”

...

Inside a cavern on Hell's twenty-ninth level.

Three Demon Cores hovered together in midair, forming a strange equilibrium.

Several Demon Seeds were fighting fiercely nearby.

Demonic markings representing Pride had appeared on Jing'er's body, and a terrifying aura kept pouring out of him.

This sort of free-for-all was extremely troublesome!

Everyone present wanted to claim the cores. Whenever someone got close to them, several Demon Seeds would immediately join forces to attack that person.

Most Demon Seeds were proud and unruly, with no trust between them whatsoever. Almost everyone fought for themselves, and even those in small groups reserved some of their attention to guard against their teammates.

“Whoosh!!!”

Suddenly, a powerful wave of death energy surged in from outside the cavern. A figure swiftly followed it inside and charged straight toward the Demon Cores in the center.

The Demon Seeds erupted in anger. “Stop him!”

Which new Demon Seed was this? Didn't he know the rules?

Trying to steal a prize while everyone else was busy fighting?

Four Demon Seeds who had been fighting fiercely nearby all turned their attacks toward Fang Heng.

“Hmph!”

Fang Heng snorted.

The first Demon Seed rushing at him immediately turned pale and lost the ability to fight.

What had happened to that man?

The remaining three were alarmed and confused.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!!”

The next instant, more than a dozen dark Nether Orbs shot toward them first.

“Bang! Bang!!!”

At first, the three Demon Seeds paid little attention to the orbs and simply tried knocking them aside. But the instant their hands touched them, the orbs exploded, unleashing a terrifying surge of death energy.

What the hell?

Such fearsome fluctuations of power!

“Boom!!!”

One Demon Seed's face changed as the force erupted around his right arm, and he retreated quickly. Looking down, he saw that decay, aging, and other negative effects had eaten a huge hole through the arm he'd just used to block the orb.

Black death energy clung to the wound and was rapidly spreading over the surrounding skin.

“Whoosh!!”

Fang Heng swept past the three retreating Demon Seeds and headed straight for the cores at the center of the cavern.

His speed was at the absolute limit!

The other Demon Seeds fighting inside noticed something was wrong. They stopped and turned to look at him.

Who was that brat?

“He's dangerous! Stop him, quickly!”

Two more Demon Seeds tried to surround Fang Heng and intercept him from opposite sides.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!!”

The Nether Orbs circling Fang Heng automatically launched their attacks.

Having seen what happened to the others, the two Demon Seeds dared not be careless. They used their full strength, releasing hellfire in an attempt to explode the incoming orbs.

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

In midair, Hell energy and necromantic energy collided and exploded repeatedly!

Surprise appeared in the eyes of both Demon Seeds.

The necromantic power contained in the Nether Orbs was terrifying.

When the two energies collided, the death energy instantly devoured the Hell energy. Both Demon Seeds were blasted backward by the resulting burst of necromantic power.

Whoosh!!

Taking advantage of their retreat, Fang Heng darted forward and appeared beside the three floating Demon Cores.

Not good!

He wanted to take the cores!

Everyone's heart sank.

No one had expected the newcomer to possess such terrifying strength. In the blink of an eye, he'd reached the Demon Cores!

Several Demon Seeds immediately charged him, watching Fang Heng closely as they tried to intercept him.

“Hmph.”

Fang Heng swept a cold gaze across them. Without any obvious movement beyond a snort, he formed another seal with both hands.

“Descent of the Tree Domain!”

A rapidly rotating magic array suddenly appeared beneath his feet, and dense vines surged from it, immediately converging on the three Demon Cores.

Terror appeared on the Demon Seeds' faces.

It's over.

They couldn't reach him in time.

In barely a breath, the dense vines had wrapped around all three cores floating in midair.
