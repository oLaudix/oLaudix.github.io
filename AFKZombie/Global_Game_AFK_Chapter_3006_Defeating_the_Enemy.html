Chapter 3006

Defeating the Enemy

From Chinese Chapter 3007 · 破敌

Interesting!

The Titan had used some spatial ability to attack him even while he was in Secondary Space Projection.

Although it looked like an ordinary slap, the Guardian clearly possessed extraordinary Spatial Studies abilities.

Without Undying Body, those two slaps would probably have left Fang Heng badly injured.

But the Titan couldn't be feeling too good either, could he?

"Hm?"

The Titan Guardian also sensed something wrong. He lowered his head and spread his palm.

There, a spiderweb of cracks had appeared across his hand.

It looked as though he had held every advantage when he slapped Fang Heng away, but he'd sensed something was wrong at the moment of impact. Striking Fang Heng had felt like slapping a nail.

The Titan's expression grew extremely solemn. He extended a finger and lightly pointed toward Fang Heng.

Huh?

Fang Heng had already recovered quickly from the slap thanks to Undying Body and was preparing to attack again when an invisible force suddenly pinned him down.

"Oh? Psionics?"

In that case...

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

"Boom!"

Power erupted from his body. A complex magic array emerged beneath his feet, pouring out thick black mist while branches and vines sprang up and gathered from every direction!

Eternal Tree Domain!

Whoosh!

The burst of his full physical strength, combined with the waves from the summoned Eternal Tree Domain, quickly broke the Psionic suppression. Fang Heng became a dark streak and rushed straight at the Guardian!

The watching players' faces changed.

What an immense energy fluctuation!

If they'd been competing in close combat before, now they were exchanging magical attacks.

Frenzied vines spread rapidly to cover the area around Fang Heng!

When immortals fought, ordinary people didn't dare come near. The players immediately retreated.

Seeing the vines spreading around Fang Heng, the Guardian realized his Psionic ability had failed. Alarmed, he opened his mouth wide.

"Roar!"

Scorching explosive lava poured from the Titan's mouth!

The blistering heat instantly raised the temperature throughout the trial grounds.

Under the direct impact of the molten blast, the vines converging on the Titan burned to ashes.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng's body flickered sideways, evading the flames.

The Guardian's senses remained locked on Fang Heng. He raised his right arm and tracked his opponent's path.

"Whoosh!"

His enormous palm swept down toward Fang Heng again.

Whoosh!

Huh? What?!

The Titan's pupils shook violently.

Fang Heng's speed had suddenly increased to an absurd degree in midair. His body slipped straight through the space beneath the descending palm.

Annual Rings—Acceleration!

"Thud!"

With Sacred Greatsword in hand, Fang Heng slammed into the Guardian's massive body, using the speed gained from Annual Rings—Acceleration to deliver a crushing blow!

The sword left a deep mark on the Titan's body.

Dark-golden spiderweb cracks spread outward from the wound across a large area.

"Boom!"

The Sacred Greatsword's secondary ripple effect detonated against the Guardian!

After those consecutive blows, the Titan actually wavered and staggered backward.

"Hmph!"

The corner of Fang Heng's mouth curled coldly at the successful strike.

Humanity's ancestor? A Titan?

Was that all?

"Whoosh!"

Before the Guardian could steady himself, Fang Heng vanished from the players' sight again.

He hadn't entered Secondary Space Projection. Annual Rings—Acceleration had made him so fast that their eyes could no longer follow him!

"Bang! Bang!"

The balance of the battle reversed completely!

The players in the hall could see only a dark streak appearing and disappearing.

Fang Heng flashed through the air and struck the Titan again and again. Every attack dealt enormous damage and spread giant dark-golden spiderweb cracks across his body. The Guardian couldn't block them at all!

At the same time, countless vines appeared from nowhere and surged toward him, already climbing up his legs and torso.

Razor-sharp steel needles extended from beneath the tangled vines, trying to pierce the Titan's petrified skin and drain his blood.

Unfortunately, the needles couldn't break through his outer defenses. They could only bind him in layer after layer of vines.

Within moments, the Guardian's body was covered in dense networks of dark-golden cracks. His appearance had become terrifyingly grotesque.

The Southern Federation players stared in disbelief.

What was happening?

Fang Heng alone had completely suppressed the Titan Guardian, humanity's supposed ancestor?

"You... dare..."

The Guardian felt his power draining rapidly. His life itself was in grave danger.

"Whoosh!"

Before everyone's astonished eyes, Fang Heng vanished and almost simultaneously appeared directly in front of the Titan.

"Shhk!"

Holding his sword in one hand, Fang Heng drove the Sacred Greatsword into the Guardian's forehead and shouted coldly, "Divine Punishment!"

"Boom!"

Guided and amplified by the sword, crimson lightning crashed down from above and struck the Titan!

The Guardian trembled violently. Layers of earth and stone fell from his body as dark-golden light shone through.

Still not dead?

Then another!

"Boom! Boom, boom, boom!"

Lightning fell repeatedly. The Titan could no longer counterattack. Dark-golden cracks spread rapidly from the greatsword embedded in his forehead across his entire body.

"You cannot kill me!" the Titan shouted fiercely. "I am this world's Guardian! If you kill me, not only you but your entire world will be completely exposed..."

"Old man, stop worrying about things that don't concern you." Fang Heng sneered. "We can protect our own world. Don't come here and tell us what to do."

"Boom!"

Another crimson lightning bolt descended before he'd even finished speaking!

The golden glow coating the Guardian's spiderweb-covered body shuddered uncontrollably, then shattered explosively before Fang Heng!

"Bang! Bang, bang, bang!"

Chunks of stone veined with dark-golden cracks flew outward.

Huh?

Fang Heng keenly sensed something among the scattered fragments and swept out a hand.

"Whoosh!"

A white crystalline stone, also covered in dark-golden cracks, flew into his palm.

What was this?

Although no game hint appeared, Fang Heng could sense energy fluctuating within the crystal.

He could absorb it!

Flicking his wrist, he activated his mental power. The pure energy inside the stone transformed into Qi, rushed into his body, and settled in his Sea of Consciousness. The crystal rapidly shrank in his hand until it weathered away completely.

— End of Chapter 3006 —
