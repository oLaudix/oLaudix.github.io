Chapter 3075

A Wider World

From Chinese Chapter 3076 · 世界

Fang Heng ended the call and checked the time.

He'd become so absorbed in cultivation that thirty hours had passed without him noticing.

It was understandable. Ordinary warriors lacked sufficient mental power and often had to rest after practicing mysticism seals once or twice.

Fang Heng had no such worries. Practicing continuously didn't affect him at all.

The ointment on his forehead had long since lost its potency and dried onto his skin.

His meridians hadn't been fully cleared yet, but progress had reached eighty-seven percent.

Fang Heng closed his eyes and sensed the changes again.

His mental energy flowed noticeably faster once it entered his meridians.

Impressive!

A faster flow meant he could activate his mental abilities more quickly while consuming less energy.

No wonder he'd heard that many scholars of spell-based disciplines studied a little mysticism on the side.

These improvements didn't show up directly in numerical stats.

Fang Heng stopped cultivating, stood up, and looked around. More than a hundred zombie clones had excavated new tunnels beneath and around the villa. Abe Akaya's vines crushed the excavated rubble into pieces, which were then bagged and piled along the edges.

He'd still need to find a way to dispose of the construction waste.

He could handle that after he came back.

Fang Heng went up to the second floor to shower, then met Anxi and set off for the arena.

...

There were 128 arenas of various sizes within the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's inner city.

Most had been funded and built by the seven overlord factions and were open to the public for a fee.

The fees were said to be steep.

During competitions, spectators could buy tickets to watch the matches.

Many warriors also attended to observe the strength and development of potential opponents.

The arenas resembled enormous football stadiums, except that they were completely enclosed and had relatively small central fighting areas.

After verifying their identities, Anxi led Fang Heng into the backstage lounge.

“Mr. Fang Heng, this is Elder Dilan.”

Fang Heng cupped his fists toward the Noel Clan elder. “Elder Dilan.”

After freeloading so many training sessions from the Noel Clan, the least he could do was show proper respect.

Elder Dilan seemed amiable and courteous. He chuckled and waved his hand. “A newly recruited warrior? Very good. Sorry to summon you so late and interrupt your rest, but something unexpected came up.”

“As a Noel Clan warrior, it's my duty.” Fang Heng nodded. “This is my first arena match. I'll follow your instructions, Elder Dilan.”

“Of course, of course. Come, have a seat.”

Dilan gestured for Fang Heng to sit and continued, “This match shouldn't be particularly difficult. Seven of our competitors are young warriors raised by the Noel Clan, while two others are outside recruits like you.”

“It's your first fight, so don't worry too much about winning or losing. Just get a feel for it.”

“All right.”

Fang Heng agreed aloud, though he had no intention of losing.

He wanted to get his three monthly victories over with quickly so he could spend more time concentrating on training afterward.

Elder Dilan himself wasn't especially concerned about the outcome. He regarded the match as training and paid little attention to Fang Heng, who was merely there to make up the numbers. His main concern was instructing the youngsters.

They were, after all, the Noel Clan's future main fighting force.

Soon it was time to enter.

The group moved into the preparation area.

Only then did Fang Heng notice how many spectators were present. The arena was over seventy percent full.

As soon as the warriors entered, thunderous cheers erupted.

Fang Heng took a seat beside his team in the waiting area.

Anxi couldn't enter the combat zone, so she waited by the corridor outside.

A moment later, the host announced the arrival of both teams and the preparations for battle.

Their opponents weren't one of the seven great overlord races, but a medium-sized faction.

“Brother, what a coincidence. Are you here to make up the numbers too?”

Fang Heng looked toward the young man speaking to him and found his face vaguely familiar.

Right. He'd passed the recruitment trial in the same group as Fang Heng.

“Make up the numbers?”

“Yeah. Don't you know?”

Liu Hu shook his head and whispered, “I heard they originally planned to send three mid-tier warriors. But two of them suddenly got poached, and there wasn't enough time to find replacements. The elder decided to give up on winning.”

“Look at our roster. Not a single mid-tier fighter. We're all newcomers. Obviously, we're just here to get some experience.”

“Is that so...?”

Fang Heng looked over the youngsters in the waiting area. “Then why waste your own time participating?”

Liu Hu grimaced helplessly. “None of the other teams want me. I lost my first match and haven't had a chance to fight since. I'm hoping the elder will remember me and give me an opportunity next time.”

Fang Heng frowned inwardly.

It sounded like newly recruited warriors didn't have it easy...

“Hmph!”

Another warrior who'd accepted the assignment snorted disdainfully. “If that's how you all think, you'd be better off giving up now.”

“A warrior who starts thinking about defeat before even fighting has already lost his ambition. What's the point of pursuing the martial path then?”

Liu Hu flushed with embarrassment.

“I... that's not what I...”

Determination shone in Fan Chen's eyes. “So what if we might lose in the end? Isn't this still a chance to show what we're capable of? If you're going to fight, give everything you've got and leave the elder with a proper impression.”

Liu Hu felt his fighting spirit reignite and nodded. “You're right, brother. I've learned something today.”

Fang Heng said nothing and raised his head toward the arena platform.

Both sides sent members of their own species into the first round.

“It's the Kata Clan. They're difficult opponents,” Liu Hu observed quietly. “They're naturally very strong physically.”

“The Kata Clan's dimensional strength rating ranges from thirteen to thirty-five, which is extremely high. The Noel Clan is rated twenty-one to forty-two. They may look close on paper, but crossing the twenty-level threshold makes a substantial difference.”

Fan Chen commented from nearby, “Dimensional strength and racial talents are only part of it. Individual ability matters too.”

Fang Heng's heart gave a sudden leap as he listened.

Dimensional strength?!

Other species had dimensional strength too?

After thinking about it, that actually made sense.

Of course other species would have their own dimensional strength!

No wonder the Shadow Clan possessed far stronger attributes than ordinary species, even apart from their special abilities.

Their dimensional strength was probably part of the explanation.

He just didn't know how the dimensional strength levels these two mentioned compared with the game's own ratings.

If they used the same standard, would that mean the Noel Clan's dimensional strength could actually exceed his own?

Fang Heng considered this and asked, “Why do dimensional strength levels vary within a species?”

Fan Chen glanced at him, puzzled that he didn't understand such basic knowledge.
