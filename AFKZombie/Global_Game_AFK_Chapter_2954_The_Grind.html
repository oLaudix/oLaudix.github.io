Chapter 2954

The Grind

From original Chinese Chapter 2955 · 肝

In the underground base's research district.

Li Zhixuan had gathered all the researchers in the starship-construction area.

After spending a huge amount of Contribution Points, she and several colleagues had completed a simulated three-dimensional model of the starship on the base's second underground level.

Now Fang Heng didn't need to say another word. Everyone understood what he was doing.

Building a starship!

And an enormous one, unlike anything they had ever attempted.

The scientists were dumbstruck.

Hadn't they been assigned to build a Star Map communications base and a Kinsfolk warp passage?

How had that turned into constructing a ship?

And doing it in the real world, no less?

Even with the Kinsfolk system's assistance, building a starship in reality would be at least ten times harder than building one inside the game.

Zaka and the other players were even more astonished.

They had all known Fang Heng was working on something on the second underground level.

But something this huge?

Build a starship?

Us?

Was that even a job people like them could do?

Excitement flashed through Alec's eyes.

As expected, no one in this world could threaten Boss Fang anymore. The Vampires were about to conquer the stars!

"Everyone, starship construction is the Federation's highest-level secret mission. For security reasons, you were brought here under the cover of a different assignment. This project concerns humanity's future. I hope every one of you will devote your full efforts to completing the ship."

The Federation researchers were thoroughly taken in.

Oh.

So it was a secret Western Federation project.

That made sense.

The public assignment had been a cover to maintain secrecy.

They had been recruited to build a giant interstellar warship from the start!

No wonder.

Could an ordinary person really construct an underground facility on this scale?

The Federation had to be supporting Boss Fang from behind the scenes!

Listening to Li Zhixuan's speech, Fang Heng inwardly gave her a thumbs-up.

Who would have guessed? Li Zhixuan usually seemed so quiet and scholarly, yet she could lie without missing a beat or feeling the slightest guilt!

She had completely fooled the researchers.

After a pause, Li Zhixuan looked toward Zaka and the others. "As for the rest of you, once construction is complete, you'll have the option of remaining aboard as members of the ship's crew."

Wayne and the other players felt their hearts leap. They turned toward Fang Heng. When he nodded, their apprehension vanished.

Wayne had been worried that they'd offend the Western Federation during this operation and suffer for it once they parted ways with Fang Heng. He had even considered finding himself an escape route.

But Boss Fang had already thought of their future!

They were effectively building a starship for themselves!

Once the ship was finished, they could become part of its crew!

Li Zhixuan calmly surveyed the room and bowed. "You're among the Federation's finest researchers. Thank you for everything you're doing for humanity."

"Our warp passage is currently Level 3, and its carrying capacity is limited. It's running twenty-four hours a day transporting the essential materials needed to build the ship, so we can't obtain assistance from Kinsfolk mechanical creatures. Construction will be extremely difficult."

"Bear with it for now. First we'll build the main body. Once the hull is complete, we can upgrade the passage immediately..."

"If everything goes smoothly..."

After convincing the researchers, Li Zhixuan approached Fang Heng and spoke quietly. "If we begin with the main hull, we could just about finish the basic structure in a month. Think of it as an empty shell. Fitting out every compartment, installing weapons, and completing the onboard systems could take more than six months."

"Good."

An empty shell would be enough for the moment.

Its compartments would be empty, it would have no offensive capabilities, and its defenses would be weak. But once the shell was complete, it could warp as a whole and escape the Western Federation's restrictions.

And he still had his zombie clones.

He could speed construction up considerably!

Following Li Zhixuan's speech, the researchers and players threw themselves into around-the-clock shifts with renewed energy.

The goal was to build the starship's basic shell within a month.

But the researchers soon began looking at Fang Heng more and more strangely.

Every so often, a newly constructed ship compartment would seem to materialize from the lower levels, as though conjured by magic.

They could tell it had only recently been built.

It was rough and needed extensive finishing and later equipment installation.

But considering the unbelievable construction speed...

The researchers began to wonder whether several other teams were working around the clock on the fourth underground level.

There was simply no other explanation for such efficiency.

"How does it look?"

"The main compartments are usable. Some details still need modifying before we can assemble them. If the zombies can mass-produce these compartments, we could shorten the construction schedule by 50%. I'll make some adjustments to the plan..."

Li Zhixuan nodded and called the researchers together to discuss the next stage.

Excellent.

Fang Heng continued directing the zombie clones below to construct compartment shells.

The zombie clones...

From here on, it was time to grind without stopping.

Fang Heng opened the game notifications and took a glance.

They had now explored most of the planet.

Its mineral reserves were enormous. Even mining at full speed, he estimated that stripping the place bare would take two months.

But one thing puzzled him.

His Lickers hadn't seen a single alien organism during their explorations.

Were there really no Zerg on this world?

Then what had wiped out the Kinsfolk mercenary teams on the previous mining expedition?

...

Five days later.

Everything was proceeding in an orderly fashion.

Since learning of Fang Heng's starship project, Wayne's players had been energized and worked harder than ever.

Over the past few days, they had watched the three-dimensional blueprint gradually become a physical structure under Fang Heng and the research team's efforts!

They'd have stories to boast about afterward.

Ever seen a starship?

I built one with my own hands!

Zaka grinned to himself as he went back to sorting ore.

Nearby, Fang Heng checked his Kinsfolk Contribution at a newly installed central AI terminal.

Although Wayne's and Zaka's teams had been sorting ore like men possessed, they were gradually falling behind the growing output.

The good news was that after the main starship section was completed, the warp passage had reached Stage Four the day before, easing the bottleneck in materials transport.

Should he recruit some advanced Kinsfolk robots to speed things up next?

As he considered it, two players hurried down from the upper level, looking nervous.

"Boss Fang, Boss Wayne, we've got trouble. Strange creatures have been found outside. They might be hostile native life forms."

— End of Chapter 2954 —
