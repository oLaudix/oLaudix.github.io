Chapter 2070

Function

From original Chinese Chapter 2071 · 功能

It was a divine artifact, after all!

Fang Heng frowned as he studied the simulated character in his sea of consciousness.

In other words, all the trouble an alternate identity caused could simply be wiped away afterward?

Not bad!

Moreover, the simulated character's skills could be adjusted freely, provided they were never stronger than those of his true body.

At first glance, that seemed almost useless.

But Fang Heng spotted something important.

Talent skills!

Talents counted as skills, too. His simulated identity could inherit them—or choose not to.

That meant when his zombie clones reached their automatic revival time, they wouldn't immediately revive if his simulated character lacked the relevant talent.

He had gained a tool for partially controlling the revival of his zombie clones.

“Nice, nice…” he murmured.

A hidden function!

Overall, this divine artifact was excellent for disguising an identity. It might have limited usefulness to ordinary players, but Fang Heng was different.

He possessed special abilities that could alter his attributes. Combined with a simulated identity, they could make his disguises far more convincing.

Hold on!

With the artifact's help, perhaps…

Fang Heng's eyes suddenly lit up.

Since posting his commission on Old Black's shady website, he had received plenty of replies. One of the proposed items might actually be useful!

He quickly logged into the site again, opened his mailbox, and searched the anonymous responses.

Found it!

The message contained no text, only a screenshot of an item description.

[Item: Crusader Trial Talisman]

[Type: One-use consumable.]

[Description: Using this item allows the player to enter the higher-level game world Eternal Holy City and trigger a special mission, the Crusader's Trial. A player who passes the trial may qualify to become a Crusader and enter the Divine Realm to receive the Crusader inheritance.]

[Description: This item may only be used by one person. It allows a stay of up to 72 hours in Eternal Holy City.]

An interesting world-teleportation talisman.

It resembled the Punoze invitation used by the undead faction.

But he was currently inside Holy Court territory, where Punoze invitations were restricted and unusable.

This trial talisman, on the other hand, clearly belonged to the Holy Court faction.

Fang Heng had heard of Eternal Holy City. It was one of the Holy Court's main headquarters in the higher-level game worlds.

According to the description, a player who passed all the Crusader evaluations could even enter the Divine Realm and receive an inheritance as a Crusader.

Tempting indeed.

Since he'd posted his commission three days earlier, more than a dozen respondents had offered him this type of trial talisman. So it wasn't especially rare.

He hadn't dared use one before.

After all, the Holy Court had placed a bounty on him. Was he seriously going to walk into its headquarters in a higher-level game world?

Wouldn't they recognize him the instant he arrived?

The risk of exposure had been too great.

But things were different now.

With the divine artifact, he could create a false identity that only divine-level abilities could pierce. He could even conceal his zombie-clone talent.

The chance of the Holy Court identifying him would drop dramatically!

Of course, there was still some risk. But Fang Heng reasoned that as long as he kept a low profile, he was unlikely to be discovered immediately.

The Divine Realm's hunters were close behind him. He had to leave quickly.

One problem at a time. Staying here was impossible, so he might as well try it!

Having made up his mind, he canceled the commission and called Chu Yan, asking him to obtain a Crusader Trial Talisman in-game.

…

Two hours later, Fang Heng returned to the game world.

Whoosh!

The moment he logged in, he sensed the force locking onto him again.

[Hint: During your offline period, your relocation of the Yin Vein Rift and related destruction in Duncha earned a total of 520,000 Undead Faction Reputation and 220,000 Undead Faction Honor Points. Exact totals cannot be calculated until the event ends; rewards are settled hourly.]

[Hint: You are being tracked. The item The Illusion God's Joke in your backpack has activated. The tracking effect has been weakened by an additional 72%.]

Excellent!

Fang Heng was delighted. The divine artifact was worth it already!

No time to waste. Find Chu Yan!

He immediately took bat form and flew toward a coffee shop at a street corner.

“Boss Fang.”

Chu Yan watched him shift back into human form at the shop entrance.

“I got the item. The source is reliable. You can use it without worrying.”

He handed Fang Heng a sheet of paper covered in runic markings.

“Thanks. I'm being tracked, so I have to leave right away. You should be careful too. Once I've dealt with this trouble, I'll come find you.”

“Don't worry about us, Boss Fang. We've found a place to settle for now. But Eternal Holy City is a high-level world under Holy Court control. They've spent tens of thousands of years building their influence there. You must be careful.”

Chu Yan knew about the Crusader Trial Talisman. It was valuable, but hardly unheard of in Duncha.

Players could use it to enter Eternal Holy City and undertake Crusader trials. If they completed every test perfectly, they might earn the qualifications to become Crusaders and receive a divine blessing in the Divine Realm.

Or so the rumors claimed. Chu Yan had never heard of anyone actually doing it.

A single talisman lasted only 72 hours. Most players could complete a few rounds of tasks, collect rewards, and improve their standing with various NPCs before the time ran out.

Becoming a Crusader would probably require a dozen or more trips, let alone reaching the Divine Realm.

What Chu Yan had never imagined was that Fang Heng would use this method to escape Duncha.

It was rather like fleeing from a tiger and diving into a crocodile pit for shelter.

Chu Yan certainly wouldn't have dared try it—especially now that the Holy Court was investigating him too.

“Mm. The Divine Realm's pursuers may arrive soon. You should get out of here. Stay in touch.”

Fang Heng took the talisman and immediately poured mental energy into it.

Hum…

A faint light shimmered across its surface.

Huh?

Chu Yan raised an eyebrow. Before his eyes, Fang Heng's facial features were changing, and his figure grew noticeably taller.

Within seconds, he looked like a completely different person!

A moment later, light enveloped Fang Heng's entire body.

Whoosh!

With a flash, he vanished.

— End of Chapter 2070 —
