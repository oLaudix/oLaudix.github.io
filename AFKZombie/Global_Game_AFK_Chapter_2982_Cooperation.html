Chapter 2982

Cooperation

From Chinese Chapter 2983 · 合作

“Fang Heng, that's an interesting power. I'm not at my best, but I've already used every trump card I have. I doubt you've shown your true full strength either. There's no need to continue. This time, I admit defeat.”

As she spoke, Sura willingly let the power sustaining her body dissipate. Blood continually seeped from her skin.

This time, however, her body did not regenerate.

“I hope you'll inherit our race's will... and allow our species to continue, evolving and improving even further...”

Seeing Sura gradually release her power, Fang Heng also lowered his hand.

In truth, he was nowhere near as relaxed as he appeared.

He hadn't used his ultimate trump card, Everlasting Moon, or any of his Sacred Studies or Necromancy abilities. But against someone like Sura, whose physical power had been refined to the extreme, most of those abilities could only provide support. Everlasting Moon was the exception.

More importantly, her physical strength had far exceeded his imagination.

Had he not concealed a large number of Lickers beneath the ship with their Burrowing ability before the fight, using Life Sharing to spread the damage among them, he genuinely wouldn't have endured it.

Even his Undying Body couldn't have healed that much damage!

He watched Sura with deep wariness.

If Sura alone was this difficult, could he really handle both Proust in the Death Realm and his Cultivator, Mu Qingxin, at the same time?

Even with the Kinsfolk's Eye of Stars assisting him?

It still wasn't a safe bet.

Sura's body continued to decay. Slowly she raised her right hand and pushed it into her open mouth.

“Gulp, gulp...”

She pulled a cocoon from inside her body and tossed it to Fang Heng.

“I'll find a suitable place to enter dormancy again. I hope the Cultivators' war will be over by the time I awaken.”

Fang Heng caught the cocoon, surprised by how decisively she had conceded.

[Game Hint: You have obtained Ouroboros Cocoon (Complete).]

“Wait.”

“What is it?”

After thinking for a moment, Fang Heng threw the Ouroboros Cocoon back to Sura.

“Pa!”

With most of her body already dissolved, Sura reached out with her left hand to catch it. She stared at Fang Heng. “What do you mean?”

“Don't leave just yet. I want you to help me with something.”

“What?”

“I'm going to challenge another newborn to a fight. He's quite strong, and I want you to restrain his ‘Cultivator’ so she can't interfere.”

Fang Heng gestured toward the cocoon in Sura's hand. “Consider that your payment. Once the fight is over, we'll be even.”

Sura did not reply immediately. After regarding him in silence, she said, “Cultivators generally don't intervene in fights between newborns, though there are exceptions. But now I'm curious. Where is your ‘Cultivator’?”

Fang Heng spread his hands. “Don't even ask. She's unreliable. I don't trust her.”

“A fair bargain. I'll do everything I can, but I can't guarantee I can restrain a Cultivator of the same tier.”

After some thought, Sura swallowed the cocoon again. “There's still a problem. The auras we've exposed have already drawn the Shadow Clan here to hunt us. How are we going to leave safely?”

“With your abilities, leaving should be easy. As for me, you don't need to worry. Remember these coordinates, and come find me aboard my starship within seven days. I've already granted you access.”

Fang Heng quickly recited the world's coordinates, formed a seal before him, and slowly closed his eyes.

Hiss, hiss...

A dark magic array appeared beneath his feet.

Thick black mist poured from the array and rapidly enveloped his entire body.

Sura watched Fang Heng vanish into the mist, lost in thought.

As the surrounding World of Ten Thousand Forests rapidly contracted and disappeared, she chuckled softly.

“What an interesting fellow...”

Not far away, the various races had sensed that something was wrong and were converging rapidly on the wrecked starship.

“What's going on?”

“No idea.”

Suddenly, everyone noticed the forest ahead shrinking swiftly, followed by a sharp explosion.

“BOOM!!!”

A dense swarm of Zerg poured out of the collapsing woodland!

“Watch out! Zerg!”

As chaotic fighting broke out, three elves drawn by the unfamiliar aura exchanged startled looks among the trees farther back.

“That aura just now...”

“Yes.” The eldest elf nodded solemnly. “That was the aura of the Tree of Life. And I could see signs that the trees had been cultivated by our own kind.”

The others fell silent for a moment.

Why would the Tree of Life's aura appear on a human? And why did the vegetation bear traces of elven cultivation?

How strange!

“I left a tracking mark on the divine tree, but some power is interfering with it. I can't follow its signal...”

“The mark will last at least three years. Our immediate priority is to abandon this mission, return to our people, and report to the Elven Temple. I'm sure the temple will have a way to trace it.”

“Agreed.”

...

Seven days later.

The Death Realm.

A gloomy gray haze hung in the air.

Ever since the Death Realm Jewel had been transferred away, the realm's death aura had grown thinner by the day.

“The concentration of death aura has dropped drastically lately. It isn't just a shortage of Nether Crystals anymore. Every member of our clan is feeling the effects of the weakening aura.”

“The changes to the environment have forced us to compromise, but some stubborn members still refuse to leave. There have also been rumors circulating lately that cast you in an unfavorable light.”

“Oh? Really?” Fang Heng glanced at his recognition rating for the Undead World quest. “What are they saying?”

Notar replied helplessly, “People outside claim your arrival caused the changes to our world. Many Nether Clan members have been forced into the new world, but they still long for the Death Realm and hope to return to their homeland someday.”

Fair enough.

Fang Heng could understand that.

After all, this was their ancestral home.

But he still had to complete his Nether Lord advancement quest.

“Mr. Fang Heng, can we really do it?”

Notar looked at him hopefully. “Can you truly eradicate the Calamity and restore peace to the Death Realm?”

“Hmm... probably...”

Fang Heng privately thought that wiping out the Calamity might be possible. Restoring peace to the Death Realm, however, was another matter entirely.

The Death Realm Jewel had already been moved elsewhere. Over time, this realm would become an ordinary world.

“We've come this far. Let's give it everything we've got.”

“Yes.”

Notar nodded gravely. “My people and I will do everything we can. Please follow me.”

— End of Chapter 2982 —
