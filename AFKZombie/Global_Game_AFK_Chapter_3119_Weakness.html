Chapter 3119

Weakness

From Chinese Chapter 3120 · 虚弱

"Attack!"

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!

The swarm of Lickers immediately pounced toward the Evil Embryo's mountain of flesh.

Provoked, the Evil Embryo waved its dense array of fleshy spikes and counterattacked!

In an instant, the two sides were locked in fierce combat throughout the cavern!

Lickers repeatedly froze under the effects of illusions and were impaled, only to be freed by their companions.

After a period of fierce struggle, some of the Lickers gradually approached the Evil Embryo's main body.

"Slash!!!"

Razor-sharp claws left a blood-red mark across the Evil Embryo's flesh.

The mark disappeared almost immediately as the flesh regenerated.

What terrifying defenses and self-healing ability!

Nasir frowned repeatedly as he watched.

This was going to be difficult!

Attacking the Evil Embryo with Lickers was like taking a pair of nail clippers and trying to carve a mountain.

Yet a playful smile appeared at the corners of Fang Heng's mouth.

The Evil Embryo's regeneration wasn't the real problem.

Its damage-reflection ability was.

Whenever a Licker slashed with its claws, a layer of shadow immediately spread across them and inflicted a passive corrosive damage-reflection effect.

Purification!

A sacred scepter appeared in Fang Heng's hand, and he cast Sacred Purification toward the creatures from a distance!

The negative effects attached to the Lickers were removed.

Done!

The Lickers pounced on the Evil Embryo again, slashing furiously!

The monster's physical defenses were even more terrifying than its resistance to magic. It was harder to deal with than Fang Heng had expected.

However...

Fang Heng glanced at the game notifications out of the corner of his eye.

[Hint: Your zombie clone has dealt -124 damage to an unknown life form.]

[Hint: Your zombie clone's special ability has activated. Your zombie clone has absorbed 0.1 Constitution attribute points from the target.]

Attribute Absorption!

In addition to Division, the Lickers' modifications included Attribute Absorption.

That effect was exceptionally effective against boss-level creatures like the Evil Embryo, whose defenses and life force were terrifying!

The Lickers didn't need to deal heavy damage. They merely needed to break through its defenses. Every successful attack could drain a small amount of its attributes.

Each individual drain was minor, but the sheer number of his Lickers made all the difference!

Little by little, it would add up.

Drain it completely dry!

Fang Heng raised his hand again, pulled a stack of sealing rune papers from his backpack, and scattered them outside the sacred barrier.

"Bang! Bang, bang!!"

Lickers burst from the exploding rune papers and charged toward the Evil Embryo in the center of the cavern, dividing again and again as they advanced!

Nasir's eyelids twitched wildly.

Good grief!

There were so many!

In the blink of an eye, thousands of Lickers filled more than half the cavern, surging toward the monster at its center!

"Hiss, hiss-hiss-hiss..."

The Evil Embryo frantically thrust its thornlike tendrils outward.

Another chaotic battle erupted.

Nasir watched from the side, casting a cautious glance at Fang Heng.

Was this Fang Heng's true strength?

A Summoning Studies scholar!

At first glance, the Lickers didn't seem particularly powerful, but that was because their opponent was a legendary creature!

On closer inspection, Nasir could tell that each Licker had attributes comparable to a low-ranking warrior.

Their self-healing ability was especially terrifying.

He watched one Licker get severed by one of the Evil Embryo's tendrils, only to regenerate its body within seconds!

And there was their even more terrifying ability to revive shortly after death.

The Evil Embryo, however, was also tremendously powerful.

It was as immovable as a mountain.

By comparison, the Lickers were like wave after wave of ants that couldn't be killed.

Neither side seemed able to deal a decisive blow to the other.

Nasir looked toward Fang Heng again.

Fang Heng appeared completely confident and showed no intention of stopping. He simply allowed his summoned creatures to keep attacking.

In a short while, dense rings of Lickers surrounded the Evil Embryo's mountain of flesh.

The Lickers were constantly pierced by spiked tendrils or sent flying.

Yet some always slipped through the defensive net of spikes, landed on the Evil Embryo, and attacked without stopping!

...

In the upper cavern...

Outside the ritual magic array...

The Lanyue priests knelt before the ritual array, praying and keeping it active.

Black shadow tendrils continuously extended from the array, crept toward the kneeling priests, drained their life force completely, and dragged their bodies into the array to be devoured!

One priest after another had their lives consumed by the Evil Embryo.

Nearby, the Lanyue patriarch and several clan elders had never witnessed anything like this before. Terror entered their eyes.

"The Divine Embryo has never been this hungry."

"What's happening?"

The Lanyue Clan's ancestors had summoned the evil god through a ritual and had raised it at the bottom of the labyrinth ever since. Their people offered sacrifices and prayed to it in exchange for knowledge and various powers.

In the past, the evil god consumed the lives of hundreds of clan members each year. But this was the first time it had become so strangely agitated, ravenously devouring its followers.

An elder said gravely, "The Evil God is growing weaker. It needs to consume more flesh and feed on more suffering."

"Why is it weakening? Could it be because of the two sacrifices down there?"

Everyone's expression grew grave.

Nobody could answer the question.

Could someone survive after being thrown into the evil god's sacrificial cavern?

Impossible!

...

Two hours later.

Outside the labyrinth...

Several Noel Clan elders arrived with a group of warriors and more than twenty starships to provide reinforcements. They immediately began organizing a team to explore the entire labyrinth thoroughly.

Worried about the two men trapped inside, Mu Shunan stepped forward and volunteered. "I was there when Fang Heng and Nasir disappeared. I know something about the layout and conditions inside the mist-filled cavern. I request permission to join the search team."

"Very well."

The leading Noel Clan elder nodded lightly.

"Elder, we've questioned members of the Lanyue Clan. The labyrinth has always been sacred ground. Normally, no one except priests goes near it. Ordinary clan members have no idea what takes place inside."

"We've also searched their memories using mental suggestions, but found no clues."

"We won't wait any longer. Move out."

The Noel Clan elder waved a hand and led the squads into the labyrinth to begin searching.

Anxious for his companions' safety, Mu Shunan led the group deeper along the passage he'd taken earlier.

Just as during their first exploration, the teams didn't encounter a single enemy along the way.

Soon they reached the enormous magic array where Fang Heng and Nasir had vanished.

The two Noel Clan elders most knowledgeable about Alchemy approached the formation and bent down to study it.

After waiting a full five minutes, the commanding elder grew impatient. "Well, elders? What have you found?"

The two elders stood up, exchanged glances, and shook their heads.

"We can confirm it's an unusual large-scale magic array of at least Transcendent level. Activating it requires a specific sequence."

— End of Chapter 3119 —
