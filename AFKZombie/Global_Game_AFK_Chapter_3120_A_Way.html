Chapter 3120

A Way

From Chinese Chapter 3121 · 办法

“However, there isn't much energy left in the magic array. We can confirm that Fang Heng and his companion couldn't have been teleported very far. They're most likely still somewhere in the cavern.”

The elder leading the group nodded and turned to the teams behind him. “Split up and search. There must be members of the Lanyue clan hiding in these caves. Find them at all costs. If they refuse to cooperate, kill them on sight.”

“Yes!”

Meanwhile, at the bottom of the labyrinthine cavern.

Nasir sheltered inside the holy barrier, watching the swarm of Lickers relentlessly hurl themselves at the enormous evil embryo. Astonishment gradually filled his eyes.

The assault had already gone on for nearly two hours!

Yet the Lickers were still locked in combat with the evil embryo.

What Nasir could never have imagined was that the seemingly immovable mountain had actually fallen into a severe disadvantage!

Fang Heng's summoned creatures carried the effect of holy purification in their attacks. They carved deep wounds across the evil embryo's exterior, and the purification continued inflicting secondary damage afterward.

Layers of rotting flesh slowly peeled from the evil embryo, falling to the ground and dissolving into foul-smelling pools of pus.

Compared to two hours earlier, the evil embryo had shrunk dramatically to a third of its original size.

Fang Heng glanced at the game notifications.

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Licker) has dealt 17,633 damage to an unknown creature.]

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Licker)'s passive ability has activated. The zombie clone has absorbed 0.2 Agility from the target creature. The zombie clone has reached its maximum absorbed attribute limit and cannot improve further.]

After more than two hours of continuous attacks, the Lickers had drained an enormous amount of basic attributes. Their damage against the evil embryo had increased dramatically, plainly visible to the naked eye.

Even the speed and strength of its spiked-tentacle attacks were nowhere near what they had been at the beginning.

Countless Lickers had surrounded the evil embryo, biting and clawing at it without pause.

The evil embryo was visibly weakening.

That was about enough.

Nasir swallowed nervously.

He had never imagined witnessing anything like this.

The evil embryo, once as unshakable as a mountain, was slowly collapsing before his eyes. More than two-thirds of the eyeballs embedded in its flesh, which had formerly opened and closed incessantly, were now shut.

Fang Heng silently assessed the evil embryo's condition.

It had reached its limit!

Its defense had been reduced to zero, and every Licker attack now dealt over ten thousand damage. Even so, the evil embryo's health regeneration didn't seem to have suffered a fatal blow.

No point waiting any longer.

He would turn up the pressure and hasten its destruction.

“Nasir, stay here and watch. I'll go lend a hand.”

Nasir's heart skipped a beat, but he agreed. “All right. Be careful yourself!”

Fang Heng flashed toward the evil embryo, putting away the holy scepter and switching to his holy greatsword.

Sensing the threat he posed, the evil embryo sent the dense mass of spiked tentacles protruding from its bloated body straight at him!

“Whoosh!!!”

The heavy sword in Fang Heng's hand swept forward!

“BOOM!!!”

A vast cluster of spikes shattered beneath the powerful strike!

The torn flesh rapidly dissolved under the influence of holy power.

“Hum…”

A secondary wave of holy energy rippled outward, instantly wiping out great swathes of spikes.

“Tap!”

Fang Heng lightly touched the air with his toe, changed direction midair, and used a double jump to flash toward the evil embryo's right flank.

“BOOM!!!”

The greatsword swept across once more!

A single slash destroyed most of the fleshy spiked tentacles extending from the demonic embryo!

With those spikes dissolving, the Lickers quickly climbed onto the evil embryo itself, surrounding it several layers deep and dealing damage in a frenzy!

Keep going!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes, lifted his holy greatsword again, and brought it crashing down on the evil embryo.

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

The entire cavern filled with the sounds of rotten flesh bursting apart and the hissing of shadow energy being purified.

From within the holy barrier behind him, Nasir watched the evil embryo steadily shrink as great masses of pus and rotting flesh fell away. He could only stare in amazement.

So Fang Heng hadn't been showing anything close to his true strength in the arena all along!

With power like this, he would unquestionably rank among the very top fighters there.

After more than ten minutes of relentless fighting, a thunderous explosion rang out and the entire evil embryo abruptly burst apart!

“Hiss! Hissss!!!”

Huge chunks of rotten flesh flew in every direction. Many struck the holy barrier in front of Nasir and hissed violently, frightening him into taking another two steps backward.

The remaining chunks landed on the cavern walls and floor, corroding the rock furiously.

An unbearable stench filled the cavern.

Hm?

Fang Heng noticed a dark piece of wood among the remains of the exploded evil embryo.

It had fallen into the rotting flesh but remained intact, unaffected by its powerful corrosive properties.

Could it be something valuable?

An idea stirred in Fang Heng's mind, and he raised a hand.

Whoosh!

Whoosh!

Guided by his mental power, the dark piece of wood flew toward Fang Heng, who caught it in his palm and examined it.

What was this thing? The wood looked extraordinary and felt exceptionally heavy. He had no idea what kind of tree it came from. A crow emblem was engraved on the front, while the back was covered in dense, unusual patterns.

They resembled alchemical inscriptions, but he couldn't tell which ancient language system they belonged to.

“Brother Fang Heng, you all right?”

Nasir waved to him from within the holy barrier.

“Yeah, I'm fine.” Fang Heng walked toward Nasir, held up the piece of wood, then tossed it to him. “Take a look. Know what this is?”

Nasir caught the wood and turned it over several times before tossing it back. “No idea. Doesn't seem like ordinary wood, though. Put it away for now. I'll look through some records when we get back.”

“All right.”

Fang Heng shrugged and stuffed the wooden piece into his backpack.

“Brother Fang, I don't know what that bit of wood is, but I do recognize that.” Nasir pointed toward the place where the evil embryo had died. “Now that is something really good.”

“Hm?”

Fang Heng followed Nasir's pointing finger toward the center of the cavern where the evil embryo had perished.

A cloud of murky gray energy floated in midair.

“What's that?”

“Ancient knowledge.”

“The evil embryo devoured plenty of evil beings from the Endless Void. Some contained ancient knowledge within them, knowledge the embryo couldn't digest, so it accumulated inside its body,” Nasir explained. “If you absorb it, you can gain a great deal of occult knowledge and improve your occult abilities. I've heard you've studied occultism. This would definitely be useful to you!”

Fang Heng nodded slightly.

In that case, it really was something good.

“But be careful,” Nasir warned. “The ancient knowledge may still be mixed with some of the evil embryo's aura. Absorbing it rashly could cause a backlash. It's best to take it back first, find a way to purify that aura completely, and only use it once you're sure it's safe.”

— End of Chapter 3120 —
