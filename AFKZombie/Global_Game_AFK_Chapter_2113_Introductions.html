Chapter 2113

Introductions

From original Chinese Chapter 2114 · 引荐

Everyone recovered from their shock and stared intently at Klevi.

Previously, they'd doubted Glenan's story, believing the young man had been fooled by a few smooth words from strangers.

But what they'd just witnessed far exceeded their imagination!

The power of nature could truly be made visible!

“Ahem.”

Sandy cleared his throat and addressed the crowd. “Well? Does anyone still have doubts? Please believe me: as followers of nature, we always respect its laws. To accuse us of deception is an insult to everything we stand for.”

The crowd parted, allowing an elderly man to emerge from a small door at the back. He bowed slightly to Fang Heng's party in apology.

“My name is Yule, president of this town's Tree Whisperer Alliance. Please forgive our earlier discourtesy. We've suffered a great deal recently. Please, take a seat, and we can discuss matters properly.”

They gathered around a desk.

The atmosphere abruptly turned solemn, and for a moment nobody spoke.

Sandy broke the silence. “As we've already explained, we came here following nature's guidance. We want to learn everything we can about the World Tree. We heard you had documents relating to it, so we came to see you.”

Yule considered his words before replying. “Since its founding, the Tree Whisperer Alliance has devoted itself to protecting nature and preserving its legacy.”

“As for the mysteries of the World Tree... To tell you the truth, we know very little ourselves. I'm afraid you won't find many relevant documents here.”

Fang Heng frowned.

“However...”

Yule changed direction. “Our Tree Whisperer Alliance does still maintain a certain connection with the Tree Spirit race.”

“What?!”

Glenan couldn't contain himself. He shot up from his chair, eyes shining as he stared at Yule.

Many of the others also looked astonished.

The Tree Whisperer Alliance was still in contact with the Tree Spirits?

Most of its members were hearing this for the first time.

Fang Heng nodded. “Could you introduce us to them?”

Yule shook his head. “I'm sorry, but that would be difficult. Besides, it has always been the Tree Spirits who contact us. We've never had any way to reach them ourselves.”

Fang Heng was about to press the matter when fresh game notifications appeared.

[Game Hint: Your current quest, Tree Whisperer Alliance—Secrets of the Ancient Divine Tree, is complete. The next-stage quest, Find the Tree Spirit Race, has been triggered.]

Quest Name: Find the Tree Spirit Race.

Difficulty: S.

Quest Description: Through your contact with the Tree Whisperer Alliance, you have learned of the Tree Spirit race. Attempt to establish contact with them.

Quest Requirement: Meet members of the Tree Spirit race.

Quest Reward: Unlock the next-stage quest.

[Game Hint: Due to a reward chosen during your prerequisite quests, you have received additional quest guidance.]

[Game Hint: Once your favorability with the Tree Whisperer Alliance reaches 500, Mr. Yule can introduce you to the Tree Spirit race, granting additional favorability with the Tree Spirits.]

[Game Hint: You may also complete the quest through other means.]

Fang Heng skimmed the notifications and nodded toward Mo Jiawei.

The instructions were perfectly clear.

The old man still had doubts about their identities and didn't want to introduce them to the Tree Spirits so rashly.

That was understandable.

Their next questline would probably involve completing tasks for the Tree Whisperer Alliance to raise its favorability.

Once that reached the required level, meeting the Tree Spirits should follow naturally.

Sandy, being an NPC, hadn't received any notifications. He continued pressing the issue. “Then how often have the Tree Spirits contacted you in the past?”

Yule considered for a moment and frowned. “That's difficult to say. Sometimes we receive word from them within a single week. At other times, several years may pass without any contact.”

Fang Heng glanced at the others and interrupted Sandy's questioning. “Mr. Yule, if possible, we'd like to stay here for a while and consult your books.”

Seeing the look Fang Heng gave him, Sandy quickly agreed. “Precisely. We have received a revelation from the God of Nature, warning that this world may be facing an unprecedented catastrophe. If there's anything you need from us, please don't hesitate to ask.”

Yule looked troubled. “Very well. The community center happens to have many empty rooms. You're welcome to stay here, if the accommodations suit you.”

“There's no need to trouble you. There's a hotel next door, and we'll book a few rooms there. It's already late, so we'll return tomorrow.”

Fang Heng rose and took his leave.

Once they were outside the community center, Mo Jiawei and the others turned to Fang Heng.

He spread his hands. “We'll stay in town for a while. The next stage probably involves the Tree Whisperer Alliance. Besides following the game hints, let's look for clues elsewhere and learn more about this world.”

“Agreed.”

· · ·

The following evening,

Sandy had practically taken over the community center's reading room.

He had borrowed every book there concerning the Tree Spirit race and piled them around himself.

He'd already skimmed through most of the material.

“Fang Heng, this world may be far more dangerous than it looks. The claim that the Tree Spirits once controlled the entire world could very well be true.”

Fang Heng asked, “Found anything relevant to our quest?”

“Mm. Judging by photographs of ruins discovered around the world, the Tree Spirits really existed and possessed unknown powers. But several centuries ago, the Federation began deliberately covering up evidence of their existence... And from the old newspapers I checked, there are clues suggesting the Federation and the Tree Spirits were once in conflict.”

“If you ask me, the Federation must have much more information.”

As he spoke, Klevi returned with Gu Qingzhu, and the two entered the room.

“How did it go?”

Gu Qingzhu shook her head gently.

Klevi said quietly, “We went outside to investigate. Some special power is influencing the natural energy of this world. Whenever we use natural magic, our abilities are weakened considerably. That power covers the sky, but we haven't been able to determine much else yet.”

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

They hadn't found anything especially useful.

It seemed their breakthrough would have to come through the Tree Whisperer Alliance.

Unconsciously, everyone turned toward Mo Jiawei.

He was looking down at his phone.

This world even had a short-video platform.

Strictly speaking, he wasn't slacking off.

They could gather plenty of information about this world through short videos too.

— End of Chapter 2113 —
