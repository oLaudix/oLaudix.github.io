Chapter 2967

Entering the Battlefield

From Chinese Chapter 2968 · 入场

Without Fang Heng, the Western Federation lacked the ability to complete high-level missions.

Construction of the star map continued, but progress was slow.

In truth, the Western Federation didn't even want the star map completed too quickly. They had been deliberately dragging their feet.

Without Fang Heng and Charlotte's assistance, the Western Federation's experience farming speed had also fallen drastically.

A single high-level mission had taken them more than a month to complete.

“Reporting!”

“Come in.”

Under the watchful eyes of the Western Federation's senior officials, a young soldier entered the conference room and reported, “Captain, the president has approved your emergency mission request. We can depart for the target star system at any time.”

“Good!”

Captain Giselle stood up and surveyed everyone present.

“Everyone, the mission begins in ten minutes. Make your preparations.”

Two days earlier, Captain Giselle had accepted an emergency mission issued by the Kinsfolk system.

A mission involving the Ouroboros Seed.

Its difficulty was the highest possible: SSS-grade.

Under normal circumstances, Captain Giselle and the Western Federation would never have accepted such a high-risk mission.

But this assignment was different. It was a support mission, and several factions would be participating at once.

The Western Federation figured they could tag along, put in an appearance, and perhaps get lucky.

What if they managed to find an Ouroboros Seed?

At the very least, they could meet several Kinsfolk allies, try to build good relationships, gain experience fighting alongside alliance forces, and broaden their horizons.

The Federation therefore formed an observation delegation to accompany the fleet on the mission.

It even included an expert group from the Presidential Office, much to Captain Giselle's displeasure.

“Prepare for departure.”

Seven starships roared to life. Pale-white points of light surrounded them as they entered jump mode one after another.

...

Half an hour later, the human starships emerged from the jump passage.

The Western Federation fleet led by Captain Giselle arrived at the battlefield in formation.

Looking through the starships' protective barriers, they saw vast stretches of red fungal carpet shrouded in Calamity as far as the eye could see. Countless low-level insect nests lay scattered across it like scars.

Captain Giselle's expression turned grave.

The strength of an SSS-grade insect nest was truly terrifying. Lower-grade nests were practically everywhere, and the Western Federation had never faced an environment this hostile.

At their fleet's current strength, they could probably only handle some low-level nests around the battlefield's outer edges.

Senior officials from the Federation's various departments had gathered in the flagship's control room to observe the situation outside.

Besides inspecting the insect swarm, they were also watching the Kinsfolk's allied forces.

Good grief.

The gap in strength was enormous.

The allied races routinely deployed fleets with dozens of ships, including many enormous warships.

By comparison, the Western Federation's forces weren't even worth looking at.

“Other allied battle fleets have hundreds of ships in their formations and carry more advanced equipment. Our current strength really is rather limited.”

“Don't belittle yourselves. We've simply started later than they did. What they can accomplish, we can accomplish someday too. For now, we need to rely on the Kinsfolk and find ways to strengthen ourselves as quickly as possible.”

“Let's treat this as an opportunity to broaden our horizons.”

“Mm.”

Everyone nodded.

The starships entered combat mode. Their onboard cannons deployed and began coordinating attacks against the insect swarm.

Meanwhile, the delegation secretly observed the warships of other races.

Generally speaking, most races relied on Kinsfolk starships in combat.

However, each race had modified its ships to suit its own strengths and needs. Some carried extraordinary defensive systems and weapons, including anti-magic hulls, spatial barriers, and compressed-space cannons.

By comparison, their own ships looked terribly shabby.

They were almost entirely standard Kinsfolk products, with scarcely any modifications at all.

“Beep, beep...”

A soft alert sounded through the main control room.

“We've received a message from another race on the Kinsfolk battlefield. It appears to be from an alien fleet behind us. We're trying to decode it...”

A moment later, the researchers finished decoding the message. The researcher reading the translated text on the screen frowned deeply.

Captain Giselle asked, “What does it say?”

“They're telling us to move toward the rear flank and stay out of their way. They say we shouldn't loiter in the main battle zone and interfere with their deployments. They want us to get the hell out of their way.”

“What does that mean?”

The staff member swallowed and replied softly, “The Shadow Clan, which leads the alliance, has convened the forces to arrange an attack on the central SSS-grade insect nest. At the appointed time, fleets will launch simultaneous assaults from several sectors, each under a designated commander. We weren't invited to take part in the planning meeting...”

Captain Giselle's expression darkened.

“Captain, if we don't move, they'll run straight over us...”

“Bang!!!”

Fury rising in their chest, Captain Giselle slammed a fist onto the ship's table and ordered through gritted teeth, “Evade toward the right rear sector.”

Damn it!

They had been far too naive.

In this world where the strong preyed upon the weak, none of the Kinsfolk allies took them seriously.

Only strength could earn respect.

It was all because of those idiot experts from the Federal Inspectorate and the Presidential Office!

If either Fang Heng or Charlotte were here, they wouldn't feel nearly so helpless.

“Pay no attention to these alien barbarians. We need only proceed according to our plan. At present there's a gap between our strength and the Kinsfolk's high-tier allies, but that's nothing to fear. Given a little more time, we're confident we can gradually close it.”

Vion had also joined the accompanying observation delegation as a member of the Presidential Office's expert group.

Seeing the situation, he started trying to save face.

“Fall back for now. Don't get into a conflict with the allied forces.”

The Western Federation's seven starships began slowly retreating toward the right rear.

After withdrawing some distance, they were clear of the alien ships.

Suddenly, points of flickering spatial light appeared off the fleet's left front flank.

The flashes rapidly multiplied until they formed a broad field of light.

An interstellar fleet!

Judging by the intensity of the jump beams, another alien fleet must have arrived at the battlefield.

Looking toward the battlefield's center, they saw a fleet of more than ninety ships arrive.

The advanced energy-beam weapons aboard the ships immediately activated, unleashing a dense barrage into the insect swarm ahead.

Watching the fierce battle through their windows, the Federation soldiers couldn't help feeling a little envious.

The attack power of a large fleet like that was on an entirely different level from their ordinary squadron.

When would they ever be able to assemble such a mighty fleet?

Ten years?

Or twenty?
