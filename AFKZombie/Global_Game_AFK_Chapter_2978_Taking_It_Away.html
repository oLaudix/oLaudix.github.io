Chapter 2978

Taking It Away

From Chinese Chapter 2979 · 带走

Vion and Giselle kept stealing anxious glances at Fang Heng.

Fang Heng's people had secretly mined enormous quantities of ore from the Shadow Clan alliance's territory.

Previously, the Shadow Clan had been focused on the insect nests and hadn't had the manpower to investigate the creatures raiding its mines thoroughly.

If they didn't leave soon, they were bound to be discovered eventually.

Zaka was getting impatient too, frequently looking toward Fang Heng.

The main battlefield was a mess, and the Ouroboros Seeds had been badly wounded. This was the perfect opportunity to kill monsters and snatch treasures.

How could Boss Fang restrain himself from rushing down and grabbing a share?

Fang Heng looked as calm as ever, staring toward the battlefield's center.

Under the combined assault of the Shadow Clan and Crocodile Clan, the Cultivator mother body was no longer capable of meaningful resistance. Her health rapidly drained away until she finally exploded before everyone's eyes.

A crocodile squad quickly found an Ouroboros Seed cocoon among the fragments left by the dead mother body and respectfully handed it to the Shadow Clan.

Seeing the nest completely destroyed, the crocodile captain was in a fine mood. He laughed, patted Fang Heng on the shoulder, and said, “Brother, it seems that 'Cultivator' you mentioned wasn't much of a threat after all. The war is over.”

With the nest destroyed, Fang Heng and the other invited allied races had outlived their usefulness.

Next, the Shadow Clan and the races leading its alliance would begin dividing up the planet's mineral resources.

The Crocodile Clan started ushering everyone out.

“Your mission is complete. You can pack your things and head home now. Leave the rest to us.”

Fang Heng ignored the mockery in the crocodile's tone and drew his gaze away from the squad searching the battlefield for cocoons.

“Indeed. I was worrying unnecessarily. The mission is complete, so we'll take our leave.”

Zaka curled his lip and silently cursed the Crocodile Clan for being no better than the Shadow Clan's bootlicking dogs. Then he followed Fang Heng and Captain Giselle's party back toward their ships.

They'd collected mountains of ore and made a fortune over the previous days, but were they really just going to leave like this?

It felt like a roaring beginning ending with a whimper.

Zaka glanced at Fang Heng and quietly reminded him, “Boss Fang, shouldn't we stop stealing ore now? Once they turn their attention back and search carefully, they might find clues that lead them straight to us.”

“Mm. We've stopped mining. Go back and prepare for the jump. Wait for my signal. We still have two things to do. I'll be back soon.”

Fang Heng turned to Captain Giselle. “Captain Giselle, the mission is over. I'd advise you to withdraw immediately. Staying any longer could cause unnecessary trouble.”

“All right. I understand. I'll jump out of here immediately.” Giselle looked at Fang Heng and nodded gravely. “Thank you.”

Seeing Fang Heng break away from the team and leave alone, Vion curled his lip.

“Tch. Who knows what trick he's up to this time...”

...

The Eye of Stars positioning site in Area Six.

After receiving news that the front line had stabilized, the Iron-Eater Clan shut down the Eye of Stars and put it into standby mode.

The Iron-Eater Clan continued guarding the prism devices as usual.

If the situation changed, they would immediately reactivate the Eye of Stars' positioning system for another attack.

At the perimeter, the Iron-Eater guards noticed someone approaching and raised their alert level.

“Mr. Fang Heng.”

Recognizing Fang Heng, the Iron-Eaters lowered their guard.

Over the past few days, Fang Heng had excavated underground tunnels inside several prism tower construction areas. Construction materials for the Eye of Stars prism towers had been delivered continuously through those passages.

That was why the Iron-Eater Clan had been able to finish the towers ahead of schedule.

Fang Heng walked toward a prism tower and asked, “How is the Eye of Stars prism tower doing?”

The Iron-Eater leader nodded respectfully. “Thanks to your help, everything is currently very stable.”

“Excellent.”

Fang Heng swept his gaze across the Iron-Eaters gathered around the tower.

The Iron-Eater Clan had an exceptional natural aptitude for constructing alloy structures.

What a pity.

He couldn't put that talent to work for himself.

“I assume you've heard about the main battlefield. The insect nest has been dealt with. There's no further need to operate the Eye of Stars.”

An Iron-Eater officer responsible for operations and maintenance hurried over. “Mr. Fang Heng, our instructions from the front line are to remain on standby.”

“I don't care whose orders you've received. All I know is that the Shadow Clan told me I could leave now.”

“Huh?”

The Iron-Eater officer blinked at Fang Heng in confusion and suspicion.

What did he mean by leaving?

If he wanted to go, why not just go? Why come all the way over to announce it to the Iron-Eater Clan?

Fang Heng raised his head, glanced at the towering prism structure behind the officer, and shrugged. “What are you standing around for? I paid for the materials used to build these Eye of Stars positioning and defense module towers. Naturally, if I'm leaving, I'm taking them with me. I'd appreciate your help dismantling them.”

The Iron-Eaters erupted into an uproar.

Good grief! Take them away?

He had to be dreaming!

Granted, the humans had supplied all kinds of ore for the later stages of construction. But the most valuable components—the base and main prism—had been prepared in advance by the Shadow Clan and the other races.

Nobody had ever made such a demand before.

“Whoosh!”

Fang Heng pushed off the ground, appeared before the Iron-Eater officer, seized him by the throat with one hand, and lifted him into the air.

“I'll say it again. Dismantle the prisms and load them aboard my ship. You have ten minutes.”

The Iron-Eater officer begged desperately, “Please, don't kill me! At least allow me to report this to the Shadow Clan...”

Fang Heng raised the officer higher and said coldly, “What? Are you suggesting I'm lying to you?”

As he spoke, dense swarms of Lickers surged up from the underground tunnels, surrounding the entire Iron-Eater group in the blink of an eye.

“Hurry up.”

The officer and the other Iron-Eaters turned pale and fell speechless.

The Iron-Eater Clan had little combat strength of its own. They knew they couldn't win and didn't dare fight.

After some hesitation, somebody began kneeling, and soon the surrounding Iron-Eaters were all on the ground, begging for mercy and knocking their heads against the earth like pounding garlic.

“Mr. Fang Heng, if you take the prisms, the Shadow Clan will certainly hold us responsible. We can't bear that punishment. The lives of our entire clan are in your hands. Please show mercy.”

Fang Heng frowned. What were they getting at?

They were making him sound like some great villain.

On second thought, the Iron-Eater Clan was also being pushed around by others, and they'd always treated him decently enough.

Thinking of that, Fang Heng threw the officer aside. “In that case, I'll give you a way to survive. Once you've finished transporting the prisms, come back with me. I'll protect you afterward.”

Huh?

The Iron-Eaters were dumbfounded.

“I've offered you a chance to live. The choice is yours. If the prisms aren't transported within ten minutes, don't blame me for what happens.”

The Iron-Eaters exchanged glances. Without further hesitation, they immediately began dismantling the Eye of Stars prisms.

In fact, much of the Eye of Stars prism equipment was designed to be dismantled. Mechanisms for that purpose had been incorporated during construction.

Removing 80% of the main structure took just three minutes.

Kinsfolk transport robots rapidly carried the enormous prism components through the underground passages and into the flagship.

— End of Chapter 2978 —
