Chapter 2198

Inheritance

From original Chinese Chapter 2199 · 传承

Fang Heng wasn't the only human taking part in the infiltration of the Valley of Tree Rings.

The Leaf Clan had already arranged for three members of the Tree Whisperers Alliance to join the operation.

They'd originally intended it as something of a gamble.

With Fang Heng participating, Senzo believed their chances were considerably better.

During a break, Fang Heng struck up a conversation with the three Tree Whisperers and finally learned a little more from them.

“I see. A battle of succession.”

Fang Heng had been puzzled by the relationship among the four great Tree Spirit clans for some time.

Now he was beginning to understand.

They were rivals!

Whenever the Tree Spirits' Divine Tree began to decline, the race would choose a new generation of rulers at the appropriate time.

In the past, the four clans had fought over the position whenever that happened.

The Soul Clan had won the previous struggle. As a result, they took control of the Divine Tree and established a unified Council of Elders to command the entire Tree Spirit race.

Now the Council of Elders had fallen into disuse, leaving the race without a leader. The next leader would inevitably emerge from one of the four great clans.

Conflict among them was unavoidable.

And without the Council to mediate, this struggle could spiral even further out of control.

Fang Heng raised his head toward the barrier surrounding the Council of Elders ahead.

The four great Tree Spirit clans had gathered in four separate areas, as though by mutual agreement.

When the Wood Clan finally arrived, the elders of all four clans went to the center to negotiate.

Meanwhile, Fang Heng and the three Tree Whisperers stood behind an elder of the Leaf Clan.

Since relations between Fang Heng and Vannis were practically hostile, Grand Elder Senzo had assigned another Leaf Clan elder, Osman, to lead their operation.

“Everyone, ahead is a special energy barrier established by the Council of Elders. We've investigated it already. The Council has been completely abandoned. The only way to open the outer barrier is for all four clans to combine their strength.”

“Grand Elder Senzo believes that every clan wants to lead the Tree Spirits, and none will yield easily. Once they enter the Council grounds, they'll probably begin fighting over the treasures left behind.”

“The clan elders are still discussing their approach. After they break the outer barrier together, all four clans will enter the Council grounds. I'll then lead you around the rear and into the Valley of Tree Rings.”

Osman continued, “Each clan is wary of the Council's sacred grounds. They're unlikely to be the first to investigate the Valley of Tree Rings. That should buy us a little time, though not much.”

“Mm. Understood.”

“Anyone entering the Tree Spirits' sacred land without the Council's permission will suffer punishment from the Divine Tree and backlash from their bloodline. No one knows how much power those restrictions still hold over our people. We'll wait outside the entrance to the valley to support you. You must find the Divine Wood Altar quickly.”

“All right.”

Everyone silently committed Osman's instructions to memory.

Fang Heng nodded to show he understood.

They remained where they were, waiting quietly.

Before long, the grand elders of the four clans reached some kind of agreement and joined forces, using their origin power to break the Council's outer seal.

The ground trembled faintly beneath their feet, and specks of light appeared across the pale-green barrier ahead.

“BANG!”

With a faint cracking sound, the entire barrier shattered before their eyes.

Almost simultaneously, the four Tree Spirit clans surged forward from four directions, charging into the Council grounds under their elders' leadership.

“Move!”

Osman called out and immediately led Fang Heng and the others inside.

At first, the clans maintained a fragile peace in the outer halls of the Council, not yet openly turning on one another.

But by the time Fang Heng and Osman passed the great hall, faint sounds of arguing were already coming from deeper inside.

The rest of the journey passed without incident.

Osman guided Fang Heng through the hall, took a narrow path behind the Hall of Leaf Speech, and followed a vine-covered corridor until they reached the mouth of a small valley.

“Everyone, we're here. Beyond this point is the entrance to the Valley of Tree Rings, sacred land of the Council of Elders. Go. Our people will be waiting outside to receive you. Be quick.”

“Understood.”

Fang Heng responded and, without wasting any more time, darted into the narrow opening along with the other three Tree Whisperers.

Fang Heng glanced at the notifications.

No wonder the Tree Spirits believed entering this place would trigger bloodline suppression and prevent them from using their full strength.

The entrance to the Valley of Tree Rings was extremely narrow, scarcely wide enough for one person to pass, and in places they even had to turn sideways.

Fang Heng walked at the front, slowly following the passage.

After roughly ten minutes, the ravine gradually widened ahead.

Fang Heng involuntarily slowed his pace.

The three Tree Whisperers following him saw him pause. Rather than waiting, they quickened their steps and overtook him.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Suddenly, several mottled snakes darted from crevices in the rock walls on either side!

“Watch out!”

Anna, a burly man leading the group, spotted the shadows ahead. He shouted a warning and swung his machete!

“CRACK!”

A patterned snake more than half a meter long was cut in two in midair and fell to the ground.

The other two weren't quite as quick to react.

Although they heard Anna's warning and tried to dodge, one was bitten by a snake that burst from the rocks!

Anna swung his machete repeatedly, protecting the injured companion as they withdrew together.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The group hurriedly pulled beam weapons from their backpacks and returned fire, clearing away the pursuing snakes.

“This is bad. The snakes are venomous!”

Seeing his companion's lips turning purple, Anna immediately realized the danger. He washed the bite with clean water and applied an antidote from his backpack to the wound.

Color gradually returned to the injured man's pale face.

For the moment, he'd recovered.

Anna and the others looked warily toward the passage ahead.

It was long, and who knew how many more venomous snakes lurked inside the cracks along its walls?

“This doesn't look good.”

Anna picked up a pebble and hurled it hard into the passage ahead.

“CLACK!”

The stone struck the rock wall heavily.

Whoosh… whoosh, whoosh…

At once, more than a dozen venomous snakes of varying sizes emerged from the rock crevices, only to slip quickly back into hiding.

Damn.

Everyone's heart sank.

Osman had warned them that the Valley of Tree Rings was full of danger. None of them had imagined they'd encounter such trouble before even reaching the valley proper.
