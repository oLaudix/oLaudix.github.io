Chapter 2114

Protest

From original Chinese Chapter 2115 · 游行

Seeing everyone staring at him, Mo Jiawei looked up in confusion.

“Old Mo, got any inspiration?”

Mo Jiawei pulled a miserable face. “Oh, come on. What inspiration would I have?”

Sandy crowded closer. “Boss Mo, there's no need to be modest! Why not just say whatever comes to mind? Point us in any direction.”

Mo Jiawei scratched his head and answered casually, “Didn't the game tell us? Maybe they won't give us quests because our favorability isn't high enough?”

Sandy considered this. “You mean our initial relationship is too low, so we can't trigger the follow-up missions?”

“Yes, yes! Exactly!” Mo Jiawei nodded hastily. “I'll search for some decent gift shops nearby. We can buy presents and deliver them one by one. You can say a few flattering words, and sooner or later we'll find a way forward.”

After tossing out the suggestion, Mo Jiawei went back to fiddling with his phone.

“Huh?”

Suddenly, something on the screen caught his eye. He gave a surprised murmur and frowned.

“What is it?”

Fang Heng came over and looked at the screen.

A protest march?

It was a short video about an environmental demonstration.

Fang Heng looked puzzled.

Several of the protesters looked familiar. He'd seen a few of them the night before.

“Play it again,” he said.

Mo Jiawei nodded and replayed the video.

The clip showed a local environmental charity protesting against Wanling Technology Corporation. The protesters accused the company of destroying forestland, unilaterally occupying an excavated archaeological site, and severely damaging its ruins.

“Wanling Technology Corporation...” Fang Heng murmured, rubbing his chin.

Gu Qingzhu frowned. “There are ruins near this town too?”

“Oh, ruins connected to the Tree Spirits...”

Sandy appeared behind them at some point. After watching the whole video, he stretched and said, “Eighty percent of the ruins people claim to discover online are fakes or fabrications. I'm not sure whether there are any near this town, though.”

“Interesting. Come on, let's ask someone about it.”

As they opened the door, Mo Jiawei spotted Glenan passing outside.

He waved. “Hey, young man. We need to ask you something.”

“Me?” Glenan's eyes lit up, and he hurried toward them. “Coming!”

“Here, watch this video.”

After watching the clip, Glenan fell silent for a moment.

“The ruins were discovered by outsiders about half a year ago. They're in a forest not far beyond the town. Soon afterward, Wanling Technology Corporation took complete control of the site.”

“Control?” Fang Heng wondered. “Didn't the Federation intervene?”

“Don't get me started! The Federation owns ninety percent of Wanling Technology's shares. Everyone knows the Federation controls the company from behind the scenes!”

Glenan continued indignantly, “Wanling Technology has no actual right to enter the ruins. But they used certain special methods to purchase development rights for the entire forest and obtained authorization from Federation officials. That's how they took possession of the whole area.”

“The forest originally belonged to the town. After the mayor signed the sale agreement, he fell into a coma and has never woken up. There's definitely something wrong with that!”

Glenan closed the door behind him and lowered his voice. “You think something suspicious is going on at those ruins too, don't you? I've suspected for ages that they might be connected to the Tree Spirits. Wanling Technology is also very interested in the Tree Spirits. They must have found something!”

Fang Heng thought for a moment. “What does Mr. Yule say?”

“Mr. Yule has been trying to protect the ruins, but Wanling Technology is incredibly heavy-handed. There was a clash during our last demonstration, and several members of the Tree Whisperer Alliance were taken to the police station. Mr. Yule is still trying to get them released...”

Glenan looked at Fang Heng. “Mr. Yule has forbidden us from causing more trouble, but I'm still worried. Could you take me to see the ruins? I want to find out what's happening there. And if possible, I'd like to warn Wanling Technology to leave the place alone!”

[Game Hint: Your team has triggered the quest—Glenan's Request.]

Quest Name: Glenan's Request.

Difficulty: SS.

Quest Description: Wanling Technology Corporation controls the Tree Spirit ruins outside town. Glenan wishes to invite you to investigate. If possible, warn Wanling Technology to stay away from the ruins.

Quest Rewards: Tree Whisperer Alliance favorability and possible follow-up quests.

Quest Note: This mission is extremely dangerous. Reaching 20% completion is sufficient to complete it.

Special Quest Note: This quest may alter your favorability with certain factions. Choose carefully. (Hint: This is an optimized branch route from your previously selected quest reward.)

Finally! After an entire morning of fumbling around, the crucial quest had finally appeared!

Fang Heng exchanged glances with the others and nodded. “All right. Once it's dark, we'll go take a look.”

· · ·

A little over an hour later, darkness had fallen completely.

Glenan brought Fang Heng and the others to the edge of the forest outside town.

“The ruins are in there. They're not exactly a secret. Plenty of people in town know about them. I even used to play there when I was little.”

“Some time ago, a scientific expedition came to survey the area. That's when outsiders learned about the ruins. The Federation considers them relics from ancient times.”

As Glenan explained, he looked up at several concealed cameras hanging from branches overhead. “After Wanling Technology bought the land, they sealed off the entire forest and installed surveillance cameras at the entrance. Anyone entering without permission gets driven out.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng glanced at the cameras in the branches, crouched down, and picked up a pebble.

Whoosh!

The stone shot from his fingers, slicing through the air.

Bang!

A surveillance camera high above them shattered instantly!

Glenan startled and turned toward Fang Heng.

“Then we'll just take care of all of them.”

Fang Heng picked up a few more pebbles and smashed every camera near the entrance in turn.

“There. Problem solved. Let's go.”

— End of Chapter 2114 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Ownership. Glenan alleges that the Federation owns 90% of Wanling Technology and that the former mayor fell into a coma after signing the forest sale. These remain character claims.
  • Quest threshold. The SS-rank quest Glenan’s Request can be submitted at only 20% completion, but Fang Heng continues.
  • Source place. 万灵科技 is consistently rendered Wanling Technology Corporation.

Chinese Expressions

  • 没头苍蝇 (méi tóu cāngying). A “headless fly” is someone wandering aimlessly; here Fang Heng wants a solid lead rather than random searching.
