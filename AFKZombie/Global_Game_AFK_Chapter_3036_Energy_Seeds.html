Chapter 3036

Energy Seeds

From Chinese Chapter 3037 · 能量种子

Ten minutes later, the Flying Squirrel Clan's Mouse King landed at a Waterstar Clan jump port.

Kappa looked at Fang Heng. “Boss Fang, humanity is currently wanted by the Shadow Clan, and the Waterstar Clan has ties to the elves. Showing yourself rashly could cause trouble.”

“Mm. You go meet them. I'll follow.”

As Fang Heng spoke, his aura rapidly disappeared and his form gradually faded into space until he vanished completely inside the ship.

Kappa looked astonished.

What an amazing ability! He'd disappeared completely.

Fang Heng maintained his soul form, floating behind Kappa.

After advancing Necromancy to the Super-Divine level, Fang Heng could freely enter and leave his soul form. He could even attach himself to objects for short periods while in that form, hiding his aura completely.

Fang Heng was very confident.

Anyone below Divine level would be unable to detect him, even with an ability to perceive spirits.

Kappa couldn't see Fang Heng, but he could hear his voice beside his ear.

“Let's go. Represent the Flying Squirrel Clan and speak with Waterstar. I'll follow you and give you instructions along the way. Just do what I say.”

“A-all right.”

Kappa shivered involuntarily.

Boss Fang had so many tricks. He could even turn invisible and follow right beside him without leaving the slightest trace.

...

Kappa disembarked with several ship guards.

Nick, the captain of the Waterstar Clan's spaceport security team, was already waiting at the exit.

“Welcome to the Waterstar Clan. I hear you've found the Energy Seeds we're looking for?”

“Greetings. We're from the Flying Squirrel Clan. I'm Captain Kappa.”

Kappa bowed slightly.

The Flying Squirrel Clan ranked relatively low in strength among the Kinsfolk, but its members were widely distributed. They were a branch of the rodent races, possessed special talents for prospecting and mining, and held grudges. Most races preferred not to make enemies of them.

“We came across some substances resembling Energy Seeds. As we happened to be passing through, we brought them for you to examine. If they're what you're looking for, so much the better.”

Kappa handed the Waterstar officer a small cloth pouch that Fang Heng had given him in advance.

The pouch contained roughly four or five hundred Energy Seeds.

That seemed like a lot, but condensing them had taken Abe Akaya only five minutes of energy expenditure.

Nick examined the seeds. Seeing the pouch filled to the brim, excitement appeared in her eyes. “Yes, these are high-grade Energy Seeds. We're willing to buy them for twice our advertised price. By the way, would you mind telling us where you got them?”

“We once traded with a small race while doing business abroad. They couldn't pay us, so they settled their debt with these seeds. Apparently, they'd acquired them by chance a long time ago. I'm afraid tracing their origins would be difficult...”

Kappa offered a few vague explanations and changed the subject. “I also heard you're recruiting scholars knowledgeable in Natural Studies and ancient scripts. Do you need something written in an old language translated?”

Nick passed the pouch to a subordinate. “That's correct. We found some ancient writing and want it translated. Does the Flying Squirrel Clan know anything about ancient scripts?”

“We've studied some.”

Nick looked surprised, then more serious. “May I ask which scholar?”

Kappa pointed at his own nose.

“Me.”

“The script we need translated is rather unusual. You may never have encountered it.” Nick waved to her people and received a sheet covered in ancient characters, then handed it to Kappa. “This is part of the text. Do you recognize it?”

Kappa took the sheet, and his face immediately crumpled into a miserable expression.

What the hell was this?!

It looked like ghost-drawn talismans. He couldn't understand a thing.

The Waterstar representatives noticed his expression and smiled. “There's no hurry. You can take it back and work on the translation at your own pace.”

“Ahem.”

Kappa coughed lightly. Naturally, he knew this was a test the Waterstar Clan gave outsiders. He forced himself to remain composed. “This language is indeed rare. I'll return to my ship and consult some references.”

“Of course. If you need anything, please contact me. We've already verified the Energy Seeds you supplied, and the Kinsfolk contribution points have been credited to your account. Please check them. Your ship can also receive supplies here, and you're welcome to explore the planet's designated trade district.”

Nick smiled. “I won't keep you any longer. Contact me whenever you need something.”

“Thank you.”

Afraid they'd see through him, Kappa nodded politely and hurried back to the ship.

...

“Don't worry. They can't detect my presence in this state.”

As Kappa returned to the main control room, Fang Heng's figure slowly appeared behind him.

“Boss Fang, you're incredible. My nerves nearly failed me back there.”

Kappa gave Fang Heng a thumbs-up.

As they spoke, a Flying Squirrel operator approached and whispered, “Captain, we've checked. We just received 5.1 million Kinsfolk contribution points.”

That many?

Kappa clicked his tongue inwardly and stole a glance at Fang Heng.

Good grief.

Boss Fang really was something else. Five million points from a casual transaction.

Even Fang Heng opened his mouth slightly in surprise.

The Waterstar Clan was that generous?

Should he simply have Abe Akaya produce more seeds and farm additional contribution points?

The thought vanished almost as quickly as it appeared.

Five million was already a great deal. If he farmed much more, the elves might not be able to afford that many points, and it could cause even greater trouble.

The Flying Squirrel ship's personnel promptly sent the ancient script to the Human Federation, asking their scholars to attempt a translation.

The Human Federation soon replied that they had never encountered similar writing. They possessed little information about it, and even another year or two probably wouldn't be enough for a proper translation.

“Boss Fang, the Waterstar Clan is wary of us. If we can't translate this passage, it'll be difficult to use them to find the elves.” Kappa shrank his neck and asked cautiously, “Are you preparing to use force?”

“No rush. The hook's already been set. They'll bite soon enough.”

“Huh?”

Kappa looked utterly confused.

A hook?

What hook?

“The Energy Seeds you just gave them.”

While examining the ancient script projected on the screen, Fang Heng stroked his chin and explained, “I remember Klevi once mentioning the Tree Spirit Clan's Tree of Life. My guess is that the seeds are being supplied to the Tree of Life, so I left Abe Akaya's marks inside them in advance. That lets me track their location. When the time comes, we can follow the vines back to their roots and find where the Elven Clan lives.”

“Oh! So Boss Fang had everything planned from the beginning.”

Kappa swallowed nervously.

He had a premonition.

The Elven Clan was in serious trouble.
