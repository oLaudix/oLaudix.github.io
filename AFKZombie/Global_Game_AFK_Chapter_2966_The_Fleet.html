Chapter 2966

The Fleet

From Chinese Chapter 2967 · 舰队

The starship's main control room.

“Boss Fang, we've been waiting for you for ages.”

Zaka and Chen Yu had both been in excellent spirits lately. When Fang Heng returned, they reported on the starship's latest progress.

“We've already divided up nearly all the resources in this world. Also, our cooperation with the Flying Squirrel clan has gone very smoothly, and they'd like to join our fleet.”

As the team's strength grew, they had encountered quite a few Kinsfolk allies while farming contribution points and materials.

Many races were willing to join Fang Heng's group and form an interstellar expedition fleet together.

The Flying Squirrel clan was one of their recent acquaintances.

After witnessing Fang Heng's insectoid-biological modified starship during a mission, they were utterly astonished. They immediately latched onto this powerful backer and refused to let go, joining Fang Heng and the Federation in carving up that world's resources.

[Game Hint: Flying Squirrel Fleet (5) has temporarily joined the player's interstellar fleet. Current number of ships in the player's fleet: 125. Fleet level increased to LV: 72.]

[Game Hint: The Nethersea Fleet's overall strength has entered the top 100 of the Kinsfolk Alliance rankings. Current rank: 98.]

Dr. Li Zhixuan nodded. “We've recently deciphered part of a stone tablet discovered in the Ancient Humans' world. One passage describes a species called 'Gods.' This species may have been the source of the crisis that threatened the Titans.”

“Judging by the description, the Gods were extraordinarily powerful. The Titans may have offended them and were gradually driven to the brink of destruction.”

Gods...

Fang Heng murmured the word and frowned.

“That's all the information we have for now.” Dr. Li Zhixuan nodded. “We'll continue our research and hopefully uncover more useful information.”

Chen Yu took over. “Boss Fang, let's put ancient history aside for now. We have something to deal with. We've just received a message from another race in the alliance.”

“Oh? What did they say?”

“A mission invitation concerning the Ouroboros Seed. One of the Kinsfolk Alliance races is undertaking a mission to destroy an SSS-grade Ouroboros Seed insect nest.”

Fang Heng's interest stirred.

An SSS-grade insect nest!

The alliance races were certainly no weaklings.

Chen Yu explained, “The mission was initiated by the Shadow Clan. According to their invitation, they recruited fleets from several races and started the mission chain to eliminate the SSS-grade nest twenty days ago.”

“Perhaps our fleet caught their attention after entering the top hundred. The Shadow Clan sent us an invitation to join them in destroying the Ouroboros Seed nest.”

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

As his Kinsfolk honor level had risen, his permissions within the Kinsfolk communications network had gradually unlocked.

He'd looked through some publicly available information on Ouroboros Seeds in the Kinsfolk mainframe.

Any Ouroboros Seed Calamity exceeding a certain strength threshold was classified as SSS-grade by the Kinsfolk system.

The one in the Death Realm, for example, fell within the Kinsfolk's SSS-grade category.

“An SSS-grade insect nest is extremely powerful.”

“Mm. We've also learned a little about the mission from other sources. To complete it, the Shadow Clan invited fleets from many races to cooperate in advance. They even redeemed an advanced Eye of Stars from the Kinsfolk. The Iron-Eater Clan specializes in assembling it.”

“They probably still need more ships on the front line, because the Shadow Clan keeps recruiting participants.”

Is that so...

Fang Heng was clearly interested.

Beside him, however, the Flying Squirrel captain repeatedly shook his head. “Mr. Fang Heng, the Shadow Clan ranks among the top three Kinsfolk allies in strength, and they possess a powerful modified fleet. But their people have difficult, eccentric temperaments. They're hard to cooperate with.”

“So you advise against it?”

“Strongly. We'd probably suffer losses.” The Flying Squirrel captain thought for a moment, his expression turning troubled. “But if we refuse them to their faces, they might take offense, think we've shown them disrespect, and turn hostile.”

“Hmph!” Alec snorted disdainfully. “Tch. They get angry just because someone doesn't give them face? Don't they think they're a little too important?”

Fang Heng motioned for Alec to calm down.

He'd only read about the Eye of Stars in the Kinsfolk descriptions; he couldn't judge its true power yet.

This was the perfect chance to examine it firsthand.

Then there was the Ouroboros Seed.

His Ouroboros Seed was currently LV: 13 and had only three modification slots. He couldn't equip Stealth, Biological Cannon, and Splitting all at the same time.

An SSS-grade nest would surely contain a high-level cocoon.

That should be enough to reach LV: 15 and unlock another modification slot.

With luck, he might even find better modification options.

Besides, he could take the opportunity to observe the Kinsfolk Alliance's high-end combat strength and assess it for himself.

Fang Heng nodded. “Send the Shadow Clan a reply. Tell them we're very interested in the mission and will arrive as soon as possible to help them complete it.”

“Huh?”

The Flying Squirrel clan members were plainly worried.

“There's no rush. We're only going to take a look.”

“Well...” The Flying Squirrel clan had suffered badly at the Shadow Clan's hands before. After thinking it over, the captain nodded. “We're willing to accompany you, but if anything unpleasant happens, the Flying Squirrel clan will withdraw.”

“Of course.”

...

The V-0129 interstellar world.

A Western Federation starship base established within the game.

Captain Giselle and senior officials from various Federation departments were gathered in a conference room.

Something was strange.

Recently, the human faction's overall contribution level within the Kinsfolk had been rising at an extraordinary pace.

In just two months, the entire alliance faction had advanced by two major tiers!

The speed of that improvement struck Captain Giselle as highly suspicious. A few days earlier, Giselle had tried contacting the Kinsfolk leadership to ask what was going on.

The Kinsfolk leadership had always been cold toward humanity.

Only today had a response arrived.

A Kinsfolk executive explained that the system evaluated and ranked a faction based on the combined population of the entire race on their planet.

What did that mean?

Apart from the Western Federation, were other regional Federations on their planet also cooperating with the Kinsfolk?

Captain Giselle's heart skipped a beat, recalling the giant starship they'd seen during the earlier evacuation.

Could it be...?

Fang Heng?

“No, no. Impossible.”

Giselle shook his head, driving the impossible answer from his mind.

Back then, the starship had been nothing but an empty shell. Two months should barely have been enough to finish building it. Could it already be running missions?

Not to mention the astronomical contribution required to advance two major tiers in succession.

It couldn't be Fang Heng.

So had something gone wrong with the system?

The good news was that the faction's rise in rank also raised the grade of war machinery they could redeem. Many high-level missions that had previously been unavailable were now open to them.

Unfortunately, following Fang Heng's disappearance and the fallout from the Emery incident, the Federation's mission completion rate had once again slowed considerably.
