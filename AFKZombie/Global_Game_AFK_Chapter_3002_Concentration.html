Chapter 3002

Concentration

From Chinese Chapter 3003 · 浓度

What was that?

Puppet creatures?

Fang Heng focused on the figures approaching him.

They looked like some kind of earth-and-stone golems, twice as large as an ordinary person. Their bodies were bulky, but their speed didn't seem impaired at all.

Whoosh!

An earth golem threw a crushing punch at Fang Heng!

"Hmph!"

With a twist of his wrist, Fang Heng summoned his Sacred Greatsword and swept it back in a counterattack!

"Boom!"

The golem's enormous fist struck the Sacred Greatsword. It paused for an instant, and then the entire golem shattered with a deafening crash!

What?!

The Southern Federation players' expressions changed abruptly. Horror entered their eyes as they stared at Fang Heng.

He had destroyed an earth golem with one blow?

They knew firsthand how strong those creatures were. Their entire team had struggled desperately just to defeat one of them.

Yet this player who had appeared out of nowhere had smashed one apart with a single punch?

Whoosh!

Before the Western Federation players could react, Fang Heng lightly pushed off the ground and quickly dealt with the remaining golems.

"Bang! Bang, bang!"

A dark shadow flickered across the ice-crystal square, and earth golems exploded wherever it passed.

Moments later, Fang Heng reappeared and swept the Southern Federation group with an icy gaze.

Their faces immediately turned deathly pale.

What a terrifying bastard!

Who the hell was he?

Fang Heng had no time for them. Seeing that they no longer dared block his path, he ignored them, walked through the golem wreckage, pushed open the metal door, and stepped inside.

Beyond the door lay an enormous temple hall.

Dark-golden light bathed every corner of the temple.

At the hall's center, an energy mass resembling a glowing sphere hovered in midair, continually releasing waves of power.

Those high-frequency, high-quality energy fluctuations carried an intense heat that seemed to scorch the soul, making it difficult to approach.

The Southern Federation players who had entered first were seated cross-legged around the sphere's outer perimeter, eyes closed, apparently absorbing its energy.

Hah!

So the Southern Federation really had planned to monopolize the Apocalypse Treasure's power.

No wonder they had stationed guards outside to keep others from looking inside.

The players who followed Fang Heng into the hall quickly understood too. Forgetting their quarrel with the Western Federation, they sat down cross-legged and began cultivating, hoping to absorb the energy escaping from the sphere.

Fang Heng stared at the glowing energy mass.

He sensed a gentle power radiating from it.

Titan bloodline power!

Fang Heng stepped closer. An increasingly strong compressive force pressed against him from the air ahead.

The closer he came, the stronger the pressure and repulsion grew.

The sphere was powerful.

But compared with the Divine Realm's Divine Jewel or the Death Realm's Jewel of Death, it was far too weak.

Archbishop Whiteman saw him approaching the jewel and quietly cautioned, "Grand Sage."

"Mm."

Fang Heng acknowledged him, stopped, and studied the sphere with a dawning realization.

He remembered the sacrificial stone pillars discovered not long ago in the Ancient Human ruins.

Tiny fragments of energy had been embedded in those pillars.

Now that he thought about it, they resembled the remains of a complete jewel that had been shattered.

Whatever.

Absorb it first!

Fang Heng immediately sat cross-legged, closed his eyes, and extended his senses to draw in the energy.

In an instant, energy surged toward him from the blazing sphere as though being swallowed by a whale!

It flooded into his body and then swiftly streamed into his Sea of Consciousness.

At the same time, the special mark on the back of his hand began glowing faintly.

The energy was awakening the ancient Titan bloodline sleeping within him!

[Hint: Current player bloodline concentration: 10.76%...]

A complete jewel released thousands of times more energy than the fragments he'd found while exploring the Ancient Human ruins!

Fine, then he'd absorb it!

Every last bit, all at once!

Push that bloodline concentration higher!

Fang Heng fully opened his senses and began absorbing energy at maximum speed!

A visible vortex of energy gradually formed above his head!

He drew in the vast majority of everything the sphere released.

The players seated in a circle farther out sensed something wrong and opened their eyes to look at him.

The Eastern Federation had always cooperated with Fang Heng. When team members looked toward Yi Qingzhi, their leader frowned and snorted. "What? Is there too much energy for you? Why aren't you continuing your cultivation?"

The Eastern Federation players immediately fell silent and resumed cultivating.

Fang Heng might have absorbed most of the energy, but what remained was still enough for them.

Augustine and the Western Federation squad exchanged bitter looks.

Knowing how terrifying Fang Heng was, none dared complain. They shut their eyes and pretended they hadn't noticed.

While there was still some soup left, they had better hurry and drink it.

With relations between their team and Boss Fang already this bad, they might otherwise walk away empty-handed.

The Central Federation team was more conflicted.

Archbishop Whiteman and his people had formed a protective barrier around Fang Heng, plainly keeping strangers away.

The Central Federation cooperated with the Holy Court, so starting a fight was awkward.

But was it really appropriate for a single Grand Sage to absorb most of the jewel's power?

Didn't that seem rather excessive?

The Southern and Northern Federation players opened their eyes, stood, and gathered around Fang Heng.

There was only so much energy.

If one man ate it all, what would be left for anyone else?

Whiteman and his Holy Court followers immediately stepped in front of the cultivating Fang Heng.

One Southern Federation player snapped, "Move!"

Whiteman regarded them coldly. "You're the ones who should move. Disturb the Grand Sage's cultivation, and you make enemies of the entire Holy Court."

Their expressions changed.

True, the Holy Court's performance against the undead had recently been abysmal, and its reputation among players had fallen sharply.

But none of the Federations dared genuinely underestimate Fang Heng.

The Holy Court was weak?

Only compared with the undead faction.

The Holy Court had colluded with the Central Federation for years and built deep roots throughout the human world. Offend it, and its countless believers alone would give them more trouble than they could handle.

Archbishop Whiteman stared at the newcomers and said sternly, "I'll say this one last time. Step back."
