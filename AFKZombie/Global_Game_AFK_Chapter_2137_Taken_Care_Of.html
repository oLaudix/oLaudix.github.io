Chapter 2137

Taken Care Of

From original Chinese Chapter 2138 · 搞定

“Wait, my ass! What's your name? Your employee number? Don't think I won't file a complaint! You've got ten seconds to open this damned thing! I've said all along that this system is completely useless. All it does is make our work harder! Why can't you people understand that?!”

“I'm sorry. I work in security, and I don't know how to open it...”

“Listen carefully! See the door lock on the right? Cut off the external power supply, then enter the password. It's 1092419!”

“Y-yes, all right...”

Mo Jiawei smiled apologetically, then gave Fang Heng a thumbs-up.

Heh! Not bad luck!

Ask, and you shall receive!

Mo Jiawei hurried over, found the internal control, and deactivated the emergency lockdown.

Whoosh!

The alloy door blocking their path slowly rose.

Behind it stood an elderly man with graying hair and a furious expression. He sprayed Mo Jiawei with complaints. “I'm going to find your supervisor and make him shut down this damned security system for good! It's just ruined my experiment! My precious experiment! Half a month's work, completely wasted! Does he have any idea?!”

“I agree. Our head of security is a complete idiot.” Fang Heng glanced at the old man. “By the way, do you know where the Water of Life laboratory is on the twenty-sixth floor?”

The old man gave Fang Heng a surprised look, momentarily lost for words. Then he pursed his lips. “Twenty-sixth floor. Second-to-last room, all the way inside.”

Let's go!

Ignoring the old man, the pair continued up to the twenty-sixth floor.

Compared with the chaos below, the upper floors of the institute were remarkably peaceful.

Most researchers still had no idea what was happening outside and were calmly continuing their experiments.

The majority didn't even know the entrance passages had been sealed earlier.

And even those who did know wouldn't have paid much attention.

There was a similar incident every one to three months.

Every time, it turned out to be an emergency drill.

Everyone had become accustomed to them and assumed this was just another routine exercise.

Eventually, Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei found the Water of Life research laboratory on the twenty-sixth floor.

The researchers inside were conducting experiments. When Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei opened the door and walked in, they didn't immediately realize anything was wrong. They just found it strange.

Security personnel normally never entered the laboratory without good reason.

The deputy laboratory director approached and asked, “Gentlemen, this is a high-security laboratory. Who are you looking for?”

Fang Heng shut the laboratory door behind him and took out the metal staff he carried. “Actually, I accepted a job on the black market. I'm here to find the Water of Life. Would you mind helping me locate it?”

The researchers stared at Fang Heng and the iron staff in his hand, utterly bewildered.

“Leave immediately!” The deputy director frowned and marched toward him. “If you don't go, I'll call security!”

Whoosh!

Before anyone could react, Fang Heng flashed forward and appeared in front of the deputy director, gripping his collar and lifting him off his feet.

The next instant, Fang Heng hurled the man high into the air with one hand, sending him crashing onto a laboratory bench.

Crash!!!

The entire workbench shattered into pieces!

The deputy director lost consciousness instantly.

“Sorry. I am security.” Fang Heng looked around at the others. “Now, I'll say it again. I'm here for the Water of Life. Who can help me?”

The researchers stared blankly at Fang Heng.

“D-don't do anything rash.”

One laboratory worker met Fang Heng's fierce gaze and shuddered. “Water of Life! Right, the Water of Life! I know it! We have it! I'll take you there! Take as much as you want! Just don't hurt anyone...”

The man raised his hands to signal that Fang Heng should stay calm and led him toward the back of the laboratory.

“Everyone else stays where they are. I don't want to have to hurt anyone.”

Fang Heng spoke coldly, then followed the researcher down a passage at the back with Mo Jiawei.

They walked along the corridor and reached a second storeroom.

The researcher opened the door with trembling hands.

“Huh?”

Fang Heng froze at what he saw inside.

The researcher nervously glanced at the pair and whispered, “Th-this is all Water of Life. Take as much as you want. We're just researchers. We don't know anything else...”

“Huh?”

Bottled water?

The entire room was practically stuffed full of large water dispenser bottles.

It looked less like a laboratory and more like a drinking-water company's warehouse.

Mo Jiawei stared at the room, completely baffled.

The legendary, precious Water of Life?

This stuff?

There was so much of it?

“Y-you can take all of it...” The researcher swallowed carefully. “Er, if possible, could you leave a little? We still need some for experiments...”

Fang Heng frowned and walked to one of the containers. He lifted its lid and dipped a finger inside.

A game notification immediately appeared in his vision.

[Game Hint: You have discovered—Water of Life.]

Item: Water of Life.

Description: Water containing a special power. It is said to cure all diseases and prolong life.

Description (Activated by Advanced Nature Studies): The Water of Life contains a form of high-tier natural energy. You may absorb it to gain Nature Studies academic skill experience points.

Indeed, the game description was clear. It was unquestionably the Water of Life.

Yet Fang Heng couldn't shake the feeling that something wasn't right.

The quest had been far too easy so far, nowhere near what an SSS difficulty rating should imply.

And the item's properties didn't even list an item grade.

Nothing about this matched the word 'precious.'

Was this really the vital material needed to open the passage to the Inner World?

Fang Heng had his doubts.

Mo Jiawei also sensed something was off. He turned to Fang Heng and asked, “Fang Heng, what do we do?”

Fang Heng turned to the researcher. “Is the Water of Life really valuable?”

“Er... Have you been misled by the rumors outside?” The researcher shrank back nervously. “It's true the water contains a special power that can slow aging and has beneficial effects on many illnesses.”

“But claims that it cures every disease or greatly extends life are exaggerated. The effects aren't nearly as miraculous as people say...”

“All the Water of Life here came from Tree Spirit ruins collected by the company. I've heard they brought everything here. We conducted a great many experiments early on but never managed to reproduce it. We believe the Water of Life contains a special form of energy that our equipment cannot detect... which is what produces these unusual effects...”

— End of Chapter 2137 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Quest item. The in-game description confirms that the barrels contain Water of Life, despite the unexpectedly mundane storage and lack of item grade.
  • Nature Studies. Advanced Nature Studies identifies high-tier natural energy that can be absorbed for skill experience.

Chinese Expressions
