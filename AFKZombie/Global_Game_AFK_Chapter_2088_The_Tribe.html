Chapter 2088

The Tribe

From original Chinese Chapter 2089 · 部族

In addition, as his bloodline advanced, Fang Heng could clearly sense an improvement in his control over the Demon Seed's power within his sea of consciousness.

Unfortunately, the increased energy he'd absorbed also accelerated the awakening of the seed's original power.

He had a feeling it would erupt soon.

The small degree of control provided by his Intermediate Demon Bloodline was nowhere near enough to suppress the rampaging seed.

It looked like he'd still need Glutton's Touch to restrain the seed's awakening source.

With that in mind, Fang Heng raised his head.

"Let's go. We'll continue."

Meng Si drew his neck in and didn't dare argue. He led Fang Heng out of the cave toward another altar.

Huh?

This time was different.

Only a few minutes after leaving the cavern, Fang Heng sensed something and frowned. He stopped and stared down a side passage on his right.

An extremely strong blood-and-vitality fluctuation was lurking in the distance.

A big one, by the feel of it.

And no weakling.

Seeing him stop, Meng Si and the others also halted and looked back.

"Is that the creature you were planning to lure out to fight the Holy Court?" Fang Heng asked.

That creature...?

Meng Si's group immediately understood what he meant, and their faces changed.

"No."

"Oh?" Fang Heng looked surprised.

Meng Si shook his head and explained.

"That's the territory of an abyssal tribe's garrison. They aren't part of our Demon Seed faction. They came out from the depths of the Abyss of Hell a few years ago, though they'd occupied an altar belonging to Envy before then. During the purge more than ten years ago, they surrendered, and they've guarded that altar ever since. They're extremely territorial. Anyone who enters will be attacked."

An abyssal tribe?

Fang Heng frowned, still puzzled.

"The Demon Seeds are the origin of demons, but they aren't the only powers in Hell," Meng Si continued. "The great demonic tribal legions and their offshoot tribes still make up most of Hell's forces. The tribes are constantly fighting one another. Their wars have gone on for years without interruption. Perhaps hellish creatures are simply born chaotic."

"The Chaos Power accumulated by an altar can also strengthen the tribes to some degree. They need to protect the altars, and the Demon Seeds need guardians. So each side gets something out of the arrangement. The Demon Seeds leave the tribes a small portion of the altar's Chaos Power in return for guarding it."

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes. "Are they strong?"

"Yes. They're the Hell Horned Demon tribe. They were once under the command of the Seed of Wrath, but ever since Wrath disappeared, they've acted independently."

Meng Si swallowed and looked at Fang Heng cautiously.

"The power you sensed probably belongs to their three-headed horned demon leader. I'd strongly advise you not to provoke them."

"Heh."

Fang Heng gave a contemptuous chuckle. "Come. Take me there. I want a look."

He'd already drained several altars and discovered that each held very little experience. Together, they'd barely been enough to take his Demon Bloodline from beginner to intermediate.

No wonder those altars had practically no guards beyond a few novice players.

If he wanted to absorb large quantities of Chaos Power quickly, he would need another method.

Dealing with the Hell Horned Demon tribe, for example.

Before long, Meng Si and the others led Fang Heng down the side passage and through a rocky tunnel to its end.

The space suddenly opened onto a vast lava-covered clearing.

The cavern's terrain was unusual: the ground rose and dipped unevenly, dotted with bubbling pools of molten rock.

The glow of the lava illuminated every corner.

In the distance, large numbers of horned demons were gathered in the volcanic area.

Hell Horned Demons were bigger and bulkier than ordinary humans, with powerful, roughly defined muscles. They resembled enormous bulls, moved on four thick limbs, and had a distinctive long horn on their heads. When attacking, they could rear their upper bodies and strike with their forelimbs.

A layer of red mist covered the entire cavern, noticeably denser than the mist in the outer regions.

Meng Si stopped at the entrance, unwilling to go any farther. He pointed toward the depths of the cave.

"This is where the Hell Horned Demons gather. There's a high-grade altar farther in. Their tribe seized it a long time ago."

Fang Heng surveyed the demon horde, glanced at Meng Si's group, and nodded.

"Very well. Stay here for now. Don't wander off."

Whoosh!

With that, Fang Heng jumped down into the lava-covered terrain alone!

Meng Si and his companions felt their hearts lurch.

What the hell?!

Had he gone mad?!

Was he planning to fight the entire Hell Horned Demon tribe by himself?

As Fang Heng descended, his appearance changed rapidly, returning to its original form.

Disguised identity switched!

The scattered horned demons occupying the lava field sensed an intruder entering their territory and raised their heads toward Fang Heng, who had dropped from the cavern above.

Whoosh!

One horned demon reared up and hurled a compressed fireball at him with its right hand!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

At the same time, rapidly spinning magic arrays appeared around Fang Heng's body!

Switching back to his real identity had reactivated his passive Zombie Clone skill!

More magic arrays materialized in quick succession around him.

Then red figures sprang out of the circles.

Lickers!

Large numbers of Lickers emerged first and pounced toward the horned demons below!

Meanwhile, the ground around them loosened, and masses of zombies clawed their way up from beneath the earth.

Realizing that another faction had invaded their territory, the Hell Horned Demons immediately retaliated!

Dense volleys of compressed fireballs bombarded the oncoming zombie horde!

The zombies staggered steadily forward, and the two forces collided like opposing torrents.

Fang Heng quickly scanned the flood of game notifications.

The Hell Horned Demons had lived in Hell's environment for generations. They were proficient in fire-element spells, but their physical attributes were also formidable.

They could knock down zombies and Lickers with short charges powered by sheer muscle.

Their melee strikes carried additional fire damage, and they could launch compressed fireballs at range.

His zombie horde could endure the punishment thanks to their enormous HP pools and the Undying Body's powerful regeneration.

But fire damage was a special form of elemental damage.

He couldn't guarantee that his zombies would never die.
