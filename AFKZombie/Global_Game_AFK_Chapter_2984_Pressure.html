Chapter 2984

Pressure

From Chinese Chapter 2985 · 逼迫

Chen Yu approached Fang Heng with a note of wonder in his voice. “Boss Fang, I never imagined I'd get to see the Death Realm one day. I am going to make it back alive, right?”

“Who knows?” Fang Heng shrugged and turned to Alec. “Is everyone ready?”

Chen Yu's expression grew serious. “The Eastern Federation combat personnel have assembled and are ready to fight at any moment. Our ship commanders will also follow your instructions in directing the fleet.”

Alec nodded. “Your Majesty, the Vampire Clan has also finished preparations. Every member is ready for battle.”

“Good.”

Fang Heng turned toward the leader of the Iron-Eater Clan, who had just disembarked. “Any problems on your end?”

The Iron-Eater leader quickly surveyed the terrain and shook his head. “None. We'll cooperate fully and complete construction as quickly as possible.”

The Iron-Eaters had not been particularly willing when Fang Heng first captured them.

But they'd grown used to their situation over time.

Working for one master or another—was there really a difference?

Besides, their treatment aboard the starship was considerably better than what they had enjoyed before.

Iron-Eaters had always been a race that liked full stomachs and an easy life, taking each day as it came.

“Good. Everyone, let's get to work.”

According to the Iron-Eaters' calculations, with sufficient supplies and a designated construction site, they could erect the Eye of Stars prism towers within seventy-two hours.

...

Two hours later.

The main battlefield.

Huge numbers of skeletal dragons circled through the sky. Under the control of Nether Clan War Knights, they swooped low again and again, charging into the Calamity's Zerg swarms.

A closer look revealed red organisms clinging to the dragons' broad backs and rib cages, almost like layers of muscle.

Lickers!

Every Licker attached to a skeletal dragon had entered its entrenched artillery mode.

Carried by the low-flying dragons, they continuously bombarded the Calamity swarms from range.

Mage groups from the four Nether Clan empires and the Undead Council's elite mages stood on the starship decks, casting Necromancy spells to support the ships' long-range energy beams.

Flocks of Vampires in bat form flew around the ships, providing cover and support.

On the main front, the fleet crushed everything in its path!

The Nether Clan had never imagined that the terrifying Calamity could be driven back so rapidly, with Fang Heng's various teams working together to carve a bloody path through it.

It almost gave them an illusion.

Perhaps the Calamity wasn't as strong as they'd thought?

Then how had it pushed the entire Death Realm to the brink of destruction?

The fleet continued advancing until it reached the predetermined Eye of Stars construction site.

After the ships halted, Zaka immediately ordered the players to unload antigravity blocks one after another and directed the Iron-Eaters to build the Eye of Stars targeting prisms at full speed.

From the bridge of the flagship, Fang Heng gazed through the glass toward the distant central River of Netherworld.

Mu Qingxin must have sensed him by now.

Was she still not going to attack?

If she kept waiting until the Eye of Stars was complete, Fang Heng would be the one to make the first move.

...

Deep within the River of Netherworld.

Mu Qingxin had indeed sensed Fang Heng approaching. She awoke from dormancy and looked down at the river, which was already nearly dry.

The Death Realm's power had been dissipating rapidly of late.

Proust's growth had slowed more and more, far below expectations. He had only recovered a little over half his strength and remained far from advancing.

Mu Qingxin raised her head and sensed the surroundings again.

So Fang Heng and his allies were establishing a defensive line around the perimeter?

The Eye of Stars was a weapon the Kinsfolk had designed against the Zerg over the past millennium. Mu Qingxin had been trapped in the Death Realm so long that she had no idea how far the outside world's technology had progressed.

Nevertheless, she sensed that a great battle was imminent.

Meanwhile, one Eye of Stars prism after another was being completed by the Iron-Eaters, and Fang Heng grew increasingly calm.

It seemed Proust had yet to awaken from dormancy.

Mu Qingxin hadn't chosen to attack either.

Excellent!

He could construct the Eye of Stars openly and without restraint.

As each prism device was completed, detachments of local Nether Clan members, Vampires, Eastern Federation soldiers, Undead Council mages, and zombie clones took turns guarding it to ensure nothing went wrong.

...

Two days later.

All twenty-four Eye of Stars prism targeting and defensive devices were positioned and successfully entered their linked targeting-lock state.

As the prisms connected into the third-stage network, a tremendous energy fluctuation erupted from the towers.

Even the Nether Clan could sense the enormous power within the prisms.

The warriors gathered from the four empires all had the vague feeling that the decisive battle was about to begin.

The instant the lock was completed, the Zerg swarm surrounded in the center instinctively sensed danger.

Mu Qingxin stared toward Fang Heng's distant position.

“Proust! Wake up!”

The entire Calamity swarm went berserk and surged furiously toward the defensive perimeter.

“Only reacting now? Heh. Too late.”

Fang Heng smiled coldly.

Fight!

A vast area, including the River of Netherworld, had already been completely encircled.

At the forefront, Nether Clan War Knights raised their death blades high against the frenzied insects charging straight toward them.

“Form the line! Prepare to intercept!”

Countless Zerg poured across the front, exploding into masses of flesh beneath the fleet's dense energy-beam bombardment.

Behind them, Undead Council teams and Nether Clan spellcasters deployed domain spells, terrain effects, and wide-area debuffs that greatly weakened the swarm's attributes.

High above, skeletal dragons carrying scores of Lickers in biological-cannon mode swept over the battlefield again and again, inflicting tremendous damage.

Fang Heng stood before one of the prism defense-network towers and watched the front. A game notification appeared on his retina.

[Game Hint: Using the Eye of Stars prism device to establish targeting coordinates.]

[Game Hint: Current targeting progress: 13%. Energy accumulation: 100%. Firing unavailable.]

[Game Hint: An Ouroboros Seed—Newborn has been detected. Related quest triggered: Kill.]

Quest Name: Kill.

Quest Description: You understand the fate of an Ouroboros Seed newborn. Kill all competing newborns as soon as possible and absorb their nutrients.

Quest Requirement: Kill the opposing Ouroboros Seed.

Here it came!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

The Ouroboros Seeds he'd encountered during previous Kinsfolk missions had all suffered grievous injuries. At most, they possessed ten percent of their peak strength.

But this time he was facing an Ouroboros Seed at full strength—Proust!
