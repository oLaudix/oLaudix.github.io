Chapter 2977

Time to Run

From Chinese Chapter 2978 · 跑路

Fang Heng stared fixedly at the lifeform gradually emerging from the center of the fleshy tumor.

As the decaying outer shell peeled away layer by layer, it exposed the half-humanoid being inside.

The right half of her body had become completely insectoid. Dense blood-vessel-like tendrils protruded from the gaps between her muscle fibers, twitching in time with the nest pulsing below, while her left half retained the outline of a human woman.

Her aura was incredibly powerful!

Judging by his perception, she was a 'Cultivator'!

Aboard the flagship, everyone's expressions turned grave.

“Boom!!!”

The next moment, the nest beneath the Cultivator's body abruptly exploded of its own accord!

Dense, enormous swarms of insects poured out of the shattered nest!

They even included large numbers of Ouroboros Seed aggregates!

Exactly as Fang Heng had predicted!

During the lull after an Eye of Stars bombardment, the 'Cultivator' gestating inside the nest had awakened. The nest burst apart and launched its own assault, and the insects immediately counterattacked in every direction!

Seeing this, the crocodile captain shouted orders to stabilize the situation. “Don't panic! Maintain formation! Fall back! Restrict their movement and prevent them from escaping the Eye of Stars' attack range!”

Faced with such a fierce counterattack, the warships spread out in formation and began retreating.

Elite squads from the various races entered the battlefield one after another and started killing.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes, surveying the fighting before glancing toward the Shadow Clan captain and the allied captains nearby.

The Shadow Clan captain seemed to notice his look. He turned toward Fang Heng, his eyes carrying an unmistakable warning.

“There's no need for humanity to enter the battle for now. You can remain on standby.”

“Of course. With the alliance's strength, taking down the nest shouldn't be a problem.”

Fang Heng knew the Shadow Clan was confident of winning and didn't want him entering the battle again to claim a share of the spoils. He shrugged and pointed toward the Ouroboros Seed aggregate hovering above the destroyed nest, reminding everyone of something.

“As far as I know, the Ouroboros Seed has so-called 'Cultivators' in addition to its insect body. That one may be a Cultivator. You should take extra care.”

Before the Shadow Clan captain could respond, the crocodile captain beside him sneered disdainfully. “Hah! A 'Cultivator'? Nothing more than its final struggle before death. We don't need you humans worrying about us.”

The nest's eruption had indeed caused considerable trouble for the allied forces, but the retreating warships soon regained control and began rapidly wearing down the living insect swarm.

With the nest completely destroyed, there were no fresh insects to replenish it, so the power of the swarm's assault was gradually diminishing.

The crocodile looked provocatively at Fang Heng. “Well? What do you think?”

“Not bad.”

Captain Giselle glanced worriedly toward the Ouroboros Seed mother body still suspended above the battlefield and quietly asked, “Fang Heng, is something wrong?”

“I imagine the Shadow Clan has special abilities of its own. Even if the Cultivator attacks, it's hard to say who would win.”

Whoosh!

As he spoke, the mother body hovering above the shattered nest opened her eyes and flew straight toward a nearby crocodile squad.

“Whoosh!”

The Ouroboros Seed mother body was unbelievably fast, turning into a black blur almost instantly.

“Bang!! Bang! Bang!!!!”

The crocodile squad couldn't even see how she attacked. A black shadow flashed past their eyes, and they were sent flying, their bodies covered in hideous tearing wounds.

“Stop her!”

The Shadow Clan's elite fighters personally entered the fray.

Hundreds of dark figures rushed out of the various warships and sped toward the Ouroboros Seed mother body.

Shadow Orbs!

The mother body was enveloped by shadow orbs, and countless shadows appeared beneath her feet, pulled in different directions by the orbs. Her speed dropped drastically.

Only then could most of the observers make out how the mother body was attacking.

Spikes!

The Ouroboros Seed mother body raised her wrist toward a Red-Ghost Clan warrior attempting to ambush her from the right. Dense, barbed fleshy spikes shot out from within her body, piercing the warrior and blasting him away.

The Shadow Clan warriors attacked in unison. Thin sheets of shadow spread along the ground, swiftly surrounding the mother body.

Hmm?

Fang Heng let out a soft sound of surprise.

The mother body's spiked attacks were extremely effective. The spikes shot out at incredible speed and were covered in barbs, tearing away flesh with even a glancing touch.

Yet Fang Heng clearly saw the spikes pass through the Shadow Clan warriors' bodies without causing any substantial damage.

“It's Shadow Transformation, the Shadow Clan's special ability.”

Fang Heng turned toward the Red-Ghost Clan captain beside him.

Noticing Fang Heng's confusion, the captain explained without being asked, “It's an innate Shadow Clan ability. When attacked, part of their bodies enters a shadow state, allowing them to evade damage directly.”

“Impressive.”

Fang Heng nodded lightly.

At first glance, it seemed similar to a skill that made the body intangible for a very brief period, granting immunity to physical attacks.

Whether it could also negate spells was another question.

At least for now, the Ouroboros mother body didn't seem to possess any spellcasting abilities.

Beyond their shadow transformation, the Shadow Clan warriors were also formidable in close combat. Coupled with shadow magic to control their opponent, more than a hundred elite Shadow Clan fighters quickly restricted the mother body to the central area and slowly wore down her health.

The surrounding Ouroboros Seeds tried to rush forward and help the mother body escape, but squads from the other allied races blocked them.

The Shadow Clan alliance appeared to have gained complete control of the battle.

The fighting continued for more than ten minutes. The Ouroboros Seed mother body tried several times to break through, but each attempt failed. A large portion of the swarm had been eliminated, and some warships had sustained damage, but the insects remained trapped within the Eye of Stars' attack range.

The Eye of Stars' attack cooldown had ended!

Spatial energy lights flickered once again across the cloudy sky, and the dark-purple barrel of the Eye of Stars gradually emerged from the fluctuations in space.

“Boom!!!”

A deep-purple column of energy descended from the enormous barrel, instantly engulfing the entire central region!

Vast swarms of insects were reduced to ashes without a sound beneath the beams.

The concentrated energy bombardment continued for more than ten minutes.

When the beams finally dissipated, all that remained of the Ouroboros Seed aggregates on the battlefield were lumps of flesh writhing on the ground. The mother body lay grievously wounded, twitching and writhing uncontrollably as foul-smelling blood continued pouring from her body.

“Attack!”

The tide of battle turned.

Elite squads from every alien race rushed onto the battlefield to finish off the survivors.

At last, the mission was almost over.

Captain Giselle and the other Western Federation players breathed sighs of relief.

The trip hadn't been entirely wasted.

At least they would earn a few rewards simply by having joined the team.

Besides, they'd gained a rough understanding of the Kinsfolk allies' combat capabilities.

After returning, the Federation would carry out a full assessment of the various alien races.

Time to leave.

— End of Chapter 2977 —
