Chapter 2164

Pursuit

From original Chinese Chapter 2165 · 追击

“Remain on alert. Conduct a limited reconnaissance of the surrounding area. Additional squads will arrive shortly.”

“Yes, sir!”

Inside the command center, an operator suddenly noticed something. His expression changed as he pointed toward the center of the screen.

“Look at that!”

Huh?!

Was that…?!

The commander reacted at once. “Team C! Unknown life-sign readings have appeared ahead and to your right! A large number of creatures are rapidly approaching your position!”

The members of Team C stiffened and raised their beam weapons, aiming them at a small grove in the distance.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

Faint noises came from the trees.

The sounds of something moving at high speed.

The squad stared toward the source.

For a moment, their breathing turned heavy with tension.

Lickers!

An instant later, a dense mass of red creatures erupted from the grove like a surging flood!

“Watch out!”

The squad captain's face changed dramatically.

Why were they encountering these disgusting things again?!

“Switch to injection guns!”

After several previous encounters with Fang Heng's Lickers, Federation combat squads now carried gene-disrupting agents specifically developed to counter them.

At the same time, Federation flying war machines hovering overhead unleashed a dense barrage of beams upon the approaching Lickers!

“Boom! Boom, boom!”

The Lickers were blown backward in an instant.

Blood and flesh sprayed everywhere.

Amid the chaos, a figure darted swiftly toward the right.

“It's Fang Heng!”

A soldier spotted him and shouted immediately.

“Command, we've located Fang Heng.”

Tension gripped the command center.

“Keep him occupied! Reinforcements from the Dependents are already on their way!”

“Understood!”

The squad abandoned its attack on the Lickers and pursued Fang Heng instead.

Fang Heng raced past the Federation unit on the right, speeding farther down the coastline.

The Federation's pursuit had not slackened over the past few days. If anything, it had become more intense.

According to his plan, he would act as bait himself and draw away the Federation's attention while Gu Qingzhu and her companions evacuated the Niam Alloy barrier.

But one thing hadn't gone according to plan.

The sudden appearance of that quest involving pursuit by the Dependents.

That was worrying.

Fang Heng felt an uneasy premonition.

“Whoosh… whoosh…”

Specks of light from spatial jumps began appearing rapidly all around him!

Federation aerial combat machines!

“Pew! Pew, pew!”

Disc-shaped machines hovered in midair, continuously firing blue beams from their pointed tips.

Fang Heng had to twist and dodge through the dense barrage.

Suddenly, his brow furrowed.

What was that?

Several pale-blue spatial-jump lights had appeared in the air ahead.

Blue?

Something about them felt wrong.

The blue lights converged rapidly, completing a spatial transition at extraordinary speed.

What?!

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

A prism!

A pale-blue prism roughly three meters long floated in the air.

A member of another race stood atop it.

Fang Heng's heart stirred.

It was roughly humanoid, but its skeletal frame was longer and more slender than a human's, while a strange gelatinous substance covered its face.

There was also a cape-like layer of downy material attached to its back…

A Dependent!

The name flashed through Fang Heng's mind.

Two Dependent scouts stood atop the prism, facing him. Using their psychic prism, they rapidly read his identity information and tried to establish a tracking lock.

[Analyzing and tracking target location…]

[Sample analysis disrupted by an unknown force.]

[Tracking and positioning disrupted. Additional time required to establish an approximate regional lock…]

“Hm? Something is interfering with the information scan?”

A trace of puzzlement crossed the scouts' minds.

“Human, what is your relationship with Ouroboros? Why are fragments of Ouroboros genes present within your body?”

Fang Heng's thoughts raced. Ignoring their question, he accelerated toward the right, trying to flee.

It seemed Ji Haiting hadn't lied to him.

The Dependents really had come because of Ouroboros!

The prism beneath their feet accelerated, carrying them after Fang Heng.

The two scouts appeared to hover above its surface.

When Fang Heng failed to respond, both Dependents raised their hands and formed triangular gestures before their chests.

“Crack… crack, crack!”

What?!

Hearing the strange noises, Fang Heng was astonished to see the space directly ahead of him rapidly solidifying before his very eyes!

A psychic ability!

A psychic wall!

Fang Heng frowned and tensed every muscle. Switching to his alloy metal staff, he swung it fiercely at the wall before him.

“Bang!”

The psychic barrier, not yet fully solidified, shattered beneath the blow!

Fang Heng charged straight through!

The instant he broke through, a needle-like pain stabbed deep into his brain.

Again!

A psychic attack!

More than ten times as powerful as the attacks he'd suffered from psychic-modified soldiers!

[Hint: The player has taken 15,000 points of psychic piercing damage. This damage cannot be negated.]

“Hmph!”

Fang Heng looked back coldly at the Dependents chasing him atop their prism. With a disdainful snort, he raised a hand high in their direction.

Did they really think they could knead and mold him however they pleased?!

“Whoosh!”

The white cloth wrapped around his back tore open.

His blood-red greatsword instantly burst apart into countless crimson arrows, which shot toward the two Dependent scouts!

Sensing the enormous power within those blood arrows, the scouts looked alarmed and quickly formed a different, circular seal with their hands.

“Bang! Bang, bang!”

The blood arrows struck the red crystalline shield that appeared around them, exploding one after another!

The two scouts wore strange expressions.

Their database contained no record of an attack method like this!

Worse, the attack was exceptionally powerful, and their external crystalline defensive structure was rapidly breaking down!

What now?

For a moment, crimson blood exploded continuously before their eyes, obscuring everything around them.

“BOOM!”

What?!

The crystalline shield in front of them shook violently.

“Crack, crack, crack!”

A huge fist-shaped indentation appeared in it, and fine fractures spread outward in rings from the point of impact!

“BANG!”

With an explosive crash, the entire crystal shield shattered!

A massive fist burst through the surrounding blood mist and came straight at them!

“Bang! Bang!”

Fang Heng sent the two Dependent scouts flying backward with two brutal punches!

Their heads spun. The scenery seemed to rush past them as they were hurled through the air, and by the time they recovered enough to look forward again, Fang Heng had disappeared completely from sight.

— End of Chapter 2164 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Dependents. Two scouts ride a pale-blue psychic prism and use mental attacks and crystalline defensive barriers.
  • Damage. The source specifies 15,000 unavoidable psychic piercing damage from the scouts’ mental attack.

Chinese Expressions

  • 任人揉捏. The source prints 扔人揉捏, apparently a typo for 任人揉捏; literally being kneaded and molded at another’s whim, as though one had no power to resist.
