Chapter 2154

Stealing from Within

From original Chinese Chapter 2155 · 监守自盗

“I'll say it once more: time is short. Think it over carefully.”

As Ji Haiting spoke, specks of crystallized spatial energy gathered around him and his guards.

Whoosh!

In an instant, the light flashed, and Ji Haiting and his companions vanished through spatial transition.

Fang Heng peered beyond the metal door, then turned toward Aidolard, who was crouching in a corner of the stairwell with his hands over his ears.

Aidolard was suffering.

What the hell!

What had he just witnessed?!

The company chairman chatting cheerfully with Fang Heng—and practically helping him steal the company's own property?

Heavens!

He was going to die!

Noticing Fang Heng looking at him, Aidolard hurriedly shook his head. “Huh? What? What were you talking about? I'm deaf! I didn't hear anything! I know absolutely nothing!”

“Enough nonsense. Get up and lead the way.”

As Fang Heng's expression chilled, Aidolard felt a shiver race up his spine. He scrambled off the floor and led him through the now-open passage.

Before long, they arrived at Secret Research Laboratory No. 3.

Inside, two metal pieces were displayed behind glass partitions on separate large workstations like museum exhibits.

Large instruments were still running automatically, testing and analyzing the Niam Alloy.

Fang Heng walked closer and examined the pieces through the glass.

They weren't one complete block. The alloy had already been cut into fist-sized fragments, arranged separately on the two workstations.

Fang Heng pressed a hand against the glass partition and gave it a small push.

Crash!

The glass wall shattered.

He stepped up to the experimental table and touched one of the metal fragments.

A game hint appeared.

[Hint: The player has obtained an item—Niam Alloy.]

[Hint: The player lacks the relevant skill and cannot analyze this metal alloy. Analysis of the item requires at least LV: 550 Research Laboratory.]

Whoosh.

The metal fragments disappeared into his backpack.

[Hint: The player has obtained Niam Alloy ×2.]

[Hint: Handing this item to the Tree Spirits will grant Tree Spirit favorability, large amounts of faction contribution points, and Tree Spirit contribution points. (This quest route is recommended. It will greatly reduce quest difficulty and improve completion efficiency.)]

“Done!”

He'd obtained the Niam Alloy before the Tree Spirits!

As for what came next...

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes, glanced at the notifications, and considered matters for several seconds.

The Tree Spirits outside were still battling Wanling Technology and the Federation inside the institute.

They had no idea that Fang Heng had already infiltrated the building and taken the Niam Alloy.

But they'd naturally notice something was wrong when they finally arrived here.

So...

Fang Heng looked toward Aidolard behind him.

“Hey, Aidolard.”

Aidolard stiffened immediately.

“Are these the only two pieces of Niam Alloy?”

“Yes. I heard my colleagues say Niam Alloy is extremely rare. These might look like two little fragments, but the company expended considerable effort to obtain them from the Dependents.”

“Very well.” Fang Heng pursed his lips and asked casually, “What's Ouroboros?”

Aidolard stared in surprise. He clearly hadn't expected a completely unrelated question. After collecting himself, he answered, “I'm not very familiar with it. I've only seen a little information. Apparently, it's an unconfirmed special race.”

“A race...” Fang Heng murmured. “Can you be more specific?”

“This race is supposedly born with a calamity-like nature. According to the Dependents, the appearance of Ouroboros heralds a natural disaster. After Ouroboros appears, ninety-nine percent of the world's species will be destroyed because of it. Then the Ouroboros race disappears, and living things gradually flourish again. In legend, Ouroboros is depicted as a serpent endlessly circling back on itself, symbolizing the cycle of the world.”

Fang Heng stroked his chin and frowned.

How strange.

Ouroboros referred to an unknown race?

Then why was it connected to him?

“Are there detailed records about Ouroboros? Anything in the institute?”

“Those materials are classified at Level A. Normally there are ways to obtain them, but when the institute came under attack, all its records were automatically deleted. You won't find them here.”

Aidolard shrank back. “If you need the information, you could try the black market. There's very little on Ouroboros, but it isn't all especially high-level classified material.”

“Very well. One last thing. Help me with it, then find somewhere to hide. You don't need me to explain what you should and shouldn't say.”

“What is it?”

“Make this place look a little different. Remember...”

Fang Heng leaned toward Aidolard and whispered a few words into his ear.

“Ah? You...”

Aidolard looked at Fang Heng, bewildered.

He'd already taken the Niam Alloy. Why stage a scene afterward? Did he think everyone at Wanling Technology was an idiot?

“Get moving!”

Another chill ran down Aidolard's back, and he hurried away.

Watching him leave, Fang Heng closed his eyes and sensed the Lickers and worms throughout the institute.

When he opened them again, he'd made his decision.

Leave first!

· · ·

Outside the central research institute.

Fang Heng once again slipped out of the building through Secondary Spatial Projection.

Outside, the Lickers were still fighting Federation war machines that periodically arrived through spatial transition.

They repeatedly threw themselves at the machines, enduring beam bombardment with their own bodies while clawing relentlessly at the metal armor.

Fang Heng glanced at the game hints constantly updating before him.

[Hint: The player's zombie clone (Fusion Licker form) has dealt 1,244 damage to a large war machine (Spider Armor V-88)...]

[Hint: The player's zombie clone (Fusion Licker form) has destroyed a large war machine (Spider Armor V-88), earning 73 Tree Spirit contribution points.]

[Hint: Current quest—Eliminate the War Machines. Completion increased to 52%.]

Not bad.

Fang Heng was rather relieved.

Fortunately, he'd modified the Primordial Devouring Body form of some Lickers before entering the World of Myriad Forests, increasing their damage output. Otherwise, he'd have had no effective way to deal with these armored machines.

As spatial-transition points appeared faster and faster around the institute, war machines were arriving at an increasing rate.

The Lickers could probably hold out a while longer.

Come to think of it, he might as well farm more Tree Spirit quest contribution points.

Though by now Fang Heng had given up hope of increasing his favorability with them.

He glanced toward the rear.

The Tree Spirits had firmly occupied the entrances around the research institute.

Because of their enormous size, the treants couldn't enter the building, so they remained outside to fight the Federation war machines arriving in subsequent waves.

— End of Chapter 2154 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • The staged evacuation. After obtaining the alloy, Fang Heng orders the laboratory to be made to look as if the metal was evacuated during the crisis.
  • Ouroboros myth. The researcher describes Ouroboros as an alleged calamity-associated race, not a verified scientific finding.

Chinese Expressions

  • 监守自盗. Stealing what one has been entrusted to guard; the researcher panics that Wanling Technology’s chairman is helping a thief.
