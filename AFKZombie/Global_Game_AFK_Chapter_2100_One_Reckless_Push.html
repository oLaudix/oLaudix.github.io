Chapter 2100

One Reckless Push

From original Chinese Chapter 2101 · 莽一波

“Mm.”

Ji Xiaobo's expression grew serious. He nodded and explained, “The Holy Court has probably figured out that the Mork Temple is connected to the Yin Vein fissure in Duncha. They've already begun attacking us. With Lord Dani and Senior Sister Qingran helping, we've managed to hold out for now.”

Ji Xiaobo briefly explained the situation.

The Holy Court and the undead had once again entered a full-scale confrontation.

Lord Dani had led the undead faction deep into the Brighton Empire, pressing toward the last enormous main city under Holy Court control.

Reinforcements for both the undead and the Holy Court had gradually converged outside that city.

Yet with victory seemingly within reach, they had encountered a major obstacle. They had been unable to break into the city.

The fighting in Chiboreya had entered another fiercely contested phase.

Perhaps because the Holy Court was still dealing with the Yin Vein in Duncha and could spare little strength to reinforce Chiboreya, the balance had begun to tip toward the undead.

At a disadvantage, the Holy Court had abandoned direct confrontation. On one hand, they were defending their final city like a turtle hiding in its shell. On the other, they had repeatedly sent elite teams around the front lines to attack the Mork Temple from behind. They had tried every method imaginable to eliminate the Yin Vein fissure.

Recognizing the Mork Temple's importance to the war, Lord Dani and Li Qingran had dispatched additional guards to protect it.

Both sides had been matching wits and testing one another's tactics.

“Brother Fang, it's wonderful that you're back! Lord Dani has a major advantage at the front now. I hear she's already begun laying siege to the Holy Court's last main city!”

Ji Xiaobo enthusiastically punched the air. “We've just been waiting for your return, Brother Fang. Then we can push straight through in one go! Drive the Holy Court out of Chiboreya and win this little war once and for all!”

Push straight through in one go?

Could they really do it?

Well...

Perhaps it wasn't impossible.

Listening to Ji Xiaobo, Fang Heng found himself genuinely tempted.

Unfortunately...

The Divine Realm's pursuers had already locked onto him. Otherwise, he would definitely have stayed to grind out another round and collect a fresh haul of Main God Points...

Wait.

Maybe it wasn't impossible after all.

There was still some time before the Divine Realm's pursuers arrived.

One more haul!

One last round of grinding!

Get the points, then leave!

At the very least, the Holy Court in Chiboreya still didn't know he'd returned. He could use his zombie clones, open a teleportation passage, and launch a reckless all-out assault!

If they couldn't win, he'd activate his ultimate move, the Everlasting Moon, and make his escape!

Naturally, that plan carried enormous risks.

If the Holy Court discovered him, the Divine Realm's pursuers might arrive ahead of schedule.

There were risks, but there were rewards, too.

At least the gamble seemed worthwhile!

Fang Heng calculated for a moment, then nodded. “All right. Let's do it! We'll push through in one go. Help me contact Lord Dani in a little while. I need to discuss the plan with her. And remember—keep this confidential. We absolutely cannot let word get out beforehand.”

“Understood!” Ji Xiaobo was beside himself with excitement. He thumped his chest emphatically. “Leave it to me.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng immediately began controlling the great masses of zombies still roaming Chiboreya, gathering them together and slowly spreading them toward the heart of the Brighton Empire.

At the same time, he transformed into a bat and flew rapidly toward the undead faction's front line.

Ordinary mutated infected zombie clones moved too slowly, so he would let them gradually work their way toward the battlefield.

As for what they would actually do next, he could decide after meeting Lord Dani.

· · ·

Three hours later, Fang Heng finally reached the forward camp.

Because Lord Dani had driven the Holy Court back all the way to the Brighton Empire's last stronghold, the journey had taken longer than before.

Fang Heng first visited the camp's quartermaster to exchange all his honor points for Main God Points.

The quartermaster looked him up and down with respect in his eyes. “Thank you for everything you have done for the undead faction. May death be eternal.”

[Game Hint: The player has exchanged all remaining honor points and received a total of 1.778 million Main God Points.]

Not bad.

Fang Heng checked the number in his game interface and nodded in satisfaction.

A total of 1.77 million Main God Points—considerably more than he'd expected.

Lord Dani was currently leading the undead faction against the Holy Court more than ten kilometers away, so there were few players in the camp. No one noticed Fang Heng's return.

He wasn't in a hurry. He stayed at the quartermaster's counter to browse the missions currently available.

The faction had issued an endless variety of missions against its enemies.

Some were even rated SSS.

The rewards were fairly generous.

Maybe he could pick up a couple while he was here.

Fang Heng rubbed his chin, thought for a moment, and accepted one of the high-level missions.

The quartermaster gave him a long look before nodding. “Follower of Death, you have accepted an exceptionally difficult task. Yet I have a feeling that perhaps only you can complete it. I wish you good fortune.”

Fang Heng waved a hand.

“Thank you.”

· · ·

Two days later.

The Holy Court's front line.

After several successive battles, relentless harassment by undead creatures, and Lord Dani's determined pursuit, the Holy Court's forces had withdrawn into the Brighton Empire's rearmost territory.

With the Yin Vein ravaging Duncha and the undead faction harassing the Holy Court in other disputed worlds, it could spare few reinforcements.

The Holy Court therefore relied on the natural defenses of the Chinoya Swamp to halt the undead advance.

Meanwhile, it had gradually come to understand the importance of the Mork Temple and was determined to destroy it completely.

But with only a limited number of teleportation passages remaining in Chiboreya, and the Yin Vein situation in Duncha becoming increasingly urgent, the Holy Court simply lacked the forces to act decisively. For now, it could only send reinforcements to stabilize the front and resort to delaying tactics.

— End of Chapter 2100 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Strategy. The opening title 莽一波 describes a bold or reckless all-in push. Fang Heng plans to gamble on one more attack before the Divine Realm arrives.
  • Numbers. 177.8W means 1.778 million Main God Points. The source later rounds this figure down to roughly 1.77 million.
  • Timing. The chapter moves from the battle preparations to a scene explicitly marked “Two days later.”

Chinese Expressions

  • 临门一脚 (lín mén yī jiǎo). Literally “the final kick at the goal”; success seemed within reach before the undead encountered their obstacle.
  • 莽一波 (mǎng yī bō). A reckless all-in push. Rather than remove the gaming slang, the translation retains Fang Heng’s impulsive, high-risk thinking.
