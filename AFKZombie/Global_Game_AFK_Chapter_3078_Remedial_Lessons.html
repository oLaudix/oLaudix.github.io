Chapter 3078

Remedial Lessons

From Chinese Chapter 3079 · 补课

“Whoosh!”

As expected, Chris saw only a blur.

When Fang Heng reappeared in his field of vision, a deafening sonic boom had already erupted in front of him.

“BANG!”

The shockwave blasted Chris backward, sending him tumbling off the platform.

It looked terrible, but he hadn't actually been hurt.

His opponent had held back.

Chris quickly pushed himself up and cupped his fists toward Fang Heng again to express his thanks.

Well, great.

He'd been utterly crushed and now had to thank the person who beat him.

“We won!”

The Noel Clan newcomers competing in their first arena match shouted excitedly.

The elder leading their team watched Fang Heng leave through the passage on the other side of the arena, an intrigued expression in his eyes.

What an interesting fellow.

This kid could already overwhelm low-tier warriors of his own nominal rank.

Perhaps his actual strength was already high-tier.

They'd been lucky enough to discover a real treasure.

Even after the match had ended, Liu Hu's head was still buzzing.

What had just happened?

They'd actually won?

He'd come expecting to make up the numbers. Later, encouraged by Fan Chen, he'd prepared to fight desperately and make an impression on the elder. Yet before he even stepped onto the platform, they'd already won?

He'd gained a victory without lifting a finger?

Fan Chen also looked rather surprised. He watched Fang Heng depart and shook his head slightly.

In the waiting-area corridor, Anxi greeted Fang Heng with a smile. “Mr. Fang Heng, congratulations on your victory! That was incredible. There'll be a celebration banquet afterward. I can arrange...”

“No need. I don't like that sort of thing. Take me back to the villa.”

“Yes.” Anxi nodded respectfully. “I'll explain to the elder. I'll escort you back first.”

On the way, Fang Heng recalled the arena fights.

They hadn't been very interesting.

Only that spatial technique had been slightly intriguing.

Level One matches were simply too weak.

This world wasn't nearly as terrifying as he'd imagined.

Time to go back and grind experience!

If everything went smoothly, he should complete the first stage of his mysticism quest before sunrise tomorrow.

“Anxi.”

“Yes, Mr. Fang Heng? How may I help you?”

“I need another private mysticism lesson. Try to book Mu Shunan again and see when he's available.”

“Of course.”

...

The following afternoon, Fang Heng returned to the Noel Clan building with Anxi.

As he passed through the first-floor lobby, many young warriors of the clan looked at him with respect.

News of Fang Heng winning four fights with a single punch apiece the previous night had already spread throughout the Noel Clan's warriors.

“Mr. Fang Heng, you displayed overwhelming power during yesterday's battles. You've entered the assessment period for mid-tier warriors. Although you haven't completed it yet, Elder Dilan has requested that the Noel Clan grant you mid-tier benefits early.”

“Very well. Please thank Elder Dilan for me.”

Anxi was a little disappointed to see no reaction on Fang Heng's face.

In truth, he didn't care.

Even the lowest-ranked warriors could receive clan training.

Promotion would just mean having to deal with more troublesome arena matches.

Soon they reached the mysticism training room, where Fang Heng met Mu Shunan again.

“Fang Heng.”

Mu Shunan had been meditating with his eyes closed. Seeing Fang Heng, he waved enthusiastically, his eyes bright with the excitement of someone hearing juicy gossip. “I heard you won four straight matches last night. Absolutely crushed everyone?”

“News travels fast.”

“Hahaha! Of course it does. Who knew you specialized in close combat? Warriors like that are rare.” Mu Shunan smiled and nodded. “We should team up sometime.”

“Sure.”

Fang Heng agreed and sat opposite Mu Shunan.

“Come on, let's see what two days of training have done for you.”

Smiling, Mu Shunan extended a hand and lightly touched Fang Heng's wrist.

The instant his finger made contact, surprise flashed in Mu Shunan's eyes.

Huh?

Just two days earlier, Fang Heng's meridians had been clogged like a swamp. Now, in less than two days, every one of them had been cleared?

That fast?

How had this youngster trained?

“Incredible! Brother Fang Heng, you have an extraordinary talent for mysticism.”

Mu Shunan looked up at him and clicked his tongue in admiration. Then he nodded and continued, “You've completed the first stage of clearing your meridians. They can now accommodate a much greater flow of mental power. Next, you should strengthen your muscles' activity so your body can withstand even more mental energy, progressing from skin to muscle, then organs and bones.”

“Unlike ordinary physical conditioning, mysticism training emphasizes the body's adaptability to mental energy. Once you've completed all those stages, you'll essentially have reached Master-level mysticism. Then we can proceed.”

“Mm...”

Fang Heng pondered the explanation.

He was already a Master in mysticism.

So at this stage, he was still making up for lessons he'd skipped.

Fang Heng nodded in understanding and asked, “Specifically, what do I need to do?”

“Practice.”

Mu Shunan summarized it in a single word before explaining, “There's no shortcut here. You have to keep circulating mental power through your meridians to temper the body and gradually improve its adaptability. Certain secret medicines can also accelerate this stage. They're expensive, but I hear you've been promoted to mid-tier warrior, so you should have a monthly allowance for purchasing supplies.”

“I'll send the secret medicine's formula and preparation method to your assistant. You can leave purchasing the ingredients and making the medicine to her. Each dose remains effective for about eight hours. Just drink a bowl before you begin training.”

“All right.”

Fang Heng agreed and began learning the routes for directing mental energy through his body under Mu Shunan's close guidance.

This step wasn't difficult for a player with Fang Heng's extraordinary mental power.

After a few attempts and corrections, he memorized the essential details of the method.

At the same time, a game notification appeared before him.

[Hint: Player has triggered a chained quest—Mysticism Advancement (Grandmaster).]

Quest Name: Mysticism Advancement (Grandmaster).

Quest Requirement: Complete Physical Mysticism Adaptability Enhancement (Current: 0.02%).

Quest Reward: Next stage of the quest.

“One more thing. Don't chase speed blindly at this stage. Take it slowly. Mental energy striking your skin and muscles can cause injuries, and those take time and medicine to heal. If you rush and accidentally damage your internal organs, recovery could take a very long time. It might even interfere with your arena matches.”

— End of Chapter 3078 —
