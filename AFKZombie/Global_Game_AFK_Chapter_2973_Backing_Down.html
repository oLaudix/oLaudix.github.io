Chapter 2973

Backing Down

From Chinese Chapter 2974 · 认怂

At first, the Crocodile Clan was extremely displeased.

Their mines kept being harassed by insects, and they even suspected other allied fleets might be secretly targeting them.

Worse still, there was nothing they could do about the insects. It was humiliating.

But as time passed, the crocodiles discovered that it wasn't just their own mines being attacked. Mining areas belonging to other factions were being raided too.

That immediately put the Crocodile Clan at ease.

People didn't resent scarcity so much as inequality. If everyone was getting beaten up, then it was almost as if nobody had been beaten at all.

The faction losing the most was actually the Shadow Clan.

The Shadow Clan was immensely powerful but had a very small population, so it didn't occupy any mines directly. Instead, it demanded 50% of the profits from all materials extracted on the planet as a protection fee.

Rumor had it that the Shadow Clan had received word of the attacks and sent a team to investigate, but nothing more had been heard afterward.

Apparently that hadn't worked out either.

In fact, the Shadow Clan had received reports from its allied races about insects attacking the rear-area mines.

Large numbers of mines were under attack, and the affected area was spreading rapidly.

The Shadow Clan had no idea Fang Heng was behind it. Their instincts suggested the insects might be consuming ore at high speed to complete some form of rapid evolution.

Since the reconnaissance teams couldn't eliminate the source of the insects in the mines, the Shadow Clan simply decided to abandon those areas and concentrate their forces on a stronger offensive at the main battlefront.

Fang Heng and the other allied fleets also received the Shadow Clan's notice concerning the recent attacks on mining areas.

Captain Giselle of the Western Federation raised an eyebrow as he read the report.

Strange insectoid creatures attacking mining areas...

And that familiar method of stealing ore...

Giselle exchanged glances with the Presidential Office experts beside him.

Could it be...?

Fang Heng?

The group instinctively looked outside the ship.

At some point, an underground tunnel entrance had appeared beneath Fang Heng's flagship.

Kinsfolk robots were continuously entering and leaving it, pushing large crates aboard the starship.

There was no doubt now.

Everyone present was one hundred percent certain that Fang Heng was controlling the so-called insect swarms attacking the mines.

But...

Was he really not afraid?

If the Shadow Clan discovered these tricks he was pulling behind their backs, humanity might become the common enemy of every Kinsfolk allied race!

The atmosphere aboard the flagship turned tense.

A familiar feeling.

Here we go again.

“Everyone, I know what you're thinking. Keep your mouths shut and act as if you know nothing about this.”

...

Neither the Western Federation nor the Flying Squirrel clan could have dreamed that the Shadow Clan and its allies would actually back down in the face of the Lickers.

Instead of reinforcing the guards at the mines, they withdrew them.

The Shadow Clan ordered its forces concentrated at the main battlefront.

How could Fang Heng possibly pass up an opportunity like that?

The moment he heard the news, he ordered mining at full speed.

Maximum output!

Everything at full capacity!

The Flying Squirrel clan had an exceptionally keen sense for detecting, classifying, and refining ores, which paired perfectly with the zombie horde's AFK resource gathering.

Every fleet's mining team was activated and operating at full load!

One major reason the Shadow Clan had set its sights on this special world was the planet's wealth of high-grade mineral resources.

Now Fang Heng was plundering all of them in one mad rush.

Standing in the starship's main control room, Fang Heng looked through the glass toward the Iron-Eater Clan constructing the prism pillars in the distance. He rubbed his chin, already beginning to calculate something else.

Zaka arrived to collect another batch of shovels for emergency delivery. After confirming the Shadow Clan's latest news with the alien allies, he returned to report. “Boss Fang, we've just confirmed it. Apart from a few high-grade mines, the Shadow Clan has temporarily abandoned guarding the mining areas...”

“Mm...”

Seeing Fang Heng deep in thought, Zaka found it odd.

Hadn't everything been settled?

Unable to restrain himself, Zaka asked, “Boss Fang, what are you thinking about?”

Fang Heng drew his gaze away from the window.

They were acquiring so much ore that refining it and exchanging it directly for Kinsfolk contribution would actually be a bit of a waste.

“I'm thinking, since we have so many mineral resources anyway, why don't we lend the Shadow Clan alliance another hand? We could accept a few more missions to build Eye of Stars prism pillars and help the Iron-Eater Clan finish construction sooner.”

“Huh?”

Zaka was utterly dumbfounded.

Help the Shadow Clan build prism pillars for free?

Was he joking? Wouldn't that just be throwing away their hard-earned ore?

They'd lose even more that way!

Since when was Boss Fang so eager to help others?

That didn't sound like him at all!

“It's settled, then.” Fang Heng snapped his fingers. “Contact the Shadow Clan alliance and tell them we still have spare capacity. We'd be happy to help the Iron-Eater Clan construct additional prism pillars, and we hope to obtain the alliance's authorization.”

Huh? He was serious?

Zaka opened his mouth. He truly couldn't understand Fang Heng's purpose, but simply replied, “O-okay. I'll take care of it right away.”

Meanwhile, the Western Federation players had been staying obediently aboard their ships for days, watching crates of ore being hauled into Fang Heng's fleet day and night in a frenzy of activity.

Even the people from the Eastern Federation were helping transport and sort ore.

All Captain Giselle had to do was log into the Kinsfolk system to see his human faction's honor points soaring without pause.

By his estimate, enormous amounts of ore were being exchanged every hour.

How much were they earning?

He'd be lying if he said he wasn't envious.

Sigh!

No wonder the Eastern Federation had managed to expand such an enormous fleet in so little time.

Captain Giselle sighed deeply and shot a look of undisguised disgust at Vion and the other experts nearby.

If those pig teammates hadn't been pointing fingers and causing trouble, the Western Federation might have been able to get a share of the pie too.

Vion looked out through the window, burning with jealousy. He'd even considered secretly informing the Shadow Clan, but one look at Captain Giselle's warning, watchful gaze made him abandon the idea.

On the main battlefield, the Shadow Clan's fleets had assembled. Using their overwhelming strength to withstand the insect swarm's frontal attacks, they successfully stabilized the situation.

“Captain, a report from the humans.”

A Shadow Clan soldier handed over a message and reported, “The human fleet is asking to participate in more prism construction work. They're willing to offer us additional help at no cost, provided we give our authorization.”

A flash of surprise passed through the Shadow Clan captain's eyes. “And then? What are they asking for in exchange?”

The soldier shook his head. “They haven't asked for anything.”

A crocodile captain nearby burst into laughter. “I've heard that although humans are weak, they know how to please their betters. Looks like they're admitting defeat and bowing their heads to us.”

The Shadow Clan captain hummed softly. Without giving it much thought, he waved a hand. “Very well. If they want to work, let them do more.”

“Yes.”

— End of Chapter 2973 —
