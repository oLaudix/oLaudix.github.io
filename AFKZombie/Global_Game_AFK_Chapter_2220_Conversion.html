Chapter 2220

Conversion

From original Chinese Chapter 2221 · 转化

As soon as those words were spoken, everyone stepped forward at once.

The soldiers looked at Fang Heng with barely concealed eagerness in their eyes.

Places were limited!

Rumors had already spread that the first stage of the experiment had produced astonishing results.

Even their commanding officer, Kleiman, had come in person!

Word was that there were only enough of the newly developed potions for a small group of people.

The second-stage test potions were even rarer, but the upper limit of the increase in psychic strength was supposedly far higher than in the first stage.

Taking part in the experiment was an excellent opportunity to grow stronger!

Even with the possibility of considerable danger, plenty of them wanted to volunteer.

And if the worst happened—even if they lost their lives—they would have died for the Federation!

Fang Heng shrugged and pointed at random. “You two.”

“Yes, sir!”

Hu Haiyu and the other selected soldier brightened immediately and stepped out of the ranks.

Everyone turned toward Fang Heng in curiosity.

How exactly would the second stage of testing work?

Was there another new potion he had yet to bring out?

Fang Heng did nothing of the sort. He simply turned to the vampire prince Jia'er, who had been following him, and nodded. “Jia'er, let's begin.”

All eyes shifted to the tall, middle-aged man beside him.

Jia'er had entered this game world through the vampires' teleportation passage not long ago and had stayed at Fang Heng's side ever since.

Shi Yanwu studied him again, an uneasy thought stirring.

The first time he saw Jia'er, he had sensed an invisible pressure emanating from the man.

He had even secretly ordered someone to investigate Jia'er's identity earlier.

Strangely, the Federation's records contained no matching information whatsoever.

Who was this man, exactly?

Jia'er regarded the two Federation soldiers about to participate in the second-stage human trial with evident interest.

Judging by a vampire's perception of blood and vitality, they were terribly weak.

Their physical constitutions were hardly better than children's.

“Oh? So it's the two of you? Very good. Come here. A little closer.”

The two soldiers approached Prince Jia'er as instructed.

“Humans have a saying: to gain power, you must pay an equivalent price. Are you willing?”

There was a playful glint in Jia'er's eyes as he looked at them.

“Heh. I'm only joking.”

Before either could answer, he chuckled and extended a finger toward each of them in turn.

Hiss! Hiss!

The two test subjects suddenly stiffened as an immense force poured through the space between their brows and into their bodies.

At the same time, a dark-red glow gathered on each of their foreheads.

Fang Heng watched quietly from the side.

There were two methods he could think of for improving someone's physical constitution in an extremely short time.

One was a potion developed by Qiu Yaokang.

The other was…

The vampires' bloodline inheritance!

A dose of the potion could raise a person's attributes by around fifteen points.

He had already checked the details with Jia'er.

If a vampire prince personally performed the conversion, an ordinary human could immediately gain roughly 100 to 120 points in their basic physical attributes!

And if they later entered a vampire altar for a blood baptism, reaching a total improvement of 200 points would be nothing unusual.

Of course, gaining entry to a vampire altar was another matter altogether.

Both soldiers felt their blood surge and churn through their bodies. A bloody sheen spread across their pupils.

An endless sea of blood seemed to swallow their vision!

Wave after wave of warmth rose from somewhere deep inside them and circulated with their blood, washing through every part of their bodies. The sensation was so soothing that they almost wanted to cry out.

Both closed their eyes again at nearly the same moment.

Having administered the vampire conversion, Jia'er gave Fang Heng a slight nod and returned to his side.

A moment later, the transformation was complete.

Fang Heng looked at the two Federation soldiers as they slowly opened their eyes. “How do you feel?”

Hu Haiyu exchanged a glance with his fellow subject.

Every physical attribute had risen dramatically during the conversion, leaving Hu Haiyu with a peculiar sensation.

The entire world seemed different from before.

Sharper. Clearer.

Neither soldier understood what had happened to them. Yet when they looked toward Fang Heng, they could faintly sense the power overflowing from him—and even felt an inexplicable urge to submit.

It was as though their instincts were telling them not to disobey anything Dr. Fang said.

Hu Haiyu swallowed. Even he didn't understand why he felt this way. “Wonderful. I've never felt better.”

“Mm.” Fang Heng nodded and gestured toward the Dark Radiance Stone in the hall. “All right, you two. You can begin your psychic tempering with the stone now.”

The two men were quietly excited. They headed straight toward the largest Dark Radiance Stone in the center of the hall.

“Wait.”

Fang Heng stopped them. “Use the two smaller stones beside it. Their short-term effects are less severe.”

“Yes, sir.”

They nodded again and made their way to the smallest Dark Radiance Stone at the far left. Sitting cross-legged before it, they entered meditation together.

Fang Heng leaned toward Jia'er. “Well?”

“Rest assured, my king. Setting aside their ability to wield that power, their basic attributes are already comparable to those of ordinary vampire marquises. They'll have no trouble enduring the fluctuations from a small Dark Radiance Stone.”

“Good work.”

Under everyone's watchful gaze, Hu Haiyu and the other soldier sat motionless before the stone, undergoing psychic tempering.

Minute after minute passed.

Like monks sunk deep in meditation, the two remained utterly still.

An entire hour went by.

The onlookers' expressions had shifted from puzzlement to astonishment, and finally to outright disbelief.

An entire hour of uninterrupted meditation?

How could they keep it up for so long?

Weren't they suffering any of the adverse effects of psychic tempering?

“Haaah…”

Fang Heng was getting sleepy just watching them. He yawned. “That should about do it. Has Chairman Ji still not arrived?”

Cheng Fan reluctantly tore his eyes away from the two cultivators. “I'm sorry, Dr. Fang. The meeting with the Federation hasn't ended yet. The chairman will come as soon as it's over.”

“It's fine. I'm in no hurry.”

Fang Heng looked toward the Federation personnel nearby. “It'll probably take them a while to finish, and you'll need plenty of time to gather the test data afterward. I've just had an idea for another experiment, so I'll head back and make a few adjustments instead of waiting here. I'll leave the follow-up records to you.”

“Of course, of course.” The Federation researchers had no idea how Fang Heng had accomplished any of this. Convinced they were witnessing the work of a brilliant scientist, they nodded enthusiastically. “Thank you for your hard work, Dr. Fang.”

Chinese Expressions
