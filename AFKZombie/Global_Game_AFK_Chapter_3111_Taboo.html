Chapter 3111

Taboo

From Chinese Chapter 3112 · 避讳

"Mm, not bad. This place looks quite suitable for training."

Huh?

Nasir's face fell.

Seriously?

More training? Wasn't this supposed to be a chance to relax?

He'd heard from Anxi that Fang Heng was a training fanatic who barely did anything else. His life was essentially divided between finding instructors and staying home to train.

Now it seemed she hadn't exaggerated.

"I'm going to take a look around. I'll be nearby. You know how to find me."

With that, Fang Heng leaped lightly down from the starship.

The soil was firm.

Perfect for digging tunnels.

Fang Heng disappeared into the dense forest, quickly vanishing from the sight of the warriors on the deck.

Nasir and Mu Shunan exchanged a look and could only smile helplessly.

Fang Heng ventured deep into the forest beyond the starship's landing zone. After traveling some distance, he stopped, flipped his wrist, and tossed two rune papers forward.

"Bang!"

Both papers burst apart, releasing two Lickers from their sealing runes. They landed on the ground.

The Lickers began trembling violently.

Division!

The two Lickers split into a total of sixty-four. Under Fang Heng's control, they immediately began digging a passage downward.

A short while later, an opening leading underground appeared before him.

Fang Heng jumped into the hole and used more rune papers to summon additional zombie clones, beginning the construction of an underground cavern.

Meanwhile, he cleared a spacious area underground and started setting up Abe Akaya's summoning magic array.

More Lickers excavated tunnels and expanded the underground space.

The entire area buzzed with industrious activity.

...

Five hours later, the zombies' tireless excavation had produced an underground complex roughly one kilometer across.

Fang Heng stood before the newly completed Abe Akaya magic array.

"That should do it..."

"Whoosh!"

The magic array in his right pupil spun rapidly!

The summoning array activated and rotated slowly, faint traces of black energy seeping from it.

Fang Heng frowned.

Still no good?

Just as in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, Abe Akaya's summoning array was meeting considerable resistance.

So his guess had been wrong.

The Kinsfolk had merely installed detection prism towers in the capital.

Forget it.

He would focus on training for now.

Grind Mysticism!

Fang Heng ordered Abe Akaya to release natural energy, sat cross-legged, and began converting it into Mysticism experience.

At the starship landing zone...

Nasir was casually chatting with Mu Shunan when both suddenly sensed something and looked toward the nearby forest.

An extraordinarily dense concentration of natural energy had abruptly gathered among the trees.

Good grief.

What was going on?

The two exchanged startled looks.

Nature Studies energy?

Was it Fang Heng?

But wasn't that fellow studying Elemental Studies?

...

Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

Kinsfolk headquarters.

Administrator Lila struggled to suppress her anger as she stared at the Kinsfolk captain standing below her.

"Why is Fang Heng still alive?"

"Administrator, Fang Heng happened to receive an urgent assignment outside the city, so he avoided our assassination operation."

Lila's expression darkened. "How convenient."

"From what we've learned through our Kinsfolk contacts, we've already eliminated a number of pan-humans recently. The Noel Clan may have noticed our preparations against Fang Heng. Perhaps they arranged a special assignment to remove him temporarily from the capital for his own protection."

The Kinsfolk captain lowered his head. "There's no need to worry, Administrator. Fang Heng will return sooner or later. We'll prepare thoroughly and strike the moment he comes back."

"And what if he doesn't return?" Lila said coldly. "No. I'm not waiting that long. Do you know where he's gone?"

"Fang Heng is carrying out an assignment in the Lanyue Clan's territory."

"We've placed a warrior among those accompanying him. According to the information provided, the Noel Clan is conducting a special ore-collection mission there that will likely take one or two months."

"Administrator, there have been no Black Mist incidents in the past two days. We suspect the source may have been eliminated during our operations. Of course, it's also possible the source is connected to Fang Heng."

"The Lanyue Clan... Does he think he can hide from us there?"

"I remember that the Lanyue Clan once considered submitting to us..." A thoughtful glint entered Lila's eyes. "Send someone to make contact. If they cooperate with us in eliminating Fang Heng, the Kinsfolk are willing to grant their entire clan the respect it deserves."

"Yes!"

...

Three days later.

Fang Heng returned to the starship for the first time and took a shower in his cabin.

After three days of training without sleep or rest, he needed to unwind a little.

He wouldn't grind tonight. He'd get a proper night's sleep and start again tomorrow.

At this pace, another week should be enough to max out Transcendent Mysticism.

He also heard that the local Lanyue Clan had supplied the starship with a variety of regional delicacies. Curious, he headed to the dining hall for a hearty meal before strolling onto the deck to digest it.

An evening breeze blew past.

Fang Heng noticed Nasir and Mu Shunan playing a board game in a corner of the deck.

Right. He'd almost forgotten.

He'd heard that coming to the Lanyue Clan's world was Nasir's suggestion. Nasir had implied there was another reason for inviting him here.

With so many people around earlier, Nasir hadn't been able to explain.

Now was the perfect chance to ask.

He could also consult the two of them about breaking through to the Demigod level.

Nasir was puzzling over his next move when Fang Heng approached. He smiled and waved. "Brother Fang Heng! How has training gone over the past couple of days?"

"Mm. I've made some progress."

Since Nasir had brought up training, Fang Heng pursued the subject. He looked at him and asked, "I've been approaching another bottleneck lately, but when I asked Anxi, she said there are no instructors teaching people how to break through to the Demigod level. Do you know why?"

"What? What did you say?"

Another breakthrough?

Nasir had only been making small talk. Hearing that Fang Heng had advanced again, he jolted and turned to stare at him as though trying to see what had changed.

"Nasir?"

Nasir waved a hand. He didn't particularly want to talk anymore.

He'd suffered enough shocks since meeting Fang Heng.

"That's normal."

Mu Shunan, who had finally rebuilt his confidence in cultivation, had overheard their discussion. "The Demigod level is exactly what its name suggests: a state approaching godhood."

"Out of reverence and taboo, no complete written inheritance for this level has ever been left behind. Most practitioners can only reach the Demigod level through oral instruction and their own exploration."

After all, Mu Shunan didn't know how far Fang Heng had progressed in Alchemy, so he wasn't affected as directly as Nasir. Ignorance was bliss; his confidence remained intact.

Fang Heng looked bewildered and pressed him, "Reverence and taboo? What exactly are they afraid to offend?"
