Chapter 2190

The Choice

From original Chinese Chapter 2191 · 选项

Still, he shouldn't expect too much.

After all, taking on the Dependents with technology from their own high-school textbooks was hardly going to be easy.

Then there was the last option.

The damaged waymark radar!

Fang Heng's gaze settled on it.

He had a vague hunch.

This radar might be connected to future quests involving the Dependents.

Even if he was wrong and it had nothing to do with those quests, he could still obtain a few world waymarks for free.

That settled it!

[Hint: The player has selected the reward — Damaged Waymark Radar.]

The device was about the size of a fist, with a somewhat damaged display and several buttons on its side.

Fang Heng pressed a few buttons at random, but nothing happened.

It seemed completely unusable.

He'd give it to Victor to see whether it could be repaired once he returned.

Fang Heng turned his attention back to the quest description.

[Hint: Settling rewards…]

[Hint: As a result of the player's actions, the current quest — Find the Divine Tree's Remnant Branch — has been canceled.]

[Hint: The player has gained 20 Tree Spirit favorability and 5,000,000 Tree Spirit Honor Points.]

[Hint: Main quest progress has increased to 57.1%.]

The quest was complete, and the main quest had jumped straight to 57.1%!

Totally worth it!

An enormous profit!

He'd saved himself a tremendous amount of time!

Fang Heng turned and noticed Levin staring at him in horror.

By now, Levin's face was withered, and his eyes were filled with disbelief.

This man before him…

The bizarre power he'd just unleashed…

Levin drew a deep breath, exhaustion covering his face.

He could feel the natural power that had been suppressed within him for so long springing back to life.

At the very last moment of his life, finally…

At least the Tree Spirits had been freed from their shackles.

A bright future for his race was finally within reach!

A faint look of relief spread across Levin's face, but the light in his eyes gradually faded until only lifeless gray remained.

Fang Heng glanced at the fallen elder and forced his heavy body upright.

Hm?

What's that…?

He approached and spotted the Azure Lotus Seed lying where Levin had fallen.

[Hint: The player has obtained — Azure Lotus Seed.]

[Item: Azure Lotus Seed.]

Rank: Legendary.

Description: One of the Tree Spirits' sacred artifacts. Activating it summons a special domain that enhances Nature Studies skills and restores a large amount of HP to allied units every second.

Description: Requires Nature Studies to activate and consumes the caster's life force. Its effects depend on the life force consumed and the caster's Nature Studies level.

Description: This item can be absorbed through sacrifice.

Fang Heng read the description and frowned.

It seemed to be the sacred artifact Levin had used in his final moments to help the others escape.

It actually consumed life force?

Useless!

He'd rather have a few more Leaves of Life.

He could try sacrificing it for energy when he had the chance.

If that didn't work, he'd sell it.

Fang Heng stuffed the Azure Lotus Seed into his backpack, then looked again toward where the Electronic Light Spirit had been.

There they were.

Several dark-colored crystals lay where the creature had dissipated.

Fang Heng waved a hand toward them.

The crystals flew into his palm.

[Hint: The player has obtained Tier 3 Iridescent Crystals ×7.]

Iridescent Crystals! Tier 3 advancement materials?

Fang Heng was a little surprised.

Since entering the World of Myriad Forests, he hadn't seen a single Evolution Crystal drop. He'd assumed it was a special rule of the world.

He hadn't expected one to drop now.

Tier 3 Iridescent Crystals were required to advance through levels 41 to 50.

He was currently only level 40.

He had no use for them yet.

Apparently he'd been farming a monster well above his level.

Fang Heng shrugged and searched the area more carefully.

[Hint: The player has discovered — Psychic Core.]

[Item: Psychic Core.]

Type: Special Item.

Description: A special item created by the Dependents to modify the Electronic Light Spirit. It can restrict and control the Electronic Light Spirit's power and is also effective on certain other life-forms.

Description: The player may use it to control certain life-forms.

Description: Prerequisite for use: Master-level Psychic Studies.

Fang Heng frowned.

Directly controlling living beings?

That sounded a little like Beast Taming.

But the prerequisite was awfully high.

Master-level Psychic Studies.

He'd keep it for now.

Just then, Ji Haiting's voice came through his earpiece.

“Bzzzt… bzzzt… Hello? Fang Heng? Can you hear me? Fang Heng?”

Wanling Technology really did make quality earpieces. It was still working after everything that had happened.

Fang Heng nodded. “Yes, I'm here.”

“Phew!”

At the other end, Ji Haiting had finally restored the connection.

Seeing Fang Heng safe through the pinhole camera, he breathed a tremendous sigh of relief.

“You…”

Taking another look at the surroundings through the tiny camera, Ji Haiting asked with grave uncertainty, “The Electronic Light Spirit… it…?”

Fang Heng understood and nodded. “Mm. Got lucky.”

Ji Haiting fell silent for a long time.

This…

Was that really something a person could accomplish through sheer luck?

“Boss Ji, my strength is severely reduced right now. It'd be best if you sent someone to pick me up.”

“Of course. Wait there, I'll send people immediately. The Tree Spirits are still inside the facility, and their strength has increased enormously. It isn't safe here. I'll arrange for an escort to get you out.”

Fang Heng took a deep breath. “All right. Thanks.”

Before long, a Federation semi-mechanized unit dropped through the ceiling passage.

Its captain nodded on seeing Fang Heng. “Dr. Fang, you've had quite a fright. We'll evacuate you immediately, then arrange a full medical examination. We may also need you to help us reconstruct what happened.”

Escorted by Federation troops, Fang Heng made his way out of the research institute.

More than an hour later…

Inside a recovery ward…

After half an hour's rest, Fang Heng had regained most of his strength.

The door opened.

Ji Haiting instructed several accompanying psychically modified guards to wait outside, then entered the room alone.

“Fang Heng, nothing seriously wrong?”

“I forced myself to use a skill beyond my limits and suffered some backlash. I've nearly recovered.” Fang Heng looked at Ji Haiting. “What's happening outside?”

“The Spirit Domain blockade network has been completely destroyed. Our communication channel with the Dependents is gone.”

Ji Haiting gave a bitter smile. “The Federation has never faced anything like this. They're at a loss, too. I doubt they'll be able to do much in the short term except wait for the Dependents to contact us again.”

“Without the network to transfer them here, traveling from the Dependents' star system to our world will take at least another six months. For now, the Federation will have to deal with the Tree Spirits on its own. The Federal Council has already called a meeting to discuss its next move.”

— End of Chapter 2179 —
