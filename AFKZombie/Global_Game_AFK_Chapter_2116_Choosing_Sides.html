Chapter 2116

Choosing Sides

From original Chinese Chapter 2117 · 阵营选择

Sandy looked bewildered. “What ancient tree? What are you talking about?”

Glenan took a deep breath, suppressed his anger, and pointed toward a spot deep within the cavern.

“There used to be a withered ancient tree over there. Mr. Yule told us it was once an offshoot of the Divine Tree that protected the Tree Spirits. It was proof that the Tree Spirits had once existed.”

“The Divine Tree once sheltered this land, but that ancient offshoot withered away completely and became what you see now.”

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and followed Glenan's gesture into the cavern.

There stood a dead tree more than three meters tall.

Its leaves had long since fallen, leaving only a bare trunk and a handful of dried branches.

All signs of life were gone.

Now, countless cables of different colors were wrapped around its branches like tentacles.

Fang Heng frowned, suspicious.

Was Wanling Technology conducting some kind of special analysis on the ancient tree?

Game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Game Hint: Current quest completion has increased to 45%.]

[Game Hint (triggered by special reward): You have reached the minimum completion requirement and may submit the quest. Due to the exceptionally high difficulty, immediate retreat is recommended.]

Fang Heng glanced at the game's recommendation.

It wanted him to stop at only 45%?

Not happening!

He continued studying the cavern below.

Wanling Technology's researchers clearly found the dead tree fascinating. All their equipment had been arranged around it.

What exactly were they trying to do?

As Fang Heng pondered this, he noticed a group approaching the cliff entrance from a distance.

A middle-aged man at their head stopped.

The group looked up toward the entrance where Fang Heng's party was hiding.

More than a dozen guards behind the man immediately halted and spread out around him in a protective formation.

“Heh, intruders... I was rather surprised when I received a report about a possible infiltration. I didn't think anyone would actually break into Wanling Technology. It's been a long time since I've seen such interesting people.”

“You don't look like Tree Spirits. Are you members of the Tree Whisperer Alliance? I knew you might come, though I didn't expect you to use this approach.”

The middle-aged man smiled pleasantly up at the cliff. “Allow me to introduce myself. I'm Ji Haiting, executive director of Wanling Technology Corporation. We've never had the opportunity for a proper discussion. I believe there may be considerable misunderstandings between us. In fact, Wanling Technology would be very willing to cooperate with you.”

“After all, we all live in the same world, don't we?”

The group hiding above the cliff exchanged glances.

Mo Jiawei asked, “Fang Heng, looks like we've been spotted. What should we do?”

“I'll go down and speak with him. Stay here in case anything happens.”

Klevi said softly, “Brother Fang Heng, I'd like to come down too. I can sense some fluctuations of natural energy around that withered tree.”

“Mm, all right. Stay close and be careful.”

Fang Heng glanced at her, then stood up in full view and jumped from the cliff.

Klevi followed him, drifting gently down through the air.

The instant Fang Heng landed, Ji Haiting's guards fixed him with wary looks.

The armed security personnel raised their weapons at once, as though facing a formidable enemy. Nearby researchers hurried out of the way.

Unlike the guards outside, the people clustered around Ji Haiting wore matching uniforms and carried no visible weapons.

Fang Heng gave them a second glance.

Their clothing was peculiar.

The garments appeared to be one-piece suits decorated with flowing lines, with a diamond-shaped crystalline pattern just above the abdomen.

Very strange.

They looked nothing like ordinary fashionable clothes, nor did they resemble the uniforms of normal security staff.

“No need to be so nervous. We're only going to talk.”

Ji Haiting raised a hand, signaling for the armed guards to lower their guns. He turned back to Fang Heng. “Thank you for trusting us. We will show our sincerity as well.”

[Game Hint: Your Charisma attribute has taken effect. You have gained additional initial favorability with Ji Haiting.]

Fang Heng looked at the notification and couldn't help complaining inwardly.

He hadn't gained favorability with the Tree Whisperer Alliance, but he'd somehow won over the enemy instead?

What sort of situation was this?

He fixed his gaze on Ji Haiting. “Wanling Technology Corporation. What are you doing here?”

“As you can see, some basic scientific research. In recent years, Wanling Technology has been studying the Tree Spirit race, and we've made a few modest breakthroughs. By analyzing and simulating seeds that contain the source of their power, we identified an area with similar energy signatures...”

“After that, things were simple. We hired scientific and exploration teams to search the designated area. They soon found these ruins.”

Ji Haiting spread his hands. “In other words, by your terminology, we came to search for the Tree Spirits—and for the Ancient Divine Tree.”

Fang Heng felt a stir of interest and looked toward the dead tree behind Ji Haiting.

“The Ancient Divine Tree? Is that it?”

“That? No, of course not.” Ji Haiting shook his head. “It falls far short of the Ancient Divine Tree and has long since withered. But we've confirmed that it does have a small connection to the Ancient Divine Tree.”

Fang Heng nodded toward it. “If it's already dead, what are you still doing to it?”

“Testing.” Ji Haiting explained calmly, “We're studying the withered tree's biological activity. We want to try reviving it completely. What do you think? Interested? Perhaps we could work together. Why not consider it?”

[Game Hint: You have triggered the side quest—Invitation to Cooperate.]

Quest Name: Invitation to Cooperate.

Difficulty: D (choice-based quest).

Quest Description: Wanling Technology Corporation has invited you to work together to investigate the Ancient Divine Tree's secrets. Accepting will terminate the Tree Whisperer Alliance questline, erase all Tree Whisperer Alliance contribution points and reputation, and affect your favorability with certain characters.

[Game Hint (triggered by your earlier special reward): You are advised to continue cooperating with the Tree Whisperer Alliance. Choosing that questline will greatly shorten the overall process and reduce its difficulty.]

Fang Heng looked at the quest notification.

A faction-choice mission.

Originally, he might have hesitated. Now there was no need.

The game had practically spelled out the answer.

— End of Chapter 2116 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Faction choice. The game recommends staying with the Tree Whisperer Alliance. Accepting Wanling Technology’s D-rank choice quest would end the alliance questline and clear its rewards.
  • Name inconsistency. One source line writes 纪海峰, while the surrounding lines consistently use 纪海庭 for the Wanling executive. The translation retains Ji Haiting.
  • Dead tree. Ji Haiting distinguishes the withered offshoot from the true Ancient Divine Tree.

Chinese Expressions

  • 不采纳 (bù cǎinà). A curt “Not accepting it!” when Fang Heng rejects the game’s recommendation to stop the mission at 45%.
