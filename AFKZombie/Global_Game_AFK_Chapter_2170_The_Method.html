Chapter 2170

The Method

From original Chinese Chapter 2171 · 方法

“At that point, the Spirit Domain network's energy-detection functions will also shut down, and some of the suppression on the Tree Spirits will be lifted. In theory, they could open a portal to the Inner World, and their power would increase slightly.”

“But it won't last long. Ten minutes at most. Once we've erased the traces you've left behind, we'll restart the network immediately. That shouldn't be enough time for the Tree Spirits to open an Inner World portal at short notice.”

Fang Heng lowered his head in thought.

Indeed, ten minutes was very little time to open a passage to the Inner World in a hurry.

Unless…

Unless the Tree Spirits were already prepared!

Seeing Fang Heng deep in thought, Ji Haiting said, “Fang Heng, that's another of the risks we'll have to accept.”

A risk?

Fang Heng rubbed his chin, narrowed his eyes, and let a smile creep across his face.

The Tree Spirits!

So he was supposed to risk his neck fighting the Dependents while the Tree Spirits watched from the sidelines—and conveniently enjoyed ten minutes of freedom?

That wouldn't do.

He'd have to drag the Tree Spirits into this too.

Why keep the fun all to himself?

And while he was at it, he might as well get something out of them.

Noticing Fang Heng's rather wicked smile, Ji Haiting couldn't help asking, “Fang Heng, have you thought of something?”

“Nothing much. Just a good idea. Let's proceed with the plan we discussed. Keep in touch.”

“Very well.”

Ji Haiting briefly went over the details with Fang Heng, then nodded to his subordinates nearby.

Moments later, points of spatial-jump light appeared around the group, and they quickly vanished.

Fang Heng looked away, closed his eyes, and tried to sense something again.

The vampire tracking mark he'd left on Vannis during their last meeting had almost entirely faded.

But he still remembered the coordinates where Vannis had last been located.

Let's go!

Time to take a look!

Fang Heng paid his bill, walked around a street corner, and quickly transformed into a bat, flying high into the sky.

…

Above a primeval forest.

Fang Heng flew rapidly in bat form.

According to the intelligence he'd obtained from Ji Haiting, the Tree Spirits had also established a headquarters in the Outer World.

But because the Tree Spirits had never trusted humanity, neither the Tree Whisperer Alliance nor the Federation knew its location.

Fang Heng, however, had tracked it with his vampire marking ability.

Along the way, he spent considerable effort destroying another wave of Dependent pursuers before finally entering a stretch of untouched primeval forest.

Judging by his senses, it should be nearby.

After circling overhead and examining the area, Fang Heng slowly landed on a tall tree.

This was the exact location where Vannis's presence had vanished from his perception.

So…

The Tree Spirits' headquarters was…

The forest around him was perfectly quiet. Nothing he could see suggested there was any sort of Tree Spirit base here.

Had he guessed wrong?

Fang Heng closed his eyes and slowly extended his perception.

His senses spread outward from his body.

Found it!

His eyes snapped open, and he looked ahead.

In a small area not far away, his perception was being faintly absorbed.

He never would have noticed it without a careful inspection.

Something like a barrier was hiding there.

Fang Heng cautiously approached.

He stopped directly before the source of the effect.

Slowly, he raised a hand and reached forward.

The instant his fingers touched it, faint ripples appeared in the empty air.

With a little pressure, his fingertips slipped into the ripples.

The barrier didn't appear to have any offensive capabilities.

After a moment's thought, Fang Heng simply stepped through it.

“A teleportation passage.”

Having crossed to the other side, he glanced over his shoulder.

A ripple spread across the air behind him, then quickly returned to normal.

Some kind of absorption barrier.

An illusionary concealment field that absorbed external perception.

“Who's there?!”

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

An angry shout rang out, and several arrows flew toward him from the distant trees!

“Pfft! Pfft!”

Two arrows pierced Fang Heng's chest in an instant!

“Thud!”

Their enormous momentum carried him backward, pinning him to a giant tree along with the arrows.

“Crack, crack…”

Vines immediately began winding around the points where the arrows had pierced him.

[Hint: The player has suffered 3,091 damage, has been afflicted with Vine Entanglement, and has been affected by Parasitic Spores…]

“The Tree Spirits…”

Fang Heng muttered to himself, pulled the arrows out of his body, and tossed them aside.

The gaping wounds healed visibly before his eyes.

He looked toward several figures hidden in the distant forest.

“I mean no offense. I've come to see Great Elder Senzo.”

The Tree Spirit guards who'd attacked from concealment exchanged puzzled glances.

No one had entered their territory in over a century.

And this human was extraordinary.

He'd been shot with arrows and still looked perfectly calm.

He was clearly no weakling.

Two Tree Spirits remained hidden in the trees as they issued a warning. “Human, this is not a place you should enter.”

“I have an extremely important matter to discuss with Great Elder Senzo. Tell him Fang Heng has come to see him. It concerns the Federation's latest movements and the Niam Alloy. It's urgent, and I need to meet him immediately.”

“Stay right there. Don't move.”

Hearing him mention Niam Alloy, the guards realized it might be important. They ordered him to remain in place while one of them returned to report to the clan.

Soon, more than a dozen Tree Spirits emerged from the undergrowth, led by Vannis.

Vannis happened to be in charge of guard duty that day. When he heard that a human had entered their territory demanding to see Great Elder Senzo, he found it extremely strange and came to investigate personally.

“Fang Heng? What are you doing here?”

Vannis frowned when he recognized him.

Fang Heng?

How had he managed to find this place?

“Did you come alone?”

Fang Heng recognized Vannis too.

“Elder Vannis, what a coincidence. I know the Tree Spirits dislike being disturbed by outsiders, so as you can see, I've come to visit entirely by myself.”

Vannis scrutinized Fang Heng's face, his tone accusatory. “How did you find us?”

Fang Heng almost laughed at the coincidence.

Why, thanks to you, of course.

“I'm a believer in nature. Following nature's guidance, finding you isn't particularly difficult.”

Vannis and the others exchanged glances, clearly dismissive of all this talk about guidance from the God of Nature.

A human favored by the God of Nature?

What a joke!

Dream on!

The Tree Spirits were the race chosen by the God of Nature.

Vannis decided Fang Heng simply didn't want to explain. He didn't press further, instead asking coldly, “What do you want?”

(End of chapter)
