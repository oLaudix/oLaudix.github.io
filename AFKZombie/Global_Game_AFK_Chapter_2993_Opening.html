Chapter 2993

Opening

From Chinese Chapter 2994 · 开启

Three days later.

The Jike Archipelago.

The islands were covered in snow year-round. Because of the extreme cold, no humans lived here and only a handful of wild animals appeared.

Today, however, was different.

Large numbers of ships had gathered and moored at the edges of the ice.

On the thick ice sheet, crowds of players had assembled in groups large and small.

The teams from the Central Federation and four regional Federations were particularly conspicuous.

Each was an enormous group of at least five hundred people, including well-equipped soldiers and mercenaries hired by the Federations.

By comparison, the player teams were much smaller, with even the largest numbering only twenty or thirty.

The players had obtained Keys of Apocalypse through various means and gathered here independently, beyond the Federations' control.

Fang Heng stood quietly among the crowd, concealing his aura.

With all the major Federations attending, Fang Heng had decided to come alone to avoid unnecessary trouble. He had also slightly altered his appearance so no one would recognize him at a glance.

Even if he encountered trouble that required help, he could simply find Vampires who had entered the Land of Apocalypse and order them around.

But something puzzled him.

Given the Federations' usual conduct, why were they so readily allowing independent player teams to participate in the treasure hunt?

Something about it was unusual.

Before long, the time reached midnight.

The Keys of Apocalypse in the players' hands began glowing faintly.

Tiny specks of light gathered and drifted upward over the ice, gradually forming a symbol in midair.

“Hummm...!”

Space rippled outward in concentric waves. At the same time, shards of fragmented space assembled into an oval portal.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes slightly.

An unusual method of constructing a spatial passage.

He felt he'd seen something similar somewhere before, but couldn't recall the details.

Quiet whispers spread among the players.

An officer stepped out from the Central Federation team, surveyed the crowd, and announced gravely, “Everyone, according to the Central Federation's investigations, the treasure lies in the Land of Apocalypse. It may represent an immense power, possibly even the future of humanity.”

“The Federation will not obstruct teams exploring its mysteries without cause. In return, please do not interfere with Federation operations. Thank you in advance.”

“Good luck to you all.”

The Central Federation officer nodded to someone behind him. Starting with the Central Federation personnel closest to the portal, the groups began entering the gate one by one.

The regional Federation teams followed, and the independent players entered last.

Fang Heng blended into a group of players, stepped briskly through the portal, and entered the Land of Apocalypse.

...

The Land of Apocalypse.

A white haze of icy air filled his vision. Thick walls of ice surrounded him, and the floor beneath his feet was frozen solid.

Fang Heng frowned slightly.

The cold here was more than twice as intense as outside.

Most players qualified to enter were far from ordinary and could endure it for a while. Naturally, Fang Heng was unaffected.

Looking around, he seemed to be inside an immense labyrinth built entirely from walls of ice.

The corridors were wide enough for more than thirty people to walk abreast.

The portal apparently connected to multiple exits on this side, scattering the player teams.

Because the Federation contingents were so large, their scattered members could still assemble squads of several dozen, and they were continuing to take attendance.

Ordinary player squads, meanwhile, had already found their companions and begun venturing deeper into the maze in twos and threes.

Small teams really were more flexible.

If they got lucky and found the treasure before the Federations, wouldn't they become rich overnight?

Fang Heng didn't rush off. After quickly examining his surroundings, he expanded his perception and slowly looked ahead.

Hm?

That almost imperceptible aura...

There was no mistake. It was an Ouroboros Seed!

There was an Ouroboros Seed in the Land of Apocalypse too!

Fang Heng set off briskly in the general direction of the Seed's aura.

Before long, he'd left the other players behind and lost his bearings amid the maze's twisting corridors.

The icy walls were exceptionally hard and even prevented spatial penetration, so he couldn't pass straight through them.

With his current strength, he might be able to smash through a wall directly, but it would take considerable effort and risk collapsing the entire labyrinth.

Who knew what mechanisms or traps were inside? If he brought the whole underground structure crashing down and destroyed the treasure with it, wouldn't that be a disaster?

After some thought, Fang Heng decided to play it safe and search within the approximate range indicated by the Ouroboros Seed's aura.

Hm?

He suddenly stopped and looked down the straight icy passage ahead.

“What's that...?”

“Crack, crack, crack...”

Ice gradually gathered on the frozen ground, condensing into humanoid crystalline creatures.

Ice-element creatures?

The Ice Crystal Puppets were enormous, more than two meters tall. Once fully formed, they stepped toward Fang Heng.

One reached him, clenched its fist, and swung viciously at his face with a whistle through the air.

“Whoosh!”

Fang Heng punched back!

“BOOM!!”

Although he had struck second, his fist arrived first, smashing into the puppet's right arm and blowing it apart.

Too weak.

At least for now, the difference in their strength was obvious.

Hm?

Fang Heng looked up.

The puppet's shattered right arm was rapidly reforming under the influence of the surrounding icy aura.

“Heh!”

Fang Heng snorted, leaned forward, and threw another punch, shattering the entire puppet.

“BANG!!”

The fragments of ice crystal quickly melted back into the frozen floor.

At the same time, a faint pale-blue energy rose from the debris and rushed toward Fang Heng, entering his body and merging into his Sea of Consciousness.

Hm?

Fang Heng closed his eyes briefly to sense the power.

It was ice-element energy!

His Elemental Studies were currently capped at Advanced LV:MAX.

After entering his body, the power settled quietly in his Sea of Consciousness, neither moving nor dissipating.

Fang Heng was certain he could absorb and convert that elemental energy into Elemental Studies experience.

It simply couldn't improve his current skill while he remained at MAX level and had not advanced.

Was this power related to the Land of Apocalypse's treasure?

Judging by the energy's flow, he would only receive elemental-energy rewards for personally killing the Ice Crystal Puppets.

Not bad.

His Sea of Consciousness was vast, and storing some elemental power presented no difficulty at all.

He'd save it for later.

Since he was here anyway, Fang Heng figured he might as well grind some ice creatures and stockpile Elemental Studies energy while heading toward the Ouroboros Seed's aura.

— End of Chapter 2993 —
