Chapter 2053

Leakage

From original Chinese Chapter 2054 · 泄露

The undead invading Duncha were every bit as troublesome as the reports had claimed.

Their powerful regenerative abilities meant that even if all the cathedral's sacred guards gathered here, it would be difficult to eliminate them quickly.

Silufi was now certain. Everything matched the intelligence from the Divine Domain. These creatures had undoubtedly been summoned by Fang Heng.

But why was he in the Temple of Zeus? What was he trying to do?

“We're going to the Temple of Zeus. Everyone else, remain here!”

“Understood!”

Three cardinals who had been leading the defense against the undead gathered around the Archbishop.

Silufi was more concerned about the Temple of Zeus than the zombies still occupying the cathedral. She waved her scepter, conjured a sacred barrier, and led the three cardinals and a group of inquisitors toward the passage connecting to the temple.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Several Lickers immediately pounced at her.

CRACK!

Their claws struck the barrier, scattering dark-golden sparks like electrical discharges. The creatures were thrown backward.

The sacred shield completely shut out their attacks.

The zombie clones' weakness—insufficient attack power—was suddenly obvious. Their claws couldn't penetrate Silufi's defense.

Protected by the barrier, her group crossed the Prayer Hall and entered the rear passage.

But just as they were about to pass through its entrance, Silufi sensed something deeply abnormal. Her expression turned grave.

What was that?

She snapped her head up and stared toward the far end of the corridor, disbelief filling her eyes.

BOOM!

A mass of visibly dark mist surged toward them from the other end!

Not good!

“Sacred Purification Barrier!” Silufi shouted.

Hum—!

A golden sacred shield appeared before the group.

The three cardinals and the inquisitors also sensed the danger. They summoned their Books of Beginnings and poured power into the barrier.

Hiss! Hiss!

Black energy swept forward like a tide, repeatedly striking the sacred purification shield. A harsh sizzling sound filled the air.

The Archbishop and the three cardinals exchanged astonished looks.

That disturbing power…

The Demon Seed!

It was the Demon Seed's corruption!

This is bad!

They could only barely contain the corruption flowing down this one passage. More of it was escaping through windows and other corridors.

Fang Heng!

There had been unconfirmed rumors that he was linked to the Demon Seed. Now those rumors seemed very likely to be true.

A Demon Seed contamination leak was an even more serious disaster than the spread of the Yin Vein!

And the source was the Temple of Zeus.

…

At that moment, Chu Yan and the Devout Guild players had just left the cathedral.

They still hadn't found the stone inscription used to train Crusaders. The underground areas were heavily guarded, leaving them no opportunity to break in.

Their visit hadn't been a complete waste, though.

During the chaos, Chu Yan and his companions had quietly removed the cathedral's soul bell and drum under the pretext of protecting them.

The Holy Court's defenders had now taken charge of the whole cathedral and regained control. With little else to steal, Chu Yan decided to leave early and arrange their escape route.

When he reached the agreed meeting point and saw that Fang Heng still hadn't appeared, he looked anxiously back toward the cathedral.

His expression changed immediately.

What?!

The other players sensed it too and turned to look.

Thick, violent black mist was pouring out of the cathedral's second-floor windows!

That direction… the Temple of Zeus!

What was that disturbing black fog? What new stunt was Boss Fang pulling now?

Chu Yan stared for a moment, then reacted first.

“This is bad! That mist is dangerous! We can't wait any longer. Move! Everyone out!”

…

Outside the Temple of Zeus, Silufi and the three cardinals exchanged glances and nodded together.

They had to capture Fang Heng, whatever the cost!

Whoosh!

All of them drove their sacred power to its limit.

Dazzling golden light exploded outward, sweeping aside the surrounding Demon Seed contamination.

“After him!”

Using the brief opening, they rushed straight toward the Temple of Zeus.

The group sped down the corridor and quickly reached the entrance to the hall.

Everything ahead was shrouded in swirling black mist, making it difficult to see.

There he is!

The source of the fog!

Through the layers of darkness, Silufi spotted a human figure standing at the center of the hall.

Everyone fixed their eyes on him. The power emanating from that figure made even the three cardinals feel alarmed.

The Demon Seed! An awakened Demon Seed!

Within the dense cloud, Fang Heng slowly raised his head.

His eyes were now completely filled with profound blackness.

The yellow crystal's energy had been almost entirely consumed, and roughly a third of the gray fog that had once filled his sea of consciousness had been dispersed.

At first, Fang Heng had believed this was good news. It would buy him more time.

But as the outer layer of Envy's power was rapidly cleared away, he realized he'd made a serious mistake.

Every assumption he'd made about the Demon Seed had been wrong.

After clearing some of the gray mist Envy had left behind, he'd finally discovered a part of the Demon Seed's secret.

The secret of the ritual!

The ritual Xia Xi had performed on him was completely different from the Demon Seed ritual Gu Qingzhu had once undergone.

Gu Qingzhu's ritual was a form of Demon Seed inheritance.

First, a Demon Seed was found. Then someone searched for a human—or other living creature—compatible with it. Part of the Demon Seed was infused into the host, allowing the body to adapt gradually to its power and become a suitable vessel.

More of the Demon Seed could then be absorbed through that vessel, until the host finally mastered its full strength.

But Fang Heng now understood with absolute clarity:

The Demon Seed inside him was already complete!

Its power belonged entirely to him. It wasn't the sort of inheritance Gu Qingzhu had described.

He could control the Demon Seed's power completely, with no conflict, and merge with it perfectly.

Or perhaps it would be more accurate to say that he himself was the Demon Seed.

The problem was that his body was far too weak to withstand the full extent of that power.

— End of Chapter 2053 —
