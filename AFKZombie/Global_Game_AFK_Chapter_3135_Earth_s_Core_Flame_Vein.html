Chapter 3135

Earth's Core Flame Vein

From Chinese Chapter 3136 · 地心炎脉

Nearby, Kappa lay sideways on a rock with a reed between his teeth. Watching the convicts laboring so diligently, he couldn't help sighing inwardly.

When would these miserable days ever end…?

So much ore was being extracted, only for the Kinsfolk to take it all away in the end.

It was heartbreaking.

Why hadn't Boss Fang come to rescue them yet?

“Kappa, looks like life isn't treating you too badly here.”

Suddenly, Kappa heard a familiar voice beside his ear. Startled, he sat bolt upright on the rock and looked around.

Boss Fang!?

Finally, you've come!

Still in his spirit form, Fang Heng hovered beside Kappa. “You look pretty comfortable staying here.”

“Boss Fang, please don't tease me.”

Kappa rolled over and quickly hid behind the rock. With a miserable expression, he whispered, “Comfortable? I haven't seen the sun in ages. At this rate, our Flying Squirrel Clan will turn into the Mole Clan.”

“Sorry. I was away from the Seven Realms Martial Capital for a while, and only learned you were imprisoned after I got back. To keep things brief, the Kinsfolk have run into some serious trouble and may decide to act against you. I need to get you both out before they make their move.”

Kappa immediately brightened up.

He could finally escape this damned place!

With Boss Fang taking action, what was there to be afraid of?

“Breaking you out won't be difficult. The problem is that we don't have a starship to leave in. That could be troublesome. Any ideas?”

Kappa's little black eyes darted about as a plan came to him. “I remember quite a few scrapped ships and vessels awaiting repairs were left in an abandoned cavern a long time ago. We can get our hands on one, modify it ourselves until it can fly, and we'll be fine.”

“Good.”

Fang Heng thought the plan was workable and prepared to act immediately.

“Boss Fang, wait.” Kappa suddenly remembered something and stopped him. His eyes gleamed as he whispered, “Boss Fang, I almost forgot. This world has another very special treasure.

We have to take it with us before we leave.”

Treasure?! Fang Heng's interest was immediately aroused. Lowering his voice as well, he asked, “What treasure? Tell me more.”

“This planet is the largest of all known worlds discovered in the Seven Realms Martial Capital so far. Most of its surface is covered in ice and snow. Publicly, the Kinsfolk claim there is a special ore near the surface called Extreme Cold Purple Ore, which can maintain energy circulation at very low temperatures. That's why they send so many convicts to mine it every year.”

Fang Heng had heard of Extreme Cold Purple Ore.

It was fairly valuable, but it wasn't considered an especially rare mineral, much less some priceless treasure.

The reserves on this planet were so vast they could supply the entire star region with ease. As a result, its price had always remained relatively low.

“I only found out after arriving that the Kinsfolk aren't really interested in the purple ore. What they want are the Earth's Core Flame Veins hidden deep underground. The natural mineral extracted from those veins can sell for an astronomical price!”

“Earth's Core Flame Ore.”

Fang Heng's eyes lit up. “Are you sure?”

“Absolutely, completely sure! I was the one who surveyed and confirmed the ore deposits! Mining it is difficult, and the deeper you go, the more extreme the heat becomes. They need specially designed Kinsfolk mechs to excavate it, so production is very slow.”

Kappa nodded enthusiastically and continued, “The security teams transport the ore they've collected into storage, then move it to a refining zone for processing. Every week, a starship takes the refined ore back to the Kinsfolk.”

Fang Heng understood exactly what Kappa was suggesting.

So before leaving, they should seize all the Kinsfolk's ore, right?

Well…

He'd already offended the Kinsfolk quite thoroughly, and since he was here anyway…

Why not take everything in one fell swoop?

Fang Heng looked at Kappa. “How extensive are the mineral deposits on this planet?”

“A lot. An enormous amount!”

Kappa nodded repeatedly and lowered his voice. “This planet is huge. Just the Earth's Core Flame Veins I can sense have tremendous reserves. The amount hidden beyond the reach of my perception is impossible to estimate.”

Excellent.

Fang Heng nodded inwardly.

Might as well go all the way and seize the entire planet.

It was a Kinsfolk-controlled world, after all. He wouldn't feel the slightest guilt about taking it from them.

Korkalot still hadn't noticed that Fang Heng had arrived. He stood nearby, cold-faced, suppressing the rage in his heart.

He was an esteemed prince of the elves. When had he ever been treated like this?

If he got a chance to leave, he'd take savage revenge on the Kinsfolk!

Huh?

Hm? Korkalot suddenly sensed something and looked toward the rock.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

Several alchemical runes erupted, and a number of Lickers instantly sprang out, rapidly splitting into more creatures as they moved at high speed!

What was that?!

Excitement immediately shone in Korkalot's eyes.

Boss Fang's summoned creatures!?

There had never been an insect-creature attack in this mining district before. The convict miners panicked the instant they spotted the Lickers.

None of the miners had weapons. Naturally, they weren't about to face insect monsters with nothing but their bodies, so they dropped their tools and fled toward the exit.

There weren't many guards stationed on the ninth underground level.

Hearing shouts from deeper in the cavern, the guards turned toward the disturbance.

What the hell?!

Before they could figure out what was happening, they saw masses of miners racing out of the cavern, shouting as they ran.

Behind them came a dense swarm of insect creatures!

The ordinary security guards were far below even the level of beginner warriors. Armed only with beam weapons and advanced protective combat suits, they immediately drew their guns and opened fire when they saw the insects.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

Energy beams struck the Lickers' outer defensive skin and exploded.

No effect!

After firing several times, the security team discovered to its horror that ordinary beam weapons couldn't even pierce the swarm's outer defenses!

Damn it!

What was the point in fighting?!

The guards turned and fled, mixing into the crowd of miners.

Korkalot looked back in excitement.

Fang Heng had somehow appeared at the rear of the cavern, standing atop a rock as tiny streams of blood began seeping from the center of his forehead.

Hahaha!

It really was him!

Boss Fang had come to save them!

Korkalot was instantly invigorated. He shouted at the chaotic crowd of miners, “Damn it! All of you, shut up and crouch down! Otherwise those insects will attack you, and you'll die!”

Most of the laborers had suffered at Korkalot's hands before. Some ignored him and kept fleeing, while others obeyed, covering their heads and crouching down.

Sure enough.

The insect swarm wasn't targeting the ordinary laborers. As soon as the convicts crouched, the creatures stopped pursuing them and charged furiously at the fully armed Kinsfolk security teams instead.
