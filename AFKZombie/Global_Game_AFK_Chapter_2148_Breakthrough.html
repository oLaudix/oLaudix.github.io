Breakthrough

Quest Name: Break Through the Institute's Perimeter.

Quest Description: The Tree Spirits wish to break through the outer defenses of Wanling Technology's northern district central research institute to obtain the Niam Alloy stored within.

Quest Requirement: Assist the Tree Spirits in breaking through the institute's outer defensive line.

Quest Reward: Tree Spirit favorability, Tree Spirit faction points, and potentially subsequent quests.

Reading the notifications that appeared one after another, Fang Heng felt even more irritated.

The Tree Spirits had tricked him, yet they still had the nerve to deduct his favorability?

“Are you certain the Niam Alloy is inside the institute?”

“Yes, we've confirmed it. We previously attempted to infiltrate the institute to steal some Niam Alloy, but unfortunately our people were discovered partway through. We therefore activated our contingency measures, temporarily interfering with the nearby spatial-transition system to prevent Federation reinforcements from arriving through it.”

Senzo shook his head. “Humans depend too heavily on the transition capabilities provided by the Spirit Domain blockade. Once those transitions are disrupted, no reinforcements from the surrounding area can reach the institute within three hours.”

Fang Heng sighed inwardly.

Really, the Tree Spirits had brought this trouble upon themselves.

If they'd explained their actual objective from the beginning, they might have finished the job and left already.

Fang Heng was starting to think the Tree Spirits existed solely to make his main quest harder.

“The interference with the transition system will wear off in another hour,” Senzo continued coldly. “The institute responded more calmly to the attack than we anticipated. Moreover, some of our people were discovered during the earlier infiltration and are currently trapped inside. We must rescue them.”

Having more or less sorted out the situation, Fang Heng nodded. “I see. So right now, the main objective is to get your hands on Niam Alloy, correct?”

“My people will find a way to obtain the alloy. What we need now is to defeat the human army outside the institute.”

“Very well.”

Fang Heng shrugged. “We can cooperate, but I have a condition. This time you'll follow my arrangements. Have your people continue attacking the institute. I need a little time. In five minutes, I'll join you and we'll break through the front together.”

Senzo frowned and stared at Fang Heng for several seconds.

Just as he'd thought!

From the moment they first met, he'd sensed that Fang Heng was fully capable of breaking through the institute's outer defense network!

He had deliberately withheld his help to extract greater benefits!

Humans!

Such deceitful, cunning creatures!

Senzo's face grew even colder. He nodded. “I'm not the only one who speaks for the Tree Spirits. I'll discuss it with the others. I also hope you won't disappoint us.”

With that, Senzo turned and walked back toward the others.

Several Tree Spirits accompanying him remained behind. Standing loosely around Fang Heng with cold expressions, they appeared to be monitoring him.

[Hint: The player's favorability with the Tree Spirits -5.]

Again?

Fang Heng stared at the notification on his retina, speechless.

Which sentence had offended them this time?

Could the favorability keep dropping forever?

It seemed cooperation with the Tree Spirits was definitely going to fall apart sooner or later.

For now, he'd clear out the enemies guarding the institute, then find a way to obtain the Niam Alloy himself...

Having made up his mind, Fang Heng concentrated on the battle ahead.

The Lickers, most of which had retreated, soon began regrouping at the rear.

The treants in front, meanwhile, were being driven steadily backward by the semi-mechanized Federation and Wanling Technology troops and their beam weapons. They clearly wouldn't last much longer.

Several members of Yate's Tree Whisperer Alliance team swallowed nervously as they looked at Fang Heng.

This fellow really had guts!

He actually dared negotiate with a Tree Spirit elder like that?

So...

How was he planning to deal with so many semi-mechanized soldiers?

Fang Heng raised his head toward the vampire teleportation passage overhead.

Tall figures began stepping out, dropping heavily to the ground before slowly climbing to their feet and assembling behind him.

What were those monsters?

Sensing the unusual activity, the Tree Spirits turned toward the crimson passage.

Fusion Tyrants!

The Fusion Tyrants emerging from the passage were enormous, each standing three meters tall.

Yet because the treants were also huge, they didn't stand out quite so dramatically on the battlefield.

In a short while, several hundred Fusion Tyrants had assembled behind Fang Heng.

Rather than entering combat, they formed a line and raised their arms together.

Hiss... hiss, hiss...

What was that sound?

Yate and the nearby Tree Whisperer Alliance members heard rustling noises coming from the Fusion Tyrants and looked more closely.

What the hell!

Countless tiny larvae were emerging from beneath the Fusion Tyrants' decaying skin, dropping to the ground and wriggling rapidly away.

Worms?!

Tiny worms!

The alliance members recoiled in fright.

Even the Tree Spirits left to watch Fang Heng frowned and instinctively moved farther away from the insects.

In the blink of an eye, the ground was blanketed in a dense carpet of wriggling worm creatures!

Fang Heng raised his head toward the distant battlefield.

After acquiring the Vampire Spatial Gate skill, his zombie creatures could pass directly through the vampire passage while in vampire form.

The drawback was that they couldn't bring weapons with them.

Without their superheavy beam weapons, the Fusion Tyrants' direct combat strength was greatly reduced.

But later, following Dr. Ding Min's advice, Fang Heng had improved his tactics and fixed another five hundred Fusion Tyrants in place.

He'd left them idling in the basement of the Zombie Apocalypse world, slowly accumulating parasitic worms inside their bodies through their skill.

Whenever an emergency arose, he could pull the Fusion Tyrants directly onto the battlefield through the vampire teleportation passage!

Their main purpose wasn't to fight themselves. It was to unleash the fully accumulated swarms of worms they carried!

Wanling Technology's anti-Licker injections were certainly effective, but they also had their weaknesses.

For instance, against a battlefield carpeted in tiny worms...

Use needles?

That would hardly work, would it?
