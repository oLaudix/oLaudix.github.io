Chapter 2249

Three-Clan Alliance

From original Chinese Chapter 2250 · 联合

Outside the Prayer-Curse Temple.

Vannis led his people to the area where the Wood Clan was hiding.

The moment they stepped forward, a sudden series of whooshing sounds rang out!

What?!

Vannis's pupils contracted.

Dense wooden spikes shot from the forest ahead!

An ambush!

Anger surged through Vannis. He had intended to demand an explanation from the Wood Clan. He hadn't expected them to abandon all propriety and attack first!

The Wood Clan!

They didn't know when they were being shown mercy!

Countless razor-sharp leaves gathered around Vannis and formed a tornado that swept toward the incoming spikes.

"BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!"

The spinning leaves shredded the approaching wooden spikes, which then exploded into fragments in midair!

Vannis shattered the ambush with a single move, snorted, and shouted into the forest.

"Wood Clan! What sort of cowards hide in the bushes? Come out and speak to me!"

There was no answer.

Only more wooden spikes came flying toward him!

Hmph!

Vannis was thoroughly enraged.

He formed a hand seal before his chest.

"BOOM!"

An even denser storm of leaves rolled toward the approaching spikes!

"Very well! Don't blame me for what happens next!"

Remembering how he'd nearly lost his life in the teleportation passage, Vannis grew even angrier. Killing intent flashed in his eyes.

"Attack! Leave none alive!"

The Leaf Clan members charged into the trees, trying to flush out the hidden Wood Clan fighters.

Suddenly, several of the fighters in the front rank sensed something and froze.

"Hiss… hiss, hiss…"

Snake-like sounds came from the undergrowth.

The next second, Vannis's expression grew grave.

Beneath the plants ahead, roots hugged the ground and slithered rapidly toward their feet like snakes!

"Shhk! Shhk, shhk!"

The instant the roots touched their feet, they wrapped tightly around them!

The Root Clan!

The roots raced up the guards' ankles. Several Leaf Clan members in front were bound on the spot and couldn't move!

This was a signature Root Clan skill.

Serpent's Touch!

The other clans had imitated the technique to some extent, but none could use it nearly as effectively. Once caught in the Root Clan's version, escape was extraordinarily difficult.

"Whoosh!"

Soul Pursuit!

Vannis had just stepped forward to help his trapped clansmen when he caught a thin black flash from the right. Startled, he dodged and retreated rapidly.

He narrowly avoided the black attack. Cold sweat appeared on his forehead.

When he looked up, the clansmen bound by roots had completely stopped struggling. They stood motionless like lifeless puppets, as though their souls had been taken from them.

Soul-Pursuing Spike!

Of the four clans, the Soul Clan's techniques were the strangest.

Its direct combat strength ranked last, but its unpredictable abilities made it the clan everyone least wanted to face.

What was going on?!

Why was the Soul Clan involved too?

Only then did Vannis realize the attackers weren't just from the Wood Clan. The Root Clan and Soul Clan were here as well!

Were the other three clans ganging up on them again?

Fortunately, once he retreated, the enemies hidden in the undergrowth didn't pursue him.

"Soul Clan! Root Clan! Since when did you join forces with the Wood Clan?!"

Remaining cautiously outside the Soul Clan's attack range, Vannis glared at the forest, his expression dark and uncertain.

"Rustle… rustle…"

There was a faint movement among the trees.

A young woman emerged from the undergrowth.

Fang Heng had followed at the back of the group. He couldn't help turning his attention to her too.

The Soul Clan.

Among all the Tree Spirit clans, its members looked the most human!

"Vannis."

"Tipper."

Seeing her emerge, Vannis became extremely solemn. "What is the Soul Clan trying to do?"

"The Council of Elders ordered the clans not to harm their own kind. We also agreed on this ourselves. Yet first you cooperated with humans to seize a Wood Clan Prayer-Curse Temple, and then you killed Wood Clan members, including two elders."

Tipper shook her head. "That is something we cannot tolerate."

Vannis's pupils contracted as several thoughts flashed through his mind.

They'd killed two Wood Clan elders?

Before arriving, Vannis had asked his own Great Elder about the situation and questioned Ovi as well.

He'd only been told that a clash between the Leaf Clan and Wood Clan had led to fighting and the deaths of several Wood Clan members.

But two elders?

Was Ladiard really that ruthless?

Vannis felt a flicker of doubt, then sneered. In truth, he rather approved of Ladiard's methods.

Two elders killed?

Hah!

Then they were dead!

So what?

The Leaf Clan believed in repaying every injury!

If they were too weak to survive, their deaths were deserved!

"Hmph! We're not allowed to harm our own kind? What a joke! Now you want to invoke the Council's orders? Where were those orders before? Outside the Valley of Annual Rings, how did you three clans treat us? Do Wood Clan lives count while Leaf Clan lives mean nothing?!"

Wood Clan Elder Chrono said coldly, "Enough! Back then we deliberately held back outside the valley. We never actually killed a fellow Tree Spirit. The Wood Clan members died during the chaos at the hands of the Stone Spirit Puppets—"

"Hahahaha! What a fine excuse! You stopped us from leaving and watched our injured people die to the puppets. And you call that not harming your own kind? You call that dying in the chaos?"

Remembering that battle, Vannis's eyes reddened with fury. He laughed wildly. "Fine! Excellent! So all three of your clans insist on joining forces today? The Soul Clan and Root Clan are no better than the others! You just want to seize our Prayer-Curse Temple! Spare me the excuses. Come and try! The Leaf Clan will fight you to the end!"

Tipper stared at the visibly agitated Vannis.

"Vannis, we don't want the conflict to escalate. We can discuss this."

"Discuss? What is there to discuss?"

Vannis sneered. "First you seal off our teleportation energy and trap us here, and now you want to talk? Fine, then answer me! Where is Elder Ladiard? You invited him to a four-clan meeting today, and he still hasn't returned. Are you holding him hostage?!"

Tipper frowned deeply.

The situation was veering in a direction she didn't want.

This was bad.

The Soul Clan and Root Clan were rather more restrained than the other two clans.
