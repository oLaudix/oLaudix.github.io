Chapter 3124

Stable Structure

From Chinese Chapter 3125 · 稳固结构

After demonstrating his recent progress, Fang Heng slowly lowered his hand and continued, “I know the inheritance for the Demigod level can't be shared publicly. That's why I used the excuse of further Transcendent-level training to come and ask you for guidance.”

“All… all right…”

Mu Shunan unconsciously scratched his head again.

What kind of monster was this kid?

His alchemy had already reached the peak of the Transcendent level, and now his Occultism had reached the same stage.

And then there were the disciplines he primarily specialized in.

Those couldn't possibly be at low levels either.

“Like you, my Occultism is only at the peak of the Transcendent level. But I've spent more than ten years studying how to advance to the Demigod level, so I know some of the theory. As for exactly how to do it, I can't really say. With your talent, you might even find a better method. Just take this as something to consider.”

“All right.”

“At the Transcendent level, Occultism has already integrated the laws inside and outside the body, allowing them to interact more closely. To reach the Demigod level, we need to form a vortex within ourselves. The vortex accelerates the influx of external law power into the body, briefly disrupting the balance of laws outside. Then, through the vortex's rotation, the energy we release from within can influence those external laws.”

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes as he listened.

Another connection.

It was all about laws again.

In theory, advancing either Occultism or Alchemy to the Demigod level required influencing the laws outside the body.

Alchemy broke them apart and then reconstructed them.

Occultism likewise involved a small-scale disruption followed by exerting an influence.

The methods differed, but the intended results were similar.

Seeing Fang Heng appear to understand, Mu Shunan continued, “The so-called vortex theory is only a theory. How to apply it and control the circulation of energy within the body differs from person to person, depending on their constitution and the disciplines they've mastered. Ultimately, you must figure out your own cultivation method. Nobody else can do that part for you.”

“Mm, I understand. Cultivation is ultimately one's own responsibility.”

Fang Heng murmured to himself as he considered Mu Shunan's words and tried to sense the changes in the power within his body.

The internal and external laws had already reached a state of equilibrium.

Now he needed to shape the power inside him into a vortex, then gradually establish self-sustaining rotation.

It didn't sound particularly difficult. He understood the theory perfectly.

Let's give it a try!

[Hint: You have triggered a quest—Occultism Advancement (Demigod).]

Quest Name: Occultism Advancement (Demigod).

Quest Description: With a companion's help, you have gradually learned a method for advancing your Occultism to the next stage.

Quest Requirement: Construct an internal vortex and stabilize its circulation.

Quest Completion: 0%.

There it is!

Fang Heng glanced at the game notification.

The game's prompts were an enormous cheat even outside the game itself.

Thanks to that cheat, he could now be certain Mu Shunan's proposed path of advancement was correct.

The question was how to put it into practice!

Fang Heng immediately sat cross-legged, entered a state of internal observation, and began by trying to control the changes in the laws within his body.

First, with the laws inside and outside already in equilibrium, creating a vortex would be impossible because external laws would interfere. He therefore had to close the channels through his skin's pores, separating the internal from the external.

Only then could he begin directing the energy flowing within him.

Very quickly, Fang Heng realized the difficulty.

Because he had studied so many different disciplines, his mental powers of various attributes were incredibly mixed and disorderly. Even with his tremendous mental strength, controlling multiple kinds of energy simultaneously was troublesome.

Energy moved sluggishly through his body, not to mention the conflicts between its different forms.

If those energies clashed, he would be in serious trouble.

This wasn't easy.

After trying a few times, Fang Heng realized that creating a circulating vortex might demand considerably more effort from him than from other people.

Having grasped the problem, Fang Heng opened his eyes again and lowered his head in thought, searching for a solution.

“Not simple, is it?”

“Mm, you're right. It's a bit troublesome.”

Seeing Fang Heng stop cultivating, Mu Shunan smiled and nodded encouragingly. “That's fine. Take your time. It took me a long while to reach this point too. There are a few tricks. If you can greatly increase your mental strength and sort through, or even discard, some of your more disorderly mental powers…”

Hm?

Mu Shunan was in the middle of sharing the experience he'd accumulated over more than ten years when he noticed something wasn't right. He looked at Fang Heng, who had already closed his eyes to cultivate again, and horror appeared in his gaze.

What the hell?!

Something was wrong with this kid!

The faint energies moving through the surroundings were accelerating as they poured into Fang Heng's body.

That was clearly the state reached after an internal vortex had formed and begun communicating with the outside world to establish equilibrium.

What?!

Did that mean he'd already finished constructing his internal vortex?

Mu Shunan stared at Fang Heng as though he'd seen a ghost.

Was this kid deliberately making a fool of him?

At that moment, Fang Heng was controlling the newly formed vortex inside his body.

Only now did he truly understand why Occultism was said to be the foundation of most academic disciplines.

Advancing in Occultism also involved the laws.

Once the internal vortex took shape and he reopened the passages connecting his body to the outside, any external law power entering him was absorbed by the balanced vortex. It kept the laws within his body in an extraordinarily stable state.

It could even absorb external energy for his own use!

Energy from outside was gradually flowing into his body!

In other words, once the vortex formed, it would continuously draw in external power and strengthen his various abilities, even if he did nothing.

Although the rate was extremely slow.

If he cultivated somewhere saturated with Abe Akaya's natural power, combined it with the Transcendent-level Occultism technique of opening his pores to absorb external energy, and then drove the completed vortex at full strength, his rate of absorbing external natural power would undergo another qualitative leap!

He couldn't test it yet, but Fang Heng could feel that his current cultivation speed was at least ten times what it had been with Transcendent-level Occultism!

In addition, when casting certain spells, mental energy compressed and circulated through his internal vortex would receive a further enhancement from external law power as it was released.

Together, those two effects offered roughly a two-to-threefold boost!

The predecessor who'd invented the internal vortex technique must have been a genius!

Fang Heng was secretly astonished.

Then a game notification appeared on his retina.

[Hint: Quest completion has reached 100%. You have forgotten the skill Occultism (Transcendent) and acquired the skill Occultism (Demigod).]

Success!

It had taken even less time than he'd expected!

And the reason he'd managed it so quickly was simple.

Besides the extensive experience he'd gained studying the laws in the cavern, his greatest asset had actually been the power of his bloodline!

The diverse energies in his body were too chaotic to control?

No problem!

He had the Titan bloodline's special ability!

Convert them directly!

All the chaotic energies inside his body had now been uniformly transformed into 'Qi'.
