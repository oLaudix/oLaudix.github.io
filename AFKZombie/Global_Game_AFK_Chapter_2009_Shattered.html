Chapter 2009

Shattered

From original Chinese Chapter 2010 · 破碎

A surge of sacred power!

Someone from the Holy Court?!

Who was he?!

No—surely he wasn't going to…!

Ji Shaojie watched Fang Heng grip the massive sacred sword in both hands and prepare to bring it down.

"DON'T!"

Ji Shaojie seemed to see what was about to happen. He couldn't help shouting at the top of his lungs.

Whoosh!

"Sever!" Fang Heng roared.

A golden arc swept through the air as the sacred greatsword descended.

BOOM!

With a deafening explosion, the enormous Dragon Soul Crystal shattered before everyone's eyes. The sword's impact broke the crystal into countless fragments that flew in every direction.

Hum!

In the next instant, a sacred ripple burst from the point of impact and spread rapidly outward. The sacred ripple effect attached to the greatsword had activated!

Bang! Bang, bang!

The wave struck the flying fragments, blasting them apart again into even smaller pieces and fine dust.

For a moment, glittering specks of red dust filled the players' vision. Everyone was stunned.

"You actually dared to—!"

No one had imagined Fang Heng would dare destroy the entire Dragon Soul Crystal right in front of them, much less that he would do it before anyone could stop him.

Gone. It was all gone!

Without the Dragon Soul Crystal, their hopes of advancing their dragon-descendant bloodlines had been completely cut off. Years of effort had been ruined in an instant. It happened so suddenly that their minds went blank.

Meanwhile, a fine layer of powder from the shattered crystal settled onto Fang Heng's body.

[Hint: Player has entered the Dragon Soul Crystal Burning state…]

[Hint: This item cannot be effectively absorbed. Absorption rate: 0.01%.]

As the powder on his skin was absorbed, Fang Heng felt a warm current flow into his body.

Huh?!

That strange sensation…

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow. The Fox Hunting Squad under Miyamoto Teru immediately came to mind.

Of course! They must have experienced something similar!

In the next moment, his blood vitality surged, and a powerful force welled up from within him.

[Hint: Under the influence of an unknown force, all of the player's attributes have increased slightly and fluctuated. Specific increases cannot be determined due to missing information…]

So that was how it worked…

The game notification gave Fang Heng an idea. In that instant, he changed his original plan to withdraw.

Perhaps… this was an opportunity. Something he could use!

The agitation in his blood was quickly suppressed, leaving his mind perfectly clear. His figure faded into the air.

Secondary Space Projection!

A short distance away, Ji Shaojie frowned when he realized that Fang Heng had immediately used a secondary-space ability after breaking the crystal.

This was going to be troublesome.

His own skill involved some manipulation of spatial power, so he was more sensitive to it than ordinary players. Even so, he was nowhere near capable of entering a secondary spatial projection himself.

Catching someone proficient in spatial studies would be extraordinarily difficult!

And why had that man destroyed the Dragon Soul Crystal in front of them in the first place?

Before Ji Shaojie could figure it out, another player shot past him toward a large piece of the crystal falling from the air.

The player caught the fragment and stuffed it into his bag, delight flashing across his face.

The others suddenly understood.

The Dragon Soul Crystal had already been broken. Forget about Fang Heng, who had escaped! The sensible thing was to salvage whatever benefits remained!

With someone taking the lead, the other players scattered and rushed toward the fragments lying all around them.

Even broken, these were still pieces of an artifact-grade treasure!

Moreover, the backlash associated with the original Dragon Soul Crystal had been greatly weakened by its fragmentation.

No one paid further attention to Fang Heng's escape. They were all scrambling to seize whatever pieces they could find.

"Stop! The Dragon Soul Crystal belongs to our research institute!"

The Fox Hunting Squad members serving Miyamoto Teru were aghast. When they saw they couldn't stop the other players from looting the fragments, they joined the scramble themselves.

In moments, the twenty-odd fist-sized pieces had been divided among the crowd.

The crystal's lingering aura stirred everyone's blood. Their breathing quickened and their emotions grew unstable, making them more irritable and aggressive.

The players had never been particularly friendly with one another, and now the tension rose again. They formed several small groups and confronted each other warily. Miyamoto Teru's Fox Hunting Squad had the largest numbers and was among the strongest.

"Everyone, the Dragon Soul Crystal belongs to our research institute. Broken or not, it still belongs to us. You can't just take it away like this, can you?"

"Yours? Like hell! I picked it up, so it's mine!"

"Exactly! You claim this belongs to you? Where's your proof? This stone is clearly a family heirloom! Trying to swindle me, are you? You're nothing but Miyamoto Teru's dog! Who do you think you are?"

Standing nearby, Ji Shaojie listened to their curses. He could feel his own blood boiling, and his expression darkened.

He knew the crystal's scattered aura was influencing his emotions to some degree.

Taking a deep breath, he surveyed the wary groups.

He wasn't one of Miyamoto Teru's subordinates. Their relationship was largely one of mutual exploitation.

During the chaos, he'd managed to claim three Dragon Soul Crystal fragments of different sizes. Carrying even those three placed a considerable burden on his body.

But with three pieces in hand, there might still be a way forward.

"Let's go!"

Ji Shaojie glanced at the portal behind him and hurried toward it with two teammates.

Several other players noticed and stopped arguing with the Fox Hunting Squad, rushing after him.

He was right. This wasn't a place to linger. The passage was reportedly unstable and could fail without warning. Leaving first was the safest choice!

Quite a few players remained, however, hoping to gather more fragments.

The large pieces had all been claimed, but there were still plenty of smaller ones. How could they leave before picking everything up? If they couldn't get a feast, surely they could at least have the scraps!

The Fox Hunting Squad's expressions changed when they saw what was happening. They might have been able to stop the others, but they had no chance of holding back Ji Shaojie and his team by force.

Ji Shaojie had always acted cautiously.

As his group approached the passage entrance to leave, he suddenly stopped and looked up.

A faint ripple appeared in the space ahead of them.

Fang Heng stepped out of the secondary spatial projection and stared at them coldly.

"Leaving so soon?"

— End of Chapter 2009 —
