Chapter 3127

Breaking Through

From Chinese Chapter 3128 · 破除

Anxi stayed hidden inside the car at a distance. She'd tried to contact the outside world immediately, but interference from an external magnetic field made her phone useless. All she could do was huddle in the driver's seat, anxiously watching Fang Heng confront the five warriors sent by the Kinsfolk on his own.

That man was Carlton!

Anxi's heart jolted when his face was exposed.

A high-tier warrior recruited by the Kinsfolk!

A member of the Crystal Clan!

The Crystal Clan had very few members, with dimensional levels ranging from 20 to 35. Besides their relatively high dimensional level, they possessed an extraordinarily powerful racial crystallization ability.

Carlton was one of the Crystal Clan's most prominent representatives. He'd won many battles throughout the Seven Realms Martial Capital and ranked among its finest high-tier warriors.

The others seemed to be experts from the Crystal Clan as well.

What made the Crystal Clan terrifying was their ability to instantly crystallize their bodies, gaining incredibly strong defenses. They could also blend elemental abilities into the crystals, producing combined effects far greater than the sum of their parts.

They excelled both offensively and defensively.

Among the Seven Realms Martial Capital's elite warriors, academic mastery was generally quite similar. The factor that truly separated them was racial talent.

Anxi's heart pounded wildly.

This was going to be trouble.

Surprise also appeared on Fang Heng's face. His punches now carried terrifying force, and he'd expected that one blow would shatter his opponent completely. Instead, it felt like he'd struck an exceptionally hard alloy.

Heh…

He'd still underestimated the Seven Realms Martial Capital.

Carlton was even more astonished.

No one had ever sent him flying through sheer physical strength before.

After that single strike, cracks had spread across his lower abdomen. He'd almost been shattered.

Thanks to his racial talent, the damaged crystalline body rapidly healed.

Still shaken, Carlton suddenly felt a frightening aura erupt from Fang Heng. His brows shot up and he shouted, “Watch out!”

The Crystal Clan warriors looked up. An extraordinarily dense aura of death burst from Fang Heng, and twenty-four Netherworld Orbs emerged from his body, spinning rapidly around him!

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

After circling dozens of times in midair, the Netherworld Orbs shot toward the surrounding Crystal Clan warriors!

Seeing the black spheres approach, the warriors didn't dare take them lightly.

They cast elemental spells to intercept them.

Elemental—Crystal Barrier! They combined their racial crystallization talent with ice elemental power, forming a brilliant crystalline barrier before themselves!

It was dozens of times more resilient than an ordinary ice-crystal barrier!

“BOOM!!!”

The Netherworld Orbs struck the crystalline ice and exploded!

Thick black mist burst outward!

The concentrated eruption of death power instantly blasted a black hole through the crystal barrier!

What terrifying strength!

The warrior directly in the path of a Netherworld Orb looked horrified.

The Crystal Barrier had been broken in a single strike?

At almost the same moment, the undead's sinister mist rapidly spread outward from the point where the death aura had erupted.

All five warriors simultaneously felt their hearts tighten.

Within the sinister undead mist, they suddenly sensed something wrong with their blood.

It began surging faster and faster beyond their control.

Whoosh! Fang Heng's figure flashed through the dense undead mist and appeared before Carlton.

One punch!

Carlton's pupils contracted. He hastily crystallized the part of his body the fist was about to hit.

“BOOM!!!”

Carlton took another direct punch to the chest, spat out a mouthful of blood, and was flung violently backward.

His chest felt unbearably tight as he hurtled through the air and crashed heavily to the ground.

He looked down, and his expression changed dramatically.

As before, countless fine cracks had appeared in the crystallized area struck by the fist. But this time there was something different: a wound was forming there, continually corroded by black energy!

The black aura carried corrosion, aging, weakening, and other negative effects. It spread along the cracks in the crystal, preventing the damaged structure from healing.

Not only couldn't it heal—the wound was actually continuing to spread!

What ability was this?

Carlton looked up at the curtain of sinister undead mist before him, his heart filled with dread.

“Whoosh!!!”

Fang Heng had single-handedly torn an opening through the encirclement formed by the five warriors.

It's over! The mission has failed!

And Fang Heng wasn't even leaving!

Could he be…

Cold sweat beaded on Carlton's forehead.

Did this kid have no intention of letting them go?

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

Muffled explosions rang repeatedly from the area shrouded in sinister undead mist.

The Crystal Clan warriors couldn't locate Fang Heng within the mist. Panicking, they resorted to defensive abilities to protect themselves, but whenever they raised a Crystal Barrier, an incoming black Netherworld Orb instantly smashed it apart.

They failed to notice that the more frightened they became, the faster their blood circulated. It had accelerated so much that it was beginning to disrupt their internal energy.

“Whoosh!!”

Fang Heng emerged from the undead mist like a ghost and drove a fist forward!

A straightforward, head-on punch!

With his raw physical attributes and the undead element's many negative effects, he smashed through the Crystal Clan's defenses again and again!

“BOOM!!!!”

The Crystal Clan's bodily defenses, seemingly invincible to outsiders, were like paper before Fang Heng. A single punch sent a warrior flying, and dense cracks spread over his fully crystallized body.

Black undead energy surged beneath those cracks!

One blow was enough to inflict a grievous injury.

“Pfft!!!”

The Crystal Clan warrior was sent flying by Fang Heng's attack. He had barely pushed himself off the ground when he spat another mouthful of blood, unable to regain control of his energy.

What was happening?

What was wrong with his body?

Sweat covered the Crystal Clan warrior's forehead.

Less than half a minute into the battle, everyone realized something was terribly wrong. Their bodies seemed to be under assault from two different forces, inside and out. Their blood boiled and burned with unnatural speed, while external death energy gnawed into them through the cracks in their crystals.

The warrior could no longer withstand both assaults at once and rapidly withdrew from his crystallized state.

“BOOM!!”

The moment his crystalline defense vanished, his body could no longer endure the terrifying damage. It exploded into pieces before the eyes of the other four Crystal Clan warriors!

The survivors were visibly horrified.

Dead?!

All of them now felt the threat of death firsthand. Looking toward the mass of black undead mist, they were filled with fear and wariness.
