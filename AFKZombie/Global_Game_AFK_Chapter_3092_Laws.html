Chapter 3092

Laws

From Chinese Chapter 3093 · 法则

He could hold it for at least another week, but this couldn't be a permanent solution...

Fang Heng raised his hand.

The God-Given Scepter appeared in his palm.

“Sacred Purification!”

“Hum...”

Dark-golden sacred light flared and quickly spread through the basement.

“Hiss, hiss...”

The dense Black Mist hissed under the sacred fluctuations, but none of it dispersed.

The result was the same no matter how many times he tried.

Sacred purification had no effect on Black Mist.

Nor was it just sacred power. Fang Heng had repeatedly tried natural studies, spatial studies, elemental studies, and all sorts of other methods. Nothing could effectively dispel the mist.

What could he do?

After getting used to AFK grinding, suddenly being unable to use it would be a real headache.

As Fang Heng fretted, his phone rang.

“Mr. Fang Heng,”

Anxi said over the phone, “Instructor Nasir has some free time this afternoon. I've arranged a private alchemy lesson for you and am already on my way to pick you up. Would half an hour from now be convenient?”

“Mm. All right.”

Fang Heng agreed and took one final look around the seventh underground floor, shrouded in Black Mist.

He could be sure the mist was connected to the game.

Among the races of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, only the Kinsfolk seemed particularly frightened of it. The other six great factions were less concerned.

Perhaps he could discreetly question some other Noel Clan warriors and find clues.

...

Noel Clan headquarters.

The recent attack on the Kinsfolk hadn't caused much disturbance within the Noel Clan. Training and arena matches continued as usual.

When Nasir heard Fang Heng had booked another alchemy lesson, he thought little of it, assuming the youngster had encountered some cultivation difficulty.

Seeing him arrive, he invited him to sit.

“Fang Heng, you're here. Have a seat.”

Fang Heng sat opposite him and nodded. “Nasir, thank you for your guidance last time. I studied diligently after returning home and finally made a breakthrough. I've now reached the bottleneck before the Transcendent level.”

“Pfft!!”

Nasir had just raised his teacup and nearly sprayed tea from his mouth.

“What did you just say? You've hit another bottleneck already?”

He stared at Fang Heng as though he'd seen a ghost.

How long had it been?

Advancing through Grandmaster level so quickly might be explainable. Perhaps Fang Heng had accumulated a foundation but lacked the correct method, so a small hint had allowed everything to fall into place.

But he'd already reached the Transcendent bottleneck too?

Noticing Nasir's astonishment, Fang Heng guessed what he was thinking. He extended his hand and traced a gentle arc through the air.

An alchemical magic array condensed in the void.

Then again, without his zombie clones' help, he couldn't possibly have maxed out Grandmaster alchemy so quickly.

Seeing that display, Nasir's eyes nearly popped from their sockets.

Damn!

It was real!

How had he done it?

Only a few days had passed, but his technique looked as natural and effortless as if it had been polished through countless repetitions.

There were no shortcuts in alchemy. No matter how talented someone was, they still had to practice. An ordinary person couldn't achieve this degree of mastery without three to five years of work.

“Fine.”

It took Nasir a while to recover, and a bitter smile appeared on his face.

He himself had needed more than ten years of relentless cultivation to barely break through into the Transcendent level.

“Hmm...”

Nasir gathered his thoughts and asked, “Fang Heng, most warriors study alchemy as a supporting discipline. I don't know your achievements in other fields, or whether you have any understanding of 'laws.'”

Apart from necromancy, Fang Heng's highest-ranked discipline was Demi-God-level sealing studies.

But none of his fields seemed to involve “laws.”

“I haven't heard of them.”

“Mm. When many disciplines reach the Transcendent realm, they begin touching upon laws to varying degrees.”

“In the broadest sense, 'laws' are the rules governing how a world operates. At a smaller scale, you might understand them as the effects of the surrounding environment. Take alchemy: advancing to the Transcendent level means being able to influence the laws within a certain area through alchemical techniques.”

Fang Heng frowned. “Sorry, it's hard to understand from the explanation alone. Could you give an example?”

“Certainly. Watch.”

Nasir extended a finger and quickly condensed a magic array in midair.

“Whoosh!”

A flame sprang from the alchemical array.

“This is a basic fire-element array.”

Nasir stood and walked to the far corner of the room.

A large, continuously operating alchemical array had been arranged on the floor there.

Nasir stopped in its center, raised his right hand again, and condensed another array in midair.

“Whoosh!”

It was the same kind of fire-element array.

A flame ignited at its center.

“Perceive them carefully. See whether you can detect the difference.”

Fang Heng stared at the two operating arrays and expanded his perception.

They were clearly identical magic arrays.

If he had to name a difference...

He looked up. “The strength of the flames?”

“Correct. There's a slight difference in flame strength.”

Nasir explained calmly, “An identical fire array can produce different results in different environments. Its effect is influenced by an external force. We call that influence a law.”

Fang Heng frowned and tried to grasp the concept.

Some force...

The environment...

Laws...

Why did that sound a little like...

A domain?

Fang Heng raised his head. “Nasir, have you heard of domains?”

“Oh?” Nasir's eyes lit up. “So you know of domains! That makes it much easier to explain. A domain can be described as an area affected by a series of strengthened laws.”

“At the current Transcendent stage of alchemy, we need to use alchemical arrays to influence some of the laws within an area.”

Fang Heng looked at the array beneath Nasir's feet. “By relying on alchemical magic arrays?”

“Exactly.”

Nasir grew serious. “Fang Heng, don't be fooled by how simple this array looks. Setting it up took me considerable effort. First you must establish the surrounding environmental laws, then simulate them effectively, predict the results of modifications, and consider compatibility. One careless change can cause a conflict between the entire set of laws...”

Fang Heng considered this, then asked, “If a domain represents a more advanced change to laws, does that mean that if the laws surrounding us were altered, the alchemical array under your feet would cease to function entirely?”
