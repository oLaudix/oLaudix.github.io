Chapter 2082

Being Watched

From original Chinese Chapter 2083 · 窥视

Inside the rock face, Fang Heng immediately left his secondary-space projection.

The walls of the passage were also covered in Hellish Evil Eyes.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

They all opened at once, launching purple beams and illusion attacks at him.

"Hah!"

Fang Heng drew his sacred scepter and counterattacked!

The cramped tunnel held far fewer Evil Eyes than the cliff outside, greatly reducing the number of beams striking him at once.

With the damage reduction from his Undying Body, he could withstand their attacks without much trouble.

He endured the volleys and swung the scepter at the open eyes lining the passage.

Bang!

Melee attacks dealt extra damage when an eye was open. One blow burst an eyeball, spraying foul-smelling fluid everywhere.

[Hint: You struck a Hellish Evil Eye's weak point, dealing critical damage...]

[Hint: You have killed a Hellish Evil Eye. Its corpse may be harvested for eyeballs to complete the associated quest.]

What a nuisance.

Fang Heng glanced at the notification. He had no particular interest in completing the emergency trial quest, so he couldn't be bothered to waste time harvesting the corpses.

Instead, he continued deeper, swinging his scepter to thin the Evil Eyes as he went.

Gradually, he noticed that fewer and fewer were attached to the walls.

After traveling more than two hundred meters through the winding tunnel, he could no longer see any Evil Eyes at all.

The trial quest notification hadn't changed; it still showed the objective of clearing the creatures on the previous level.

So what was this place...?

Fang Heng frowned and surveyed his surroundings.

His senses told him he had been descending farther underground all this time. That meant his chances of finding a surface teleportation passage were getting slimmer.

Should he simply break an opening through the rock and climb up?

If he had his zombie clones, it might be worth trying. But he couldn't use them right now, and his Crusader Mark was still active.

He wasn't willing to risk exposing himself by doing something too reckless.

With no better choice, he kept exploring.

Huh?

An energy fluctuation?

As he moved forward, he sensed a faint, almost imperceptible fluctuation lingering somewhere farther down the passage.

He couldn't determine what kind of energy it was, but it felt unmistakably familiar.

He had probably encountered it before.

Strange. What was it...?

While he was puzzling over that, his brow lifted.

Someone was here!

He could sense someone watching him from the shadows.

More than one person!

They had concealed their auras well, but they couldn't hide the fluctuations of their blood and vitality.

Players?

There were subtle differences between the blood-and-vitality fluctuations of players and NPCs. Fang Heng could only make a rough judgment, not identify them with absolute certainty.

Still, they felt very much like players.

Why would players be here? And why were they spying on him?

Unless...

A thought occurred to Fang Heng.

Could they belong to an opposing faction?

He deliberately pretended not to notice the watchers and continued forward.

Not far ahead, around a bend in the passage, a five-person player team concealed themselves among the jagged rocks.

They were observing Fang Heng in secret.

"He's with the Holy Court," one whispered.

"Yes. But the area outside is full of Hellish Evil Eyes. How did he get in?"

"No idea. His aura doesn't seem particularly strong."

"What should we do?"

"Our mission is to protect the altar and kill as many Crusaders as possible. If we let him keep exploring, he might interfere with our plan."

Their leader, Meng Si, considered for a moment, then nodded. "We can't let him come any closer. Set an ambush and eliminate him. Try not to make too much noise."

"Understood."

The others nodded and spread out into hiding positions.

Meng Si's five-person team had accepted this quest more than half a month ago, and they'd spent the entire time preparing. They'd been waiting for the Holy Court to arrive today.

What they hadn't expected was for Fang Heng to break through the Evil Eyes' blockade all by himself.

The five players took positions in five corners and waited for him to enter their encirclement.

Tap...

Fang Heng stepped into the trap, seemingly unaware.

Meng Si raised his eyebrows and shouted, "Now! Attack!"

Whoosh!

Five figures sprang from concealment and lunged at Fang Heng together.

Huh?

Halfway through his charge, Meng Si felt a sudden jolt of unease.

An ordinary person caught in an ambush would at least show surprise, or immediately try to dodge, flee, or retaliate.

But this man wasn't startled at all. He stood perfectly still, the faintest trace of contempt on his lips, as if he'd known they were waiting there all along!

Damn it!

Something was wrong!

Whoosh!

A golden sacred scepter suddenly came straight toward Meng Si's face.

What the hell?!

Startled, he hurriedly raised his saber to block.

BOOM!

Holy power and hellish power collided and exploded. The force sent Meng Si flying backward.

Attacks from the other four directions struck Fang Heng in quick succession, and his assailants' expressions changed dramatically.

What was going on?

He wasn't even trying to defend himself!

Their swords cut several wounds across his body. Flames exploded against him and left scorched patches of skin.

Then they witnessed his terrifying regenerative ability.

The cuts and burns began closing before their very eyes!

Not good!

"Something's wrong! Retreat!"

Meng Si shouted as he slammed into the enormous rock wall behind him. He scrambled to his feet, endured the sharp pain, and fled into the shadows.

The other players also withdrew immediately after their first strikes.

Fang Heng frowned as he watched them flee.

That brief exchange was enough for him to judge that they were quite capable—roughly on the level of top-tier players from an intermediate game world.

Judging solely by their base attributes, they might even be a little stronger than Lindro's party.

They'd attacked once and retreated decisively, leaving him no chance to pursue.

But his vampire blood-sense had already fixed their positions.

They hadn't fled far. They were still concealed nearby.

After being sent flying by Fang Heng's scepter, Meng Si rubbed his still-numb wrist, shaken by what had happened.

The other team members looked equally grave.

That first probe had left them with an unsettling impression: none of their attacks on Fang Heng seemed to have had any effect at all.

"Is he a player?" someone asked.

Meng Si recalled the exchange and shook his head. "I can't tell. But he's extremely strong, and he didn't even use a skill. He simply overwhelmed me with his raw attributes. I'm no match for him."
