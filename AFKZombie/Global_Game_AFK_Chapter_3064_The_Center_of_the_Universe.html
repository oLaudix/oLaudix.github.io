Chapter 3064

The Center of the Universe

From Chinese Chapter 3065 · 宇宙中心

Three months after the Western Federation's Star Map Project was completely suspended.

The human world remained peaceful.

Rumors circulated outside that the Shadow Clan alliance had mysteriously perished while preparing to invade humanity. Several smaller fleets later attempted attacks of their own, only to fall silent as well.

They were supposedly struck by some unknown force.

Every fleet that accepted a Kinsfolk-related mission to attack humanity vanished without a trace.

Naturally, no one dared accept those missions afterward.

The Kinsfolk weren't offering much in reward anyway. Considering the risks, who would be willing to take the job?

“Boss Fang, thanks to your help, we managed to collect those Grandmaster-level mysticism materials. It took an enormous effort, but we've arranged to have them delivered. They couldn't find you when they came, so we asked Mr. Mo to pass them on. Please check when you get a chance.”

Every few days, Chen Yu contacted Fang Heng on behalf of the Eastern Federation to express his gratitude and report recent developments. More importantly, he had been ordered by the Federation's senior leaders to discreetly inquire about Fang Heng's whereabouts.

Boss Fang had disappeared again these past few days.

No matter where they looked, no one could find him or learn where he had gone.

More troubling still, reports from the outside were gradually suggesting that the Kinsfolk had grown increasingly dissatisfied with humanity's actions and might be preparing to use their ultimate military force.

Only days earlier, humanity had received a final warning of extermination from the Kinsfolk.

Barring extraordinary circumstances, the Kinsfolk would not enter worlds connected to the Black Mist or the Ouroboros Seed in person. But that didn't mean they had no way to attack humanity.

For example, they possessed long-range, star-system-scale weapons of destruction.

It was said that a single shot could annihilate an entire world.

Of course, the Kinsfolk would pay a considerable price to do so.

They used this threat to demand that humanity restart the Star Map Project and complete its second phase within a set deadline.

The humans had no idea whether the threat was merely a bluff, but they dared not gamble.

What if it wasn't?

The Human Federation attempted to visit Fang Heng directly, but found no one. They learned that he had left several days earlier.

So Chen Yu called to check on him and discuss the problem posed by the Kinsfolk.

“Huh?”

Hearing where Fang Heng had gone, Chen Yu froze. “Where? The Seven-Realm Martial Capital?”

“That's right, the Seven-Realm Martial Capital,” Fang Heng replied calmly over the phone. “I received the advancement books. Thank you. Mm, I've heard about the Kinsfolk as well, but you don't need to worry too much. Interstellar high-grade beam cannons consume an enormous amount of energy. We don't think they'd go to all that trouble over a little planet like ours.”

Fang Heng shrugged and continued, “Still, we can't afford to be careless.”

“Mm. You're right, Boss Fang. That's what the federations think too. The world faces an unprecedented challenge. If we want to survive, we'll have to rely on ourselves.”

“We've launched the Black Mist Project together with the regional federations. Besides relocating some people into other worlds, we've begun trying to establish permanent bases in Black Mist regions and bringing some players into the mist.”

Currently, in addition to building defensive facilities and rapidly improving their strength through the game, the federations were orderly preparing two methods of evacuation.

If the worst came to pass, humanity would try to preserve the seed of its civilization.

The first approach was to hide completely inside the Black Mist. Even if their entire world were destroyed, some players could remain within the mist and avoid the outside devastation.

The other was to carry refugees aboard starships to settle in other worlds.

Their world hadn't yet been targeted by a stellar-class weapon, so neither escape route had been used. Preparations were nevertheless complete.

As Chen Yu spoke with Fang Heng, he accepted a report handed over by his assistant and skimmed the information on the Seven-Realm Martial Capital. Most of it was blank.

“Boss Fang, we have very little information about the Seven-Realm Martial Capital. You're going there to...?”

“If I had to put it simply, I'm going to resolve our Kinsfolk problem—and to learn,” Fang Heng replied with a shrug. “The Kinsfolk aren't the only highly intelligent species in the universe, and they certainly aren't the only powerful cosmic overlords.”

Chen Yu's heart stirred.

Was Boss Fang making another move?

“Do you have another plan, Boss Fang?”

“Mm.”

The elves' ancient heritage was not much younger than humanity's. More importantly, unlike humanity, their legacy had never been completely severed, so their body of knowledge was far better preserved.

That was why Fang Heng had obtained so much information from the elves.

“At present, the seven greatest overlord factions in the universe—including the Kinsfolk—jointly determine its future course and establish its rules.”

“Under those rules, the human world's territory falls within the Kinsfolk's designated sphere of influence.”

That was the general arrangement, though not an absolute one.

Many powers lay hidden in every corner of the cosmos. Less than one percent of the universe had been explored, and the forces concealed in the darkness were beyond counting.

The Ouroboros Seed, for example, had once suppressed all seven great races by itself, becoming a thorn in the side of each overlord.

“Simply put, the Seven-Realm Martial Capital is the highest authority in the known universe. The great overlords conduct their affairs there. The various species call it the Cosmic Administrative Office.”

“Our human world has never been registered with the cosmic authorities. It's classified as an undiscovered world without higher intelligence. In their eyes, we're probably no different from cattle or sheep.”

“Because of that classification, the Kinsfolk could wipe us out without violating either the letter or the spirit of their laws.”

“So on this trip I'll try to register the human world at the Seven-Realm Martial Capital. Once we're officially recognized as a low-tier world, an extermination attack by the Kinsfolk would violate rules jointly established by the seven overlord alliances. The other overlords would have grounds to oppose them, and we'd have the right to file a protest.”

So that was possible?

The assistant beside Chen Yu brightened immediately and began writing furiously, recording every word Fang Heng said.

In truth, even minor species like the Iron-Eating Beasts and Flying Squirrels knew nothing about this. Only the elves had some knowledge. The moment they informed Fang Heng, he set his plan in motion.

“As for the other reason, that's cultivation...”

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes slightly.

He still wasn't strong enough.

At the very least, Fang Heng could still see a great deal of room for improving himself.
