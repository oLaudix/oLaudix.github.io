Chapter 3062

Desperate Gamble

From Chinese Chapter 3063 · 孤注一掷

Once the various species in the Kinsfolk Alliance discovered they had lost all communication with their ships, they became even more uneasy and hastened their return.

When the Shadow Clan alliance finally reached the outskirts of the fleet's anchorage and saw the scene in the distance, astonishment spread across every face.

Korkalot's eyes also filled with shock.

What the hell?

Was that an Ouroboros nest?

From where everyone stood, the entire fleet assembly area had been blanketed by overlapping layers of dark-red vines.

An endless mass of tangled tendrils.

The ships were wrapped like cocoons, clustered together like fruit growing from the roots of an enormous vine.

Looking closer, one could even see dark-yellow pupils opening here and there along the tendrils.

These rootlike vines were no ordinary plants; they resembled a fusion of living creatures and vegetation.

Was this some special form of the Ouroboros Seed?

What the hell was going on?!

They had been gone for less than an hour. How had their ships ended up like this?

An oppressive atmosphere settled over the entire allied force.

“Impossible!”

The Shadow Clan commander looked utterly grim.

They had never encountered an Ouroboros Seed in such a form.

Korkalot stood rooted to the spot, gazing at the plants swallowing the ships in the distance, his eyes full of disbelief.

He could sense the Tree of Life's power within those plants!

Was that the Tree of Life belonging to their Elven Clan?

Since when had the Tree of Life taken on such a grotesque form? And since when could it leave the elven world and fight elsewhere?

“My lord, we...”

The allied squads were growing frantic, and their gazes all turned toward the Shadow Clan.

The situation was dire.

From the looks of things, every one of their ships might already have fallen completely to those bizarre vines.

And without their ships, they could be stranded in this world for a very long time.

If the insects attacked again during that time...

“Commander, the Ouroboros Seed only just took over the ships. It hasn't completely corroded them yet, so the damage is limited. If we can recapture them now, there's still a chance...”

“Mm.”

The Shadow Clan commander stared at the living sea of vines, anger flickering in his eyes. “You're right. All squads, listen up! Prepare to attack and retake the ships!”

“Yes!”

Every unit was strung as tight as a bowstring.

They understood that the situation had become critical. If they couldn't take back their ships, they might all die here!

“Fire! Adjust your power. Don't damage the ships!”

The squads advanced slowly until the vessels were within their long-range attack distance.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

Long-range spells and energy beams swept over the vines covering the ships.

Afraid of damaging the hulls beneath the plants, the units deliberately held back their firepower.

But the first volley was completely absorbed by the thick layers of vines.

The squad commander flushed, feeling like an utter fool.

He had actually worried about holding back the attack, when they couldn't even breach the enemy's defenses!

Just as he prepared to order an all-out assault, a rustling sound suddenly came from ahead.

Looking up, he saw alien insects crawling out of the tangled vines wrapped around the vessels.

The insects clung tightly to the vine roots and settled into firing positions.

The commander's eyelid twitched.

Don't tell me... Could it be...?

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!”

Concentrated biological artillery!

In the next instant, a dense barrage of crimson biological cannon fire roared toward the Kinsfolk Alliance troops!

“Watch out!”

“Scatter!”

“Defensive barriers!”

Almost simultaneously, the ground beneath the Kinsfolk Alliance forces heaved violently!

Abe Akaya revealed its monstrous, terrifying side!

Countless vine roots burst out of the earth and plunged straight into the battlefield.

The roots whipped through the air while Lickers clinging to them fired continuous long-range artillery barrages!

More Lickers climbed straight up the vines from underground and leaped into battle, throwing the Shadow Clan alliance into utter chaos in moments!

Rampaging insects and masses of vines.

Every unit in the Shadow Clan alliance was plunged into disarray.

Korkalot's heart hammered in his chest. Watching the scene, he involuntarily backed away several steps.

The deadliest force on the battlefield was not the insects, but the dark-red vines radiating the aura of the Tree of Life!

A single strike from those vines inflicted multiple negative status effects. Often, a victim would be distracted for a heartbeat, only for the thorns piercing their body to suck them dry, leaving nothing but bones.

Anyone unlucky enough to become entangled in the tendrils crawling over the ground would be dragged deep underground and devoured entirely.

As Korkalot watched the battle, a powerful feeling of relief suddenly welled up inside him.

Thank goodness he hadn't offended Fang Heng earlier.

He hadn't expected Fang Heng to have yet another trump card—and one connected to the Elven Clan's Tree of Life, no less!

But...

When had the elves' Tree of Life become this powerful?

With the Tree of Life and the alien insects working together, the Shadow Clan quickly found itself at a disadvantage.

Korkalot glanced uneasily toward the Shadow Clan warriors at the center of the formation.

Relying on their innate racial strength, the Shadow Clan held the front line and bore the brunt of the pressure.

Yet they had begun to realize that even though they possessed the means to kill the insects permanently, they had no way to deal with the ships buried beneath those densely layered vines!

There was nowhere to even begin!

“Hold formation! Don't panic! We still have a chance!”

Amid the chaos, the Shadow Clan commander kept issuing orders to adjust their formation, but his unease only deepened.

This couldn't continue!

Their entire force would die here.

They had never seen an Ouroboros Seed like the one in this world. It could fuse with mysterious plants, and its strength might already exceed the Apocalypse level.

“Fall back toward the center! The enemy is extremely strong! Protect the flanks and prepare to retreat!”

The words had barely left the Shadow Clan commander's mouth when a dark figure suddenly appeared on his right.

What?!

“Enemy attack!”

“Protect the commander!”

The commander's expression changed, and part of his body swiftly turned to shadow.

“BOOM!”

Fang Heng materialized abruptly and drove his fist toward the commander. As the punch passed through the shadowy figure, it hissed like boiling water turning to steam.

Fang Heng had encountered Shadow Transformation many times by now.

And its weakness was simple: concentrate an extremely dense Death Will around his fist.

With his transcendent, god-level necromancy and terrifying mental power, the blow instantly dissolved most of the black mist making up the right half of the commander's body!

The damage was no less severe than if his fist had struck solid flesh!
