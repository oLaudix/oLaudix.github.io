Chapter 3095

It Exploded

From Chinese Chapter 3096 · 炸了

Nasir was in an excellent mood. Humming a tune, he strolled into the elevator, ready to boast to someone about the alchemy genius he'd discovered.

He himself had taken more than ten years to break through to the Transcendent level, despite his outstanding aptitude. Who knew how long Fang Heng would take?

Anxi glanced at the training room door, still feeling puzzled.

Wasn't Mr. Fang Heng a close-combat specialist?

Close combat combined with alchemy?

That was a very uncommon combination.

Inside the large training ground on the other side of the wall, Fang Heng spent over two hours rebuilding the alchemical array and once again injected mental power to activate it.

The array slowly started, giving off a faint blue-green glow.

Fang Heng kept it operating at an extremely slow speed while expanding his perception to monitor every stage.

“BOOM!!!”

With a thunderous roar, the array barely completed a third of its cycle before exploding violently into pieces before him.

The training hall's arrays activated automatically in response, absorbing all the energy released by the blast.

Hm?

It actually exploded?

An explosion of this scale couldn't hurt Fang Heng, but it certainly surprised him.

Strange.

Muttering to himself, he sat down on the floor, rubbed his chin, and recalled the entire construction process, pondering the cause of the explosion.

He'd already examined it with the Omniscient Eye. There was nothing wrong with the array itself.

So the problem lay in the design.

Specifically, in how he'd measured the external energies.

In simple terms, precision.

His estimates of the different kinds of environmental energy were based entirely on rough feelings.

For example, he might sense an energy activity level of around 800 and design the corresponding part of the array for approximately 800.

But the actual value could be anywhere between 750 and 850. He couldn't be sure.

That was far too vague.

With imprecise figures piled on more imprecise figures, the resulting error became large enough for the laws to destabilize and destroy the array.

To construct a properly adapted magic array, he needed far higher precision.

At minimum, he needed measurements accurate to two decimal places.

The solution was simple enough.

Grind it out!

With the Omniscient Eye and his immense perception attribute, he could observe the array's changes when it exploded and determine whether a value was too high or too low.

So he'd keep adjusting the proportions—more water when there was too much flour, more flour when there was too much water—until he found the exact balance.

Having identified the problem, Fang Heng opened his eyes and immediately began constructing another array.

Then keep going!

Grind!

...

Several days later.

As he finished yet another array, a sharp light flashed through Fang Heng's eyes. He pressed his hands against the formation again.

“Hum...”

The array began circulating slowly, giving off a faint light.

Five whole minutes passed, and the array continued flowing steadily at the center of the hall.

Success!

Game notifications immediately appeared on Fang Heng's retina.

[Hint: Player has completed construction of a law-type magic array.]

[Hint: Player has completed the current quest—Alchemy Advancement (Transcendent).]

Whoosh!

The magic array reflected in Fang Heng's right pupil spun rapidly. Scenes of his relentless training over the past several days replayed in his mind like a film, deepening his understanding of law-type alchemical arrays with each passing moment.

After a while, Fang Heng opened his eyes again and rose from his cross-legged position.

It might appear that he'd simply gotten lucky and constructed one law-type array successfully.

But with the game's assistance, his perception of external laws had become extraordinarily fine.

If his previous sensing accuracy had been 1, it had now improved more than a hundredfold, to 0.001.

Such an improvement couldn't be achieved overnight by ordinary means. It was worth over a decade of hard cultivation!

Fang Heng raised his head and extended his hand, attempting to arrange a simple small law-type array on the floor.

Soon, the alchemical array was complete.

Fang Heng pressed his hand against its patterns.

“Hum...!”

The array operated smoothly.

Success again!

He clenched his fist and fixed that feeling firmly in his memory.

The Alchemy Association training ground kept environmental-law fluctuations in an exceptionally stable equilibrium. It was essentially an ultra-easy version of law-array construction, specially created for students.

After advancing to the Transcendent level, he could still arrange law-type arrays elsewhere. It would simply take longer and have a somewhat lower success rate.

Moreover, although he couldn't see a detailed description of his Transcendent alchemy skill, Fang Heng could feel that his perception and mental attributes had improved as well.

Alchemy was still difficult to apply directly in combat.

However...

With his new understanding of laws, Fang Heng realized he could use law-type arrays to create an ideal cultivation environment!

All he needed was to use law-type alchemical arrays to greatly increase the activity of natural energy in an area. That would dramatically accelerate his absorption of natural energy!

Natural studies!

What he lacked now were the corresponding alchemical diagrams for natural laws.

After all, Nasir had only given him a simple practice diagram for a fire-element law array.

The library?

The Alchemy Association possessed many diagrams. Since this was one of its branches, there ought to be a library here.

Fang Heng was already eager to try.

Thinking further, he could reinforce the basement's sealing and alchemical arrays while he was at it.

And perhaps the library even contained books about Black Mist!

With that thought, Fang Heng pushed open the door and saw Anxi waiting in the corridor.

“Mr. Fang Heng.”

Anxi had been waiting outside the training hall throughout his retreat. Seeing him finally emerge, she was surprised. “You've finished your seclusion?”

“Mm. Thank you for your trouble. Have you been here the whole time?”

“Yes. I wait here during the day, and someone takes over at night.”

“How long have I been in there?”

“Exactly one week.”

“Really? That long...”

Fang Heng frowned.

The Black Mist in his basement was probably near its limit by now.

He needed to deal with it quickly.

Fang Heng glanced sideways at Anxi.

From her expression, the basement problem hadn't erupted yet.

“Mr. Fang Heng? Something on your mind?”

“Nothing. I remember I still have another arena match this month...”

“Not a problem. Since you're at a crucial point in advancing your academic studies, I've applied for a postponement. Your excellent performance in the first two matches convinced the Noel Clan to approve it. You only need to win the remaining ten matches within the next quarter.”
