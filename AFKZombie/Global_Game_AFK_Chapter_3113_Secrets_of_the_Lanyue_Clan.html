Chapter 3113

Secrets of the Lanyue Clan

From Chinese Chapter 3114 · 澜月族秘闻

"Don't rush. Take it slowly. Perhaps one day inspiration will suddenly strike."

Nasir spoke casually and tossed Fang Heng a packed breakfast. "Figured you'd still be training. Here, I brought you something to eat."

Countless geniuses had failed at the threshold of the Demigod level. A true breakthrough was never easy.

"Thanks."

Fang Heng accepted the breakfast and nodded. "If only I could examine a completed magic array that modifies laws. I should be able to find some inspiration in it."

"It's not that simple. A completed alchemical array would certainly be dismantled thoroughly. We wouldn't get a chance to observe it."

Nasir changed the subject. "But after so many years of research, we've developed a few shortcuts."

Fang Heng's interest was stirred. "Such as?"

"Inheritances that some races have secretly preserved."

"That's why I brought you to the Lanyue Clan. Their alchemical magic-array traditions go back a very long way. During my last visit, I investigated and found several Alchemy books in their council of elders. Some dealt with high-level law arrays, which already fall within the scope of Transcendent Alchemy."

Nasir's expression gradually turned serious. "I examined several ancient texts and found signs that their contents had been tampered with. There should be further records on much deeper branches of Alchemy."

"But when I asked the Lanyue people, they insisted those books were their entire surviving inheritance."

"You think they're lying?" Fang Heng asked.

"Most likely. Texts about Demigod-level cultivation are taboo, so it would be perfectly normal for the Lanyue Clan to hide that part of their inheritance."

Nasir continued, "Last time I tried sneaking inside, I was discovered. That's why I brought you this time, Brother Fang Heng. I want us to investigate the Lanyue Clan's ancient ceremonial temple together."

"I see." Fang Heng nodded, immediately interested. "When are you planning to make your move?"

"I've wanted to go for days, but I didn't want to interrupt your training. Whenever you're free, we can pick a night and sneak back in to take another look."

"How about tonight?"

"Works for me."

They quickly settled on the time.

Mu Shunan only had a superficial understanding of Alchemy and wasn't interested in taking part. He stood to one side, pretending he hadn't heard them.

"Aside from the possibility that different races have hidden advancement methods, most Transcendent warriors must rely on themselves to break through."

"Talent also plays a part. The gods must possess the greatest natural gifts. I've heard members of that race are born with special law abilities. They can perceive laws at any moment and influence the world's laws with the slightest movement. If only there were some way to inherit a divine bloodline."

Nasir's imagination ran wild as he sighed. "Then not only the Demigod level, but even the God level would be easy to reach."

Mu Shunan curled his lip and laughingly cursed, "Keep dreaming! Every individual god is terrifyingly powerful. You want to steal their bloodline? Are you tired of living?"

"Hahaha! Just a thought."

Nasir laughed and looked at Mu Shunan. "Speaking of theoretical knowledge, the Noel Clan has one of the longest inheritances around. You've spent so long with them. Have you learned of any shortcuts?"

Mu Shunan's expression shifted slightly.

Nasir had asked casually, but when he saw that reaction, he couldn't help pressing, "There really is something?"

"Not exactly. But a while ago, the Kinsfolk approached me privately through a friend."

Fang Heng's curiosity grew as well, and he looked up at Mu Shunan.

"They wanted me to join their warrior team and work for them."

"You didn't agree, did you?"

"Of course not. When I first arrived in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, the Noel Clan helped me. I owe them a debt, and I'm not an ungrateful person." Mu Shunan paused. "Still, their offer was genuinely tempting."

"What did they offer?"

"A kind of bead that allows a person to perceive the power of laws."

Nasir froze. "Huh? A bead? Something like that really exists?"

"Yes. I was shocked too. The universe is vast and full of wonders. Just because we don't know something exists doesn't mean it isn't real."

Mu Shunan said admiringly, "I've used one of those beads personally. The effect is remarkable. It temporarily improves your perception of external law energy. Unfortunately, the duration is rather short."

"So that's why. No wonder so many warriors have recently joined the Kinsfolk. Most were probably drawn in by these law beads."

Nasir sounded a little envious. "But their strength doesn't seem to have improved all that much. The beads must only be aids, not something that can decide a breakthrough on their own."

Even so, something capable of attracting so many warriors to the Kinsfolk was unlikely to be ineffective.

If an ordinary person needed ten years to advance to the Demigod level, improving the process by even a third could save three years of arduous cultivation.

As Fang Heng listened, a thought occurred to him.

After killing several warriors employed by the Kinsfolk, he had found a few beads on their bodies.

Could they be the law beads Mu Shunan was describing?

Fang Heng flipped his wrist and produced one of the beads he'd found earlier, placing it in his palm.

"Does it look like this?"

"Yes, exactly that!" Mu Shunan stared at Fang Heng in surprise. "How did you get one?"

"I took it from a warrior working for the Kinsfolk. I'd appreciate it if you two kept that to yourselves." Fang Heng shook his head slightly. "How do you use it?"

From a Kinsfolk warrior?

Mu Shunan and Nasir exchanged glances.

They'd recently heard that several Kinsfolk-employed warriors had died violently in the streets and that the Kinsfolk were still hunting the killer.

Could it be...?

Mu Shunan had already drawn his own conclusions. Rather than linger on the subject of the Kinsfolk, he said, "Simple. Swallow it."

Swallow it!

Fang Heng didn't overthink it. He opened his mouth and swallowed the bead whole.

The bead contained no ordinary energy. It dissolved in his mouth into a cool liquid.

As the liquid entered his stomach, Fang Heng instantly found that the laws around him had become incomparably clear to his senses.

The effect was remarkable!

He immediately stopped talking, closed his eyes, and concentrated on sensing the surrounding laws, considering how an alchemical magic array might change their behavior.

The effect of a single bead lasted about an hour before his enhanced perception slowly faded.

Checking again, he found that his Demigod Alchemy advancement quest had progressed by about five percent.

Not bad at all.

If he could get his hands on more of them...

Fang Heng considered the possibility in silence.
