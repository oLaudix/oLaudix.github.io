Chapter 3037

Materials

From Chinese Chapter 3038 · 资料

Fang Heng studied the projected ancient characters and frowned to himself.

He had the feeling he'd seen writing like this somewhere before.

“Found anything, Boss Fang?”

“Mm. I think I've encountered something similar elsewhere.” Fang Heng considered for a moment, then turned around. “Alec, go back to the Tree Spirit Clan and bring someone here for me.”

Alec was a Vampire accompanying Fang Heng. Hearing the instruction, he nodded immediately. “Yes.”

A little more than ten minutes later, Alec brought two Tree Spirit elders onto the ship through Abe Akaya's teleportation passage.

For Abe Akaya in Complete Form, the energy cost of such a transfer was insignificant.

The two elders bowed respectfully to Fang Heng. “Boss Fang.”

“Mm. No need for formalities. Come over here.”

Fang Heng pointed to the ancient characters on the screen. “Take a look at this writing. Any thoughts?”

The elders studied the characters. Something stirred in their minds, and they exchanged glances, the same puzzled expression appearing in both pairs of eyes.

“Well? Don't hold back. Tell me whatever you think.”

“Boss Fang, this writing resembles the language and script of our Tree Spirit Clan very closely, but the meaning of the characters when joined together is different. We could attempt a translation, though it would take time.”

One elder paused and added, “We suspect this script may have some connection to our ancient Tree Spirit ancestors. Could you tell us where it came from?”

“It's an ancient Elven language. Have you heard of the Elven Clan?”

Elves?

The two elders looked at each other and shook their heads.

“Never heard of them.”

Fang Heng nodded and gestured to Kappa. “I'll have people obtain more materials for you. Don't put too much pressure on yourselves. Just translate as much as you can.”

“Yes!”

...

The following day.

At the Elven Clan's daily morning meeting.

Doris, the elder responsible for ancient-record translation, received a review report from the Waterstar Clan concerning a translation of the elves' ancient language.

“Huh?”

Doris made a small sound of surprise.

The task seeking scholars of ancient languages had been posted to outsiders for decades.

At first, people had shown interest, but over the years fewer and fewer candidates had come to the Waterstar Clan to accept it.

The elves had even hired a group of linguists, providing them with fine food and accommodation while they studied within elven territory.

Even so, progress remained slow.

The scholars weren't really to blame. They were confronting a language no one had ever seen, and the elves could provide far too little source material.

Yet this new translation looked convincing, and seventy percent of its contents matched what the elves had already translated. It clearly wasn't random guesswork.

Doris grew more serious. “Who submitted this translation?”

“The captain of a Flying Squirrel ship. They arrived yesterday. He claims to understand some ancient scripts.”

Doris thought for a moment. “Has his identity been checked? Is he safe?”

“The rodent races are numerous, with a vast network of connections, so it's difficult to investigate them thoroughly. But his strength is average, his ship is low-grade, and he has no accompanying escort ships. We don't expect a major problem, though we'll continue investigating.”

“Mm. Bring him to see me first. Only the translator himself.”

“Understood!”

Before long, Captain Kappa was invited by the Waterstar Clan to their main ship.

Suspecting that the translation Fang Heng had instructed him to submit had attracted the elves' attention, Kappa felt nervous as he followed two Waterstar guards along the corridor.

To bolster his courage, Kappa insisted on bringing two assistants.

“Don't be nervous.”

Fang Heng could clearly hear Kappa's accelerating heartbeat. “Remember, you're a scholar proficient in ancient scripts. With me here, no one can hurt you.”

Easy for him to say, but...

Kappa was miserable.

Why did he have to go through all of this?

“Once this is over, I'll give you another ten percent of the profits.”

Kappa's expression immediately changed. He became excited and full of fighting spirit.

“Mr. Kappa, we're about to teleport to an ancient cavern. Your two guards can wait outside while we show you the inscriptions we need translated.”

“Very well. Lead the way.”

The Waterstar Clan had always been subordinate to the elves. Once they'd verified Kappa's identity, they invited him into the teleportation hall.

Of course, they weren't going to tell Kappa that the passage actually led to the Elven Clan's sacred territory.

An ancient ritual cavern.

Upon entering the hall and seeing the teleportation array, Kappa had already guessed part of the truth, but pretended not to know.

After several rounds of teleportation, he was brought into an ancient elven cavern.

It was a vast, deep cavern, but its ceiling was open in places, allowing shafts of sunlight to spill through the cracks.

Several towering trees grew inside, giving the place a vibrant appearance.

“Welcome, expert from the Flying Squirrel Clan.”

Kappa looked up and focused on an elderly elf sitting cross-legged beneath a large tree.

“Are you an elf?”

“That's right. I'm Doris. The Waterstar Clan invited me here, and I've long overseen the translation of the ancient relics in this cavern.”

Doris studied Kappa. “I've examined your translation. Very impressive. Some passages you translated are highly similar to our own results. Have you worked in translation before?”

“Yes.”

Kappa nodded respectfully. “When our Flying Squirrel Clan was mining abroad, we once encountered a rare species calling themselves Tree Spirits. Their ancient language resembles the writing in this cavern somewhat.”

“Tree Spirits...”

Doris murmured the words, then looked up. “Do you have any records concerning them?”

“Nothing beyond some written accounts, and those concern events thousands of years ago.”

“Very well. I expect the Waterstar Clan has explained the task to you. If you're willing to accept it, please remain here and research the wall inscriptions for the time being. You will not be able to leave before completing the mission. You must also disclose nothing you see here to outsiders. Can you comply?”

“I understand the rules. I'd like my two assistants to come in to help record the translation. I also have some ancient Tree Spirit texts and translation notes aboard my ship that I'll need brought here...”

“Very well, I agree. If you need anything, ask the guards. As long as your requests aren't excessive, they'll try to accommodate them.”

Doris was also interested in the ancient Tree Spirit records. With a wave, she instructed the Waterstar attendants to do as Kappa requested, then closed her eyes and returned to cultivation, blending with the environment around her.
