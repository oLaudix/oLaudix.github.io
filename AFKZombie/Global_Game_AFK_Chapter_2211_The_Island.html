Chapter 2211

The Island

From original Chinese Chapter 2212 · 海岛

The biggest obstacle to continuing the main quest was finding a suitable Tree Spirit ally.

The Leaf Clan was the faction he'd dealt with most since entering the game.

And they were an absolute disaster as teammates.

The other Tree Spirit clans didn't look much easier to get along with.

Starting over and building favorability with them from nothing…

That would be extremely difficult!

“Let's split this into two parts. Once Abe Akaya is back in the Zombie Apocalypse, it should begin expanding immediately. Later, we can open a few ritual passages in the Vampire Apocalypse too, let its branches spread there, and accelerate its growth. And there's that tracking beacon radar we got from the Dependents…”

Fang Heng considered his next steps. “I'll try contacting the other Tree Spirit clans to see whether any of them are willing to cooperate. That part will be harder, so we need to think of other…”

“But Fang Heng…”

Mo Jiawei had been listening and thinking for a while. Finally he asked, “Do we really have to cooperate with the Tree Spirits?”

“Hm?”

Mo Jiawei scratched his head. “I mean, do you really care that much about becoming the World Lord of the World of Myriad Forests?”

Fang Heng was taken aback.

Did he care…?

Was that even a question?

Of course he cared!

Being a World Lord meant ownership rights over an entire world.

He absolutely had to get them!

In fact, becoming World Lord was only his first step.

Afterward, he still needed the power of the World of Myriad Forests to confront the entire Holy Court…

Hm?

As he considered what he'd just said, Fang Heng's eyelid twitched. He suddenly understood what Mo Jiawei was getting at.

That's right!

He'd been trapped in the same mistaken assumption all along.

Follow the main quest and its instructions, finish the final step, and he'd obtain World Lord status.

The right to rule the World of Myriad Forests.

Becoming the lord of a high-tier game world sounded wonderful.

It had been his goal from the beginning.

But in practical terms…

Did he truly need the title of World Lord?

On reflection, not really.

He already controlled the Zombie Apocalypse and the Vampire Apocalypse. The former still had beginner protection, so outside forces couldn't attack it. In terms of security alone, wasn't it far better than this miserable world?

What he truly wanted was this world's power!

As for its resources…

He had zombie clones. He could always move things gradually!

Move everything!

Even in the absolute worst case…

A month or so from now, the World of Myriad Forests would open to the outside. Other players, and perhaps even the Holy Court's game factions, would then be able to enter and compete for the main quest and World Lord authority.

When that happened…

A sharp glint appeared in Fang Heng's eyes.

By now, he understood most of this world's main quest.

To complete the final task, a player had to choose a Tree Spirit clan, help it rise to power, and unite the entire race.

So…

The simplest solution was…

He only had to eliminate every Tree Spirit clan.

Just like the anti-Federation Night Owl faction had done before. If he couldn't complete the main quest, he could simply cut off everyone else's path to obtaining World Lord status!

If I can't do the quest, nobody can!

We can all go down together!

Problem solved!

Fang Heng turned to Mo Jiawei and patted his shoulder. “Old Mo, you're a genius.”

“Huh?” Mo Jiawei hadn't expected such enthusiasm. He scratched his head, a little embarrassed. “Ha ha, nothing special.”

Having realized this, Fang Heng felt as though an enormous weight had been lifted from his shoulders.

He finally didn't have to cooperate with those pigheaded teammates anymore!

The mental burden vanished at once!

What he needed to do next was simple.

Increase his own strength by every possible means.

And the easiest way to do that…

Fang Heng turned his attention to Abe Akaya, which was slowly shrinking under Klevi's care.

Exactly. Abe Akaya!

The various Tree Spirit clans were each cultivating their own Divine Tree seeds. He didn't know where they'd obtained their successor trees, but since Abe Akaya shared the same origin as the Divine Trees, similar cultivation methods should work on it too.

What's more…

According to Sandy's research, Divine Trees could devour others of their own kind to become stronger rapidly.

Fang Heng licked his lips in excitement.

Devour them all!

He would still need to contact the Tree Spirits and gather intelligence.

But he no longer felt the sickening pressure of being forced to cooperate with them for the sake of the quest.

His mood was completely different.

Ji Haiting didn't understand much of what Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei were discussing. He'd just received a report from a subordinate and approached them with a grave expression.

“Fang Heng, the Federation has detected movement among the Tree Spirits. They seem to be heading here and are expected to reach the island in one to two hours.”

“Can you tell which clan they belong to?”

“All three clans are coming. The Leaf Clan will probably arrive first.”

The Leaf Clan again?

Heh…

Fang Heng couldn't help laughing.

He and the Leaf Clan really were destined to keep running into each other!

…

Beyond the island.

After sensing a wave of energy that resembled Divine Tree Seed Essence, Leaf Clan Elder Ladiard had immediately rushed over with his clansmen.

Strange.

During their journey, the seed-essence fluctuations they'd sensed earlier had completely disappeared.

The island looked perfectly peaceful.

Ladiard suddenly turned toward a patch of bushes.

“Who's there?”

Fang Heng stepped out. “It's me, Fang Heng. Don't be alarmed.”

Fang Heng?

Ladiard frowned at the sight of him.

How odd.

Why did this human turn up absolutely everywhere?

“What are you doing here?”

“I came back to the outer world to recover from my injuries. I felt a surge of natural energy a little while ago, so I came to investigate.”

Fang Heng emerged fully from the bushes and spread his hands. “I arrived a little earlier than you, but the fluctuations had already stopped. I've checked the area and found nothing.”

Ladiard wasn't suspicious.

Instinctively, he considered Fang Heng an inferior human.

Even if the human had somehow stumbled upon Divine Tree Seed Essence, he couldn't activate it and would have to obediently hand it over to the Tree Spirits.

Ladiard surveyed the area and signaled his subordinates to search the island thoroughly.

Watching the Tree Spirits spread out, Fang Heng casually probed for information. “Elder, how's the Leaf Clan doing? Is the Divine Tree Seed Essence still safe?”

“Hmph!”

Ladiard's face darkened with anger at the question.

Although he'd remained in the outer world, the Leaf Clan maintained close contact with its members. He already knew about the disputes that had erupted inside the Inner World's Elder Council.

“All three other clans attacked the Leaf Clan at once. The Grand Elder couldn't hold out against them alone, and the Wood Clan eventually seized the Divine Tree Seed Essence. But they didn't get off cheaply. We took most of their Nature's Heart Crystals in return…”

— End of Chapter 2211 —
