Chapter 3082

Transcendent

From Chinese Chapter 3083 · 超凡

“I heard from Anxi that you've studied alchemy. Your race may use a different educational framework from ours, so I'll first assess your general level.”

As he spoke, Nasir lightly tapped the air, directly tracing an alchemical magic array in midair.

Fang Heng's heart stirred.

Impressive.

Alchemy in the void.

Nasir began demonstrating right in front of him.

“Alchemy has a complete progression of study, from basic material transmutation and the creation of organic substances all the way to elemental fusion, small-scale modifications of laws, and the reconstruction of the constants underlying the order of laws.”

“How far have you progressed?”

Fang Heng lowered his head, following Nasir's line of thought.

He could currently control almost every kind of magic array.

But that was built upon his powerful mental strength and the assistance of the Omniscient Eye.

“I think I'm at the stage of elemental guidance.”

Fang Heng extended a hand and quickly sketched the outline of a magic array on the floor.

“Burn!”

At his low shout, a tongue of flame sprang up from the array.

“Very good. Extremely practiced, and your mental-power control is excellent.” Nasir nodded appreciatively. “From what I can see, you've already reached Grandmaster standard.”

Grandmaster!

No remedial lessons needed!

Fang Heng relaxed, his mood improving considerably.

“The next step involves fusing and separating elements. The emphasis is on microscopic precision of control.”

Nasir didn't notice his reaction. He calmly explained, “Alchemy's practical combat performance is average, so most practitioners supplement it with another discipline—for instance, elemental studies. Have you studied elemental magic?”

“A little.”

“Mm, excellent. If you have elemental perception, this will be much easier. Watch closely. Condense elemental power, then use alchemy to separate and manipulate two different elemental powers. This requires sustained, microscopic control over the alchemical array.”

Nasir opened his hand in front of him.

Fang Heng's pupils contracted sharply.

What?

Five different elemental fluctuations appeared at the tips of Nasir's five fingers.

Incredible.

Fang Heng's elemental studies were at Grandmaster level, and his elemental spells were powerful. But he knew perfectly well that this was largely thanks to his formidable mental power.

At present, he absolutely couldn't control five elemental abilities simultaneously on one hand with Nasir's exquisite precision.

And Nasir wasn't finished.

He slowly closed his hand, bringing the fingers together. The five elemental energies actually fused into one at his fingertips.

Whoosh!

Nasir abruptly raised his index finger.

The five elements separated instantly into five elemental spheres, spinning rapidly around his finger.

Nasir looked at Fang Heng, pleased by the faint astonishment on his face. “Well? Not bad, right? Took me years to learn that trick.”

Fang Heng nodded, offering sincere praise. “Very impressive.”

Clearly, Nasir's accomplishments in elemental studies were considerable as well as in alchemy!

“Haha.”

Nasir laughed, waved away the five elemental energies, and said, “Next I'll teach you to control elemental energy flows at a microscopic level. When you've mastered it, you'll gain deeper insights into both alchemy and elemental studies. Two benefits for the price of one.”

Fang Heng was already impatient to begin. He nodded. “Good.”

“The training method isn't difficult. I'll teach you several relevant magic arrays. You just need to complete them step by step, and your control will gradually improve almost without you noticing.”

“At its heart, it's a matter of putting in time and slowly polishing your skill.”

“I'll demonstrate. Remember everything.”

At Nasir's level, arranging magic arrays no longer required alchemical materials. He casually traced one magical line after another across the floor with his finger.

“Okay.”

Fang Heng watched intently. His right eye reflected the magic array, recording its entire construction as Nasir laid it out.

There were twelve arrays in total.

Every one was a large-scale magic array. Setting them all up took Nasir more than two hours.

Fang Heng committed them to memory and mentally rehearsed them.

Overall, these alchemical arrays were extremely difficult to arrange.

“Remember them?”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng opened his eyes again, observed the energy flowing through the arrays, and nodded lightly.

“These twelve arrays each have their own elemental flows, and each trains a different aspect of control.”

Nasir advised, “Don't rush when arranging them. We're not trying to be fast. Observe closely and experience every change that occurs as the arrays are constructed. Learn to control the movement of elemental magic with microscopic precision.”

“Come, try it.”

“Okay.”

Eager to give it a try, Fang Heng immediately raised his finger and began drawing an exact copy of Nasir's array on the floor.

Instead of pursuing speed, he concentrated on sensing the circulation of elemental mental power.

Watching from the side, Nasir praised him silently.

He'd grasped the essential point so quickly. What outstanding talent.

And there wasn't the slightest error in his array.

As Fang Heng completed construction of the large-scale magic array, a game notification flashed on his retina.

[Hint: Player has completed an Elemental Flow Magic Array and comprehended elemental flow (Excellent). Player has triggered the quest—Alchemy Advancement (Transcendent).]

Quest Name: Alchemy Advancement (Transcendent).

Quest Description: Break through the limitations of alchemy under an instructor's guidance and raise alchemy to the Transcendent level.

Quest Requirement: Complete Stage One—Microscopic-Level Power Control and Fusion.

Quest Completion: 0.3%.

Quest Reward: Alchemy—Transcendent Level.

Not bad.

Fang Heng quickly scanned the game notifications.

One attempt earned him 0.3% progress. That was a little easier than grinding mysticism hand seals had been.

And once he returned home, he could get his zombie clones to grind alongside him, greatly accelerating his progress.

“Very good. I expect you sensed what microscopic control means while arranging that array. Actually, this is about all I can teach you at this stage. The rest depends on your own cultivation. If you encounter problems while practicing, you're welcome to come discuss them with me anytime.”

With that, Nasir rose to his feet.

Alchemy was much like the other disciplines. Instruction provided direction, but genuine breakthroughs depended on personal comprehension, and speed depended on aptitude.

Now it was a matter of spending time grinding through this stage.

In Nasir's estimation, overcoming it would take at least two or three years.

There was no hurry.

“Hm?”

Nasir hadn't heard Fang Heng respond. He looked up and discovered that Fang Heng was already immersed in drawing another alchemical magic array.
