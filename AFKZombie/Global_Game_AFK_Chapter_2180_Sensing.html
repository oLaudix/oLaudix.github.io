Chapter 2180

Sensing

From original Chinese Chapter 2181 · 感应

“Hey.” Fang Heng considered something, then turned to Heli. “All the Dependents’ records and information are stored in the central system, right? How can I get hold of them?”

Heli’s expression changed at once.

“The data is encrypted using the Dependents’ special psychic language. Even if you copied every bit of it, you wouldn’t be able to decrypt it…”

“Is that so…”

Fang Heng was still speaking when Levin approached, surrounded by the other Tree Spirits. He looked at Heli and asked gravely, “Human, what’s down there?”

“Hm?”

Confused, Fang Heng turned toward the area Levin was pointing at.

On the right side of the hall was a circular mechanical hatch set into the floor, designed to open onto the level below.

An entrance to the lower section?

He remembered Heli explaining earlier that the equipment below supplied energy to the entire Spirit Domain blockade network.

“That’s the entrance to the central energy section. I’ve never been inside. All I know is that the Dependents guard it themselves, and no one else is allowed to enter.”

Levin stared at her. “Open it.”

Heli glanced at him. Not daring to refuse, she walked to the side of the room and gently pressed a green button.

Click…

The metal panels in the floor retracted, revealing a circular shaft leading downward.

Looking into it, all they could see was a deep, azure-blue glow.

A winding circular passage descended into the depths.

It was deep.

There was no telling what lay at the bottom.

The shaft was almost perfectly vertical—nearly ninety degrees.

It hardly seemed designed for humans to travel through.

Levin asked quietly, “Fang Heng, can you feel it? The fluctuations of natural energy.”

What?

Fang Heng turned toward him with a puzzled look.

The power of nature?

Levin stood at the opening, staring down into the seemingly bottomless blue depths. Thought flickered in his eyes. “There’s an extraordinarily powerful energy field below. It’s absorbing the natural energy of our world.”

Fang Heng frowned.

Natural energy?

True, he could sense something faintly.

But what was this business about an energy field absorbing it?

“What do you mean?”

“Ever since the passage between the Inner World and the outside world was severed, our people have established settlements in the outside world and tried everything we could to repair it. But at some point, we began noticing that the world’s natural power was steadily disappearing, drained away by some peculiar force.”

“At first we paid it little attention. We thought it was merely a natural fluctuation. But as the power of nature weakened, so did our own strength. Eventually, we discovered that the source of this decline was the Spirit Domain blockade above us.”

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

Indeed, the Spirit Domain blockade network could absorb energy.

“Levin, what are you getting at?”

“I can feel it. The real source of the energy absorption is down below. The blockade network overhead is probably just an external network that spreads its influence.”

Levin turned to Fang Heng and said, enunciating every word, “We need to destroy it completely.”

Fang Heng’s pupils contracted.

Destroy it!

In that instant, the realization struck him, sharpening his gaze.

Well, well! Those Tree Spirits!

Levin and the others had come prepared.

They hadn’t had Vannis and his group teleported here merely to lend Fang Heng a hand.

They had planned this from the beginning.

Besides temporarily interrupting the blockade to reopen the passage to the Inner World, they wanted to destroy the Spirit Domain network outright!

Levin continued, “I can sense that the source which has been siphoning away natural energy is directly below. Once we destroy it, the force that has suppressed our race all these years will vanish, and the entire Spirit Domain blockade network will cease to exist.”

Ever since the Tree Spirits noticed their power weakening and being suppressed, they had searched for the cause.

Rumor had it that the suppression came from the Spirit Domain blockade network covering the planet.

But that conclusion had been drawn from secondhand accounts and assorted research.

The Tree Spirits had never possessed definitive proof.

Today, however, Levin had sensed it for himself.

The thing draining the world’s natural energy was hiding beneath them!

Before coming here, he had discussed this possibility with Grand Elder Senzo.

The Tree Spirits were not content merely to disable the blockade temporarily. If possible, they intended to dismantle the entire network established by the Federation and the Dependents, freeing their race completely!

“Fang Heng, don’t go!”

Having heard the conversation through the intercom, Ji Haiting immediately issued a warning over the communications channel.

“That’s the energy hub of the entire Spirit Domain network! It’s extremely dangerous. The Dependents guard the central area personally and strictly forbid intruders. Fang Heng, stay out of there!”

Fang Heng’s interest stirred.

The more dangerous a place was, and the more firmly people told him not to enter, the more he wanted to have a look.

Besides…

The quest reward was tied to main quest completion.

After a brief silence, Fang Heng turned toward Levin. “What if I said I wanted to stop you…?”

“I strongly advise against it.”

Levin shot him a warning glance, then nodded to Vannis beside him.

“Hmph.”

Vannis snorted at Fang Heng, then signaled for the Tree Spirits behind him to prepare to move.

Creak, creak, creak…

Vines extended from Vannis’s body and wrapped themselves around the edge of the shaft.

Using them for support, he slowly lowered himself into the passage.

The remaining Tree Spirits followed one after another.

Soon, Vannis and his companions disappeared into the winding depths.

Only the soft hiss of vines rubbing against the walls could be heard below.

The blue shaft resembled an invisible abyss, swallowing their entire group.

“You two stay here.”

Levin studied the opening for another moment, ordered two Tree Spirits to stand guard, and entered the shaft himself.

They were really going all in?

Fang Heng remained at the entrance, looking down as he thought.

He hadn’t actually intended to stop the Tree Spirits from investigating.

After all, a special quest was involved, and it affected the main quest as well.

“Tree Spirits…” Fang Heng muttered. He contacted Ji Haiting through his earpiece. “Boss Ji, can you hear me? How much longer do you need over there?”
