Chapter 2132

Reproach

From original Chinese Chapter 2133 · 埋怨

Everyone returned to the warehouse.

When Vannis saw the dead Divine Tree offshoots piled on the ground and the magic array Fang Heng had laid out on the floor, surprise flashed across his face. His expression shifted several times as he walked forward, crouched beside the array, and began inspecting it closely.

Yule waited respectfully to one side.

The inspection lasted more than half an hour.

Vannis's expression changed repeatedly, and he reached out more than once to touch the array's patterns.

Finally, he looked away from the array and turned to the Divine Tree offshoots. Displeasure appeared plainly in his eyes.

“Is this how you treat Divine Tree offshoots?”

Mo Jiawei was stunned.

What did he mean?

Was he actually dissatisfied?

Yule stepped forward to apologize. “I'm terribly sorry, Elder. Recovering these offshoots from Wanling Technology took an enormous effort. We simply couldn't bring them back in perfect condition.”

Vannis looked thoroughly displeased. “You accomplish nothing and only make matters worse.”

[Game Hint: You have unlocked favorability with the Tree Spirit race.]

[Game Hint: Your initial favorability with the Tree Spirits is -5.]

[Game Hint: Due to your performance, your favorability with the Tree Spirits has decreased by an additional 10 points. Current favorability: -15.]

Mo Jiawei frowned at the notifications, growing instantly annoyed.

What was that supposed to mean?

They'd gone to all that trouble to recover the offshoots and even restored the magic array. And instead of thanking them, this fellow was complaining?!

“Forget it.” Vannis waved a hand. “The Divine Tree offshoots are important tools for reaching the Divine Realm. Find somewhere suitable to store them. I'll come to you when I have news.”

With that, Vannis turned to leave.

Mo Jiawei immediately stepped forward to stop him. “Wait.”

Vannis slowly turned his head. “Is there something else, human?”

“We've heard a little about the Tree Spirits' world. We understand your people used Divine Tree offshoots to enter the Inner World.”

Vannis gave the two of them a contemptuous glance. “That's right. What of it?”

Mo Jiawei grew increasingly irritated by the man's tone.

For the sake of the quest, he decided to swallow his anger.

“Is there anything you could give us to do? We'd like to help.”

“Help?”

Vannis looked surprised, then sneered arrogantly. “Mere humans. Why should I trust you? And do you truly believe that, with your abilities, you could be of any assistance to the Tree Spirits?”

Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei exchanged looks.

These Tree Spirits...

They really were thoroughly unpleasant!

Vannis had already taken a couple of steps toward the warehouse exit when something occurred to him. He stopped and turned back toward Fang Heng. “Oh, yes. I heard the Federation is hunting you two? Because of your actions, Crescent Town has come under close surveillance by the Federation and Wanling Technology...”

Yule stepped forward, attempting to explain on Fang Heng's behalf. “Elder, we were trying to stop...”

“Fool!” Vannis cut him off coldly. “Who told you to act without orders? Not only did you take matters into your own hands, you told outsiders about the Inner World! What excuse could you possibly offer?”

Yule lowered his head. “My apologies. We promise nothing of the sort will happen again.”

“Hmph. Very well. In view of your years of devoted service, I'll overlook it this once. There will not be a second time.” Vannis looked at Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei again. “You two. Even now that the Federation is hunting you, are you still willing to take such enormous risks in service of the Tree Spirits?”

Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei exchanged another look and laughed inwardly.

If it weren't for the quest, who would bother with you?!

Beside them, Sandy was already nodding shamelessly, his face full of devotion. He spoke with utmost respect. “To spread the radiance of the God of Nature across the world, we are willing to sacrifice everything for the Tree Spirits!”

Mo Jiawei stared at Sandy in shock.

NPCs really had no bottom line. How could he say something so shameless with a straight face?

And what left Mo Jiawei even more speechless was that the Tree Spirit looked thoroughly pleased with himself.

[Game Hint: Your team's favorability with the Tree Spirits has increased by 1.]

Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei glanced at each other, then looked back at Sandy.

Nobody can flatter someone like you...

“Very good. I can feel your sincerity.”

Seizing the opening like a snake slithering up the very stick used to strike it, Sandy continued pleading. “My lord, might I have the honor of consulting the ancient books handed down by the Tree Spirits? I wish to immerse myself in a civilization greater than humanity's. In just these past few days, from the fragments of information recorded in human history, I've already begun to realize what a magnificent civilization the Tree Spirits possess. I would devote my entire life to exploring it. Yet if I must rely on my own investigations, even a lifetime would scarcely reveal one ten-thousandth of your glorious heritage...”

“Mm. You certainly have dedication. We can discuss it after this matter is resolved.”

“Ah! Thank you so much!” Sandy looked overwhelmed. “To have the chance to study the great inheritance of the Tree Spirits... I-I'm so... so moved! Forgive me, I know this is impolite, but could I possibly ask one more favor...”

Vannis was in a very good mood.

The Tree Spirits stood at the very pinnacle of existence. Even a tiny ray of their civilization's brilliance was enough to make humans bow in submission and weep with gratitude.

“Enough. Since you're so devout, I'll grant you personal permission to consult the Tree Spirits' archive in advance. However, if the mission fails, that privilege will be revoked.”

Even Vannis couldn't withstand Sandy's relentless pleading. He turned back to Fang Heng's group. “I know nothing about this 'restoring a magic array' you speak of. I only know that in the past, we entered the Inner World through Divine Tree offshoots—the dozen or so dead trees you see here. The offshoots contain markers for the passages into the Inner World.”

“Let's suppose you really have restored the markers inside those dead trees. Even so, without a source of power, you still cannot activate the teleportation to the Inner World.”

Fang Heng considered his words.

That was how the Tree Spirits understood it.

The magic array was essentially a coordinate marker.

They'd already obtained the coordinates.

But getting to those coordinates required energy.

Originally, the Divine Tree offshoots had provided that energy.

In fact, this wasn't far from what Fang Heng's team had concluded.

They, too, needed an energy source to guide and activate the magic array.

Their goals were aligned, at least in that respect.
