Chapter 2018

Advancement

From original Chinese Chapter 2019 · 进阶

Thud… thud, thud…

Under the repeated attacks from outside, the coffin on the ground began trembling faintly. Soon it was shaking violently!

Almost there!

The coffin's durability was dropping rapidly!

A ruthless light flashed in Ji Shaojie's eyes. A dim yellow glow covered the longsword in his hand as he brought it down with all his strength.

Break already!

Whoosh!

Just before the blade struck, Ji Shaojie's heart suddenly lurched.

That was…

An extraordinarily powerful pulse of blood vitality erupted from inside the coffin!

BOOM!

Before his sword could fully descend, an intensified shock wave of blood vitality burst out in front of everyone. Great quantities of highly purified blood stored inside the coffin splashed outward with the explosion!

The wave was overwhelming. Ji Shaojie and the players around him felt violent pulses of blood vitality slam into them head-on.

Bang! Bang, bang!

They hurriedly raised their weapons to defend themselves, but the impact forced them back again and again.

When they looked up, they saw a figure standing atop the ruptured Vampire King's Coffin.

Fang Heng!

Ji Shaojie stared at him gravely.

That strange pulse of blood vitality… and all the vampires in the area…

They reminded him of Fang Heng's other identity.

The World Lord of the Vampire Apocalypse!

Confusion filled Ji Shaojie's eyes as he watched Fang Heng.

The man who had summoned a sacred dragon and wielded a radiant, artifact-grade greatsword yesterday… and this man, now radiating the aura of a vampire… were the same person!

Two fundamentally different types of power… How could he possess both at once?!

Everyone could clearly sense the characteristic blood vitality of vampires gathering continuously within Fang Heng.

He slowly opened his eyes and looked at the people in front of him.

Game notifications flooded his vision.

[Hint: Player's blood refinement has reached 100%. Blood refinement is complete.]

[Hint: Your skill, Vampire Prince Bloodline, has evolved into Vampire King Bloodline. The previous Vampire Prince Bloodline skill has been removed…]

[Hint: Player has absorbed blood power. Vampire King Bloodline skill level has increased…]

Rows of notifications appeared before Fang Heng's eyes. His pupils condensed into jewel-like scarlet gems!

Hiss, hiss…

A red magic-array pattern rapidly appeared reflected in the pupil of his right eye.

Fang Heng gently clenched his fist, feeling the blood vitality surging through his body.

Skill: Vampire King Bloodline (LV 37)

Type: Bloodline skill.

Description: Players may possess multiple bloodlines simultaneously, but certain bloodlines are incompatible.

Description: Zombie clones conflict with the vampire bloodline and can inherit only a very small portion of its bonuses.

Skill grade: SSS.

Level limit: This skill may reach LV 45 at most. (Its level cannot exceed the player's current level. The current bloodline has reached its highest grade; the player may seek a way to fuse and advance the bloodline.)

Derived Skill 1 — Vampire

Skill description: Upon learning this skill, gain an additional 3,000% maximum HP, Strength +55, Agility +75, Constitution +70, and Perception +75. Additionally gain Strength equal to skill level × 6, Agility equal to skill level × 8, Constitution equal to skill level × 6, and Perception equal to skill level × 2.

Derived Skill 2 — Progenitor's Blood Absorption

Skill description: An ability to absorb blood inherited from the vampire progenitor. The player may absorb fresh blood and rapidly restore HP, mental strength, perception, stamina and other resources according to the strength of the absorbed blood.

Derived Skill 3 — Self-Healing

Skill description: Vampires possess powerful regenerative abilities. Gain an additional 500 HP regeneration per second. Outside direct sunlight, gain 2,000 HP regeneration per second.

Derived Skill 4 — Nightwalker

Skill description: Gain advanced night vision. Take an additional 10% damage from light-attribute attacks. The player is immune to critical-hit effects from all physical skills except those targeting the heart.

Derived Skill 5 — Bat Form

Skill description: Using the power of the bloodline, the player can assume bat form, enabling long-distance flight at high altitude.

Derived Skill 6 — Blood Manipulation

Skill description: Control an enemy's blood and accelerate its flow, with a chance to cause negative effects including fear, increased stamina consumption, accelerated loss of mental strength, interrupted spellcasting, reduced skill success rate, instant death, blood shock and bleeding. (This skill has no cost; specific effects depend on the target's strength.) It may be cast instantly or channeled continuously, with its effects gradually increasing during channeling.

Derived Skill 7 — Blood Mark

Skill description: Mark a living creature that possesses blood, then track its precise position through that blood. (The effect depends on the Perception attributes of both parties.) Deal an additional 20% damage to the marked enemy.

Derived Skill 8 — Blood Sacrifice

Skill description: Consume 10% of the player's maximum blood gauge. (The consumed portion of the gauge slowly recovers over 6–12 hours.) Open a vampire spatial gateway capable of transporting vampires. The gateway has 200,000 HP and a maximum duration of 20 minutes. (The player cannot travel through it personally. Only vampires may respond to the summons and enter through the gate; items cannot pass through.) Summoned vampires gain an additional 20% to all attributes for three minutes.

Derived Skill 9 — Blood Domain

Skill description: Gain more powerful control over blood within a range of 500.

Fang Heng examined the notifications quickly, carefully sensing the changes in his body.

First were his attributes. Their bonuses had doubled compared with before, and his HP had increased by more than double!

His zombie clones could even inherit part of those bonuses!

Several of the other additional skills had received moderate numerical improvements. He had also gained three new abilities: Blood Mark, Blood Summoning and Blood Domain.

Blood Mark was a support skill, combining tracking and positioning with additional damage against its target.

Blood Summoning…

It was a teleportation passage!

Fang Heng's eyes lit up. Excellent!

With a passage like this, the vampires under his command could answer his summons and enter a battlefield at any time, from anywhere!

An incredibly powerful and useful skill!

The downside was that it consumed his blood gauge rather than mental strength. That hurt, especially since the cost was calculated as a percentage of his maximum gauge. Even with his enormous HP bonuses and his undead body's recovery, he couldn't afford to use it carelessly.

More importantly, he still didn't know how large the eventual teleportation passage would be, or exactly how well it would work.

Still, having the ability was better than not having it!

His plan to invade the Holy Court was still taking shape. With the help of the vampires arriving through this passage, it would undoubtedly be much more secure!

— End of Chapter 2018 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Skill naming inconsistency. The game panel lists derived skill 8 as 血之祭祀 (“Blood Sacrifice”), but Fang Heng later calls it 血之召唤 (“Blood Summoning”). Both terms have been kept, with this discrepancy documented.
  • Numerical notation. 20W (20万) health points is written as 200,000 HP. The 500-unit range for Blood Domain is given without explicit distance units in the Chinese.
  • Regeneration and level limits. The original gives +500 HP per second, or 2,000 per second without sunlight, and states that the bloodline skill cannot exceed player level.
