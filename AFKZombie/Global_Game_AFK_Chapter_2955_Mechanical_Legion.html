Chapter 2955

Mechanical Legion

From original Chinese Chapter 2956 · 机械兵团

Hostile life forms?

Everyone's expression changed.

More than half a month had passed since they arrived on this planet, and they hadn't seen a single native creature. That was strange in itself.

Fang Heng looked serious too. "Let's go outside and have a look."

They followed the passage up to the surface.

By now, the entire area paved with antigravity concrete blocks was protected by a visible energy barrier.

Colette, Vion, and the other Federation personnel had also hurried over as soon as they detected the anomaly.

Beyond the barrier, several black flying objects were slowly approaching through the distant yellow sandstorm.

What the hell were those?

The creatures were spherical and floated high above the ground. As they approached, they began circling the outer barrier while emitting a high-pitched buzzing noise.

Li Zhixuan signaled her researchers to gather data, then warned quietly, "Fang Heng, that isn't ordinary acoustic interference. It's some kind of unknown language. We can confirm they're intelligent life forms capable of communicating information."

Colette arrived beside Fang Heng with the military contingent.

Hearing Li Zhixuan's assessment, he immediately ordered the base's loudspeakers to broadcast messages in human languages.

The creatures apparently understood none of them. After several repetitions of their incomprehensible buzzing, they grew agitated and began flying more erratically around the barrier.

Vion looked up at the rapidly moving spheres and murmured, "This doesn't look good."

Fang Heng studied the floating objects.

He couldn't sense a trace of blood or vitality from them.

Vion turned to his subordinates. "Extract the recordings and send the audio back to headquarters for analysis and decoding immediately!"

"Yes, sir!"

After circling a while longer, the black spheres pulled away and began firing short bursts of dark beams at the energy barrier!

So they were mechanical!

The dark beams struck and exploded against the outer shield. It immediately shifted to full-power mode, consuming its reserve energy to reinforce its defenses.

The Federation soldiers raised their energy weapons and returned fire.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!

But the black spheres were small and incredibly agile. They darted around high above the soldiers, exploiting their altitude.

Every beam the soldiers fired missed.

The spheres dodged while continuing to retaliate, striking the outer barrier with more short bursts of dark energy.

Fortunately, there weren't many of them.

At this intensity, their attacks couldn't breach the shield.

"Stop firing."

After expending a full volley of ammunition, the soldiers lowered their weapons.

Li Zhixuan studied the spheres' movements and frowned. "They barely move to evade, and each time they dodge at the last possible moment. They likely have some kind of energy-detection system that predicts incoming attack trajectories and calculates extremely precise evasive maneuvers."

Fang Heng raised a hand toward the objects overhead.

Mind Control.

Huh?

That didn't work either?

The black spheres swallowed his psychic power like black holes.

Completely nullified.

"Interesting..."

Fang Heng murmured and summoned the Book of Elements with a flick of his wrist.

"Lightning Strike!"

Boom!!

A bolt of lightning fell instantly from above and struck one of the black spheres head-on.

Bang!!

The sphere shattered the moment it was hit.

Fang Heng raised his eyebrows in surprise.

The black spheres possessed fairly strong magical defenses. Had his Mental Strength been any lower, an ordinary player's Lightning Strike might not have affected them at all.

"Beautifully done!"

Fang Heng's success immediately raised the players' morale.

Having suffered a casualty, the black spheres suddenly stopped attacking. They slowly regrouped in a line high overhead, then retreated into the sandstorm and vanished.

They were withdrawing?

Everyone exchanged looks.

A researcher approached to report. "We've uploaded the audio to the Kinsfolk central AI. It has identified them as the Cangniao Mechanical Legion."

Zaka asked, "What the hell is that? A mechanical legion?"

"Yes. The Cangniao Mechanical Legion is an awakened mechanical civilization from another region of space. It's mentioned in the Kinsfolk database, and it is extremely territorial."

As Colette explained, he quickly read through the translated warning and handed the report to Fang Heng.

"That noise was a warning. The Cangniao regard this planet as one of their reserve resource sites. They consider our unauthorized mining an infringement of their interests."

Alec curled his lip. "Hah. They say it belongs to them, so it's theirs? Where's the proof? I can claim the mine belongs to us too."

"What we encountered was a Cangniao advance reconnaissance unit. More regular units will likely arrive soon."

Colette looked at Vion. "Our current defenses probably won't hold against them."

"In the Kinsfolk's threat rankings, the Cangniao Mechanical Legion is only one tier below the Black Fog—a threat on the same level as the Ouroboros Seed."

"Headquarters has been informed of the attack. They should dispatch a starship immediately to evacuate us."

Vion had been nursing his anger for days. Now that the Cangniao had attacked, he saw an opportunity to regain the initiative.

He looked toward Fang Heng. "We'll evacuate as soon as the starship arrives. Fang Heng, have your people prepare. Send the ore you've mined recently back to headquarters with us."

Vion had already worked out his scheme, looking as though he had Fang Heng trapped.

Once the ore was loaded aboard the Federation starship, wouldn't the Western Federation Council control its fate?

After all Fang Heng's hard work mining it, he'd have no choice but to hand it over obediently!

Seeing Fang Heng lower his head and think in silence, a grave look on his face, Vion felt even more delighted.

Hahaha!

Hadn't this brat been arrogant all along? Now look at him!

Colette sighed inwardly and patted Fang Heng's shoulder. "Boss Fang, we should move the supplies and prepare to evacuate as soon as possible."

Fang Heng looked up as if waking from a trance and stared at him blankly. "Hmm? What?"

What was he pretending for? Vion sneered. "I'm saying the Federation starship is coming to collect us, Boss Fang. Hurry up and prepare the ore for transport."

"Oh. I understand."

Fang Heng flatly refused.

He was thinking about how to finish constructing his starship as soon as possible. Ore was a trivial detail by comparison. He waved a hand. "We don't have much stored ore left, so there's no need to bother. Carry out your evacuation plan as you see fit. Don't worry about me."

— End of Chapter 2955 —
