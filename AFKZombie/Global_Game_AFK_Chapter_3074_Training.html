Chapter 3074

Training

From Chinese Chapter 3075 · 修炼

Fang Heng asked, “When you say we need three victories each month, do you mean the team's overall match result?”

“Yes.”

Fang Heng frowned.

He still didn't know the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's overall strength. Considering the worst-case scenario, if he wound up carrying nine useless teammates...

He'd have to beat ten opponents by himself.

To be safe, choosing a D-grade match seemed wiser.

“That one.”

After some thought, Fang Heng pointed to a match on the screen.

It was scheduled for the evening after next.

The mission involved transporting rare ore. They would help a species called Purple Fluorescence move high-value mineral cargo, which was disputed between the Noel Clan and another major species in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

A ten-versus-ten succession battle.

Each side could send one person at a time for a one-on-one fight. The winner could remain on the field for the next round.

It was a Level One arena match, the lowest difficulty.

Fang Heng also wanted to test the arena's standards.

“Very well. I've registered you. I'll contact you once your application has been approved.”

Fang Heng was surprised. “Approved?”

“Yes. Every battle is led by a Noel Clan elder. The elder does not personally fight, but decides the order of combat, provides tactical guidance, and chooses the participating warriors.”

Anxi nodded. “If the roster isn't full, elders sometimes arrange for clan members to participate. In rare circumstances, they can also order warriors to fight. In principle, a warrior won't be compelled to participate more than three times a month.”

“I understand.”

Within the Noel Clan, the recruited warriors were essentially mercenaries.

Youngsters like Klaus and his companions, as well as the clan's own talented people, were the true members the Noel Clan intended to nurture.

As for newcomers like himself, the elders might not know their true strength. Registering didn't guarantee selection.

“Anxi, find me other Level One and Level Two matches during this period. Any time is fine. Just arrange a total of three matches for me.”

Anxi glanced at Fang Heng in surprise. “Any match at all?”

“Any.”

“Understood.”

Anxi realized that Fang Heng was a complete newcomer who didn't understand the arena system at all.

Arena competitions came in many forms. Some were primarily meant to train novices, and the Noel Clan hadn't expected to win them from the start.

The main reason so many warriors spent their days in the preparation areas was to find strong teams and improve their odds of victory.

Fang Heng knew nothing about those intricacies. Most of his attention was focused on his academic studies.

The introductory mysticism hand seals could clear his meridians. In theory, completing the gestures alone should grant skill proficiency.

In that case...

He wondered whether his zombie clones could help.

Who cared?

He might as well try!

The thought of cultivation brightened Fang Heng's expression. He nodded. “Anxi, take me back to the villa.”

“Of course, Mr. Fang Heng.”

...

Back at the villa, Fang Heng immediately went into the basement and summoned his zombie clones to perform the hand seals.

More than a dozen zombies staggered together in the center of the basement.

Their fingers weren't particularly nimble to begin with. Asking them to form intricate seals was even harder.

Forget perfect details—even getting them to form all 128 gestures in sequence took a tremendous effort.

It consumed a great deal of Fang Heng's attention. Finally, when one zombie clumsily completed all 128 hand seals, a game notification appeared before his eyes.

[Hint: Your zombie clone has completed the Meridian Clearing Seals (Mysticism). Current quest progress +0.002%.]

It worked!

Fang Heng's eyes lit up.

With the game's cheating advantage and his zombie clones...

He could absolutely grind this!

One zombie clone might progress slowly, but that didn't matter when he had so many of them!

He just had to summon more clones to practice together!

There was only one problem...

Fang Heng looked around the basement thoughtfully.

There wasn't enough space.

He'd need to expand it.

First he could set up several magic arrays to isolate sound and vibration, then expand the basement outward.

Digging tunnels.

His old profession.

Anyway, he should get more zombie clones here first.

Fang Heng immediately extended his mental power and contacted Abe Akaya to open a world-transmission passage.

The magic array in the middle of the room began rotating slowly.

Abe Akaya's vines emitted a faint green glow.

“Hiss, hiss... hiss, hiss...”

Wisps of Black Mist seeped out of the formation.

“Hm? What's wrong, Abe Akaya? You can't do it?”

Fang Heng frowned.

Abe Akaya immediately sent him a mental response.

Opening a passage now was unlike previous world-transmission attempts. The process was meeting enormous resistance.

Abe Akaya could try to force open the passage, but doing so would consume time and energy and certainly create powerful spatial fluctuations.

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

The time and energy costs weren't serious. Abe Akaya had plenty of energy and could continuously draw more from Hell and the Demon Core.

The problem was the spatial fluctuations.

A powerful burst might attract unwanted attention.

He'd finally found a good place to train, and wanted to keep grinding here for a while—ideally until he'd maxed out all his academic disciplines.

But come to think of it, why was he encountering this strange resistance?

Looking back, he had met similar obstacles when entering the game and activating Abe Akaya's summoning array.

Had the Kinsfolk installed some special device in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital that interfered with certain game abilities?

“Abe Akaya, forget it for now.”

Fang Heng waved for Abe Akaya to stop opening the passage and pulled a stack of rune papers from his backpack.

He had more than two hundred sheets. Including the zombie clones' splitting ability, that would be enough for now.

He'd make do.

He could find another solution later!

Fang Heng settled back into idle cultivation, sitting cross-legged on the floor. He applied the special ointment Mu Shunan had provided to his forehead, then began forming seals.

The ointment sent a cool sensation across his forehead. Its stimulation synchronized with the seals, and Fang Heng could feel his meridians opening considerably faster under their influence.

[Hint: Player has completed seal practice (Mysticism). Current quest progress +0.23%.]

Excellent. At this rate, he should finish training in roughly three days.

Fang Heng ordered his zombie clones to continue digging deeper tunnels and once again immersed himself fully in cultivation.

...

“Ring, ring, ring...”

After an unknown amount of time, the clamor of his phone interrupted his concentration.

“Mr. Fang Heng.”

“Mm? What's the matter?”

“There's a match tonight. One warrior can't attend because of an unexpected issue, and they're asking whether you'd be willing to substitute. If you agree, I'll pick you up at the villa in half an hour.”

“All right. I'll be waiting.”

— End of Chapter 3074 —
