Chapter 2034

Breach

From original Chinese Chapter 2035 · 破口

Because Fang Heng lacked Main God points, none of the Licker forms had been made permanent. If a clone died and revived, it would return to its ordinary form.

That was a bit of a waste.

But it didn't matter!

Seizing the teleportation passage was the most important step of his plan, and he couldn't afford to fail.

He had to increase his zombie horde's fighting strength!

Transforming them into Lickers was the most direct way to do that at the lowest cost!

Meanwhile, as Fang Heng opened his vampire passages, Holy Court Arbiter Muhiting suddenly sensed something. He frowned and slowly turned toward a distant point in the city.

A Holy Court preacher noticed his expression and approached quietly. “My lord, have you detected something unusual?”

A puzzled light appeared in Muhiting's eyes. He nodded. “Yes. I felt an unusual spatial fluctuation. It resembled the power of vampires.”

Vampires?

Spatial fluctuations?

The others exchanged baffled looks.

How could vampires possibly appear in a place like this?

Had he sensed it incorrectly?

The preacher was equally puzzled. After considering his words, he asked, “My lord, should we send someone to investigate…?”

Before he could finish, two Holy Court guards rushed in from outside the square, knelt on one knee, and reported, “Arbiter! Huge numbers of undead creatures have suddenly appeared in every corner of the city! They resemble ghouls. The city guards are trying to eliminate them!”

The guards were frantic, sweat already beading on their foreheads. “The undead are incredibly strong. We…”

Undead?!

Where had these undead creatures come from?

Strange. Duncha hadn't seen any undead for tens of thousands of years!

And now they had appeared at precisely this critical moment?

Was this a planned operation?

An unexpected accident?

Or had the city been infiltrated long ago?

Several possibilities flashed through Muhiting's mind. He said gravely, “Put the entire city under martial law! Deploy the city garrison and wipe out the undead!”

“Understood!”

The entire city was soon in an uproar.

The Holy Court teams originally gathered around the teleportation hall were immediately mobilized.

With the city itself in trouble, there was obviously no time to keep sending troops to Chiboreya. Teleportation was temporarily halted as well.

Among the various teams was a small number of players who had joined the related mission.

At first, the players didn't understand what was happening when they heard the city was under attack by undead. But after following the Holy Court squads out of the central square and seeing the chaos outside, they immediately recognized the creatures.

Those weren't ghouls at all. They were unmistakably Lickers!

The Holy Court soldiers of Duncha might not know what they were looking at, but how could the players fail to recognize them?

Oh no!

Fang Heng!

World Lord Fang Heng!

That guy had already taken part in the war between the Holy Court and the undead faction.

Who could have expected him to appear here as well?!

The players' faces turned pale.

Just what kind of person was Fang Heng? Why had he suddenly come to Duncha?

“Come on! Let's help!”

As soon as the mission appeared, the player squad leader waved his hand and led the team forward to help defend the city.

By then, the entire city had descended into chaos!

The Lickers exploited the terrain, leaping between buildings and darting back and forth!

The Holy Court guards struggled to deal with such agile creatures. They could barely hold small patches of ground in difficult defensive battles.

Whenever anyone left the safety of their squad, Lickers would immediately spring out from every direction to attack!

Where had so many Lickers come from?

It was as though the entire city had suddenly been plunged into a zombie apocalypse.

Cold sweat broke out on the players' foreheads.

“Watch out!”

Suddenly, a red figure shot out in front of them.

A player on the right immediately raised his massive shield to protect his teammate and slammed forward with a shield-counter skill!

Bang!

The heavy impact pushed the shield-bearing player half a step backward, while the Licker was knocked off course and sent flying!

“Kill it!”

The players immediately fired several spells at the creature!

Struck by multiple spells, the Licker howled and flew backward. Then it twisted in midair and darted around a corner on the other side of the street, vanishing from sight.

This…

The players tightened their grip on their weapons, feeling immense pressure.

And that had only been a single, ordinary encounter with one ordinary Licker!

Looking across the city, there had to be thousands of them!

At the same time, Arbiter Muhiting led an elite squad charging out from the right!

The Lickers were astonishingly strong!

Even he found them alarming.

Aside from their slightly weaker attack power, they had almost no weaknesses!

Stranger still, when Holy Court believers were slaughtered by the Lickers, bizarre zombie creatures emerged from their corpses!

These zombies were every bit as difficult to deal with!

Muhiting realized the situation was extremely troublesome.

The undead's power to enslave the dead!

As troublesome as ever!

Fortunately, the city had plenty of guards. For the moment, squads could gather together and fend off the Lickers attacking from all sides.

But the Lickers also severely restricted the squads' movements. Whenever a small group tried to break away, the creatures outside immediately surrounded and killed them.

Damn it!

Where in the world had these things come from?!

Duncha had been at peace for far too long. The sudden undead assault had thrown everything into exceptional confusion.

Muhiting gritted his teeth and forced himself to think calmly.

That's right!

The vampire aura and spatial disturbance he'd sensed earlier!

They'd come from that location!

Muhiting immediately realized the key to the problem.

The source of the undead was most likely the place where he'd detected that surge of blood-vitality energy!

The blood-vitality fluctuation had been accompanied by a spatial disturbance!

He still didn't understand why vampires would suddenly join forces with the undead, but…

That no longer mattered.

The only way to end the city's crisis was to destroy the teleportation source!

Otherwise, they'd be worn down for nothing!

Muhiting immediately raised his staff and shouted, “Everyone! Follow me! Let's destroy the vampires' teleportation source together!”

“Understood!”

The Holy Court squads gathered and slowly advanced toward the vampire teleportation gates, fighting off repeated harassment by the Lickers.

Editing and Source Notes
  • City versus world. The mission names the city 奇切尔 (provisionally “Qiqie’er”), while 敦查 (Duncha) is the game world. These are not the same proper name.
  • Arbiter / inquisitor. The Chinese alternates between 仲裁官 (arbiter) and 审判官 (inquisitor) for 穆希廷 (Muhiting). The English reflects the wording in context.
  • “Ghouls”. Holy Court guards initially mistake Lickers for ghouls. The narration deliberately corrects their identification.
