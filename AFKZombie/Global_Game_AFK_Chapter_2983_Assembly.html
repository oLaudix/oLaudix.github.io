Chapter 2983

Assembly

From Chinese Chapter 2984 · 集合

Fang Heng followed Notar into the command tent.

Inside, senior officials of all four Death Realm empires were gathered, including the four emperors themselves.

Every gaze turned toward Fang Heng.

“Fang Heng, our frontline defenses have completely collapsed. Tell us honestly—how confident are you in this plan?”

Fang Heng looked around at everyone. “Ten out of ten.”

Quiet discussion immediately broke out in the tent again.

“Fang Heng.”

The ruler of the Gabu Empire stood up and addressed him. “We discussed this before you arrived. On our own, we cannot stop the Calamity's spread, no matter how long we hold out. Even if we had only half a chance in ten, we'd fight to the death for it.”

“Tell us your plan, Fang Heng. We are willing to trust you and cooperate with everything we have.”

They spoke of trusting Fang Heng, but in truth, the Nether Clan's four empires had no other way out.

As the Calamity spread and the Death Realm's power dwindled, they had become utterly unable to resist it.

Without humanity's assistance, the entire Nether Clan might already have been exterminated.

When they first heard Fang Heng wanted to wage a decisive battle against the Calamity, almost everyone opposed it.

But with the persuasion of Renee of the Fanni Empire, Notar, and their companions, the four empires had gradually reached a consensus.

Better to fight to the death than slowly wait to die!

“Thank you.”

Fang Heng nodded. “Since you've decided, let's begin immediately.”

...

Although the Nether Clan of the Death Realm had lost much of its strength, Fang Heng still considered them an indispensable ally.

After arranging the battle plan, he arrived at the outer war zone already eroded by the Calamity.

Not far away, Undead Council Chairman Georgi had been waiting for some time with the council's senior members.

Fang Heng immediately approached and bowed. “Chairman, mentors, thank you for coming here in person.”

Georgi nodded with a smile. “Fang Heng, if we can resolve the Calamity once and for all, we old bones would naturally like to help. We'll follow your instructions later, so don't hesitate on our account.”

“Thank you for the Undead Council's wholehearted support.”

Georgi waved away the courtesy. “There's no need to be so formal. But Fang Heng, we still have plenty of time. The new-world plan is progressing smoothly. You don't need to rush.”

The Undead Council had been surprised to hear Fang Heng intended to confront the Ouroboros Seed.

After all, they'd tried it before.

Even the combined power of the entire Undead Council and Death Realm had failed to truly kill Mu Qingxin and Proust.

Fang Heng had been away for barely two months, yet he had returned confidently announcing a final showdown with the Calamity?

As far as the council could tell, Fang Heng had already reached the divine stage of Necromancy. His overall strength had settled into a relatively stable tier.

During those two months, council mentors had given him nearly daily individual instruction, and they knew his Necromancy was improving steadily.

But two months alone were not enough to produce a qualitative breakthrough.

With their current capabilities, defeating Mu Qingxin and Proust simultaneously would still be extremely difficult.

“I understand, Chairman. If it turns out to be impossible, I won't force the issue.”

“Good. That's what I wanted to hear.”

Georgi nodded. “That said, if we manage to eradicate the Calamity, perhaps the road to divine-level Necromancy will become clearer. It would benefit our entire discipline. So do what you must. Whatever happens, the Undead Council will stand firmly behind you.”

“Yes, I understand, Mentor.”

“There's something else...” Georgi paused. “Recently, the Undead Research Association translated a large number of Death Realm texts and inscriptions from stone tablets recovered from the collapsed Empress's Mausoleum. We've encountered quite a few puzzles.”

“Some descriptions of soul reincarnation and records concerning the Death Realm contradict each other in multiple places. Unfortunately, with the Calamity occupying the realm, we can't gather more evidence to check them.”

Georgi waved a hand. “We can discuss that later. Fang Heng, keep your guard up. There may still be secrets behind the Death Realm that no one has uncovered.”

Other secrets besides the Death Realm Jewel?

“Understood.”

Fang Heng nodded, set his questions aside, and looked toward the sky. “The appointed time has arrived. They should be here soon.”

Seeing that, the Nether Clan warriors on the front lines and the Undead Council elite participating in the operation all raised their heads to follow his gaze.

Tiny spatial ripples appeared among the dark clouds overhead.

The ripples intensified and joined together until the outline of a spatial warp appeared.

They were here!

The starships!

A spatial passage opened, and the enormous interstellar starship Nethersea emerged from warp space into the Death Realm!

It was the first time the Nether Clan had seen an alien starship, and they all stopped to stare curiously.

More than a hundred vessels emerged from warp space behind the Nethersea, filling the sky.

Immediately after arriving, each ship raised its defensive barrier to shield itself from the surrounding death aura, then hovered alongside the others above the frontline.

A smile tugged at Fang Heng's lips.

The Death Realm.

It was still a game world, which meant his fleet could enter it too!

Of course, the original Death Realm had been a different story. Besides lacking the coordinates, its overwhelming death aura would have disrupted any warp passage, making entry impossible.

Even if a ship somehow entered, the death aura would have corroded it until it ceased functioning.

But since the Death Realm Jewel had been moved, the concentration of death aura had fallen sharply over several months. It was now comparable to an ordinary high-tier game world.

On top of that, Fang Heng had equipped every ship with top-grade defensive barriers capable of resisting the Death Realm's atmosphere.

He had also prepared a large supply of highly concentrated energy blocks in the realm in advance, ensuring the ships could replenish their power and keep their shields active.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!”

The moment their warp was complete, the ships entered combat mode and fired their beam weapons together, blanketing the Calamity with energy attacks.

Most Nether Clan members had never seen gigantic technological vessels like these. Their faces filled with astonishment.

The ships were enormous, yet they emitted none of the power fluctuations the Nether Clan were accustomed to sensing.

The Undead Council teams knew the Kinsfolk existed but were also seeing an interstellar fleet in person for the first time. They began whispering among themselves.

A small warp passage opened aboard the flagship.

Chen Yu, Alec, and elite Eastern Federation squads in full protective combat gear stepped through and left the vessel.

These Eastern Federation players were also visiting the so-called Death Realm for the first time and surveyed the eerie world cautiously.
