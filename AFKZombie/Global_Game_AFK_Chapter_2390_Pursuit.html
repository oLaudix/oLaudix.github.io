Chapter 2390

Pursuit

From original Chinese Chapter 2391 · 追击

Divine Realm.

Grand Arbiter Okney and his companions opened their eyes simultaneously.

The isolating barrier surrounding the World of Myriad Forests had completely vanished.

A sharp glint flashed through Okney's eyes. "The barrier is gone. Pursue him!" he ordered.

"Yes, sir!"

The five of them sat in a circle and closed their eyes once more, following their perception of Fang Heng to establish a descent passage.

After three hours, one of the Divine Realm's Sin Cleansers opened his eyes and reported to Okney, "Sir, a special force is interfering with Fang Heng's location. We may not be able to pinpoint him accurately, and our descent point could be substantially displaced."

"Hmm. No matter. Keep tracking him. The Great Sage has ordered that Fang Heng must be brought back to the Divine Realm."

Okney knew Fang Heng must have prepared another trap in the World of Myriad Forests.

It didn't matter.

Faced with absolute strength, he refused to believe Fang Heng had any chance of resisting.

They could make countless mistakes. Fang Heng could not afford even one. The moment he exposed an opening, Okney swore he would capture him and imprison him in the Divine Realm forever!

Meanwhile, Fang Heng had already returned from Hell. He was sitting quietly among the black stone pillars of the Root Clan encampment in the Inner World, awaiting the Holy Court's pursuers from the Divine Realm.

His choice of hiding place had been carefully considered.

The surrounding black mist might interfere with the Holy Court's tracking and positioning.

How much it would interfere was down to luck.

Unfortunately, the black mist here was too concentrated for Mo Jiawei to endure. Otherwise, Fang Heng would have brought him along no matter what.

With that thought, Fang Heng slowly closed his eyes.

A major battle was imminent.

He put aside everything else and rested, adjusting himself to his best possible condition.

Time to take a gamble!

How much he earned this time would depend on how it went!

Meanwhile, in the Outer World, Tipper had assembled her Soul Clan subordinates outside the Root Clan settlement.

On Fang Heng's orders, the vampire duke Bellamy had brought a group of vampires to assist the Soul Clan in wiping out the Root Clan.

Since the vampires' dimensional power had been strengthened, the backlash within her body had lessened considerably. With Fang Heng's authorization, she could even exercise limited control over groups of Lickers.

They had been lying in ambush around the settlement for a full five hours.

Tipper felt increasingly uneasy.

She had never expected Fang Heng to find a way to deal with Sharuba so quickly, much less intend to eliminate him in one stroke.

The very speed of it left the Soul Clan unsettled.

Sharuba's strength was terrifying. He had even destroyed the entire Soul Clan single-handedly.

Could he really be dealt with so quickly?

…

Root Clan encampment, Inner World.

Deep in the dense black mist, Fang Heng concealed himself behind a towering black stone pillar in a corner of the camp.

[Warning: The Divine Realm's investigation team will arrive within 3 minutes. Player, exercise caution.]

A crimson warning appeared on his retina.

"They're here."

Fang Heng's heart stirred. He immediately extended his perception to its limit!

Soon, he turned his gaze toward the distance.

High above the area outside the Root Clan encampment, a faint spatial fluctuation appeared.

Tiny motes of sacred power began to emerge.

Good luck!

Fang Heng silently cheered.

The black mist had indeed affected the Divine Realm's teleportation. Their descent point had shifted slightly, placing them outside the Root Clan encampment rather than directly beside him.

The deviation wasn't large, but it was enough for him to do plenty!

He immediately rose and darted deeper into the black mist.

Outside the encampment, the spatial fluctuation continued to expand under the influence of sacred power, releasing countless tiny golden motes.

Stimulated by the sacred attribute, the surrounding black mist immediately surged toward them!

"Hiss! Hiss, hiss, hiss!"

The mist churned and steamed violently, as though boiling!

The intense fluctuations immediately attracted the attention of the Root Clan guards inside the encampment.

"Report to the Great Elder, quickly!"

Geoffrey received the report and immediately led a group of subordinates to investigate.

What the hell was that now?

He raised his head toward the rapidly coalescing sacred energy overhead, his heart sinking.

Golden motes swirled together in midair, forming a luminous sphere at their center as though something were gestating within it.

He had never encountered this power before. It seemed particularly effective at restraining the Demon Seed's power.

Whatever it was, it couldn't be good!

Geoffrey frowned and raised his staff high.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!"

A barrage of nature arrows wreathed in black mist shot toward the sphere!

"Bang! Bang, bang!"

Before the arrows could reach it, the surrounding sacred fluctuations caused them to explode.

The released black mist surged toward the sphere!

"Ineffective."

Everyone could see that the arrows and black mist had only slowed the sphere's formation slightly.

Its glow continued to intensify visibly!

Boom!

Within seconds, sacred light burst outward, forcibly dispelling the surrounding darkness and establishing a dark-golden sacred domain nearly a hundred meters across!

At its center, Grand Arbiter Okney descended with four Sin Cleansers.

Okney opened his eyes, surveyed the thick black mist, and immediately furrowed his brow.

Demon Seed corruption!

At such a high concentration?

What was going on?

Wasn't this supposed to be the World of Myriad Forests?

Why did it look more like the Demon Seed's territory?

Had Fang Heng's arrival turned the place into a corrupted land?

And where was Fang Heng?

Okney looked around, then addressed the Root Clan members below. "Where is Fang Heng?"

Geoffrey and his followers had no idea what had just happened. They could only tell that these newcomers were terrifyingly powerful. Hearing the question made their heads ring.

Fang Heng!

Were these people reinforcements Fang Heng had summoned?

One Sin Cleanser shook his head. "Sir, there's no need to waste time on these Demon Seed servants. I'll deal with them."

He stepped forward through the air and slashed with the sacred longsword in his hand.

"Hummm…!"

As the blade swung, a sword beam condensed from sacred power extended outward!

The beam expanded several meters across the sky and bore down directly upon the Root Clan members below.

Not good!

Geoffrey felt overwhelming pressure seize his heart. His pupils contracted as he struck his staff heavily against the ground!

The black mist enveloping the encampment rushed together, forming a vast barrier in the sword beam's path.

"Boom! Boom, boom!"

The sword beam exploded against the mist barrier. The spreading sacred shock wave swept away great swaths of darkness throughout the camp!

Geoffrey staggered as though struck by a tremendous blow. All color drained from his face, and he pitched backward, only avoiding a fall when two quick-reacting Root Clan guards caught him.

What kind of terrifying power was that?!

Geoffrey was shaken to his core.

A single sword strike had left him feeling utterly helpless!
