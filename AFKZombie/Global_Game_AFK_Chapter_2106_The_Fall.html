Chapter 2106

The Fall

From original Chinese Chapter 2107 · 失落

Georgi felt his heart turn cold.

The Holy Court's last city in Chiboreya...

It had fallen completely!

While Georgi's troops had been occupied, Fang Heng had directed a group of Lickers past the battle and into the main city behind them.

The city's defenses had been weak to begin with. With their powerful climbing and regeneration abilities, the Lickers had turned the place upside down in no time.

Now Georgi's force was the Holy Court's only remaining army.

His hands and feet went cold.

He suddenly realized that the vast swamp before him had become the greatest obstacle to his own escape!

Even if they wanted to cut a bloody path out, they couldn't!

All they could do was continue their futile resistance against the dense masses of Lickers closing in from every direction.

The undead players steadily picked off the Holy Court's ordinary troops from the perimeter, until only Georgi and his elite Paladins remained.

Seeing Georgi's name displayed in blood-red, the players naturally recognized him as a major boss. Nobody dared approach rashly. They stayed at the edges and wore him down.

With the Lickers tying down the boss, the players could keep throwing spells at him from a safe distance.

Fang Heng had no intention of wasting resources on a direct confrontation either, so he stayed hidden and watched.

A moment later, game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Game Hint: Your zombie clones have caused destruction in the main city. You have gained 520,000 undead faction reputation, 170,000 contribution points, and 260,000 honor points.]

[Game Hint: The undead faction has seized control of Chinoya City. You have gained 200,000 undead faction honor points.]

[Game Hint: Current mission—Crush the Holy Court. Completion: 92%.]

After reading the notifications, Fang Heng looked toward the city behind the battlefield.

According to the plan he'd made with Lord Danni, she was to lead an elite force straight into Chinoya City as soon as they crossed the swamp.

It seemed she had succeeded.

Grinding down Commander Georgi would still take some time.

His mission probably wouldn't reach 100% until Georgi's unit was completely wiped out.

More than an hour had passed since the battle began.

As for what came next...

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

The Divine Realm's pursuers would arrive in just over an hour.

He couldn't keep running from them forever.

For some time, Fang Heng had been thinking about how to strike back.

The Holy Court probably didn't yet know that its forces in Chiboreya had been completely destroyed.

Perhaps he could use that opportunity to make some preparations.

At the very least, he needed to test the strength of the Divine Realm's pursuit team!

After a moment's thought, Fang Heng made his decision. He transformed into a bat and sped toward Chinoya City.

· · ·

Inside the city's central fortress,

the battle had just ended.

Lord Danni had led the undead faction's elite troops straight through the fortress in one determined assault, capturing Chinoya City.

The original plan had been to secure Chinoya as a base and put the Holy Court forces outside in an impossible position.

The faster they captured the city, the more they could help Fang Heng at the front.

But Danni and the others hadn't expected to learn, after taking the city, that Fang Heng's zombies and the forces outside had already massacred most of the Holy Court's main army.

There was no longer any need to return. They simply had to hold the city and wait for the other units to join them.

The elite players couldn't help marveling at how easy the mission had been.

With Fang Heng's assistance, they had hardly encountered an obstacle. They had practically been carried the whole way.

One effortless step after another, and they'd won.

While the players were chatting, a window in the fortress hall shattered with a crash.

A bat flew in from outside, landed before them, and resumed human form.

“World Lord Fang Heng.”

Everyone turned toward him with evident respect.

For many of them, this was their first time meeting Fang Heng face-to-face.

“Mm.”

Fang Heng nodded, then turned to Danni with a serious expression. “The Divine Realm's people will be here soon. I'll need your help.”

The players exchanged looks. Most of the relaxed, cheerful atmosphere vanished, leaving only tension.

The Divine Realm!

· · ·

Within the Divine Realm,

Sacred Arbiter Ockney was leading several Holy Court inquisitors through a Divine Realm descent passage into a lower-tier world.

This special transport method allowed them to avoid ordinary teleportation passages.

But it also came with major restrictions.

Travel time, energy consumption, and so on.

Earlier, Ockney's pursuit of Fang Heng had been severely disrupted.

They had clearly confirmed Fang Heng's location, yet some special power enveloped that world and prevented them from breaking through.

The players told him that the world was called the Zombie Apocalypse. It enjoyed special protection from the game and belonged to Fang Heng.

To avoid exhausting the Divine Realm's teleportation energy, Ockney and his subordinates returned to the Divine Realm to await orders.

They assumed Fang Heng had become frightened and withdrawn completely into the Zombie Apocalypse.

In that case, the Divine Realm had accomplished its goal. Fang Heng's movements had been thoroughly restricted.

They never imagined they would receive news of him again today.

Fang Heng had actually been bold enough to return to Chiboreya!

The Divine Realm immediately activated its descent method, sending Ockney's team into that game world.

As the light gradually brightened,

the team materialized in the center of a large, ruined cathedral.

Ockney slowly opened his eyes.

“Hmm?!”

The instant he did, he sensed something was wrong.

The air was filled with dense black necromantic energy.

As soon as he opened his eyes, that darkness surged into the cathedral hall, accompanied by wailing wraiths!

It rushed in like a tide and immediately filled the hall.

Swirling black fog engulfed Ockney and the Divine Realm inquisitors behind him almost instantly.

Their vision was swallowed by boundless darkness!

Rustling sounds came from within the blackness.

Besides the wraiths, a great many other creatures seemed to be rushing toward them!

Ockney frowned slightly and raised his sacred longsword.

“Whoosh!”

A blade of light swept forward!

The dark fog seemed to split beneath the enormous dark-golden slash, parting into upper and lower curtains of shadow.

Then came a deafening boom!

The sword light exploded within the darkness!

A powerful sacred shock wave swept through the hall, completely dispersing the fog.

Holy radiance illuminated the entire chamber!

The wraiths screamed as they dissolved in the bursting light!

At the same time, the sacred energy hurled the Lickers that had been hiding within the darkness backward!

— End of Chapter 2106 —
