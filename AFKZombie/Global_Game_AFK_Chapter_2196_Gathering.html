Chapter 2196

Gathering

From original Chinese Chapter 2197 · 聚集

“Come with me.”

With that, the Tree Spirit led Fang Heng deeper into the forest.

Not far ahead stood another natural barrier, one that affected sight alone.

The patrol escorted Fang Heng through it.

Not long after entering the Tree Spirit territory, they came upon a group of clansmen gathered together.

Vannis was among them.

“Fang Heng! Why are you here?”

Vannis had been discussing their next move with the others. Seeing Fang Heng return with the patrol, he was immediately astonished.

Fang Heng!

He was still alive?!

And how had he managed to enter the Inner World?

Hearing the shout, Fang Heng turned toward Vannis.

“Stop!”

Vannis blocked Fang Heng's path into the center of the camp and stared at him. “Fang Heng, where's Elder Levin? What happened to him?”

“He's dead.”

Fang Heng had no affection for Vannis. He glanced at him coldly and continued, “After you left, Levin and I tried to break the Electronic Light Spirit and lift the blockade. Unfortunately, he used up all his life force in the battle and died of exhaustion.”

Vannis's clenched right fist began trembling. Then he jerked up his head and glared at Fang Heng. “You're lying! It must have been you! You killed Elder Levin! There were only two of you there! Tell me! Was it you?!”

A mocking smile appeared on Fang Heng's lips. “That's funny. I wasn't the only one who entered the energy section. I wonder who it was that turned tail and ran with his men the moment things got dangerous, abandoning Elder Levin to face everything alone. And now you're trying to pick a fight with me…”

“Shut up! Utter nonsense!”

Fang Heng had touched a nerve. Enraged, Vannis raised his hand and attacked first!

After all, a position of Grand Elder had just become vacant within the clan, and Vannis was far from its only aspiring successor. Everything Fang Heng said could damage his chances of taking the position!

What Vannis couldn't bear even more was…

Everything Fang Heng had said was true!

Several green whirlpools materialized before Vannis.

In an instant, countless sharp leaves shot from them like a torrential downpour!

Each leaf gleamed with a pale-green edge.

Fang Heng watched them approach and dodged rapidly, evading wave after wave of attacks.

When dodging became impossible, the blood-red longsword behind him suddenly burst apart!

Hiss! Hiss, hiss, hiss!

The dense barrage of leaves struck the blood-red curtain and swiftly dissolved.

After blocking the attacks, Fang Heng showed half his face through the blood curtain and taunted Vannis with a cold smile. “What's wrong? Getting nervous? Afraid I'll expose your dirty secrets?”

“Lies!”

Vannis's expression darkened completely. Green markings appeared across his face, now twisted with rage.

Fang Heng felt something stir.

What was this now?

The fluctuations of natural energy around them were becoming unusual.

Was Vannis causing it?

Did the Tree Spirits have still more special abilities now that the blockade was gone?

Fang Heng sensed something was wrong and was about to withdraw when the ground beneath him suddenly softened.

An area-effect spell!

Vannis glared at him while several of his trusted subordinates simultaneously cast Swamp and Entangling Roots.

Fang Heng frowned and looked up.

Whoosh!

Vannis's speed increased dramatically. Cloaked in pale-green light, he appeared almost instantly in front of Fang Heng.

“BANG!”

His fist sent Fang Heng flying. A visible dent appeared in Fang Heng's chest where he'd been struck, and his body slammed into a tree behind him with a dull thud.

Surprise flickered in Vannis's eyes.

With the seal lifted, his physical attributes had improved beyond recognition.

He hadn't expected this human to survive the blow! How had he not killed him with one punch?

Then he'd simply keep going!

Killing intent filled Vannis's eyes.

He couldn't let this human keep talking!

“Stop!”

Vannis was about to follow up and silence Fang Heng permanently when a sharp command rang out.

“Grand Elder.”

Turning, they saw Grand Elder Senzo arriving, surrounded by Tree Spirit clansmen.

Senzo had heard the commotion and come to investigate.

He hadn't expected to find Vannis and Fang Heng fighting.

At first he hadn't intervened. Only when Fang Heng began losing ground against Vannis's unsealed strength did he finally step forward.

He regarded Fang Heng with tremendous curiosity.

Fang Heng was still alive?

And he'd managed to enter the Inner World?

How had he done it?!

Had another clansman brought him inside?

Fang Heng rose to his feet, the indentation in his chest from Vannis's punch healing rapidly.

A game notification appeared.

[Hint: The player has completed the prerequisite quest — Trial of the Leaf Clan.]

Quest name: Trial of the Leaf Clan.

Quest difficulty: S.

Quest description: Hold out against Vannis for two minutes without being defeated. Demonstrate your strength before Elder Senzo and earn his recognition.

Fang Heng pursed his lips and recalled the blood he'd been preparing to use in a counterattack. So this quest had been triggered in some kind of hidden way.

That was rare.

So Senzo had been standing nearby watching the whole time without stepping in?

Fang Heng glanced at Senzo out of the corner of his eye and silently cursed the scheming old fox.

Unaware of Fang Heng's thoughts, Senzo asked, “Fang Heng, how did you enter the Inner World?”

“I'm a believer in nature. Naturally, I have my ways. Besides, Elder Levin gave me permission to enter. Elder Vannis can confirm that.”

Vannis glared at him murderously, his teeth practically grinding with hatred.

But Fang Heng wasn't actually lying about that part.

The other Tree Spirits exchanged glances and began whispering.

Was that true?

A believer in nature?

A human?

Vannis wanted nothing more than to kill Fang Heng and couldn't resist speaking up. “Grand Elder, he…”

“Enough, Vannis. I understand. Stand down.”

“Yes, sir.”

Vannis retreated behind Senzo, his expression dark.

Senzo really had been surprised to see Fang Heng battling Vannis, but after a moment's thought he'd already made his decision.

However Fang Heng had entered the Inner World…

For the moment, he was someone worth making use of.

Senzo looked at him. “Fang Heng, your strength is remarkable. As it happens, we were looking for you too. Perhaps this is the God of Nature guiding us.”

Huh?

Fang Heng frowned at the words.

Now he was even more confused.

The Tree Spirits had actually wanted to seek him out personally?

Was this some kind of joke?
