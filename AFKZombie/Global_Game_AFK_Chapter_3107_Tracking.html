Chapter 3107

Tracking

From Chinese Chapter 3108 · 追踪

When Fang Heng left the villa a few days ago, he hadn't noticed anything unusual. Today was different. He suddenly sensed several powerful blood-energy fluctuations coming from covert surveillance positions established around the villa.

The watchers had suppressed their auras, but they couldn't suppress their blood-energy fluctuations, which his vampire bloodline detected easily.

Fang Heng remained suspended in midair in soul form, extended his senses, and focused on the warriors.

Interesting.

Those warriors were clearly here for him.

Were they working for the Kinsfolk?

Had they specifically come to investigate him?

When had he given himself away?

After some thought, Fang Heng decided to ignore them for now and accelerated toward the distant Mechanical Clan hotel where the Flying Squirrel Clan members were staying.

...

At the hotel operated by the Mechanical Clan...

Kappa and the Elven prince Korkalot checked the time.

This was troublesome. Their routine hearing was scheduled for today.

The final review would take place today. Once their documents and the on-site questioning were approved, humanity would be officially registered with the Seven-Realm Martial Capital. In the future, if the Kinsfolk tried to use some devastating weapon against humanity's planet, it would be protected jointly by the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

Kappa had a strong premonition that the people secretly watching them would definitely make their move!

"Come on. We'll be late if we don't leave now."

Korkalot gave Kappa a sideways glance, strode out of the building, and climbed into a waiting taxi. "Boss Fang is with us. What are you afraid of?"

Kappa took a deep breath at the hotel entrance, patted his cheeks, and got into the vehicle as well.

"Driver, take us to the Seven-Realm Martial Capital Department of External Affairs."

"You got it. Sit tight, both of you."

The driver, a member of another race, greeted them and drove toward the city center.

They had barely passed two intersections when Kappa's expression turned miserable.

In the rearview mirror, he could clearly see several cars following them.

And the pursuers weren't even trying to hide it.

They were finished.

Once they left the streets controlled by the Mechanical Clan, the Kinsfolk's pursuers would probably attack!

Kappa and Korkalot exchanged glances, wondering whether to turn back. Suddenly, a familiar voice sounded beside Kappa's ear.

"Kappa, don't worry. I'm right beside you."

Hearing Fang Heng's voice, Kappa immediately perked up, delight spreading across his face.

"Boss Fang!"

Kappa had seen Fang Heng use soul form when entering the Elven Clan's territory, so he recognized it immediately.

As far as he understood, it was a form of special spirit possession.

Korkalot still hadn't understood and asked in confusion, "What?"

"Boss Fang is here. We'll be fine now."

With Fang Heng supporting them, Kappa stopped worrying altogether. Following his instructions, he told the driver, "Change of plans. Forget the department. Head toward the outskirts first, somewhere with fewer people."

"Sure thing."

The driver had no idea what was going on with his passengers, but he followed their request, turned around, and drove toward the outskirts.

As soon as the taxi left the central district, the cars behind them closed in brazenly.

The driver finally realized that something was wrong. Looking back at Kappa and Korkalot, he asked, "What's going on with you two? Are those cars following you?"

"Heh-heh, just some friends. Don't worry, brother. Whatever happens next, just keep driving forward."

"Boom!!!"

Before the driver could respond, everything changed in an instant.

The pursuing cars suddenly exploded without warning!

Several figures leaped from the raging flames.

Fang Heng returned from soul form to his human body and stood before the wrecked vehicles.

Without anyone noticing when, a thin layer of Black Mist had spread across the surroundings.

Seven warriors working for the Kinsfolk stared at Fang Heng in the mist, their expressions growing grave.

"Captain, that kid looks familiar. He's the warrior who recently joined the Noel Clan. He's quite strong."

Fang Heng had fought three matches in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's arena. His last victory as a low-ranking warrior over a high-ranking opponent had attracted considerable attention.

Because he specialized in close combat, many warriors had watched recordings of his matches.

Dario, the captain assigned to carry out the elimination order, glanced at the taxi speeding away and slowly turned back to Fang Heng. "Fang Heng?"

Standing in the path of all seven warriors, Fang Heng asked, "Did the Kinsfolk send you?"

"Hmph. You know perfectly well." Dario snorted and called to his men, "You three, chase them. Don't let them get away."

"Yes, sir!"

The three mid-ranking warriors acknowledged the order and were about to give chase.

"Whoosh!!!"

Fang Heng abruptly became a dark shadow and charged at them at terrifying speed.

So fast!

"Good! Come on, then! I'll take you on!"

Among the seven pursuers was Alray, a high-ranking warrior who also specialized in close combat. He stepped across with an excited expression and blocked Fang Heng's path.

Reaching the high ranks as a close-combat warrior was exceptionally difficult. Alray had been extremely interested in Fang Heng, and now his excitement rose further. Elemental energy swirled around his body, while his eyes burned with fighting spirit.

Alray hadn't actually been Fang Heng's intended target. He had planned to deal with the small fry first.

There were seven opponents, two high-ranking and five mid-ranking. Letting even one escape could become very troublesome.

Fang Heng intended to bypass Alray, but as he drew closer he frowned.

Hm?

Alray stood across his path, his body shrouded in elemental power. He seemed to have become one with the earth itself, giving an impression of absolute immovability.

For an instant, Fang Heng even experienced a strange illusion.

It was as though he could not pass the mountain standing before him.

Laws!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and understood immediately.

This man had fused elemental laws with his power.

And unlike ordinary warriors, Alray incorporated that law energy directly into close combat.

Very interesting!

If he couldn't go around...

"Then let's see if I can smash through!"

Whoosh!

Fang Heng changed targets, lowered his body, and charged straight at Alray.

"Thud!!!!!!"

Dario's pupils contracted sharply as a dull impact sounded beside him.

"Bang!!"

Fang Heng appeared before Alray and punched him backward. Blood poured from all seven of Alray's orifices, while a spreading layer of black corruption appeared at the point of impact. The sight was horrifying.

What!?

Everyone's expression changed.

Alray was a high-ranking warrior known as the Mountain. His defense was strong enough to withstand even the ultimate attacks of other high-ranking warriors. Nearly everyone who had ever fought him found him an exceptionally difficult opponent.

Yet he couldn't even withstand one punch from Fang Heng?

Dario had watched Fang Heng's recorded fights and knew his close-combat abilities were formidable. But he was moving even faster now, and his attacks were far more powerful than in the recordings.
