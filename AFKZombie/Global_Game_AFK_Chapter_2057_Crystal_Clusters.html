Chapter 2057

Crystal Clusters

From original Chinese Chapter 2058 · 花簇

The old man was balding, but his ears were very sharp and stood high above his head. He didn't appear human.

His exposed skin reddened and burned under the sacred light. He was clearly suffering.

“You stupid human! Haven't you figured it out? The magic array here activates automatically when it detects fluctuations in our auras! If you don't want to suffer, stop releasing yours!”

Fang Heng raised his head toward the ceiling.

Whoosh!

A slowly rotating magic array appeared reflected in his right pupil.

Through the Omniscient Eye, the enormous array overhead was broken down into its components, one after another.

An enormous Holy Court magic array. Interesting!

So this was where the sacred energy came from. And those unusual crystal clusters in the central flower bed were its source?

The design was exceptionally clever.

The more Fang Heng examined it, the more impressed he became by the person who had created it.

Its energy-release mechanism automatically adjusted the strength of its suppressive effects: the greater the resistance it encountered, the stronger the sacred power it released in response.

“Hey, brat! Didn't you hear me?”

Enraged at being ignored, the old man struggled to his feet despite the painful light and approached the alloy bars of his cell.

“Kid! Stop it, right now!”

Fang Heng turned, gave him a cold glance, and looked away again.

Stop it? As if I didn't want to!

The original Demon Seed in his sea of consciousness had never been activated before. Now that it had unexpectedly erupted, he couldn't control it completely.

He had no way to make it stop!

“Old man, quit shouting!”

Fang Heng's arrival had attracted the interest of many other prisoners, who began looking over curiously.

Across the hall, a short dwarf was sprawled on his cell floor. He looked Fang Heng over briefly, then lay back down, letting the sacred light burn him as he waved a hand lazily.

“If you've got the guts, go over and teach him a lesson. If you can't, shut up and let me sleep.”

“You rotten little bastard!” the old man snapped. “If I can't sleep, neither can you! Nobody's getting a peaceful night!”

“You two have been locked up for so many years, and you still can't stop quarreling.”

A different voice joined in. “From what I can see, this young friend probably can't even control the Demon Seed inside his own body.”

Fang Heng's expression changed slightly at the words Demon Seed. He looked toward the speaker.

The dwarf, who'd been ready to go back to sleep, seemed to hear something momentous. He instantly sprang off the floor, hurried to the bars, and stared at Fang Heng, trying to study his face.

Fang Heng also turned toward the new speaker.

He wasn't human either.

A lizardfolk!

The old man looked completely baffled.

“Demon Seed? What's a Demon Seed supposed to be?”

The dwarf laughed as he continued studying Fang Heng.

“Heh. I keep telling you to read more books. You don't even know what a Demon Seed is, and you're not embarrassed to ask?”

The lizardfolk addressed Fang Heng.

“Young friend, are you a Demon Seed inheritor? Why does your control of its power seem so poor? Was it forced into you? Did nobody teach you how to control it? Where's the person who performed the ritual?”

Fang Heng glanced at the others, then focused on the lizardfolk.

“What is this place?”

“Tsk. I was starting to think you were mute,” the old man interrupted impatiently.

“It's the Demon-Sealing Cavern, the prison where those Holy Court bastards keep us. See that magic array above? It suppresses our power. The harder you resist, the stronger it gets. Now stop wasting time and shut down that Demon Seed of yours!”

“Heh. A prison?” the dwarf scoffed. “Maybe it's a prison to you. I stay here voluntarily to cultivate with the help of the Holy Court's power.”

“Fine, fine! You're amazing! The whole world knows how amazing you are!” The old man rolled his eyes and turned back to Fang Heng. “Will you stop it already?!”

Fang Heng ignored him and examined the shackles on his wrists.

They were extremely heavy and continuously released sacred energy. Clearly, they were designed to work together with the cavern's huge sealing array, amplifying its suppression.

“Brat! What are you staring at? Stop it!”

“Quit yelling!” the dwarf snapped at the old man, then turned toward Fang Heng with growing excitement.

“Can't you see? He can't control the Demon Seed!”

“I don't care what a Demon Seed is! Just make it stop!” the old man shouted.

“Idiot! Don't you realize? This is our chance to escape!”

The dwarf's eyes shone eagerly.

“Little brother, ignore that lunatic. You still can't control your Demon Seed, right? That's fine. We can help. See the cell next to yours? Go look over there. The person inside might be able to solve your problem!”

Over there?

Fang Heng looked in the direction the dwarf indicated—toward a cell adjoining his own.

At first glance, it seemed completely empty.

But on closer inspection, a faint cluster of gray mist was gathering in one corner.

An undead aura?

It was extremely weak, detectable only with careful concentration.

Growing cautious, Fang Heng stepped closer.

As though sensing his approach, the mist floated forward a few meters and formed a vague figure before him.

An evil spirit!

Its form was indistinct, surrounded by a dim veil of fog. Its face was devoid of life, its skin as pale as paper, and its long hair floated about messily, radiating a sinister chill.

Even under the sacred light, the thin mist around it couldn't be completely dispelled.

An evil spirit was an ancient undead consciousness—undying and indestructible.

Its direct combat power ranked near the bottom among Legendary creatures, but evil spirits were usually extraordinarily knowledgeable.

“You…”

The spirit opened its eyes, hollow and impossibly deep.

“Hey, evil spirit!” called the lizardfolk. “What you've been searching for has arrived! This young friend is a Demon Seed!”

The spirit focused its empty eyes on Fang Heng. It seemed to perceive something unexpected.

“You are a Demon Seed,” it murmured.

“Yes! A Demon Seed!”

The dwarf, now convinced their chance of freedom had arrived, shouted excitedly.

“Didn't you say finding a Demon Seed was one possible way out? Well, here he is! Hurry up and do something so we can all escape!”

Fang Heng also turned his attention to the evil spirit.
