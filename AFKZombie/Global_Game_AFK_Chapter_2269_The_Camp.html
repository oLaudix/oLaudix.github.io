Chapter 2269

The Camp

From original Chinese Chapter 2270 · 营地

"BAM!"

Fang Heng anticipated the attack and raised both hands to block, but the kick still drove him back more than a dozen steps.

Huh?

Had Vannis grown weaker?

The thought stirred Fang Heng's curiosity.

Partly, Vannis clearly hadn't used his full strength. But the Leaf Clan's weakened condition was another factor.

It seemed the damage to the Divine Tree offshoot had seriously affected the Leaf Clan.

"Hmph!"

After delivering the kick, Vannis felt some of his anger drain away. He snorted heavily. "Human! I'm warning you—don't try anything underhanded again!"

Fang Heng still intended to use someone else's resources to produce his own benefits. He simply straightened up without replying.

Vannis turned back to Ovi.

The boy was far too young. Led astray by a human, he was no longer suitable for the assignment.

If Ovi already dared withdraw the clan's precious Leaves of Life and ore reserves without permission, what would he dare do next?

How could constructing a single magic array require so many Leaves of Life? Who knew whether Fang Heng had used the opportunity to swindle the Leaf Clan out of its resources?

Still, the damage was done.

The array was already built; abandoning it now would mean wasting all their previous work.

He would give them one more day.

Vannis forced down his anger and said sternly, "From this point on, Zete will oversee construction of the magic array. All arrangements are subject to his approval."

Ovi was startled and turned to look at Zete, standing beside Vannis.

He knew Zete. The man had long been one of Vannis's trusted subordinates, but his knowledge of magic arrays was extremely superficial.

He understood only slightly more than an ordinary member of his generation.

To put someone who knew practically nothing in charge...

Ovi pleaded again. "Elder, please give me another chance! Once the testing is complete, whatever the outcome, I will accept any punishment."

"Silence! Haven't you caused enough trouble already?" Vannis glared at him and continued, "You're coming back to the settlement with me. You'll spend a week in confinement reflecting on your mistakes! When the Great Elder returns, you can explain yourself to him in person!"

"Yes, Elder."

Ovi knew Vannis's temper and didn't dare make matters worse by arguing. He lowered his head and accepted the punishment.

"Let's go!"

With a wave of his hand, Vannis ordered Zete and a dozen or so Leaf Clan members to stay, then departed with everyone else.

Zete stepped forward and looked down at Fang Heng with a superior air.

"Fang Heng, I don't know how you bewitched Ovi, but I'm not like him. Don't try any tricks with me. I'll be watching you. Understand?"

The Leaf Clan was still the Leaf Clan, Fang Heng thought.

They were preparing to demolish the bridge before they'd even finished crossing it.

"If you do a good job, I'll also help you obtain what you were promised." Zete studied the array and asked, "What do you intend to do next?"

"We've already run the array once and discovered a few minor flaws to correct. Since our dimensional energy for testing has been exhausted, we need to collect more from a dimensional world. We'll also have to modify the collection array on that side. This trip will probably take longer and consume more White Froststone."

"More modifications?" Zete frowned. "Where will the White Froststone come from? Can you finish within one day?"

"I've already prepared the ore, and one day is enough." Fang Heng looked at him. "I suggest we choose a relatively low-level dimensional world for the test."

Since Fang Heng was providing all the ore, Zete had no objection. "Very well. Begin as soon as possible. Remember, you only have one day."

Zete waved for the Leaf Clan members in the temple to continue preparing the teleportation passage.

Watching them work energetically, Fang Heng felt a surge of anticipation.

Next, he could finally test how well Abe Akaya absorbed dimensional power.

...

One hour later.

The teleportation array was ready.

Including Zete, twenty Leaf Clan members would accompany Fang Heng.

The array beneath their feet glowed pale white and slowly began rotating.

"Everyone, we're about to enter the Land of Mist. Prepare for departure."

"Humm..."

When he opened his eyes again, Fang Heng found himself surrounded by thin mist.

Soft, marshy ground lay beneath his feet.

Sparse trees extended in every direction beneath a haze of poisonous fog.

The magic-array stone slab!

There it was!

Fang Heng's gaze immediately settled on a large ancient stone slab to the right of the teleportation point.

Under the watchful eyes of Zete and the others, Fang Heng approached the slab, crouched down, and carefully examined the formation inscribed on it.

In Ovi's opinion, Zete knew only a tiny amount about alchemy and magic arrays.

To Fang Heng, Zete was a complete amateur.

The main reason Zete had come was to watch Fang Heng and prevent any suspicious behavior.

After waiting more than ten minutes, Zete grew impatient. Fang Heng was still staring at the slab as though transfixed. Zete coughed and asked, "Are you finished yet?"

He couldn't understand what was so interesting about a broken old stone slab.

"I got a little absorbed. But I'm almost done."

Fang Heng stood and surveyed the surroundings.

"We'll need to build a temporary camp here, then move the stone slab to its center."

"What? Build a camp as well?"

Zete frowned. "Fang Heng, this is a dimensional world. Every minute we remain here consumes a considerable amount of White Froststone energy."

What a ridiculous suggestion.

Building a temporary camp was pointless. Once the dimensional-energy collection array was activated, countless native creatures would be drawn toward it, and the camp would inevitably come under attack.

More importantly, White Froststone was rare. They couldn't afford to waste so much here.

"The camp is essential. Once the array modifications are complete, we'll more than make up for the additional White Froststone consumed during preparation."

Zete remained silent, suspicious that Fang Heng was plotting something.

What a nuisance, Fang Heng thought.

If Ovi had been here, Fang Heng wouldn't have wasted time explaining any of this.

His voice turned colder. "Zete, are you questioning my decision and wasting my time? Understand this: I'm supplying the White Froststone that brought us here. Without me, we couldn't enter at all. Every minute you waste is a minute of my ore being consumed."
