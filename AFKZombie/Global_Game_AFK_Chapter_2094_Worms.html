Chapter 2094

Worms

From original Chinese Chapter 2095 · 虫

[Hint: Your current Emergency Trial Quest failed to meet the minimum requirements. Quest failed. Crusader Points −100; Crusader EXP −1,000.]

[Hint: The Seed of Envy's Sacrificial Rune has left its designated region. The Seed of Envy will soon detect this, potentially triggering unknown consequences. Exercise caution.]

The notifications appeared.

Fang Heng opened his eyes and found himself back in the Eternal Holy City's central courtyard.

Many players who'd withdrawn early from the trial were still gathered there, talking in small groups about what had happened.

Most of them had simply been unlucky. They'd suffered serious injuries during the first wave of chaos and couldn't remain in the battle.

Fang Heng gritted his teeth. Sweat poured from his forehead and ran down both sides of his face.

"Are you all right?"

A Holy Court guard frowned at his pallor and unsteady movements. He turned toward two nearby players and ordered, "He's in terrible condition. You two—take him to the rest area for treatment!"

"Understood."

The two players stepped forward, supported Fang Heng between them, and began leading him toward the rest area beside the hall.

One glanced curiously at his strange expression.

"Brother, what happened to you? Did you just come back from the trial? How are things going over there? Surely it wasn't bad enough to leave you like this?"

The next instant, something astonishing happened!

Fang Heng's upper clothing suddenly split apart, torn open from beneath.

Looking more closely, the players saw several red worms more than half a meter long attached to his body, greedily sucking his blood.

They'd fed so heavily that their bodies had turned a deep crimson.

"What the hell?!"

The two players cried out and recoiled, terrified that the hideous worms might touch them.

Their screams drew everyone's attention.

Players and NPCs across the central courtyard turned toward Fang Heng. A crowd quickly gathered, pointing and whispering.

"Make way! Let me through! What's happening to him?"

Several Holy Court guards saw the commotion and hurried over.

Fang Heng clenched his jaw, dropped to one knee, and steadied himself with one hand. With the other, he seized one of the Glutton's Touch worms attached to his body.

He pulled hard!

RIP!

The worm came free, tearing away a broad patch of bloody skin and flesh with it!

Slap!

He flung it to the ground. Its enormous mouthparts continued chewing the remains of his flesh.

The crowd felt their scalps tingle. When they looked back at Fang Heng, there was fear in their eyes.

What on earth was that thing?

Hiss, hiss!

But an instant later, the worm he'd thrown down captured everyone's attention.

It writhed violently on the ground, releasing streams of black mist!

A dangerous power was rapidly gathering inside it.

The arriving Holy Court guards' expressions changed.

"Watch out!"

That Glutton's Touch had absorbed most of Fang Heng's energy, including much of the Demon Seed's power!

Its overall strength had reached an extraordinary level!

BOOM!

In moments, the worm swelled to nearly ten times its original size!

It became a gigantic creature six or seven meters long!

Hard black plates covered its entire body, with sharp spikes protruding between them. Its head was filled with pointed fangs, giving it a grotesque and vicious appearance.

Dense black mist poured from its body!

Then a second worm transformed, followed by a third!

The Glutton's Touch creatures Fang Heng had torn free mutated one after another!

The Holy Court guards recognized the energy contained within the mist and shouted in alarm.

"Be careful! It's corruption!"

"Get back!"

"Quickly! Evacuate the central courtyard immediately! Stay away from that black mist!"

Many players had no idea what corruption actually was. But seeing the NPC guards react as if facing a mortal threat, they retreated at once.

Holy shields appeared across the courtyard.

The arriving priests began casting Holy Purification to cleanse the spreading corruption before it filled the area.

BOOM!

The Glutton's Touch worms revealed their bloodthirsty nature. Agitated by the holy power, they grew even more frenzied, thrashing around and attacking wildly in the middle of the Holy City!

A Crusader instructor stationed near the quest commission area heard the noise and came out to investigate.

His expression changed when he saw the rampaging worms, and he immediately joined the guards in trying to restrain them.

He turned to the courtyard guard captain and demanded sharply, "What's going on?!"

"A trainee just returned from the trial. Those strange worms were already attached to him, and then..."

They had fallen from a trainee?

Had he encountered them while exploring the Abyss of Hell, then accidentally carried them back into the city?

But why were the worms releasing the aura of a Demon Seed?

That wasn't normal!

The instructor frowned. "Where is that trainee now?"

"He was..."

The captain turned toward the spot where Fang Heng had been kneeling and froze.

He was gone?

Fang Heng had already taken advantage of the chaos to reach the teleportation hall at the rear of the Eternal Holy City.

Along the way, under the stunned gazes of the surrounding players, he'd torn the remaining Glutton's Touch worms from his body one after another!

Each creature hit the ground, rapidly entered its second stage, swelled enormously, and went berserk, wreaking havoc throughout the Holy City!

The entrance to the rear teleportation hall fell into chaos.

After pulling off the last worm, Fang Heng checked his attributes.

Every attribute had fallen below twenty points.

The Demon Seed's original power in his sea of consciousness had become completely undetectable.

That should do.

Fang Heng concentrated on his next move.

After absorbing so much Demon Seed power, the second-stage Glutton's Touch creatures had undergone a qualitative increase in strength.

But for what he intended, they still weren't enough.

They were too slow and cumbersome.

Causing some disorder in the Eternal Holy City was the limit of what they could accomplish.

The real key lay elsewhere!

Fang Heng turned his wrist.

A runestone appeared in his hand—the Seed of Envy's Sacrificial Rune.

An ordinary person who used it carelessly would probably be tracked down by the Demon Seed of Envy.

But if he destroyed it and Envy sensed what had happened...

Surely Envy would be even angrier!

— End of Chapter 2094 —
