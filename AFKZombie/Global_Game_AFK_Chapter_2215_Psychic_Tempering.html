Chapter 2215

Psychic Tempering

From original Chinese Chapter 2216 · 磨砺

Fang Heng looked up at the Darkglow Stone and immediately had a theory.

There were two options. The first was to increase the intensity of the stone's Psychic Tempering effect.

The second, judging by past experience, was to have his zombie clones inherit the Psychic Tempering effect too. With enough clones, he could simply overwhelm the problem through sheer numbers!

“Mr. Fang Heng, let's go. I'll show you another laboratory.”

Worried about Fang Heng's condition, Cheng Fan led him out of the Star Meditation Hall while explaining, “After the initial meditation training, students receive a period of instruction covering existing theories and practical experience.”

“To advance much further, we implant chips in their brains. These monitor each individual's condition, reducing the risk of training accidents, while improving adaptation to psychic power. There are also personalized bodily modifications. Some are extremely dangerous, even potentially fatal…”

“At present, the main limit on the development of Psychic Studies is innate aptitude. Even the most gifted practitioners, after technological enhancement and the use of specialized equipment, are still more than ten times weaker than the Dependents.”

Fang Heng understood most of it now.

The Federation's psychic training essentially had two parts. First, increase Psychic Studies levels and unlock abilities. Second, learn to use those abilities effectively, bringing out their full power.

In that second part, physical attribute limits prevented practitioners from continuing to level, so they relied instead on experience and technique to improve the effects of skills.

It was more of a finishing touch. By comparison with other disciplines, ordinary people might improve final effectiveness by around 120% at most.

The third part—physical modification and technological enhancement tailored to each person's body—was almost like increasing Psychic Studies levels with external equipment.

From Cheng Fan's description, that stage was clearly extremely risky.

Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

“Do you have more Darkglow Stones?”

“Huh?” Cheng Fan was momentarily startled, then nodded. “Yes. When the laboratory was established, the Dependents provided more than a dozen of them. But their energy fluctuations were too dangerous, so we chose one of moderate strength and sealed the rest away.”

“Come on. Show me those too.”

Training efficiency depended on the Darkglow Stone.

That was assuming his physical attributes were high enough to make him immune to the damage from Psychic Tempering.

It was still only a theory, and he needed to test it.

“Um…” Cheng Fan hesitated. “Mr. Fang Heng, could you give me some time? I need to register the visit, and regulations require a Federation officer to accompany anyone entering the hazardous areas below.”

“Sure. Go ahead, I'm in no hurry. Oh, and I want to conduct some special experiments on humans.”

“Human experiments?”

“Mm. Find me a few trustworthy people with psychic enhancements.”

Fang Heng considered the implications. If his theory was correct and the only thing limiting human advancement in Psychic Studies was physical attributes…

He might be able to help Wanling Technology dramatically improve its psychic practitioners in a very short time!

“Tell them I'll let them try to advance their psychic abilities further.”

What?!

Cheng Fan's pupils contracted.

Improve psychic abilities?

Could that really be done?

Realizing how important this might be, Cheng Fan lowered his voice. “Mr. Fang Heng, should we keep this from the Federation?”

“Either way is fine with me. Ask Mr. Ji.”

The human faction was currently in a desperately weak position.

Fang Heng had no objection to giving it a modest boost.

If he ended up opposing the Tree Spirits, the Federation might be a valuable ally.

It could produce unexpectedly good results.

“All right.”

“Go make the arrangements. I need to head back and prepare a few things too.”

“Understood.”

…

Federation Central Council.

Ever since the Spirit Domain blockade network built by the Dependents had failed, the upper ranks of human society had known their greatest crisis had arrived.

There were endless meetings and deployments every day.

Ji Haiting had been unable to excuse himself from several of those meetings and had to attend in person.

When his subordinates contacted him, he was still attending a meeting while organizing resource transfers and supply shipments.

Fang Heng was interested in Psychic Studies too? A tour?

Fine. As long as his identity was concealed, there shouldn't be any problems.

Wait. What?

He had a way to improve Psychic Studies abilities dramatically?

Reading the messages arriving on his phone, Ji Haiting felt his heart begin pounding uncontrollably.

Was it real?

When he'd first met Fang Heng, Ji Haiting had hoped to use him to gradually explore powers beyond humanity's present understanding.

Research into Lickers and Nature's Heart Crystals had already begun over the past two days. But because of the Inner World situation, their attention was divided. Ji Haiting knew results wouldn't come quickly.

Never in his wildest dreams had he imagined that the first breakthrough would concern Psychic Studies!

Ji Haiting lowered his head and thought rapidly.

He'd already developed a considerable degree of trust in Fang Heng.

If Fang Heng believed he had a method, it would probably work. The main uncertainty was how much the experimental subjects' psychic abilities could ultimately improve.

There was another question too…

How much could he leverage such a discovery in negotiations with the Federation?

With the Spirit Domain blockade gone and the Tree Spirits becoming increasingly active, the Federation desperately needed more power.

If Fang Heng could truly improve psychic combat capabilities…

An improvement of even 30% would give Ji Haiting confidence that he could persuade the Federation to make concessions for Fang Heng.

And if it reached 50%…

He might even convince the Federation to cooperate with Fang Heng against the Tree Spirits.

After a moment's thought, Ji Haiting immediately messaged Cheng Fan: “Cooperate fully with Fang Heng. I'll handle the Federation. Above all, don't reveal his identity for now. Use the fabricated identity of Dr. Fang Heng when dealing with the Federation. I'll be there as soon as I can.”

…

Two hours later, Cheng Fan escorted Fang Heng back to the Federation training facility.

They descended to the sixth underground level.

Outside a massive alloy door, Shi Tingwu, captain of the Federation's Second Psychic Unit, waited with a squad of psychic soldiers and partially mechanized troops.

The sudden order to cooperate fully with human psychic experiments made Shi Tingwu uneasy.

Psychic Studies hadn't seen a major breakthrough in a very long time.

Even the Dependents had been unable to make progress.

In their eyes, humans simply lacked the talent needed to advance far in Psychic Studies.
