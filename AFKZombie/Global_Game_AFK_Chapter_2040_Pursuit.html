Chapter 2040

Pursuit

From original Chinese Chapter 2041 · 追袭

“There!”

Mu Xiting's hawklike gaze fixed on a figure beside the underground parking entrance, to the right of the teleportation gates. He pointed straight at him.

It's him! Mu Xiting knew that face. He was certain he'd seen the man near the portal earlier.

At his shout, everyone's eyes swung toward Fang Heng.

Standing at the underground entrance, Fang Heng turned and gave them a cold, mocking glance. Inwardly, however, he was delighted.

Here they come! The big fish have finally taken the bait!

These three Holy Court NPCs were terrifyingly powerful. Their protective sacred flames alone could wipe out enormous groups of zombies with ease. If he managed to kill them, the rewards would surely be considerable.

“Stop right there!”

Seeing Fang Heng behaving suspiciously, the three Crusader instructors roared in unison. Their voices boomed like thunder as they crossed their hands before their chests.

How dare this fanatic challenge God!

Six blasts of Flames of Absolution erupted from their joined hands. The sacred fire lit up the black sky, streaking toward Fang Heng like meteors.

BOOM!

The flames struck his figure with a deafening impact.

Did they get him?

The accompanying Holy Court troops held their breath, staring at the figure engulfed in fire.

The Flames of Absolution were a special Crusader combat art, said to contain a portion of divine power and capable of cleansing any evil.

“Hm?”

With a faint sound of surprise, Fang Heng's silhouette vanished like a ghost amid the scattering golden embers.

Impossible!

The newly arrived Holy Court members exchanged incredulous looks. The Crusaders' signature move had been evaded so easily? Was it spatial magic?

Before they could work it out, Instructor Jige launched himself forward, speeding in the direction of the spatial disturbance.

The other two instructors looked at one another. Both were puzzled.

They had sensed sacred energy coming from Fang Heng. As a result, their Flames of Absolution had been greatly weakened, inflicting less than thirty percent of their normal damage. Against an evil-aligned enemy, the attack should theoretically have dealt several times more damage.

Then… was that man not one of the undead after all?

“That's spatial energy!”

A Holy Court inquisitor who had followed them watched the place where Fang Heng had disappeared, then turned to the instructors. “And there's a strange sacred aura around him. Who is he?”

“We can't say yet. There's definitely something abnormal about his energy, but Instructor Jige is proficient in spatial magic. Let him handle that man. We should deal with those first.”

The instructor named Lifier had already made a rough assessment from that brief exchange. Fang Heng wasn't weak, and his abilities were strange, but he hadn't reached the level of an expert from the Divine Domain. Jige should be more than capable of crushing him.

Lifier glanced at the seven floating teleportation gates. A few Lickers and enormous numbers of ordinary zombies were still pouring out of them.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

With a wave of Lifier's hand, several sacred flames shot toward the gates.

Boom! Boom!

The passages shuddered violently beneath the bombardment, until one crimson gate burst apart with a thunderous crash.

…

[Game Notice: You have been attacked. Your passive skill, Void Body, has activated. You are immune to this attack…]

[Game Notice: You have been struck by Flames of Absolution. Due to your advanced Sacred Studies, damage has been reduced by an additional 60%…]

[Game Notice: You have been attacked by Flames of Absolution…]

[Game Notice: You have entered the Sacred Burning state. Each second you take damage equal to 7% of your maximum HP, calculated after defenses and resistances…]

“Shit!”

Inside his secondary spatial projection, Fang Heng retreated rapidly toward the underground tunnels, his heart hammering.

He'd wanted to lure over one last big catch. He just hadn't expected anything this big!

He'd almost gotten himself killed.

His maximum HP was currently only a tenth of its normal value. Two consecutive long-range attacks from the powerful Flames of Absolution had nearly finished him. Luckily, he'd escaped into the secondary spatial projection just in time.

But the big fish pursuing him evidently had no intention of giving up. The instructor was following close behind like a shadow.

Was this going to end in disaster?

Fang Heng's heart sank as his mind raced for a solution. He needed to shake the man off.

“Isn't Duncha supposed to be a mid-tier game world? Why are these people so absurdly strong?”

He realized he'd badly underestimated the world's power level. Grumbling inwardly, he accelerated.

Whoosh!

When the skill's duration ended, Fang Heng emerged from the secondary spatial projection and darted into the passage on his right.

Instructor Jige had some knowledge of spatial studies. When he saw Fang Heng reappear, he sneered. He couldn't enter Fang Heng's secondary spatial projection, but tracking it from outside was easy enough.

“Knew you'd have to come out eventually. Let's see where you run now!”

Keeping his senses firmly locked on Fang Heng, Jige rushed around the corner.

Whoosh—!

His heart suddenly jolted.

What was that?

An overpowering smell of blood struck him. A curtain of crimson filled his entire field of vision, and even his perception was overwhelmed by the sudden surge of blood vitality.

Fall back!

Jige retreated several steps.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

In the next instant, vast sheets of suspended blood exploded in succession.

Hum—!

A sacred shield materialized around Jige. Blood splashed against it and began corroding the barrier with a continuous sizzling sound. The shield flickered wildly.

Jige stared in astonishment at the crimson explosions still bursting before him.

Only a moment earlier, the man had been surrounded by sacred energy. Now, in the blink of an eye, that power had transformed into terrifying blood vitality!

A Vampire!

The blood contained an extraordinarily formidable force—the kind he had previously encountered only in the Divine Domain.

How could anyone in a mere mid-tier world wield power so far beyond its level? Who exactly was this man?

Jige frowned deeply.

BOOM!

As the last explosions tore through the passage, the tunnel ahead collapsed completely. Rubble blocked his way.

On the far side, Fang Heng was breathing hard as he sprinted into the basement on the right.

He'd used up most of the blood he'd spent half a day refining in that clash, but at least he'd successfully stopped his pursuer.

  • Sacred damage and immunity. 涤罪之焰 is translated as Flames of Absolution. Fang Heng benefits separately from Void Body immunity and an advanced Sacred Studies damage reduction; the 7% maximum-HP-per-second Sacred Burning effect is retained.
  • Spatial projection. 次级空间投影 is translated as secondary spatial projection; it allows Fang Heng to evade pursuit without making him untrackable to Jige.
