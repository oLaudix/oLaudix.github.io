Chapter 2015

Condensation

From original Chinese Chapter 2016 · 凝聚

Ahead, two native species seemed to be locked in a large-scale battle!

Zombies?

The players looked into the distance, their eyes filling with surprise. No zombie creatures had ever appeared in previous investigations of this unknown world.

Yet right in front of them, an enormous zombie horde was fighting a swarm of steel ants!

The zombies moved slowly, but their overwhelming numerical advantage and high health allowed them to steadily wear the ants down. The ants charged fearlessly into the horde again and again.

At first glance, it looked as though they were smashing into a solid wall of zombies. The wall barely moved, while the attacking steel ants suffered terrible losses!

Steel ants fell constantly under the zombies' attacks. Then fleshy cocoons quickly formed on their corpses. Newly formed zombies crawled out of the cocoons, joined the battle, and swelled the horde's numbers even further!

An infection ability?!

Ji Shaojie and the other players looked on warily.

"These zombies are extremely strange."

"They are. None of our earlier investigation reports about this world mention anything like this horde."

"Could they be connected to the demi-humans? Or perhaps to the Dragon Soul Crystal that shattered earlier?"

The speaker was Tarr, a team leader from the Federal Crisis Response Department.

Lei Yu had important duties and couldn't remain trapped in the game world for several months, so this operation was led by another team leader instead.

As Tarr studied the zombies steadily devouring the ant swarm, something occurred to him.

That's right. Zombies! Fang Heng!

"I remember that the World Lord Fang Heng has an ability related to zombies. He can summon large numbers of powerful zombies to fight for him…"

Tarr paused, then continued, "They sound very similar to what we're seeing here."

"Could it be him?"

The players exchanged glances. Naturally, the Federation paid attention to people who shared the same name as a person of interest. But this alone wasn't enough to establish his identity.

"The place where the Dragon Soul Crystal exploded is farther ahead, and these zombies aren't much of a threat to us," Ji Shaojie said, looking into the distance. "We don't have time to deal with them. Stay alert. We'll go around and find the crystal first."

"Agreed."

The group avoided the battlefield between the zombies and steel ants and continued in the direction of the Dragon Soul Crystal.

· · ·

Elsewhere in the unknown world, Victor was directing a group of mechanics as they built a teleportation passage.

After working through the night, the Stage Four passage was already sixty percent complete. Large numbers of zombie clones helped with the construction.

Victor spotted Fang Heng arriving through a portal and waved to him.

"Fang Heng, everything's going smoothly. We should finish tonight—by the early hours of the morning at the latest."

The Spatial Rift Device received an additional construction-speed bonus from the game, so once the necessary materials were available, it could be assembled very quickly.

In fact, the time-consuming part wasn't the device's main structure, but the compatible circuit boards, wiring and electrical conversion components needed to make it work.

Many of those specialized parts had to be crafted by hand by Victor and a few senior engineers.

Vampire Prince Jia'er also noticed Fang Heng's arrival. He flew down and knelt on one knee to report.

"Your Majesty, as you instructed, we've continued luring steel-ant swarms into the Dragon Soul Crystal dust. After killing the strengthened ants, we've been extracting their blood. We've now accumulated 274 blood pools."

"Excellent! Take me to see them."

Fang Heng transformed into a bat and followed Jia'er toward the area where the crystal had exploded the day before.

Blood pools?

Puzzled, Gu Qingzhu followed them as well.

After more than two hours of travel, they arrived at the site of the explosion.

Even from a distance, Gu Qingzhu could smell an overpowering stench of blood. Vast clouds of blood-red mist shrouded the area ahead.

What exactly was going on…?

She frowned and followed Jia'er closer to the region covered in Dragon Soul Crystal dust.

Inside were countless zombies packed together!

Vampire marquises were still luring large numbers of steel ants into the dust. After their blood vitality was enhanced by the crystal, the ants continued fighting against the zombie horde.

The ants had become much stronger, but unfortunately for them, there were simply too many zombies. They still couldn't win and were being rapidly consumed.

On the hills overlooking the battlefield stood row upon row of vampire altars.

The altars continuously emitted a scarlet glow, drawing in and gathering the power left in the ants' blood after they died.

Compared with the previous day, Fang Heng could sense that the concentration of crystal dust in the area had fallen considerably.

He soon resumed human form and walked to the vampire ritual circle on the hill.

Fifteen hours had passed, and they'd filled a full 274 blood pools!

Gu Qingzhu came to a stop on the hillside beside them, staring at the vast pools of blood in astonishment.

Each blood pool was as large as a big swimming pool. Even she was surprised at the tremendous power concentrated inside them.

Fang Heng…

She couldn't help glancing at him. What did he need all these blood pools for?

"Let's begin," Fang Heng said softly.

He stood before the alchemical magic array they'd prepared in advance and slowly raised both hands.

With this much blood, he should have enough to complete the final advancement of his Vampire King bloodline.

Mental strength poured rapidly into the vampire magic array.

Whoosh!

The many vampire arrays began operating violently. At the same time, the blood inside 474 pools started boiling fiercely!

Hiss… hiss, hiss…

The blood churned, releasing great clouds of pale blue-white vapor.

Gu Qingzhu watched his every movement. He was refining it!

As Fang Heng refined the blood, the volume within the pools shrank rapidly.

Jia'er and more than twenty vampire princes and dukes flew over, resumed their human forms, and knelt behind him. Together, they poured their mental strength into the magic array to help sustain it.

With their support, the blood began to separate from the pools under the force of the ritual. Drop by drop, it floated upward and slowly gathered in front of Fang Heng.

The mass of blood spread like an enormous canopy, completely covering the area below.

Hiss! Hiss, hiss!

The canopy rotated slowly, releasing blue-white mist. It was like a gigantic millstone, refining the blood again and again until it became ever purer!

The process continued for a full two hours.

Almost there!

Whoosh!

Fang Heng suddenly raised his eyebrows, opened his scarlet eyes and stared straight ahead.

— End of Chapter 2015 —
