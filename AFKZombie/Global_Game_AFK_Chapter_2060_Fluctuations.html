Chapter 2060

Fluctuations

From original Chinese Chapter 2061 · 波动

The dwarf had hidden at the back of his cell, relying on a protective barrier to resist even a fraction of the waves of sacred energy. Even so, he was suffering terribly.

He looked at Fang Heng with undisguised fear.

Was this the Demon Seed's power? It was terrifying!

Meanwhile, in the Demon-Sealing Temple above the cavern, the dozen or so Holy Court judges and oracles operating the array suddenly turned pale.

“This is bad! Something's happening in the cavern!”

Everyone immediately poured their mental strength into the array to keep it stable.

“Focus! Run the array at full power! Protect the Demon-Sealing Cavern!” ordered the Grand Inquisitor overseeing the central formation.

He was deeply unsettled. In hundreds of years, the cavern had never reacted so violently. What exactly was happening below? Was it connected to the mysterious man the archbishop had just imprisoned?

He had never seen a single person force a magic array to consume mental strength at such an insane rate!

In less than two minutes, the ten oracles maintaining the formation were completely drained. A second group rushed forward to replace them, continuing the suppression.

“Inform the archbishop immediately! Something's wrong in the cavern! And bring in everyone outside who can help!”

If ten people weren't enough to suppress it, they would use a hundred!

The oracles collectively poured mental strength into the array, pushing it to its absolute limit to suppress the turmoil below. More reinforcements arrived, rotating in whenever the operators became exhausted.

Before long, five or six full groups had already taken turns sustaining the formation.

The Grand Inquisitor, whose eyes had remained closed throughout the ordeal, abruptly opened them. His expression was graver than ever.

“Grand Inquisitor, how is it?”

“We've suppressed it for now.”

He exhaled at length. His sacred robes were soaked with sweat.

“Where's the archbishop? What did he say?” he asked his assistant.

“The archbishop is conducting a prayer ritual to communicate with the Divine Realm and cannot be disturbed. We were ordered to make our own decisions and protect the cavern at all costs.”

“Very well. It's quiet again, but we must remain on guard. Another surge could come at any moment. Until the archbishop finishes communicating with the Divine Realm, we can't let our guard down.”

He leaned toward his assistant and lowered his voice. “Bring in another group of reinforcements.”

“Understood!”

…

Inside the Demon-Sealing Cavern.

As the black aura flowing from Fang Heng gradually faded under the sacred light, exhaustion washed over him. His legs gave way, and he dropped to his knees before the flowerbed, bracing himself with both hands.

Sweat ran down his cheeks.

Most of the Demon Seed's core power in his sea of consciousness had been consumed. With it weakened, the external power of 'Jealousy' once again gained the upper hand and firmly locked down the Demon Seed's original power.

Finally. He had done it.

Fang Heng panted heavily as the array overhead gradually calmed. The prisoners in their cells began recovering as well.

A demon who had been meditating stood and regarded him solemnly. “Demon Seed, you don't look too well.”

The lizardfolk prisoner joined in. “Young friend, you caused quite a disturbance. The Holy Court will be down here to investigate soon, and in your current condition you won't be able to fend them off. Let us out, and we'll help you.”

“That's right! We can help!” the dwarf shouted, having finally recovered.

Fang Heng didn't reply. Supporting himself with one hand, he stood and glanced into the cell containing the evil spirit.

Only a faint trail of gray smoke remained. It showed no intention of taking physical form; the sacred light had injured it badly enough that recovery would take some time.

Fang Heng wiped the sweat from his forehead.

By the evil spirit's plan, he had succeeded. With most of the Demon Seed's energy consumed, it had entered a dormant, recuperating state.

But the spirit's solution really had been nothing more than a stopgap.

Fang Heng estimated that the Demon Seed would recover in a matter of days, perhaps sooner. Once its power reached a critical threshold again, he would face the same risk of losing control.

What then? Would he have to repeat this whole ordeal?

That was ridiculous. This attempt had nearly killed him.

After thinking for a moment, his gaze returned to the crystal flower clusters in the bed.

There was another way!

Take them away! Pack up every last one!

If the Demon Seed went berserk again, perhaps the Sacred Luminous Lattice Stones could suppress it temporarily.

That settled it!

He surveyed the prisoners around him. He hated exposing his secrets before so many witnesses, but compared with the danger posed by the Demon Seed, he couldn't afford to care.

Having recovered a little strength, Fang Heng slowly extended his right hand toward the flowerbed.

Every prisoner's attention snapped back to him.

What was he doing now?

Click, click…

Tiny nanorobots emerged from his right sleeve, dropped to the floor, and moved toward the flowerbed. They began assembling a spatial teleportation hub.

Pinpricks of light gradually gathered in front of the robots.

The prisoners were astonished again. Spatial fluctuations! Those specks of light carried traces of spatial energy!

What on earth was he planning? Was he going to create a short-range spatial jump and escape?

“It's useless. This cavern was specially modified to prevent spatial abilities from—”

An elderly prisoner stopped halfway through his warning.

Judging from the way those sparks of spatial energy were accumulating, Fang Heng was planning something far more ambitious than a small jump!

Impossible!

As the faint outline of a teleportation hub took shape, the prisoners could scarcely believe their eyes.

Was this madman trying to open a full-sized spatial passage and escape from here?

The cavern did have defenses specifically designed to prevent prisoners from escaping with spatial abilities. But they would do nothing against a large-scale spatial passage of this kind!

One by one, the prisoners fell silent, exchanging looks.

Could he really pull it off?

Ordinarily, the Holy Court would have come down to investigate the disturbance in the suppression array by now. Yet more than ten minutes had passed without anyone appearing.

If he really succeeded…
