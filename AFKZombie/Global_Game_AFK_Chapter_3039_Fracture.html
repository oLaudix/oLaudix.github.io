Chapter 3039

Fracture

From Chinese Chapter 3040 · 碎裂

“Bang!!!!”

Tewen's pupils contracted sharply.

Before his patrolman could launch his sneak attack, he exploded into a cloud of blood in midair.

“And you.”

Fang Heng raised his right hand, lifting the other elven patrolman into the air with the power of his blood.

Under the horrified eyes of the elves, blood began pouring uncontrollably from the man's facial features, gathering beneath his neck and slowly tightening around it.

He couldn't utter a single word.

Fang Heng turned to the patrol captain. “His life is in your hands. I'll ask one last time: where is the elven prison?”

Tewen swallowed. “Farther back. Follow this path, then turn right at the crossroads. The Tree of Life region and the prison are guarded. You won't get in.”

“Is that so... Well, that's none of your concern.”

Fang Heng shook his head lightly, transformed into a dark shadow, and vanished from Tewen's sight.

“Cough! Cough, cough...”

Tewen and the surviving guard were abruptly released from the blood power restraining them. They fell to the ground, clutching their throats and coughing violently.

“Captain, are you all right?”

“I'm fine.”

Tewen stared in the direction Fang Heng had disappeared, horrified.

Who was that human? His strength was terrifying, and he'd managed to invade their elven world.

Not good!

Tewen suddenly remembered Fang Heng's questions about the prison and Klevi. His heart skipped a beat. He struggled to his feet. “An unknown intruder has entered our world! Come on, return with me at once and report to the elders! Tell them to reinforce the prison!”

“Yes!”

The two hurried back toward the Tree of Life.

Tewen was gambling.

He was betting that Fang Heng knew nothing about the Elven Clan's internal layout, so he'd deliberately directed him down the wrong route.

That would give them time to return, report the incident, and prepare an interception at the prison.

Soon, Tewen and the other guard reached the elven settlement and gave the chief of security a complete account of their encounter with Fang Heng.

The chief didn't know who Klevi was either. After hearing the report, he immediately sent additional guards to the prison, ordered a thorough search around the Tree of Life for signs of the human intruder, and personally reported the incident to the Council of Elders.

Several council elders frowned when they received the report.

They knew about Klevi, of course.

A human had invaded?

And he'd come looking for Klevi?

Could this have something to do with the newly discovered ancient Tree of Life?

The Elven Clan had concealed itself from outsiders, yet someone had come straight to their door.

The elders dared not treat the matter lightly. Brand, the senior elder responsible for Klevi's case, grew especially uneasy and personally led a group to inspect the prison.

...

The Elven Clan's root prison lay deep beneath the area occupied by the Tree of Life.

By the time Elder Brand and his people arrived, the elven guards had strengthened the prison's defenses but had found no trace of an intruder.

Since he'd come, Brand decided to see Klevi and try to extract information about the invader from her.

“Klevi.”

The cell door opened.

Brand looked at Klevi sitting in a corner of the cell, disgust evident in his eyes. “Your friend has come looking for you.”

Hearing his voice, Klevi turned toward Brand, approached, and knelt before him. “Elder, Klevi has done wrong. You may punish me however you wish, but I beg you to spare my friend.”

“Hmph!”

Brand snorted.

Looking at Klevi, merely remembering the impure blood flowing through her veins filled him with anger.

“Klevi, you should know how important the Tree of Life is to us. The only reason you're still alive is that you remain useful. And you dare plead with me? What gives you the right? If you take us to that Tree of Life, we'll naturally let you and your friend leave. But if you keep stubbornly refusing, your friends will die because of you.”

Klevi bit her lip and said nothing.

“Hmph.”

Seeing that Klevi remained unmoved, Brand snorted again, his lips curling into a cold smile. “Still so stubborn. Do you honestly expect your friend to rescue you? The entire Tree of Life region is under the tree's surveillance. If they dare enter, they'll have only death waiting for them. I can guarantee they'll die even more miserably than you.”

Klevi's face grew pale at Brand's threats.

“I'm telling you, not only your friends but the entire human world will be destroyed! Just wait and see. Those so-called friends of yours will die in front of you, one by one!”

The more Brand spoke, the angrier he became. With an abrupt wave of his hand, he turned and led his group away.

Before they'd even left the prison, a guard hurried up to report in a low voice. “Elder Brand, we've just completed an external search. We found no traces of human intrusion.”

“Mm. I doubt they dare enter the forest anyway. Increase security and keep watching. Report any movement to me immediately. If you find the intruder, you have permission to kill him on sight.”

“Understood!”

Brand's expression darkened further.

The human forces on the front line were proving difficult to deal with, and the Kinsfolk still hadn't ordered a general assault. Nor were the elves confident they could conquer the entire Black Fog-infected human world while fighting away from their homeland.

If they could kill Klevi's rescuers, perhaps they could break her resistance and force her to reveal the exact location of the other Tree of Life.

But Brand never dreamed that Fang Heng hadn't followed the false directions from the two patrolmen at all.

After leaving their sight, Fang Heng had quickly returned to his soul form and followed the two guards instead.

By repeatedly attaching his spirit to other things and changing hosts, he had even hidden right beside Brand, entering the prison without anyone noticing.

The Tree of Life really was very similar to Abe Akaya. Fang Heng therefore knew that charging directly into the tree's territory would expose him to its senses.

So he simply chose to infiltrate instead.

Once Brand and his group had left, Fang Heng entered Klevi's cell in his soul form.

Klevi was curled up in a corner of the cell, hugging herself and trembling slightly.

“Klevi.”

“Klevi!”

“Hmm? Brother Fang Heng?”

Klevi suddenly raised her head and stood up.

At first, she thought she was imagining things.

Only when she recognized Fang Heng's voice did she cry out in delight, looking around in bewilderment for its source.

“Klevi, I'm hiding in my soul form right now. You can't see me.”

Unable to conceal her excitement, Klevi nodded, then suddenly remembered something and spoke urgently. “Brother Fang Heng, it's extremely dangerous here. Please leave! Don't worry about me. The elves have set their sights on Abe Akaya. They want to use Abe Akaya's power to revive the Tree of Life. You mustn't let them find Abe Akaya, or it will be in danger!”

— End of Chapter 3039 —
