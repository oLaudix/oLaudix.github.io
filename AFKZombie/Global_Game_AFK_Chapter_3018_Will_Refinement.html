Chapter 3018

Will Refinement

From Chinese Chapter 3019 · 凝练

Since Fang Heng's disappearance, Hain had remained in the Divine Realm, searching for him through every channel available.

The instant he learned Fang Heng had appeared in the Eternal Holy City, he hadn't even taken time to inform the Grand Sages. He left a note and hurried down to greet him.

At last, he'd found Fang Heng in Hell.

"Greetings, Grand Sage."

"Hain, it's been a long time. You've worked hard." Fang Heng smiled and nodded. "You'll accompany me this time."

"Yes, sir!"

Suppressing his excitement, Hain straightened and said solemnly, "The Divine Punishment Knights will follow the Grand Sage to the death."

"Almost everyone's here. Let's begin."

Fang Heng stood at the head of the assembled army and raised his Sacred Greatsword high. "For our fallen comrades! For eternal peace in the world! Follow me, and together we'll wash Hell in blood!"

"Wash Hell in blood!"

Thousands of Holy Court soldiers chanted in unison behind Fang Heng.

Sacred light gathered and erupted, sending waves of holy power outward and rapidly purifying the surrounding infernal environment.

"March!"

The Holy Court knight orders advanced, gradually clearing a path deeper into Hell.

Behind them, the priest teams cast sacred purification spells, cleansing broad swaths of the hellish terrain.

Slow and steady.

This grand, conspicuous campaign to wash Hell in blood was something Fang Heng had deliberately arranged.

If they swaggered through Hell like this, Envy would probably notice long before they reached the twenty-ninth layer and come personally to stop the invasion.

And then...

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

He'd finally see just how much strength separated him from Demon Seed Envy!

...

The Holy Court army was enormous, and its individual members were hardly weak.

Even so, Hell's suppressive environment began slowing their advance after they reached the sixteenth layer.

Ahead, waves of hellish creatures poured out of the rock caverns, roaring as they charged the Holy Court formations.

Glory Knight squads held the front under the buffs provided by the priest teams, fighting bravely to block the fiends. Behind them, the priests purified the terrain while casting healing spells on the front lines.

The army's progress slowed noticeably.

It looked as though the sixteenth layer was the Holy Court's limit.

After considering the situation, Fang Heng raised his God-Bestowed Staff.

"Sacred Blessing!"

"Hum..."

Golden light burst from the staff, showering the Holy Court knights ahead with sacred beneficial blessings.

Their bodies immediately glowed with dark-golden sacred light.

Under Fang Heng's buffs, the knights felt power surging from within.

The effectiveness of sacred support skills generally depended on skill level, Sacred Studies proficiency, talent, and a player's mental attributes. With Fang Heng's immense mental power, high Sacred Studies level, and talent, the effects were terrifying!

Lightning of Judgment, defensive blessings, willpower blessings, regeneration, Sacred Blessing, Blessing of Honor—he poured the entire collection of buffs onto the Holy Court army at once.

Its strength skyrocketed!

The heavily enhanced front-line knights firmly withstood wave after wave of hellish creatures, while purple bolts of judgment lightning repeatedly crashed down from above.

The situation reversed in an instant!

The Holy Court army surged forward with unstoppable momentum.

Hain and his knights didn't join the front-line battle, remaining firmly at Fang Heng's side. Seeing this, excitement glimmered in Hain's eyes.

Since Grand Sage Croesus's death, when had the Holy Court last been able to hold its head so high?

He'd never imagined Fang Bai would be this bold—launching an assault on Hell so soon after becoming a Grand Sage inheritor.

Fang Heng glanced over the battlefield and nodded to Hain, signaling the army to continue advancing.

Not enough.

This was only the sixteenth layer.

To attract Envy's attention, they would have to grind their way to at least the twentieth.

While contemplating his upcoming battle against Envy, Fang Heng suddenly saw a game notification appear on his retina.

[Hint: Player has triggered quest—Distant Holy War.]

Quest name: Distant Holy War.

Quest description: Long ago, one of the Holy Court's Twelve Sages fell during a mission to seal an entrance to Hell. Hell inflicted a wound upon the Holy Court that never healed. Now it is time to seek justice from Hell.

Quest requirement: Cleanse Hell to at least 60% completion. (As completion increases, the player gains Holy Court recognition and gradually condenses the Will of the Twelve Sages. If the quest fails, the will-condensation attempt also fails. Failure to condense the will causes backlash.)

Quest reward: Initially Condensed Will.

Huh?

Fang Heng's interest was piqued by the notification.

The Twelve Sages.

Was this an advancement beyond Grand Sage?

He'd previously thought the two were the same thing.

Now it seemed the Twelve Sages wasn't merely a title, but perhaps a higher form of the Grand Sage advancement?

But what exactly was this newly introduced will condensation?

It sounded somewhat like a recognition metric.

Troublesome. Failure could bring some sort of penalty, and if that interfered with his plan to kill Envy, he'd be in trouble.

"Grand Sage?"

Seeing Fang Heng deep in thought, his expression grave, Hain couldn't help asking, "Is there a problem?"

"No."

As soon as he spoke, another game hint appeared on Fang Heng's retina.

[Hint: Player has successfully cleansed part of Hell. Holy Court reputation increased. Current Sacred Will condensation: 0.7%.]

Fang Heng glanced at the message and shook his head. "Don't worry. We continue."

"Understood."

As the army advanced deeper into Hell, the quest completion rate slowly increased, and the will-condensation value climbed alongside it.

The Holy Court's army was large, so it moved slowly, eventually fighting its way to the nineteenth cavern layer.

Several Holy Court paladins hurried up from the rear and whispered to Fang Heng, "Grand Sage, we've received word from the Divine Realm. Grand Sage Dean is coming with reinforcements. We're advised to slow our advance and regroup with him before proceeding."

Oh?

Surprise flashed through Fang Heng's eyes. After a moment's thought, he looked toward the path ahead.

That made sense.

Once they entered the twentieth layer, they would enter the detection range of Echidna, Envy's demonic pet. Envy should then learn that Hell was under attack.

He hadn't expected the Holy Court to dispatch another Grand Sage.

But that was fine.

When he confronted Envy, having a second Grand Sage on his side would improve his chances.

"Very well. Slow the advance. Pass down the order: once we've cleared the entrance to the twentieth layer, we'll stop there and rest briefly."

"Yes, sir."

— End of Chapter 3018 —
