Chapter 2237

The Kill

From original Chinese Chapter 2238 · 击杀

At the same time, Fang Heng kept a close eye on Lorenzo's HP.

Helping the Federation improve Psychic Studies had been exactly the right decision!

The mound-shaped barrier could withstand physical bombardment, but not Psychic Piercing!

Under the focused attacks of fifty enhanced Psychic Studies soldiers, Lorenzo's HP was plunging rapidly!

If Fang Heng had tried this alone, there was no guarantee he could even have won. And if Lorenzo attempted to flee, Fang Heng would have had few good ways to stop him.

“AAARGH!”

Repeated psychic attacks tore through Lorenzo's sea of consciousness. It felt as if his mind were about to burst. Sweat poured down his forehead, and he could no longer maintain his nature spell.

The ringed wooden shield lost control and collapsed completely!

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

The Lickers clinging to it were sent flying by the blast.

A dense storm of beams struck Lorenzo and exploded!

Without his defenses, the elder's HP was wiped out in moments!

Game notifications flashed across Fang Heng's retina.

[Hint: The player has killed Wood Clan Elder Lorenzo.]

[Hint: While the player remains anonymous, killing Tree Spirits will not affect favorability or contribution with any Tree Spirit clan. Revealing the information will alter these values. Please choose carefully.]

[Hint: The player may reveal this information for an additional reputation reward. Doing so will change favorability with certain Tree Spirit clans and alter branch quests.]

[Hint: The player has obtained 100 Main God Points.]

[Hint: The player has obtained Tier-1 Prismatic Crystals ×12.]

Success!

Fang Heng glanced through the notifications and again chose not to make the kill public.

After all, he might need to deal with the Wood Clan on future quests. There was no point in making a mortal enemy of them now.

Besides, he didn't care much about those additional quest rewards.

And there was something else, more important.

Whoosh!

Before the Federation troops had even confirmed Lorenzo's death, Fang Heng dashed to the place where the elder had fallen and began searching carefully.

Found them!

Two dark-gold jars sat undisturbed on the ground.

[Hint: The player has obtained Tree Spirit Ritual Vessels ×2 and spent 100 Main God Points to store them in the backpack.]

Excellent!

They were his!

Fang Heng relaxed. Then something else on the ground caught his eye.

Hm? What's that?

He looked at a piece of branch lying not far from the Ritual Vessels.

After the barrage of explosions, the golden jars were dusty and had lost some of their shine.

But this small branch was an unusually vivid green.

Fang Heng picked it up and examined it.

[Hint: The player has obtained an item—Unknown Branch.]

[Hint (Nature Studies triggered): You realize the branch contains some unknown information, but you lack the necessary skill to interpret it. Consider raising your Nature Studies tier or finding another way to decipher the information.]

Interesting.

Fang Heng thought for a moment, then put the branch in his backpack.

He could bring it back and let Klevi take a look.

Behind him, Federation soldiers advanced to inspect the battlefield once it was safe.

“Dr. Fang, are you all right?” The operation was not yet over, and Shi Yanwu remained on alert. He approached with several Psychic Studies soldiers and glanced warily at the temple entrance. “Could there be more enemies inside?”

“I'm fine.” Fang Heng nodded and looked back at the entrance. “Possibly. Let's search the whole temple.”

“Very well.”

With the strongest Wood Clan elder gone, any remaining defenders would be far less threatening.

Shi Yanwu checked with command over the radio, then assembled his soldiers and led them, together with a semi-mechanized squad, into the temple.

Soon, they entered the central prayer hall again.

The two Tree Spirits who had returned through the passage with Lorenzo were still unconscious on the floor.

The Federation soldiers examined them, shackled them securely, and carried them away.

“Report! The Prayer-Curse Temple has been searched. No additional enemies found, apart from the two unconscious Wood Clan members.”

“Excellent!”

Shi Yanwu nodded, feeling reassured.

The operation was now a complete success.

The Psychic Studies squad had performed exceptionally well in combat.

As its captain, he was more than satisfied.

Shi Yanwu examined the hall, his eyes soon settling on the teleportation array in the center.

“Dr. Fang, do you know what that formation is?”

“Yes. Some kind of teleportation array.”

Fang Heng was already crouching in front of it, examining it closely.

Until now, he'd never had the chance to look at it properly. Finally he could inspect it up close.

It appeared to be a large-scale formation with teleportation capabilities.

But the destination clearly wasn't the Inner World.

Judging by the Tree Spirits' earlier conversation, it led to somewhere called the Winterfrost City Region.

Fang Heng turned to Shi Yanwu. “Any information on Winterfrost yet?”

“We searched the databases and found nothing.”

“I see…”

Could it be somewhere outside this world?

Fang Heng frowned.

The magic array had suffered minor damage during the battle.

Repairing it shouldn't be too difficult.

Should he just restore the formation and activate it to take a look at the place on the other end?

There was another complication.

He realized the formation was perfectly integrated with the entire Prayer-Curse Temple around it.

They couldn't be separated for independent use.

Either he'd have to move the entire temple together with the formation inside it…

Or use Abe Akaya's powers to reproduce an identical structure.

That would take additional time and consume Abe Akaya's energy.

“Captain, we've searched the area. We found some unused energy ore inside the temple and collected a few other items left by the Tree Spirits for testing.”

Shi Yanwu nodded to his subordinate, then turned back to Fang Heng. “Dr. Fang, found anything? We're detecting other Tree Spirits approaching this location. Our troops aren't suited to continuous fighting right now, so command has instructed us to collect anything valuable and withdraw quickly.”

“Understood.”

Fang Heng stood up.

The Wood Clan clearly valued this Prayer-Curse Temple.

The moment something went wrong, they'd sent reinforcements.

He thought the Federation's decision was sound.

There was no need to stay and fight the Wood Clan head-on.

The primeval forest was, after all, Tree Spirit territory.

An ambush was one thing.

But if they were going to fight a pitched battle, they shouldn't do it on terrain that favored the enemy.

Fang Heng looked up. “From what I can tell, the Tree Spirits were using a special teleportation-transfer array here, powered by White Froststone ore. We still don't know exactly what they're doing, but it appears connected to a place called the Winterfrost City Region.”

— End of Chapter 2237 —
