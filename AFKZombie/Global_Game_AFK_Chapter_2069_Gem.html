Chapter 2069

Gem

From original Chinese Chapter 2070 · 宝石

Forget it!

A small loss was a small loss.

After a moment's consideration, Fang Heng agreed to the condition. He was being hunted, after all. Getting the divine artifact first was more important!

Whatever he lost now, he could earn back later.

“All right,” he told Li Qingran. “Tell them I agree. But I'm still trapped in Duncha, so they'll have to wait until I leave before…”

“Wait, Fang Heng,” Li Qingran interrupted. “Don't forget that Rescue Chiboreya is a team mission. Only one team member needs to report to Mentor Gu Luo to submit it. We're already at the entrance to the Tower of Bones. If you agree, we can finish the mission at any time.”

Fang Heng opened his mouth.

Right!

He had so much going on that he'd forgotten about this benefit of team missions.

“Okay. When can they complete the trade?”

“Whenever you're ready. To ensure it's safe, we'll immediately sign a contract notarized by the Main God. As soon as Rescue Chiboreya is completed at over 1,000% contribution, the agreement will take effect automatically. The artifact will be delivered straight to the Main God Space, where you can collect it.”

She paused. “Once you approve, we can sign within ten minutes.”

“Good! Wait for me. I'll log in right now.”

…

Two days later, Fang Heng entered the game again.

The moment he opened his eyes, he looked toward a certain point behind him.

Trouble!

Almost as soon as he logged in, he felt a distant power detect and lock onto him.

The Divine Realm's pursuers!

They clung to him like glue. He couldn't shake them!

Frowning, Fang Heng transformed into a bat and took to the sky.

Game notifications appeared.

[Hint: During your offline period, your relocation of the Yin Vein Rift caused damage in Duncha, affected the Holy Court, and killed some of its members. Total rewards: 52,170,000 Undead Faction Reputation and 54,090,000 Undead Faction Honor Points. Exact totals cannot be calculated before the event ends; rewards are settled hourly.]

[Hint: Main mission Rescue Chiboreya progress has increased by 419%.]

[Hint: Your teammate Li Qingran has invited Zakaria and Lars to join the Rescue Chiboreya team. Accept?]

Accept!

[Hint: Li Qingran wishes to submit the mission to Mentor Gu Luo for early completion. Do you agree? If you choose early settlement, the system will calculate remaining unclaimed mission progress according to the least favorable plausible outcome for the player, estimating the maximum progress obtainable under those circumstances.]

Fang Heng frowned.

The mission was already approaching 2,000% completion. If the Yin Vein Rift remained active for another ten days or two weeks, perhaps even 5,000% would be within reach.

It was a shame to settle now.

But with the Holy Court's pursuers so close, he needed to finish the mission early. Otherwise, his own life might be at stake.

Accept!

[Hint: Your team has chosen to end the mission early.]

[Hint: Simulation in progress…]

[Hint: Additional progress awarded for Rescue Chiboreya: 1,622%.]

[Hint: Total mission completion: 3,577%.]

Shock!

Far away in the Punoze game world, Li Qingran and the two players who had just joined the team were stunned by the new notification.

More than 3,500% total completion?!

That was far higher than expected!

And this was after an early settlement, calculated according to the worst-case outcome permitted by the game's normal logic.

From experience, players knew that settling a mission early was usually a terrible deal. Even if they did nothing and simply waited until the final settlement, their completion would normally be at least a third higher than the Main God's prediction.

Lars and his companion exchanged delighted glances. This trade was turning out better than they could have imagined!

Before them, Mentor Gu Luo nodded with obvious satisfaction.

Not only had Fang Heng solved the Holy Court's attempted conquest of Chiboreya; he had counterattacked, redirected the Yin Vein into Duncha, crippled the Holy Court there, and turned its lower-world operations upside down.

The results had vastly exceeded Gu Luo's expectations!

He was deeply pleased.

The undead had a remarkable successor!

When it came time to award the rewards, Gu Luo was exceptionally generous.

He waved his hand, and dark light descended over the assembled players.

[Hint: Your Advanced Necromancy has been upgraded to Grandmaster-level Necromancy.]

Grandmaster!

The players were thrilled, their breathing quickening.

Fewer than ten players in the entire Necromancy discipline possessed Grandmaster-level academic mastery!

For ordinary players, this was practically reaching the heavens in one leap. Trading a relatively unhelpful artifact for this advancement was an incredible bargain!

Fang Heng also received notifications.

[Hint: Your team has completed early mission settlement.]

[Hint: Based on mission completion, you have received an additional 500,000 Main God Points.]

[Hint: Seek out Mentor Gu Luo to receive your Grandmaster-level Necromancy advancement and special rewards.]

[Hint: Your contract with player Lars has been fulfilled. The agreed item has been deposited in the Main God Space. Collect it yourself; after 24 hours, it may be lost.]

Excellent!

Fang Heng breathed deeply and nodded with satisfaction.

Mentor Gu Luo certainly knew what he needed: a direct gift of 500,000 Main God Points.

For his Grandmaster Necromancy advancement, he would still need to meet Gu Luo personally and receive his instruction.

Now, go get the artifact!

Fang Heng landed on the roof of a city building and returned to human form. His outline faded gradually into space.

Whoosh!

Entering the empty Main God Space, he reached out and caught the gem floating in the void.

The Illusion God's Joke was a palm-sized stone, so faint and translucent that it almost seemed to blend with the space around it.

[Hint: You have obtained The Illusion God's Joke.]

Not bad at all.

Fang Heng nodded privately.

Log out first!

Back outside the game, Fang Heng turned the artifact gem over in his palm.

Its power was remarkably well sealed. No energy leaked from its surface that he could detect.

He began studying its first ability, The Illusion God's Joke.

As he injected mental energy, a simulated character model appeared in his sea of consciousness.

It didn't take him long to understand the skill.

Essentially, he could create a new character identity, altering its appearance at will.

It was somewhat like the human-skin masks he'd used in the past, except this artifact offered far better disguise.

It could change not only the face but height and body build as well. No skill below divine rank could see through the false identity!

Unfortunately, the disguise lasted a maximum of 72 hours. When that time expired, the character disappeared completely.

He could create an identical identity afterward, of course, but all the previous identity's quest progress would be lost.

— End of Chapter 2069 —
