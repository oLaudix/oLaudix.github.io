Chapter 3123

Outrageous

From Chinese Chapter 3124 · 夸张

“Hm? What is it?”

Fang Heng asked, “Do you know how to advance Alchemy to the next stage, the God level?”

God level!

This kid was already thinking about the God level?

Nasir's expression changed slightly. He glanced around and warned him quietly, “Fang Heng, you absolutely mustn't talk about that openly from now on.”

“Hm?”

Nasir's expression turned grave. “Do you understand what the God level signifies?”

“What?”

“The Demigod level means approaching the power of the Divine Race. It's already subject to strict control. The God level, on the other hand, means possessing strength on equal footing with the Divine Race.”

“The Divine Race and its subordinates will never allow other races to wield abilities equal to theirs. Do you understand?”

Nasir assumed Fang Heng was a human from some universe who knew little about the wider world, so he patiently explained, “The Divine Race rarely appears in person, but its power still affects most people. Even if you genuinely intend to advance, you must never discuss it in front of outsiders.”

“Someone might use it against you.”

“All right. I understand. Thank you.”

Fang Heng nodded in thanks, but another thought suddenly occurred to him.

The God level meant power equal to the Divine Race?

Then what about the Super-God level in the game?

Power surpassing the gods?

After warning Fang Heng, Nasir lowered his voice even further. “Fang Heng, I'm actually the same as you. I want to uncover the deeper mysteries of alchemy. But with the restrictions imposed by our current circumstances, I'm afraid it'll be very difficult. I don't know how to break through to the God level either, though I'm certain it has something to do with the laws.”

“Your progress in alchemy is already far ahead of mine. If you discover a clue someday, please remember to let me know.”

“Of course.”

Fang Heng nodded in agreement. After considering it for a moment, he asked, “If the God level means having power equal to the gods, what about the Super-God level? Wouldn't that mean power surpassing the gods?”

Surpassing the gods?

Nasir stared blankly at Fang Heng for a long moment before answering, “Fang Heng, I've never even heard of a Super-God level. Don't ever talk like that in public! Where did you hear such a thing?”

“Haha.”

Seeing Nasir's reaction, Fang Heng laughed it off. “Just a random thought. I'd better get going.”

Nasir watched Fang Heng leave, but his thoughts refused to settle.

Just a random thought?

You certainly didn't look like you were merely thinking aloud!

Something felt off!

After leaving the cavern, Fang Heng immediately flew toward the starship, preparing to return to the Seven Realms Martial Capital ahead of the others.

The law-based magic arrays in the cavern could no longer give him any further insight toward breaking through to God-level Alchemy.

He still needed more time to grind his Alchemy experience to the maximum.

He would go back and take a look first.

Then consider advancing his Occultism.

…

At the Noel Clan corporate headquarters in the Seven Realms Martial Capital.

When Fang Heng returned from his month-long seclusion, Anxi had good news for him.

The Seven Realms Martial Capital's review committee had promoted his warrior ranking to high-tier.

This meant that, regardless of victories or defeats, he could participate in no more than three arena matches per month.

The Noel Clan had also improved his benefits.

As Mu Shunan happened to be available, Fang Heng went straight to him after returning by starship, eager to learn how to break through in Occultism.

Time to grind!

Not a moment to waste.

In the Noel Clan's ground-floor lobby, many warriors looked at Fang Heng with surprise and admiration as he appeared.

Word that Fang Heng and Nasir had jointly slain an evil god in the Lanyue Clan's territory had spread only among the Noel Clan's senior warriors. The lower-ranking warriors knew little about it.

But advancing directly from a low-tier warrior to a high-tier warrior in just one month was extraordinarily rare in the entire history of the Seven Realms Martial Capital.

And Fang Heng's record certainly merited it.

He was undefeated and had beaten two high-tier warriors in succession.

Among the admiring crowd, a few people in the corners also wore expressions of astonishment.

Fang Heng!

He really wasn't dead!

In fact, thanks to the Noel Clan's protection, the Kinsfolk had only a vague idea that the Lanyue Clan's operation against Fang Heng might have failed.

They didn't even know for certain whether he was alive.

Still, they'd considered his chances of survival extremely low.

Two warriors secretly recruited by the Kinsfolk exchanged looks and quietly slipped out of the crowd.

“Mr. Fang Heng, while you were away, there were multiple attacks targeting pan-human races. Many people have been killed. Please be extremely careful.”

Anxi knew perfectly well that publicly escorting Fang Heng through the Noel Clan headquarters would attract the Kinsfolk's attention, probably prompting them to target him again.

She'd previously urged him to be careful and even helped arrange another place to live.

Unfortunately, Fang Heng insisted on returning to his original villa.

As he put it, they'd find him sooner or later anyway, so there was little point in sneaking around.

Given that attitude, the Noel Clan elders decided to let Fang Heng return openly and stroll through the clan headquarters. They could use his appearance to identify which people within the Noel Clan were Kinsfolk informants.

It would be a useful chance to clean house.

Fang Heng knew about the Noel Clan's plan beforehand and agreed to cooperate.

In truth, he didn't want trouble either. Being able to cultivate in peace would have been ideal.

But moving house was just too much hassle.

It hadn't been easy to excavate such a huge basement.

Inside a quiet cultivation chamber.

Mu Shunan sat cross-legged. Sensing Fang Heng enter through the door, he opened his eyes and smiled, gesturing for him to sit. “Brother Fang Heng, you're back? How was it? Made some progress in alchemy?”

“Mm. A little progress.”

Hah!

Mu Shunan sucked in a sharp breath and looked Fang Heng up and down as if staring at a monster.

Good heavens.

He'd only asked casually. Had Fang Heng really made another breakthrough?

And the kid said it so casually! He'd already reached Transcendent-level Alchemy. If he'd made further progress beyond that…

Could he have reached the Demigod level already?

“Brother Mu, I actually came to consult you about the path to Demigod-level Occultism.”

Demigod-level Occultism!? Mu Shunan froze again and blurted out, “You've already reached the peak of the Transcendent level?”

“Yes.”

Fang Heng extended a hand, and a faint glow of occult runes bloomed over his palm.

“Ah, this…”

Mu Shunan's eyes widened at the occult runes flowing around Fang Heng's body.

What was going on with this kid?

Hadn't he said he'd stay with the Lanyue Clan to study alchemy?

How had his Occultism proficiency shot up so wildly?

True, Occultism was the foundation of most academic disciplines, and insights in one field could benefit another.

But surely an improvement of this magnitude was far too outrageous!
