Chapter 3005

Guardian

From Chinese Chapter 3006 · 守护者

"Hm? What did you say? Power of faith?"

Confusion appeared in the Titan Guardian's eyes as he looked at the Holy Court players standing before Fang Heng.

Under his gaze, Archbishop Whiteman suddenly felt an immense pressure bearing down upon him. His face paled, and even breathing became difficult.

"Hum..."

Sensing danger, his Sacred Shield automatically formed around him.

"Oh?"

The Titan Guardian looked puzzled when he saw the shield. After a pause, understanding seemed to dawn. With a note of regret, he said, "So it's a crude imitation of the gods. Humans, you've taken the wrong path. This is not the road the Titans should follow."

"Bang! Bang, bang, bang!"

The Guardian extended one finger and tapped lightly in the air. The shields surrounding Whiteman and the others shattered. They coughed blood and staggered backward.

The onlookers' expressions changed.

A Holy Court archbishop had been grievously injured by the Titan's most casual gesture.

The Guardian looked at them indifferently. "Is this how you acquired your strength? How regrettable. You chose false faith in pursuit of power and even attacked your own kin. You've disgraced the bloodline within you. You're unfit to remain in this world..."

Yi Qingzhi, leading the Eastern Federation party, hurriedly spoke up. "Titan Ancestor! That's not what happened! Mr. Fang—"

"Silence. No explanations. Destroying the seal only adds to your crimes."

The Titan lightly moved his finger toward Archbishop Whiteman, who stood before Fang Heng.

Whoosh!

An invisible ripple erupted from his fingertip!

Already internally injured and pinned in place by the Titan's pressure, Whiteman couldn't move. All he could do was sense a terrifying force racing toward him.

"Bang!"

The next instant, that force exploded in front of Whiteman. The resulting blast of air struck him and the surrounding Holy Court players head-on, making it hard to keep their eyes open.

Only when the wave dissipated did the oppressive force vanish. The Holy Court players gulped down fresh air.

Looking up, they found Fang Heng standing before them. He had blocked the Titan's attack.

"Grand Sage!"

Whiteman felt as though he'd just walked through the gates of death. He and the others bowed to Fang Heng in gratitude.

Without his intervention, that single attack would probably have killed them.

"Fall back."

Fang Heng left them with those words, looked up at the Guardian, and asked coldly, "You claim to be humanity's Titan ancestor. What proof do you have?"

The Titan Guardian was clearly surprised Fang Heng had blocked the blow. He frowned at the question. "What proof would you require?"

"Hah." Fang Heng's face hardened and killing intent surged from his body. "You want to be my ancestor? First see if you can survive me!"

Whoosh!

Fang Heng lightly pushed off the ground and sprang toward the Titan Guardian.

Humanity's ancestor?

Hah!

First he'd test how strong this thing really was!

The players watching from inside the hall were horrified.

Was Fang Heng trying to get himself killed? Ancestor or not, the giant was undeniably terrifying, and Fang Heng had actually attacked him first?

Yi Qingzhi, commander of the Eastern Federation team, stared intently with furrowed brows.

He'd gone mad!

Judging from all the information previously gathered by the Eastern Federation, the giant almost certainly was a Titan ancestor.

Fang Heng should have realized that too.

According to ancient records, the Titans had once been the largest clan in the universe.

Their strength was terrifying.

What on earth was this kid thinking?

Augustine of the Western Federation knew how powerful Fang Heng was. He quickly signaled his team to withdraw toward the metal door, ready to retreat at any moment.

When two immortals fought, ordinary people had better stay far away!

"Whoosh!"

Fang Heng rushed in, summoned his Sacred Greatsword, and struck the Guardian's petrified body with a heavy, dull impact.

"Bang!"

The spreading sacred ripples instantly produced a second explosion across the Titan's body.

Their blinding light forced the players to avert their eyes. When they looked again, the Titan appeared completely unharmed.

No effect?

The Guardian seemed to have suffered no injury at all. He abruptly raised his right hand.

"Thud!"

A heavy impact!

Nobody in the hall could see how the Titan had swung his palm. They heard only a dull blow, then saw Fang Heng struck by a massive slap. His Sacred Shield burst instantly!

A cloud of blood erupted amid the sacred light, and Fang Heng was hurled backward, drenched in blood!

Not good!

Whiteman's heart tightened.

Was the difference in strength insurmountable?

Wait!

The onlookers realized with surprise that Fang Heng hadn't lost control of his body. Even as he flew backward, he picked a landing point, bounced across the ground several times to disperse the impact, and adjusted his stance.

He looked miserable, blood seeping through his skin, but he clearly hadn't lost the ability to fight.

"Look! The Titan's body!"

Someone suddenly exclaimed in a hushed voice.

The others looked up. Where Fang Heng's Sacred Greatsword had struck the Titan, spiderweb cracks had appeared across the stone-like surface, threaded with golden lines.

The Titan Guardian stared at Fang Heng in disbelief.

The power of a human inheritor...

It could actually injure his body?

Whoosh!

Before the Guardian could think further, Fang Heng charged him again!

This time the Guardian took no chances. He locked his senses tightly onto Fang Heng and raised his right palm in advance.

"Whoosh!"

Huh?

The players froze. Fang Heng had disappeared from space in the middle of his charge.

Secondary Space Projection!

"Whoosh!"

As though he could see Fang Heng even within the projection, the Guardian swung his right arm, twisting his body as he slapped fiercely to his right.

"Bang!"

An explosive impact!

The Titan's slap knocked Fang Heng straight out of Secondary Space Projection and sent him flying again, blood mist erupting around him!

What the hell?

The players collectively gasped.

That had been Secondary Space Projection.

Fang Heng had already entered the projection, yet the Guardian's slap had knocked him right out of it.

The Titan could attack through Secondary Space Projection itself!

"Bang!"

Fang Heng was sent flying. His back struck the ice wall hard. He landed, braced himself with his hands, and rose quickly, a cold smile touching his lips.
