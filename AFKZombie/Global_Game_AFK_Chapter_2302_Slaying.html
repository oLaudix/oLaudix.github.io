Chapter 2302

Slaying

From original Chinese Chapter 2303 · 斩杀

They passed through the long corridor and finally entered the Prayer-Curse Temple's main hall.

What?!

Ladiard's pupils contracted as he stared at the figure standing in the center of the hall.

He recognized the man almost immediately!

"Fang Heng!"

The Leaf Clan's disastrous predicament had a great deal to do with the man before him!

But wasn't he dead?!

Fang Heng slowly turned, his gaze settling on Ladiard's group.

"Not bad..."

He murmured to himself.

Judging by the fluctuations in their blood energy, the Leaf Clan had sent one elder and two members approaching elder level.

If he killed them all, perhaps they'd drop something worthwhile.

"Fang Heng. It's you."

Ladiard stared at him, his guard rising to the utmost.

Something was strange. Fang Heng's aura was concealed extraordinarily well.

Ladiard hadn't sensed his presence until entering the hall itself.

"What have you done to the Prayer-Curse Temple? Where is everyone else?"

"Nothing much. I thought I'd pay an old friend a visit, and happened to come across a Prayer-Curse Temple."

Fang Heng glanced at Ladiard. "Never mind that. Since you're here, there's something I'd like to ask. About the Divine Tree's coordinate branches—I imagine the Leaf Clan still has some extras. What would I have to offer to exchange for a few?"

"Hmph! Stop playing games!" Ladiard snorted. "Since you've come here, you'll return to the clan with me!"

"Hiss..."

The next instant, several vines emerged from the ground and rapidly crept toward Fang Heng.

"Whoosh! Whoosh!"

At the same time, numerous magic arrays appeared around Fang Heng!

A swarm of Lickers erupted from them and charged the nearby Leaf Clan members.

"Careful! Don't let him escape!"

Ladiard had fought Lickers before and knew their strengths and weaknesses. He immediately assumed Fang Heng meant to use them to create a diversion and flee.

"Swoosh! Swoooosh!"

Huh?

Ladiard's heart skipped a beat.

What was happening?

The Lickers were moving incomparably faster than the last time he'd seen them!

"BANG!"

A natural-energy arrow pierced one Licker's heart and sent it flying backward, pinning it to a wooden wall. The creature writhed furiously.

It was actually beginning to free itself from the arrow!

Before Ladiard could consider what that meant, even more Lickers surged forward!

"Hiss! Hiss, hiss!"

The next moment, the Prayer-Curse Temple itself underwent a terrifying change!

Countless crimson wooden spikes erupted from the walls and every corner of the floor.

More vines kept spreading, crawling toward the Leaf Clan members!

Caught between the vines, spikes, and Lickers, Ladiard's group was immediately in trouble.

"Fall back!"

Ladiard suddenly recalled what Vannis had once experienced and grew more cautious.

When he'd first heard the account, he had scarcely believed it.

So it had all been true!

Fang Heng!

How had he secretly gained control of the Prayer-Curse Temple?

Ladiard pushed his protective barrier to its limit and escorted his group back out of the temple under the relentless assault of spikes, vines, and Lickers.

What?!

They had barely escaped the temple when Ladiard felt a sharp premonition of danger and looked up.

More than a dozen figures hovered high above the building.

Humans?

The fluctuations of blood energy emanating from them were remarkably similar to Fang Heng's!

The vampire princes raised their hands.

Not good!

Ladiard's pupils contracted.

From overhead, dense waves of crimson blood bombs rained down upon them!

"BOOM! BOOM! BOOOOM!"

Following Fang Heng's orders, the vampires had been waiting in ambush above the temple. The instant the Leaf Clan emerged, they unleashed an indiscriminate bombardment!

The high-intensity assault continued for an entire minute!

Ladiard and the two newly promoted elders struggled to raise defensive shields against the attack. Just as they prepared to retaliate, another thicket of spikes erupted from the ground around them!

At the same time, Lickers attacked from every direction!

Only now did Ladiard fully grasp the situation: they had fallen into a trap!

Fang Heng had ambushed them!

"Don't get tangled up with them! Follow me, we need to retre—"

"Whoosh!"

Before he could finish, Ladiard sensed an attack from his right. He instantly fired a highly condensed natural-energy arrow in that direction!

The arrow collided with an oncoming blood-energy spike and exploded!

Blood energy!

Fang Heng!

Ladiard fixed his gaze on the spot ahead.

Fang Heng's face emerged from the scattered blood energy.

With his mental strength more than doubled, Fang Heng could manipulate blood energy far more freely, and his attacks had grown correspondingly stronger!

His first strike had missed. Without pausing, Fang Heng held both hands out as though gripping something invisible.

The scattered blood gathered rapidly and condensed into a slashing blade that materialized in his hands.

"WHOOSH!"

Fang Heng swung the blade viciously forward!

"CRACK!"

Gritting his teeth, Ladiard raised another dark green shield to withstand the blow.

"BOOOOM!"

The shield lasted less than half a second before shattering. The resulting shock wave sent Ladiard and more than a dozen Leaf Clan guards behind him flying!

What was going on?

How had this brat suddenly become a completely different person?

Ladiard was visibly astonished.

He had witnessed Fang Heng fight several times and thought he understood his abilities. How could the man's strength have risen so dramatically in such a short time?

"WHOOSH!"

Still flying backward from the blade's impact, Ladiard saw Fang Heng turn into a streak of blood-red light and close on him again!

"BANG! BANG, BANG!"

Fang Heng burned through blood energy without regard for the cost, launching a relentless assault on Ladiard!

Cold sweat appeared on Ladiard's forehead.

He was actually beginning to lose the ability to defend himself!

Never had he imagined a human could force him into such a predicament.

"What?!"

His heart jolted as a vast curtain of blood energy formed ahead, spreading out to engulf him completely!

It looked as though the entire bloody curtain would envelop and devour him!

For a moment, everything before his eyes turned scarlet!

"Whoosh!"

Ladiard pressed his palms together and shouted, "EXPLODE!"

"BOOM! BOOM, BOOOOM!"

Violent natural energy erupted outward with Ladiard at its center!

The expanding blast scattered the surrounding blood energy in every direction!

Fang Heng, who had been advancing with a blood spear raised, was also forced back by the shock wave!

Seizing the brief respite, Ladiard finally put some distance between them. But before he could launch another attack, his brows shot upward.

— End of Chapter 2302 —
