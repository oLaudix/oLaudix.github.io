Chapter 2125

Taking Action

From original Chinese Chapter 2126 · 入手

What?!

The instant Fang Heng's eyes fixed on him, the guard was seized by a terrifying palpitation.

His heart pounded violently in his chest!

As though it was about to burst!

He felt his blood begin to boil, and his body slipped beyond his control.

Bang!!!

Inside the thick armor, the guard's body exploded uncontrollably!

Watching the armored figure slowly collapse, Fang Heng shook his head inwardly.

These so-called mechanically modified soldiers merely had some machinery implanted in their bodies. That enhanced some of their physical attributes enough to carry such thick external armor.

In reality, their flesh and blood were only marginally stronger than those of ordinary people.

The power of a Vampire Monarch could easily cause the blood inside them to explode.

Put simply, it bypassed the outer armor and damaged the body directly.

The twenty-odd semi-mechanized soldiers nearby had no idea what had happened. All they'd heard was a pained scream from their companion over the communication channel before he collapsed.

His inexplicable death frightened them. They stared at Fang Heng with growing fear and slowly backed away.

“Hmph!”

Fang Heng snorted.

In a high-tier game world, relying on Lickers alone wasn't enough to crush every opponent.

Whether Ji Haiting had been bluffing about how difficult the Federation reinforcements would be to handle didn't matter. There was no point wasting more time here.

Finish this quickly!

Fang Heng shifted his gaze to another fully armored mechanized soldier.

Under his stare, the semi-mechanized soldier's body exploded within his armor once again, bursting into a mass of blood and flesh!

The empty suit wobbled and fell to the floor.

The remaining semi-mechanized soldiers were dumbfounded.

What was going on?

No physical contact whatsoever—he'd just looked at their companion, and the man had died?!

How on earth was he doing it?!

The external alloy armor that had once given them such a comforting sense of security suddenly seemed as flimsy as paper!

What kind of ability was this?!

Cold sweat coated the squad captain's forehead. “Attack! Don't give him another chance to use that ability!”

The dozen or so remaining soldiers immediately charged Fang Heng.

But they quickly realized he moved far faster than they did.

Their heavy armor was still far too restrictive!

And in such a confined space, it made them even more clumsy!

Only moments later, the corridor was filled with empty, uncontrolled suits of armor.

Having swiftly eliminated the remaining semi-mechanized soldiers, Fang Heng used his vampire senses once more.

This time, Ji Haiting had already teleported much farther away. The trace of his presence had become extremely faint.

There were still plenty of signs of living blood and vitality throughout the research institute, though.

Never mind that.

First, find the dead Divine Tree offshoots and research materials Wanling Technology had collected.

Everything else could wait!

With that thought, Fang Heng used his senses to pinpoint several weaker life signatures inside a room of the research building, then strode onward.

· · ·

Wanling Technology Corporation's Northern District headquarters.

Ji Haiting and his psychically modified subordinates returned to a safe area through spatial transition.

When Ji Haiting emerged from the transition device, his face was pale.

Two consecutive high-intensity transitions had taken a tremendous toll on his body.

Without pausing to recover, he immediately ordered, “Quickly! Analyze that unidentified creature at once! I want every piece of information about it!”

“Understood!”

Meanwhile, outside Wanling Technology's Northern District research institute.

Federation military reinforcements had arrived ten minutes earlier.

“The institute has suffered an S-class incident. We've detected several sources of infection, so containment may prove troublesome. We've established isolation measures and evacuated personnel from the surrounding area...”

Boris listened to his subordinate's report and glanced at the cordoned-off building. Then he gave orders to the Federation emergency response teams that had arrived first. “The military is taking over the institute. I need you to establish a perimeter outside and keep all unauthorized personnel away.”

The emergency response team leaders exchanged glances.

What on earth had happened that required direct military intervention?

No reason to argue. Better get out of the way!

After dismissing the relatively weak Federation emergency teams, Boris was approached by another subordinate, who reported quietly, “Sir, our people are trying to enter the institute through the Area A entrance, but unidentified creatures are resisting them. We're having trouble breaking through.”

Unidentified creatures?

Boris frowned.

This incident had received the highest internal threat classification.

When he'd first received the report, he'd assumed it was an operation by the Tree Spirits, a disturbance caused by information thieves, or perhaps an experiment gone wrong.

But so far, there had been no trace of the Tree Spirits.

And what was this business about an attack by unidentified creatures?

“Assemble the troops. Guard the outer area and keep everyone away. Take me inside to have a look.”

“Understood!”

By now, Fang Heng had worked his way into the institute's underground laboratory area.

After dispatching several guards at the door, he followed the traces of blood vitality into a laboratory.

The instant he opened the door, gunfire erupted.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

More than a dozen researchers hiding inside opened fire on him.

Fang Heng had already sensed their presence. He didn't even bother to dodge, simply standing where he was.

Pfft! Pfft, pfft!

Although they weren't far apart, the untrained researchers were terrible shots. Only a few pale-green beams struck Fang Heng, leaving bloody holes in his body!

To the researchers' astonishment, the wounds made by the beams rapidly closed before their eyes!

“Quite the welcome you've prepared.”

Fang Heng closed the door behind him and glanced at the game notifications.

Another string of messages had appeared at just the right moment.

[Game Hint: Your zombie clone (modified Licker) has been hit by a beam attack...]

[Game Hint: Your zombie clone (modified Licker) has suffered 3,225 damage from a cutting attack and entered a Severed Limb state...]

Looks like the reinforcements outside had arrived.

He needed to speed things up.

While keeping part of his attention on the researchers, Fang Heng directed his zombie horde to block several of the institute's entrances and passages, delaying the reinforcements' entry.

“W-who are you? What do you want?!”

The researchers turned pale at the sight of his terrifying regeneration.

The weapons in their hands were utterly useless!

“I have a certain respect for intelligent people, so I'd rather not hurt any of you. I hope you'll cooperate.”

— End of Chapter 2125 —
