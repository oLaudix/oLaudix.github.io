Chapter 2139

Judgment

From original Chinese Chapter 2140 · 判断

Security there was several levels tighter than here!

Was this a diversion?

Had the theft of the Water of Life merely been a pretext to lure them away?

Interesting!

Combined with the earlier theft of so many dead Divine Tree offshoots...

Ji Haiting believed he could finally draw a conclusion.

The Tree Spirits!

Fang Heng was almost certainly working with them.

They wanted to break through the Spirit Domain's blockade!

Ridiculous!

Did they really think they could succeed so easily?

“Tell the central institute to enter the highest state of emergency. And activate the emergency spatial transition passage. I'm going there immediately.”

“Understood!”

Meanwhile, Fang Heng and Mo Jiawei had just left the research institute using spatial teleportation scrolls.

The institute's internal defenses had been much weaker than Fang Heng anticipated.

Thinking back on it, he felt he could have completed the mission using Lickers alone, without even summoning the Shadow Demon Rat.

A piece of cake.

The pair stood on a rooftop, looking toward the distant central technology building.

Fang Heng had absorbed every last barrel of Water of Life stored in the laboratory warehouse, raising his Advanced Nature Studies to Level 21.

Not a bad haul.

The next step, though, was a little troublesome.

According to the original plan, the Tree Spirits were supposed to help them withdraw and then return to town together.

Now the job was practically finished, and not a single Tree Spirit had shown up.

What were they playing at?

Mo Jiawei couldn't make sense of it either. He guessed, “Could the Tree Spirits have had bad luck and gotten caught up in the trouble somehow?”

“Mm. It's possible.”

“Huh? Look over there. That's strange. Why are they pulling out so quickly?” Mo Jiawei watched the area around the building, muttering, “Aren't they going to investigate properly...?”

Fang Heng followed his gaze. The Federation soldiers and Wanling Technology security teams outside the building were already withdrawing in groups.

“That really is strange.”

Fang Heng nodded as he watched the technology building. “Forget it. The mission's complete. Let's split up. I'll take the Water of Life back and hand in the quest.”

“Mm. I'll stay and observe a little longer, see if the Tree Spirits' contact turns up. After a while, I'll also try asking around outside to find out what's happening.”

“Good.”

After a brief discussion, Fang Heng shifted into bat form, leaped off the rooftop, and sped back toward town.

· · ·

After advancing his vampire bloodline to Monarch level, his flying speed in bat form had increased considerably.

Fang Heng made it back from the city to the small town in less than an hour.

Inside the temporary warehouse, Sandy was loudly discussing new discoveries with Klevi and the others, several books in his hands. When Fang Heng returned, he stopped and looked over.

“Back already? Did you get it?”

“Mm. I've got it.”

Fang Heng nodded, glanced at the excited Sandy, and walked toward the pile of dead Divine Tree offshoots in the warehouse. “You seem excited. Found something?”

“Heh. Quite a lot of information about the Inner World.”

Sandy hadn't slept for two nights straight. He'd spent the past two days studying the books he'd managed to obtain from the Tree Spirits. He was still bursting with energy and enthusiasm. “There's no way anyone could invent a world governed by so many intricate rules through pure imagination. I'm certain the Inner World the Tree Spirits speak of really exists.”

As he spoke, Sandy came closer. “But never mind that. Fang Heng, the Tree Spirits promised to come, but they still haven't shown up. Shall I find Mr. Yule and ask him to hurry them along?”

“No need to wait.”

Deep down, Fang Heng disliked the Tree Spirits' arrogance.

If they didn't come, so much the better!

He took a barrel of Water of Life from his backpack and looked at Klevi. “Klevi, give it a try.”

“Wow! So this is the Water of Life?”

Sandy's eyes lit up. He circled the barrel twice. “Why does this feel wrong? They put it in something like this? Doesn't that make it seem rather cheap?”

“All right.”

Klevi nodded obediently and stepped forward from Gu Qingzhu's side to examine the water in the barrel.

With some effort, Sandy pried off the lid.

As Klevi sensed the power of nature within the water, surprise flashed through her eyes.

“What's wrong?”

“Brother Fang Heng, something doesn't seem right.” Klevi hesitated and frowned slightly. “I'm not entirely sure. I'll need to try it.”

“That's fine. Go ahead. It doesn't matter if it fails.”

“Mm.”

Klevi nodded lightly and pointed at the barrel.

A layer of pale-green life energy enveloped it, lifting it slowly into the air. It floated toward the dead Divine Tree offshoot.

Until it reached a position directly above the tree.

Hiss... hiss, hiss...

The barrel gradually tilted.

The Water of Life poured slowly over the dead offshoot.

A faint hissing sound came from the tree.

Klevi stepped forward and placed both hands against the withered wood. Tiny spots of green light glimmered from her palms.

The glow continued for more than a minute.

Then Klevi withdrew her hands, shook her head, and looked at Fang Heng. “Brother Fang Heng, it failed.”

“Mm. Do you know why? Can only the Tree Spirits revive a Divine Tree offshoot?”

“That's possible, but there's something strange. The dead tree didn't react to the Water of Life at all.” Klevi frowned in thought. “I suspect the Water of Life has absolutely no effect on restoring these trees.”

“What? How could that be? It doesn't make sense!”

Sandy was the first to protest. He turned toward Fang Heng. “Boss Fang, don't tell me you were fooled? Are you sure this stuff is really the Water of Life?”

“No. There's nothing wrong with the Water of Life.”

Fang Heng lowered his head in thought.

The game had explicitly confirmed he'd obtained the Water of Life, and the quest was now marked as ready to submit.

So the Water of Life itself wasn't the problem.

Then where did the problem lie?

Either Klevi's assessment was wrong...

Or...

Fang Heng's eyes gradually sharpened.

The Tree Spirits who'd arranged to meet them still hadn't appeared.

It was hard not to connect the two things.

In all likelihood, the problem lay with the Tree Spirits!

Fang Heng asked, “Still no word from them?”

“Nothing.”

“What about Mr. Yule?”

“Mr. Yule seems perfectly normal. I just saw him walking around in the garden.”

Walking in the garden?

Fang Heng frowned, immediately sensing something amiss.

Given how deeply Yule respected the Tree Spirits, wouldn't he have rushed to welcome them if he'd known they were coming?

Yet he was strolling in the garden?

That was clearly abnormal.

“You stay here. Klevi, come with me. We're going to see Mr. Yule.”

“All right.”

— End of Chapter 2139 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Unsuccessful revival. Klevi attempts to revive an offshoot using Water of Life, but observes no reaction from the dead tree.
  • Suspicion. The failure, the absent Tree Spirits, and Yule’s unusually casual behavior prompt Fang Heng to investigate.

Chinese Expressions

  • 声东击西. Literally “make noise in the east and attack in the west”; Ji Haiting recognizes the headquarters raid may be a diversion.
  • 屁颠屁颠. An informal description of rushing around eagerly, almost obsequiously; Fang Heng expects Yule to greet the Tree Spirits with enthusiasm.
