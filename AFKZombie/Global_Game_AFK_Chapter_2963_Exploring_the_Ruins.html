Chapter 2963

Exploring the Ruins

From Chinese Chapter 2964 · 探索遗迹

Compared with their last meeting, Liu Haiyang's tone now carried several additional shades of respect, though even he hadn't noticed the change. “Boss Fang, unfortunately we can't use our inventories in this world, so we can't transport supplies that way.”

“That's fine. You can use the laboratories aboard the starship. Let's begin.”

“Very well.”

Liu Haiyang nodded and began directing the research team to establish a research station.

Fang Heng, meanwhile, carried on with his AFK grinding.

This world might be rather barren, but at least it had ordinary ores.

He might as well fill the starship's dozens of warehouses first.

Whenever he had free time, he simply closed his eyes, rested his mind, and steadily cultivated his god-level Necromancy.

They stayed for more than ten days.

After completing a short local jump, the starship hovered near the foot of a cliff.

The players jumped down from the ship's deck one after another.

Guided by an ancient map, the research team had carried out detailed surveys and finally determined the approximate location.

At the base of a vast mountain range.

It was such an obscure nook that the Lickers probably wouldn't have found it in a lifetime of searching.

After a careful search of the mountains, the vampires soon noticed something unusual.

“Your Majesty, please follow me.”

Everyone followed a vampire to a protruding section of cliff face.

Looking up, they saw marks imprinted upon the rough, jagged rock.

The researchers Liu Haiyang had brought immediately took photographs from every angle, archived their findings, and began using the images to decipher the ancient script.

Fang Heng watched quietly from the side.

Weeds grew thickly across the cliff. Strange, irregular marks crisscrossed large patches of rock.

But someone was claiming these were written characters?

Seriously?

After gathering evidence for quite some time, the researchers established a temporary camp at the base of the cliff. They spent another full two days discussing and analyzing the findings, and finally prepared to act on the afternoon of the third day.

Under everyone's gaze, Liu Haiyang approached a section of rock, cut his palm with a knife, and pressed his hand against the cliff.

Blood slowly trickled down the stone.

“Click!”

A faint glow rose from the cliff face.

The light blossomed.

“Click, click, click... Click, click, click...”

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

An alchemy magic array?

There really was one here?

The rock on either side gradually pulled outward, revealing a passage leading into the mountain.

“It worked!!”

Cheers immediately erupted from the surrounding researchers.

Fang Heng nodded to Prince Bulle beside him.

“Go inside and investigate.”

“Yes!”

Prince Bulle led several vampires inside first. Only after they confirmed there was no danger did the others file into the cliffside passage.

The cavern within was enormous.

Huge murals had been carved into every wall.

Most had been damaged, probably because of their immense age.

“Titans...”

Dr. Li Zhixuan looked up at the paintings and couldn't help murmuring to herself.

“Dr. Li, have you found something?”

“The fact that our blood opened the passage proves we really are descendants of the Titans...” Looking up at the murals, Dr. Li continued, “These paintings were probably left by the Titans. According to our records, the ancient Titan ancestors were roughly three times our size.”

“The Titans faced some kind of threat to their survival. To prevent their entire race from being wiped out, they scattered their tribal bloodlines before the disaster arrived, hoping their descendants might one day restore their former glory.”

Listening from the side, Zaka found the whole thing highly dubious. He curled his lip and muttered, “What's this supposed to mean? I came here to freeload a mission, and now you're telling me I've come to acknowledge my ancestors?”

Dr. Li Zhixuan glanced at Zaka and shook her head. “Whether you believe it or not, Titan blood runs through your veins, even if it's become very diluted.”

Fang Heng didn't care about that. “Is there any information about the inheritance?”

“I suspect it may be a power originating from the Titan bloodline... As the bloodline grew diluted, it gradually lost its original strength...”

Still studying the mural, Dr. Li Zhixuan continued, “The Ancient Humans possessed some of the Titans' memories and inheritance. They left behind certain methods for reawakening parts of the bloodline...”

A Titan inheritance?

That stuff really existed?

From the sound of it, it might be something that could increase their strength?

Wayne and the other players exchanged glances, suddenly interested.

“Everyone! Come look! Over here!”

A researcher had discovered something deeper in the cavern and called the others over.

Fang Heng followed the group and found a towering stone pillar standing at the back of the cavern.

Dense black patterns covered the pillar, and rows of sharp barbs jutted out from its sides.

Dr. Li Zhixuan examined it, exchanged looks with several researchers, and confirmed, “This looks very much like the inheritance artifact we're seeking.”

“Let me try.”

Liu Haiyang stepped up to the pillar, extended a hand, and tightly gripped one of the protruding barbs.

“Hiss...”

Liu Haiyang frowned slightly.

Fine spikes sprang from the barb and instantly pierced his skin.

Fresh red blood ran down the barb and onto the pillar, slowly trickling toward its base.

“Drip.”

Only then did everyone notice a hollowed-out section beneath the pillar, containing something resembling a stone cup.

Blood trickling down the pillar gathered drop by drop in the cup.

“Hum...”

Immediately, the entire pillar blossomed with a gentle glow.

Huh?

Was that...?

An energy reaction?

Something stirred in Fang Heng's heart.

A peculiar energy radiated from the pillar.

Very strange.

It didn't belong to any category of energy he'd encountered before.

It seemed more like a kind of attribute-less energy.

The energy entered his body and quickly flowed into his sea of consciousness.

Huh?

Fang Heng could clearly sense the power settling in his sea of consciousness, where his body could call upon it.

He simply didn't know how to use it yet.

At the most basic level, the energy could defend his sea of consciousness and automatically resist mental attacks.

How to use it actively was another matter.

As Fang Heng considered this, he suddenly heard a researcher nearby let out a moan of pleasure.

Huh?

Most of the researchers had never cultivated. Their development of the sea of consciousness was practically zero, and they certainly couldn't control it.

Looking more closely, was this energy actually transforming their bodies?

Before Fang Heng could try anything, Wayne shouted excitedly from nearby.

“Everyone! Guide the energy in your sea of consciousness through your body! It can improve and awaken your bloodline!”

Fang Heng immediately tried drawing the energy out of his sea of consciousness and directing it through his body's meridians.

The gentle energy was gradually transforming and strengthening both his sea of consciousness and his body.
