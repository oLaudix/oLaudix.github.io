Chapter 2163

All Set

From original Chinese Chapter 2164 · 搞定

Fang Heng turned toward Mo Jiawei. “What? What's done?”

“The blueprints! The blueprints for a barrier that blocks the Spirit Domain network! Didn't you get the Niam Alloy yesterday, only to find that you didn't have the construction drawings?”

Mo Jiawei grinned at Fang Heng, waggling his eyebrows with obvious pride. “You'll never guess where I got them.”

Fang Heng stared in surprise.

He truly hadn't expected Mo Jiawei to obtain the blueprints.

“Where?”

“Black Castle.”

Fang Heng's eyes lit up.

Of course! Black Castle!

They'd been investigating the Dependents' quest line all along!

“I suddenly remembered last night that Black Castle had previously been trying to enter a high-level game world belonging to the Dependents. This morning I tracked down their contact and exchanged some of our information on the Dependents with them. What a coincidence—their database had just been updated, and a large portion of the new material concerned the Spirit Domain blockade network!”

Mo Jiawei laughed triumphantly and waved the drawings in his hand. “We've got the blueprints, and I checked the required construction materials too. We can obtain all of them.”

Sandy was the first to whoop with excitement. “Boss Mo, you're incredible!”

“Oh, and one more thing. Black Castle's mission also requires a barrier made of Niam Alloy. They asked whether we could lend ours to them after we're done with it, if that would be convenient. I also looked into the alloy. It can absorb electromagnetic forces and block most spiritual-energy detection. There's always demand for it on the market, but almost no supply…”

“Ahem, there's more. The finished barrier is bigger than we'd expected, so it can't pass directly through a teleportation passage. I talked to Victor before coming here. The best option is to have him teleport here and construct it on-site. We'll need to buy a factory. With everyone out there hunting us, maybe we should buy a small island instead, dig underneath it, and…”

…

Ten days later.

Divine Tree Abe Akaya had completed the dispersal of its own power, condensing its inheritance into a final seed. The seed passed through the teleportation passage into the Forest of Myriad Trees and was stored in Fang Heng's backpack.

Because Abe Akaya's power had completely dissipated, the teleportation channel between the Zombie Apocalypse and the Forest of Myriad Trees had temporarily ceased functioning.

An abandoned factory stood on the coast.

Mo Jiawei had spent some time purchasing it a few days earlier.

Victor had organized personnel to perform the final assembly of the Niam Alloy barrier there.

In fact, they had already built and tested a simulated version of the alloy barrier inside the prison, then rebuilt it at the factory. That had spared them a great deal of unnecessary trouble.

“Fang Heng, construction of the alloy barrier is complete. Next we need some additional testing to ensure that it functions properly.”

Victor calculated the range and strength of the special Niam Alloy barrier.

“The barrier does more than shield us from external magnetic-field probing and prevent our energy signatures from escaping. It also simulates the surrounding environment's energy field. That process will happen gradually after activation. At the moment it switches on, though, the energy within the barrier will suddenly disappear from the Spirit Domain blockade network's readings. There's a strong chance the Federation will notice. We should prepare in advance.”

“Mm. Understood. Let's begin.”

Fang Heng had already prepared for that possibility.

The Federation might detect the barrier and use the Spirit Domain network to warp troops directly over to investigate.

His job would be to buy time for Gu Qingzhu and the others to leave with the Niam Alloy barrier once it had finished simulating the surrounding energy field.

With that in mind, Fang Heng patted his cheeks and prepared for battle.

“Prepare to activate the defensive barrier.”

Victor raised a hand toward several technicians. “Beginning countdown… five… four…”

“Bzz…”

As the countdown ended, a dark-colored barrier appeared, enveloping the entire abandoned factory.

“Activation successful. Frequency calibration underway. The Niam Alloy barrier is automatically simulating the surrounding environmental energy field…”

Time passed, second by second.

Three minutes later, lines of game text appeared on Fang Heng's retina.

[Hint: Because the player has constructed a Niam Alloy barrier without authorization, some of the player's current quest lines may be affected in unpredictable ways.]

[Hint: An abnormal, unidentified genetic sequence exists within the player's body, causing changes to the game line and fluctuations in the overall game difficulty…]

[Hint: Compensation for increased game difficulty. The player has triggered a side quest — Pursuit by the Dependents.]

Quest Name: Pursuit by the Dependents.

Quest Description: While reviewing the Ouroboros incident, the Dependents discovered Ouroboros genetic fragments left on the battlefield. They are now investigating the viral incident. Your existence has attracted their attention, and they will conduct targeted screening to locate you.

Quest Description: The Dependents possess technology beyond the current world's technological level and have a very high chance of locating the player through unknown means. Exercise caution.

Quest Requirement: Escape pursuit by the Dependents. For every eight hours you survive in the Forest of Myriad Trees, you will accumulate one increase in the quest reward. If the player dies during this quest, the player will lose access to the Forest of Myriad Trees and the quest will be settled immediately.

Huh?

A quest involving the Dependents?

Fang Heng frowned at the unexpected flood of game notifications.

This wasn't good…

…

Federation Central Command.

“Report!” An operator frowned at the warning data flashing across the screen. “Sir, we just detected anomalous readings from the Spirit Domain blockade network. At coordinates 198233, 239454, energy in the area appears to have abruptly vanished from detection. We strongly suspect the initial activation of a Niam Alloy barrier.”

Since confirming the theft of the Niam Alloy, the Federation had been closely monitoring for signs of such a barrier, determined to investigate any anomaly.

“Deploy a warp reconnaissance team immediately.”

“Understood!”

The Federation's war machinery sprang into action, warping personnel to the vicinity of the detected anomaly.

“I want the records for that area.”

“Report! It's a coastal industrial zone. The factory there belonged to Alet Company and was purchased outright by an unknown firm just a few days ago. Its registered business is kitchenware.”

As he spoke, the main screen switched to an image of the coastline.

It looked perfectly peaceful.

A Federation semi-mechanized squad arrived on the battlefield through a spatial jump.

“Command, this is Team C. We've reached the operational area and are searching it. No suspicious targets observed so far.”

(End of chapter)
