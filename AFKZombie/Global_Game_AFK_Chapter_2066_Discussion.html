Fang Heng scrolled through several more pages.

Much of the remaining discussion concerned him. After all, the zombie hordes that had appeared in both Chiboreya and Duncha the day before were impossible to ignore.

Judging by the information circulating on the forums, players had also concluded that Fang Heng must be closely connected to the Yin Vein incident.

“Even ordinary players have figured it out…” he murmured.

He'd heard that the Holy Court was negotiating with the Central Federation, trying to have him taken away for investigation under some criminal charge.

The Federation hadn't approached him yet, probably because Zombie Apocalypse's vampire world was under his control. For the moment, it was holding back.

Fang Heng sighed and considered the situation.

The Holy Court was serious this time. Duncha was dangerous. He needed to leave as soon as possible.

But operating outside the game made that difficult.

Maybe he should check the shady website Old Black had given him. He could post a commission there.

With that in mind, Fang Heng pressed the secret magnetic card against his laptop.

Crackle…

The display broke into static before returning to normal.

But before he could log into the site, his phone rang again.

“Fang Heng.”

“Lord Dani.” Fang Heng was surprised to hear her voice. “Aren't you fighting the Holy Court in Chiboreya?”

“No. So I guessed correctly—you still don't know what happened in the game.”

On the other end, Dani shook her head and summarized the events of the previous night's battle.

She had led the undead faction's armies against the Holy Court's main forces in Chiboreya. Fang Heng's enormous zombie army had wreaked havoc behind enemy lines, delaying reinforcements and repeatedly forcing the Holy Court to divert troops from the front.

As a result, the Holy Court's commander-in-chief, Georgi, came under tremendous pressure.

Dani's undead army fought brilliantly, pushing the enemy backward in one engagement after another.

By the early hours of the morning, the Holy Court's frontline legion had been caught between Dani's troops and the zombie hordes surging in from the rear!

Despite his reluctance, Georgi made the painful decision to sacrifice part of his army to hold the line while he led the rest of the main force in a desperate breakout.

This should have been Dani's finest opportunity.

The undead players were fired up. They swept aside the Holy Court soldiers left to fight to the death and launched a furious pursuit!

But something unexpected happened first.

Fang Heng's zombies, arriving from behind the front, blocked their route.

The creatures were packed so densely that every road ahead had become completely impassable.

That delay cost Dani and the undead their best chance to annihilate the Holy Court's main force.

Even now, enormous numbers of Fang Heng's zombies were still standing there, unmoving.

They wouldn't attack undead-faction players, but they wouldn't get out of the way either!

The masses of zombies had effectively turned into a protective barrier for the Holy Court.

The undead players had no choice but to halt the offensive and discuss what to do. Assuming the zombies were following some special plan Fang Heng had arranged, they decided to wait and see.

And by waiting, they missed their chance to pursue the enemy altogether.

They were still waiting now.

Dani had finally reached Mo Jiawei, who put her in contact with Fang Heng.

Fang Heng opened his mouth in disbelief.

Was that even possible?

Then he remembered.

There were certainly tens of millions of mutated infected zombies gathering in the ant-race world. But most of them couldn't make it to the teleportation entrance in time.

He had estimated that, even optimistically, getting a tenth of them into the main battlefield would be an achievement.

Still, that was far too many zombies to control individually. He had simply issued a broad attack order: advance in one direction and attack anything belonging to the Holy Court faction.

Then he'd gone to Duncha. The zombies left in Chiboreya had continued mechanically following that original instruction.

And now they had accidentally obstructed Dani's advance.

“Ahem.”

Knowing he'd caused this problem unintentionally, Fang Heng cleared his throat awkwardly.

“That was my mistake. But I'm afraid I can't fix it right now. I've run into some trouble in Duncha, and I may be trapped there for a while.”

Dani already knew the general situation. She paused. “Do you need help?”

“If you can help, that would be great.”

He quickly explained his predicament. “The Divine Realm has sent pursuers after me. Duncha doesn't have a teleportation passage I can use, so I need to get back to Zombie Apocalypse as soon as possible—or anywhere else, really. I just need to leave Duncha.”

Dani frowned. Fang Heng was attracting ever greater trouble. Even the Divine Realm had spent considerable resources sending people after him.

After thinking it over, she realized how difficult the problem was.

“As far as I know, every super-large spatial teleportation passage in Duncha is controlled by the Holy Court. They'll be on high alert now, guarding all the passageways to prevent you from leaving.”

“Exactly,” Fang Heng groaned. “That's the problem…”

“I'll see what I can do. I'll contact you immediately if I learn anything.”

“Thanks.”

After hanging up, Fang Heng resumed browsing the shady site Old Black had provided.

Here, too, news about the Holy Court and the undead dominated the forums. But the hottest topics were the Demon Seed and the Yin Vein.

Some posters argued that a player could never control a Demon Seed, so the Holy Court's story must contain a fundamental error.

Others circulated rumors about a Demon Seed descending into the real world, claiming its arrival would bring about sweeping changes to reality itself.

In short, everyone was debating whether Fang Heng truly was a Demon Seed.

Separately, the consensus on the forums was that the Yin Vein had been part of a secret undead plan stretching back tens of thousands of years. Its sudden release supposedly meant the undead had finally finished their preparations.

They had kept their strength hidden, and when they finally struck, the blow would be fatal!

The undead were about to rise. Next, it would be the Holy Court's turn to suffer!

What complete nonsense, Fang Heng thought.

He skimmed a few more posts, then published an anonymous commission seeking a way to leave Duncha.

He offered ten Universe Crystals as payment. Among players, those crystals were exceptionally valuable hard currency.

Once the commission was posted, Fang Heng shut his laptop and lay back in bed.

His nerves had been stretched tight since the previous night. The Demon Seed had also tormented both his body and sea of consciousness. Now, at last, he could relax and rest for a while.

His head had barely touched the pillow when he fell fast asleep.

— End of Chapter 2066 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Forum claims. The claim that the undead planned the Yin Vein for tens of thousands of years is forum speculation, not established narration.
  • Universe Crystals. 宇宙结晶 is translated Universe Crystals, the hard currency Fang Heng offers for help escaping Duncha.
  • Zombies blocking allies. The zombies do not attack undead players but also do not move aside; the accidental obstruction is explicit in the Chinese.
