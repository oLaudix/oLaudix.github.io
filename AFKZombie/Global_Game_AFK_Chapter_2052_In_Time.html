Chapter 2052

In Time

From original Chinese Chapter 2053 · 来得及

Chu Yan saw a progress bar appear above Fang Heng's head. Nervously swallowing, he took an involuntary step backward.

“Boss Fang, Holy Court reinforcements could arrive at any time. Do we have enough time?”

“Mm. Take your people outside. Use the confusion to see if you can find the stone inscription. I'll come meet you afterward. Don't worry about me.”

“All right!”

Chu Yan knew he couldn't help here. It was better to take advantage of the chaos and look for something valuable—particularly the stone inscription used to train Crusaders.

He hurried out of the Temple of Zeus.

Fang Heng kept his attention on modifying the magic array.

His zombie clones were creating plenty of trouble outside. And this was the Holy Court's own headquarters cathedral, so its defenders probably wouldn't use weapons capable of causing large-scale destruction here.

The zombies should be able to hold them off for a while.

Besides, the enormous stone wouldn't fit in his backpack.

After coming all this way, it'd be a waste not to profit from it somehow.

If he couldn't take it home, he would simply use its power right here!

The Holy Court had already laid the groundwork for activating the energy inside the Luminous Grating Stone. All he needed to do was improve the extraction efficiency.

Put simply, he would turn up the operating power.

All the way to maximum!

Time passed. Three minutes later, the progress bar filled.

[Notice: You have successfully modified the magic array.]

Excellent. Done!

Whoosh!

A rotating magic array appeared in Fang Heng's right pupil, mirroring the array he'd just finished on the ceiling.

Brilliant golden light erupted from the crystal!

The enormous magic array began operating at astonishing speed, producing a shrill hum that made the air itself seem to tremble.

Fang Heng immediately jumped down from the Fusion Tyrant's shoulder.

Standing directly under the yellow crystal, he closed his eyes again and attempted baptism beneath the intensified sacred light.

It works!

With the crystal's power activated to its greatest extent, the warm sacred energy flowing into his body increased enormously.

[Notice: You are attempting to enter the Baptism state…]

Streams of warmth poured into him and raced directly toward his sea of consciousness.

The gray mist entrenched there reacted immediately and surged toward the invading sacred energy.

Devour it! Destroy it!

The power of the Demon Seed of Envy still annihilated the sacred energy with ease.

Yet Fang Heng could feel the black mist itself being slowly worn down.

This time, the weakening was significant enough that he could actually sense it!

That was a major improvement.

[Notice: Due to an unknown influence, the power of the Demon Seed within you has been weakened. Remaining time before Demon Seed control increased by 28 hours…]

Still not enough! Keep going!

As the Demon Seed destroyed each new wave of sacred energy inside Fang Heng's consciousness, still more of the surrounding sacred power was stimulated and drawn toward his body.

…

Meanwhile, inside the solemn and sacred Holy Court cathedral, the believers' prayers were abruptly interrupted by a commotion.

Their chanting stopped, replaced by screams and frantic cries.

The atmosphere filled with unprecedented tension. Chaos swept through the cathedral!

No one knew where the Lickers had come from.

They suddenly poured out from corners all over the building, darting through the crowds with terrifying agility. Every sweep of their claws claimed another believer's life.

The worshippers fled in every direction.

Most of them had no ability to defend themselves, and the Lickers slaughtered them with ease. As each victim fell, a flesh cocoon immediately began forming over the corpse.

New zombies crawled from the cocoons one after another!

Within moments, the number of zombies inside the cathedral increased dramatically. They formed great surging currents that swept across the Prayer Hall.

Everything was happening too quickly.

Using the believers' bodies as nourishment and incubation sites, the Lickers had rapidly expanded the first wave of undead troops.

The Holy Court guards in the Prayer Hall reacted as soon as they noticed the attack. They rallied together and established a defensive line.

The cathedral's sacred guard corps was better equipped and more powerful than an ordinary paladin unit. They swiftly secured the entrances to the Prayer Hall and began advancing inward, clearing the zombies section by section.

…

Archbishop Silufi had been praying on the second underground floor, listening to the latest instructions from the Divine Domain.

When she heard the disturbance outside, she frowned. An ominous feeling prompted her to break off the communication and investigate.

“What happened?”

She opened the door and found several inquisitors gathered outside.

They stopped speaking among themselves and respectfully reported.

“Archbishop, the cathedral's Prayer Hall has been attacked by Lickers and zombies. We suspect Fang Heng is involved. We organized a response immediately, and the situation in the Prayer Hall is now preliminarily under control.”

“Fang Heng?!”

Concern flashed across Silufi's face.

He'd actually come to the cathedral?!

Then anger rose within her.

The information she'd just received from the Divine Domain strongly implicated Fang Heng in the appearance of the Yin Vein fissure in Duncha. He was probably the principal culprit.

The Divine Domain had already issued its highest-level wanted order for him.

They hadn't even had a chance to go looking for Fang Heng, and now he'd come straight to them!

Did he think the Holy Court was so easy to trample over?

“Where are the cardinals?”

“The three cardinals immediately began clearing the undead. Fang Heng himself hasn't been located.”

Silufi nodded inwardly. With three high-ranking cardinals handling the situation, stabilizing the Prayer Hall should be no problem.

But how had the undead appeared inside the cathedral in the first place?

An inquisitor seemed to notice her confusion.

“According to several Holy Court–aligned otherworlder players, World Lord Fang Heng summoned these undead. The zombies first appeared inside the Temple of Zeus. We suspect he may still be there.”

“Send people to guard the lower levels. We must not let him escape this time!” Silufi ordered.

“I'll go to the Temple of Zeus personally.”

“Understood!”

She led her followers toward the temple on the upper floors. When she reached the first level and saw the Prayer Hall in complete chaos, her brow tightened further.

— End of Chapter 2052 —
