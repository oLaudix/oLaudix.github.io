Chapter 2227

The Kill

From original Chinese Chapter 2228 · 击杀

Whoosh!

What?!

The Tree Spirit concentrating on the magical exchange from the shadows saw a sudden blur. A figure had appeared right in front of him.

Fang Heng swung his fist.

BANG!

Caught off guard, the Wood Clan guard took the punch square in the face.

Huh?

Fang Heng felt something strange.

It was like striking solid granite. A tremendous shock traveled back through his fist.

THUD!

The Wood Clan guard staggered backward from the blow.

That tough?

Fang Heng was surprised.

He'd expected to send the guard flying with a single punch!

But then saplings and roots sprouted rapidly from the guard's body!

Swish! Swish, swish!

The roots shot toward him!

A massive sacred greatsword materialized in Fang Heng's hand. He slashed forward with all his strength.

CRACK!

The blade severed the approaching roots!

Pushing lightly off the ground, Fang Heng leaped again and closed in on the retreating guard.

This time, he didn't throw another punch. He reached out and seized the Wood Clan member.

Whoosh!

He lifted the guard into the air and hurled him high toward the rear of the warehouse!

Overwhelmed by Fang Heng's physical strength, the guard sailed through the air.

In an instant, the Wood Clan member was exposed to the Psychic Studies squad behind Fang Heng!

“Now! Take him down!”

Shi Yanwu shouted as soon as he saw the opening.

After wasting so much time exchanging ranged attacks from the perimeter, Dr. Fang had finally created an opportunity for them!

Dozens of psychic-piercing attacks converged on the airborne guard!

Unable to move while flying through the air, the Wood Clan member took the full barrage.

His face turned deathly pale under the dozens of simultaneous psychic strikes. He fell into continuous dizziness, and his HP was emptied in seconds!

[Hint: The player has assisted the Federation team in killing a Wood Clan guard.]

[Hint: While the player is anonymous, killing Tree Spirits will not affect favorability or contribution with the Tree Spirit clans. Revealing the player's identity will affect those values. Please choose carefully.]

“Excellent!”

Fang Heng shouted and repeated the maneuver. He dashed toward the next nearest Wood Clan member, reached behind the cover, grabbed him, and flung him high through the air!

Another barrage of concentrated psychic-piercing attacks came from the Federation squad!

The airborne Wood Clan member had no way to resist, and his HP was quickly reduced to zero!

“Stop him!”

The remaining Wood Clan members realized Fang Heng was a deadly threat and immediately tried to intercept him.

“Pierce!”

Wooden spikes erupted from the ground beneath Fang Heng!

Whoosh!

But Fang Heng was astonishingly fast! His perception far exceeded that of an ordinary player, and he dodged before the spikes had even finished forming!

Ordinary spells couldn't keep up with him!

Even if they connected, they would struggle to inflict meaningful damage!

After less than two minutes of fighting, the Wood Clan squad leader watched several more of his people get thrown into the air and finished off by the Federation soldiers.

“Retreat!”

Realizing things were going badly, the squad leader gritted his teeth. Humiliating though it was, he had to order a withdrawal to preserve his remaining troops.

“Trying to run? Stop them!”

The Federation soldiers had been made to struggle for long enough. Their opponents were almost exhausted.

And now they wanted to escape?

Shi Yanwu had no intention of giving up. He charged forward with his team and launched concentrated psychic attacks, bringing down two more Wood Clan members.

He was about to continue the pursuit when Fang Heng placed a hand on his shoulder.

“Captain Shi, let's not chase them.”

“Hm?”

Because of the adverse effects of psychic tempering, most Psychic Studies practitioners had relatively weak constitutions and weren't suited for pursuits. Shi Yanwu hadn't seriously intended to chase them far; he'd mainly wanted to see whether they could catch anyone.

After all, there were Federation semi-mechanized units positioned outside.

Perhaps they could intercept another one or two?

“Don't rush. If we leave the line out, we might catch a bigger fish.”

Shi Yanwu stared briefly, then nodded.

“All right.”

He didn't really understand what Fang Heng meant by catching a bigger fish, but it scarcely mattered now.

The Psychic Studies squad's trial battle had been an unqualified success.

He'd observed every detail of his men's performance.

Their combat strength had soared.

Even without Fang Heng, he was confident the squad could eventually have defeated the dozen or so Tree Spirits on its own.

Fang Heng's intervention had merely shortened the process dramatically.

Shi Yanwu reported immediately over the radio. “Command, the Tree Spirits have been routed. The warehouse is secure. Request further instructions!”

A reply soon arrived over the command channel.

“Team C reporting! We've located the escaping Tree Spirits. The enemy has broken through our encirclement at Point C.”

“All squads, do not pursue. Maintain your positions and stay alert.”

“Understood. The Tree Spirits have withdrawn from town. The town is now completely sealed, and our squads are clearing out the remaining Tree Whisperers Alliance resistance.”

“Captain Shi, good work. Guard your current position. A search team will enter the warehouse shortly to assist in searching it.”

“Understood!”

In just five minutes, the battle had ended with the Tree Spirits fleeing.

Fang Heng quickly reviewed the fight in his mind.

There hadn't been a single elder present, yet dealing with these ordinary Tree Spirits had still required considerable effort.

If the enemy had been more numerous, the battle would have been even tougher.

Perhaps this was the true difficulty of a high-tier game world.

Good!

They ought to be strong!

The stronger, the better!

Otherwise, how would he use the World of Forests to fight the Holy Court?

And it wasn't as though they were impossible to defeat.

Hm?

As Fang Heng emerged from a rear corner of the warehouse, something glittering on the floor caught his eye.

An evolution crystal?

He instinctively waved a hand.

The sparkling crystal on the floor flew into his palm.

[Hint: The player has obtained an item—Prismatic Crystal ×1.]

An item for advancing to level 41!

Its drop rate seemed fairly low.

They'd killed five Tree Spirits and only one had dropped.

Fang Heng looked down in thought.

Perhaps he could take this opportunity to raise his character level again.

“Search the place.” Shi Yanwu waved his soldiers forward to thoroughly inspect the warehouse. Seeing Fang Heng lost in thought, he approached and asked quietly, “Dr. Fang, are you all right?”

“I'm fine.”

Fang Heng snapped out of his thoughts and waved a hand, then looked toward the exit.

He'd had an ulterior motive for letting some Wood Clan members escape.

Before they left, he'd secretly marked them with vampire tracking marks.

He could follow them whenever he wanted.
