Chapter 3118

Let's Play

From Chinese Chapter 3119 · 耍耍

Had he been wrong again?

Was the race of gods actually connected to the Holy Court?

But in terms of strength alone, the Holy Court's second-highest combatants, the Grand Sages, were only comparable to the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's strongest mid-ranking warriors.

By that reasoning, the God Kings were probably only on par with its strongest high-ranking warriors.

Yet according to Nasir and the others, the power of the race of gods was legendary. They could destroy heaven and earth with a single punch or kick.

Even an ordinary member of the race would be far more terrifying than a God King.

The gods could supposedly destroy the entire Ouroboros Seed species with ease.

But Fang Heng remembered that in the Divine Realm, the God Kings had battled newborn Ouroboros Seeds bred by the Breeders for centuries without reaching a decisive outcome.

Something didn't add up about their relative power levels.

Fang Heng asked, "Do the gods also possess this kind of power?"

"Huh? You didn't know?"

Nasir was taken aback. Seeing that Fang Heng wasn't pretending, he nodded. "The gods are born with divine bodies. They can perceive laws and wield every type of power, including some that ordinary people cannot master. For instance, that sacred power of yours is supposed to be exclusive to them."

"Hmm..."

Fang Heng nodded slowly.

He'd noticed something before.

In the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, almost every academic discipline had a counterpart identical to the one in the game.

Except for three.

Sacred Studies, Hell Studies, and Necromancy.

None of those three disciplines appeared among the courses offered in the capital, nor had he ever seen an arena warrior use them.

At the time, Fang Heng hadn't thought much of it. He assumed Sacred Studies was related to faith.

Perhaps the Holy Court hadn't managed to spread its religion and deception across the wider universe.

And perhaps the powers of Hell and the Death Realm simply hadn't spread through the universe either.

Now it seemed things weren't so simple.

Nasir warned him, "Brother Fang Heng, I don't know what happened to you, but I'd advise you not to use that power in front of outsiders from now on. It could bring you a great deal of trouble."

"Thank you. I understand."

Fang Heng focused on the distant Evil Embryo again.

The sacred barrier could resist its spreading tendrils, but its energy was slowly being depleted.

As more tendrils approached, the rate of depletion would increase.

Nasir had evidently thought of that too. He asked quietly, "Brother Fang Heng, how long can you maintain that technique?"

"Hmm... In theory, if this is its only method of attack..."

Fang Heng examined the Evil Embryo and the shadow tendrils outside the barrier as he considered his options. "Probably a dozen years or so."

"Cough! Cough, cough..."

Nasir nearly choked.

Good grief. He could maintain it for more than ten years?

Then what the hell was there to be afraid of?

Mu Shunan might have his shortcomings, but his ability to flee was second to none. Once he escaped and called for help, the Noel Clan would definitely send a rescue team.

Forget ten years; reinforcements should arrive within ten hours.

Even if they then spent another ten days figuring out how to get in, surely they could rescue the pair somehow?

They were practically guaranteed to survive!

After struggling to speak for a while, Nasir finally caught his breath and exclaimed, "Brother Fang Heng, what an incredible technique."

"It just happens to counter this creature."

Fang Heng rubbed his chin, took a stack of sealing rune papers from his backpack, and flung them forward.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!"

The papers burst apart in midair, releasing more than a dozen Lickers.

Nasir had been contemplating simply waiting for rescue. When he saw Fang Heng's next move, his heart jolted.

Sealing Studies rune papers?

And summoned creatures too.

For a moment, Nasir looked at Fang Heng very strangely.

How bizarre.

Was there anything this brat *couldn't* do?

Ordinary disciplines were one thing, but he even knew the Sacred Studies of the gods?

Could he be an illegitimate child of the divine race?

The shadow tendrils immediately spread toward the Lickers.

Fang Heng raised a hand.

Sacred light blossomed.

A group Sacred Protection skill enveloped the Lickers.

The Lickers switched into their Sacred Studies forms and charged forward, each surrounded by a sacred shield.

"Hiss, hiss-hiss!!"

As soon as the dark tendrils approached a Licker, they were repelled by the sacred shield around it, giving off a hissing sound.

The Lickers didn't slow down. They continued racing toward the Evil Embryo's mountain of flesh!

Sensing the threat, the Evil Embryo's enormous body repeatedly expanded and contracted while the eyeballs on its surface opened and closed.

"What powerful summoned creatures!"

Nasir stared at the Lickers approaching the Evil Embryo, admiring them inwardly.

They moved incredibly fast!

Although the Evil Embryo's illusions occasionally paralyzed them and forced them to stop, the Lickers renewed their charges the instant they recovered.

As the threat approached, the Evil Embryo withdrew its solid tendrils from the void. They suddenly opened out, twisted together in spirals, and formed dense clusters of sharp, fleshy spikes.

"Hiss! Hiss-hiss-hiss!!!!"

Countless fleshy spikes whipped through the air.

Attack!

As the Lickers entered range, the spikes lashed furiously toward them!

The Lickers moved with astonishing agility, darting back and forth among the wildly swinging spikes and attempting to evade the attacks.

Most strikes missed, but whenever a Licker froze under the illusion's effects, the spikes seized the opportunity to pierce its body!

The spikes were extraordinarily sharp. A successful hit broke through the Licker's outer defenses and drove deep into its flesh.

"Glug, glug..."

Like the Evil Embryo's blood vessels, the spikes ravenously drained nutrients from the Lickers' bodies.

[Hint: Your Licker has entered a restrained state.]

[Hint: Your Licker's life force is being drained...]

Fang Heng frowned at the game notifications appearing on his retina.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!

In the next instant, two other Lickers pounced forward. Their razor-sharp claws slashed at the Evil Embryo's spikes, severing them after several successive strikes.

The dozen or so Lickers rapidly retreated beyond the reach of the solid tendrils.

Nasir's expression grew solemn.

Compared with its ranged attacks, the Evil Embryo was like a hedgehog covered in spikes. Its defenses were formidable.

Combined with its troublesome illusions, a single mistake could get a fighter impaled. Without help from companions, the victim might be drained to death on the spot!

"Fang Heng, this is going to be difficult."

"Mm." Fang Heng nodded lightly. "No rush. We've got time. Let's play with it a little longer."

Hm?

He had more tricks?

Nasir looked at the Lickers again, and his pupils abruptly contracted.

What was that now?

The Lickers' bodies began trembling uncontrollably. Then they started splitting where they stood.

One became two, two became four...

In an instant, the dozen or so Lickers multiplied into more than five hundred.
