Chapter 2308

Captured

From original Chinese Chapter 2309 · 俘获

"Report!"

A Leaf Clan guard hurried up and reported quietly, "Elder, Elder Nanu is missing."

Sergey froze, then turned sharply. "What did you say? What do you mean, missing?"

The guard looked uncomfortable. "When we counted the survivors, we couldn't find Elder Nanu."

Sergey's expression changed, as did those of the two elders beside him.

Nanu was a veteran elder. He wasn't among the clan's strongest, but he was hardly weak. How could he have died unnoticed in a battle like that...?

Looking back, Sergey saw the area behind them completely swallowed by a red tide of Lickers.

Go back to search for Nanu? They might all become trapped there.

Those humans!

Sergey forced down his anger. A trace of belated fear crept into his heart.

If an elder as experienced as Nanu could die unnoticed in the chaos, could Sergey himself be next?

"Withdraw to the clan first!"

"Yes!"

...

In the Leaf Clan settlement's main hall, Senzo flew into a rage when he learned that Elder Nanu had vanished during the fighting.

How dare Fang Heng!

"Report!"

Another clansman rushed forward. "The Lickers have returned again."

Again?!

Was there no end to this?!

The veins stood out on the hand Senzo had wrapped around his vine chair. This was outrageous!

The guard lowered his head almost to the floor. "There's... there's something else..."

Senzo's gaze turned icy. "What else?"

The guard swallowed nervously, not daring to look up. "We're under targeted long-range beam bombardment from the Federation."

Meanwhile, outside the settlement barrier, the human reinforcements had finally arrived.

Whatever else the Human Federation might lack, it was extremely skilled at suppressing enemies with long-range firepower.

With the Fusion Tyrants drawing fire at the front, the humans set up an array of precision beam weapons more than five kilometers behind them.

Then they simply charged the weapons and fired.

Shi Yanwu's Psychic Studies squad supported the operation, mainly protecting the Federation's beam-weapon machinery.

Through the remote surveillance linked to Fang Heng, the Federation command center had a rough picture of the battle.

Under combined attacks from the zombie swarm and beam weapons, the Leaf Clan's outer barrier was approaching collapse, shuddering violently.

Frankly, even the newly arrived Federation troops were astonished. They'd expected a counterattack, not a counterattack that somehow carried them all the way to the enemy's headquarters.

And from the look of things, the enemy might even be losing.

No one had foreseen the Leaf Clan being driven this far, forced to hide behind its defensive barrier.

Fang Heng still hadn't taken action. He watched from nearby.

A game notification appeared.

[Hint: You have captured Leaf Clan elder Nanu. You have gained 20,000 Tree Spirit reputation points.]

Fang Heng glanced at the message.

Another captured Tree Spirit elder meant another bargaining chip.

After several engagements, Fang Heng had realized that the Leaf Clan's Divine Tree still had considerable dimensional energy in reserve.

He estimated it would be difficult to exhaust that reserve quickly. And Great Elder Senzo hadn't even appeared yet.

There was probably another trump card waiting.

Wiping the clan out in one push looked difficult. At the very least, their current Dimensional Level advantage wasn't enough.

He needed a backup plan.

For now, they could fight. Losing wouldn't be disastrous.

Demonstrating their strength would earn him greater leverage in negotiations.

The harder he hit the Leaf Clan, the more Divine Tree branch dimensional coordinates he could demand later.

Once he had those dimensional coordinates, he could return home and farm for a few days. With his Dimensional Level raised to Lv. 17, he might be able to crush the entire Leaf Clan.

Nice and safe.

What Fang Heng hadn't expected was the Federation's possession of long-range targeted beam weapons, or that it would transport them from so far away through a spatial-jump system.

The weapons required some time to establish their targeting coordinates. Once locked on, they could unleash a continuous barrage of high-powered energy beams.

With that support, the Leaf Clan settlement's outer barrier lost energy at astonishing speed!

The shield was visibly shaking now.

"Fang Heng! I know you're out there! Come out!"

An angry roar rang out from within the Tree Spirit settlement.

Fang Heng frowned slightly and looked ahead.

Senzo's voice. That crafty old bastard.

The vampires all turned to Fang Heng.

He rubbed his chin and simply pretended not to hear, ignoring the challenge.

They weren't finished fighting. It wasn't time to negotiate.

"BOOM! BOOM, BOOOOM!"

The Federation's concentrated beam fire inflicted tremendous damage.

After four more rounds of advances and retreats, the Divine Tree's dimensional power ceased glowing.

Without that energy enhancement, the settlement's outer barrier finally shattered under the concentrated assault of zombies and Federation beams. It entered a recharge-and-restart state.

"We've done it!"

The Federation commanders watching the barrier break were ecstatic.

Good grief. Even when the Federation had worked with the Dependents, they hadn't managed to destroy the Tree Spirits. Could they now actually eliminate an entire Tree Spirit clan?

Fang Heng continued studying the strangely quiet settlement from a distance, frowning.

Once the outer barrier collapsed, the Leaf Clan guards immediately withdrew. Before him, the area once again looked like an ordinary stretch of primeval forest.

Was the clan trying to lure him in? Had they abandoned the outer barrier to draw him inside, intending to unleash another Divine Tree-powered attack?

Fang Heng had to consider every possibility.

"Command center."

"Mr. Fang Heng, go ahead."

"Do the beam weapons still have their targeting lock? Bombard the camp first."

"Understood."

After a two-minute delay, the long-range beam weapons launched a saturation bombardment of the Tree Spirit settlement.

"BOOM! BOOM, BOOOOM!"

Dust filled the settlement as successive explosions shook the ground violently.

The buildings inside were visibly blown apart and reduced to ruins.

But after the bombardment, the settlement sank into deathly silence again, as though they'd attacked nothing more than an ordinary forest.

Fang Heng quickly understood.

On his previous visit, Ovi had taken him to the area where the Divine Tree sapling was cultivated.

That area was deep underground.

Most of the Leaf Clan's central facilities were probably located underground as well.

Even if the surface was reduced to rubble by the Federation's beam attacks, the deeper structures would remain largely unharmed.

"Hmph..."

He'd have to go in and look for himself!

Fang Heng snorted, ordered the vampires to stand guard around the perimeter, and directed the Lickers into the Leaf Clan settlement.

Under cover of the vast swarm, he slipped inside as well.

The Lickers poured through the broken defensive barrier and into the settlement.
