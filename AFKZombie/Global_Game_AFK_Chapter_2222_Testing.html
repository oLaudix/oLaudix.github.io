Chapter 2222

Testing

From original Chinese Chapter 2223 · 测试

“Minister Lei, we've known each other for a long time. You know me.” Ji Haiting's expression grew serious. “My position has never changed. Humanity cannot submit to the Dependents or the Tree Spirits. We must possess enough strength to protect ourselves.”

“I know what you mean. I'll report this matter to the higher-ups.”

Minister Lei glanced at Ji Haiting, then checked the time. “Enough of that. It's getting late, and the verification trials for Psychic Studies should be underway. Come on, let's go see how things are progressing.”

“Very well. After you.”

…

Before long, Lei Changhong and Ji Haiting returned through the teleportation passage to the Federation's Psychic Studies Research Institute.

Inside the institute's main laboratory…

Hu Haiyu and the other participating soldier had just ended three and a half hours of training. They were brought into the research room for follow-up physical examinations.

A research assistant knocked and entered respectfully, handing over two reports.

“Sirs, we've completed the initial assessments of the subjects' physical condition. The results are well beyond our expectations. They're extraordinarily good. Their physical indicators are roughly four to seven times those of ordinary people—a level that conventional human training simply cannot achieve.”

“We've also conducted preliminary checks for side effects. So far, there's only one: the subjects seem averse to exposure to strong light.”

“That's particularly strange. The light itself doesn't appear to harm them, but they experience psychological discomfort. We'll investigate further and continue watching for any other effects.”

It was Ji Haiting's first look at the detailed reports. He read them carefully, then gazed through the one-way glass at the two soldiers who had undergone the second-stage trials.

Four to seven times the ordinary physical attributes!

What a surprise Fang Heng had delivered!

Ji Haiting almost wanted to try it himself.

Lei Changhong closed his file. “How are the subjects feeling now?”

“Excellent. Both say that the three and a half hours of continuous training produced a tremendous breakthrough in their Psychic Studies abilities. Neither reports any physical problems. They're excited and feel nowhere near their limits. In fact, they're eager to resume psychic tempering and find out how far they can go.”

Lei Changhong and Ji Haiting exchanged a look.

Standing beside them, Shi Yanwu nodded to himself.

He understood the soldiers' feelings perfectly.

Even he could barely resist the temptation.

He wanted to join the second-stage trial himself and see just how powerful Psychic Studies could become with Dr. Fang's technological support!

“Have you run any combat trials?” Minister Lei asked.

Numbers on paper were only a preliminary indication.

Seeing was believing, after all.

He needed to watch the abilities being used in actual combat.

“Sir, we've just completed a mental-barrier test. The psychic barriers manifested by the subjects were 4.2 times stronger than before treatment.”

Lei Changhong's pupils contracted.

That much?!

The laboratory supervisor nodded. “Sir, the next round of practical combat testing is ready. It'll take place in Training Ground Three and should begin in about five minutes. Would you like to watch?”

“Certainly. Let's go.”

Soon, the researchers followed Minister Lei to Training Ground Three.

“Minister Lei, this is the opponent we've selected for the test subjects. His individual combat rating is close to theirs before the experiment. This training match will demonstrate how the improved Psychic Studies abilities perform in battle.”

Through the third-floor observation window, they could see Hu Haiyu and the other soldier waiting on one side of the arena.

Shi Yanwu introduced them. “Minister Lei, the man on the left is Hu Haiyu. He had an S-grade strength rating and has received two first-class merits and three third-class merits. He volunteered for this trial.”

“Mm. Impressive.”

Shi Yanwu was excited too.

The figures alone were enough to inspire confidence.

But how would they perform in battle?

In his estimation, the results would be excellent.

They might even overwhelm their opponents completely!

The thought of the entire corps undergoing such an improvement made it difficult for him to contain his excitement.

Inside the training ground, the participants prepared to enter under the researchers' supervision.

Just as the test was about to begin, an aide hurried into the observation area and whispered to Minister Lei, “Sir, Deputy Minister Luobuer has arrived with some people.”

Luobuer was another deputy minister in the military department, though his responsibilities were different: he oversaw the Federation's semi-mechanized forces.

So he'd heard the news too.

Minister Lei considered the matter briefly, then nodded. “Bring them in.”

Moments later, a slightly heavyset middle-aged officer entered the observation room, surrounded by attendants.

“Minister Lei! Congratulations. I hear your Psychic Studies project has made a major breakthrough. Even the Federation leadership has decided to invest heavily in it.”

“It's nothing like that. We've made a little progress and the rumors have run wild. The project is still in the research stage.”

Minister Lei understood precisely why the other deputy minister had come.

They were both military men, sometimes cooperating and sometimes competing. Luobuer had long overseen the semi-mechanized corps and promoted mechanical augmentation within the armed forces.

The troops under his command numbered hundreds of times as many as the Psychic Studies Corps.

Because Psychic Studies depended heavily on talent, few Federation soldiers could truly master it. In a one-on-one fight, they were no match for semi-mechanized soldiers equipped with external armor.

That was why the Federation had traditionally devoted far more resources and funding to mechanical augmentation.

But now that Psychic Studies had achieved a major breakthrough, the leadership had received the reports and was preparing to increase its investment.

Luobuer couldn't sit still after hearing that.

So he'd come to see things for himself.

“A little progress? I've heard it's much more than that! The leadership has already agreed to increase funding. After all, our semi-mechanized soldiers still can't defeat the Tree Spirits on their own.”

— End of Chapter 2222 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Continuity and mechanics. The 4–7× measurements are physical indicators, while the 4.2× figure refers to the strengthened psychic barrier. They describe different test results.

Chinese Expressions
