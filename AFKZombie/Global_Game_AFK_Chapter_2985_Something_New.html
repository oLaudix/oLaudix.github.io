Chapter 2985

Something New

From Chinese Chapter 2986 · 新东西

“Whoosh!!!”

Fang Heng pushed lightly off the ground and sped past layers of warships and ranks of Nether Clan War Knights, heading straight for the center of the frontline battlefield.

Behind the swarm, a powerful figure also shot out from the River of Netherworld!

“BANG!!!”

A heavy impact exploded in the middle of the battlefield!

A visible shock wave swept outward on both sides!

“Fang Heng!”

Anger flashed in Proust's eyes.

Compared with their previous meeting, Fang Heng's attributes had risen considerably!

As for Proust himself...

The dwindling power of the Death Realm had actually made him weaker.

Fang Heng slowly closed his hand around Proust's fist and said coldly, one word at a time, “Heh. Proust, you seem to have gotten weaker.”

“GET LOST!”

Proust roared.

An explosion of force erupted from his fist!

Both fighters leaped backward to open some distance, then charged at each other again.

Compared with exchanging ranged spells, Fang Heng was better at—and much preferred—close combat!

Especially when he held the advantage in melee.

“Whoosh!!”

Fang Heng drove a fist forward with a sharp whistle of displaced air.

“Bang!!”

His punch struck Proust's right shoulder, which immediately broke apart into clusters of tiny insects that dispersed the impact.

“Hiss, hiss!!!”

The scattered insects even lunged toward Fang Heng, swiftly biting a huge chunk out of his right arm and leaving a deathly gray coating on the wound.

Death Realm aura!

Fang Heng retreated sharply. The death aura on his wound was quickly dissolved and absorbed, and his right arm regenerated. He grinned mockingly. “Heh, brother, I didn't realize you had a few new tricks. Here, try one of mine!”

He tapped the ground with his toes and launched forward again, meeting Proust in another melee exchange!

“Whoosh!!!”

His next punch struck Proust in the chest. Proust repeated the same move, scattering his body into tiny insects to absorb the damage.

This time, however, something changed. Under the force of the punch, a layer of gloomy death aura swiftly spread over the red insects.

More than half the insects enveloped by the death aura died from a single blow!

Proust was startled and quickly backed away to examine what had happened.

Was that...?

Death aura?

“Whoosh!!!”

Naturally, Fang Heng wasn't about to give Proust time to recover. He closed in and launched several punches in rapid succession.

Since his last fight with Proust, Fang Heng had made major progress in Necromancy, Psionics, and Sacred Studies. He had also absorbed dimensional energy, raising his attributes across the board.

On top of that came the daily intensive training provided by the Undead Council mentors.

As his control over death aura improved, the mentors—knowing he disliked spellcasting—had suggested applying necromantic power directly to his fists.

It worked extraordinarily well!

Despite Proust's immense resistance to Necromancy and superb regeneration, he no longer dared take Fang Heng's attacks head-on. He was forced increasingly onto the defensive, dodging instead.

Fang Heng's lips curled into a smile.

“Heh, why do you keep running away? Scared?”

By now, besides the Lickers attached to the skeletal dragons circling overhead, countless more had burrowed beneath the battlefield.

They were all his enormous reserve of health!

As one side grew stronger and the other weaker, Fang Heng found the fight far easier than his previous confrontation with Proust. He even had time to throw out a few taunts.

The two became black blurs on the battlefield, and it looked as though Fang Heng had already suppressed Proust.

Not far away, Mu Qingxin watched the battle coldly.

Under ordinary circumstances, Cultivators like her did not interfere in battles between newborns.

For now, Fang Heng had the upper hand.

But this slight advantage was nowhere near enough to kill Proust completely.

What worried Mu Qingxin most was the special ability Fang Heng had used last time.

Although it was essentially a spell attack, it incorporated a terrifying mental shock.

Even she was wary of it.

Fang Heng!

Mu Qingxin watched him, her eyes filled with vigilance.

How could his strength have improved so dramatically in such a short time?

Just how had he done it?

Not far away, the Undead Council's senior mentors stayed out of the fight, observing from a safe distance while guarding against Mu Qingxin, who remained concealed.

Of course, if Fang Heng faced real danger, they would intervene regardless of the cost.

For now, however, the strength he displayed made the elderly council members nod repeatedly.

They were extremely satisfied!

Especially when they saw that their recent instruction had borne fruit.

Monstrous talent combined with tireless hard work.

Chairman Georgi nodded to himself.

Perhaps one day Fang Heng would reach a realm beyond even their imagination.

In the blink of an eye, the two had been fighting for more than ten minutes.

Fang Heng could see Proust's health bar.

It was still absurdly long.

He had scarcely suffered any real damage.

Fang Heng continued provoking him. “Proust, you've been here so long. How are you still the same? Don't you have anything new to show me?”

After being repeatedly suppressed, Proust snorted coldly.

A long time?

Long, my ass!

It hadn't even been half a year!

In the past, Proust measured his periods of dormancy in centuries. He could no longer remember how long he'd slept after being suppressed by the Empress of the Death Realm.

“And what about you? Don't think you can kill me with just this. Show me what new tricks you've learned!”

“Gladly. Then please give me some pointers!”

As Fang Heng spoke, the aura around him abruptly transformed.

Hm?

What was that?

Proust's expression changed instantly.

A burst of sacred light erupted from Fang Heng.

Sacred Form.

Shattering Strike!

“Whoosh!”

Sacred radiance flashed.

A dark-gold sacred spear pierced Proust's chest in an instant!

Sacred attacks were particularly effective against both undead attributes and the Ouroboros Seed. Caught off guard by this sudden attack, Proust burst into searing sacred flames around the spear wound and was momentarily staggered.

How could he use sacred power?!

Shock appeared in Mu Qingxin's eyes as well.

Whoosh!!!

While Proust was still flying backward, Fang Heng flashed behind him and swept his hand toward the oncoming body.

“Divine Punishment!”

“BOOM!!!!”

A violent peal of thunder!

Purple-red lightning erupted from the sky and struck Proust instantly, producing a damage number in the hundreds of thousands.

Proust was briefly stunned.

Fang Heng snorted coldly.

Keep going!
