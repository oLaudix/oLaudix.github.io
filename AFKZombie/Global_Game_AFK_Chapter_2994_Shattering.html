Chapter 2994

Shattering

From Chinese Chapter 2995 · 击碎

Along the way, Fang Heng occasionally encountered more Ice Crystal Puppets rising out of the frozen ground.

He simply shattered each one with a punch.

After wandering through the maze for another half hour, he stopped.

Ahead lay an open area enclosed by walls of ice crystal.

Hm?

A thin layer of pale-green vines was somehow growing across the ground beneath the thick ice.

Fang Heng realized he'd reached an area where plants could grow.

A tall ice-crystal pillar stood in the center.

Green vines climbed around it.

The ground surrounding the pillar was covered in blooming flowers.

Their species was unfamiliar to Fang Heng, and every blossom wore a layer of frost.

Tall ancient trees coated in frost stood in the central area, forming a small grove.

Fang Heng looked up at the eerie forest and expanded his perception.

Strange. Was that nature energy?

He'd just been encountering elemental power. Now it had switched to nature energy?

Fang Heng took a tentative step forward.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!

As soon as he approached, the Ice-Thorn Flower Demons sensed an enemy and turned their open blossoms toward him.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!”

The next instant, countless frost-covered leaves flew toward Fang Heng together, engulfing him in their attack range.

“Hmph!”

Fang Heng snorted and abruptly released his aura!

“Bang! Bang, bang!”

The leaves flying toward him shattered in midair.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng lightly kicked off the ground and rushed straight into the Ice-Thorn Flower Demons, ignoring the masses of frosted leaves flying around him.

“Bang! Bang, bang, bang!!!”

Countless icy leaves exploded against his body, forming small patches of ice crystal on his skin without affecting him in the slightest.

They couldn't even penetrate his defense!

Hm?

Fang Heng frowned.

The outer bark of the tall ancient trees shielding the Flower Demons rapidly peeled away. Roots pulled free of the ground as the trees moved their trunks into his path.

Treants!

A Frost Ancient Tree Elder swung its thick, frost-coated branch viciously at Fang Heng!

Heh!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and swept his metal staff forward!

“BANG!!!”

One strike!

The entire Frost Ancient Tree Elder exploded into fragments right before him!

Faint nature energy rose from the shattered tree debris, flowed swiftly into Fang Heng's body, and settled in his Sea of Consciousness.

Fang Heng's interest was immediately piqued.

Nature energy!

His Nature Studies had also advanced to LV:MAX, and without an advancement book, he couldn't raise the level further.

As before, the nature energy didn't dissipate automatically. It simply remained within his Sea of Consciousness.

Previously he'd encountered elemental power.

Now it was nature energy.

This region seemed more like a place designed to test players' abilities and provide them with academic energy.

Could this be the so-called treasure of the Land of Apocalypse?

“Whoosh!”

Fang Heng stopped thinking about it and dashed between the many Frost Ancient Tree Elders and Ice-Thorn Flower Demons, repeatedly swinging his alloy staff.

“Bang! Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

Large numbers of nature-element creatures shattered throughout the grove, sending streams of pure nature energy into his body.

After clearing the creatures for a while, Fang Heng stopped attacking and stepped back from the battle.

Something wasn't right.

The Frost Ancient Tree Elders and Ice-Thorn Flower Demons were growing incredibly quickly. Almost as many monsters appeared as he killed, seemingly without end.

Under other circumstances, he wouldn't have minded. He could simply fight a war of attrition and grind nature energy along the way.

But this time was different.

He still had other things to do.

That's right.

The thing in the middle.

That strange ice-crystal pillar.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng accelerated again, smashing two Frost Ancient Tree Elders blocking his way with his metal staff. He ignored the other creatures and ran straight toward the ice-crystal pillar at the center.

He looked closely as he approached.

The outermost layer consisted of tangled vines. Beneath them lay thick ice crystal, and under that was a stone pillar covered in carved patterns.

“BANG!”

One blow from his staff shattered the ice and vines, exposing the hidden stone pillar beneath.

It looked like some kind of ceremonial pillar.

Some of the carved patterns on its surface contained runes that could be activated.

Fang Heng reached out and touched one of the runic crystals.

“Hummm...!”

The crystal glowed faintly as soon as his finger touched it.

At once, a wave of nature power flowed out of the ceremonial pillar and straight into his body through his fingertip.

Nature energy!

Hundreds of times more than he'd gained from killing the nature creatures!

Fang Heng's eyelid twitched. What a wonderful surprise!

Absorb it!

Take it all!

Keeping his right hand pressed against the stone altar, Fang Heng flipped his left wrist and summoned the sacred Book of Oaths.

Skill—Lightning of Judgment!

A beneficial effect with a chance to retaliate with lightning when struck by physical attacks.

Fang Heng quickly applied the Lightning of Judgment retaliation buff to himself, then stood there openly. Ignoring the surrounding nature creatures, he continued absorbing energy from the altar.

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!!!

The nature creatures gathered in the frozen forest attacked him continuously. Lightning of Judgment triggered again and again, sending purple bolts into the attackers.

Fang Heng's defenses were already strong enough to withstand most attacks, and with Undying Body constantly healing him, enduring their damage was easy.

Combined with his enormous Mental Strength, Lightning of Judgment was devastating!

Each lightning bolt had a small area-of-effect component. Whenever one fell, a patch of enemies was instantly cleared away, leaving a stream of nature energy that rushed into Fang Heng.

Fang Heng looked away from the surrounding creatures and back down at the altar.

Give it to me!

Once he'd familiarized himself with the energy flow, he directly used his Mental Strength to draw power out of the altar.

Immediately, nature energy dozens of times more intense flooded into his Sea of Consciousness.

An ordinary player's Sea of Consciousness would probably never withstand that much nature energy.

Fang Heng, however, was different. His Sea of Consciousness had expanded several times, and Abe Akaya, the Demon Seed, and Necromancy all enhanced it. He had an enormous amount of space inside.

Absorb it like crazy!

The more, the better!

After more than five full minutes, the stone altar's nature energy was completely exhausted. Fang Heng didn't release it until he'd wrung out the very last trace.

With its power gone, the stone pillar rapidly weathered into dust.

Fang Heng looked around.

With the central pillar destroyed, nature creatures stopped spawning on the ice, and the vines across the frozen ground quickly receded.
