Chapter 2043

Calculations

From original Chinese Chapter 2044 · 盘算

The sacred barrier the survivors were maintaining together was powerful, but the energy cost was just as staggering.

The two Crusader instructors from the Divine Domain had already resorted to using their own origin power to sustain it. Both were as pale as paper, and their hearts ached.

A moment earlier, they had suddenly lost all sense of Jige's aura.

Their close friend was probably at the very center of that frozen hell. There was virtually no chance he had survived.

And in just a few minutes, nearly a thousand reserve Crusaders who had failed to retreat had also gone silent beneath the spreading cold.

More than a decade of effort training the Holy Court's future elite had been wiped out in an instant!

Yet there was nothing they could do. Their only remaining option was to save as many of the survivors as possible and escape the amusement park.

“Look over there!”

One player pointed toward a patch of blue mist in the distance. Vague shapes were slowly emerging from the frost.

Undead creatures?

When the onlookers made out the figures, their expressions changed drastically.

They were frost undead skeletons!

Scattered undead skeletons emerged from the mist and began gathering together.

…

At the center of the Yin Vein passage.

The underground basement was now a mass of ruins, completely covered in frost. Beneath the ice, the huge magic array continued to spin at high speed.

The Yin Vein fissure hung directly above the array. Space itself looked torn open, while dark electrical arcs crackled continually along its edges.

Freezing mist poured from the fissure, spiraling together until it formed a twisting column of blue ice climbing into the sky. From a distance it looked like a beam of frozen blue light.

A coffin lay on the ground beside the fissure. Several undead skeleton leaders shuffled forward and pushed it away from the opening.

It was no ordinary coffin; it had been specially made to shield its occupant from the freezing effect outside.

As soon as the magic array had activated, Fang Heng had hidden inside it.

Now, he abruptly opened his eyes.

Game messages streamed across his vision.

[Notice: You have attempted to transfer the teleportation passage, Yin Vein Fissure. This action will cause a major change in the Duncha game world. Because you have accepted related quests, the quests are being merged…]

[Notice: Duncha has officially been designated a frontline combat region. Reputation, contribution, and honor will now be tallied together. Your current rewards are being calculated. Precise totals cannot be determined until the event concludes; the game will settle provisional earnings once every hour.]

[Notice: You killed Crusader Officer Jige. You have obtained 4,500,000 Undead Faction Reputation and 5,000,000 Undead Faction Honor.]

[Notice: You killed members of the reserve Crusaders, Holy Court reinforcement forces, paladin orders, and knight orders. Total reward: 8,480,000 Undead Faction Reputation and 7,660,000 Undead Faction Honor.]

[Notice: You have inflicted destruction upon the Duncha game world. Your current quest, Rescue Qiboreya, has changed. Quest completion increased by 162%…]

Excellent!

Fang Heng clenched a fist in satisfaction.

So the man who had chased him was a Crusader officer—from the Divine Domain, no less. No wonder he'd been so troublesome.

Heh. And what good did that do him? The Yin Vein aura had blasted him apart anyway.

Five million Honor, just from him!

And the many Holy Court reinforcement groups caught in the original explosion were also credited as Fang Heng's personal kills.

He'd struck it rich!

All together, the rewards could be exchanged for 126,000 Main God Points. And the earnings weren't over yet.

The game would recalculate his contributions every hour based on the damage he caused to the Holy Court. As long as he kept the passage active, greater destruction would mean even larger rewards.

Another line of notices suddenly appeared.

[Notice: Your team has successfully activated Merck's Magic Array. Quest completed: Recreating Merck's Grand Vision. You have obtained 5,000,000 Undead Faction Reputation, 1,000,000 Undead Faction Honor, and a Book of Soul Imprisonment (team reward).]

[Notice: The Book of Soul Imprisonment is Legendary equipment and binds automatically when obtained. As team leader, you have authority to distribute the reward.]

There it is!

Fang Heng's spirits lifted again. The Merck quest had earned him another 10,000 Main God Points, along with an upgraded version of the Book of the Dead.

He would keep the Book of Soul Imprisonment himself. The two Yuan brothers were grateful enough just to have finished the quest; they naturally had no objection to his taking the item.

[Notice: You have distributed the reward.]

[Notice: You obtained: Book of Soul Imprisonment.]

Tier: Legendary (bound).

Description: An enhanced Book of the Dead with stronger soul-imprisoning abilities. Improves the user's ability to bind souls.

Death penalty: This item is guaranteed to drop on death and will lose a large amount of durability.

Replacement: Replaces the Book of the Dead and retains all of its effects.

Additional Skill 1 — Soul Walker: Attacks against souls performed using the Book of the Dead gain an additional 50% final damage.

Additional Skill 2 — Forced Imprisonment: When using the Book of the Dead to cast necromantic summoning spells, the player gains a 50% bonus to the number summoned and a 50% bonus to their duration.

Fang Heng skimmed the descriptions.

Not bad, I suppose.

It was only Legendary equipment, after all. Compared with the Holy Court's divine artifact, the Book of Oaths, it was far weaker.

Still, a passive percentage increase to soul damage was straightforward and useful. He'd previously found it fairly difficult to attack the souls of fallen Holy Court experts with the soul chains summoned through the Book of the Dead. An extra fifty percent should help.

The second skill was rather underwhelming. He didn't need the book to summon undead skeletons, so he wouldn't benefit from the additional summoning bonuses in that case.

Better than nothing.

He equipped the Book of Soul Imprisonment immediately.

Thump!

The coffin shook violently from outside. Judging that the time was about right, Fang Heng opened the lid.

An icy wind rushed in, making even him shiver.

The plan had succeeded, so it was time to tally his gains.

The Merck quest was finished. His book had been upgraded, and he'd earned 10,000 Main God Points from it.

The Yin Vein eruption had yielded 126,000 more, while his earlier rampage through the Brighton Empire's interior had earned over 50,000.

Roughly 180,000 Main God Points in total!

On top of that, the Rescue Qiboreya quest was now at 258.2% completion.

— End of Chapter 2043 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Chinese numerals. The source uses 450W, 500W, 848W and 766W, where W (万) means ten thousand: 4.5 million, 5 million, 8.48 million and 7.66 million.
  • Reward conversion. 12.6W Main God Points means 126,000. The chapter later states the total from several sources is around 180,000.
  • Book of Soul Imprisonment. 拘役之书 is rendered Book of Soul Imprisonment. It replaces and preserves the effects of the Book of the Dead; it is separate from the sacred Chapter of Imprisonment appearing later.
