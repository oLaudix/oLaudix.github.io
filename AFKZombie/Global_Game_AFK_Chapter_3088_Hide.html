“Mm.” Fang Heng nodded calmly. “I did it.”

“Huh?”

Kappa froze, staring at him blankly.

What did that mean?

“There's a problem with Abe Akaya's teleportation and the game's spatial transport. Something similar happened on the ship earlier. I was concerned, so I found a safe place to test it.”

Kappa and Korkalot exchanged glances.

Uh...

So Boss Fang's idea of a safe place was a sewer right outside Kinsfolk headquarters in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital?

“You've had a hard time. The Kinsfolk must have been giving you trouble lately. I imagine you're pretty annoyed.” Fang Heng looked up at the skyscraper. “Since we're already here, I might as well help you vent some frustration.”

He didn't really have any particular objective in mind.

His zombie clones had successfully passed from the game into reality through Abe Akaya's portal. If they traveled directly through the sewers to the villa's basement, they'd be easy to track, and the trail could lead back to him.

Fang Heng was counting on staying with the Noel Clan to continue his cultivation.

It would be better to sacrifice the clones and let them respawn in the villa's basement.

If he had to sacrifice them anyway, why should the location matter?

He might as well launch an assault on Kinsfolk headquarters!

“Great! Great!”

Kappa's eyes lit up and he nodded excitedly. Boss Fang really was a true friend—he'd come to help them settle the score!

As they spoke, a commotion broke out near the cordoned sewer access.

Kappa scrambled up Korkalot and onto his shoulder again, peering into the distance.

The security team that had entered earlier came fleeing out in a sorry state.

Before the perimeter guards could question them, vast swarms of insects burst from the sewer behind them!

Insects!

Where had they come from? Why were there so many?!

Balin, the district security commander, was still urging everyone to prepare protective gear. The sudden flood of insects startled him, and he immediately ordered his guards to open fire.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

High-frequency piercing beams erupted from their weapons.

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

But instead of punching bloody holes through the insects' hides as expected, the beams simply exploded on impact!

Completely ineffective?!

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital's security teams weren't weak. Each guard was carefully selected and much stronger than an ordinary person. But they weren't combat specialists, and their strongest attacks were beam weapons at range and energy swords up close.

Seeing their ranged beam weapons fail utterly, they began to panic.

What kind of insects were these?

How could their defenses be so strong?

Only when several beams struck the same creature did the combined explosive force knock it backward.

“Retreat!”

As the insects approached, Balin shouted for his team to abandon its line and scatter to either side.

Huh?

They weren't chasing?

Several of the first guards to flee realized they'd survived and breathed a sigh of relief.

Looking up again, they saw that the insects had broken through their defense line in almost an instant.

The creatures didn't pursue the guards. Instead, they pushed through the eastern line and sped onward.

What did that mean? Were the insects not particularly aggressive?

Confusion flashed in Balin's eyes. Before he could relax, he saw which way they were heading and his face stiffened once more.

Damn it! They were heading straight for Kinsfolk headquarters.

Were these creatures specifically targeting the Kinsfolk?

Looking back, he saw that the crisis showed no sign of ending. A seemingly endless stream of insects continued pouring from the sewer.

Yet the swarm didn't appear hostile toward his people.

Balin frowned deeply.

The swarm was terrifying, but it hadn't actually harmed the guards. Apart from frightening them half to death, his elite squad hadn't suffered any real casualties.

So long as they moved aside, the creatures didn't pursue them.

A guard hurried over, wiped the sweat from his forehead, and asked carefully, “Captain, what do we do?”

Balin glanced at his subordinate.

You're asking me? What am I supposed to do?

He was paid a certain amount to do a certain job.

He certainly wasn't going to volunteer for a suicide mission.

Balin snapped, “Have you reported everything to the Kinsfolk?”

“Yes. We've sent a detailed report. The Kinsfolk building should already be preparing its defenses.”

“Good. Then wait. Tell the men these insects aren't normal. They're almost certainly targeting the Kinsfolk. Keep your distance and make a show of doing your duty. Look after yourselves. Don't throw your lives away. It isn't worth it.”

“Understood.”

Unable to stop the swarm, the guards and security contractors simply stopped trying. They withdrew, extended the cordon, and watched wave after wave of insects emerge from underground and race toward Kinsfolk headquarters.

That number was terrifying...

There had to be at least ten thousand...

Despite the unfolding emergency, crowds of spectators eager for excitement refused to leave the nearby street corners.

Most were quite confident in their own strength.

Hidden in the crowd, Kappa instinctively drew his neck back.

With this many insects, the Kinsfolk would have their hands full.

As the swarm approached, the entire Kinsfolk headquarters entered its highest alert level.

Its security teams were utterly baffled and felt unbelievably unlucky.

Who could have imagined that one day hordes of alien insects would assault Kinsfolk headquarters in the middle of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital? This wasn't an isolated base in a dangerous region. The downtown building had no defensive fortifications at all!

Seeing the enormous swarm approaching, the building's front guards erected several successive defense lines at the entrance.

“Fire!!”

Dense energy beams swept across the approaching insects in a storm.

Useless!

The Lickers' defenses made them effectively immune to such dispersed area attacks.

The Lickers endured the barrage and charged straight ahead.

“Don't retreat! Stop them!”

As the swarm drew closer, sweat broke out on the guards' foreheads. They switched to high-frequency energy swords, hoping to intercept the creatures in melee combat.

After all, the insects hadn't engaged the previous security forces at close quarters.

Perhaps they were weak in melee?

But the instant a guard entered a Licker's attack range, the creature leaped forward and closed the distance at astonishing speed.

“Whoosh!”

Razor-sharp claws sliced through the air.

The protective suit's energy shield activated, forming an invisible barrier. The claws scraped across the guard's chest in a shower of crackling sparks, and a tremendous impact sent him flying backward!
