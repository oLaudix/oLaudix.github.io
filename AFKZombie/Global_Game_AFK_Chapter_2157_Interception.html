Chapter 2157

Interception

From original Chinese Chapter 2158 · 阻截

Outside the research institute.

Time passed second by second.

Fang Heng remained perfectly composed.

The Fusion Tyrants had already been equipped with superheavy high-energy beam weapons, but they hadn't entered battle yet. Instead, they stood in a row at the rear, ready to fight at any moment.

Best to save the good weapons for when they really mattered.

Their ammunition was limited, after all.

Fang Heng knew the Tree Spirits wouldn't be able to find the Niam Alloy. He therefore conserved ammunition while preparing for the Federation's imminent full-scale assault.

And besides...

He'd noticed something.

Destroying Federation war machines after they arrived through spatial transition earned more faction contribution points!

Why let those points go to waste?

Fang Heng sent away a group of Tree Whisperer Alliance helpers who were only getting in his way, then drove off the Tree Spirits who remained as well. He continued slowly farming enemies using only his Lickers!

After all, the only reinforcements arriving through spatial transition so far were unmanned war machines.

Individually, those machines were formidable, but unfortunately they couldn't do much against the Lickers!

As he continued fighting, Fang Heng suddenly raised an eyebrow.

It hadn't been half an hour yet, but the number of spatial-transition points surrounding the institute was clearly increasing faster.

Soon a Tree Spirit elder hurried out of the institute, swept his gaze around the entrance, and fixed on Fang Heng.

The elder rushed over and shouted, “Fang Heng! The transition interference device will fail completely in five minutes! We're still searching for the Niam Alloy. You must buy us more time!”

Fang Heng gave the elder an OK gesture from a distance.

At the same moment, game notifications appeared on his retina.

[Hint: The player's current quest—Eliminate the War Machines—is complete and entering settlement.]

[Hint: Quest completion rate: 12,098%.]

[Hint: The player gains 12 Tree Spirit favorability and 1,209,800 Tree Spirit faction contribution points.]

[Hint: The player has triggered a side quest—Cover the Tree Spirits' Retreat.]

Quest Name: Cover the Tree Spirits' Retreat.

Quest Difficulty: SS.

Quest Requirement: The Tree Spirits are still searching the institute for traces of Niam Alloy. Help them withstand the Federation war machines arriving through spatial transition, buying them more time and ensuring their safe withdrawal.

Failure Penalty: Decreased Tree Spirit favorability.

“Not bad, not bad. That's another twelve Leaves of Life earned...”

Fang Heng glanced at the hints, then looked skyward again.

The Tree Spirits guarding the institute's perimeter were growing nervous too.

Naturally, they weren't foolish enough to place all their hopes in Fang Heng. They tightened their grips on their weapons.

In the night sky, the white lights of forming transitions gradually dimmed, then vanished for nearly two minutes.

Suddenly...

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The entire night sky was illuminated by flashing spatial-transition points!

Like a sky full of brilliant stars!

That many?

Even Fang Heng was startled by the spectacle.

“Stop them!”

The Tree Spirit commander defending the institute's perimeter frowned and immediately gave the order.

Dense volleys of turquoise-green arrows shot toward the lights!

At the same time, the Fusion Tyrants raised their superheavy beam weapons and opened fire!

Boom! Boom, boom!

Beams filled the sky and exploded against the transition points.

Fang Heng's face grew serious.

It wasn't working very well.

Now that the interference with the Spirit Domain network had failed, the barriers protecting spatial transitions were far stronger!

Their current firepower was beginning to fall short.

Only concentrated volleys from large numbers of attackers could destroy the points before the transitions completed.

The first joint attack by the Tree Spirits and Fang Heng's Fusion Tyrants destroyed fewer than half of the forming points.

Worse, transitions around the perimeter were completing dramatically faster than before!

Huge numbers of war machines materialized on the battlefield and immediately attacked the Lickers surrounding the institute!

The Lickers and treants met them head-on!

Against such fully armored mechanical creatures, the worms were of little use.

The Lickers had to do the fighting themselves!

Notifications began flashing rapidly across Fang Heng's retina.

[Hint: The player's zombie clone (Fusion Licker form) has dealt 3,337 damage to a large war machine (Spider Armor V-88)...]

[Hint: The player's zombie clone...]

Fang Heng didn't join the fight directly. He watched from the sidelines, then turned toward a nearby Tree Spirit.

“I need to exchange some items. Mm, Leaves of Life. Exchange all my remaining points for Leaves of Life.”

The Tree Spirit frowned, feeling painfully reluctant.

But the Great Elder had ordered them to keep Fang Heng cooperative at least until the operation ended.

So the Tree Spirit had no choice but to grit his teeth and hand over another dozen or so Leaves of Life.

“Thank you.”

Fang Heng put them away.

The Tree Spirits might turn against him at any time. Better to spend the points immediately than let them go to waste.

With that settled, Fang Heng looked back toward the battle.

The first Federation troops to arrive were large numbers of unmanned mechanical units.

Because their massed beam fire couldn't actually kill Lickers permanently, the situation was still manageable for the moment.

But the Lickers' damage output was clearly falling behind.

Most of the actual damage was coming from the Fusion Tyrants at the rear, wielding their high-energy beam weapons.

However...

The Fusion Tyrants also had to deal with the spatial-transition points overhead.

And more troublesome still...

After only five minutes, the superheavy beam weapons ran out of ammunition.

The number of war machines arriving through spatial transition immediately surged.

Fang Heng frowned deeply.

This was the strength of the entire Federation brought to bear.

More or less what he'd expected.

Very difficult to withstand.

The Lickers were repeatedly blasted away by beam cannons, only to recover through Undying Body and return to battle. For the moment, they barely managed to hold the mechanical army back from the institute.

The Tree Spirits' summoned treants weren't so fortunate.

Under the war machines' concentrated fire, their numbers plunged.

“Hm? Here they come!”

After another five minutes of fighting the Federation, Fang Heng sensed something.

Dense spatial-transition lights flashed at the rear of the battlefield as a fully armed Federation semi-mechanized force arrived!

Every soldier in the Federation squad carried a long-range sniper injection gun.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The moment they entered the battlefield, the Federation semi-mechanized squads pulled their triggers, showering the Lickers with needles!

Rows of game hints refreshed across Fang Heng's retina.

[Hint: The player's zombie clone (Fusion Licker form) has been affected by an unknown injection agent. Some of the zombie clone's (Fusion Licker form) enhancement effects have been disabled...]

— End of Chapter 2157 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Quest values. The source gives a completion rate of 12,098% and a reward of 120.98万 (1,209,800) Tree Spirit faction contribution points.
  • Ammunition. The Fusion Tyrants’ heavy beam weapons eventually run out of ammunition.

Chinese Expressions

  • 好钢用在刀刃上. Literally “use good steel for the blade’s edge”; conserve scarce firepower for the moment it matters most.
  • 不赚白不赚. If there are points to be earned, why leave them on the table? Fang Heng exploits the reward mechanics.
