Verification

From Chinese Chapter 3071 · 验证

After lecturing for over two hours, Gaspar grew thirsty. He took a large gulp of tea and nodded. “I've talked enough. You give it a try.”

“Mm...”

Fang Heng tried to understand Gaspar's explanation of the environment and the rules governing external spatial energy, slowly closing his eyes.

He thought he could grasp it.

The so-called laws of space were essentially the spatial forces present everywhere.

His extraordinary Perception allowed him to sense the fluctuations of spatial energy all around him.

That part wasn't difficult.

The next step was to cooperate with the flow of that energy as much as possible whenever he used his own spatial power.

Fang Heng opened his eyes again and followed Gaspar's instructions, releasing fluctuations of his own spatial energy to match the currents of power around him.

In other words, move with the current.

Interesting!

This was an area he'd never explored before. Without a teacher's guidance, who knew how long he would have spent fumbling around before understanding it?

Gaspar noticed the spatial fluctuations Fang Heng was releasing and felt his heart jump.

What incredible talent!

Gaspar even began to suspect that Fang Heng's earlier clumsiness had been an act.

Fang Heng kept his eyes closed, concentrating on the flow of spatial energy.

Suddenly, a line of small text appeared on his retina.

[Hint: Player has received guidance and triggered a special quest—Spatial Studies Advancement (Grandmaster).]

Quest Name: Spatial Studies Advancement (Grandmaster).

Quest Description: Complete the advancement to Grandmaster spatial studies under an instructor's guidance.

Current Quest Progress: 0%.

[Hint: Player has gained new insight into the flow of spatial laws. Quest completion has increased...]

After a while, Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

It felt pretty good.

He checked the game prompt again.

After a little more than ten minutes of sensing the flow of external space, his quest progress had already reached 2.21%.

The Black Mist game really was wonderful. It was like a special cheat that constantly monitored exactly how far his cultivation had progressed.

Gaspar glanced at Fang Heng, suppressing his surprise. He planned to investigate this youngster's background after the lesson. “Not bad. It looks like you've begun to understand. You're moving in the right direction.”

“I'm tired. Spend some time practicing what we've covered. Once you've gained more insight or run into trouble, book another lesson.”

“Thank you.”

Fang Heng stood up.

Advancing through cultivation was far easier than he'd imagined.

A bottleneck was like a thin sheet of paper covering a window. Once you pierced it, the road ahead was wide open.

Fang Heng could hardly wait to find somewhere quiet and continue cultivating, hoping to complete his advancement quickly.

“Mr. Fang Heng, was the lesson satisfactory?”

“Very. I learned a great deal.” Fang Heng left the training room and turned to Anxi, who was waiting outside. “Is there somewhere quieter? There are too many people here, and it's noisy. I prefer peace and quiet.”

“You have a private lounge and room in Noel Clan headquarters. We can also rent a villa for you.”

“A villa? Does it have a basement?”

A basement?

Anxi found the request odd, but nodded after thinking. “We have one available now, near the suburbs of the main district. Another warrior rented it previously, though, and it hasn't been fully cleaned. If you don't mind, we can arrange it immediately. Otherwise, we'll find another suitable villa as soon as we can.”

“Don't bother looking. I'll take that one. Please bring me there quickly.”

“Of course. I'll arrange it right away.”

...

Two hours later, Fang Heng arrived at the villa.

“Are you satisfied with it?”

“It's not bad. Go attend to your other work, and book me another private spatial studies lesson tomorrow. Preferably with today's instructor.”

“Yes, sir.”

Anxi respectfully handed over the electronic keycard and left the residence.

Back in her vehicle, she glanced toward the villa, suspicion glimmering in her eyes.

As a personal assistant, Anxi always asked about her assigned warrior's progress after each lesson.

According to Gaspar's feedback, Fang Heng was exceptionally talented in spatial studies and already possessed Master-level proficiency—an impressive foundation.

Even so, he should need some time before moving on to the next stage.

Another appointment already?

Perhaps he wanted to refine his training plan.

After all, the Noel Clan covered all study costs, and the warriors frequently wasted resources.

That was why assistants were expected to control such spending.

Anxi decided to speak with the instructor again tomorrow and reassess the situation.

Inside the villa, Fang Heng knew nothing of Anxi's little calculations. After circling the first floor, he headed straight for the basement.

Not bad.

The basement level covered over a hundred square meters.

For an ordinary person, that would certainly be enough.

But for Fang Heng, it was nowhere near sufficient.

He had plenty of zombie clones, after all.

With a flick of his wrist, several sheets of rune paper appeared in Fang Heng's hand.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

The papers burst apart in midair, and more than a dozen Lickers sprang out of the explosions, quickly forming fleshy cocoons on the spot.

The zombie clones couldn't cultivate the ability to sense external spatial energy in his place. But who could say they wouldn't be useful at the next stage?

Better to be prepared.

Besides, once he advanced to Grandmaster spatial studies, he would need to rely on Abe Akaya to convert natural energy into spatial energy and rapidly increase his skill level.

Fang Heng ordered the zombie clones to construct an Abe Akaya summoning array in the basement, then sat cross-legged and resumed his spatial studies training.

Guiding spatial power was no easy task.

With his eyes closed, Fang Heng sensed the shifting currents of spatial energy outside his body and adjusted the frequency of his mental power and spatial fluctuations.

...

“This...”

The following afternoon, when Gaspar saw Fang Heng effortlessly condense a sphere of spatial energy in his hand, astonishment filled his eyes.

What was going on?

When he saw Fang Heng yesterday, the young man's understanding had been little better than a complete beginner's.

But today...

He was utterly different!

This terrifying cultivation speed wasn't normal at all!

He'd advanced from apparent beginner to this level in just a single day?

Gaspar even suspected Fang Heng had pretended not to understand anything yesterday just to toy with him.

“Master Gaspar, what do you think?”

Gaspar steadied himself and nodded. “Excellent. You've learned to sense external spatial energy and guide your own power into harmony with it. In practical terms, you've already reached Grandmaster spatial studies. All you lack is the official professional certification.”

“As for further spatial training, I can't guide you through that. Everyone develops their own method of study, though I can point you in a general direction.”

“My own teacher told me that paths beyond Grandmaster mostly involve gradually changing the internal energy cycle, then sensing the external cycle, until one can finally control it.”

“I'm still exploring that stage myself. Your talent for spatial studies is exceptional. Perhaps we can exchange insights in the future.”

As soon as he finished speaking, a game notification appeared before Fang Heng.

[Hint: Player has completed the quest—Spatial Studies Advancement (Grandmaster). Your Master-level Spatial Studies skill has been removed. You have obtained Grandmaster-level Spatial Studies.]
