Chapter 2238

A Lead

From original Chinese Chapter 2239 · 痕迹

“Dr. Fang, we've recorded the entire array. For safety, let's withdraw now.”

“No.” Fang Heng waved a hand. “You go ahead. I want to take another look.”

“But…”

Shi Yanwu hesitated.

His orders were to protect Fang Heng and escort him safely back to the research institute.

What was he supposed to do when Fang Heng refused to leave?

A message soon arrived from command. “Captain Shi, excellent work. Your squad may withdraw. Dr. Fang will remain behind and act independently. We'll assign another team to provide protection.”

“Yes, sir! Understood!”

The battle had taught the command staff something important.

Fang Heng's individual abilities were extraordinary.

At the very least, he could look after himself.

“All right.” Shi Yanwu nodded to him. “Please be careful.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng watched the Federation soldiers leave with their collected supplies, until the Prayer-Curse Temple was empty once more.

He moved to a secluded corner, switched into his Nature Studies form, and concealed himself in a state of stillness.

…

Two hours later.

A group of Wood Clan members hurried into the temple grounds.

The elder leading them had a face like thunder.

The moment they received reports of trouble in the town, they'd rushed here to investigate.

Yet something had gone wrong anyway.

The entire Prayer-Curse Temple had been damaged!

The elder who had guarded it and all his subordinates were missing.

Everyone feared the worst.

The Federation!

Wood Clan Elder Chrono clenched his fists.

Still, something about the incident struck him as suspicious.

They knew how strong the Federation was.

Unless it committed its full forces, could it really defeat an elder guarding the temple so quickly?

That seemed unlikely.

After examining the site, a clansman came to report. “Elder, we found unusual signs of damage to the magic array.”

Chrono approached the formation and studied it. His brow quickly furrowed.

So that was it.

Despicable humans!

They must have secretly sabotaged the array, causing it to malfunction during operation.

Now the situation made sense.

Elder Lorenzo wasn't dead. He'd merely fallen into a Federation trap and become temporarily stranded on the far side of the teleportation passage!

At least that was one piece of good fortune amid the disaster.

Seeing Chrono remain silent, a clansman cautiously asked, “Elder, what should we do now?”

“Repair the magic array and assign additional personnel to the Prayer-Curse Temple. The Divine Tree has already absorbed some power, and a new temple is under construction. We only need to hold out another month before it can be put into use. Keep at it.”

“Understood!”

Fang Heng listened from concealment and immediately perked up at the mention of the Divine Tree.

The Divine Tree!

One of his targets!

Unfortunately, the conversation wasn't revealing much else of value.

Seeing Chrono preparing to leave, Fang Heng acted on an impulse and secretly placed a vampire tracking mark on him from a distance.

[Hint: The player has successfully marked Wood Clan Elder Chrono.]

“Who's there?!”

The instant the mark landed, Chrono sensed something. He whipped around and stared toward Fang Heng's hiding place.

Damn!

He'd been discovered!

Fang Heng's heart skipped a beat.

Chrono might be even stronger than Elder Lorenzo had been, and unlike the latter, he wasn't injured.

Time to run!

Fang Heng didn't dare hesitate. He immediately entered secondary-space projection and sped away.

Chrono frowned, watching the direction he'd gone.

Who had that been?

Such powerful concealment! It had nearly deceived even him.

Someone from the Leaf Clan?

“Elder, should we…”

“Don't pursue.” Chrono stopped the Wood Clan members about to chase Fang Heng. “Increase security. Protect the teleportation passage at all costs, and make sure Elder Lorenzo can return as soon as possible.”

“Yes, Elder!”

…

That night, at the island research institute.

The emerald leaves in Klevi's hand gradually blossomed with rings of luminous green light.

“Ah! It's a spatial marker.”

Klevi opened her eyes, surprise shining within them as she triggered the natural power inside the branch.

“A marker?!”

Fang Heng was startled too.

The branch in Klevi's hand was the one he'd obtained after killing the Wood Clan elder.

He had handed it to her for inspection as soon as he returned.

“Yes. A spatial marker. It points to another world.”

Klevi nodded, certain she hadn't misread it.

Fang Heng frowned.

The formation they'd found at the Tree Spirit temple had also been connected to teleportation.

So the Tree Spirits were using it to travel into another world?

A world called the Winterfrost City Region?

Things were getting more complicated by the minute.

The World of Forests was already a vast world. Could it really contain a connection to yet another world?

Fang Heng looked toward Sandy, who was standing nearby. “Sandy, any ideas?”

Sandy was still studying the jars. He scratched his head at the question.

“Nope! Not a clue. I've never seen anything like this in the old records. But those jars look like Ritual Vessels for absorbing energy. I did some research, and they used to be made specifically for cultivating Divine Trees. I still need to look into exactly how to use them, though…”

Another lead that didn't seem very useful…

Everyone exchanged looks, unsure where to go next.

Mo Jiawei asked, “Should we reproduce the marker and teleportation array and try following them in? Then we'll finally know what's going on.”

Fang Heng shook his head. “I'm worried the Holy Court will discover us.”

Mo Jiawei immediately understood.

The Divine Realm was still hunting Fang Heng.

The World of Forests was protected, but other worlds weren't necessarily safe.

If the World of Forests was connected to other events, the Divine Realm might intervene too.

Then the difficulty of the whole quest could spiral out of control.

Klevi checked Abe Akaya's condition and shook her head. “It won't be possible soon, anyway. The array has to operate together with a Divine Tree temple. In Abe Akaya's current condition, reproducing an entire temple would take time.”

Fang Heng considered his options.

He'd already collected more than enough information for the Leaf Clan's side quest to investigate the Wood Clan.

The task requirements had been exceeded, at the very least.

He could turn it in to the Leaf Clan and try to use them to probe for more information.

If that failed, he'd simply have to wait.

The Federation was still investigating too.

With its resources, it should eventually uncover something.

That settled it!

“Let's split up. Klevi, stay here and look after Abe Akaya. Gu Qingzhu will keep you safe.”

“Mm. All right.”

Mo Jiawei said, “I'll ask Starfish Company about getting some spatial crystal ore. We need it to power the Tree Spirits' formation. Better to stock up beforehand.”

“Good.”

“All right, then I'll go through the Federation's databases again and see if there's any information about the Tree Spirits that could help us extract the energy stored in these Ritual Vessels…”

Sandy was already thinking of other approaches to extracting the energy.

Once everyone had their assignments, they went off to work.

Fang Heng transformed into a bat and headed toward the Leaf Clan's gathering place.
