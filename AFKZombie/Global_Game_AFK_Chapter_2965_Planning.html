Chapter 2965

Planning

From Chinese Chapter 2966 · 谋划

Two months later.

Aboard the Nethersea starship.

In the cultivation compartment, Fang Heng abruptly opened his eyes.

The Undead Council's accumulated knowledge, built up over tens of thousands of years, was an invaluable treasure to him.

As he organized and refined his mental and soul powers, his ability to control and comprehend undead aura had improved tremendously.

By now, Fang Heng felt that his use of undead power had reached the realm of complete mastery.

Fang Heng raised his head and looked at Georgi, the chairman of the Undead Council, on the screen.

Georgi nodded approvingly, his eyes filled with admiration. “Excellent, Fang Heng. You're the most gifted scholar I've ever met. In just two months, you've reached the stage of complete mastery.”

“Thank you to all my mentors for your patient guidance.”

Fang Heng had spent most of these two months grinding.

First came his Necromancy training.

His Necromancy levels had largely been raised directly through completing missions and infusions of energy, leaving him with serious shortcomings in actual spell control and practical proficiency.

There were plenty of things even ordinary players considered basic that Fang Heng didn't understand at all.

That was why, aboard the starship, Fang Heng connected to the Undead Council remotely and received individual instruction and training from its mentors every day.

“No need to thank us. That's enough for today. By the way, rumors circulated yesterday that the God King of the Divine Realm has emerged from seclusion. They say he's comprehended a new power. After arranging some deployments for the Holy Court, he entered seclusion again.”

“The Holy Court sent representatives here today for discussions. We expect an announcement that both sides will enter a period of peace.”

Fang Heng nodded to himself.

The Holy Court had gained absolutely nothing from its war with the undead.

On the surface, the two sides remained at war, but in practice there had been no further clashes. Naturally, the Undead Council hadn't lowered its guard. It remained wary of the awakened God King, fearing he would launch another counteroffensive.

But perhaps this new God King had a decent grasp of the situation? He'd barely emerged before opening peace talks with the undead?

Meanwhile, after the Death Realm Pearl was transferred to the undead world, the Nether Clan gradually grew accustomed to their new home. As the 'new world' steadily improved, the migrants' sense of belonging toward Fang Heng also increased.

Conditions in the Death Realm kept deteriorating. The Calamities now occupied more than 90% of its territory.

Those Nether Clan members who would rather die than yield and refused to enter the new world had gradually fallen in the war against the Calamities.

The Nether Lord advancement quest's Nether Clan approval rating had quietly climbed to 87%.

Without Fang Heng and the Undead Council supporting them, the Death Realm would probably have been swallowed completely by the Calamities long ago.

Still, something about the Death Realm puzzled both Fang Heng and the Undead Council.

Ever since suffering his last serious injuries, Proust had hidden in the Nether River to recuperate.

In theory, he should have recovered by now and launched another assault on the Death Realm.

So why had so much time passed without the slightest movement?

Was he saving up for one massive attack?

In truth, Fang Heng had other plans for the Death Realm.

If Proust didn't wake up soon, Fang Heng intended to attack first.

Against Proust alone, he wasn't particularly afraid.

The problem grew much trickier with Mu Qingxin, that 'Cultivator,' also involved.

He needed to plan carefully.

On another front, Fang Heng initiated a new round of large-scale screening in the vampire world.

He screened suspicious individuals within the vampire ranks to safeguard the bloodline's purity.

He couldn't allow other factions with ulterior motives to infiltrate them.

He also began working with the Eastern Federation to provide unlocking training for vampire players.

Apart from his zombie clones, the vampires were now his strongest fighting force. Fang Heng began unlocking their dimensional power levels to increase their combat strength dramatically.

This was especially useful now that he was frequently using the Vampire Gate skill.

Several times after accepting Kinsfolk missions, Fang Heng had simply used the Vampire Gate to summon vampire warriors into various real worlds for live combat training.

At the same time, he could use the Kinsfolk mission system to farm contribution points relentlessly and take on Ouroboros Seed-related missions.

His Ouroboros Seed had now reached LV: 12, unlocking a third modification slot.

Working with the Eastern Federation, his Kinsfolk contribution skyrocketed. He exchanged it for advanced Kinsfolk weapons, arming the entire starship to the teeth.

In the Kinsfolk evaluation system, Fang Heng's starship had already reached the highest possible rating: MAX.

After analyzing and testing the Titan remnants they'd discovered, the Federation also successfully developed spatial-jump capabilities. Through Fang Heng, they purchased two small Kinsfolk starship keys and then carried out modular modifications to the ships.

More precisely, each craft was roughly half Titan spaceship and half Kinsfolk starship.

The Eastern Federation had successfully acquired interstellar flight capability.

In just over two months of cooperation with Fang Heng, the Eastern Federation had built no fewer than thirty-two small spacecraft, forming a large interstellar fleet under Fang Heng's leadership.

Now Fang Heng faced his biggest problem.

He was short of Kinsfolk points.

Although the ships were grinding twenty-four hours a day, traveling between worlds to complete Kinsfolk commissions and gather resources to exchange for points, the expenses were just as enormous.

Redeeming the keys and some components for thirty-two small ships, not to mention completing the main starship's compartments and offensive and defensive modules, had already consumed a fortune.

Then, with a grand sweep of his hand, Fang Heng spent every point he'd saved over the previous month on one of the Kinsfolk's highest-grade special weapons: the Eye of Stars.

The Eye of Stars was the Kinsfolk's ultimate weapon, specially designed to fight Ouroboros Seeds.

Its power was sufficient to injure even an SSS-grade Ouroboros Seed insect nest.

Fang Heng had been interested in the weapon ever since it first appeared as an unlockable item in the exchange menu.

With its help, he might be able to go toe-to-toe with the Ouroboros Seed in the Death Realm.

Unfortunately, even after redeeming the Eye of Stars construction blueprints, he'd still need to build twenty-four Eye of Stars prism positioning devices around the target area.

Each positioning tower cost a fortune to manufacture.

And even after the Eye of Stars had been constructed, every attack would consume an enormous amount of contribution points.

His Kinsfolk honor farming speed was already terrifying by anyone else's standards, but at this rate it would still take several months to accumulate enough.

There was no helping it.

For now, he'd just have to keep grinding slowly.

Hopefully he could finish before Proust revived in the Death Realm.

Fang Heng lamented how little time he'd had to grow. He was still building his foundation from scratch, unlike those great factions that had accumulated resources for thousands or tens of thousands of years and could squander them at will.
