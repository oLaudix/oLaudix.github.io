Chapter 3065

Expansion

From Chinese Chapter 3066 · 扩散

The universe was vast.

Besides the Black Mist game, there were other ways to acquire abilities and advance the various academic disciplines in the outside world.

After more than three months of arduous cultivation, most of Fang Heng's academic disciplines had reached the Master level. Obtaining advancement books directly from the game beyond that point was extremely difficult.

The elves had told Fang Heng that the real world also possessed numerous inherited methods of advancing academic studies.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital gathered the universe's foremost fighting strength. It would be easier to find a path to greater power there.

Of course, Fang Heng was also eager to learn how his current abilities measured up against the rest of the universe.

Finally, with their connection to the Kinsfolk's central system temporarily severed, the vast quantities of ore his fleet had mined recently had nowhere to go.

This journey to the Seven-Realm Martial Capital was also an opportunity to find a good buyer for those precious minerals.

All in all, Fang Heng was already growing impatient to arrive.

“All right, we're almost there. I'll contact you again when I have news.”

With that, Fang Heng ended the call.

Aboard the Elven Clan ship, Prince Korkalot had lost his former arrogance. He told Fang Heng, “Boss Fang, we're approaching the outer star system of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital. All ships and personnel entering must queue for identification and inspection. It will probably take three hours.”

Fang Heng frowned. “My identity may have been restricted by the Kinsfolk. It would be inconvenient for me to undergo inspection.”

Ever since witnessing Fang Heng's power, Korkalot had held him in awe. After returning to the elves, he had personally reported to the Elven King and requested permission to follow Fang Heng for a period of training.

Korkalot understood the situation perfectly. If he returned home and settled down, the Third Prince's reputation would effectively strip him of any chance to succeed to the elven throne.

Only by going out into the universe alongside Fang Heng might an opportunity present itself.

“Well...”

“Don't worry. I'll remain in my cabin. No one will notice me.”

“Understood.”

After thinking it over, Korkalot realized that made sense.

Given Fang Heng's strength, there really weren't many people who could find him if he wanted to stay hidden.

Once everyone had left the cabin, Fang Heng stood in its center and slowly closed his eyes.

“Hum...”

The outline of a magic array gradually formed beneath his feet.

Hm?

Fang Heng immediately sensed something unusual.

Strange.

He hadn't entered or left the game through his special ability for some time. Why was it taking so long now? It was as though some force were resisting his entry.

Fortunately, the magic array hadn't failed completely. It was merely operating much more slowly. Wisps of black mist began seeping from the formation until they enveloped Fang Heng completely.

Only after the array had been operating for a full ten minutes did Fang Heng return to the game.

...

Inside the game, Fang Heng opened his eyes again and muttered to himself.

“Strange...”

What had caused that unusual sensation?

Hm?

Fang Heng suddenly noticed a dark-red warning in his game log.

[Warning: Player is attempting to spread the Black Mist... Current Black Mist completion ≈ 0%.]

[Hint: Player has attempted Black Mist expansion. Special exchange options have now been unlocked.]

Spread the Black Mist?

Special exchange options?

Fang Heng stared at the game notification, puzzled.

On closer examination, he discovered a small additional exchange panel beside his inventory.

Opening it revealed only three exchangeable items.

Black Mist Crystals—large, medium, and small.

“I don't get it.”

Fang Heng shook his head after some thought and decided not to dwell on it.

...

Aboard the Elven Clan ship.

Before long, border-defense troops from the Seven-Realm Martial Capital entered to conduct their inspection.

“Report! Traces of Black Mist aura detected in Cabin Seven.”

The border-defense squad leader turned to Korkalot. “What's the meaning of this?”

Korkalot was also confused.

Traces of Black Mist?

Cabin Seven?

That was the very cabin Fang Heng had occupied earlier!

Right.

Korkalot suddenly remembered that Fang Heng's world had been affected by the Black Mist. Perhaps some of that aura had clung to him.

Kappa understood immediately and began frantically signaling to Korkalot with his eyes.

Korkalot failed to notice and replied coldly, “The ship recently carried out a mission. We may have been contaminated while passing through a Black Mist region.”

“Is that so?” The border-defense captain sneered. “That explains it. It seems you picked up an intruder while passing through the Black Mist. You elves are lucky you encountered us.”

“Under the Seven-Realm Martial Capital's border regulations, we'll temporarily seal the entire vessel. During the lockdown, we'll disinfect it thoroughly to ensure the Black Mist doesn't affect the capital.”

“You'll be charged the relevant fees when the vessel departs. The passengers may enter the capital after passing inspection.”

Korkalot immediately looked indignant. “Search and lockdown? What right do you have to do that?”

We're screwed!

Kappa slapped his forehead.

That idiot Korkalot. Didn't he understand that the damned officer was just fishing for a bribe?

...

Five hours later.

A magic array appeared out of thin air inside the Elven Clan ship.

As it rotated, black mist slowly drifted out.

Fang Heng's figure materialized within the array.

“Click!”

Fang Heng froze.

Before his senses had fully recovered, a pair of handcuffs had already been snapped around his wrists.

“Hum...”

A faint light flashed across the cuffs, revealing intricate alchemical patterns.

As the light flickered, the cuffs transformed into two bands of markings encircling his wrists.

A ring of border guards stared at Fang Heng.

Their squad leader stepped forward, scanned Fang Heng's head with a handheld scanner, then shook his head in boredom and waved dismissively.

“Boring. I thought we'd found something interesting. Just a pan-human wanted by the Kinsfolk. No wonder he tried sneaking into the Seven-Realm Martial Capital. Take him away and lock him up. Let's see if we can squeeze some money out of the elves.”

“Yes, sir.”

A guard nearby whispered, “Sir, should we notify the Kinsfolk about him?”

The squad leader rolled his eyes. “Do you really need to ask?”

The guard grinned. “Understood, sir.”

Dealing with the Kinsfolk was a pain. If they handed him over, the Kinsfolk would interrogate everyone endlessly as usual, and there'd be nothing to gain.

Better to save themselves the trouble. They would toss Fang Heng among the ordinary prisoners and slaves and be done with it.

And they could use him to demand a little more compensation from the elves.

After all, helping someone cross the border illegally could be treated as a serious offense—or a minor one, depending on what suited them.

— End of Chapter 3065 —
