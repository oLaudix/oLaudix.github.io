Chapter 2272

Creeping Vines

From original Chinese Chapter 2273 · 爬藤

Countless streams of acidic fluid sprayed from the moths' mouths and landed on the Lickers, hissing as they burned against their flesh.

Unfortunately for the moths, the effects were still limited.

The Mist-Hidden Moths' attacks were weak. Neither their needle-like strikes nor their acid could seriously harm the Licker horde.

Their attacks were effectively neutralized!

Zete sighed in relief as he watched the Lickers block the moths. He turned to glance at Fang Heng inside the camp.

Lickers...

They were more summoned creatures controlled by that human.

Individually, they were far weaker than Tree Spirits, but their numbers were immense, and their vitality was frighteningly tenacious.

Even the Tree Spirits would find them a headache in a real fight.

No wonder Elder Vannis was so wary of the human.

Fang Heng was still operating the array.

Seeing the battle outside stabilize, he relaxed slightly.

Keep going!

Speed up the formation!

"Hey, you lot."

The Leaf Clan guards assigned to watch him turned their heads at his call.

"Stop standing around. Come help."

Help?

The guards looked at one another.

"I'm standing right here. What exactly are you watching for? Don't waste time. Come stand inside those circles and pour in your psychic power to accelerate the array."

They exchanged another glance but couldn't think of a good objection. Quietly, they stepped forward and joined in supplying psychic energy.

As the array accelerated, the Ritual Vessel absorbed dimensional energy even faster!

What nobody except Fang Heng realized was that the subsidiary array on the floor below was accelerating just as rapidly!

Dimensional power poured into the hidden Ritual Vessel at an increasing rate!

...

An hour later.

Outside, Zete and the other Leaf Clan guards were moving stiffly from exhaustion.

Faced with an almost solid black wall of Mist-Hidden Moths blocking the view, Zete repeatedly raised his hand and launched natural-energy arrows.

Each fan of arrows cleared a large patch of enemies.

But an even larger swarm immediately surged forward to fill the gap.

Zete had to admit that without the living wall of Lickers blocking the moths' approach, the Leaf Clan defenders could never have withstood a swarm this dense.

Huh?

Zete suddenly noticed the moths were rapidly thinning out.

Instead of attacking the camp, they were dispersing into the surrounding mist.

Were they retreating?

Just then, a guard rushed up from behind and whispered, "Team Leader, the array's operation is complete. It's been shut down."

Finally. It was over.

After casting spells nonstop against the moths, even Zete's thoughts felt sluggish. He glanced back toward the temporary camp.

The other clansmen had stopped operating the formation and were resting where they stood.

Sustaining such an intense array required tremendous psychic power. Most of the Leaf Clan members had nearly reached their limits.

"What about the Ritual Vessel? How much dimensional energy did it absorb?"

At the mention of the vessel, the reporting clansman's face lit up. "Team Leader, the Ritual Vessel has finished absorbing energy."

"What?!"

Zete stared at him, unsure he'd heard correctly. "You mean it's completely full?"

"Yes! We've confirmed it. The vessel has reached maximum dimensional-energy capacity!"

Full capacity!

In just one hour!

That meant Fang Heng's modifications were extraordinarily effective!

Efficiency had increased by much more than a mere doubling.

In the past, filling a single Ritual Vessel from a low-energy world like this took at least two days.

Even including the time spent constructing the temporary camp, Fang Heng had completed the job in only nine hours.

The efficiency was almost terrifying.

"Come. Show me."

Zete hurried into the camp with his subordinate and personally examined the Ritual Vessel, nodding with satisfaction.

Very good!

The vessel was completely intact.

Zete glanced at Fang Heng standing beside the formation. "Fang Heng, did everything go smoothly?"

"Very smoothly."

"Then next time, could we..."

Fang Heng knew what he wanted to ask and nodded. "As long as the arrays in this camp aren't destroyed while we're away, we can use them directly next time without rebuilding anything."

"Excellent!"

Zete was even more pleased.

In theory, that meant they could fill a Ritual Vessel in just one hour on future trips!

"Everyone, prepare to return to the Prayer-Curse Temple immediately."

"Yes!"

"I still need to inspect the array. Give me thirty minutes."

Zete considered this and agreed. "Fine. We leave in thirty minutes."

Everyone watched Fang Heng walk out of the temporary camp.

Standing before the foul-smelling remains covering the ground, Fang Heng slowly spread his hands.

The blood and severed remains of countless dead moths rose into the air, gathering into a reeking mass of blood.

The mass churned with a hissing sound, releasing wisps of pale blue-white vapor.

...

Half an hour later, Zete personally escorted the Ritual Vessel back to the temple.

The vessel was promptly placed at the very center of the giant magic array.

The two guardian elders couldn't conceal their surprise.

Had they really filled the vessel so quickly?

"Team Leader, we've sent word to Elder Vannis. He's on his way. Should we wait for him?"

"No. Proceed to the next test immediately."

Zete was excited.

Helping cultivate the Divine Tree sapling would bring enormous merit!

It seemed he'd stumbled upon a golden opportunity!

"Fang Heng, can we begin?"

Fang Heng checked the formation, then nodded when he was satisfied. "Yes. Whenever you're ready."

Zete turned toward the two guardian elders. "Elders, please help stimulate the Divine Tree sapling's growth."

"Very well."

Both elders nodded.

Fang Heng closed his eyes and infused the array with psychic power.

The formation began rotating slowly.

Cyan light spread through it, and a pale green glow rose from the Ritual Vessel at its center.

Bathed in that energy, the Divine Tree sapling in the middle of the array began growing quietly!

At the same time, a magic array spun rapidly within Fang Heng's right pupil!

Beneath the Prayer-Curse Temple, on the third basement level.

Inside a concealed room, another subsidiary array began turning.

"Shhk, shhk..."

Abe Akaya's roots slowly emerged along the lines of the formation.

Only a few slender roots appeared at its center, nothing more.
