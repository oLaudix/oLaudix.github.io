Chapter 2109

Preparations

From original Chinese Chapter 2110 · 准备

Some experiments required low temperatures, so the laboratory had undergone another major upgrade recently. A new refrigerated research facility had been constructed on the fourth underground level.

Several large glass experimental chambers stood in its center.

To suit one of Qiu Yaokang's peculiar preferences, the chambers had been built in the shape of coffins.

“Here they are. I've examined these Fusion Tyrants and discovered that after injecting a compound mixed with secretions from Abe Akaya, they remain calm at extremely low temperatures.”

Qiu Yaokang looked up at the Fusion Tyrants inside the glass coffins and considered the results. “It has something to do with the cursed vampire bloodline.”

Fang Heng also looked toward the high-pressure glass coffins.

Fusion Tyrants!

Unlike ordinary Fusion Tyrants, these few had been fused with pieces of the vampire monarch progenitor's severed body.

Back then, the power of those remains had been uncontrollable, so Fang Heng had handed them over to Qiu Yaokang for safekeeping.

After his initial research, Qiu Yaokang had kept them stored here ever since.

“Because the power couldn't be controlled, we stopped the follow-up experiments halfway through. But we did discover a few things. The vampire monarch's will has been erased, yet the tissue is still biologically active. It's an extremely terrifying fusion energy.”

Qiu Yaokang glanced at Fang Heng. “Its effects differ from the unusual genes in your body. It's a completely different form of power. Very strange—almost like a destructive force mixed with a force that absorbs energy to repair itself. They're intertwined and constantly acting on each other. I can't explain the details in a few sentences without showing you the report. In short, the two forces will remain in this state until they're completely exhausted.”

With that, Qiu Yaokang clapped his hands.

A research assistant came forward and set a case full of syringes on the nearby laboratory table.

“What's this?”

“A product of our recent research. It's a drug that temporarily suppresses that special power. Manufacturing it is quite expensive, so this is all we've got. Take it if you need it. If we have sufficient resources later, we can mass-produce it.”

Qiu Yaokang shrugged. “I mentioned it to you before, though I doubt you paid attention. This drug only works against this particular kind of life energy, and its effects don't last long. Be careful when using it.”

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

“You can even do that?”

Qiu Yaokang gave him a sideways glance. “It's all science. Why wouldn't I be able to?”

Fang Heng was left speechless. He picked up the case from the table.

[Game Hint: You have obtained Experimental Anti-Gene Agent GFI-221 (specialized agent, effective against certain energy-based life forms) ×24.]

Item: Experimental Anti-Gene Agent GFI-221.

Description: A high-tier laboratory product that affects specific types of targets. After injection, the target loses 98% of its biological activity for thirty minutes. The effect diminishes significantly afterward.

“This stuff...”

Fang Heng muttered to himself and placed the case straight into his backpack.

Perhaps it would come in handy.

· · ·

Outside the prison, birds sang among flowering plants, and the surroundings were peaceful.

The Divine Tree Abe Akaya radiated vitality in the sunshine.

The demi-humans who had earlier been taken away by the Federation's research institute had not yet left.

Most disliked staying inside the prison and had instead pitched tents in the forest.

Fang Heng looked up at Abe Akaya.

Apart from his bloodline power, the zombie clone army, and his ultimate move, the Everlasting Moon, Abe Akaya was arguably the strongest trump card currently available to him.

Fang Heng compared its strength with what he had sensed from the Divine Realm's pursuers.

Abe Akaya still seemed a little weaker.

Perhaps entering the World of Myriad Forests would allow the Divine Tree to advance further.

When Abe Akaya learned that they would soon be opening the teleportation passage, it sent him an emotion of delight. Layers of branches and leaves gathered before Fang Heng.

[Game Hint: Do you wish to open the teleportation passage to the World of Myriad Forests? Once opened, you must enter within twelve hours. The game-time countdown will begin after teleportation is completed.]

“Open it.”

The Divine Tree's branches gradually shifted aside, revealing a pale-green spatial passage at their center.

[Game Hint: You have opened a teleportation passage to the World of Myriad Forests. You may select teammates to enter the world through this passage.]

[Game Hint: Remaining passage energy: 10/10. One energy point recovers every twenty-four hours. Each teleportation consumes one energy point.]

Fang Heng studied the messages.

The quest could be completed by multiple people.

And there were no restrictions requiring them to be players rather than NPCs.

In that case...

He could bring a few capable helpers along!

Fang Heng turned toward a section of Abe Akaya's trunk.

Klevi peeked out from behind the enormous tree.

“Klevi, I need your help.”

“Of course.”

Klevi obediently stepped out from behind the trunk.

She had heard a little about the World of Myriad Forests during the past two days. Standing behind Fang Heng, she asked, “Will Sister Qingzhu be coming with us?”

Fang Heng looked to his right.

“Mm. I'll come too.”

Gu Qingzhu stepped forward and nodded. “My people's condition has stabilized. We'll remain here to recuperate for a while. You've already helped us so much, and if there's anything I can do for you this time, I'd like to join.”

“Good.”

Fang Heng readily agreed.

Gu Qingzhu was strong, and as a demi-human she had certain advantages, particularly when communicating with characters in a game world.

Klevi, meanwhile, might prove invaluable because of her elven heritage and understanding of natural energy.

And there was something else...

Fang Heng shifted his gaze to the right.

“Rustle, rustle...”

The overlapping vines surrounding Abe Akaya parted, revealing a passage behind them.

“Fang Heng! Me too! Don't forget me!”

Sandy came running over, shouting all the way. “I heard you're heading off to conquer another world, right? You absolutely must take me! The treasures of an alien civilization—oh, heavens above! I will not allow them to be abandoned out there! That would be a desecration of culture...!”

Fang Heng immediately felt a headache coming on and rubbed his forehead.

All right, then.

He had no idea where Sandy had heard the news, but the man had been demanding to join the trip to the World of Myriad Forests since yesterday.

He might as well bring him.

If Ji Xiaobo weren't still at the Mork Temple dealing with the Holy Court, he would probably be clamoring to come along too.

Besides these companions,

there was one more person Fang Heng absolutely had to bring!

Mo Jiawei!

Thinking about it carefully, he'd been pursued by the Holy Court and the Federation one after another lately. Perhaps there was something wrong with his luck.

Fang Heng felt he badly needed a little help from good fortune.

He looked at Sandy. “Where's Mr. Mo? You can come, but on one condition. You have to convince Mr. Mo to accompany me.”

Sandy nodded frantically, thumped his chest, and declared with absolute confidence, “Leave it to me! I'll find him right now! I'll drag him back even if I have to tie him up!”

— End of Chapter 2109 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Research. Experimental Anti-Gene Agent GFI-221 has twenty-four doses and is described as reducing the target’s biological activity by 98% for thirty minutes, after which its effect greatly weakens.
  • Source name irregularity. The Chinese briefly writes 阿布赤 instead of 阿部赤耶; both refer to Abe Akaya.
  • Portal energy. The passage has ten energy points, regenerates one per twenty-four hours, and consumes one per traveler.

Chinese Expressions

  • 绑也把他给绑回来 (bǎng yě bǎ tā gěi bǎng huílái). Sandy promises to bring Mo Jiawei back “even if I have to tie him up”; the exaggerated insistence is intentional and preserved.
