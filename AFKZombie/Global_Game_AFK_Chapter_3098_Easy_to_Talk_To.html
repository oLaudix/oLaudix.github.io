Chapter 3098

Easy to Talk To

From Chinese Chapter 3099 · 好说话

Nasir paused, realizing Fang Heng was probably still cultivating. “Actually, I didn't come for anything important. I heard you'd made a breakthrough and rushed over to see for myself. Sorry to interrupt your training.”

“Not at all.” Fang Heng waved and invited him into the study. “As it happens, there's something I'd like to ask you.”

“No need to be so formal. It's not a matter of teaching you. We'll discuss it and improve together.”

“Have you heard of the Black Mist game?”

“I've heard a few rumors. Apparently it's a special power that can grant certain abilities, but I've never seen it personally.”

Nasir frowned slightly at the sudden mention of Black Mist. Then a thought struck him. “I've heard pan-human races are especially susceptible to Black Mist. Has it affected you too?”

“Mm. Black Mist has spread to my star system. Part of my reason for coming to the Seven-Realm Martial Capital is to learn more about it so we can avoid greater trouble in the future.”

“I see... No need to be too anxious, brother. As far as I know, Black Mist isn't actually dangerous.”

After thinking, Nasir added, “Only a very small part of the universe has been affected. Few people really understand it. I hear the Kinsfolk are exceptionally wary of Black Mist, and the detection devices around the city were installed at their insistence. I remember our Alchemy Association helping with the installation. I'll ask around and see whether I can find anything useful.”

“Thank you.”

Fang Heng cautioned, “Nasir, there are some disputes between the Kinsfolk and humanity. Please don't mention me. I wouldn't want to cause you unnecessary trouble.”

“I've heard the rumors. The Kinsfolk have always been hostile toward pan-human races. But our Alchemy Association isn't afraid of them, and neither is the ancient Noel Clan, which employs you. Still, you're right that caution is sensible. I'll keep that in mind.”

Nasir looked at Fang Heng, thinking of recruiting him, and smiled. “Brother Fang, would you help me with something too?”

“If it's within my power, certainly.”

“We both work for the Noel Clan. You've probably heard that things are unstable outside lately. Many warriors have quit the clan. Several members of my team were poached recently, and we haven't found suitable replacements. I've heard you're very capable. Would you like to give our team a try?”

“Sure.”

...

Two days later, Nasir and Fang Heng entered a combat preparation room on the eighth floor of the Noel Clan headquarters.

“Everyone, this is Fang Heng, the newcomer I recommended.” Nasir cleared his throat to draw attention, patted Fang Heng's shoulder, and introduced him. “You've all heard of him, right?”

Many of the people present looked over and nodded in greeting.

“Where's the elder?”

“He went out to look for reinforcements. He should be back this afternoon.”

“Good.”

Nasir led Fang Heng to a shapely female warrior. “Fang Heng, this is Hainiya, of the Sea Clan. Her race is exceptionally gifted with water elements—almost every member is a natural elemental scholar. Hainiya is also an Alchemy Association member and one of our team's core fighters.”

The Sea Clan resembled humans, but their deep-blue hair formed thick, floating locks like braided dreadlocks, looking as though it were made from gathered deep-sea water. A layer of water seemed to flow over their skin.

Hainiya's eyes glimmered blue-green as she nodded to Fang Heng. “Hello, Fang Heng.”

“Hello.”

Fang Heng returned a cupped-fist greeting.

“Hainiya was once recruited by the Kinsfolk and helped establish some Black Mist defenses, so she knows a little about it.”

“That was a long time ago. I'd rather not discuss the Kinsfolk, Nasir.” Hainiya declined calmly and turned to Fang Heng. “Are you interested in Black Mist?”

“My world has encountered it. I want to understand it better.”

“Then you've probably asked the wrong person. I don't know much either. I did assist with arranging Black Mist-sensing prisms. With the help of magic arrays, their detection range and precision can be greatly increased.”

“The prisms are purely for detection. When Black Mist reaches a certain concentration, an alarm sounds and the Kinsfolk respond immediately.”

After a moment, Hainiya added, “I can sense that the Kinsfolk are afraid of Black Mist. Unless circumstances are exceptional, they avoid approaching it directly. But many people from other races have encountered it themselves without experiencing any actual effect.”

Fang Heng asked, “Do you know how to eliminate Black Mist completely?”

“Black Mist dissipates on its own over time,” Hainiya explained calmly. “You only need to eliminate the source producing it.”

Fang Heng thought that was troublesome.

By her explanation, wasn't he himself the source of the Black Mist?

He could hardly eliminate that.

“If your world has Black Mist, perhaps you could use spatial studies to expel it elsewhere.”

A middle-aged man walked over from the side.

Nasir smiled at him and introduced him. “This is Nordin, of the Noel Clan, one of the strongest members of our team.”

“Hey, I wouldn't go that far. Hello, Fang Heng.” Nordin continued the discussion. “We could establish a two-way spatial passage and exploit the difference in aura concentration at each end to channel the Black Mist out.”

Hainiya shook her head. “That treats the symptom, not the cause.”

But Fang Heng's eyes lit up.

That might work!

Just as it had back in the Necromancy Research Society.

He could arrange arrays at both ends. Once the passage opened, the Black Mist would naturally flow from the higher-concentration end to the lower-concentration end, out of his basement!

Who cared whether it treated the cause? If it solved his immediate problem, that was enough!

Nordin didn't argue. He smiled at Fang Heng. “If you need help, I can assist.”

While they were talking, the preparation room door opened and everyone looked toward it.

A thin, elderly man entered.

“Elder Leander.”

Everyone nodded respectfully.

“Mm. You're all here.”

The elder waved a hand.

Nasir led Fang Heng toward him.

“Elder, this is Fang Heng, the man I mentioned.”

“Oh?”

Elder Leander was surprised by Fang Heng's youth. He looked him over approvingly and nodded. “I've watched both of your matches. Very good. Would you be interested in joining our team?”

“Yes.”

In good spirits, Leander waved cheerfully. “Excellent. Then stay with us.”
