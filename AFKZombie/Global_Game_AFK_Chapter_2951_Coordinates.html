Chapter 2951

Coordinates

From original Chinese Chapter 2952 · 坐标

Li Zhixuan drew a deep breath and stared at the three-dimensional hologram.

How had Fang Heng gotten his hands on the blueprints for the supermassive starship that the Western Federation had worked so hard to acquire?

She had even participated in that project.

The Kinsfolk had given the Western Federation the blueprints only after a long series of missions and negotiations.

But the Federation currently lacked the manpower, Contribution Points, and materials to build it. They had decided that medium-sized starships would provide more fighting power for the investment.

These blueprints were supposed to be locked away as top-secret documents in the Western Federation's central warehouse.

Why were they here?

How had he obtained them?

"Well? With this, won't we be able to leave this place on our own?"

Li Zhixuan studied the projection for a long while before recovering. She nodded. "For the ship to launch, several essentials must be in place: a complete hull, sufficient energy, a functioning Star Map warp system, the Kinsfolk Star Map, and destination coordinates."

"We can trade for the warp-network equipment through the Kinsfolk system. The complete Star Map network data is the objective of my assignment. I've memorized it, and with a few days I can reproduce a copy."

"We've already prepared the remaining energy. All that's left is to assemble the starship and find a suitable warp point..."

As she worked through the requirements, Li Zhixuan slowly looked up at Fang Heng.

Suddenly, she had a strange feeling.

Had Fang Heng planned every part of this in advance?

Looking away, she continued, "Building a starship will require enormous time and effort. I'll need more personnel. For certain precision components, I recommend exchanging directly through the Kinsfolk system..."

...

"What do you mean, we can't go in?"

"Sorry, you two."

Alec blocked the entrance to the newly built underground passage and grinned at Vion and his companion. "Boss Fang says the mine down there is our new extraction zone. No outsiders allowed."

Vion frowned. "Not even us?"

Zaka wondered whether this idiot had any self-awareness.

They meant you in particular, moron!

"Boss Fang has given instructions. Until we reach a proper cooperation agreement with the Western Federation, it wouldn't be convenient to deepen our cooperation. We don't want arguments later."

"But our researchers are inside!"

Zaka spread his hands with a shameless look. "Really? Couldn't tell you. Maybe the researchers have some arrangement with Boss Fang. Either way, I can't let you in."

"You!"

Vion's anger began to boil.

Since when was there somewhere on Federation territory that he wasn't allowed to enter? How dare this brat stop him?

Did the fool have no idea who held the power here?

Vion turned angrily to Colette. "Colette! Is your military going to allow this?"

"The military's assignment is to assist with ore extraction and wait here for support. The researchers' current activities fall outside our orders, Vion."

"Fine! Fine! So you're on their side, are you? We'll see how you explain that to the council!"

Vion didn't dare force his way through. He threw up his hands and stormed off.

Colette nodded to Alec and Zaka before returning to the ship himself.

Vion was furious.

He resented not only Fang Heng's attitude, but the fact that people actually dared defy the presidential expert team and stand up to them.

After several days of delay, Fang Heng's group hadn't softened at all. Instead, they seemed to be daring the Federation to do its worst.

Unfortunately, electromagnetic interference made communications unreliable. A single round trip of messages could take ages.

Worse still, the military wasn't taking his side!

Whose side were they on?

Zerg stood guard over the tunnel entrance, and Vion had only a handful of people. Forcing his way in was impossible.

Back in the base's control room, he watched the external camera feeds. Wayne's players made trip after trip, carrying crates of supplies into the underground passage.

They had been moving research equipment before. Now they were hauling supplies too?

Vion asked, "What's the meaning of those supplies? They belong to our ship. Why are they being moved outside?"

The operator replied, "They're research supplies. I heard they plan to establish an underground research base to support construction of the Kinsfolk Star Map facility."

A satellite base?

What a load of crap!

Vion knew how terrible the conditions were on this planet. Without Federation assistance, they couldn't build anything outside in such hostile geological conditions!

Digging tunnels?

The quicksand would simply collapse on top of them!

What game were they playing by hauling those materials out?

And the military!

They had gone insane too, indulging Fang Heng's madness!

Vion ground his teeth and kept watching the surveillance feeds.

Three hours passed.

Vion gradually calmed down.

But then unease crept over him, and cold sweat trickled down his forehead.

Something wasn't right!

He had stood there watching for over three hours, and he could say with certainty that not a single researcher had come back out!

In this environment, the researchers needed protective suits to stay alive outside.

Their shields should have run out of energy after about two hours, forcing them to return.

So why hadn't they?

Vion clenched his fists.

Could it be...

Had they truly constructed an even better shelter underground?

"Come on! Take me to the laboratory!"

"Yes, sir!"

The moment Vion entered the temporary base's laboratory section, he sensed something was wrong.

Military personnel normally guarded this area.

Where had everyone gone today?

He threw open the laboratory doors—and froze in shock.

What was this?

The entire lab looked as though it had been looted. Every piece of research equipment and every document was gone.

Everything?!

So those crates they'd been moving contained the lab's reserve materials and research instruments?

Beyond his anger, Vion now felt a rising fear.

He was beginning to suspect the situation had escaped his control.

The laboratory housed sophisticated instruments that would be ruined within minutes of exposure to the outside environment.

Fang Heng and the military weren't fools. If they'd authorized the transfer, it meant they probably had built a safe zone outside!

At the very least, they must have some kind of protected storage area!

How had they done it?

Underground?

What sort of advanced technology would allow something like that?

The more Vion thought about it, the more frightening the implications became. He almost didn't dare think any further.
