Chapter 2158

Runestone

From original Chinese Chapter 2159 · 符文石

Not good!

Fang Heng frowned.

Without the Undying Body effect, the Lickers' ability to sustain the fight plummeted!

Under the mechanical army's sweeping attacks, they couldn't catch even half a second's breathing room!

Bang! Bang, bang!

Flesh and blood burst apart across the battlefield!

“That's about enough...”

Watching the Licker numbers dwindle, Fang Heng murmured to himself.

It wasn't that he couldn't continue fighting.

He could enter the battlefield personally, charge straight into the enemy's semi-mechanized formations, and postpone defeat for much longer.

But...

There was no need.

Fang Heng looked at the Tree Spirits beside him. “You can see the situation. It's bad. Three minutes at most. After that, my forces will collapse.”

The Tree Spirits looked grave.

They knew he was telling the truth. Without arguing, they immediately returned to the institute to report.

Less than a minute later, Fang Heng noticed a large group of Tree Spirits emerging from the building.

Senzo was among them.

Their expressions were stiff and gloomy.

It wasn't difficult to guess they'd failed to find the Niam Alloy.

To Fang Heng's surprise, he spotted an old acquaintance in the group: Vannis.

Vannis was walking at the very back with his head down, thoroughly dejected. Then he caught sight of Fang Heng outside and looked up in surprise.

Hm? What was going on?

Fang Heng?!

Why was he here too?

Senzo approached the institute entrance and surveyed the battle outside.

The situation was terrible.

Most of the treants had already been destroyed in previous waves. Only the Leaf Clan and Root Clan still retained most of their fighting strength.

They'd managed to hold out this long largely because Fang Heng's Lickers were acting as meat shields at the front, giving the Tree Spirits behind them an opportunity to attack.

Even so, the Tree Spirits had suffered severe losses.

And they'd found absolutely nothing!

The various Tree Spirit clans invited by Great Elder Senzo looked even more displeased.

“Have everyone begin withdrawing.”

“Understood!”

At this point, a safer retreat would require them to swallow their pride and ask Fang Heng for help.

Senzo drew a deep breath, prepared himself to pay a steep price, and approached Fang Heng with several Tree Spirits.

“We'll withdraw through the rear of the research institute shortly.”

Fang Heng turned toward the dispirited Tree Spirits. For some reason, his mood immediately improved.

Pretending not to know, he asked, “Did you get what you came for?”

Elder Senzo took a deep breath and shook his head.

They'd expended enormous manpower and resources and sacrificed the lives of their people, only to obtain nothing.

It had been a disastrous failure.

“Why? Never mind. Whatever happened, we're in a difficult position now.” Afraid he might burst out laughing, Fang Heng turned toward the distant battlefield. “Under these circumstances, the Federation probably won't just let us walk away.”

“I need your assistance to cover our retreat.”

Fang Heng neither accepted nor refused. He simply stood there silently.

Senzo sighed to himself.

He understood this human now.

Benefits came first with him.

But Senzo had no choice.

Things had reached this point. Unless the Tree Spirits were willing to pay, they wouldn't escape safely.

“The Tree Spirits are willing to give you this rune. I believe you'll find it useful.”

As he spoke, Senzo held out a small, stone-like rune.

[Item: Protection of the Forest (Rune).]

Type: Rune.

Grade: Unknown.

Description: Contains the protective power of the ancient Tree Spirits. This item can be engraved onto equipment or weapons (some equipment and weapons may be incompatible). When the player wears or wields an engraved item, damage received from nature-type spells is reduced by an additional 20%.

Description (triggered by Advanced Nature Studies): The player may directly absorb the natural energy contained in this item and convert it into Nature Studies experience, or infuse that energy into another object to produce other special effects.

Description: The rune appears to contain an immense quantity of natural energy.

Seeing the rune, Vannis was shocked and shouted from behind them, “Elder! Why are we giving a human...?”

“Silence!”

Senzo snapped at Vannis, then turned to Fang Heng. “Fang Heng, the Federation's war machines will only grow more numerous. We must act quickly.”

“Very well.”

Fang Heng reasoned that there was little more he could squeeze from the Tree Spirits. He accepted the rune and nodded. “I'll try to break through from the front immediately and draw the Federation's attention. Whether you manage to escape, though...”

“Mm. That will be sufficient.”

[Hint: The player's current quest—Cover the Tree Spirits' Retreat—has changed. The player must cover the Tree Spirits' escape from the institute grounds. Quest difficulty increased. Additional reward gained: Protection of the Forest (Rune).]

[Hint: The player's favorability with the Tree Spirits has decreased by 5.]

Fang Heng clicked his tongue at the notification.

The Tree Spirits.

Even when begging for help, they were still so unwilling that his favorability dropped.

Whatever.

They could part on decent terms.

Since he'd accepted their payment, he'd help them one more time.

After thinking it over, he decided it was better to help. What if he took things too far and the Federation wiped out the Tree Spirits entirely? He still needed to figure out how to enter the Inner World, and the Tree Spirits might know the way.

Having settled on that, Fang Heng immediately regrouped the Lickers and launched a frontal counterattack against the Federation!

At the same time, he darted into the center of the battlefield himself!

“Here he comes!”

The semi-mechanized Federation soldiers at the rear instantly grew tense when they saw Fang Heng entering the battle.

Before deploying, they'd carefully memorized all the intelligence on him!

“Reporting to command: target identified—Fang Heng!”

“Target threat level S! Don't let him get close!”

“Maintain suppressive fire! Try the latest injection agents!”

“Understood!”

Before Fang Heng could reach the Federation squads, a dense volley of sniper needles flew toward him!

The blood-red greatsword on his back erupted with a bang!

Blood energy instantly spread into a wall that shielded him from the front.

Puff! Puff, puff!

Every needle struck and was absorbed by the crimson barrier.

Behind him, the Tree Spirits used the opportunity to abandon the institute and withdraw toward its rear.

Vannis watched Fang Heng charge alone into the semi-mechanized troops, fighting with apparent ease and without being overwhelmed. Astonishment filled his eyes.

This human...

Could he really be that powerful?

“Vannis!”

A sharp shout interrupted his amazement.

“Don't just stand there! Move!”

“Yes!”

Vannis pulled his gaze away and hurried after the retreating Tree Spirits.
