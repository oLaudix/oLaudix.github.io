Chapter 3030

Condensation

From Chinese Chapter 3031 · 凝练

The instant the vines wrapped around the Demon Cores, all the Demon Seeds in the cavern turned pale and staggered.

“Boom! Boom, boom!!!”

The next instant, the Nether Orbs orbiting Fang Heng automatically smashed into the pursuing Demon Seeds, blasting them backward!

“This...”

Jing'er stood nearby, his face deathly pale as he stared at Fang Heng in undisguised astonishment.

What in the world were those strange vines?!

It wasn't as though the Demon Seeds had never tried touching the cores before. But they couldn't draw out or absorb the cores' power in a short time. Even Demon Seeds originating from the same power couldn't do such a thing.

Yet those vines seemed to possess an ability similar to Eseklyna's Tongues of Envy, enabling them to influence Demon Seeds through the Demon Cores!

Hell power rampaged uncontrollably through Jing'er's body. Just keeping it under control and maintaining his fighting state took enormous effort. He could barely use half of one tenth of his full strength.

Jing'er stared fixedly at Fang Heng.

He knew perfectly well that Fang Heng was controlling those strange vines. Forget being reduced to five percent of his peak strength—he couldn't be certain of defeating Fang Heng head-on even at full strength.

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!!!!”

As if stimulated, Abe Akaya's vines surged out even more wildly, reaching toward the Demon Seeds who had been knocked away. Two who fled too slowly were caught and wrapped layer upon layer into huge, tightly bound bundles, unable to break free.

“Go!”

Zhuang Yufei was also suppressed by the Demon Cores, but she recovered faster than Jing'er. Seeing the scattered Demon Seeds fleeing and the vines racing toward them, she tugged his arm and said coldly, “If we don't leave now, it'll be too late! Hurry!”

Jing'er gritted his teeth, forcibly suppressed the demonic energy in his body, and burst forward with all his strength, fleeing the cavern alongside Zhuang Yufei.

Several clearheaded Demon Seeds immediately forced out their potential and escaped while maintaining their combat abilities. The remaining few tried to fight back, gritting their teeth as they charged Fang Heng in hopes of smashing the vines surrounding the cores.

“Bang! Bang, bang!!!”

The twenty-four Nether Orbs circling Fang Heng shot toward the attacking Demon Seeds and exploded in midair, sending out waves of necromantic force that blew every would-be attacker backward again!

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!!!”

Abe Akaya's vines showed their savage nature again, swiftly catching the Demon Seeds in midair and dragging them back, wrapping each of them into a tightly bound bundle beside the Demon Cores.

Abe Akaya absorbed the Demon Seeds' power rapidly, draining them completely dry!

As the quest completed, Fang Heng's expression changed slightly.

The sacred energy inside him began fluctuating fiercely, constantly rising and spilling from his body.

Unfortunately, he was still in his Necromancy form. The sacred power was suppressed by necromantic energy the instant it emerged, creating an extremely uncomfortable conflict.

Realizing what was happening, Fang Heng immediately shifted back into his Sacred Studies form.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!”

The Nether Orbs that had been independently seeking and attacking enemies hurried back, merging into his skin as lines of demonic markings.

His sacred power could finally surge out freely, rushing toward his Sea of Consciousness, where it rapidly condensed into a crystalline body.

Several Demon Seeds in the cavern who hadn't had time to act exchanged bewildered glances.

The vine bundles containing the captured Demon Seeds twisted and struggled a few times before going completely still. When the vines withdrew, nothing remained but debris scattered on the floor.

What the hell.

Who was that man, anyway?!

The remaining Demon Seeds knew they couldn't beat him. Seeing most of the aura around Fang Heng withdraw as he stood motionless, showing no intention to attack, they didn't know what to make of it.

What was going on?

But then they remembered how terrifying his strength had been.

Run!

While he wasn't attacking, they had to get away!

Once one fled, the others didn't hesitate either. They rushed out of the cavern and vanished in moments.

“Whoosh!”

As his Sea of Consciousness gradually settled, a sacred crystal gathered quietly in one region while the necromantic crystal already inside occupied another. The two remained separate without interfering with one another.

In both size and quality, however, the necromantic crystal on the right was clearly much more powerful.

Fang Heng opened his eyes. A faint sacred mark appeared between his brows.

So that object in his Sea of Consciousness was what the quest called the Will of the Twelve Sages.

With the appearance of the will crystal, his ability to control and sense sacred power had improved dramatically.

Unfortunately, Envy had drained his sacred abilities earlier, and there was barely any power left inside him.

Huh?

Fang Heng suddenly realized that the almost exhausted sacred power within him was recovering at an extremely slow pace.

Only a tiny trickle, but it really was recovering.

Was it related to the Will of the Twelve Sages?

A game notification gradually appeared.

[Hint: The player's Grand Sage bloodline experience +2.]

So that was it.

Because he was in Hell, the experience gain was extremely slow. Presumably, he would recover power somewhat faster after leaving.

Still, that wasn't bad.

Fang Heng stopped thinking about it and raised his head to survey his surroundings.

“Tch, they sure run fast.”

Seeing that all the Demon Seeds had scattered, Fang Heng curled his lip.

Forget it.

They would resurrect even if he killed them. There was no need to waste time hunting every last one down.

For a moment, Fang Heng considered using the Demon Cores to control the Demon Seeds' actions.

But in the end he abandoned the idea.

After all, Demon Seeds were much too difficult to control.

Having them work under him would only create unnecessary trouble.

It was enough to keep the Demon Cores and let them supply Abe Akaya with a continual stream of energy.

Abe Akaya was much smarter than Envy's demonic pet. If it encountered other Demon Seeds, it could use the cores to suppress them on its own.

Fang Heng glanced at the game notifications one last time.

All five Demon Cores were now in his hands. Among the seven Demon Seed powers needed for Abe Akaya's evolution to Complete Form, only one remained.

Greed!

As for the next step...

The Eternal Tree Domain surrounding Fang Heng slowly receded. He closed his eyes.

Countless vines belonging to Abe Akaya converged from outside the cavern.

“Abe Akaya, begin.”

“Hiss, hiss...”

Sensing Fang Heng's will, Abe Akaya's vines slowly coiled around his body. Tiny barbs emerged beneath them and pierced his skin, drawing power out of him.
