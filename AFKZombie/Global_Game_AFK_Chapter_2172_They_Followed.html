Chapter 2172

They Followed

From original Chinese Chapter 2173 · 追来

Two minutes later, sixteen pale-blue spatial-jump flashes appeared in succession.

Dependent warriors arrived through the warp devices.

Looking around at the surrounding forest, the warriors exchanged glances.

Fang Heng?

Not here?

Their tracking equipment had led them straight to this place.

It couldn't be wrong.

Fang Heng had to be nearby!

One Dependent quickly noticed something and pointed at the simulated-environment barrier the Tree Spirits had erected a short distance ahead.

“Whoosh!”

Rings of blue light spread outward from the Dependent's fingertip.

When the expanding ripples touched the Tree Spirit barrier, the two forces interacted, causing large waves to roll across its surface like water.

Pale-blue ripples appeared all over the barrier.

“It's a simulated-environment barrier.”

As the scout spoke, another Dependent raised both hands and formed a triangular seal.

“Whoosh!”

Pale-green light shot from the center of the triangle, striking the barrier almost instantly.

“BZZ!”

The barrier shook violently.

Under the force of the beam, a red tear opened in the simulated-environment barrier, revealing the Tree Spirits' settlement beyond.

“Pew! Pew, pew!”

Before the Dependents could investigate further, a dense flight of arrows shot out from behind the barrier!

“The Tree Spirits!”

The Dependent warriors frowned and immediately expanded their crystalline shields.

“Bang! Bang, bang!”

The arrows exploded against the red crystal barriers.

The war machines hovering nearby finally acquired enemy positions. They converged rapidly and unleashed long-range beams toward the Tree Spirits.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

A dense barrage of beams immediately swept indiscriminately across the Tree Spirit settlement!

“Boom! Boom, boom!”

Explosions erupted throughout their territory.

The Tree Spirits had relatively weak personal defenses, so they dared not take the hits directly. They retreated behind cover, continuing to loose arrows in retaliation.

Damn it!

They'd been discovered after all!

Vannis's heart sank as he watched from behind the barrier.

The Federation was attacking in force.

But why had the Dependents suddenly joined the battle as well?

As the fighting began and the Tree Spirit settlement was exposed, the Federation received the report and realized the situation was troublesome.

Just as they'd suspected, Fang Heng was mixed up with the Tree Spirits.

That would only make things harder!

Even so, the Federation was surprised.

Had the Tree Spirits actually exposed their own settlement?

In the past, the Federation had expended enormous effort trying to locate their headquarters, without success.

Why had it suddenly been revealed now?

There was no time to ponder it. The Federation immediately dispatched numerous mechanized reinforcements through the spatial-jump network.

More and more points of warp light appeared around the Tree Spirit settlement.

The entire race was on emergency alert. To protect their home, all available Tree Spirits joined the defense, using the terrain to launch enchanted arrows and repel the intruders.

“Quickly! Activate the emergency barrier!”

“Bzz!”

A massive blue interference barrier rose high above the primeval forest.

The interference disrupted the Federation's spatial-jump machinery, greatly slowing the arrival of reinforcements.

The Tree Spirits had built layer upon layer of protection around their settlement over the past century.

The Federation wouldn't conquer it so easily!

Meanwhile, Fang Heng was still locked inside his prison cage.

Hearing the beam cannon explosions outside and seeing the blue barrier overhead, his lips curved into a smile.

Right on time.

The Dependents and Federation pursuers had arrived.

The Tree Spirits were fighting the Federation again.

Except…

Why hadn't Senzo come to see him yet?

…

Inside the Tree Spirit settlement, Great Elder Senzo felt an itch in his nose and couldn't help sneezing.

He'd only just returned from the Root Clan settlement, where he'd discussed future cooperation with their Great Elder. Before he could even catch his breath, he'd received news of Federation spatial jumps outside.

How had the Federation found them?!

With the situation so urgent, Senzo had no time to think. He immediately led his clansmen outside to investigate.

When he arrived and saw the surrounding forest already engulfed in battle, his heart sank.

This was serious!

The Tree Spirits were now in open combat with the Federation.

To his astonishment, he also saw Dependents among the Federation's warped-in machines!

“Vannis! What is going on?!”

Vannis was leading the clan in fierce resistance. When he saw Great Elder Senzo arrive, he immediately explained what little he knew.

“Great Elder, the Federation suddenly attacked us.”

In short…

Vannis had no idea what had actually happened.

The Federation had simply appeared out of nowhere, discovered their settlement, and launched an attack.

Senzo found this even stranger.

Everything about the Federation's movements suggested that they'd known the approximate location in advance and come prepared.

Had their settlement's location been leaked?

Who could have leaked it?

“Great Elder, we've requested help from the Wood and Root Clans. They're on their way.”

“Mm.”

Senzo acknowledged the report.

Relations among the Tree Spirit clans were not nearly as harmonious as outsiders imagined. Nevertheless, they were all of one race. Under the Federation's pressure in recent years, they'd temporarily put aside their prejudices and strengthened cooperation, establishing hidden teleportation points near their respective settlements.

They had agreed that if the Federation discovered and attacked any clan's settlement, the other three would send reinforcements.

“What happened before this?” Senzo still sensed something was wrong. “Was there anything unusual before the Federation appeared?”

At that reminder, Vannis suddenly remembered something. His expression changed.

“What happened?”

“Fang Heng! He must have led the Federation here!” Vannis realized he'd found the crucial clue and gnashed his teeth. “I knew that human hadn't come with good intentions!”

Senzo's heart sank. “What did you say? Fang Heng? He came here?”

“Yes. He turned up at our territory a few hours ago, insisting on seeing you. Since you weren't here, I temporarily locked him in a cell.”

Senzo took a deep breath, his eyes grave.

Fang Heng had arrived, and the Federation had followed close behind.

How could that be a coincidence?

The two were probably connected.

And how had Fang Heng found their territory in the first place?

“Stay here and lead our people against the Federation. Reinforcements from the Root and Soul Clans are on the way. I've already instructed Osman to prepare for evacuation. Once we repel the Federation, we'll relocate immediately to Backup Assembly Point Two. I'm going to see Fang Heng.”

“Yes, Great Elder!”

With that, Senzo hurried off with two clansmen to find Fang Heng.
