Chapter 3085

A Major Grind

From Chinese Chapter 3086 · 大刷一波

“Whoosh!!”

Fang Heng shot forward again, his right hand clenched into a fist as he attacked.

“Bang!!”

Another faint sonic boom rang out.

The alien warrior didn't even see how Fang Heng struck. A tremendous force sent him flying out of the ring.

Victory!

Thunderous cheers swept through the entire arena.

Somewhere in the spectator stands, two elves exchanged glances.

“Are you certain it's him?”

“Absolutely. He looks identical, and his name is Fang Heng. He's fighting for the Noel Clan.”

“Mm. Let's split up. Find a way to report to His Highness immediately. I'll gather intelligence about Fang Heng.”

“Understood.”

The Noel Clan warriors in the preparation area all rose to celebrate.

After easily winning two rounds, Fang Heng gave Elder Meng Hong a brief cupped-fist salute, hopped off the stage, and headed back through the waiting-area passage.

Anxi was already waiting at the passage entrance.

“Mr. Fang Heng, what a wonderful match! Congratulations on another victory. Your vehicle is ready. Shall we return to the villa now?”

“Mm. Let's go.”

Meanwhile, seeing Fang Heng leave immediately after the fight dampened the others' joy in victory.

They'd hoped to use the celebration banquet as a chance to exchange a few words with him. Perhaps they could rely on his influence in the future.

Apparently the youngster was very strong, but not especially sociable.

“I won't attend the celebration. You go ahead.”

Elder Meng Hong gave the order without betraying his emotions and left the arena as well.

Behind his impassive expression, his thoughts were racing.

Today's match had revealed part of Fang Heng's strength.

The Noel Clan badly needed close-combat warriors like him, and his arrival filled a major weakness.

After today's performance, plenty of people would likely try to approach Fang Heng.

It might be time to improve his compensation.

...

Back at the villa.

After taking a shower, Fang Heng returned to the basement and resumed his secluded cultivation.

His alchemy advancement was progressing somewhat slower than expected.

In the later stages, the progress gained from practicing arrays gradually declined.

No rush. He had nothing else to do, so he'd keep grinding.

By the following afternoon, as he completed his final alchemical array, a game notification sounded.

[Hint: Player has completed drawing an alchemical magic array. Current-stage quest complete. Player's alchemy has advanced—Grandmaster-level Alchemy.]

Done!

Fang Heng waved his hand, dispersing the array etched into the floor, then extended his open palm.

“Hiss, hiss...”

A tiny cluster of elemental energy appeared on each fingertip.

Fang Heng tried fusing the five different elements into one.

“Bang!”

The instant the five elements touched, they annihilated one another and released a violent shock wave that sent the zombie clones throughout the basement flying.

Not bad.

Fang Heng sensed the elemental energy after the attempted fusion.

Although it was his alchemy rank that had improved, elemental studies had actually benefited most in terms of immediate combat power.

Even without alchemical arrays, his elemental control was now far beyond that of scholars at the same level.

Next came the following stage of alchemy.

Alchemy differed from other academic disciplines. It had memory inheritances that could greatly improve skill level, but there was no such thing as alchemical energy. He couldn't rapidly grind levels by converting natural energy into Qi (炁).

Alchemy was primarily about mastering alchemical magic arrays.

In the past, he'd accumulated alchemy experience through inheritances, reading alchemy books, and drawing magic arrays.

Fang Heng summoned two zombie clones and instructed them to arrange a simple small magic array on the ground.

A moment later, the array was complete.

A game notification slowly appeared on his retina.

[Hint: Player's zombie clone completed a small basic magic array (Simple, Perfect). Player gained Transcendent-level Alchemy EXP +0.2.]

Uh...

Just one level of Grandmaster alchemy required tens of millions of experience points. And each completion gave only 0.2?

Far too little.

Fang Heng rubbed his temples.

No hurry, no hurry.

After all, the clones could draw basic arrays quite efficiently, completing several in a minute.

Under the current circumstances, rapidly maxing out Grandmaster alchemy would require three things.

First, enough zombie clones. Second, sufficient expendable materials. Third, a large enough venue.

As for materials, he always carried some in his backpack, but nowhere near enough for the terrifying consumption of tens of thousands of zombie clones.

He needed to make arrangements.

Fang Heng picked up his phone and called Anxi.

“Mr. Fang Heng, is there anything I can help you with?”

“I need basic alchemy materials. Standard sets are fine. Deliver them to the villa's courtyard and I'll handle the rest. Hmm, start with fifty thousand sets. I might need more later.”

What?

Basic alchemical materials?

Anxi was puzzled.

She understood why someone studying alchemy would need the basic materials.

But fifty thousand sets in one order?

What could he possibly need so many for?

Anxi quickly recovered and agreed. “Certainly, Mr. Fang Heng. I'll place the order now. Your materials will be delivered to your villa within three hours. Please watch for the delivery.”

“Thank you. I appreciate it.”

Fang Heng ended the call and looked toward Abe Akaya.

The next problem was the insufficient number of zombie clones.

Previously, Abe Akaya had encountered resistance from some unknown force when opening a teleportation passage.

Forcing the passage open would cause intense fluctuations.

To avoid trouble, Fang Heng had temporarily stopped trying.

But that wouldn't work anymore.

As his cultivation progressed, his supply of zombie clones was clearly inadequate, and the speed of his leveling was beginning to fall behind.

If he wanted to keep cultivating at an exceptional pace, he needed to bring in more clones.

After all, only sufficient quantity could produce a qualitative change.

“I'll have to think of something and take a little risk.”

Muttering to himself, Fang Heng glanced at Abe Akaya's magic array, opened a map of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital on his phone, and studied its layout.

“Hmm... the Kinsfolk...”

...

Three hours later.

Boxes of alchemy materials ordered by Anxi were unloaded from trucks, filling the villa's entire courtyard.

This many?

The trucks were still unloading box after box.

Fang Heng was stunned. He stepped forward to inspect them and frowned.

The supplies Anxi had provided were premium products, exquisitely packaged.

He wanted to tell her he didn't need any of this fancy nonsense. The most basic raw ingredients would have done.

The zombie clones certainly weren't picky.

Oh well. They'd already arrived.

Fang Heng told the delivery workers to put everything in the back courtyard, then returned indoors. Assuming his soul form, he headed toward the district controlled by the Kinsfolk in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.
