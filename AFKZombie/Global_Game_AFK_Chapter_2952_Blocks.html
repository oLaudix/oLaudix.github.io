Chapter 2952

Blocks

From original Chinese Chapter 2953 · 土块

"Report! Communications briefly came back online. The Federation Council ordered us to stay patient and keep watching. They're convinced Fang Heng will compromise soon. We mustn't lose our nerve."

Bullshit!

Vion wanted to curse.

Lose their nerve, his ass!

Compromise? Fang Heng had already gone out and started building his own base!

They were on the verge of being abandoned inside the Federation's temporary camp!

"Who issued that order? Tell that idiot Fang Heng has already built a base outside!"

"Minister Anatoly issued it. The military also sent headquarters a report that Fang Heng is establishing a base, and urged them to dispatch reinforcements immediately."

"During earlier communications, the higher-ups repeatedly asked whether we'd personally seen Fang Heng's underground base and whether we could confirm its size and scale."

Another member of the expert team spoke calmly. "The minister wants us to verify the report. He suspects Fang Heng and the military may be setting a trap."

A trap?

Vion paused, then looked toward the empty laboratory hall.

Were they trying to trick him?

Thinking it through...

That was possible.

Otherwise, why would Fang Heng refuse to let them inspect the underground area?

Wouldn't displaying the base openly have broken their opponents' resolve far more effectively?

That was close!

Looking back, the sudden appearance of Fang Heng's supposed underground safe zone had been extremely suspicious.

It really could be nothing more than a bluff!

He'd almost fallen for it!

But...

Something still didn't add up.

What about the researchers?

He had clearly watched them leave the ship, and the laboratory supplies had been taken away too!

Where were they?

Unless...

The military and Fang Heng had conspired to hide all the researchers and equipment somewhere aboard the ship?

Vion scratched his head.

The ship's safe zone was small. Hiding so many people would be difficult.

"I understand. I'll investigate thoroughly."

Vion nodded and ordered the earlier surveillance recordings brought up so he could review what had actually happened.

He replayed the footage several times. It plainly showed Wayne leading the researchers down into the tunnel. None of them had appeared again since.

Was it an elaborate deception, or had they really built something?

Vion was so unsettled that he refused to leave the control room, continuing to stare at the monitors.

The whole night passed.

Vion's eyes were heavy with exhaustion.

Apart from Alec's team and a few scattered Zerg creatures, no one had entered or left the tunnel. The ship's safe zone had remained sealed throughout the night.

Fang Heng had no idea someone had been watching him all night.

With the engineers and researchers cooperating, the antigravity equipment in the underground passage had finally been installed in its basic configuration.

The players could now remove their protective suits and move freely underground.

The engineers would continue refining and expanding the system, reinforcing the inner passages and improving their defenses.

"Fang Heng, we've finished developing modified concrete blocks for this corrosive environment. We added a special metal to the mixture, strengthening the blocks and making them immune to erosion by the sandstorm."

Now that was useful!

Fang Heng took the blueprints from Li Zhixuan and nodded approvingly.

He knew enough scientific and engineering skills to understand the design.

With corrosion-resistant blocks, all they needed was mass production. They could install antigravity modules inside them and lay the blocks along the transport routes.

That would greatly accelerate ore transportation.

Li Zhixuan looked concerned. "According to your design, production of the special antigravity blocks will be very low. One crucial step even requires alchemical energy conduction..."

"Let me try. Do you have a prototype?"

"Yes."

Li Zhixuan beckoned to a nearby player.

Two researchers wheeled over a cart bearing a dark-gray block.

It was a cube with sides one and a half meters long.

"This is still only half-finished. We need to inscribe an alchemical imprint to improve its internal energy flow. Completing that step requires at least Intermediate Alchemy, and our team lacks anyone with those skills."

"That shouldn't be a problem. I've happened to study a little alchemy."

Fang Heng pressed his palm lightly against the block.

Whoosh!

A faint light flashed across its surface, leaving behind a pale imprint.

That quickly?

Had he really finished it?

Several researchers stared at him in surprise.

Fang Heng asked casually, "These things are excellent. What's our current production capacity?"

Production capacity?

The researchers exchanged bewildered looks.

If they had to estimate... perhaps one every three hours?

What could he possibly want so many for?

"Forget it." Fang Heng could already read the answer on their faces. "Find a way to expand production. We'll need a lot of antigravity blocks. The more, the better."

Li Zhixuan said, "Mass production requires more raw materials and molds. We'll have to acquire those through the Kinsfolk warp system."

She and several researchers followed Fang Heng to the Kinsfolk central AI exchange terminal and watched him click through the exchange interface at lightning speed.

Buy everything!

They all stared in astonishment.

Good grief...

Why was he ordering so many materials? Was he setting up a production line?

What on earth was Fang Heng planning?

Now that he had a grinding goal, Fang Heng was wide awake again.

The underground base had been divided into two levels.

The researchers generally worked on the upper level.

The lower level was still unfinished. Fang Heng assigned it to the zombie clones for ore refining and inscribing alchemical runes onto the antigravity blocks.

The Kinsfolk warp system operated around the clock. Whenever the cooldown expired, another batch of forging tools was transported in.

The blocks were built with a special alloy energy-conduction layer and antigravity module, covered by a concrete-metal composite, then given an outer defensive energy shield.

Thirty-six hours later, the first batch of antigravity blocks was ready, and the research team immediately assembled for field testing.

Outside the ship.

Zaka and his players had been waiting for some time.

Soon, Lickers dragged the blocks out of the cavern and onto the surface.

Each block contained an antigravity module, so although they looked heavy, they floated in midair. Their behavior could be adjusted through internal settings.

They had three modes: Mobile, Adjustment, and Fixed.

In Mobile Mode, the blocks were much easier to move.

Zaka stepped forward and tried pushing one.

It was far lighter than expected, like pushing a small wooden cabinet on wheels.

Zaka nodded to himself. Hearing the researchers' instructions over his headset, he turned to the players behind him. "Brothers, time to get started. Let's push some blocks!"

The players' job was to build roads!

They would lay antigravity blocks outward one by one, gradually covering the surrounding area with a flat surface.

The blocks were joined together in a continuous platform.

Through their internal energy-conduction systems, all the blocks could be controlled from the cavern's central control room, allowing their modes to be switched to Fixed.

As more of the sandy ground was covered, the players could finally stand on the antigravity platform.

"These things really work!"

One excited player even jumped up and down on the surface.

It was as solid as ordinary ground.

At last, they could say goodbye to that awful feeling of stepping in soft shit.

— End of Chapter 2952 —
