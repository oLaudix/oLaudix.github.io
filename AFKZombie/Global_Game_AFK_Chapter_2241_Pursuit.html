Chapter 2241

Pursuit

From original Chinese Chapter 2242 · 追击

Xidi swiftly formed a hand seal.

"Explode!"

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!"

Huge wooden spikes thrust outward from the ground with Xidi at their center!

Ring after ring of spikes burst from the earth like the petals of a blooming lotus, spreading rapidly in every direction!

"Shhk! Shhk, shhk!"

The charging Lickers were pierced clean through by the massive spikes and hoisted into the air!

Tiny vines and barbs grew around the wounds, pinning the Lickers firmly in place!

"Creak… creak…"

The spikes slowly sank back into the earth, dragging the struggling Lickers down until they disappeared beneath the ground!

[Hint: Your zombie clone (Fusion Licker form) has been affected by the special skill Wood-Spike Burial and has died. Your zombie clone will revive in 4.32 hours…]

"Wood Burial!"

That instant-kill skill again?

Fang Heng was controlling the Lickers from a concealed position nearby. Seeing more than twenty death notifications appear across his vision at once, he took notice.

Whole groups of Lickers were impaled, dragged underground, and instantly killed!

"Hah! Interesting. Let's try that again!"

Let's see how many times you can use it!

Fang Heng could tell Xidi was already on his last legs.

At full strength, the elder would indeed have been difficult to handle.

But things were different now.

He could overwhelm him with sheer numbers!

Attack!

Let's see how many more times he can use that wide-area technique!

Dense packs of Lickers continued to spring from the undergrowth, throwing themselves at Xidi and the other Wood Clan members in the center.

The guards had barely begun to relax after watching their elder wipe out so many Lickers when yet another wave emerged from the surrounding bushes. Their hearts sank again.

What the hell were these things?!

Why were there so many, and why were they so relentless?!

Xidi's expression was equally dark.

Those despicable Leaf Clan bastards!

They had conspired with a human to set a trap for them!

At last, he believed he understood.

The human had come first, and the Leaf Clan had arrived soon afterward.

That alone had been suspicious.

And although the Leaf Clan had seemingly allowed them to withdraw, the human's creatures had pursued them almost immediately.

Something was clearly wrong!

This was all a Leaf Clan plot!

They wanted to use the human's hands to get rid of him!

Did they really think he couldn't see it?!

But Xidi had to admit he had now been driven into a corner.

His eyes blazed with hatred. In a low voice, he told his companions, "I'll create an opening. The moment you get a chance, scatter and run. If any of you makes it out alive, tell the clan elders the Leaf Clan has allied with humans. They were behind the repeated attacks on our temples. And the Leaf Clan is responsible for my death too!"

At the word "death," the guards were stunned. They all turned to him. "Elder, you…"

"Make sure the Great Elder knows to beware of the Leaf Clan! Do you understand?" Xidi had resolved to die. He glared at them and shouted, "Answer me! Do you understand?!"

The guards' eyes reddened. "Yes! We understand!"

Xidi looked at the Lickers pouring out of the thickets around them and formed a seal between his hands.

Only by sacrificing himself could he give his companions a chance to carry the warning home!

"Whitewood Burial!"

"BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!"

The power of nature surged!

The earth shook violently with Xidi at its center!

The next instant, countless pale trunks erupted from the surrounding ground. Thick, twisting wood grew at a visible speed, sprouting lush branches and leaves. In the blink of an eye, it trapped the swarms of Lickers attacking from every side!

This was an inherited secret technique of the Wood Clan: it used the caster's own life as nourishment to produce vast numbers of trunks and bind the enemy!

The white branches possessed a special life-draining effect. Once they wrapped around a victim, escaping them was extremely difficult!

Feeding on the life force they absorbed, the branches grew even faster!

Seizing the time Xidi had bought them, the remaining four Wood Clan members broke through the surrounding Lickers and fled in different directions!

"Damn it!"

Still controlling his Lickers from hiding, Fang Heng froze when Xidi suddenly went all out with a desperate ultimate technique.

That decisive?

He'd expected to wear Xidi down gradually, not for the elder to sacrifice himself immediately.

Realizing Xidi was trying to buy time for his companions to escape, Fang Heng stopped hesitating and burst out of the undergrowth!

After them!

Although he hadn't appeared openly during the fight, the Wood Clan members could probably identify him from the Lickers.

Ideally, he wouldn't let a single Tree Spirit escape!

"You're not getting away!"

Xidi's life was nearly spent. As he watched his companions fleeing in every direction, the light faded from his eyes until it disappeared entirely.

At the same moment, Fang Heng charged toward one of the escaping Wood Clan members.

"Whoosh!"

His metal staff swept forward, whistling through the air.

"THUD!"

The guard took the full blow. His body jerked, and he was sent flying backward!

Troublesome!

Feeling the impact travel up the staff, Fang Heng inwardly cursed.

It was like hitting solid granite!

Hard as hell!

The Wood Clan could take far more punishment than the other Tree Spirit clans!

A single hit wouldn't come close to seriously injuring this one!

Then another!

"Whoosh!"

Fang Heng tapped the ground with his toes and raced after the guard he'd knocked away.

"BANG! BANG, BANG!"

He pursued the guard and battered him relentlessly with his metal staff, finally killing him. By the time he looked around, the other three Wood Clan members had vanished into the forest.

Damn.

He could still vaguely sense their blood-energy fluctuations, but chasing them one by one would take too long. Worse, those traces would fade entirely if he delayed much longer.

There was no way to catch all of them!

Forget it.

After a quick calculation, Fang Heng shook his head and reluctantly gave up the pursuit.

At least he'd secured a kill.

This round hadn't been a loss.

Turning around, Fang Heng looked toward the area covered by dense white branches.

Xidi had died by using a self-sacrificing technique, so Fang Heng hadn't received a kill notification.

But through the white growth, he could see several sparkling crystals.

At least the Iridescent Crystals needed for leveling had dropped.

Fang Heng raised a hand, and several Iridescent Crystals flew into his palm.
