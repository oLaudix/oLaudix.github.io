Chapter 2276

Fluctuations

From original Chinese Chapter 2277 · 波动

Fang Heng was unaware that when Abe Akaya returned to full strength, it had released an extraordinarily powerful burst of energy in an instant. All the Tree Spirit clans mistook the fluctuation for the aura of the Divine Tree seed and were drawn toward it.

After the initial few seconds of explosive energy, Abe Akaya quickly settled down and restricted its growth to the island and a small area around it.

The Wood Clan's teleportation passage was closest to the island. Two hours later, two Wood Clan elders arrived with a group of Leaf Clan guards.

Entering the island area, the newcomers frowned at the lush red forest before them.

Strange.

By now, ninety percent of the Divine Tree seed's energy fluctuations had dissipated, but with careful sensing, they could still detect a faint trace of its aura on the island.

They were quite certain.

A Divine Tree seed had once been present here.

Whether it was still here was another matter.

But what exactly was going on?

How could a Divine Tree seed have appeared here?

From a distance, the island itself seemed unusual.

The trees carried a faint blood-red tint.

Wood Clan Elder Chrono waved a hand. "Search the area first. Once you locate the Divine Tree seed, withdraw immediately."

"Yes!"

The Wood Clan guards were about to disperse into the island when a sharp shout stopped them.

"Wait!"

Just as the Wood Clan prepared to enter, Vannis arrived with his subordinates.

At the sight of his enemies, Vannis felt his anger flare again.

"What do you think you're doing? The Divine Tree sapling isn't something you Wood Clan people can simply lay your hands on."

The Wood Clan elder snorted. "How ridiculous! Are you suggesting the Divine Tree sapling belongs to your Leaf Clan? Who gave you that right?"

"Hahahaha! Rights aren't given to you. You earn them! Enough talking. We didn't finish our fight last time. How about we settle it here, to the death?"

Vannis was itching for another battle.

Because Fang Heng had made a major breakthrough in array research, Great Elder Senzo had repeatedly warned Vannis not to fight unless the situation changed drastically.

But Vannis had never been one for restraint.

As soon as he saw the Wood Clan, his first instinct was to attack.

Chrono developed a headache at the prospect of another fight.

The Leaf Clan was already stronger than the others.

And Vannis himself was one of the most powerful elders alive.

"Hmph. What about Senzo's promise that the clans would stop killing one another? You were there when he said it. Was all that talk just hot air?"

"Heh," Vannis sneered. "You think you're my kin? Are you worthy of the name?"

While the two argued, the Soul Clan and Root Clan arrived in succession. Seeing the Wood and Leaf Clans ready to fight, they immediately guessed what had happened and gathered around.

The four great clans once again occupied four separate corners.

Although no one fully understood the situation, each clan had its own calculations.

Soul Clan Elder Tipper glanced at the island.

It wasn't particularly large, but it wasn't small either. A careful search would take the better part of half a day.

"Everyone, please hear me out," Tipper said. "We all came because we sensed the Divine Tree seed's aura, hoping to claim it. Rather than waste time confronting one another, why not agree on a fair way of dividing the opportunity?"

Vannis sneered repeatedly. "Fair? When has the world ever been absolutely fair?"

"With that temper of yours, couldn't you at least listen to Elder Tipper's suggestion first?"

The others were wary of Tipper.

After all, she'd carried the Divine Tree Remnant Branch last time and might very well have brought it again.

If a fight broke out, they would all suffer.

Tipper swept her gaze over the gathering. "I suggest that each clan select ten members below elder rank. They can enter the island in batches and search. Whichever clan finds the Divine Tree seed and brings it back here gets to claim it. None of the elders present may intervene. What do you say?"

Tipper was trying to keep any losses within reasonable limits. She feared another all-out confrontation like their previous battle, which would only weaken the Tree Spirit race further.

Ordinary clansmen fighting was one thing, but there were only so many elders in total. Losing more of them would be disastrous.

The assembled elders exchanged looks, then nodded in agreement.

They could give the Divine Tree Remnant Branch that much respect.

None of them wanted heavy casualties anyway.

Chrono nodded first. "Elder Tipper's suggestion is reasonable. The Wood Clan agrees. What about everyone else?"

Miles glanced at Tipper.

He didn't know whether she had brought the Divine Tree Remnant Branch this time.

If they fought directly, his side might not prevail.

But if they followed Tipper's proposal, the Leaf Clan would at least have a chance to acquire the Divine Tree essence.

"The Leaf Clan has no objection."

"Excellent. Then let's begin."

Each clan selected ten members, who formed pairs and entered the island's forest in successive groups.

Meanwhile, on the first basement floor beneath the island.

Klevi and the others watched the outside situation clearly through hundreds of hidden surveillance cameras.

"They're here."

Mo Jiawei said, "All the researchers and laboratory staff have already evacuated through the spatial transition system. We've removed and wiped the research records. Only the three of us remain on the island."

"Strange. The Tree Spirit elders don't seem to be going in."

"If it's just ordinary Tree Spirits, I can hold them off for a while," Gu Qingzhu said, studying the monitors. "Any word from Fang Heng?"

Mo Jiawei lowered his voice. "Not yet. They probably tracked Abe Akaya's aura here. There's no point staying. Why don't we just get out?"

"Mm."

Klevi nodded quietly. Sensing Abe Akaya's mood, she continued, "Ever since it recovered its strength, Abe Akaya has seemed extremely excited. I'd like to stay a little longer and observe it. If things turn dangerous, I'll reverse the summoning and withdraw Abe Akaya immediately."

"All right. That works."

Mo Jiawei nodded.

They could wait a little longer.

With Gu Qingzhu and Abe Akaya there, they should be able to hold out for now.

Perhaps Fang Heng would contact them if they bought a little time.

Reconstructing Abe Akaya's magic arrays each time was a nuisance, after all.

While the three discussed their options, Mo Jiawei noticed something and pointed at a surveillance screen. "Huh? Look at that!"
