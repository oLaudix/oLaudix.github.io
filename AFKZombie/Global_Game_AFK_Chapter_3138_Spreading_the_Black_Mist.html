Chapter 3138

Spreading the Black Mist

From Chinese Chapter 3139 · 扩散黑雾

Not good!

“Scatter! Run!”

The warriors were terrified.

They were all low- or mid-tier warriors, with a gulf in strength between them and Chen Hanyu as wide as the heavens.

Even Chen Hanyu hadn't been able to defeat Fang Heng.

How could they possibly win?!

Their only chance was to flee!

Scatter and run! If they could make it back to the starships, they might survive!

Seeing the warriors scatter in every direction almost instantly, Fang Heng snorted disdainfully. He quickly marked them all with Vampire Marks, then charged toward the closest one.

Not one of you is getting away!

Whoosh!!!

The warrior Fang Heng targeted sensed a terrifying presence closing in from behind. Knowing death was near, he gritted his teeth.

“Damn it!

I'll fight you to the death!”

Whoosh!!! The warrior wheeled around and threw a punch.

Two different forces collided! “BOOM!”

“BOOM!”

The warrior's entire body exploded into a mass of blood and flesh in midair.

That madman!

Seeing such a horrifying sight, the other fleeing warriors no longer dared waste a moment. They accelerated desperately, trying to put as much distance as possible between themselves and that monster.

Fang Heng followed the Vampire Marks and patiently hunted them down one by one. Only after every warrior had been eliminated did he return to the starship district and look toward the ships already being invaded by Lickers.

Without high-tier combatants like the warriors, the ships lacked firepower and specialized equipment to counter the insects. They could delay the Vampires' pursuit for a little while, but couldn't withstand it.

The exterior defensive barriers had already been damaged by the Vampires' concentrated attacks, and large numbers of Vampires were entering through the ships' various access points.

Hm? Something occurred to Fang Heng, and he frowned slightly.

Looking more closely, he saw an extremely faint, thin layer of mist hanging over the starship district.

The game's black mist!

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes and studied it carefully.

Not only the Lickers, but also the Vampires who'd entered this world through the Vampire Teleportation Gate were constantly releasing tiny traces of black mist.

The concentration was so low it had no discernible effect. However, with large numbers of Vampires and zombie creatures gathered on the ships, the mist was gradually accumulating.

[Hint: You are spreading black mist.]

[Hint: Current world's black-mist spread: 0.0001%.]

Hm?

Fang Heng caught sight of the game notifications appearing on his retina.

There it was again.

Black-mist spread!

He'd seen similar notifications before.

“Your Majesty.”

Vampire Prince Bule arrived with several elite Vampires and quietly reported, “Your Majesty, we have occupied the cavern region and located the starships awaiting repairs. Most of those who resisted have been eliminated. The remaining prisoners and captured security personnel are being detained for now.”

“Mm. Well done.”

Fang Heng drew his attention away from the game hints and turned toward Bule.

The strength of a Prince-level Vampire was roughly equivalent to that of a top mid-tier warrior.

That was sufficient for ordinary fighting, but not quite enough in the Seven Realms Martial Capital or in battles against the Kinsfolk.

How could he raise the elite Vampires' combat power another level?

Enhancing their dimensional power was too difficult at the moment, so he'd leave that aside.

That meant the only remaining path was to improve their academic disciplines.

Fang Heng examined Bule thoughtfully.

When Fang Heng had advanced his Necromancy to the Super-God level, he'd successfully fused undead and Vampire powers.

If Vampire power was to advance further, the path should lie in Necromancy.

Bule felt a little uneasy under Fang Heng's stare.

“Do you have orders for me, Your Majesty?”

“Bule, your bloodline is the source of your strength, but also the shackles limiting your further progress. Have you ever studied Necromancy systematically?”

“Yes.” Bule straightened immediately and nodded. “You previously mentioned that we'd need to cultivate Necromancy to advance further. We haven't neglected those studies, but our understanding of Necromancy is limited and our progress has been slow.”

“What level are you at now?”

“Grandmaster level.”

Fang Heng considered this.

That made sense. The Vampires had never been exposed to Necromancy in the past, so learning it meant starting from scratch. Compared with the old monsters in the Necromancy Association, who'd often studied for tens of thousands of years, they were only beginners.

Reaching the Grandmaster level was already quite impressive.

After all, the highest level in the Necromancy Association was merely Demigod-level Necromancy.

In theory, its instructors should already be dealing with the laws at that stage.

But that didn't seem quite right. When Fang Heng had learned Necromancy, he hadn't encountered the laws.

Thinking more carefully, Hell, Holy, and Undead powers differed greatly from ordinary academic advancement. They didn't appear to require contact with the laws.

Fang Heng recalled the orb he'd seen in the illusion of the River of the Netherworld.

The entire world's structure had been centered on the Death Realm Orb.

After losing the orb, the Death Realm had gradually declined into a wasteland.

Could the Death Realm Orb be related to the laws?

“Your Majesty?”

Fang Heng raised his head. “Mm. Take me to the warehouse first.”

“Yes!”

…

On the first level of the mountain cavern.

Inside the converted large warehouse.

Bule led Fang Heng inside.

The warehouse manager was a member of the Fungus Clan named Collier.

Members of the Fungus Clan were individually weak, but possessed a terrifying reproductive ability.

“My lord.”

Collier already knew that the Vampires had conquered the Frozen Soul Land. He obediently lowered his head and submitted.

Fang Heng asked, “Where's the Earth's Core Flame Ore?”

“It's transported to the Kinsfolk every week on schedule. Most of this week's extracted ore is still being refined and will take some time to finish. Only half a crate has completed refining. It's currently stored in Warehouse Three.”

“Your Majesty.”

Garr arrived hurriedly with two Vampire guards.

Kappa and Korkalot accompanied them.

Garr knelt on one knee and reported, “We've successfully seized the starships and gained access to their main control rooms. Unfortunately, the security teams managed to report the attack to the Kinsfolk beforehand.”

Korkalot frowned. “Boss Fang, as far as we know, the Earth's Core Flame Ore is very important to the Kinsfolk. They'll probably send forces to suppress the rebellion soon. We need to prepare.”

“Mm.”

Fang Heng nodded and asked, “How badly damaged are the starships? How long until they're completely repaired?”

“We've inspected them all. At least eight hours, even at the fastest pace.”

Fang Heng pursed his lips.

Another Marquis-level Vampire flew over, knelt on one knee, and reported, “Your Majesty, the Vampire elders have finished setting up Abe Akaya's teleportation magic array.”

“Good.”

Fang Heng nodded and quickly considered the situation.

With Abe Akaya's array in place, there was nothing to worry about.

He might as well use this planet as a battlefield, fight the Kinsfolk, and find out what they were capable of.

Even if he lost, the worst outcome would be some expenditure. He'd merely have to consume a large amount of Abe Akaya's energy and escape through Abe Akaya's teleportation array.

If possible, he'd repair all the starships on the planet and use them to transport all its resources away.

With that in mind, Fang Heng's thoughts stirred and he glanced at the game notifications on his retina once more.
