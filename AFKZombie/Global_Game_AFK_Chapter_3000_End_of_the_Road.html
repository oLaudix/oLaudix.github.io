Chapter 3000

End of the Road

From Chinese Chapter 3001 · 尽头

Chen Yu happened to be among the team. His strength was near the bottom of the group, so the others had kept him protected in the center, and he hadn't immediately recognized Fang Heng.

Yi Qingzhi ventured, "Friends from the Holy Court, could you try breaking through these spatial creatures from the outside? But please be extremely careful. Don't get too close. They're terribly difficult to deal with. If you approach them carelessly, you might end up trapped in the middle just like us."

Whiteman certainly wasn't going to make that decision himself. He merely looked to Fang Heng.

"Got it. I'll get you out right now."

Fang Heng spotted Chen Yu and gave a slight nod. Then he told Whiteman and the others, "Stay here and wait for me."

The Holy Court believers nodded respectfully. None of them saw anything wrong with that.

"Yes, Grand Sage."

A Grand Sage could single-handedly overpower even the Calamity.

Wouldn't dealing with these spatial creatures be child's play?

Huh?

Chen Yu froze at the sound of the voice.

That voice?

Then he saw the mask covering the newcomer's face and shouted in delighted surprise, "Boss Fang?"

Fang Heng?

Yi Qingzhi's heart skipped at Chen Yu's shout. He turned to the masked young man as well.

Who else could Chen Yu call Boss Fang but Fang Heng?

So this was Fang Heng?

The man who had helped the entire Eastern Federation establish its fleet?

Respect immediately rose in Yi Qingzhi's heart.

But since when had he gotten mixed up with the Holy Court?

And judging by the way they were standing, didn't the Holy Court people seem ready to follow Fang Heng's every command?

"Whoosh!"

While Yi Qingzhi was still trying to make sense of it, Fang Heng lightly pushed off the ground and approached the center.

His pace was neither particularly fast nor slow.

What?

He was coming straight over?

Yi Qingzhi grew anxious and shouted, "Watch out!"

Too late.

The moment Fang Heng approached the hall's center, more than a dozen points of spatial light began flickering around him.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!"

Glowing spheres sprang out from those points and lunged at Fang Heng from every direction.

"Bang!"

A muffled explosion erupted in midair from one of the spheres attacking him.

Nobody even saw Fang Heng make a move. The instant the glowing sphere drew near him, it burst into a cloud of blood mist!

"Bang! Bang, bang, bang!"

For a time, explosions rang out in rapid succession.

What the hell?

Before the astonished onlookers, almost every glowing sphere that rushed at Fang Heng exploded in the air and was smashed into bloody mist!

Traces of spatial energy escaped from the blood mist and flowed into Fang Heng's Sea of Consciousness.

Fang Heng did nothing else. He paid no attention to the clouds of blood erupting around him and continued walking step by step toward the hall's center.

The Holy Court followers watched him from behind with reverence.

They were certainly shaken, but not overly surprised. Along the way, they had already witnessed a portion of the Grand Sage's power.

The Eastern Federation's elite squad, however, was genuinely stunned.

Those same glowing creatures had forced the squad to defend helplessly in the center of the hall for over twenty minutes. They still hadn't found a way out, yet Fang Heng was strolling toward them as though it were nothing.

Was this guy really that terrifyingly strong?

And his way of fighting seemed entirely different from that of the Holy Court members they had seen before.

Fang Heng ignored their stares.

These monsters were simply too easy for him to deal with.

Their spatial jumps caused such pronounced fluctuations that his senses could detect them effortlessly. He calculated their approach angles in advance and detonated the blood inside their bodies.

A straightforward crushing advantage in attributes.

Clearing the glowing creatures along the way, Fang Heng walked straight past the Eastern Federation elites to the stone pillar at the center of the hall and gently pressed his hand against it.

"Hum..."

A dense surge of spatial power immediately poured into his body through his palm before racing into his Sea of Consciousness!

There it was!

Spatial energy!

Compared with elemental and natural energy, spatial energy was extremely rare.

Absorb it!

Drain every last drop in one go!

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!"

As Fang Heng absorbed the ice-crystal pillar's energy, every glowing creature in the hall was enraged. They swarmed out of secondary spatial projections and charged him!

"Bang! Bang, bang, bang!"

Exactly the same result!

Anything that approached Fang Heng was instantly blasted into bloody mist!

It went on for more than three full minutes.

Only after draining all the energy from the altar did Fang Heng finally lower his hand.

By then, every glowing creature in the hall had been wiped out. Silence settled over the chamber.

Yi Qingzhi took a deep breath and cautiously led the Eastern Federation group over to him.

"Thank you for your assistance, Mr. Fang."

Yi Qingzhi studied him, secretly puzzled.

Fang Heng's appearance had changed dramatically, and he was even associating with the Holy Court?

Weren't those two sworn enemies?

"I'm not Fang Heng. You can call me Fang Bai." Fang Heng waved a hand, glanced at Chen Yu, and added, "Or you can call me Boss Fang, like he does."

Yi Qingzhi raised an eyebrow. Several thoughts flashed through his mind before he nodded. "I understand, Boss Fang. Thank you for helping us."

At that moment, Archbishop Whiteman arrived with the rest of the Holy Court party.

Seeing Chen Yu dusty, disheveled, and utterly dejected, Fang Heng asked, "How did you end up looking like that?"

"Don't ask. Those bastards from the Southern Federation screwed us over."

Chen Yu smiled bitterly and began pouring out his grievances. "When we arrived, the Southern Federation was fighting those spatial creatures. They invited us to help."

"Then, halfway through, they used us. They activated a skill to escape and left us trapped here instead."

Yi Qingzhi raised a hand to stop him and addressed Fang Heng. "Boss Fang, we can discuss that later. The Western Federation entered some time ago. The Apocalypse Treasure could fall into their hands at any moment. Shouldn't we catch up first?"

"All right."

Without further delay, they continued following the direction indicated by the Key of Apocalypse.

Soon they passed through the spatial trial hall and another short stretch of labyrinth. The ice-crystal floor opened up again.

The expanse of ice broadened until it resembled a vast plain.

They continued across it, and before long a gigantic metal door, fully seven meters high, came into view at the far end, set into an ice-crystal wall.

From a distance, they could see quite a few people gathered outside its entrance.

The Key of Apocalypse pointed directly toward the metal door in the center.

Huh?

Fang Heng raised an eyebrow.

A ripple of energy was leaking from a crack in the door.

An extremely familiar energy fluctuation.

"Come on. Let's take a look."

Fang Heng arrived outside the metal door at the end of the ice with the Eastern Federation and Holy Court parties.

— End of Chapter 3000 —
