Chapter 2988

Hardline

From Chinese Chapter 2989 · 强硬

Fang Heng examined the game notifications carefully, excitement spreading across his face.

Excellent stuff!

After absorbing the cocoon, not only had he recovered all the experience lost to the Kinsfolk's Eye of Stars, but he'd advanced to LV:21. He had even gained another special modification option.

It seemed to correspond to Proust's signature ability: splitting into red worms.

He could find an opportunity to test it in combat after returning.

Fang Heng looked away from the notifications and slowly turned his gaze toward Sura and Mu Qingxin behind him.

“Fang Heng!”

Mu Qingxin glared at him so fiercely that her eyes looked ready to burst. She appeared eager to swallow him whole.

“Hmph! You can stay here with him!”

Fang Heng snorted, lightly pushed off the ground, and darted toward Mu Qingxin!

Her pupils contracted.

Fang Heng!

He actually dared attack her first?

After enduring an entire Eye of Stars bombardment, he still had strength left to fight?

“Courting death!”

Mu Qingxin rapidly transformed her right hand into a long whip and lashed it at him with a whistle of displaced air.

“BANG!!”

The two collided in midair with a dull impact.

Hm?

To Mu Qingxin's surprise, Fang Heng had not been weakened nearly as much as she had expected.

He still had that much fighting strength?

Seeing Fang Heng take over, Sura swiftly left the battlefield and retreated toward the rear.

She had fulfilled her agreement with Fang Heng.

She would not interfere in the remaining fight.

Fang Heng closed in rapidly, engaging Mu Qingxin at point-blank range.

The two figures clashed in midair, separated, and slammed together again and again...

Mu Qingxin sneered.

She was confident she was stronger than Fang Heng!

And he'd just exhausted himself fighting Proust.

Fang Heng's most powerful weapon was the Kinsfolk's targeting energy beam.

But that attack had an obvious limitation: it required lengthy preparation before firing.

All she needed to do was beware of his sudden bursts of power and avoid being locked onto by the Kinsfolk's high-tier weapon.

“Whoosh!”

Hm? What was that now?

Mu Qingxin's heart lurched as she sensed an eerie energy fluctuation.

Looking up, she saw a black moon appear behind Fang Heng.

Although its black radiance represented darkness, it emanated an uncanny sacred power.

Fang Heng raised his head and stared at Mu Qingxin.

The reason he'd held back his ultimate move was to save it for her!

[Game Hint: You have activated Everlasting Moon.]

[Game Hint: You are currently in a game world of the highest sequence...]

[Game Hint: Fusing with the current world. Your skill form is changing. Fusion in progress...]

[Game Hint: Current status detected: Sacred Form (Divine Punishment). Fusing skill...]

[Game Hint: Current world detected: Death Realm. Fusing skill...]

[Game Hint: You and your teammates have entered the Cycle of Reincarnation state.]

[Game Hint: Special domain fused: Realm of Reincarnation. You have obtained the special summoned creature: Eye of Chaos.]

Death aura and sacred aura pressed upon Mu Qingxin simultaneously. The two utterly different powers squeezed her from both sides, causing unbearable discomfort. She struggled desperately but couldn't break free in the slightest.

Pairs of dark pupils opened within the void.

They resembled yin-yang fish, half black and half white.

Time seemed to slow and almost stand still for Mu Qingxin. Even the currents within her Sea of Consciousness appeared to freeze. The yin-yang Eye of Chaos was reflected in her pupils, and she completely lost control of her body.

Under the domain's influence, Mu Qingxin remained upright and floating in midair. A phantom of her soul was dragged from her body by an unknown force, rising slowly until it hung above her physical form.

A black inverted cross appeared behind the soul phantom.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!”

The inverted cross instantly pierced through her soul!

Almost simultaneously, corresponding penetrating wounds appeared across Mu Qingxin's physical body.

The Ouroboros Seed's powerful regeneration caused those wounds to heal rapidly.

But in the next instant, the inverted cross began piercing her soul again and again!

Mu Qingxin's face went deathly pale.

At the edge of the battlefield.

Sura watched with a grave expression.

“So that's his trump card...”

It dealt three kinds of damage at once—to the body, the Sea of Consciousness, and the soul.

Fang Heng had not used this move when they fought before.

Powerful!

Even from where she stood, she could sense the terrifying fluctuations radiating outward.

A domain-level ability.

Mu Qingxin was utterly helpless against it.

The Nether Clan and Undead Council mentors also looked increasingly solemn.

It seemed every time Fang Heng used his ultimate skill, its form was different.

They could see it clearly.

This version focused on soul damage; the harm to body and mind was only secondary.

With every thrust of the inverted cross, Mu Qingxin's soul power was shattered a little more.

Even her exceptionally strong original will was weakening under the repeated attacks!

The Eastern Federation players aboard the ships looked equally grave.

The area ahead seemed to have been separated into a special world. Time flowed differently there. All they could see was Mu Qingxin apparently losing the ability to move, bursts of blood erupting from her body as her face gradually grew paler.

Some unknown controlling ability?

The Eastern Federation had long realized that Fang Heng controlled powers far beyond his apparent level.

They couldn't see those powers directly, but they could sense them.

That region was filled with terrifying energy.

A starship operator broke the silence. “Report! Eye of Stars targeting complete.”

“Wait a little longer.”

The Eastern Federation officer serving as deputy captain glanced back toward Alec. Only after Alec nodded did he raise his hand. “Proceed!”

“Eye of Stars targeting complete. Initiating warp...”

“Attack countdown: five, four...”

“One!”

High above Fang Heng and Mu Qingxin, the spatial fluctuations of a warp scattered the surrounding dark clouds.

The cold barrel of a Kinsfolk war machine emerged from the spatial distortion.

It locked onto the target below.

Mu Qingxin was under absolute control and completely unaware of the outside world. Even if she had sensed the danger, she couldn't have escaped the restraints.

“BOOM!!!”

Purple light poured down once more, instantly enveloping Fang Heng and Mu Qingxin!

A third time!

The Kinsfolk's high-tier energy beam continued its relentless annihilation barrage!

For a full five minutes!

The attack finally ended.

The purple radiance gradually faded.

Everyone stared toward the center of the battlefield.

Fang Heng's wounds were regenerating rapidly. Mu Qingxin seemed to have no external injuries at all, but she looked extremely weak.

Whoosh!!

The very next instant, Mu Qingxin fled toward the right without the slightest hesitation.
