Chapter 3084

Immunity

From Chinese Chapter 3085 · 免疫

Both sides' combat levels were noticeably higher in this match.

From Fang Heng's perspective, at least the fighting wouldn't be completely boring.

Soon the score reached eight to seven, with the Noel Clan narrowly ahead.

Liao Yun entered for the opposing team.

Hearing the name, Fang Heng's interest stirred and he looked up.

This was the fellow the elder had asked him to watch.

Liao Yun was powerfully built, his body brimming with explosive strength.

The Noel Clan warrior still on the platform grew visibly more serious when Liao Yun appeared.

“Begin!”

A melee fighter against an elemental specialist.

Liao Yun stood motionless.

“Bang!”

A burst of force exploded beneath his feet. In the next instant, he shot forward, darting quickly to the right.

Fang Heng's pupils contracted.

A martial technique!

It used explosive muscle power and internal force to produce a sudden burst of speed.

The burst lasted only an instant.

But for a warrior, that instant could be deadly.

“Freeze!”

The Noel Clan warrior immediately used an ice-element skill, trying to restrain Liao Yun's speed.

Ice spread across the ground and instantly formed frost around Liao Yun's feet.

“Bang!!!”

With another eruption of force, the frost around his feet shattered.

Whoosh!

Power burst beneath Liao Yun's feet again. His speed surged to an entirely new level, and he flashed across the distance, appearing on the Noel Clan warrior's right and driving a fist forward!

“Boom!!!”

His punch struck an ice curtain, shattering it with a thunderous crash.

Whoosh!

The referee appeared before Liao Yun and intercepted his next punch.

Staring coldly at him, the referee said, “You win.”

Seeing his ice curtain destroyed, the Noel Clan warrior shook his head inwardly, saluted Liao Yun with cupped fists, and jumped off the platform ahead of time. Returning to his companions, he spread his hands helplessly.

“Sorry. I'd hoped to wear him down a little, but that was all I could do.”

“Don't say that. You made him expend quite a bit of stamina. He can't use that move many times.”

Everyone had noticed the same thing.

Liao Yun was inherently a strong counter to elemental fighters, and his special martial technique made him even more dangerous. Unless an elemental specialist far surpassed his level, defeating him would be difficult.

Meng Hong's expression remained unreadable. “Fang Heng, you're up.”

“Yes, Elder.”

Fang Heng stood and made his way onto the platform.

He'd seen through the move.

That last technique actually involved spatial studies.

The martial technique, Instant Step, produced only a very brief acceleration. Liao Yun could extend it across such a long distance because he simultaneously executed an extremely short spatial jump.

He wasn't entering a spatial projection in the normal way. He was using spatial power for instantaneous reinforcement.

It resembled the time-acceleration effect attached to Tree World Descends, though it was vastly inferior overall.

In other words, Liao Yun combined spatial studies with close-combat ability.

While considering this, Fang Heng stepped onto the stage opposite Liao Yun.

Liao Yun looked him over.

An unfamiliar face.

A new warrior recruited by the Noel Clan?

Was he here to exhaust Liao Yun, or did he have an ability that could counter him?

The referee glanced at Fang Heng. Seeing his signal that he was ready, he announced solemnly, “Begin.”

Whoosh!

What?!

Liao Yun's pupils contracted sharply. Before he could move, his opponent was already rushing straight toward him!

Such speed!

The newcomer was a melee fighter too!

“Bang!!!”

Seeing Fang Heng charge directly at him along the shortest possible path, Liao Yun immediately counterattacked.

Fight!

They were both close-combat warriors.

Take a single step backward and he'd surrender the initiative, falling further and further behind!

Neither would back down like a coward!

What?!

“Boom!!!!”

Their fists collided. Liao Yun's heart lurched as an enormous force surged toward him. He couldn't resist it at all, and his entire right hand instantly went numb.

Whoosh!

Fang Heng swept his right leg across.

“Smack!!”

His kick sent Liao Yun flying. The man landed heavily on the ground beyond the arena platform with a loud crash.

He'd lost?

Liao Yun sprang up, mouth opening as his mind went blank.

He could vaguely remember the whole exchange.

He'd already lost?

He hadn't even managed to use his spatial abilities. He couldn't withstand a single punch from the other man.

Liao Yun raised his head toward Fang Heng on the platform, shock filling his eyes.

This guy...

Such terrifying strength from one punch!

The difference between them was absurd!

Liao Yun rose, shook his slowly recovering right hand, and saluted Fang Heng with cupped fists.

He had no idea how powerful his opponent truly was, or even how much strength he'd used. But he knew one thing: if Fang Heng hadn't held back and had wanted him dead, he'd already be a corpse.

The referee also regarded Fang Heng with wary eyes.

This youngster!

Melee combat was the most dangerous kind. A moment's carelessness could mean serious injury or death.

Under those circumstances, the referee would never have reacted in time to stop the fight.

There would be another name on the arena's list of dangerous individuals.

The referee nodded. “Red side wins. Prepare for the next match.”

The waiting areas on both sides fell quiet.

He'd won?

Nobody had expected Fang Heng to defeat Liao Yun in mere moments.

It had been a complete crushing victory.

A strange look flashed through Elder Meng Hong's eyes.

Elder Dilan had spoken highly of Fang Heng. Meng Hong had believed most of it, but he'd only expected the youngster to wear down the opponent a little.

He'd never imagined such an extraordinary surprise.

With that strength, Fang Heng definitely qualified as a high-ranked warrior.

A barely perceptible smile appeared on Meng Hong's face.

They'd stumbled upon a treasure.

Had he known, he'd have sent Fang Heng up first and crushed the entire match outright.

Liao Yun was already the opposing team's main fighter. They had only one person left, who was unlikely to turn things around.

After a stunned pause, the Noel Clan warriors finally erupted in cheers.

Where had this absurdly powerful youngster come from?

He'd even crushed Liao Yun?

The opposing side suddenly felt overwhelming pressure.

They'd originally hoped Liao Yun could eliminate three opponents by himself.

Now they had only their last fighter left.

And Fang Heng looked as though he hadn't expended any energy at all...

They probably couldn't beat him either.

Soon a small-statured alien stepped onto the platform and saluted Fang Heng.

The referee gave the smaller fighter a cautioning look. “Begin!”

Mind Pierce!

The alien warrior knew he stood no chance in a straight fight, so he'd settled on a plan before entering.

A sneaky attack with mental abilities!

If he could steal a win, great.

If not, he'd surrender immediately!

But it had absolutely no effect!

Damn it!

The alien warrior's face went white.

His Mind Pierce attack sank into Fang Heng's mind like a stone dropped into the ocean.

This guy was completely immune!

— End of Chapter 3084 —
