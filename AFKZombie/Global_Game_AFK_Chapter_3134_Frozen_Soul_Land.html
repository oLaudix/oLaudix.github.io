Chapter 3134

Frozen Soul Land

From Chinese Chapter 3135 · 冰魄之地

Before his sudden departure, Fang Heng had only sent Anxi a message saying he was heading out to gain experience and seek a breakthrough. He expected to be back within a month at most.

Given how deeply rooted the Noel Clan was in the Seven Realms Martial Capital, he figured the Kinsfolk wouldn't turn openly hostile toward the entire clan just because of him.

After twenty hours of intermittent warp jumps, the large transport ship finally arrived at the Frozen Soul Land.

The cargo-bay doors opened.

An icy blast rushed into the starship.

The temperature plummeted.

Every convict laborer had been issued a thick padded coat beforehand. They wrapped themselves in the coats and disembarked under the supervision of the prison security team.

A little later, Fang Heng looked up and watched the ship that had brought them depart again, disappearing into spatial fluctuations as it jumped away.

“Young brother, the Frozen Soul Land is controlled by the Kinsfolk. They don't cope well with harsh environments, so they've hired the Aerospace Security Company to handle safety here. To put it plainly, this whole planet is run by that security company.”

Uncle Hong continued quietly, “The climate here is brutal. At least a thousand laborers die here every year. The security company doesn't treat us as people. If your luck runs out and you die, nobody will care.”

“They'll assign jobs in a bit. Tell them you're a repair technician and know how to fix machinery. Then stick with me, and I'll show you what to do.”

“All right.”

The Frozen Soul Land's environment was extraordinarily harsh.

Violent winds drove airborne ice crystals against their faces, stinging painfully. Under the guards' supervision, the laborers slowly trudged across the ice until they reached a mountain wall.

They formed more than twenty long lines before a cave entrance in the cliff.

“Uncle Hong, you seem to know this place well.”

“I've spent more than thirty years here, so of course I know a few things.” Uncle Hong sighed ruefully. “Another ten years this time. I don't know if I can endure it. Oh, right, didn't you say you had friends here?”

“Mm. They should have arrived about a month ago. One's an elf, the other's from the Flying Squirrel Clan.”

“Haven't heard of them, but I'll ask around later. I still know a few people here.”

“Thank you.”

Fang Heng extended his perception and made a rough survey of the area.

He couldn't detect Kappa or Korkalot's auras. He'd stay for now and see what he could learn.

After a simple registration procedure, the laborers were divided into teams.

Fang Heng and Uncle Hong were registered as skilled maintenance workers and assigned to a group of more than ten people. Together they were led into a separate cavern inside the mountain.

The head of security told them that this would be their sleeping quarters from now on.

Conditions were miserable—dark and damp. But at least there was a fire to warm themselves by, and they wouldn't have to endure the bone-piercing cold outside.

After spending over an hour getting settled, everyone was issued repair tools and told to prepare for work.

Uncle Hong patiently explained the basic rules of working in the Frozen Soul Land to Fang Heng and the younger members of his clan.

“Work here is calculated in labor hours. Once you've completed your required hours, you're released. For skilled work like ours, every actual hour counts as one and a half labor hours. That's an advantage. Some rarer trades receive even bigger labor-hour bonuses.”

Two security guards had already identified Uncle Hong as the group's leader. One nodded to him. “All right, enough chatter. Get up and work. There's a batch of equipment that needs urgent repairs. Bring your people and follow me.”

Soon the guards led them deeper into the mountain cave.

Several old mining machines had broken down and been moved temporarily into the cavern for repair.

After explaining the assignment, the guards left the maintenance group and hurried away.

Uncle Hong picked up a wrench, walked to a machine, and began studying the large mining equipment, calling the younger members of his clan over to help.

Fang Heng stayed to the side, looking around and occasionally lending a hand.

With his advanced Mechanical Repair skill, completing the job would be easy for him.

He simply couldn't be bothered spending his time on it.

On closer examination, he found that the damaged equipment consisted of advanced Kinsfolk mining machines. They were designed to withstand extremely high temperatures, yet many of their failures had evidently been caused by overheating.

Just what kind of ore were they mining to ruin their machines like this?

Fang Heng turned his attention toward the mine entrance.

He could see laborers carrying large wooden crates full of ore back and forth.

After more than half an hour, a convict laborer entered the cavern and whispered something in Uncle Hong's ear.

Uncle Hong's interest was piqued. He glanced at Fang Heng several times, a trace of suspicion in his eyes.

“Uncle Hong, is something wrong?”

“Your friends are named Korkalot and Kappa, correct?”

“Mm.”

“I have news of them.”

“Where are they?”

“Your Flying Squirrel friend is quite something. He's now the chief of the exploration team on the ninth underground level, and Korkalot serves as his deputy. But meeting them won't be easy. You've only just arrived, and security won't let you wander into the lower levels.”

Uncle Hong paused, then asked, “By the way, I still haven't asked your name. I'll try to get a message to your friends. Once they hear you're here, they might find a way to bring you over.”

Fang Heng couldn't help laughing.

Good grief, and he'd been so worried about them!

Well, sure enough.

Gold shines wherever it goes.

The Flying Squirrel Clan really was outstanding at mineral prospecting. He hadn't expected Kappa to rise through the ranks even in a prison camp.

“No need to trouble yourself, Uncle Hong. I'll manage. Thank you.”

Fang Heng thanked him softly. As he spoke, his figure began fading before Uncle Hong and the other prisoners.

What the hell?

Everyone turned toward Fang Heng and watched his body become insubstantial, vanishing from sight in the blink of an eye.

Huh?

He'd just escaped like that?

…

On the ninth underground level of the cavern.

As the leader of the ninth-level operations team, Kappa had the authority to direct all the laborers underground.

With Korkalot also serving as his guard, the other prisoners jokingly called him the little tyrant of the prison.

After using his senses to locate an underground flame vein, Kappa led a group of laborers to the site and began excavating.

During this period, Korkalot had become Kappa's bodyguard. Most of the time he stood beside him with an icy expression.

He was thoroughly displeased.

He was a prince of the elves, for heaven's sake.

When had he ever sunk so low?

Not only had he become a prisoner, he even had to act as a bodyguard for someone from the Flying Squirrel Clan?

The laborers could all sense the chilling atmosphere around Korkalot and didn't dare approach him, afraid he'd vent his anger on them.
