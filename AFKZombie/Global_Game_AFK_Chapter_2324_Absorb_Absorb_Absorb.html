Chapter 2324

Absorb, Absorb, Absorb

From original Chinese Chapter 2325 · 吸吸吸

Even without Fang Heng's help, the vampires skilled in magic arrays were confident they could finish it.

In less than three minutes, the group completed Abe Akaya's summoning array together.

Fang Heng stood before the array and looked toward the Divine Tree sapling in the center of the hall, which had fallen into a state of frenzy.

His only concern was whether Abe Akaya might also be affected by the corruption.

Never mind!

The concentration of black fog in the hall was already falling.

The Wood Clan outside was probably on its way.

There was no time to think.

He'd try it first!

"Activate the magic array with me."

"Yes!"

Fang Heng brought his palms together, his mental strength surging into the stone-slab array.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!"

A red vortex appeared in the spinning array's center, radiating faint dark crimson light.

"Hiss! Hiss, hiss!"

Several of Abe Akaya's roots emerged from the array.

[Hint: You have summoned part of Abe Akaya's root system using a special magic array.]

[Hint: Abe Akaya is currently affected by an unknown force. Its abilities are greatly reduced.]

Fang Heng shouted, "Abe Akaya! Can you absorb that thing?!"

Abe Akaya had only just entered the polluted area and hadn't yet understood what was happening. At Fang Heng's warning, it immediately sensed the Divine Tree sapling ahead, releasing black smoke.

Its excitement soared.

What a feast!

Like a starving ghost discovering a banquet after three days without food.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh..."

Abe Akaya's vines burst from the magic array and rapidly stretched toward the sapling, wrapping around it.

"Be careful." Fang Heng immediately warned Abe Akaya through their mental connection. "That thing may be poisonous."

"Whoosh!"

But with a banquet before it, Abe Akaya could hardly contain itself. It would taste a little first and see if it was poisonous!

[Hint: Abe Akaya is absorbing energy... EXP increased by 120,000.]

[Hint: Abe Akaya is absorbing energy... It has gained 325 dimensional-energy points...]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has absorbed unknown energy. The energy's level is too high and exceeds its control...]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has entered the Confused state...]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has lost 300,000 EXP...]

Notification after notification flashed across Fang Heng's retina.

The moment Abe Akaya absorbed the sapling's energy, its vines began twitching violently, writhing almost uncontrollably.

"Hey, are you all right...?"

Fang Heng frowned.

He'd told it not to gulp down too much. Now look what had happened—it had poisoned itself!

Fortunately, from Abe Akaya's perspective, it had only swallowed one mouthful of corrupting energy. The overall negative effect was minor, and it recovered after two seconds of confusion.

It had gained a little over three hundred dimensional-energy points, but lost nearly two hundred thousand EXP overall.

A quick calculation showed that it had come out behind.

Abe Akaya sent Fang Heng feelings of grievance and helplessness.

Such delicious food was right in front of it, yet it couldn't eat! How frustrating!

Fang Heng rubbed his chin and studied the sapling.

He was even more frustrated than Abe Akaya.

He had finally dealt with every obstacle and gone to all the trouble of summoning Abe Akaya.

Now, at the last possible moment, it turned out the sapling couldn't be absorbed?

Was this some kind of cruel joke?

Fang Heng approached the sapling again and studied the changes in it.

According to what Sergey had said, the corrupted seeds could indeed destabilize the sapling and disable it for a while.

But because of the black fog within Fang Heng himself, the damage had unquestionably exceeded anything Sergey expected.

The real culprit behind the sapling's instability wasn't the corrupted Divine Tree seed, but the power of the Seed of Jealousy that had spilled out of Fang Heng's body.

Following that line of thought...

If the corruption had come from his own Demon Seed, could he somehow take it back?

The logic seemed sound.

But he barely understood how the corruption had escaped his body in the first place. How was he supposed to retrieve it?

With no time to think, Fang Heng simply reached out and touched the sapling again.

Might as well try!

Absorb!

He activated his mental strength and tried to draw the Seed of Jealousy's power back out!

Huh?!

It actually responded?

His heart stirred.

Holy crap!

It really worked!

Astonishment flashed in Fang Heng's eyes.

To his surprise, under his mental control, the black corrupting energy slowly flowed back into his hand.

However...

Fang Heng frowned.

Throughout the process, he had to control the circulation of his mental strength with absolute concentration. If he relaxed even slightly, the corruption immediately flowed backward again.

Damn it!

It was like driving with a manual transmission.

Fortunately, the Dimensional Level bonuses had greatly increased his mental strength, allowing him to maintain the extraction for a while.

Then he'd keep going!

Fang Heng forced himself to maintain the flow, slowly drawing the corruption back into his sea of consciousness.

As the corruption withdrew, the previously frenzied sapling visibly calmed down.

"Quickly! Abe Akaya! You can do it now!"

Abe Akaya had been waiting eagerly. At Fang Heng's call, its vines immediately spread out and fastened themselves to the sapling.

"Gulp! Gulp!"

It drank greedily!

[Hint: Abe Akaya has absorbed dimensional energy stored in the Divine Tree sapling. Current dimensional energy converted into EXP: +898,200.]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has absorbed the Divine Tree sapling's energy. Abe Akaya EXP +2,112,200.]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has absorbed unknown energy. The energy's level is too high and exceeds its control...]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has entered the Confused state...]

[Hint: Abe Akaya has lost 543,000 EXP...]

Although Fang Heng hadn't removed all the corruption from the sapling, and Abe Akaya still suffered some negative effects, it was far better than before.

At least the exchange was profitable now!

Excitement flickered in Fang Heng's eyes.

Excellent!

Then increase the intensity!

Keep absorbing!

Fang Heng quickly realized this absorption differed from when he'd drained a Leaf Clan sapling offshoot previously.

That offshoot had contained mostly dimensional power. At the time, Abe Akaya hadn't yet awakened dimensional abilities, so all that energy had been converted directly into EXP.

This time, though, Abe Akaya was absorbing a main sapling!

Most of the main body's energy was natural energy, which provided Abe Akaya with EXP. It contained dimensional energy too, but in a smaller proportion.

Overall, he might gain less dimensional energy than he'd obtained from the Wood Clan's offshoot previously.

Fang Heng could understand why.

If it were his own tree, he'd also reserve only some dimensional energy for emergencies and spend the rest on increasing its Dimensional Level. There wouldn't be much left for an intruder to absorb.
