Chapter 2006

Into the Backpack

Translated from Chinese Chapter 2007

The Dragon Soul Crystal was far too large to fit into a backpack.

First, he'd break it into pieces. Then he could stuff the fragments into his warp backpack slots and carry away as many as possible! Even if he couldn't take all of them, bringing back a few fragments would be better than leaving empty-handed!

“All right. Be careful. I'll cover you.”

Gu Qingzhu agreed with Fang Heng's plan. She nodded, stepped back slightly, and kept watch over their surroundings.

“Got it!”

Fang Heng didn't hesitate. He stepped forward, clenched his right fist, and struck again.

BANG!

His fist slammed into the surface of the ice.

A fist-shaped impression appeared immediately.

Crack! Crack, crack!

Fine fractures raced across the surface, spreading and widening.

BOOM!

The block shattered completely, exposing the entire Dragon Soul Crystal.

To Fang Heng's surprise, neither his punch nor the exploding ice had damaged the crystal in the slightest. It remained perfectly intact.

His punch couldn't break it?

That wasn't how he'd expected things to go.

Fang Heng frowned.

The Dragon Soul Crystal was incredibly hard! *Hummm…!* In the next instant, a visible wave of blood vitality spread outward from the crystal.

As the wave passed through him, Fang Heng felt the blood vitality within his own body stir slightly.

The frozen players around the chamber seemed to sense it too. Their expressions suddenly changed.

“Not good! They're waking up! Hurry!” Gu Qingzhu warned.

“Mm!”

Without hesitation, Fang Heng reached out and touched the Dragon Soul Crystal again.

A game notification appeared in his vision.

[Hint: Player has discovered an item — Dragon Soul Crystal.]

Put it in the backpack!

Whether it worked or not, he might as well try! The stone was enormous, so he probably couldn't store it directly. And if ordinary force couldn't break it, he'd use the Holy Court's divine greatsword!

Fang Heng was already prepared for his next move, his right hand ready to summon the sacred greatsword. But a split second later, he froze in astonishment.

Whoosh!

What?!

The Dragon Soul Crystal simply vanished before his eyes.

Could it really…?

Had he just stuffed the whole thing straight into his backpack?! He could pick it up directly?! Before he could make sense of what had happened, an intense burning pain erupted inside his body.

What was that?! *Hiss, hiss…*

It felt as though a raging fire had been lit inside him!

The flames seemed to spread outward from his very core. His skin rapidly grew scorching hot, as if it were about to catch fire.

“Fang Heng?”

Gu Qingzhu hadn't expected him to pick up the crystal so easily. Seeing him standing there motionless, she shouted to him.

Her voice snapped Fang Heng back to his senses. He gritted his teeth.

“Give me a hand! Let's go!”

He immediately sprinted back toward basement level seven!

A divine artifact!

The Dragon Soul Crystal was actually a divine artifact! Noticing that it hadn't appeared in any of his warp backpack slots, Fang Heng suddenly understood.

Divine artifacts could bypass the usual differences between game worlds and reality. They could be placed directly into the backpack, and the game treated them as items without even occupying a normal inventory slot or adding to its carrying weight!

But the Dragon Soul Crystal was unusual.

Storing it in his backpack apparently inflicted an extraordinarily powerful negative effect. Every second that passed was agonizing.

The burning pain inside him intensified. Fang Heng clenched his teeth!

The agony threatened to swallow his consciousness. Only one thought remained in his mind.

Take the loot! Just get the Dragon Soul Crystal out of here first! Gu Qingzhu frowned and immediately hurried after Fang Heng toward the upper levels of the institute. The instant he left the ice cellar—

BANG! BANG, BANG, BANG!

One massive block of ice after another exploded behind them.

The frozen players awakened one by one, opening their eyes and looking toward the center of the chamber.

Every one of them saw the same thing: the place where the Dragon Soul Crystal had stood was empty.

Astonishment flashed across their faces.

The Dragon Soul Crystal…

Someone had stolen it! With the institute under such tight guard, who could have done it?

“After them!”

The players reacted at once and raced after the crystal's lingering aura. Almost everyone who had been cultivating with its help was already a powerful player.

And with their strength further enhanced by the Dragon Soul Crystal's energy, the surge of blood vitality made them even deadlier in close combat! They rushed toward the exit and soon spotted the backs of Fang Heng and Gu Qingzhu.

That young man! Where had he come from?!

Ji Shaojie fixed his gaze on Fang Heng, profoundly surprised.

The Dragon Soul Crystal contained an enormous treasure.

An SSS-rated bloodline inheritance: the Dragonborn bloodline! Such a bloodline could vastly improve a player's strength, perhaps even transforming it completely.

To awaken that bloodline, many players—including Ji Shaojie—had voluntarily worked for Miyamoto Teru in exchange for the chance to cultivate here.

But there was no guarantee that any of them would ultimately receive the Dragonborn bloodline.

The energy within the crystal was so violent that ordinary people could barely approach it, let alone place it in their inventories.

They all carried specially made Ice Soul Jade Pendants that could resist a small portion of the crystal's effects.

But those pendants had drawbacks of their own.

Such as freezing the wearer in ice.

By now, Fang Heng could no longer sense what was happening around him.

The terrible pain was draining his stamina. Under the Dragon Soul Crystal's influence, charred-gray burn marks had even appeared across his skin.

His internal organs felt as though they were being smelted in a furnace.

Fang Heng was certain that without the enhancements of his Undying Body, he never would have survived this long.

All he could do was barely make out the direction he needed to run. Hold on a little longer! Just until he reached the game world!

Gu Qingzhu was running several paces behind him. Seeing that the pursuers were about to catch up, she gave a cold snort, stopped abruptly, spun around, and swung her silver sword.

Hiss! Hiss, hiss, hiss!

A cascade of silver sword-light poured out like a waterfall!

The pursuing players recoiled in alarm. None dared take the attack head-on, and they fell back.

“Careful!”

A burly player sprang out from the side, holding a massive shield in front of him. The Calamity Shield!

A layer of dark radiance coated the shield's surface.

The incoming silver sword-light struck the Calamity Shield and was absorbed and neutralized, one slash after another.

Ji Shaojie burst out from the crowd and leaped forward. In the blink of an eye, he exchanged several blows with Gu Qingzhu.

— End of Chapter 2006 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Undying Body. 不死体 is rendered “Undying Body,” a defensive/healing effect described as allowing Fang Heng to withstand the crystal’s burning.
  • Ice Soul Jade Pendant. 冰魄玉佩 is provisionally rendered “Ice Soul Jade Pendant.” Its freezing drawback is retained.
  • Calamity Shield. 灾厄之盾 is translated “Calamity Shield.”
  • Inventory mechanic. The crystal is a divine artifact that can occupy the backpack without using a normal slot or adding ordinary weight. The severe burning effect is also preserved.
  • Source. Translated in full from the Chinese PDF you uploaded, original Chapter 2007. The original Chinese chapter number is one ahead of the established English numbering.
