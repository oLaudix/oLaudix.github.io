Chapter 2996

Transfer

From Chinese Chapter 2997 · 转移

“Why don't we try removing the seal?”

Archbishop Whiteman warned, “The seal may be suppressing something evil. Opening it recklessly might fully revive the creature wrapped around the pillar.”

“Uh...”

“The alchemical magic array operates in a very unusual way. I've never seen one like it...”

“Does anyone have a better idea?”

“Could we try opening only half of it?”

The players proficient in alchemy gathered around the sealing stones beside an array and spent quite some time studying and debating them, but couldn't find an effective solution.

Luo Ao was running out of patience.

“Do it. Release the seal. I'll take responsibility if anything goes wrong.”

If they kept waiting, the other Federations would find the place, and dividing the Apocalypse treasure would become much more difficult.

Archbishop Whiteman sighed inwardly and nodded. “Very well. But everyone, make thorough preparations first.”

Several alchemy experts conferred beside a stone slab, then tried moving the block embedded in the altar.

“BANG!!!”

The stone was extremely heavy. As they lowered it from the sealing slab to the floor, it landed with a tremendous impact.

Hm?

Look! What's that?!

“Crack, crack, crack...”

As the first seal was removed, a faint noise came from the central pillar. The rotten flesh wrapped around it began writhing far more violently, as though the creature restrained by the pillar was struggling in agony. The grotesque face in the flesh gradually became clearer.

The players were at a loss and could only stand around the fleshy pillar.

“Crack, crack, crack-crack-crack...”

The remaining altars throughout the hall began shaking violently, entirely out of control.

Oh no!

The seal was being forced open.

Luo Ao's expression changed.

“Thud! Thud! Thud, thud!!!”

One by one, the blocks weighing down the sealing altars were lifted uncontrollably and dropped to the floor. The outer layers of rotting flesh surrounding the central pillar began rapidly peeling away.

Until the monster sealed within was revealed.

A human?

The sealed figure was tall and slender. Most of his body had decayed into a pool of rotten flesh, while the remaining parts exposed white bones and strands of muscle.

“That is...”

Archbishop Whiteman stared at the figure gradually stepping free, a chill running down his spine.

“Crack, crack-crack...”

At some point, tiny black beetles had begun emerging from every corner of the hall. They scuttled across the floor, making a continuous clicking noise, and rapidly gathered on the decaying human in the center.

“Watch out! Fall back!”

Whiteman suddenly realized the danger and shouted. At the same time, he and the Holy Court priests behind him raised a defensive barrier.

Whether the thing was the Apocalypse treasure or not, it was unquestionably dangerous!

The next moment, the human within the rotten flesh slowly opened his eyes and looked toward Luo Ao and the Central Federation players. Suddenly, he raised a hand toward them.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!”

Rotten flesh from his body launched toward the players like a barrage of explosive shells!

“Bang! Bang, bang!!”

Dense projectiles of rotting meat exploded against the sacred barrier.

The flesh was highly corrosive. After bursting, the remaining corruption continued spreading and inflicted additional damage on the barrier.

“Second group, cover us!”

“Retreat! Hurry, retreat!!”

The confined hall was unsuitable for an effective battle. Luo Ao shouted for the players to withdraw in an orderly formation.

The Federation elites immediately unleashed various spells to destroy the incoming flesh, relying on the sacred barrier cast by Archbishop Whiteman and the other Holy Court believers as they steadily fell back.

They retreated through the melted opening in the ice and finally exited the cavern's defensive region.

What now?

Back in the outer ice-crystal labyrinth, the players exchanged quick glances.

They seemed to have released something extraordinarily dangerous.

But where was the Apocalypse treasure?

Thinking back on the brief fight, the sealed mass of flesh had only just escaped and had not yet recovered its full strength. They hadn't seen anything else connected with the treasure.

Was their intelligence wrong?

“Maybe we have to defeat that creature before we can claim the Apocalypse treasure?”

“Kill it?”

Just recalling that grotesque flesh monster made the players' skin crawl.

Luo Ao turned to Archbishop Whiteman. “Archbishop, do you think you can handle the mysterious monster we just saw?”

Whiteman looked solemn. “If my guess is right, there's a good chance it is Ouroboros.”

Luo Ao's expression changed at once. “What? You mean the Eternal Cataclysm?”

“It looks very similar.”

Whiteman couldn't be sure. He continued gravely, “If it truly is Ouroboros, we're in serious trouble. We must find a way to destroy it completely, or this entire world will be swallowed by the Calamity.”

Luo Ao took a deep breath and looked toward the cavern entrance. “Form up! Establish a defensive line and prepare to eliminate the monster!”

The Federation elites had barely formed ranks when the black beetles they'd seen earlier began pouring rapidly from the cavern.

“Destroy them!”

Players in the rear unleashed fire spells together, concentrating their attacks on the entrance. The beetles emerging from the cavern had little magical resistance; they crackled under the concentrated flames and burned to ashes.

“He's coming.”

Archbishop Whiteman fixed his gaze on the cavern entrance, raised his staff, and focused intently.

In the shadows not far from Luo Ao's group, several pairs of eyes were watching them closely.

“My lady, Haye has been suppressed for so long, yet his strength hasn't diminished at all. Aren't you worried about Fang Heng?”

“Not at all.”

Xia Xi watched the Central Federation players calmly. “There may be a difference in strength, but Fang Heng isn't stupid. He'll build up his power and use the Titan bloodline against Haye. After all, he's nothing more than a monster that's lost its mind.”

“But...”

Her subordinate remained worried.

The last time they'd sealed Haye, Xia Xi, Mu Qingxin, and Su Ziyue had joined forces to imprison him. They'd even left portions of their own origin power behind to keep the seal intact until now.

“If Fang Heng dies against an incomplete Ouroboros Seed that has lost its reason, that only proves he isn't qualified.”

The subordinates fell silent, a chill creeping through them.

They couldn't understand Xia Xi at all.

She'd devoted tremendous effort to cultivating Fang Heng, sparing no pains throughout the process.

Yet after the Seed was grown, she'd adopted a hands-off approach, offering only occasional guidance.

Now she spoke with such indifference, as though she'd abandon Fang Heng without a second thought if he failed.

— End of Chapter 2996 —
