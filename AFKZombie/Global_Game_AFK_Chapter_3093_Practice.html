Chapter 3093

Practice

From Chinese Chapter 3094 · 实践

“Exactly! That's precisely it!”

Nasir was delighted by Fang Heng's comprehension and nodded. “By the same reasoning, if we moved this magic array outside, the different surrounding laws would cause it to fail completely.”

“I understand. Would it be fair to say the alchemical array beneath your feet is a fixed, small domain with locally modified laws?”

“Exactly right.”

Nasir snapped his fingers and nodded repeatedly. It had taken him considerable effort to understand the mechanism behind this advancement himself. “To overcome this barrier, you must first perceive the power of laws.”

“That depends on each person's perception. Generally, the stronger your perceptive ability, the easier it is to detect the laws of power around you and then design an array capable of changing them.”

“Perception...”

Hearing this, Fang Heng closed his eyes, expanded his perception, and tried to sense the laws of the surrounding world according to Nasir's guidance.

The structure and flow of power.

Nasir's heart stirred as he saw Fang Heng immediately close his eyes and try to perceive the world.

He was putting it into practice already?

In fact, Fang Heng often examined the world through his senses. He'd simply never focused specifically on “laws.”

As for the laws governing this particular area...

In Fang Heng's understanding, laws were the structural composition of the forces within an environment.

Normally, different forms of power mixed in an environment, reaching a certain equilibrium within space.

His two domain skills acquired through the game could disrupt that balance.

One was Tree World Descends, which established a powerful natural-studies domain.

When activated, it filled the area with natural power, allowing natural studies to achieve exceptionally strong effects within its boundaries.

The other was Death Realm Descends.

It not only strengthened necromantic energy greatly, but also severely suppressed other kinds of power.

Viewed this way, Death Realm Descends represented a more advanced application of laws than Tree World Descends, simultaneously applying enhancement and suppression.

The environment around him now was ordinary.

Fang Heng contemplated the changes in surrounding energies while recalling how both domain skills had felt when activated.

A notification slowly appeared on his retina.

[Hint: Player is attempting to perceive changes in laws.]

[Hint: Player has triggered the current quest—Alchemy Cultivation (Transcendent).]

Quest Name: Alchemy Cultivation (Transcendent).

Quest Requirement: Attempt to design and complete a magic array adapted to the surrounding environment (0/1).

Quest Reward: Transcendent-level Alchemy Advancement.

Fang Heng stopped perceiving and slowly opened his eyes.

Nasir asked, “How does it feel?”

“Mm. I'm beginning to sense it. Changes in laws and the flow of energy through space do follow certain patterns.”

“Gulp...”

Nasir swallowed as he stared at Fang Heng.

Good grief.

He could really sense it?

The flow of power!

He'd heard other alchemy instructors discuss that concept before.

Yet even now he didn't fully understand what that supposed flow of power meant. He couldn't get a handle on it at all.

Nasir stared at Fang Heng, suddenly feeling something strange. It was as though Fang Heng already understood laws better than he did.

Could this youngster genuinely be a one-in-ten-thousand alchemy genius?

Nasir ventured, “Want to try?”

“Mm. Of course.”

Fang Heng was eager himself and readily agreed.

He understood the underlying logic now, but putting it into practice would inevitably present challenges.

Whether he could succeed was another matter.

The difficulty was unknown. Only an actual attempt would tell.

Besides, the various energies in the environment constrained and influenced one another. A tiny adjustment could have enormous consequences. One miscalculation or error in directing power could destabilize the alchemical array. If the array merely exploded, that would be manageable; a serious mistake could even cause a backlash against its creator.

“Follow me.”

Nasir was eager too and headed toward the training room door.

With anyone else, he would have assumed the person was boasting.

But when Fang Heng spoke that way, Nasir somehow thought it sounded perfectly reasonable.

“Arranging arrays that alter laws is too dangerous. A mistake causing an explosion in this building would be trouble. I'll take you to the Alchemy Association. They have a proper training facility.”

“Good.”

Outside, Anxi waited in the corridor. She nodded respectfully. “Mr. Fang Heng, Mr. Nasir, has the lesson ended?”

“Mm, for now. We're going to the Alchemy Association for the next stage. You can take us there.”

“Certainly.”

Anxi immediately made the arrangements.

...

The Alchemy Association was also located in the center of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital, not far from the Noel Clan building.

An hour later, the two arrived.

“Fang Heng, the Alchemy Association's main headquarters isn't here. This is only a fairly large branch. I'm the branch's deputy director, but I rarely involve myself in internal administration. It's largely an honorary position.”

“We'll need to borrow some association facilities shortly. Strictly speaking, they're not open to outsiders.”

Nasir led Fang Heng inside and continued explaining cheerfully. “The association exists as a platform for alchemy scholars to exchange ideas and share knowledge. People help each other when they can; if they can't, that's fine too. Usually there's little trouble. Fang Heng, you're already a Grandmaster alchemist, so you're more than qualified to join. What do you say?”

“Sure. I'll join.”

Seeing that Nasir was inviting him, Fang Heng accepted immediately.

A platform for mutual assistance and exchange.

It sounded useful.

Delighted at having recruited a terrifyingly gifted expert, Nasir continued, “Excellent. I'll take care of your application. You'll need to fill out a form, and I'll authorize a special approval. It'll be quick.”

Anxi followed behind, quietly astonished by their conversation.

The Alchemy Association wasn't nearly as simple as Nasir made it sound. Even its entry threshold was Master-level alchemy. Its members were powerful scholars, most of them warriors employed by major factions or even trusted retainers trained by those powers. It was therefore a neutral organization that no major faction wished to offend.

Ordinary alchemy scholars would fight tooth and nail for membership.

And Nasir was personally recommending Fang Heng?

What did he see in him that made him so important?

Was it his extraordinary talent?

As they continued forward, Nasir explained, “The association has a purpose-built training ground where the elemental laws are maintained in a relatively orderly equilibrium. That reduces some of the difficulty of arranging arrays that affect laws.”
