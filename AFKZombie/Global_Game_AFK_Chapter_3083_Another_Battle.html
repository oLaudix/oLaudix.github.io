Chapter 3083

Another Battle

From Chinese Chapter 3084 · 再战

“Uh...”

Nasir scratched his hair.

Wasn't he a little too eager...?

Perhaps he'd been stuck at this stage for ages and was impatient now that he'd finally found a breakthrough method.

Oh well.

Nasir thought no more of it. He rose quietly and left the training room.

After leaving, he took the elevator downstairs and happened to encounter Mu Shunan coming toward him.

Nasir raised a hand. “Hey, what a coincidence!”

“Ah, finished your lesson?”

Mu Shunan spoke casually.

Only he knew this wasn't a chance encounter at all.

He'd already wandered past this spot several times.

Until he found out what happened, he wouldn't feel at ease.

Otherwise, he might lose another night's sleep.

“Yep. Finished. That youngster has good talent.”

“Just good?”

“Yeah. What's wrong?”

“Nothing.” Mu Shunan finally exhaled, smiling sincerely for the first time in days. “Just asking. We're all brothers here. Thought I'd check on him.”

At last... Thank goodness!

Apparently Fang Heng's terrifying potential was limited to mysticism.

Mu Shunan told himself he'd frightened himself half to death over nothing.

Nasir sensed something amiss and stared at him. “There's something wrong with you, kid.”

“Come on, don't talk nonsense. I've borrowed yesterday's arena recording. Want to watch it together?”

“Sure. Let's go.”

...

Inside the training room, Fang Heng hadn't even noticed when Nasir left. He redrew all twelve magic arrays one by one, spending a full three hours.

His progress rose to 5.2%.

Each of the twelve arrays trained a different ability. Completing all twelve also awarded a substantial extra improvement.

Since these arrays focused on comprehension, his zombie clones probably couldn't help.

Besides, every practice array was large. The zombies didn't have enough open space to arrange them all.

After some thought, Fang Heng decided to leave his zombie clones AFK grinding mysticism while he personally trained alchemy.

Both could advance together without interfering with each other.

At his current efficiency, he estimated he could max out his alchemy progress in a day and a half.

Not slow at all.

After thinking it over, Fang Heng stood and left the room.

“Mr. Fang Heng.”

Anxi was waiting at the door and stepped forward to welcome him.

“There's an LV:5 arena competition tomorrow afternoon. I've registered you, and your application has been approved.”

“Good.”

Fang Heng nodded and checked the time. “I won't return to the villa tonight. Rent me a training room. I'll rest here, and you can take me to the arena when it's time tomorrow.”

“Certainly. I'll arrange that right away.”

Anxi deeply admired Fang Heng's devotion to cultivation.

Throughout the time she'd known him, she'd never seen him waste a moment. He was either cultivating or on his way to cultivate.

...

The following afternoon.

The large arena in the Seven-Realm Martial Capital.

Led by Anxi, Fang Heng entered the backstage lounge.

Things were somewhat different from his previous arena match.

The atmosphere in the lounge was noticeably tense.

The warriors in the room all studied Fang Heng as he came through the door, with traces of admiration in their eyes.

Elder Meng Hong was notorious for his bad temper and his exacting expectations of the warriors' attitudes.

And this youngster dared show up without arriving two hours early to warm up?

Fang Heng was baffled by their gazes.

What was with those looks?

He'd arrived ahead of time. Why did everyone else appear to be here already?

They'd even started discussing tactics!

Meng Hong turned toward him and paused. “You're Fang Heng?”

“This junior is Fang Heng. Greetings, Elder Meng.”

“Mm.” Meng Hong showed no particular expression. “Sit down. Elder Dilan recommended you to me. He says you're quite strong. I hope you don't make me regret this decision.”

“Thank you for giving me the opportunity, Elder.”

Meng Hong paid him no further attention. “This match is important. Both sides have sent powerful fighters. The opposing team has eight intermediate warriors. Three deserve special attention: two elemental specialists and a close-combat expert named Liao Yun.”

He paused, looked at Fang Heng, and asked, “You're also proficient in close combat?”

Everyone turned toward Fang Heng.

So this was Fang Heng.

Some warriors had heard his name.

Apparently he was a newcomer.

He'd achieved an impressive one-versus-four victory in his first match.

But that was only an LV:1 arena battle. The experienced warriors present merely assumed he was reasonably capable.

As for Liao Yun, he was a particularly troublesome member of the opposing team.

He was extremely strong and difficult to handle even among intermediate warriors. His win rate was high, and he regularly eliminated three or four opponents by himself. Rumor had it he would likely advance to senior warrior next month.

“Yes.”

“Are you confident you can deal with Liao Yun?”

“Yes.”

Fang Heng had no idea who Liao Yun was.

But if the elder was asking, the answer was obviously yes.

After his last arena battle, he had a rough understanding of the warrior ranks.

He could currently crush low-ranked warriors.

By that logic, even if he couldn't crush an intermediate warrior, winning after a hard fight shouldn't be a problem.

The warriors in the room once again gave Fang Heng searching looks.

If the elder weren't present, they'd already be whispering among themselves.

Who knew how capable he was, but he certainly talked big.

“Good. That's the spirit a warrior needs.” To everyone's surprise, Elder Meng Hong praised him, then continued, “I'll leave Liao Yun to you.”

“Understood.”

Fang Heng nodded without further discussion.

Once they'd finished discussing tactics, it was nearly time. Elder Meng Hong led everyone into the arena's waiting area beside the fighting platform.

Every time Fang Heng came here, the arena was filled with noise.

He looked around.

The Noel Clan was clearly taking this competition far more seriously than the last. Its selected fighters had been chosen with care.

With nothing else to do, Fang Heng remotely directed his zombie clones to continue practicing mysticism seals. He adjusted the finer points of their hand movements, testing whether he could improve their efficiency.

Unfortunately...

There was little effect.

The zombies' seals were already irregular, and without mental-power circulation to coordinate them, having the game recognize their training at all was an achievement.

“The match begins.”

Fang Heng raised his head.

Before he'd noticed, the competition had begun.

The Seven-Realm Martial Capital gathered some of the strongest combatants in the universe, but Fang Heng had observed that most elite warriors specialized in elemental disciplines.

That made sense.

Elemental powers were exceptionally effective in combat, offering tremendous destructive power and defensive techniques.

Combined with each race's unique abilities and dimensional level, the result could be far greater than the sum of its parts.
