Chapter 3022

Separate Actions

From Chinese Chapter 3023 · 分头行动

Dean frowned deeply.

Why?

Why hadn't that brat asked for help yet?

Could he be scheming something?

It was plain that the Tongues of Envy were slowly draining the sacred power from his body. The longer the battle went on, the worse things would become for him.

While resisting the negative effect of his sacred power continually draining away, Fang Heng blinked through space once more. Seizing an opening, he slammed another punch into Eseklyna!

“Boom!!!”

Eseklyna burst apart again, exploding into a mass of flesh and blood.

“Hee-hee-hee...”

Fang Heng slowly turned his head toward the right.

Eseklyna had resurrected by some bizarre means, a mocking smile on her face. “It's useless, little boy. Struggle all you like—in Hell, I can resurrect endlessly. You'll never kill me!”

“Hmph!”

Fang Heng snorted and, refusing to believe her, charged at Eseklyna again!

“Hmm... Looks like it's about time.”

Hidden nearby, a Demon Seed skilled at concealing his aura saw that Eseklyna's attention was fully occupied by Fang Heng. He took the Demon Flower he had prepared from his robes.

“Pop!”

The Demon Flower shattered in the Demon Seed's palm.

...

The twenty-ninth level of Hell.

At the edge of a cliff above deep-blue lava.

Zhuang Yufei spread a perception barrier. More than twenty-five Demon Seeds gathered with her and waited quietly for the battle to begin.

“Pop!!”

Suddenly, the Demon Flower in Jing'er's hand shattered.

“Move!”

Almost at the same instant, at Jing'er's roar, twenty-five Hell Demon Seeds burst from the perception barrier and charged toward the lava beyond the cliff!

“Roar!!!!”

Stimulated by the Demon Seeds' auras, the blue lava flowing below erupted.

Tens of thousands of strands of blue lava shot from the pool, weaving the skeletal outline of a gigantic three-headed serpent. Its outer shell of blue magma peeled away in pieces like an eggshell, revealing Echidna's true body.

Echidna, the three-headed giant serpent of Hell!

Detecting the invaders, Echidna immediately sent the Envy Demon Seed a psychic warning. All three serpent heads spewed scorching blue hellfire, sweeping it toward the attacking Demon Seeds!

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

Each Demon Seed had their own tricks, and they swiftly dodged Echidna's assault.

“Kill it! Quickly!”

“Roar!!!”

Echidna roared again.

The tide of blue magma fused with the lower half of Echidna's body erupted upward, surging and bursting in midair.

The flying droplets of magma condensed into hexagonal prisms of deep-blue flame.

In an instant, dense rain of blue crystalline fire fell across the entire area.

The Demon Seeds scattered to dodge.

The five Demon Cores rotating around a stone pillar behind Echidna began spinning rapidly under the influence of blue magma veins covering their surfaces.

Twisted, serpentine markings appeared on each core, triggering high-frequency energy resonance between them and accelerating their rotation even further!

“Pfft! Pfft!”

Several Demon Seeds with weaker bodies were affected by the cores. They spat blood and were hurled backward.

Jing'er's face also paled. Affected by the Demon Cores, his body swayed uncontrollably in midair.

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!!!”

The next instant, the dense blue rain of fire came crashing down onto Jing'er. On striking his body, it crystallized into blue prisms that stabbed deeply into him and knocked him backward!

Jing'er gritted his teeth, veins bulging across his forehead, and shouted, “Zhuang Yufei! Use that!”

“Whoosh!!!”

Zhuang Yufei, who had remained hidden throughout the battle, finally acted. Suppressing the backlash of her demon blood, she flashed into the fray from the right and drove the Demon-Slaying Sword in her hand into Echidna's body.

“Roar!!!!”

Echidna let out an agonized shriek.

The instant the sword entered Echidna's body, dense magical patterns spread outward from the blade in every direction!

It worked!

Zhuang Yufei's eyes brightened, but in the next instant a violent force struck her hand and sent her flying uncontrollably.

“It's weakened! Kill it! Ten minutes! This is our last chance!”

Jing'er shouted, forcibly straightening his weakened body. Enduring the Demon Cores' adverse effects, he charged toward Echidna.

The other Demon Seeds knew it was time to stake their lives as well. Gritting their teeth, they rushed at Echidna.

...

A cavern on Hell's twenty-fifth level.

Eseklyna was toying with Fang Heng when her psychic senses were suddenly disturbed.

Not good!

Sensing trouble on Echidna's side, Eseklyna's expression changed abruptly.

The other Demon Seeds!

They're after the Demon Cores!

What a coincidence!

Right when she was entangled with these Holy Court bastards!

Those Demon Seeds must have planned it all!

Even the Holy Court had been used by them!

Eseklyna didn't believe in coincidences of this sort. Her expression shifting, she lost all desire to continue fighting Fang Heng. She twisted in midair and darted off to one side.

“Whoosh!!!”

Fang Heng tapped the ground and swiftly intercepted her, sneering, “What's wrong? Weren't we playing? I haven't had my fill yet. Why the hurry to run away?”

Eseklyna stared at Fang Heng, her face turning grave.

In their fight, she had felt she possessed the upper hand, but she hadn't used her full strength because she feared the other Holy Court Grand Sage on the battlefield. She had intended to slowly drain Fang Heng with the Tongues of Envy.

Now, however, it seemed he hadn't been using his full strength either.

Had he been stalling for time as well?

The ominous feeling in Eseklyna's heart grew stronger, but before she could think further, Fang Heng attacked first again.

“Whoosh!!!”

Fang Heng instantly surged a step forward.

Shield-Shattering Strike!

A golden streak whistled toward Eseklyna and smashed down fiercely.

“Bang!!!”

The dense mass of Tongues of Envy shielding Eseklyna blocked the attack, bursting in a mixture of dark mist and sacred light.

Using the force of the explosion, Eseklyna leapt backward.

“Come on, don't run! We're not done having fun. Stay and play a little longer.”

Fang Heng pursued her again, deliberately taunting her to draw her hatred.

“Holy Court! This is my territory! Don't push me too far!”

Sensing the danger to her demonic pet Echidna, Eseklyna had no wish to remain entangled with Fang Heng. A ruthless light flashed in her eyes.

“Whoosh!!”

In an instant, Eseklyna's chest burst open. Dense Tongues of Envy extended from her heart, quickly wrapping around her entire body. Two fleshy wings formed from entangled tongues rose from her back, holding her aloft.

Malicious vertical pupils opened one by one along the Tongues of Envy, staring fixedly at Fang Heng.

— End of Chapter 3022 —
