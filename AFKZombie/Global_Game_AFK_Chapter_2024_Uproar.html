Chapter 2024

Uproar

From original Chinese Chapter 2025 · 鸡飞狗跳

Fang Heng clenched his fist lightly.

The Holy Court actually dared to enter the Forest of Bewilderment on its own initiative?

They were practically delivering themselves to their deaths. He'd kill them all in one go!

Behind Fang Heng, the teleportation magic array in the Ghost Tower flashed repeatedly. Zombie clones emerged from the tower after passing through multiple teleportation passages, then staggered toward the valley entrance.

Holy Court!

Let's fight!

…

Not far from the outer edge of the Ghost Tower's territory, several elite Holy Court squads lay in ambush in the dense forest.

After a series of investigations, they'd confirmed that the undead had constructed a Ghost Tower inside the Forest of Bewilderment. They also suspected the tower was closely connected to the earlier attack on the Brighton Empire.

Front-line commander Aorji had ordered the tower's complete destruction.

When Fang Heng had invaded their heartland last time, he'd thrown the entire Holy Court into chaos and even interrupted construction of its teleportation passage. The Holy Court would not allow that to happen again!

This mission involved not only the Holy Court's main elite force but also a small contingent of players.

“It's just ahead.”

Hidden among the low bushes, the Holy Court squad captain observed the area ahead with a wary expression.

The zombies Fang Heng controlled were anything but ordinary. Each creature's individual strength had already surpassed the standard for intermediate-tier games.

Moreover, the Forest of Bewilderment's terrain made it impossible to bring in a large army. The entire force, including the players, numbered only forty.

“Those zombies are extremely tenacious and difficult to handle,” the captain explained. “There's no need to get bogged down fighting them. We'll create an opening. Your job is to seize the chance to destroy the Ghost Tower.”

Hunter, vice president of the Embers Player Guild, nodded.

Embers was the leading large guild among Holy Court-aligned players in intermediate-tier game worlds, ranked number one in that faction.

Normally, the guild would run a mile from this kind of mission.

A direct confrontation with World Lord Fang Heng?

No thanks!

High risk, low reward. There was no reason to get involved.

Other Holy Court-aligned guilds felt the same way. The moment they heard the mission concerned Fang Heng, their interest vanished. The task had sat on the board for half a day without a single player daring to accept it.

In the end, a Holy Court commander had approached Embers personally to request help. The guild had reluctantly agreed.

Every Embers member participating this time was a high-tier player, each stronger than the average. Their backpacks were packed with various explosive devices.

As long as they could enter the valley and get close enough, destroying the Ghost Tower shouldn't be difficult.

Hunter wasn't looking for glory. He just wanted to finish without anything going wrong.

“Our objective is to destroy the passage. We—”

The Holy Court squad captain was about to issue his orders when he noticed something strange in the distance. He frowned.

The Ghost Tower in the valley was giving off unusual fluctuations.

Everyone's heart sank as they turned to stare at it.

“What's that…?”

Zombies!

The horde that had been gathered at the valley entrance was changing. One by one, the zombies began to move, shuffling forward.

It was as though a floodgate had opened. An unending stream of zombies poured out of the valley.

The players were alarmed.

What was happening to the zombies? Had their presence provoked them?

“Everyone, the horde is behaving strangely. We don't know whether it's connected to us, but ignore them. Once we've destroyed the teleportation passage, we withdraw immediately. Understood?”

“Yes!”

At once, the sacred power the Holy Court elites had been suppressing erupted from their bodies.

More than twenty elite fighters charged toward the valley entrance.

The Holy Court had planned this operation carefully. The whole point was a surprise attack!

The burst of holy light blasted away the zombies blocking their path. The Holy Court squad forcibly carved a corridor through the horde.

“Follow them,” Hunter ordered, gesturing to his players.

Huh?!

The moment they entered the valley, Hunter sensed something wrong. The passage inside was even more densely packed with zombies, nearly filled from wall to wall.

There were this many of them?!

“Charge through!”

The elite Holy Court paladin leading the assault roared and drove ahead, using an explosion of sacred power to hack a bloody path through the zombies.

Hunter stopped worrying about it and urged his people onward.

After following the Holy Court squad for more than two hundred meters, he looked up. Half of the Ghost Tower was visible ahead through the dense necromantic mist.

That was…!

Hunter suddenly noticed something. His stomach dropped as his eyes settled on a young man standing beneath the tower.

The figure looked youthful in build and dress, but his face was covered by a demon mask.

Fang Heng!

A dreadful premonition seized Hunter.

“Kill him!”

The elite paladins spotted Fang Heng too. Their leader rushed forward first, shouting, “Hunter! I'll take care of him! You keep going with the mission!”

Scorching streams of sacred light erupted from the paladins, instantly hurling away the zombies around them.

The Holy Court elites used that burst of power to open a passage toward the tower for the players behind them.

“Boss, aren't we going?” one of Hunter's men asked.

“Not yet!”

Hunter stopped the subordinate who was eager to follow and held back from the fight. He felt profoundly uneasy.

Fang Heng was standing perfectly still, as though waiting for the Holy Court fighters to approach.

It could easily be a trap!

The elite squad rapidly closed the distance.

Suddenly, Fang Heng raised his head toward the charging paladins and thrust out his hand.

Bang!

The greatsword strapped to his back exploded apart in an instant!

A pungent smell of blood and a powerful surge of blood vitality struck the Holy Court squad, almost taking their breath away.

Even Hunter, back at the valley entrance, caught the acrid bloody odor and frowned.

BOOM!

An instant later, the paladins found themselves facing a massive crimson curtain of blood!

Hiss! Hiss, hiss!

Dense ranks of blood spears condensed out of it and stabbed forward.

“Watch out! He's a vampire!”

The sheer terror of that blood power struck fear into the paladin captain's heart. Countless crimson spears filled the sky, each carrying an astonishing amount of blood vitality.

Bang! Bang, bang!

The spears smashed through the paladins' outer holy shields in an instant!

— End of Chapter 2018 —

  • Embers guild. 余烬玩家公会 is rendered Embers Player Guild; its vice president is 亨特, rendered Hunter.
  • Zombie ranks. The enemy force is described as a forty-person strike team that includes players, not an entire invading army.
  • Attack imagery. Fang Heng’s blood greatsword is dispersed into blood under his control; the surrounding blood forms projectiles. It is not an ordinary metal blade that explodes.
