Chapter 2960

Entering

From Chinese Chapter 2961 · 进入

[Game Hint: Player has entered coordinates of an unknown world through a spatial jump.]

The jump lasted more than five minutes before a line of game notifications slowly appeared across his retina.

After a brief bout of shaking, the starship gradually steadied.

Ahead, sunlight gradually emerged from the black chaos beyond the starship's reinforced glass windows.

Outside stretched an expanse of lush green grassland.

“Jump complete. Initiating the starship's scheduled inspection procedures...”

“The starship has sustained serious damage and requires immediate maintenance...”

As soon as the jump ended, Dr. Li Zhixuan began reviewing the ship's status on the main control screen, directing the operators to carry out inspections and draw up an emergency repair plan.

Wayne and the other players mostly breathed sighs of relief.

That had been quite a gamble.

The violent shaking during the jump had genuinely made them fear something might go wrong with the ship.

They had finally arrived safely.

And judging by the scenery outside, this place didn't look half bad.

After nearly a month of staring at yellowish sand and dust, the sight of blue skies and white clouds instantly lifted their spirits.

“Running an external environment scan.”

“Signs of life detected in this star sector...”

“Assessing biological threat level... Threat level: None...”

“Atmospheric pressure: 1.1 standard atmospheres...”

Because resources were scarce, the ship carried only the most basic detection equipment. Its capabilities were rudimentary, and its scanning range was extremely limited.

“The environment looks pretty good.” Fang Heng nodded toward Dr. Li Zhixuan. “Let's go outside and take a look first.”

“All right. I'll assess this world's safety rating and work on the ship's repairs. Keep in touch.”

Wayne's group emerged in protective suits and tried jumping up and down on the spot to get a feel for the environment firsthand.

Once Dr. Li Zhixuan confirmed the local air was breathable, everyone put away their protective suits.

The environment here was remarkably similar to that of their own world!

Fang Heng tossed several rune papers forward, releasing Lickers that scattered in every direction to search for usable ores and sources of energy.

“Boss Fang, how about giving us some time off?”

Fang Heng spread out his perception and, after confirming that no threatening native creatures were nearby, nodded. “Mm, fine. Take two days off.”

“Phew! A holiday! We've got a holiday!”

The players finally relaxed completely, sprawling across the grassland in every direction.

They had spent more than half a month sorting ore in that godforsaken place where the sun never shone. At last, they could unwind.

Before long, Dr. Li Zhixuan and a team of researchers disembarked to gather information about the local world.

With her arms folded, Dr. Li Zhixuan felt the gentle breeze brush her face and breathed in the scent of nature.

Standing beside her, Fang Heng asked quietly, “How's the ship?”

“Mm. The damage is about what we expected. If we have all the materials, three days should be enough for the repairs.”

In good spirits, Dr. Li Zhixuan gave a light laugh. “But since you've given us two days off, we'll need five days now.”

“Fair enough.” Fang Heng shrugged. Looking into the distance, he asked, “Any leads on the Ancient Humans?”

“I'm not sure. I don't have enough information. My guess is that whatever inheritance the Ancient Humans left here might be something like ruins...”

After a moment's thought, Dr. Li Zhixuan added, “The Eastern Federation has the detailed records. But since humanity's ancestors once lived here, finding traces of them shouldn't be too difficult.”

...

The starship remained in the new world for a full seven days.

Over those seven days, the Lickers scouted more than half the world.

The planet covered a vast area. The Lickers found wild animals scattered across it, but their combat strength was generally very low. They hadn't formed any large populations and mostly appeared in isolated pockets.

If this were a game world, it probably wouldn't even qualify as a low-level one.

The planet's ore reserves were pitifully small. Most of the scattered deposits were low-grade ores, and energy crystal deposits were practically nonexistent. It almost felt as though someone had already mined the place.

Wayne's team also organized several searches, but unfortunately found nothing.

By now, the starship's damage had been repaired, and work had begun on constructing the next set of compartments and internal systems.

With supplies in short supply, many items couldn't be manufactured directly and had to be redeemed through the Kinsfolk exchange system.

A terrible bargain.

Nor had their searches uncovered any traces of the Ancient Humans.

Fang Heng and Dr. Li Zhixuan discussed the situation, wondering whether the intelligence itself was wrong.

“I've been undercover in the Western Federation for years, so I don't know much about the later research into the Ancient Humans and can't help there. But I can confirm the coordinates are correct. This world's environment also matches the records.”

“Then let's search more carefully for a while longer. Hopefully we'll find something.”

Fang Heng planned to stay a little longer and keep searching while continuing construction and upgrading the starship until it could accept missions from the Kinsfolk system.

He had just left the control room when something occurred to him. He slapped his forehead.

Idiot!

He had suddenly realized something.

He was outside the game right now!

The starship might have lost contact with the outer worlds, but he could use the mark on the back of his hand to return directly to the game!

Then he could contact the Eastern Federation!

Worth a try!

Fang Heng glanced around and closed his eyes.

This time, it felt a little different from his previous trips in and out of the game.

After closing his eyes, he saw a door shrouded in a layer of black mist.

As before, Fang Heng tried pushing it open.

The door opened a crack.

In an instant, thick black mist poured through the gap!

Everything before his eyes dissolved into nothingness.

Had anyone been standing nearby, they would have seen billows of dense black mist rapidly surge from Fang Heng's body until they enveloped him completely and he vanished from sight.

...

After an unknown amount of time, Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

The Death Realm.

He was back.

Fang Heng realized that he had successfully returned to the game.

It seemed to have worked.

Still, the scene he'd witnessed while returning to the game had been a little strange.

Was it because he'd unlocked the 'lock' in the real world?

Lost in thought, Fang Heng raised his head and frowned slightly.

He recalled that his last visit to the Death Realm had been more than a month ago.

Compared with then, the concentration of death aura here had fallen dramatically.

Was it because he'd taken away the Death Realm Pearl?

“Mr. Fang Heng.”

After receiving a report from his clansmen, Notar hurried over with several others to welcome him.

“Notar, it's been a while. How are things in the Death Realm?”

Notar sighed inwardly. “To tell you the truth, things are very bad. Our people have already begun moving into the new world in successive groups.”
