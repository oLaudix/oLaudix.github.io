Chapter 2262

Spirit-Incantation Prayer

From original Chinese Chapter 2263 · 灵咒祷言

"I'll stay behind and try to buy you a little more time." Fang Heng patted Ovi on the shoulder. "If everything goes well, we'll meet at the other end of the tunnel."

"All right."

Ovi looked at him, then extended his hand and offered Fang Heng a peculiar diamond-shaped leaf.

"What's this?"

"The Leaf Clan's Book of Inheritance—the Spirit-Incantation Prayer." Ovi explained, "My father was once an honorary elder of the clan, and I was taught the Divine Tree cultivation inheritance from childhood. This section of the prayer contains teachings about cultivating the Divine Tree. Please keep it safe for me. If anything happens to me, you must deliver it to a Leaf Clan elder."

Fang Heng was intrigued. He looked up. "Why not take it yourself?"

"No. I'm already carrying the Divine Tree sapling." Ovi gave a faint, bitter smile and shook his head. "As you humans say, don't put all your eggs in one basket."

[Hint: Player's current quest—Escort the Divine Tree Sapling—has changed. New quest name: Ovi's Entrustment.]

Quest Name: Ovi's Entrustment.

Quest Difficulty: SSS.

Quest Description: The Prayer-Curse Temple is facing an extreme crisis. Ovi has been entrusted with escorting the Divine Tree sapling to safety. You and Ovi must separately safeguard the Spirit-Incantation Prayer and Divine Tree sapling and escape together.

Quest Requirement 1: Ensure that Ovi and the Divine Tree sapling return safely to the Leaf Clan's settlement.

Quest Requirement 2: Personally escort the Spirit-Incantation Prayer out of the Prayer-Curse Temple and deliver it to any Leaf Clan elder.

Quest Rewards: Leaf Clan favorability, Ovi favorability, Leaf Clan reputation and contribution, Tree Spirit reputation, and eligibility to join the Leaf Clan.

"All right. Leave it to me."

Fang Heng turned the diamond-shaped leaf over in his hands and casually asked, "How was this thing made?"

"It's a special way of recording written information. Only members of the Leaf Clan can activate it with psychic power. Ordinary people can't read it."

"Oh? Is that so?"

[Item: Spirit-Incantation Prayer (Rune Leaf).]

Description: A special rune-type item under the effect of a low-tier seal.

Description: This special item is guaranteed to drop upon death.

[Hint: A low-tier seal has been detected on the rune. With Advanced Alchemy and Intermediate Sealing Studies mastered, the player may attempt to decipher it. Success probability: 100%.]

"Oh," Fang Heng said after glancing at the notification.

"Looks like some kind of seal. Mind if I give it a try?"

Before Ovi could respond, Fang Heng poured natural energy into the leaf.

The leaf's runes glowed faintly, and Fang Heng broke the simple underlying seal almost instantly.

"Humm..."

A pale cyan beam shone from the leaf.

It projected onto the wall ahead, where rows of recorded text immediately appeared.

"This..."

Ovi stared at Fang Heng activating the Spirit-Incantation Prayer so effortlessly, momentarily at a loss for words.

He actually...

Well, it wasn't as though this was the first time Fang Heng had astonished him.

Fang Heng casually put the leaf away.

The Tree Spirits' understanding of alchemy and sealing techniques was terribly primitive. Breaking through their defenses was child's play.

As far as he was concerned, their methods of concealing information were equally crude.

"Get going. I'll take care of everything here."

"All right. Be careful!"

Fang Heng watched Ovi and several Leaf Clan members escort the sapling into the underground tunnel. Then he looked toward the passage entrance, already piled high with construction rubble by his zombies.

Given the Tree Spirits' abilities, breaking through that wall of sand and soil wouldn't be difficult.

So...

He needed to make things a little harder for them.

Fang Heng ordered the surrounding Lickers over and had them bury the entrance to Ovi's underground passage completely.

While the zombies worked, he approached a smooth rock wall, took out the Spirit-Incantation Prayer rune again, and injected psychic power to project its contents onto the stone.

A great many zombie clones shuffled toward the wall and began reading the projected text and illustrations.

Ordinary books—historical records, journals, magazines, and all sorts of other reading material—could be read through zombie clones. But doing so provided no skill experience, and the accumulated information flooded Fang Heng's mind in disjointed fragments.

Reading a single book wasn't so bad. Once there was too much information, however, his head started buzzing.

When all that scattered information poured into him at once, most of it wouldn't stick. If he needed it later, he often had to expend mental effort searching through his memories to retrieve it.

Besides, these memories rarely became firmly established and usually faded away entirely within a few hours.

But using zombie clones to read books that granted skill experience was undoubtedly the best option!

Before long, game notifications began appearing one after another across his vision.

[Hint: Player's zombie clone (normal form) has completed part of the reading. Player's understanding of Spirit-Incantation Prayer +2...]

Excellent. He could leave the zombie clones to farm experience on their own.

Fang Heng turned to look at the tunnel entrance, now completely sealed with sand and earth.

From the other side, he could faintly hear the rustling of digging.

It seemed the Tree Spirits outside had arrived.

Fang Heng narrowed his eyes.

Perfect. Time to give them another difficulty increase!

...

On the far side of the passage buried beneath a great mass of rock and earth, the three Tree Spirit clans waited while their people cleared the obstruction.

Every other collapsed section of the Prayer-Curse Temple had already been excavated and thoroughly searched.

They had found nothing.

Only this section on the third basement level remained!

The missing Divine Tree sapling had to be here!

As the clansmen cast Wood Swamp spells, the packed sand and rubble blocking the passage slowly sank into the ground.

They had already cleared more than ten meters of the corridor.

Yet the path ahead remained choked with earth.

An ordinary collapse couldn't possibly have packed a passage this far back.

Someone had clearly blocked it deliberately!

Suddenly, two Tree Spirits casting spells near the blockage felt their pupils contract.

What was that?

Faint specks of light had appeared in the blocked passage ahead.

Huh? Spatial fluctuations!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The next instant, Lickers sprang out of the spatial distortions and lunged at the nearest Tree Spirits!

"Watch out!"

"Shhk! Shhk, shhk!"

Arrows wrapped in natural energy shot forward and struck the Lickers bursting from Secondary Spatial Projection, knocking them back!

Lickers!

Them again!

The moment they saw the creatures, the Tree Spirits realized a human was causing trouble behind the scenes.

They immediately returned fire with their archery techniques and joined forces to repel the attack.

Fortunately, their combined strength was more than enough.

The Lickers could not inflict any meaningful damage. Before long, they were dragged beneath the ground and buried completely.

However, hundreds of Lickers continued emerging from Secondary Spatial Projection, completely interrupting the Tree Spirits' excavation.
