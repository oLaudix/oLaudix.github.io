Chapter 2961

Transport

From Chinese Chapter 2962 · 输送

The situation in the Death Realm had been steadily worsening of late.

The war between the Death Realm and the Calamities was still raging.

Under the Calamities' attacks, the armies of the Death Realm's four great empires were rapidly pulling back their defensive lines. Worse still, as the environment deteriorated, the death aura was growing so thin that Nether Stones could no longer even form.

More and more members of the Nether race were choosing to migrate to the new world.

After listening to Notar's account, Fang Heng rubbed his chin.

This really was difficult.

The Calamities in the Death Realm were on an entirely different level from those he'd encountered during previous Kinsfolk missions.

One was an aggregate of shattered Ouroboros Seed fragments; the other was a complete Ouroboros Seed, accompanied by a Cultivator no less.

Then again, they'd previously tried using the Kinsfolk defensive line to attack an SS-grade insect nest.

The results had been fairly good.

Could the same trick work in the Death Realm?

They were all Ouroboros Seeds. If he installed enhancement devices here, could he weaken the Ouroboros Seed's power?

Previously, the Death Realm had been so saturated with death aura that any technological devices brought inside would quickly corrode.

But the concentration of death aura had now fallen by more than tenfold.

Fang Heng scratched his head.

Maybe it was worth a try.

One step at a time.

For now, the starship problem came first.

“I understand. I'll figure something out. Take me to the teleportation passage.”

“Very well.”

Soon, Fang Heng returned through Abe Akaya and used Abe Akaya's teleportation to enter the Vampire Apocalypse.

Then Fang Heng found Chen Yu in his friends list and tried sending him a message.

“Boss Fang!”

Chen Yu had recently been transferred to handle day-to-day affairs in the Vampire Apocalypse. Receiving a sudden message from Fang Heng startled him, and he immediately made contact.

Fang Heng was always as elusive as a divine dragon, showing his head but never his tail. And now he'd actually contacted him first?

This had to be something big!

Before long, guided by the vampires, Chen Yu reached the Vampire Temple and saw Fang Heng sitting on the Vampire Throne.

They hadn't met in more than a year.

Back then, Fang Heng had been nothing more than a newcomer to the Zombie Apocalypse.

Now he'd reached heights Chen Yu couldn't even hope to attain.

“Brother, I need an urgent favor. Contact your superiors for me. Tell them the person 'Blue Eyes' asked me to protect is safe. We've traveled together aboard a starship and entered that world, but we haven't found any traces of the Ancient Humans yet. We need their help with the next steps.”

“Huh?! Boss Fang, what are you talking about?”

Just as he had a year ago, Chen Yu scratched his head in bewilderment.

A starship?

A world? Ancient Humans?

What the hell was all this? He couldn't understand a word!

“It's fine. Report to your superiors and repeat exactly what I said. Pass it up the chain of command. Someone will understand. Quickly, now. I'll wait for your news.”

“Understood, Boss Fang. I'll pass the message along right away.”

...

The latest news about the Ancient Humans exploration project was soon passed from Chen Yu's superiors up through successive levels until it reached the Eastern Federation's highest leadership.

The Eastern Federation leadership was just as dumbfounded. They immediately convened an emergency meeting and summoned the relevant departments to form a joint special task force.

The task force had questions swirling through everyone's heads.

Fang Heng had already entered that world?

A starship? A Western Federation starship?

Then what role had the Western Federation played in all this?

Set the confusion aside for now.

In truth, the Eastern Federation hadn't expected the Ancient Humans project to progress anywhere near this quickly.

The first step was supposed to be acquiring the Kinsfolk star map.

After obtaining it, they would still have to decipher it, analyze the coordinates, find a means of interstellar travel, and finally assemble an expedition to investigate the target world.

Good grief. The Federation was still stuck on the first step with no progress at all. Not long ago, they'd merely heard from Su Guantong that she'd encountered Fang Heng while carrying out the star-map acquisition mission.

Since the two sides had gotten along fairly well in the past, they'd decided to ask Fang Heng for help.

So what was going on?

How had Fang Heng already flown Dr. Li Zhixuan to that world after only half a month?

The team originally responsible for the project was even more baffled.

With news from the Western Federation tightly blocked, the Eastern Federation had only heard that Fang Heng and Dr. Li Zhixuan were stranded in an outer star system and unable to return.

The Eastern Federation was powerless to do anything beyond the planet, so all they could do was wait.

Then Chen Yu's message arrived. They came to the meeting in a daze, only to discover Fang Heng had already established contact with the Federation's leadership.

Once the appointments were settled, task force leader Liu Haiyang immediately gathered the team and set them to work.

“We can't reach Su Guantong for now. She's probably still in the Western Federation's star-map research facility, and I doubt she can get away to contact us.”

“First, let's find a way to speak with Fang Heng and establish the situation. Also, grant Chen Yu special approval to join the task force as deputy leader.”

The task force leader rubbed his temples and thought for a moment. “I'll enter the Vampire game world immediately and try to coordinate with Fang Heng in person.”

With a preliminary plan in place, Liu Haiyang hurried into the Vampire Apocalypse with Chen Yu.

By now, Zone Five of the Vampire Apocalypse had become a safe area under the control of a single vampire faction.

It was jointly administered by the Eastern Federation and the vampires.

The vampires living there cultivated as usual. Following review by the Council of Elders, a small number could be selected for advancement and then travel through teleportation passages into other game worlds for various trials. The place had practically become a miniature headquarters.

But the vampires enforced such strict loyalty screening that even players with vampire bloodlines rarely entered the upper ranks.

For lower-level players, meanwhile, the entire Vampire Apocalypse was practically paradise.

With both the Federation and the vampires maintaining everyday security, its crime rate was even lower than that of the normal world.

At the Vampire Temple, Liu Haiyang and most of the task force met Fang Heng again.

Once Fang Heng confirmed that they had built a starship and successfully jumped into the ancestral world at the ancient coordinates, the entire task force fell silent.

All right, Su Guantong had indeed asked Fang Heng to help contact Dr. Li Zhixuan, but nobody had told them to build an entire starship and fly straight to the Ancient Humans' coordinates!

“I'm sorry. This news is just so... uh... so far beyond anything we imagined that we can't quite take it in...”

Liu Haiyang composed himself and slowly organized his thoughts. “Fang Heng, cooperation between you and the Eastern Federation has always gone smoothly. I don't want to hide anything from you, but we hope you'll keep the information we're about to share confidential.”

“Of course. Go ahead.”

“According to the latest information we've obtained, the world at the Ancient Humans' coordinates is connected to humanity's ancestors, the Titans. Our research indicates that the Titan race faced extinction. To preserve the spark of humanity's survival, they chose to scatter the human bloodlines of their various tribes across different worlds.”

— End of Chapter 2961 —
