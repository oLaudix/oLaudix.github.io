Chapter 2124

Comparison

From original Chinese Chapter 2125 · 比对

“Not yet. We've compared their appearance against every life-form in our database. According to the analysis, the closest match is...” His subordinate cautiously glanced at the Lickers fighting nearby. “Human.”

Human?!

“Human... They're actually human...”

Ji Haiting froze at the answer, repeating the word under his breath.

When he raised his head again, his face was filled with delight.

“Good! Excellent!” He gestured toward his escort. “Capture one alive. I want more accurate biological data.”

“Understood, Director Ji.”

The guards immediately stepped forward and combined their psychic powers to lift a Licker engaged with a mechanized soldier into the air.

Unable to keep its balance, the trapped Licker thrashed wildly.

Ji Haiting watched with his own eyes as the Licker, whose arms had been severed by a semi-mechanized soldier's high-crystal ion cutting blades, rapidly grew two new limbs in midair!

His eyes gleamed with excitement and anticipation.

The vitality of biological genetic modification!

Bzz, bzz...

A dark-golden mesh of psychic energy gradually formed around the Licker, sealing it firmly within.

The creature struggled endlessly but couldn't break through its psychic cage.

Three psychic modification specialists worked together to float the cage backward, successfully completing the capture.

“Director Ji, can we prepare to evacuate?”

“There's no hurry.”

Ji Haiting remained where he was, watching the Lickers fight and observing their terrifying regenerative powers from a safe distance.

Then he spotted a figure emerging from behind the horde. His eyelids twitched, and astonishment filled his eyes.

“Fang Heng? How could you be...”

“Heh. Hello again, Mr. Ji. I didn't expect us to meet again so soon.” Fang Heng nodded in greeting. “You left rather quickly last time. I still had quite a few questions I wanted to ask you.”

“Fang Heng, you've surprised me greatly!”

After his initial shock, Ji Haiting quickly regained his composure.

“These creatures under your control possess extraordinary vitality. Let me guess—genetic modification? Their regenerative ability far surpasses that of mechanically modified life-forms! I've investigated your background. You're different from those people who call themselves guardians of nature. You're not like them.”

Ji Haiting regarded Fang Heng appreciatively and continued, “I've always had a good eye for people. From the moment we met, I knew you were capable! We could work together. Just as I proposed before, by combining living genetic modification with specialized psychic modifications, we could create the future!”

[Game Hint: Special Character—Ji Haiting appreciates you and has once again invited you to cooperate. If you accept, you will join the Wanling Technology Corporation faction.]

“Is that so?” Fang Heng glanced at the notification and shook his head. “Unfortunately, we can't work together.”

“Why not?”

“You haven't been honest with me.”

Ji Haiting paused, then burst into laughter. “Hahaha! Fang Heng, honesty is relative. There are indeed some things I can't conveniently discuss, but you'll learn about them eventually. Besides, do you really think the Tree Whisperer Alliance has been completely honest with you? At least I'm always generous to my friends. Ask around.”

“True enough. But your timing is unfortunate.”

“Hmm?”

Ji Haiting's expression changed abruptly as Fang Heng's figure twisted several times in the air and gradually disappeared.

Spatial fluctuations?

Not good!

Ji Haiting's heart tightened!

Whoosh!

In the next instant, Fang Heng reappeared directly in front of him!

Right in his face!

Fang Heng gazed coldly at him.

Less than half a meter separated them!

Crackle... crackle!

Tiny sparks of electricity spilled from Ji Haiting's body.

Fang Heng felt an invisible force pushing him away, preventing him from approaching any closer.

Clack, clack, clack...

The surrounding semi-mechanized soldiers aimed their weapons at Fang Heng in unison, while Ji Haiting's psychic guards locked onto him with their psychic power.

“Stop! All of you, lower your weapons! You will not show Mr. Fang such disrespect!”

Ji Haiting ordered them to stand down and turned to Fang Heng. “There's no need for things to come to this, Fang Heng. Truly. What is it you've come here to obtain?”

“I hear Wanling Technology has been collecting ruins containing Divine Tree offshoots. I want to find them.”

“And your reason?”

“That's not something I can tell you.”

“Is that so... It seems there's still a lack of trust between us.” Ji Haiting sighed inwardly and shook his head. “We can still work together. My offer will always remain open. Contact me whenever you've thought it over. And one last warning: the Federation forces outside won't let you go so easily. Your genetically modified creatures are certainly strong, but we aren't entirely ignorant of genetic modification ourselves...”

“Outside?”

“Goodbye, Fang Heng. I've never believed in the Tree Spirits' so-called inheritance or faith. I believe every kind of power—mechanical modification, genetic enhancement to the limit, the expansion and activation of psychic and mental abilities, even the so-called natural power of the Tree Spirits—is simply another manifestation of power. Clinging to those supposed beliefs is nothing but cowardice and stupidity.”

As he spoke, faint spatial ripples appeared around Ji Haiting once more.

Whoosh!

In the next instant, Ji Haiting and more than a dozen psychically modified guards vanished through spatial transition.

Spatial transition again!

And that electrical barrier just now!

What a nuisance!

Fang Heng frowned.

The remaining mechanized guards immediately aimed at him and opened fire.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

A dense barrage of electrical attacks swept toward Fang Heng once again!

He stepped forward into a secondary spatial projection.

After evading the volley of beam-weapon fire, Fang Heng emerged again behind the semi-mechanized soldiers.

Time to finish this quickly!

Clang!

The divine greatsword appeared in Fang Heng's hand.

Whoosh!

He swept it forward, the blade whistling through the air!

High-crystal ion cutting blades could slice through most metals with ease.

But they were utterly useless against a divine artifact bearing the 'Indestructible' effect!

Bang! Bang!

Two mechanized soldiers were sent flying by the greatsword and slammed heavily into the wall behind them.

These guys were even more troublesome than the Holy Court's armored turtles!

Fang Heng grumbled inwardly, stepped forward, and fixed his gaze on a semi-mechanized guard directly ahead.

— End of Chapter 2124 —
