Chapter 2193

Coordinates

From original Chinese Chapter 2194 · 坐标

Fang Heng immediately unfolded the Tree Spirit map of the Spirit-Prayer Temples.

Several things had changed.

Numerous coordinates now appeared clearly on its surface.

[Hint: The player has acquired coordinates for the Spirit-Prayer Temples.]

Fang Heng examined the marked locations.

There were nineteen Spirit-Prayer Temples on the map.

One was marked with a special symbol.

It was the Spirit-Prayer Temple within the Tree Spirit Council of Elders, and the largest of them all.

He was currently very far from the Council's temple.

The nearest temple, though…

Fang Heng made a rough estimate.

In bat form, he could probably reach it within three hours.

Should he visit the nearest Spirit-Prayer Temple first?

After giving it some thought, Fang Heng made a quick decision.

Let's go!

Time was limited, and proceeding step by step would take far too long.

He'd visit a Spirit-Prayer Temple and try making contact with the Tree Spirits of the Inner World!

“Everyone, now that the Tree Spirits are so much stronger, there's a good chance they'll consider us invaders. We need to be especially careful.”

Ji Haiting nodded in complete agreement.

“Let's split up. I obtained a map showing the Tree Spirits' Spirit-Prayer Temples earlier. I'll follow it and investigate, and try to establish contact with the Tree Spirits. Hopefully I'll discover something.”

“All right. We'll build a temporary camp here. Fang Heng, please be careful.”

“Mm.”

“Brother Fang Heng.”

Klevi approached shyly and offered him a leaf-shaped crystal. “Take this. We can contact each other in an emergency.”

Fang Heng accepted it, puzzled. “What's this?”

“A tool the elves use for long-distance psychic links. It can transmit images as well as voices. Its power was suppressed in the outside world, but the Inner World is rich in natural energy, so it works better here.”

Klevi explained, “The leaf crystal needs to be recharged regularly. There's not much energy left, so save it for emergencies.”

“Mm. All right.”

Fang Heng put the crystal away.

At least they now had a way to communicate in emergencies.

After discussing their plans, Gu Qingzhu stayed behind to protect the others, guard the teleportation passage, explore the immediate vicinity, and help Ji Haiting construct the camp.

Meanwhile, Abe Akaya would continue spreading over a certain area of the outside world to recover its strength rapidly.

The blockade network could no longer detect its energy fluctuations, but the Federation was bound to notice a sudden explosion of tree growth.

Ji Haiting would try to conceal the evidence of Abe Akaya's expansion and keep the Federation calm.

After all, Fang Heng had no desire to turn the Federation against him in the outside world.

They agreed to meet in person every four hours to exchange information.

Once the final details were settled, Fang Heng transformed into a bat and flew toward the nearest Spirit-Prayer Temple.

…

The Tree Spirits.

The Leaf Clan.

As a withered branch of the Divine Tree slowly revived, the teleportation array began glowing emerald again.

The Tree Spirits kneeling in a circle around it wore expressions of unprecedented reverence.

They had no idea that Fang Heng had helped them break the Spirit Domain blockade.

They believed it was the result of their own desperate struggle.

The blessing of the God of Nature.

Vannis looked toward Senzo, visibly moved. “Grand Elder.”

“Mm.”

After thousands of years of waiting, the Tree Spirits had finally reached this day.

Senzo stared ahead and drew a deep breath. “Begin.”

“Activate the teleportation!”

The ritual commenced.

The Tree Spirits poured their energy into the magic array together.

A teleportation passage condensed at the center of the ritual.

Senzo stepped through first.

They had returned to the Inner World.

A world dominated by forests of giant trees.

The rich breath of nature washed over them.

“Grand Elder, is this our homeland?”

Many of the Tree Spirits had spent their entire lives in the outside world. Having never entered the Inner World, they were deeply moved by the sight.

“Mm.”

Senzo gazed at the luxuriant forest, nostalgia for the past flickering in his eyes.

A moment later, his gaze became resolute.

“If I remember correctly, there should be a teleportation passage nearby. Follow me. We must report back to the Tree Spirit Council of Elders.”

“Yes, sir!”

Vannis followed Senzo, but doubts stirred in his heart.

Report to the Council of Elders?

Had the Grand Elder received some kind of order from the Council?

Meanwhile, the Leaf Clan was not alone. With the blockade removed, the other Tree Spirit clans also realized their suppression had ended and began returning to the Inner World by their own means.

Almost every clan's first objective was to report to the Council of Elders.

An hour later…

Vannis and Senzo's group arrived before a towering giant tree.

A spire formed entirely from living wood stretched into the clouds.

The Tree Tower.

A special Tree Spirit building that combined a teleportation passage, watchpost, regional defenses, and an emergency communications relay.

Now, however, its exterior had yellowed and decayed, and fine creeping vines covered its surface.

Senzo led his followers to the entrance at its base, where they all looked to him questioningly.

“The Tree Tower…”

Senzo murmured to himself.

It looked as though nobody had maintained it in a very long time.

Even the Tree Spirit guards who should have been stationed at the entrance were nowhere to be seen.

This didn't look good.

Having been cut off from the Inner World for so long, Senzo had harbored an ominous suspicion all along.

Now his unease grew stronger.

“Let's go inside.”

Senzo stepped forward.

The wooden door slowly opened before them.

Creeeak…

The Tree Tower was clean inside, but there was no sign of recent activity.

Senzo said nothing as he led his clansmen up the staircase on the right.

They eventually reached the entrance to the top level.

The Wood Clan guards who should have been keeping watch from the tower's heights had, over the long years, turned into two pieces of rotting wood.

Fresh vines had long since begun growing from their decayed remains.

Everyone fell silent at the sight.

“Grand Elder, this…”

Without speaking, Senzo passed the remains and entered the teleportation hall. After looking around, he shook his head gently.

“The teleportation passage is completely unusable.”

The central network that once controlled all Tree Spirit teleportation passages no longer responded at all.

That meant every teleportation passage the Tree Spirits had built across the Inner World was now unusable.

“This place has been abandoned for a long time. The main teleportation network isn't responding to energy at all. Come—we'll travel to the Council of Elders directly. Quickly.”

“Understood!”
