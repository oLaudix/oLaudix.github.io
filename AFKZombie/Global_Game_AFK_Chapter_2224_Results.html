From original Chinese Chapter 2225 · 效果

What?!

Whoosh!

Across the arena, a semi-mechanized soldier who had just drawn his gun felt an overwhelming force slam into his right side. His pupils contracted as he was thrown helplessly through the air.

BANG!

A heavy impact rang out.

In the third-floor observation room, everyone fell silent.

They were stunned beyond words!

Nobody had imagined the match would end so quickly!

The instant it started, an invisible psychic force sent both semi-mechanized soldiers flying in opposite directions, slamming them into the reinforced glass.

It was a complete rout!

After only a few hours of training, the Psychic Studies soldiers could utterly overpower their mechanized counterparts!

With a mere wave of the hand, they'd incapacitated both opponents before either could properly draw a weapon.

Standing in the arena, Hu Haiyu watched the two Federation soldiers struggle back to their feet. An unprecedented feeling of exhilaration swept through him.

Incredible!

The practical power of his psychic manipulation had increased by more than threefold!

Previously, lifting something weighing twice as much as himself had been about his absolute limit.

Now it felt effortless.

With a casual upward motion of his hand, he lifted one of the semi-mechanized soldiers clean off the ground and held him suspended in midair.

“Looks like the fight's already over.”

As long as he focused his psychic power, the mechanized soldiers were no match for him!

From the observation gallery, the others watched a mechanized soldier floating helplessly in the air. Their expressions turned peculiar. More than a few stole glances at Luobuer.

The mechanized soldiers had been completely crushed!

What happened to the new chips' supposedly powerful resistance to psychic attacks?

Nobody could see any evidence of it!

Luobuer stared down at the field, his eyes filled with disbelief.

What was going on?

Had the new-generation implant chips failed to interfere with Psychic Studies at all?

No—that wasn't quite right!

The chips were interfering. Their effect was simply far too limited.

They could react reasonably well to a straightforward psychic-piercing attack.

But Hu Haiyu's attack had worked indirectly on the soldiers' bodies by projecting and manipulating psychic force externally.

That largely bypassed the implanted chips' defenses.

“I think that's enough to establish the result.”

Delighted by the obvious increase in the Psychic Studies soldiers' strength, Minister Lei turned to Luobuer. “Shall we let them put on their armor and try again?”

Luobuer looked at Lei, then at the two Psychic Studies practitioners below. “Congratulations, Minister Lei. I think we should. It would be useful to see where their limits lie.”

Minister Lei nodded. “All right.”

Three minutes later…

The Federation's semi-mechanized soldiers returned wearing powered armor.

Less than a minute into the second match, Hu Haiyu and the other Psychic Studies practitioner had both armored soldiers dangling in midair. They repeatedly smashed them against the walls, filling the arena with booming impacts.

Ji Haiting could hardly believe his eyes.

In the past, producing an effect like that had required more than twenty Psychic Studies practitioners acting together!

Even then, the odds of failure were high.

The more people involved, the easier it was for their joint psychic manipulation to fall into disorder.

But now just two of them were enough…

Luobuer was genuinely convinced.

A breakthrough like this was wonderful news for the entire military department!

“Minister Lei, Chairman Ji, I'll report the testing results to the leadership personally and recommend immediate development. Contact me whenever you need anything. Our mechanization department will give you its full cooperation.”

“Thank you. We appreciate it.”

Ji Haiting's eyes shone with excitement.

Luobuer's promise meant they were about to receive the Federation's fullest support. In the future, they could mobilize Federation resources far more freely, making practically everything easier.

…

Five days later.

An underground research facility on a secret island provided by the Federation.

Klevi and her companions stood before a magic array, their chants rising and falling in rhythm.

Specks of blue-green light slowly appeared above the array.

Abe Akaya's roots emerged from within it, reaching outward and burrowing into the surrounding ground.

Success.

Klevi opened her eyes and smiled as she saw the roots extending from the continuously operating array.

Abe Akaya's branching vines spread through the formation and entered the World of Forests.

The roots drove deep into the earth and rapidly expanded outward.

Vines and branches sprang from the floor and soon covered the entire underground facility.

Abe Akaya had successfully taken root in the World of Forests and was beginning to sprout and grow.

The energy it slowly absorbed in this world would allow it to sustain the magic-array passage on its own. There would be no need for anyone to keep operating it manually.

Fang Heng soon established a psychic communication link with Abe Akaya as well.

After regrowing twice, it was still in a weakened state and needed to absorb a great deal of energy to recover fully.

Relying on its own absorption was still too slow.

It would be best to get some more Leaves of Life from the Tree Spirits to accelerate its recovery.

As he was thinking about this, Mo Jiawei's voice sounded in his earpiece. “Boss Fang, the Federation people have arrived. I'm taking them to the second-floor conference room.”

“Got it. I'll be right there.”

On the research facility's second floor…

Minister Lei entered the conference room escorted by a group of Federation guards.

Over the past few days, Ji Haiting had helped reconcile Fang Heng and the Federation. They had put their previous grievances aside and begun cooperating closely.

Their arrangement, however, was somewhat unusual.

Ji Haiting acted as the intermediary.

Strictly speaking, Fang Heng had no direct relationship with the Federation. All cooperation passed through Ji Haiting.

It made things a little more cumbersome, but also left everyone room to maneuver.

Even if the Dependents returned in force, the Federation wouldn't necessarily have to break with them openly.

With the Tree Spirits posing such a serious threat, the Federation's first priority was survival.

“We're producing the relevant potions as quickly as we can. With Federation resources behind us, output can gradually increase. We estimate it'll take a month to equip the entire Psychic Studies Corps. After that, we'll keep supplying them until all Federation troops have access.”

“As for the second-stage blood-energy infusion…” Fang Heng shook his head. “The timeframe is too tight. This isn't something we can speed up just by throwing resources at it. Extending it across the whole military will take a long time.”
