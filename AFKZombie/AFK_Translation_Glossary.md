**Version:** 10 (19 October 2026)  
**Authority:** Names and wording already agreed in *our own* English translation. Published third-party English translations are **not** an authority.

## Unresolved source spellings and roles

- The Chinese spellings for Georgi, Okney, Chrono, and Xidi should be added only after verifying their source chapters, but their *English* spelling is fixed from the internal translation audit.
- **Chapter 2263:** “Root Clan Elder Krono” contradicts the surrounding Wood Clan association. Check the Chinese original before changing the affiliation; the name correction alone does not resolve it.
- Do not rely on third-party English editions to decide character identities or names.

## Batch rules

1. Before translating a new chapter, load this small glossary and keep the spellings exactly as written. Do not independently re-romanize already established names.
2. For a name not in this list, use the Chinese characters and context to check whether it is an alias, title, abbreviation, or recurring known character before assigning a new English spelling.
3. When a *new* name or important term is confidently introduced, append one concise row describing who/what it is. Keep the list short; ordinary nouns need no glossary entry.
4. If a conflict appears, flag it rather than silently substituting a new name. Changes to a locked name require an explicit decision.
5. Save the updated glossary in the next chapter ZIP as `AFK_Translation_Glossary.md`, plus keep a versioned master copy. Reuse the existing HTML reader CSS/formatting unchanged.
6. Preserve the Chinese-original content and Chinese dialogue register; do not shorten chapters or invent story details. Translation-style refinements must never change proper names or formatting.
