Chapter 3117

Methods

From Chinese Chapter 3118 · 手段

At the bottom of the labyrinthine cavern...

Nasir studied the magic arrays carved into the rock walls for a while, then frowned deeply with a bitter expression.

They were finished.

The arrays were too advanced. They were already at the law level, far beyond his ability to break.

Something powerful enough to keep an evil god sealed inside also kept Nasir sealed in with it.

"Hiss, hiss-hiss..."

Suddenly hearing a sound behind him, Nasir's heart jumped. He turned toward the Evil Embryo slowly stirring on the cavern floor.

"Hiss, hiss-hiss..."

The rotting flesh slowly expanded and contracted, while the yellowish eyeballs embedded in the mass opened and closed in succession.

The moment Nasir met their gaze, an agitated sensation rose from the depths of his heart.

This was bad!

A mind-affecting spell.

Nasir forced himself to look away and swallowed a pill. He concentrated on protecting his Sea of Consciousness and warned, "Fang Heng, be careful. I think that thing is waking up."

"Mm."

Fang Heng also sensed the illusory effect emanating from the eyes. But his Omniscient Eye granted such high-level immunity to illusions that he lost focus for only an instant before becoming completely immune to the negative effect.

Even so, he didn't dare be careless.

This could be troublesome.

The Evil Embryo was still some distance away. In theory, the farther an illusion traveled, the weaker its effects became.

He normally ignored illusion effects entirely, yet this one had briefly dazed him.

If the Evil Embryo came closer, the combined illusory assault from all those eyes might overwhelm him with negative effects.

[Hint: Player is affected by a negative pressure effect. Agility attribute partially reduced.]

"Hiss, hiss-hiss, hiss-hiss..."

As the eyeballs covering the rotting flesh repeatedly opened and closed, the blood vessels protruding from the mass began writhing and twisting, pulling free of the surrounding space.

Dark shadows started spreading from the Evil Embryo's enormous body.

The shadows gathered and slowly approached the two men in the corner of the cavern.

They were finished!

The Evil Embryo had definitely awakened!

Seeing this, a fine layer of cold sweat appeared on Nasir's forehead.

Simply resisting the illusion's negative effects took most of his concentration. How was he supposed to fight at the same time?

Besides, the cavern wasn't particularly large once the Evil Embryo's massive body was taken into account. The two men had very little room to dodge.

The dark tendrils advanced slowly, but the power within them made Nasir's heart quiver with dread.

"Brother Fang Heng, looks like we'll have to fight for our lives."

Nasir glanced at Fang Heng, intending to say something else, but suddenly froze.

Hm?

Fang Heng didn't seem affected by the creature's illusion. He stood there staring directly at the Evil Embryo, then suddenly raised a hand.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!"

Elemental spheres of different colors condensed and shot toward the dark tendrils gathering around the Evil Embryo!

Huh!?

The dark tendrils seemed to exist in a different dimension from the elemental spheres. When the two met, nothing happened; the spheres simply passed straight through!

"Boom! Boom, boom, boom!"

The elemental spheres struck the mountain of rotten flesh and exploded, tearing large, bloody wounds across its surface.

Small pieces of flesh splattered onto the ground, instantly corroding tiny black pits into the rock.

But those injuries were insignificant to a creature of the Evil Embryo's size.

"Glug, glug, glug..."

The creature made a series of wet gurgling sounds as the wounds on its body rapidly healed.

Fang Heng's expression grew grave.

Those might have been ordinary elemental spheres, but with his Grandmaster-level Elemental Studies and immense mental strength, they were extremely powerful. A direct hit would seriously injure even a high-ranking warrior.

"Hiss, hiss-hiss..."

The dark shadows continued creeping toward them.

"Fall back first."

Seeing Fang Heng's attack fail, Nasir retreated into the narrow passage they'd used to enter.

But the dark tendrils had already locked onto the two men and continued their slow advance.

Fortunately, the tendrils weren't fast.

Nasir's face grew solemn. He pressed his palms together, rapidly formed a seal before him, and pushed it toward the ground.

"Hum..."

A defensive light curtain rose and enveloped them both.

In the next instant, however, the approaching dark tendrils ignored the curtain entirely, passing directly through it and continuing toward them.

They were doomed!

Nasir's face changed abruptly.

He had nothing left.

He didn't know what these tendrils were, but none of his techniques affected them.

Their only other option was to attack the Evil Embryo itself. But the monster was terrifying; merely looking at it triggered powerful illusions, let alone attacking it directly.

What could they do?

A chill ran down Nasir's spine.

He was about to close his eyes and wait for death when his heart suddenly jolted.

What?

He turned around and saw Fang Heng form a hand seal. The aura around Fang Heng abruptly changed.

Golden light spilled from Fang Heng's body.

Immediately afterward, a barrier of sacred power rose around both of them.

Nasir stared at Fang Heng as though he'd witnessed something incredible.

"You..."

Before he could ask, Nasir looked back and saw the black tendrils touch the sacred barrier.

"Hiss, hiss-hiss-hiss..."

The barrier immediately crackled and hissed.

The tendrils evaporated almost instantly, giving off blue-white smoke!

As though badly wounded, they abruptly recoiled.

Apparently realizing the sacred barrier was different, the tendrils withdrew a short distance before cautiously approaching again and touching it bit by bit.

"Hiss! Hiss-hiss!!!"

But every time a dark tendril neared the sacred barrier, it was dispersed and forced back a short distance.

The shadow tendrils had been stopped!

It worked!

Nasir was overjoyed and wiped the cold sweat from his forehead.

Fang Heng also breathed a little easier.

The Evil Embryo was a strange one.

Just as he'd suspected, the shadow tendrils seemed to involve something on the level of faith. Sacred Studies, which also dealt in the power of faith, was more effective against them.

Nasir looked at him and whispered, "Brother Fang Heng, how did you learn the methods of the gods?"

"Hm? The gods?"

Seeing Fang Heng's expression, Nasir seemed to misunderstand. He mimed sewing his mouth shut and said solemnly, "I understand. You only used it to save us. I swear on my life I won't tell anyone."

Sacred Studies was a method used by the race of gods?

Fang Heng frowned even more, thoroughly confused.

Hadn't he already concluded that the Holy Court had nothing to do with the race of gods?
