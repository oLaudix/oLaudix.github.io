Chapter 3059

Defense

From Chinese Chapter 3060 · 防守

The Shadow Clan had led the fleet's main forces away to clear out the alien insects. As usual, roughly one quarter of the troops stayed behind to guard the vessels.

Vice-Captain Evans was responsible for the entire fleet's defenses.

As far as Evans was concerned, this sort of routine defensive duty carried virtually no danger.

They had a massive fleet of over a hundred ships. If the insects dared approach, the fleet's energy weapons alone would give them more than they could handle.

For some reason, though, Evans had been sneezing repeatedly today. He couldn't shake the feeling that something bad was about to happen.

A little earlier, the elves had even reported unusual movements among the insects, urging everyone to be alert and prepare for battle.

Evans had paid no attention.

What a joke.

Since when were the elves so skilled at reconnaissance?

Then, without warning, alarms blared throughout the ships.

"Report, Commander! Alien insects detected outside the fleet!"

Every ship detecting the approaching insects sounded shrill alarms. Almost all vessels entered battle configuration one after another.

"Huh? They've actually come?"

Evans looked through the ship's windows, surprise appearing in his eyes.

In the distance, clouds of dust billowed upward, revealing the insect swarm beneath them.

It really was a swarm of alien insects.

Strange. Ouroboros Seeds usually adopted a defensive posture. Unless their hives were attacked, they rarely launched offensives themselves.

Why would they attack the ships on their own?

But since they had come, Evans didn't think any further about it.

"Stay calm. Order all vessels into defensive formation and exterminate the swarm!"

"Yes!"

The ships unfolded their side decks, exposing rows of long-range beam cannons. Crew members hurried to their battle stations as dense arrays of guns locked onto the approaching insects.

"Fire!"

"Boom!"

The first thick, dark-red beam cut through the dust cloud and exploded amid the swarm, blasting a huge crater in the ground. Then beams of every color fired in dense succession, raining down continuously on the insects.

Huh?

Evans watched the swarm through the window and frowned.

The volley had scattered their formation somewhat, but it didn't seem to have inflicted any significant damage.

Just as the meeting reports said, these insects had remarkable vitality.

So what?

He refused to believe they couldn't be killed!

Evans scoffed inwardly. "Increase the energy frequency."

"Yes!"

"Report, Commander! Warnings of large numbers of insects approaching!"

"Large numbers?" Evans looked at the swarm outside, which seemed to number only about two hundred. "Where?"

The next instant, the ground outside the ships shook violently. Huge cracks opened, and dense masses of Lickers crawled out of the fissures.

The swarm had bypassed a great distance underground and surfaced directly around the ships, lunging at them!

Damn it!

They'd been underground all along!

Before the insects could climb aboard, the vessels immediately activated their defensive barriers, stopping the Lickers outside.

Evans breathed a little easier.

Fortunately, the insects' physical attacks did relatively little damage to the shields.

So far, the pressure was manageable.

But the creatures possessed some bizarre rebirth ability and couldn't be eliminated permanently. If the battle dragged on, even the fleet wouldn't be able to keep it up forever.

No need to rush. Observe carefully; there had to be a weakness.

While Evans studied the battlefield for an opening, a researcher hurried forward. "Captain, the elven vessel requests permission to teleport aboard. They report having discovered a weakness in the insects and need to explain it in person."

Evans frowned.

Come to think of it, the elves had been the first to notice unusual insect movements. Perhaps they really did know something.

"Very well. Authorize the transfer."

Soon Elder Deborah entered the flagship's control room with Fang Heng.

Evans frowned at the masked human beside her, vaguely sensing something strange about him.

"And this person is...?"

Whoosh!

Fang Heng didn't answer. He struck first, turning into a black blur and rushing straight toward Captain Evans!

To catch the bandits, first seize their leader!

What?!

Evans had never imagined the elves would rebel now. In the instant he was distracted, Fang Heng's enormous fist was already bearing down on his face!

"Whoosh!"

The fist swept through the air with a violent rush of wind.

Evans's expression changed drastically, and the entire right half of his body transformed into black mist!

Whoosh!

Fang Heng's fist passed straight through the mist. He turned and regarded Evans.

"Hah! Interesting!"

The Shadow Clan's special racial ability.

Shadow Transformation.

It was somewhat like a soul form, yet fundamentally different. A transformed Shadow Clan member remained visible as a dark silhouette. Supposedly innate to their species, it made them immune to most physical attacks and even a limited range of magical attacks.

"Watch out! Protect the captain!"

More than a dozen elite Shadow Clan guards in the control room reacted at once. Three formed seals in front of themselves, while seven others lunged directly at Fang Heng.

At the same time, the ship's guards raised their guns and aimed at Fang Heng's group.

Shadow Spheres!

Dark spheres floated into the air, casting Fang Heng's shadow onto the floor.

Fang Heng immediately felt a strange force pulling at his body and locking him in place.

Looking up, he saw seven elite Shadow Clan fighters enter Shadow Transformation in unison, becoming streaks of black mist as they rushed toward him.

"Hmph!"

Fang Heng snorted.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Dark Nether Orbs instantly coalesced from his tattoo and shot toward the surrounding attackers!

"Hiss! Hiss, hiss, hiss!"

As they struck the transformed fighters, sizzling noises erupted. The orbs passed straight through their shadowy bodies.

The elite guards' expressions changed drastically. Wherever the Nether Orbs touched, their shadow-formed bodies began rapidly corroding and dissolving!

What were those black spheres?! How could they inflict such horrifying damage on Shadow Transformation?

As the orbs swept toward them again, the elite fighters dared not withstand another hit and retreated hastily.

When they returned to physical form, the places where the Nether Orbs had grazed them were left bloody and mangled.

What terrifying ability was that?

The Shadow Clan had rarely encountered a power that countered Shadow Transformation so thoroughly. They stared at Fang Heng in fear.

"Go!"

Fang Heng raised his hand toward the Shadow Clan once more.

"Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!"

Twenty-four Nether Orbs flew straight at the Shadow Spheres suspended in midair.

"Boom!"

The Shadow Spheres burst apart under the Nether Orbs' impact, releasing thick clouds of eerie necromantic mist in every direction.

— End of Chapter 3059 —
