Chapter 2970

Extortion

From Chinese Chapter 2971 · 欺压

Huh?

He'd just been thinking of seeking them out, and now they'd come to him first?

“Approve the jump. Let them come over.”

What a poor deal. Led in by a soldier, the Iron-Eater Clan's leader entered the conference room.

The leader bowed to Fang Heng. “Great human captain, we've received orders from the Shadow Clan. We hope you will protect us and buy us enough time to build the Eye of Stars prism positioning devices.”

The Iron-Eater Clan excelled at forging and construction, but its members were relatively weak compared with other races, so they maintained a humble attitude.

“Mm. We'll protect you. How far has construction progressed, and how much longer will it take?”

“Esteemed Captain, that is precisely why we've come.”

The Iron-Eater leader respectfully handed a document to a nearby soldier. “This is a list of materials we're currently missing for construction of the prism tower. We've managed to gather some of them ourselves, but the remainder is beyond our means. The Shadow Clan assured us that the humans would provide those materials.”

Fang Heng took the list and examined it. His brows drew together slightly before he passed it to Chen Yu and the others to look over.

The Iron-Eater Clan's attitude was so respectful that there was nothing to criticize.

Fang Heng remained calm. “I understand. Is there anything else to report?”

“No. The shortage of materials will slow construction of the prism defense system. We hope the supplies can be replenished as soon as possible. If construction is delayed, all the allied races will blame the Iron-Eater Clan. Thank you very much.”

As soon as the Iron-Eater representatives left, Zaka couldn't hold back. He curled his lip. “Fuck, that's extortion! It's not enough that they get our fighting strength for free to deal with the insects. Now they want us to hand over construction materials free of charge too? How come every damn advantage goes to them?”

Fang Heng looked toward the Flying Squirrel captain. “Has the Shadow Clan always operated like this?”

The Flying Squirrel captain gave a bitter smile. Inwardly, he thought this was actually a relatively mild case.

Fang Heng understood his expression. After a moment's thought, he said, “No rush. Let's communicate with the Shadow Clan first.”

Before long, another soldier arrived with a report. “The Shadow Clan says they're aware of the situation. Given the wartime emergency, they hope we can find our own solution. They say there are many mineral deposits in this world, and we're free to gather the materials ourselves.”

Everyone had been furiously cursing the Shadow Clan, but on hearing this, the curses stopped. They looked at one another.

Uh...

Such a wonderful opportunity actually existed?

Surely those people had no idea how terrifyingly fast Boss Fang could strip a mining district of resources?

Sure enough, Fang Heng's eyes lit up at once.

“How generous. They're allowing us to mine freely?”

Fang Heng immediately turned to the Flying Squirrel captain. “Mousse, have you located the mining areas?”

Mousse's heart skipped a beat at being called on.

The Flying Squirrel clan possessed a special mineral-sensing ability. They could detect different minerals across vast distances and even determine the reserves and grades of ore deposits.

“There are several mines nearby.”

Mousse nodded, then hastily added a qualification.

“Boss Fang, most allied forces have concentrated their military strength on the front line, but they've already claimed nearly all the mining areas. They may not be extracting the ore, but they occupy the deposits. If we force our way in and start mining, we could offend them and even spark a conflict...”

The Flying Squirrel clan was timid. They certainly wouldn't dare confront the Shadow Clan head-on.

In fact, the Shadow Clan and its allies were counting on Fang Heng not daring to touch the mines they'd claimed.

This was one of the Shadow Clan's usual methods for exploiting other fleets.

They coerced other races into assisting with construction, then refused to supply the materials, effectively making them pay out of their own pockets for the privilege of working.

“Tch.”

Alec's fists were itching for a fight again. He said irritably, “They tell us to find mining areas while secretly occupying almost all of them. Obviously they want us to pay for the materials! Those dog bastards. Boss Fang, shall we make a move?”

Mousse, the Flying Squirrel captain, immediately shrank his neck.

Good grief. Were these people really ready to fight at the slightest disagreement?

Humans were terrifying.

“No need to fight just yet. We're not some evil faction. We should try to resolve problems through communication, seeking common ground while respecting differences.”

Wearing an honest, good-natured smile, Fang Heng looked at Mousse. “We'll cooperate as before. Our side will handle mining, transporting, and smelting the ore, while your Flying Squirrel clan helps locate and sort the deposits.”

“Of course. We'd be delighted to help.” Mousse nodded repeatedly, but still warned anxiously, “Boss Fang, the Shadow Clan is extremely strong and domineering. Please don't underestimate them.”

The Flying Squirrel representatives hurried away.

The remaining people all turned toward Fang Heng.

“No rush. Zaka and Alec, come with me to inspect the mines. Everyone else, hold your positions and deal with the insect swarms as planned.”

“Yes! Understood!”

...

A mining area near Area Six of the Eye of Stars.

In fact, Fang Heng and the Federation had underestimated just how disgusting the Shadow Clan's allies could be.

Before Fang Heng's group had even arrived in this world, the major allied forces had already divided up every resource location.

Ownership of every mine had been clearly assigned.

The alliance deliberately withheld much of the material needed to build the Eye of Stars, forcing the other 'suckers' who accepted construction missions to find their own supplies.

Officially, of course, those teams were free to gather materials locally.

But in reality...

The smaller races didn't dare offend the alliance by seizing mines already occupied by the stronger ones.

What?

You say you can't find any ore?

Well, that's your own incompetence. What's that got to do with the Shadow Clan? Sorry, then you'll simply have to pay for it. After all, you can't let the entire alliance fail its mission because of your mistake, can you?

Looking toward the mine from a distance, Fang Heng saw a flag marking it as territory occupied by the Crocodile Clan.

The Crocodile Clan ranked among the top thirty Kinsfolk allies in overall strength. Like the Shadow Clan, it maintained an enormous fleet and had always been one of the Shadow Clan's loyal underlings.

“Come on. Let's go take a look.”

Outside the mining area.

Noticing Fang Heng and his companions in protective suits, the crocodile guards immediately raised their weapons and aimed at them. “Humans! Leave this place! This mine belongs to us! It's our private territory! Get out at once, or we'll treat you as intruders!”

The Crocodile Clan possessed powerful physiques and could adapt to most hostile planetary environments, so they needed no protective suits outdoors.

When Fang Heng made no move to leave, the crocodile guard captain pulled his weapon's trigger in warning.

“Whoosh!”

An energy beam struck the ground beside Zaka, blasting out a small crater and making him jump.

“Hahahaha!”

The crocodile guards burst out laughing. “Get lost, humans! This is no place for the likes of you!”
