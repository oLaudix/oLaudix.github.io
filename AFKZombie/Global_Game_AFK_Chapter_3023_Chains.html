Chapter 3023

Chains

From Chinese Chapter 3024 · 锁链

Not far away, the Holy Court knights were still locked in battle with the high-level fiends. Grand Sage Dean's expression was as grave as could be.

What a terrifying Demon Seed aura.

Demonic energy raged from Eseklyna, wave after wave of Hell power washing across the entire cavern and hissing against the sacred barriers erected by the Holy Court.

Not even the Divine Realm's Demon-Suppression Temple had ever contained a fiend of such strength.

“Heh? Finally getting serious?” Fang Heng watched Eseklyna and said coldly, “Then let's have some real fun today.”

“Hmph! You're asking for death!”

Worried about the Demon Cores, Eseklyna had lost all patience for exchanging words. She moved abruptly, trying to break through toward the right.

“Boom!!”

The two collided in midair!

With one punch, Fang Heng shattered the dark Tongues of Envy gathered around her and slammed heavily into Eseklyna's flesh!

In her complete form, Eseklyna's attributes had improved enormously. Fang Heng's punch only made her sway violently and blasted a bloody hole through her body, while the Tongues of Envy around her frantically swept toward him!

“Whoosh!”

Fang Heng retreated rapidly!

“Running?”

Eseklyna had killing intent in her eyes. She sneered and pointed her right hand toward Fang Heng. “Prison of Envy!”

Dark-red light exploded outward from Eseklyna!

The surrounding space twisted, and writhing Tongues of Envy began appearing around Fang Heng.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!!!”

The tongues solidified and coiled madly toward him!

At the same time, phantom images of the tongues appeared in Fang Heng's pupils.

An illusion!

Bang!!!

The phantom images reflected in his eyes shattered in an instant.

A step too late. By the time his gaze cleared, thick, twisting Tongues of Envy had completely engulfed him!

Fang Heng's speed faltered. He was restrained in midair, unable to move.

[Warning: The player's Grand Sage bloodline level has decreased...]

For a time, game warnings flooded Fang Heng's vision as his max-level Grand Sage bloodline plummeted in level!

The Tongues of Envy were rapidly drawing the sacred energy out of his body.

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!!!”

The tongues extending from Eseklyna wrapped Fang Heng tightly, binding the two of them together.

“Whoosh!”

With a powerful tug, Eseklyna dragged Fang Heng right in front of her. She licked her lips. “Hehehe... Such delicious power. I'd love to drain you completely. Unfortunately, this energy is useless to me. Nothing but garbage...”

This is bad!

Seeing Fang Heng restrained, Dean's heart sank. Instinctively, he stepped forward to help, but remembered Fang Heng's earlier instruction and forcibly stopped himself.

Fang Heng still hadn't asked for help.

What was he waiting for!?

At this rate, that demoness would suck him dry!

Did he still have some hidden trump card?

Enough of this.

Dean was about to intervene when he suddenly noticed a cold smile creeping across Fang Heng's face.

Huh!?

Something was wrong.

Dean restrained himself once again, forcing himself to calm down and observe the fight.

Amid the dense demonic aura, Fang Heng's sacred power continued to be siphoned away.

A faint sneer on his face, Fang Heng said, “Heh, Eseklyna, I admit you're surprisingly strong.”

Eseklyna stared at him.

She had him completely under her control, so why was unease rising in her heart?

“What? Want to beg for mercy now? Too late.”

“Not exactly... I just wanted to say that I've figured out your resurrection ability. It's nothing but a little trick involving spatial power, and there are quite a few restrictions on using it, aren't there?”

“What did you say?”

Eseklyna's expression changed.

Hiss!

The next instant, something unexpected happened!

A dense network of tiny chains suddenly appeared all over Fang Heng's body!

“Hiss! Hiss, hiss!!!”

With the two so close together, the chains lashed out and pierced straight toward Eseklyna!

What!

Eseklyna's pupils contracted. She tried to dodge immediately, but it was too late. The chains plunged into her body!

Demon-Binding Chains!

Ever since Fang Heng had obtained the crafting recipe for the Demon-Binding Chains, he had never slacked off in searching for a way to forge them.

Some time ago, while farming missions across different star systems, he'd finally redeemed all the high-grade materials through the Kinsfolk system. He had then entrusted the Council of the Undead with forging three full sets of Demon-Binding Chains, making ample preparations.

But when he encountered Eseklyna, Fang Heng discovered that she possessed some form of resurrection. To ensure the chains would work, he'd had no choice but to use himself as bait.

“Aaah!!!!”

Eseklyna howled in agony.

As the Demon-Binding Chains pierced her flesh and firmly locked down her core, thick demonic energy poured madly out of her body.

“You!! Damn you!”

Feeling her power rapidly draining away, Eseklyna stared at Fang Heng, furious and alarmed.

“At the very least, whenever you attack and absorb energy, you have to exist in your real body... And so...”

Fang Heng looked at Eseklyna with a cold smile, as though gazing at prey that had already walked into a trap.

The sight of his smile filled Eseklyna with profound dread.

Lunatic!

Had he calculated everything? Even being bound by her? Even letting her steal so much sacred power?

She'd been caught in that brat's scheme from beginning to end!

Rage consumed Eseklyna. She glared at him. “You think this is enough to hurt me? Give me some time and I'll break these shackles. But the sacred power you've lost will never recover! Die!”

“Boom!!!”

The Tongues of Envy draining Fang Heng's power suddenly exploded!

Fang Heng was hurled backward into the rock wall, the sacred light enveloping his body rapidly fading.

Huh?

What's going on?

Why isn't he dead!?

Eseklyna stared in disbelief as Fang Heng braced himself against the broken wall and stood up again.

How?

She'd clearly drained all the sacred power from his body! He should have less than one percent of his strength left!

Why hadn't he died?

Seeing Fang Heng survive such a tremendous attack with only serious injuries, Eseklyna's eyes filled with intense hatred. She longed to pursue him, tear him apart, and swallow him whole.

But she couldn't.

Reason told Eseklyna that this wasn't the time!

The situation with her pet Echidna was even worse.

She couldn't delay any longer.

The Demon-Binding Chains were already suppressing her and severely weakening her strength.

Unable to spare any more time to kill Fang Heng, she immediately dashed toward the exit on the right side of the cavern.

Pulling himself up against the damaged rock wall, Fang Heng shouted, “Dean! Stop her, quickly!”
