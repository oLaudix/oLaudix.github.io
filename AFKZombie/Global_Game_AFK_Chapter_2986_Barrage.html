Chapter 2986

Barrage

From Chinese Chapter 2987 · 冲刷

“BOOM! BOOM, BOOM, BOOM, BOOM!!!”

The continuous effect of Divine Punishment!

Lightning flashed as a relentless storm of bolts hammered down on Proust, washing over his body again and again!

Every strike knocked huge numbers of charred red threadlike worms loose from his body.

After a full three minutes, the skill finally ended, and Fang Heng exited his sacred form.

Sacred Form dealt extra damage to Proust.

Unfortunately, the Death Realm environment weakened its attacks somewhat.

“Hah, hah...”

Proust finally broke free of Divine Punishment's control. He slowly raised his head, the flesh scorched black by violet lightning gradually regenerating, and stared at Fang Heng.

“An interesting skill. But it doesn't last long enough. Otherwise, that really would have been troublesome...”

“Heh, I think so too.”

Fang Heng lightly tapped the ground with his toes. “Want to try another move?”

“Hm?”

The next instant, a rapidly rotating magic array appeared beneath Fang Heng.

Eternal Tree Domain!

Nature energy erupted!

Dense vines, branches, and roots spilled out of the array. The surrounding space seemed to split apart under their pressure, filling instantly with masses of green vegetation!

“Abe Akaya! Go!”

Proust had not yet recovered from the grievous damage of Divine Punishment. His heart lurched as the surrounding space became completely overgrown in the blink of an eye.

Instantly, countless vines formed sharp spikes and stabbed toward him.

“GET LOST!!!”

Proust roared.

“BOOM!”

A burst of force erupted around him, blowing apart the mass of attacking vines.

“Whoosh!”

Annual Rings—Acceleration!

Fang Heng abruptly appeared directly in front of Proust.

“BOOM!!!”

He threw a punch!!

Proust's body instantly scattered into intertwined masses of tiny insects, dispersing the impact.

But this blow was stronger than any before it. Proust could absorb only part of the force and was knocked backward by Fang Heng's fist.

At the same time, the death aura coating Fang Heng's fist rapidly spread across Proust from the point of contact!

“Whoosh!!”

Annual Rings—Acceleration!

Again! Pursue him!!

Fang Heng streaked through the air like a shadow and appeared in front of Proust again in the blink of an eye.

The strength enhanced by Annual Rings now exceeded the amount his tiny insects could absorb. A tremendous impact rang out as Proust's body was sent flying backward again!

“Again!”

“BANG!”

“BANG!!”

The spectators could no longer follow Fang Heng's movements. They could only see Proust struggling to control his body as a dark blur pursued him and smashed him downward again and again, accelerating him toward the ground with each impact.

For Proust, the most damaging part was the death power coating those fists. Vast numbers of the tiny insects composing his body were withering. Under Fang Heng's overwhelming strength, Proust also lost control of his body, staggered repeatedly by the impacts.

After several successive blows enhanced by Annual Rings, Proust was completely unable to control his movements. Punch after punch drove him faster toward the earth.

Until the final blow!

Annual Rings—Acceleration!

Explode!

“BANG!!!”

Proust slammed violently into the ground!

“Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh...”

Dense vines rushed toward him, yanked him deep beneath the surface, and wrapped layer after layer around the ground above, binding everything tightly!

At the same time, Fang Heng flung sealing talisman papers downward, sticking them firmly to the outer layer of vines.

In the distance, Mu Qingxin watched the battlefield, her expression growing grave.

Fang Heng's melee strength had exceeded her expectations, but she judged that the domain he'd summoned had a time limit. They only needed to endure it.

Then what was Fang Heng doing now?

Trying to seal Proust away?

Wasn't that underestimating both her and Proust?

As she wondered, Mu Qingxin saw Fang Heng quickly retreat from Proust's sealed position.

Hm?

He was backing off?

What did that mean?

Apparently noticing her gaze, Fang Heng abruptly turned and shot Mu Qingxin a challenging look, then pointed a finger toward the sky.

Hm?!

What was he doing?

It wasn't only Mu Qingxin. The Nether Clan and Undead Council members couldn't understand why Fang Heng had suddenly retreated after managing to pin Proust down.

Was this some special technique?

Half a second later, Mu Qingxin's expression changed.

What?!

Violent spatial fluctuations suddenly erupted high above the battlefield!

The dark clouds gathered overhead were pushed outward by the disturbance. Several clearly visible cannon muzzles gradually appeared in the center of the spatial ripples.

The Nether Clan warriors had never seen enormous mechanical weapons descend from the sky before. Their eyes widened in shock.

No!

Mu Qingxin reacted immediately and instinctively tried to intervene.

“BOOM! BOOM, BOOM, BOOM!!!!”

Too late!

The next instant, deep-purple energy beams poured from above, blanketing an enormous area of the clustered insect nests!

Proust was right at the center of the bombardment!

These were modified energy beams.

Mu Qingxin narrowed her eyes, sensing a powerful threat within them.

The purple light purified every Zerg swarm within the affected area!

The bombardment continued for five full minutes before the beams gradually faded and the cannon muzzles withdrew into the spatial passage overhead.

The land that had been covered by fungal carpet was now scorched black, as though burned to charcoal.

Fang Heng stared toward the spot where Abe Akaya had sealed Proust.

Still not dead!

“Crack, crack, crack-crack-crack...”

As Abe Akaya and Eternal Tree Domain disappeared, the ground bulged outward in pulses, as though something beneath it were struggling to break free.

“BOOM!!!”

A gigantic pit suddenly exploded open in the ground.

Proust appeared at its center, gasping for breath and looking exceptionally battered.

“Fang Heng...”

Fang Heng stared back with a mocking edge to his voice. “What? Not convinced?”

Proust gritted his teeth and was about to charge after him.

Suddenly his body lost control again, suspended in midair by a strange force.

Fang Heng pointed at him with one hand and barked, “Divine Punishment!”

“BOOM!!!”

A purple bolt of punitive lightning crashed down at once!

“BOOM!”

“BOOM, BOOM!!!”

One after another, enhanced bolts of deep-purple Divine Punishment lightning continued raining down!

Having already lost most of his strength, Proust could not counterattack. He could only endure the repeated lightning barrage, relying on his enormous HP and self-regeneration to stay alive!

Fang Heng watched Proust's health bar.

It was slowly, steadily dropping.

— End of Chapter 2986 —
