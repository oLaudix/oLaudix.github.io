Chapter 2153

Lead the Way

From original Chinese Chapter 2154 · 带路

Aidolard stared at the horrifying scene, his legs trembling. The man before him was a demon!

“I don't like being lied to. I suggest you cooperate, or you'll die an even more miserable death. Now, I need a researcher's uniform.”

Aidolard didn't dare deceive him. Trembling, he hurried to a cabinet, took out a researcher's coat, and handed it to Fang Heng. “I know about the Niam Alloy. My colleague works in that area. I'm not lying. But the advanced research section has been sealed. The security uses retinal locks, so I can't open it. I can only take you there...”

“Mm. Lead the way.”

Fang Heng changed into the uniform and followed Aidolard out of the laboratory, extending his perception again to assess the situation outside.

Without assistance from the Lickers and worms, the Tree Spirits' advance had slowed drastically.

That suited him perfectly.

While the Tree Spirits drew Wanling Technology's attention, he could infiltrate the institute's underground section with ease.

He still had plenty of time.

Aidolard soon led him into the underground high-level research area.

A lowered metal gate blocked the passage ahead.

“We're here. The S-class research zone is inside. The institute just entered crisis mode, so the entrance has been locked down. I wasn't lying.”

Aidolard glanced nervously at Fang Heng and shrank back.

“Can't you open it?”

“No. Only the project's direct supervisor or someone with institute-director privileges has the necessary authorization. There's also a retinal check. We can't get inside.”

Fang Heng examined the alloy gate before him.

Well, nothing for it.

Mo Jiawei hadn't come along this time.

He'd just have to force it open.

Fang Heng was about to test whether he could smash the gate apart when a clacking sound suddenly reached his ears.

“Hm?”

The alloy door slowly opened before him.

When he saw the person standing on the other side, surprise flashed across Fang Heng's face.

Ji Haiting!

Ji Haiting stood opposite him, surrounded by a group of psychic-modified bodyguards.

Although it was invisible, Fang Heng could clearly sense a thick psychic barrier separating them.

Ji Haiting's personal guards watched Fang Heng tensely.

“Fang Heng. I'm delighted to see you again.”

“You again. What a coincidence.”

“Hahaha! Isn't this fate?”

Ji Haiting laughed heartily. “Fang Heng, you're truly impressive. Once again, your strength has exceeded my expectations. No wonder even the Dependents are so wary of you.”

The Dependents?

Fang Heng's heart stirred.

As expected!

This world was connected to the Dependents.

Glancing at the vigilant bodyguards around Ji Haiting, Fang Heng frowned. “Did you detect me earlier? Have you been waiting here?”

“Not at all. We only just arrived. In fact, I was preparing to leave. Then the system reported that someone had entered the research institute's outer section. I suspected something was wrong and came by to take a look. I certainly didn't expect to find you.”

Ji Haiting spread his hands, sincerity on his face. “Fang Heng, my offer still stands. If you're willing, we can cooperate.”

He paused before extending the invitation again. “Not cooperation with the Federation or the alien Dependents. Something more personal—with me, and with Wanling Technology under my control. Believe me, together we could create the future.”

Strangely enough, the idea was somewhat tempting.

But the game reward advised against choosing this route.

After thinking it over, Fang Heng said, “If possible, I'd like some time to consider it.”

Ji Haiting was taken aback.

He'd expected another immediate refusal, as before. Instead, Fang Heng had agreed to consider it. Ji Haiting burst into laughter. “Hahaha! Excellent! Of course. Take all the time you need!”

He nodded to a subordinate. A magnetic card passed through the psychic barrier and flew into Fang Heng's hand.

“My contact details are on that. Just activate it with mental strength. As long as you're inside the Spirit Domain blockade, I'll immediately sense your location and come to meet you personally.”

[Hint: The player has obtained an item—Unknown Magnetic Card.]

Fang Heng put the card away and nodded. “Right now I need Niam Alloy. You're going to stop me, aren't you?”

“No. You guessed wrong.”

Ji Haiting spread his hands. “It's merely Niam Alloy. If you want it, I'll gladly hand it over. All I ask is that you answer one question—and answer it seriously.”

“Go ahead.”

“I've investigated your identity. I suspect you aren't from our world. So I want to know which side you're on. Who are you working for? Is it Ouroboros?”

Fang Heng's pupils contracted sharply, and his expression shifted.

“What?”

“Ouroboros. Fang Heng, we uploaded genetic samples from the Lickers you control to the Dependents' Spirit Domain network. We detected traces of an Ouroboros genetic sequence.”

Ji Haiting kept his eyes on Fang Heng. “You surely know how determined the Dependents are to track down Ouroboros. I've temporarily concealed the result using my personal influence, but the Dependents will eventually investigate. You don't have much time left. If they discover your whereabouts...”

“You won't escape!”

Fang Heng frowned.

They'd found traces of Ouroboros genes in the Lickers?

How had that happened?

Could it be the Hela virus?

Undying Body was connected to the Hela virus. Perhaps that was why the Lickers carried Ouroboros gene fragments.

But there was another possibility.

Himself!

Perhaps the Lickers had inherited the Ouroboros fragments from Fang Heng!

Or was there some other explanation?

Ji Haiting waited while Fang Heng sank deep into thought, then urged, “Fang Heng, time is running out. I must leave. I need an answer.”

Fang Heng looked up. “No. I work for myself.”

“Hahaha! Wonderful! So do I!”

Ji Haiting looked at Fang Heng with undisguised approval. “The Niam Alloy is in Research Laboratory No. 3. Take as much as you like. As I've said, I look forward to working with you. Whenever you've made up your mind, contact me.”

— End of Chapter 2153 —
