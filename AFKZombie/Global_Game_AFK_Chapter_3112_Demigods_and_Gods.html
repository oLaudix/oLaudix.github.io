Chapter 3112

Demigods and Gods

From Chinese Chapter 3113 · 次神与神

"The gods, of course."

Nasir gradually recovered his composure, conceded the board game, and said, "Laws are believed to be the domain of the gods. Starting at the Transcendent level, an academic discipline gradually enters divine territory. Advancing beyond that means understanding laws more deeply and learning to wield them."

"The Demigod level approaches the power of the gods, while the final God level is considered to grant the same abilities as the gods themselves. To the gods, that's an act of gross disrespect."

Fang Heng furrowed his brows as the implications dawned on him.

"Are you saying that gods are a kind of living creature?"

"An ancient race," Mu Shunan explained. "They possess immense power from birth. Every movement can affect the laws. Even a warrior who has truly mastered a God-level academic discipline would be no match for a god."

Fang Heng's heart jolted.

Gods?

An entire race!

If he understood it that way, things might finally make sense.

He thought back carefully.

Sura had told him that the Ouroboros Seed was once destroyed by the gods.

Of course, not completely. The Ouroboros Seed had stubbornly survived and continued evolving, preparing to make a comeback.

And there were the Titans.

The Titan ruins discovered in humanity's world also contained descriptions of gods on their stone walls.

He had previously assumed those references meant the God Kings of the Divine Realm and the Holy Court.

Now it seemed the race of gods was something entirely different from the God Kings.

"Do gods really exist?" Fang Heng asked.

"Of course."

"The gods call themselves the rulers of the world. Some have estimated that, in terms of dimensional level alone, members of their race must reach at least level 200."

"Their power is terrifying, but their numbers are extremely small. If I remember correctly, the last recorded appearance of a god was more than two hundred years ago. The seven overlord races of the Seven-Realm Martial Capital know far more about gods than ordinary people like us. I've even heard rumors that the capital was founded under the gods' instruction."

Fang Heng frowned inwardly.

The race of gods.

They sounded extraordinarily strong.

A dimensional level above 200 would be terrifying from attribute bonuses alone.

"Yes, gods exist, but they rarely appear before us. That's always been the custom throughout the universe. Out of awe and fear of the gods, practitioners generally avoid openly progressing once their academic power approaches the divine level."

"Gods are unpredictable. Even if people want to advance further, they won't announce it openly, much less teach public courses," Nasir added with a curl of his lip. "If you offend a god, you might die without even knowing what killed you."

Fang Heng looked at the pair. "What about you two? I imagine you've both reached the threshold of the Demigod level. Have you tried advancing further?"

"I'd be lying if I said I hadn't. Once you reach this stage, I don't believe any warrior would stop advancing solely out of fear of gods."

Mu Shunan gave a self-mocking smile. "But breaking through isn't easy. I've been at the perfected Transcendent stage for more than ten years and have tried constantly to advance. Even now, I've only scratched the surface. I'm still far from a real breakthrough."

"I'm a little behind. My Alchemy hasn't reached perfection yet, though I'm not far off."

As Nasir spoke, a bitter smile appeared at the corners of his mouth.

He had originally been Fang Heng's Alchemy instructor. Who could have imagined that in just one short month, Fang Heng would surpass him academically?

How was anyone supposed to cope with that?

Nasir had no intention of keeping secrets. "I don't know exactly how to reach Demigod-level Alchemy. But my teacher once told me that it involves a further application of laws. To enter the Demigod stage, you must use magic arrays to permanently influence and modify laws."

Fang Heng murmured the words to himself.

"Permanently modify laws..."

Taken literally, until now they had been influencing laws temporarily by keeping alchemical magic arrays running.

At the Demigod stage, they would be able to alter the laws of the surrounding environment permanently.

One activation of a magic array, and the effect would last forever?

How could that be done?

Curious, Fang Heng asked, "Is there a corresponding training array?"

"No. My teacher left behind no diagrams or written material. Only a handful of people within the Alchemy Association have truly broken through to the Demigod level."

Nasir shook his head. "Generally, people who reach that level receive warnings from various parties. As a result, the association's elders aren't willing to teach it publicly. And the final God level is considered a taboo that absolutely must not be crossed."

Troublesome.

Fang Heng sighed inwardly at the difficulty of the situation.

It seemed his effortless, cheat-like advancement could carry him to the threshold of the Demigod level, but progressing beyond that would require an enormous amount of work.

As far as Alchemy was concerned, creating an array that influenced laws might not be as difficult as he imagined.

But permanently changing them... Perhaps there was some method of altering the laws' underlying structure?

Thinking it over, Fang Heng extended his hand and traced several lines of a magic array in the air.

No, that wasn't right.

He had barely traced part of the pattern when a sense of frustration and inability to proceed overwhelmed him. He shook his head, stopped, and closed his eyes to concentrate again.

Seeing Fang Heng sink into thought and then sit down to cultivate in the middle of their conversation, Nasir and Mu Shunan exchanged looks, shook their heads, and quietly withdrew.

After a long while, Fang Heng opened his eyes again.

It was difficult.

Although he could now clearly distinguish the activity of different energies in his surroundings, he still couldn't figure out how Alchemy could directly alter the operation of the surrounding laws.

The laws around him were originally in balance.

Could it be...

What if he simply broke that balance and then reconstructed a new one?

That would be much harder than continuously influencing an existing balance through a magic array.

He would try again.

...

The night passed.

Fang Heng sat on the starship's deck, deep in thought.

Even after an entire night, he had failed to develop a complete line of reasoning.

But when he checked the game notifications, the quest's progress had risen to 1%.

Perhaps that meant he was thinking in the right direction?

"Well? Any insights yet?"

Fang Heng turned toward Nasir and Mu Shunan, who were ambling over after breakfast, and shook his head. "I tried, but it's difficult. I still haven't found a proper approach."
