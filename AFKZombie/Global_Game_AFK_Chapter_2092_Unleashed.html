Chapter 2092

Unleashed

From original Chinese Chapter 2093 · 泄露

Whoosh!

The moment Fang Heng emerged from his secondary-space projection, he pressed both hands against the altar!

Absorb!

Absorb with everything he had!

Whoosh!

He drove his spiritual power to its limit!

The Chaos Power the altar had accumulated and converted over many years surged into his body!

Game notifications appeared in rapid succession.

[Hint: You have absorbed and converted Chaos Power. Intermediate Demon Bloodline skill EXP +162...]

[Hint: Your Intermediate Demon Bloodline has reached Level 4.]

[Hint: You have absorbed and converted Chaos Power. Intermediate Demon Bloodline skill EXP +161...]

At the same time, the surrounding horned demons were enraged by Fang Heng's renewed contact with the altar. They immediately charged toward him!

Dense waves of fireballs bombarded him head-on!

BOOM!

But just before the fireballs struck, thick black mist suddenly erupted around Fang Heng, enveloping his entire body!

That was...

Meng Si's group stared in disbelief.

The energy radiating from that mist...

A Demon Seed!!

There was no mistaking it!

How could this be?!

He was actually a Demon Seed!

No wonder he could absorb the altar's power!

The players were utterly shocked.

Fang Heng!

He was a Demon Seed!

For the past few days, speculation that Fang Heng might be a Demon Seed had been circulating intensely online.

But followers of Hell had scoffed at those rumors.

How could a player possibly become a Demon Seed?

The idea was ridiculous!

Did those people even understand what a Demon Seed was?!

Yet here it was, happening right before their eyes.

Perhaps they were the ones who didn't understand anything!

BOOM! BOOM, BOOM!

The dense volley of concentrated hellfire spheres struck the black mist surrounding Fang Heng. They exploded against it as if hitting a solid wall, without affecting him in the slightest!

Under the protective mist, notifications continued flashing furiously before Fang Heng.

[Hint: You have absorbed and converted Chaos Power. Intermediate Demon Bloodline skill EXP +168...]

[Hint: Your Intermediate Demon Bloodline has reached its maximum level.]

[Hint: A power of the same origin has been detected — Seed of Greed.]

[Hint: As you possess the Seed of Greed, you may directly advance to Advanced Demon Bloodline.]

[Hint: Your Intermediate Demon Bloodline has been removed. You have obtained the skill Advanced Demon Bloodline.]

Bloodline: Advanced Demon Bloodline.

(Description: Players may possess multiple bloodlines, but some are incompatible. Zombie clones and vampire bloodlines conflict; zombie clones can inherit only a very small portion of bloodline bonuses.)

Skill Rank: S+.

Level Restriction: Maximum Level 25. The current skill level cannot exceed your player level. You may search for ways to advance the bloodline further.

Derived Skill 1: Demon Bloodline.

Skill Description: Immediately grants an additional 450% maximum HP, Strength +27, Agility +17, Constitution +27, plus Strength equal to skill level × 4, Agility equal to skill level × 1.5, and Constitution equal to skill level × 4.

Derived Skill 2: Survival in Flame.

Derived Skill 3: Demonic Eye.

With the Demon Seed's help, his bloodline had advanced directly once again!

If the Chaos Power he'd absorbed from the earlier altars had been little more than drops of water, this time he'd turned on a tap and filled a whole basin!

He absorbed it greedily!

But as he drew in this enormous quantity of Chaos Power, Fang Heng noticed something besides the skill advancement: the Demon Seed's source within his sea of consciousness was being nourished rapidly!

The previously sluggish source was full of vitality again!

It had even grown a little stronger than before!

Upgrading his demon bloodline meant increasing his control over the Demon Seed's power in his sea of consciousness.

But it was clearly nowhere near enough!

The extra control amounted to less than a tenth of the increase in the seed's power!

Immediately, the Demon Seed's energy began escaping his control, spilling out of his body!

Dense black mist poured outward once more!

Meng Si and the others continued staring, exchanging stunned glances.

The power of a Demon Seed!

Even the aura leaking out made their souls tremble.

And Fang Heng didn't seem to be in a good condition.

Was he unable to control this power completely?

Within the black mist, Fang Heng struggled to restrain the turmoil in his sea of consciousness while extracting every last trace of Chaos Power from the altar.

By the time he was finished, his sea of consciousness was already showing signs of collapse.

He couldn't go on!

He had to stop this immediately!

Fang Heng turned his wrist and used the last of his strength to smash the stone box he'd been carrying.

CRACK!

The container shattered!

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The dozen or so Glutton's Touch worms concealed inside immediately sensed his blood and fastened onto his shoulders and arms!

Their sharp mouthparts pierced his skin and plunged deep into his muscles, greedily drawing on his original power!

[Hint: You have been affected by Glutton's Touch. For the duration of this effect, your abilities will be continuously drained by Glutton's Touch...]

Seeing the notifications appear, Fang Heng deliberately allowed the worms to continue draining him!

The worms fed as though they'd never enjoyed such a feast, sucking up his power with abandon.

Good!

The Demon Seed's rampaging energy was finally beginning to weaken!

Fang Heng sensed the source power in his sea of consciousness shrinking as the worms absorbed it. At last, he was slowly regaining control.

The problem was that the worms were also draining his other attributes, gradually weakening his body.

Glutton's Touch made no distinction between types of power. It swallowed everything indiscriminately!

As his sea of consciousness stabilized, Fang Heng gritted his teeth and shifted some attention to his surroundings, preparing to crush his teleportation scroll and escape the dangerous region.

Huh?

Wait. What was that?

Just as part of his awareness withdrew from his sea of consciousness, he noticed something inside the altar from the corner of his eye.

At its base lay an object resembling a runestone.

A familiar fluctuation emanated from it!

The power of the Seed of Envy!

Fang Heng raised his eyebrows and reached toward the rune.

[Hint: You have discovered the Seed of Envy's Sacrificial Rune.]

Description: A rune used by the Demon Seed of Envy to suppress a high-grade altar. It can continuously extract Chaos Power from the altar. If this item is destroyed or removed from the altar's vicinity, the Seed of Envy will detect the disturbance, and you may attract its attention (with a chance to trigger various special quests).

— End of Chapter 2092 —

Editing and Source Notes
  • Bloodline growth. Advanced Demon Bloodline increases maximum HP by 450% and grants level-scaled attribute bonuses; the specified upper level is 25.
  • Runestone risk. Removing or destroying Envy’s Sacrificial Rune can attract the Seed of Envy and trigger unknown events.

Chinese Expressions

  • 生龙活虎 (shēng lóng huó hǔ). Literally “lively as a dragon and a tiger.” Describes the formerly dormant Demon Seed returning to vigorous life; the imagery is explained here rather than inserted artificially.
