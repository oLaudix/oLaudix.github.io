Chapter 2084

Glutton's Touch

From original Chinese Chapter 2085 · 之触

No wonder the energy fluctuations had felt so strange.

Had Envy taken over an altar that originally belonged to Gluttony, absorbing power meant for the other Demon Seed?

"Now! Do it!"

A shout suddenly rang out from the edge of the cavern.

Fang Heng looked up.

Meng Si stood on a raised platform in the upper-right corner, his hands forming a rune seal before him. An aura resembling the power of a Demon Seed erupted from his body.

"Bind!"

At the same time, the other four players, each standing on a rocky ledge in another corner of the chamber, formed seals of their own.

Power surged from all five positions and expanded rapidly!

In an instant, a vast red sealing formation appeared, enclosing Fang Heng and the central stone altar.

"A seal?"

Fang Heng studied the dark-red barrier surrounding him with a slight frown.

It didn't appear to have any offensive power. Their intention was to trap him.

But what came next? How were they planning to defeat him?

Hiss! Hiss, hiss!

Fang Heng looked up. Three scarlet chains shot out of the players' hand seals, passed through the barrier from outside, and came flying at him from three directions.

He twisted aside to avoid them.

Did they really expect to restrain him with attacks like that?

Click. Click, click...

An odd cracking sound came from the stone altar.

What was that?

Fang Heng turned.

Dark-red worms were crawling out through the gaps between the altar stones. Each was about the length of a finger.

Huh?

Worms?

And those were...?

Before he could investigate further—

HISSSS!

The altar erupted with a violent hissing sound, spraying a thick cloud of gas outward.

Fang Heng sensed danger and immediately held his breath.

But the gas worked through contact with the skin alone.

A wave of weakness swept over him.

[Hint: You are being affected by an unknown power.]

[Hint: Your attributes have been partially weakened. You have entered a state of Imprisonment. Duration: 240 − 210.72 (reduced by your base attributes and an unknown effect) = 29.28 seconds.]

The restraint was incredibly powerful!

Affected by the gas, Fang Heng couldn't move at all. It was a spell-type effect, and even his Undying Body couldn't completely negate it.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

The worms crawling from the altar possessed remarkable jumping strength. They sprang down to the ground, then bounced toward Fang Heng.

Hiss!

The moment a worm landed on his body, it exposed a sharp mouthpart and plunged it straight through his skin, fastening its entire body to him.

Fang Heng's heart jolted.

What the hell were these things?!

Another notification appeared before his eyes.

[Hint: You are being affected by the special power, Glutton's Touch. For as long as the effect persists, Glutton's Touch will continuously drain your attributes and abilities. Drained attributes will gradually recover within twenty-four hours after Glutton's Touch dies.]

Fang Heng frowned.

Something that could continuously drain the power from his body?

Apparently, it was a special ability inherited from the Demon Seed of Gluttony.

He could feel his strength slowly slipping away, absorbed by the worms!

"Hahaha! We've got him!"

One of the players maintaining the seal cheered at the sight of the worms clinging to Fang Heng.

"Keep going!"

"Drain him dry!"

Seeing that Fang Heng still seemed ready to resist despite being affected by Glutton's Touch, Meng Si's expression tightened. He didn't dare let down his guard.

"Don't get careless! Quickly! Restrain him!"

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Three red chains immediately wrapped around Fang Heng, binding his arms and legs tightly.

Trapped, Fang Heng slowly closed his eyes.

More and more worms leaped onto him and attached themselves to his body.

Meng Si and his companions stared, growing increasingly astonished.

What was happening?

Glutton's Touch was used both in ritual sacrifices and to protect the altar.

Ordinary hellish creatures would be drained of their attributes and killed within ten or twenty seconds after even a single worm latched onto them.

How could this man have so many attached to him and still not be drained dry?

Whoosh!

The instant the half-minute imprisonment ended, Fang Heng regained control of his body and snapped his eyes open.

Crack! Crack, crack!

The red chains wrapped around him shattered one after another!

The players controlling them turned pale from the backlash. They abandoned the chains and concentrated on maintaining the outer sealing barrier.

Fang Heng glanced at Meng Si and the others beyond the seal. Then he slowly lifted a hand and began tearing the worms from his body by force.

Rip!

Their razor-sharp mouths had bitten deep into his muscles. Every worm he pulled free tore away a large patch of bloody flesh.

The gruesome sight made Meng Si and the others wince.

Slap!

One by one, Fang Heng ripped off the creatures and threw them to the ground.

Huh?

Suddenly, he let out a small sound of surprise and looked down.

The worms he'd torn free, still covered in his blood and flesh, began writhing and convulsing violently. Their bodies expanded almost instantly!

In the blink of an eye, each had become a gigantic worm more than two meters long!

One opened its enormous maw, displaying ring upon ring of sharp fangs, and lunged at Fang Heng.

"Maintain the seal! Hurry! Keep it under control!" Meng Si shouted.

This was Glutton's Touch's special ability!

In its first stage, it attached itself to an enemy and voraciously drained their power. Usually, this alone was enough to kill the victim.

But if an enemy somehow survived that stage and managed to break free, it entered a second stage.

Once thrown off, Glutton's Touch consumed all the power it had just absorbed, strengthening itself a hundredfold and transforming into a giant monster!

ROAR!

The worm roared and charged straight toward Fang Heng.

Fang Heng turned his head and cast a cold glance at the players maintaining the seal from the rocky ledges.

The moment their eyes met his, Meng Si and the others felt their hearts skip a beat.

What was he about to do?!

Whoosh!

Without even turning back, Fang Heng raised his hand toward the giant worm attacking him.

BOOM!

The second-stage Glutton's Touch exploded in midair!

Shattered!

Its flesh burst apart in every direction.

Boom! Boom, boom!

Within the next few seconds, the other transformed Glutton's Touch worms exploded one after another, leaving only scattered heaps of bloody remains.

— End of Chapter 2084 —
