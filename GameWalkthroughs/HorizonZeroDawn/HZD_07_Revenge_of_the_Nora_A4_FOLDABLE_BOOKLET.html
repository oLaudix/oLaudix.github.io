07A · THE MISSED FLOWER
Start at Sona’s excavation; then rendezvous at Red Echoes
□
01. Before leaving the excavation, get Metal Flower Mark I (D).
Stand in the excavation pit where you found the recording. Find the construction crane at the northeast edge of the pit. From that crane, turn east and climb the grassy ramp out of the quarry; continue uphill, then turn north at the first opening. The pink/red triangle of flowers is on this ridge. Use Focus if vegetation hides it. Expected Metal Flowers: 5/30.
□
02. Select Revenge of the Nora and travel to Red Echoes.
Open Journal → Main Quests → Revenge of the Nora and follow its Go to Red Echoes marker. The meeting point is a hilltop camp north of Mother’s Crown / west of Devil’s Grief. Use the marked roads; if riding, dismount before hostile machines. There may be Watchers, Scrappers and Sawtooths along the road; fighting them is optional.
□
03. Rest at the marked campfire to begin the assault.
At Red Echoes, approach the specific campfire marked by the quest and choose Rest at Campfire; merely lighting a different campfire does not advance the quest. Let the Sona/Varl scene finish. Afterward, the map displays three cultist camps to the east.
□
04. Cross toward the westernmost of the three camps.
From Red Echoes, head downhill east, cross the river using the fallen-log bridge, and light the nearby campfire if available. Follow the road east/northeast toward the closest, western camp. Stop in the tall grass before the first enemies see you.
CHECKPOINT You are outside the westernmost Eclipse outpost. The next page begins with its alarm and enemies.

07J · COUNTERATTACK
Destroy the cache • finish the battle • achievement
□
37. Shoot the Blaze cache with a FIRE Arrow.
Aim at the green barrels / canister stack, not the metal wall. One Fire Arrow starts the chain explosion. Watch the destruction and the Nora assault begin.
□
38. Immediately deal with the Corrupted Fire Bellowback.
As the Nora charge through, use cover while identifying the Bellowback’s large cargo sac and gullet. Aim for those exposed components; exploding a sac is particularly effective. Dodge fire blasts and charges. Let the Nora keep other enemies occupied.
□
39. Finish the remaining Cultists and machines.
Shoot Corrupted Watchers and exposed human shooters from the edge of the fight. Keep changing cover, healing as needed. When the final enemy falls, watch Sona’s aftermath scene. The main quest Revenge of the Nora completes.
□
40. Loot the arena and talk to Sona/Varl if available.
Before leaving, collect useful weapon coils and machine parts, and search the visible supply crates. Speak to Sona and Varl for any remaining dialogue. Do not start traveling toward Daytower yet.
ACHIEVEMENT
Victorious with the War-Chief
Complete the mission and defeat the Eclipse at the Ring of Metal. This should unlock automatically when the quest completes.
OPTIONAL LORE“Holo-Haunting” — World text datapoint (nearby). After the assault, look west of the Ring of Metal, near the campfire outside its wall. The record lies under a small concrete overhang/collapsed section of wall. If you have to leave the stadium to the west, it is an easy short search.
CHECKPOINT Main quest finished. Chapter 08 will continue with the route toward Daytower and remaining Nora-area cleanup.

07I · SOLO INFILTRATION
Through the ditch and grass to a clear shot at the depot
□
32. Remove the two Cultists directly below your overlook.
With Focus, look west/southwest toward a wooden platform and wall. One Cultist patrols between that area and the pool; another works near the tall grass. Wait for the patrol to move out of the Bellowback’s sight, headshot the patroller from above, then descend and Silent Strike the worker from the grass.
□
33. Follow the ditch WEST along the exterior wall.
From the eliminated worker, turn west and crouch into the sunken trench beside the wall. Use crates and a wooden ramp as cover. Mark the Bellowback to your northeast, the Watcher to your southwest and the guards ahead before moving.
□
34. Cross the pool enclosure, then take the ramp NORTH.
When the Watcher walks away, continue west into the low enclosure with a pool; quickly turn north up its ramp into tall grass. Wait for any patrols to turn away, then move west into the broken-walled ruins containing more grass. Keep crouched.
□
35. Stealth-kill the two Cultists inside the ruins.
From the western grass, look southwest. There is a patrolling Cultist and a stationary Cultist. Let the patrol pass near the grass, eliminate him quietly, then move behind the stationary guard. Use the next southeast exit from these ruins.
□
36. Reach the final grass patch and line up the Blaze.
Outside that southeast exit, crouch in the grass. Look south for the prominent green Blaze stockpile. There may be two patrols between you and it: lure or Silent Strike them. Once no one blocks the shot, switch to your Hunter Bow with FIRE Arrows.
CHECKPOINT You can clearly see the green Blaze depot from concealment. Only now fire at it.

07B · CAMP ONE
Western camp • two Corrupted Watchers • first vessel
□
05. Mark the entire western camp with Focus.
From the grassy approach, scan the Corrupted Watchers outside the eastern side of the square-shaped ruined building and the human Cultists within. Tag as many as possible. Look for the alarm so no one can run to it unseen.
□
06. Enter from the northwest side, if you want stealth.
Circle to the northwest corner of the rectangular ruins; look for a ladder or climbable rubble giving access to an upper ledge. Pick off the sentry near the alarm, then drop down and disable the alarm using its interaction. If the fight starts early, focus on stopping a Cultist from sounding it.
□
07. Eliminate everyone in the camp.
Use headshots on isolated humans and Fire Arrows on Blaze containers when enemies cluster. Nora braves will assist if you are detected. Kill all remaining Cultists and Corrupted Watchers; verify the quest camp counter updates to 1/3. Loot the supply crates.
□
08. Pick up Ancient Vessel “Miriam” before leaving.
Leave the southern side of this first camp and follow the road heading east. Just south of the road, near the ruin, find a small purple-highlighted Ancient Debris pile and loot the vessel. It is not inside an enemy’s inventory. Expected Vessels: 2/12.
OPTIONAL LORE“Luna Here I Come” — World text datapoint. From the Vessel area, look north of the road among the large western Devil’s Grief ruins. The scan target sits on an elevated, broken metal/concrete support jutting from a ruined wall. Look upward with Focus rather than checking only the ground. It concerns lunar work.
CHECKPOINT First camp cleared. Miriam obtained. Continue toward the southern camp by the road and gas station.

07C · SOUTHBOUND COLLECTIBLES
Ancient Vessel USRC • old gas station • optional records
□
09. Head south to the USRC Ancient Vessel.
From the Miriam debris just south of Camp One, continue south around the broken buildings and through the woods until you hit a road. Follow the road northeast. When you pass ruins on the south side, inspect the purple Ancient Debris pile near the roadside for Vessel “USRC”. Watch for corrupted machines. Expected Vessels: 3/12.
□
10. Reach the ruined gas station and light its campfire.
From the USRC debris, follow the road uphill east to the roofless/ruined gas-station forecourt. There is a nearby campfire. Use it as your return landmark for the next several collectibles.
OPTIONAL LORE“All the Same” — World text datapoint. Stand beside the old gas pumps in the forecourt. Enable Focus and scan the recording embedded in / next to one of the pumps.
OPTIONAL LORE“China-Sick” — World text datapoint. Still at the same station, go to its northeast corner. Search the rusted wall-mounted box / damaged wall with Focus.
OPTIONAL LORE“Mexico” — World text datapoint. A small optional search northeast of the gas-station campfire, in a ruined structure beside two rusted cars. Look low among the wreckage.
For the assault Keep the gas-station campfire in mind. The southern outpost lies east of the station, but entering directly along that road is not the easiest stealth approach.
CHECKPOINT USRC obtained and the gas-station campfire activated. Next: southern Eclipse outpost.

07H · THE RING OF METAL
Follow Varl • coordinated eliminations • find the Blaze
□
28. Stay with Varl until he helps you enter the arena.
Follow him north from the tower. At the Ring of Metal’s outer wall, follow his boost/climb interaction to reach the high interior route. Move slowly; the main camp contains Cultists, Corrupted Watchers and dangerous machines.
□
29. Take out the first pair of Cultists together.
At the high platform, stop when Varl assigns targets. Mark them with Focus. Aim at the one he indicates, then use a precise headshot (or Strike from Above if prompted and safe); he handles his partner. Do not jump into the camp before the objective moves forward.
□
30. Follow Varl and repeat the synchronized takedowns.
Advance across the ruined ledges into the next cover area. One pair can be killed from above; later Varl tells you to take the left-hand Cultist. Enter the tall grass and use Silent Strike on your man while Varl kills the other. Regroup afterward, then climb the next ledge.
□
31. Examine the arena and identify the giant Blaze cache.
At the overlook, Aloy and Varl can see the Cultists’ supply of green Blaze canisters and dormant Corruptors. You cannot safely shoot the cache from this initial viewing point. Varl stays behind; you must infiltrate the lower camp alone.
Why stealth matters There is a Corrupted Fire Bellowback and many guards below. Fighting the whole area before blowing the cache makes the next section harder than necessary. Use Focus marking and cover instead.
CHECKPOINT You are alone at the high overlook; the objective is to sneak close enough to shoot the Blaze.

07G · THE TALLEST TOWER
Metal Flower Mark I (G) • Vantage “Denver Stadium”
□
23. Follow the quest marker to the Tallest Tower.
Leave the northern camp, follow the road southeast, take the eastbound fork, then go east/northeast until the enormous broken tower at the Devil’s Grief stadium comes into view. Stop before talking to Sona. Light the tower’s campfire and loot any visible supply chests.
□
24. Detour SOUTH for Metal Flower Mark I (G).
From the tower, open your Metal Flower map and select the search circle directly south/southeast of Denver Stadium. Head south toward the southeastern Devil’s Grief campfire by water. From that fire, go east/northeast around a large ruined building to the flower triangle by the outside wall / water’s edge. Beware Corrupted Sawtooths; sneak by if needed. Expected Flowers: 7/30.
□
25. Return to the Tallest Tower and climb for Denver Stadium.
Go back to the tower before speaking to Sona. Enter the interior and head toward its southeast corner, near burning torches. Find yellow-painted handholds. Climb the wall, cross the upper ledges and a narrow beam, then use the next handholds to reach the highest accessible projection ledge.
□
26. Activate the “Denver Stadium” Vantage.
Stand at the marked eye/Focus point overlooking the stadium. Activate the view, listen to the narration and read the Vantage note if you like its lore. Rappel back down using the tower’s descent point. Expected Vantages: 3/12.
□
27. Speak with Sona at the base of the tower.
Once the collectible detour is finished, talk to Sona and watch the assault briefing. She and Varl remain by the camp. Then follow Varl when the next quest objective appears.
CHECKPOINT Vantage and Flower obtained; Sona briefing complete. Next: infiltrate the Ring of Metal with Varl.

07D · CAMP TWO
Southern outpost • approach from the hill, not the main road
□
11. Approach Camp Two from the SOUTH.
From the gas station, take the road east briefly, then circle around the ruins to the south instead of charging straight into the camp. Climb the hill overlooking its southern edge. Nora braves may already be waiting in the grass there.
□
12. Scan the outpost, including enemies away from the center.
Use Focus from the hill to tag all Cultists. This southern camp is more open and has a central alarm; several enemies patrol at the perimeter. Watch for a Cultist near Blaze containers at the eastern edge.
□
13. Disable the alarm and clear Camp Two.
Start by headshotting exposed sentries from the hill or detonating the exposed Blaze. Use tall grass to approach the alarm in the center once there is an opening, and interact with it. Kill the remaining Cultists, loot the camp, and wait for the counter to show 2/3. If detected, Nora help is available.
□
14. Return to the gas-station campfire for the south-side sweep.
Leave the camp via the road back west toward the gas station. The next two datapoints and the KZ vessel are south of this campfire, not in the cultist compound. Do these now before heading north.
OPTIONAL LORE“Cram” — World text datapoint. From the gas-station campfire, go south and search inside the first ruined building to the east (left as you walk south). Enable Focus along the broken walls.
OPTIONAL LORE“She’s the One” — World text datapoint. Continue south of the station toward the small hill patrolled by Corrupted Scrappers. The record is on/near the top of that hill with trees. Clear the machines first if needed.
CHECKPOINT Two camps cleared. Next: KZ Vessel, Metal Flower I(E), then northern camp.

07E · SOUTHWEST SWEEP
KZ Ancient Vessel • Metal Flower Mark I (E)
□
15. Collect Ancient Vessel “KZ”.
From the small hill south of the gas station, continue south/southwest around the ruins. Open your purchased Ancient Vessel map and aim for the southern Devil’s Grief search circle. In the lower ruined paved area, look for a square broken archway/concrete wall and the purple Ancient Debris beneath/near it, slightly southwest of the search icon. Expected Vessels: 4/12.
□
16. Turn WEST through the woods to Metal Flower Mark I (E).
From KZ, travel west and slightly south to a campfire in the trees. From that fire, go north toward a road. You will pass a ruined building; search the second low ruined building just south of that road, checking its eastern interior corner for the red/pink flower triangle. Machines (sometimes Sawtooths) can patrol here. Expected Flowers: 6/30.
□
17. Scan any NEW machines seen here (optional detour).
This area may have a Freeze Bellowback southwest of the woodland campfire and Broadheads northwest. If they are new in your Notebook, scan them with Focus from safety; you do not have to fight them now. Their scans count toward the 26 original machine entries.
□
18. Return to the road and head for Camp Three.
From the flower ruin, use the map to select the remaining northern Eclipse outpost. Return east/northeast toward the road network and approach the outpost from the east-side road; do not try to cut over impassable ruined walls.
CHECKPOINT Vessels 4/12, Flowers 6/30. The only camp left is the northern outpost.

07F · CAMP THREE
Northern outpost • high-ground entry • last alarm
□
19. Go around the camp to the high ground at its NORTHWEST.
Approach on the road east of the northern camp. Continue north past the first possible entrance, then take the westbound road through ruined buildings. Reach the ruin northwest of the camp and climb to the elevated Nora braves’ position.
□
20. Use Focus to reveal the hidden enemies.
From the ruined upper platform, mark Cultists on the visible platforms and along the ground. Some can stand in ditches or underpasses/tunnels beneath the camp, so scan both high and low. Identify the camp alarm before firing.
□
21. Thin the camp from the elevated ledge.
Headshot exposed Cultists while remaining behind the ruined wall. If they start searching, retreat into the tall grass west of the ledge and wait for suspicion to clear. Once the camp is mostly empty, descend into the grass and sneak toward the alarm.
□
22. Disable its alarm, kill the stragglers and loot.
Destroy/disable the last alarm and locate any remaining guards in the trenches. When all are dead, the objective should show 3/3 camps cleared. Loot supplies and craft Fire Arrows before leaving.
Human headshots Your earlier notebook had many headshots, but the related achievement requires 30 human enemies killed by headshots, not 30 headshot impacts. These outposts are a good opportunity to finish qualifying kills.
CHECKPOINT All three Eclipse outposts cleared. Next objective: Go to the Tallest Tower and meet Sona.
