04A · First Vantage & Corrupted Zone

CHAPTER 04
PAGE 1 / 3
FROM MAIN EMBRACE GATE. You finished Chapter 03 at the gate after speaking to Varl. The War-Chief's Trail is available. For now, do not follow the yellow Sona marker: this page collects the nearby Vantage and clears a Corrupted Zone first.
  1. 1. Set your starting point at the campfire immediately outside the Main Embrace Gate.

    Face away from the gate (toward the Sacred Lands). Open the map and look for the Vantage search marker southeast of the gate (Air Combat Academy). You already bought the Vantage map at the gate. Put a custom waypoint on that search area; you are not searching the Embrace behind you.

  2. 2. Follow the road northeast from the gate and cross the river on the wooden bridge.

    Stay on the road outside the palisade until you reach the first bridge. Cross it. On the far side turn southeast, toward the Vantage search circle rather than toward the Nora Hunting Grounds uphill to the northeast.

  3. 3. Cross the next river and walk south past a Strider Site.

    There is no bridge at this second crossing: wade through the shallow water. Keep following the southern road until you see a large ruined tank / armored vehicle partly sunk into the earth. This is the landmark to stop at.

  4. 4. At the ruined tank, leave the road WEST toward the rocky hill.

    The Vantage is on top of this hill. Do not search the ground around the tank. Walk north around the base of the rock, looking for yellow-highlighted handholds along its northern / northwestern side. Climb them to the top.

  5. 5. Collect “Vantage — Air Combat Academy”.

    At the top, use the Focus on the purple Vantage icon / cache overlooking the ruins. Activate the view, not just the supply crate. This should count as Vantage 1/12 and award First Vantage found if you have not collected another.

  6. 6. Go SOUTH from the Vantage hill to the nearby Corrupted Zone.

    Open the map: the Corrupted Zone sits south of the Vantage, near a ruined church/steeple. It is a level-15 area with four Corrupted Scrappers. Approach crouched from its northern side and mark enemies with the Focus.

  7. 7. Destroy every Corrupted Scrapper; verify the zone clears.

    Separate the machines with cover and use fire attacks if you have them. Shoot exposed parts, dodge their lunges, and heal as necessary. If you are underleveled on Hard, you can return later; it does not lock. When the red zone disappears, this counts as Corrupted Zone 1/11 and may unlock First Corrupted Zone cleared.

  8. 8. Return NORTH to the river bridge and the road crossroads.

    Retrace the route toward the first bridge you crossed from the gate. Do not head all the way back to the gate. Near the bridge / crossroads, find the Nora hunter Cren, who directs you to the Hunting Grounds up the hill. Continue on page 2.

04B · Nora Hunting Grounds

CHAPTER 04
PAGE 2 / 3
THREE TRIALS · THREE BLAZING SUNS. At Cren near the bridge, head uphill to the northeast to the Nora Hunting Grounds lodge. Each trial begins when you descend the starting zipline. Speak to the Keeper again after each attempt to claim the mark or retry.
  1. 9. At the Hunting Grounds, knock over BOTH nearby Grazer dummies.

    Before talking to the Keeper, inspect the path and wooden platform around the lodge: one dummy is near the steps on the approach and the other is just below/near the merchant's side. Use heavy spear attacks. Your running total should increase from 13 to 15 / 23 if you have the previous 13.

  2. 10. Check your weapons BEFORE starting any trial.

    Your original Tripcaster may only place Shock Wires. For the third trial you need Blast Wires, so buy the Carja Tripcaster from the merchant beside the Keeper if you do not own one. Craft several Blast Wires. A Blast Sling is optional, but it makes it easier to scare Grazers down a selected path.

  3. 11. Talk to the Keeper; accept “Hunting for the Lodge” and begin Parts Alone.

    Choose Parts Alone Trial. Target: tear off 10 Blaze canisters from live Grazers in 1:30 for a Blazing Sun. These are the cylinders on their backs. Use ordinary impact arrows, not fire arrows; burning them can explode them rather than tear them off.

  4. 12. Parts Alone — shoot canisters, not the Grazers’ bodies.

    Ride the trial zipline and crouch near its landing area. Activate Concentration and shoot canisters off several different Grazers as quickly as you can, before they stampede. Watchers may interfere; keep your focus on canisters. Return to the Keeper to confirm a Blazing Sun (1:30 or faster). Retry here if necessary.

  5. 13. Before Logpile, identify the log traps from the starting platform.

    Choose Logpile Trial. Target: kill 3 machines with falling logs in 2:00. From above, locate the suspended piles of cut logs beside the paths the Grazers take. You trigger a trap by shooting its support / fastening; direct bow kills do not count.

  6. 14. Logpile — send the herd into a narrow path, then drop logs onto it.

    Take the zipline, make noise or fire a shot to startle the herd and watch where it runs. The northern narrow ravine has useful log traps. Move where you can see the herd beneath a log pile; shoot the fastening only when machines are underneath. Return to the Keeper for Blazing Sun (2:00 or faster). If you miss, retry after you know their escape route.

  7. 15. Blast Wire — lay explosive wires across a Grazer escape path BEFORE spooking them.

    Choose Blast Wire Trial. Target: kill 6 Grazers with Blast Wires in 3:00. Equip the Carja Tripcaster and its Blast Wire ammo. Zipline down, then stretch multiple wires across a wide section of the path the herd is likely to take. Attach each wire between two solid spots across the path, not lengthwise.

  8. 16. Scare the Grazers across your wires; claim all three Blazing Suns.

    Once wires are placed, approach or shoot near the far side of the herd to drive it toward your explosives. Add more wires if some Grazers escape. Return to the Keeper and verify all three Blazing Sun marks are shown in the trials menu. That completes the two “at one Ground” achievements if not already earned.

  9. 17. Leave the lodge; return SOUTHWEST to Cren and the river crossroads.

    Do not continue northeast into new territory. At the fork near Cren, the route toward our next flower goes NORTHWEST along the central road through a Strider Site. The last page begins at that fork.

PAGE 2 CHECK: Two Nora Hunting Grounds dummies (+2). Three trial marks should read Blazing Sun (1:30 / 2:00 / 3:00). If you lack the Carja Tripcaster, mark Blast Wire for a later return rather than pretending it is complete.

04C · Flower & Mother’s Rise

CHAPTER 04
PAGE 3 / 3
ROAD NORTHWEST · STOP AT MOTHER’S RISE. You are back at the river crossroads near Cren. The main quest remains The War-Chief's Trail, but this short detour collects a nearby Metal Flower and introduces a required ally-related side quest.
  1. 18. From the crossroads, take the CENTRAL road NORTHWEST through the Strider Site.

    Do not take the path all the way west back to the gate, or the northeastern path uphill to the Hunting Grounds. Follow the central road northwest until you see destroyed wooden cottages in the area of the Sawtooth hunt you did with Rost.

  2. 19. Pick up Metal Flower Mark I (C) behind the destroyed huts.

    Open the world map and look for the Metal Flower search circle near these ruined huts. Go to the rocky raised ground on the WEST side / behind the huts. The flower is sitting in the open on a small stone ledge, with its familiar purple flowers. No rock-breaking, zipline or ruins entrance is required. You should now have at least 3 / 30 Metal Flowers.

  3. 20. Return to the northwest road and continue toward the massacre-site objective.

    Follow the road north, then northeast. Light the campfire by the road and merchant. At the upcoming forks, stay on the route trending north/northwest toward the objective. You will eventually pass under a watchtower; there is a fork beyond it.

  4. 21. At the watchtower fork, DETOUR SOUTHWEST to Mother’s Rise.

    Before crossing the bridge northwest toward the massacre site, take the southwest branch, cross the bridge leading toward the settlement, and climb the sloping ramp to Mother’s Rise. Light its campfire so it becomes a convenient return point. Do not advance the massacre-site objective yet.

  5. 22. Mother’s Rise dummy #1 — just beyond the uphill entrance.

    Starting at the campfire on the approach to Mother’s Rise, climb the small rocks behind the fire / left side of the first little house. Find the Grazer dummy there and knock it down with a heavy spear attack.

  6. 23. Mother’s Rise dummy #2 — beside the cooking fire.

    Stay in the settlement. Look for the cooking spit with food over the fire on the opposite side of the small cluster of houses. The second dummy stands beside it. Heavy-attack it. Your total should now be 17 / 23 if you were at 15 after the Hunting Grounds.

  7. 24. Speak with Yan in the NORTHERN part of Mother’s Rise.

    Yan is the Nora man praying about his sister. Ask about Nakoa to accept the side quest A Daughter’s Vengeance. This is part of the chain needed for All allies joined. Only accept it now; its next objective leads away from our current route.

  8. 25. Speak to Fia about “Insult to Injury” if the quest is offered.

    Fia is the local healer concerned with medicinal Dreamwillow. If Insult to Injury is already in your journal from Enara at Mother’s Heart, use the quest marker to speak to Fia; otherwise accept the quest here. We will handle its actual objectives when traveling near the massacre site / Jun’s cabin.

  9. 26. STOP at the Mother’s Rise campfire. Save.

    Leave The War-Chief's Trail incomplete for now. The next printable chapter starts at Mother’s Rise, then covers the nearby massacre site, Sona’s trail, and the relevant side-quest detours. Don’t travel to Meridian or Daytower yet.
