09B · THE CART AND THE TRAIL
□
01. Check the Nakoa quest before walking out.
In Journal → Side Quests, select A Daughter’s Vengeance. It should now direct you to Lonesome Rock. You previously spoke to Yan, Solai, Balahn and Zaid; the Daytower dialogue is finished.
□
02. Optional lore: two readable glyphs inside Daytower.
If you have not collected them, use your Focus on the fort’s eastern-side table for “The Derangement”; look above the northern campfire on a raised platform with barrels/sandbags for “The Claim.” These are lore only; skip if not convenient.
□
03. Stock ammunition; head west through the opened fort gate.
Buy ammo materials and heal. Follow the marked road into Carja territory toward Lonesome Rock (its quest marker is northeast of Cut-Cliffs / Meridian). If a Stormbird patrols the valley below, you can ride or run past; fighting it is not required now.
□
04. Approach the Lonesome Rock outpost, not a Bandit Camp icon.
Follow the Nakoa quest marker to the Carja-guarded outpost. This is a story-related outpost; it does not count as one of the six Bandit Camps. Save at a nearby campfire if available.

09B · THE CART AND THE TRAIL

□
05. Enter Lonesome Rock and let the confrontation play out.
Approach the outpost gate. The three guards become hostile after the encounter. Defeat them, then stop fighting and do not leave until you investigate the cart.
□
06. Inspect the prisoner cart twice, using Focus and interaction.
In the outpost courtyard find the wooden cart beside the stairs. Scan it with the Focus, then approach the highlighted cart and inspect it to advance the objective. If needed, loot the guards and check for a supply crate.
□
07. Highlight the actual wheel tracks with Focus.
After examining the cart, activate Focus again and identify the tracks on the ground. Lock onto the trail so it remains visible while moving. This is the important navigation reference — do not follow an unrelated road marker.
□
08. Follow those tracks to the second fortified compound.
The trail leaves Lonesome Rock and continues to an old compound with guards. Follow the highlighted track all the way until the objective changes. There may be machines en route; fight or bypass them as you prefer.

If you are close to the Palace, you may scan “The Mad Sun-King” near the entrance and “The Liberation” near the palace guards’ seating. The reading is worthwhile; these glyphs do not affect 79/79.

HUNTER’S BLIND — CONTINUED

34. Speak to Talanah and Ilsadi after the fight.

Select Find the cause of the Glinthawk attacks. Follow the quest marker upriver; destroy the two feeding Glinthawks among the machine carcasses. Use Focus near the far end of the carcass area.
□
36. Find the tracks at the back and climb upward.
Near the last Snapmaw corpse, locate the Focus trail leading toward handholds and wooden platforms. Follow the tracks and climb to the hunters’ encampment. Do not leave after just killing the Glinthawks.

Finish the conversation with Talanah near the hunters. Fast-travel to Meridian, re-enter the Lodge and complete the final dialogue (including Ahsis if marked). Confirm Hunter’s Blind is complete. Deadliest Game becomes available next.

09C · RESCUE NAKOA
09D · ENTER MERIDIAN
□
09. Scout the compound before crossing the wall.
Use Focus from outside to tag the archers on high walkways and patrolling guards. Enter through the damaged/broken wall and remove the enemies. Use headshots/stealth if convenient; alarms or detection do not invalidate this quest.
□
10. Locate the stairs going DOWN to the prison.
Once the courtyard is safe, go to the building/shed on the far side of the compound and use its interior staircase to the basement. Do not keep searching the roof. Speak with Nakoa and the two other captives.
□
11. Unlock the prisoners if prompted, then return outside.
Follow the current objective; if it requests a guard’s key, loot the marked guard and return to the captives. Once freed, head back out. Zaid arrives with reinforcements for the final encounter.
□
12. Defeat Zaid and finish Nakoa’s dialogue.
Zaid carries a rapid-fire Firespitter. Use walls and corners as cover while shooting between bursts; eliminate the nearby soldiers as needed. After the scene, talk to Nakoa until A Daughter’s Vengeance displays Completed.

09D · ENTER MERIDIAN

□
13. Switch back to the main story.
Select The City of the Sun in your Journal. Follow its waypoint to Meridian’s city entrance. You can clear nearby map icons on the way, but there is no need to hunt extra Bandit Camps just to open the city.
□
14. Speak to the checkpoint commander and then Erend.
At the city approach, interact with the checkpoint commander as the main quest requests. Erend joins you at the entrance. Exhaust any dialogue you want, then follow him into Meridian.
□
15. Stay with Erend through the crowd scene.
Follow his quest marker through the busy terraces and hear the dispute about his sister Ersa. Do not detour to the Lodge yet; Erend leads you directly to Olin’s apartment.
□
16. Enter Olin’s apartment with Erend.
Follow Erend into the apartment. The quest objective changes to Investigate Olin’s Apartment. Look around for world-building details, but the required clues are hidden beneath the floor.

09F · ENTER THE HUNTER’S LODGE
□
17. Find the suspicious carpet on the LOWER floor.
On the ground floor, turn on Focus and scan the rug/carpet in a corner of the main room. Examine it to expose the outline of a trapdoor; you cannot open it by hand.
□
18. Go upstairs, then climb the loft ladder.
Climb to the apartment’s second level, find the ladder leading into the loft, and approach the stack of metal ingots/crates. Push the stack over the edge. Its weight breaks open the trapdoor below.
□
19. Descend into the secret basement.
Return to the ground floor, drop through the now-open hatch and activate Focus. Investigate all the highlighted evidence instead of immediately leaving the cellar.
□
20. Read Olin’s two important lore records.
Scan and examine the wall map, the Hologram Datapoint “A Message for Olin” and the Scanned Glyph “Olin’s Journal.” These reveal key story information and are worth reading here before moving on.
□
21. Report to Erend — then STOP the main quest.
Return upstairs and talk to Erend. You will gain The Field of the Fallen and an objective to find Olin at an Excavation Site. Both can wait. Do not ride to the excavation yet: we are going to secure the optional Hunter’s Lodge chain first.

09F · ENTER THE HUNTER’S LODGE

□
22. Make Hunting for the Lodge your active errand.
If you already received Hunting for the Lodge from a Hunting Grounds Keeper, select it under Errands. Otherwise, speak to Ukan near the central market or simply approach the Hunter’s Lodge on the western side of Meridian.
□
23. Talk to Ligan at the Lodge entrance.
You need three Half Sun trial medals to qualify. Your Nora and Valleymeet Blazing Suns already exceed this. Ligan admits Aloy; a Hunting Grounds quest remaining in your journal does not invalidate those medals.
□
24. Go upstairs and speak to Sunhawk Ahsis.
In the main hall take either staircase to the upper floor and find Ahsis. Talk to him about joining. He directs you toward a sponsor.
□
25. Return downstairs to Talanah.
Find Talanah near the base of the stairs opposite the entrance and speak to her. Accept the assignment to gather trophies from specific machines. The quest items begin counting only NOW.

09G · YOUR NEW TROPHY HUNT
09H · HUNTER’S BLIND
□
26. Track the new machine-trophy objective.
After speaking to Talanah, the Journal and map mark valid machine sites. Required: 3 Sawtooth Trophies, 2 Ravager Trophies and 1 Stalker Trophy. Older kills and ordinary machine parts do not substitute for these items.
□
27. Hunt THREE Sawtooths at marked machine sites.
Follow the quest’s Sawtooth site markers and kill them. Fire is useful; stay mobile during lunges. Loot each carcass and confirm its quest trophy registered (0/3 → 3/3).
□
28. Hunt TWO Ravagers and remove a cannon if needed.
Ravagers resemble Sawtooths but carry a heavy Ravager Cannon. Tear the cannon off before killing a Ravager if you still need Tore off 5 heavy weapons; it also removes a dangerous attack. Loot both Ravager Trophies.
□
29. Find ONE Stalker and collect its trophy.
Track the marked Stalker site. Use Focus to spot its red proximity mines and dart gun, and watch for distortion from its cloak. Shock damage can control it. Kill, loot, and confirm 1/1 Stalker Trophy.
□
30. Return straight to Ahsis with all six trophies.
Fast-travel to Meridian and go upstairs in the Hunter’s Lodge. Hand the 3 + 2 + 1 trophies to Ahsis. The errand Hunting for the Lodge completes and Hunter’s Blind becomes available.

09H · HUNTER’S BLIND

□
31. Speak to Talanah in the Lodge again.
Under Side Quests, select Hunter’s Blind. Speak to Talanah downstairs, then follow her out and talk to her once more for her introduction to Ilsadi, the villager requesting help.
□
32. Travel to Lone Light using the new quest marker.
The quest points to Lone Light outside Meridian. Use a campfire or follow the road to the marked village; don’t mistake it for the separate Lonesome Rock outpost where you rescued Nakoa.
□
33. Defend Lone Light from the Glinthawks.
Upon arrival, Glinthawks attack in waves. Scan one if it is new. Shoot their Freeze sacs or use a Ropecaster to bring them down, then finish them while grounded. Kill all waves until the objective changes.
