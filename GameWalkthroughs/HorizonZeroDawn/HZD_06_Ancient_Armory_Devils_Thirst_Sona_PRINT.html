HORIZON ZERO DAWN REMASTERED · PLAYTHROUGH 179-ACHIEVEMENT ROUTE · CHAPTER 06

Chapter 06 · Ancient Armory, Devil’s Thirst & Sona

Begin at Mother’s Crown campfire after Chapter 05. Follow the instructions in quest order; lore entries are optional unless they are required by the current quest. No need to hunt lore elsewhere.

06A · Mother’s Crown → Ancient Armory

START: Mother’s Crown campfire, where Chapter 05 stopped. GOAL: Metal Flower Mark I (F), armory opened with your TWO Power Cells.
  1. 1. Turn back through the SOUTH gate of Mother’s Crown.

    From the village campfire, walk downhill through the settlement to the southern gate. Set the Metal Flower search area east of Mother’s Rise as a map marker (if you bought the Metal Flower map). Do not follow Sona’s story marker yet.

  2. 2. Follow the road south toward Mother’s Rise, then head EAST toward the old tower.

    At the road junction near Mother’s Rise, head east toward the river and the ruined tower on a rocky mound. The Ancient Armory is east of Mother’s Rise, not in the settlement itself. Look for the fallen radio/telegraph mast leaning over the rocks; that is your landmark.

  3. 3. OPTIONAL LORE — “Hartz Wins Bahamas” (World text datapoint).

    If passing the northwestern outskirts of Devil’s Thirst, look beside an old car at a ruined gas-station structure. Scan the datapoint with Focus. This is a short extra search; skip it if the gas station is off your route.

  4. 4. Climb to the hole beside the collapsed radio tower.

    Use the yellow-marked climbing holds on the rocky hill below the fallen mast, reach the top, then drop into the opening in the ground. Descend by the ledges/handholds, not by jumping into the deepest shaft.

  5. 5. Swim into the bunker and pick up Metal Flower — Mark I (F).

    Follow the water passage toward the metal facility. Before continuing fully into the armory, get out onto the left-hand bank of the pool (left as you face into the bunker). The Flower stands amid plants. Collect it now: expected total 4 / 30.

  6. 6. Walk deeper into the bunker and approach the sealed armor door.

    Find the visible armor behind a locked barrier. At the bank of five hololocks beside the first sealed door, insert your two collected Power Cells into the unpowered sockets. If both are in your inventory, the lock can be activated now.

  7. 7. Set the FIRST five hololocks to UP → RIGHT → DOWN → LEFT → UP.

    Think of each lock as a clock face: 12 o’clock, 3, 6, 9, 12. The first door opens. Walk into the armor chamber; this is only the first lock, not the final unlock.

  8. 8. OPTIONAL LORE — scan BOTH Ultraweave files inside the chamber.

    “Ultraweave Progress” is on a shelf near the entrance; “Ultraweave Trials” is by the equipment in the center. Look around with Focus and read them. Check the next lock; it needs three more Power Cells we will obtain during later story quests. Leave the armor in place.

STAGE CHECK: Ancient Armory discovered; first lock open; Metal Flowers 4/30; 2 cells installed safely. You do NOT need to find more cells now.

06B · Ancient Armory → Devil’s Thirst

SAME REAL EXIT as your entry. Collect one Ancient Vessel, then reach the Tallneck ruins from the SOUTH.
  1. 9. Leave the bunker by retracing the WATER passage and climbing shaft.

    From the armor chamber, turn around, go back through the first unlocked door, swim out, and climb the yellow holds to the fallen radio tower. Use the same exit you entered. You do not need to open the second armor lock now.

  2. 10. OPTIONAL LORE — “Schott v. Frost” (World text datapoint).

    Near the bunker, look east across the water in an old, broken building. Scan the debris/ruin for this text record. It is outside the bunker, not beside the armor.

  3. 11. Collect Ancient Vessel — “Arches” near the river waterfall.

    Follow the river east of Mother’s Rise toward the waterfall at the southwest edge of Devil’s Thirst. Check the southern bank, immediately east of the waterfall, for a small ruined structure. Search its purple-marked Ancient Debris pile; that is the vessel. Expected: 1 / 12 Ancient Vessels.

  4. 12. Travel NORTHEAST into the Devil’s Thirst ruins.

    Follow the river/road toward the cluster of tall, broken Old World buildings on the map, east of Mother’s Rise. As you come from the south, find and light the campfire on the southern/eastern edge of the ruins before exploring. Use this as your return point.

  5. 13. OPTIONAL LORE — “Refugee Museum Opens” near the old water tower.

    While approaching the southern/southeastern edge of the Tallneck’s walking loop, check the ground at the base of a rusted water/steel tower for this World text datapoint. Activate Focus; do not search the top of the tower.

  6. 14. OPTIONAL LORE — “Haere Mai” in a low ruined building.

    From the Devil’s Thirst campfire, check the ruins east/southeast of it, among broken walls at ground level. Scan the old-world datapoint. If another ruin is directly between you and the Tallneck, finish this short stop before crossing the area.

  7. 15. OPTIONAL LORE — “Jeff Andreatis Show” in the ruined building on a rise.

    Look north of the same Devil’s Thirst campfire toward a ruined building on a low hill, close to the Devil’s Thirst location icon. Search its interior with Focus. This is an optional record, not part of the Tallneck activity.

  8. 16. Find the Tallneck’s patrol loop and clear the WATCHERS beside its ruins.

    Stand south of the huge circling Tallneck. It patrols around two ruined buildings. Scan any new machine species before killing them. Clear or silently evade the Watchers in the eastern/southeastern ruin; you need an upper-floor jump point, not a ladder on the Tallneck’s legs.

STAGE CHECK: Ancient Vessel “Arches” 1/12; Devil’s Thirst campfire activated. You are near the Tallneck’s jump building.

06C · Tallneck, Vantage, and nearby lore

GOAL: first Tallneck override + Colorado Springs Vantage. Then turn to the Sanctuary errand already accepted from Den.
  1. 17. Climb the ruined building INSIDE the Tallneck’s patrol loop.

    Use the yellow-painted handholds and broken floors of the ruined structure east/southeast of the Tallneck. Reach the upper floor facing its neck. Stop at the outward-facing ledge rather than dropping to the ground.

  2. 18. Jump onto the Tallneck’s NECK as it walks past.

    Wait until its neck platforms are level with your ledge. Make a running jump to a yellow-marked spine/antenna platform, then climb from spine to spine until you reach the dish-shaped head. You cannot override it from the ground.

  3. 19. Override the Tallneck and rappel down.

    At the top, perform the Override interaction. This should unlock “First Tallneck Overridden”, reveal a substantial map area and give you one skill point. Rappel using the prompt at the edge. The Observer Log machine datapoint is obtained automatically.

  4. 20. Go NORTH from the Tallneck to the Colorado Springs Vantage building.

    Open your map and mark the Vantage search circle directly north of the Devil’s Thirst Tallneck. Enter the tall broken building within that circle from its northwest / billboard side; look for yellow handholds. Do not hunt for the Vantage down at street level.

  5. 21. Climb inside the tower, including the long jump across its interior.

    Ascend the wall to the interior ledge. Follow the yellow-edged platforms northeast, make the running jump east across the gap, climb the pillar, then work back west/up to the highest accessible platform. Keep following the marked holds rather than trying to scale bare concrete.

  6. 22. Activate Vantage — “Colorado Springs”.

    Find the Supply Crate and eye-shaped projection point on the upper west-facing ledge. Use Focus at the viewpoint and activate its audio narration; the full story is part of the Vantage collectible. Expected 2 / 12 Vantages, if you took Air Combat Academy in Chapter 04.

  7. 23. OPTIONAL LORE — “UK Vets Struggle” and “Leaks Spark Fears”.

    Both World text records are north of the Tallneck area, near the ruined buildings beyond the log bridge and the road/campfire heading northeast. Use Focus at the ruined building north of the log bridge for “UK Vets Struggle”; look around the nearby north-side ruins for “Leaks Spark Fears.” If you are leaving the zone, do not spend ages on these.

  8. 24. Switch journal to the Sanctuary errand.

    You accepted this from Den at Mother’s Crown in Chapter 05. The marker lies north of the Tallneck, among Devil’s Thirst ruins. Do not follow Sona’s camp marker yet. Prepare fire ammunition, traps, healing, and enough arrows for two Sawtooths.

STAGE CHECK: Tallneck: Devil’s Thirst 1/5; Vantages 2/12. Next job is to rescue Den’s missing braves.

06D · Sanctuary: rescue Den’s braves

FROM THE VANTAGE AREA → NORTHERN RUINS → TWO SAWTOOTHS → REPORT TO DEN.
  1. 25. Enter the Sanctuary search area and inspect the Corrupted Watcher remains.

    Use the errand marker to reach the street north of the Tallneck. At the old battleground, activate Focus and scan the dead Corrupted Watcher, then interact with the corpse. This reveals a trail.

  2. 26. Highlight the trail and follow it WEST through the streets.

    Lock the tracks in Focus so their path stays visible. Follow them until they lead to a ruined building containing two live Sawtooths. Before charging in, look up at its raised platforms for the stranded Nora braves.

  3. 27. Kill BOTH Sawtooths before approaching the braves.

    Place a few Tripcaster Shock Wires across entrances to the ruined building; engage from the street, using Fire Arrows and walls for cover. Try separating the beasts so you do not fight both in melee range. There is no timer and no bonus for speed.

  4. 28. Talk to Ferl from beneath the survivors’ raised platform.

    Once both Sawtooths are dead, go inside the ruined building and find the belongings/supplies at ground level beneath the two braves. Examine the supplies and speak with Ferl when prompted. You do not need to physically climb up to their perch to complete the interaction.

  5. 29. OPTIONAL LORE — “1st Amendment Virtual?” (World text).

    Before leaving the same ruins, check rubble beside a pillar near the Sawtooth building. Use Focus to scan this record. It is in the western part of Devil’s Thirst, near the river-facing ruins. Skip if you cannot readily locate the small pile.

  6. 30. Return to Den at Mother’s Crown to finish the errand.

    Fast-travel to the Mother’s Crown campfire (or ride north/west along the road). Descend toward the south gate and find Den outside the house where you accepted Sanctuary. Tell him the braves survived and collect the reward.

  7. 31. Return to Devil’s Thirst and reactivate The War-Chief’s Trail.

    Fast-travel to the Devil’s Thirst campfire you lit earlier. Open your journal and choose The War-Chief’s Trail. Your current objective should be “Go to Sona’s Last Known Location.” The earlier massacre-site investigation is already finished.

  8. 32. Leave the Tallneck area toward Sona’s hilltop camp.

    Go toward the story marker east/southeast of the Tallneck. Climb the rocky rise above the old city using the marked handholds; the target is an abandoned campsite on higher ground, not the Tallneck’s circular route. Save at a nearby campfire if it appears.

STAGE CHECK: Sanctuary complete and reported to Den. You are at the abandoned hilltop camp for Sona’s main quest.

06E · Find Sona and finish The War-Chief’s Trail

HILLTOP CAMP → BLOOD TRAIL → DEAD SAWTOOTH → SONA → CULTIST EXCAVATION → STOP.
  1. 33. Inspect all clues at Sona’s abandoned hilltop camp.

    Turn on Focus and identify the bow, bandages, campfire, food scraps and arrowheads. Approach and examine the interactive clues (not just Focus-scan them). The quest will update to following tracks. Do not leave before the objective changes.

  2. 34. Highlight the blood/footprint trail and follow it along the RIDGE.

    With Focus, lock the tracks and follow their glowing line north/east across the cliff path. Take the yellow handholds, small bridge gaps and ladders where the trail climbs; do not cut straight downhill into the city. You may encounter corrupted machines on the path — sneak past or use fire.

  3. 35. Approach the dead Sawtooth at the end of the trail.

    The trail ends by a Sawtooth carcass in an open area. Walk up to it and examine it; a cutscene introducing Sona begins. Afterward, loot what is safe to loot before following the war-chief.

  4. 36. Follow Sona to the ledge above the cultists’ excavation camp.

    Stay near her as she leads you toward a camp below the ridgeline. Do not slide down immediately. From the overlook you can see human cultists, Corrupted Watchers, and exposed green Blaze containers.

  5. 37. Prepare the ambush from ABOVE.

    Scan the enemies; check for the green Blaze barrels and human carriers’ exposed canisters. Shoot an isolated human in the head if you still need the 30 human headshots achievement (you had 25 after Chapter 03). Use Fire Arrows on clustered Blaze to cause explosions. Let Sona’s braves join when the fight starts.

  6. 38. Clear every cultist and corrupted machine at the excavation.

    Use nearby ledges and rope bridges for alternate sightlines; do not drop into the center until the dangerous targets are dead. Shoot the Corrupted Watchers early if possible. When combat ends, talk to Sona; Varl arrives, and the objective becomes investigating the dig site.

  7. 39. Find and listen to the REQUIRED recording at the dig site.

    After the conversation, turn on Focus and locate the trail of spilled Blaze. Follow it toward the small shack in the southwest corner of the excavation. There is a recording device/datapoint on a table or wooden pallet. Scan and listen: this recording is part of the quest itself, so do not skip it.

  8. 40. Report to Sona and Varl. STOP after the quest completes.

    Return to the two Nora leaders and finish the conversation. The War-Chief’s Trail completes and Revenge of the Nora becomes available. Do not head to the next Eclipse outpost. We will handle the three camps and nearby activities in Chapter 07.

STAGE CHECK: Chapter 06 done: first Tallneck, Colorado Springs Vantage, Arches Vessel, Metal Flower I(F), Sanctuary and The War-Chief’s Trail. Keep your new quest at the initial objective.
STOP HERE: With The War-Chief’s Trail finished, Revenge of the Nora is ready. Chapter 07 will guide the next Eclipse camps. Expected: 4/30 Metal Flowers · 2/5 Power Cells · 1/12 Ancient Vessels · 2/12 Vantages · 1/5 Tallnecks (provided Chapters 01–05 were completed).
