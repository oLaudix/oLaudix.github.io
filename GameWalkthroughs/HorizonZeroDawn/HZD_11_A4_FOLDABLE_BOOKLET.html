11A · SUN AND SHADOW
Find Lahavis in Brightmarket and investigate Elida’s disappearance
□
01. Activate Sun and Shadow and travel to Brightmarket.
From Meridian, follow the road northwest to Brightmarket, a settlement beside a lake and bridges. The side quest begins with Lahavis on a small bridge near the town center. If it is already in your Journal, select it first.
□
02. Talk to Lahavis; then find his daughter’s garden.
Hear his concern about Elida. Follow the new marker toward the eastern side of Brightmarket, beside a large house. The garden is outdoors; turn on Focus before disturbing clues.
□
03. Examine THREE clues in the garden.
Use Focus to find and individually examine the bottle, spyglass and shovel. Do not leave as soon as one item glows. Near the shovel, highlight the resulting trail on the ground.
□
04. Follow the ground trail through town to the docks.
The highlighted trail crosses Brightmarket’s bridges and reaches the northern docks / small boathouse. At the dock, find and examine the rope showing that a boat is missing. This changes the objective to the island.
□
05. Cross the water to the quest-marked island.
Swim toward the crescent-shaped island indicated by the active marker. Before rushing ashore, use Focus to tag the Snapmaw patrolling around Elida’s makeshift camp.
OPTIONAL LORE — TWO ACTUAL GLYPHSBrightmarket: check them while you are in town
At the inn, scan the glyph “The Nora” on the bar counter. For “History of Sunfall”, look just north of the local merchant. These are optional Carja-written lore records. If either compass cube is inconvenient, move on.

11F · CONFRONT OLIN
End the excavation mission, decide Olin’s fate, and set the next checkpoint
□
27. Finish every remaining Eclipse enemy.
Check the site after both Corruptors fall. A hidden surviving cultist on a platform can stop the objective from advancing; use Focus to tag remaining enemies. Olin survives and can be approached when the site is clear.
□
28. Talk to Olin and hear his explanation.
Listen to his account of the Eclipse, HADES, his forced work and his captive family. This is the major lore payoff of The City of the Sun; do not skip the conversation.
□
29. Choose whether to spare or kill Olin.
For the warmer story outcome, choose to spare him. Either choice still unlocks the errand Collateral to rescue his family and does not change the “All allies joined” requirement. You do not need to reload for an achievement.
□
30. Confirm The City of the Sun is COMPLETED.
The game awards the quest XP / skill point and leads into Maker’s End. The separate main quest The Field of the Fallen also remains available. No additional Olin-specific achievement should unlock here.
□
31. Leave Collateral and the next main quests for Chapter 12.
Do not begin Maker’s End yet. Do not rush to the Spurflints for Collateral or ride to Erend for The Field of the Fallen. We will route them with the next ally prerequisite, Sunstone Rock, when the next guide begins.
IMPORTANT — POWER CELL TIMINGNo Power Cell is at this excavation
The next story Power Cell comes much later, during Maker’s End. We will provide the exact route to the top of Faro’s tower when you enter that main quest; you have not missed it in this chapter.

11E · THE CITY OF THE SUN
Finally return to the unfinished main quest: Find Olin
□
22. Re-select The City of the Sun in the Main Quest tab.
We already investigated Olin’s apartment in Chapter 09; do not revisit it. The pending objective is Find Olin at the Excavation Site. Travel to the marker at Rockwreath, in the Carja area north of Cut-Cliffs / east of Dimmed Bones.
□
23. Prepare and approach the excavation from the upper rim.
Refill healing supplies, ready Fire Arrows and some Tear or accurate high-damage shots. Use the approach path indicated by the active story marker. The quarry has upper ledges, human guards and dormant war machines. Stop on the rim and tag enemies with Focus.
□
24. Enter far enough to trigger the excavation cutscene.
The Eclipse revive ancient machines. An unknown voice briefly interrupts Eclipse Focus communication. After the scene, remain near upper cover if possible; several cultists are positioned on ledges overlooking the dig.
□
25. Remove the human cultists from cover.
Start with archers and isolated guards on raised platforms. Concentration + headshots can finish the human headshot achievement, while the ledges offer Strike from Above opportunities when safe. Use ropes and ramps if you need to change elevation.
□
26. Handle the TWO Corruptors carefully.
Both are powerful ancient war machines. Fire causes overheating; when vents/cores open, hit the glowing weak points. Use boulders or quarry geometry to block missiles, avoid melee lunges, and separate the two machines if possible. You can lay traps before drawing their attention.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITIESRecord the rare machine encounters
If Corruptor is not catalogued, scan it before killing both. Its kill contributes to the machine-type achievements. If your headshot / aerial-kill achievement is already done, simply fight in whatever style is comfortable; no extra farm is required.

11B · SUN AND SHADOW
Finish the island investigation; find Atral; report to Lahavis
□
06. Defeat the Snapmaw and speak with Elida.
Use Fire and precision attacks as desired; watch its freeze projectiles. When the Snapmaw is dead, find Elida on the island and listen to her initial explanation.
□
07. Inspect the TWO bedrolls before leaving.
Use Focus inside Elida’s camp to examine both sleeping spots, then speak to Elida again. She admits that her secret Shadow Carja lover, Atral, is missing. The next marker points to his signal fire.
□
08. Locate Atral’s signal fire on the opposite shore.
Travel to the north shore of the lake. At the fire, turn on Focus and highlight the tracks leading away. Follow the tracks up the hillside to the Shadow Carja position called Kestrel’s Perch.
□
09. Clear Kestrel’s Perch and locate Atral inside.
There are guards outside, inside, and on upper levels. You can use the climbable path to the right of the gate to enter at roof level, or fight through the entrance. After clearing the immediate guards, find Atral in a cell/interior room and speak to him.
GOOD ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITYDo your human-kill objectives here if still missing
This is a strong spot for Headshot 30 human enemies. A kill must be finished by a headshot; the Notebook’s total “Headshots” statistic alone can be larger. If you have Strike from Above, use the rooftop above isolated guards for its 3-kill achievement. No need to grind if already unlocked.
□
10. Return to Brightmarket and tell Elida.
Fast-travel or ride back. Speak to Lahavis if the active marker requests him first; he tells you Elida has returned. Then find Elida in her garden, deliver Atral’s news and hear her response. Your emotional response is not an achievement prerequisite.
□
11. Speak to Lahavis again to COMPLETE the quest.
Lahavis moves nearby after the scene. Tell him his daughter needs him. Check your Journal: Sun and Shadow — Completed. This satisfies the Elida requirement for All allies joined; the final achievement will unlock much later.

11C · A MOMENT’S PEACE
Follow Vilgund’s rumor back toward the northern Banuk camp
□
12. Select A Moment’s Peace — already accepted.
You took this from Vilgund in Meridian in Chapter 09. Select the Side Quest in the Journal. It leads far northeast toward snowy Nora territory, not to The Frozen Wilds DLC entrance. Fast-travel near the Banuk camp if you have a suitable campfire.
□
13. Enter the Banuk Encampment and DO NOT attack its machines.
At the quest site, machines behave strangely peacefully. Walk among the docile Watchers, Scrappers and Ravager. Speak to Siluk, Aluki and especially shaman Tikuk near the machines / main campfire. Let their conversations play out for the lore.
□
14. Find the signal with the Focus.
From inside the camp, look up toward the northern mountainside / cliffs above the settlement with Focus activated. A faint Undefined Signal becomes scannable. Scan it to point toward its source; there is no secret door on the ground to find.
□
15. Start the climb from the Banuk shrine behind camp.
Go to the rear/northern end of the settlement, climb the wooden stairs to the shrine, then take the climbable ledges to the LEFT of the shrine. Follow yellow handholds, narrow ropes and gaps upward. Use the actual climbing path, not a direct scramble under the map pin.
□
16. Reach the upper campfire and continue toward voices.
Follow the mountain route to a campfire with Glinthawks nearby. They may be docile while the signal lasts, so do not attack unnecessarily. Continue farther north along the mountain pass to meet Bajund and Menuf.
OPTIONAL LORE — MACHINE PEACEHear the Banuk beliefs before climbing
The encounter with Tikuk, Aluki and Siluk is the key lore. They describe the Blue Light, machine “songs” and why their camp is unusual. It is worth exhausting their dialogue while the machines are still docile.

11D · THE SIGNAL BREAKS
The Oseram, the strange artifact, and the Banuk defense
□
17. Listen to Bajund and Menuf, then find their companions.
These two Oseram warn that the device is fragile. Continue up the marked path to Dorgeld and Garnund, who are working around the source of the signal. Talk through the confrontation.
□
18. Examine the device using Focus.
At the signal source, scan the Unknown Artifact rather than only looking at it. Listen to Aloy’s remarks and the Oseram’s dialogue. This device has unusually important lore implications; its exact origin is not explained here.
□
19. Descend to the Banuk camp immediately.
Follow the return marker. The upper trail offers a rappel / zipline shortcut near the Glinthawk campfire; use it if visible, or retrace the marked climbing route. The machine-calming signal is failing, and the camp will be attacked.
□
20. Defend the camp from hostile machines.
Back at the settlement, eliminate the attacking Watchers / Scrappers / Ravager as the quest updates. For the Ravager, shoot off its Ravager Cannon if you still need Tore off 5 heavy weapons. The locals help; check all hostile machines are down.
□
21. Speak to Tikuk, then report to Vilgund.
After the defense, talk to Tikuk at the camp. Then travel all the way back to Meridian and speak to Vilgund, southwest/west of the city near the Lodge. Confirm A Moment’s Peace — Completed. This secures Aluki for All allies joined.
LORE NOTEWhy this matters to the larger story
The exact function of the processor is a mystery during this quest. The full meaning becomes clearer after the later main-story GAIA revelations. Do not confuse this Banuk camp quest with Into the Frozen Wilds; the expansion is still on hold.
