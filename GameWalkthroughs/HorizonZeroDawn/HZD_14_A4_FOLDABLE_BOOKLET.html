14A · ROAD TO THE RUINS
From Meridian → northern Sundom → snowy approach
01. Set Maker’s End as the active main quest.
From Meridian, use your nearest discovered northern campfire or follow the northwest road toward the huge ancient complex. The quest marker, not an invented compass shortcut, is your route anchor.
02. Pass the Shattered Kiln area.
The Shattered Kiln Bandit Camp lies along the northern approach. If still uncleared, you can clear it now for the six-camp achievement and speak with Nil if he is there. If already cleared, do not repeat it.
03. Follow the snowy road past the rusted war vehicles.
After crossing into snow, you will pass tanks and military wreckage. Keep following the Maker’s End marker toward the occupied ruins. Do not confuse these roadside wrecks with the entrance to Faro’s office.
OPTIONAL LORE · ROADSIDE WRECKSThree Odyssey datapoints
Around the tanks on the snowy approach, use the Focus to look for Odyssey Injustice?, Odyssey Drives Ready and Odyssey to Nowhere. These are World text datapoints; skip without penalty if a wreck is difficult to search.
04. Collect the mysterious supply stash.
As you near the campfire and dig site, the unknown voice over Aloy’s Focus points out a stash of equipment. Track its nearby quest marker/search area, loot the cache, then return to the road.
05. Save at the campfire outside Maker’s End.
This is your last convenient staging point before a series of Eclipse fights. Stock Fire Arrows, impact arrows and healing resources. The stationary Deathbringer ahead can fire several weapons at once.
MAP ICONS NEAR THIS CAMPFIREOptional cleanup, NOT a chapter gate
There is a Faro Automated Solutions Vantage nearby, a Metallurgic Ancient Vessel north of the campfire, and a Corrupted Zone to the east. Collect/clear whichever map icons you want; return to this campfire before going into the dig site.

14C · DAMAGED DEATHBRINGER
Scan first · clear cultists · defeat machine
12. SCAN the Deathbringer before starting the fight.
As the cutscene ends, activate the Focus and scan the enormous, stationary Deathbringer. This is an important entry for All machines catalogued. Scan cultists or other new machine types too.
13. Pick off the cultists around it.
Remain behind broken masonry/scaffolding and take out the human enemies first. Thin them out before committing to the Deathbringer, since their arrows and explosives make staying in cover harder.
14. Attack the machine from solid cover.
The damaged Deathbringer cannot walk, but it fires heavy guns. Peek out, fire, then duck back. Fire accelerates overheating and exposes glowing orange cooling parts and heat vents: shoot those with impact arrows.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITYTore off 5 heavy weapons
The Deathbringer has Gun Turret components that can count if torn off. If this achievement is still missing, target them with tear-capable ammunition only if the angle is safe. It is not essential to do it in this fight; Ravager Cannons and Thunderjaw Disc Launchers are easier later.
15. Loot the marked Eclipse officer’s Focus.
After the machine is destroyed, find the quest-marked cultist officer near the battlefield. Search the body and inspect the Focus. Watch the story sequence before entering the bunker.
16. Enter the broken building behind the Deathbringer.
Follow the newly updated quest marker through the damaged wall / narrow crack. Continue until Aloy reaches the sealed Faro facility. The access scan lets her enter.
CHECK BEFORE GOING INSIDEDid the Focus and catalogue register?
Confirm you scanned the Deathbringer and searched the officer. If the quest marker still points to the battlefield, do that interaction before searching for another entrance.
