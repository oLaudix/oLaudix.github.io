02A · Mother's Heart

CHAPTER 02
PAGE 1 / 3
Starting position: You have just killed the Sawtooth in The Point of the Spear. Your recorded totals: 5 achievements · 1 Metal Flower · 1 Power Cell. If the quest still says Talk to Rost, speak to him first; the next quest is Mother's Heart.

Main quest — Mother's Heart

  1. 1. Meet Rost at Mother's Heart, not at his house.

    Open the Quests menu and track Mother's Heart. Follow its yellow marker along the road to the village entrance. Rost is waiting outside the gate, by the bridge. Talk to him, finish the farewell conversation, and walk across to the gate. Teersa lets you inside.

  2. 2. From the entrance, take the path climbing to the RIGHT.

    Follow that path until you find Teb, the boy you rescued during the childhood tutorial. Speak to him and open his merchant inventory. Take the Nora Brave outfit he offers for free and collect his free sample box if available. Equip the outfit if it's better than your current one.

  3. 3. Keep walking uphill on the main village path.

    You will encounter Karst, the trader who sold you the Tripcaster. Speak to him to take his free sample box (if offered), refill ammo supplies if needed and sell spare junk. You do not need to buy any specific weapon to complete the Proving.

  4. 4. Continue uphill toward the Matriarchs' Lodge objective.

    A scene with visiting Carja representatives plays. Afterward, approach the man the quest highlights who is wearing a Focus (Olin), and speak with him. Erend joins the conversation. Once their dialogue finishes, the quest sends you to the Blessing.

  5. 5. Follow the new yellow marker BACK DOWN toward the ceremony.

    Approach Teersa in the blessing area and let the cutscene finish. You will then be told to head to the Hunter's Lodge, the sleeping building for Proving candidates near the village entrance.

Don't sleep at the Hunter's Lodge yet. First collect the only two achievement-related objects in the village: the Grazer training dummies below.
  1. 6. Find BOTH training dummies beside the Hunter's Lodge campfire.

    At the campfire outside the lodge where the candidates sleep, look beside the nearby building for one Grazer dummy. The second is just behind / north of that same building, closer to the guard tower. Knock both over with heavy spear attacks. They are a pair in the lodge area, not at Rost's house.

    Notebook → Statistics → Other → Training Dummies: previous count + 2 = total.

  2. 7. Now enter the Hunter's Lodge.

    Talk to Bast; speaking with Vala afterward is optional but worthwhile for the story. Interact with Aloy's bunk/bed to sleep. This ends Mother's Heart and starts The Proving. You cannot freely wander away once the trials begin.

Page 1 check: Both Mother's Heart dummies toppled; free outfit collected; now asleep at Hunter's Lodge. No separate story achievement is expected from the festival itself.

02B · The Proving

CHAPTER 02
PAGE 2 / 3
Starting position: Aloy wakes for the Proving and climbs the mountain with the other candidates. This is a linear mission; there are no Power Cells, Metal Flowers, or Grazer training dummies to search for on this route.

Main quest — The Proving

  1. 1. At the first hunting test, kill ONE Grazer.

    After the climb and cutscene, the game tells you to claim a Proving trophy. Use the Focus to mark a Grazer and shoot it (or place a Shock Tripcaster wire across its route). Loot the dead Grazer for its trophy; the kill alone is not enough.

  2. 2. When Bast ruins your trophy, kill a SECOND Grazer.

    Do not chase Bast or search for the first trophy. Shoot another Grazer and loot its replacement trophy. Then follow the yellow marker to the start of the running course.

  3. 3. After the other candidates take the normal route, take the alternate Brave Trail.

    At the Proctor's scene, follow Aloy's new objective. Use the zipline, cross the fallen tree, and grab the first climbable cliff ledge. Keep following the white/yellow-painted handholds and ledges. Traverse sideways where the path ends, climb up, and continue over the wooden logs.

    If you're unsure which direction to climb, use the yellow main-quest marker as the destination and look for the nearest painted climbing handholds. This is a fixed platforming path, not a hidden collectible detour.

  4. 4. Use the later zipline to reach the finish.

    Continue across the cliffside and remaining fallen-tree crossing until you find the final zipline. Take it to complete the run. A cutscene then interrupts the celebration.

  5. 5. During the ambush, use the boulders for cover.

    The enemies above the candidates are human attackers. Shoot exposed heads when possible (these can help toward the 30-headshot achievement), but prioritize staying alive. There is a climbable ledge on the right-hand side of the enemy position if you want to flank the archers with Silent Strike.

  6. 6. After the next cutscene, clear the second wave.

    Take cover again, shoot the archers from the sides, and deal with any melee attackers who rush your position. Collect healing supplies from bodies if safe, but do not wander far from the fight area.

  7. 7. Deal with the heavily armed gunner in the final wave.

    Stay behind rocks when he fires the Deathbringer Gun. Kill nearby enemies first, hit the gunner with Fire Arrows and headshots while exposed, then grab his dropped heavy gun if you can. Use it against the remaining enemies until the scripted scene begins.

  8. 8. Let the closing cutscenes play.

    You finish The Proving and automatically continue into The Womb of the Mountain. There is nothing to backtrack for on the Proving trail.

Achievement earned: Triumphed in the Proving — automatic story achievement.
Next: Aloy wakes inside All-Mother Mountain. The second Power Cell is available very soon. Keep page 3 ready.

02C · The Womb of the Mountain

CHAPTER 02
PAGE 3 / 3
Starting position: Aloy wakes inside All-Mother Mountain immediately after The Proving. Power Cell #2 is inside this building, behind a red-lit locked door you bypass through a low ventilation passage. Do not leave the mountain without it.

Main quest — The Womb of the Mountain

  1. 1. Wake up and retrieve Aloy's nearby belongings.

    Exit the sleeping room and follow the main quest marker into the adjoining room to recover her clothes / Focus. Continue through the ancient doors into the corridor of the mountain facility.

  2. 2. BEFORE following Teersa away, find the RED-GLowing locked metal door.

    In the next corridor, turn right instead of continuing toward the quest marker. Look for a sealed ancient door with a conspicuous red holographic panel. You do not need to open that door.

  3. 3. Standing facing that red door, look DOWN and LEFT.

    There's a short, low ventilation opening / tunnel beside the locked door, close to the floor. Crouch and crawl through it. Keep going to the room beyond the locked door.

  4. 4. Pick up Power Cell #2 inside the room beyond the vent.

    Find the small green-highlighted pickup on the floor / shelf area. Confirm it enters your inventory, then crawl back through the very same vent to return to the corridor. Your total is now 2 / 5 Power Cells. Do not try to follow the route young Aloy used anywhere else.

  5. 5. Resume the yellow quest marker and recover the remaining gear / Focus.

    If the objective still asks you to search for equipment, check the room opposite the locked red door. Once the relevant scenes finish, speak with Teersa, follow her to the huge ancient sealed door, listen to the revelation, and then follow her out of the mountain.

  6. 6. Back outside, go down to the village and talk to Teb.

    Follow the main quest marker to the guard gate. Climb the nearby ladder and speak with Resh. This starts a scripted machine attack on the gate.

  7. 7. Defeat the corrupted machines and the Corruptor.

    Place Tripcaster Shock Wires near the entrance if you have time. Take down the smaller charging machines first. When the larger Corruptor enters, scan it with your Focus (important for the machine catalogue), keep moving to avoid its jumps, and target its exposed weapons / weak points. Fire works well. Loot the Corruptor after killing it to obtain the override component.

  8. 8. Stop once the immediate gate defense is complete.

    Your journal should now lead into A Seeker at the Gates. Don't set off toward the distant main gate yet: the next printable chapter will handle the newly accessible Nora areas, any remaining nearby dummies, collectible maps, and Metal Flower Mark I (B).

Chapter 02 completed. Power Cells 2 / 5; Metal Flowers remain 1 / 30 unless you picked up extras; Mother's Heart dummies +2. Steam achievements: at least Triumphed in the Proving added to your previous five (other combat achievements may unlock naturally). Fought back the corruption is later, at Daytower — not from this first Corruptor fight.
