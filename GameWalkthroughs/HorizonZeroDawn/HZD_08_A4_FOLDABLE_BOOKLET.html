08A · CAULDRON SIGMA
Return to Mother’s Crown first · interior entry route
□
01. Fast-travel to Mother’s Crown; select the SIGMA activity.
Open the map and choose Cauldron SIGMA immediately north of Mother’s Crown, at the base of the mountains. You received its location from Dral in Chapter 05. Make sure you have healing, Precision/Hardpoint Arrows, and enough materials for ammo. If SIGMA is already completed, go straight to step 11 on page 4.
□
02. Clear the entrance and override its metal door.
At SIGMA’s mountain entrance, clear the Watchers and Scrappers around the door; use the grass if you prefer stealth. Approach the cauldron doorway and interact with it using Aloy’s Override tool. Move inside and rappel down the shaft at the end of the entry room.
□
03. Use the steam vents against the first Watchers.
Follow the passage to a triangular vent on the left wall. Hit it to release a cloud of steam, then slip through and Silent Strike the Watcher. In the next chamber there are two more Watchers; another wall vent and a vent on a crate can conceal Aloy. You may fight them openly if preferred.
□
04. Find the upper walkway and activate your first bridge.
Avoid the triangular energy barriers. Take the open passage at the far side, which briefly leads through an exterior-looking cavern. Continue into the next tunnel, climb the ladder and handholds, and reach a walkway overlooking the earlier chamber. Override the red/glowing network uplink beside the gap; its light bridge extends across.
□
05. Ride the overhead freight system.
Cross the newly extended bridge, follow the passage, and override the door at its end. In the next industrial room, wait for a suspended moving freight unit, jump to grab the rails beneath it and ride until you can safely drop to the farther platform. Follow the path rather than dropping into inaccessible machinery.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITYMachine catalogue / silent strikes
Scan every unfamiliar machine before killing it. There is no required stealth kill count here if you have already unlocked Stealth killed 10 machines; otherwise the Watchers make easy targets.

08F · DAYTOWER
Corruptor fight · main quest achievement · Nakoa quest lead
□
22. Return to the mountain pass WEST and approach Daytower.
From Two-Teeth, rejoin the main road toward the western Carja border fort marked by A Seeker at the Gates. Save at a campfire if available. The gate will be under attack: do not expect the guard to let you in until the machines outside are dead.
□
23. Defeat the attacking Corruptor and corrupted support machines.
Scan from a safe distance to identify the Corruptor, plus corrupted Scrappers and Watchers. Kill smaller enemies first if they interrupt shots. The Corruptor leaps/charges and fires projectiles; roll sideways and use cover. Fire Arrows help against corruption-susceptible machines; precise damage to exposed components is also useful.
□
24. Speak with the fort commander to complete the main quest.
When the outside fight finishes, approach the gate. Once inside Daytower, follow the A Seeker at the Gates quest marker and speak to Captain Balahn (on the fort balcony/overlook). Finish his dialogue. The achievement Fought back the corruption should unlock and The City of the Sun becomes the next main quest.
□
25. IMPORTANT: advance A Daughter’s Vengeance here.
You previously took this quest from Yan at Mother’s Rise and spoke with Solai in Mother’s Crown. During your Balahn conversation, ask about Nakoa. Then follow the quest marker to Zaid within Daytower and question him. The objective advances to Lonesome Rock. Stop at that point; next chapter will finish the quest on the Carja side of the border.
□
26. Accept Balahn’s errand if you want its story.
While talking to Balahn, ask about the missing Carja patrol to obtain In Foreign Lands. It leads back toward the Forsaken Village and later a Snapmaw island; this is not a required achievement quest. Accept now, complete later if you want, and leave the main story alone until the next chapter.
REQUIRED ALLY CHAINNakoa matters for All allies joined
Unlike most local errands, A Daughter’s Vengeance is on the verified list for the final All allies joined achievement. Advancing it at Daytower now saves another trip. It is not completed until you rescue Nakoa later.

08E · HUNTER’S GATHERING
Two dummies · nearby errands · Two-Teeth Bandit Camp
□
16. Go downhill WEST to Hunter’s Gathering.
Leave the Hunting Grounds and follow the path down to the Hunter’s Gathering settlement immediately west of them. Light the central campfire. Do not rush out: there are two remaining Grazer training dummies here.
□
17. Destroy BOTH Hunter’s Gathering dummies.
Stand at the settlement campfire and face the rocky rise at the north/northwest edge of the gathering. One dummy is north of the fire; the second is slightly northwest along the path that ascends toward the Banuk figure “I Name You”. Heavy-melee each until it topples. Check the Notebook statistic.
□
18. Accept the two local quests, if interested.
Find Lubavad near the main campfire for Underequipped. Find Gera near a tent toward the western side for the errand To Old Acquaintance. These are optional for the 79 achievements and can wait; you don’t have to chase their markers immediately. Listen to the dialogue for local lore.
□
19. Follow the westbound mountain road toward Daytower; detour to Two-Teeth.
From Hunter’s Gathering, use the map and travel west/northwest on the marked road toward Daytower. At a northern road fork, take the side road that climbs east/northeast to Two-Teeth Bandit Camp, located in the hills east of Daytower. If you already cleared this camp, skip its fight and find the dummy only.
□
20. Clear Two-Teeth from the high ground.
Before entering, use Focus from a ledge northwest of the camp to tag bandits, especially the alarm guard and sniper. Take quiet headshots, then disable the alarm and finish the bandits. Free captives if prompted. Wait until the camp changes to a cleared settlement.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITYExcellent place for deliberate combat achievements
If still missing: Headshot 30 human enemies requires lethal headshots, not just hit counts. With Strike from Above unlocked, use elevated terrain to kill enemies from above toward its 3 qualifying kills. Do not force this if the enemies are already dead; other camps remain.
□
21. Get the Two-Teeth dummy and nearby lore.
After the camp is cleared, start at the campfire and walk upstairs to the upper level where the alarm stood. Find the single Grazer dummy beside the storage shed; heavy-melee it. For optional lore “Robar Is Coming!”, search the lowest section of the old bandit camp using the compass cube/Focus signal.

08B · SIGMA CORE
Free the Bellowback · override the central Core · new overrides
□
06. Clear the central chamber and restore the next route.
After the freight ride you will see more machines; a Shell-Walker can appear in this section. Scan it, then remove its shield/claw or cargo attachments if you fight it. Reach the marked uplink on the elevated platform and override to extend the next bridge.
□
07. Use the rope, climb and final bridge to reach the Core.
Cross the platform and ride the rope/zipline down to the next lower area. Climb the marked handholds, activate the next red uplink, and follow the tunnel. Rappel into the Core room — you will recognize it by the large forcefield around a Fire Bellowback.
□
08. Prepare before opening the Core enclosure.
Scan the Fire Bellowback and kill the two roaming Watchers. Loot/prepare if useful. The Bellowback is still safely behind the shield. Look for an elevated discharge pylon beside the central enclosure with an override point on its top. If using tripwires/traps, place them now, before releasing it.
□
09. Override a pylon, then defeat the released machines.
Climb a side pylon and override its panel. This drops the shield and releases the Bellowback; more Watchers join the fight. Deal with the smaller machines when safe. Concentrate high-damage arrows on the large back cargo sac and the gullet under its head. Bursting either weak point deals major damage and disables attacks.
□
10. Override the central Core itself — this is the trophy trigger.
After the Bellowback dies, climb to the exposed central Core console, not another discharge pylon, and use Override. First Core Overridden should unlock. The escape lift returns you toward the exit. You also gain the SIGMA overrides: Grazer, Lancehorn, Scrapper and Sawtooth.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITY7 types of machine overridden
A type counts only when you actually use that override, not merely unlock it. You already tested Strider. Start using Grazer / Scrapper / Lancehorn / Sawtooth as you naturally meet them. We will track the remaining unique species later.
OPTIONAL LOREM/SIGMA: Core Log 763E
Completing the Core supplies a machine datapoint. Read it in the Notebook if you enjoy the fabrication lore; it is not required for an achievement.

08C · OPEN THE ROAD WEST
Corrupted Zones · approach Valleymeet · timed-trial preparation
□
11. Return to A Seeker at the Gates in your Journal.
After SIGMA, fast-travel back to Mother’s Crown (or use a nearby campfire). Select A Seeker at the Gates. Since you already talked to Marea, the objective should be to clear two Corrupted Zones, unless you completed that requirement while exploring.
□
12. Finish only the Corrupted Zones still needed.
Check the quest counter 0/2, 1/2 or 2/2. If under 2/2, select the nearest marked red zone west/southwest of Mother’s Crown. One zone contains corrupted Grazers whose Blaze canisters can be ignited; another ruined village has corrupted Watchers and Scrappers. Clear every hostile machine in the circle until the zone icon changes.
□
13. Confirm the gate objective changes to Daytower.
Once 2/2 have registered, your objective should become Go to the Carja Border Fort. Do not hunt more zones merely to advance the quest, though you can clear other map icons whenever you pass them. At least one zone is required toward the “First Corrupted Zone cleared” trophy; all 11 will be needed eventually.
□
14. Travel northwest toward the Valleymeet Hunting Grounds.
From Mother’s Crown’s north gate, follow the road northwest. At the fork leading to the Valleymeet Hunting Grounds icon, take the mountain ascent to the Keeper, climbing the marked handholds where the road ends. The Hunting Ground is east of Hunter’s Gathering; we will visit that settlement next.
□
15. Buy / equip the elemental ammo you need before the first trial.
Speak to the Groundskeeper and the nearby merchant. Bring: Fire Arrows (Hunter Bow), Shock ammunition (War Bow or Sling), Freeze ammunition (War Bow or Sling), and Tearblast or strong Tear arrows for exposed Bellowback components. Missing an ammo type? Buy an appropriate weapon or return here later rather than wasting attempts.
TIMING NOTEYou can retry each trial without limit.
Blazing Sun is the highest medal. The medal persists once earned; do not worry if you need several attempts or better equipment. For all achievements, this Hunting Ground ultimately needs all three Blazing Suns.

08D · VALLEYMEET TRIALS
The time limits matter · every trial is repeatable

Valleymeet — three Blazing Suns (gold)

FIRE FIGHTBlazing: 0:40
Objective: Damage four machines with exploding Strider Blaze canisters. How: Talk to Keeper to start, scan the Strider herd from the ledge and take the eastern/left-side zipline nearest the herd. In Concentration, fire a Fire Arrow into a Blaze canister on a tightly grouped Strider; a single detonation can hit several machines. Target a second canister immediately if the counter is short. You are scoring damaged machines, not number of arrows.
□ Blazing Sun earned
SHOCKBlazing: 1:00
Objective: While a Bellowback is stunned by Shock, remove three canisters from its underside. How: Before zipping down, mark a Bellowback with Focus. Land and use Shock Bombs or Shock Arrows until the Shock status fully triggers and it collapses. Move to a clear side angle with line of sight under its belly; hit the row of canisters with Tearblast Arrows or tear ammo while it remains stunned. Stun a second Bellowback if necessary. Merely damaging its sacs does not count.
□ Blazing Sun earned
FREEZEBlazing: 1:30
Objective: Kill five machines while Frozen. How: After starting, slide toward the Striders. Throw Freeze Bombs into groups or apply Freeze Arrows until the blue/frost status is active. Finish Striders quickly with heavy spear hits / high-damage arrows. Do not spend time trying to freeze the tougher Bellowbacks. The kill must land before Freeze wears off.
□ Blazing Sun earned
ACHIEVEMENT CHECKPOINTHunting Grounds progress
If you already earned all 3 Blazing Suns at the Nora Grounds, finishing these gives 6 of 15 required base-game Blazing Suns. Three is enough for Hunter’s Lodge entry in Meridian — we will actively guide you through that chain in a later chapter.
