05A · Find the missing Dreamwillow

MOTHER’S RISE → TWO CACHES → FIA. START AT MOTHER’S RISE CAMPFIRE, immediately after Chapter 04. Your main quest is still The War-Chief’s Trail. This page deals with Fia’s already-accepted Insult to Injury quest. If you did not accept it, speak to Fia, the healer tending a wounded Brave, inside Mother’s Rise first.
  1. 1. Select Insult to Injury and look at its two cache markers on the map.

    Do this before leaving the settlement. These caches are not in Mother’s Rise. We will search both, return to Fia, and then travel north toward Sona’s battlefield.

  2. 2. Go to the road campfire SOUTHWEST of Mother’s Rise for Cache #1.

    Follow the road back toward the Main Embrace Gate or use the discovered campfire on that road. From that road campfire, look southwest for a large rocky outcrop. Go to its southwestern end and find yellow climbing ropes on the rock.

  3. 3. Climb the rock and take the zipline to the tree platform.

    From the top of the rock, ride the zipline to the platform in a tree. Loot the Supply Cache. It contains no Dreamwillow; that is the intended quest result. You have not missed a hidden chest.

  4. 4. Travel east to Cache #2, NORTHEAST of the Nora Hunting Grounds.

    Return to the nearby road campfire, cross the river to the southeast, and continue east past Grazers. Use the quest marker for your exact destination: it is in the woods northeast of the Nora Hunting Grounds, not at the Hunting Grounds lodge.

  5. 5. Reach the second tree platform using the yellow-painted climbing route.

    Approach the cache’s tree from the south/southeast, climb the rocks, then look north/northwest for two logs with yellow jumping ropes. Jump between them, then leap east onto a ledge. Follow the yellow handholds, drop onto the beam below, turn around and jump to the tree platform.

  6. 6. Loot Cache #2. Return to Fia at Mother’s Rise.

    This cache is also empty of Dreamwillow. Fast travel to Mother’s Rise if you have a pack, or ride/walk back. Talk to Fia about both empty caches. Do not head to Jun’s cabin until this conversation; she now sends you to him.

STAGE CHECK: Fia has told you to find Jun. No Steam achievement is tied to these two caches, but they advance the optional side quest.

05B · Investigate Sona’s battlefield

MOTHER’S RISE → MASSACRE SITE → JUN’S CABIN. MAIN-QUEST DETOUR NOW. Turn The War-Chief’s Trail back on for the massacre-site investigation. We will grab one optional lore datapoint and then continue the Fia/Jun quest while we are already north of Mother’s Rise.
  1. 7. Leave Mother’s Rise and go NORTHEAST to the watchtower-road fork.

    Descend the settlement ramp, cross the bridge you used to arrive, and return to the fork beside the watchtower. Take the route northwest across the bridge (the branch you deliberately did not take at the end of Chapter 04).

  2. 8. Continue past the Grazer Site and cross the NEXT bridge to the north.

    Keep following The War-Chief’s Trail marker. Immediately after the second bridge, leave the road and go west/uphill into the wrecked battlefield, rather than walking past the objective.

  3. 9. At the massacre site, scan and examine BOTH machine remains.

    Activate your Focus near the many fallen machines. Look specifically for a destroyed Corruptor and a destroyed Corrupted Watcher. Scan each; turn the Focus off and use the normal examine prompts. Dran, a survivor, appears. Talk to him for Sona’s last known location.

  4. 10. OPTIONAL LORE — “Harriet Choi Dies” (Text Datapoint — World).

    From the battlefield, take the short northbound path to the ruined house immediately beside/above the battlefield. Check the ruin next to a roof-support beam for the datapoint. Scan it with Focus. If you cannot locate it quickly, skip it; datapoints are not required for our achievements.

  5. 11. Switch back to Insult to Injury and take the mountain trail toward Jun.

    From the massacre-site area, follow Jun’s side-quest marker north and then northeast, away from Devil’s Thirst. When the trail splits, keep to the northwest/western route into the snowy mountains; climb the yellow-marked ledges and light the campfire on the approach.

  6. 12. At Jun’s cabin, go around to the EAST side and pry open the front door.

    You should hear Jun shouting for help. Go around the cabin instead of searching for an entrance underneath it; pry the locked door open with Aloy’s spear. Speak to Jun about the stolen Dreamwillow. This activates the outcasts’ trail.

STAGE CHECK: You spoke to Dran at the massacre; Jun is free and has sent you after the outcasts. The War-Chief’s Trail remains unfinished on purpose.

05C · Recover the Dreamwillow

JUN’S CABIN → OUTCASTS → MOTHER’S CROWN. THIS PART HAS A SAWTOOTH FIGHT. If you want to save supplies, light the mountain campfire by Jun’s cabin first. You do not have to fight the outcasts — only the machines threatening them.
  1. 13. At Jun’s cabin, activate Focus and highlight the outcasts’ tracks.

    Follow the track northeast, then north up the mountain. Do not jump directly off the first cliff: scout the valley ahead from above. The outcasts are stranded on a rocky ledge below.

  2. 14. Kill the two Watchers and the Sawtooth attacking the outcasts.

    Fight from high ground if possible. Snipe Watchers first; against the Sawtooth, use fire, a Tripcaster Shock Wire, and the terrain. You can drop to the grassy valley if you need to, but there is no reason to deliberately aggro the machines at point-blank range.

  3. 15. Speak to the outcast leader Kurnst and recover the Dreamwillow.

    Once the machines are dead, go down into the valley and talk to Kurnst. Ask about the Dreamwillow; he hands over a portion. Do not attack the outcasts. The quest progresses through dialogue.

  4. 16. Return uphill to Jun’s cabin and finish Insult to Injury.

    Take the path back up from the valley and talk to Jun. Choose whichever response you prefer when Aloy reacts to his behavior. This finishes the side quest; no particular dialogue response is needed for an achievement.

  5. 17. Descend from Jun’s cabin and travel NORTH to Mother’s Crown.

    Use the rappel / zipline near Jun’s cabin to get back down the mountainside. At the main road, open the map and select Mother’s Crown (north of Mother’s Rise, west/northwest of Devil’s Thirst). Follow the road toward the southern settlement entrance; do not cut across cliffs.

STAGE CHECK: Insult to Injury complete. You have arrived at the SOUTH entrance of Mother’s Crown. Continue to the Mother’s Crown instructions below before leaving the settlement.

05D · Mother’s Crown: quests and dummies

SOUTH GATE → VILLAGERS → NORTH GATE. BEFORE LEAVING MOTHER’S CROWN, collect the town’s easy-to-miss quest updates and two Grazer dummies. The errands you accept here will be handled in future chapters near their real objectives; do not immediately run off to complete each one.
  1. 18. At the SOUTH gate, talk to Dral beside the wounded Nora Braves.

    Ask about the metal ruin to learn the location of Cauldron SIGMA. This unlocks its activity marker; we will visit the Cauldron on a later route.

  2. 19. Just inside the settlement, speak with Marea.

    Use the A Seeker at the Gates dialogue. She explains why Daytower is closed and marks two Corrupted Zones on your map. Both will need clearing later, but do not leave the settlement for them yet.

  3. 20. Near the southern entrance, speak with Den to accept Sanctuary.

    Den stands by a house near Marea. He is looking for missing Nora Braves. Accept the errand now and leave it in the journal until the walkthrough directs you into its area.

  4. 21. Walk uphill and speak with Taim to accept Luck of the Hunt.

    Find him by one of the houses farther up the village slope. This is another errand that can wait; we are only collecting it while you are already here.

  5. 22. Find Solai to update A Daughter’s Vengeance.

    Solai is near the northern gate, tending vegetation close to Taim’s area. Ask about Nakoa (Yan’s missing sister). Her information updates the quest; the actual search for Nakoa continues much farther west, later.

  6. 23. At the NORTH gate, speak with Lut about Valleymeet Hunting Grounds.

    He is positioned by/under a watchtower near the north entrance. This reveals the next Hunting Ground. You do not need to do the trials now.

  7. 24. Smash BOTH Grazer training dummies near the NORTHWEST gate.

    Look west/left of the northern gate, near the palisade. The two dummies stand close together. Hit each with a heavy spear attack until it falls. If your Notebook showed 17 after Chapter 04, it should now show 19 / 23.

  8. 25. Light the Mother’s Crown campfire. STOP HERE.

    Check your quest journal, restock from the town merchant if needed, and save. Do not follow the Sona marker yet. Chapter 06 will visit the Ancient Armory (Metal Flower Mark I F), the Devil’s Thirst Tallneck and then Sona’s camp.

END OF CHAPTER 05: 19/23 dummies (if prior counts match); 3/30 Metal Flowers; 2/5 Power Cells. You have finished Insult to Injury and investigated the massacre site; War-Chief’s Trail is still active.
