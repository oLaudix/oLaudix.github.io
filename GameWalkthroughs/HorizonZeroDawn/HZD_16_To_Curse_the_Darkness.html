16 · To Curse the Darkness

Eclipse network · Helis’s camp · sabotage · escape
1. Start from the Grave-Hoard ending.
Track To Curse the Darkness. The last chapter ended outside the ruin after speaking with Sylens.
ACHIEVEMENTCrashed the Eclipse network
Story-related; earned upon completing this quest. No new Power Cell or ally quest is required here.

Approach & Infiltrate

2. Travel to the western quest marker.
Fast travel to Evening Signs if unlocked. Take the westbound road, then the left branch at the first fork; follow the stream through the narrow pass. If using a closer unlocked campfire, follow the marked quest road instead.
3. Find Sylens’s handholds.
Continue past the roadside merchant toward the cliff at the stream’s upper end. Climb the marked grips to the ledge campfire. Do not search for a normal road into the base.
4. Rest at the campfire.
Interact with the quest campfire to advance to night and trigger Sylens’s briefing. Stock medicine and healing potions before proceeding.
5. Enter the crevice beyond the campfire.
Use Focus to tag corrupted Redeye Watchers and Longlegs. Move between grass patches toward the far climbing grips. Bypass enemies or remove isolated Watchers with Silent Strike.
6. Climb into the Scrapper area.
Cross toward the next cliff grips. Corrupted Scrappers can detect you with radar; if spotted, use their exposed components or keep moving toward the route out.
7. Reach the overlook.
Climb the far rock face until the scene reveals the camp and the relocated Tallneck. Continue toward the Eclipse soldiers.
COMBAT OPPORTUNITYOnly if still locked
Scan unseen machine types. Silent Strike kills on Watchers count toward stealth kills; human headshots in the next area count only when lethal. Corrupted machines are not a good substitute for deliberately targeting fire-vulnerable normal machine types.

Helis & The Tallneck

8. Cross the Eclipse camp.
Tag soldiers from the grass. Work across the bridge toward the large tent on the opposite side. Eliminate guards only as needed for safe access. Remember the middle of the bridge: this is your later escape point.
9. Search Helis’s tent with Focus.
Scan the six audio devices in and around the tent, especially on tables and other surfaces. Listen now if desired; the recordings reveal Helis’s beliefs and personal history.

10. Inspect the armor and mural.
Interact with the ceremonial armor and examine the wall decoration if prompted. These are optional environmental lore, not additional audio recordings.
11. Push over the rotten tree.
At the edge beside the camp, use the interaction prompt to make a crossing. Walk across and continue through the wreckage toward the Tallneck.
12. Climb the Tallneck scaffolding.
Use the ramp route rather than the ladder that leads nowhere. Reach the head and trigger the sabotage sequence.
POINT OF NO RETURNFinish the tent first
The bridge is destroyed during the escape. Missed audio devices can be recovered at the base of the waterfall afterward; no need to force a return through the destroyed camp.

Escape & Checkpoint

13. Escape along the cliff.
When control returns, follow the objective and visible climbing grips. Run between exposed sections and heal as needed. Do not engage the Deathbringer or stop to loot.
14. Keep moving through the forest.
Follow the escape path past the pursuing soldiers. Take the zipline on the left when reached; in Remastered its supporting trees may be absent, so look for the cable itself rather than the trees.
15. Return to the bridge.
Run into the earlier camp and approach the rappel point near the center of the bridge. Interact to trigger the scripted fall into the river.
16. Speak with Sylens and check the waterfall.
Complete the conversation. If any Helis recordings were missed, search around the waterfall landing area with Focus before leaving.
17. Verify quest completion and achievement.
To Curse the Darkness must be complete. Check Steam for Crashed the Eclipse network. Next main quest: Deep Secrets of the Earth. Stop before entering the Sunfall facility.
