Horizon Zero Dawn Remastered · 79 / 79 route

01 · Opening & Rost's house

PLAYTHROUGH 1
PAGE 1 OF 2
How to use: Go from top to bottom. Tick the box after doing the action. Follow the game's marker whenever a step says to. This chapter ends before Mother's Heart; don't advance there until you've checked page 2.

Main quest · A Gift from the Past playing as child Aloy

  1. Follow the tutorial through the underground ruin.

    Get the Focus, scan the indicated objects, and follow the purple Focus trail to the exit. Do not waste time looking for the Power Cell yet: the blocked room requires adult Aloy's spear.

Main quest · Lessons of the Wild still playing as child Aloy

  1. Do exactly what Rost teaches.

    Gather Ridge-Wood, craft arrows, scan Watchers, and shoot the weak spot in their eye. When you reach Teb, stay in the red grass and guide him past the machines without being seen. Return to Rost.

    Unlock: “Followed Rost's teachings” (automatic story achievement).

Main quest · The Point of the Spear adult Aloy begins

  1. As soon as adult Aloy appears outside Rost's house, open Skills.

    Spend your starting points on Silent Strike, Lure Call and Concentration. This gives you a reliable method for easy stealth machine kills and better bow aiming.

  2. Do not leave Rost's yard yet. Knock down all SEVEN Grazer dummies.

    Use a heavy spear attack on each wooden, machine-shaped target until it topples:

    • 1 immediately left of the house;
    • 1 on the ledge below and left of that one;
    • 3 on the right, between the two exit gates;
    • 2 left of the campfire.

    Notebook → Statistics → Other → Training Dummies should show 7 / 23.

  3. Pick up Metal Flower Mark I (B) near Rost's house.

    Use the west/back gate of Rost's yard. Take the zipline immediately along the path below it; then walk across the rope to the isolated ledge. Pick up the flower from the little triangular bed of flowers.

    Unlock: “First Metal Flower found” (if this is your first one). This is flower 1 / 30.

  4. Return to the main quest marker and talk to Rost.

    He is standing at a viewpoint along the route downhill from his home. Finish the conversation. You can ask “Anything else?” to pick up the Odd Grata errand, but it is not required for achievements. Activate a nearby campfire.

STOP FOLLOWING THE MAIN MARKER TEMPORARILY. You now have open-world freedom. Before collecting the Tripcaster and meeting Rost again, make the short detour to the childhood ruins on the next page.

01 · Ruins, Tripcaster & Sawtooth

PLAYTHROUGH 1
PAGE 2 OF 2
Current main quest: The Point of the Spear. Do the numbered detour first, then resume the quest marker. No side-quest grinding is required here.

Detour · Childhood Ruins before meeting Rost at the northern gate

  1. Open the map and locate the “Ruins” icon east of Mother's Watch.

    These are the underground ruins where you found the Focus as a child. Place a map marker there; travel to the opening and drop/climb down. This is not the separate “Ancient Armory” bunker.

  2. Get Metal Flower Mark I (A) from the lower floor.

    From the bottom of the ruins, follow the passage until a small flight of stairs and the partly open door. After going through, look for a left-hand passage blocked by pale rock spikes. Smash them with heavy spear attacks. The flower sits among the plants inside.

    Metal Flowers should now be 2 / 30.

  3. Get Power Cell #1 before leaving the ruins.

    Return to the main ruin passage. Continue past the “Happy Birthday Isaac” recording/body area and head upstairs. On the upper level, turn right toward another room blocked by mineral spikes. Break through with your spear and take the Power Cell from the desk/table.

    This starts the Ancient Armory quest. Don't go to its objective yet; the armor needs five cells collected throughout the story.

Resume · The Point of the Spear follow main-quest markers again

  1. Track “The Point of the Spear.” Kill Striders for Blaze.

    Find the Strider herd marked by the quest, scan them if you haven't already, then kill and loot them until you have 3 Blaze. Open Crafting and make the Fire Arrows the quest requests.

  2. Follow the quest marker to Karst's house, southeast of Mother's Cradle.

    Before entering the merchant dialogue, knock down the one Grazer dummy right outside his house with a heavy spear attack. Talk to Karst, trade for the Tripcaster, then equip it on your weapon wheel.

    Dummy total should now be 8 / 23.

  3. On the way through Mother's Cradle, collect its other Grazer dummy.

    In the settlement, check the southwest corner; look behind the building by the campfire and knock down the dummy there. This is separate from the one outside Karst's house.

    Dummy total should now be 9 / 23.

  4. Now follow the marker to Rost near the Northern Embrace Gate.

    Save/rest at the campfire when prompted; follow the destruction trail. Scan the Sawtooth and mark its walking path with the Focus. Lay Tripcaster Shock Wires across that path, lure it through a wire, then hit it with fire arrows while it's stunned. Repeat until dead, loot it, and talk to Rost.

    Unlock: “Defeated the Sawtooth” (automatic story achievement).

CHAPTER COMPLETE. You should have: two story achievements (“Followed Rost's teachings”; “Defeated the Sawtooth”), 9/23 dummies, 2/30 Metal Flowers, 1/5 Power Cells, and your Tripcaster. Skill/stealth-kill totals may vary.
