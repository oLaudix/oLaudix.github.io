13A · MARAD’S LEAD
Sun Palace → northern travel marker
□
01. Return to the Sun Palace in Meridian.
From Dimmed Bones, fast-travel to a Meridian campfire, then track Into the Borderlands. Go up to the Palace of the Sun, where Blameless Marad will meet you at the guarded entrance.
□
02. Speak to Marad and FOLLOW him to Avad.
Do not go hunting for Erend elsewhere in the city. Marad escorts you to the Sun-King Avad, where the group discusses Ersa and the Oseram rebel Dervahl. Let the scene and conversations finish.
□
03. Talk with Avad, Erend and Marad.
After the initial discussion, speak to Avad to hear the remaining explanation, then speak to Erend and Marad nearby. Your quest destination becomes Pitchcliff, a settlement far north of Meridian.
OPTIONAL LORE · SUN PALACERead TWO palace glyphs while you are already here
Founding of Meridian: scan the document on/near the bench or seat behind Avad. The Sun-Kings: go toward the open lounge/courtyard behind Erend and Marad, among the cushions and couches. Check for cube symbols on the compass and scan with Focus. Both are optional.
□
04. Activate the Pitchcliff quest marker.
Open the quest journal, confirm Into the Borderlands is active, and set its distant marker. Travel to the closest discovered campfire, then take the northern road toward Pitchcliff. Do not follow the separate Maker’s End marker.
NO EXTRA DETOURSYour map markers are yours to clear
You can collect nearby Vantages, Flowers and Vessels as usual. The checklist below does not require clearing Cauldrons or Hunting Grounds before proceeding to Pitchcliff.

13F · THROUGH THE PALACE
Cliff route · Solarium · Dervahl
□
27. Follow the cliffside walkway and find the climb holds.
From the broken wall, track Dervahl’s route. Near the trees and bridge, look below the walkway for yellow handholds; descend carefully and traverse the wooden beams/rock ledges. Follow the climb markers, not a shortcut jump.
□
28. Climb up to the bridge and eliminate TWO lookouts.
Use the climb points and ladder to regain the upper crossing. Two Oseram mercenaries guard the way. Take them down with ranged headshots or stealth; this is a convenient last chance for human-kill achievements.
□
29. Go through the circular room and upstairs.
Move through the arch into the round chamber. Follow its stairs upward toward the Solarium (Sun Palace courtyard) and watch the scene with Dervahl and Avad.
□
30. DESTROY the sonic device first.
Once Aloy regains control, locate Dervahl’s active sonic emitter beside Avad. Approach and interact with it. The sonic trap must be disabled before you can finish the confrontation.
□
31. Defeat Dervahl and his mercenaries.
Follow through the nearby gate to Dervahl. He carries a Firespitter and has Oseram soldiers supporting him. Keep behind the palace cover, focus the weapon wielder and prevent archers from pinning you down.
□
32. Prepare for the incoming Glinthawks.
Dervahl summons flying machines after the human fight. Scan a Glinthawk if it is a new catalogue entry. Their bodies are weak to Fire; apply burning to bring them down, then use impact shots or Critical Hits.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITIESMachine catalogue and combat-category kills
The Glinthawks here provide a story-gated opportunity to scan/kill a flying Acquisition machine. If your 10 Vulnerable machine kills achievement is still incomplete, burning Glinthawks can help. If it already unlocked, simply finish the fight.

13E · THE WAREHOUSE
Avad’s lead → bomb → hidden escape route
□
21. Go to the Sun Palace and report to Avad.
Track The Sun Shall Fall. In the royal audience area, speak to Avad and hear the new lead tied to the name Aelund Forgeman. Erend and Marad are involved in the discussion.
□
22. Head to the warehouse near the Hunter’s Lodge.
The warehouse is in the southwestern portion of Meridian, close to the Lodge you visited earlier. Follow the main quest marker to its entrance and meet Erend there.
□
23. Inspect the bomb on the GROUND floor.
Inside, do not fire at the explosive material. Use Focus and interact with the bomb and green Blaze residue/barrels as prompted to determine how the trap is arranged.
□
24. Climb upstairs and examine the window and barrels.
Go to the second floor. Investigate the wooden window brace/double doors and the Blaze barrels. Interact when prompted so Aloy and Erend can push the explosives outside.
□
25. After the blast, SEARCH the warehouse area.
The crisis is not over. Use Focus on the ground near the warehouse to find tracks leading away from it. Highlight the trail and follow it rather than returning straight to the Palace.
□
26. Follow tracks to the house, then descend to its cellar.
The trail leads toward a house at the northwest edge of Meridian. Enter, go downstairs, and exit through the broken outer wall. You will reach a walkway below the bridge used by Dervahl’s men.
MAIN QUEST · DO NOT SKIPThe bomb is only the first half of The Sun Shall Fall
The quest continues through the city walls to the royal Solarium. The achievement unlocks only when the whole quest finishes.

13B · PITCHCLIFF
Meet Erend · find the spy · approach the camp
□
05. Enter Pitchcliff and locate Erend.
Continue into the settlement and find the merchant/campfire area marked by the quest. Erend will meet you here as you search for Marad’s operative. Speak to him before following any trail.
□
06. Use Focus at the operative’s meeting spot.
From the central meeting area, scan for tracks and follow the highlighted trail back along the road, then around the rear/west side of the nearby buildings. Stay on this trail instead of following the distant camp marker prematurely.
□
07. Find the spy near the well and examine the body.
The operative lies behind buildings near a well. Interact with the body and the map drawn in blood. That evidence provides the location of Dervahl’s camp and updates the main quest.
□
08. Leave Pitchcliff, head north and take the uphill branch.
Follow the quest marker toward the road north of town, then through the fork near a river/campfire. Take the branch heading uphill past the spiked torches until you approach Dervahl’s hideout.
□
09. Meet Erend at the camp overlook BEFORE attacking.
Erend waits on the high ground above the fortified camp. Speak to him, watch the setup, and use Focus to tag Oseram soldiers and the captive machines. You can choose stealth or an open fight.
OPTIONAL COLLECTIBLEBanuk Figurine: Mother, on the mountain en route
The ribbon-marked mountain approach near the northern road holds a Banuk Figurine. Collect it if your map route takes you past it; no separate detour is required.

13C · THE REBEL CAMP
Fight Oseram · capture key quest item
□
10. Clear the LOWER camp guards first.
Use cover and the marked enemies. You can eliminate humans silently with headshots, or release chained machines by shooting their restraints so they attack the camp. Released machines can also target Aloy.
□
11. Move through the camp in the quest-marker direction.
Push through the lower and upper defended areas. There may be Longlegs and Ravagers held in/around the camp. Continue fighting until the quest lets you approach the house at the northern end.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITIESIf you still need these, this is a convenient place
All machines catalogued: scan Longleg and Ravager if not registered. Tore off 5 heavy weapons: strip a Ravager Cannon if you have not finished that achievement. Human headshot kills and Strike from Above also work here. Do not farm an achievement you already have.
□
12. Enter the NORTHERN house and experience the sonic trap.
Follow the quest marker into the house. A powerful sound attack prevents you from continuing downward. Do not attempt to force your way past it yet. A heavily armed enemy will appear.
□
13. Defeat Dervahl’s Champion.
Fight the Oseram heavy carrying a Firespitter. Keep distance, use cover against his sustained fire and shoot from angles that do not expose you for long. After he falls, loot his body.
□
14. Take the earplugs from the Champion.
This is the critical quest item that lets Aloy withstand the sonic attack. If the stairwell still stops you, check that you looted the Champion and that the interaction prompt has updated.
DO NOT MIX UP CAMP TYPESDervahl’s camp is NOT a Bandit Camp achievement site
Clearing these Oseram enemies advances Into the Borderlands, but it does not add another camp to the separate Cleared all Bandit Camps achievement.

13D · ERSA’S WORKSHOP
Sonic device → story reveal → datapoints
□
15. Disable the sonic device at the stairwell.
With the earplugs obtained, return to the house’s stairs. Find the blinking switch/control on the wall near the sonic emitter and interact with it. Only then continue down to the basement.
□
16. Go downstairs and find Ersa.
Follow the basement corridor and open the room indicated by the objective marker. Watch the scene with Ersa and Erend to learn what actually happened and what Dervahl intends to do next.
□
17. Explore the workshop using Focus before leaving.
After the conversation, scan the basement room for Machine Parts, a Recording Device and Dervahl’s Journal. These objects are relevant both to the quest and to the story.
□
18. Read/listen to the TWO workshop datapoints.
Recording Device: look toward the northeastern side of the workshop, near the doorway, scan and play its audio. Dervahl’s Journal: scan the southeastern part of the workshop and read it. The journal contains the shipping clue.
OPTIONAL LORE · IMPORTANT TO UNDERSTANDING DERVAHLRecording Device + Dervahl’s Journal
These are the most worthwhile lore entries in this chapter. The journal is involved in the investigation; the Recording Device provides extra context for Dervahl’s hatred. If the compass shows a cube, scan before leaving.
□
19. Finish examining the workshop clues, then talk to Erend.
Do the quest-required scans until the objective advances. Speak to Erend after collecting the evidence. Into the Borderlands completes and The Sun Shall Fall begins.
□
20. Return to Meridian; prepare for a city battle.
Fast-travel to Meridian or return by road. Replenish healing items and Fire Arrows and ensure a reliable ranged weapon is equipped. Flying machines appear later in the next mission.
