Northern Road

01. Activate The Grave-Hoard in Main Quests.
Start from your post-Maker’s End location. Open the world map, select The Grave-Hoard as the tracked quest, and fast-travel to the closest discovered campfire in the far northeastern mountains of Nora territory. Do not confuse it with Maker’s End (northwest).
02. Approach from the campfire SOUTHEAST of the objective.
If you have this campfire unlocked, follow its road west, then north around the mountain toward the ancient military ruin. This route is verified; do not attempt an unmarked cliff shortcut. If the campfire is undiscovered, follow the marked mountain roads from northern Nora lands until you reach it.
03. Handle the Glinthawks only if they attack.
Glinthawks fly over part of the approach. You may pass without fighting. If they attack, use Fire Arrows or pull one down with a Ropecaster; no fixed kill count is needed here for an achievement.
04. Follow the marked route past rusted military wrecks.
Look for old tanks and ruined equipment. The actual entrance is in the rocky, snow-covered military site at the end of the road. It is open for the quest; older pre-quest information about an ice-blocked entrance does not apply now.
05. Save at the nearby campfire, then proceed inside.
Check health and medicine before the bunker: there is a puzzle, several Eclipse fights and a mobile Deathbringer. Carry a bow for precise impact damage, Fire ammo and some tear ammo. No new weapon purchase is mandatory.
ROUTE GUARDRAILDo not enter the Frozen Wilds yet
The Cut is north of the northern Grave-Hoard region, but our first-playthrough DLC block is reserved for later. Stay with the Grave-Hoard objective on the world map.
ALREADY COMPLETEDDo not repeat these earlier quests
Hunter’s Blind → Deadliest Game → Redmaw, Honor the Fallen, A Moment’s Peace, Sunstone Rock, The Sun Shall Fall and Maker’s End have already been covered in your preceding chapters. This route does not restart their objectives.

The Entrance

06. Clear the entry guardroom, or isolate enemies from cover.
Inside the first military chamber are a Corrupted Watcher, a regular cultist and a tougher Eclipse soldier. Use Focus to tag them. Silent Strike the Watcher if useful, then deal with the cultists from a safe angle. They block progression; do not assume you can simply run through.
07. Read the FOUR entrance Comms Logs before descending.
Use Focus on the soldiers’ bodies, desks and makeshift partitions at this entry level. Collect Comms Log: Lt. Murell, Comms Log: Sgt. Guliyev, Comms Log: Cpl. Mills and Comms Log: Sgt. Wandari. The last is easy to overlook if you rush toward the drop.
08. OPTIONAL: take the Stranded Necklace before the zipline.
At the entrance level, walk west and then north around the old tank. Inspect the corpse tucked behind it. Its Stranded Necklace is a Death Stranding easter-egg item, not a 79/79 achievement requirement.
09. Locate the wire-zipline NORTH of the entry chamber.
Return east from the necklace and look for the large pit beyond the entry ruins. A sloping cable/wire above the gap functions as the zipline. Check the four entrance audio logs BEFORE taking this one-way descent.
OPTIONAL LORE · WHY IT MATTERSThese are reports from the Old World front line
The four Comms Logs capture soldiers’ final movements as the automated armies overwhelm their defenses. Listen rather than only scanning if you enjoy environmental storytelling.
ACHIEVEMENT CHECKHeadshot 30 human enemies / Stealth killed 10 machines
This entrance has qualifying cultists and a Watcher. Count lethal headshots only; do not count mere impacts. If either achievement is already done, proceed normally.

Barracks And Records

10. Ride the cable into the lower barracks.
After your first zipline, follow the accessible corridor northwest. Check nearby desks/bunks for Log: Cpl. Acosta (A) and Log: Cpl. Acosta (B) (audio).
11. At the first fork, inspect the EAST/SOUTHEAST branch.
Move northeast until the corridor forks. Take the small branch east/southeast to scan Reminder. Again. (audio), then enter the chamber immediately to the northeast for Just a Little Longer and So Sorry! (audio).
12. Make the northwest side-room detour BEFORE the next drop.
From that branch, head northwest into the room complex. Ignore the hole with a second cable-zipline for the moment. Find two texts on consoles: Log: Cpl. Sarai (A) and Log: Cpl. Sarai (B).
13. Return to the hole and descend on the SECOND cable.
Once you have the five lower-barracks audio logs and both Sarai texts, return southeast to the hole and take the improvised zipline. This leads toward the ancient door and the power puzzle.
14. Approach the sealed door and Focus-scan it.
Follow the northwest corridor. The door has no power; the objective updates to Restore Power to the Ancient Door. Follow its nearby cables west into the circular lock room.
BEFORE THE SECOND ZIPLINEAudio log tally: 9 of 16
You should now have 4 entry Comms Logs + Acosta A/B + Reminder. Again. + Just a Little Longer + So Sorry!. Also 2 of the 4 text records: both Sarai files. If a scan is missing, the purple cube/Focus is your best local locator.

Restore Three Circuits

15. UPPER circuit: scan the Code Nexus to the WEST.
The first group of four Holo Locks is on the upper ring. Use the beam and a running jump to reach the opposite walkway. Face the control panel and set the four discs left to right as printed below.

16. Drop to the lower ring and solve SET 2.
At the southern end of the upper walkway, look southeast to the lower beam; jump/climb onto it and drop safely to the lower walkway. Turn northwest; scan Code Nexus Reminder (the lower-circuit audio clue) beside the controls, then apply row 2.
17. Continue counterclockwise to SET 3.
Pass the ladder, reach the final group of four locks, and enter row 3. Its display may look unreadable/blank: the ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ arrangement is confirmed and works.
18. Climb back UP the ladder and OPEN the door.
Once all three sets illuminate blue, return to the original sealed door. It should now be interactable. This is the crucial checkpoint for Power Cell #4.
READ THE PANEL, NOT THE COMPASSArrows denote the position of each RED segment
All four discs within each set are counted left to right as you face that set; the arrows are not directions to walk. The second set’s audio clue is Code Nexus Reminder.

Power Cell #4

19. Immediately through the door: PICK UP POWER CELL #4.
In the corridor just beyond the restored door, pause before the next fight. On the left / floor near a Supply Box and Medicinal Mushrooms is the Power Cell. Pick it up; confirm Ancient Armory is now 4/5. Do not search the later Deathbringer chamber for it.
20. Scout the armory from the corridor.
The next room has multiple Eclipse cultists, including tougher officers, and a Corrupted Scrapper. Scan the Scrapper if you somehow still need its machine entry; Focus-tag every human target from the narrow doorway.
21. Use the hallway and stairs as a defensive choke point.
The armory has open sightlines and limited grass. Put Blast Tripwires on approaches if equipped, pick one enemy off with Precision Arrows, then back up into the Power Cell corridor to avoid getting surrounded. The enemies often hesitate at this threshold.
22. Clear all required enemies; loot only if desired.
Kill the cultists and Corrupted Scrapper until the journal changes from Kill the Eclipse in the Armory to exploring again. Check whether your human headshot achievement has popped, but do not farm extra enemies.
23. Cross the far pit using the yellow beam and handholds.
Exit the armory east. Jump from the yellow projecting beam to the ledge across the gap. Follow the yellow climbing grips north/northwest into the next section of U.S. Robot Command.
MANDATORY PICKUPThe fourth Power Cell is BEFORE the armory enemies
The story path leads straight past it. It is not behind a false wall, at the bottom of a side shaft, or after the final boss. If the next combat starts and your count is still 3/5, return to the corridor now.

The Last Messages

24. Enter the archive/computer room to the NORTH.
Before following the main marker straight into the command center, turn into the adjoining room with the workstations. Five audio datapoints are clustered here; this is the densest optional lore pocket of the quest.
25. Collect all FIVE archive audio files.
Scan the consoles and recordings around the room: Edited and Approved 1, Edited and Approved 2, I Believe in You, Please Reply!, Unit Status Report. Listen to the raw messages and then their edited versions; the contrast is striking.
26. Return west to the U.S. Robot Command center.
Enter the room with the large globe / Earth holographic display. Use the Holo Projector to see the advance of the machine plague. Complete Aloy and Sylens’ dialogue as it updates the objective.
27. Read USRC Deployment Records on the table.
After the holo-map, search the southern side of the command-room conference table and Focus-scan USRC Deployment Records (quest text #3 of four).
28. Take the NORTH exit toward the next chamber.
Follow the objective through the nearby doors. You will reach an elevated walkway overlooking an excavation where Eclipse cultists are working on an ancient war machine. Do not drop into combat before the reveal/cutscene.
LORE HIGHLIGHTRead “Edited and Approved 1/2” with the original soldier logs
The edited recordings sanitize front-line casualties and fears for public consumption. If you enjoy the writing in Horizon, this juxtaposition is among the strongest optional moments in the whole base game.
DATAPOINT COUNT BEFORE BOSS15 / 16 audio • 3 / 4 text
Audio: 4 entry + 5 barracks + Code Nexus Reminder + 5 archives = 15. Text: both Sarai logs + USRC Deployment Records = 3. Final audio and text come AFTER the Deathbringer.

Active Deathbringer

29. After the cutscene, tag cultists from the HIGH walkway.
This is a mobile, fully functional Deathbringer, unlike the stationary damaged one in Maker’s End. Scan the Deathbringer if its entry is missing; then use Focus to find Eclipse snipers on ledges and enemies below.
30. Remove exposed human enemies without drawing boss fire.
Snipe the most dangerous cultists (especially those on elevated positions) while the Deathbringer is not aggroed, if your position allows. Keep lethal headshots for Headshot 30 human enemies if you still need them.
31. RAPPEL DOWN before openly attacking the Deathbringer.
Do not remain on the initial hanging catwalk while trading shots with the machine. The Deathbringer’s heavy fire can destroy this walkway, dropping Aloy. Use the marked rappel point off the raised platform or the safe side descent to reach ground level.
32. Fight at ground level, moving between cover.
Keep lateral movement during heavy weapon bursts; large missiles, rotary guns and flame attacks can strip cover. Stay far enough out to read its attack animations, but close enough for precision on exposed components.
33. Build Fire, then hit the glowing heat components.
Fire arrows can help trigger overheating. During an exposed phase, target orange Cooling Rods, Heat Vents or the Core with hard-hitting impact arrows. During certain attacks, leg/knee weak points become exposed; destroying them can interrupt or topple it.
34. Finish the machine and any remaining cultists.
If Tore off 5 heavy weapons is still missing, tear an accessible detachable turret only if safe. Otherwise use the weak-point cycle; do not spend ten minutes forcing a particular achievement on this boss. Loot the machine if desired.
DIFFICULTY NOTICE · HARDThis is not the Maker’s End Deathbringer
The machine walks and has more active weapons. Do not rely on the stationary-boss strategy from Chapter 14; the initial walkway is a hazard, not reliable permanent cover.

Project Zero Dawn

35. Enter the marked door SOUTHWEST of the battleground.
With the Deathbringer and cultists defeated, follow the objective into the surviving command complex. Take the southwest door, then the hallway that bends west.
36. Scan the LAST quest text just BEFORE the War Room.
In a side room/hallway corner on the approach, find Biosphere Degradation. This is quest-text datapoint #4/4 inside the Grave-Hoard. Do not rush the projector and skip it.
37. Activate the War Room Holo Projector.
Enter the conference/War Room. Interact with the holo display and watch the archive explaining Operation: Enduring Victory and the military’s relationship to Project: Zero Dawn. The critical exposition is in the hologram itself.
38. After the playback, scan the final audio recording.
Before leaving, turn toward the southwest side of the War Room and Focus-scan SecureCom EVZD-XX1X011X. You should now have all 16 Grave-Hoard audio logs.
39. Take the newly opened SOUTH exit to the ladder.
After the holographic meeting ends, an exit opens in the southern side of the room. Go through the doorway and climb the ladder in the escape elevator to reach the outside.
40. Speak with Sylens OUTSIDE and finish the quest.
Listen to his explanation of the Shadow Carja/Eclipse Focus network and Aloy’s next objective. The Grave-Hoard completes and To Curse the Darkness becomes available. Stop here; do not infiltrate the network camp yet.
ACCURATE ACHIEVEMENT CHECKPOINTNo story achievement pops merely for this discovery
The story-related Crashed the Eclipse network is tied to To Curse the Darkness; Discovered the truth comes at Deep Secrets of the Earth. Grave-Hoard completion is an essential main-quest gate, not itself a Steam badge.
