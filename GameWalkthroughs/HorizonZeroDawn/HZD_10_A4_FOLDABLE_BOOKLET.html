10A · DEADLIEST GAME
Begin inside Meridian’s Hunter’s Lodge
□
01. Talk to Talanah to accept Deadliest Game.
Select Deadliest Game under Errands. Find Talanah on the lower floor of the Lodge and exhaust her dialogue; follow her outside and speak again if prompted. Only now begin hunting the new quest trophies: one Thunderjaw and one Stormbird.
□
02. Prepare for two major machines before leaving.
Equip Tearblast/Tear arrows, your strongest high-damage ammunition, a Ropecaster if available, healing supplies and a suitable resistance outfit. Store spare resources; you need space to loot both machine carcasses. These are not ordinary Watchers.
□
03. Track the Thunderjaw trophy site first.
Use the Thunderjaw marker supplied by Deadliest Game; a suitable site is northeast of Meridian, near a campfire. Scan the machine with Focus before the fight if this species is new to your machine catalogue. Tag the two large Disc Launchers on its back.
□
04. Remove the Disc Launchers before prolonged combat.
From cover, fire Tearblast Arrows into the two launchers on its back. This disables a major ranged attack. Achievement opportunity: Tore off 5 heavy weapons. Each qualifying launcher detached contributes, if the achievement is not already complete. Pick up a fallen launcher and fire it back at the Thunderjaw.
□
05. Finish the Thunderjaw and LOOT its corpse.
Dodge charges, use rocks as cover, and focus damage on exposed weak points. Freeze can help if you can apply the status. When it falls, loot the body and check that Thunderjaw Trophy 1/1 appears under quest progress. A kill without looting is not enough.
DON’T WASTE ANOTHER HUNTThe trophy is a QUEST ITEM
Previous Thunderjaw kills do not supply this trophy retroactively. If you already fought one earlier, the quest still needs a new kill with Deadliest Game active.

10F · THE LAKE SHRINE
Utaru pilgrim · report to Namman
□
26. Descend and travel southeast to the Lake Shrine.
From Sun’s Climb, climb back down and select the Lake Shrine objective. The marker lies at the edge of the lake southeast of Sun’s Climb / east of Meridian. The shoreline is guarded by two Snapmaws.
□
27. Kill both Snapmaws — clear the site.
Scan one if it is new. Use cover and range to avoid their lunges and freeze projectiles. Fire can be effective; a Ropecaster can temporarily isolate one while you fight the other. Both Snapmaws must be killed before the pilgrim can safely approach.
□
28. Speak with Rea, the Utaru pilgrim.
When the area is safe, wait for Rea to move to the shrine by the water and talk to her. Listen to the story of the person she lost. Check that this is pilgrim 3/3 if you followed the booklet order.
□
29. Fast-travel to Meridian and find Namman again.
Travel back to the city’s central/southeastern temple area above the elevators, near the open-air Sun-Ring. Follow the Return to Mournful Namman objective to his position. Do not assume completion merely because all three markers disappear.
□
30. Report the pilgrims’ outcomes and finish the quest.
Speak to Namman until Honor the Fallen is marked Completed. This finishes another required quest for All allies joined. Your dialogue choices with the pilgrims and priest are not a special achievement condition.
LORE OPPORTUNITYThe Red Raids from three perspectives
The pilgrims’ dialogue is the main lore here; it explains how the Oseram, Banuk and Utaru remember Carja persecution. There is no need to search every shrine for an unverified extra datapad.

10E · HONOR THE FALLEN
First: Shrine of Kings · Second: Sun’s Climb
□
21. Travel to the Shrine of Kings, northeast of Meridian.
Use the shrine objective marker; the shrine lies south of Cut-Cliffs. Look near the entry for an Oseram pilgrim, Brageld. Speak to him and learn why the Sun-Priest is keeping mourners out.
□
22. Convince the Sun-Priest, then return to Brageld.
Talk to the Sun-Priest at the shrine entrance and use the available conversation to make him allow access. Talk to Brageld again after the priest relents. This second conversation registers the first pilgrim as helped.
□
23. Travel toward Sun’s Climb, east of Meridian.
Activate the Sun’s Climb objective. It is a high freestanding rock pillar in the swamplands east of Meridian. Approach its southern side to find the climbing route; running around the cliff base is not the same as reaching the shrine.
□
24. Climb and eliminate THREE Glinthawks.
Follow the yellow handholds and ledges toward the top. Three Glinthawks occupy the summit: use Fire or your preferred anti-air tactics, ground them if possible, and kill all three. The pilgrim appears once the site is safe.
□
25. Talk to the Banuk pilgrim at the summit.
Speak with Kimik and listen to the remembrance ritual. Stay until the quest objective updates to show this pilgrim helped. His account of the Red Raids is excellent optional story lore — don’t rush the dialogue.
OPTIONAL MAP COLLECTIBLEMetal Flower at Sun’s Climb
There is a Metal Flower Mark III on a nearby rock pillar accessible via a tightrope at the summit. Your collectible map marks it; grab it while you are already up here if you want. No separate route needed.

10B · STORMBIRD TROPHY
One flying target · then return to the Lodge
□
06. Follow the quest marker to a Stormbird site.
Select the Stormbird Trophy objective. A marked site is in the country southeast of Meridian. Choose an open area with space to dodge. Scan the Stormbird with Focus before fighting, if you have not catalogued one.
□
07. Prioritize the Lightning Gun on its chest.
It has a dangerous lightning weapon mounted in the center of its chest. Use Tearblast or accurate high-damage shots to disable it. Keep moving during ranged barrages and dodge its dives; Shock resistance helps.
□
08. Ground it when possible, then target weak points.
A capable Ropecaster can tie it down temporarily. Use the opening to shoot wing engines and other exposed components. Don’t stand underneath a hovering Stormbird. There is no trial timer, so a deliberate fight is fine.
□
09. Loot the Stormbird for the second trophy.
After the kill, loot the carcass and check Stormbird Trophy 1/1. If either quest trophy is missing, inspect your Journal before leaving; normal machine parts are not a substitute.
□
10. Return to Meridian and speak to Ligan.
Fast-travel to the city and enter the Hunter’s Lodge. Check upstairs where Ahsis usually stands: both Ahsis and Talanah are gone. Return to Ligan at the entrance and speak to him. Deadliest Game completes and Redmaw begins.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITYTwo machine categories and a heavy weapon
The two kills help machine-category achievements; the scans help All machines catalogued. For Tore off 5 heavy weapons, Thunderjaw Disc Launchers are a clear opportunity — but do not keep farming if you already unlocked it.

10C · FIND REDMAW
Mercenaries · Focus tracks · the giant Thunderjaw
□
11. Make Redmaw your active quest.
Under Side Quests, select Redmaw. Leave Meridian for its marker in the area south/southeast of the city, near the Tallneck and Stalker territory. Do not confuse this with your earlier Thunderjaw trophy site. Save before the encounter.
□
12. Help Talanah fight the attacking Outlanders.
At the marked position, Talanah is surrounded by human mercenaries. Use nearby grass and cover to mark them with Focus, then remove the attackers. Achievement opportunity: any lethal headshots and elevated Strike from Above kills can count toward your remaining combat achievements.
□
13. Speak to Talanah after the human fight.
Do not just head for the next Thunderjaw icon. Interact with Talanah, hear what happened between her and Ahsis, then follow her down the indicated route. Stay with her until she stops and mentions losing the trail.
□
14. Use Focus to pick up Redmaw’s tracks.
When Talanah stops, activate your Focus, locate the large machine trail in the ground and highlight it. Follow the trail, not your own guess about the nearest Thunderjaw site, until the quest scene triggers.
□
15. Find Ahsis already fighting Redmaw.
At the hunting ground ahead, the huge Thunderjaw Redmaw is engaged with Ahsis. Let the confrontation and dialogue play out, then get ready to intervene. Treat Redmaw as a serious fight despite your current character level.
GOOD TO KNOWNo ally can be lost through combat dialogue here
We need the complete Redmaw quest to recruit Talanah. You do not need a particular dialogue choice to secure the achievement; finish the quest and its closing conversation.

10D · FINISH REDMAW
Complete the Lodge story, then switch to Namman
□
16. Disable Redmaw’s weapons, then finish the fight.
Target the Disc Launchers first with Tear damage, take cover from its lasers, and keep dodging charges. You can pick up detached launchers and use them against it. Focus on exposed components rather than firing randomly into its armor. Keep healing supplies available.
□
17. Speak to BOTH survivors after Redmaw falls.
After the machine is defeated, follow the prompt to talk with Talanah and Ahsis. Do not leave merely because the Thunderjaw is dead. Loot Redmaw and other enemies if convenient.
□
18. Return to the Hunter’s Lodge for the last conversation.
Fast-travel back to Meridian and speak with Talanah at the Lodge. Confirm the quest Redmaw completes and the achievement Hunted Redmaw with Talanah unlocks. If it does not, check whether a final marked dialogue is still waiting.
□
19. OPTIONAL LORE: read the Redmaw records upstairs.
On the upper floor of the Hunter’s Lodge, scan the readable glyphs “Record of Redmaw 1,” “Record of Redmaw 2” (available after completion) and “Legendary Hunts”. If you missed “Bylaws of the Lodge”, look downstairs near the counter. All are lore-only.
□
20. Select Honor the Fallen — already accepted.
You spoke with Mournful Namman in Chapter 09, so you should have Honor the Fallen in your Journal. Select it now. Three separate shrine markers appear: Shrine of Kings, Sun’s Climb, Lake Shrine. Complete each and return to Namman at the end.
ACHIEVEMENTS EARNEDThis ends the Hunter’s Lodge chain
Hunted Redmaw with Talanah should now be yours. The Talanah requirement for All allies joined is also satisfied; Honor the Fallen handles another required ally.
