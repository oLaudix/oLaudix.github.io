12A · SUNSTONE ROCK
Go to the southern prison; gain Janeva’s task
□
01. Activate Sunstone Rock’s location and travel south.
From the Rockwreath area, open the map and set a waypoint on Sunstone Rock, the prison at the southern edge of the Jewel. Fast-travel to the closest discovered campfire and follow the road/waypoint to its fortified entrance. This side quest begins on arrival; no previous NPC is needed.
□
02. Help the guards defeat the TWO Behemoths.
When you approach the prison, you should see Carja guards fighting two Behemoths. Scan one with Focus if Behemoth is not yet in your Machine Catalogue. Freeze, target its exposed cargo/weak points and avoid its wide charges.
□
03. Enter the prison and speak with Warden Janeva.
After both Behemoths fall, go inside Sunstone Rock and find Janeva, the prison warden. Ask about the attack and escaped criminals. You are now tasked with tracking Ullia, Gavan and Rasgrund, in any order.
□
04. Mark the three escaped prisoners in your quest log.
Keep Sunstone Rock active. Open its objectives to see markers for Blackwing Snag, Brightmarket and the Dusk Mesa ravine. The next pages use that exact order; don’t hunt unrelated icons unless you want to.
OPTIONAL LORE · GLYPH 17“Captains and Capers” — before leaving the prison
Outside Sunstone Rock, near the cornfield beside the road leading into the prison, inspect a boulder on the right when approaching the settlement. The glyph is a colorful Carja adventure story; compass cube/Focus helps. Not required for 79/79.
ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITYTransport-machine kill and scan progress
The two Behemoths are good opportunities to register the Behemoth machine type (scan and kill) for the machine-catalogue and machine-category achievements.

12F · THE FIELD OF THE FALLEN
Follow Erend; uncover the staged ambush
□
27. Follow Erend to the original ambush site.
Stay on his quest trail southwest along the river. When he stops where Ersa’s group was supposedly killed, switch on Focus. Don’t race off toward the next marker until the investigation objective updates.
□
28. Scan and EXAMINE the FIVE battlefield clues.
Locate the helmet, bloodstains, thicket, arrows and spear. Use Focus to highlight each, then approach and interact with the physical evidence as prompted. The thicket reveals an inconsistency in the official story.
□
29. Tag the cart tracks and FOLLOW them.
After the scene, use Focus to highlight the wagon/cart tracks. Follow them away from the initial site, along the river and up the sloping, winding path toward the Old World ruins called Dimmed Bones.
□
30. At Dimmed Bones, prepare for a human ambush.
As you approach the central ruins, Erend triggers a scene and Oseram mercenaries attack. Several use raised shooting positions, so fight from cover; melee enemies close in from lower ground.
□
31. Defeat the mercenaries, then anticipate TWO Ravagers.
Once the human attackers fall, two Ravagers enter the area. Plant tripwires if you have breathing room. Tear off their Ravager Cannons if you still need progress on Tore off 5 heavy weapons; scan one if it is new.
OPTIONAL, MAP-MARKED COLLECTIBLEAncient Vessel — Jomei
Before you leave Dimmed Bones, inspect the Ancient Debris by the entrance/approach to the ruins. The Jomei vessel’s purple marker should be easy to locate on the map. You already handle map-marked collectibles yourself.

12E · FAMILY RESCUE → EREND
Finish Collateral; begin The Field of the Fallen
□
21. Clear all Eclipse cultists.
Remove the archer first from the high ground if possible, then deal with the gunner and remaining guards. Stay behind the stone and building corners when the Deathbringer Gun is firing.
□

A dead Eclipse guard carries the key for the locked family cellar. Loot the bodies rather than searching the building blindly. If the key is already obtained, proceed straight to the cellar.

Look for the locked basement doors at ground level along a side of the building. Open them with the key; go inside and speak to Olin’s wife and child.

Switch to the Main Quests tab and activate The Field of the Fallen, which has been available since inspecting Olin’s apartment. Set the objective to meet Erend at Red Ridge Pass.

Follow the marker to the road at Red Ridge Pass. Kill the attacking Lancehorns and Watchers, then speak with Erend. Scan any machine you have not registered yet before killing it.

12B · THE FIRST TWO FUGITIVES
Ullia at Blackwing Snag; Gavan near Brightmarket
□
05. Set the Blackwing Snag marker and head to its camp.
Travel northeast of Sunstone Rock to Blackwing Snag Bandit Camp, built around a huge Old World aircraft wreck. If the camp icon is still hostile, this is one of the six base-game Bandit Camps you need to clear.
□
06. Clear the Bandit Camp, but do not leave yet.
If bandits still control the place, work from elevated cover, remove guards and alarm threats, then free captives if shown. Do not assume the prisoner quest is complete just because the camp turns green. If you cleared it earlier, skip the bandits; Ullia still appears for the quest.
□
07. Find and defeat Ullia on the raised platform.
Use the quest marker to find Ullia, the escaped Tenakth prisoner, on the camp’s central wooden lookout/platform. Defeat her and watch the quest objective update. Interact afterward if the game prompts you.
OPTIONAL LORE · WORLD DATAPOINT #56“What Scares Me...” — inside Blackwing Snag
Search the northern part of the camp, close to the massive crashed-metal wreckage and palisade/bridge area. Look for the small cube on your compass. It is an optional Old World text datapoint.
□
08. Travel to Brightmarket for Gavan.
Choose Find Gavan in Sunstone Rock, fast-travel to Brightmarket (visited in Chapter 11), and go to the inn. Speak to its innkeeper; the dialogue does not reveal Gavan directly, so the next clue is outside.
□
09. Find the manacles outside the inn.
Leave the inn from its northern side. By the small bridge/rocks, switch on Focus and examine a set of discarded handcuffs/manacles. Highlight the tracks that appear; follow them southwest out of Brightmarket.
□
10. Save Gavan from the smugglers.
Follow those tracks to small buildings west of Brightmarket. Defeat the men threatening the injured Gavan, then speak to him to finish his prisoner objective.

12C · RASGRUND & THE WARDEN
Take the trapped ravine slowly and secure Janeva
□
11. Activate Rasgrund’s objective and travel toward Dusk Mesa.
Fast-travel near Meridian Village and follow your active marker to the ravine at Dusk Mesa, southwest of Meridian. The approach resembles a narrow rock cleft/cave; this is not an open-field pursuit.
□
12. Use Focus to spot his Blaze traps BEFORE climbing.
Rasgrund has booby-trapped the ravine. Scan forward every few metres for proximity explosives and shoot dangerous traps from a safe distance. Do not sprint into the gorge or attempt to shortcut across the top.
□
13. Follow the intended climbing route through the ravine.
Use the visible yellow handholds, crossing rope and wooden log. Continue south/southeast as the objective leads you past a gap, a drop into water, and another set of handholds. Clear any visible traps before each jump.
□
14. Reach Rasgrund’s elevated rock platform.
Finish the climb to the isolated platform with Rasgrund. His confrontation resolves through the scripted scene; there is no need to force an ordinary melee duel. Wait until his objective is confirmed complete.
□
15. Return to Sunstone Rock; report to Janeva.
Fast-travel to the prison. Speak to Janeva inside and report the three fugitives: Ullia, Gavan, Rasgrund. Confirm that Sunstone Rock appears under Completed Side Quests.
REQUIRED FOR 79/79Janeva is now secured for All allies joined
This is the one achievement-sensitive quest in the three-quest chapter. It does not pop an achievement now. Janeva must be eligible to assist during The Looming Shadow much later.
IF THE CAMP WAS ALREADY CLEAREDDo not “re-clear” Blackwing Snag
The bandit-camp completion and Ullia’s prisoner objective are distinct. A green camp icon is fine; only the Sunstone Rock quest completion matters for Janeva.

12D · COLLATERAL
Follow Olin’s lead to the guarded farmstead
□
16. Select the Collateral errand.
After The City of the Sun, the game places Collateral in the Errands tab, even if you chose to kill Olin. Activate it. We are rescuing his wife and child, not recruiting an ally.
□
17. Travel to the Spurflints farmstead search area.
Use the quest marker in the Spurflints, near Copper Deeps. A useful reference is the campfire south of the Copper Deeps Tallneck. Follow the road south until the objective changes to investigate the area.
□
18. If Olin is alive, speak to him first.
Olin can be found near the base of a rock formation close to the objective area. He can guide you to the farmstead. If you killed him, skip this step and follow the marker/Focus to the Eclipse-occupied farmstead yourself.
□
19. Choose whether Olin assists in the fight.
If alive, Olin offers to join a direct assault. Accepting is fine; he draws attention. If you prefer stealth, go alone and approach from the raised ridge south of the buildings. Neither choice affects achievements.
□
20. Scan the entire farmstead before attacking.
Tag the cultists with Focus. Look for an archer on the southern ledge and a tougher heavy gunner carrying a Deathbringer Gun. Starting from the ledge gives good cover and headshot opportunities.
GOOD ACHIEVEMENT OPPORTUNITYHuman headshots and 3 Strikes From Above
If you are still missing either, this small camp has human enemies and elevated ledges. Make final blows headshots and use aerial stealth kills when feasible; do not delay the quest if both achievements already popped.
