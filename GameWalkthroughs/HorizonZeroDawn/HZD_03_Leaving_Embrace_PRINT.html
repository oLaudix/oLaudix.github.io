03A · Back to Rost's House

CHAPTER 03
PAGE 1 / 2
START HERE: You have completed The Womb of the Mountain, defeated the Corruptor outside Mother's Watch, and your main quest is now A Seeker at the Gates. Do not follow its Strider marker yet. You already collected Metal Flower Mark I (A) in the childhood ruins and Power Cells #1 and #2; we are not revisiting those ruins.

Outside Mother's Watch — before leaving

  1. 1. Knock down the TWO dummies outside the Mother's Watch gate.

    Stay outside the wooden palisade of Mother's Watch — don't search the village or All-Mother Mountain. At the entrance facing the open Embrace, look to both sides of the approach to the gate. There is one Grazer-shaped training dummy on each side. Use a heavy spear attack on both. These are not the two Mother's Heart dummies from Chapter 02.

    Expected total if the earlier 11 were counted: 13 / 23. Check Notebook → Statistics → Other → Training Dummies.

  2. 2. At the campfire outside Mother's Watch, save and check the nearby merchant.

    Find the merchant beside the campfire on the road out of Mother's Watch. Open the merchant's Special Items tab. Purchase Map — Metal Flowers, Map — Vantages, Map — Ancient Vessels, and Map — Banuk Figures if they're available and you can afford them. They show approximate search areas on your map, not exact object positions.

Detour — Metal Flower Mark I (B)

  1. 3. Set a custom waypoint to Rost's House (your old home).

    Open the world map and find Rost's House in the western part of the Embrace — the house where Chapter 01 began. Set a waypoint there. Follow roads or use the nearby campfire for fast travel if available. Don't follow the yellow main-quest marker to the Strider site yet.

  2. 4. At the house, locate Rost's grave, then return to the house itself.

    The grave is a small optional story stop nearby. After visiting it, return to the house and its fenced property. The gate we need is the WEST / BACK gate of Rost's property — the one that was locked when you attempted this flower before The Proving. It should now be open.

  3. 5. Go through the WEST gate and follow the downhill garden path.

    Walk through the back gate, past Rost's small garden area. Continue along the path and look to the RIGHT for a zipline. This is not the east-gate zipline that you used to meet Rost during The Point of the Spear.

  4. 6. Ride the zipline and immediately take the tightrope to the cliff ledge.

    At the bottom of the zipline, find the horizontal rope you can walk across. Balance over it toward the nearby rocky ledge. At the end, look for the triangle of small purple flowers on the ground. Pick up the Metal Flower in the center: Mark I (B).

  5. 7. Leave via the climbing ledges / nearby path.

    The zipline is one-way. Use the climbable ledges and path leading away from the flower to get back up; alternatively, open the map and fast travel to an unlocked campfire if you have a Fast Travel Pack. You now have 2 / 30 Metal Flowers (unless you found extras).

PAUSE CHECK: Two Mother's Watch dummies down; Mark I (B) collected; collectible maps purchased if available. The next page begins wherever you finish the flower detour.

03B · A Seeker at the Gates

CHAPTER 03
PAGE 2 / 2
START HERE: Metal Flower Mark I (B) is collected. You're now returning to the A Seeker at the Gates main-quest marker. The game wants you to override a Strider and travel to the Main Embrace Gate, where Varl is waiting.

Override a Strider — first mount

  1. 8. Activate A Seeker at the Gates in the Quests menu.

    Open the map and follow the highlighted Strider Site objective in the central Embrace. It's the same general area where you collected Blaze from Striders with Rost in The Point of the Spear. You do not need to guess which herd to use; follow the game's actual quest marker.

  2. 9. Crouch in tall grass near the Striders. Do not shoot the one you want to ride.

    Scan the Striders with the Focus to see their positions. Wait until one walks close to the edge of the herd, away from Watchers. Approach it from behind while crouching. The Corruptor component you looted at Mother's Watch lets you override these machines.

  3. 10. When the “Override” prompt appears, hold the indicated key.

    Stay near the Strider and hold the interaction key until the override finishes. The machine's lights should turn blue, showing it is friendly. If spotted before the override finishes, retreat into cover and try again on another Strider.

  4. 11. Mount your newly overridden Strider.

    Stand beside the blue-lit machine and use the Mount prompt. Mounting is part of the quest objective. Follow the yellow marker toward the Main Embrace Gate on the eastern side of the Embrace. Stay on the main road; you don't need to stop for random machine fights.

At the Main Embrace Gate — stop here

  1. 12. Arrive at the Main Embrace Gate and go through.

    Ride up to the large guarded wooden gate where the quest marker ends. Pass through it for the scene about the attack on the Nora. Dismount if necessary.

  2. 13. Before leaving, check the merchant by the Main Embrace Gate.

    Look for a merchant near the gate. If the four collectible maps were unavailable earlier, check the Special Items tab here. Otherwise save your shards for useful equipment.

  3. 14. Speak with Varl immediately beyond the gate.

    Varl explains that Nora War-Chief Sona is missing. Finish the conversation to receive the next main quest, The War-Chief's Trail. A Seeker at the Gates is not finished yet; it continues later when you head toward Mother's Crown and Daytower.

  4. 15. Stop at the campfire outside the gate and make a save.

    Do not head toward Daytower or clear the nearby Corrupted Zones on this printout. Chapter 04 will begin here at the Main Embrace Gate and cover the first Vantage, nearby Corrupted Zone, Nora Hunting Grounds, and the route toward Sona.
